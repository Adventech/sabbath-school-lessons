---
title:  Christ’ Thawhkikna Tangko
date:   16/08/2026
---

Paul in lungdamthu bulphuh in 1 Cor. 15 kipansak hi. Lungdamthu a gen ciangin: (1) Corinth mite kiangah a thugen pen (2) amau un zong a saan uh pen, (3) amau mahun a telcian uhpen, (4) amau un hotkhiat a ngahna uh pen (1 Cor. 15: 1, 2) cihi. Hih kamhonna in, hong pailai ding munte ah eite hotkhiat ihihna dingin Christ thawhkikina in thu phamawh khat ahihna hong theikhol sak hi (Rom. 10: 9, 10). Tua thawhkikna in lungdam thu’ a dingin a thubulpi mah ahih manin; tuathu anial mite in Christ sungah upna a nial mah hipah uhhi.

`1Cor. 15: 1–4; Luka 24: 44–47; leh Rom. 1: 1–4 simin. Hihmun tengpan thu kibang bang om hiam?`

1 Cor. 15: 1–4 sungah, Paul’ thupuak a tom-cing hi. “Laisiangtho sunga ombang” a cihpen Laisiangtholui kammal pawlkhat leh a buppi zong acihna hithei kha ding hi. Zeisu sihna thawhkikna in Laisiangtholui sunga Pasian’ kamciam teng picing sak hi.

`1Cor. 15: 2, 11 simin. Hih Laisiangtho munte in upna leh thuhilhna pen banghang in liangko kikim sak hiam? Hih tegel kizopna bang omthei ding hiam?`

Christ thokik takpi hi cih a umte peuhmah in tua thawhkikna pen tangthu piang taktak hi cih up photphot kul hi. Hiteng ah, 1Cor. 15: 5-8 in laisiangthothak sungah mun thupi khat mahah hong om hi. Christ a thawhkik khit ciangin mi tampi mah in mu ngeingai uh a, tua a mu mite zong tuni in nungta gige lai cih Paul in hihlai a gelh hunlai in gen hi (1Cor. 15: 6).

Bulpat mahmah leng, Paul in, amaute kiangah pai unla amau’ muhna dong un a cihna ahi hi. Christ thokik takpi cihthu amah in kiptak in a pulaakna ahi hi.

Tua mite pen mitmuh teci te ahi uhhi. Zeisu in “hihthu khempeuh a thei tecite” (Luka 24: 48) a cihte hi zawsop ding uhhi.

`Christ’ thawhkikna uptheihna ding bang thu paulam peuh i nei uhhiam? Eima’ mitnuh lohpipi khawng, leitung vai leh vantung thu khawng uptheih ding bangteng peuh i neithei ding uhhiam?`
