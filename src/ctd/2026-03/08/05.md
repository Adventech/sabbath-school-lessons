---
title:  A Thokik Pumpi
date:   19/08/2026
---

1Cor. 15: 35–39 sungah, Paul in a thokik pumpi tawh kisai a tomcing in pulaak kik hi. Tuavai pen dotna nihtawh kamhong hi: “Misite koibang in ki phongkik hiam? Bang pumpi tawh hong tho ding hiam?” (1Cor. 15: 35). Hih dotnate pen 1 Cor. 15: 36-49) sungah dawngkhin zo hi.

`1Cor. 15: 36–41 simin. Hihmun in 1Cor. 15: 35 sunga dotnate koibang in dawngkik hiam?`

Paul in, thawhkikni ciangin thu piang ding etkaak theih ding thuthum hong pia hi. Amasa, (1Cor. 15: 36-38) in pumpi pen khaici tawh kibang a, singkung khat hong suahna dingin tua khaici a sihphotphot kul cihi. Athuhilhna kician petmah: thawhkikna cihpen Pasian’sep nalamdang hi cihi. Anihna, a kibang pumpi (1Cor. 15: 39, 40); i leitung ah Pasian in ganhing’ pumpi tawh a kibanglo, kimlehpam tawh a kilawm dingin mihing’ pumpi hongpia hi. Tua mahbang in mihing’ pumpi in vantung nangawn tawh kilawmlel ding hi. A deihna bulpi pen minthang pumpi in tawmkhat mah hoihzaw hamtang cihna hi a (1Cor. 15: 40, 41), minthanna tawh a thokik pumpi in, amasa leitunga apuksa pumpilui sangin hoihzaw tham ding cihna ahi hi.

Hihthu in, hi leitung pumpi leh a thokik pumpi a tehkaakna namlii tengtawh kitel cian mahmah lai hi. Hih leitung pumpi in leivui, kisiabaih, thanem in cilehsa hibek hi. A thokik pumpi in vanpumpi, kisiatheilo, thahat in khalam pumpi hi (1Cor. 15: 40–44). Tua bang acih ciangin tua namnih tegel kizopna neilo a cihna hilo hi. Paul in Greekpau _sōma_ “pumpi” cihpen; akiphum pumpi leh a thokik pupi kizopna ah zangkhawm veve hi. Leh etkik zawleng, a tunga tehkaakna li tengin zong kizoplohna omsak veve hi. I pumpi thak pen ahihleh, tu laitakin a muat thei pumpi tawh kibanglo ding hi.

Paul in “khalam thu” peuhmah a omlo nate tawh tehkaak lo hi. Zeisu in “a ginalo hih pumpi pen Ama’ aa mahbang in a minthang pumpi in hong kheelsak ding hi” (Phil. 3: 21). Pumpi taktak mah nei ding hang a, ahizong in muatna kisiatna omlo ding hi. Tu laitak in muatna, natna leh sihna kithuak zel ahih manin; hibangte a omlo pumpi Zeisu’ hong kamciam pen ngaihsut haksa kisa mahmah hi.

`Tu a lei pumpi in ciangtan neilua ahih manin, thuakzawhna anei dingin hih leipumpi pen hong kheelsak ding cih kamciam in koibang in hong lungmuang sak hiam?`
