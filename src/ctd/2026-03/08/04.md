---
title:  Christ, Gah Masa
date:   18/08/2026
---

Zeisu a nuntak keinakleh, leitung ah i lam-etna teng khempeuh pen khemna vive hiding hi (1 Cor. 15:12–19). “A mantakpi ci leng Christ thokik taktak hi” (1Cor. 15:20). Zeisu’ thawhkikna pen thu piangtaktak ahi hi. Tua tawh kizui in Christ hong paikikni ciangin, Christ sungah a sisa mite khempeuh hong thokik ding uhhi (1Cor. 15: 20–23).

`1Cor. 15: 20–23 simin. Zeisu in “gahmasa” a cihin bang a cihnopna hiam?`

Tulaitak in hih leitung hunsia teng khempeuh pen, Christ sunga asi mite thawhkik na tawh kizosiang ta ding hi (1Cor. 15: 24; Mang. 20: 5, 6). Adam nunung penpa Christ in, hih kumpigam leh ukzawhna teng lakik in Pa kiangah aap kik ding hi (1Cor. 15: 25–28). Christ in Pasian’ khutnuai ah Amah ki-aap in (1Cor. 15: 28) Adam leh Christ kizop na omzia hong theihsakna ahi hi. Hotkhiatna ngiimna sungah Adam masazaw in a hihzawh loh thu ahi, Pa’ deihna nuai ah ki-aapna pen Adam nunungpenpa (1Cor. 15: 45) Zeisu amah ngiat in hihzo hi.

1 Cor. 15: 29–34 sungah, Paul in, Christ’ thawhkikna nialna a haihuaina a tomcing in gen hi. Thu ummi khatin, Christ’ sihna leh a thawhkikna sungah, Christ mahtawh a ki pumkhatna pen; kituiphumna tawh Christ in limlah (Rom. 6:3, 4; Col. 2:12) ahih manin, a thawhkik takpina nialna bangmah neilo hi. A ngaihsut haksa khat panin Paul mahin “misite tang dingin a kituiphum te” (1 Cor. 15:29) a cihpen ahi hi.

“Tuatawh kisai ngaihsutna tampi om lakpan a kilawmpen ding khatin; a sikhinsa itlehngaihte tawh thawhkikni ciangin kimuhkik ding ngiimna tawh, kituiphumna a cihnop na hihtuak pen hi. Christ sungah a sisa a etteh taakcing mite’ nundan a dawnthukna zong hihtheih tuak hi. Hihthu ah, misite tangding in kituiphumna sangin, misite hangin kitui phumna hihtuak penzaw hi”—Carl P. Cosaert, “1 Corinthians,” Andrews Bible Commentary: New Testament (Berrien Springs, MI: Andrews University Press, 2022), p. 1652.

A nihna ah, thawhkikna a omkeileh, sihtheihna sungah gamtatna in zong khiatna nei tuanlo hi (1Cor. 15: 30–32). Leitung gualnopna sunga kibual lelleng hoh hampha zawsop lai ding hihang (1Cor. 15: 32).

`2Tim. 1: 12 sunga Paul’ kammalte lungngai kikin. Eima’ mailam thu ding zong koibang in lungmuang thei ding ihi hiam?`
