### Tukalsung Simding
1 Cor. 15; Luka 24: 44–47; Mang. 20: 5, 6; Col. 2: 12; 2 Tim. 1: 12; 1Thess. 4: 13–17.

> <p>Kamngah</p>
> “Christ a kithokik saklo hileh kote’ thuhilhte in amawkna hi ding a; note’ thu upna zong amawkna mah suak ding hi. Christ kithokik sak lo hileh note upna zong amawkna hi ding a; na mawhna sung mahah note zong a omlai nahi ding uhhi” (1 Cor. 15: 14, 17).

Paul’ hunlai pekin zong misite’ thawhkikna a nial zong na omkhinzo liang hi. Sihna in mihing pumpi tungah banghih cih tua mite in thei uhhi. Mi’ si-luang in tuisuah in, tuakhit leivui a suahkhit ciangin bangmah hinawnlo ta hi. Tanglai pekpan mahin mite sizel uh cih thei uhhi. Amau’ nuntakmaa in zong mite sisikhin uh cihzong thei uhhi.

Misite thawhkikna ding thu pen, tulai mite a dingin a hithei ding mahbang in a gen hihtuak lel hi. Tua hang mahin Paul in a gen kul liangtak hi.

Zong thupi veve tham hi. Zeisu thokik keileh; Amah mahmah zong Ama’ cihbang mipa hilo pah ding a; singlamteh in muibun nawnlo in, eima mawhna leiba zong kiloh lo ding hi. Lungkiatna sungbek ah eizong omcip ding hihang. Ahihhang, i Topa thokikzo a, vantungah kahto khinzo in, eite hong ciahpih dingin hong paisuk kik ding hi.

Tukal in 1 Cor. 15 sunga Christ’ thawhkikna bulphuh ding hihang. Corinth mite pawlkhat in, amau’ kimkot a leitung mite huzaapna bangin thawhkikna omkei peuh ci mawkmawk uhhi. Tua thu a dawnthukna in Paul in Christ’ thawhkikna bekmah eite’ a ding hotkhiatna lam-etna khatbek om cihi.
