Ellen G. White, “God’s People Delivered,” pp. 635–652, in The Great Controversy.

“Christ in Pasian ahihna pen thu ummite a ding tawntung nunna khamuanna hi. Zeisu in ‘Kei hong um mite, a sih hangin a nungta ding hi: Kei hong umin a nungta mi in a singei nawnkei ding hi. Hihthu na um hiam?’ cihi. Hiteng ah Christ in Ama’ hongpai kikna galdon et in a pauna ahi hi. Misiangtho a sisate zong kisiatheilo dingin phongkik ding a; a nungtalai midikte zong kikheel in sihna ciamlo in vantung kahto ding uhhi. Hi tengah Christ in nalamdang a bawlsa Lazarus a photkikna pen; misiangtho a sisate thawhkik na’ng lim lahkholna zong ahi hi”—Ellen G. White, The Desire of Ages, p. 530.

“Pasian’ Tapa in a ihmu misiangthote a photkikna awging hangin leitung kinak liin mahmah hi. Amaute unzong tua photna aw dawngkik uh in a minthang pumpi tawh hong thoto uh a ‘Gualzawhna, sihna leh han tungah gualzawhna! Sihna aw na thahatna koi ah om hiam? Sihna aw, nagu koi ah om hiam? Han aw, na gualzawhna koi ah omta hiam?’ (1Cor. 15: 55) ci in kiko ding uhhi.

Tua ciangin a nungtalai misiangtho a thokikte in amau’ awte sangsak in gual zawhna awngin kiko ding uhhi. Natna leh sihna thahatna tawh hansungah a lumsuksa pumpite in; sihtheih nawnlohna cidamna tawh teitang takin hong thokhia ding uhhi. A nungtalai gige misiangthote ahih uhleh mitphialkal sung khatin kikheel uh in hong tho khia mite tawh, meii tungah i Topa dawntuah ding uhhi. Kimuhkhopna in bangzah minthang ding hiam! Sihna tawh a kikhen lawm it-te tawh kikhen ngei nawnlo dingin kikhawlkhawm kik ta ding uhhi”—EGWhite, The Story of Redemption, pp. 411, 412.

**Kikupding Dotnate:**

`Christ’ thawhkikna mitmuh tecite thu lungngai kik in (Sawl. 1: 22; 2: 32; 3: 15; 4: 33; 5: 30–32). Tanglai kum tulnihlai in Ama’ thawhkikna tecite koibang kihithei lai mawk ding hiam?`

`Christ’ thawhkikna in lungdamthu thulaigil khat ahi hi. (1Cor. 15: 1–4). Thawh kikna omlo hileh, i tangkoko Christ’ sihna zong a mawkna hiding hi (1Cor. 15: 14). Christ mahmah in zong mawkish hiding hi. Banghang? Christ’ thawhkikna vang koibang in nagenkhia thei ding hiam?`

`Paul’ kammal kiukiau ngaihsun dihin: “misite thokiklo hizen leh ‘eite in ne in dawn lel ni, zingciang silel ding hihang’” (1Cor. 15: 32). A cihnop bulpi bang hiam?`

`Na khan sungvuah misite’thu kikum un. Gupngahte vantung ah kilato pahpah hi leh, 1Cor. in banghang in thupi mello ding hiam?`
