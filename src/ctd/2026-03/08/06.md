---
title:  Sihna Tungah Anunungpen Gualzawhna
date:   20/08/2026
---

`1Cor. 15: 54–57 simin. Hihmun in sihna tungah a nunungpen gualzawhna koibang in hong gen hiam?`

1Cor. 15 pen, Paul in, a tumdang deuh thukhat pulaakna tawh kamhong hi: “Cileh sa in Pasian gam luah theilo” (1Cor. 15: 50) cihi. Lasiangthosim tampite in, Paul in van-tung ah nakhat peuh in ki omlo cihthu gen hi ci uhhi. Ahihhang Paul’ gen pen thu tuampi khat hizaw hi. 1Cor. 15: 50 sung tehkaakna in “cilehsa” pen “kisiatna” tawh teh- kaak a: “Pasian’ ukna gam” ahileh “a kisia theilo” cihi. 1Cor. 15: 42-49 sunga thupiang mahbang in, hiteng ah zong Paul in tu a lei-pumpi (misiluang) leh a thokik pumpi tehkaak hi. A kiphum pumpi in kisiathei leh sihna pumpi hi a; a thokik pumpi ahihleh a kisiathei lo leh sitheilo pumpi cihi (1Cor. 15: 50, 53, 54). A baihpen dingin, hih pumpi in vantung a luahtheihna dingin, a khengval kikheelna khatmah a kantan kul ding hi.

A tomin, Paul in kisiat theihna leh sihtheihna pen eima’ pianpih mawhna pumpi cihi. Jew mite laigelhna sungah, “cilehsa” cihin apuk mihing hihna hi a, Ama’ hong paini ciangin tuategel natna satna teng khempeuh kipuahpha ding hi.

Mawhnei mihing ihihna hemkhia in (1Cor. 15: 54) minthanna sungah lut (1Cor. 15: 51–53; 1Thess. 4:13–17) ciangin, “gualzawhna in sihna khuplet zohi” (1Cor. 15: 54) a cihpen tangtung ding hi. Tuaciang in, hangsan laulo takin “Sihna aw, na thahatna koi ah om hiam? Hell aw, na gualzawhna koi ah om hiam?” (1Cor. 15: 55) ci in la kisata ding hi. Hiteng khempeuh in Christ hong paikikni ciangin a piangding teng ahi uhhi (1Cor. 15: 51, 52).

Ngaihsun limlim in: Sihna sungah mit i sitsuk khit ciangin i muhkik masakpen dingin Zeisu hong paisukna mah hipah ding a, misite Amah’n phong ding hi. Thu ummi khat a sihkhit kum tultampi sawtkhin tamahleh, “mitphiat kalsung, pengkul nunung hong gin ciangin, misite in a sithei nawnlo dingin hong tho ding a; i vekpi in ikikheel ding uh hi” (1Cor. 15: 52).

`Nuntakhun tomlua ci in a phun ngeilo om ding hiam? Zeisu hong paibaih mahmah cihpen eimahtek in muding hihang. Ama’ hongpai ciangin i ngaihsut masakpen dingin “Kalaisai, Topa, hong paibaih takmah ciai” peuh cikha sop ding hihang. Hih ngaihsutna in “zekaina” koibang in hong telsiamsak naci hiam?`
