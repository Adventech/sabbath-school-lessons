---
title:  A Thokik Christ bekmah Eima’ Lam-etna!
date:   17/08/2026
---

1Cor. 15: 9–19 sungah, Paul in, thawhkikna nialna hangin a lauhuai a sulzuite thu Paul in hilhcian hi. Thawhkikna omlo tawh thu ummite in tu leh mailam ah lam-etna bangmah neilo hi.

`1Cor. 15: 9–19 simin. Christ thokik kei zenmawk leh bang kisuplawh hiam?`

Tanglai lawkite a tamzaw un thawhkikna umlo uh a, a phadiak in Greek mite in kha leh pumpi khenkhia in pumpi a sih ciangin kha ahihleh ama’ painop nana ah pai ci in um uhhi. 1Cor. 15: 12-19 sungteng pen Paul in dotna khat tawh pan in, thuthukte pulaak khia hi. Note pawlkhat in koibang teng in misite’ thawhkikna omlo ciliang zawzen nahi uhhiam? (1Cor. 15: 12 laikungpu letna). Tualai in mitmuh teci tampi omlai ahih manin thawhkikna uplohna peuhmah pen kisaangthei hetlo hi (1Cor. 15: 5-8). Tuasang a suuk zaw thamlai khatpen, thawhkikna omlo hileh; amaute’ lam-etna in zuauthu tungah kinga hi a, amau zong mawhna sung mahah omciplai ahi uhhi.

Misite’ thawhkikna a om keileh, Paul in, (1) Christ thokiklo (1Cor. 15: 13, 16); (2) eite’ thuhilhna in amawkna (1Cor. 15: 14); (3) eima’ upna in amawkna (1Cor. 15: 14); (4) eite zong teci zuaute (1Cor. 15: 15); (5) eima’ upna in a mawkna (1Cor. 15: 17); (6) eite mawhna sungmah ah omlai (1Cor. 15: 17); (7) misisa te zong a mawkna mah hi veve (1Cor. 15 :18) cihi.

Thawhkikna a omkeileh, thuhilhna leh upna in amawkna hi (1Cor. 15: 14). Greek pau in “amawkna” cihpen _kenos_ hi. Letkhiat siam mahmah hinapi zailua liang hi. A let mite in zong _kenos_ pen “ahawm” cihna hi a, thumaan omlo “maanlo, muibun lo, ngiimna neilo, mungtup omlo, amawkna” cihte kikumkum uh cihi.

A khiatna taktak bangpeuh hitaleh, thawhkikna omlohna mun limlim ah; upna pen a mawkna cih Greek pau in _mataios_ (1Cor. 15: 17) ahi hi. Hih _mataios_ pen _kenos_ tawh kilamdang lua khollo ahihhang, a deihna bulpi pen; Zeisu nungta keileh, upna pen a mawkna, khemna hilel a: i mawhna tengzong maisak nailoh hiveve hi (1Cor. 15:17). Eite zong tecizuau ihi ding uh a, mi a khem leh khem a thuak ihi ding uhhi (1Cor. 15: 15).

`Misite pawlkhat vanah, pawlkhat hell ah lengmang pahpah mawkleh; 1 Cor. 15 pen i koicih mawktam maw? Misite ihmu uh cihpen telcian mahmah ding bang hang in thupi thei ding hiam?`
