---
title:  Abeisa Hunte pan Pilna Sinna
date:   28/07/2026
---

Paul in eima pumpi nialna pen ama’ tuahkhak thute bulphuh in, milim’ buaina lamah hong kiheita hi. Thukhat panin, 1Cor. 9: 27 sunga thu pen 1Cor. 10 sungah picin sakna hi a, amah mahmah mi ginalo a suahlohna dingin, amah mahmah a kideek kidaam ngiatpa ahi hi. Corinth mite in amah etteh saknuam mahmah a, a ettehtaak penpa in Zeisu hizaw cih zong theisak nuam hi (1Cor. 11: 1).

`1Cor. 10: 7–11 simin. Israel in sehneel lakah bang mawhna bawl uh a, amau aki piatuam hamphatna hangin tua mawhna koibangin suksia semthei hiam?`

1Cor. 10: 1–5 sungah, gamlak a Pasian mite’ thu Paul in hemgen kiau hi. Meiipi leh tuipi in Pasian’ makaihna, ompihna leh kepcinna teng hong phawksak kik hi. Tua khit ciangin, annek tuidawn in zong Pasian’ vaihawm sakna hong phawksak leuleu hi. Israel in meii leh tuipi sungah a omna uh zongpen kituiphumna tawh kipawlsak a, Christian te kituiphumna tawh kibang sak hi. Tua mahbang in annek leh tuidawn zong Paul mah in Topa’ nitak-an ci in genteh lailai hi.

Thukhat panin, 1Cor. 10 in, Christian te in Israel’ phutkhak tengmah a phukha a cihna zong ahi hi. Ahihhang tua tangthu banglian zilkik nuam nawnlo ahih manin, Israel mite tangthu suutphapha kik hi. Israel mite’n hamphat na’ng tampi nei uh hinapi’n, mi tampi in a sia vive utzaw tham uh (1Cor. 10: 6) a, milim biakna (1Cor. 10: 7), zahmawh zualzangna (1Cor. 10: 8) cihte ahi hi. Amaute tungah “Pasian lungkimlo” (1Cor. 10: 5) a cihpen lamdang sakhuai hetlo hi.

Israel mite in mawhna lianpi bawl uhhi ci in kawklel ding baihmahmah hinapi, Paul in, Christ’ tangthu tampi a theitel mahmah Christian te in, hamphatna tuamtuam te nei uh hinapi’n mawhna lian bawl zawtham uh hihtuak cihi. Tua ahih manin vauna hong piakna sungah “lungkip takin ka omzo hi ci in a kingaihsunte a pukkhak lohna dingin kikem uh hen” (1Cor. 10: 12) cihi.

“A kingaihsunte” a cihpen, pawlkhat in amau apukthei ding dinmun ah a omlam a kiphawk hetlo om hihtuak hi. Tuni in tuabang i om kha diam? “Lungkip takin ka omzo hi ci in a kingaihsunte a pukkhak lohna dingin kikem uh hen”. Hih vauna angah khalo kua peuh i omkha tam maw?

`Eima’ thuakzawhloh ding zia-etna hong kipialo Laisiangtho in cihi. “Ahih hang suahtakna ding lampi hong honsak” (1Cor. 10: 13) zel cihi. A tuapipi mah banghang in mawhna sung kitukbaih lailai thei hiam?`
