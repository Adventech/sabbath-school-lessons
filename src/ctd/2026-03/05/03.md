---
title:  Angkhuallo Itna
date:   27/07/2026
---

`1Cor. 9: 1–6 simin. Hihmun in itna tawh eima’ pumpi kinialna a khiatna pen gamtatna tawh koibang in hong lakkhia hiam?`

1Cor. 9 sung etpak in, Paul in ama’ Sawltak dinmun a kemcing bang hipak lel a, atung lama pilna leh itna tawh kisai agenna teng tawh kipawl khollo bang ahi hi. Laisiang tho cihpen acil apat in alian aneu a kikhen sitsetsa in kigelh hituanlo cih phawk in. Tua a hih manin, Paul in Cor. 9 sungah a gelh thute pen a masiah a tetawh kizomlo zong hituan lo hi. 1 Cor. 9 in, Christ leh i sanggamte tungah angkhuallo itna neihdan gamtatzia hong hilhcian ahi hi. Itna hang bekbek in Paul in ama’ hamphat na’ng pawlkhat nusia hi.

“Annek Tuidawn” (1Cor. 9: 4). Hitengah annek tuidawn acihpen sumtang tawh panpih na a cihnopna hipi pen hi. Sawltak khat ahihna tawh, Paul in ama’ kep pawlpi mite kiang pan panpihna a ngah ding ahi hi. Biakna makai dangte in tuabang ahih hunuh zong om hi. Himah taleh Paul in tuabang hihlo in, puanbuk khuina tawh amah kivaak hi (Sawl. 18: 3).

“Thu ummi zi tonpih thei” (1Cor. 9: 5). Zineisa Sawltak khat in Pasian nasep khualzinna ah pawlpi panpihna tawh azi tonpih thei hi. Tuabang nupa te lakah Priscilla leh Aquila (Sawl. 16: 3), Andronicus leh Junia (Rom. 16: 7) cihte om uhhi. Ahihhang Paul in zi neilo (1Cor. 7: 8) hi. Zi nanei lel peuhleh, a nupa un pawlpi’ panpihna tawh khualzin khawmthei lelpeuh ding hi.

“Nektawm zon tawh thuah ding hilo” (1Cor. 9: 6). Paul leh Barnabas a nihgel un, Pasian’ nasep khualzinna sungah thaman ngah theilua uhhi (1Cor. 9: 4-6). Paul pen a nuntak na’ng in puanbuk khuipa hi (Sawl. 18: 3) a, Barnabas’ nasep bel kitheihpih khalo hi. Amah pen a kipiazo mahmah mipa (Sawl. 4: 36, 37) ahih manin, amah leh amah ki vaak tawm lel hi.

1 Cor. 9: 7-11 sungah, Paul in 1Cor. 9: 6 sunga thuteng mah kipsak in, amah leh Banabas in pawlpi’ panpihna tawh nuntak theihna’ng thu om naci hi (1Cor. 9: 11, 12). Topa tektek in hong thupiakna ah “lungdamna thu a hilhte in tua nasepna mah panin nuntakna a ngah ding uhhi” (1Cor. 9: 14; 1Tim. 5: 18). Ahihtei hangin “hih hamphatna bangmah ka hong lakei uhhi” (1Cor. 9: 12) cihi. Tuabang in Paul in angkhual lohna lim khat lahkhia (1Cor. 9: 1-18) in,Corinth ah lungdamthu hilhna tungpan hamphatna teng nial zaw hi (1Cor. 9: 19-23).

`Nangma’ hamphat theihna ding omgige napi, hong hehpihna muibun takin teci pan theihna dingin; nutsiatna tawh a hoihzaw thei ding bangteng hiam?`
