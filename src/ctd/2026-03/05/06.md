---
title:  Milim Biakna tungah Gualzawhna
date:   30/07/2026
---

1Cor. 8: 1–3 sungah, itna in milim biakna pan hong kemcing hi, Paul in ci hi. Hih thu suutpha kik in 1Cor. 10: 23-11: 1 sungah picin hi. 1Cor. 8: 3 sungah eite in Pasian i itna gen hi. Tualai ah, “a kuamapeuh in note’ angsung ding bek zong kei unla; midangte phat tuamna ding zong zaw un” (1Cor. 10: 24) cihi. Tua in midangte itna ahi hi.

`Maku 10: 17–22 leh Maku 12: 28–31 simin. Hihmun tegel a kibatna bang om a, 1Cor. 10 sunga dinmun bang ciangin koibang in zang ding ihi hiam?`

Maku 12: 28- 31 sungah Zeisu in alian thukham nih: Pasian itna leh midang itna a henkhawm mahbang in, Paul in zong 1 Cor. 10 sungah tua mahbangin hih hi. A hau tangvalpa’ tangthu (Maku 10: 17-22) sungah, Zeisu in hih ki-itna thukham tegel henkop leuleu a, tualai ah zong Thkna 6: 4 (Maku 10: 18) leh (Maku: 10: 19) sunga thukham suangpek nihna mah na hemgen veve lai hi.

Tua tangval haupa’ buainapi pen, Pasian leh a vengte sangin ama’ neihsa teng itzaw tham ahi hi (Maku 10: 22). Vantung sumkholna te sangin ama’ neihsa teng manpha sazaw hi. Mi zawngte sangin ama’ sum manpha sazaw hi (Maku 10: 21). Amah milim biapa hipah hi.

Zeisu’ thuhilhna tawh kizui in, Zeisu in, nakhempeuh sangin Pasian itzawkna leh eimah ki it mahbang in midangte itna cih thukhun pen 1Cor. 10: 27, 28 sungah pulaksa mahbang in, tua bang dinmun akisapna munah zong zat theih dingin Paul in a hemin hong hanthawn hi. A cihnopna bulpi in, a hithei nakhempeuh in midang khat lungsim a sukkhak leh hoihna piangsaklo (1Cor. 10: 23) cihi.

Hih thukhun mahmah pen “nahih na khempeuh uh Pasian’ min thanna dingin hih un” (1Cor. 10: 31) acihna sungah pilvang takin na gawmkhawm thei hi. Pasian’ min thanna dingin hihding a cihtei hang, milim biakna mahmah in mel leh lim tuamtuam tawh hong piangkhia theiveve cih Paul in hong lak hi. Pasian bekmah in a neih den ahi, vangliatna leh minthanna khempeuh dinmun ah a lutthei na khat peuhpeuh pen milim biakna mah hong hiveve hi (Isa. 42: 8).

1Cor. 10: 31-11: 1 sunga Paul’ kammalteng pen 8-10 sungteng a thuteng a ding thukhupna ahi hi. Ama’ hamphat na’ng a zonlohna hong telgen mahmah hi. “Mite in hotkhiatna a ngahtheih na’ng un” (1Cor. 10: 33) cihi. Tua mahmah Christ a ettehna mah hipah hi (1Cor. 11: 1).

`Nangmah na ki it bangin navengte na koici it semsem thei ding nahiam?`
