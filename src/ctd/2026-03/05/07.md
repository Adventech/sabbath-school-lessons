EGWhite, “Idolatry at Sinai,” lai. 315–330, Patriarchs and Prophets.

“Eite khatlehkhat kikhawlhna hoihtak in zangsiam hileng hamphatna lian mah mah ding hi. Vantung hamphatna a ngahsa mikhat peuhmah in, midangte’ lampi tungah khuavak tangsak ding vaipuak neipahpah hi. Tua hileh Pasian a it takpi mite in, amau pumpi angkhual milim biakna pan suakta pah ding uhhi”—Ellen G. White, in The Advent Review and Sabbath Herald, November 18, 1884, p. 730.

“Paul in eima kampau gamtat te in midang tungah koibang in sukha thei ahih manin eileh ei ki sittel dingin ama’ sanggamte hanthawn hi. Eimah mawhna neilo ding bang kihiphial taleh zong; upna neute a puktheihna ding peuhmah bangmah hihloh ding “nekna dawnna ah Pasian’ min thangsak ding” in hong hanthawn hi”.

“Corinth mite tungah Paul’ vauna kammalte in hun khempeuh a ding leh tuni tu hun eite a ding mahmah zong hithei lai hi. Milim biakna a cihteh milim mahmah bia ngau ngau cihna hi tuanlo a, eima’ ang khualna, gualnopna leh eima’ lunggulhna teng bek picin sak denna cihte zong ahi hi”—EGWhite, The Acts of the Apostles, pp. 316, 317.

“Nangma thuneihna bangin picing takin nasepna tawh Pasian’na sepna akhak ahal thei khat peuhmah na theihkhak leh; hih nawn peuhmah ke’n. Midangte’n thumaan a muh theihlohna dingun a mituh a humsak na peuhma hihke’n. Nakhempeuh a kihihtheih hang, nakhempeuh in phattuamna hong pia theilo hi”—Ellen G. White, Testimonies for the Church, vol. 9, p. 215.

**Kikupding Dotnate:**

`Paul in, a khangham Christian khat’ gamtatna in a khangmoi khat gamtatna khantohna’ng khaktan thei cihi. Koibang danin hithei ding hiam ngaihsun dih in. Na khempeuh tungsiah ah Pasian itna; midangte zong eimah tawh akibangin itna in hih tona dawntheih na’ng lampi khat bek banghang in hithei sese hiam?`

`Christian te in akidop kei uhleh, a biakkhak theihuh bang milim om hiam? Na hoih hinapi, milim i suahtheih na bangteng om hiam? Na don mahmah nakhat in nangma’ milim hong suahlam na koici phawk ding hiam?`

`Paul in midangte thuhilh napi; lungdamthu hilhna ah amah miginalo a suahloh nadingin (1Cor. 9: 27) ama’ pumpi kakideek kidaam cihi. Tukalsung sinna tungpan in; nangmah, lungdamthu hilh dingin hong kilawmsaklo bangthu om hiam?`

`1Cor. 10 sungah, Paul in milim biakna lauhuaina a genna ah “milim biakna pelh un” (1Cor. 10: 14) cihi. Milim biakna banghang in siahuai thei hiam?`
