---
title:  Milim Biakloh ding Vauna
date:   29/07/2026
---

`1Corinth. 10: 5–22 simin. Banghang in milim biakna pelh huai hiam?`

1Cor. 10: 14–22 sunga, Paul in, milim biakna an thu genkik hi. Milim biakna ante cihteh tuni tuhun ngeina tawh kibanglo kha ding a, ahihhang Laisiangtho hunlai in tam mahmah tuak hi. Lawkite biakinn a pasiante tungah gannote tawh abiakna piak uh ciang un, a phellang pen thunei siampite tungah kipia a, amau un tuasa zuak uhhi. Phelkhat leu leu pen mipi sumzuakna (jynfolUaps;) ah kipuak ziau lel hi.

Tua sate pen sa dangte tawh khentuam in kikoih tuanlo in kizuak khawm lel ahih manin, Christian te in theilosa in tua milim biakna sa peuh leikha hiau ding uhhi. Sawltakpa’n ngaihsut a piakna ah tuabang sate suakta takin Christian te in leithei lel uh cihi.

Tuabang a milim biakna sa Christian innkuan in nethei ding uh hitaleh (1Cor. 8: 1-13), tua lawkite biakinn taattung te biakpiakna pawikhaamna sungah Christian te kihel ding kikhaam hi. A thu kiplet bel kitelcian lel hi: milimte in bangmah hilo lel ahih manin Christian te in amau’ inn vuah tua sate suaktak takin nethei ding uh a (1Cor. 8: 4), ahih hang, amau’ pawite in dawi biakna tawh kizom ahih manin; Christian te in tua pawi ah kihelhuai lo hi (1Cor. 10: 20, 21).

Topa’ nitak-an nekna sungah kihelna (1Cor. 10: 16) in Topa tawh kikhawlhna ahih mahbang in tua dawibiakna zeh sungah kihelna zong dawi tawh kikhawlhna ahi hi (1 Cor. 10: 20).

Tua hi a, Paul mahin, “note in Topa tawh nekkhawpna hai sungpan leh dawi tawh nekkhawpna hai sungpan in na dawnkhawm theikei ding uhhi. Topa tawh nekkhawpna an leh dawi tawh nekkhawpna an zong na nekhawm theikei ding uhhi” (1Cor. 10: 21) cihi. Zeisu in zong: “kuamah in To nih’ sila hikhawm theilo hi” (Mate 6: 24) cihi.

Paul mah in, Pasian in lungsim takpi tawh cihtakna deih cihi. Milim biakna in “Topa hehna piangsak” (1Cor. 10: 22) cihi. Tuabang a hihlohna dingin Paul in 1 Cor. 8: 4-6 sungah, a khial ngeilo thukham khat ahi “Topa note Pasian na lungsim khempeuh uh, na nuntakna khempeuh uh leh, na tha hatna khempeuh uh tawh na it ding uhhi” (Thhkna 6: 4, 5) cihi. Thkna 6: 5 sungah, nakhempeuh tungsiah ah Pasian bek itna hi a, Zeisu mah in “Nangmah na ki it mahbang in na vengte na it in” (Maku 12: 31; Siam. 19: 18) cihi.

`Milim icih pen suang milim bekpeuh zong hituanlo hi. Na khat peuhpeuh zong milim kisuah thei lel hi. Nangma nunna sungpan bang milim peuh na paih khiat ding kisam hiam?`
