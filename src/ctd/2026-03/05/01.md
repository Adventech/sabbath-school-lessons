### Tukalsung Simding
1Corinth 8; Sawl. 15:20; 1Cor. 9:1–6; 1Cor. 10:5–22; 	Thkna. 6:4, 5; Maku 12:28–31.

> <p>Kamngah</p>
> “Na hihna khempeuh uh, nekna dawnna ahi zongin, Pasian’ min 	thanna dingin nahih un” (1Corinth 10: 31).

Corinth Masa 8-10 in 5 leh 6 sunga tenga zahmawhvai teng a thukhupna bek thamlo in, Corinth mite in laikhak mahtawh a dotna tenguh (1Cor. 7: 1) a dawnkikna mah hipah hi. Tua dawnna tengin Corinth masa sung buppi huamsuk pah ding hi.

1Cor. 7 sunga, kikheelna a cihcih peh ahihleh, zahmawh zualzangna (1Cor. 5-7) leh milim biakna (1Cor. 8-10) sungteng tawh akizom thulute ahi hi. Laisiangthothak sung ah tuategel tonkhawm in kipulaak hi (Sawl. 15: 20, 29; Sawl. 21: 25: 1Cor. 6: 9; Eph. 5: 5; Col. 3: 5; Mang. 21: 8; Mang. 22: 15).

A huampi in, 1Cor. 5-7 sungah Paul in, zahmawh zualzangna thu khen a; 1Cor. 8-10 sungah ama’ lungkhampi pen in milim biakna ahi hi. Christian peuhmah in tuategel peuhmah pelh ding cihi (1Cor. 6: 18; 1Cor. 10: 14).

Nungkal in, Kha Siangtho biakinn hihna thu mukhin hang (1Cor. 6: 19, 20) a, tua zahmawh zualzangna kipelhthei hi cihi. Tukalsung in, ‘na khempeuh Pasian minthanna ding’ in hihna tawh milim biakna kipelh thei cih muleuleu ding hihang (1Cor. 10: 31).
