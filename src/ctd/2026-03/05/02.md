---
title:  Pilna leh Itna
date:   26/07/2026
---

`1Corinth 8: 1–13 simin. Paul in banghang in pilna leh itna tehkaak sese a, bangthu om hiding hiam? Bang a thupi bawl hiam?`

Thuthukzaw khat a pulaakna dingin Paul in milim biakpiakna an paulam in: midangte itlohna (1Cor. 8) gen hi. Milim biakpiakna an thubuaina in, Corinth pawlpi phel nih suah hi. A omin zong a omlo pasian dangte peuh kitheisak uh in, tuate in nakhem peuh nektheih ding phalna hongpia ci in um uhhi (1Cor. 8: 4). Tuate pen Paul in “a hatte” (1Cor. 4: 10) cihi. Tuabang gamtatna a langdote ahihleh “a thanemte” (1Cor. 8: 9-12) cihi.

Paul in tuate tuabang in a minvawhna pen, amaute in nidanglai lawki sungah up-khialhna aneih tenguh a nutsiat zawh nailoh uhhang ahi hi. Milim biakpiakna ante a thahat te in anek uh ciangin, Christian biakna leh milim biakna in nuamtak in tonkhawm theilel kici ding hi. Tua ahih manin Paul in “a thahat te” in a thanemte a dingin “tuuk theihna’ng palkhak ding” hisak nuamlo hi.

Milim’ maiah biakpiakna a kinei ante nekna pen Laisiangtho in siatmuh mahmah hi (Sawl. 15: 20, 29; 21: 25. Mang. 2: 14, 20). Ahihhang, hih Laisiangtho mun sunga te cih bangin Paul in genkhiatna neituanlo hi. Hihthu ahihlhcianna sungah Paul in a lung hih mawhna pipen bel, pilna zatkhialhna tungtawn in kithutuah lohna khawng hong pian ding patau hi.

Paul in pilna sungah gitlohna om ci in mawhpaih lo a; pawlpi sungah kikhenna a piangsak thei kiliatsak na’ng pilnate a cihnopna ahi hi. Itna kihello pilna in pilna taktak hi lo hi (1 Cor. 8: 2). Pilna taktak cihpen Pasian a it mi pen Pasian in a theih mi ahi hi (1 Cor. 8: 3).

Paul in Thuhilhkikna 6: 4 siksan in thu um mite in Pasian khat bekmah thei ding uh cihi (1Cor. 8:4-6). A lunglut huaina khat, Thkna 6: 4, 5 sunga Pasian pen khat bek hi a cihna tawh kizui in, na Topa “na Pasian na it ding hi” cihi. Moses mahbang in Paul a dingin zong itna omlo pilna in a mawkna hilel hi.

“A thahat” mite in amau’ pilna kimuang uh a, milim biakpiakna ante nekna zong lauhuai tuanlo hi ci uhhi. A hunlehmun tawh kizui in, tuabang in a hihtheihna ding uh zong Paul in sangveve ahihna Wednesday leh Thursday ni ciangin mu ding hihang. Cihtei hang, “upnaneu” (1Cor. 8: 9) te’ tuktheihna’ng palkhak ding ahih khakleh, pelhpah lian ding cihi. Christian peuhmah in Christ leh midangte itna hangin eima’ pumpi mahmah ki deek kidaam ding hihang.

`Itna a kihello pilna peuhmah a sialam hong suak ding Paul in cihi (1Cor. 8). Koibang hun ciangin itna omlo pilna in siahuai thei hiam?`
