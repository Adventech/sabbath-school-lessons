title:  Zulhtatnate Siansuahna
date:   20/07/2026
---

Zahmawh vai peuhmah siansuah haksa mahmah hi. Paul in zong haksa nasa mah mah hi. Hihbang hun ciangin Laisiangtho tungah citak thumaan takin thungetna leh itna tawh siansuah ding kisam hi. I ngiimna bulpi pen puahphat kikna ahihlam mangngilh hetlo ding hihang.

`1Corinth 5: 1–13 simkik in. Hibang hun ciangin koicih ding Paul in ci hiam?`

Paul in 1Cor. 5 sunga zahmawh thusia peuhmah pawlpi’ khalna kisam cihi. Tua a zualzang mipa hemkhia ding (1Cor. 5: 2), mawhsakna thukhen ding (1Cor. 5: 3), Satan tungah piakhia ding (1Cor. 5: 5), lakhia, paikhia ding (1Cor. 5: 13) cihi. Pawlpi mite in tuapa peuhmah “khawlhpih lo ding” (1Cor. 5: 9, 11), bek thamlo “tuabang mipa tawh an zong ne khawmlo ding” (1Cor. 5: 11) cihi.

Paul’ kammalte khauhpai mahmah ahih manin khangthak te a ding bang zak thadah huai mahmah phial mahleh, a tangthu pian hunzui in theihtel kul hi. Maizumna mel theilo in mawhna bawlpa tungah thukhen ahihna theihhuai hi. Gamtat dan a uangluat hun ciangin kamkhauh kisampha hi. Thu khat peuhpeuh ah tomcing takin telkheh zong kulveve hi.

**“Note lakpanin hemkhia un”** (1Cor. 5: 2; 1Cor. 5: 13). Pawlpi’ khalhna a cih- nopna ahi hi.

**“Satan tungah piakhia un”** (1Cor. 5: 5). Pasian’ thu mangin Ama’ hong kepna sungah nungta nuamlo ahih manin; Satan’ khutsungah omding in kilawm hi. “Ama’ khentatna gah lo tahen” cihna hipeuh hi.

**“Khawlhpihlo ding”** (1Cor. 5: 9, 11) **“tuabang mipa tawh anzong nekhawm lo ding”** (1Cor. 5: 11). Zahmawh zualzang mite tawh kinaikhawlhna panin, amau’ zualzang na tawh midangte a kilawhsak dinguh lauhuai kici hi. Tanglai in an nekkhopna pen khat leh khat kithupit sakna ahi hi. Eite pen i kimkot in hong huzaap mahmah ahih manin, tua bang thusia hong om ciangin eima hihtheihzah in kikemcing ding hihang.

**“Ama’ kha a kihotkhiat nading”** (1Cor. 5: 5). Pawlpi’ khalhna cihpen puahphat na ahi hi. Mawhneite in amau dinmun kiphawk in, mawhnei nundan nusiasak hi. Paul in “pumpi gimsakna” (1Cor. 5: 5) acih pen a tunga mah hihtuak hi. 2 Cor. 2: 5-10 sungah a kisikkik mipa acihpen; hih 1Cor. 5 sunga zahmawh zualzangpa mah hihtuak hi. A gamta khial pawlpimi khat in, pawlpi tawh akikhawlh kik theihna dingin, pawlpi in a khalhna in muibun in a ngiimna tangtun hi.
