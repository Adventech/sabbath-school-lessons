### Tukalsung Simding
1 Cor. 5:1–13; 2Cor 2:5–10; 1Cor. 6:1–13; 1 Thess. 4:1–8; 1Cor. 6:19–7:9.

> <p>Kamngah</p>
> “Na pumpi uh Pasian in hongpiak, note sungah hong teng Kha Siang tho’ biakinn ahihlamtak theilo nahi uhhiam? Note pumpi nomau neihsa hilo a, Pasian aa ahi hi. Note pen Pasian in hong leisa nahih manun, na pumpi uh Pasian’ minthanna dingin na zang un” (1 Corinthians 6:19, 20, NIV).

Mihing’ khuak pen a&jr§Kyf tawh kibang hi. Ngaihsutna tungtawn in amau kiang a pai khempeuh hup in kem pahpah hi. I khuaksungah bangteng honglut cihpen kiphawk kha mello (ciamteh sese keileng kithei zolo) hi. Hita mahleh i khuak sungah omveve a, tuateng mahin ngaihdan sepdan tengmah hong sukha veve hi.

Tua hang mahin, ei Christian te nangawn, eima kimkot a thupiangte in hong suk-khak ding baih mahmah hi. Acil apat limlim in Christian pawlpi in hih buaina tengmah tuakkha limlim hi. Gentehna in, Sunday tanna koipan hong piang hiam? Pawlpi in huih lakpan in dawkkhia uh maw? Hi deksuai lo hi. Amau’ kimkot a ngeina pan ahi hi.

Tua thu-khun mah pen Corinth mite lakah zong kimu kha hi. Pawl kikhen nopna lungsim tawh kisai a thuumna (1 Cor. 1-4) khit ciangin, Paul in zahmawh zualzangna, thu kineihna, angzuakna, kiteenna leh khattang omna (1Cor. 5-7) cihte lamah paipih hi. Leitung dinmun in amaute tungah thunei mahmah hi.

1 Cor. 1-4 sunga Pawl khennopna lungsim in, a nunglam a gamtat hoihlo acihcih te a dingin kong honsak hi. Pawlpi sung mawhnate Paul in koibang in hilhcian a, Paul lai gelhna sungpan in bang pilna peuh kingah thei ding hiam?
