---
title:  Upna leh Gamtatna kikal Kibatlohna
date:   19/07/2026
---

Christian tangthu sung tawntung ah Laisiangtho siamte, pastor te leh thu um mite in pawlpi koici om ding cih entel nuamin laisiangthothak sim uhhi. Sawltak te hunlai a pawlpi zong lamdang kisa mahmah khin hi. A thupi mahmah khat, mihingte in thubuai nei ciat cih zong kiphawk kha nawnlo pah zawsop hi. Pawlpi in koibang dan hilo ding cih eite’n zong laisiangthothak sungah simkhin kha ding hihang. Corinth mite tungah Paul’ laikhakte tawh kipat huai mahmah hi.

`1Corinth 5: 1–13 simin. Paul in bang thubuaina tawh pulaaksak in buaisak mahmah hiam?`

Paul in “a pa’ zi “ (1 Cor. 5:1) acih ciangin sisan kinaihpih tawh zualzangna hi a, mi khatleh a nukik hihtuak hi. Hih dinmun pen “Chloe’ innkuan” (1Cor. 1: 11) in thu puak hi. Sisan kinai zualzangna pen a lipkhaphuai zia “lawkite nangawn in a gamtatloh te” (1Cor. 5: 1) cihi. Tu in Christian pawlpi masa sungah piang tazen maw leh? Pawlpi mi khat in tua bang in gamta cih azak ciangin Paul pataulua hi (1Cor. 5: 1, 2).

Hitamahleh hih dinmun in suksia semlai hi. Corinth pawlpi mite in tuabang dan gamtatna phal in koih hithiat bek thamlo kipaak mahmah lai cih azak ciangin Paul dipkua semsem lai hi (1Cor. 5: 1, 2). Tua ciangin Paul in zong tua mimawhpa puahphat ding bek thamlo; upna leh gamtatna kikal ah kitelsiam lohna a om pawlpi zong puahphat sawmta mai hi.

Paul in zong a nu tawh zualzang liang lawmlawm pa a maisakzo tua pawlpi mah mah zong puahphat ding gen tangtang hi. Hi taleh tuabang a zahmawh zualzangna na ngawn kisial mahmah lai uhhi (1Cor. 5: 2, 6). Tuapen Paul a dingin thuak hak mahmah hi. Hih mite in bangsung khial uh hiam?

Sisan kinaihpih tawh zualzangpa pen, pawlpi ina thuak tentan zawhna thu bang mah kitheihpihlo hi. Amah haulua mahmah in pawlpi in noptuam pih mahmah hiam? A hih keileh “a hihnop peuhpeuh hihthei” (1Cor. 6; 12) ahih manin, amau hih ding kilawm te, hihding in kilawm satheilo hiam? Kithei zo nawnlo hi.

Bangbang hitaleh, amaute in Laisiangtho palsatna kician mahmah taleh, a mit uh taw khin (Siam. 18: 7, 8) ahih manun kisial mahmah lai uh maw?

`Laisiangtho in phal hetlo hitaleh “itna leh saansiamna” tawh pawlpi in sang in aki maisak zel bangpeuh omthei ding hiam?`
