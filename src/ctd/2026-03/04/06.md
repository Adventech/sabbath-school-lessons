---
title:  Kiteenna leh Khattang omna
date:   23/07/2026
---

Paul in eite’ pumpi pen “Kha Siangtho biakinn” (1Cor. 6: 19) cih a kipsakna pen zahmawh zualzangna a khaamna tawh kipawl ahi hi. Kha Siangtho teenna biakinn hihna bek in siangtho nundan hong nungta sakthei bek hi. Pawlpi icih in, i kimkot a omte khem peuh leh Christian kipawlkhopna hong kikhentelna ahi hi. Tuabang ahihtheihna ding in Kha Siangtho ompihna bektawh hithei hi.

`1Corinth 6: 19–7: 9 simin. Hih munin “zahmawh zualzangna pelhun” (1Cor. 6: 18) acih thukham koibang in hong telcian sak hiam?`

1Cor. 7 sungah zahmawh vai theihhuia thu tampi tak om hi. A baihpen dingin, 7 na pen phelnih kisuah thei hi: (1) kiteenna vai hilhcianna (1Cor. 7: 1-24) leh (2) khattang omna tawh kisai hilhcianna (1Cor. 7: 25-40). 1Cor. 7 in zahmawh vai pen gen ding thupi leh kisam ahihna hong telcian sak hi.

Ahita zongin, 1Cor. 7 i simuh ciangun, Paul in Corinth khua a pawlpi sung thu om tetawh kizui in dotna kiciante a dawnna cih phawk ni. Ama’ thu pulaakna pawlkhat sungah, amah’n kiteenna thupimuh khollo bang hikha ding a, ahihhang tua in ama’ thubulpi hi zawlo hi (1Tim. 4: 1-3; 1Tim. 5: 14; Heb. 13: 4).

A ciaptehhuai thupiakna ah, “zahmawh zualzangna pelh un” (1Corinth 6:18) cih pen Christ tawh kizopna (1Cor. 6:17), Kha Siangtho biakinn hihna (1Cor. 6: 19) cihtawh a thukhup suk hi. Zahmawh zualzangna pelhna dingin lampi hoihzaw dang a omlai hiam? Omlo hi.

Pasian in zahmawh piangsak himah a, kiteenna sung bekah zatnuam ding cihi. Zahmawh cihpen pasal khat leh numei khat kiteenna sungah hamphatna khat hi a, Lai siangtho in hong sehsak ngiat ahi hi. Paul in “zahmawh zualzangna pelh un” acih laitak in Joseph (Pian. 39: 6-18) phawk gige kha ding hi. Potiphar’ zi in duh-aamna sungah a mainawtmaa in Joseph in “innsung panin taikhia” (Pian. 39: 18) cihi. Hihthu pen Pian. 39: 6-18 sungteng ah liivei sang tawmzaw lo in pulaak hi.

LaiSiangtho inbel a cihlian lohhang, tua zahmawh a dingin Joseph in zineih ding kiteenna ngak phot hi (Pian. 41: 45). Joseph pen Kha Siangtho tawh a kidim mipa ahi hi (Pian. 41: 38). Pasian’ maiah zong thumaan gamta nuampa ahi hi. Amah pen Pasian Kha Siangtho tawh a kidimpa ahi hi (Pian. 41: 38). Pasian’ maiah zong thumaan gamta nuam pa ahi hi.

`Eite, pawlpi tawh ihi zongin, pawlpi ngeina tungah hong thuzawh thei muh dan hoihlote koibang in pelhthei ding ihi hiam?`
