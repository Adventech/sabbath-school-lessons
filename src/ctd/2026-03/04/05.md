---
title:  Zahmawh Zualzangna’ Gu-thoihna
date:   22/07/2026
---

`1Thess. 4: 1–8 simin. Hihmun in siangthona leh zahmawh zualzangna pelh in omna koibang in kizomsak hiam?`

Paul in a tunga thutengpen mikhatpeuh tungah agelh ahihhang; a thukhunpi ahih leh, tangzai takin Christian khempeuh a dingin zattheih ahi hi. Hih tungtang ah a dothuai khat: Corinth ah bang thu teng piang hiam? Banghang in piangthei hiam?

Corinth thu ummi pawlkhat in, lungdamthu tawh suahtak khit nungsang nakhem peuh hihtheih ding kiphata ci in um uhhi. Amau’ cihdan tak hileh, gilpi pen an a dingin kibawl hi a, pumpi pen zahmawh a ding leh zahmawh in zong pumpi a ding cihi (1Cor. 6: 13). Paul in tuathu pen Christian’ suahtakna thu telkhial ahihlam uh hilhcian kik hi. Zahmawh lamah vangkeplohna in Christian’ dinmun tawh kituaklo leh, lungdamthu tung tawn in hongpiak suahtakna zangkhial hi cihi (Rome. 8: 2; Gal. 5: 13).

Eite pen mawhna pan kisuakta a, mawhna bawltheih na’ng “suakta” ihi kei uhhi (Rome 8: 2; Rome 6: 18, 22). Ataktak in “pumpi in … Topa a ding, Topa in zong pumpi a ding” (1Cor. 6: 13) ahi hi. Eite in Christ’ neihsa (1Cor. 6: 15) ihi a, eimah ihihna bang bang in i gamtatna piangsak hi. Khatlehkhat zong kikai-awk bikbek in kizom uhhi. Tua thu mahmah pen 1 Cor. 6 sungah nam thum in hong khenkhiat sak hi.

Amasa, eite pen “Topa Zeisu leh Pasian’ Kha Siangtho min tawh” (1Cor. 6: 11) **sawpsiangsa**, a **kisiansuahsa** leh **mawhmaisa** hong cihi. 1Cor. 6: 9, 10 sunga a kigualsuk diudeu mawhna teng leh; 1Cor. 6: 9, 10 sunga mipi maiah nasia takin a mawhpaih zahmawh zualzangna cihte in, sawpsiangsa, a kisiansuahsa leh mawhmaisa te’ nundan sungah mun luah zolo uhhi.

Anihna, eite in **Christ’ pumpi ah nate** (1Cor. 6: 15). Tuapen eite leh Christ kipumkhat ding cihnopna ahi hi (1Cor. 6: 17). Zahmawh zualzangna cihpen Topa tawh kipumkhatna palsatna (1Cor. 6: 13, 15) ahi hi. Kiteenna omlopi in zahmawh zangkhawm te in zong anihgel un “pumkhat” suakkhin uhhi (1Cor. 6: 16). Kha Siangtho tungtawn in Christ tawh kikhawlhna in, zahmawh vaite ah zong Christian’ nundan a neithei dingin khentat theihna bulpi mah hipah hi.

Athumna, eimau pumpi in **“Kha Siangtho biakinn”** (1Cor. 6: 19, 20) hi. Zah- mawh vaite ah vangkiamlo siangtho takin nuntak theihna ding in, Kha Siangtho hangin Christ tawh kikhawlhkhop ding lampi khatbek om hi. Rome 12: 1 sungah “a hingtang in biakpiakna, Pasian’ maiah a saantaakcing in” na kipia un cihi.

`Zahmawh zatkhialhna in mihing tungah siatna hong tunthei cih lungngai dih in. Christian te a dingin bangzah lauhuai hong ci hiam?`
