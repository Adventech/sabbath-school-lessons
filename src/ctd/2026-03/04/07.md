Ellen G. White, “A Message of Warning and Entreaty,” pp. 298–308, in The Acts of the Apostles.

1 Cor. 5: 10, 11 leh 1 Cor. 6: 9, 10 sungah a kigualsuk diudeu siatnate lakah, milim biakna leh zudawn zukhamna in zahmawh zualzangna tawh kizom in kigualsuk hi. Paul in. 1 Cor. 10: 7 (Pai. 32: 1-6) a suutkikna ah, milim biakna pawikhaamna peuhmah uangtak in nekna dawnna tawh zahmawh zualzangna lam tungpahpah cihi (1 Cor. 10; 8).

EGWhite in zong “angkhual in anhai laite in siangthona thupha sangthei nailo ding uhhi. Mihing’ pumpi in a thuakzawh thahatna in; eima pumpi tungah hong tung nate a thuakzawhna in lamdang mahmah ahihhang, uangtak in nekna dawnna tawh mawhna a bawlzom veve te in, amau’ pumpi thuakzawhna kiamsemsem sakding hi. Simmawhna tawh palsat athuak pumpi’ lunggulhnate dipsakna sungah, Christian akici mite in, amau’ nasep uh thanemsak a, pumpi, lungsim leh gamtatzia te kiamsuk sak hi” cihi.—Ellen G. White, The Sanctified Life, pp. 25, 26.

“Mikhat in angkhualna a paihkhiat ciangin, lungsim sungpan pasian-zuau khem- peuh a hepkhiat ciangin; tua a awngthawlna munah Christ’ Kha Siangtho hong lut hi. Tuabang mipa in; itna tawh na a sem upna nei a, gamtatna leh lungsim mahmah niinsak lo in kisiansuah hi”—Ellen G. White, The Home Missionary, November, 1889.

“Pasian in eite pen, a sangpenpen vantung dinmun siangthona ah hong lamto nuam hi. Tua dingin a Kha Siangtho in eite hong hanthawn den hi. Pasian’ Kha Siangtho in a puahphatloh mihing’ lunggulhnate peuhmah sungah sihna khaici om hi”—Ellen G. White, Manuscript 12, 1888.

**Kikupding Dotnate:**

`Corinth ah thu um tampite in, leitung’ ngeina tawh kipawl theih ding lunggulhna nei uhhi. Tua in Christian dinmun a dingin banghang in lauhuai hiam? Tuabang khialhna i bawl khaklohna dingin eimah mah koibang in kidal ding ihi hiam?`

`Paul’ dotna kician mahmah “Note pumpi in Kha Siangtho teenna biakinn ahih lamtak theilo nahi uhhiam?” (1Cor. 6: 19) cih in 1Cor. 5-6 sunga “Theilo nahi uhhiam” (1Cor. 5: 6; 1Cor. 6: 2, 3, 9, 15, 16, 19) cih dotna sagih tetawh a thukhup hi. Tuate khempeuh in dawnna kip hongngen hi a “himah ei” cih ding vive ahi uhhi. Paul in pawlpi a deihsakna pen hih dotna teng panin koibang in natelcian thei hiam? Eite’n zong banghang in awlmawh ding ihi uhhiam?`

`Kiteena pen Pasian hong piak hi (Pian. 1: 27, 28; Pian. 2: 18-24) a, zahtak ding ahi hi (Heb. 13: 4). Mite in khangluivai luadeuh e acih hun ciangin; Eden panin Pasian in hong piakkhong ahihlam leitung ah koibang in lahkhia thei ding ihi hiam?`
