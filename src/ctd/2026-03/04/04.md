---
title:  Pawlpi’ Dinmun Kepcinna
date:   21/07/2026
---

1Corinth 6: 1–11 sungah, Paul in pawlpi sungah thubuai anei mite Christian te in a vanaih dingdan hong kikuppih hi.

`1Corinth 5: 3, 12, 13 leh 1Corinth 6: 1–13. Paul in Corinth mite leh eimah zong bang hong hilhciam nuam hiam?`

1Cor. 6: 1 sunga Greek pau pragma pen “thubuai” cihna hi a, “na khatpeuh, na” in kiletkhia hi. Hitengah pen “Upadi lamthu” hihtuak zaw hi. 1Cor. 6: 1-11 thu pen gitloh na thusia lam hilo cih theih kul hi. Mipi thukhenna zum a thusiate tungah thuneihna pen Rom. 13: 1-5 sungah kipsak khin hi. Paul cihnopna pen Thuhilhkikna 22; 22-24 sung a Moses in ahihbang in, zahmawh zualzangna thusia a omphet in zum ah thu bawlpah pen a cihnopna hi. Hihthu entel leng pawlsung a thusia khatpeuh a om ciangin Paul in laisiang tho bulphuh in thukhen cih lahkhiatna ahi hi.

1Cor. 6: 11 sung thusia pen zahmawh zualzangna tawh kipawl Laisiangtho munte tawh thukhup suk (1Cor. 5 leh 1Cor. 6: 12-20) khin a, 1 Cor. 6: 1 sunga thubuai zong pen zahmawh zualzangna mah hihtuak hi. Tua thusia ahihleh mipite thubuai maw, neihsa kituhna maw, zahmawh buaina mah mawh kitelcian lo hi.

Tua thubuai in bangpeuh a nahi zongin, pawlpi mite in mipi thukhenzum a natun uhpen Paul nuamlo hi. Amaute un “lawki thukhente” (1Cor. 6: 1) kiang a kitut uh sang un Christian u lehnau sanggamte in, amau sungah thu siansuah theilo uh ahi hiam?

Pawlkhat bangin a upmawh pongmawk un, 1Cor. 6: 1 sungah thusia nei tegel pen 1Cor. 5: 1 sunga pa leh tapa khawng hihtuak zawsop hi. Thu khat peuhpeuh ah, a buaina leh a thubul theihtel huai hi. Paul in, a pualam mite in Christian kipawlna dinmun hong muhdan ding lung himawh pen hi. Christian te in dailenbuk sunga puanniin te khawng mipi’ muhna ah phokhia sedah uhhen (1Cor. 6: 6).

Innsung thubuai khawng zong leitung mite kikhensak dah lel uhhen. Rome te hunlai in, mihau, kumpi milian cihte in zumpi te sungah maipha ngahzaw hamtang uhhi. Tuatawh a kilehbulh in Christian te in Christ-bang thukhenna zangin, leitung ngeina leh dinmun tetawh kipelh uhhen.

`1Corinth 5: 10, 11 leh 1Corinth 6: 9, 10 sunga Paul in a gualhsuk diudeu gitlohna sazian enpha in. Paul in milim biakna, guktakna, huaihamna leh sum duh luatna cihte pen banghang in zahmawh zualzangna tawh gualkhawm zenzen hiding hiam?`
