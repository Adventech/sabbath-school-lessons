---
title:  Upna, Lam-etna leh Itna
date:   13/08/2026
---

Tu in, itna in, lungduai, hoih, lungdamthei, kikheello, thu um, lam-en in thuakzo ci hang a; Zeisu in tuateng tawh kidim hi. Khatvei Zeisu sungah tuateng i muh khit ciang in, kalsuan kikna ding pen zuih ding hipah hi. Tuapen Corinth mite a dingin Paul’ deih sakna ahi hi.

Tua hi a, “lo” a cihteng hemkhia peuhleng, Corinth mite gamtatzia taktak teng hong kilang ding: hazatna lungsim nei, kisathei, kiphasak, hehpakpak, angsungkhual, a kilawmlo gamta, mawhna ciamteh, thumaanlote ah lungdam hi. Paul in Corinth mite gamtatna teng kekkek hong gualsuk diudeu hi”—Verlyn D. Verbrugge, “1 Corinthians,” in The Expositor’s Bible Commentary: Romans–Galatians, revised edition (Grand Rapids, MI: Zondervan, 2008), p. 372.

Corinth mite in kisin ding tampi nei uh a, eite zongin i nei hi. Itna in bang teng hih a, bangteng hihlo cih i sinkhit ciangin; Paul in, itna maan taktak neihtheihna dingin, tawn tung itna neih masak ding hanthotna tawh a thu khupsuk hi.

Khatvei ciangin, genkholnate kisam nawnlo ding a; eite in pau namkhat bek kipau ta ding; tua ciangin, mihing’ pilna in kicingzolo leh Pasian’ theihna pilna (1Cor. 13: 12) in, pilna thak silsel hong hiding hi. Khalam silpiak khempeuh in zong amau’ omna’ng a kisapna teng a picin takciang in khawlta ding uhhi (1Cor. 13: 10). “Itna ahihleh om paisuak ding hi!” (1Cor. 13: 8).

Tua mahbangin Christ hong kumkik ciangin, upna pen muhna hita ding hi (1Cor. 5: 7), sawtpi panin i lam-et tengin thutaktak hita ding hi (Rom. 8: 24). Adiak in thum- gawm Pasian in Ama’ dinmun ah kiptak in ompaisuak ding hi. Upna leh lam-etna cihte in zong a tawntung in kip paisuak ding hi. Upna in hotkhiatna ngahna (Rom. 4: 3) leh lam-et lunggulhna in zong leitungthak ah lungdam nopsakna hi ding a, tatkhiatna ngahna ci in kip paisuak ding hi. Pasian hong itna a hihleh zong kip paisuak ding hi.

A sawtlo in i Topa’ maitang muta ding hihang (1Cor. 13: 12). Tuani hong tun dongin eite in i nuntakpih ding hoihna thum teng: Upna, Lam-etna leh Itna ahi uhhi. Hih thum kigawmna in Kha Siangtho tungtawn in Christian nundan a cingtaakna ahi hi. Tua a hih manin Christian tetungah tuathu kisuan kinken ahi hi (Rom. 5: 1–5; Gal. 5: 5, 6; Eph. 1: 15, 18; 4: 1–5). Itna ahihleh a lianpen mah higige a, Pasian omzia pulaakna lim taktak mah higige lai hi (1 John 4: 8).

`“Pasian in itna” cih kammal lungngai photin. Akhiatna koibang taktak in i tel hiam? Adeihna phelkhat ciang bek khawng telzo ihihang, tua phelkhat nangawn eite a dingin banghang in lungdamthu hithei zawzen hiam?`
