---
title:  Itna in a Hihlohte
date:   11/08/2026
---

`1Cor. 13: 4–7 simkik in. Itna tawh kisai Paul in banghang in a hoihlam hilo in a sialam tawh hilhcian zawsop hiam?`

Zanni in, itna in ahih na sagih entel khin hihang: tuni in, itna in ahihloh na nam giat en ding hihang.

Itna in . . .

**1. Hazatna lungsim neilo (_zeloō_).** _Zeloō_ pen ahoihlam in zangleng ‘a hoihpen silpiakte deih’ (1Cor. 12: 31). ‘Khalam sialpiakte deih’ (_zeloō_) (1Cor. 14: 1), ‘Pasian’ thu hilh ding ngaihsut lianpen’ (1Cor. 14: 39) cihi. Sawl. 7: 9 sungah ahihleh, lehpai zawsop hi. Khalam silpiakte deihna hoih lel hinapi; silpiak ngahte hazalo ding, tua hazatna in Pawl khenna piangsak hi (1Cor. 3: 3).

**2. Kisatheilo (perpereuomai).** Hih perpereuomai cihpen midangte kiangpan pahtawina ngahna dingin kisaktheih kampauna hi. Itna peuhmah in eimah kibulphuhna neilo hi. Anuai ah kitel semsem lai ding hi.

**3. Kiphasaklo (_physioō_).** Hih _physioō_ in 1 Corinthians 8: 1 sungah Paul kammalkician mahmah “theihna in kisathei sak a; itna in mite kipsak hi” cihi. Amah a ki bulphuh a kiphasak acihna ahi hi.

**4. Heh pakpaklo (_aschēmoneo_).** Hih _aschēmoneo_ in a khiatna zailua mahmah hi. Zai citateu zongin mihing kikhawlhna leh lungsim omzia lamah zahtak huailo, zumhuai, ki lawmlo, muh thadah huai gamtatna hi. Paul in a genthapai keei Corinth mite lakah mi thahatte leh thanemte a cihnopna hihtuak pen hi (1Cor. 4:10, 1 Cor. 8).

**5. Angsung khuallo (_zēteo_).** 1Cor. 10: 24 sunga Paul in: “A kuamapeuh in note angsung dingbek zongkei unla, midangte’ phattuam nading zongzaw un” acih tawh a kibang ahi hi. Itna in midangte a dingin ama’ hamphatna piakhia liang hi (sinna 5). Mimalkim in midangte a ding vive kizonsak leng i veikpi in kihampha pah hi.

**6. A kilawmlo in gamtalo (_paroxynō_).** Hih kammal _paroxynō_ in a hehbaih mahmah te lungsim sung omzia a cihnopna ahi hi. Itna i cihin lungtomlo cihna ahi hi.

**7. Mawhna ciamtehlo (_logizomai_).** Hih _logizomai_ cihin ciaptehna lamtawh kisai a; itna in mi’ mawhna ciamteh lo cihi. A lehlam panin itna in maisak cihna ahi hi.

**8. Thumaanlote ah lungdam ngeilo (_chairō_).** Itna in mi’ mawhna ciamtehlo bek thamlo tuate sungah zong lungdamlo hi. Midangte it takpi leng, amau’ khialhna sungah i lungdam kei ding uh a, huh theihna’ng lampi zongzaw ding hihang.
