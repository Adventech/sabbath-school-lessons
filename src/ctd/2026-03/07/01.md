### Tukalsung Singding
1 Corinth 13; Mate 24:12; Gal. 5:22, 23; 1Tim. 1:14; 1 John 4:8.

> <p>Kamngah</p>
> “Tu laitakin a omlai thuthumte pen, upna, lam-etna leh itna hi a; tua 	thumte sungpan itna mah a thu lianpen ahi hi” (1Cor. 13: 13).

Itna in nakhempeuh zothei hi. Tua hangmah in Paul in zong nakgen mahmah hi. Mi tampi in i theih Greekpau _agapaō_ pen Laisiangthothak sungah itna ci in kilet a, Paul’ laikhak sungah 135 sang tamveizaw om hi. Tua pen laisiangthothak sunga omzah alang phial mah ahi hi. Corinth pawlpite tungah Paul in a laikhakna ah a thu bulphuhpi ahihlam hong pulaakna mah hipah hi.

Laisiangthothak sungah mun kician mahmah pawlkhat; Rome 8: 35–39, 1Cor. 2: 9; 1Cor. 8: 3; Galati 2: 20; Colossian 1: 13; 1Thess. 3: 12 leh a dangzong omlai hi.

Ahihhang 1Cor. 13 a khengzo bangmah omlo hi.

Nungkal in, itna lotawh, nakhempeuh, khalam silpiakte nangawn a mawkna cih mukhin hihang. Tukal sungin, 1Cor. 13 sungh akibang itna khat, a thukzaw in ensuk lai ding hihang.

I muhding thupen, itna cih lungsim ngaihsutna ciang khatbek hilo a, lungsim puak zia leh ngaihdan khat ahi hi. Tua ngaihdan pen eima’ nuntak sungin gamtat kampauna tawh lahkhiatna ahi hi. Tualo peuhmah khiatna nei vetlo hi.

Itna in bang hi a, bang hih cihpen, Zeisu’ nundan sungah kician takin hong kilah khia khin hi.
