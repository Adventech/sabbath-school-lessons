Nungkal in 1Cor. 13 sunga om itna’ thukhun kisin khin hihang. Tu in Paul’ kam malte a thukzaw kanlai in to khia ding hihang.

`1 Corinth 13 simin . Itna thu a gen teng tomkaih in.`

Paul in kampaute (1Cor. 13: 1), kamsaanna, theihtelna, pilna, upna (1Cor. 13: 2), phattuamna neite (1Cor. 13: 3) a mawkna hi cilo hi. Itna a omkeileh amawkna hi cibek hi. Paul in a gen itna pen “Strawberry duh ing, ka lawmte itlua ing, kazi kata ngailua ing” cihpeuh hilel lo hi. Video sunga i muh ki it kingaihnate peuh hilo hi. Kiteenna mopawi sermon khawngin kizang mahmah ahihhang hukna lanna tawh itna te hilo hi.

Hih itna pen lawmngaihna, migitna, hoihna leh hehpihna khawngah khiatsuk theih ding hilo hi. Cihtei hang, tuate in zong amau’ dinmun tawh itna pen atawm atam in pulak veve uhhi. Hih itna pen Kha Siangtho in mihing tungah hong tuam piakngiat ahi hi. Tua hi a, 1 Corinth 13 sunga Kha Siangtho in hongpiak itna in, lawmngaihna, migitna, hoihna leh hehpihna cihte zong piangsak veve hi. Gamtatna, lungsim leh ngaihsutna teng khempeuh tawh Christ leh vengte tung kipiakkhiatna ahi hi.

`Mate 24: 12 simin. Zeisu in bang vauna hiteng ah hong pia hiam?`

Tua ahih manin _agapē_ in kisampha mahmah hi. Christ’ vangliatna hangin, eima’ innsung, pawlpi sung leh vengsung ah hi itna kivengsak theilo hi. Singlamteh tungah eite a dingin sihna nangawn a thuaklawh Zeisu’ limlahna kinei gige hi. Hih sangin a hoihzaw a vanglianzaw itna lahna bangdang omnawn ding hiam? Eite in tuabang itna zah liang ki lahkhia zolo ding hi taleh, Pasian’ hehpihna tungtawn in, eima nundan sungah eima’ hih zawhzah buangbel lahkhiat ding mah hiveve hi.

`Hihbang itna i lahkhiatna ah na dangte khempeuh sangin, hih bang itna a kisam phadeuhte tungah, a thahat a puahphatna lam a piansak theih ding hun in bang hun hipen ding hiam?`
