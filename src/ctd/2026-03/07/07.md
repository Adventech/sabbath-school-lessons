Ellen G. White, “The Need of Love,” pp.545, 546, Review and Herald, August 28, 1888.

“Amau’ nundan bangzah sang hitaleh, a lungtang un Pasian leh a vengte itna tawh a kidim kei ngalleh; Sawltak taktak hitheilo uhhi. Upna lian mahmah nei in, na lamdang pipi te bawlthei phial hileh zong, itna a omkei ngalleh a mawkna ahi hi. Lungsim taanzau mahmah in; itna taktak tawh ahilo thudang khatpeuh tawh a neihsa a lamsa teng mizawng te tungah piakhia zomahmah phial leh zong, tua piakkhiatna in Pasian’ deihna picingsak zo tuanlo ding hi. Ama’ hanciam zawhna tawh martyr’ sihna tuak phial taleh, itna tawh a hihkei nakleh, amahpen Pasian’ muhna ah amahleh amah akikhempa, lunggulhna kheng val tawh a kineihkhempa hilel ding hi” EGWhite, The Acts of the Apostles, p. 318, 319.

“Eite in gamtat hoihna ding sermon hoih tampitak neilel hihang. I kisapphat pen a hihleh, a kisia ding kha te itna, Pasian’ tokhom panin hong luangsuk itna ahi hi. Christian maantaktak te in a pumpi bup un itna tawh kidimsak hi. Pumpi tung nakhempeuh khuak, lungtang, huhna khutte khete sukha ahih manin, Pasian’ sehna munmun ah kiptak in ding tangzo in, khelbaite lam a pialpih lohna dingin amau’ paina’ng lampi kawisaklo in tang sak uhhi. Kha mangthang te a dingin a kuangkhiaden Christ hong itna in Christian biakna a dingin nuntakna bulpi ahi hi”—Ellen G. White, Lift Him Up, p. 134.

“Christ’ lungtang panin hong luangkhia itna bek in hong damask thei hi. Sing kung sungah a tui a luan a, pumpi sungah sisan a luan mahbang in; tua itna a luansukna mipa bek in kha liammaa teng damthei bek ding hi”—Ellen G. White, Education, p. 114.

**Kikupding Dotnate:**

`Itna thu thuhoih Paul in a gualhsuk teng picing thei dingin na um hiam? Na up keileh bangteng peuh behlaphuai lai nasa hiam?`

`Paul in “itna zongun” (1 Cor. 14: 1) a cihteh bang a cihnopna ahidiam? 1Cor. 13: 4–7 sunga acihna tawh koibang in kizom thei ding hiam?`

`Na nisim nundan sungah koibang itna na zat ding kisam nasapen hiam? Na khua- tual pawlpi in bang kisampha pen nasa hiam? Banghang in Paul in itna pen; thuhilh siamna, kampau siamna leh pilna tetawh genteh sese hi ding hiam (1Cor. 13: 8)?`

`Paul in, Corinth pawlpi mite kipumkhatna omlohna pen itna tawh kipumkhat kikthei ding cihi. Banghang hiam? I pawlsungah koibang in kizangthei ding hiam?`
