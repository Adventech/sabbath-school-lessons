---
title:  Itna in Ahihteng
date:   10/08/2026
---

Corinth Masa 13: 4–7 in tua chapter buppi laigil ahi hi. Paul in itna’ dinmun teng bulphuh in – itna in bang hi a, bang hi lo; bangteng hihthei a, bangteng hihtheilo cihtawh pulaak hi. Kha Siangtho tawh a kidim mikhat in koibang in gamta cih ettheih dingin tua mipa’ itna hong lahkhia hi. Itna a lahkhiatna sungah, Paul in sepna kammalte zang hi. Paul a dingin itna cihpen lungsim leh lawpna peuh sangin gamtatna hipha zaw hi.

Tua ahihleh itna in bangteng hih hiam?

**1. Lungduai (_makrothymeō_).** _Makrothymeō_ pen tona, haksatna te lakah lungduai cihna hi. Khatlehkhat kithuakzawhna zong ahi hi (Eph. 4:2).

**2. Migi (_chrēsteuomai_).** _Chrēsteuomai_ pen laisiangthothak sungbup hiteng bekah om a, mundang ah tua pan a kaakhia laimalte bel om uhhi. Septuagint kici Greek Laisiangtholui sungah hih kammal pan in a kaakhia kammal tampi om a, Late sungah Pasian’ hoihna leh hong hehpihna tawh kipawl kammalte tampi takmah om hi (Late 145:9). Itna in hoihna lah cihciang in, Paul in, midangkhat i it nakleh Pasian hong veiina leh hong hehpihna lim ciing ding cihi.

**3. Lungdam (_synchairō_) thumaan sungah.** _Synchairō_ mite tawh lungdam khawp theihna ahi hi (Luka 1:58; Luka 15:6, 9; 1 Cor. 12:26; Phil. 2:17, 18).

**4. Thuakzo (_stegō_) nakhempeuh.** Laisiamte in _stegō_ pen kemcinna tawh “siin”; puahphat nading in “thuak” cihte kikum uhhi. 1Cor. 9: 12 sunga tua thuakzawhna cihpen, laisiang- thot eite khempeuh a dingin teeltheih ding anihna vive ahihi.

**5. Um (_pisteuō_) nakhempeuh.** _Pisteuō_ pen Greekpau upna (_pistis_) panin hong pai ahi hi. 1 Cor. 13 sunga, na khempeuh um cihpen, khatlehkhat kitelkhialhna teng hemkhia cihi.

**6. Lam-en (_elpizō_) nakhempeuh.** Laisiangthothak sungah, _elpizō_ pen thuhoih hong piang ding cih lam-en tawntung a cihna ahi hi.

**7. Lungduai takin (_hypomenō_) ngak.** 1 Corinthians 13:7 sunga _stegō_ leh _hypomenō_ cih laimal tegel in kilamdanna neilo uhhi. Haksatna sungah thuakzo cihna ahi hi. Paul in kam tawpna ah _stegō_ a zatkikkik noploh manin _hypomenō_ zang hihtuak hi. A kibang thu khat pen kammal kibanglo a zatna hangin upna leh lam-etna a kibulphuh zawkdeuh nading in a zat hihtuak hi. Thukhat panin, upna leh lam-etna hangin itna kipkho thei hi.

`1Corinth 13: 4–7 leh Galati 5: 22, 23 simkaak in. Hihmun tegel kikal ah a kibang thu namnih namu hiam? Eima’ nunna sungah hibang itna koibangin neithei ding hiam?`
