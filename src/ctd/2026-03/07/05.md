---
title:  Zeisu’ Limsuun Khat
date:   12/08/2026
---

1Cor. 13: 4–7 simsuk leng, limte khempeuh eima’ lahkhiat kikna ah atawm atam in kithanem mahmah cih i phawk ciangin hong dahhuai ngeungau ding hi. 1 Corinth a gelhlai in Paul in Zeisu’ lungsim neih tuak mahmah hi. Christ in ahihleh tua omzia teng khempeuh picing takin hong lahkhia khin hi. Tua hi a, Paul in hong lahkhiat itna pen Zeisu’ limsuun khat ahi hi.

`John 13: 1, 34; John 15: 9, 12; 1Tim. 1: 14; 2Tim. 1: 7, 13; 1John 3: 16; leh 1John 4: 7–12, 19–21 simin. Hihmun tepan itna thu bangteng i kisin thei tam maw?`

Pasian in itna hi (1John 4: 8). A Tapa hongpiak dongin hong it hi (John 3: 16). Zeisu in zong hih itna pen picing takin hong lahkhia hi (Heb. 1: 3). Itna bangzah in lahkhia hiam cih i theihnop leh Zeisu sawtveipi et ding ahi hi. Laisiangthothak sungah Zeisu’ limsuun i muhnopleh; 1Cor. 13 sunga Amah’n itna’ hoihna a lahna teng hiding hi.

**1. Zeisu in lungduai hi.** “A mawhnei phabel kei kahihna hangmah in, nidang ciangin Amah umin nuntak tawntungna a ngah ding mite etteh theih dingin Christ Zeisu in a lungduaina keitungah cingtakin hong lahtheihna ding” (_makrothymia_)” (1Tim. 1: 16).

**2. Zeisu Hoih hi.** Laisiangtho in “Topa Hoih hi” (1Pet. 2: 3) cihi. Hiteng ah “Topa” in Zeisu a cihna ahi hi. “Hoih” cihpen Greek pau _chrēstos_ hi a, which comes from the same sepna _chrēsteuomai_ pan hong kaa “hoihna lahna” pan hongpai hi (1Corinthians 13: 4).

**3. Zeisu thumaan sungah lungdam.** Zeisu in Pa’ deihna zui in Pa’ itna a ngah ciangin lungdam hi (John 15: 9–11; John 17: 12–14).

**4. Zeisu in nakhempeuh thuak.** Hebrew 12: 2, 3 sungah Zeisu in “Singlamteh thuak” ci hi “maizumna kihta lo in singlamteh tungah sihna thuak ding ut”. Kuamah dang Zeisu bangin a thuakzo omlo hi (Phil. 2: 8). Ama’ maiah a om lungdamna hang hi!

**5. Zeisu in nakhempeuh um.** Ananias in Paul kikheel takpi hiam ci in a dot ciangin (Sawl. 9: 13, 14), Zeisu in, “Keima’na sem dingin ka teelkhin hi” (Sawl. 9: 15) cihi. Zeisu in mihing pen amau’ hihna bangin muziau lo a, Ama’ vangtawh bang hong suak ding cih tawh mu hi.

`Itna maan taktak in koibang cih Zeisu in koibang in hong lahbehbeh lai hiam?`
