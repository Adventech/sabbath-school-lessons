---
title:  Khalam Galdona
date:   13/09/2026
---

`2Corinth 10: 1–11 simin. Corinth mite tawh akizopna ah Paul’ lungneemna pen thanemna peuh kisakha zawsop hi. Corinth mite laka kamsang zuaute tawh a ki zopna ah Paul in a hangsanna kammal bangteng hiam?`

2 Corinth 10 na pen Paul in ama’ aituam in thu pan hi: “Keimah, Paul in, Christ’ migitna leh lungduaina tawh note kiangah ka hong thuum hi” (2Cor. 10: 1). Hihthu in tua pawlpi sungah kamsangzuau thuhilhna simlutta cih a lunghihmawhna lahna ahi hi. 2Cor. 10: 1 sunga Paul ‘ kammalte in, langdo mite’ mawhzonna tengpen lung hihmawhna tawh pulaak hi. A mawhzondan in; Paul in gamlapi panin a laikhak penbel, lauhthawnna sim-mawhna bang hinapi: midang tawh maitang kimuhna ah a meidawipa hi ci uhhi (2 Cor. 10: 10, 11). Meidawi thanemna cihte peuhmah pen, vangliatna tawh a kidim, Christ bang in lungnemna leh migitna tawh kidimna Paul in cihi.

Kamsangzuaute peuhmah hangsan tak lungmuang in talsik kul (2Cor. 10: 2) hi na pi, Christ’ migit lungduaina tawh helh kulse hi (2Cor. 10: 1). Khatvei, Zeisu in “Keimah in, kiniamkhiat in ka lungnem hithiat hi” (Mate 11: 29) cihi. Ahihhang Zeisu in zong sumkhekte pen hangsan takin talsikpah in a sabuai uh lumlehsak in buluhte ci in taai hi (Mate 21: 12, 13). Pharisee tebang pen a maitang mahpan in, mi simtatte, a kaang kizut hankhukte cigiap hi (Mate 23: 23–27). Zeisu mahbang in Paul in zong, eite pen Pasian’ galvan namkim (Eph. 6: 12–17) zangin khalam galdote ihihlam theilua hi.

2Corinth 10 sunga Paul’ kammalte pen, nuntakna nangawn a lauhuaisak galthu vive (2Cor. 10: 3–6) ahi hi. Hihpen mihing maimai kidona hilo a; Christ kiangah mi zawh na’ng galdona siangtho ahi hi. Tuatawh kizui in, thumaanlo leh ngahsutna maanlo peuhma talsik do in Pasian’ kammal siksan in hepkhiat ding ahi hi. Tua hileh ngaihsutna khempeuh “zogawp in Christ’ thu mangsak thei” (2Cor. 10: 5) cihi.

Hih khalam galdona ah, Paul in Christ’ thupiakna tawh gamtang hi. Tua thupiakna in suksiatna lam hilo in puahphatna lam hizaw hi (2Cor. 10: 8). Khalam makaite in amau Pasian thupiakna tawh gamtang a kicih dinguh baihlam mahmah hi. Bangpeuh aci uh zongin, Christ’ thupiakna a nei peuhmah in Amah bang ding cihpen phawk uh henla, lungsing sungah lungnem in kiniamkhiat ta uhhen.

Paul in thuneihna septheihna a gen ciangin, Christ’ thupiakna tawh a sep hi a; tuabang in a cihna zongpen, Corinth mite in kamsangzuaute’ thu peuh ngai uh ahih manun Christ tungah a cihtakna uh a kisiatsak khak ding a launa hang ahi hi.

`Eite zong kamsangzuau tetawh kizopna sungah koibang in lungnem in hangsan thei ding ihi hiam? Banghang in a nihtuak in hihtuak nasa hiam?`
