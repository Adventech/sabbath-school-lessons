### Tukalsung Simding
2Cor. 10: 1–17; Jer. 9: 24; 2Cor. 11: 1–15, 22–28; 2Cor. 12: 20, 21; 2Cor. 13: 5.

> <p>Kamngah</p>
> “Kote’ zat galdona hiam pen leitung galhiam lelte hilo a; galte’ panmun phualpi a susiazo ding Pasian’ galhiam khauhte ahi hi”(2 Corinth 10: 4).

Paul in thusialua tuakngeilo hihtuaklel a, tu in thubuai dangkhat a siansuah ding hong om zel a: kamsang zuaute ahi uhhi. Hih mite in amah leh a nasep dokinken uhhi. A suksia zawlai in hih kamsangzuaute in Corinth pawlpimite zolsim den uhhi. Paul hih thu buaina sungah kidona pen khalam’ galdona cihi.

Hihpen a thu omzah sangin a uang genuh ahithei diam? Hilo hi. Tua mite in Paul lellel do hizawlo in Christ mahmah a do ahihna Paul in theitel mahmah hi. Paul pen a kiliansak mahmah angkhual makaite bang hilo hi. Ama’ aana kipkhona dingin ama’ khutnuai mite’ tungah a kiliansak keei makai namte hilo hi.

Amah’n a tangko dingin thupiak angah ngiat tua thupuak pen: nuntakna, sihna leh tawntung thu hi cihpen amah’n telngiat hi. Hihmah sem dingin Pasian in amah asawl ahihzong amah’n tellua hi. “Christ Zeisu’ Sawltak dingin Pasian in hongteel keimah Paul” (1Cor. 1: 1) ci ngiat hi.

Thu zuau hilhte hong tun ciangin, pawlpi in Laisiangtho mah siksan in itna tawh kipkho takin om ding kisam hi. Lungdamthu kisia hetlo in siangtho sitset in kemcing ding hang a, kha khempeuh in tawntung lam-etna neisak ding hihang.
