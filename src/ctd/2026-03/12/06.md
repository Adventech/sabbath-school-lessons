---
title:  Akisiklote tungah Thuumna
date:   17/09/2026
---

2 Corinth. 12: 14–13: 10 sim lecin, Paul in in pawlpi kiangah a thumveina ahawhnathu ahi hi (2Cor. 12: 14; 2Cor. 13: 1). Kamsangzuaute khawng sangin amah niamzaw hetlo ahihna pulak khinzo hi. Tu in lungmuang takin Corinth ah hong paikik in, a kisikmello Corinth mite puahphat ding kahanciam ding cihi. Ama’ hong hawhna thu bulpi sungah hihzong khat kihel hi. Paul in a kampau gamtatna khempeuh pen pawlpi puahphatna ding vive ahi hi (2Cor. 12: 19).

`2 Corinth 12: 20, 21 simin. Bang mawhna khawngpeuh in Corinth pawlpi kha dinmun a dingin lauhuai sak hiam?`

2Corinth 12: 20, 21 sungah mawhna sazian Paul in a gualhsuk teng pen; Paul’ lai-khakmah ahi Rom. 1: 29-31; Gal. 5: 19-21 sunga tetawh zong kibang uhhi. Amaspen nih te ahihleh 1Cor. 3: 3 sunga om masa a, Paul in pawlpi sungah hazatna leh ki langneihna om cihi. Amah a thumveina a paihun ciangin bangmah a kilamdan melloh ding patau hi. “Kei kong tung ciangin note’ omzia ding kadeih bangin na omloh dinguh” leh a lehlam in “note in keima’ maisuah na muhnopzia bangun nong muhloh khak dinguh” (2Cor. 12: 20) kalau hi cihi. A cihnopna bulpipen amaute tawh kizopna ah “Christ migitna leh lungduai na” (2Cor. 10: 1) tawh hih ding sangsik in “a thumaanlo a citaklo khempeuh gimpia ding kakipan zawsop hi” (2Cor. 10: 6) a cihnopna ahi hi.

Ama’ lunghihmawhna pipen in “thaangtatna, zualzangna leh hukna lanna hangin na mawhnate uh” (2Cor. 12: 21) na kisik keivua leh, tua mawhnate in pawlpi sungah pawl kikhenna piangsak hi cihi.

Tuabanah, Paul in, pawlpi’ khalna tawh mawhneite puahphatna ngiim hi (2Cor. 13: 1–4). Thanemna pen mawhnei nundan a dingin paulam hitheilo hi. Gualzawhna nundan adeihte a dingin vangliatna om hi (2Cor. 13: 4). Corinth pawlpi mite pawlkhat in zahmawh mawhna abawl uhpen, amau’ nunna sungah Pasian’ vanliatna omsaklo hizaw uhhi. Amaute kisik uh henla, thumanna lamah hong paipih vangliatna ngah lehuh Paul in deihsak hi. Taaina thuhilhna pen anunung penin koih nuam hi.

Tua ahih manin Paul in “Note gamtakhial khalo in … thumaan in na gamtat dinguh ,,, kote’ thungetna pen note kicingtaak theihna ding” (2Cor. 13: 7–9) cihi. Thungetna hoihlua mai! Amaute upna sungah a omleh omloh amau mah kisittel ding sawl hi.

`2Cor. 13: 5 simin. Upna sunga om cih bang acihna hiam? Upna sungah na omlam koibang in thei ding nahiam?`
