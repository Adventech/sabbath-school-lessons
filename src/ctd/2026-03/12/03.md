---
title:  Topa sungah Kisial
date:   14/09/2026
---

Zanni in, Paul leh a naseppihte in, amau’ nasep pen khalam galdona ci in hahkat takin Pasian kiangpan galhiam namkim zang uhhi. Tuni in, kamsangzuau peuhmah in mihing’ dinmun ciangbek tawh gamtang cih i mu ding uhhi. A kisialhdan uhlah khengval lua hi. A langkhat ah Paul in Topa sung bekah kisial hi. Amah’n zong “Mikhat in a kisialh nopleh Topa’ sungbek ah kisial tahen” (2Cor. 10: 17) ci in gelh hi.

`2Corinth 10: 13–17 simin. Kidemna lungsim in lungdamthu hilhna ah bang zah lauhuai hiam?`

Paul in a kisialhna kammalte pen zalom tawntung in kamlette in lunglutsak mah mah hi. Ahihtei hang tuabang kiliatsakna cihpen amau’ tanglai hunin pheivei lelpeuh a, mipite a sukkhak lohna dingin a mipi mahin kemcing zawsop uhhi. Paul in zong tuathu theiveve ahih manin, amau’ nung zuiziau hi. Tua banah, Paul in ama’ kisialhna dan pen tua kamsangzuaute kisialhna dan tawh kibang hetlo cih zong kitelcian sak mahmah hi. Amah Topa sung bekah kisial hi (2Cor. 10: 17).

Tuazong pen, Laisiangtholui sungpan in a lakkhiat hi a: “a kisathei te in, Keimah leitung ah migitna, thumaan leh thutang a zang kahihna leh tuate sungah nuam asapa ka hihna a theihna uhah kisathei uhhen” (Jer. 9: 24). Jeremiah’ kammal zangin Paul in; Christ hong itna, thumaan thutak a zangpa leh dikna bek tawh a gamtapa bulphuh hi cih hong lakkhia hi,

Thukhat panin, Paul’ kisialhna peuhmah pen Christ sungah Pasian in a hihsak teng hangbek ahi hi. Tua hi a, Paul’ kisialhna in Laisiangtho tawh kituak hi. A langkhat ah, amah a langdo mite’ kisialhna ahihleh, amau leh midang ki enkak in kidemna sungah kisial uhhi. Tua pen haina taktak ahi hi! (2Cor. 10: 12).

2Corinth 10: 14–16 sungah, Paul in, Corinth leh a kimkot huamah a nasepna teng pen lungdamthu tangkona mah bulphuhpi in nanei hi. Christ’ sihna, leh thawhkikna sungah hotkhiatna thupha a tangko theihna dingin, Zeisu a itna mahin Paul tawsawn den hi.

Corinth khua ah amaumah akiphattawm kamsang zuaute tawh kibanglo in, Paul pen Pasian in pakta hi (2Cor. 10: 12, 18). Amah pen “Christ Zeisu’ Sawltak dingin Pasian in a teeltuam” (1Cor. 1: 1) cihi. Hih sapna tungah ama’ nuntak tawpna dongin citak ngiat hi (2Tm, 4: 7)

`2Corinth 10: 12–18 simin. Pawlpi makai leh pawlpi mite in, kidemnopna koibang in pelhthei ding hiam? Bangmah thupitna mel a neilo nate sungah awk ding banghang in baihmahmah se thei hiam?`
