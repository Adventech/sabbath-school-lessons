---
title:  Kamsangzuau teng Pholhkhia
date:   15/09/2026
---

Laisiangthothak sungah Christiante te lakah kamsang zuau tetawh kisai hilhkhol na tampitak mah om hi. Zeisu mahmah in a nungzuite pen tua thumah hilhkhol hi (Mate 7: 15–20). Sawltakte in zong tuamah genbel in nei uhhi (Gal. 1: 6–9; 1Tim. 6: 3–5; 2Pet. 2: 1–3).

`2Corinth 11: 1–15 simin. Paul in a tuahkhak kamsangzuaute tawh kisai tonate koibang in pulak hiam?`

Paul in kamsangzuaute nasepna pholaaksak hi. Tua santak mahah ama’ nasepna pen Christ-bulphuh ahihna zong pulak hi. Corinth pawlpi pen mo khattawh genteh in, tua mo pen Christ tungah a aapding a pa pen amah kahi cihi (2Cor. 11: 2). Pawlpi a itman in a cih cihi (2Cor. 11: 11). Tua hangmah in amaute in Paul a panpih ding uh hinapi, sumlam vangik peuhmah pia nuamlo hi (2 Cor. 11: 7–12).

A langkhat ah, “Sawltak lthupi mahmahte” (2Cor. 11: 5) (kamsangzuaute a cihnopna hihtuak) pen Eve akhem gulpi tawh genteh hi (2Cor. 11: 3). Eden huan sunga Satan mahbang in, Corinth pawlpi sunga kamsangzuaute in khemna leh suksiatna bawl uhhi (2Cor. 11: 3, 4). Corinth mite in Christ tungah lungsim takpi tawh a ki-aapna, a cih takna tenguh pan lam apialkhak ding Paul’ lunghihmawhna bulpi ahi hi.

Buluhte in Paul’ hilh thupuak tawh akibanglo a dangkhat – Zeisu a tuamkhat leh lungdamthu a dangkhat hong hilh uhhi (2Cor. 11: 4). Hihthu in Zeisu’ thu a hilh mi khem peuh pen Pasian’ thupiakna ngahmi hikhinlo cih lahna ahi hi. Tuathu tawh kisai in Zeisu ngiat in “Topa, Topa hong ci khempeuh in vangtung gam lutlo ding uh a, Ka Pa’ deihna bang ahihte bek lut thei ding hi” (Mate 7: 21) cihi. Galati 1: 6-9 sungah Paul mahin, a dang lungdamthu khat hong hilh a om peuhmahleh tua mipa in samsiatna ngah tahen ci khin hinapi; Corinth mite pawlkhat in hih khialhna mahmah phal gige tazen uhhi.

Paul in tua sawltakzuaute minvawh in “Sawltak a kineihkhem, nasepna ah zuau in Christ’ Sawltak taktak a bat theihna dingun amel kiseelsak uhhi” (2Cor. 11: 13) cihi. “Satan nangawn in Pasian’ vantungmi tawh a kibangin hong kibawltawm thei hi. Satan’ sawltakte’n zong thumaan nasem bangin hong kibawltawm” (2Cor. 11: 14, 15) cihi. Christ’ nasem kineih in Satan’ mimuan thukante ahih uhpen dahhuai mahmah hi. Paul in “hunbei ciangin amau nasepna thuak ngawn ding uhhi” cihtawh thukhup ta hi (2Cor. 11: 15).

`Pawlpi sungah khialhnate Paul in khauhtak in dawngthuk hi! Tua thu in eite e leh, bangthu hong gen naci hiam?`
