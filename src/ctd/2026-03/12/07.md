Ellen G. White, “The Laodicean Church,” p. 125, in The Advent Review and Sabbath Herald, September 30, 1873.

“A beisa hun a mawhna khialhnate bawlkikkik loding in Topa in Amite kemcing hi. A teelsa mite nangawn ahihzawh leh khemding in upkhialhna leh a kihhuai nate thapia in, a lamdang mahmah in thumaanlohna ah dingin, meel kiseel khemna tetawh hong khem kamsangzuaute pen om denden hi”—Ellen G. White, in The Advent Review and Sabbath Herald, January 7, 1904.

“Eima’ muhdan khempeuh zong sittel sitset in, eimah upna ah kipleh kiplo theih telna dingin, a nungta Pasian kammalte encian dingin Topa in eite hong deih sak hi. Thu maan ka um hi aci mi tampi takmah in, kei kalungnuam hi ‘kei kahau hi, neihlehlam tampi kanei hi, bangmah kitasam ke’ng’ (Mang. 3: 17) aci uhhi” —Ellen G. White, Counsels to Writers and Editors, p. 36.

“Thumaan kician takin hong kilakkhin hinapi, Pasian thumaan dinmun ah amau’ upna maanlo tengpeuh mihing in koih mawkmawk hi. Tuabang mite in amau’ upna pen Pasian’ kammal tawh etteh sittel in, amau upna maanlo tawh Pasian’ kammal simlo in, amau’upna maan mah hi a cihtentan kei nakleh; amaute un khuamial sungah mittaw in vakvailo ding uh a, khialhna sungah om paisuak lo ding uhhi.

Mi tampi in Laisiangtho pen amau’ ngaihdan in letkhia in amaumah kikhemtawm in midang zong amau’ telkhialhna mah tawh khem sawnsawn uhhi. Pasian’ kammal i sim ciangin, lungsim kiniamkhiat in sim ding hihang. Angkhualna khempeuh, eima’thubek thu sakna cihte hemkhia ding hihang. Sawtveipi i neihsa ngaihdan peuhmah pen khial ngeilo ci in ngiahsut huailo hi”—Ellen G. White, Counsels to Writers and Editors, pp. 36, 37.

**Kikupding Dotnate:**

`2Cor. 10: 1–6 simkik in. Pasian thumaan a dingin ‘khalam galdona’ a tuah ciangin Paul’ hihdan koibang hiam? Eima’ khalam galdona ah zong koibang in kizang thei ding hiam?`

`Laisaiangtho in hun beima in, kamsangzuau tampi takte in mipite thumaan panin pialsak ding uh cihi. Na khuatual pawlpi in tua kamsangzuaute khaamzawhna ding in bang hihthei ding hiam? Pawlpi’ mission nasepna picinna dingin hihthu in bang hangin kisampha mahmah thei hiam?`

`Thuaksiatna kigual deudau in kisialh theihna’ng hunlem, Paul in banghang in ngahthei dihdih hiam (2Cor. 11: 16–33)? “Topa sungah kisial” cihin bang acihna hiam?`

`Pawlpi mite in upna sungah kip/kiplo cih banghang in kisittel kulkisam sese hiam? (2Cor. 13: 5)? Bang kilamdanna omsak hiam?`
