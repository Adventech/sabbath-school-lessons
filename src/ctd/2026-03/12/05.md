---
title:  Lungdamthu a dingin Thuaksiatnate
date:   16/09/2026
---

Kamsang zuaute pen Satan’ mimuante a cihkhit ciangin (2Cor. 11: 1–15), Paul in, tua kamsangzuaute thugenna pen Corinth mite in vangaizel cihthu in khiatna neivetlo a hihna a muhteltheih nadingun; mihai bangin; kisialhna tawmcik khat tawh amaute’ kimawlna bang lianin kimawlpih hi (2Cor. 11: 16–21). Tua mite khawng Corinth mite in pimuh mahmah uh ahilhleh; Paul in pimuh huai semzaw tham hi. Lungdamthu a dingin a thuaksiatna khempeuh pen a citak Christ’ nasempa ahihna hizawsop hi (2Cor. 11:22, 23).

`2 Corinth 11: 22–28 simin. Bang thubulpi bawl hiam?`

Paul in Jew mi ahihna pen, tua kamsangzuate mahtawh a kibang (2Cor. 11: 22) hinapi, Christ a ding anasepna ahihleh khengvalpek leuleu hi. “Amaute in Christ’ nasem ahihleh, hong haipau mai ning; Christ’ nasem ahoihzaw kahi hi” (2Cor. 11: 23) cihi. Ama’ nasepna ah khengval; a thongkiatna ah khengval; a satthuakna ah thuaksia zaw hi.

Tua ciangbek hilo lai hi. A thuaksiatna sazian sungah; sawmthum leh kuavei satna ngavei (2Cor. 11: 24), molhpum tawh satna, suangtawh denna, tembaw kisiatna, tuipi thukah lauhuaina (2Cor. 11: 25), khualzinna ah lauhuaina, gun sungah lauhuaina, buluhte lauhuaina, mipihte kiangpan lauhuaina, Gentile mite kiangpan lauhuaina, khuapilian sung ah lauhuaina, gamlak ah lauhuaina, tuipi ah lauhuaina, thu umlote tungpan lauhuaina (2 Cor. 11: 26), gimtak in nasepna, ihmut khamlohna, gilkialna, dangtakna, an kitasapna, khuadam giahna ding neilo in ka om hi (2Cor. 11: 27). Tua bangteng bek hilo in, pawlpi tuamtuam te a dingin lunghihmawhna atuamtuam kaneilai cihi (2 Cor. 11:28).

Christ’ nasem taktakte bekin lungdamthu a dingin hibang thuakna thuaknuam bek uhhi. Paul in a thuaksiatna teng kisial ding hileh tampi a gending omlai hi. Ahizong in a laikhak a nununglam tengah, ama’ kisialhna hangthu a pulaak ciangin Christ a ding ama’ sepna teng bulphuhlo zaw in: Christ in ama; a ding ahihsak teng bulphuh zaw hi.

Pasian’ vangliatna peuhmah pen mihing’ thanemna sungah kilangh phadeuh cih Paul in thei gige hi (2Cor. 12: 9, 10). Ka pumpi sungah ling khat hong koihsak (2Cor. 12: 7) cihi. Paul in ama’ sepzawhna tungah a kisialhtheihloh nadingin Pasian in tua ling tawh khaam hi. Tua lignin amah kiniamkhiat sak, ama’ thanemna phawksak in vantung vangliatna bek muang sakin, Pasian’ hoihna leh hehpihna sang semsem sakzaw hi.

`Lungdamthu a dingin na thuak ngei khin hiam? Tua nathuakna pan bang teng nakisin kha hiam? Paul in a thuaksiatna te a hihbang in, nangma thuakna zong koibang in nahih hiam?`
