---
title:  Siangthona ah Sapna
date:   02/09/2026
---

2Cor. 6: 3–10 sungah, Paul in, Corinth mite pen Pasian tawh kilemsak hi. Ama’ haksatna leh a gualzawhna teng gualsuk deudau in Christ’ nungzui hihna leh Pasian’ nasempa khat hihna in bang cih hong lak hi. Atom in, amah’n haksatna zong gualsuk a (2Cor. 6: 4, 5), siangtho nuntakna (2Cor. 6: 6), nasepna galhiam (2Cor. 6: 7), nasepna sungah kisukkhakna te (2Cor. 6: 8-10) ahi hi. Paul in Corinth mite pen Pasian tawh kilem dingin a thuhilh khit ciangin, a siangtho in nuntak ding; thu umlote leh a siangtholo nate’ huzaap khempeuh pan kikepsiam ding cihte Paul in hanthawn lai hi (2Cor. 6: 14–17).

`2Cor. 6: 11–7: 1 simin. Hihmun in siangtho nuntakna acih koici gen hiam?`

Hih munah Paul in pawlpi sungah ki it kingaihna kisampha mahmah ci hi (1Cor. 6: 11–13). Pasian tawh a kilemna mite ahih uhleh; mi khatlehkhat a kilem sawm ding uhhi. Tua mite in a pheilam in midangte tawh kilemna neite hong suak uhhi.

Tua banah, a siangtho dingin anuai a thu guk tawh hong samlai hi (1) “Thu umlo mite tawh hakkol kikimlo puakhawmlo ding” (2Cor. 6: 14); (2) “Amaute’ lakpan in hong paikhia un” (2Cor. 6: 17); (3) “Kikhenkhia un” (2Cor. 6: 17); (4) “A sianglote lawngkei un” (2Cor. 6: 17); (5) “Lungsim leh pumpi aninsak thei khempeuh panin ki siansuah in” (2Cor. 7: 1); (6) “Pasian zahtakna tawh nungta in siangtho sinsen un” (2Cor. 7: 1). Hih hanthotna tungtawnin Pasian in milim biakna tawh kikhen in siangtho nuntakna hong kal ahihlam hong phawksak hi.

A langkhat lamah, tuathu in, Christian pawlpi peuhmah biakinn siangtho ahihna kamciam sagih hong laklai hi: (1) “Amaute lakah Ka teng ding hi”; (2) “Amaute tawh Ka omkhawm ding hi”; (3) “Amau’ Pasian Ka hi ding hi”; (4) “Amaute Ka mi hi ding uhhi”; (5) “Note Ka hong sang ding hi”; (6) “Amaute’ Pa kahi ding hi”; (7) “Keima tapa leh tanu te nahi ding uhhi” (2Cor. 6: 16, 17, 18).

2Cor. 6: 16 sunga kamciam 4 tengpen 2Cor. 6: 17 sunga thupiak kizom thum teng hong pianna bulpi ahi hi (2 Cor. 6: 17 a thupatna ah Tua ahih ciangin cihpen enin). Hih thu in, siangthona cihpen mi khatpeuh hanciamna pan hi mawkmawk lo in, lungsim sung ah Khasiangtho nasepna hi cih hong lak hi. Siangthona pen Pasian kiangpan hongpai ahih teihang, thu um mite ahih uhleh; amau’ sep ding teng semveve in milim biakna nial sinsen ding uhhi.

`2Corinth 6: 16–18 sunga Pasian’ kamciam in siangthona pen koibang hi ci hiam?`
