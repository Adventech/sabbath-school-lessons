### Tukalsung Simding
2Cor. 3:1–9; 2Cor. 4:7–18; 2Cor. 5:11–15; Col. 1:19–23, Eph. 2:13–16; 2Cor. 6:11–7:1; 2Cor. 7.

> <p>Kamngah</p>
> “Gimna katuah zelzel uh hangin ka sikei uh a; lung hihmawhna ka tuahuh hangin ka lungkia kei uh a; gal tampi ka tuah uh hangin pawl kaki tasam ngeikei uh a; liamna baina katuah hangin ka sikei uhhi. Kote pumpi tungpanin Zeisu’ nuntakna a kilattheihna dingin hih ka leitung nuntakna pumpi mahmah uh zong Zeisu sihna bang mah ka thuak zelzel uhhi” (2 Corinthians 4: 8–10).

Nungkal in, Paul in ama’ Lungsim Takpi leh Kitheihna Lungsim kipsakpha in, amah in Corinth mite tungah a itna kiamsak zohi cih mawhsakna panin amah kidaalzo ta cih mukhin hihang. Ama’ khalam tate a dingin deihsakna lianpen tawh nasem den hi. 2Cor. 7 lam hong zuatpih 2Cor. 2: 12-17 sungthu khat tawh kamhong hi. Tuabang in hihna tawh Christ a ding nasepna diktak khat pulaak khia hi. Tua thu ah Paul’ ngaihdan panin pilna tampi kingah thei hi.

Tukal in, 2 Cor. 3-7 sunga Christ a dingin kha angahna teng Paul in a genna en ding hihang. EGWhite in “mawhneite hong kikheelna leh thumaan hangin hong siangtho na in; Pasian nasem khat pen Pasian in Ama’ nasem dingin a sapna teci lianpen ahi hi. Sawltak khat hihna teci zong a kikheel mite lungtang tungah kiciamteh a, amau’ nundan thak in tecipang hi. Amaute’ sungah Christ leh ama’ vangliatna lam-etna piangsak hi. Pasian’ nasem khat pen ama’ sepnasa ciaptehna teng in nakpi takin thahatna piakik thei hi” —Ellen G. White, The Acts of the Apostles, p. 328.
