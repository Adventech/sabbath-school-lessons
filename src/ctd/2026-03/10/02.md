---
title:  Nasepna Maan taktak Gahte
date:   30/08/2026
---

`2Cor. 3: 1–9 simin. Eite zong Christ’ laikhak koibang in hithei ding hiam?`

Thuneite tungah hoihsakpihna lai cihpen Greco-Roman hun lai in laang mahmah hi. Ahihhang, Paul in tuabang laite tawi selo lel hi. Corinth mite nundan a puahpha Kha Siangtho vangliatna in Ama’ nasepna maantaktak cih teci panna ahi hi. Corinth pawlpi a din theihna zongpen, ama’ pilna leh hanciamna zong hikhollo cih Paul in telcian hi (2 Cor. 3: 4–6). Ama’ zaa khanna ding khawng ahihzong omlo hi (2Cor. 3: 5; 1Cor. 2: 2).

Paul in ama’ Pasian nasepna pen thuciam nih tawh genteh hi: thuciamlui pen Moses tawh hi a; thuciamthak ahihleh amah leh anseppihte tawh cihi. Lai a nawhsim mi in, thuciamlui in hotkhiatna’ng lam-etna hong pialo ci uh a; ahihhang tuabang hilo hi. Hot khiatnapen thuciam alui athak ah zong omveve hi.

Thuciamlui pen lungdamthu mahilhna ahi hi. “Gentile mite zong a upna uh hangun Amah tawh kipawl theihna dingin Pasian in a bawlna dingthu Laisiangtho in na matheih hi. Abraham kiangah lungdamthu na mahilhkhol a, Pasian in leitung mite khem peuh thupha a piakna dingin nangmah hong zang ding hi” (Gal. 3: 8) naci hi.

2Cor. 3: 1–4: 6 sungah, Thuciamlui cihpen, amau’sepna hanciamna tawh Pasian a lungdamsak ding mite lim kipuasak muding hihang. Thuciamthak ahihleh Pasian in amau te a ding ahihsak ding kamciamsate khempeuh muangin Pasian’ hehpihna tungah a kinga te cihna ahi hi.

Paul in lungdamthu tungah mite’ dawnthukna kibanglo namnih genkhia a: thu um mite dawnthukna leh thu umlote dawnthukna ahi hi. Pasian in ahongpiak a kibanglo lung damthu, Laisiangtho lui ah khat leh laisiangthothak ah a dangkhat cihbangin namnih a gen hilo a; “eite’ sepzawhna hang hilo, Ama’ ngiimnasa bang leh a dikna hangin Pasian in eite hong honkhia in, Ama’mi dingin hongsam hi. Leitung pianmapek in Zeisu Christ tungtawn in tua dikna a hongpia hi” (2Tim. 1: 9).

Hihthu in 2Cor. 2: 14-4: 6 sunga tangthu kisuut kik hilel hi ci in nial ding hilo hi. Tua tangthu sungah pawlkhat “hotkhiat” leh pawlkhat “kisiatna tung” (2Cor. 2: 15) cih thu a hilhcian nopna hang ahi hi. Moses’ nasepna peuhmah pen uploh muanlohna tawh dawngthuk uh ahih manun; Moses’ nasepna pen kisiatna leh sihna nasep ci in kimusak phial uhhi. Corinth mite in Paul nasepna umuh ahih manun, Paul’nasepna in; dikna nasep, nuntakna hong pia Kha Siangtho nasep hong suak hi.

Hibang in Corinth mite in hotkhiatna ngahlawh uh ahih manun, Paul’ nasep na pen thumaan cih tecipanna ahi hi.
