Ellen G. White, “The Message Heeded,” pp. 323–334, in The Acts of the Apostles.

Nungkal in, a tunga thu pen The Acts of the Apostles bulphuh in simkhin hihang. Simkik taak mahmah lel hi. Tu in ahihleh, Paul’ dahsakna lai lamah lungngai ni in, a lai gelhlai in a lungsim puakzia, leh tua mite a kisikna thuhoih a zak ciangin a lungnopna cihte lungngai ding hihang. Paul’ nasep pen thumaan taktak ahihna leh Christ a dingin na i septak ciangin zatpah theih ding pilna zong hong pia hi.

“Pasian kiangpan mawhmaina leh, Pasian hong itna hangin, eite zong Pasian tawh kilemkik thei ihihlam vannuai leitungah pulaakkhia ding hihang. Mite a kisik uh ciangin, a lungsim uh a kitap ciangin, Zeisu pen eite’ leh Pasian kilemtuahna ding mawh thoihna hi cih umin Pasian tawh kilemta uhhi”—Ellen G. White, Special Testimonies On Education, p. 223.

“Pawlpi khat ihihna tawh, khuavak lianpi ngah khin hihang. Topa in muangin hong aap hih khuavakpen leitung ah thupha leh phattuamna bawl ding ihi hi. Kilemtuah na nasep zong eite hong kipia hi. Tunglam panin vangliatna tawh mite in Pasian tawh kilem dingin hanthawn ding hihang” —Ellen G. White, Letter 32, 1903.

Pasian tawh akilem mi peuhmah in siangthona zong ding hihang. 2 Cor. 7: 1 sung tawh kisai in EGWhite in Paul’ cihnopna a genna ah “kisiansuah in Pasian zahtak” (2 Cor. 7: 1) ding cihi. EGWhite in bel thu ummi a thakte in “amau khe tungah dingzo in, Christian khangtote suak in, upna thakhauh, a lawp mahmah leh Pasian’gam a mainawt theihna dingin lungsim khempeuh tawh a ki-aap te suaksak nuam hi” cihi—Ellen G. White, The Acts of the Apostles, p. 201.

**Kikupding Dotnate:**

`Paul in eite pen lungdamthu a kikoihna “leibeelte” (2Cor. 4: 7) hong cilel hi. Mi i cih limlim pen thanem, kitam baih in ciangtan neilua ihih manun, lungdamthu tang-kona ding thanemna omsaklo in, thahatsak semsem thei hi.`

`“Mithak” (2Cor. 5: 17) cihpen bang acihnopna hiam? Hih in i nisim nunna koibangin hong sukha hiam? Christ in koibangin mithak hong suah hiam?`

`2 Cor. 6: 4, 5 sungah, Paul in lungdamthu hangin haksatna tampi gualsuk diudeu hi. Ama’ thuaknate koibang in dawngthuk hiam (2Cor. 6: 6, 7)? Nang aa te zong e leh koicih ding cih hong hilhcian naci hiam?`

`Paul in Pasian’ deihna tawh dahna leh leitung vaitawh dahna ci in tehkak hi (2 Cor. 7: 10). Dahna leh kisikna koibang in kizomthei ding hiam? Pasian’ deihna bangin dahna leh leitung vai tawh dahna koibangin khentel thei ding hiam?`
