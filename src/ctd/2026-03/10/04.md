---
title:  Christ-Bulphuh Kilemna Nasep
date:   01/09/2026
---

`2Cor. 5: 11–15 simin. Hihmun in Paul’ nasepna peuhmah Christ-bulphuh nasepna ahihlam koibang in hong hilh naci hiam?`

Paul in, Christ a dingin ama’ sepna teng a pulaak kul ding cih thei hi (2 Cor. 5: 10). Amah in “Pasian zahtak ding” leh Christ’ lungdamna thu tawh mite a zoldan ding thei hi (2Cor. 5: 11). Hih zahtakna cihpen Christ vang apiakna leh kihtakna cihte hi a; Paul in Christ a itna leh Christ in amah a itna sungah a khamuanna cihte kihel bilbial hi. Laisiangtholui sungah, Pasian kihtakna cihpen, lungsim khempeuh kha khempeuh tawh Ama’ lampite tawn, Amah it in, Ama’na sem (Thkna. 10: 12) ding cihna ahi hi.

Paul’ nasepna ah amah-kibulphuh lo in Christ-bulphuh zaw hi. Amah kiphat tawm lo hi. Ama’ kiphatsakna pen Christ hizaw hi (2Cor. 12: 9). “Singlamteh tungah i Topa Zeisu Christ’ sihna thu bek kakisialhpih ding” (Gal. 6: 14) cihi. Tua hi a, Corinth mite in amah a kisialpih theihna ding (2Cor. 5: 12) zongpen; amah a langpang te in amau mah a kibulphuh uh bang hilo in Paul in Christ-bulphuh in a nasepna ahi hi.

`2Cor. 5: 16–21; Col. 1: 19–23; Ephet 2: 13–16 simin. Paul in “kilemna nasep” a cihpen bang a cihnopna hiam?`

Christ in kilemna nasep siampenpa ahi hi. Tua mahbang in Amah in “kote tungah kilemna nasep hong pia” (2Cor. 5: 18) cihi. 2Cor. 5: 16-21 sungah kilemna ding thu pen om zelzel hi. Tua pen Paul a ding ahi a, eite a ding mahmah in kisampha mahmah hi.

Pasian in Ama’ Tapa sihna tawh mawhthoihna tungtawn in mihing pen Amah mah tawh hong kilemsak hi. Pasian tawh akilem mi peuhmah mithak ngeina (2Cor. 5: 17) a nei ahi uhhi. Tu in ahihleh “kilemna thupuak” pen Christ’ lungdamthu tangkona tawh tangko ding ahi hi (2Cor. 5: 19). Tua tawh kizui in Paul in “Christ tawh kipawlna hangin eite zong Pasian tawh a kibangin midik i suahtheihna dingin mawhna aneilo Christ pen eite tangding in mawhneimi a suaksak hi” (2Cor. 5: 20).

`Christ in nang a ding hong hihsak teng ngaihsun in. Khialhna mawhna teng, siatna teng khempeuh pen nang a ding vive ahihhang, Amah in singlamteh ah hong hihsak khinzo hi. Hiteng khempeuh pen nang leh midangte kizopna, a diakin Topa a theilo mite tawh kizopna koibang in hong huzaap hiam?`
