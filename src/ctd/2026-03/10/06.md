---
title:  Hehnepna Leh Lungnopna
date:   03/09/2026
---

`2Cor. 7 simin. Corinth mite kisik cihthu a zakciangin Paul’ lungsim koibang hihiam?`

Paul in “note kong it mahmah hi” (2Cor. 7: 3; 2Cor. 6: 11) acih ciangin ama’ lungtang pan itna bangzah a lungkhia diam? Ki it thuk ding a deihna in Paul in “na lungsim uh hong pia un” (2Cor. 7:2) naci hi. “Note lungsim sungah” acihpen Greek leh Zopau sungah a omloh hang, mikangpau sungah athu paizia tawh kituak in a nazat ahihi.

Corinth mite in zong, Paul leh a pawlte kiangah a lungsim uh hon uhhi. Tua thu hangin tang 4 na ah lungdamna thu puakkham sak hi. Paul’ kammalte in tua laitak in amah lungdam takpi ahihna pulaak hi: “Note hong muang mahmah in note hangin kaki sathei a, lungkhamna a tuamtuam katuah hangin lung himawh lo in ka lungnuam mah mah hi” (2Cor. 7: 4) cihi. Paul in hehnepna leh lungnopna tawh kidim hi. I pawlpite in Christ tungah thumaan takin hong ki-aap ciangin Pasian’ nasemte zong lungnop hehnep na ngahtheita uhhi.

2Cor. 7: 5–16 sungah, Paul in ama’ hehnepna leh lungnopna thu gen hi. Hihthu te gel in tua sungteng ah dim hi. Greekpau _parakaleō_ pen “hehnem” hi a, _paraklēsis_ pen “hehnepna” hileuleu a; 2Cor. 7 sungah sagihvei tak omkha hi. Hih laikhak in a kamhon na mahtawh kamkhup kik a; Pasian’ hehnepna lianpi hong pia ding (2Cor. 1: 3–7) cihi. 2 Cor. 7 sungah Paul’ hehnepna pen “dahsakna lai” hangin ama’ ngiimna tangtung ahih manin, hehnep lungnopna ngahthei ahi hi.

Hih lungnopna pen, Titus hong thupuak hang hiphadiak hi cihtei hang; Paul’ lung nopna piangsak taktakpa pen Pasian ahi hi (2Cor. 7: 6). Pasian pen “hehpihna khempeuh a nei, i kisapna khempeuh ah ahong huh Pasian” (2Cor. 1: 3, 4) ahi hi.

Paul pen “hehnepna tawh kidim” ahih manin “lungnuam mahmah” (2Cor. 7: 4, 7, 13). Ama’ dahsakna laikhak in dahna tampi piangsak a, tua dahna in zong Pasian’ deihna bangin kisik kikna ahi hi (2Cor. 7: 9–11). Corinth mite pen “Pasian’ deihna tawh kituak in dahna” (2Cor. 7:11) hi a, “hotkhiatna ngahtheihna ding lungsim kikheelna” (2Cor. 7: 10) ahi hi. Pasian’ nasem maan taktak te tungah bangteng peuhin lungnopna hong piak sakthei lai ding hiam?

`Nangma nuntak sungin Pasian deihna bangin dahna naneingei kha hiam? Tua bangin Pasian’ deihna bangin dahna in kisikkikna hong tun cih na koici theih thei hiam?`
