---
title:  Thuaksiatna leh Minthanna
date:   31/08/2026
---

`2Cor. 4: 7–18 simin. Paul’ thuaksiatna teng gualsuk in. Koibang in kantanzo thei hiam?`

John Huss, Bohemia gammi, biakna puahphapa in Zeisu’ thu a pulaakna “Amah in leitung Makaipipa hi a, eite ihihleh a kihhuai sithei mi hi napi hang a – eite a dingin hong thuaksak veve hi. Tua ahihleh eite’n zong banghang in thuaklo tuan ding ihi hiam; thuakna in hong siangthosak thei hi”—Ellen G. White, The Great Controversy, p. 105.

Sawltak Paul in zong, zalom tampi laipek panin Christ a dingin thuaknuam cih pulaak khinzo hi. Amah zong pen a kitambaih leibeel ahihlel lam kithei hi (2Cor. 4: 7). “Gimna ka tuahzel uhhang ka sikei uh a, lung hihmawhna ka tuah uh hangin ka lungkia kei uh a, gal tampi kaneih uh hangun pawl ka kitasam kei uh a, liamna baina katuah uh hangun kasikei uhhi. Kote’ pumpi tungpanin Zeisu’ nuntakna akilatna dingin hih ka lei-tung pumpi mahmah zong, Zeisu’ sihna hangmah in kathuak zelzel uhhi” (2Cor. 4: 8-11).

“Zeisu’ sihna” hangin acihpen, a tunga ama’ thuaksiatna teng a cihnopna hihtuak pen hi. Tuakhit deuhmah ciangin “Zeisu’ nuntakna” cileuleu a, sihna pan suahtakna leh tu mahmahin a nuntakna sunga Kha Siangtho vangliatna a cihnopna hihtuak hi. Hiteng khempeuh pen thawhkikna thua gennopna bulpi ahi hi (2Cor. 4: 12).

“Sihna leh Nuntakna” cih tegel pen 2Cor. 4: 10-12 sungbek ah zong thumvei om hi. Tuazong pen hih leinunna pen sihna tawh kihelzau lai cih hong phawksakna ahi hi. A hizongin tawntung minthan hun ciangin sihna omnawnlo nuntakna neita ding hihang (Mang. 20: 14; Mang. 21: 4).

A thupi penkhat, 2Cor. 4: 7-18 in, lungdamthu pen a kitambaih mihing in tangko ahih manin minthanna peuhmah Pasian a ding bek cih honglak hi (2Cor. 4: 15). Pasian’ na semte in thuaksiatna pen khatveivei bekmah thuakkha uhhi. Ahihhang hiteng ah eite’ tuahkhak thute pen; eite hong ngak gige tawntung minthanna tawh tehkakna in; tawlkhat sung neucik bek hilel hi (2Cor. 4: 17). Thu ummi in upna tawh nungta hi; muhna tawh hilo hi (2Cor. 4: 18; 2Cor. 5: 7).

Paul in, hong tungding nuntakna lam-etna naneilua liang ahih manin, apauna mun khempeuh ah tuathu vive hong luang daudau hi (2Cor. 5: 1–10). A sithei ama’ lei-pumpi zongpen lei-inn tawh genteh ziaulel hi. A lehlam ah “Pasian in a lamsak inn” a cihzong pen a thokik pumpi a cihnopna hi a (2Cor. 5: 1), khang tawntunga thu ummite lam-etna pi ahi hi.

`Eite in bangsung peuh kantan himah taleng; hong tungding thawhkikna, eimah thokik ding cih lam-etna neihding banghang in thupi hiam (1Cor. 15: 52)?`
