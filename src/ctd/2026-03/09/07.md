EGWhite, “The Message Heeded,” pp. 323–334, in The Acts of the Apostles.

“Lungkhamna lianpen a thuakkha mite in, midangte hehnepna hong tunthei pente hi uh a; amau tunna peuhah nitang khuavak tangsak thei uhhi. Tua mite pen, haksatna in ahoih anem dingin a puahphat sate ahi uhhi. Gimna a thuakuh ciangun Pasian a munna uh mansuah hetlo in, Pasian hong huutna itna lenkip semzaw uhhi. Tua mite in Pasian kepna hoihna tecipangte ahi uhhi”—Ellen G. White, God’s Amazing Grace, p. 122.

“A siangtho Christian nuntakna in khuavak, hehnepna leh daihna hawmsawn den hi. Tuapen siangthona, ciimna, thumaanna leh zattelna tetawh ki pulaak hi. Tua huzaap pen siangthosakna, angkhuallo itna in kemcing gige hi. Amah a neipa paina peuhah khuavak tangsak a, Christ tawh kidim hi”—God’s Amazing Grace, p. 122.

“Sawltak Paul in, pawlpi sungah mawhnate puahphat kul ci ahihhang, mawh neipa a puahphatna ah amah zong kikemcing mahmah hi. Tuabang in a puahphatna hang thu patau takin hilhcianpha hi. Tua mawhneipa lawmpa ahihna, mi’ muhtheihna dingin gamta hi. Amaute’n na asak mahbang un amah in zong nasaveve cih telcian sak hi. Amaute a don mahmahna zong pen, ama’ lungsimsuak ahihna zong theitelcian sak hi”—Ellen G. White Comments, in The SDA Bible Commentary, vol. 6, p. 1094.

**Kikupding Dotnate:**

`2Cor. 2: 1–14 sungah, Paul in ama’ nasepna sungah ama’ vang kipkho sak hi. Tuabang in Pasian’na sepna banghang in phamawh hiam?`

`Paul in ama’ khualzin na’ng geelna teng a kheelziauna tungtawn in; Christian’ nasepna ah kikheeltheihna/kipuahtheihna kisam nasa hiam? Kul-kisam bangin suahtatak in kikheel theih in banghang in thupi thei ding hiam?`

`Paul in ama’ nasepna sungah gimna launa tampi tuak hi. Hihthu in pawlpi makaite zong mihing mah hi a, midangte mahbangin dahthei uh cih hong lak hi. Amau’ nasep olzaw deuh thei dingin pawlpi mite in koici huhthei ding hiam?`

`Paul in Christ sungah a gualzawhna a genma in (2Cor. 2: 14) in a lungsim omthei nawnlo cihi (2Cor. 2: 13). A thanemna leh thahatna koibangin genkhawm thei hi ding hiam?`
