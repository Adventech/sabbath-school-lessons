---
title:  Maisakna leh Itna Kipkhosakkikna
date:   26/08/2026
---

Corinth mite kiangah a nihveina a vahawh sangin; Paul in Ephesus a tun ciangin ‘dahsakna lai’ (2Cor. 2: 3, 4; 2Cor. 7: 8, 12) a kici pen khak hi.

`2Cor. 7: 5–13 simin. Amaute Paul’ laikhakna pan bangthu hong om a, tua thu Paul in koibang in dawngthuk leuleu hiam?`

Tuakhit ciangin Paul leh Titus tegel in Macedonia ah akimuh uh ciangun; Paul in khauhtakin alaikhak na hangin thuhoih hong piang cih Titus kiangpan in za a, Sawltakpa nuamlua hi. Acil in Corinth mi pawlkhat in Paul langpang mahmah napi in tu in pawlpi buppi in Paul tawh pangkhawmta uhhi. Pawlpi mite in sawltakte’ nasep a baihzaw in puahphat sakthei hi.

`2Cor. 2: 5–11 simin. Hitengah a deihna bulpi bang hiam?`

Hihmun tengpen pawlpi kikhalna thu ahi hi. Laisiangtho siamte in tua mawhneipa pen, 1Cor. 5: 1-5 sunga anu tawh mawhna bawlpa hihtuak asa omuh a; Sawltak Paul in ama’ khualzinna ah a khentatna ginalo, amaute zong donselo cihbang in Paul tungah mawhzonna a piangsak; pawlpi sungah zong huzaap lian aneipa khat hithei ding hi ci in kikum zel uhhi. A thupaizia tawh kizui in anihna hihtuak sawnsawn zaw hi. Thukhat peuhpeuh ah, pawlpi sungah a thupipen in mawhna khat a siansuah zia thupi mahmah ahihna hihthu in hong telsak hi.

Hihthu paizia in pawlpi sung kikhalna peuhmah, mawh maisakna leh amawh a khialpa tungah itna kipsakkikna thupi ahihna hong hilh hi (2Cor. 2:6–8, 10). Tua banah pawlpi sung kikhalna pen na mahmah ahihhang kulkisam cihzong hong theisak hi. Cih nopna bulpi in, lungsim hoih deihsakna neimah taseleng; pawlpi pawlkhat in, mawhna bawlte, a Langtang mawhna hiphialmah taleh; talsik in amawhna uh siansuahna bangmah neingei hetlo uhhi.

Alangkhat leuleu ah, a khauhmahmah, maisakna neilo, a kip bekbak mi zong na omveve lai hi. Mawhna siansuahna ah itna tawh sep kul hi. Tua hi a, Paul in pawlpi it ngiat ahih manin (2Cor. 2: 4) tua mawhneipa tungah itna kipsak kik dingin pawlpi han- thawn hi (2Cor. 2: 8).

`Paul in Pasian’ kiangpan itna sangkha in, Corinth pawlpi a itna tungtawnin, Corinth pawlpi in zong mawhneipa it theita uhhi (2Cor. 2: 8) Itna tungtang bang hong hilh naci hiam?`
