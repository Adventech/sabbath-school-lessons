`2Cor. 1: 3–7 simin. Hiteng ah Paul’ lungdamkohna a hangthu bang hiam?`

Paul’ lungdamna pen a thuaksia mite tungah Pasian’ hehnepna tungah bulphuhpi pen hi. Hihmun ah, hehnem (_parakaleō_) leh hehnepna (_paraklēsis_) pen sawmvei takmah om hi. 2Cor. sunga 29 vei bang a omna lakah hiteng ah sawmvei cihpen sehthum a she khat ahi hi. Pasian zongpen “eite’ kisapna khempeuh ah hehpihna nei Pa; leh haksatna khempeuh ah hong huhden Pasian” (2Cor. 1: 3, 4).

Pasian kiangpan hongpai hehnepna pen mimal khatbek in kepbikbek ding hilo hi (2Cor. 1: 4, 5). A lungggim lungtang in Pasian’ hehnepna zong sangpah a; amah bangin a lunggim midangte tungah zong hawmsan theipan hi.

Paul amah ngiat in thuaksia mahmah photin, Pasian kiangpan zong hehnepna ngah photphot ahih manin midangte zong hehnem theita hi. “Kote in gimna kathuak uh leh; note’ phattuamna ding leh hotkhiatna ngahna ding: Kote in huhna kangah uhleh zong note zong phattuamna ding leh hotkhiatna ngahna ding mah ahi hi” (2Cor. 1: 6). Hih pen itna ahi hi!

`2Cor. 1: 8–11 sungah Paul in bang lungdamna pulaak hiam?`

Paul in a thuaknate pen “tehpih bei, tha in cinlo” (2Cor. 1: 8) lua ahih manin, amah leh a lawmteng un beina tung dingin kingaihsun liangin lau uhhi. Tawlkhat sung bangpen thawhkikna in amau’ lam-ettheihlai khatbek hi ci in ngaihsun liang uhhi. Hitaleh Pasian in amau’ honkhia in thute hong kikheel hi (2Cor. 1: 10). Sihding launa pan (2Cor. 1: 8), Pasian in amaute hongkhia kik ding cih lam-etna hong neithei uhhi (2Cor. 1: 10).

A beisa hunsunga Pasian hong zawhpihnate in mailam ah zong tuamah bangkik ding cih lungmuanna hong pia hi. Pasian in Amah a muantheihna dingun haksatna tetawh thuhilh hi. Haksatna te in khalam khanghamna hong tunthei a, aneupen in Pasian tawh hong kinaisak thei hi. Paul ‘ lungdamkohna sungah, Pasian hong lailut siakna vanglian leh Pasian hong hotkhiatna tungah eima’ lungdamna cihte a kihel in om uhhi (2Cor. 1: 11).

`Lampi khat peuhpeuh eite khempeuh in i phutkhak ahi haksatnate vengsak na dingin bangteng kizangthei ding cih nangmuhna ahi hiam?`
