`2Cor. 2: 12, 13 simin. “Dahsakna lai” akhak khitteh Paul koiah pai a, bang vahih hiam?`

Paul in Titus hong tunkal ngaklah lua in a lungsim tawldam thei hetlo hi (2Cor. 7: 5, 6). Tua pipi mahin Zeisu’ thu zong genlo in omtheilo sawnsawn hi (2 Cor. 2: 12). Paul in Zeisu itlua mahmah hi. Tua laitak lianin ama’ laikhak bangciang tung cihbel thei nailo phot hi. Titus tawh kimu meng theileh, Corinth mite’ dawnkikna zong zanuam luata hi.

Troas khua ah Paul’ nasepna picing mahmah hitaleh “tualai ah sawt taam theilo hi. Pawlpite kepcin kula, a diak in Corinth pawlpi don ding Paul’ vangikpi khat ahi hi. Troas ah Titus tawh kimuh ding lam-etna; Corinth mite kiangah a laikhak pen amaute un koibang in sanguh cih zanuam mahmahta ahih manin alung bang kiaman phial hi. ‘Ka sanggampa Titus tawh kimulo kahih manin ka lungsim tawldam cih omngei lo’ cihi. Tua ahih manin Troas panin paikhia in Macedonia kantan in Philippi ah Timothy tawh kimu uhhi”—Ellen G. White, The Acts of the Apostles, p. 323.

`2Corinthians 2: 14–17 simin. Macedonia ah Titus tawh akimuhuh ciangun, Corinth mite in ahoihlam in saanguh cih a zak ciangin Paul in bangcih hiam?`

Nuamlua mahmah in Paul in Pasian in “gualzawhna tawh Christ a paina khem peuh ah eite hong zuisak” (2Cor. 2: 14) cihi. Christ a tenna lungtang sungah “mun khem-peuhah paknamtui a kizelh bangin Christ theihna kizelsak” (2Cor. 2: 14) cihi.

“Dahsakna laikhak” in Paul’ in a aatnop gah hong gahthei bilbel ahih manin Paul in Christ sungah lungdam hi (2Cor. 7: 5–9). Gualzawhna lianpi khat ahi hi. Tua laitakin 2 Cor. 2: 17 sungah amah in Christ Sawltakpa ahihna kipsak phapha hi (2Cor. 2: 17; 2Cor. 1: 12). Hihmun tungtawn in, a thumaan Christ’ nasempa leh, nasemzuau a kikhentel theih na pen: a thumaan sawltakte in Pasian thu ahilhna vuah Zeisu itna tawh hilh uh a: Sawl- takzuaute ahih uhleh, amau’ angsung khantohna dingin lungdamthu pen inn-lusik pai in gen kawikawi uhhi.

`Bangpeuh ihih zongin, a diakin Zeisu’ mintawh ihih ciangin, bangpen in na lungsim hong khoihkha pen nasa hiam?`
