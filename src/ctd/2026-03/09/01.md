### Tukalsung Simding
2Cor. 1:3–14; 2Cor. 2:17; 2Cor. 4:2; 1Cor. 16:5–7; 2Cor. 7:5–13; 2Cor. 2:5–17.

> <p>Kamngah</p>
> “Tualai ka gelh laitakin, note’ dahna ding piangsak khalo in, note ka 	hong it mahmahna naphawkna dinguh ka deihna tawh kagelh hi a; 	lungkham sinkhamna leh khitui naptui kai in agelh kahi hi” (2Cor. 2: 4).

Sawltak Paul nuntakhun sungah lungnuam hithiat ngeilo hi. A thongkiat kihello in, ama’ nuntakna nangawn alauhthawn zoteng amah’n gualsuk hi: “Jew mite khutsung ah, sawmthum leh kuavei satna ngavei tak kathuak hi. Rome mite’n thumvei hong sat a, khatvei suangtawh hong deng uhhi. Tembaw siatna thumvei katuak a; sunkhat leh zan khat tawntung tuitungah kalam ngei hi.

Khual ka zinzinna lamin tuikhang lauhuaina, misuamte lauhuaina, ka mipihte suk ding lauhuaina, Gentile mite’ suk ding lauhuaina, khuapi sung leh gamlak ah lauhuaina, tuipi tungah lauhuaina, lam maanlote’ hih ding lauhuai na: Nasem silbawl in ka om tawn tumg a, ihmu manlo liangin ka omzel hi. Gilkial dang-tak in ka omzel a, annek ding neilo, giakna ding omlo, punsilh ding neilo in ka om hun tampi a om hi. Tuabang haksat na tuamtuamte kathuak banah, pawlpi a tuamtuamte a dingin lunghimawh in ka lunggim lai hi”(2Cor. 11: 24–28).

Corinth mite tungah a laikhakna sungah pawlpi a dingin “lung hihmawhna” nei cih i mu uhhi. Christ in eite hong itna a kiamngeiloh mahbang in, tua khempeuh kawmkal mahah amaute a itna in kiam ngeilo hi. Zeisu in eite hong itna lengkik sak in Paul in zong tua itna mahtawh a pawlpite it thei hi (2Cor. 5: 14; 1 Cor. 11: 1).
