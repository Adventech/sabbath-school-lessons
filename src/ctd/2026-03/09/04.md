---
title:  Itna a dingin Ngiimna Kheel
date:   25/08/2026
---

Corinth mite pawlkhat in Paul’ ngiimna leh itna um zolo uhhi. Tuni in Paul’ in ama’ khualzinna a kheel liangna hangthu enpha ding hihang (2Cor. 1: 15–2:4).

`1Cor. 16: 5–7 simin. Paul in khualzin na’ng mun a gelmasakna bang hiam?`

Paul in Corinth ah omkhin zo hihtuak hi. 1Cor. 16: 5, 6 sunga geelna bang hileh Macedonia kantan phot ding a, Corinth hong ciahkik ciangin, phalbi sungteng Corinth ah taam sawm hi. Corinth panin, Judea ah pai ding a, Mecedonia pan a kaihkhop Jerusalem a mizawngte a ding sumpite puak sawm hi. Ahita zongin, Corinth pan hong pai Timothy in hong thupuak thusia hangin ama’ ngiimna kheelngiat hi (1Cor. 4: 17; 16: 10; 2Cor. 1: 1).

Paul in, Ephesus panin Corinth ah paisuak vingveng dinga, Timothy hong thu puakpen siansuah sawmpah hi. A khualzin nading ama’ geelnathak ahileh Ephesus – Corinth – Mecedonia – Corinth – Judea cihbang ahi hi (2Cor. 1: 15, 16). Ephesus panin Corinth ah paitheipah hinapi, Ephesus mahah hong kilehkik hiau hi. A geelna kheelkik mai hi. Apai nunungpen lai in tuahpha khollo ahih manin,, a geelsa ombang in Corinth ah paikik nawnlota hi. Ephesus mahah ciahkik in, laikhak tamai zaw hi. Khatvei a vahawh kik a, thu suuksiat tuakzaw ahih manin laikhakzaw hi (2Cor. 2: 1, 3).

Paul’ ahawh nunung penlai in kitelkhial gawp hi. Corinth mi pawlkhat in Paul pen muanhuai keilua, hong it hongngai keilua ci uhhi (2Cor. 1: 17). Tua mawhsakna adawnna in, Corinth mite’ mit lungdamthu lamah heisak hi. Pasian in Ama’ kamciamte pen Christ sungah hong picinsak mahbang in; Paul in zong hunlem penpen khat ciangin Corinth ah a hawh ding ngiimna pen kiptakin lengige lai hi (2Cor. 1: 18–22).

“Pasian’ thuciam khempeuh ahihpa pen Amah ahi hi. Tua ahih manin Zeisu Christ hangin Pasian phatin ‘Amen’ i cihna zong Ama’ hangmah ahi hi” (2Cor. 1: 20).

Paul in a dawnthukna pen, Corinth mite’n ahihtheih zel bangin a buaihuai mah mah leh a nunzui in ‘himah/hilo’ cihte hilo hi. Christ sunga Pasian’ nasepna tepen a tawn tungin ‘himah’ ahih mahbang in Paul dawnkikna zong ‘himah den’ hi (2Cor. 1: 19).

Tua hi a, Paul in Corinth mite kiang a vahawh sangin laikhat a khakzawkna pen, amaute’ it taktak ahihna hang hizaw hi (2Cor. 2: 4). Dahna apiangsak hawhna khatkhit ciangin khatvei vahawhkik vatleh; Paul’ ngiimna lungnopna ngah zawlo in, dahna dang khat peuh piangsak zawsop ding hi (2Cor. 1: 24; 2Cor. 2: 3). Paul’ ngiimna hoih mah mah pen kitelkhial gawp zawsop hi.
