---
title:  Lungsim Takpi leh Kitheihna Lungsim
date:   24/08/2026
---

Zanni in, Paul in, Corinth mite a itna thu kisinna ah, Paul in Pasian kiang panin hehnepna a ngah mahbang in; Corinth mite haksatna ah Paul in zong hehnem hi (1Cor. 1: 1-11). Tuni in ahihleh, Corinth mi thu ummite tungah, Paul leh a naseppihte in a lahuh thumaanna hangin amau a itna teng zong muhbeh lai ding hihang.

`2Cor. 1: 12–14; 2Cor. 2: 17 leh 2Cor. 4: 2 simin. Paul’ lungsim takpi in Corinth mite a itna koibang in pulaak hiam?`

Corinth Nihna 1: 12–14 in, Paul’ laikhakna sunga a nunglam a omlai thuteng hong theikhol sak hi. Paul thumaanna leh nungzui ahihna pen Corinth mite in dong theizel uh hi. Paul in malmel neilo in khentatna nei mello gamtatna hangin Sawltakna semding in kilawmlo hi ci uhhi. Paul in a dawnna ah, amah leh a naseppihte in amau tungah hoih theihtawp in kong hoih uhhi cihi.

Paul leh a pawlte’ gamtatzia pen kammal nih tawh pulaak hi: Lungsim takpi leh kitheihna lungsim (2Cor. 1:12) ahi hi. “Kitheihna lungsim” cihpen Greekpau in _haplotēs_ hi. Tuapen hiteng ah mimal vangkepna, kampau gamtat kikepna hi a: a tomin, tatlehhek siangthona lahna ahi hi (Eph. 6: 5; Col. 3: 22). Thukhat panin zong hih “kitheihna lungsim” (Greekpau _eilikrineia_) hi a, vangkepna tatlehhek siangthona mah hiveve hi.

Paul in ama’ ngiimna teng a pulaak sukna bek hi a Corinth mite in telkhialh huailo hi. Paul in ama’ lungsim takpi leh kitheihna lungsim pen Pasian’ bulphuh ing cihi. Hih Paul’ ngaihsutna pen New English Translation Bible in hoihtak in pom a; “gamtatna siangtho leh Pasian kiangpan hong pai kitheihna lungsim” hi (2Cor. 1: 12). Tua tangneu sungmah ah, Paul mahin, Pasian’na septheihna siamna peuhmah peneima tungah hong paik hi cihi.

Paul’ a langpang te (2Cor. 1: 13, 14) akihona masate kammalte letkhial gawp hih tuak hi. Paul in ama’ ngiimnate pen kician in telnop ci in vaipuak langiat hi. Ama’ ngiim nate in thumaan kammalte hi a, ama’ ngiimna leh gamtatna te pen “Topa Zeisu’ ni” ciang in kitelkheh ta ding hi cihi (2Cor. 1: 14).

`Nangma’ deihna leh ngiimnate in bangzah in hoih a, bangzah in na lungsim takpi himah taleh; telkhialh na leh uplohna tawh kipelhthei ding hiam? Tua thute pan nangma muhna bangin midangte ngiimna khawngpeuh nangmah tekin na sittel dingpen bangzah kidop kul dingin na um hiam?`
