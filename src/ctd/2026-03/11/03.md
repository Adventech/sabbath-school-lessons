title:  Hanthotna
date:   07/09/2026
---

`2Cor. 8: 1, 5 leh 2Cor. 9: 7, 9, 13, 15 simin. A thupuak laigil bang hiam?`

2 Corinth 8 leh 9 sungah piakkhiatna dim ziazua hi: “Pasian’ hehpihna ngah” (2 Cor. 8: 1); “Amau kipia masa” (2Cor. 8: 5); “Na lungsim sungah na ngaihsut zahciat uh pia un. Pasian in piaknopna lungsim tawh apiate bek deih” (2Cor. 9: 7); “Amah in citna tawh mizawngte piakhia” (2Cor. 9: 9); “Nomau citpiakna hangin ,,, Pasian’ min kithang sak hi” (2Cor. 9: 13); “a manphatna genzawhloh Pasian hong piak hih thupha hangin Pasian tungah i lungdam hi” (2Cor. 9: 15). Corinth Nihna 8 leh 9 tegel in piakkhiatna kammal tawh thuhong in thu-khup kik hi (2Cor. 8: 1 2Cor. 9: 15). 2 Cor. 8 leh 9 peuh mah piakkhiatna lungsim bek tawh simding hihang. Amaugel in sumpi piakkhiat na’ng hangthu thupi li hong hilh hi:

**1. Pasian’ hehpihna hangin Lungdam** (2Cor. 8: 1; 2Cor. 9: 14, 15). 2Cor. 8 leh 9 tegel in “Pasian’ hehpihna” cihtawh kipan limlim hi (2Cor. 8: 1). Tua banah Paul in “I Topa Zeisu’ hong hehpihna bangzah lian cih nathei uhhi” (2Cor. 8: 9) cihi. Pasian leh Christ’ hong hehpihna pen; piakkhiat theihna dingin thukhun tawphah pi in hong pulak hi. Pasian in zong Christ hong piakna sungah tampi hong piakhia khin ahi hi. Tua hi a, eite’n zong sumpi piakna tawh eima’ nunna sungah Pasian’ hehpihna a kipsak ihi uhhi.

Piakkhiatna cih kammal pen “hehpihna” (Greek, _charis_) tawh kithuah in 2Cor. 8 leh 9 sungah omsuksuk hi. A kipatna ah _charis_ a om mahbang in a tawpna ah zong om hi (2Cor. 8: 1; 2Cor. 9: 14, 15). Paul in eima’ nuntakna sungah Christ’ hong hehpihna in midangte tungah thupha bawlna leh hehpihna phatna ci in khiatna namnih in kikhen thei hi.

**2. Zeisu’ Limlahna Zuinuam** (2Cor. 8: 9). Zeisu hau gige napi mizawng hong suak (Amah om tawntung ahihna mihing hong suahna limlahna). Tuabang ahihtheihna ding lampi khatbek om a: a vekpi in piakhia hi. Eite’n zong sumpite piakkhiatna pen midangte in Christ theihsakna.

**3. Pasian’ Thupha Hawmsawn nuam** (2Cor. 9: 10, 11). Pasian kiangpan in ngah masa ihih manun, midangte a piakhia ihi uhhi.

**4. Itna Maan taktak** (2Cor. 8: 8, 24). Piakkhiatna pen itna maantaktak sittelna ahi hi. Mi khatpeuh lungsim sungah itna a omlam theihna zong ahi hi. Mikang paunak khat ah “Na gen bangin semin” cihi.

`Nang bangzah na cingh hiam? Singlamteh khuavak sungah, nangma piak zawhzah sangin bangzah napia zaw hiam?`
