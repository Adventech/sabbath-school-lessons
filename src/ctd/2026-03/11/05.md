---
title:  Lungsimpuak
date:   09/09/2026
---

`2Cor. 8: 1–5 simin. Macedonia mite hizah a citna tawh a piaknop theihna uh a hangthu bang hihtuak pen hiam?`

Macedonia mite’ lungsimpuak hoihna a nuaia bangin kimuthei hi.

**1. Lungdamna lianpi tawh piakhia** (2Cor. 8: 2). Paul in Macedonia mite pen “zawng mahmah uh hitaseleh, lungdam mahmah ahih manun piakna lamah cingh mahmah” (2 Cor. 8: 2) cihi. Anunglam ah Paul mahin “piaknopna lungsim tawh apia mite bek Pasian in deih” (2Cor. 9: 7) cihi.

Greekpau in “Piaknopna lungsim” acihpen, laisiangthothak sungbup ah hiteng bekah om hi. A kammal innkuanpih dangbel omkawikawi mah hi: “Mi dang hoptheihna ding anei ihihleh citna taktak tawh hawm ni” (Rom. 12: 8). Hih kammal huam dangte in laisiangtho tawh kisai laituamtuam te ah nuamna leh lungdamna tawh ci in omthei zel hi. 2 Cor. 9: 7 sungah piaknopna lungsim tawh cihpen tonlo in piakkhiatna ahi hi.

**2. Cinghtak in piakhia** (2Cor. 8: 2). Macedonia mite’ citpiakna a genkhiat ma in Paul in, amaute “zawngpha mahmah” cimasa hi. Citna cihpen (Greek, _haplotētos_) hi a, 2Cor. 8 leh 9 sungah nihvei hong omkik leuleu hi. Paul in “note in na citgamtat zawh tawntung theihna dingun Pasian in hong hausak tawntung ding hi” (2Cor. 9: 11) acihteh, a deihna in, i piakkhiat zawh tawntung na dingin hong kipia ding cihi. A thuthuk zaw deuhin Paul in, “cinghtakin na piakkhiatna uh” (2Cor. 9: 13) cihi. Hihteng ah piakkhiatna ah citna pen, Christ’ lungdamthu tangkona namkhat mah ahi hi.

**3. “Amau lungsim uttawmna uhtawh pia”** (2Cor. 8: 3). Lungsim suakta takin pia uh cihna ahi hi. Aval a liap nei tuanlo napi uh in; amau’ neihsa in ciangtan om mahmah kawmkal panmah in piakhia veve uhhi. Corinth khua ah Titus a vahawhnopna thu Paul in a gen ciangin, tua ngaihdan mahpen zangin gen hi. Corinth ah hawhnopna lungsim neitawm cihi (2Cor. 8: 17).

**4. Piakhiatna pen hamphatna in ngaihsun** (2Cor. 8: 4). Huhna sum kaikhawm ding cih ciangin Macedonia mite’ ngetna sungah hibang lungsim omkhinkhian hi. “Ko zong hong huhsak un ci in hong thumthum uhhi” (2Cor. 8: 4) cihi.

**5. Amaute Pasian kiangah ki-aap masa in Topa’ deihna bangin kote hong piakhia** (2Cor. 8: 5). Paul in, “Amaute Topa tungah kipia masa uh a tuakhit ciangin Pasian’ deihna bangin kote hong pia uh” (2Cor. 8: 5) cihi. Eimah Topa’ tungah kipiakna in eimah mah midang a ding kipiakna piangsak hi. Macedonia mite in amau mission pen sum lam khengval in sem uhhi. Cihnopna in, piakkhiatna leh citna cihpen sum bek tawh kiciangtan lo hi.
