Ellen G. White, “A Liberal Church,” pp. 335–345, in The Acts of the Apostles.

“Christ hong itna tawh akidim lungsimte bekin, eite hangin zawnna thuak a, Ama’ zawnna hangin, eite hong hausakpa’ nung zuizo ding hi. Sum, hun leh huzaap peuhmah Pasian kiangpan in kingah ahih manin; tuate pen aumau un lungdamthu mainawtna ding in manpha sak thei uhhi. Pawlpi masa sungah zong tua mahbang hikhin a; tuhun pawlpi sungah zong, Kha Siangtho vangliatna tawh pawlpi mite in amau’ lungsim sungpan leitung nate hemkhia uh in, amaute’ lawmte un zong tua lungdamthu a zak ciangin amau zong ki-aap nuam cih amuhuh ciangin; amau’ tangko uh thumaan in tua mite tungah muibun mahmah cih thei ding uhhi”—Ellen G. White, The Acts of the Apostles, p. 71.

“Topa in eite’ sumpi a kisam hihetlo hi. Eite’ piakna leltawh Amah kihausak zolo ding hi. Laphuakpa in ‘Na khempeuh Nang kiangpan hong pai a, Nangma neihsa hong piak ahi hi’ (Late 16: 2) cihi. Pasian in Ama’ hong hehpihna hangin lungdamna tawh midangte tungah hawmsawn piakkhiat ding deih leuleu hi. Pasian tungah eima’ itna leh lungdam kohna te lahkhiat theihna’ng lampi in, hibekmah om hi. Lampi dang Amah’n hong bawlsaklo hi”—Ellen G. White, Counsels on Stewardship, pp. 18, 19.

“Pasian hong silpiak lian ve e; tuabang Pasian lamdang ve e. Kuama’ dem zawh loh phalna tawh hongpia a, tua tawh langdo a tate Ama’ hong itna bulpi telcian a hot khiat theihna ding ahi hi. ‘Tapa khat aneihsun piakhia in Amah a ummite peuhmah kisia lo in tawntung nunna a neihtheihna ding: tuabang hong hihsakpa a dingin, nang piak theih letsong teleh sumpite khempeuh sangin lianzaw tham cih nalahkhia ding hiam?”—Ellen G. White, in The Advent Review and Sabbath Herald, May 15, 1900.

**Kikupding Dotnate:**

`2Corinth 8: 9 lungngai kikin. Stewardship thu i gennakleh, banghang in Zeisu hong limlahna etteh kulngiat hiam?`

`John 3: 16 in vantung’ kampau pen piakkhiatna cih koibang in hong lak hiam? John 15: 13; Ephet 5: 2, 25; Galati 2: 19, 20; leh 1 John 3: 16 simin. Hihmun teng leh John 3: 16 in kibatpih bang nei a, bang thupuak kila thei ding hiam?`

`2Corinth 8–9 na simna bulphuh inla, piakkhiatna in bang phat tuamna hong ngahsak thei hiam?`

`Sumpi piakzia tawh kisai in, Zeisu’ hong limlahna bang tengpeuh zuihhuai nasa hiam?`
