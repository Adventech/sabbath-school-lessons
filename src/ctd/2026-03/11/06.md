---
title:  Kipumkhatna
date:   10/09/2026
---

Corinth pawlpi mite in Judea gam a mizawngte a ding sum kidonna ah kihel ding in Paul in hanthawn cih mukhin hihang. Ama’ ngiimna khat pen kipumkhatna tawhkuang nuam hi. Mission nasepna sungah amaute kihelsak nuam hi. Jerusalem a thu ummite mah bang in biakna pualampan thu hong um mite zong Pasian innkuan sung hong lutta cih zong theisak nuam hi.

Hihmite in khatveilai in amaute’ langpangte hikhin napi in tu in, Pasian’ thuciam thak sungah a beibaangte ahi uhhi. Christian innkuan buppi Jew mi leh Gentile mite in vanglian takin kipumkhat in, khangthak pawlpi a dingin tecipangte ahih theihna dingun Paul in deihsak hi.

Titus leh a sanggam ahi adang nih tegel in funds sumte kem uhhi. Tuabang in pawlpi kepcin nopna lungsim pen Pasian in Titus’ lungsim sungah koihsak hi (2Cor. 8: 16). Pawlpi sungpan in zong Pasian in tua unau tegel teelkhia hi (2Cor. 8: 18–23). Amau tepen “Christ’ min a thangsak, pawlpite’ tangin thupuakte” (2Cor. 8: 23) kici uhhi. “Christ’ min a thangsak” cihpen hih a citak unau tegel zong hithei a pawlpite mahmah zong hithei uhhi. Sumpi piakkhiatna pen pawlpi’ lutang ahi Christ tungah cihtakna lahna ahi hi (Eph. 4: 15).

Corinth Nihna 8 leh 9 in, sumpi cihlimlim, pawlpi tungtawn in, Pasian ciangtansa mite tungah piakding cih honglak hi. “Pawlpi khempeuh” (2Cor. 8: 18), “pawlpite in teel in a sehpa” (2Cor. 8: 19), “pawlpite’ thupuakpa” (2Cor. 8: 23) ci in kiciantak in hong hilhcian khin hi. Tua ahih manin a nuaiah hanthotna “pawlpite maiah na itna uh limtak in lak un” (2Cor. 8: 24) cihi.

Leitung ah Pasian in hong koihsak pawlpi tungah sumpite paipihna in – kipumkhat na piangsak a, kipumkhatna’ gahmah zong hizel hi (2Cor. 8: 13, 14). Sumleh pai in hong pumkhatsak thei nalian khat ahi hi. A lehlam in mi khempeuh in Pasian’ min than na’ng bek a mitusan keivua leh, sumleh pai in hong khenkham thei leuleu hi.

`Paul in pumkhat ding a deihna Rom. 15: 26, 27 in koibang in pulaak hiam?`

A nunung penin, Paul in, fund kaihkhopna cih peuhmah, donna, hehpihna, huhna, thupha saanna, biakpiakna leh kikhawlhna ci in pulaak hi. Tuate khempeuh sum pan hong pai hiam, lungngai in.

`A gamla mahmahna a om i lawm thu ummite tungah, eima piakkhiatna in leitung bup huam pawlpi pumkhat theihna ding bang semthei ding hiam?`
