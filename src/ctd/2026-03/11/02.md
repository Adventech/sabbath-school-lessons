---
title:  Zeisu’ Lim Honglahna
date:   06/09/2026
---

2 Corinth 8 leh 9 sung thuteng pen, Judea gama om mizawng daipam teng huhna dingin sum kidong ding hanthotna a hihi. Tuateng khempeuh nahih khinzo uh (2 Cor. 8: 10, 11; 2Cor. 9: 5; 1Cor. 16: 1–4) hi napi, amau leh Paul kikal ah kizopna hoihlo sese hi. Tua buaina teng a siansuah khit uh ciangun (2 Cor. 1–7), Paul in tua nasep teng thu khupna bawlta hi (2Cor. 8–9).

Amasa penin, Paul in, Macedonia mite hihdan zui dingin hanthawn a (2Cor. 8: 1–7), amaute zong zawngkhialua mahmah hinapi “piakkhiatna lamah cingh thei mahmah uh” (2Cor. 8: 2) cihi. Zawnna leh citna zong tonkhawm thei veve hi. Bangpeuh hitaleh, Macedonia mite’ citna pen Zeisu in Ama’ pumpi hong kipiakkhiat a citna mah limciing ahi uhhi (2Cor. 8: 8–15).

`2Cor. 8: 9 simin. Himun ah Zeisu’ lim koici gen hiam?`

2 Corinth 8: 9 sunga Paul’ thu gente pen, Laisiangtho buppi sungah a lamdangpen a vangneipen leh a thuthukpen ahi hi. Paul in Zeisu’ nasepna tepen sumbawl kammal tawh pulaksak hi. Biakna vai tawhzong kinai mahmah hi. Laisiangtho tangkhat sung bek ah hotkhiatna tangthu om cihna ahi hi.

A lungluthuai semsemna pen, tua tangthu pen sumbawl kam tawh kisuut suk hi. Zeisu hau mah hi. Ama’ hauhna in Amah vantung ah ma-omkhin (John 17: 5) cihna ahi hi. Hih leitung dahna sungah zawngkhal takin hong tuaksuk in vantung minthanna teng nutsiat ding khentat hi. Hong zawngkhal takpi hi (Luke 9: 58). Pasian tawh kikim uh hi napi, “Amah hong kiniamsak tawmin nasem’lim hong puazaw a, mihing bangin Amah hong kibawltawm hi” (Phil. 2: 7). “Mihing’ lim pua in, kiniamkhiat tawm in singlamteh tungah sih ding dongin thu-mang hi” (Phil. 2: 8).

Eite in Zeisu tawh i nuntak tawntung nading in Amah’n a nuntakna piakhia hi. A hong kipiakkhiatna in ngiimna khat nei a: eimah hotkhiat na’ng ahi hi. Stewardship leh mission tonkhawm hiuhiau hi. Corinth Nihna 8 leh 9 tegel in, sumtang mahmah piakkhiat na tangthu gen a, tua tangthu in Zeisu tungah kibulphut hi. Tukalsung in, Christ in Amah mah hong kipiakkhiatna tungah bulphut in biakna thukhun pawlkhat mukhia ding hihang.

`Zeisu’ suahna, nundan, sihna leh a thawhkikna lungngai phot in. Hihteng khempeuh nang a dingin ahih hi a, hi leitung khengval pek munkhat ah lam-etna khat hong neisak cih naphawk ciangin; na koici dawnthuk hiam?`
