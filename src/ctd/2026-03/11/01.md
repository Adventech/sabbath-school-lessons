### Tukalsung Simding
2Cor. 8–9; John 3:16; John 17:5; Luka 9:58; .Mang. 13:8; Rom. 12:8; Rom. 15:26, 27.

> <p>Kamngah</p>
> “I Topa Zeisu zong hau mahmah napi in, Ama’ zawnna tawh note 	mihau na suahtheihna dingun, note hangin mizawng in hong kibawl ahih manin; Topa Zeisu Christ hong itna bangzahta in lian hiam cih thu nathei uhhi” (2 Cor. 8: 9).

Corinth Nihna 8 leh 9 sungah Corinth mite in Judea gam a om sanggam unaute huhna piak ding Paul in vaihawmsak cihthu pulak hi. Hihmun in piakkhiatna pen Pasian in hongpiak hunhoih hi a, Christ’ in Amah hong kipiakkhiatna lim ciintheih hunlem ahi hi. Vantung’ kammal khatpen piakhia cih ahihi. A kammalte ngaihsun dihve: “Pasian in A Tapa neihsun a piak dongin leitung a it hi” (John 3: 16).

Tua banah, John 3: 16 sungah Pasian in Zeisu a piakkhiatna deihna pulak a “Amah a umte khempeuh kisialo in a nuntak tawntung na ding” cihi. Hihteng ah kepcinna leh mission (Stewardship leh mission) tonkhawm hiuhiau hi. Amau tegel pen a kikhen theilo dangka tang’ maitang nih tawh kibang hi. Paul in amah leh a naseppihte pen “Pasian’ thusim a zeekte” (1Cor. 4: 1) kici uhhi. Tua ahih manin eite zong a zeekte ihi uhhi.

Tukal sungin stewardship (a zeekte) leh mission hi uh a, Zeisu’ limlahna sungah thuklut mahmah khin uhhi. Stewardship leh mission pen kikhen theilo hi. Stewardship in pawlpi pen sum tha leh mihing tha tawh panpih in Pasian’ mission picingsak hi.
