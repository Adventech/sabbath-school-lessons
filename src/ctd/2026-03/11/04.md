---
title:  Ngiimna
date:   08/09/2026
---

`2Cor. 9: 7. Hihmun in sumpi piakna koici gen hiam?`

Pasian in leitung a hotkhiat ding khentatna pen leitung in mawhna sung a tunma pekpan ahi hi. Christ in eite a ding si dingin hongpai ding zong tangtawng ngiimna ahi hi (Mang. 13: 8). Pasian kilatvat khollo hi. Zeisu Christ tungtawn in Amah hong kipiak sawm ngiat hi. 2 Corinth 8 leh 9 sungah, piakkhiatna ah ngiimna pen a omloh phamawh a kisam biaknalam thukhun khat hi cihi. Tua zong lampi nih tawh kientel thei hi.

**1. Ngiim cihpen Ma-khentatna omkhin.** Paul in “kuama peuhmah in na lungsim sung vuah na ngaihsutsa zah pia un” (2Cor. 9: 7). Khentat cihpen Greekpau in _proaireō_ ahi hi. Kammal kigawm hi a _pro_ in ‘a masiah, hihkhol’ leh _aireō_ pen khentat ahi hi. Tua ahih manin _proaireō_ cihpen a masang in khentat cihna ahi hi.

“Akuama peuhin” cihtawh a kipat ciangin, Paul in a phazah dingpen khatlehkhat kibanglo ding acihna ahi hi. Ama’ cihnopna pen mi khatpeuh in piak ding a khentatna ah, kidopna tawh khentat ding hi. Ei mahin piakding hoih i sakzah pen thumaan hipah lel hi.

**2. Ngiimna ah a kilawmzah ding thukhun omhi.** Macedonian mite in “amau’ ngahna tawh kituakin” (2Cor. 8:3) pia uh Paul in cihi. Tuakhit ciangin Corinth mite a dingin a kilawmzah cileuleu hi. Amau’ kipiaknasa vaipuak a zawhsiang theihna dingun amau’ neihsa mahuh zang dingin hanthawn hi (2Cor. 8:11). “Piaknopna lungsim neih nakleh, neihlohna te siksan zawlo in, neihna tawh kizui in piakna Pasian in sang” (2Cor. 8: 12) cihi.

Laisiangtho in a kilawmbang in piakna ah sawma khat cih ciangtang hongpiak hang; sumpi pen ciangtanh omlo hi. “A kuamapeuh in na lungsim sung uhah na ngaihsut sa zahuh pia un” (2Cor. 9: 7V) a kilawmzah ding thukhun zang ding hi, Thukhat panin mi kuamapeuh in ama’ piakkhiat dingzah amah mahin khentat ding hi. Amau’ neihsa tawh ki zui in piak ding hipeuh hi. Hihpen ngiimna geelna lotawh kihih theilo hi.

`Nang nahau in na zawng phial zongin sawmakhat piak bangzah nacitak hiam? A tamzaw hihthei gige napi in; nahih ding bang paulam peuh na zang zel hiam?`
