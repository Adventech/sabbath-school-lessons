---
title:  Corinth Khuapi
date:   30/06/2026
---

`Sawl. 18:1–3; 1 Corinth. 5:9–11 leh 1 Corinth. 8:4 simin. Corinth khuapi sumbawlna, mihingte omzia leh biakna lam na koici muh hiam?`

Corinth khuapen a sumbawlna leh a hauhna tawh tanglai in minthang hi. BC 146 kum in Rome mite in susia uh a; 44 BC ciangin Julius Caesar in Rome gamhuam dingin lam kik hi. Laisiangthothak sungah hong kidawk penin hih Rome mite Corinth ahi hi. Paul hunlai in Corinth khua pen Athen tawh tungnun a kituh kinken uh hi a, a tungnun zawkna mun tampi om hi. Corinth in vanlei vanzuakna a nuam mahmah tuipigei tembaw khawlna nih nei hi.

A leikiat dan leh amun hoihna hang mahin Paul in Corinth a teel hihtuak zaw hi. “Tuabang in lungdamthu kizeel theihna’ng thu khat hong om hi. Corinth ah khatvei a kiphuh khit nakleh, tualai panin leitung khempeuh ah kipuak sawn thei ta ding ahi hi” —Ellen G. White, Sketches From the Life of Paul, p. 99.

Tuateng banah, Corinth hauhna leh sumbawlna hang mahin Paul in zong tuipuan buk khui in zuakna tawh nungta in tuakhuapi sungah lungdamthu tangko thei hi (Sawl. 18: 2, 3). Khuapi lian leh hau sungah Pasian’na sep pen tona haksatna omlo hideklo hi. Corinth khua ah biakna nam tuamtuam tawh gamlum ziazua in minthang (1Cor. 8: 5) a, taattung tampi takte zong Apollo, Athena leh Aphrodite pasiante a banah Sarapis leh Isis kici Egypt pasiante aa zong om ziazua hi.

Hihbang biakna buaihuai ziazua kawmkal ah; zahmawh zualzangna lamah zong min nei lailai hi. Greek mi leitung thu leh tangthupu Strabo in; Corinth khua Aphrodite tungah biakpiakna in a neih uh siangtho-zualzang numei 1,000 bang Aphrodite biakinn mahah om cihi. Laisiangtho siamte in hihthu hoihtak ngaihsun uh a; Corinth mite tawh kisai in Athen mite thu thansak mawkmawk ding bangin pulak uh ahih hang: tanglai leitung in zeh-zualzangna pen na zangpha mahmah lel uhhi. Corinth bek thamlo mun khempeuh ah zualzangna tawh buai kawikawi uhhi. Milim biakna leh zualzangna peuh mahpen a nisim nundan uh hingiat a; hih a dahhuai thu tengpen 1 leh 2 Corinth sungah ki teltak in hong kihilhcian ding hi.

`Corinth ah Pasian’na sepna sungah, Paul in milim biakna buaina leh mihing suahtakna tawh kisai haksatna om hi. Tuni in bang haksatna peuh in lungdamthu tangkona ah bang haksatna hong piangsakthei hiam? Koibang in zo ding? Tuni eima khuapite ah a omkhak leh zong; Corinth te aa tawh koibang in ki lamdang hiam?`
