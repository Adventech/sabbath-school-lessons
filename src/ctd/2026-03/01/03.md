---
title:  Athens pan Corinth ah
date:   29/06/2026
---

`Sawl. 17:16–34 simin. Corinth a paima in Paul koiah om a; bang hih hiam?`

Sawl. 17:16–34 sungah Paul in Corinth a paima in Athen mite tungah thuhilh cihi. Tualai in Athen ah pai ding geelna bangmah a omloh hang, Berea mite langdona phukha vat ahih manin, a lawmte huhna tawh Athen paikha vat hi (Sawl. 17: 13-15).

Timothy leh Silas tegel in Paul tawh kikhawlta dinguh ahih manun; Athen ah Paul a paipih teng un Berea ah a kileh uh hong kulta hi (Sawl. 17: 15). Sawltak 17: 16-34 sung ah Paul in amau ngakin a omsung teng a nasep teng om hi. Biakinn sungah, khuapi tual sungah leh Areopagus cihte ah Zeisu thu gen hi. Zeisu’ thu gen khawl thei hetlo a, tua bang ahih theihna dingin hunlem a ngah bangbang in zang hi.

`Sawl. 18: 1–11 simin. Paul in Corinth vatung in a taam sungteng tawntung bang hih hiam?`

Paul in a khualzin nihveina ah Corinth ah vapai hi. Tualai ah Paul kumkhat leh a lang taam hi Luka in cihi. Ama’ ngeina ombang in, Paul in a Pasian’na sepna pen biakinn sungpan in kipan hi (Sawl. 18:4-6). Sawltak 17: 1, 2 in tuapen Paul’ ngeina naci hi. Zeisu in zong a nungzuite a thupiakna (Sawl 1: 8) ombang in; Paul in Jew mite masak hi (Rom. 1: 16; Sawl 13: 46).

A nunung penin Corinth panin Silas leh Timothy tegel in hong pawm uh a, tua ciangin “Paul in ahun neih khempeuh thuhilhna in zangin, Zeisu pen Christ ahi hi ci in Jew mite kiangah teltak in a gen hi” (Sawl. 18: 5). Corinth ah a taam sung tawntung “Pasian’ thu hilh” in manlah mahmah hi (Sawl. 18: 11). Hihthu tawh kipawl in Paul in kiteltak in a pulak thu in “singlamteh tungah Zeisu Christ a sihna thu simloh, thudang peuhmah ngaihsutloh dingin ka ngaihsutsa ahi hi” (2 Cor. 2: 2) cihi.

`Athen khua leh Corinth khua ah Paul’ khualzinna tungtawn in hunneih khempeuh Pasian’thu gen ding koibangin hong hilh naci hiam? Zeisu’ thu mitung ah na hawmsawn theihna dingin nahun hoih koibang in zangthei ding nahi hiam?`
