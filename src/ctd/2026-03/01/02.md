---
title:  Paul, Pasian Sapsa Zeisu’ Sawltakpa
date:   28/06/2026
---

Paul in Corinth mite a laikhakna kamhonna in, amah Zeisu’ Sawltakpa dingin “Pasian in a hong teel” (1 Cor. 1: 1; 2Cor. 1: 1). A laikhakte kamhonna ah, tawmno khat cih lobuang, a vekpi phial mahin Zeisu tawh kipawlna vive mah ahihlam amah kitelcian a hih manin tuabang danta in kam a hong hi.

`1 Corinth 1:1 leh Rom. 1:1 simin. A thupi mahmah bang thunih pen Paul in a Pasian’na sepna sungah bulphuh phadeuh hiam? (Gal. 1:1 enkak in)`

Paul in sawltak dingin amah a kiteelna pen Pasian’ deihna cihi. Mihing maimai teel hilo a, Pasian ngiatin a teel cih telcian hi (Gal. 1; 1). Jeremiah mahbang in Paul zong Pasian in anu’ naubu sungpan a teeltuam hi a (Jer. 1: 5) Pasian’ hehpihna hang (Gal. 1: 15) hi a, dawibia minamdangte lakah Christ’ lungdamthu tangko ding ahihman ahi hi.

1 Corinth. 15: 8 sungah, Paul in, ama kiangah Christ in a thawhkik khit ciangin hong kipulaak (1Cor. 15: 5-7) cihi. Tua khitdeuh ciangin zong amah akiteeltuamna pen Zeisu tawh maitang kimuhna pan hi cilai hi (1 Cor. 15: 9-11). “Zeisu’ sawltakpa” cih in ngaihdan tampi huam hi. A neupen in Zeisu in sawl ahihna hong lak hi. Hih kammal teng mahtawh Zeisu’ sawltakpa ahihna (Rom. 1: 1; Titus 1:1; Gal. 1: 10); thuhilhsia leh lai hilhsia (1Tim. 2: 7; 2Tim. 1: 11) Paul in pulak den hi. Thu ahilh a lai ahilh zongin, Christ a ding hiden zaw hi. Tomgen leng Paul in Zeisu’ sawltakpa ahi hi.

Zeisu in, Paul Sawltak ahihna thu laigil; Paul’ nundan thu laigil ahi hi. Paul’ ngaih sutna leh lungsim khempeuh sungah Zeisu tawh kidim hi. Zeisu pen tuabang ahihna thu 1Corinth a thupatna lungdamthu a sunna ah kuavei bang gen hi. Paul in Zeisu a itzia pen tatsatlo in Zeisu ngaihsun in a thu gengen hi. Ama’ cinlehkep sunsun te in Christ-bulphuh nundan a neihtheihna dingun Zeisu hawmsawn nuam hi. Amah pen Sawltak dingin a kiteel ciangin; amaute zong Topa’ sapna om bangbang in a citak Zeisu’ nungzuite hiding in a kisam ahi uhhi.

`Paul pen Sawltak dingin kisam hi. Nang bang dingin hong kisam a, nang hong kisapna na koici theih hiam? Tuabang khatzong a om keileh; Pasian tawh na tonkhawpna ah bangpeuh kikhial kha hiam?`
