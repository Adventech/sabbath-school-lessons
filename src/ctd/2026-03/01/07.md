EGWhite, “Corinth” pp. 243–254, in The Acts of the Apostles

“Corinth ah lungdamthu a tangko ciangin sawltakpa in Athen a zat ngeidan a minngahna tawh a kibanglo khat zangkik zaw hi. Deihtak in kinialna kikupna cihte peel in ‘singlamteh tung a kikhai Zeisu Christ lobuang bangmah theihloh ding’ (1Cor. 2: 2) in lungsim khettat khin hi”—Ellen G. White, The Acts of the Apostles, p. 244.

“Paul in zong gualzawhna ciangkhat nei hinapi, tualai a amuh nate tawh pawlpi khat phuh ding pen lunghiang mahmah zel hi. Corinth khua limlim pen dotna a hau mah mah nasepna phualkhat hi ci in ngaihsun a hih manin, nutsiat sawmta hi.”

“Nusia in a hoihzaw munkhat a lungngaih laitak in; zan ciangin a maang sungah Topa hong kilang in “Lau kei in. Lungkiakei inla na thugen zomin. Hih khua sungah Keima’ mi tampi om hi” (Sawl. 18: 9, 10) cihi. Hihthu pen Corinth ah taamsuakna ding leh; ama’ khaici vawh teng Topa in khangsak ding kamciam ahihlam Paul in telcian pah hi. Tua ciangin Zeisu Christ’ dial nuai ah pawlpi lian khat mah hong po khia hi”—Ellen G. White, Sketches from the Life of Paul, pp. 106, 107.

“Corinth ah Paul in kumkhat kha guk nasem hi ci in ki ciamteh hi. Corinth ah a hanciamna teng pen a dalzo om hetlo in khangpha mahmah ahih manin bulpi munin zang phial hi. Pawlpi honpi khatmah ki phuhbeh lai hi. Tua pawlpite lakah Paul a omtheihloh manin, a kisamna munteng pen, a thuk mahmah a muibun ama’ laikhakte in panpih thei mahmah a; Pasian’ kammal bangin sanguh ahih manun, tua laikhakte biakinn te sungah kisim ngiatngiat hi” —Ellen G. White, Sketches from the Life of Paul, p. 109.

**Kikupding Dotnate:**

`Paul pen Zeisu’ sawltakpa a kicih theihna dingin Pasian kiangpan sapna ngah ngiatpa ahi hi. Eimah kua ihihlam theitel in hong kisapna bang cih theih ding bang hangin thupi hiam?`

`Tawlkhat sung, Paul in Corinth ah nasepna khawlzaw in nutsiat ding zah dongin lungkia khin hi. Bangin Paul’ lungsim kikheelsak hiam? Pasian’na sepna khat nut- siat dingzah in lungkiat laitak in hihthu in koibang in hong huhthei hiam? Eite zong tuabang ihih hun ding a om hiam?`

`Corinth pawlpi mite pen amau’ kimkot a leitung ngeina tengin huzaap gawp cih kitelcian mahmah hi. Tuni eite zong tuamah bang ihi uhhi. Koibang in leitung ah om in (John 17: 11, 25) “pumpi deihna, mit tawh mu in i deihte leh mite kisaktheih pih nate ahi leitung nate khempeuh” (1 John 2:16) in huzaap saklo thei ding hiam? Eima kimkot a ngeinate in i pawlpi khawng a sialam in hong huzaap thei bangteng peuh a om diam?`
