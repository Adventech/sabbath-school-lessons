### Tukalsung Simding
1 Cor. 1:1; Gal. 1:1; Sawl. 17:16–34; 1 Cor. 5:9–11; Sawl. 18:4–10; 2 Cor. 2:4.

> <p>Kamngah</p>
> Zankhat Paul in maangmu a, a maang sungah Topa in, “Lau kei in, lung kia kei inla, na thugen zom in. Hih khuasungah Keima’ mi tampi om a, zong hong ompih kahih manin; kuamah in nang bangmah hong cihlo ding hi” a cihi (Sawl. 18: 9, 10).

English minam missionary lianpa William Carey in, ama’ nuntakna dingin khedap khuipa ahihhang, a naseppipen in kha hotkhiatna hi cihi. Tua mahbang in Paul zong puan -buk bawl tawh nungta (Sawl. 18: 1-3) a; a nasep taktak ahihleh Christ kiangah mite paipihna ahi hi.

Tukal in, Corinth khuapi ah Paul’ Pasian’na asepna teng en ding hihang. I muh ding tengpen, tua pawlpi in; buaina tawh kidim bialbual a, kum tulnih khit tuni a ei’ buai nate tawh a kibang mello zong tam hi. Kum bangzah hiam khat Christian hihkhit, pawlpi sungah na a semkhinsa khat a dingin dotna khat: Christian kipawlna limlim buaina nei ngeilo a omngei hiam? A dawn na’ng bel kitelcian lel hi.

Paul in Corinth ah zong tua bangte phukha khin ahihhang Singlamteh thupuak (1 Cor. 2: 2) tawh naphu hi. Tuni a tona te peuhmah tua thupuak tungah cihtakna bektawh maingat ding hihang. Tu quarter sung, 1 leh 2 Corinth sungpan i muh thupuak teng peuh mah eima’ nunna sung zattheih sawm ding hihang.
