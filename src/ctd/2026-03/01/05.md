---
title:  “Hih khua sunga Tampi om”
date:   01/07/2026
---

`Sawl. 18:4–8 simin. Paul’ thuhilhna na in bang gah hiam?`

Corinth a om Jew mite lakah Paul’ nasepna pen, ama’ deihzah in gahpha mello hi. Langdona leh huatna peuh na phukha leltak hi. Laisiangtho in “amaute un amah nial uh a gensia” (Sawl. 18: 6) cihi. Greekpau in _blasphēmeō_ cihpen, mihing khatpeuh “mawhna zong; mawhsak” cihna ahi hi. Thukhat panin Paul vangkiamsak nuam a, ama’ nasepna ah gualzawh theihna’ng teng dalsak nuam uhhi.

Bangbang hitaleh, Corinth mite biakinn a Paul’ nasepna te mawksuah samlo hi. A nasepna peuhmah Pasian makaihna ahih manin, Pasian in “Keima kam panin a paikhia ka thu in a hawmpi in Kei kiang hong tungkik lo ding” (Isa. 55: 11) cihi. Biakinn ukpa Crispus leh ama’ innkuanpihte in Zeisu pen Messiah hi cih sangthei in kituiphum ding cih peuhmah Jew mite pawlkhat in lam-en vetlo uhhi (Sawl. 18: 8). Amau bek hilo “Corinth khuami tampite in zong thuhilhna ngai in um uh a, a ki tuiphum uh” (Sawl. 18: 8) zong Crispus in a thuzawhna hang hihtuak zaw hi.

`Sawl. 18:9, 10 simin. Corinth ah a phutkhak haksatna te hangin Paul’ lung sim ding na koici muhsak hiam? Pasian in Ama’ nasempa koibang in thapia hiam?`

Paul in biakinn pan a paikhiat lianleh, thapiakna khat tuakkha lian hi. Zan ciangin a maang sungah Christ ngiat hong kilangh in Isa. 41: 10 sunga kammal omsa bangin “nang hong ompih ka hih manin laukei in” cihi. Tua laitak pen Paul Corinth ah omgige in “thanem in launa tawh kaliing” (1 Cor. 2: 3) cihi. Deihtak in langdona khat hangin Berea panin Athen ah apai kul ding hita hi. Tuabang langdona hangmah tawh Corinth panin zong paikhia khin hihtuak lailai hi. Ahihhang tutung in bel tuabang hisamlo hi. Zeisu in Paul kiangah “hih khua sungah Keima’ mi tampi a om hi” (Sawl. 18: 10) aci hi. Tua mite tungah hotkhiatna thuthang puak dingin Paul pen Pasian khutzatpa ahi hi.

`Isaiah 41:10 simin. Hihmun ah bang kamciam lamdang hong kipia hiam? Nangma nisim nundan koibang in sukha thei ding hiam?`
