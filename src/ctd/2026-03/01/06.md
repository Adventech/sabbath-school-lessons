---
title:  Corinth Mite tungah Paul’ Laikhakte
date:   02/07/2026
---

`1 Cor. 1:11–13; 1 Cor. 4:14; 1 Cor. 5:11; 1 Cor. 7:1; 1 Cor. 14:37; 40; 2 Cor. 1:12; 2 Cor. 2:9; 2 Cor. 11:3 leh 2 Cor. 13:10 simin. Paul in Corinth mite tungah a laikhakna hangthu hihmun tengin koibang in hong telciansak thei hiam?`

1 Corinth. a gelh laitak in Paul in Ephesus ah om hi (1 Cor. 5: 9). Corinth lamah thute late hoih khollo cihthu Chloe te innkuan hongpai in zasak uh hi (1 Cor. 1: 11). 1 Cor. 1-6 sungah Paul in Chloe hong zaksak thubuai teng siansuah dikdek phot hi. Tua buaina tepen pawl kikhenna, zahmawh zualzangna, zumthu kineihna leh angzuakna cihte ahi uhhi.

Paul in zong tangtawh dotna lai khat na ngah kha hi (1 Cor. 7: 1). Tuateng a dawnkikna tawh 1 Cor. 7 pan a zomteng hipah hi. Tua dotnate pen kiteenna, kikhenna, tangval/nungak siangtho hihna, milim biakpiakna ante, biakpiakziate, khalam silpiakte zatdan leh thawhkikna tawh kisai telcianna dik cihte ahi uhhi. Corinth pawlpi pen thubuai tawh kidim in khankhin nailo uhhi. Na khuatual pawlpi in zong thubuai tampi nei vencin e. Ahihhang Corinth pawlpi bel suksia zawtham lai hi.

Corinth mite tungah Paul’ laikhak masapen in tulai eima’ hun tawh zong kipawl thei lua tham hi. Tuni pawlpi tampitak te in zong eimau tumtak in thubuai kinei ngeingai hilo hiam? Hih laikhak in eite hong nak thuhilhlua hi. “Paul laikhak khempeuh lakpan a kicing pen, a ciaptehhuai pen leh a vangneipen laikhak ahi hi”—Ellen G. White, The Acts of the Apostles, p. 301.

Paul in Corinth mite tungah thumvei liivei bang laikhak hihtuak hi (2 Cor. 10: 9). 1 Corinth masiah in zong laikhat khak khin ahihhang (1Cor. 5: 9) tua laikhak ki mansuah hi. 2 Corinth madeuh in zong laikhat mah nakhak a, mipilte in “lungkham sinkhamna lai” (2 Cor. 2: 3, 4, 9; 2 Cor. 7: 8) ci uh a, tua zong ki mansuah kik leuleu hi. Pawlkhat in tua laipen 1 Corinth hi aci tawh; tua laikhak sungpan themkhat 2 Corinth sungah gelhkik hi a ci tawh om ziazua uhhi.

2 Corinth sungpan in, Corinth pawlpi mite pen amau kimkot leitung ngeina te in tuamcip cih zong kiphawk kha lai hi. Amaute un kidemna, vangneihna, hauhna leh tuhun in pawlpi a ding tona a suakthei peuhmah pimuh mahmah uhhi. Tuatawh a kikelki in Paul in Christ a bulphuh ngeina, lungdamthu limlang sungpan leitung etna cihte phuhsak sawm hi. Eite in zong hih leitung pen lungdamthu limlang sungah ettheih ding bangzah thupi nasa hiam?

`2 Corinth 2:4 simkik in. Tua mite Paul in bangzah in lainat in don ci hiam? A kilehbulh in midangte tungah eima’ lungsim bangzah in vot thei ding hiam?`
