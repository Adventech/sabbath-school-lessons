---
title:  Zeisu’ hong Hehpihna
date:   20/09/2026
---

2 Corinth Nihna laikhak a thukhupna pen, a thupatna mahbang in Zeisu hong hehpihna mah tawh ahih manin hong lawpsak mahmah phial hi (2Cor. 1: 2; 2Cor. 13: 14). Paul in hih alaikhak pen Hong hehpihna vive tawh hong in khup hi. Tu Quarter kipat tuungin imuhsa mahbang in Zeisu’ thu ngaihsut leh gen cim thei ngiatlo hi. “I Topa Zeisu zong haumahmah napi in, Ama’ zawnna tawh note mihau na suahtheihna dingun, note hangin mizawng in hong kibawl tawm” (2Cor. 8: 9) cihi.

Zeisu’ hong hehpihna pen pahtakhuai in nialzawhloh hilai hi. Vantung ah hauhna tawh om tawntung khin hinapi; tuateng nusia in hong kizawn bawltawm hiau hi. Galilee lampi tungah leivui sik ngiatngiat in hong paisuk hi. “Amah kiniamkhiat tawmzaw in singlamteh tung nangawn ah sih ding a ut hi” (Phil. 2: 8).

Tuateng khempeuh pen eite’ “hauh” theihna ding, vantung ah Amah tawh i om khawm theihna dingin hong hihsak ngiat ahi hi. Eite in mawhna kidim leitung, thuaksiat na ciang khawngbek thei ihih manin; Zeisu in vantung innpi nusia in, eite a ding a nunna piakhia dingin hong tuaksuk cihpen telbanzo mahmahlo lel hihang.

`Rom. 16: 20; Galati 6: 18; Philippi 4: 23; leh 1Thess. 5: 28 simin. Bang a thupi thu hong hilh namukha hiam?`

Paul in a laikhak sungah Zeisu’ hehpihna cipahpah hi. Kepkhi bangliang in “Zeisu Christ’ hehpihna hangin … mi tampitak tungah hong kipia” (Rom. 5: 15) cihi. Tua alian hehpihna angah mite in “Zeisu hangin nungta” (Rom. 5: 17) uhhi. 2Corinth sunga omsa mahbang in, Paul in zong, a laikhak dangte ah Zeisu’ hehpihna mah thupatna leh thu khupna mahin zangzel hi (Rom. 1: 7; Rom. 16: 20; 1Cor. 1: 3; 1Cor. 16: 23; Gal. 1: 3; Gal. 6: 18; Phil. 1: 2; Phil. 4: 23). Hih thulupi in ama’ lungsim sungah a kidim mahbang in Corinth mite lungsim sungah zong kidim saknuam hi.

Hihmah pen pawlpi khempeuh a dingin deihsak hi. Ephet mite kiangah acihdan lungngai lecin: “Tatsatlo in i Topa Zeisu Christ a it tawntung mi khempeuh tungah hehpihna thupha hong tung tahen” (Eph. 6: 24). Tua a vultheilo itna sangin a kiamzaw itna tawh eite in Zeisu hong itsak nuam ding hiam? Piangtheilo hi. Paul in a deihsakna pen Zeisu’ “hehpihna a tam semsem in, mi a tam semsem” (2Cor. 4: 15) tungah a tunthei ding Paul deihna hi a, ama’ a dingin a kicin mahbangin amaute a dingin zong kicingsak nuam hi (2Cor. 12: 9).

`Tuabang in Pasian’ hehpihna nang kiang hong tung cih ngaihsun in. Nang paukhiatsa leh sepsate in bangtawh kituak ding hiam? Tuate munah Pasian in hehpihna tawh bang hong pia zawsop hiam?`
