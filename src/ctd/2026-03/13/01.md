### Tukalsung Simding
2Cor. 8: 9; Rom. 16: 20; 1John 4: 8–11; 2Cor. 13:11; Phil. 2:1, 2; Gal. 4: 4–6.

> <p>Kamngah</p>
> “Topa Zeisu’ hong hehpihna thupha; Pasian hong itna leh Kha Siang 	tho tawh kikhawlhtheihna note khempeuh tungah hongtung tahen” 	(2Cor. 13: 14).

Paul in, pawlpi sungah a omloh phamawh mahmah sawlna nga tawh 2Corinth pen thukhup kik hi (2Cor. 13: 11).

Amasa sawlna, “Lungdam un”, amasa laikhakna sungah zong omkhinzo hi.

Anihna sawlna, “Na omzia uh kipuahpha un”. Hih kammal pen Greekpau in (_katartizō_) hi a, 1Cor. 1: 10 sungah om khinzo hi.

Athumna sawlna, “Khatlehkhat thugente nakizui un” cihpen 2 Cor. 1: 3-7 sungteng a tomlak na ahi hi. Paul in laikhak nihna pen thapiakna tawh thukhup hi. Pasian kiangpan thupiak na akingah mahbang in midangte zong thapiaciat ni (2Cor. 1: 4, 6).

Alina leh angana sawlna, “lungsim kikhat in, kilem un” (2Cor. 13: 11), cihpen kipum khat ding sawlna ahi hi. Lungdamna, kipuahphatna, thapiakna, pumkhatna leh kilemna cihte pen “itna leh kilemna Pasian” (2Cor. 13: 11) maiah omzia ding ahi hi. Tua pen thum kigawm Pasian in mihing’ lungtang sungah ansaepna gah ahi hi (2Cor. 13: 14).

Hehpihna, itna leh kikhawlhpihna pen thumgawm Pasian nasepna gah ahi hi. Hih Christian te omzia thumteng in, Pasian hong ompihna telcianna in dingkip hi.
