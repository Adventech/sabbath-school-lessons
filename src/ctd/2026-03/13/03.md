---
title:  Pasian Hong Itna
date:   21/09/2026
---

“Topa Zeisu hong hehpihna, Topa Pasian’ hong itna leh Kha Siangtho hong khawlhkhawppihna in note khempeuh tungah om tahen” (2Cor. 13: 14). Hih kammal teng tawh Paul in laikhak nihna thukhup hi. Thumgawm Pasian’ min a kizom in losuk diudeu a – Tapa, Pa leh Kha Siangtho cihbang ahi hi. Pasian pen koibang hi a, bangteng hong hihsak cihte pen hih thumgawm Pasian’ nasepna ahi hi.

`John 3: 16, 17; Rom. 8: 37–39 leh 1John 4: 8–11 simin. Hihmun tengin Pasian’ hong itna koibang in hong hilh hiam?`

Khatna John 4: 8 in “Pasian in itna hi” cihi. Itna cihlimlim Pasian’ omzia ahi hi. Pasian in khatbek a neihsun a Tapa eite a dingin sihna dongah hong piakkhiatna na panin itna theithei pan ihihlam John in thupi gen hi (John 3: 16). Hotkhiatna nasep sem dingin Pasian in Zeisu hong sawl hi (John 3: 17). Tua in hotkhiatna ngiimna nasep sungah thukhat mah hipah hi (Sawl. 3: 20, 21; 1 John 4: 10, 14). Zeisu in Amah pen Apa’ sawl ahihlam lungdamthu sungah tamveipi mah pulaak hi (Mate 10: 40; Maku 9: 37).

A ciapteh huai mahmah in Paul in “eite mawhna nei ihih laitak in eite a dingin Christ hong si ahih manin; Pasian in eite hong it mahmahna thu hong lahkhin hi” (Rom. 5: 8) cihi. Innkuan sung pasal leh zi, nulehpa leh tate, lawm ki itte ki itna nuamtak in kikhawlhna panin, Pasian’ hong itna muvat thei hihang. Tua Pasian hong itna mahpen Ama’ bawlsa nate lakpanin zong muthei hihang.

Tuatawh kizui in EGWhite in “Pasian in itna hi’ cih laimal pen pakmom khempeuh tungah, lopapo khempeuh tungah kigelh hi. A ithuai vasate in huihlak ah amau awtawnh lasa uh a; ahoih anem dikdek pakpalhte in zong huihlak teng namtuisak hiuheu hi. A hing dildial singtehte tawh a kidim singkungte in taam sungteng nangawn adopbawl mahmah Pa Pasian’ kepcinna leh Ama’ tate khempeuh lungnuam in a omsak nopna thu pulaak uhhi” —Steps to Christ, p. 10.

Bangbang hitaleh, eima’ mawhthoihna dingin asihlawh dingin Pasian in a Tapa hong piakna sangin a lianzaw bangmah omlo hi. Eite a ding anuntakna piakhia dingin a Tapa Zeisu hong sawl manin, Pasian in ei hong itna kithei ta a: eite’n zong “i sanggamte a dingin nuntakna piakngamna” (1John 3: 16) tawh dawngthuk ding hihang.

Paul in Corinth mite ki pumkhat saknuam hi. Ahihhang, itna omlo in ki pumkhat theilo hi. Tuahang mahin amah’n “itna in mite kipsak hi” (1Cor. 8: 1), itna omkeileh na khempeuh amawkna hi (1Cor. 13: 1–3) ci in hilh hi. Tua ahih manin nasepna khempeuh itna tawh sepkul a (1Cor. 16: 14), tua itna pen Pasian hong itna keekna ahi hi.

`Zeisu ngiatpen tawntung Pasian taktak hikei zenleh, Ama’ lungdamthu sungah bangteng i suplawh tam maw?`
