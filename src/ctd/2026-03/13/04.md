---
title:  Itna Pasian
date:   22/09/2026
---

Tanglai lawki hunlai in pasiante in mihingte it cih upna neilo uhhi. A pasiante uh pen ngongtat mahmah uh a, thoih in mai-et kulden cihi. Laisiangtho sungah itna Pasian i muhkhak zenzen pen thuthak thuhoih mahmah ahi hi. Paul’ hunlai in lamdang a kisak mahbang in, Paul in zong Pasian pen “itna leh kilemna Pasian” (2Cor. 13: 11) naci hi.

`2Corinth 13: 11 simin. Hitengah a kigenthu panin bang lam-etna naneithei ding hiam? Himun in hong hilh thu koibangin na nuntakpih thei tammaw?`

“Itna leh kilemna Pasian” a cihpen thunih in kiletkhia thei hi. Athu masa pen, Pasian pen ‘Pasian pen itna leh kilemna phungpi’ hi. Athu nihna pen, Pasian pen itna leh kilemna tawh kiciamteh thei hi. Hih tegel kikal ah bangmah ngaihsut luat ding zong omlo lel hi. Itna leh kilemna tegel pen Pasian’ omzia theihtelna leh ciamtehna cihi. Tua hang mahin Amah in itna leh kilemna hong pia hi.

Muntampi mahah Paul in Pasian pen “thuakzawhna leh thahatna” (Rom. 15: 5); ‘lam-etna’ (Rom. 15: 13); ‘kilemna’(Rom. 15: 33; Rom. 16: 20; 1Cor. 14: 33; Phil. 4: 9; 1Thess. 5: 23); ‘Hehpihna nei Pa” (2Cor. 1: 3), leh ‘kisapna khempeuh hong huh Pasian’ (2Cor. 1: 3) cihi. Pasian pen hih thupha khempeuh bulpi ahi hi. A beingeilo hong itna tawh a hongpiak ahi hi.

“Kilemna Pasian” pen Laisiangtho sungah omkawikawi ahihhang, “itna Pasian” cihlian pen (2Cor. 13: 11) sungbek ah om ahih manin, eite’n zong thupibawl in lahkhiat kik ding a kisampen pen ahi hi.

Kamlet tampi takte in, Paul in itna Pasian acihna pen thumgawm Pasian thupha tawh a laikhak nihna thukhup hi. Tuabang in 2Cor. 13: 14 sungah ahihna hangin Pasian cihpen thum kigawm ahihlam hong tel lahna zong ahi hi.

“Hihtengah ‘Pasian” cih kammal pen thum lakpan khat a dingin zang ahihhang, Zeisu leh Kha Siangtho pen a laikhakna khempeuh a helkawikawi a; pawlpi masate in a muhuh thumgawm Pasian mahpen eite’n zong i muhtheihna dingin hong hatthotna zong a hihi. Paul in kiteltak in hong pulaak khin ahihlam a kitheihna dingin tua thumgawm Pasian pen ki tomlak in ‘Trinity’ a kicih ziauna zong; Paul in a gengen khit kum zalom khat khit bangciang hipan lailai hi”—Tom Wright, Paul for Everyone: 2 Corinthians (London: Society for Promoting Christian Knowledge, 2004), p. 148.

Eite in itna tawh kikhawl in thum a kigawm a nungta tawntung Pasian khat sung ah i um uhhi. Tua thumgawm Pasian in ei hong it a, eite zong khatlehkhat ki itna tawh Amau’ thum ki-itna hong lengkik saknuam hi.
