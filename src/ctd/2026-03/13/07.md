Ellen G. White, “Let Not Your Heart Be Troubled” pp. 662–680, in The Desire of Ages.

“Zeisu Christ’ hong hehpihna bekmah in suang-lungtang panin sa-lungtang ah hong kheelsak thei a, Pasian a dingin hong nungta sakthei hi. Mihing in kha peuhma thu tang saktheilo hi. Gamtat hoihlo natna pen Zato Mangpipa lobuang in damask theilo uh hi. A sangpen vantung’ silpiak, Pa in a neihsun, hehpihna leh thumaan tawh a kidimpa bekin a mangthang te honkhia thei bek hi”—EGWhite, The Signs of the Times, May 2, 1892.

“Pasian in ki itna hi’ 1 John 4: 16. Ama’ omzia leh thukham in ki itna hi. Tua bang hikhinzo in hilailai ding hi. ‘Asang, alian, a nungta tawntung pa’ (Isaiah 57: 15); ‘a om tawntung pa’ (Habakkuk 3: 6); ‘a kikheel ngeilo, a kihei hangin khua zong mial tuanlo’ (James 1: 17) hi.

Piansak sate kipulaakna vang khempeuh in mong neilo itna vive ahi hi. Pasian uk-zawhna in piansaksa khempeuh a dingin thupha kicinsakna ahi hi. Sialehpha kidona lianpi’ tangthu sungah, vantung panin hong kipat laipek panin, langdona khempeuh a ki hepkhiat a, mawhna khempeuh a kisuksiat siang ni ciangdong in, a kikheel ngeilo Pasian’ hong itna lahkhiatna hi den ding hi”—EGWhite, Patriarchs and Prophets, p. 33.

“Kha Siangtho in zong, Pa Pasian mahbang in a mi in omcih theih ding ahi hi. Kha Siangtho in zong pumpi mahtawh om hikeileh, eima’ khate in zong Pasian’ tate ahih lam teci hong pansak theilo ding hi. Amah zong siangtho Pasian hikeileh, Pasian lungsim sunga thusimte mukhia theilo ding hi”—Ellen G. White, The Faith I Live By, p. 52.

“Vantungah thumgawm Pasian anungta mimal thum om uhhi. Hih vangliatna thumteng amin uhtawh pulaak leng; Pa, Tapa leh Kha Siangtho hi a, Christ a sang mite ki tuiphumna, Christ sung mahah nundan thak a nungta nuamte tawh hih vanglian thumteng in naseppih in panpih ding hi”—Ellen G. White, Evangelism, p. 615.

**Kikupding Dotnate:**

`Mi tampi’n a theih uh “Amazing Grace” cih la khat om hi. Zeisu hong hehpihna in bang a lamdang om hiam?`

`Tapa taimang gentehna sungah Pasian hong itna lahkhiatna hoih mahmah khat om hi. Hih gentehna sunga a pa in itna nei pa ahihna koibangin kithei thei hiam?`

`Khuatual pawlpi te sungah “Kha Siangtho tawh kikhawlh khawpna” om takpi cih koibang in lahkhia thei ding hiam?`
