---
title:  Kha Siangtho Hong Khawlhpihna
date:   23/09/2026
---

Zeisu hong hehpihna in Pasian hong itna hong lakkhia bekthamlo, tua itna in a muibut theihna dingin Kha Siangtho khawlhpihna hong paipih lailai hi. Tua laitak mahin khawlhpihna in zong Pasian itna sung mahah zungkha leuleu hi. Tua ahih manin itna a omkei nakleh khawlhpihna zong omlo pahhi. Paul mahin “Note Christ tawh kipawl in na nuntakna uh in note hong thahatsak hi. Kha Siangtho tawh kikhawl in, khatleh khat ki hehpih in dikna na kibawl uhhi. Tua ahih manin kei tawh ngaihsutna kibangin note khat lehkhat ki it in, lungsim kituak in na omna uhtawh keima lungdamna nahong cingtaaksak na dingin ka hong kun hi” (Phil. 2: 1, 2).

Pawlkhat in, Kha Siangtho cihpen, thahatna, huzaap peuh hilel hi a cihuh hang maan hetlo hi. A tawpna ah, Paul in, Pa leh Tapa tegel pen thahatna vangliatna bangle in banghang in hong pulaak hiding hiam? Khiatna nei vetlo lel hi. Pa leh Tapa tegel in a mimal in kikhawlh khawm uh mahbang (2Cor. 1: 3; 2Cor. 11: 31) un, Kha Siangtho in hong mimal in hong khawlhpihna in, Amah pen mimal in om cihna ahi hi (Rom. 8:15, 16; John 14: 16, 17, 26; John 15: 26).

“Kha Siangtho tawh kikhawlhna” (Phil. 2: 1) pen namnih in kitelthei hi. Kha Siangtho tungtawn in khatleh khat kikhawlhkhawpna leh, Kha Siangtho mahmah tawh ki khawlhkhawpna ahi hi. Laisiangtho teite pawlkhat bangin hihpen khatlehkhat cihna hilo hi a ci zong om uhhi. Bangpeuh ici zongin, khatlehkhat kikhawlh theihna pen Kha Siangtho tawh kikhawlhna pan mahin a kingah ahi hi.

`1Cor. 2: 10, 11; 1Cor. 3: 16; 1Cor. 12: 11; leh 2Cor. 3: 6, 17 simin. Paul in Corinth mite tungah Kha Siangtho’ thu bangtang hihlh hiam?`

Paul in Kha Siangtho nasepna a gen ding tampi om hi. Corinth Masa leh Nihna sungah Kha Siangtho cih sawmli vei gen hi. Kha Siangtho in pawlpi phattuamna piang sak (1Cor. 14: 12) a, mission nasepna ah mite thahatsak (1Cor. 2: 4, 5), Pasian’ thusimte eitung ah hong kilanghsak (1Cor. 2: 10, 11 in, eite hong hilh (1Cor. 2: 13), eite sungah hong teeng (1Cor. 3: 16; 1Cor. 6: 19), eite dikna dingin Christ tawh hong sepsak (1Cor. 6: 11), pawlpi tungah kha’ silpiakte guan (1Cor. 12–14), a hotkhiat ngah dingin eite hong ciamteh (2Cor. 1: 22), mihing lungsim sungah thukham hong gelhsak (2Cor. 3: 3), Christ sungah nunnathak hong neisak (2 Cor. 3: 6) in mawhna pan hong khahkhia hi (2 Cor. 3: 17). A taktak in Kha Siangtho lotawh eite nungta theilo hihang.

Kha Siangtho in Pasian taktak ahihna theihcianna pen eite a ding Pasian hong itna i matzawk na’ng in banghang in thupi thei hiam?

Paul’ telcianna dampen, Pasian cih thum kigawm ciangbek hilo, tua thum kigawm tengin Lutang Pasian hi uh a, ei hong hotkhiatna ding maapang khawm uhhi (Eph. 1: 3, 13, 14). Ephet laikhak sungah Paul in, Pa’ cingtaakna (Eph. 3: 19), Tapa’ cingtaakna (Eph. 4: 13), leh Kha Siangtho’ cingtaakna (Eph. 5: 18) tawh eite kidim dingin hong sawllai hi.
