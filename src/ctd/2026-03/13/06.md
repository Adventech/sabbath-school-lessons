---
title:  Thumgawm Pasian Eima aa
date:   24/09/2026
---

2Corinth 13: 14 sim leng, hehpihna bulpi in Christ, itna bulpi Pasian leh kikhawlhkhawp theihna bulpi Kha Siangtho hi cihtheih na’ng thu a om hang, thumaan tawh kigamla khollo lel hi.

`1Corinth 1: 3, 4, 9; 1Cor. 10: 16; 2Cor. 1: 2, 12; Rom. 8: 35; Rom. 15: 30; Galati 2: 20; leh Ephet 3: 19 simin. Hihmun in hehpihna, itna leh khawlhpihna pen Thumgawm Pasian tawh koibang in kizom sak hiam?`

Pa, Tapa leh Kha Siangtho te in eite hotkhiatna dingin nasemkhawm uhhi. Hehpih na, itna leh khawlhpihna in tua Thumgawm Pasian kiang panin hong luangkhia ahi hi. Amau khatsim in hotkhiatna tangthu sungah maapanna khatciat nei uhhi. Paul in tuathu thei khinkhian in ama’ laikhakna sungah thupi bawl mahmah hi. Etpak dingin, hotkhiatna ngiimna pen Galati 4: 4–6 sungah, sumbawlna kammal kician khat tawh tehkaak in, hot- khiatna sungah a Thum mahun a maapanna uh teng pulaak sak hi.

Pa Pasian in Zeisu sawl cihciangin, tua ngimmna a bulpi in Pa cihna (Gal. 4: 4) ahi hi. Tapa in numei sungpan in hong piang (Gal. 4: 4) cihpen sunglutna tawh tanglai kamciam khat a picinna hi (Pian. 3:15). Tapa in zong eite hong tankhia in, Satan in hong khemnasa mun ngei mahah Pa tawh kikhawlkik thei dingin hong koihkik hi (Pian. 3: 5).

Paul’ laikhak dangdangte sungah Thumgawm Pasian thu tampi takmah omlai hi. A thum mahun semkhawm dimdiam in mission sem dingin pawlpi kipsak (1 Cor. 12: 4–6), khalam thahatna hong guan hi (Eph. 3: 14–19). Tuapen, Thumgawm Pasian’ kikhawlh theihna a kipsak kipumkhatna tawh akibang pumkhatna khatmah ahi hi (Eph. 4: 4–6).

Paul’ telcianna dampen, Pasian cih thum kigawm ciangbek hilo, tua thum kigawm tengin Lutang Pasian hi uh a, ei hong hotkhiatna ding maapang khawm uhhi (Eph. 1: 3, 13, 14). Ephet laikhak sungah Paul in, Pa’ cingtaakna (Eph. 3: 19), Tapa’ cingtaakna (Eph. 4: 13), leh Kha Siangtho’ cingtaakna (Eph. 5: 18) tawh eite kidim dingin hong sawllai hi.

Corinth mite tungah a laikhak thukhupna sungah (2Cor. 13: 14) Paul in, a hoih zaw tham ding thukhupna khat bawl a – vannuaibuppi vangliatna thum, vantung vangliat na thum kigawmna in eite tawh tu leh a tawntung omkhawm dingin kamciam khat pia hi.

`Pawlpi mite kikhawlh theihna in Lutang Pasian kikhawlh theihna koibang in limpua thei ding hiam?`
