EGWhite, “Calvary” pp. 741–757, in The Desire of Ages.

“Tuhun mite tampitak lungsim sungah, Calvary Singlamteh pen a zahtakkai leh a ciaptehhuai na tetawh kidimsak uhhi. Kipawlna thupi te in khailupna thute tawh kizom pha mahmah uhhi. Ahihhang Paul’ hunlai ahihleh tua singlamteh peuhmah pen huathuai in lipkhaphuai salel uhhi. Singlamteh tungah sihna thuak mikhat peuh mihing’ Honkhiapa ci in pahtawi ding cihpen; zahkona leh langdona a sam hipah lel hi.

Jew mi leh Corinth a Greek mite in hihthu koici muh ding cih Paul in telcian lua hi. Ama’ thungai Jew mite lakah tuathu tangko ding acih limlim in zong a hehlua ding mi tampi takmah ompah hi. Greek mite bangin pen abul abal omlo thu acih dinguh hihtuak lel hi. Tua singlamteh in mihingte a lamto ding leh a honkhia ding cih peuhmah, Paul pen a lungsim cidam nawnlo in muding uhhi.

Ahihang Paul a dingin, singlamteh pen a lunglutna sangpen nahi zel hi. Nazareth mi singlamteh tunga kikhaipa’ nungzuite bawlsiatna matna a thuak uhpan kipan tua sing lamteh’ minthanna in khawl nawnlo ta hi. Tuahun lai in Paul in, Zeisu’ sihna in ama’ nundan a kheelsakna hangin ama’ ngiimna khempeuh vantung’ deihna tawh a kipawl theihna hangin, mong neilo Pasian’ itna ki pulaak hi. Mawhnei mi khat in Pa Pasian hong itna pen, Tapa hong ki piakkhiatna sungah amuh ciangin; vantung huzaapna sung ah ki-aap ahih manin, lungsim kikheelna khat hong pian leh, Christ in ama’ bangkim hita hi”—Ellen G. White, The Acts of the Apostles, pp. 245, 246.

**Kikupding Dotnate:**

`Gethsemane huan sungah, Zeisu in, “Pa aw, ahithei ding ahihleh, hih hai tawh hong kipelhsak in” (Mate 26: 39) cihi. Hih thungetna tungtawn in singlamteh tungah Zeisu hong piaksak in bangzah mantam ci hiam?`

`Paul in, “Pasian’ haina pen mihing’ pilna sangin pilzaw” (1Cor. 1: 25) cihi. Pasian’ pilna leh mihing’ pilna koibang in kilamdang hiam?`

`A kikhai Christ thu pen Jew mite a dingin dongtanna hi a Greek mite a dingin haina ahi hi. Tulai eima’ thuhilh bangkhawng in, tulai khangthakte a dingin tua mahbang hisakthei ding hiam?`

`Paul in, “Kha Siangtho a neilo mite in Pasian Kha Siangtho in a hong piak nate sangtheilo uhhi” (1Cor. 2: 14) cihi. Hihmite lungsim a sukhazo dingin amau kiangah Zeisu’ thu koic gending ihi hiam? Ei’ tatlehhekna bekin amaute a baanzo diam?`
