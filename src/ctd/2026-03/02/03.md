---
title:  Sihna Thuakte a dingin Haihuai
date:   06/07/2026
---

Mihing’ haina leh vantung pilna tehkaakna ah, Paul in “sihna thuakte a dingin tua singlamteh thupuak in haihuai” (1 Cor. 1: 18) cihi. 1 Cor. 1: 18-31 sungteng ah haihuaina leh haina gukvei om lakpanin hih in a om masapen ahi hi.

`1 Corinth. 1:20, 21, 23, 25 leh 27 simin. Paul in, Sihna thuakte a dingin sing lamteh thu in haihuai a cih ciangin a cihnopna bulpi hihmun tengin koibang in hong telciansak thei hiam?`

Greekpau in 1 Cor. 1: 18 a haihuaina cihpen _mōria_ ahi hi. Hih kammal in lai siangthothak sungah nga vei om (1 Cor. 1; 18, 21, 23; 1 Cor. 2: 14; 1 Cor. 3: 19) hi. Hih leh a dang hihte innkuanpih kammal te in zong laisiangthothak sungah tamveipi mah om uh a, tuate lakpan a lang bangpen Paul’ laikhak sungpan vive ahihi. Hih kammalte innkuan te in 1 Cor. sungah tam ompenpen hi.

1 Cor. 1; 18, 23 sungah Paul gen haihuaina cihpen in, mihing’ lungsim cidamlo cihlam hizawlo in, gamtatna leh ngaihsutna ginalo, khensatna ginalo leh Pasian langdona cihna hizaw hi. Hihthu pen 1 Cor. sungah ama’ laigelh thulu te mahmah in laktel ngiat hi.

Hih khuapi a Paul’ dinmun ngaihsutsak in. Pilna, theihna leh leitung dinmun sang a kisial mahmah mun ah hong paikha hi. Tuathu tawh kizom in, Galilee gam Jew mi khat ahi Nazareth khuami Zeisu, Rome mite in a khailup uh, sihna pan a thokikpa; amaute’ mawhna bek thamlo leitungbup mawhna a lohsakpa tangthu zong Paul in genlai hi.

Hihpa’n thuthuk genthei ding maw? A kua ciamnuihnuih ahia leh? Hihthu pen thu thuk ngaihsutna thak hitheilo ding, thupilna tuamtuam tetawh etciantheih zong hi mello; lungsim cidamlo a mawk paupau, pilna nei Corinth mite a dingin thupi lakluat ding zong om mello ci uhhi.

Paul’ thupuakte pen, dawibia te a ding haihuai mahmah khinta a, Jew mi tampite a dingin zong haivai semzaw tham hi. Jew mite’n Messiah dingin a lam-etpa uh Rome mite un khailumta maw? Messiah cihpen Rome mite’ khailup ding hilo in, tuate a lawnkhia dingin a lam-et uh hizaw hi.

Tuabang lian mahin, Paul in Corinth ah langdona tampi tuakkha hi. Tuateng bilbial kawmkal ah Jew’ kha leh Gentile’ kha khempeuh tungah lungdamthu in a gualzo dikdek hi.

Hihteng ah thupuak khat om hi. Bang langdona peuh phukha taleng, thumaan zakna ding lungsim kihong mi tampi Pasian in nei hi. Tua mite in Corinth bangin gitlohna sungah om mahtaleh; tua mite phak theihna dingin Pasian’ khutzat hihtheihna ding kisa in om ding hihang.
