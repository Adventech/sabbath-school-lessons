---
title:  Singlamteh Lungdamthu
date:   05/07/2026
---

Paul in Singlamteh lungdamthu pen eite tungah Pasian vangliatna cihi. “Singlam the tungah a kikhai Zeisu Christ’ sihna” pen Paul thuhilhna thubulpi ahi hi (1 Cor. 2: 2).

`1 Corinth. 1:17–31 simin. Paul in hiteng ah bang thupi sak hiam?`

1 Corinth. 1:18–31 sungah, Paul in mihing’ haina leh vantung’ pilna tehkaak hi. Singlamteh in mihing siatpen na leh Pasian’ hoihpen na hong lahkhia hi. 1 Cor. buppi sunga tengpen 1 Cor. 1: 17 bekin hong pulaak khia ziau thei hi. Christ’ singlamteh in vang neilo a kisakkhak lohna dingin (1 Cor. 1: 17), singlamteh thupuak in eite’ thuhilhna khempeuh sungah thulaigil hiden ding hi (1 Cor. 2: 2 etbeh in).

Paul in, amah pen tuiphumna pia ding hilo a, singlamteh thupuak hilh ding bekin hong kisawl cihi. Hih thu pulaakna in i kantel ding thu nih hongpia hi.

Amasa, Greekpau “sawl “ cihpen _apostellō_ hi a, apostle (sawltak) pan mahin hong kaakhia ahi hi. Tua hi a, Paul ading sawltak’ nasep bulpi taktak pen lungdamthu tangko mah hipah hi.

Anihna, Paul in tuiphumna thu a genna pen tuiphumna thupilo, thuhilhna zah in thupi zolo cihna zong hituanlo hi. A kituiphum mite in, amau tuiphumna a pia kuate cih a thupitsak luatna uh pen a taigawp hizaw a; amau kituiphumna taktak Zeisu nialna hizaw a cihna ahi hi.

“Thugen siamna pilna” (1 Cor. 1: 17) a cih ciangin, thugen siam peuhmah a lung-sim sunguh sia hi acihna zong hituanlo hi. Mihing’ pilna tawh Singlamteh thupuak peuh peengsak lohna ding acihna hizaw hi. Hihthu inbel Greco-Roman thugensiam dangnaalte acihna ahi hi. Athen ah Paul in tangthu, pilna leh thupilna cihte zang himah taleh gah gahpha mello lel hi. Tua ahih manin ‘Paul in bangmah ngaihsut mel a nei hetlo Corinth mite a matzawh na’ng nasepna dingin lampi dangkhat zuihsawm zaw ta hi.’ A buaihuai kinialna cihkhawngte pelhin ‘Corinth mite lakah, singlamteh tungah sihna thuak Zeisu longal bangmah kathei kei’ a cihbang in hihding khensat hi”—Ellen G. White, The Acts of the Apostles, p. 244.

`Singlamteh thupuak peuhmah semnen in zaibawl luatna in koibang in peeng zawsop sakthei hiam? Corinth mite tungah tangthu, pilna leh thu theihna tetawh Athen ah ahih sangin Corinth ah gahphazaw hi. Eite zong lungdamthu i tangkona dingin tangthu, thu pilna leh science pilna te in hong huhtheih hun a omthei ding hiam?`
