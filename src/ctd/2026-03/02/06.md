---
title:  Christ, Pasian’ Vangliatna leh Pilna
date:   09/07/2026
---

1 Corinth. 1:19, 20, 30, 31 sungah Paul in, Pasian’ pilna leh mihing’ pilna peuh mah kibang hetlo, a tuam vilvel ciat cihi. Theihhuai thu, tuabang pilna zong Paul in nial tuan samlo napi, Pasian’ pilna a demsawm pilna ahihleh nial cinten hi. Mihing’ pilna in mawhna sungpan mihing suahtak zolo hi. Pasian’ pilna Christ bekin hihna semthei hi.Anuai a sazian enin.

| Hotkhiatna ngah eite a dingin | Singlamteh thu in Pasian’vangliatna | 1Cor. 1:18 |
| Pasian hong sap mite a dingin | Christ in Pasian’ vangliatna | 1 Cor. 1: 24 |

1 Corinth. 1:18 leh 1 Corinth.1:24 tegel in Christ pen Pasian’ vangliatna hi a, mite a mawhna sung uhpan honkhia zo cih honglak hi. “A haihuaithu ci in a ngaihsut uh i thu hilhna a ummite Pasian in hotkhiatna apiazaw hi” (1 Cor. 1: 21) cihi. “Hotkhiatna ngah eite “ (1 Cor. 1: 18), “a um mite “ (1 Cor. 1: 21), “Pasian in a hong sap eite “ (1 Cor. 1: 24) cihteng khempeuh in a kibang vive uh hi a, upna tawh hotkhiat ngahte ahi uhhi. “Tua Christ’ lungdamthu in … thu ummi khempeuh a honkhiazo Pasian’ vangliatna ahi hi” (Rom. 1: 16).

Christ in Pasian’ vangliatna bek hilo a pilna zong hilai hi. Cihnopna ah, Pasian in tuathu tungtawn in mawhna buaina talsik a, tua buaina pen mihing pilna in a talsik zolo hi. Mihing te’n Pasian a theih zawhna dingin hih leitung pilna in hih zolo hi (1 Cor. 1; 21). Lehgen leng, Christ tungtawn in eite in hotkhiatna dingin mipil kisuak hi (2 Tim. 3: 15).

`1 Corinth. 1: 24–29 simin. A phawkhuai kammal pawlkhat “haihuai, thanem, vanglian, pil “ cihte in bang a deihna hiam?`

1 Cor. 1: 24-29 a sinte’n a phawk ding uh haihuai (haina), thanem (thanemna) cih te ahi hi. A deihna bulpi in, mihing’ pilna in singlamteh thupuak pen haina leh thanemna ci in ngaihsun cihi. Ahihhang, “Pasian’ haina pen mihingte’ pilna sangin sangzaw lai a, Pasian’ thanemna pen mihingte’ hatna sangin hatzaw lai hi” (1 Cor. 1: 25). Hihthu in Pasian thanem in hai hi cihna zong hilo a, Pasian’ vangliatna leh pilna in mihing dinmun khempeuh sangin nakpi in sangzaw tham cihna ahi hi.

`“Pasian in note hong sap laitak in, na omzia uh ngaihsun un. Leitung mite hong muhna panin note a tamzaw mipil, mi vanglian, mi thupi zong nahi kei uhhi” (1 Cor. 1: 26). Eite a dingin bang thupuak om hiam?`
