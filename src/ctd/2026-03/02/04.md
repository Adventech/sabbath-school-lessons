---
title:  Hotkhiatna Ngahte a ding Vangliatna
date:   07/07/2026
---

1 Cor. 1: 18 sunga thupuak in mikhat peuhmah lampialsak ngeingei ding hi. Sing lamteh cihpen a enpa etdan tawh kizui in khiatna nei hi. Pasian a langdo mite a dingin tua thu in haihuaina hi a, Ama’ hotkhiatna a lam-ente a dingin vangliatna ahi hi.

`Colossian 1: 20 leh 1 Peter 2: 24 simin. Zeisu in eite a dingin singlamteh tung ah bang hong hihsak hiam?`

Nungin i muhsa bangin, lungdamthu tangkona ah “thugen siamna pilna te in Christ’ singlamteh vangliatna hang kisalo ding” (1 Cor. 1: 17) cihi. 1 Cor. 1: 17 khuavak sungah, haihuaina pen Pasian’ vangliatna hizaw a, mihing’ pilna hilo (1 Cor. 1: 18) cih hong telsak hi. Mihing’ pilna tawh a kikelkii mahmah Singlamteh in, mihing’ pilna pen haihuai mahmah ahihlam hong lahkhia hi.

1 Cor. 1: 18 pen Greekpau in “sihna a thuak mite” cihpen amau’ gamtatna bangin a thuak uhhi cihi. Tua Laisiangtho munpen “Amau leh amau sihna a kitun mite a dingin singlamteh thupuak in haihuaina ahi hi” ci in kisimthei lai ding hi. Greekpau in apollymi (susia ding) cihpen 1 Cor. 1: 19 sungah “susia” ci in kiletkhia hi.

Hiteng ah bang teng piang hiam? Atang 18 sunga thute leh tua mite kisiatna tang 19 na pen Paul in Isa. 29: 14 sung a siksat ahi hi. Atang 19 ah, Pasian in suksiatna nungah omkhinkhian ahih manin, amau leh amau kisusia a cihtawh hong kilehbulh pian hi. Ahih hang kitukalh hetlo hi. A cihnopna pen amau leh amau ki suksiat ding a kisa peuhmah Pasian in susia ding hi cihna ahi hi.

Sihna thuakte cihtawh tehkaakna in “hotkhiatna ngah eite” (1 Cor. 1: 18) cihpen hotkhiatna peuhmah Pasian kiangpan bekin hong pai cihna ahi hi. Paul in hotkhiatna ngah eite acih ciangin, eimahmah kihonkhia tawm cihna hilo hi. Eimah kihonkhia tawm theilo hihang. Eima’ hotkhiatna in a pualam munkhat ah bulkip nei hi.

Suksiatna pen amau kihih tawm hi a, hotkhiatna ahihleh hong kipiak kul a, mawh nei mite tungah hehpihna silpiak ahi hi. Pasian in a um mite honkhia taktak cihpen 1 Cor. 1: 21 ah kitelcian mahmah hi. Haiahuaina cihpen bel, Christ’ singlamteh tungtawn in Pasian in mihing a ding hong piakkhong thupen nialna hi a (1 Cor. 1: 30), suksiatna sihna hong tun hi.

`“Mawhna thaman in sihna hi a, Topa Zeisu Christ tungtawn in Pasian hong silpiakkhong pen tawntung nuntakna ahi hi” (Rom. 6:23). Paul in 1 Cor. 1: 18, 19 sungah ama pulaksa khat koibang in pulak kikpha hiam?`
