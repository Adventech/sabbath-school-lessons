---
title:  Messiah Khat Kikhailum
date:   08/07/2026
---

“Jew mite in na lamdang kal uh a, Greek mite in pilna thupi sak uh” (1Cor. 1: 22) Paul in cihi. Singlamteh – Pasian geelna, Messiah, kikhailupna – pen Jew mite’n a kalh uh nalamdang hilo hi. Greek mite’n a deih uh pilna namte zong hibeek tuanlo hi. Mite lam-etna tawh kilehbulh gawp hi.

Nungzuite in a kikhailum Zeisu a saan daan uh pen, eite khempeuh in zong sim ding hang a (Maku 8: 31, 32; Maku 9: 30-32; Maku 10: 32-34), tua ngaihdan khempeuh in thutuampi khat ahihna leh Jew mite a ding bang pen kihhuaina ahihliang thu tel ding hi hang. Gengensa bangin Jew mite in Messiah cihpen Rome mite a zogawp dingin lam-en mahmah uh ahihhang; tuabang hong hibeeklo in, aneupen in galvai beek in zong zolo hi.

Zalom tampi sungmah ah Singlamteh pen Christian te a dingin upna limpua ahi hi. Zalom khatna a Christian mite a dingin, singlamteh tungah aki khailum Pasian cih limlim up-hak a sakmahmah uh pen; tu zalom sawmnihlehkhat Christian’ lungsim sungah ngaihsut haksa mahmah hi. Ahihhang, hihthu in lipkhaphuai lua ahih manin tuate pen a ciinhuai thu ci in ngaihsut huai hi.

A kikhailum Messiah lim tawh hotkhiatna ngiimna a kizawh dong semding cih vannuai buppi tungah lahkhiatna ahi hi. Tua a singlamteh mahmah leh a tungah a si Topa cihthu pen hih leitung ah mawhneite a dingin lamdanglua mahmah hi. Mawhneilo vanmi te nangawn in Topa Zeisu bia uh cih ngaihsun in.

`Sawl. 13: 16–47 (a diakin 26, 38, 47) simin. Singlamteh’ thu bang teng hong hilh hiam?`

Paul in lungdamthu hilh dingin Christ in kei hong sawl cihi.Tua ahih manin Paul in zong a kikhailum Messiah’ thupuak hilhpah hi (1 Cor. 1: 23). 1 Cor. 2: 1-5 sungah tua thu mah tomsuut kik hi. Sawltakpa in Christ’ thupiakna bangin citak hi. Lungdamthu tang kona ah zong “mipilte kam thukpite” zanglo hi (1 Cor. 2: 1). “Singlamteh tungah Zeisu Christ a sihna thu” (1 Cor. 2: 2) bek bulphuh hi. Paul’ kampau leh thupuak in “leitung mi pilte zat kammal te leh siamnate kazang kei a, Pasian’ Kha Siangtho vangliatna tawh thu kahong hilh hi” (1 Cor. 2: 4) cihi. “Mihingte pilna” pen muhtangpi mahin “Pasian’ vang- liatna” tawh kilehngat linlian hi (2 Cor. 2: 5).

`A kikhailum Messiah cihpeuhmah Jew mileh Greek mite’n lam-en vetlo uhhi. Eite’ lam-etna bangin Pasian in nasem hetlo cih hihthu in bangci hong hilh naci hiam? Eima’ lam-etna bangin nate hong pianloh ciangin, hih ngaihdan pom siam theihding banghang in thupi nasa hiam?`
