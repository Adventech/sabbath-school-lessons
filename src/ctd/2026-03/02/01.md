### Tukalsung Simding
1Cor. 1:17–31; Col. 1:20; 1Pet. 2:24; Sawl. 13:16–47; 1Cor. 2:1–5.

> <p>Kamngah</p>
> “Sihna a thuak mite a dingin singlamteh tungah Christ’ sihna thu pen a haihuai thu ahi hi. Ahi zongin hotkhiatna a ngah eite a dingin Pasian’ vangliatna ahi hi (1 Corinth. 1: 18).

Cicero, Rome te laigelh siam leh thugen siampa in, Rome mite in singlamteh thu peuhmah pen, mawhna daan piakna bangin lungsim sungah zong ngaihsut khakloh ding cih thuhilh ngei cihi. Tua Cicero in Zeisu’ suahma zalom lang lai in sikhin a hihhang, ama’ thu pulaakna tungtawn in Rome mite in singlamteh pen bangzah in hua uh cihna ahi hi. Lungsim sungah zong ngaihsun lo ding hiliang zen hi.

A kilehbulh in Paul in, “singlamteh thupuak in Pasian’ vangliatna” (1Cor. 1; 18) cihi. Paul a dingin Singlamteh in Pasian leh mihing kikal ah kilemtuahna (Eph. 2: 16; Col. 1: 20) hi a, Zeisu’ kiniamkhiatna lim lianbel (Phil. 2: 8), leiba lianpi a kilohna mun zong ahi hi (Col. 2: 14).

Tua singlamteh in Corinth mite’ buaina a dingin a dawnna ahi hi. 1 Corinth sung ah gamlapi zong pai kullo in Paul in a lungkham penpen thu in: pawlpi sungah pawl kikhenna hipen hi. Paul in a lungkham mahmah lam kamhonna sungah a genkhit (1Cor. 1: 1-3) leh lungdamkohna (1 Cor. 1: 4-9) khitlian ciangin hih thulu tawh kipansak pah hi (1 Cor. 1: 10-17).

Tukal sungin, singlamteh thupuak vangliatna in Corinth ah thupiang buaina teng khempeuh a ding a dawnna ahihna thu kisin ding hihang.
