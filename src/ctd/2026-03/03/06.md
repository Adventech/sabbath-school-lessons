---
title:  Singlamteh a Lehtaankik Nundan
date:   16/07/2026
---

Mihing makaite tungah kingalel in pawlneu kikhenlo ding cih ciangin, i makaite panpihlo ding cihna zong hituanlo hi. Pawlpi’ nasepna a makaihte peuhmah pahtawi in panpih ding hihang. Pasian in hih leitungah Ama’na semding in mihing mah aseh hi. Pawlpi makai a kicite’ nundan peuhmah in Singlamteh tungah a kipiakhiapa a sutnakleh; tuate thugenna pauhmah ngaihtaak in zuihtaak hi.

Tuabang a cihna thu in, Pasian’ thu a ngai ding mite a khemtuk ding vangliatna teng a lumletzo dingin singlamteh in vangliatna a neih hang ahi hi. Christ tawh akibang makaite in, amau’ nasepna gualzawhna uhpen Pasian hangbek hi ci uhhi. Zeisu in zong leitung ah Ama’ nasepna sungah Pasian’ min thangsak hi (John 17: 4).

Paul in, a citak Christian nasepna peuhmah; Singlamteh thu i cihcih thu tungah zungkha ding cihi. Singlamteh i cihpen Pasian in Ama’ hotkhiatna’ng pilna hong lahkhiat na ahi hi. Tua mahbang in, mihing’ pilna ahihleh haihuaina naci leltak hi. 1 Cor. 4: 1-13 sungah, Paul in Singlamteh koibang hi cih hong laktel hi. Amasa in, Christian makaite dinmun ding pen Pasian mah in hong ciangtanh sak hi (1Cor. 4: 1-5). Anihna, Christian’ nasepna maantaktak a ding ciaptehna in thuaksiatna hi (1Cor. 4: 9, 11-13) cihi. Hih a nihna zaw ahihleh, khantohsak huai mahmah hi.

`2Corinth 11: 23–28 leh Colossian 1: 24 simin. Christ a dingin thuaksiatna cih in bang a cihnopna ci hiam?`

Christian makaite in Zeisu’ khekhap zuihna tawh, amau’ Pasian’na sepna hangun amau’ sanggam pasal numei khatpeuh a ding sihna dongin a thuakngamte ahi uhhi. Paul in amah leh Apollos pen “thahna thuak dingin thukhenna ngah mite” (1Cor. 4: 9) kici hi. Amau mahmah pen gilkial dangtak, puansilh neilo, inn neilo, satthuak (1Cor. 4: 11) cihi. A ban zomin, amau pen simmawhna, bawlsiatna, gensiatna thuak uh a “mite muhna ah tuni ciangdong niinbulom leh leivuithem bang bek kahi uhhi” (1Cor. 4: 12, 13) cihi.

Corinth mite pen mihaute, kumpite, mipil misiamte nahi uhhi (1Cor. 4: 8, 10) ci in lehgen liang hi. Kiphatsakna in pawlpi sungah kikhenna piangsak thei ahih manin Christian makai dik takte sungah mun ngahlo hi Paul in cihi (1Cor. 4: 6).

`Pawlpi sungah nangma dinmun in bangpeuh hitaleh Christ a dingin bangzah nathuak khin hiam? Nangma dawnna sungpan bang pilna peuh kingah thei ding hiam?`
