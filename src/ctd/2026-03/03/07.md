Ellen G. White, “The Training of the Twelve,” pp. 17–24, in The Acts of the Apostles.

“Pasian’ thumaan upna sungah a kiplai a beibang mite’ ki pumkhatna in, amau’ neihsa thumaan in a kip vangliatna tawh kidim in, amau zong Pasian’ teeltuam mite hi uh cih pulaakna zong ahi hi. Amaute’ kipumkhatna in galte’ ngiimna geelna teng suksiatsak a, cikmah hunin omnawnlo sak hi. Tulai thumaan in, lungsim sungah umin, a nuntakpih te, Pasian’ mite suaksak in, a vanglian huzaap hong suak hi” —EGWhite, Testimonies for the Church, vol. 1, p. 327.

“Thumaan tausang tungah kipumkhat in a ding ding mite Pasian in makaih in mai nawtpih hi. Christ in zong leitung a ding hong kipiakhiatna tawh ‘tua mite pen mi tuam vilvel, gamtathoih gamtate’ suaksak hi. Hih siansuahna nasepna pen, pawlpi sungah a thumaanlote, pawl kikhenna leh kilemlohna te beisiangsak ding hi. Suksiat ding sangin lamphat zawk ding, amau’ maiah hong piang sepding khempeuh thasaan in semsak hi.

Pasian in Ama’ mite pen upna sungah kithukhat dingin a bawl ahi hi. Singlamteh madeuh in Christ’ thungetna ah zong, Amah leh Pa in aki pumkhat mahbang in, nung zuite inzong kipumkhat ding a, tua hileh Pa in Amah a sawlna leitung mite’n umthei ding cihi. Lungsim a sukha penpen hih thungetna in khang tawntung hong bansuksuk a, tuni dong mah ahi hi. ‘Hih mite a ding bek thu hong ngen kahikei a, amaute’ thugenna hangin kei a hong um mite a ding zong a hong ngetsak kahi hi’ (John 17: 20)”—Ellen G. White, Testimonies for the Church, vol. 4, p. 17.

**Kikupding Dotnate:**

`Zeisu in leitungah nasep azawh dong mah ki pumkhatna dingin thungen hi. “Amaute khempeuh pumkhat a suahtheihna ding …Nangmah in Kei nahong sawlna thu leitung mite in a uptheih na’ngun” (John 17:21–23) cihi. Christ sungah pumkhat na in; leitung hon dingin Pasian in Tapa sawl ahihna koibang in kipsak thei hiam? Pumkhatlohna in banghang in pawlpi a ding thaang hithei hiam?`

`1Cor. 4: 9-13 sim inla, hihteng ah nungzuite koibangin kipulaak hiam entel in. Hih nungzuite akipulaakna pen in; leitungmi te’n a pimuh makaite dinmun tawh koibang in kilamdang hiam? Pasian’ dinmun leh leitung dinmun koibang in ki lamdang ci hiam?`

`1Cor. 4: 16 sungah, Paul in, amah etteh dingin Corinth mite hanthawn hi. Mihing makaite na etteh nuam hiam? Makai khat etteh cihleh hunlo taaklopi in pahtawi mawkmawk cih koibang in kilamdang a, a lauhuai hiam?`
