---
title:  Pawlpi sungah Pawlneu Kikhenna
date:   12/07/2026
---

Paul in, “note lakah pawl (neu) kikhenna om khalo in, lungsim leh ngaihsutna ki thukhat sak un” (1Cor. 1: 10) cihthu in 1 Cor. 1 pan 4 na sungteng huamzo hi. Laisiang tho siam tampi takin tua kipumkhat in Paul’ laikhak khempeuh huam khin hi ci uhhi.

`1 Corinth. 1: 12–17 simin. Khuatual makaite tung kinga in pawlneu kikhen na in bul leh bal bangmah neilo cih hihmun teng in hong koici hilh naci hiam? Paul in koici siansuah hiam?`

Paul in, Corinth mite lakah kipumkhatna a omlohna pen kamkhauh mahmah tawh pulaak hi. Greek pau schisma (pawl khenna 1Cor. 1: 10) leh eris (kitot kiseelna 1Cor. 1: 11) cihte zang hi. Schisma pen noun hi a, (verb _schizō_ ‘khenkham’), laisiangthothak sung ah kithutuaklohna tawh pawl kikhenna thu a genna mun tampi ah om hi. Noun ahi eris (kitot kiseelna) zong, Christian te in zatloh dingin tamveipi pulaak hi.

`Rom. 1: 29; Rom. 13: 13; 1Corinth. 3: 3; 2Corinth. 12: 20 leh Gal. 5: 20 simin. Tua eris (kitot kiseelna) tawh bang mawhna teng kigualsuk lai hiam? Bangzah suksia ci hiam?`

Corinth mite kithutuah lohna teng a pualam ah hong kidawkkhia a, khatlehkhat thu kinei (w&m;pGJ) uhhi (1 Cor. 6: 1-3). Pawlpi mite thu kineihna tawh kisai Paul in “Note a dingin zumhuai mahmah hi” (1Cor. 6: 5) cihi. Topa’ nitak-an (nekkhop) nekna sung na ngawn ah a kithutuahlohna tenguh hemkhia hetlo uh cihi (1Cor. 11: 17-22).

Pawlpi mite lakah ki pumkhatlohna pen lauhuai mahmah a, Paul in zong lung hihmawhpih mahmah ahih manin; Corinth mite a laikhakna sungah zong, thubuai a gen masakpen mah hipah hi.

`1Corinth 1: 12–27 simkik in. Pawlneu kikhenna pen pawlpi ki thutuahna dingin a lauhuai mahmah thu ahihlam pulak in. Na khuatual pawlpi in tuabangte koibangin pelhna dingin bang hihhuai nasa hiam?`
