### Tukalsung Simding
1 Cor. 1:12–17; Rom. 1:29; 1Cor. 1:10; 1 Cor. 3:1–4; Phil. 2:5–8; 2 Cor. 11:23–28; Col. 1:24.

> <p>Kamngah</p>
> “Sanggamte aw, note khatlehkhat kithutuak in, pawl kikhenna omkha lo in, na lungsim uh, na ngaihsutna uh leh na ngiimna te uh nakikhatsak na dingun, i Topa Zeisu Christ min tawh ka hong thum hi” (1 Corinth. 1: 10).

Gamsa te enleng pawlkhatte in tam leh tawm leh alom, ahon, apawl in kikhawl uhhi. Ngia hon, dolphin ngahon leh miksi galkap hon cihbang in ahonpi in om uhhi. Tua banah Chimpanzee zawngte in zong a hon in ki-itna kip mahmah in a tawmpen 15 pan 150 dong bang hon omthei uhhi. Cihteihang, ahonpi mahin lung kituakkim zo tuanlo a khatveivei a kuhpite kilaai gawp thei uhhi.

Mihing zong tua ganno te mahbangin, hon kikhawlh bek thamlo, tua mihon lak mahah a kilaai gawp mah omthei veve hi. I pawlpi sungah zong tua mahbang ahi hi. A zahtakhuai makaipite sungah zong kipawlkhenna omthei veve hi. A suuksia zaw kanlai in honkhat leh honkhat kithutuak zolo lailai uhhi.Na pawlpi sungah tuabang namu ngei hiam?

Namuhkhak leh Paul in zong Corinth ah Mukhin talsik khin hi. Tukalsung in 1 Cor. 1-4 sung ending hang a, Paul in pawlpi sunga kinialna te Christ sungah ki pum khatna tawh gualzawhpih in a daihsakna teng entel ding hihang.
