---
title:  Christ bangin Sila’ Dinmun
date:   15/07/2026
---

`1Corinth 4: 1, 2 simin. Mikhat peuhin mihing makaite tungah muhdan maan a neihtheihna ding hiteng ah bang hong kihilh hiam?`

1Corinth 3: 1–4 sungah, Paul in khalam lohna hangin pawlkhenna om cih genkiau hi. Ahizong in tuathu a telkheh madeuh in “eite in Christ’ lungsim i nei uhhi” (1Cor. 2: 16) cihi. Hihthu inbel Christ’ ngaihdan leh tatdan acihna hihtuak pen hi. Lamkhat panin, thu ummi in “Christ’ lungsim” a neih ciangin Christ’ bangin ngaihsun gamta hi.

Hih lungsim pen nisim nundan sung khempeuh ah zat ding pen baihlo hi. Greco- Roman hunlai in, gam leh minam vai, thu pilna leh ngaihsutna, biakna lamah kidemna tamthei mahmah hi. Leitung ngeina tawh saan theihna ding deihna hangin Corinth pawlpi in leitung dinmun zuisak ta hi. Tuhun i pawlpi a dingin zong tuabang lauhuaina omthei ding hi.

`Philippi 2: 5–8 simin. Hihmun in “Christ’ lungsim” koibang in hong telcian sakthei naci hiam (1 Cor. 2: 16)?`

Corinth mahbang in Philippi ah zong pawlneu kikhenna mah (Phil. 2: 1-4) tawm kha mah na om veve hi. Phil. 2: 1-8 sungah, Christ bangin sila’ dinmun ah omna in, eima lunggulhna peuhmah sihsak ding; Zeisu bangin eileh eimah sangin midang thupha piak ding kalngiat hi.

Christ tawh akibang sila dinmun a omna pen Paul in “Christ’ nasemte” (1Cor. 4: 1) cihi. Hihthu in Christ a dingin tua mite in a huhte leh naseppihte acihna ahi hi. Mihing makaite peuhmah muhna maan taktak tawh muhtheihna dingpen Christ’ makaihna etteh in bulphuh ding cih kician mahmah hi.

Nasem silate peuhmah pen “kemcingte” (1 Cor. 4: 1, 2) zong kici hi. Kemcingpa cihpen midangkhat’ neihlehlam teng kemcing dingin muanhuai takin a ki-aap pa cihna ahi hi. Eite’n bangpeuh nei taleng, avekpi in Christ’ neihsa vive ahi hi.

`Thungetna tawh Philippi 2: 5–8 sunga thupuak pen lungngai dihin. Ama’ pumpi a kinial liang Pasian hong itna koibang in manthei ding ihi hiam? Tua itna mah eite’n zong i neihtheihna dingin; eima lunggulhna koibang in sisak thei ding ihi hiam?`
