---
title:  Zeisu Bulphuh
date:   13/07/2026
---

`1 Corinth 1:10 simin. Paul in “Lungsim, ngaihsutna leh ngiimna kithukhat” a cihpen bang a cihnopna naci hiam?`

Pawlneu kikhenna in Christ tungah mikhat’ cihtakna nialna ahi hi (1Cor. 1: 10). Pasian in, “Ama’ Tapa i Topa Zeisu tawh kikhawl dingin” (1Cor. 1: 9) hong sam hi. Eite’ Topa in Christ ahih manin Amah bulphuh ding hihang. A lungluthuai dotnate a dawnna ah “Christ kiphelsuah ahiam? Note a dingin Paul kikhai hiam? Paul min tawh kituiphum nahi uhhiam?” (1Cor. 1: 13) cihi. 1Cor. 1: 13 pen ‘hilo hi’, Christ kiphelsuah lo hi ci in dawnkikna ahi hi. Eite a ding a kikhaipa Christ hi. Eite in Pa’ amin, Tapa’ amin leh Kha Siangtho amin tawh i kituiphum uhhi (Mate 28: 19).

Paul in, eite pen “Christ’ pumpi nahi uh a, (mimalsim) in a pumpi na khat ciat nahi uhhi” (1sCor. 12: 27). Pumpi in na tampi aneih mahbang in, na khatsim in ama’ nasep ciat om a; pumpi khat mah hi vevelai hi. Pumpi in hoihtak in na aseptheih na’ng in, a na khempeuh in zong amau’ panmun ciat panin nasem ding uhhi. Hih thugentehna pen Paul in kithutuahloh sangin kithutuah ding a deihzawkna ahi hi. Ki batlohna tuamtuamte sungmah ah kithutuahna Paul in lam-en hi. Tua bek thamlo Paul in kibanglo mah taleng kithutuah veve ding lam-en hi.

Bangbang hitaleh, ngaihsutna leh ngaihdaan khempeuh i Topa Christ tungah aap ding hi. Christ in eite’ Topa anahih sese zong Paul a dingin belhmun nahi pah a, tuazong 1Cor. kamhonna (1 Cor. 1: 2, 7, 8, 9, 10) hipah hi. Paul in pawlneu kikhenna leh mihing makaite’ thu a genmaa in; eite in Zeisu Topa in nei ihihna pulaak masa hi. Pawlpi in mihing makaite’ bulphuh lo hi. Christiante in Zeisu bulphuh uhhi.

Zeisu in Topa ahihna pen 1Cor. a kipatna lamah pulaak khin a, tuamah in “Lung sim, ngaihsutna leh ngiimna kithukhat un” (1Cor. 1: 10) a cihpen hong telcian sak hi. “Ki thukhat “ a cihna, Greek pau in _katartizō_ pen; na khat peuhmah ama’ ommun ngei ah koihpha kik cihna ahi hi. Mihing makaite tungah kinga in pawlneu kikhenna hong om ciangin; pawlpi in ama’ dinmun ngei a tunkik dong puahphat kul a, tua mahmah zong angkhualna sisak in Christ sungah kithukhatna bek tawh piang thei hi.

`A beisa kum sawmsim lai in, Seventh-day Adventist pawlpi in Bible study pia ding small group ki thupisak mahmah hi. Pawlneu kikhenna leh small group cihin koibang in kilamdang hiam? Tua small group te pawl kikhenna a suahlohna ding koici kidawp kul hiam?`
