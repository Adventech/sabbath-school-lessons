---
title:  Pilna leh Khanghamna
date:   14/07/2026
---

Pawlneu kikhenna cihpen mihing makaite’ muhdaan tawh apiang tamzaw hi. Pawlpi kithutuahna leh pawlpi mite khalam cidamna a dingin lauhuaina hi a, Christiante Pasian’na sepna ah muhkhialhna tawh tua pawlpi makaite pimuh luatna hangin, thu um mite sukha thei hi. Tuabang gamtatna panin kidemna hong piangsak a, pawlpi zong kikhenna piangsak hi. Tuabek thamlo, eite in Christian ihihna ah mihing makaite bulphuh luatna tawh; eima’ nunna sungah Christ pen Ama’ dinmun panin a hemkhia kisuak thei hi.

`1Corinth 3: 1–4 simin. Corinth mite khangham lohna teng Paul in hiteng ah koibang in pulaak hiam?`

Kha lam a khangham thu umte in Pasian’ pilna kipahpih sak (1Cor. 2: 6, 7) a, tua in Kha Siangtho tungtawn in hong kizom sak (1Cor. 2: 13), tuhun khangthak pilna tawh hong enkaak siamsak (1Cor. 2: 6), mihing’ pilna (1Cor. 2: 13). Pasian pilna pen Christ’ Singlamteh sungah kihongkhia hi (1Cor. 2: 1-4). Tua sang a kitelzawlai in, Pasian’ pilna pen, Christ’ thuaksiatna, sihna leh thawhkikna sungah hong kilakkhia hi. Ki thukhat ding Paul thuumna a tomgen kikmaa in (1Cor. 3: 1-17) Paul in ama’ laisimte peuhmah in pilna diktak leh Christ sungah khanghamna neih ding phawksak nuam hi.

A pil a khangham Christian te ahihleh kha-mite hi a, cilehsa hilo, naungek hilo hi (1Cor. 3: 1). “Kha Siangtho a nei mite bekin Kha lam thute theithei” (1Cor. 2: 13, 14) ahih manin amaute un khalam thute pen khalamthu mahtawh enkak hi. Apil a khangham Christian te in, nawizu hilo, antak tak tawh kivak uh (1Cor. 3: 2; Heb. 5: 12) hi.

Thu ummi “nawi bek a nelaite pen, naungek ahih manun amaan leh amaanlo thu bangmah phawkthei nailo uhhi. Ahizong in antak icih pen, asia leh apha a khenthei a lim leh a limlo khenthei ta, khangham khinsa mite ahi uhhi” (Heb. 5: 13, 14). Apil a khang-ham Christian te in mihing tuamtuam hinapi un “kei Paul pawl; kei Apollos pawl” (1Cor. 3: 4) cilo uhhi.

Tua mihing tuamtuamte khempeuh pen “Pasian’na a semkhawm mite” (1Cor. 3: 9) ahi uhhi. Pawlpi khat in a om eite in Pasian’ lo leilet, innpi leh biakinn (1Cor. 3: 9, 16, 17) ihi uhhi. Christ tungtawn in Pasian tawh i kipawl uhhi (1Cor. 3: 11).

`Na pimuh mahmah khat hangin nakpi takin na lungkia dikdek ngei hiam? Tuabang nahih ngei khakleh, tua tungtawn in bang pilna sinhuai nasa hiam?`
