---
title:  Kibanglona panin Kipumkhatna ah
date:   03/08/2026
---

Khatbek hihna kammal pen 1Cor. 12: 4-6 sungah “Kha Siangtho a kibang, To a kibang leh Pasian a kibang” cihteng pen 1Cor. 12 sungah dimkeei hi. Paul in, Kha Siang tho akibang khatbek (1Cor. 12: 11), pumpi zong khatbek (1Cor. 12: 12), pumkhat (1Cor. 12: 12, 13, 20) hi a, Kha Siangtho khat (1 Cor. 12: 13 cikik leuleu), khatlehkhat ki thu tuakin kilemkim (1Cor. 12: 25) cihi.

Ki pumkhat ding deihna tawh kipawl in, Paul in, Christ’ pumpi sungah a kipawl “na tampi” (1Cor. 12: 12, 20) om a, “Ama’ sungah hong baptized a” (1Cor. 12: 13), “na khatbek neilo, nam tampi nei” (1Cor. 12: 14), ‘pumpi khempeuh’ (1Cor. 12: 17), ‘a mal tuamtuam’ (1Cor. 12: 18), ‘pumpi’ nate’ (1Cor. 12: 22, 23), ‘nate a vekpi’ (1Cor. 12: 26) cihi. Hihteng pen, kithutuahna leh kibanglona tungtawn in, khalam silpiakte’ a deihna in, a kibanglona panin kipumkhatna a hanthotna ahi hi.

Hih pumkhatna leh kibanglona in Pasian’ omzia pulaakkhia hi. Pa in zong khat, Tapa in a dang khat leh Kha Siangtho zong a dangkhat mah ahi uhhi. Tua mimal thum te khat ciat mahin nasemkhawm in pawlpi puahpha in nasep na’ng vangliatna guan hi (1 Cor. 12: 4-6; Ephet 4: 11-13).

`1Cor. 12: 12–31 simin. Na a tuamtuam te tampi anei pumpi tawh pawlpi leh a mite a genteh sese theihna in banghang hiam?`

1Cor. 12 sungah, deihna bulpi khat pen, pumpi tunga nate khempeuh in, khatciat in a omuh a, kibang hetlo (1Cor. 12: 15-20) uh hinapi, khatlehkhat kithutuah uh a, nasem khawm thei uhhi (1Cor. 12: 21-26). Khe in ama’ paina ding mun mit in ensak a, mit in bangmah lawng theilo ahihhang, khut bekin lawngthei hi. Nate pawlkhat in, na dangte sangin thanemzaw (1Cor. 12: 22), vangniamzaw deuh (1Cor. 12: 23) hitaleh, avekpi un a kisam vive mah ahi uhhi (1Cor. 12: 22).

A dahhuai thukhat, Corinth mite in silpiak pawlkhat pakta uhin pawlkhat donnop lohna lungsim nei uhhi. Corinth mite, tuabang mawhna pan kidaal theihna dingin, Paul in “a hoihpen lampi” (1Cor. 12: 31) ahi itna nei dingin sam hi. Thukhat panin, bang silpiak peuh hitaleh, itna tawh pilvang takin a kizat nakleh, Pasian lungdam sakna ahi hi.

`1Cor. 12: 8–10, 28; Rome 12: 6–8; leh Ephet 4: 11 sungah silpiak sazian enphakik in. Nang silpiak bang hiam? Christ’ pumpi koibang in puahphatsak ding nahi hiam?`
