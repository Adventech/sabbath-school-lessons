---
title:  Thuhilh Theihna Silpiak
date:   06/08/2026
---

Thuhilh theihna silpiak in khalam silpiakte’ thu Paul genna ah athupi dinmun khat ah om hi. Kamtuamtuam pautheihna silpiak a genmaa in, Pasian’ thu hilhtheihna silpiak genmasa hi (1Cor. 12: 10, 28; 1Cor. 13: 8). Kampau theihna silpiak gen masaleh thuhilh theihna silpiak tawh etkak ciangin; kampau theihna in thuhihlh theihna tawh akizopna ah ama’ thupitna kiamtuam ding cih a ngaihsutna ahi hi (1Cor. 14: 4, 5, 6, 22).

`Ephe 4: 11–13 leh 1Cor. 14: 3, 4 simin. Khalam silpiak teng a huamsa in, thuhilh siamna silpiak pen koibang in hilhcian tuam hiam?`

Thuhilh siamna silpiak in, puahphatna, hanthotna leh hehnepna ahi hi (1Cor. 14 : 3; Sawl. 15: 32). Thuhilhsiamna cihpen, mailam thu ding bekbek genkhol bek thamlo, tulai hunin zong nundan kipattah dan ding zong gen ding ahi hi. Greekpau in _prophēteuō_ pen, “thu khatpeuh genkhol” ahih keileh “mikhat tungtang thu gensak” cihna ahi hi.

Etpak theih dingin, telcian masak dingin Sawltak 2: 29-31 (Amos 3: 7) sungah David pen kamsang khat ahihna tawh “mukhol, thei khol”

cihi. Telcian ding nihna pen Sawl. 15: 32 sungah Judas leh Silas tegel zong kamsang mah nakici hi. Amau thuhilhna uhtawh thu ummite tawh sauveipi thu kikum in, amaute’ lungsim thumaan sakin thahatsak hi.

Ephet 4:11–13 sungah, nungzuite’ hun sungteng peuhmah khalam silpiak tawlnga ngeilo hi. A tawp dong mahin kipsuak ding hilel hi (Sawl. 2:39). Mi khatpeuh in kamsang kahi a cihleh Laisiangtho siksan in amah mah kisittel hen. A zaipi in genleng thukhun 4 om ding hi.

Amasa, ama’ thugen hong tung ding hi (Thkna. 18: 22; Jer. 28: 8, 9). Anihna, ama thugen te in kamsang dangte genna tawh kithutuak ding hi (Thkna. 13: 1–3; Isa. 8: 20). Athumna, ama’ nisim nundan pen Christ tungah ki-aap ding hi (1John 4: 1–3). Alina, kamsang zuaute pen amau’ gamtatna tawh kithei ding Zeisu in (Mate 7: 15–20) cihi: kamsang maante zong tua mahbang hong hiding hi.

Kha Siangtho silpiak in hun nunung a beibaang pawlpi’ dinmun lahna hi Mang muhna laibu in cihi (Mang. 12: 17; Mang. 19: 10). Ei Seventh-day Adventist te in, tua khalam silpiakte pen Ellen G. White tungah kipia a, ama’ laigelhte in pulaak hi ci in um hi hang.

`Ellen G. White’ kamsang silpiak pen, banghang in eite’n umthei ihi uhhiam? Ama’ maapanna leh thu neihna tawh kisai dot ding bang omtheilai hiam?`
