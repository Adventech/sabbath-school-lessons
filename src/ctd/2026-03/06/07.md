Roswell F. Cottrell, “Spiritual Gifts,” pp. 5–16, in Ellen G. White, Spiritual Gifts, vol. 1.

“Mite in Topa muangin nasem dingin kuankhia uhhen. Tua hipeuhleh Pasian in amaute ompihpah ding hi. Khate ngahpih ding a kikheelna omsak ding hi. Nasem mi khat in thuhilh siampa, adang khatin laigelh siampa, adang khat in lungsim hoih deihsakna taktawh thungenpa leh adang khat in lasa siampa cihbang hithei ding hi. Midang khatin zong Pasian’ thu gensiamna neithei ding hi.

Tua silpiak malkhat simin Pasian’ vangliatna hongsuak ding a, Amah in a nasem pa panpih ding hi. Pasian in mikhat tungah kampau siamna, adangkhat tungah pilna leh a dangkhat upna pia hi. Ahihhang a vekpi un lutang khat nuai ah nasem khawm ahi uhhi. Silpiak tuamtuamte in sepzia tuamtuam te piangsak himah taleh, “septheihna ding siamna a hong pia Pasian akibang ahi hi” (1Cor. 12: 6).

“Eima silpiak neusa in kuamah’n simmawh kei hen. Nasem in kuankhia un. Na lian semthei ke’ng ci in uplohna tawh kuamah’n mawk khutzep in omkei uhhen. Nang mah ki en kei in. Na makaipa en in. Kiniamkhiatna, lungsimtakpi leh itna tawh nahih theihzah semin”—EGWhite, in The Advent Review and Sabbath Herald, April 12, 1906.

“Eimah mimalsim in midangte lungsim tawh panpihna kisam hihang. Pasian in ei’ lungsim bek hilo, mi’ lungsim sung peuhah nasem zawsop thei ding hi. Mimal tuam tuam tungah silpiakte in, “Pasian’na sem ding, Christ’ pumpi puahpha dingin, misiang thote kisakhol sak ding hi” (Ephet 4: 12).

“Eite’ maiah haksatna omden ding ahihtei hang, i makaipa zui cinten in, khutkilen ki pumkhat in tua haksatna te maingat ni”—EGWhite, The Upward Look, p. 141.

**Kikupding Dotnate:**

`Khalam silpiak tawm ngaihsunlai in. A kiletkhialo kampau sangin banghang in thuhilhna thupizaw hiam? A kisapleh, Paul’ hilhcianna na matkik theihna’ng in 1Corinth 14 simkik in.`

`EGWhite’ nundan leh a nasepna teng kikum unla, i pawlpi in banghang in amah khalam silpiak ngah takpi ci in umthei ihi hiam? Hih silpiak panin pawlpi in bang thupha saanlawh ihi hiam? Tua silpiakte zatsiamna ding bang tona peuh om hiam?`

`Nang hong ittaktak mi li leh nga khawng gualsuk in. Nang hong itna in itna taktak ahih nakoici theih hiam? Khalam silpiakte kikupna ah Paul in ki itna thu hizahta in a genna thu koibang in hong hilhcian naci hiam?`

`Itna thupi mahtaleh, thumaan a gen mikhat ngaihtaakcing ahihna khentel theih na dingpen hihbek banghang in hilo thei ding hiam?`
