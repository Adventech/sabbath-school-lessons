---
title:  “A Hoihpen Lampi”
date:   04/08/2026
---

“Itna cihpen silpiak tampi lakah khat hiziau lo hi. Tua silpiakte khempeuh in amau’ ngiimna mun a tun hamtang theihna lampi ahi hi”—Carl P. Cosaert, “1 Corinthians,” Andrews Bible Commentary: New Testament (Berrien Springs, MI: Andrews University Press, 2022), p. 1643.

`1Cor. 13: 1–7 leh 1Peter 4: 8–11 simin. Kha silpiakte tawh kisai in itna in bang dinmun ah maapang hiam?`

Corinth masa 13 in khalam silpiak peuhmah itna tungtawn in a kilawm bangin kizang thei hi cih hong hilhcian hi. 1Cor. 13 a kipatna ah Paul in 1Cor. 12 sunga silpiakte siksan in, itna a om keileh tuate khempeuh in manneilo ahihna pulaak phadiak hi. Tua hi a, pilna (1Cor. 12: 8) leh upna (1Cor. 12: 9), “mualte a kinsak zo” upna (1Cor. 13: 2) in zong, itna omkei peuhleh a mawkna ahi hi (1Cor. 13: 2) cihi. Upna omlo in pau tuam tampi pautheihna (1Cor. 12: 10, 28, 30) in “a bil-aphuai zam leh daaltuah kitum tawh ki bang lel” (1Cor. 13: 1) cihi. Genkholna silpiak te thupitna zong itna om keileh tuamah bang ahi uhhi (1Cor. 13: 2).

1Cor. 13: 4-7 sungah, itna neihna leh neihlohna teng bulphuh leuleu a; itna in bangteng hih a, bangteng hihlo cih ahi hi. Itna a kilangsak dingin a kammal zatte pen, eima ngahna lam hizawlo in, eima’ hih ding lam vive hizaw hi. Tua hi a, itna neite in (1) lungduai (2) migi (3) thumaan sungah lungdam (4) hehhak thuakzo (5) na khempeuh um (6) nakhempeuh lam-en (7) lungduai takin ngak hi.

A kilehbulh in itna in (1) hazatna lungsim neilo (2) kisatheilo (3) kiphasaklo (4) heh pakpaklo (5) angsung khuallo (6) a kilawmlo gamtalo (7) mawhna ciamtehlo (8) thumaanlote ah lungdam ngeilo hi.

Hiteng ah gamtatna 15 tengin, tua silpiakte a kilawm takin zattheihhna dingin kiptakin lampi hong lah hi. Adiak in, itna taktawh kipawlte kikupna in 1Cor. 12 leh 14 sungteng ah om uh hi a, tualai munnih sungah Paul in khalam silpiakte tawh kipawl kinial na omthei teng a hilhcianna ahi hi. Khalam silpiakte peuhmah pilna tawh zatsiamna ding pen itna in a thupi pen ahi hi. Itna pen upna leh lam-etna tawh liangko kikim in a kikoih hi a, “tua thum sungpan in itna lianpen hi” (1Cor.13: 13).

`Banghang in upna sungah itna in thubulpi hithei sese hiam? Pasian hong itna bang i neihtheihna dingin, thungetna tawh khuavak lengkik sakna sangin lampi hoihzaw bangdang omlai hiam?`
