---
title:  Pau Tuamtuam Silpiak
date:   05/08/2026
---

Pau tuamtuam te silpiak tawh kisai in, Laisiangtho sungah tua silpiakte alim aliim honglahna (Maku 16:17; Sawl. 2: 1–13; Sawl. 10: 44–48; Sawl. 19: 6) sungah, 1Cor. 13 sungah pau tuamtuam silpiak sungah gamdangpau pautheihna siamna pen khalam silpiak taktak ahihlam telgen mahmah hi.

Kam tuamtuam pautheihna silpiak pen Paul in 1Cor. 12: 8-10 (1Cor. 12:28, 30; 1Cor. 13: 1, 8 enkak) sungah pulaak hi. 1Cor. 14 sungah zong genphapha lai hi. Greek pau in _glōssa_ “kam” pen 1Cor. 12-14 sungah sawmnih vei sang tamzaw om a; 1Cor. 14 sung bekah zong sawmlehnga vei tak om hi. Tua banah, Greek pau mah _heteroglōssos_ “pautuam” zong 1Cor. 14: 21 sungah om hi. Hih nambat sang penin kamtuam pauna tawh kisai Paul lunghimawh cih kipsakna khat hipah hi. Hih khalam silpiak pen zatkhialh na leh simmawhna in Corinth pawlpi sung mipi biakpiakna ah buaina tuamtuam piangsak hi (1 Cor. 14:23, 27, 33, 40).

`1Cor. 14: 5, 13, 26, 27 leh 1Cor. 12: 10, 30 simin. Pautuam silpiak tawh kisai Paul in bang thu hilhcianna tuam neilai hiam?`

Kampau tuamtuamte pautheihna silpiak pen tua pautuamte’ khiatna zong gentel theihna pilna tawh tonkhawm ding (1Cor. 14: 9) a: tua ahihkei ngalleh tua silpiak zatna in phattuamna neilo (1Cor. 14: 6) hi. Hiteng ah Paul in a khiatna gentheihna leh telcianna thupisak hi. Kamtuamtuam pautheihna a gensiasia hizawlo in, genkholnate nangawn a awlmawh lozo Corinth mite’ lungsim puakzia kilanglua sa ahih manin a gen ahihlam zing ciangin mutel tading hihang.

Hiteng ah, Corinth mite khempeuh in gamdang pau pauthei lehuh cih lunggulhna a neihna zong phawkhuai (1Cor. 14: 5) a; ahihhang tuabang piang dingin lam-et tuaklo hi (1Cor. 12: 10).

“Kha Siangtho sungah kituiphum ding ngetmaa in, pautuamtuam pautheih kul cih ngaihsutna pen, 1Cor. 12 leh 14 sunga Paul’ hilhcianna teng palsatna ahi hi”—Raoul Dederen, Handbook of Seventh-day Adventist Theology, electronic ed., vol. 12 of Commentary Reference Series (Hagerstown, MD: Review and Herald Publishing Association, 2001), p. 620.

`Na pawlpi sungah kamtuam pauthei a omkha hiam? Christ a dingin midang te a vaphak na’ngin koibang in zangthei uhhiam? Hihthu in Paul hong hilhcian kamtuam pauna tawh kisai koibang in hong telcian sak hiam?`
