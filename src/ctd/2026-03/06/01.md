### Tukalsung Simding
1 Cor. 12; Eph. 4: 11–13; 1 Cor. 13; 1 Pet. 4: 8–11; 1 Cor. 14: 27; Amos 3: 7.

> <p>Kamngah</p>
> “Na deih lianpen uh ki itna mah hisak un. Kha Siangtho hong piak 	siamna a tuamtuam te sung panin, thuhilh siamna ding na hanciam 	pha penpen un” (1Cor. 14:1).

Mihing’ pumpi mahbang in, pawlpi in zong na tampi kigawmkhawm hi a, na khat sim in akibanglo nasep khatciat leh siamna khat ciat nei uhhi. Tua silpiakte pen itna tawh a kizat ciangin, tua silpiakte in Thumgawm Pasian tawh kipawl theihna lungtang hong guan hi.

Tukal sungin 1Cor. 12-14 sunga kha silpiak thuhilhna teng entel ding hihang. Hi tengpen biakna sunga Christian hihna sungah amau’ lam-et gamtatna (1Cor. 11-14) pan in tawmno khat kisin ding hihang. Paul’ thu bulphuhpi penin kithutuah lohna tawh buaina teng ahi hi.

Hih thubuaina teng vengsakna dingin; pawlpi in pumpikhat hi a, nasep tuamtuam a nei na tampi kihel in “Christ’ pumpi a khansak theihna ding” (Ephet 4: 12) deihna ahi hi. Tom gen leng, ‘a kibanglo nate panin, ki pumkhatna hong tungsak thei Topa Pasian in a pawlpi tungah kha silpiakte hong pia hi.

A taktak in cileng, Paul in 1Cor. 1-4 sunga pawlneu kikhenna a gengen teng a lungsim sungah tenglai a; a dawnna pen, pawlpi mite lakah Christ sungah kipumkhatna leh kithutuahna a neihloh uh hang ahi hi. Tu in Paul in, khalam silpiakte tawh kisai in ama’ telna bangbang pulaak in, tuathu mahmah khangto sakngiat hi. Paul a dingin, pawl neuneu kikhenna pelh theihna ding pen Christ leh Kha sungah kipumkhatna bek ahi hi.
