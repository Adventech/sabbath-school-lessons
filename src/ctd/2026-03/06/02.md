---
title:  Silpiak Tuamtuamte
date:   02/08/2026
---

1 Cor. 12: 1 sungah Paul in, thulu thak khat hong pulaak leuleu a, “tu in tua tawh kisai in” cihi. Greek pau in _tōn pneumatikōn_ cihpen “Khalam Silpiakte leh kha mite” namnih in kiletkhia thei ahih manin: Laisiangtho thukante in Paul gennop pen “khalam silpiakte maw, kha mite maw” cih kikum zelzel uhhi. 1Cor. 12: 4 sungah Paul in ‘Kha silpiak’ ci sitset ahih manin, Paul in khalam silpiak gennuam hihtuak zaw tham hi.

1Cor. 12: 2, 3 sungah Zeisu in Topa hi ci in hangsan takin pulaakna pen Kha Siangtho silpiak a masapen hi Paul in cihi. Laisiangthothak hunlai in Zeisu in Topa hi cih ding pen Caesar in Topa hilo hi cihna zong ahi hi (Sawl. 17: 7; John 19: 12, 13). Tuapen a simtham in kumpipa langdo ding hanthotna hi pah a, sihdaan piaktaak hipah hi.

Zeisu leh Paul in, bawlsiatna lak sihlawh theihna ding mun ah zong Pasian sungah upna pen Kha silpiak hi ci in pahtawi veve hi. Upna pen silpiak bulpi taktak ahi hi. 1Cor. 13: 13 sungah zong upna mah amasapen in gual hi. 1Cor. 12: 9 sungah upna pen khalam silpiak hi cih Paul in telgen mahmah hi. Cihtei hang, silpiak dang tampitak zong omlai hi. Kha Siangtho in a kibanglo silpiak tampitak mah hawmkhia a, a kisam bangbang in “Ama’ deihna bangin mimalkim tungah” (1Cor. 12: 11) hongpia hi.

`1Cor. 12: 1–6. Hihmun in bang bulphuh pen hiam?`

Hiteng ah “a tuamtuam” acih in silpiak namtampi cihna ahi hi. 1Cor. 12: 1 sunga “khalam silpiakte” ci in Paul in a minvawh pen a tang 4 pan 6 sungah muhdan namthum tawh telgen kik a: “silpiak” (_charisma_), “Pasian’na sepna” (_diakonia_), leh “gamtatna” (_energēma_) ahi uhhi. Hih kammal tengin khiatna tuamtuam a neihtei uh hang, Laisiang tho in hoihtakin a gawmtuahsa ahih manin sittel khentel zong kul tuanlo hi.

Khalam silpiak peuhmah in, thumgawm Pasian’ omzia siksan in kipumkhat theih nading in hong kipia ahi hi (Ephet 4: 8-11). Kha Siangtho in thu ummite silpiak a piakuh ciangin, thu ummite lakmah ah Christ a dingin na a semkhia sak pen Pasian mah hileuleu hi (1Cor. 12: 5, 6). Thu ummite zong a mimal in silpiak kipia ciat (1Cor, 12: 11) ahih hang, thu ummite khempeuh kigawmkhawm in phattuamna piangsak hi.

`Paul in kipumkhatna a bulphuhna phawk kikin. Banghang in pawlpi a dingin thupi thei hiam?`
