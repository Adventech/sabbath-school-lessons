title:  Tuiphum John in Mi Kigingsak
date:   08/10/2026
---

`Maku 1:2-5 simin. Tuiphum John nasepna in bang ngiimna bulpi nei hiam?`

Maku lungdamthu in Tuiphum John hong piankhiatna pen Isaiah’ genkholna a tangtunna ahi hi (Isa. 40:3). John in a neu panin a picindong a khantohna teng pen Pasian tungah cihtakna vive ahi hi. Gamlak a John’ kikona aw a za Satan in, mihing tun- gah ama ukzawhna a mansuah ding laumahmah hi (EGWhite, The Desire of Ages, p. 224). John’ thungaite’ gitlohna teng kipholak ahih manin Satan patau hi. Israel tungah Satan’ ukzawh na in lauhthawn in omta hi. John cihtakna nundan leh Messiah a muan- na teng kiamsak nuam in Satan in hahkat niloh hinapi guallel lel- tak hi.

`Mate 11:1-6 simin. John thong sungah a omlaitak in John’ upna Zeisu in koi bang in kipsak kik hiam?`

Zeisu mawhbawl sakding in Satan in zolzolo ahih manin thangpai mahmah hi. Tuaciang in a nasepdan honglaih a, Zeisu’ sanggampa John pen thong sungah koihta hi. John thongkiatna in Zeisu sukha mahmah hitaleh Satan in mawhbawl dingin zolzo peuh mah tuanlo hi.

Tuabang in John a suksiatna tawh Zeisu’ lungsim sungah vangikpi khat guan hi. John in Zeisu kiangpan thapiakna kammal zakha hi. John in ama’ dinmun lauhuai kawmkal ah, Zeisu vangli- an mahmah hinapi ama’ mailam ding bangmah kitelcianlo a hih hang; sihna dong mahin citak giugeu hi.

Tanglai kamsangte mahbang in, eite zong, kisikkikna, ci- htakna thupuak, Christ’ dikna cihte tangko dingin hong kisam hi (Mang. 14:6-12). John tungah ahih mahbang in Satan in eite zong, Pasian’na sepna sungah eima nunna mahmah a patauh huai dingin hong bawl ding hi.

Numei gilo khat leh Pasal ginalo khat gamtatna hangin thong sungah thahlupna a thuak John mahbang in; eima nunna zong hong hoihmelloh hangin, lungkiat luat ding zong hilo a, Pasian hong makaihna phawkkikkik ding hizaw hi. Mihing in bangbang in hong hih tamahleh, upna tawh Pasian baan peuhleng, Amah’n hong dawng ding hi. Ama’ deihna bang hihzo dingin hong kam- ciam khin hi (Sawl. 1: 8). Tulai Christian leh Christian lote lakah, eima dinmun in koibang peuh ahi zongin Christ a dingin thumaan teci pang ding hihang.

`John thong sung a om bangin, nang zong na telzawhlohpi in thusia natuak kha ngei hiam? Koibang in Pasian um veve in muang veve thei nahi hiam?`
