title:  Kamsang Nasepna ah Kisam
date:   09/10/2026
---

Pasian in a hunzui leh a deihna khat tawh kamsangte sam- zelzel hi. Tua hun a ding in amaute na om zel uhhi. Hihthu tawh kisai in SDA pawlpi in kamsang silpiak a ngah laisiangtho a gelh- lo EGWhite kici kamsang khat um uhhi (mailam ah sintoto ding). Daniel 8: 14 bulphuh in “Ni tulnih leh zathum khit ciangin biakin- npi ki siansuah ding” acih pen bulphuh in, Millerites kici Miller’ nungzui Adventist te in Zeisu hong pai ding a lam-et khit deuh ciangin, Ellen Harmon (White) in amah akisapna masapen 1844 kum December kha in ngah hi.

Ahihhang Christ hong pailoh ciangin Miller’ nungzuite in tangthu sungah a nasia penpen Lungkiatna Lianpi khat hong tuak- kha uhhi. Tuahang mahin Pasian in Ellen Harmon tungah mang- muhna masapen apiakna hipah hi. Tua sunga thupuakpi pen in amah mah in, letkhial mah tase leh uh, a lungkia thu um mite thapiakna’ng mah hipah hi. Hih mangmuhna in hih leitung pan vantung Jerusalem zuatna lampi lahna hipah hi. Tua lampi tungah Adventist thu ummite in Khuapi Siangtho zuanin khualzin in pai uhhi. Amau un “Zeisu mitsuan gige peuh lehuh, amau’ mai ah pai gige a, khuapi lamah makaih ahih man in, amaute bit mahmah uhhi” EGWhite, Spiritual Gifts, vol. 2, p. 31.

Hih mangmuhna pen lungdamthu ahi hi. Hih buaina tawh lungkiatna sungpan in Pasian in khantohna thak khat piangsak zaw ding hita hi. Zeisu in zong amau tawh om khawm hi. Amah hong paikik ta ding hi. Amah in hotkhiatna lampi tungah makaih gige hi. Amau un Topa “mitsuan gige” ding hibek hi. Hibang buaina leh lungkiatna sungah amau’ lam-etna mansuahna’ng thu bangmah omsak tuanlo hi.

`Tuabang in EGWhite kici, kum 17 apha khangnonu pen, kamsang nasep sem ding in kisamkhia hi. A kipatcil in bel baihlam hetlo hi. Hih silpiak pen citak siangtho in a ki saansak hang, EG- White in bel Pasian’ kamtaai khat hih ding patau mahmah lai hi. Pasian’ kamsang khat hihna pen baih hetlo leh vangnei luakhollo lel hi. Tua pipi mah EGWhite in Pasian’ sapna sungah koibang in ki-aap khathei hiam?`

Maikal ciangin, Laisiangtholui kamsangte phutkhak tona leh a nasepte uh i kisin ding uhhi. Tuate lakpan pawlkhat pen EG- White nunna leh a nasepna sungah hong kidawk ding a, mai Fri- day ciangin kisin kik ding hihang.
