---
title:  Isaiah, Lungdamthu Kamsangpa
date:   06/10/2026
---

Isaiah pen Laisiangtholui lai a kamsanglian khat hi a, Pasian in a maitang sappah ahihi (Isa. 6:1-8). Pasian’ liatna leh siangtho- na tawh a kituam ciangin, ama’ gitnatlohna hoh phawk zawsop hi. Tua laitak in vanmi seraph khat van biaknatau pan mei-aam khat tawi in hong leng a, Isaiah’ mawhna teng sawpsiangsak hi. Tua ciangin Pasian in “Kua sawl ding ka hiam, Kote a dingin kua pai ding hiam” ci in dong hi. Isaiah dawnthukna in ei a dingin zong hoih hi “Hiah om ing Topa, kei hong sawl in” (Isa. 6:8) cihi.

Isaiah a kisapna pen Israel’ tangthu sungah a thupipen mah hipah hi. Ama’ gitnat lohna lakmah ah Israel leh makaite’ mawhna hangin a vau denna pen thupi hi. Isaiah’ genkholna te in, Pasian omzia, mihing omzia leh hotkhiatna ngiimna hong pulaakna teng laisiangtho sungpan khuavak hong musak hi. A vanglianpen kam- sanna khat pen, Zeisu hong sihna bek hilo, tua sihna in bangteng hong hihsak cihpen Isaiah 53 sungah omsuk sitset hi.

`Isaiah 53 simin. A lamdang hih genkholna pen singlam- teh masiah kum 500 laipek khawng in kigelh hinapi, eite ki- angah Christ’ sihna in bang hong hihsak cih koibang in hong pulak hiam? Hih kamsanna in kamsanna silpiak vai bangteng hong telciansak thei hiam?`

Isaiah’ genkholna in Messiah’ deihna leh a mission pulak hi (Isa. 53:3-5). Christ pen “gangote kiangah tuuno a kipaipih bang”

(Isa. 53:7) leh, Ama’ kipiakkhiatna in Amite’ mawhna pan a hot- khiatna thu hong hilhcian hi. Eite aiawh in Christ’ hong sihna pen mihing a dingin mawhthoihna bulpi ahihlam hong lak hi.

“Eite khempeuh in tuu bangin lam i khial khin a; eite khempeuh eima sanah i paikhin hi. Topa in, eite khempeuh’ mawhnate Ama’ tungah a suan hi” (Isa. 53:6). Mawhna omzia tak- tak hiteng ah hong kilang hi; Singlamteh omzia, mawh kithoihna zong hong lak hi. Laisiang tho alui athak mundangte banglo in hotkhiatna’ng ngiimna pen kitel sinsen in hong lak hi.

Isaiah a kisapna pen thubaih hi vetlo hi. Mite in amau’ kisi- atna ding leh mawhna sulzui kigenkhol limlim pen, khiatna nei- lo bangin ngaihsun leltak uhhi (Rom. 6: 23). Ama hunlai Isaiah’ kamsaanna aw leh tuhun zong kibang lel hi (Isa. 2:4, Isa. 5:20). Mipi lam panin thumaan gamtat ding leh kipuahphat ding a nialna un Jerusalem kisuksiatna tung sak hi. Tua mawhna mahin, Christ hong paini ciangin leitung leh a sung teng migilote kisiatna tun ding hi.

`Isaiah 53 enkik inla, Zeisu in ei a dingin hong hihsak khempeuh ciamteh in. Kamsangpa in Christ’ sihna tungtawn in hong lahna panin bang lam-etna na neihlawh thei hiam?`
