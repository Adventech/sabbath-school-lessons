---
title:  Elijah’ Thupuak
date:   05/10/2026
---

`1 Kumpite 18:19-39 simin. Kamsanna in koici naseptheih cih hiteng pan na mu hiam?`

Elijah’ a kisapna leh a kampaudan in Pasian’ thupuakpa din- mun hong lak hi. Israel lampialna khawlsak dingin kumpi Ahab kiangah Pasian in Elijah sawl in kumtampi keugawt ding thu vapuaksak hi. Amite un milimte nusia in thu a mankik theihna dingun Pasian in hih haksatna zang hi. Kumpi leh gambup in kam- sang Elijah mawhpaih uh a, thahlup ding nangawn sawm uhhi.

Carmel mual ah, Elijah in Baal leh Asherah kamsangte tawh kitalsik hi. Pasian’ thupuakpa in Kha tawh kidim in, Israel te milim leh pasiante biakna pan Topa kiang ki lehkik dingin sam hi. Mipi in zong amau teelna bangin minam bup lampial ahih lamuh theitel uhhi. Tu in Pasian leh Elijah pen amau’ pasian lelte a om

zong ommello pasiante sang in lianzaw cih ahilh ciangin, hong pom kiknuam ta uhhi.

Christ nihveina hong paikik madeuh in, Elijah thupuak tang- ko ding leh, mipite in amau’ milimte nusia in Topa’ nilianpi ni ah a dingtang sak dingin Pasian in hong sawl hi. Pasian in Ama’ teelsa nate zangin Amite hong hopih den hi. Ama’ kammal leh Kha sil- piak in hanthotna, thu hilhna, ngaihsut piakna leh thapiakna hong neisak hi.

“Eite in khanlawhna maantak neihtheih ding kisampha pen hihang. I zon masak pen ding nasep zong hipah hi. Pasian thupha saantheihna dingin kuhkal takin hanciam zong kulzek a, Pasian in hong piak noploh man hizawlo in, a sangthei dingin eimah kiging nailozaw hihang” Ellen G. White, Selected Messages, book 1, p. 121.

`Malachi 4:5, 6 simin. Pasian’ nung a zui mite Elijah thupuak in koibangin huzaap hiam?`

Khanlawhna maan taktak pen lungsim sung leh innsung pan kipan hi. Innkuan cih in pawlpi a dingin kuamah’n a nialthei- hloh innkiusuang ahi hi. Innsung ah khanlawhna in pawlpi sung, vengsung leh minam sungah vantung ki itna kizeelsak hi. Chris- tian inn kuan a dingin Pasian’ geelna in tuamah ahi hi. Khanlawh- na in mission nasem dingin Pasian’ mite’n thangahsak hi.

`Tuni in eima innsung, lungtang sung leh pawlpi sungah, bang milim biakna peuh a omthei ding hiam?`
