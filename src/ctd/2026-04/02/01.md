### Tukalsung Simding
Pian. 15:6, Gal. 3:6-9, 1 Kumpi. 18:19-39, Isaiah 53, Dan. 2:46-48, Maku 1:2-5, Mate 11:1-6.

> <p>Kamngah</p>
> Tua ciangin, “kua sawl ding a, eite a dingin kua vapai ding ahi hiam” Topa cihna aw kaza hi: Tua ciangin ke’n “Hiah om ing, kei hong sawl in” ka ci hi (Isaiah 6:8).

Laisiangtho hunlai in, kamsangte in Pasian’ mite’ tangthu sungah dinmun thupi ah ding uhhi. Khatveivei tausang laizang ah, vangnei takin, sihlehnun ahihkei buangleh mipi laizang ah ding uhhi. Cihtei hang kamsang khat hih ding pen a haksa sapna khat ahi hi. Amau nundante uh baih hetlo uh a, dahna, nialna, telkhialh- na leh lauhthawnna tawh kidim uhhi.

Pasian’ kamsangte peuhmah in kamsang dingin sapna peuh- mah sangpak hetlo uh hi. Lamdang hetkei e. Kamsang tampitak mah khauhtak in dona thuak a, amau thupuak a sangding mite in

nialzaw uhhi. Pasian kamsangte pen ki awlmawhlo, ki simmawh, kisaat in thong ah kikhum zawsop hi. Pawlkhat gamlak ah sabet in kibeng in, suangtawh kideng bekthamlo thahthuak zong om uhhi.

Tuhun ciangin gitlohna, huaihamna, zualzangna leh suksiat- na tawh a kidim hun hi a, Satan huzaap nuai ah ki om ahih manin kamsangte’ aw peuhmah kinial in ki awlmawh lolel hi. Pasian’ kamsangte leh kamtaaite tungtawn in lam-etna hong kipia ahih manin kamsangte’ thu mangding in hong kisam hi.

Nungkal in, Eden ah pukna khit ciangin, Pasian in mihing tawh kizopna phutkik dingin a nasepna kisin khin hihang.

Tukal ahihleh a puksa leitung a dingin Pasian in Ama’ kammal omsa bangin, Ama’ mite a puahphat kikna dingin kamsangte a sapna thu kisin ding hihang.
