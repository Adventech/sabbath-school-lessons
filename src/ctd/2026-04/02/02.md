---
title:  Abraham, Thuciamna Hubitpa
date:   04/10/2026
---

Piancil pan Mangmuhna dongmah, thumang in Pasian’ he- hpihna a dawngthuk a beibaang mite om kinken den hi (Jer. 31:7; 31-34). Gentehna pakin, Babel tausang pan kikhen khiatkhit kum honkhat khit ciangin; Pasian in Abram (Abraham) pen, ama’ inn- kuan bek hilo, a tulehta khang a dingin thuciamna tungah a ci- takpa ci in teelkhia hi. Ama’ kimlehpam ah lungsim gitnatlohna tampi om kawmkal ah, Abraham in Pasian’ sapna sungah citak in om hi (Pian. 12:1-9).

Pasian in thuciamna sunga kammalte Abraham hilhcian a, Christ hong kipiakkhiat na tungtawn in mihing ki honkhia ding cih hilhcian hi (Pian. 15:6-17). Abraham’ upna sittel na’ng leh Pa- sian’ Tapa hong kipiakna lahna dingin; Abraham in Pasian kian- gah a tapa Isaac piakhia dingin kisam hi (Pian. 22:1-17). A citak pa a dingin a cimtakhuai lua sittelna khatmah hipah hi. Abraham in Pasian’ thu amanloh theihna’ng paulam dingteng geelsak lecin, Abraham in bel mangveve hi. Isaac thahlupna ding Abraham khut pen Pasian in letcipsak in tuutal khat piakhia zaw hi.

Kamsang khempeuh zong hong tungding lauhuaina pulaak dingin kisam hikhinlo hi. Cihtei hang mipite cihtakna lamah puah- pha ding leh, lampial lohna’ng thupia ding, mizawng cimawhte nenniamna leh thuciam palsatna pan thu hilh dingin kisam uh ahi hi. A thupipen in Messiah sungah hotkhiatna kamciam pulaakkhia dingin kisam uh hipen hi.

Laisaingtho kamsangte teci panna leh sawltak hun kheng tuhun ciangin leitung ah hong tung ding lauhuainate Khasiangtho in hong hilhna ombang in midangte hilhsawn ding ihi hi. Tua thuhilhna sungah a laigilpi in a citak Zeisu’ mite kitatkhiatna lung- damthu hi a, thuciamna sungah kiptak in a omte bekmah hiding hi (Mang. 12:17).

`Pian. 15:6, Galati 3:6-9 leh Heb. 11:8 simin. Mimaimai khat mah hinapi, Abraham’ nundan leh tatdan in Pasian’ nasem dingin a kilawmna na koicimuh hiam?`

Abraham hun leh ei’ hun a kibanna ciangkhat zong omhi. Abraham hunin milim biakna in a huzaap mahbang in; tuni eite’ hun in zong Christian biakna hepkhiat theihna ding leitunglam mipilte in nakpitak in nasem uhhi. Tuabang thute kawmkal ah, Pasian’ mite in midangte tawh kikhawlhna ah Pasian’ limmeel kidawksak ding a, vantungmi thum te tangko thupuakte zong tangko dingin hong kisam hi (Mang. 14:6-12).

`Laisiangtho sungah Abraham in “Topa um ahih manin; tua upna pen dikna in kisimsak” (Pian. 15:6) cihi. Hiteng ah van- gneitak in hong pulaak lungdamthu kamciamna na koici muh hiam? Bang lam-etna na neihlawh thei hiam?`
