---
title:  Daniel, a Citak Kamsangpa
date:   07/10/2026
---

Christian a tamzaw in Daniel 1 sung a, cidamna thukhun ombang in amah ding tang sitset ahihna theikhin uhhi. Daniel pen

cihtakna ah ettehcingpa ahi hi (Dan. 1:8). Kumpi an limmahmah te a kipiak ciangin, tehlehgah leh tui bek bulphuh in nebek ding a, kumpipa annek sabuai tung ate nelo dingin khensat hi (Dan. 1:12-16). Hih mahmah pen Daniel 6 sunga bangin cihtakna in sih- na dong hong tunthei, nunglam ciangin hong tung ding sittelna neucik khat hipan hi.

`Dan. 2:46-48; Dan. 4:18; Dan. 5:13, 14; leh Dan. 6:25-27 simin. Hih munteng in Daniel in a kiimlehpam a om milimbia te lakah koibang in tecipang cih hong lak hiam?`

Daniel’ Pasian’ vangliatna tawh kisai tua milimbiate teci panna en ni. A tangthu uhbel a tuamvive a hihteihang thukhun thupi khat bek om a: i nunna, i cihtakna leh eima sungah Pasian in na hong sepna tawh, midangte in vantung Pasian in “pasiante’ Pasian, kumpite Topa” ahihna hong muhpih thei hi.

Daniel’ genkhol thubulpi in thukhenna hi a, Christ nihveina hong paikik ding thu tawh thukhup hi. Daniel’ min khiatna mah- mah pen “Pasian in keima’ thukhenpa” ahih tawh zong kizawi- tawn lel hi. Ama’ laigelhte pen kuhkal takin simleng tuni tuhun tawh ki tuak mahmah hi. Daniel genkholna in, nuntaksung ah na neucik khat hiphial mahleh pilvang takin Pasian thukham kepcin ding hong phawksak hi.

Kamsang khat ahihna tawh, Daniel in minamte hong kahtohna kiamsukna pen Pasian’ thu manlohna sulzui ahihlam zong hong laklai hi. Kamsaanna mang sungpan Daniel in Pasian’ ngiimna leh Ama’ gam gualzawhna thu pulak hi (Dan. 7:13, 14).

Daniel 2 enpah leng, Christ pianma kum 500 laipek in a gelh ahi hi. Daniel in hih gamlian 4 teng hong khantoh leh kiamsuk hun ding leh tuhun ei hun zong milimpi’ khe peek bangin, Europe gamteng omdan a genkholsa bangin “tua kumpi gamte in kigawm ding hanciam in zilehta in kiman ding uh a; ahih hangin sik leh buan a kimat theihloh mahbang in, amau te a kigawm theikei ding uhhi” (Dan. 2:43).

Europe tawh kisai in tuni dong mah, kiteenzauna tawh kipumkhat sawm mahmah napi un, a gambup mahun thukham tuamtek ngeina tuamtek mah tawh om uhhi. Soviet Union a puk madeuh khawngin zong, thukan siampen te’n zong pukna ding peuhmah mu khalo uh a, tua mahmah in Pasian’ kamsangpa Dan- iel a muanhuaina thukipkho khat hipah hi.

`Daniel 2 sunga gam khempeuh a kigenkholsa bang in hong khangto in paikhia lel uhhi. Hong pailai ding Pasian’ gam thu a genkholna teng a muanhuaina bang hong gen naci hiam (Dan. 2:44)?`
