---
title:  Sawltakte leh Kamsangte
date:   19/10/2026
---

Laisiangthothak in kamsangte leh sawltakte’ nasep pulak- sak hi. Sawltakte pen “kamtaaite/ Pasian kiangpan a kisawlte” cihbang hi a, Greek pau in apostolos ahi hi. Lungdamthu sungah “sawltakte” pen nungzui 12 teng hiphadiak hi. Laisiangthothak a mundang tengin Christ’ nungzui tengbek hilo in amaulo zong pawlkhat hellai hi.

Paul in ama’ laikhak sungah amah mah 12 vei sawltak kici hi. Rom. 1: 1, 2 sungah Paul in sawltak ding amah akisapna leh ama’ thuhilhna tengpen laisiangtholui kamsangte thupuak tawh kizomsak hi. Paul in amahngiat pen Gentile mite a ding sawltak kici ngiat hi (Rom. 11:13). “Pasian deihna tawh kizui” in hong koih hi cihi (1 Cor. 1:1).

Pate panin tate tungah cihbang in a kiguizui siampite tawh kibanglo in, kamsang leh sawltak peuhmah Pasian in Ama’ deihna tawh kizui in sehngiat hi. Paul in ama’ sawl- tak dinmun kinial- na hong om ciangin, amah a kisapna tawh amah kidal hi (1 Cor. 9:1, 2). Paul in ama’ sawltak dinmun pen Pawlpi’ bulpi ahi Christ’ nasepna tungah kilam cih mu sitset hi Eph. 2:20). Pasian hong pulaakkhiatte a ngahpa hihna tawh, sawltakpa in kam- sangte hu- muapna teng mah tawh kikimna nei hi (Eph. 3:1-5).

2 Peter 3:1, 2 sungah Peter in, kamsangte pen maitaaite leh a kammal uh a thupite ci a, tu in zong sawltakte in tua thuneih- na mah pua gige uh kici hi. Hih munteng ah lai- siangtholui hun tengmah siksat pi in kinei hi. Kamsangte in laisiangtholui hunlai a Pasian’ kamtaaite a nahih mahbang un; sawltakte in zong lai- siangthothak hun a, Pasian’ kamtaai te mah hi uh a; pawlkhat in genkholna te nei in kamsang silpiak zong ngah uh hinapi’n; sawl- tak hilo citciat uhhi.

Kamsaanna silpiak zong pen laisiangthothak hun sungin nung-zuite tungbek ah ciangtan in kikoih hituanlo hi. Kamsang khempeuh zong sawltak hikhin tuanlo hi (Sawl. 15:32).

`Sawltakte leh kamsangte in Ephet 2: 20 leh Eph. 3: 5 sungtawh kizui in, Christ leh pawlpi tawh koibang in kizopna nei uh hiam?`

Laisiangthothak sungah sawltakte gelhna om bangin, amau’ gelh khempeuh pen Zeisu bulphuh ahihlam thei khin hihang. Thu dangkhat panin, Zeisu nunna, sihna, thawh- kikna, siampilian sep- na leh hong kumkikna tengmah kibulphuh ngiat hi.

`Sawltakte leh kamsangte un Zeisu’ nundan, Anasepna kiptak in ciaptehna pen laisiangtho sung bekah kimu thei hi. Eima upna ahi, Zeisu in eite aiawh in hong sihna tawh kisai in lai- siangtho bekmah in thuneihna sangpen neithei ding cih koi- bang in hong talcian sak hiam?`
