---
title:  Laisiangthothak Kamsangte Nasepte
date:   20/10/2026
---

`1 Cor. 14:3; Ephet 4:11-13 leh Mangmuhna 22: 6 in lai- siangtho thak kam- sangte nasep te koibang in hong hilhcian hiam?`

A tunga muntengin, hong huhthei mahmah ahihang, lai- siangthothak kamsangte nasep teng a zaipi in hong lak hetlo hi. Amau sep a tamzaw pen laisiangtholui kamsangte septeng khawngmah hi a, tua silpiak kizomsuaksak hi.

1Cor. 12:10 sungah genkholna pen a dang kha silpiak in pulaak hi. Christ’ pumpi tawh gentehna sungah (1 Cor. 12:27, 28), kamsangte in zong Pasian thu pulaakna ngahte mah hinapi, sawl- takte khit ciangin kigualsuk pan hi. 1Cor. 14: 3 a bang hileh: “Pa- sian’ thu a gente pen mite tungah gen hi a, hanthotna, hehnepna apia ahi hi” cihi.

Ephet 4: 11-13 sungah 1 Cor. 12 mah bangkhalam silpiak honpi khatmah kigual suk diudeu hi. Kamsangte pen sawltakte lakah ki pulaak a, thugente, pastor te leh laihilh siate (Eph. 4:11) cihbangin kigualsuk hi. Hih mite in “hih nasep a tuamtuam te khempeuh sem dingin, Pasian’ mi khempeuh a kigingkhol sak in, Christ a ding nasem in, Christ’ pumpi a khansak theihna ding deihna ahi hi” (Eph. 4:12, 13). Hihteng ah leh muntuam tuam ah zong Zeisu pen amau’ thupuak bulpi mah ahi hi.

Sawltak laibu in kamsang nihte’ Judas leh Silas thu genkhia a (khatbeek in laibu gelh khalo), ‘hanthotna thu tampitak tawh amaute’ lungsim thanuam sakin thahatsak uhhi’ Acts 15 :32). Hun khatkhat ciangin kamsangte in vauna kampeuh pauziaulel napi; hun khat ciangin vauna khauh peuh zong zangthei uhhi. Amau zong a hunzui in nasem, thapiakna kam, sit telna hunin, a za mite in zaknophuai sa uh a, lam-etna leh kamciam a om kam-malte tawh thapia thei uhhi.

Thapiakna nasepna zong kamsanna sungah a kihelmah hiveve hi (1Cor. 14:31). Haksat hun leh bawlsiatna hunsung ah kamsanna silpiakin hanthotna leh lam-etna bulpi mah ahi hi. Mangmuhna 22: 6 sungah Zeisu pen “a siangtho kamsangte’ Topa Pasian” leh “athu in citak in thumaan” cihi.

Pasian’ nasemte kiangpan hong paikhia khat peuhpeuh pen, Pasian a dingin a gen- khiat uh hi a “citak in thumaan” hi. A muan- huai mahmahlo hih leitung ah, Topa nasem kamsangte thugen pen muanhuai a, eite a dingin vangliatna tawpna a neipa sungah omna in eima thuneihna lianpen ahi hi.
