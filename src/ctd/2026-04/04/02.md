---
title:  Zeisu zong Kamsang Khat
date:   18/10/2026
---

Moses in “Topa na Pasian un note’ lak ahi na sanggamte uh lakpan in note a dingin kei tawh akibang kamsang khat hong khangsak ding hi. Note in ama’ thu nangai ding uh hi” (Thkna. 18:15) ci in genkhol hi. Tua kamsang kua aitam? Laisiangtho mah in kician takin Zeisu mah kawkpah hi. “Moses in kei a hongsawl bangin, note sungmah panin note a ding kamsang khat Topa na Pa- sian un hong sawl ding hi. Ama’ hong thugen khempeuh namang ding uhhi” (Sawl. 3:22 leh John 6:14).

`Zeisu in bang kamsang nasep sem hiam? Hih dotna pen Zeisu’ hunlai mahin koibang in kidawng hiam? (Mate 16:13- 16; Maku 8:27-29; Luka 9:18-20).`

Zeisu in Christ, Messiah, laisiangtholui kamsang khempeuh in a kawkpen uh ahi hi. Laisiangthothak sungah Zeisu pen Messi- ah thu genkholna a picinna in kiciamteh hi. Luka 24: 13-35 sun- gah, Zeisu in Amah pen Messiah thu genkholna in a kawkpen kahi hi cihi. John 1: 1-5 sung a kigenteng ahihleh, mihing maimai khat, mihing kamsang khat peuh tawh kituakzo hetlo hi.

Thu pen Piangsakpa hi ci a “na khempeuh Amah tawh piang hi a, Amah lotawh a piang khatbeek omlo” (John 1: 3) cihi. A kip- iangsak khempeuh Ama’ piansak hi a, vannuai bup khuavak kum billion 93 bang zai hi.

Piancil 1 sunga “Pasian in ci” a cihna khempeuh a Zeisu kampau za hihang. “Khuavak om tahen” (Pian. 1: 3) a cipa pen hotkhiat na’ng ngiimna (John 1:4, 5) hangin “mite tungah khua- vak” (John 1: 4) hi a, Amah in leitung khuamial hawlkhia hi.

Pasian genkhol thute in thumaan in kipkho hi. A thumangte a dingin “Note lungsim sungah zingsol hongsuak in khua a hong- vak mateng in khuamial lakah meitang bangin ngaihsun un” (2 Pet. 1:19). Genkhol thute in, tua thute hong kipatna a bulpi ahi “Zingsang Aksi” Zeisu in Ama’ gamsungah hong saanni dong hih mawhna kidim leitungah khuavak hong piaden ding hi.

Tuani ciangdong mahin Topa’ nunna, sihna leh a thawhki- kna tungtawn in Christ in tawntung nuntakna hong pia gige hi. Dotna khatbek omthei a, sangding maw sanglo ding cih hibek hi.

`Zeisu in Piangsakpa cih lungngai kawmsa in (John 1:1-3), sin- glamteh tungah nang a ding hong sihsakpa zong hi cih zong phawklai in. Pasian hong itna a om takpina koibang in hong hilh naci hiam?`
