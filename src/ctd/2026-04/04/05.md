---
title:  Numei kamsangte leh Pawlpikem Sianeute ki- pawlna
date:   21/10/2026
---

`Luka 2:36-38 leh Sawl. 21: 9; 21:9. Numei kamsangte thu bangteng hong hilh hiam?`

Laisiangtholui hunlai in, Pasianin Ama’ kamtaai dingin pasal numei samlel hi. Laisiangthothak hun leuleu ciangin nu- mei kamsang om ahihna zong tecikip om veve hi. Anna in lai- siangthothak sungah numei kamsang masapen ahi hi. Tuanu in zong a nuntak sung bup kipiakhia in “asun azan in Pasian bia in, antang in thu a ngenngen mikhat ahi hi. Pasian tungah lungdamna ko in, Ama’thu a gen hi” (Luka 2:37, 38).

Sawl. 21: 9 sim lecin Philip in “nungak siangtho kamsang 4 nei” cihi. Laisiang- tho thak tangthupu Eusebius in, amau teenna mun ding leh amau kiphumna mun ding khawng a naipen in hong theisak hi. Kamsanna silpiak a ngahkha numei khangno honkhat zong kimu hi.

Amau un bang gen cihlian kitheihsaklo ahihhang; a kithei- hsak khat ahihleh hih numei khangnote in kamsanna silpiak nei a: tua kamsanna silpiakte pen Laisiangtho sungah kikoihlo cih kithei hi. Amaute zong kamsang dangte mahbang in, kamsang maante hi uh a; amau’ kammalte amau’ laigelhte pen laisiangtho sungah a kikoihlo hi bek hi.

Numei kamsangte tawh kisai in pawlpikem khangno siate in sawltakte tawh kinai takin semkhawm kha uhhi. Sawltakte in Topa nasepna sungah makai khangnote puahpha hi. Paul mahmah in Timothy a pulaakna ah “upna sungah tapa taktak” (1Tim. 1:2; 1 Tim. 4:12) cihi.

Paul in Pastor nasep zia a bulpi teng Timothy hilh hi. Ama’ sapna vai hibang in gen hi: “Kata Timothy aw, tanglaipek a kip- anin nangbang mite a dingin a kigensa thutawh kizui in nangtun- gah athu kong aap hi. Hoihtakin gal nadozawhna dingin galhiam in tua thute zangin” (1 Tim. 1:18).

Titus 1:4 sungah zong, Paul in Titus pen “kata taktak Titus” cihi. Paul in Titus pen Crete ah thu ummite makai dingte teel din- gin vaipuak pia hi. Khangno Titus tungah hih vaipuakpi a puak zawhna’ng in khalam ah ngaihsutna a piakna ah “Hih thute genin, mite taai in na hanthot ciangin, na aana neihteng khempeuh zang- kawm in. Mite’ sim- mawh in omkei in” (Titus 2:15) cih genbeh lai hi.

`Khangno, khangham, pasal, numei—i vekpi mah in Pasian’na sepna sung ah vaipuak kineithei ciat hi. Nang vaipuak bang hi a, a hoihzaw sem dingin na koici hih ding hiam?`
