---
title:  Zeisu leh AThu Laigilsak
date:   23/10/2026
---

I sinsa uh bangin, Pasian in kamsangte amau hunciat in sam in nasep thupi guan hi. Amau nasep bulpi in mihingte Pasian thei- sak kik in Amah tawh kikhawlkik sak ding ahi hi. Hihthu mahpen EGWhite in hong makaih dan hilimlim hi. Ama’ nasep in namnih in kikhen theilai hi: Mite Zeisu kiang paipih in Ama’ Thu Lai- siangtho lam kawk hi.

EGWhite in a damsung teng Zeisu veii mahmah in, mi- dangte kiangah Pasian’ itna hawm sawn nuam mahmah hi. A minthang vangvang ama’ laibu bu nga Khang tawntung Kidona in Pasian in ki-itna hi cihtawh kamhong in kamkhup kik hi. Christ kiang zuatna cihzong pen minthang veve a, Hehpihna sungah Khantohna, i lungnopna, i kizat theihna cihte khempeuh pen Christ tawh kipawltheihna tungah kinga hi.

Tua zong pen Amah tawh nisim, naisim kizom in Ama sun- gah belhna hang hi a, Ama’ hehpihna sungah hong khangto sak hi. “Eite’ upna hong patsakpa leh hong zawhpih pa zong ahi hi” Steps to Christ, p. 69. 1890 kum kiim khawngin Kumcin Lungdeih laibu pen ama’ nisim ciaptehna laibu (diary) sungah gelh hi.

“Christ’ nuntakna thu kagelhlai in, ka lungsim hong sukha mahmah hi. Ka husan ding nangawn mangngilh liang ing. Christ in leitung a dingin a thuakna teng kangaihsut ciangin thuakhak kasalua hi” Manuscript 70, 1897. EGWhite in Zeisu’ thu gen tha- nuam mahmah hi.

Laisiangtho pen ama’ bulphuh nihna mah hipah hi. Ama’ laigelhte pen “Khuavak neuzaw” hi a, mihingte “khuavak golzaw” laisiangtho ah makaih cihi (Selected Messages, book 3, p. 29). Pasian in Ama’ hong itna omzia hong pulaak denna leh hot- khiatna ngiimna omna laisiangtho sim ding amah’n hanthawn den hi. A nasepcil laipek in a gengen khat pen “Ka laigelh a sim mite aw, Pasian’ Thu pen note’ upna leh gamtatna thukhun hisak ta un”—Selected Messages, book 3, p. 30.

Kum tawmno khit ciangin zong: “Topa in na laisiangtho uh hong simsak nuam hi. Ama’ Thu khengzo dingin khuavak dang peuhmah hong behlapsak lo hi. Ama’ Thu a ne kha te a dingin a kha’ nuntakna sisan ahi ding hi. Tua ciangin gamtat hoihnate in khuamial sungah a taang meivak bang hong hiding hi” —Letter 130, 1901.

“EGWhite in Christ a ding kamsang a hikha peuhmah in “Christ hilhbang in hilh ding” ci in um hi. Topa ngiat in a genkhiat sate, Ama mimal bek hilo, laisiangtholui sunga kamsangte leh sia te khempeuh zong a cihnopna hi. Mihing’ thuhilhna a hihleh hem- khia ziau uhhi” —The Desire of Ages, p. 826.

EGWhite in Zeisu leh laisiangtho peuhmah pen Christian nundan a ding in a laigil ah koih hi. Mai Friday ciangin, amah’n mangmuhna leh humuapna a muhdan kisin ding hihang.
