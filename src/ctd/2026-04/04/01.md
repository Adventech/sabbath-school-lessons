### Tukalsung Simding
Mate 16:13-16; Eph. 2:20; Mang. 22:6; Luka 2:36-38; Sawl. 21:9; Eph. 6:10-18.

> <p>Kamngah</p>
> “Siamna a tuamtuam a om hangin, hongpia Kha Siangtho khat ahi hi. Nasepna nam tuamtuam om ahi- hhang in, Topa akibang ahi hi. Nasepna ding siamna a nam tuamtuam a om hangin, septheihna ding siamna a hongpia Pasian a kibang ahi hi” (1Cor. 12:4-6).

A pa in mawtaw a hawlhawl laitak in, a tapa in a pa’ maw- taw tawh a kibangteng ciamteh ding in enthapai hi. Apa in maw- taw pailai khat kawk in “kata aw hia mawtaw pen maw kaneih masakpen mawtaw tawh a kibang linlian hiven” cihi. Inn a tun uh ciang un, a pa in a mawtaw neihmasak pen ii neihna laibu leh tu in a neihpen ii neihna laibu tegel enkaak hi.

Mawtaw khat tunga omteng leh a dangkhat tung a om a kibang teng leh a kikheel teng telhilh mahmah hi. A pata un engine nihtegel a etkaak uh ciangun a vekpi un kibang uhhi. A kikheel tampi a omhang, engine kikheel lo hi.

Laisiangtholui leh thak hunlai in Pasian in kamsangte tung- tawn in hong hopih a; tuamah a kizopsuakna leh a kithukimna pen Khasiangtho – a bulpi, a engine, vantung humuapna in a omngei mahin om gige lai hi.

Kha Siangtho in a tawntung in kamsang maante khalam silpiak tawh thapia tawn tung hi. Amah in Christ pen kamsangte thupuak’ laigil in koih a, tua zong Hebrew laibu in kitelcian sak mahmah hi (Heb. 1:1-3).

Tukal sungteng in laisiangtho thak sunga kamsangna sil- piakte entel ding hang a, kamsangte in bangteng hih, kua in kam- sang sem, kamsang leh sawltak a kitawikhaina leh kamsang zu- aute a lauhuaina sintel ding hihang.
