---
title:  Laisiangthothak sunga Kamsangzuaute
date:   22/10/2026
---

`Mate 24:23, 24 simin. Hiteng ah Zeisu in bangthu hong vau hiam? Hih vauna banghang in thupi sehhuai thei nasa hiam?`

Zeisu ngiat in kitelcian takin a genkhiatna ah: “Christ a ki- neihkhemte leh kamsang kineihkhemte hongpiang ding a, ateel- sate nangawn a khemna dingun lim leh nalamdang lianpipite bawl ding uhhi” (Mate 24:24). Hong kikhemna ding Topa hong hilhk- holte in mihuup mahmah a, “na lamdang lianpipite” kihel ding hi.

Leitung ngeina khengvalte in bangzahta in hong huup theiphial mahleh: tuate in Pasian’ nasepna hi kicipah thei tuanlo hi. Leitung ngeina khengval galte nei hihang. Paul in zong: “eite dopen leitung mite hilo a, vantung huihlak a om ukna aana neite, hih leitung khuamial a uk a tuamtuamte ahi uhhi” (Eph. 6:12). Hih vangliatnate in kamsangzuaute tungtawn in nasem hi.

Kamsangzuaute cih zong thuthak hituanlo hi. Lai- siangthothak in kamsangzuaute cihpen laisiangtholui hunlai te mah gen uhhi (Luka 6:26). Laisiangthothak in kamsang zuau pen “tuu-vun silh a sungah gitlohna a dim ngiate” (Mate 7:15) hi a; “amau’gah tawh kithei ” (Mate 7:16) hi. Amau’ nasep bulpipen zong khem ding vive hi (Mate 7:17- 20). Zeisu ngiat in “kamsang- zuaute hong piang ding uh a, mi tampi khem ding uhhi” (Mate 24:11); ahih theihleh “a teelsate nangawn khem ding” (Mate 24:24; Maku 13:22; 1 John 4:1) cihi.

`Ephet 6:10-18 sungbang hileh, khemna khat peuhpeuh panin koibang in kidaal zothei ding ihi hiam?`

Upna tawh nungta in “thumaan awmdal silh” cihtakna kawnggak gak in, thunget na tawh Kha Siangtho tungah ki-aap; Ama’ Thu sungah zungkha peuh leng, khalam leh laisiangtho thu- maan lampan hong pialpihthei hiteng pan hong kembit thei ding hi.

`Hih leitung khemna tawh a zuutgawppa kiangpan kidaal za- whna dingin nangmah bang galhiam tawh kituamthei ding nahiam?`
