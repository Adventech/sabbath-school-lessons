`Ephet 4:11-14 simin. Banghang in Pasian in hih silpiakte a pawlpi pia hiam? Kamsanna silpiak in pawlpi a ding Pasian’ geelna koibang in picin thei hiam?`

Khalam silpiakte’a deihna in pawlpi phuhna, lungdamthu tangkona ahi hi. Paul in kamsang silpiak sungah “Kamsangte” pen khenli in khenkhia hi. Silpiak dangte khatvei ta bek kihel hi. Kamsanna a kihelzah et ciangin khalam silpiak sungah kamsanna mah thu pipen kicithei hi. Kamsangte dinmun tuamtuam pawlkhat en ni:

1. Kamsang peuhmah Pasian’ sap hi (1 Kum. 19:16, 19-21 leh Isa. 6). Kilawm kisa mello zel uh a, sangpaklo hitaleh, a nung ciangin thumang kik uhhi.
2. Pasian in kamsangte mangmuhna, mang leh awging tu- amtuam tawh hopih hi. Khat veivei mailam thu lah hi. Gam leh minam vai thupiang dingte zong hilh zel hi (Daniel 2).
3. Laisiangtho sungah kamsan laitak in pumpi tha bei in husanglo hinapi; thahatna thak khat nei uh cihi. (Dan. 10:8–10, 18, 19).
4. Kamsangte in amau’ thupuak lampi tampi tawh hawm- kik a – aw tawh (Sawl. 21:10-12), gamtatna tawh (Eze. 4), Laikhak (Mang. 2, 3). Hih kamsangte in lai-aathuhpa zang uhhi. Baruch in Jeremiah huh hi (Jer. 36:1-4).
5. Kamsang khat thupuak pen ama’ masang a kamsangpa’ thutawh ki tuiton ding hi. Pasian in Amah leh Amah kitukalh ngei- lo hi (Isa. 8:20).
6. Kamsang khat’ thupuak sungah omte leh; tua kamsang nundan sungah kilang a, a bulpi vantung teci pansak ding hi (Mate 7:15-20).
7. Satan in vantung langdo dingin hahkat den hi. Hih mah- mah in kamsang silpiak taktak te lenkip zawsop hi. Hun nunung ciangin kamsangzuaute hong om ding uhhi (Mate 24:24; 2 Pet. 2:1).
