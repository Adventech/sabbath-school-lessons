Tuhun thu-ummi a tamzaw in, tanglai laisiangtho kamsangte omtaktak cih um uh a hihhang; tuhun in zong tuabang omthei ding cih a um uh diam? Joel 2: 28-31 sunga hun nunung a ding kam- sang silpiak kamciam up ding ahihleh, koici bang hiding hiam?

`Sawl. 2:36-42 simin. Penticost pawi ah Peter’ thuhilhna thu bulpi in bang hiam? Peter’ thungai mite dawnthukna koi- bang hiam?`

A.D. 31 kum Penticost pawi ah Kha Siangtho a kibuahna pen, hun nunung ciangin tualai a sang in a nasiazaw lai in hong kibuah ding lim lahkholna ahi hi. Penticost cihpen Zeisu lapsang- na, kisik kikna laangsakna, Pasian’na semthei dingin mite ki- sakkhol sakna ahi hi. Christ bulphuh in Peter’ thupuak pen Kha Siangtho in vangliatna guan ahih manin, kha 3,000 kisik kik in ki tuiphum uhhi.

Hihpen “guahmasa” Kha Siangtho thupha hi a, khaici man- pha khat hongpo pen Pawlpi – thu um mite kipawlkhawpna, Zei- su Christ hong paikik ding ngak kawmin hong itna a tangko, a hawmsawn pawlpi khat a phut hi. Guah-masa icih “khaici manpha a posak mahbang in; guah-nunung zongpen anlak na’ng anmin nading in hong kipia ahi hi” —EGWhite, The Great Controversy, p. 611.

Thukhat panin, tuhun kamsang silpiak hong kilatna in – guah-nunung hi a – penti- cost lai in guah-masa tawh hong kilatlai mahbang hikik ding hi.

Joel 2: 28-31 sungah i muhsa mahbang in, hun-nunung kamsang silpiak zongpen khangham khangno, numei pasal tun- gah suakta takin hong kipia ding hi. Kha Siangtho in a teeltua- mte tungah Pasian in hopih ding hi. Kha Siangtho in hun-nunung kamtaai khat a teel hunhun in, ama’ thupuak in Zeisu laamto, mite kisik kiksak, Pasian’na sep kizawh theih na’ng mite kisakhol sak ding hi.

Hih gamtatzia namthum teng pen, EGWhite’ kamsang nasepna ii mualsuangpite hiding uhhi. Ama’ damsung tengin a kampauna a laigelhna sungah Zeisu mah laamto in; Sev- enth-day Adventist te zong lungsim taktawh kisik dingin samin; a mangthangte zonna sungah Pasian’na semding in minpia dingin hanthawn hi.

`Ellen G. White’ nasepna tungtawn in silpiak lamdang hong kipiakna hangin bangzah na hamphat lawh ngei hiam?`
