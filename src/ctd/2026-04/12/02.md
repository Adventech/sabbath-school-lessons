---
title:  Joel’ Hong Kamciamna
date:   13/12/2026
---

`Joel 2:28-32 simin. Hnn nunung ciangin Pasian in bang hih ding cih hong kamciam hiam?`

Joel pen, zalom sagih lai a kamsang khat hi a, mailam thu- piang ding muhna thupi mahmahte hong musak hi. Hih a tunga mun simleng, tanglai ama’ hun a ding a gen maw, mailam gamlapi a ding mawh cih khat lungsim ah om giauguau hi.

Biakna’thu siamte in kamsanna pen namnih in khen thei a – tangthulam leh leitung beina’ng ci in khen uhhi. Tangthulam kamsanna in minamte vai, nambat leh min peuh gen uhhi (Jer. 25: 1-11).

Leitung beina’ng kamsanna ahihleh, kammal lianpipi, hun a ding in nambat’lim peuh leh, vannuai a limte peuhtawh gen uhhi (Daniel 7). Khatveivei ciangin hih kamsan na namnihte a kith- uapkha zong omtheilai hi (Mate 24 enin). Joel 2: 28-31 eleh? A nunung zaw sungah lut tuakzaw hi.

Cihnopna ah tua genkholnate pen tua kamsangpa nunsung bek thamlo ama’ khit peuh ciangin a tangtun ding lam-et omlo cihna ahi hi. Tuasang in mailampek ciangin a tangtun ding etkhol kulzaw cihna ahi hi. A sunga thu kihelte leh tua kamsang silpiak a dingin ahun kipiak ngaihsut huai hi. “Topa’ ni tua a lauhuai hun a hong tunma in ni hong mial in, kha sisan hong bang ding hi” (Joel 2: 31). Tua ni in bang ni hiam? Zeisu hongpai masak ni maw? Nihveina hongpai ni maw? A nihmah maw leh?

Joel kamsanna sungah ni leh kha sungah limte i lungngaih ciangin, laisiangtho mundang khawngah zong tuabangdan om- kawikawi cih phawkvat thei hihang. Zeisu ngiat in zong a nung- zuite kiangah ni, kha leh aksi te sungah Ama’ hunnunung minth- anna om ding cih genkhin zo hi (Mate 24: 29). Tua mahbang in mangmupa John in zong tua limte mah (Mang. 6: 12, 13) sungah pulaak khin hi. Zeisu leh John tegel un Topa hong pai madiak in leitung thupiang ding limlahnate mah bulphuh cinten uhhi.

Tua hi a, Joel’ kamsanna ah, a kibang tua limte in, hun nunung huam thei ding hi kici hi. “Tua ciangin vantung leh leitun- gah hilhkholna lim ahi, sisan kiluansakna, meikat na leh meikhu lompi hong om ding hi” (Joel 2:30, 31).

A kigenkhol bangin a tangtung kamsannate lungngai phakik ni. Mailam thu piang dingte Zeisu hong paikikna ding bangte

`Pasian i muantheihna dingin eite koi bangin hong huhthei ding hiam?`
