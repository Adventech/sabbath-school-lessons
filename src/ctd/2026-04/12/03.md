date:   14/12/2026

`Sawl. 2:1-21 simin. Christ hongpai masakna ding thu- pen Peter in banghang in Joel kammal zang hiam? Hihmun ah kamsanna in bang munpan mapang hiam?`

Hih laisiangthothak mun teng ah, laisiangtho kammal a sau- pen kizatna khat om hi. Peter’ ngaihdan teng simpha leng, “Pe- ter aw, hihte banglim a hiam? Nang hunsungah tuabang dan ding omthei kei si e” cihnophuai mahmah hi. Peter in zong, “limte a tangtun ni om hiven. Nungdeuh in Zeisu a kikhaina, naithum sungbang nimial veh e” (Maku 15:33) hong cihtuak hi.

Peter a dingin a uphuai thukhat mah ahihtei hang, tuni eite a ding eleh i lunglut beek hia? Joel’ kamsannate tuasang in a kicing- zaw tangtunna khat lam-enlai hihang. A letkhiatna ding tawhtang zong Joel sungmah ah ompah hi. Joel 2:23 sungah Joel in guahma- sa leh guah nunung thu gen hi. Sawltak 2 zong pen hihthu guah- masa a tangtunna ahi hi. A nunungpen a tangtunna’ng ahihleh, guah nunung a zukmadeuh in Kha Siangtho hong kibuakna ding penmah hipah ding hi.

Mite in kamsanna cihpen “mailamthu genkholna” ciziau uhhi. Khialkhollo tei ahih hang, laisiangtho kamsangte in mite a kisikkik dingin sam hi. Bangbang hitaleh, tuabang in Peter in a genciang in mi tampi kikheelsak zo veve hi. “Amaute un hihthu a zakuh ciangun a lungsim uh nakpi takin sukha a, Peter leh sawltak dangte kiangah, “sanggamte aw, bangci gamtat ding kahi uhhi- am?” (Sawl. 2:37) ci in a dong uhhi.

Peter in a dawnkikna ah “Na mawhna uh kisik unla, na mawhna uh hong kimaisak theihna dingin, Zeisu Christ mintawh ki tuiphum ciat un. Tua hileh, Pasian piakkhong Kha Siangtho nangah ding uhhi. Banghang hiam cihleh, Pasian in Ama’ kamci- amna pen, note tung nasuan nakhakte uh tung leh gamlapi a om mikhempeuh, i Topa Pasian in a sapmi khempeuh tungah a hong bawl ahi hi” (Sawl. 2:38, 39)

Tuiphum John zong tuabang khatmah ahi hi. Zeisu hong paimasakna ding thu a tangko mahbang in, amaute’ mawhna uh- pan kisikkik ding zong thuhilh hi. “Vantung ki ukna hun hong nai- ta ahih manin na mawhnate uh kisik kik un” (Mate 3:2) cihi. Tua hi a, Joel 2 in zong mailam ah ama’ tangtun hun ding nanei veve hi. Joel leh Peter mahbang in, hun nunung kam sangte in kisik kik ding, Zeisu kiang pai ding leh hun nunung Pasian’ pawlpi makaih dingin hong sam ding uhhi.

`Hih ngeina khengvalte i muhciang in, pawlkhat leuleu in a hiding mah salelpeuh uhhi. Hihmun lianah mite in nungzuite peh zukham sa uhhi (Zukhamte in gamdangpau siampahpah uhhi). Amau’ dawnthukna in, thu hilopi khat ahihtei hang, ngeina khengval te nangawn, ngeina banglel ahihtheih nading in, tua mite bangin tawsawn hiam?`
