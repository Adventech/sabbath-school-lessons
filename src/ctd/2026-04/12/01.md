### Tukalsung Simding
Joel 2:28-32, Sawl. 2:1-21, Eph. 4:11-14, Mang. 12:17, Mang. 19:10, Sawl. 2:36-42.

> <p>Kamngah</p>
> “Tuakhit ciangin mi khempeuh tungah kakha ka sung ding a, na putekte un mangman in, na tangvalte un mangmu ding uhhi. Tua hun ciangin nasem numeite leh nasem pasalte nangawn ah kakha kasung ding hi” (Joel 2:28, 29 ).

Kumpi pawlkhatte in “nitum vaihawmna” cih thukhun nei- thei uhhi. Hih kumpi thukhun in nitum tawh bangmah kizopna nei selo hi. Thukhun khat peuhpeuh a hun ani a ciangtan acin ciangin ama’thu in beiziau cihna kammalzat ahi hi. Kumpite in tuabang thu khunte thutuamtuam hangin koihthei uhhi.

Ettheihpak dingin, gamsung a lauhuaina a om ciangin, gam- mite a ding hunhoih vaihawmna in hun ciangtan thukhun peuh nei gawp thei uhhi. Kumpi pawlkhat in zong gammite’ hamphatna a kilakhiasa khawngte, hun ciangtan a cin ciangin tua thukhun ama’ thu in a bei dingin nitum vaihawmna thukhun neithei leuleu uhhi.

Tanglai in Pasian in zong a Kha Siangtho tawh kamsang sil- piak hong piak ciang in nitum vaihawmna mah a zangkha hiam? Tua thute mahmah zong Zeisu hong tunkik ni ciang mahin beilel ding hilo hiam?

Nungkal mahmah in, hun phamawh a om ciangin, Pasian in a kamsangte tawh Amite hopih cih sinkhin hihang.

Tukal ciangin, hun nunung kamsang sialpiakte, a nasep leh a kipkhona cihte – leh EGWhite’ kamsanna leh a nasepna cihte leh Zeisu hong paikik ding a naihmahmah na cihte sittel ding hihang.
