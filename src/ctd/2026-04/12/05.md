`Mangmuhna 12 sungah, kidona lianpi pen a zaipi ini, a kipat pan a tawp dong mah hong kilak dildel hi. Mang. 12:17 simin. “Zeisu’ teci panna” in bang hiam? Mang. 19: 10 leh Mang. 22: 8, 9 enkak in.`

Mang. 12:17 sungah, hun-nunung’pawlpi omzia ding nam- nih hong lakkhol hi. Tua pawlpi in Thukham Sawm (Sabbath ki- hel) leh “Zeisu’ teci panna” a lenkip ding hi. Tua teci panna icih bang hiam? Laisiangtho letna pawlkhat in “Zeisu’ teci panna” pen thu-um mite’n Zeisu a pulaakna uh leh evan nasepna sungah Zeisu kilatsakna (Mang. 19:10) in Zeisu’ teci panna letkipna hi a, Chris- tian teci panna ah Zeisu bulphuhna ci uhhi.

Version dangte leuleu in, Zeisu ngiat in Ama’ pawlpi tungah teci hongpang ngiat hi ci uh a, “Zeisu hong lah thumaan” cileuleu in, Zeisu mahmah in teci pangsak uhhi. A taktak in cileng a bulpi Greek pau a pen a namnih in kilet theilel hi. Tua hileh John in bang a cihnopna hiam?

Tua hilhcianna tegel etkak ciangin, a laigualhdan kinaih mahmah veve uh a, (a bulpi Greek sungsang in eima laisiangtho sungah kimu zaw hi). “Pasian’ thukham” cihpen Pasian kiangpan hongpai ahihmah bangin; Zeisu’ teci panna zong Zeisu sungmah ah bulkip ahi hi. Tua hi a, teci hongpang Zeisu mah ahi hi. Amah’n pawlpi maan pholak hi. Koici hih hiam? Kamsang silpiak tung- tawn in hih hi.

A dawnna te uhpen Mang. 19:10 leh Mang. 22:9 sungah ompah hi. Hiteng ah Zeisu’ teci panna cihpen “Khasiangtho thu genkholna” hi cihi. John amah ngiatpen kam- sang khat ahihna tawh “Zeisu’ teci panna” pen ama’ mimal sungah pua ngiat hi. Kamsang khat icih pen Amite kiangah Pasian’ kamtaai dingin a kituamsappa hi a, hanthotna piakna, kisik kik ding sapna, thapiak- na leh mailamthu genkholna cihte ahih dingin saptuam ahi hi.

Mang. 12:17 sungah, Zeisu in Ama’ pawlpi pen hih kam- sang silpiak tungtawn in phut hi cih hong lak hi. Laisiangtholui mite Israel leh Judah mite kiangah Pasian in kam- sangte sawl in, Christian masate kiangah zong sawltak John a sawl mahbang in; leitung’ tangthu ni nunung ciangin zong kamsangte hong pianglai ding uhhi.

`EGWhite’ nasepna pen “Khasiangtho thu genkholna” a hi- htheihna dingin bang thupiang khawng namuh khak a om hiam? (Rev. 19:10).`
