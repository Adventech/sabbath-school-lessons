Ephet 4 sungah, khalam silpiakte’ deihna bulpi pen thu-um mite’n Pasian’ nasep mazansakna ding, Paul in cihi. EGWhite kamsang nasepna zong tua deihna mah ahi hi. Ama’ humuap tawh thupuakte in thu-ummite pen a thukzaw Pasian tawh kikhawlhna nei sakin, tua itna mah midang kiangah a manlang in hawmsawn kiksak hi.

“Christian a kicite” lakah “Seventhday Adventist pawlpi in leitung mite maiah Christ a laamto pente hiding hi. Vantungmi thumna thupuak in Sabbath maan pulaak ding in hongsam hi. Tua thupuak sungah akihel hih thumaan tangko ding hang a; a lianzaw honghuupna Zeisu Christ peuhmah nutsiat ding hitheilo hi.

Christ’ singlamteh ah hehpihna leh thumaan kimukhawm uh a; thutanna leh kilem na kinamkha thei uhhi. Mawhnei peuh- mah in Calvary ki ensak ding a; naupangno khat’ upna bang tawh Honpa hong thupha bawlte muangin, Ama’ dikna sang in, Hong hehpihna um ding hi” —Gospel Workers, pp. 156, 157.

Tua banah, thu ummi peuhmah, EGWhite in Pasian’na mis- sion sem dingin han- thawn hi. “Pawlpi mite in a mimal mahmah in nakpi takin hanciam dingin Topa in hong ngen hi” —Evange- lism, p. 113.

“Topa’na sepna sungah a thadah om theilo ding hi. Mimalk- im in mibengte bangin semkhawm ding uhhi. Leitung siatna sung panin siangtho Christ hong itna sung tunding in kha mangthang zong ding uhhi” —Evangelism, p. 115. EGWhite ngiat in 1880 kiim in Europe ah missionary sem a, Australia ah1890 kiim in sem hi.

Pasian in kamsang tungtawn in ki-aap ding leh mission a ding hongsap manin hun nunung Pasian’ mite tungah, Satan “hehlua” (Mang. 12: 17) hi. Tua mite pen Zeisu’nung zui in hun nunung thupuak, kamsang’ makaihna leh khuamuhna aneite ahi uhhi: “Topa in eite’ mai leh kimkot a lauhuaina te hong lak khin hi. Kha Siangtho genkholna tungtawn in, leitung a galmat ding khemna zong hong pholhkhiat khin a, Amite kiangah ‘hihpen lam- pi hi, hih tawn un’ (Isa. 30: 21) hong cihi.

“Kidona Lianpi in Satan hong khemna teng pholhkhia hi”— Ellen G. White, manuscript 31, 1890.

Mihing amau’ nunna Zeisu a piak uh ciangun Satan thangpai lua a, Pasian’ kam- sang aw a minsiat theihtheih na’ng hah- kat hi. Tuabang ahihna ah EGWhite zong minsia sak in; “Abul abal” omlopi khawng hong cihsak hi. Ei’hun tawh a kituaklo hun khat in laigelh bel himah hi. Ama’ kamsanna te uplahna omsak in kamsangzuau peuh tangsak uh hi. “Abul abal” a omloh theihpen na dingin EGWhite laigelhte peuhmah tulai khangte in simlo in omsak hi.
