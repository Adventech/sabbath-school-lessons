---
title:  Kamsanna in Christ Pahtawi
date:   20/12/2026
---

Kamsanna cihlimlim Pasian in Amah leh Amite akizopna ahi hi. Satan (gulpi) in numei (Pasian’ pawlpi) tungah hehmahmah in, ama’ suanlehkhak a beibaangte galsim dingin a kuankhiat pen lamdang sakhuai nawn khollo hi (Mang. 12: 17).

Numei’ suan hihna khentel in ciaptehna in bangteng ahi diam? Amau un Pasian’ thukham leh Zeisu’ teci panna kemcing uhhi. Mangmuhna 19: 10 in tua teci panna cihpen “Khasiangtho tawh pulaakna” ci hi.

`1 Cor. 2:2 leh Galati 6:14 simin. Paul in ama’ nundan leh a nasep’ ngiimna koici muh a – na lianpi sem dingin a nun- dan koici muh hiam?`

Sawltakte in amau’ mitmuh tongsan bek thamlo, kamsangte leh misiangthote in kum tampi sung a genthapai densa ahih manin Zeisu sangthei uhhi. Christian biakna sung ah laisiangtholui leh a thak sung mahmah ah muhtheih teci a diil in om a: amasa in, Honpa khat hongpai ding acihcih leh anunung in, Honpa’ dinmun khempeuh Zeisu in picingsak cih hong lahkhia hi.

A umnuam ta dingin uptheih dingin hiteng kicinglua mah- mah khin hi. “Pasian’ geelna pen, tua minam in liangko kikim in, Pasian, a Tapa leh Kha Siangtho’ vangliatna sungah upna lam khangto ding cih ahi hi” —Ellen G. White, The Spirit of Prophe- cy, vol. 3, p.182.

Buaina a tamzaw peuhmah ngaihsut pilna lam hilo a; gam- tatna lam hizaw hi. Lutang vai hilo, lungsim vai hizaw hi. Mite in lungsim’ leh ngaihsutna kong a khak uh ciangun, zuikha maithei tak uhhi.

`John 1:1-5 simin. Hihmun teng in Zeisu pen kua hi ci in gen hiam?`

Zeisu, Pasian’ thu pen, om khinzo a, cilehsa hongsuak in eite lakah hong teng ahih manin; Zeisu in Pasian’ kamsangte tungtawn in hongpiak thute a picinna hithei hi. Zeisu’ thu kiciantak in a gen mello kamsang pawlkhatte nangawn, Kha Siangtho mahin humuam veve hi.

`Vannuai bangzah in zai cih ngaihsun inla; a piangsakpa zong ngaihsun lecin, ei tangding in a simipa Zeisu mah ahi hi. Tu- athu in bang lam-etna hongpia naci hiam?`
