---
title:  Kamsangthu in Christ Sungah Pumpi Pumkhat- sak
date:   24/12/2026
---

`John 17:20-23 leh 1 Cor. 1:10-13 simin. Hih laisiangtho munnih sungah bang zolna bulpi nih om hiam?`

Zeisu in singlamteh a vanaih ciangin, Ama’ lungtang sungah lungdeih lianpi khat om hi. Amah leh Pa in itna, ngiimna leh sepna sungah a pumkhat mahbang un a nungzui te pumkhat ding deihsak lua hi. Pumkhat na’ng Zeisu’ thungetna pen a thanem kibangkim na peuh hilello hi. Ama’ hong kumkikma in – a kipicing sak ding, a thahat kamsangthu khat a ding thungen hi.

Tua kamsangthu in, a kibanglo pianzialuite, minam tampite leh muhdan ngaihdan tampi khempeuh in, Kha Siangtho vangliat- na tawh Amah leh Pasian sungah pumkhat ding deihsak hi.

Sawltak Paul in zong Corinth thu ummite peuhmah ki thutu- ak in, thumaan khat genkhawm, pawl kikhenna omkhalo dingin a thuumna ah hihthu bulphuh hi (1 Cor. 1:10). Hih kamsanna pum- khatna in banghang in thupi thei hiam?

Zeisu mahin “amaute khempeuh pumkhat a suahtheihna dingun ka hong ngetsak hi. Nangmah in Kei nahong sawlna thu lei- tung mite in a uptheihna dingun, eitegel pawl khat ihih mahbang in, amaute zong eite tawh pawlkhat hong hitahen” (John 17:21).

Pasian in numei pasal khempeuh hotkhiat hileh deih ahih- hang, a kihotkhiat theih ma un, leitung honkhia dingin Pasian in Zeisu sawl ahihna a upding uh deihmasa hi. Minam tuamtuam te in Zeisu pen amau’ Topa leh Honpa in sang in, khatlehkhat an- gkhual lo ki itna tawh na hong sepkhop theih ciangin; Zeisu in kei kua kahi ci in a pulaakna teng leitung in hong uppih pan ding hi.

Zeisu’ lunggulh pumkhatna in ngiimna leh vangliatna kidim hi. Acil apat limin tua pumkhatna pen vangliatnate in lauhthawn den hi. Etpak dingin, Sawltak 6: 1 sungah Christian biakna pen kikhenkham ziazua denkeei hi.

Hih pumkhatna pen Khasiangtho in a hawmkhiat silpiakte tawh nasepna hang ahi hi. Ephet 4: 11-15 sungah, Kha Siangtho silpiak peuhmah thu umte huhna leh Christ’ pumpi lapsangna hi a; “Pasian’ Tapa i theihna leh i thu upna ah ki thukhat in eite a khang cing suakin, Christ’ cingtaakna ciang i baanto ding hi” (Eph. 4:13).

Kha Siangtho in a letmat thuhna ahi kamsang silpiak pen, Pasian’ mite in Zeisu hong kumkik ding hun hong naisemsem ciangin Zeisu Christ um uh in hotkhiat ahih theihna dingun, pum- khat sakna ahi hi (Sawl. 16:31).

`Seventh-day Adventist pawlpi icih pen leitung buppi ah langdo pawlpi (protestant) khauhpen ihih laitak in; mite in Zeisu hong uptheihna dingun pum- khatna koibang in lahkhia thei ding ihi hiam?`
