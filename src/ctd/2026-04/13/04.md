---
title:  Kamsangthu in Pasian’ Thumaan Kemcing
date:   22/12/2026
---

Laisiam pawlkhat in tuhun in Christian pawlpi 45,000 sang tamzaw om ci uhhi. Zeisu bektawh akipan upna khatin koibang in upna nam tampi in kaaziazua thei hiam? A dawnna bel hunsau- pi sunga laisiangtho letdan tuamtuam tampite tungah kinga cihi. Maan khinlo mah ding ahihtei hang, pawlpi khat’ thuhilhna tawh a kilehbulh in pawlpi dang khat in hilh leuleu hi.

Acil apat in (Pian. 2:16, 17; Pian. 3:1-5) sungah, buaina cihpeuhmah Pasian’ thu maan panin mihingte lampialsakna Sa- tan’ lampi lianpen ahi hi. Satan in Adam leh Eve tegel kiangah a cihsa mahbang in, thumaan a khomkhat leh thukhial tawmta hel kiukiau in, numeite leh pasalte ama’ ukna nuaiah kaaiden hi. Sa- tan in Pasian’mite lampialpih den hi (2 Khang. 36: 14-16). Zeisu nangawn lampialpih nuamlai (Mate 4:1-11) hitaleh, Zeisu in bel Satan’ zolnate pen “Laisiangtho in cihi” (Mate 4:4) cih tawh kitel takin dawngziau pahlel hi.

`2 Timothy 4:1-5 simin. Paul in Timothy kiangah Pasian’ Thu tawh kisai bang ngaihsutna thupi pia hiam? Mailam thu- piang dingte hangin Timothy in khalam ah a kisakkhol ding banghang in kulsak hiam?`

Kamsang’ mitmah tawh, Paul in, pasalte leh numeite in Pa- sian’ thu a it nawnloh dinguh mailamhun khat mukhol hi. Amau’ deihbang in tangthu phuah tawmtawm tetawh khalam siate tungtawn in lungnopna zong ding uhhi. Tua mite a dingin, Pasian in zong, amau’ zonzon zuauthute a uptheihna dingun, khemna khauhpipite khahkhia ding cihi.

“Pasian in amaute’ lakah nasem dingin zuau thahatna paisak ahih manin, amaute un amaanlo thu um uhhi. Tua ahih manin thu- maan umlo in mawhna sungah nuam asa khempeuh in thukhenna thuak ding uhhi” (2 Thess. 2:11, 12).

A lungsim uh ahong mite ahihleh, Zeisu’ kamciamna pen “thumaan thei” (John 8:32) cihi. Kamsanna pen thumaan leh thukhial, khuavak leh khuamial khenkhiat na’ng Pasian’ khutzat ahi hi.

Thukhat panin, hizah a tam ziazua pawlpi tuamtuamte lak- pan in thumaan a telthei te ihi uhhi. Ni sagih Sabbath (laisiangtho thukip) napaih kei nakleh, pawlpi a tamzaw in thukham liina a kepcinloh uh bang hilo in nangma’ deihteelna 99.5 bang a hem- khia khin nahi hi.

Misite in Zeisu tawh maangkhawm dingin vantung tungpahpah ahih keileh, tawntung hell meikuang sungah tungpah ci in na upkei nakleh, den a 0.5 a omlai teng panin 99.5 mah na paaikhia leuleu hi.
