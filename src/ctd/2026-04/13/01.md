### Tukalsung Simding
1 Cor. 2:2; John 1:1-5; 2 Tim. 3:16, 17; 2 Tim. 4:1-5; Isa. 40:1-5; John 17:20-23;1 Cor. 1:10-13.

> <p>Kamngah</p>
> Tua ciangin, zingsang in amaute thobaih in, Tekoa gamlak ah a paikhia uhhi. Amaute a paikhiat laitak in Jehoshaphat dingin “Judah gam leh Jerusalem a tengte aw, ka thugen za un; Topa na Pasian uh umun; tua hileh note nakip ding uhhi. Ama’ kamsangte umun; tua hileh namaavang ding uhhi” a cihi (2 Khang. 20:20).

Pasian in mitsi miaimuaisa khawngin hong umsak ngeilo hi. A kilehbulh in, Pasian om ahihna teci te kip mahmah hi. Piansak nate bek zong, mawhna sungah kum 6000 bang a omkhit tei hang; piansak theihna vanglian anei Pasian cihbek hilo; itna Pasian zong ahihna teci pang uhhi (James Webb Space Telescope pan maan etna).

Tampi mah omlai hi. “Mual siangtho tungah Amah tawh ka omkhawm laitak un, vantung panin hong ging awte komau mah- mah un kaza uhhi. Tua ahih manin kamsangte gen thute amaan taktak a hihna thu ka teltheih uhhi. Note lungsim ah zingsol hong suak in, khua a hongvak mateng in khuamial lak a meitaang bangin hihthute ngaihsun le-uh cin hoih mahmah ding hi” (2 Pet. 1:18, 19) Peter in cihi.

Peter bangin Pasian’ aw bel za buang sam keini in, kamsangte gen thute amaan taktak ahihna teltheih sambuang hihang. Pasian in kamsangte tungtawn in hong genkhia a, laisiangtho sun- gah akigelh om a, akigelhlo zong om hi. Hong kipiasa tecite kic- ing khengval zawsop hi.

Dotna khatbel omlai a: A sangthei dingin lungsim i hong hiam? Laisiangtho tangthu sungah mitampi in honglo uhhi. Pasian hong hehpihna sungah tua bang khialhnate i bawl kei buang diam?
