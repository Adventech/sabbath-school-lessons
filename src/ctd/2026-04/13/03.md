---
title:  Kamsanna in Laisiangtho Kipsak
date:   21/12/2026
---

Laisiangtho icih khalam silpiak tungtawn in, Kha Siangtho tawh a piang ahi hi. 2Peter 1: 21 in Pasian’ nasepdan hong lak a: “Mihing’ deihna bangin mailam thu genkholhna te a kingah hilo a; Kha Siangtho sawlna tawh Pasian kiang panin a hongpai thute a kigen hizaw hi” cihi.

`2 Timothy 3:16, 17 simin. Hihmun in Laisiangtho’ thu- neihna leh humuapna koibang in pibawl hiam?`

Laisiangtho om keileh, mihing in mawhna kidim leitung a lampi bei in kinusiacip ding a, tua in kisiatna lam zuatna ahi hi. Tuhun ciangin laisiangtho pen munkhempeuh ah namtampi in kingah theita ahih manin buaihuai khollo hi.

Zeisu’ hunlai bang hileh, simtheih dingin laisiangtholui bekmah om a, siampite leh rabbi te bek in simthei uhhi. Tang- pi tangta in laisiangtho tawh kizopna bul neitheilo uhhi. Pasian’ kammal khat in tualai in manphalua hi.

`Luka 24:27 simin. Zeisu in a thawhkik khit ciangin, a lungkia thanem nung zuite in Ama’ ngiimna a teltheihna din- gin koibang in huh hiam?`

Zeisu, kamsang lakpan a lianpen—Khat bek a om Thu (John 1:1-3)—in laisiang- tho pahtawi hi (John 7:38). Kamsang silpiak tungtawn in a kisang thupha lianpen in, laisiangtho a kip- sakna ahi hi.

“Laisiangtho pen Pasian’ awging hi ci in, a thuhilhnate a zuinuamte in, Kha Siangtho’ silpiak sungah, thu um taktakte a dingin hong ki vaihawmsak khalam lawpna leh vangliatna te ni- sim in kisin in sangden ding uhhi” —Ellen G. White, in Signs of the Times, March 8, 1910.

Kamsang thugenna in laisiangtho laamto den hi. Pasal nu- mei khempeuh a dingin upna leh gamtatna thugui pen Pasian’ Thu hi ci in kawk gige hi.

`Zeisu in Amah kua hi cih a pulaakkhiat madeuh in, banghang in Laisiangtho kawk masa thei hiam? Laisiangtho pen eite a dingin bangzah in bulpi hithei cih koibang in hong theisak hiam?`
