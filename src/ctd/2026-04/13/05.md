---
title:  Kamsangthu in Pasian’ Mite Hehnem
date:   23/12/2026
---

`Mate 11:28, 29 simin. Zeisu in banghih ding hong sawl hiam? Ama’ kiangah a paite bang kamciam pia hiam?`

Pasian in mihing’ hoihtheihna ding khempeuh ngaihsun den hi. Adam leh Eve a piansakma in, Pasian in amau’ nuam a sakmah- mah dinguh teenmun khat bawlsak masa hi (Pian. 1). Amau a ding a nuam ding teng khempeuh vaihawm sak hi. Tua siangtho nupa a pukkhit uh ciangun, Pasian in amau vazong a (Pian. 3: 9), Hon- pa khat hongpai ding kamciam pia (Pian. 3:15), amau’ tangguak omna tuam ding puan bawlsak (Pian. 3:21) hi.

Ama’ piansaksate’ hoihna ding khempeuh kholkhol sak hi. Kamsanna’ thupha in hong hehnepna leh thaksuahna in; Pasian’ mite sep dingin khang tawntung adingin hong kipia nasepte pen, thupha dangte sang in ki awlmawhlo zaw hi.

`Isa. 40:1-5 simin. A thuaksia amipite a dingin Pasian in kamsangte tawh bang thupuak pia hiam? Tua thukhunte eima tungah koibang in zangthei ding ihi hiam?`

Judah mite Pasian in gimpia ahihhang, amaute nusia ciplo ding cih Judah in thei sak nuam hi. Pasian in a kisiasa Amite, Isa- iah tungtawn in thapiakna thu khat bek thamlo, tua thupuak koici piak ding cihzong hilhcian lailai hi. Tua thupuak pen, thu damtak, itna leh donna tawh piak kul hi.

Hih thupuak in Amite’ lungtang a vaphak mengding deih ahih manin Pasian in hun themcik zong mawkbeisak nuamlo hi. Tuabang thupuakte pen Isaiah laibu sung tawntung mahah kisuut phapha hi (Isa. 12:1; 49:13; 51:3).

Sawltak Paul mahin Zeisu Christ’ deihna bangin a nung- ta dingte peuhmah bawl- siatna nasia mahmah ding cih vaukhol khin hi (2Tim. 3:12). Hongpai ding haksat hun in, hehnepna leh lam-etna kammal kisam ding hihang. Amaute inn ah ciahpih din- gin Zeisu hong paimaa in Amite a thapiakna pen kamsang silpiak mah ahi hi.

`Nangma nunna sungah koibang hun ciangin hehnepna leh lam-etna kammal na zanuam hiam? Pasian in hong hehnep khit ciangin bang thahatna hong guan lai hiam (2 Cor. 1:3, 4)?`
