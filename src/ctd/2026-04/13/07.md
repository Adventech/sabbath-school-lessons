Abeisa kum tawmkhat lai in, Kellogg company in amau zing kiallap singgah te tangkona thulu khat hong bawl hi. Mi- dangte kiangah a taisansa vanlei mite a ngahkik theihna’ng zol- bawlna in a hanthawn kammal “hong ciapsak masa limlim un” ci uhhi. Laisiangtho leh EGWhite thu nakpi takin a kisin napi’n, a laigelhte a “ciam” hetlo ei Seventh-day Adventist te hong cihna peuh ahi thei diam?

EGWhite tangthu hilhcianna khawng kahkha leng ama’ nundan leh nasepna kitel zawdeuh sam ding a; ahihhang, nangmah ngiat in, “ciam” ngiat keipeuh lecin, ama’ thu kamsang thupuakte hangin khalam thuphate manphatna telzo lo ding hiteh. A bukim takin a simpha te bekin, Pasian hongpiak thute pakta thei pan ding uhhi.

Tu quarter sungin, EGWhite in laisiangtho thumaan tawh eite hong henkip in Pasian tawh hong kizom sak hi. Ama’ kam- sang thupuakte simna sungah thupha tampi om hi.

Roger Dudley leh Des Cummings, Jr., tegel in, a lunglu- thuai mahmah thu pawl khat kankhia uhhi. EGWhite laigelhte a simngeilo te sang in, a simden mite in a thukzaw leh a kinaizaw Zeisu tawh kikhawlhna nei uh a; hotkhiatna lungmuang zaw uh; innkuan biakpiakna zong neitheizaw uh a; midang kiangah zong teci pangnuam zaw uh cihi.

Tu quarter sungteng, Pasian in kamsangte tungtawn in hong hopih cih theikhin hihang. Amau’ thupuakte in, Pasian tawh hong kizomsak a, mihing’ nundan khempeuh sungah a siangtho huzaap- na hong tunthei a, adiak in Zeisu hong hotkhiatna sungah “Topa hoihna ciam in thei un” (Late 34:8) cihi.

Gamtatna siangtho, nundan kipuahphatna, mimal evan nasepna leh midang tung ah kipiakkhiat nasepna cihte nei din- gin eite hong kisam hi. Zeisu hong kumkik ding lam- et laitak in, leitung ah a nunungpen tangkona sungah, eima dinmun picinsak theihna’ng kisakkholna ahi hi.
