---
title:  Gampualam panin Lam-etna
date:   09/12/2026
---

“Amite leh amau’ tenna mun khasiat ahih manin, amau’ pute pate’ Pasian Topa in, a kamtaaite amaute’ kiangah sawlteitei hi” (2 Khang. 36:15).

Elijah khit nangawn in Pasian in Ama’mite thapia, thuhilh lamlak dingin kamsang dang tampi zong sawllai hinapi; Israel leh Judah gam in, gamtat siatna lamah nakpailua uh ahih manun, Pa- sian in Sodom leh Gomorrah tawh genteh liang zawzen hi (Isa. 1:9, 10).

Pasian’ mite in kiphasak, huaiham in milim peuh bia uhhi. A nasemte, meigong tagahte leh huhna kisam in hongpai zinle- hlengte simmawh bawl uhhi (Isa. 58 sungah Israel gitnatlohna simin). A tawpna ah Israel tungah Pasian in thukhen a Assyrian mite’ sungah saltang sakta (2 Kum. 17:1-6) a; Judah ahihleh Bab- ylon gam ah hawlkhia hi (2 Kumpite. 24, 25).

Tuabang in Pasian in Amite gim a thuaksak laitak un zong gamdang ah nusiacip samlo in; mailam ah lam-etna a neihtheih na’ngun, Ezekiel, Daniel cihbang kamsangte sawlteitei lai hi.

`Isaiah 59:1-4 leh Nehemiah 9:30 simin. Hitengah khang tawntungin a kizang ding thu bang kigen hiam?`

Amau’ tangthu sung tawntung in, Israel leh Judah in amau’ mawhna sulzui teng vive thuak thapai uhhi. Pasian kiangpan paikhiatna hangin, Pasian’ kepcinna nusia uh a, a thukhenna uh ngah zawsop uhhi. Pasian in nenniamna leh milim biakna peuhmah zah vetloh ahihtei hang, amawh cil apat in hawlkhia pah samlo hi. Ama’ mite tawh nasem in a kisikkik Topa zuankik din- gin kamsangte un zolkiksak den hi. Mite in thumang nuamlo zaw uhhi. Pasian in Amite amau’ mawhna a nutsiat dinguh bangtanvei sawtliang in zol den cih lamdang lualiang hi. Zalom saupi, kam- sang khatkhitkhat, vauna khatkhitkhat tawh Pasian in amau’te va- phasak den hi.

Zeisu’ cihna hoihluamai hi: “Jerusalem, Jerusalem aw, kamsangte nathat a, Pasian’ sawl kamtaaite na denglum hi. Akpi in a khatawh a note ahuai mahbang in, na mite kahuai nuam tawntung a, ahizong in na utkei hi” (Mate 23:37) cihi.

Hitaleh, amaute gampua ah a omlaitak un, lam-etna neilo peuhmah in Pasian in nusia cip ngeilo hi. Amaute gitnatlohna kawmkal mahpan in Pasian in “Note a hong hu dingin, mawh- na panin akiheikhia mipeuhmah ka hong honkhia ding hi” (Isa. 59:20) cihi.

`Israel in Topa a taisat uh ciangun, Pasian’ cihtakna ngaih- sutsak un. Nangma nunna sungah hibang Pasian hong itna namuhkhak ngaihsun kikin. Amah’n lungduai takin hong thuakkeileh koilai khawng natung zentam maw?`
