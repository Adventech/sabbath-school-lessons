---
title:  Khuahunsia Vauna
date:   06/12/2026
---

Pasian in mihing honkhia nuam mahmah hi. Apukkhit uh nangawn un, Adam leh Eve tawh kikhawl dingin zonglai zaw- sop a, tawmvei khat sungteng leitung mite in “Topa’ min sam thei uhhi” (Pian. 4: 26).

Khang hong paisuksuk in, Pasian tawh tonkhawm hong tawm semsemta hi. Pasian leh Ama’ hoihna nungmah a zuicint- en nuamte in, amaukiim a mawhnate kihhuaisa in tua mawhna sulzuite tawh amau’ thuhilh tawntung hi.

Noah hun hong tun ciangin, leitung mite pen gilo semsem ahih manun Pasian in suksiat dinglam teelzaw ta hi (Pian. 6:5-7). Noah, thuman hilhpa (2 Pet. 2:5) in a ngai nuam te a dingin hong tungding suksiatna vai a vaukholna hihtuak hi. Pasian in nuntak- na hotkhiat na’ng gunkuang (tembawpi) khat bawlding in Noah thupia a, ama’ vaukholna a ngai mite kisik uh hipeuhleh hotkhiat hita ding hi.

`Hebrew. 11:7; 2Peter 2:5 leh Piancil 6:9. Noah in koi- bang dan in “thumaan hilhpa” suakthei hiam?`

Tuicin tunna pen leitung tangthu ah hun thupikhat hi a, lai- siangtholui leh thak sungah a tangthu asuut kikkikna ah, mihing’ gitlohna a thukzia leh Pasian hong hehpihna a saanzia cihte hong muhsakna ahi hi. Mawhna kidim piansaksate tungah a thukhenna; leh hotkhiatna a sang mi peuhmah in suakta cihthu sungah Pasian’ thumaanna hong kilangpha mahmah hi.

“Thumaanlohna a khanmahmah laitak in Mathuselah, Noah leh midang tampite in, Pasian taktak theihna nungtasak in, git- lohna teng a khawltheihna dingin hahkat in na sem uhhi. Tuicin tunma kum zalehsawmnih lai in, Topa in vanmisiang khat sawl in Ama’ deihna Noah kiangah pulaak sak in tembaw khat lamding in thupia hi.

Tua tembaw a lamkawm un, migilote a kisuksiatna dingin Pasian in tuicinkhat hong tungsak ding cih hilhkawm dingin Noah thupia hi. Tua thupuak um in kisikkikna leh kipuahphatna tawh, tua thupiang a ding a kisakhol mi peuhmah, hehpihna ngah in hot- khiat hi ding uhhi” —Ellen G. White, Patriarchs and Prophets, p. 92.

Noah kiangah Pasian in tuicin bek hilo tembaw sung a lut- mite hotkhiatna ngah dingin Noah leh piansaksa khempeuh tun- gah, hih khit ciangin tui peuhmah tawh leitung susia ngei nawn- lo ding ci in (Pian. 9: 8-17) sungah kamciam pia hi. Sakhituihup khem- peuh sungah kamciam, lam-etna leh tatkhiatna om hi.

`Sakhituihup namuhkik ciangin, tuasung a kamciam ompen phawk in. Pasian in Ama’ kamciam kemcing cih natheihna tawh banghang in nakhamuang thei hiam?`
