---
title:  Kisik dingin Sapna
date:   08/12/2026
---

`Thukhente 2:16-19 simin. Bang thupiak tawh nasep ki- pansak hiam?`

Kumpi ki ukna tawh kikheel bialbualna leh Israel mite’n kumpi a ngetna (1Sam. 8) uhtawh thukhente’ hun abei ahi hi. Hih kikheelna hangin kamsangte’ ngaihsut piakna thuhilhna leh kumpite, gam makaite leh mipite taai in thuhilhna kisampha semzaw hi.

Ahab maan hunin, Pasian in, thukhenna thupuak khat Eli- jah sawl in puaksak a: guah zukloh ding ahangthu pen Israel in “Topa’ thupiaknate nusia in Baalte zui nahih man in” (1 Kumpi 18:18) cihi. Tuaciang in Ahab zong kisik ding sangsik in Elijah pen thahlup dingin sabet in beng hitaleh, Pasian in kemcing hi (1Kum. 18:10-12).

Kialtunna kumthumna ciangin, Elijah in a nunungpen din- gin kumpi Ahab a vanaih leh, Ahab in guahtui kangsakpa ci in na mawhpaih zawsop hi (1 Kum. 18: 17). Tua ciangin Elijah in zong, Pasian leh Baal kamsangte kimuhna nunung pen bawlsak hi (1 Kum. 18: 19).

Vantung in vanpan mei a khiatsukna leh Elijah’ kamsang thupuak tegel in Pasian mite lungsim lawngkha in kisikna lam makaih thei hi.

Israel mite in zong Pasian kiangah ki pum-aap ding khawng a ziakai diaiduai uh mahbang in eite zong Pasian pen Piangsak- pa leh Tankhiapa ahihna awlmawh lohna hun sungah nungta ihi hi. Pasian tungah citak dingin mite hatthot ding sangsik in, Israel mite mahbang in eima’ lungdeih bangin gamta in Pasian sapna awlmawh lothei hihang.

Eima’ lungdeihna zuihna in kisiatna, natna leh sihna nan- gawn hong tunthei cih Pasian in hong vauden hi. Ama’ aw ngai hileng, “Topa in, lam konglak tawntung ding a, nahoihte tawh kong lungkimsak ding hi. A cidam, a thahat in kong omsak ding hi. Tui tampi a ngah tawntung huan, a kangngeilo ciktui tawh na kibang ding uhhi” (Isa. 58:11).

`Eima’ sunglam pianzia theihlohkal in Pasian langdona lamah hong tawsawn den hi. Tuathu nalelhlohna dingin bang teelsi- amna na kisam thei ding hiam?`
