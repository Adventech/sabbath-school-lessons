---
title:  Israel Sungah Nu Khat
date:   07/12/2026
---

Israel mite Egypt saltanna pan a khahkhiat khit ciangin Pa- sian in Canaan gam pia hi. Ama’ thukhamte a zuihnak vualeh thu- pha nakpiakluat ding kamciam hi. A palsat uhleh lah thuaksiatna leh suksiatna hong tung ding cih zong theikhol sakpah hi (Thkna. 27–30).

Joshua sihkhit ciangin, Israel mite in thuciam letkipna pan lampialta uhhi. Tuaciang in amau’ kiimkot a mite in simmawh ahih manun Pasian kiang ah kiko uhhi. Pasian in a dawn thukna in “amaute’na a sutgawp mite’ vangliatna panin amaute ahonkh- ia thukhente Topa in khangsak hi” (Thkte. 2:16). Israel in Pasian tawh a thuciamna in thaksuah in amau a neng niam mite langkhat ah a thahat theihna’ng thapia dingin kamsangte zong sawl hi.

`Thukhente 4:4-16 leh 5:1-5 simin. Deborah in Israel a ding bang hihsak hiam?`

Deborah in a phamawh mahmah kamsang dinmun ah pangin, Israel leh a makai pipa Barak in galkap-hon a kaihkhop laitak in amau tungah Pasian’ thupuak teng gensawn hi. Barak kiangah galphuangta in cihna lim apia Deborah mah hipah a, “Pai in! Topa in nang khutsungah Sisera hong piak ni ahi hi. Namai ah Topa pai hilo ahi hiam?” (Thkte. 4:14).

Israel sungah thukhen hihna tawh, Deborah pen mithupi khat ahi hi. Ama’ makaihna leh Pasian sungah a upna in, Israel mite’ galte talsik in dozawh na’ng thahatna ahihi. Barak zongpen Israel’ galkapte makaih ding a tonzel teihang galkapmaang siam- pa hi a, lawpna tawh makaih ahihhang, tua zong a gualzawhna hipah tuanlo hi.

Hihthu mahmah zongpen kamsangnu Deborah leh galkappa Barak tangthu sungah thupuak thupipen mah hipah hi. A lamdang gualzawhna pawi akhaam laitak un, Israel mi lakah a vangnei pen in, thukhennu khat leh agualzo galkapmang khatin Pasian’ minth- anna dingin mipi hong makaih uhhi. Hihpen khalam ki makaihna taktak omzia ahi hi.

Deborah kamsang nasepna in, haksat hun leh nenniam thu- ak hun in Pasian tungah cihtakna lim lahna ahi hi. Pasian in Barak a ding thupuak a piak ciangin Deborah in galkap kicing maw cih ahizong in, Israel te thanem lua e cih ahizong in, kamtam selo lel hi. Tua sangsik in Barak kiangah tuathu puakpah in, thapia a, Pasian’ vaihawmna muang ding leh lunghianna peuhmah hemkhia dingin Barak hanthawn hi.

`Tu laitak in na khempeuh nangma’ ngaihsut bang ahih pahpah lohhang in Pasian mah muangveve dingin koibang in nakisin thei ding hiam?`

`1 Kum. 18:21,38, 39 sunga Isreal mite kiangah Elijah’ thutangkona leh Israel mite’ dawnthukna simin. Bang thu pi- ang hiam?`
