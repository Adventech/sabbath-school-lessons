date:   11/12/2026

Pasian in Ama’ kamsangte tungtawn in mite tawh kiho den hi. Hun phamawh hun sungah tuapen thuphamawh pikhat mah ahi hi. Hun nunung ciangin zong Pasian in Amite tuabang mahin a hopih ding lam-et omlel hilo hiam?

Adventist te laihawmkhiatna leh Sabbath tangteng thukip kipawlna pawlpi khat hong suahna uh zong, a min-ngahnapi mah hong hipah hi. Amau’ laihawmna leh Sabbath tanna in 1860 kum ciangin a tulsim mahin hong omta hi. Vantungmi thumte (Mang. 14:6-12) thupuakte a tangko theihna dingun a kipawlna kipkhat a neihuh kul hi. Tuabang a ki pawl ding pawlkhat in a utloh uh hang, Pasian in EGWhite zangin kalsuanpih hi. 1863 May 21 ni in Seventh-day Adventist pawlpi kiptakin hong piangkhia hi.

Tua kum mahin, thu ummite lakah cidamna kipuahphatna thapiathei dingin Pasian in kamsang silpiak hongzang hi. Sev- enth-day Adventist pawlpimi tampi in cidam mello in tualai in om uhhi. 1863 leh 1865 kumte in EGWhite in cidamna lam kicingtak in mang a muh ciangin, pawlpi khantohdan hong kikheelvat in, Seventh-day Adventist te pen cidamna leh cidam-nundan a laamto mite hong suak uhhi.

1870 panin 1890 kumsung tengin, EGWhite pen Adven- tist pilna lam khantohna bulpi in hong pang hi. A cingtaak pilna (lailam, seplam, khalam) cih ama’ ngaihdan pen damtak in hong phutthei ta hi. Amah in zong “Pilna maan icih in kisinna ciangbek hilo hi. Tu laitak a i nundan a khengval nundan khat a ding kig- inkholna cihna ahi hi. Pumpi bup nuntakna buppi tawh kisai hi. Pumpi, lungsim leh kha khantoh diimdiam na’ng thahatna ahi hi” —Education, p. 13 - ci hi.

1880 kumte in, amah in, hotkhiatna tawh akizom Sev- enth-day Adventist upna thuguipi phuhna sungah thupi takin mapang hi. Thukham mah a bulphuh, Seventh-day Adventist upna sungah Pasian’ hehpihna leh dikna mah sangtawi hi. 1901 pan 1903 dong pawlpi in a leitung nasepna a gualzawh semna dingin, pawlpi kipkhat in dintheih na’ng nakpi takin panpih hi.

Tomgen leng, EGWhite’ nasepna omkei peuhmah leh tuni tuhun in Seventh-day Adventist pawlpi icihpen omkha hetlo ding hi. EGWhite pen hun phamawh sung a dingin Pasian’ kamtaainu ahi hi.
