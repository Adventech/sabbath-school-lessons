### Tukalsung Simding
2 Pet. 2:5; Thkte. 4:4-16; 1 Kumpi. 18:21, 38, 39; Isa. 59:1-4; Neh. 9:30; Mang. 1:1-3.

> <p>Kamngah</p>
> “Topa Pasian in, Ama’ nasem kamsangte tun- gah, Ama’ geelna theisak masa lopi in bangmah hih hetlo hi” (Amos 3:7).

Mihingin a pukpan kipan, Zeisu sungah tawntung nunna tawh a kilehngat mawhna sungah tawl khat nuamsa dingin a kiseh hipah hi. Pasian in tuathu thei ahih manin eima hotkhiatna ngahna dingin itna tawh na hong sem hi.

Tua ahih manin, tangthu sungah a ciaptehhuai thukhat ahi dingin, kamsangte tung tawnin Amite a dingin na hong sem hi. Hi taleh Topa’ nasepna peuh a kinial vinven ciang in, Topa’n thukhen ding tawh vauta hi. Kamsangte tungtawn in, Topa in na khat peuhpeuh a sepma in Ama’ sepding peuhmah hilhkhol masaphot hi (Amos 3:7).

Mite a kisik vualeh manlang takin maisakpah hi. Tuni in zong tua mahbang in hong hopih thapai lai hi. Kum zalom tampi a paisa sungah Pasian in Amite a hopih mahbang in leitung hun nunung a Ama’mite zong a hopih dingpen, khuahun paizia tawh kituak mahmah hi.

Tukal sinna ah hotkhiatna tangthu sungah Pasian in kamsangte zangin, Ama’ mite kiangah Ama’ deihna kiciantakin a pu- laakna kisin ding hihang. Hun thupi laitak in EGWhite’ humuap nasepna hangin Adventist nasepna nakpi takin khangto cihzong en lai ding hihang.
