---
title:  Ngak Hun laitak a ding Kamsang Khat
date:   10/12/2026
---

`Mangmuhna 1: 1-3 simin. Bang hihding in John kisam hiam?`

`Christian masate in Zeisu’ hong paikik ding kamciam- na sungah lawpmahmah sa in na nungta uhhi. Sawltak John in, Christ, meiitungah a kahtoh mu a, tuamah bangin hong tuaksuk kikding cih vantungmi genna za hi (Sawl. 1:10, 11). Pawlpi masa in theihloh thu khat talsik keei uhhi. Himah e, Zeisu in ka hong paikik ding a cihhang, cikciang hiding hiam?`

John mah, Patmos tuikulh tungah a kihawlkhiat lai in, Zei- su’ hong paikik ding thu Pasian in gen a: “En un, Amah hongpai tading a, meiipi tawh hong tonkhawm ding hi. Teipi tawh Amah asunte a kipan, mittang khempeuh in muding uhhi” (Mang. 1:7). John mahin, beina hun hong tun ding lahna mangmuhna maht- awh mu hi. Tua banah, pawlpi sagih teng thapiakna leh thuhilhna (Mang. 2, 3); thukhenna mangmuhna (Mang. 14: 6- 12); leitung a beina (Mang. 20); Christ nihveina hong paikikna (Mang. 14:1416) leh leitung a puahphat kikna (Mang. 21, 22) cihte mulai hi.

John mahin amahpen “note sanggam, Zeisu Christ tawh ki- pawlna hangin note mahbang in bawlsiatna thuak mipa kahi hi” (Mang. 1:9). Gimna cihpen ngakna hangin thuaknabek hilo a; Pa- sian Thu tangkona leh Zeisu Christ a ding teci panna hangin bawl siatna cih amah’n theigige hi.

Mangmuhna laibu sung tawntung in, John in a et ding leh amuh bangbang gelh ding in kicial hi. “Tu in keimah John in, hihthute za in kamuh ahi hi” (Mang. 22:8). John pen ninunung thupiangte a ding kamsang teci panna hi a, a mangmuhna sunga a kilak khempeuh zong ama’ hun a pawlpite tungah hawmsawn ding thu kipia hi (Mang. 22:16). Amah pen, leitung ah hun phamawh laitak in Zeisu Christ hong masakna (John 1: 6-8) a tangko Tui- phum John ngilhloh Phawkkikna ahi hi.

John mahbang in eite’n zong teci pang dingin hong sam hi. (Mang. 19: 10) sunga Zeisu’ teci panna, Amah ngiat in kamsangte tungtawn in apiak pen zong kemcing ding hi hang. John bangin mangmu lianglo himah leng, hotkhiatna limte enkawm; Ama’ hongpai ding ngak kawm in Ama’ nasepna sungah teci pansak thei veve hihang.

`Hong kipiasa khuavak lungngai kik in. Tuazahta hong kip- iakhin ahihleh, i vaipuak bangzahta in lianthei ding hiam?`
