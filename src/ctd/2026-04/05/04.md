---
title:  Kamgen Thupuakte
date:   27/10/2026
---

`2 Peter 1:18-21 simin. Pasian’ kamsangte in a pulakkhi- at thupuakte pen humuapna mahtawh ahih manin, a kigelhte mahtawh kibang in a muanhuaina bang khawng omthei ding hiam?`

Adam leh Eve a pukma un (Pian. 2:16, 17) a pukkhit un (Pian. 3:9-19) Pasian in hopihpih hi. A hunnih mahun Pasian’ kammal telcian lel uhhi. Sinai ah zong Pasian’ aw tel sitset uhhi (Pai. 20:1-20), Zeisu’ kituiphumna leh mel kilamdanna ah zong (Mate 3:17, Mate 17:5). Midang in hong hopih ciangin letkhial kha thei hihang. Pasian in mikhatpeuh a hopih nakleh, telkhialh ding leh khemna peuhmah kihel ngiatlo hi.

Laisiangtho in “Pasian in Moses hopih a ‘Keimah in Topa kahi hi’” (Pai. 6:2) ci hi. Laisiangtho laikungpu te in Pasian’ ho- pihna aw telcian mahmah uhhi. Etpak dingin Isaiah (Isa. 8:1, 11), Jeremiah (Jer. 1:4, 9, 11) leh Ezekiel (Ezek. 3:16, Ezek. 6:1). Hih teng bek hilo a dangte zong a nialnuamte in kamsangte’ thumaan- na leh a muanhuaina a dongpha tawh kibang lel hi.

Pasian in mihing teltheih kammal mahtawh hong hopih mahbang in, kamsang te’n zong a mipihte tel ding mahin pau uhhi. Noah pen Pasian’ kamsang maan leh a citak khat hi a, “di- kna thu hilhpa” (2 Pet. 2:5) kici hi. Laisiangtho sungah themkhat

beek zong gelh khalo hinapi, ama’ hunlai a mipite tungah Pasian’ thupuak a kamtawh hilh a; tua thupuak te zong laisiangtho kam- sangte laigelhte sang in humuapna kiamzaw tuanlo hi.

Kamsang dangte, Nathan, Elijah, Elisha, leh Tuiphum John cihte in laisiangtho sungah themkhat beek zong gelhkhalo uh hi- napi; amau’ thupuak hawmkhia veve uhhi.

Tua ahihleh Zeisu Christ eleh? Lungdamthu sungah Topa in “a khutmee tawh leivui lakah gelh” (John 8:6) ci in ciamteh hi. Tua simloh, Zeisu’ khut tawh akigelh lai dang om tuanlo hi. Ahihhang Amah’n a kamtawh thu hilh a, “ukna aana neite bangin” (Mate 7:29) gen kici hi. A thungai pawlkhat bangin “Hihmipa bangin kuamah’n thu gen ngeilo” (John 7:40, 46) ciliang uhhi.

Zeisu pen Pasian’ thupuakte kamtawh a hawmsawnpa khat ahi hi. Topa in laisiang tho sungah bangmah a gelhloh hangin, Ama’ kammalte’ ukzawhna aana in kiam tuanlo hi.

`Pasian in laisiangtho a gelh hetlo kamsang honkhat mah nei cihthu lunglut huai zawzen hiven. Laisiangtho gelhlo kam- sangte in zong banghang in ukzawhna aana neithei veve hid- ing hiam?`
