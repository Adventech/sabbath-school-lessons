---
title:  A Huampi leh A Tuamdang Mangmuhna
date:   25/10/2026
---

`Hebrew 1:1, 2 simin. Pasian in eite tawh kiho ding bang- hang in lunglut thei hiam? Zeisu leh Kamsangte tungtawn in bangteng hong hilh hiam?`

Vannuai leitung a Piangsakpa in eikiang ah bang hong gen- nuam aitam? Banghang in hong hopihpih hi ding? Eite pen mawhna a dim hih leitung ah a puksa mihing kihi a, sihna, thuaksiatna leh haksatna peuh masuan leltak i hihman ahi hi. Tua hangin Pa- sian in eite hong hopih a; eite hilai ah banghang in omthei, na khempeuh banghang in kisialua cihte hong telcian saknuam a; Ama’ itna sungah lam-etna hong neisak nuam hi.

Pasian a huampi in hong kipulaakna: hih leitung ah ei muhtheih ciangah, Ama’ vangliatna ciangtan omlo leh hong itna hipen hi. Kumpi David in “vantung in Pasian’ minthanna gen a, vanpi in ama’ nasepna tangko hi” (Late 19:1). Azom in Paul in zong “Pasian in leitung a piansakcil a kipan in, a kimu theilo ama’ pianzia ahi tawntung vang- lianpa leh Pasian ahihna thute ama’ bawlsa nate tungpanin teltakin hong kilang hi” (Rom. 1:20) cihi.

“Hih leitung ah damkik theihna nasep a kipsakpha kikpa Pasian vangliatna pen lamdang mahmah hi. Pasian’ hong dam- sakna vangliatna pen piansaksa te sungah om hi. Singkung khat kiphuk leh, mihing’ guh khat liam/kitan leh, pumpi in puahphat kikna nasep thakhat in panpah ngiatngiat hi. Kisapna a om mapek in, damsakna vaipuak nei tengin kiging khinkhian zo a; munkhat peuh a liam ciangin, nakhempeuh in puahphat kik na dingin nasem ngeingai pah uhhi” —EGWhite, Education, p. 113.

Piansaksa khempeuh in hoihna leh pilna tawh kidim a; Pa- sian’ vangliatna hong lak bek hilo; gitlohna a om zong hong telsak lai hi (Pian. 3:14-19; Mate 13:24-30). Hih a telmello thupuakte zong pen, Pasian in a tuamdang takin hong lahna – Zeisu Christ, a sunglut Pasian Thu, laisiangtho, a kigelh Pasian Thu bekin hong telsak thei hi (John 5:39).

Laisiangtho icih in Pasian’ thupuakte pen, khang tawntung a mite a dingin a kigelh ahi hi. Leitung piancilna leh a pukna, hih leitung a puahphat kikna ding leh hotkhiat na’ng ngiimna hong theisak hi. A tomlitlet in a kiteelkhia tangthu pawlkhat om a, Pasian in hong genkhiat ahih manin muanhuai hi (2Tim. 3:16, 2Pet.1:19-21).

`Piansaksate in Piangsakpa’ vangliatna leh hong itna pulaak gige hinapi; banghang in a kigelh laisiangtho kisam veve lai ihi hiam?`
