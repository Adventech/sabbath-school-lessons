---
title:  Humuapna Nasepzia
date:   26/10/2026
---

Akhang akhang in laisiangtho bek hilo, laibu tampi takmah hong om ziahziah hi. Tuate lakah laisiangthobu pen Pasian Thu ci in um hihang. Laibu dangte tawh bangin ki lamdang sak hiam?

`2 Tim. 3:15-17 leh James 1:17 simin. Hihmunte pan bang a thupi thupuak kingah thei hiam?`

2 Tim. 3:16 pen letkhiatdan ahoihthei pen ding kikupna om himahleh, a tamzaw in tu a omngeisa mah hoih kisazaw in, lai- siangtho buppi bulpi in vantung hi ci-kim uhhi (2 Pet. 1:20, 21). Letkhiapa khatin “Paul in laisiangtho sung buppi entel dikdek khin a, laisiangtho buppi mahin mihing pen a picing, gamtathoih gamtat theihna ding hong puah phat nadingin hong thuhilh, mawhna hong phawksak, hong puahpha, thutang sungah hong makaihna dingin hoih hi ” cihi.—G. W. Knight III, The Pastoral Epistles: A Commentary on the Greek Text (Grand Rapids, MI: Eerdmans, 1992), p. 445.

Tua ahihleh laisiangtho a piangsak masapenpa humuapna koibang in telthei ding ihi hiam? A muanhuai vantung thupuakte pen kamsangmaante tungah Kha Siangtho huh na tawh kihumuam cih itheih kul hi. Tua huhna pen a thupuakteng saanna bek thamlo in a za ding mite tungah puakkhiatna ah zong huhlai hi.

Pasian’ thupuak peuhmah mang leh mangmuhna tawh hong kipia hi (Gam. 12:6; Isa. 6:1, 8). Mitmuh teci zong Pasian in zang hi (1 John 1:13), tangthu kantelte zong Kha in huh hi (Luka 1:1-3), lungsim sungah vantung in guansuk (2 Pet. 1:21). Kha Siangtho zatdan te bek thamlo, kamsangte leh laisiangtho laikungpu te zong amau lungdeih bangin kigelh saklo in, amau hawmsawn ding thu- maan kipia ngiat hi. Pasian thupuak kammalte peuhmah sawltakte in amau’ kammal zangphial taleh lumlet gawp, susia gawp cih- bang khawng hitheilo hi.

Kamsangte thupuak peuhmah pen mipite kiangah kam a genkhiatna tawh (2 Pet. 1:19-21), lai a kigelh tawh (John 20:30, 31), pawi lah bang (Ezek. 4:1-8) tawh hong tung hi.

`Laisiangtho leh humuapna nasepzia te koibang in hih cih kitel sitset zolo ahihhang, Pasian humuap ahihlam up theih ding banghangin thupi thei hiam?`
