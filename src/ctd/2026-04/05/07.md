---
title:  EGWhite a Mamgmuhna leh Humuapna
date:   30/10/2026
---

`Seventh-day Adventist pawlpi in EGWhite pen vantung hu- muap ci in um uhhi. Ama’ laigelhte, a nundan leh a nasepna lim- lim in kamsang dinmun maan baan hi. Ama’ laigelhte laisiangtho tawh kikim in a koihloh hang, laisiangtho kamsangte humuapna zong amah’n ngah veve hi. A langkhat ah EGWhite in laisiangtho mah lamto hi. Amah ngiat Pasian kamsang pulakna aneihna leh a humuapna koibang ciangin a tel diam?`

Amasa in, EGWhite in laisiangtho pen a khial ngeilo Pasian kipulaakna cih um hi. Laisiangtho in Pasian’ thumaan leh a puk mite a dingin hotkhiatna ngiimna pulaak hi. Tua nasepna sungah Ama’ thupuakte a hawmsawn dingin kamsangte zang hi.

Anihna, EGWhite in humuapna cihpen vantung leh mihing kihona hi cihi. Pasian in Ama’ thupuak a piak khit ciangin, kam- sang in zong ama’ kammal zangin vantung lam lahna bangin hih ding hi. EGWhite cihna ah, “a laisiangtho kammalte kimuam hilo, ami mahmah kha tawh kidimsak hi. Humuam cihpen a kammal ding muamtawm hilo hi. A mi mahmah hizaw hi. Khasiangtho tawh kidim mi sungah ngaihsutna hong piang hi. Van- tung’ lung- sim leh deihna in mihing’ lungsim leh deihna tawh hong kigawm ciangin; tua mi paukhiat kammalte pen Pasian’ kammal hipah hi”—Selected Messages, book 1, p. 21.

Mangmuhna leh kamsang’ mangte ahihleh, EGWhite in “lungsim humuam” van tung pulaakna leh mihing theihna ki- gawmna ci in um hi. “Laisiangtho pen, Pasian hong piak thumaan hi a,vantung leh mihing kigawm in mihing’ kammal tawh aki- genkhia hi” (Selected Messages, book 1, p. 25) ci in gelh hi.

Pasian in Ama’ thupuakte a kamtaai te in a telcian theih- na’ng lampi tampi zang thei hi. Hite lakah aiawhna, kammal leh ngaihsutna cihte in kamsangpa lungsim sungah nasem ding hi. EGWhite phutkhak thute zong laisiangtho laikungpute mahtawh kibang hi.

AThumna, EGWhite in amah mihing ahihna tawh a phutkhak leh kizopna khempeuh pen humuapna hikhinlo cizaw hi. “Thu mawkmawkte gen ding hun om a, thumawkmawk te lung- sim sungah om ding a, laikhak mawkmawkte gelh in naseppih lawmleh gualte lai kikhak hi. Tua bang kammalte leh thupuaknate pen Pasian’ Kha Siangtho humuapna hang zong hi tuanlo hi” — Selected Messages, book 1, p. 39.

Ama’ laigelhte i sim ciangin hih kilamdanna teng phawk- khak ding kisam a, adiak in amah mitawh a kizopna leh nisim thuciaptehna ah hipha diak hi. EGWhite in amah a ki humuapna teng leh akilahna teng atelcianna theihtelna in ama’ laigelhte ngai- hsut piaknate a simte in a khengval in khiat khak loh ding thupi hi.
