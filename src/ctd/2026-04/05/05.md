---
title:  A Kigelh Thupuakte
date:   28/10/2026
---

Thugenna pawlkhatte in mihing’ lungsim sukha mahmah in a tangthu nangawn uh kheelzo hiau thei hi. Tua thugenna te a ki ciapteh keingal leh, tua kammalte kimangngil zelzel in ahunzui in

beilel thei hi. Tua hi a, kampaute kigelhsuk leh kipkho pah lian a, tua kipkhona in tua thupuakte ki khahkhia kikkik sakthei hi

Egypt pan a paikhiat khit uh sawtlo in Israel in Amalek mite a zawhgawp khit uh ciangun, Pasian in Moses kiangah “hihthu naphawk tawntung na’ng un laibu sungah gelh in” (Pai. 17:14) cihi. Laisiangtho laibu masapen bunga leh Late 90 na a gelh- pa Moses pan kipan in laibu 66 khempeuh zong Kha Siangtho makaihna tawh a kigelh vive mah ahi uh hi.

`Pai. 17:14; John 20:30, 31; 1 Cor. 10:11; leh Mang. 1:19 simin. Pasian in Ama’ kamsangte kiangah a piak thute gelh dingin a sawlna hangthu bangteng omthei ding hiam?`

Laisiangtho bu tuamtuamte pen a laikungpu tetawh kizui in lai luanzia tuamtuam mah om hi. Maikal sinna sungah humua- pdan aniam a sang kideidanlo ahih manin, laikung pu khempeuh in amau’ hihtheihdan ciatmahtawh ahih uh cih mu ding hihang. Laisiangtho munkhatkhat te pen eima upna leh nundan tawh a kizawitawn zawdeuh zong bel omthei ahihhang, tua thumaan in a dang thumaan te sang in humuapna lianzaw cih zong om tuan lo hi.

Laisiangtho laikungpute in amau akihumuam thupuak kam- tawh a genkhiat khat ahihkeileh zong mimuan khat in a gen khat peuh lai tawh gelhsuk thei hi. Thuhilhkikna laibu sungah, Moses in, a sih madeuh in Israel tungah a genkhiat nunung thute gelh hi. Lungdamthu laibu li teng in zong, Zeisu’ leitung ah anaseplai a sermon teng khumsuk ditdet hi.

Khatveivei ciangin kamsangte in humuaploh thute khawng zong helin khum thei hi (Sawl. 17:28; 1Cor. 15:33; Titus 1:12) Satan’ kammal nangawn kigelh lai hi (Pian. 3:1-5, Mate 4:1-11).

Kamsangte in midangte lungsim sungpan a lunggulh dan leh a ngaihdan khawng zong ciaptehsak theilai a, mihing ahihna uh tawh amau’ dawnthuk kikna zong pulaak theilai hi (Dan. 8:27; Mang. 22:8, 9). Tua banah, laisiangtho in etteh pahtheih ding lim hoih tampitak leh pelhngiat ding thusia tampitak mah zong hong pulaak hi (1 Cor. 10:1- 11).

Laisiangtho sim ciangin, kam hoihkim picing tawh kigelh hituanlo ahihhang, sittel nahun sungah a puk theilo thupuakte hong pia hi cih phawkhuai mahmah hi.
