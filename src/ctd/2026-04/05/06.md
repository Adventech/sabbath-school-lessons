---
title:  Pawidante, Lim lehLiimte, Gentehnate
date:   29/10/2026
---

Laisiangtho sungah limlaimal leh gentehna tampitak om hi. Gentehna in laisiang tholui biakpiakdan sungah, tuanpupate biak- na taute, Moses’ biakbuk leh Jerusalem biak innpi cihte khempeuh in; singlamteh tungah Christ hong kipiakkhiatna leh vantung ah Siampilianpa ahihna limpua vive ahi hi (Heb. 8:1-6).

Daniel genkholna sungah, leitung kumpite leh gamte pen milimpi khat pumpi tunga tetawh lim kipuasak hi (Dan. 2:31- 45), ganhing tuamtuam leh kii te (Daniel 7 leh 8) leh kumpite nih (Daniel 11) ahi hi. Tuabang in limpua Mangmuhna sungah om a, Mangmuhna 13 sung ahi hi.

Zeisu in Ama’ thuhilhna sungah, Nuntakna Khomun kici hi (John 6:35, 41-58), leitung khuavak (John 8:12, John 9:5), leng- gui taktak (John 15 :1-8) cihi. Khaici vawhpa thugentehna (Mate 13:1-9, 18-23) leh a dangte (Mate 13:24-52) cihbang in Zeisu in Ama’ kumpigam khantohna pen piansaknate tawh genteh hi.

`Ezekiel 4:1-8 simin. Banghang in Pasian in kampau ziau lello in pawi paidan in thupuak bawlsese hi ding hiam?`

Ezekiel 4:1-8 pen pawi bangin a kigual sitset khat hi a, kamsangpa in ama’ pumpi pen Jerusalem bangin kineih ding a, veilam nuainung in ni 390 siklup ding; tua khit ciang in Judah innkuan limpua in taklam nuainung in ni 40 siklup leuleu ding ci hi. Muhtheih ding a dangkhat ah, vantung in Hosea pen, Israel mite cihtaklohna limpua in numei ginalo Gomer tawh tengding in sawl hi (Hos. 1:2-9).

Pawilak bangdan in Ezekiel leh Hosea tegel a pen tuni ei a dingin dipkuathuai liang hi. A deihna bulpi pen: amau dipkuat ding mah hi a, ei bek hilo, tualai a mipite mah mah dipkua saknu- am hi. Kamsangte vauna a kinialthal ciangin (2 Khang. 36:15-21), a nunungpen Pasian’ hehpihna in mihing te dinmun pen pawilah bangdan in lakleh mihing te in kisikna maan taktak neithei ding a cihnopna ahi hi. Tuabang pawi lahbang in a lah ciangin, amaute’n zong Pasian thuhilhna a thupisim theih khakleh a cihnopna ahi hi.

`Eite Laodicea mite ihih laitak in, mikhat peuh in eima’ din- mun lahna in bang pawi khat peuh tawh hong lak tammaw? (Mang. 3:14-18.)`
