### Tukalsung Simding
Heb. 1:1, 2; 2Tim. 3:15-17; 2Pet. 1:18-21; Pai. 17:14; Mang. 1:19; Ezek. 4:1-8.

> <p>Kamngah</p>
> “A piangbaih ding thute, Pasian’ nasemte tun- gah alah dingin Pasian in Zeisu tungah a thupia a, Zeisu in maangsungah alah thute ahi hi. Christ in vantung mi sawl a, nasempa John kiangah hihthute kilangsak hi. John in zong amuhsa thu khempeuh nagen leuleu hi.” (Mang. 1:1, 2).

Sawltak John in Sawltak sawmlehnih sungah mun thupi penna ah om hi (Luka 8:51; Mate 17:1; Mate 26:36, 37). Christ vantung a kahtoh khit ciangin, John in ama’ nasep Jerusalem ah semzom hi. Khuapi a kisuksiat madeuh (A.D. 70) in, Ephesus khua ah lal in Asia gamkee te a Roman ukna nuai a om pawlpite kem hi. Roman kumpi Domitian in Christiante a bawlsiat laitak in, sawltakpa pen gampan kihawlkhia in, Aegean tuipi sung Patmos tuikulh tung ah kikoih hi.

Patmos ah, John in kamsang thupuakte ngah a, Asia gamkee a om pawlpi sagih te bek hilo khangtawntung in Christiante a ding ahi hi (Mang. 1:1-3, 9-11). A kamsaanna taibaan sikkual omzia pen: Pa Pasian kiangpan, Tapa Zeisu Christ, vanmipa, John kiang ah, tua pan laisimte leh thungaite cihbang in omtuak diudeu hi.

Hihdan pen mangmuhna humuap dan kuhkal mahmah khat hi a, Pasian in kamsangte tungtawn in mihing tungah Ama’ thupuak hong piakdan ahi hi.

Tukal sinna in, mangmuhna humuapzia tuamtuam, vantung pan thupuakte mihing kiang hong tuntheihna’ng lampi tuamtu- am ahi kam tawh genkhiatna, laitawh gelhna leh pawilah bangin lahna cihte tungtawn in mihing tawh hong kiho cihte hiding hi.
