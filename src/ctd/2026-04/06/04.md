---
title:  Lailam ah Huhnate
date:   03/11/2026
---

`Jeremiah 36:17, 18 leh Rom. 16:22 simin. Hih laisiangtho munte in Laisiang tho laikungpute’ lailam huhna a zat uhtawh kisai bangteng gen hiam?`

A hihtuak thu khat, Jeremiah in ama’ thupuakte a gelh ciangin, genkholna thute pen a muan mahmah lai-aat khat in, ci- amtehsak in gam makaite kiangah zong gensawn hihtuak hi. Lai- aat nei in vantung thupuakte nangawn hawmsawn dingin a zang kamsang pen laisiangtho sungah Jeremiah bek hilo hi.

Laisiangthothak sungah, Tertius in “hi laikhak agelh kei- mah” (Rom. 16:22) acih tei hang, Romte laibu apatna lamah Paul’ deihna bangbek in gelh a hihlam thukip na om hi. Tertius pen Paul’ kampau bangbang laisungah a ciapteh sakpa, lailam ahuhpa, lai- aatpipa ahi hi.

A hicihpen Paul a dingin thu lamdanglua zong hilo hi. Paul’ laikhak Colossian leh Thessalonian a thukhupna, vaikhakna ten- gah Paul in “keima’ khut tawh” (Col. 4:18, 2Thess. 3:17) kong gelh hi cihi. Tuabang acihna in tua laikhak buppi amah endik sitset a cihnopna ahi hi.

Hihthu in ama’ laikhak a gelhsak dingin mi’khut bel cial cihna ahi hi. Hih thu om hangin tua laikhakte Paul aa’ taktak mah maw – a siazaw lai in, hivet kei e, a cinuamte a dingin – Paul in ama’ laikhak ahihna lunghianna a omlohna dingin, a thukhupna ah ama’ khutgelh tawh ama’ laikhak taktak ahihna kipsak hi.

“A entel mi tampitakte in, Paul’ laikhak sungah lailuanzia tuamtuam omziazua pen lailam huhte hang hi ci uhhi. Tuamah bangin Peter laikhakte: 2Peter pen 1Peter tawh lailuanzia a kibat- lohna zong, Peter in Greekpau tawh laikhak a gelhding ciangin, lailam huhna lai-aat cial ahih manin tuabang lailuanzia kibatlohna omthei ahihna teltak in honglak thei uhhi.

Greeklai a siam mahmah Paul nangawn in lai-aat cial ahi- hleh; Aramaic laibek a theipa Peter in cialsem zaw ding cih zong thupaizia hi a, Greeklai in a gelhkik a lai- aatte in a lailuanzia dingpen amau’ teeldan in huzaap zaw cihding ahi hi” —The SDA Bible Commentary, vol. 5, p. 186.

Hithu hangin Pasian’ kamtaaite ngaihdan leh khiatna tungah cihtakna i neih mahbang in; laimal leh laigual phuahdan peuhmah pen, a kicialtawm lailam huhte’ laisiamdan tungah om ding hi. A kihumuam ngaihsutna a thupi hi a, tua aki humuam thute pulaak khiatna kammal ciangtan lian cihbang omtuanlo hi.
