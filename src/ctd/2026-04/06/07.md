---
title:  Ellen G. White’ Laigelhte
date:   06/11/2026
---

Ellen G. White’ phutkhak leh laigelhte in laisiangtho sunga Pasian kamtaaite a mahtawh a kibatna ciangkhat omveve hi. Ama kiangah a kilah bangbang laitawh gelh dingin vantung in thupia hi.

1909 kumin, EGWhite in, “Ka nuntakna beilawh dektak in ka liamna hangin, ka cidamna thanem in lai gelhzolo in ka om hi.

Nitak khat ciangin Topa’ vanmi khat kalupna gei ah hong pai in ‘kong piak khempeuh lai in nagelh ding hi’ hong ci a, ‘lai gelhthei ke’ng’ ci in ka dawng hi. Thu hongpia kik leuleu in, ‘Kongpiak- sate gelh in’ hong cih leu leu ciangin, hanciam sawmin, singpeek khat tawm in laigelh kasawm leh, laimal hoihtak in a gelhthei ka- hita hi” —In General Conference Bulletin, May 31, 1909.

Humuap ngah laisiangtho laikungpute mahbang in EG- White in zong lailam huhna zang hi. A lai-aatnu in lai endikte hihding khempeuh hihsak-a thupuakte laitawh gelhsak ahihhang, amau’ ngaihdan peuhmah helhetlo uhhi. Amaute pen laigelhpihte hivetlo uh hi. Amau’ naseppen EGWhite in a bawlsa tengpen khut tawh gelhsak in kamtawh genkhiat sak hibek hi.

EGWhite in zong ama’ laigelhte a hawmsawnna ah ahuh dingin, mi’ lai panzong a kawmna ciangkhat omveve hi. Ama’ lai- simte kiangah a laibu zatte vai a gen ciangin; tua laibu neite khem- ding leh a thumaanlo in zat ding ngiimna neilo a; ama’ genkhiat nop thumaan thutakte a pulak siamna dingin a zatnop kammal hoihnono amau kiangpan kingah hi cihi. Kibang lohna leh kiciat sitsetlohna te e leh?

“Khial theingeilo cih tawh kisai in, Tuabang ci ngei ke’ng; A khial ngeilo Pasian bek omhi” (Selected Messages, book 1, p. 37) cihi.

Ama’ laigelhte lakah a telhak mahmah leh mitawh a kibang theilo peuh amu om khasop ding hi. EGWhite’ mihing ahihna pen vantung in paihkhiat sak deklo hi. Eite mah bangin EGWhite zong khangto khangcing ding a, ama’ laigelhte sungah khantohna leh khangcinna kilang khia ding hi. A tungah omsa mahbang in zong suutkikhuai a, ama’ kammal khempeuh vantung panin tangtak in hong paisuk cih a ummite zong amah’n puah phatsak zawsop hi.

“Thu mawkmawkte gen ding hun om, thu mawkmawk ngaihsut ding hun om, lai mawkmawkte gelh in naseppihte khat- lehkhat kikhak ding hun om hi. Tua bang thute, tuabang thu pulak- nate ciang khawng peuhmah Pasian’ Kha Siangtho in hong ukcip lianglo hi” —Selected Messages, book 3, p. 58.
