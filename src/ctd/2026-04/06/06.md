---
title:  Vantung pan Thupuak leh Mihing Kicinlahna
date:   05/11/2026
---

`Paul in 2 Cor. 4:7 Pasian’ sumkholna koici gen hiam?`

Ezra leh Nehemiah tezong amau’ laigelhte pen tangthu kici- amtehnate laibulpi in zang cih lahna hipah hi. Ezra in Jerusalem a ding Persia kumpi thupiaklai (Ezra 4–7) leh, Nehemiah in tangthu ciaptehna sungpan khangtangthu ciaptehna zong bawlthei ahihna om hi (Neh. 7:5-72).

Tua thukhun mahpen laisiangthothak sungah zong om hi. “Luka phutkhak thu om bangin Kha Siangtho humuapna in mi- hing pianzia lungsimte hemkhia tuanlo in, tuate tawh kipawl- thei zaw cih kitel mahmah hi. Tuabang in Kha Siangtho humuap ngahte in Christ nundanpen kamgen ahi a, lai a gelh a hi zongin thubulkip ombangbang zongkhia uh a, a kizomthei dingteng zom in pulakkhia uhhi”—The SDA Bible Commentary, vol. 5, p. 669.

Jude in zong laisiangtho sung akihello 1 Enoch 1: 9 panin Jew mite nasepzia khat a lakkhiatna zong om hi. Paul in, amin genkhialo in, Greek mipilte thugenna a laksak zong omlai hi (Sawl. 17:28; Titus 1:12).

`Laisiangtho laikungpute pen mangmuhna leh mang sungah pulaakna ngah zeluh ahihhang, laibulpi dangte a zat uh ciangun, Kha Siangtho humuapna peuh mah hemkhia tuan hetlo uhhi. Tua`

Paul in Pasian’ sumkholna pen “leibeel” (mihing) hi a, Pa- sian’ vangliatna pen eima’ thanemna sungah kilakkhia ding acihna ahi hi. Pasian’ kamtaaite zong mihing’ pianzia khengval in mawh- baang lote hituanlo cih laisiangtho in hong lak hi. Ettheih pak din- gin (Moses, Elijah, David, Jonah, leh Peter). Hih mite’ laigelhte zong mihing hihna tawh kicinlohna tetawh kipelh hetlo uhhi.

“Vantung leh mihing kipawlkhopna pen, Christ sungah hong kilang a, laisiangtho sungah zong om hi. Thumaan hong kip- ia khempeuh ‘Pasian’ humuapna tawh hong kipia’ ahihhang; mih- ing’ kammal tawh a kipulakna ah mihing’ thanemna mah omveve hi”— Ellen G. White, Testimonies for the Church, vol. 5, p. 747.

Pasian’ kamtaaite’ thumaan pulak ding vantung in a sap ciangin a mihing hihna uh hepkhiat sak tuanlo cih 2Cor. 4: 7 sun- gah Paul in gelh hi. Eite tawh a kituakthei ding leh gentel ding a haksatna munte, kammalte, a laigelhte, kampauna te pen Kha Siangtho in khaktan hetlo hi. Hibang mun a tawmmahmah hang, lungsim zai neilote in palkhak ding bangin ngaihsun thei uhhi.

Laisiangtho a um Christiante in Pasian’ thu sungah eima telna zah tawh ki-aap ding hang a, telzawhloh te ahihleh koih ding hipeuh hi.

A kibatlohna pawlkhatte bel, khangthakte’teikhialhna, a thupilo khialhna neunono te hang leh, humuap ngah laikungpute kingakna mite tawh kisai zaw hi. Buaina a om lai siangtho munte ahihleh, laikungpute kammalte telmaw/tello banah, sinbeh mawh sinbeh lo cihte hang zong ahi hi.

Khialhna tuamtuam leh kibatlohna teng siansuah theihloh hang, tuate in a thupuak a dingin a thupilua nahi khollo lel hi. Pasian’ kamtaaite in kicinna a neihloh uh hang, van tung thumaan pen citaktak in pulaak hi cih muanhuai hi.

`Pasian in mawhneilo Ama’ vanmite, Ama’ kamtaai dingin teelzawlo in; eimah a puksa mite mah banghangin hong teel- zaw hiding hiam?`
