---
title:  Lai Bulpite
date:   04/11/2026
---

“Thugen Siampa, mipil ahih banah, amah in ama’ theihteng mite tungah ahilh hi. Amah in paunakte limtakin sin a, amaan ahih- na thu hoihtakin entel hi. Thugen Siampa in a zaknophuai kammal khawm a, ama’ gelh kammalte amaan taktak ahi hi” (Thuhilhsia. 12:9, 10).

Atung a laisiangtho munpan, Solomon in paunak agelh khempeuh, ama’ phuah tawm hilo ci in hong simsak hi. Pasian’ humuamna nuai ah, “a zaknophuai thu tampite” “zongkhia” ci hi. Paunak 31 sung a numei hoih i cihcih pen, Solomon’ nu na hilozaw in, kua hiam cihzong a kitheilo Kumpi Lemuel’ nu na hizaw hi (Pau. 31: 1). Paunak laisiang- tho tungah Egypt lai in hu- zaap neimahmah cih thukipkho khat hipah hi. Tuamah bangin, Pa- sian’ mite tangthu teng a kiciapteh theihna dingin, laisiangtholui sunga humuap kam- sangte in, tangthu thupite kiciaptehna pan zong la thei uh cihna ahi hi.

`1Kum. 11:41; Luka 1:1-4; leh Jude 14, 15 simin. Hih- munteng panin, humuap laikungpute in laisiangtho sungah a kihel khollo thubul pawlkhat zong zangthei cih koibang in hong theisak hiam? pen ei a dingin a theihhuai ngaihdan khat bang hang in hithei ding hiam?`
