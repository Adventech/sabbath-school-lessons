### Tukalsung Simding
Pai. 17:14; Isa. 13:1; Jer. 36:17, 18; Rom. 16:22; Luka 1:1-4; Jude 14, 15; 2 Cor. 4:7.

> <p>Kamngah</p>
> “Laisiangtho sunga om thu khempeuh pen eite hilhna ding, eite lam- etna nei in thuakzawhna ding leh i tha- hat theihna dingin a kigelh ahi hi” (Rom. 15:4).

Pasian ngiat in a phuah la na kisin nuamlel hiam? Thuhil- hkikna 32 sungah, Pasian lamlahna tawh lamal tampi Moses in gelh ngiat hi (Thkna. 31:19-22). Kamsang laigelhte kingaihsun zelnapi la lampeuhmah kiphawk kha ngiatlo hi. Late tampi takte in humuap la vive hi a, a lamal lian Topa ngiatin a uklian keileh hibek ding hi.

Laisiangtho sungah kamsangte in Pasian’ thupuak a haw- mkhiat kikna lampi thum om cikhin zo hi: (1) Kam tawh (2) lai kigelh tawh (3) pawi lahdan leh limte cihbang ahi hi.

Tukal sungteng laisiangtho lai kigelhte bulphuh ding hi- hang. Pasian kammal pen kamtaaite in sermonna leh kamsangte kamgenkhiatna om bangbang in Laisiangtho in ciamteh hi.

Kamsangte laigelh danleh a kammal luandan tuamtuam te endih in: pawi lahdan, tangthu, la kam, tamlawh kam leh laikha- kte cihbang in om hi. Teeltuam pasal numei te in Kha Siangtho in amau a makaih dandan in mihing kampaumal mah tawh genkhia uhhi.

Tukal sung amau laigelhte ending hang a, lai-aat huh (sec- retaries) zatna leh, tona omte i simkhak ciangin lungsim sungah koihkhinkhian dingte enpha ding hihang.
