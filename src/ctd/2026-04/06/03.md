---
title:  Lai a Nam leh Omdante
date:   02/11/2026
---

Laisiangtho sungah laigelhdan tuamtuamte humuap kamta- aite zatdandan in om ziazua hi. Pawlkhat in nisim thu ciaptehdan in gelh hi (Gam. 33:1-49). A langkhat ah Daniel, Zechariah leh John cihte in leitung hunbeina ding; lim lianpipi te, vannuai bup thukhun leh vantung nate pianzia cihbang te gelh uhhi.

`Isaiah 13:1 simin. Laisiangtho in Isaiah thupuak pen koibang in pulak hiam?`

Kamsangte mangmuhna pen khatveivei “Mangmuhna” kici a, Pasian kiangpan hopihna leh pulaakna hi a, (pawlkhat in “van- gik” ci in letzaw uhhi). Letkhia mi a thakte leuleu un lakam tawh agelh uh hi a, kibatna nei tampi om cihi. Kibatnanei cihpen a omsa laigual khat genphakik ahi a, a om masa tawh kibang kik cihbang ahi hi.

Etpak dingin: “tuani ciangin mite in, amaute a bawlpa pi ngaihsut ding uh a, amaute’ mit in Israel te’ Siangtho Pa en ding uhhi” (Isa. 17:7). Piangsakpa leh Siangtho Pa cihpen kibang phi- al a, khatlehkhat teci kipanpih uhhi. Kamsangte in kammal huh dingin lim leh liim, a taktak sang in uangzaw in genna cihbang omlai hi. Eite a dingin tua thute a kitelzaw deuh dingin, a laigelh dan theih ding thupi hi.

Laisiangtho sungah a om pahpah laigelhdan a dangkhat leu- leu pen “thuciamna tayasuai” na ahi hi. Jeremiah 2: 4-9 sungah minamte in thuciamnate a palsat uh manun Pasian’ mite Pasian ki- angah tayasuai ek hi. Thukhenzum limpua in laikungpu in Pasian pen kumpi lampan maidingte (sheihnay) Topa in a thukhen ding te cihbang in khen hi.

Laisiangtho sungah kamsang laigelhte, tangthu, la leh gen- tehnate zong om hi. Jeremiah zong ama’ thupuak khempeuh lai- bu a bawl dingin kisawl hi (Jer. 36:2). Laisiangtho thak ah bel a tamzaw mimal leh mipi tungah laikhak vive pian ahi hi. Hih bang in Pasian hong hopihna dante pen khat sang khat hoihzaw cih- peuhmah omlo hi. Paul in ama’ laikhakte sungah Khasiangtho hu- muapna kiciantak in lakthei a hih manin ama’ laikhakte zongpen tanglai a kamsangte lai zahmah in gikna neihsak huai hi (1Cor. 7:10, 12, 40).

`Laisiam pawlkhat in laisiangtho sung a Pasian panmun sangin mihing’ panmun peuh pibawl zaw pongmawk thei uhhi. Tuapen banghang in khalam kiamsukna hithei ding hiam?`
