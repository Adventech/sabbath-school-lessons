---
title:  Laigelh dingin Sapna
date:   01/11/2026
---

Pasian in mihing a hopih masaklam pawllai in maitang kimu in kam tawh hopihna, aw ngiat zasak, mang sungah kihil leh vanmite sawl cihbang hi zel hi. Pasian in kam tawh a genkhiat khempeuh, vantung lamlahna tawh laibu sungah a kigelhna pen Moses hunpan hipan hi.

`Laisiangtho sungah a kigelh masapen in bang hiam? Paikhiatna 17:14.`

Laisiangtholui sungah kamtaaite kiangah Pasian in Ama’ kammalte Amite a dingin kemcing ding cihthu mun tampi ah mu hihang. Moses mahbang in, Jeremiah zong, “Lai zial khat la inla, tua sungah Josiah kumpi a suah kum, nang kong hopih masak- pan a kipan tuni ciangdong Israel gam leh gamdang khempeuhte’ langkhat ah ka thugen khempeuh gelh in” (Jer. 36:2).

Amah zong genkholna thute lai in gelh ding thu kipia a: “Ju- dah mite in amau tungah tunsak ding kangaihsut siatna khempeuh za in, mi khempeuh in a siatna uh panin kiheina in neikhathei ding uh a, Keimah in zong amau’khialhna leh mawhnate ka maisak thei kha ding hi” (Jer. 36:3).

Tua genkhol thute pen mailam a thupiang dingte theihk- holhna ding leltak hilo a; mipite hotkhiatna lamah makaih dingin a kipia ahi hi. Pasian’ kamtaaite in khuahun tuam tuam sungah gel- hkha uhhi. Moses in laisiangtho laibu masapen bunga teng sehnel gam sungpan gelh hi. David in zong a late tampitak mah pen Saul in sabet in abet lai in a phuah ahi hi (Late 52, 54, 57, 59). Daniel in a mangmuhna tengpen Babylon leh Medo-Persia gam kumpi inn sungpan a gelh ahi hi.

Alangkhat ah, Paul in a laikhak agelhte Ephesians, Philippians, Colossians, leh Philemon cihte pen thongsungah a gelh hi a; John in zong Mangmuhna laibu pen Patmos tuikulh tungah hawlkhiat a thuak laitak in a gelh ahi hi. Pasian’ kamtaaite in laigelh ding thu a kipiak ciangin, amau’ dinmun bang peuh ahi zongin, Kha Siangtho in amaute thahat na pia hi.

Zeisu’ thugen khempeuh zong Pasian in a teelsa mimuanhuaite tungtawn in laibu tawh eikiang hong tung theita hi. Tua pen Pasian mimuan kamtaaite in cihtakna tawh ciamteh in kemcing ding cihna ahi hi.

`Laisiangtho khawng original lah hibeek lo khawng muangzo mahmah kei ning a ci mikhat tawh nakimuh ciangin na koici dawnthei ding hiam?`
