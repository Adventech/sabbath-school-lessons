---
title:  Pasian’ Kamtaaipa tungah Hehna
date:   08/11/2026
---

Moses, Pasian’ teelsa makaipa, in Kamciam Gam zong in mihonpi kihel neunau khat sehnel gam lakah a makaih lai in a nopna, a natna theitel dikdek hi.

`Gam. 14:4-11 simin. Hih munah bang thu piang hiam? Mipite in Moses leh Aaron tungah banghang in heh uh hiam?`

Hih mun limtak in simleng Israel mite in Pasian muanglo uh ahih manun a tungah hehuh cihi. Moses leh Aaron te unau pen mi- hing lakah Pasian’ aiawh mimah ahih manun mipi’ hehna mungt- up ahihding baihlam mahmah hi. Pumpi leh lungsim bitna ding pen mihing khempeuh a dingin lawpna bulpi ahi hi. Tuategel hong kilauhthawn ciang, ahih keileh pumpi, lungsim leh lungmuanna a ding lauhuai i sak ciangin; heh khawng suak in ngeina om kisalo thei hi.

`Gam. 13: 26-30 simin. Banghang in Israel mite laulua uh hiam? Caleb in bang kammal tawh amau hehnem thei hiam?`

Kamciam Gam a valuah theihna dingin Israel in amau talsik ding tona lianpite a dingin laulua mahmah uhhi. “Mihing in ama lungsim uplohna lamah a paisak ciangin; Satan khutnuai ah amau mah a kikoihtawm bang hi a, Satan in koiciang dong makaih ding cih kithei zolo hi” EGWhite, Patriarchs and Prophets, p. 389.

Hihbang hunin Moses in kantan zawhloh ding zah in a lian makaite tona khat talsik hi. A sawtlo in mipite patauhna pen Aaron leh Moses tungah hehgawpna leh Pasian langdona in hong kilai- hta hi. Mipi in Pasian’ teelsa kamsang/makaipipa laihna dingin makaithak hong zontak ciangun (Thkna. 34:10-12; Gam. 14:4), Joshua leh Caleb te in a puan uh balkeek in, mipite in a lau uh sangun Pasian muangzaw dingin thuum uh a:

“Topa phunsan kei hamtang un. Amaute pen zialmang pa- kding ihih manin, tua gam mite kihta kei un. Amau kiang panin amau’ kidalna kilakhia khin a, eite tawh Topa hong om hi; amaute kihta kei un” (Gam. 14:8, 9) ci uhhi.

`Nopsak hun in Pasian muan ding baih lua lel hilo hiam? Thu- sia hong tun tak tak ciangin bang kici hiam? Pasian na muan ciangin na lung himawh mahmah zaw- sop thei hiam? Na koicih hiam?`
