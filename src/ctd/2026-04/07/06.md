---
title:  Eite a ding Ettehhuai
date:   12/11/2026
---

Laisiangtholui sunga tangthute pen, a dahhuai in dipkuathu- ai mah taleh, thuhoih ding in hong kipia hizaw a: Pasian langdona tungtawn in hong piangthei thute zong kisin theihna’ng ahi hi.

`1 Cor. 10: 6-12 simin. Paul cihdan tak hileh, kamsangte in amau’ laibu sunga a gelhte banghang in Pasian in humuam hiding hiam?`

Hihmun ah Paul in, a beisa leh tuhun kizopna lamkhat giit a, tanglai Israel in Zeisu pen Topa leh Honpa in a saan bangun tuhun pawlpi in zong saang ahihna kizomsak ding in lamtang khat hong giitsak hi (Gal. 6:14-16). Abeisa hunin mihing leh Pasian kizopna te ahoih/asia khempeuh nutsiat pampaih ding hilo hi.

Pasian in thupi a sakmahmah thukhamte hongpia a, “Am- aute tungah apiang khem- peuh eite a dingin hilhkholna ahi hi” (1Cor. 10: 6). Thudangkhat ah, abeisa hun pen mai- lam hun kamhonna cih ding ahi hi. Abeisa thupiangte in tulaitak a thupiangte leh mailam a thupiang dingte hong theikhol sak hi.

Laisiangtho leh kamsangte thupuak in hong hanthotna te don peuhleng, khanglui mite’ khialhna bang nungzuih dingin Pa- sian’ hong deihsakna hilo cihi. Athu ngai in, mang leng; i nuntak- na kip ding a, khangto ding hihang (2 Khang. 20:20).

Mi pawkhat in khalam khantohna khawngpen innhoih, mawtaw hoih, puanhoih, neih leh lamh peuh tawh pulak thei uhhi. Pasian in eima kisap bangbang piak ding hong kamciam khin a, thudang te ah khantoh mahbang in khalam zong hong khangto sak nuam hi (3 John 2). Christian nundan sungah khalam picinna taktak in Pasian’ thupuak manna hong kal ngiat hi.

Pasian mawkpau hetlo hi. Apau nakleh AThu in khang tawntung hipah hi. A vang liatna leh a thahatna beingeilo hi (Isa. 40:8). Tuate khempeuh “hun beikuan a om eite’ etteh ding” (1 Cor. 10:11) ahi hi. Tuabang ahih takpi leh tua thute tuhun in bangzah in zat huai mahmah hiam?

`Laisiangtho sung a langdona leh uplahna te i simsim ciangin, tuabang khialhna te nabawl kik khakloh na’ng in bang pilna peuh nangah thei hiam?`
