`Jeremiah 36:20-26 simin. Kumpi inn sungah bang thu piang a, kamsangte’ vauna makaite in koibang in dwngthuk hiam?`

Judah a kisuksiat ding thu a than laitakin Jeremiah pen Pa- sian’ kamsang awging ahi hi. Pasian in Judah tungah lungdam het- lo ahihna kum tampi sungmah Jeremiah in vau sak den a, Babylon in hong zogawp ding a hong galmat ding hi ci in thuhilh hi (Jer. 6:8- 19, Jer. 26). Topa in amau honkhia nuam a, amau un thu mang ding uhhi.

Judah’ mawhna pen tamlua, uanglua in kikheel lo uhhi. Milim biakna, naupang meihal biakpiakna, zualzangna, ngongtat- na cihte pen Jeremiah’ hunlai in ki ngeinaseh lel a, kamsangpa’ vauna almawh deksuailo uhhi. Jeremiah kamsaanna nasep a tawp- na lam hong naih ciangin, Pasian in a lampial a mite a zolna nasia semsem hi taleh, Topa in hon- khia dingin hanciam tawntung hi.

Jeremiah 32 sungah, Pasian in Jeremiah leitang lei ding a, a thu lengige ding cihi. Sangpen Topa in cihi: “Hih gamah in- nte, lote leh lenggah lote mite in leikik ta ding uhhi” (Jer. 32:15). Kisikkik ding leh ki puahphat ding a nunung pen zolna Jeremi- ah in a gelhsakna zong Pasian in a itna mah ahi hi. Jehoiakim in tua thupuak a dawnthukna pen Pasian, a thupuak leh, kamtaaipa maiah kihhuai hi.

`Jeremiah 36: 27-31 simin. Jehoiakim in a simmawh gamtatna pen Pasian in koibang in dawngthuk hiam?`

Khatveivei ciangin Pasian thupuakte, a kamtaaite, pampaih in susia leng tua thupuak bei lel ding hi ci in ki ngaihsun kha thei uhhi. Eimah’n Pasian’ kamsanna i san noplohna in zong ei a ding Pasian hong piak thupuak beisiangsak tuanlo hi. Eima kihhuai na hangin, eimah leh innkuanpih te’n i langdo nopna uh hangin hong tung ding gimnate khauhsak sem zaw hi.

Kumpi Jehoiakim a dingin Jeremiah’ kamsanna pen mihing khempeuh a dingin lauhuai mahmah hi (Jer. 36:30, 31): Pasian’ hong vaunate awlmawhlohna in eimah siatna hong tun ding a, eima mawhna leh gitlohna sulzui teng thuak veve ding hihang.

`Mihingte in a zahtak bawlloh hangin, eimah hong phakza- wh nading in Pasian’ hong itna tawh kisai bang gen hiam?`
