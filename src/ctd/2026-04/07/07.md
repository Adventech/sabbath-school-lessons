Ellen G. White’ tuupa Arthur White pen, Ellen G. White Estate ah director sem ngei hi a, tuapa in a pi in kamsang mangmuhna a zasim in ngah hi cihi. A mangmuhna te in minute tawm- khat pan nai dong in sawt a, mi tampi in theihpih uhhi. Khatveivei ciangin amah a umlo pawlkhat in mipilak khawng ah sittelna peuh hong bawl pong uhhi.

Laisiangtho kamsangte mahbang in EGWhite in mang- muhna sungah ngeina a khengvalna zong om hi. 1868 kum in, James White in, mangmuh laitak EGWhite’ din mun kimdildel in ciaptehna khat bawlsak hi. “A kiimlehpam a om nakhempeuh phawk vetlo hi”, husang lo; a taksa khauh gega in, guh kizopna teng kipbikbek hinapi suakta tak leh vangneitak in gamtang hi- athiat; a mitte kihong hi.

Mangmuhna pan hong paikhiatkhit ciangin, tawmvei sung khua mulo ahihhang, a mitmuhna bel kisialo hi. A mangmuh ding hunpen amah ngiat in zong theikhol hetlo a, a mangmuh hun ding cikciang, bangtan sawt ding cih kuamah’n uk thei ngiatlo hi (James White, Life Incidents, pp. 271-274).

Laisiangtho hunlai mahbang in mihingte dawnkik dan namtampi omhi. Pawlkhat in Pasian’ ngaihsut piakna sangth- ei a; pawlkhat in nial uhhi. EGWhite ngiat in “Pasian in Amite thuhilh dingin hongseh hi; tua vangikpi keitungah hongsuan a, hih thupuak a ngahte in amau dawnthukna vaipuak aneih kul uhhi. A nuam hetlo hih nasep keima a dingin teelmasa het ke’ng. Mihingte hong pahtakna hongtun ding nasep hihetkei. Honpa’ min leh tha- hatna tawh kasep theihzah kasem ding hi. Mihingte in hong za in/ hong pelh phial zongin; Kha Siangtho makaihna tawh Kei in vau ding, ngaihsutna pia ding, thuhilh ding hi’ng. Kei nasep pen kei’ nopsak na’ng hilo, nasep hong piapa vantung ka Pa deihna bang in kasem ding hi” —Testimonies for the Church, vol. 4, pp. 231, 232.

EGWhite’ kamsanna thupuakte a nialte in, ama’ kamsana thupuak te a mimal in EGWhite mahmah a umlo uh hilo in, biak- na vai upna tawh anial uh hizaw hi. EGWhite in ama’ kamsanna silpiak a umzolo te tungah nunnem in thusiam mahmah hi. Tu- abang mite pen ‘lungduaitak leh sanggam itna tawh hopih ding a, ei lamah ding kip maw ei lang khat ah a kitel dong tuacih ding hi’ —Testimonies for the Church, vol. 1, p. 328.

“EGWhite in ama’ teci panna kamsang silpiak pen Pasian hong piakngiat hi ci in kizo hi. Mite in lungsim siangtho in thu- maan a zon uhleh, hih keima silpiak pen thumaan in vangnei ahi- hlam mu dinguh hi cih um hi. A telpha nuamlaite, Pasian in tel- theihna pia dingin zong um hi. Eima’ upna pen thumaan lamah a kikhaikim thei dingin Pasian in teci kipkho te hong piakhin hi ci in gelh hi” —Testimonies for the Church, vol. 4, p. 231.
