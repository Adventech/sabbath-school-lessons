---
title:  Kineihkhem
date:   11/11/2026
---

Abeisa hun a mi’ gamtatna peuh na-et ciangin, nangma’ lungsim sungah, Kei hi peuhmah leng, tua mite’ hihbang peuhmah hih hetkei ning! naci ngei hiam? Lungsim hoih lel khatin midang gamtat banglian in kigamta samlo napi’n; tuabang dan mah in, tuabang khialhna temah tawh, eima’ lungsim sung ombangin mitsi teta in kigamta thei hi.

Hihthu pen Zeisu in Ama’ omzia hong hilhcianna ah: “Mi kineihkhemte aw! Nomau mit sunga om singluang hemkhia masa un; tuakhit ciangin nasanggampa’ mit sunga niinthem om na muthei pan ding uhhi” (Mate 7:5) cihi.

`Mate 23: 30-35 simin. Thupi mahmah leh a na mahmah bangthu khat Zeisu in gen hiam? Ama’ kammal panin bang vauna peuh ngahthei ding ihi hiam?`

Hihmun ah Zeisu in a muhdah khatpen Pharisee te kinei- hkhemna hi a, kikheel nuam hetlo uh ahih manun hong suksem zawsop hi. Pharisee te biakpiakna lel khawng Zeisu in cimtaak lua lelta hi. Puantual kaang silh diadua in khalam munsang a om uh hi napi, a sungvuah misi’guh bek a omlel zaakaang kizuut han tawh kibang lel ahih manun, Zeisu in cimtaklua ta hi (Mate 23:27, 28).

A mawhna uh a kikawkkhak nangawn zong, Zeisu in kisik- kik in hotkhiatna ngah theihna’ng hun apiak zelzel nangawn, amau’ mawhna uh pulaak in nusia cih om hetlo uh hi.

Hihbang mite tawh a kiphutkhak ciangin, Zeisu in “Mi ki- neihkhem te aw” a cih giap pen a hiding mah himawk hi. A laigual taktak pen “Laithei leh Pharisee te, mi kineih khem te aw, gimna na thuak ding uhhi” (Mate 23:14, 15, 23, 25, 27).

Amau’ nundan pen, thu khat gen in a dangkhat hihzaw uhhi. Tuabang leltawh kamsangpa vanaih uhhi. Kamsangte han khawng deihtak in zeem lehuh, upna sangtawi mahmah kisa uhhi. Tua sang a thupizaw khat, kamsangte kammal tungtawn in kisik ding peuhmah nial sinsen uhhi. Kamsangte vangneihna leh hehpihna sungah kibual nuam uh hinapi; kamsangte’ thugen tetawh kisawh- hel nuam hetlo uhhi.

Pasian’ nasemte han khawng pen meelhoihsak lenlan zaw- zen uh hinapi, Pasian’ kamtaaite’ sisan buasak ngeilo bangin om phiauphuau uhhi. Tua laitak mahin kamsangte tungsiah ah a lian- zaw tham Messiah nangawn that ding in ngiimna nei uhhi.

`Thukhun ombang in tuabangte gamta hetlo hihang a: a pualam ah biakna mi siusiau in, asunglam ah huaihamna leh huatna peuh i kidim kh1a diam?`
