### Tukalsung Simding
Gam. 13:26-30; Jer. 36:20-26; 2 Khang. 36:15, 16; Mate 23:30-35; 1 Cor. 10:6-12.

> <p>Kamngah</p>
> “Leitung beikuan hunsung ah a om eite a din- gin, hih thupiangte i etteh ding hi a, eite hilhkholna dingin laisung ah a kiciamteh ahi hi” (1 Cor. 10:11).

Tulai mi pawlkhatte in, amau maanna, amau’ phutkhak, amau ngaihdan, amau kisapna, amau’ gamgi, amau upna cihte genkhia ziahziah ngamlel uhhi. Thumaan peuh mah thukip hi a, gamdaihna ding om tuanlo ci uhhi. Tuabang in eimah’n thumaan i sakpong khat pen Zeisu Christ leh Ama’ thumaan tawh kituak keileh bang icih tam?

Pawlkhat in laisiangtho thumaan sangthei in, mawhna nu- sia uh hinapi; adangte in amau’ zuauthu mah kiplet tentan uhhi. Pawlkhat in thumaan dawnthukna in a gamtat uh kheel uh a; a dangte in langdo zawsop uhhi. Mihing’ tangthu in tuabang in pai hi.

Laisiangtho kamsangte pen mihingte zaknoploh thupuak- te tangko dingin kisam hi. Tua ciangin, kosiatna, pampaihna, lauhthawnna leh thahlup thuak phial uhhi (Heb. 11:32-38).

Tuate khempeuh almawh hetlo in Pasian’ kamsangte in ci- htakna tawh amau’ thupuak ngahte tangko uh a, vantung makaihna khawng ngak hithiat tuanlo uhhi. Hihthu in Pasian’ mite a dingin nuntakna hotkhiatna ding lamlahna ahi hi. Tua hi a, kamsangpa tungah bangpeuh a piang zongin hih thupuak pen a hawmsawn teitei ding ahi hi.
