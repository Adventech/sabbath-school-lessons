---
title:  "Thupuak Susia: Khen 2"
date:   10/11/2026
---

Mihing in kamsang silpiak nam tampi taktawh dawngthuk thei hi. Moses’ vai en leng, Israel in, Kamciam Gam a luttheihna dingun a masuan uh galte a kihtakna uhtawh, Moses leh Aaron langdo hong kipan uhhi.

Kum tampi khit ciangin, Kumpi Jehoiakim in zong Pasian’ kamsanglai suksiat sawmin, Pasian thupuak haltum hi. Hih thupiang pen thudang peuhtawh a piang hilo hi. Tanglai Israel leh Judah te peuhmah, mi tawh kibanglo, kamsangte nial vinven uhhi.

`2 Khang. 36:15, 16 simin. Pasian in Ama’ kamsangte tungtawn in Judah tungah a thupuak te Judah in koici dawngthuk hiam? Judah’ gamtatna Pasian in koibang in thukkik hiam?`

Jeremiah’ kamsanna hun in Kumpi Josiah, Jehoahaz, Je- hoiakim, Jehoiachin leh Zedekiah maan hun ciang dong huam- suk hi.Babylon gamin Nebuchadnezzar makaihna tawh Israel’ kongkhak gei hong tungta uhhi. Kum 21 bek a phapan Zedeki- ah kumpi in hong kahtoh ciangin Jeremiah in a genkhol thu khempeuh hong tangtungta hi (2 Khang. 36:11). Laisiangtho in “Topa a Pasian’ muhna ah amah asia gamta hi. Topa’ kammal a gensawn kamsang Jeremiah’ maiah amah kiniamkhiatlo hi” (2 Khang. 36:12) cihi.

Jeremiah’ kamsanna silpiak akipiak dan ngaihsun in. Pasian’ kamtektek tawh a paukhia bangin kimu hi. Tua kawmkal mahah, Zedekiah in kamsangpa’ maiah kiniam- khiat nuamlo tentan hi. Jeremiah zong eite khempeuh mahbang in mihing mah hi a, Topa’ aiawh mi, Topa a dingin paukhia hi. Jeremiah pen tuabang dinmun a om mikhat ahi hi.

Kum zalom tampi sungmah Pasian in kamsangte sawlin Amite hopihpih hi (Pai. 3:15; Late 105:26; Mal. 3:1; Isa. 6:8; Jer. 25:4; Mate 11:13; Luka 11:49), “mikhat zong akisiat ding deihlo in a kisikkik ding uh deih hi” (2 Pet. 3:9). Tanglai a Israel leh Judah mite’ tangthu limlim pen; a kisik theihkhak vualeh ci in, a nungkiin mihingte hotkhiatna kamciam in, khawlhun neilo in Pasian in hahkat in zolden cihthu ahihi.

Judah’ tangthu a nununglam ciangin, Jeremiah kamsanna aw tungtawn in makaih na leh hehnepna Pasian in a piakteng pen; kiniamkhiatna, upna leh kisikna tawh dawng thuklo uh a, nuihsat- na, zahkona leh zahpihna bektawh dawngthuk uhhi.

`Pasian’ kamsangpa zahko uh, Pasian’ kammal pampaih uh in Ama’ kam sangpa zahpih uhhi. A taktak in tuhun in tuabang peuhmah hihhuai maw; hihhuai lo?`
