---
title:  Pasian Kiangpan Kibu
date:   28/09/2026
---

Pasian pen ki itna ahih manin (1 John 4:8) kuamah set milim in bawl hetlo hi. Ki itna in deihteelna pom hi. It thei ding Adam leh Eve tegel deihteelna tawh piangkhia a; tua hikeileh; itna, itna maan taktak neitheilo ding uhhi. Tuabang in Adam leh Eve in thu mangthei manglo thei dingin suahtakna neihkul hi. Tua deihteelna dinmun maan taktak pen sialehpha theihtelna singkung gah neek- loh ding thupiakna tungah kitelcian mahmah hi (Pian. 2:15-17).

Acil apat in suakta takin nethei ding cihkhit nungsang bang- hangin nelo ding cikik leuleu hiding hiam?

“NulehPa masa tegel pen mawhbaanglo siangtho in ki pi- angsak ahihtei hang; khial theilo dingin a kipiangsak hilo hi. Pa- sian in suahtakna tawh a vangnei in kibawl uh hi a, Pasian’ pilna, thumaanna leh Ama’ omzia tungah thumang kipkho dingin a pi- ansak ahi hi. Pasian leh a vanmite tawh nuamtakin kikhawl ding uh a … deihteel theihna omkeileh amau’ thumanna in kipiakkhi- atna tawh hilo in sawlngawh hiding hi” Ellen G. White, Patriarchs and Prophets, pp. 48, 49.

`Adam leh Eve in thu a manloh khit uh ciangun bang- hang in Piang sakpa’ maipan kibuk sawm uh hiam? Pian. 3:1- 8 (Jer.23:24 leh Heb. 4:13 simkaak).`

Piancil 3 in Adam leh Eve kipukna thu pulaak hi. Gulpi Satan tawh a kiho khit uh ciang un; NulehPa masate in a kikhaam

“sialehpha theihtelna singkung” pan a ne dingin kizol hi. Satan in kammal vangliatna, lungsim lawpna leh gualnopna tawh Eve zol a, Eve in zong Adam zol leuleu a, Pasian thupiak palsat uhhi. Eve in tua singkung “neek dingin hoih” “mit la mahmah” leh “mi pilsak dingin tua singgah deihhuai” (Pian. 3:6) cih thei hi.

Satan’ kamciamna pen “Pasian tawh kibang ding, sialehpha thei ding” (Pian. 3:5) cihthu ahihleh nial huai hetlo hi. A taktak ciangin Satan hohpihna pen zuau bulomtangpi tawh kipaaikan gawp hi. Thumanlohna sulzui teng ahihleh Satan in seel khinkh- ian hi.

A thumanloh khit phetun amituh hong “kihong” vat in “tangguak” in om ahih uh kimu pah uhhi. Pasian’ “limmeel” a sutna uh minthang “khuavakpuan” (Late 104:1, 2), ki hawkkh- ia lian hi. Pasian vaihawmsaksa puan in amau tuam nawnlota hi. Piangsakpa kiangpan a kibuk uh zong ahiding mah hilel hi (kibu- zo ngeilo). Mawhna in lungsim hong kisuangsak mahmah ahih manin; mawhnei mi in Pasian maipan taikhia nuam lel hi.

`Na mawhkhit ciangin maizum takin Pasian maipan nakibuk sawm hun a omngei hiam?`
