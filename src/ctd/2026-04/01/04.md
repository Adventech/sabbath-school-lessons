---
title:  Pasian in Mihing hong Zong
date:   29/09/2026
---

Piancil 3: 9 sungah Pasian in apuksa Atate hong zongmasa cihthu mu hihang. Adam leh Eve banghih uh a koi ah om cih thei

gige napi “Koi ah om nahiam” hong cih kammal pen lungluthuai mahmah hi. Mawhna in Pasian leh mihing nupakop masapen te kikhawlhna suktat sak hi. Pasian in bel thakhatthu mah in Atate leh Amah kimupha kik in, lungkiatna sungah lam-etna guanpah hi. Hebrew pau in qara’ pen “sam” Pian. 3;9 hi a, Pasian in Amite tawh kikhawl dingin a sapna mun ah kizang zel hi.

`Luka 15:11-32 simin. Hih thugentehna in Piancil 3: 9, 10 sung a thupiang teng koibangin pulaak hiam?`

Pasian in Adam leh Eve a zonna in Atate tungah itnakip honglahna zong ahi hi. Pasian in eite hong khahkhong ngeilo hi (Luka 15). Eitawh hong kikhawlh theihna dingin eima’ kibukna mun dong mah hong zuan tantan hi. Zeisu a kikhaima in Peter in thumvei sitset nial hitaleh; Christ in “nunghei kikin en” (Luka 22:61) hi. Kammal khat zong pau khia khalo pimah in a khialkha a lawm Peter “hopih” in zongkhia hi.

Piancil 3:11-13 sungah Adam leh Eve kikhawlhna a ki- kheelsakvatna lungngai un. Amau mawhna a kipulak ding sangsik un, khatlehkhat kimawhsak zawsop uhhi. Adam in “Kei tawh om dingin nong piaksa numei in hong pia a kane hi” (Pian. 3:12) ci in a sawi hem in Pasian mawhsak hi. Eve in zong ama’ thumanlohna pen gulpi mawhpaih in “Gul in hong khem in nemah ing” (Pian. 3:13) cihi.

Mawhna tawh a kitalsik uh ciangun khat beek in zong ama’ mawhna puak sawm hetlo uhhi. Kum tulguk sungin tam kikheel hetlo lel hi. Mawhna in mihing khempeuh susia dikdek khin hi.

`Sawltak Paul in eite’ dinmun pen Rom. 7: 15-24 sungah pulaak a, thumaan a sep nop ciangin a kilehbulhpi in semden ahi- hlam pulakkhia hi. “keima gamtat keimah’n katelkei hi. Kahih nopte, kahih kei a, kamuhdahte kahih hi” (Rom. 7: 15). A teltheilo kua om ding hiam? Paul’ kammal pen thukpha mahmah hi. Eite a puksa mite vive ihi a; hihloh ding cihteng ta a hihhihte cih zong kithei lel hihang. Tuabang a tuakkha ngeilo kua om ding hiam?`

Eite’ lam-etna pen “Zeisu Christ i Topa tungtawn” (Rom. 7: 25) in suahtakna bekmah ahi hi. Tua hangmah in Pasian in eite hong zong tantan in Zeisu kiang hong makaih a, mawhna in hong suksiatsaksa khempeuh pan in hong gumkhia in Pasian’ lim meel hong suunkik sak hi.
