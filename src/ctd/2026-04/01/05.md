---
title:  Huansung Panin Kihawlkhia
date:   30/09/2026
---

Adam leh Eve’ mawhna hangin hong piang a suksia pen- pen sulzui in Pasian tawh kikhenkhiatna ahi hi. Eden huan panin amaugel kihawlkhia in Pasian tawh maitang kimu in kiho thei nawnlota uhhi. Kamsang Isaiah in zong mihing omzia tawh kisai in “Note mawhnate in, note leh na Pasian uh kikhenna hong pi- angsak a, Amah in note’ aw a zak theihlohna dingin nakhialhnate un Ama’ maitang liah hi ” (Isa. 59:2) cihi. A taktakin Adam leh Eve tegel in Pasian’ tate mah hiveve lai uh, a hihtei hang mawhna’ sulzuipen pelhthei ngiatlo uh a, Eden a nutsiat uh kulta hi. Amau’ thu manloh khit uh ciangun, na khatbeek zong angei ngei nawnlo hi.

`Mawhna in mihing muntampi ah kikheelsak khin hi (Pian. 3:14-24). Pasian tawh kikhawhna eleh koibang in hong kheelsak hiam? (Pai. 19:16-22.)`

Mawhna in ei’ ngaihsut zawhloh zah dongin mihing nundan kheelgawp ahih man in, eite’n mawhna leitung bekmah thei hi- hang. Mawhna hangin, leitang “kisamsia” a; “ling leh kiak” hong pokhia hi. Mihing tungah natna hong piang in, amau’ nektawm ding zonna in “khua-ul” kaai in, a tawpna ah si in lei ah ciahkik ding hi. Mawhna hong phawk sak teng lakah sihna mahmah van- glian penpen hi.

Tua kawmkal ah Pasian tawh maitang kimu kikhawlhna mansuah lailai hihang. Bangbang hitaleh; mawhna’ gu talsik za- whna dingin Adam leh Eve a dingin kithoihna khat mah kamciam ngahpah uhhi. Pasian tawh mitang kimu a kiho theihnawnloh uh- hang; mailam a ding lam-etnapi khatmah tawh Eden nusia uhhi. Pasian mah hong kalsuan masa in hotkhiatna ngiimna hong pu- laak hi.

Piancil 3:15 sungah, “lungdamna kamciam masapen” in amau leh ei adingin lam- etna tawh kidim ngiat hi. Honkhiapa khat hongpai ding kamciamna in tuhun a dingin mawhna tawh ki- galdo in mihing’ mailam a dingin hotkhiatna hong lam-en sak hi. Eden a nutsiat madeuh un, Pasian in kituamna puan a vaihawmsak zong pen, amau a dingin mailam ah hotkhiatna lipua mah hong hiveve hi. Mailam ah Zeisu hong sihna leh a thawh kikna in; Pa- sian tawh kikhawlhna puahphatsak kik hi (John 14:1-3).

Eden mah ah, mawhna a lutkhit phetmah in, mawhna kithoihsakna ding lam-etna leh kamciam hong kipiasuk pah hi.

Mawhna a hong om phet leh, Honpa kamciam zong hong ompah hi.

`A hilo khat peuh nabawlkhit lian ciangin, nakoici ompah ngei hiam? Mawh kisa, zum giauguau, nang leh nang peuh na kimudah giauguau hiam? Pasian na upna leh, lungdamthu kamciamna in na upna khahsuahna dingpan koibang in hong kemcing naci hiam?`
