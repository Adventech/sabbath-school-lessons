---
title:  Pasian a ding Kampau mite
date:   01/10/2026
---

`Anuai a munteng in, mawhna hong lutkhit ciangin Pa- sian in mihing hong koici hopih cih koibang in hong lahkhia hiam? (Heb. 1:1, 2; Rom. 1:19, 20; Gam. 12:6; 2 Tim. 3:16, 17; John 14:7-9; John 10:27-30).`

Mawhna kawmkal mahah, Pasian in a puksa mihing tawh kiho na’ng lampi mah muveve hi. Pasian hong kilaakna namkhat pen piansaksate tungtawn ahi hi. Piansak sate pen Pasian’ nasepna lamdangpi khat ahi hi. Scientistte in vannuai a “maimaipen” nun- tak nate nangawn zong lamdang samahmah lai uhhi. Na khempeuh tungah sihna mah vang- lian penpen ci uhhi.

Pasian hong hopihna lampi dangkhat zongpen kamsangte tungtawn hi a – a mimal in Pasian in Ama’ aiawh in kampau din- gin a teel ahi hi. Gentehna in Pasian in Noah, Abraham, Moses leh

Daniel cihte tungtawn in amite a ding Ama’ deihnate pulak hi. A thubulpi in cileng Pasian’ genkholh thupuakte peuhmah pen Atate mangngilh hetlo in om pih tawntung lai cihna ahi hi.

Kamsang pawlkhat in laisiangtho sungah gelh uh hinapi; pawlkhat leuleu in bu khat zong gelhlo uhhi. Kamsang tampite kammalte pen laisiangtho bu sungah om uhhi (2 Tim. 3:16, 17). Laisiangtho limlim Pasian in Amah hong kilaakna ahi hi. Ama’ kammal te tungtawn in Amah kua hi cih kithei hi. Hehpihna tawh a kidim hong itna, eite’ nunzia ding hong lamlahna cihte hong mukhia sak hi.

Laisiangtho in eimah kuate ihihlam, koi lam pan hong pai a, koilamah pai ding cih hong telcia nsak hi. Tua sungmah ah eima nunna a ding Pasian’ deihna hong telcian sak hi. Pasian hong ho- pihna sungah a muibun penpen in Liasiangtho sungah hong pu- laak Zeisu Christ tungtawn ahi hi.

Pasian in a mangthang Adam leh Eve zong dingin Eden ah hong paisuk mahbang in, Christ in zong “a mangte zong dingin hong paisuk” (Luka 19: 10) hi. Zeisu in “Kei nongmuh uhleh Pa na mukhin uhhi” (John 14: 9) cihi. Eden ah Adam leh Eve tung a kipia kamciam tengpen; Christ in Ama’ nunna, sihna leh thawhkikna tawh picing sak hi. Eite’ aiawh in hong sihsak manin Ama’ tungtawn in tawntung nunna i ngah thei uhhi. Zeisu van a kahtoh khit ciangin, Kha Siangtho in “thumaan lampi” ah eite hong makaih in “hong tung ding thute” (John 16: 13) hong hilh ding cihi.

`Zeisu, hong itna, Ama’ nundan, hong kipiakkhiatna cihte na telcian ciangin Pa hong lahna cih theitel in bangzah na lung hong nuamsak thei hiam?`
