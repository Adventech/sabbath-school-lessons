### Tukalsung Simding
Pian. 1:27, 28; Pian. 3:1-8; Luka 15:11-32; Pian. 3:14-24; Pai. 19:16-22; John 10:27-30.

> <p>Kamngah</p>
> “Abeisa hunah Pasian in kamsangte zangin i pupite tungah a lam tuam tuam tawh akhen akhen in thu na- gen ngeizo hi. Ahi zongin tuhun nunung ciangin leitung van- tung na khempeuh a bawl in a zat, a tawpna ah nakhem- peuh a luah dingin aseh a Tapa zangin eite tungah thu hong gen hi” (Hebrews 1:1, 2).

Digital pilna in gamlapi khawng a om tanaute meel khawng hong musak ziau lel hi. Hoihmah hiven, meel kimuhmah lemzaw thampek hiven. Tuabang kimuhkhop ciang in a hoihzaw thampek kikawi, kinam, maitang kimu in kiho cihte omthei hi.

Tukal sungteng in mihing limlim amau’ Piangsakpa tawh maitang kimu kiho ding cih hong phawksak ding hi. Tua kinaihna pen mawhna in sawtpi pekpanin hong suksiat saksa ahi hi.

Pasian in a it a piansaksate tawh kiho kiktheihna dingin lampi tampi mah geel hi. Pasian in mihing hong hopih theihna dingin kampausiam mi, kamsang akicite Ama’ thu puak pulak din- gin kuhkal takin teelkhia hi. Hih kamsangte in Laisiangtho sungah lai gelh zel uh hitaleh, gelh den samlo hi.

Pasian geelna lianpen in, a kisiacipsa Ama’ limmeel puah- phat kik ding ahi hi. Tua pen hotkhiatna ngiimna hi a: puahphat- kikna; Pasian in a bawl “misiangthote teenna ding vanthak leh leithak” (2 Pet. 3:13) ahi hi. Tuani ciangdong mah Pasian in hong hopih ciangin kamsangte’ tungtawn in hong hopih ding hi.
