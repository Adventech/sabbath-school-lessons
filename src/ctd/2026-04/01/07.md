Laisiangtho hunlai in Pasian hong hopihna ki ciangtan ngei- lo hi. Maikaal sungah Pasian in hong hopih tawntung a, Christ hong paikik ni dong mah khangthak lampi tawh hong hopih ding cih mu ding hihang.

Ellen Gould Harmon White leh a peenpih Elizabeth te in November 26, 1827, kum in Gorham, Maine ah suak uhhi. Eunice leh Robert Harmon te tanu neupen leh a giatna ahi uhhi. Tuakhit deuh ciangin Portland Maine ah a innkuan un lal uh a, Robert Har- mon in lukhu bawl nasep sem hi. Ellen kum 9 a phak ciangin hong liam a, pumpi, lungsim leh kikhawlhna limlim hong thanem hi.

Amau’ inn gei a paakhuan khat sungah a om laitak in, a sangkahpih numei khat in hehna tawh suang khat a lawt leh Ellen’ maitang kha ahih manin nipikal tawlkhat sung bang khua phawklo in omcip hi. Hih liamna in a pilna sin khawlsakta in a nunsung teng cidamlo densakta hi. A maitang liamna in ama’ meel hem kisa in, ki khawlhna lampang ahihleh ciangtan hong hitamai hi.

Tuathu hangmah in Ellen Harmon in ama’ mailam khathu ding zong kician samel nawnlo tahi. Pasian in vantung a ding kilawm a sakloh ding bang patau liangta hi. Tua patauhna pen Harmon innkuan in Millerite doctrine upna ahi Zeisu nihveina hong paikik ding thu a saan ni ciang dong mah ahi hi. 1842 kum in Methodist pawlpi sungah a ki tuiphum khit nangawn in, Ellen in Pasian’ itna ngah kisa nailo hi. Amah “tawntung mang thang khin dingin ki um hi” (Life Sketches of EGWhite, p. 29; also see pp. 20-31).

Hihbang ngaihdan kawmkal ah Ellen in mang nih hongmuh ciangin, Pasian tung ah a lungsim puak kikheel ding hong kipan hi. Methodist Sia Levi Stockman tawh a kimuh takciang in Pa- sian’ hong itna leh hehpihna hong telcian pan hi. A lungkiat laitak in Pasian’ hopihna bang hi saliang hi. “Tu’n ka lungsungah upna hong dimta hi. Pasian’ hong itna leh Kha Siangtho in mawhmai kangahlam hong telsak ahih manin genzawhloh Pasian’ hong itna ka phawkkhata cihi. Pa Pasian ka muhdan zong hong kikheelta hi. Tu in Pasian pen a ngongtat, mitsitsa in thu hong mangsak aana han pa bang hu nawnlo in; a dik a nunnem nulehpa bangin kamu ta hi. Ka lungsim in Pasian sungah a thuk akip itna a muta hi”—Life Sketches of Ellen G. White, p. 39.

Tua laitak mahin, Ellen White in “misite dinmun” hong telsiang a, hell-mei kuang tawntung lo cihthu zong hong telcian tahi. Pasian’ hong itna a telthakna in ama’ lungsim puak kheel vingveng hi. Friday simin, Zeisu thuktak in it in a khantawn in Ama’na a sep sak thute kisin ding hihang.
