---
title:  Eden ah Kikhawlhna
date:   27/09/2026
---

Piancil 1 leh 2 in Piansakna tangthu gen hi. Pasian in leitung hoihmahmah khat bawl in, piansakna sungah a thupipen Adam leh Eve koih hi. Mihing ahihleh ganhing tetawh a kilamdang deuh “Pasian’ Lim sun” in kipiangsak hi. Hihthu tungtang pen mihing tung bek ah kigen a, vasa leh ganhing dangte tungah kigen hetlo hi. Amau pen picing takin om ding uh a, itna bukim sung ah Pa- sian tawh kikhawl ding uhhi.

Piancil 2:18-23 sungah Pasian in “Pasal pen khatguak om dingin hoihlo hi” (Pian. 2:18) ci in Adam a dingin Eve bawl cihi. Acil apat in mihing pen gualtawh kikhawl dingin kipiangsak hi. Mihing kipawlna pilna neite in; pumpi leh lungsim nopna in meet- na tampi tak omsak a, khat guak omna in hong that hi ciliang uhhi.

Eve a kipiansak khit limin Adam in “Hipen keima’ guh leh keima’ sa ahi hi. Amah pen mipa sungpan akilakhia ahih manin “Numei” kici ding hi” (Pian. 2:23).

Innkuan hong kipat na’ngin mi- numei khat leh mi-pasal khat kigawmkhawm sak a, tuapen kikhawlhna picingkhat nei- htheihna dingin Pasian’ vaihawmna ahi hi. Satan mungtuppi khat zong pen tua innkuan kipatna bulpi suksiatna ding ahi hi.

`Pian. 1:27, 28; Pian. 2:15-17; leh Pian 3:9 simin. Eden huan sungah Pasian leh mihing kikal koibang kizopna om ngei hiam?`

Adam leh Eve tegel in Pasian tawh maitang kimu in kiho uhhi. Ngaihsutna nei mi ahihmah bangin, Pasian muthei in Ama’ thupiaknate zathei uhhi. Tua Pasian huan lamdang kemcingte ahih na uh tawh, singkung lopa leh gannote kemcing in theihna khang- toto uhhi. Nisim “nitak nivot” (Pian. 3:8) ciangin, Pasian tawh hun tuamvilvel khat neithei uhhi.

Sabbath ni pen Pasian’ piansak teng lakpan a thupi phadiak hi a, Pasian leh a tate kihona’ng hun a kituamseh ahi hi (Pian. 2:2, 3; Pai. 20:8-11). Tuani in nulehpa masate in Pasian’ hong itna leh kepcinna a tuamvilvel in ngahthei uhhi.

`Mawhna omma in koibang hiding cihpen, Eden pan kumtul tampi beikhin ahih manin, eite a ding ngaihsut haksalua hi. Himah taleh, tu in zong Pasian in, hih leitung Ama’ piansak hunlai a omzia koibang in honglak naci hiam?`
