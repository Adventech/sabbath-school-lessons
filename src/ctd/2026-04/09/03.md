---
title:  Laisiangtho tawh Kituak
date:   23/11/2026
---

Ei mihingte in eimah kilangdo tawm in khemhat ihihhang Pasian in hong khem ngeilo hi (Gam. 23: 19). Kamsang te tung- tawn in thupuakte in zong kikheelna omlo in hong lakden hi. A kikheel ngeilo Topa’ thumaan pen Ama’ hong piak kamsang thupuak sungah kimu ding hi.

`Isaiah 8: 20 simin. Amah in a thupi bangthu gen hiam?`

Humuapna ngah kipsakna ding a nunung pen dinmun in lai- siangtho hiden hi. Kamsangte thugen peuhmah Laisiangtho genna tawh tehkak in sittel kul hi. Kamsang tak tak khatin laisiangtho tawk kilehbulhlo, kiamsaklo in laisiangtho thumaan panin pialkh- iat pihlo hi. Tuahi a, Topa’nung zui in kamsang zuaute khem a thuak nuamlote in, laisiangtho theihcian kulzek hi.

Laisiangtholui leh thak sungah, kamsang kizomsuk diu- deute in, amau’ kamsang nasepnate ah, amau’ mai a kamsangte laigelhte pulaakpha kikden hi. Anunglam cianga kamsangte in hotkhiatna’ng Pasian’ ngiimna teng pulaak khia uh ahihhang; Pa- sian in tang lai in a piakkhitsa dinmun tawphahte peuhmah tawh kilehbulh saklo hi.

`2 Peter 1:19-21, Rom. 15:4 leh James 1:17 cihte in, za- lom tampi lai in hong kipiasa Pasian leh a kamsangte thupuak koibang in honghilh uh hiam?`

Hotkhiatna ngiimna a ding Laisiangtholui tawphah bulpi pen Piancil 3: 15 sunga Pasian in hong piakkhiat masakpen genk- holna pen ahi hi. Gangawh biakna in zong hih a tunga thu pen a zaizaw in hong lak hi. Pasian’ Tuuno, Zeisu Christ, hong pai ding a, nungta in mihing a dingin si ding cihte a cialcial in hong ki pulaak khia hi.

Mihingte in a teltheih zawkdeuh uh ciangun, Pasian in Ama’ thumaan hong pulaak khia cihzong laisiangtho sungah kiciamteh sitset hi. Tua in khangthak te’n thumaan a theihtelna uh thuksak semsem hi. Tua penin “hong piang thumaan; a mainawt thumaan” ahi hi. Tua hi a, khangluite sang in khangthakte in Pasian’ thu- maan telcian theizaw ham tang hi.

Himah taleh, a thuthuk, a kipulaak zolo, a thupi khat pen in, Pasian in hongpiak masak thumaante pen, hong pulaakthak thu- maan tetawh kilangdo ngei hetlo hi.
