---
title:  “Ciam in Mu”
date:   27/11/2026
---

Seventh-day Adventist in EGWhite’ kamsang silpiak a sanna hangun, acil apat in kipan tuhun dongmah tona phukha den uhhi. 1844 kum in EGWhite in mangmuhna masapen a muh ciangin, ama’ kamsang hihna dotnapia zong om uhhi. Tua bangin Sabbath tang Adventist te (anung ciangin Seventh-day Adventists) hong suakuh a, EG White’ kamsang silpiak a lenkipte hong suak uhhi.

Amasa, kamsang silpiak in a tawpdong in kipkho ahihna upnavai tawh lahkhiat na ahi hi. Tuhun in a nial kamsanna silpiak pen laisiangtho in thapia hi. Joel 2, Mang. 12: 17 leh a dangte in; kamsang silpiak pen “ni nunung” ciangin, Pasian hun nunung mite a dingin “ciaptehna” khatin omding ci uhhi. (Gerhard Pfandl, “The Gift of Prophecy and God’s Remnant Church” The Gift of Prophecy, chapter 4).

Anihna, Adventist maitaai te in, EGWhite silpiakte in thu ummite tungah thu hoihtawh huzaap zo cih mu uhhi. Gentehna in, ama’ kamsan khuamuhna in, 1840 kum a Sabbath Conference akah, laisiangtho upna lamah lungsim kituaklo, thu ummite ki lungtuak kiksak thei hi. Ama’ silpiak in a beisa kum tampi sungin “gah hoih” mah hong gah a, ama’ laigelhte in zong Seventh-day Adventist pawlpi upna kipawlna sungah huzaap hoih nei den hi.

Athumna, in EGWhite pen a mangmuhna sungah ngeina khengval tetawh kizopna nei hi. A mangmuh laitak in, EGWhite in ngeina khengvel omziate ngah a – ama’ theihzawhloh thute, mailam thupiang dingte pulaak, ngeina Sangin a sangzaw thahat- nate, mi lakah a mangmuh hun sungin humuapnate kuamah in len- cip khaktan zolo hi.

Tuni Seventh-day Adventist in EGWhite silpiak kipsakna in hih thute tawh teci pangin pulak zel uhhi. Tua tecipanna te in maan in thahat ahih manin EGWhite silpiak zong ama’ laigelhte in tecipang ahi hi. Laphuakpa David in: “Topa’ hoihna ciam in mu un” (Late 34:8) cihi. EGWhite laigelhte thungetna tawh ki-aap in simna bek tawh, ama’ kamsanna telcian theipan ding hihang.
