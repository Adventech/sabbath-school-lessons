---
title:  Gah Hoihte
date:   25/11/2026
---

Mualtung thuhilhna (Mate 5-7) sungah, Zeisu in Christian pawlpi’ tawphah teng a tomin hong gualsuk hi. Kamsangte nasep- na zong tua tawphahte’ lakah kihel uhhi. Tua bek thamlo Zeisu in kamsang zuaute thu zong hong hilhkhol zo a, “amau’ gahte tawh na theitel ding uhhi” (Mate 7:20) cihi.

Singgah khat a hoihna theihna dingin hun pawlkhat lak kul hi. Kamsang khat’ a gamtatzia leh ama’ thupuakte pan hongpiang a sulzui teng panin, tua kamsang in mihing te Pasian’ lamah kaai maw/ Pasian kiangpan pialpih maw cih kithei ding hi.

`Jonah 1:1-3; Sawl. 15:36-39 leh Galati 2:11-24 simin. Hih munteng in kam sangte’ thu koibang in hong hilh hiam?`

Kamsang khat hihna in, mikhat mawhneilo leh khialtheilo in hong bawl tuanlo hi. Tua thute pen laisiangtho teci ahi Jonah, Paul leh Peter cihte khialhna leh thanemna teng i mu uhhi. Pasian bekmah thanemlo a; a kamtaaite tua cilo uhhi. Kamsangte zong eite mahbang in thanemna nei uh ahihhang, amau’ nundan in Pa- sian’ lam zuanngiat ding a; kamsangte peuhmah in amau hawm- khiat thumaan tawh a kilehbulh nundan peuhmah nei theilo ding uhhi.

A tonngeilo Isaiah’ nasepna in Judean kumpi 4 maan hunin, buaina kidim nianua sungah Messiah kawhmuh zo hi (Isa. 39:5, 6; Isa.53:4-6). Daniel inzong lungsim takpi tawh a mawh pulakna (Dan. 9:15), Khitui luangin Jeremiah’ ki-aapna (Jer. 9:1) cihte pen mangngilh theih hilo hi.

Tua banah amau a kibawlsiatna leh a kisimmawhna kaw- mkal mahah; Pasian’ kamsangte in Pasian’ mite itna taktak lahna hangin, paklehgah kidim nasepna ahihlam kipsak thei uhhi.

`Pasian’mi numei pasal te in amau’ gamtatna thanemna tawh bangzahvei hong lungkia sak ngei hiam? Tua thu hangin bang pilna khawng nangahlawh thei ding hiam?`
