---
title:  A Tangtungsa Kamsang Thusuakte
date:   26/11/2026
---

Kamsang kuamah in Zeisu’ lim baanzo ngeilo ahihhang; kamsangmaan in Pasian’ gam’ nundan leh thukhunte lenkip hi (Mate 7:16-20). Pasian’ kamtaai kahi ci napi in, a nundan in ama’ thupuak tawh a kilehbulh mi pen muanhuai vetlo hi.

Kamsang dingin sapna pen haksa mahmah ahihtei hang, kamsang tampi takin a nasep sungun “gahhoih” gah uhhi. Genteh- na in, Moses in Israel mite sehnel sung kantan pih in makaih zo sitset hi (Thkna. 34:10-12). Haggai’ cihtakna in Judah gamsung a tengte biakinnpi nihna a lamuh kum 19 bang a kikhawlcipna pan kipan kiksakzo hi (Hag. 1:12).

Mihingte in kamsang icih mailam’thupiang ding genkhol hi ci uh ahihhang, tua in kamsang’ nasep phelkhat bek hipan hi. Kamsang nasep icih zaipi huam hi. Kamsangte in mipite Pasian’ Thu lamah kaai, ngaihsutna hoih pia, vau in hanthawn uhhi.

`Mailam thupiang dingte mihing te’n a theihsak khit uh ciangin a kiginsak lai banah, banghang in mailam thu genkhol lai hiam? Jer. 28:9, 15-17.`

Genkholna a tangtun zong pen kamsang sittelna khatmah ahi hi. Kamsangmaan in a genkhol leh a genbang in tangtung ding hi: “kamsang khat in Topa’ mintawh thugen in, tuathu pen hong tunglo ahi a, a maanlo ahizongin ahihleh, tuathu pen Topa’ genloh thu ahi hi. Kamsangpa in ama’ utthu a gen hi a, amah nakihtak dinguh kullo hi” (Thkna. 18:22).

Genkholna pawlkhatte pen khuahunzui peuh ahih manin, hih sittelna zong pen tona lianpi om veve hi. A khuahun tawh a kituah keileh zong tangtunglo pahlel hi. Hih thukhun pen, Jonah in Nineveh khua a thuhilhna ah muthei hihang. Jonah’ sermon pen tomlitlet lel hi: “Ni sawmli ciangin, Nineveh kisia ding hi” (Jon. 3:4) cihi. Ahihhang a genbang in tangtung lota hi. Banghang?

`Jeremiah 18: 7-10 simin. Khuahun zui in genkholna tawh kisai bang hong hilh hiam?`

Khuahun zui genkholna icih in, Pasian’ thupuak pen mihing te’n a dawnthuk zia tungah a ki-ngate cihna hi. Mihingte in amau’ lungsim leh gamtat kikheel peuhleh uh, tua genkholna in tangtun- glo ziau lel hi. Nineveh khua mite in Jonah genkholthu pen lung- sim taktawh sanguh a, amau omzia kikheel uh ahih manun, Pasian in susia nawnlo hi (Jon. 3: 10).

Khangthakte in tua kamsang vauna kammal mangngilhta uh a, Nineveh khua zong gilo, ngongtat ahih manin Pasian in susia hi. Pasian in mailam thu theilua zen a, e’n theilo hihang. Eima’ teelna in eima’ mailam ding hong khentat sak thei hi.

`Banghang in khuahunzui genkholna theihtel ding thupi naci hiam? Hih theihtelna in laisiangtho thumaan lenkip thei din- gin koibang in hong huh thei hiam?`
