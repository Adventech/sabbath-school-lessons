---
title:  Ngeina Khengvalte Dinmun
date:   22/11/2026
---

Paikhiat hun laitak in, Topa in Amite lakpan mahin kam- sang silpiak thu a genna ah, “Topa in: Ka thu za un. Note lakah kamsang a omleh Topa Keimah in amau tungah mangmuhna tawh kakilak a; amah tawh mang sungah kakiho hi” (Gam. 12:6).

Kamsang thupuak leh sapna peuhmah ngeina khengval kihelthei zel a; mihing khat sunga omthei ngaihdan muhdante khengval zel hi. Kamsang khatin Pasian tawh tang tak in ahi a, kamsang mang leh mangmuhna tawh ahi zongin kiho (Dan. 8:1; Isa. 1:1 Sawl. 9: 10) a, muhtheih pumpi omziate in hong zuipah hi.

`Daniel a mangmuh laitakin ama’ pumpi koibang omzia nei cih gualsuk dih in. Dan. 10: 7-19.`

Daniel in mangmuhna sung a lutnakleh a tha beipah lian hi. Tua hunsung teng khempeuh Pasian tungbek ah kinga lian hi. Ama’ gei a om mite in mangmuhna sungah a lutloh hangun, vantung ompihna in khauhlua mahmah ahih manin, launa tawh taaimang in kibuu uhhi. Kamsang khat tungah kamsang thupuak pen mite in a awlmawh theihna dingin Pasian in pumpilam omzia zong a nazang ahi hi.

`Thuhilhkikna 13: 1-5 in kamsang silpiak hong koici hilh hiam?`

Muhtheih pumpi omzia in ngeina khengval vangliatna in nasem cih hong lakgige ahihhang, tuabang angah mi peuhmah Pasian in humuam hi cihna hipah tuanlo hi. Satan in zong ngeina khengval pen hihthei veve mawk hi. Tuahi a kamsang silpiak cih kipsak theihna thukip cihbang omtuanlo hi. Pumpi omzia banah, kamsangte pen sittel zekmah kul hi.

Ngeina khengvalna peuhmah kamsangmaan silpiak sittelna hipahlo hi. Laisiang tho tungtawn in kamsang khat sittelna bek- mah in Pasian kamtaai khat hihna kipsak thei bek hi. Tangthu sun- gah kamsang zuaute zong tampi omkhin hi (Mate 7:15).

`Minam ngeina pawlkhat sungah, tua ngeina khengvalna pen minamdang tesang in laangzaw hi. Pasian kiangpan hongpai ngeina khengval taktak sittel ding ciangin banghang in kuh- kal luat kulthei hiam?`
