### Tukalsung Simding
Dan. 10:7-19; Thkna. 13:1-5; Isa. 8:20; John 11:25; Sawl. 15:36-39; Jer. 28:9, 15-17.

> <p>Kamngah</p>
> “Ka it lawmte aw, kha kanei acimi khempeuh’ thugen um kei unla; tua thute Pasian kiangpan mah ahihiam ci in kantel un. Leitung ah kamsang a kineihkhem tampi hong omta hi” (1 John 4:1).

Diamond maantaktak pen leitungah manpha penpen hi. Tua mahbang in diamond tawh kibang in a kibawlthei na tampi om hitaleh manpha zolo uhhi. Tua hi a diamond khat, takmawkhem cih koici khentelthei ding hiam? Sittel mah hoihpen hi. Sittelna dan pen innsung mahah na tawmno khat bekmah kisam hi. Sit- telna dangte in mipil misiam ki sam lailai hi. Sittelna pawlkhat in bangmah hong dawng mello ahihhang, sittelna pawl khat kantan- zo peuhmah leh diamond taktak hi kici ngam pah hi.

Diamond pen leitung ah sak penpen hi. Diamond bekthem khat a thahatna pen, a sa mahmah thuakzo a, a satna kikheel zong thuakzo hi. Diamond khat sittelna dingin, mei sa mahmah sungah khul dingin, tuakhit ciangin tuivot sungah khiasuk ding hi. Dia- mond taktak ahihleh kisia loding a, diamond khem ahihleh pal- hzaan gawp ding hi.

Kamsanna silpiak pen, diamond sang in manpha zaw a, hih silpiakte a ngen khat pen, a tungtham tawh kisangpak cih omthei- lo hi. Tuapen sittel phot kul hi. Tukal sungin laisiangtho thukhunte zangin kamsangte leh amau thuneihna sittel ding hihang.
