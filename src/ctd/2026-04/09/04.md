---
title:  Christ Sunglut in Mihing a Suahna
date:   24/11/2026
---

1 John 4:1-3 sunga bangin, Zeisu’ sunglutna upna sittelna in; leitung biakna lian dangte in kamsang hibek hi hong cihsak- na uh leh: biakna khat peuhpeuh zui tuanlo in kha lam kizopna

omthei peuhmah hi cih ngaihdan te hong hepkhiat saktheita ding hi. Mikhat peuhin “hihthu anial mi peuhmah, Pasian’ sawlna tawh thugen hilo uhhi” (1 John 4:3).

John in hihmun a gelhlai in, Zeisu pen mihing hilo a, mihing bangin hong kilang hi bek hi aci kamsang zuaute phawkin agelh ahi hi. Hih sittelna in thukzaw semlai hi. Kamsang khatin Zeisu icih, leitungah a teeng mihing taktak khat hi cih thukimpih dingin a genna zong hituanlo hi. Christian tampi takin Zeisu pen tuabang dingin um uhhi.

Tua hunlai in John in thubuai kician mahmah khat aphut laitak ahi hi. John’ kamsanna thupuakte in, tuahun a dingin a kis- am ahihhang; khangthakte a dingin zong a thupi thumaan thutak omveve hi.

`Anuaia munte in Zeisu’ thu bangteng hong gen hiam? John 3:16; 2 Cor. 5:21; John 11:25; Heb. 4:14, 15; John 14:1-3.`

Kha Siangtho in a humuap kamsang thupuak pen, Pasian meivakpi hi a, Zingsang Aksipi in lungsimsung hong taansuk ciangin, den a kamsang silpiakte in Topa tawh hong kipawlsak in; Topa’ lam hong kawhmuh ciangin, eima’ sungah a omcip khuami- alpi veng ding hi.

Zeisu a laamto kamsang peuhmah in amau leh amau kilaam- to ngeilo ding hi (Sawl. 4:12). Na sem dingin kisakhol den ding a, amau’ a kisawlna mite kiangpan ham- phatna langeilo ding uhhi. Tua laitak mahin, zingciang i muhding thu in, Pasian’ kamsang te zong eite mahbang in mihing mah hi a, lungdamthu kisam veve uhhi.

`Zeisu in Pasian maantaktak hi a, eima’ aiawh in si, ei’ mawh- na thaman teng thuak, eima’ upna laigil cihte theihtelna in banghang in thupi thei hiam? Tua thu hilhna mansuah leng bang mansuah ihi hiam?`

Kamsang maantaktak in Zeisu pen hong sunglut Pasian hi a, mihing taktak hi cih um ding hi. A kicing mahmah Zeisu’ thumaan – a nasep, mawhbaanlohna, sihna tawh hong thoih na, thawhkikna leh vantungah siampi-lian ahih mahbang in nihveina hong paikik ding cihte pomthei ding uhhi.

Leitung ah Zeisu hong nungta hi cihthu in ngaihsut pilna maimai khengval hi. Zeisu pen kamsang khat’ thuhilhna ah a laigil hiding a, bangmah behlap theilo ding hi. Tua pa/nu thupuakte in mihingte pen Zeisu tawh kikhawlhna sungah tunpih ding hi.
