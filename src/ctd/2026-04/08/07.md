Ellen G. White koici sim ding? Ellen G. White’ laigelhte maantakin teltheihna ding lamlahna pawlkhat:

Amasa, thungetna, lungsim siangtho, lungsim kihong leh muanlahna omlo in sim kipan in. Eima’ lungsimpuak limlim in zakna, simna leh muhna khempeuh meelnei sak hi. Lungsim hong makaih ding Kha Siangtho zong kisam hi. EGWhite ngiat in “eima muhna kipsakna lel dingin laisiangtho na zonleh, thumaan baanzo kei niteh. Topa’n bang gen cih kisinna in zongin”—Christ’s Ob- ject Lessons, p. 112. Ama’ laigelhte zong tua mahbang ding hi.

Anihna, na laisim a thukizom ngaihsun in. Mite in EGWhite laigelhte amau’ tel dan pen, a tuamvilvel in akitangko ahih keileh, lai gual khat tungpeuhpan a tangthu bulpi leh alai panin lakhia ziau thei uhhi. EGWhite ngiat in “ahun leh amun ngaihsut taak hi” cihi—Selected Messages, book 1, p. 57. Hibang khawngte a awlmawhlo a dahhuai gamtatna in, thu ummite a ding a beitheilo buaina piangsak den hi.

Athumna, thukhun bulphuh in. EGWhite in a thupitsak lai- gual a thubulpi bang hiam? Gentehna in, 1849 kumin EGWhite in Michigan gamkee Battle Creek a pawlpi mi te a laikhakna ah, siksakol (setbein) lei khawng mawhsak mahmah hi (Testimonies for the Church, vol. 8, pp. 50-53). EGWhite in a thapiaknopna thukhunpi pen, Pasian hong piaksa meihlehlam leh sum pen zang- siam ding acihna hi (tualai hunin siksakol manham mahmah lai hi).

Alina, EGWhite laigelh peuhmah a thulu nuai a om- khempeuh simpha in. Ama’ thuhilhna tawh akisai kizom akipu- lak telakpan a kitelmello te sittel in. EGWhite in ama’ damlai in ama’ laigelhte a kizatkhialhna hangin dah mahmah a “EGWhite thupuak hi a kici tampitak pen; EGWhite a kitelkhialh theihtheih- na dingin, ama’ khensatna tawh a ki tuaklote a kipsaknu ahihna dingin amah’n teci pangsak nuam uhhi” —EGWhite, Selected Messages, book 1, p. 44.

Atawpna, EGWhite gen ahihlam kipsak in. Ahilopi khawng in, EGWhite gen kihisak pongmawk hi. I sim lai pen EGWhite gelh ahih kipsakna dingin laibul hoihtak enin. I muhna mun kip- sak khit ciangin, thumaan in zatna ding mun kizong theita ding hi.

EGWhite’ kamsang laigelhte sungah silpiak thupi mahmah hong kipia khin hi. Ama’ laigelhte thumaan takin i simding thupi mahmah a, mi awntampi a dingin thupha hi ding hi. Zatkhialhna hangin kamsiatna zong lianthei zel hi.
