---
title:  Laisiangtho a Letkhia Amah
date:   15/11/2026
---

Eima’ laisiangtho letkhiat dampen eima’ upmawh thu peuh in huzaap gawp thei hi. Laisiangtho pen tanglai a thucin thutang kikaikhawm banglel in a ngaihsun te in, tua laibu tuamtuamte’ kithutuakna mutel zolo ding uhhi. “Laisiangtho khempeuh pen Pasian’ humuapsa” (2Tim. 3:16) cih thukhun a lenkip mite in tua thukhun leh a kithuhualna teng pakta thei in, laisiangtho in amah leh amah a kiletkhiat zong sangtheita ding uhhi.

`Mate 5:17-48 simin. Hih mun ah laisiangtholui thuhilh- na a om pen Zeisu in koibang in hilhcian hiam?`

Mate 5:17-48 pen a tungtham bekin simna hangin; lai- siangtholui thuhilhna te pen Zeisu in Ama’ aituam thuthak tawh laih hi cih ngaihdan omvat thei ding hi. Limtak in simpha zaw leng, Zeisu in a thukzaw thute, laisiangtholui sungpan in khalam thute hong lakkhia cih muthei ding hihang. Hihmun ah Zeisu in Jeremiah kamciam (Jer. 31:31-33) picinna hi a, thukhunthak nuai ah, Pasian’ thukham pen a lungsim sungah kikoihsak in, a lung- tang tungah kigelhsak ding cihi (Heb. 8:8-10; Heb. 10:16).

Khatveivei ciangin laisiangtho mun khat a letkhiatna pen tua laibu sungmah mun dang khatah omthei hi. Gentehna in, Dan- iel 2 sungah Nebuchadnezzar’ mang lamdang ki muthei (Dan. 2:31-35) a, mangkhiatna in hong zuipah lel hi (Dan. 2:36-45). Tua mahbang in, Mate 13 sungah Khaici vawhpa gentehna (Mate 13:1-9), leh a khiatna in (Mate 13: 18-23) sungah hong zuipah hi.

Khatveivei ciang, a khiatna pen a nungah kamsang khat- peuh in hong piathei hi. Etpak dingin, Jeremiah 25: 11, 12 leh 29: 10 sung aa kigenkhol kum 70 genkholhna pen 2 Khang. 36: 22, 23 in tangtungsak hi. Laisiangtholui a limlehliim tampi takmah Christ sungah tangtung a, laisiangtholui mun tampi takmah lai- siangthothak in letkhia zel hi.

“Laisiangtholui icih, nambat, lim leh liim tetawh a kidim lungdamthu hi. Lai siangthothak ahihleh tuate’ limna leh namtu- ina ahi hi”—EGWhite, Selected Messages, book 2, p. 104. Lai- siangthothak pen humuap angah laisiangtholui a letkhiatna ahi hi.

`Laisiangtho i sim ciangin banghang in eima’ upmawhthu pel- hloh phamawh hiam? Pasian’ kammal zahmah in i muan mah- mah upmawhthu in; banghang in eitungah Pasian’ thukhenna hong tunthei ding hiam?`
