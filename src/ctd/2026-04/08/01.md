### Tukalsung Simding
Mate 5:17-48; 1Cor.16:20; Mate 4:1-11; Luka 10:25-28; Luka 24:25-27, 44-49.

> <p>Kamngah</p>
> “Laisiangtho sungah Ama’ thu a kigen- nate khempeuh, Moses’ laibu a kipan in, kamsangte’ laibu khempeuh a khiatna a gen hi” (Luka 24:27).

Zeisu a kikhaina leh a thawhkik khit ciangin, Christ in a lungkia dikdek nungzui nihte kiangah va kidawk a, tua tegel in “Amah pen Israel tankhia dingin hong pai cih ka um uhhi” (Luka 24:21) ci uhhi.

Nungzuite in Romans te ukcipna nuaipan Jewte a suahtak ding Messiah khat lam- en gige uhhi. Hih lam-etkhialhna leh a sulzui lungkiatluatna bulpi pen, laisiangtholui sung a Messiah ka- mciam pen deihkaih langneih luatna tawh letkhia khial uh ahih- man hi.

Hih nungzui nihte telkhialhna pen Christ in laisiangtho a zaizaw in hilhcianna tawh siansuahsak ta hi (Luka 24:25-27). Lai- siangtho Christ in a letkhiat dan in tua tegel mit honsak in a lung- sim uh tawhkuang hi (Luka 24:31, 32). Tua nungciang in Zeisu in Jerusalem ah mi a tamzaw te kiangah ki pulaak a “Topa in am- aute’ lungsim honsak in, amau un laisiangtho khentel theita uhhi” (Luka 24:45; Luka 24:33, 36, 44-49).

Hih thuphutkhak te pen kamsangte lai a telcian taktakte a ding a hoihlam in a gahte ahi hi. Kamsanna te telkhialhna in khalam lauhuaina hong tunthei hi.
