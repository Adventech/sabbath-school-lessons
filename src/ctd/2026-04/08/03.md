title:  Tangthu-Ngeina tawh Kizom
date:   16/11/2026
---

`Isaiah 65:17-20 simin. “vantungthak leh leitungthak ah naupangno khat pen “kum zaakhat pha in si ding hi” a cihpen bang a cihnopna hiam? (Isa. 65:20).`

mah ah Pasian in sihna tungah gualzo khin cih tangkona ahi hi. Hih ngaihdan pen khansauna kamciam leh naupangno sihna om- nawnlo cihte tawh a telkheh nuam ahi hi (Isa. 65:20). Hihteng khempeuh in hong tungding nate lahkholna ahi hi.

`1 Cor. 16: 20 simin. Hihmun pen eima’ hun leh ngeina tawh koibang in kizang thei ding hiam?`

Isaiah 65: 20 pen a tangthu paizia tawh kizui in sangleng, vantungthak leh leitung thak ah sihna leh mawhna omveve lai ding cih uppaktheih dingdan ahi hi. Ahihhang tua ngaihdan pen Isaiah in midik a sisate thokik ding (Isa. 26:19) a, sihna leh dahna beisiang ding (Isa. 25:8; Isa. 65:19) a cihtawh kilehbulh gawp hi.

Isaiah 65: 20 pen, lim kampauna vive cih kitellua hi. Bang naupang in kum 100 phathei ding? Tua sang in Isaiah 65: 17-20 pen a tangthu tawh kizui in simleng, Israel in Pasian thuciamna bangin citak uh hileh, amau’ saan ding thuphate lakam tawh a gel- hsuk hi a, tuazong thuciam tungah acihtak uhleh bekmah hiding hi (Thkna. 28).

Mate 24 sung a Zeisu in lauhuaina hong tungbaih dingte leh a nununglam nate a kizopsak mahbang in, Isaiah in hih a, tu laitak in a piang thute pen vantungthak leh leithak a ding in maitaai a hihna hong lahna ahi hi.

Isaiah sungah bawlphatna peuhmah mailam ah hikhin saklo hi. A lamdang Pasian hong tatna hangin tuhun sung mahmah in hong lutpih hi. Tua mun in tumahmah, Israel te maitang mahLaisiangthothak hunlai in, Greek ngeina sungah, tanau sung, itlehngaih zawlte tawh kimuh ciangin kinam uhhi. Rom. 16: 16; 1Cor. 16: 20; 2Cor. 13: 12 leh 1 Thess. 5: 26 sungah, Paul in hihbang kimuakna tepen Christian thu ummite lakah zat dingin ngaih sutna pia hi.

Hihteng panin, thu ummite a kimuh uh ciangun Pasian sun- gah innkuanpihte hihna laisiangtho thukhun khat kingah thei hi. Pawlkhat te ngeina ah kinap khawng lemkhollo ahih manin, khut kilen, kipom, kikuun cih khawng hoihzaw tham hi. Mi’ ngeina te awl- mawhlo zaw in Paul in pawlpi kikhawlhna sungah kisaanna leh ki-itna hong thapia mah mah hi.
