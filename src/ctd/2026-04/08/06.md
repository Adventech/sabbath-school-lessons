---
title:  Laisiangtho Hilhcianna
date:   19/11/2026
---

Laisiangtho kisinna ah ngiimna bulpi nih bulphuh ding hi- hang: Mimal khalam khantohna leh midangte zong hotkhiatna sungah Zeisu tawh kikhawl dingin makaihna. Christ in Chris- tian taktak icih Kha Siangtho in “thumaan khempeuh sungah” (John 16:13) a makaihte, “Pasian’ kamsungpan hong paikhia thukhempeuh tawh a nungta” (Mate 4:4) mite, Amah’n hong thu- piak “khempeuh” (Mate 28: 20) midang kiangah a hilhsawnte cihi.

`Luka 4:16-30 leh Luka 24:25-27, 44-49 simin. Hihteng ah Christ in laisiangtho a hilhcianna panin bang thu nasinbeh thei hiam?`

Zeisu Christ in “lampi, thumaan leh nuntakna” ahih ngeisa bangin hidenlai ding hi (John 14: 6). Ama’ thuhilhna te zong laisiangtho sungah kiptakin kikembit hi. Etpak dingin, Maku 1: 14, 15 in “Thong sungah John a kikhiatkhit ciangin, Galille gamsun- gah Zeisu hongpai a, Pasian kiangpan hongpai lungdamna thuthak – Pasian uk ding hun hong kipanta hi. Namawhna kisik unla, lung- damna thuthak umun” cihi.

A hun acihpen in Daniel 9: 24-27 sung kawkngel hi. Mual- tung thuhilhna (Mate 5-7) sungah, Zeisu in Paikhiatna 20: 13, 14 zang hi (Mate 5: 21, 27). Luka 4 sungah zong, Isa. 61: 1, 2 zang hi. Zeisu ahihleh laisiangtho thuhilhpa taktak ahi hi.

Et ding khatmah, Sawl. 8 sungah Ethiopia mangpa “biakna pia dingin Jerusalem ah hongpai” (Sawl. 8:27) a, tua in amah pen Jew’ biakna leh pawi pawlkhat tawh kinai mahmah cihna ahi hi. Amah’n Isaiah laisiangthobu khat nei a, a ciahkikna lampi ah sim gamgam cihi. Mawhna a puakhia nasempa cih genkholthu pen telzolo in a hilhcian ding mikhat zong thapai cihi.

Hihthu in a thupi pilna pawlkhat hong pia hi. Amasa, lai- siangtho simziau lelna in tuathu a thukzaw in hongtelsak theilo hi. Anihna, laisiangtho simciang in, khatlehkhat kihilh zong kul- zek hi. Athumna, laisiangtho thumaante midangte kiangah zong hawm sawn ding vaipuak mimal in nei hihang.

Philip in Isaiah’ laibu leh adang laisiangtho munte a hilhci- anna in mangpa zong kituiphum sak hi (Sawl. 8: 35-39; leh Late 51: 12 enkak). Eite’n zong laisiangtho hilhcian leng tuabang hi thei ding hi (John 17:17).

`Lai telzawhloh hunte ah huhna i kisap hun ciangin, eima’ up- mawh thu peuh banghang in kipsak huailo thei hiam?`
