---
title:  Lai Lamsang Kam Kizopna
date:   17/11/2026
---

Thuhilhsia khat’ phutkhak thu sungah, mikhat in thubuai mahmah khat a tuahkhak ciangin, ama’ a dingin Pasian’ deihna theinuam ciliang hi. Ama’ laisiangthobu hong in munkhat a kawk- suk ekleh Mate 27: 5 “Judas pai in gui-awk hi” cih simkha hi. Tua- mun lungnophuai salolua in a lawphuai khatpeuh kamukha tam ci in akawk leh Luka 10: 37 “Tuaciang in Zeisu in, ‘Pai inla tuabang in hih in” cih kawkkha leuleu hi. Tuate gel zong laisiangtho mun taktak mah hinapi, a kizoppihte tawh enkhawm in telsiam kul hi.

A taktak in, laisiangtho thupuakte letkhialhna hangin, tua- munte kizoppih thute tawh khenkhia in telcianna tuam khat hong piangsak thei hi. Hihbang in hihna hangin eima’ deihna bangin laisiangtho letkhiatna piangsak hi.

`Pian. 3:1-5 leh Mate 4: 1-11 simin. Satan in Pasian’ kammal khawng a thu tawh a kituak hetlo in koibang in susia hiam?`

Pawlkhatte in amau pilna kicinloh manin laisiangtho thupuak khawng susia kha uhhi. Pawlkhat in lah sawm limlim uh a, Satan in zong a tunga laisiangtho kipulakte tung ah a namnih mahin zangthei hi. A hanthawn bangpeuh a omta zongin, a sulzui pen siatna hi tawntung lel hi.

Eden huan sungah (Pian. 3:1-5; leh Mang. 12:9 enkak), Sa- tan in Eve kiangah mihing bangin a pauthei gulpil bangin hongpai hi. Pasian thumaan sungpan in thukhat dokkhia in, a baih mahmah ding khatin pulaak in Satan ama’ khemna kammal tawh let- khia ta hi.

Zeisu zong gamlakah Kha Siangtho in a paipih khit ciangin (Mate 4:1-11; Luka 4:1-13), “Christ khem dingin, Satan in a taang vanmi bangin gamlakah hong pai hi”— Ellen G. White, in The Advent Review and Sabbath Herald, December 18, 1888.

Christ tawh a kihona sungah, Satan in Late 91: 11, 12 pen thumaanlo in zangin; amau’pumpi nangawn a lauhuai dinmun tunpih ding a kithawite zong Pasian in kemcing veve cihlam in letkhiat sak hi.

Eden huan leh gampa lakah ze-etna tegel in; a kigelh Pasian’ kammalte letkhiatna nangawn ah kidona lianpi mah na omveve- lai cih kipsak hi. Tuathute in, laisiangtho munte peuhmah a kiim a paam tawh kizui in, a ki theihteldan phamawh thupi mahmah ahihna hong phawksak hi.

`Thumaan pulaakna dingin khemna kisam vetlo hi. Eima’ thugen khatin bangzah in thumaan hitaleh, laisiangtho thu- maan pulaak theihna dingin ki ze-et kizol cihte banghang in kullo thei hiam?`
