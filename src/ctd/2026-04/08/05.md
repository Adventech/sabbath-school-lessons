---
title:  Laimal a Khiatna
date:   18/11/2026
---

Christian kici peuhmah in amau a ding laisiangtho telhuai mahmah hi. Hotkhiatna ngiimna theihtelna dingin laisiangtho in kicinglua ahihtei hang, a lungpilpen te nangawn a tozo dingin ki- nokpha mahmah hi. Laisiangtho icih, cikmah in a kangzo ngeilo ding pilna theihna beihun neilo tawntung saal ahi hi.

`Luka 10: 25-28 simin. Pawlkhat in laisiangtho khawng letkhiatkhiat kulkei peuh cimawkmawk uhhi. Tuabang ahi- hleh Zeisu in, thukhamsiapa kiangah, “Pasian’ thukham sun- gah bang kigelh a, na koici letkhiat hiam” ci in banghang in dong sese hiam (Luka 10:26)?`

Laigual khat akhiatna theihna dingin, a kizang laimalte tel- masa phot, tua laimalte pen a thupaizia leh laisiangtho mundangte tawh kizopna koibang om, a tangthu leh amite’ ngeina bang om ci- hte eima’ muhzawhzah theihhuai hi. A nunungpen in, laisiangtho hilh na tawh a kizawitawn kullai hi. Akibang munte etkak ciangin – lungdamthu buli te sungah omphadiak a – theihna hong khangto saktuam mahmah hi.

`Mate 5:43-48 leh Luka 6:27-36 enkak in. Mate 5:43-48 sunga thu om leh a ki zawitawnpih Luka 6:36 in, Matthew 5:48 sunga “picing” cih kammal koibang in hong theitelsak semsem naci hiam?`

Cihtakna tawh laisiangtho a letkhiapa khat’ omdan pen kuh- kalna leh kiniamkhiat na kigawmkhawm ahi hi. Kuamah in eima’ aituam thumaan kineilo cihzong phawk in. Limlang sungah mi- alkhikhu bekin muthei hihang.

Upna maan ngahna’ng nakpi in semkhia den ding hihang (2 Tim. 4:1-5). Eima’ teltheih thu, a siangmahmah thu, tuate kipsakna dingin, a tamzaw in hun hongbeibei ciang in, tu in a kitel nai- lote teltheihna dingin hong huhthei ding hi. Tawntung hun sungah ahih leh hong kidawng nailo dotnate omden ding hi.

`Dam in cilel ni e, ci in na lungsim hong tokbuai mahmah na- telzawhloh thu a omhun ngaihsun kik in. Tu in na telzawhloh thukhat a dingin, Pasian leh Ama’ kam malte koibang in hong muangsak veve thei hiam?`
