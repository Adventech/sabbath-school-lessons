### Kamsanna Silpiak

Thagui lam mipilte in mihing naupangte’ kampau kisindan hong telkhia semsem ta uhhi. Eima kampaumal 7000 valbang a omsung panin, Mikangpau ahi a, kampau dang khat peuh ahi zongin, kha 6 a pha naungek khatin tel hong kipan ta hi. Kampau a nihna a hi phial zongin hong telpianta uhhi. Hihhun laitak in, a lamdang lungpilna kongpi kihong a, a suahtuung panin a zakzaksa kammal 800 kiim lakpan 40 kiim khawng bang khentel theita hi. Tua hi a: naungekte hopihpih kul hi. Nau suaktuungte a ding aw kisinna cih ding hiphial hi.

Pasian in ei tawh kiho hetlo ding hinapi, banghang in kam- pau theihna khawng tawh hong bawl aitam maw? Hih dotna pen lungluthuai mahmah hinapi “Abeisa hunah Pasian in kamsangte zangin i pupite tungah a lam tuamtuam tawh akhen akhen in thu nagen ngeizo hi. Ahi zongin tuhun nunung ciangin leitung van- tung na khempeuh a bawl in a zat, a tawpna ah nakhempeuh a luah dingin aseh a Tapa zangin eite tungah thu hong gen hi” (Heb. 1:1, 2).

Adam leh Eve tegel Pasian tawh a kizopnna uh mawhna in a suksiat ciangin, Pasian in a ut uhleh kisiacip lel hen aw ci in a khut sawpsuk ziaulel hetlo hi. Amaugel zong dingin hong pai in “Kaw’h om na hiam?” (Pian. 3:9) cihi. Mawhna pan hong hon khia ding Pa zong genkhol pah semsem hi (Pian. 3:15). Eden ah a pukna uhpan kipan, genkholna khempeuh in Zeisu mah laamto in mawhneite kiangah amau’ a ding lam-etna tua bekmah ahihlam lak hi.

Tu quarter sungin genkholna silpiak thu kisin ding hang a, Pasian in apuksa mihing tawh maitang kimu in kiho thei nawlo ahih manin; kiho theih na’ng lampi khat pilvang takin vaihawm hi. Hih Kha Siangtho silpiak tuam vilvel pen 1 Cor. 12, 1 Cor. 14, Ephet 4, leh Rom. 12 cihte sungah kician takin hong hilh khin hi. Hiteng khempeuh hong hawmkhiapi Pa pen Kha Siangtho hi a “mi a tuamtuam tungah Ama’ hoihsak bangin pia hi” (1 Cor. 12:11). Eden ah pukna khitin Pasian in ahihsa om mahbang in, Pa- sian in Ama’ thupuakte a hawmsawn dingin kha silpiak a ngahtu- am thugente koih kinken hi.

Pasian in kamsangte koici sap a, amau thumaanna koibang in sittel thei ding cih kisin ding hi. Laisiangtholui kamsangte leh a Thak kamsangte a kibatna leh a kilamdanna, amau tungah a kilak akimuam thute in amau’ nundan koici huzaap cihte zong mu ding hi hang. Kamsang pawlkhat in gen; pawlkhat in gelh; pawlkhat in a nihtuak un hih khawm hi. Tuate sittel kawmsa in; Pasian’ genkhiat dan, a muibut dan leh tua kamsangte’ thu manna hangin thuphate zong khia ding hihang.

Pasian in tua kamsanna silpiak pen ngiimna thupi nga tawh zang hi: 1. Amah mah, A thumaan leh apuksa mi a ding Ama’ deihna pulaak na’ng. 2. A thupi Thupiang te tawh kipawl muhna leh ngaihsut piakna ding 3. Christ’ pumpi ahi pawlpi tha guat- na ding 4. Ama’ nungzuite tungah khalam thahatna guatna ding. 5. Mawhneite khanglosak in kisik kiksak ding cihte ahi hi. Tua banah “Kha Siangtho genkholna” (Mang. 12: 17; 19: 10) cihpen hun nunung Pasian pawlpi a dingin mualsuang nih lakpan khat ahi

hi. A mang thang mite innlam tutna’ng in kamsanna silpiak tawh Pasian in makaih den hi (Joel 2:28- 31).

I pawlpi tungah hihthu hong kipia khin hiven, a hoihpen thei ding in i koici zat ahi tammaw?

I pianpan kipan a paukhia dingin hong kipiangsak hi a, Pa- sian’ kamsangte in eite hong hopihna hoihtak ngaih ding bangzah thupi ding hiam?
