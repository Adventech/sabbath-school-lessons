### Tukalsung simding
Pai. 4:10-16; Luka 8:22-25; John 3:1-10; Sawl.16:25-34; 1 Cor. 5:1-5, Luka 7:28.

> <p>Kamngah</p>
> “Pasian’ thu nungta in a gamtang hi a, lang- nih a hiam namsau sang in hiamzaw lai hi. Kha leh lungsim guhlehtang kituahna, guh leh guh ngek dongin khenkhia thei hi” (Heb. 4:12).

1989 kum November 9 ni in, Communist Germany ni- suahna gam TV live panin Günter Schabowski’ in Berlin Kawmpi kisiat kipatna’ng thu genkhia hi. Thuthang hawm- khiatna thunei- pa Schabowski in thuthang a hawmkhiatna sungah genkhialhna neucik khat hong om kha hi.

Nisuahna lam Germany gammite in gamdang pan sapnalai haksapipi in a bawlkhit uh ciangun, Europe nitumna gamlam ah khual zinthei ding uhhi acih ding pen; Amah’n Germany nisuahna gam mite Europe nitumna lam gamte ah zinthei ding hi ciziau hi. Thu thangkan khatin hih thukhun kilaihna pen cikciang in nung- ta ding hiam ci in a dotleh; tua Schabowski in, “Keima theihna ciangciang ahihleh, ziakailo in tumahmah hi ding hi” ci in dawn- gkik hi.

Minute tawmveino sungin, gamgi kongpite kiangah a paikh- ia ding mi tampi in kumpite tangko mahbang in hong kikaikhawm ngeingai uhhi.

Mihingte ihihleh, company khat, gamkhat a ding thugen ding thuneihna hong kipia thei hi. Pasian a dingin a paukhia ding te eleh?

Tukal sungteng, laisiangtho te in bang thuneihna nei uh a, laisiangtho a gelhlote in zong kamsang Nathan, tuiphum John leh EGWhite cihte in bang thuneihna nei uh hiam cihthu kisin ding hihang.
