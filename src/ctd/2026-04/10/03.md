---
title:  Zeisu’ Thuneihna
date:   30/11/2026
---

`Zeisu’ thuneihna bulpi tawphah in bang hiam? Hih a thuneihna teci in bang hiam? Mate 21:23-27 leh John 17:2.`

Zeisu in a thuhilh laitak in siampite hongpai uha, hih nasep- na ding koipan thu ngah cih dong uhhi. Zeisu in amau’ tual-sang te panbeek in lukhu-nelkai khu hibeeklo, rabbi sang ah akahkha zong hibeeklo hi. Amau’ dotnate amau’ mahin a dawnkik theihna ding lampi honsak in, tuiphum John in akikhial theilo a thuneihna teng dongkik zawsop hi.

Tuate dotna dawnlohna tawh, Ama’na semding in John aseh Pasian vangliatna nialna leh; John mahtawh akibang in Pasian hong sawlmah ahi Zeisu’ thuneihna a nial uh suaksak hi.

`Anuai a munteng in Zeisu’ thuneihna bangteng hong gen hiam? Mate 7:28, 29; Maku 1:21-27; Luka 8:22-25; Luka 9:1; John 5:25-27; John 7:38.`

Mihing a piangsakpa (John 1:3) leh honpa (Rom. 3:24) ahihna dungzui in thuneih na khempeuh Zeisu aa hi. Amah in Pasian Thu ahi hi. Leitungah na asep laitak in Ama’ thuneihna pen kuamah’n demzolo in hihtheilo uhhi. Zeisu’ thuhilhnate pen a thungaite’n lamdang satheilua uh a, ukna aana neite bang in thuhilh hi ci uhhi (Mate 7:29).

Lungdamthu buli tengmah in, leitungvai kihel kilkello Zei- su’ nasepna teng ciam- the hi. Zeisu in mawhna maisak (Maku 2:10, 11), dawisia hawlkhia (Maku 3:15) in mi’ ngaihsutna theikh- ia hi (John 2:24, 25). Misi thokiksak in tawntung nunna pia hi (John 10:28). Leitung ah eima’ Tankhiapa ahihna tawh, Zeisu in a Pa Pasian in a piak vangliatna te zang hi (John 17:2).

Apa muang in nazong semkhawm uhhi (John 5:19), lai- siangtho lam mah tangko tantan a, laisiangtho in zong amah’n a pulak mahbangin Amah a ding in zong teci pang lailai hi.

Zeisu in vangliatna khempeuh nei ahih manin tua vangli- atna pen a nungzuite zong hawmsawn theilai hi (Maku 6:7). Pa- sian’Thu zong hi a, Ama’ tecipanna in ama’ zat kamsang kamtaa- ite tungtawn in hong pai ding hi (Mang. 19:10).

`Zeisu in teci kipkho mahmahte hong piakhin hinapi, pawlkhat in nialteitei lai uhhi. Uptheih ding thukhempeuh hong kipia khin ahih manin; uplahna sungah pukkhak lohna ding koi- bang in kidawm thei ding ihi hiam?`
