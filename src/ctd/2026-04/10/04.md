---
title:  Laisiangtho’ Thuneihna
date:   01/12/2026
---

Laisiangtho icih kamsangte’ vanbawl hi a, Pasian humuapna tawh hongkilak ahi hi. Nuntakna ding dotna lianpi hong dawng a, mihing namte tungah Pasian hong kilahna bulpi pen ahi hi. Lai- siangtho tungtawn in, mihing in i omna hangthu kithei, Pasian’ omzia leh a deihna leh eima aading Pasian’ geelna cihte hong hilh hi.

Laisiangtho thuneihna nialtheihna a baihpen leh a tampen in, laisiangtho awl- mawhlohna leh sim ding hun lavetlo in eima nunna sungah zong zatlohna mah hipah hi.

Anuai a hunte ciangin Pasian’ Thu in bang teng sukha hiam? Jer. 38:1-4; John 3:1-10; Sawl. 16:25-34.

Laisiangtho pen thu kham dinmun hi a, nuntakpih ding teel- nate i bawltheihna ding thuneipa ahi hi. Christian te in leitung a etdan dinguh tawphah bulpi hi a, hong kilak thu maan khempeuh bulpi ahi hi. Christian upna leh gamtatna thukhun ahi hi.

Pasian’ Thu sangthei/nialthei hihang. Jehoiakim bangin nial nuamlua kisa a khengvalpi in laisiangthobu khawng haltum (Jer. 36:22-27) liang keini. Thu ummi kahi ci napi in awlmawh lohna zong, thubuaipi khatmah ahi hi. Pasian’ Thu mukhia in saan theih ciangin; kumpi Josiah bangin kikheel in midangte zong Pasian kiangah paipih thei hihang (2 Kumpi 22:10-13).

Tulai mite in laisiangtho khawng a khialhna peuh zongin simmawh bawl uhhi. Khangthak mite omdan tawh kituaklo (a kituaklo dingmah hi) ci uhhi. Science lam leh minam ngeina khawngtawh zong kituakkei peuh ci uhhi.

Christian te in mimal khalam khantohna ding leh kikheelna dingin zanglo uh a, biakna upnalam dinmun kipsakna ding bekin zang uhhi. Pawlkhat te in eima nisim nuntak na sungah tangthu- lam leh mimal in zatpahtheih ding ukzawhna aana anial bangin ngaih sun uhhi.

Tanglai hunin, laigelh na’ng pen mantamlua hi. Tua banah, mitampi in lai simthei lo uh ahih manun, Pasian’ Thu hong puak ding Ama’ kamsangte kisampha hi. Kha Siang- tho vangliatna nuai ah, Pasian’ Thu; kampau mahbang in, khutgelh in zong, lungsim sung bunsuk thei a, mawhna hong lak in kikheelna leh puahphatna hong tunthei hi.

Thu in kisikna leh thumanna lamah hongsam hi (Sawl. 5:29; Heb. 4:12). Hihthu mahmah pen mite’n a nialnopna a hangthu ahi hi. Amau nundan tungah laisiangtho thu gente deihlo uhhi.
