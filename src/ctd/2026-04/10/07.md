---
title:  Thuneihna tawh Paukhia
date:   04/12/2026
---

Kamsang te pen Pasian in Ama’ kamtaai dingin a teel ahih manin thuneihna tawh paukhia uhhi. Laisiangtho a gelh kam- sangte; laisiangtho a gelhlo kamsangte; laisiangtho hunkheng khit laisiangtho gelhlo kamsangte cihbang in kikhen thei hi. Avekpi un humuap vive hi uh a, amau’ thupuakte in Pasian kiangpan thuneih- na lailu nei uhhi.

EGWhite icih zong laisiangtho khengkhit, laisiangtho gelh- lo kamsangte lakah khat ahi hi. Seventh-day Adventists te in ama’ laigelhte Laisiangtho behlapna leh akikim in koih hetlo uhhi. Ama’ laigelhte in apaukhiatte mahbangin thunei a, amah ngiat akicihna ah “Hih kalaigelhte sungah, ka tecipannate sungah, Topa in keimah hong piaksa tengmah ka hong piasawn hi. Laidal tun- gah keima ngaihdan tawh laimal khat zong kagelh kei hi. Tuate khempeuh mangmuhna sungah Pasian hong honkhiatte vive hi a, vantung tokhom pan hong tangsuk khuavak ahi hi” —Selected Messages, book 1, p. 29.

Laisiangtho sung ombangin, EGWhite in, mawhna sungah nundan leh Zeisu i kisapzia, thu ummi khempeuh tungah hehpihna tawh hong piak tawntung nunna lam-etna khatbek mah cihte pen ama’ thupuak sungah a vanglian thu ahihi.

Ama’ tungtawn in Pasian’ thupuak panin, Vantung pan Lu- cifer a pukna pan kipan in sialehpha kidona in hun beidong in om- paisuak ding a, a tawpna ah mawhna leh gitlohna pen Satan leh a vanmite khempeuh tawh kihemkhia siangta ding cih honghilh hi.

EGWhite’ laigelhte in hun nunung tawh kisai laisiangtho thukhunte hong mukhia sak hi. Amah’n pawlpi leh gam hong ki helzau in, biakna suahtakna hong pibawl ding hi. Gamtat luhek hong zulhzau ding hong hilhkhol a, EGWhite in Pasian’ thukham laamsang hi. “Sapi ciaptehna” cihkhawng zong ama’ laigelhte tungah kibulphuhlo a, hun nunung thu piang dingte hong muhbe- hsak hibek hi.

Christian nundan neihtakpi ding zong amah’n thapia hi.

Ama’ thupuak khalam ah cidamna, pilna, innkuan kikhawlh- na, NulehPa dinmun, gamtathoihna cihte leh adang tampitak in, minundan tampi kheelsak a nundan sangzaw ngahsak hi.

EGWhite’ laigelhte pen laisiangtho sungah hong omloh hang, ni nunung a nungta mite lamlahna dingin Pasian in amah zang hi. Ama’ laigelhte in kamsanna thuneihna tawh paukhia gige hi.
