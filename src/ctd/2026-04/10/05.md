---
title:  Kipawlnalam ah Thuneihna
date:   02/12/2026
---

Hosea 12: 13 sungah “Topa in kamsang khat zangin Egypt panin Israelte paikhiat pih a, kamsang khat nasepna tawh amaute suakta in anungta uhhi” cihi. Pasian’ mite kemcing in makaihna pen laisiangtho kamsangte sepding lakpan khenkhat mah ahi hi.

Thkte 4:4-8; 2 Khang. 20:14-17; Pai. 12:1-3 leh Thkna. 1:9- 16 cihteng sim lecin, Pasian’ mite makaihdan ding lahkhia dingin kamsangte phamawh mahmah uhhi. Gal leh sa haksatna sungah, kamsangte tungtawn in Pasian in Jehaziel (Jehosaphat) leh Debo- rah tetungah ahih bangin; tatdan hilh, thapia, mipite leh makai te lam-etna guan hi.

Tua banah kamsangte mahin pawite leh biakpiakna makaih uhhi (Pai. 12:1-3). Hun dangte ah, Pasian in minam ki ukzia ding zong kamsangte tungtawn mahin thupia hi. Tomgen leng, Israel nundan limlim, Pasian’ teeltuam kamtaaite thuhilh makaihlohna mun omlo hi.

`Kamsang silpiak in Christian masate’ kipawlna leh ki makaihna ah a themneng pan kipan a makaihna bangteng omthei hiam? Sawl. 6:1-7; 1Cor. 5: 1-5; 1Cor. 7: 10-16; Titus 1: 5.`

Kipawlna zat tangzang laibu omlo, makai kician mellah omlo pi in kipawlna khat dingsak dingin kingaihsun in. Tuabang kipawlna in tona lianpi tuak ding hipah hi. Tuabang dinmun ah Christian masa pawlpite ana omkhin uh ahi hi. Zeisu lah van kahtokhin a, nungzui tetawh omkhawm nawnlo hi. A Topa uh ki- angpan sawlna ngah uh a: “Leitung khempeuh ah pai unla thuhilh un” (Maku 16: 15) cihi. Nungzui sawmlehnih kiteelkhia khin ahi- hhang, a kipattheihna dingun kipawlna kician neinailo uhhi.

Pawlpi masa in tona tampi phukha uh a, pawlpi kipawlna, mission a ding kipawl na, a thumaanlo a citaklo zualzang te pholhkhia in thukhen ding cihte bangmah omlo hi. Pawlpi masate sungah kamsanna silpiak nasepna in, pawlpi a kipkho nading leh

kepcinna dingin thupi mahmah hi (Sawl. 13:1, 2). Pawlpi masap- en in kamsang silpiak panpihna omlo pi in kipanthei ta a, Pasian’ nasepna mainawt thei ding cih peuhmah ngaihsut haksa lua hi.

`“Biakna kipawlna” cih limlim a umlo zong om uhhi. Tona tampi lakmah ah, kipawlna ah kihelkhakna hang in bang hamphatna kineithei ding hiam? Nangsang a lianzaw tham khat peuhpeuh sung na omkhak manin bang piakhia in bang ngah thei ding nahiam?`
