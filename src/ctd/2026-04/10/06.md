---
title:  Laisiangtho sung a Kihel leh Kihello
date:   03/12/2026
---

`Kamsangte 1 Khang. 29:29 leh 2 Khang. 9:29 simin. Hih munnih tegel in kamsang pawlkhatte’ thu bangteng gen hiam?`

Kamsang taktak mah hinapi, laisiangtho sungah hong kihel khalo kamsang pawl- khat om a; Nathan, Gad, Ahijah leh Iddo cihte ahi uhhi.

`1 Kumpite 11:29-39 leh Luka 7:28 simin. Laisiangtho sungah hong kihello kamsangte’ thuneihna bangteng hong hilh hiam?`

Kamsang pawlkhatte’ laigelhte laisiangtho sungah hong kihel hinapi, pawlkhat a kihelloh sese theihna athu kitheilo ahih- hang, laisiangtho sung hong kihello kamsangte zong Pasian’ hu- muap mah ahihlam thei hihang. Khalam silpiak kamsanna silpiak zong hun tawp dongin om paisuak ding cih laisiangtho in hong hilh hi.

Laisiangtho hun kheng khit ciangin laisiangtho sunga aki- hello kamsangte hong piang ding cih laisiangtho mahin gen a, “Hih nasep a tuamtuamte khempeuh a sem dingin Pasian’ mi khempeuh kigingkhol sak in Christ a dingin nasem in, Christ’ pumpi a khan- sak theihna ding” (Eph. 4:12, 13), “Topa’ ni tua a lauhuai hun a hong tunmaa” (Joel 2:31) dong omlai ding cihi.

David in mithahna leh zualzangna a mawh khit ciangin, amah a talsik dingin Pasian in Nathan paisak hi (2 Sam. 12:7- 14). Nathan in laisiangtho bukhat beek a gelhloh hang, David in Nathan thuneihna dong kik hetlo hi. Tua ahih manin kamsangte’ thuneihna pen laisiangtho sunga kihel/kihello cih tawh hizawlo in; amau’ laigelhte in Pasian humuap ahihleh hihloh tungbekah om hi.

Tua hi a, laisiangtho sungah akihello kamsang maan khempeuh, adiak in laisiang- tho hunkheng khit – tuhun kamsang EGWhite bangte – in amau mahmah laisiangtho’ nuai ah omzaw uhhi. Amau’ thupuakte pen Laisiangtho tawh sittel ding hang a, upna leh thukhunpi a dingin laisiangtho khengsaklo ding hihang.

EGWhite in “Topa in na laisiangtho hong simsak nuam hi. Ama’Thu kheng dingin thuthak dang bangmah hong piak- beh nawnlo hi. Hih khuavak in abuai mah mah lungsimte

Topa’ Thu kiang tungsak ding a, a nekha mite a dingin kha nuntakna sisan suak ding hi” cihi —Selected Messages, book 3, p. 29.

`Pawlpi khat ihihna tawh hih kammalte koibang in sang ihi hiam?`
