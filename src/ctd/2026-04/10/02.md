---
title:  Pasian’ Thuneihna Lailutsakna
date:   29/11/2026
---

Mi khatpeuh in nangmah aiawh in kampau ding thuneihna napiakma in, namuan mahmah mikhat beek teelmasa lo ding na hiam? Tua nateel in nangma dinmun theitel in thumaan takin hong pulaaksak ding hi.

`Pai. 2:11-15 leh Pai. 3:1-3, 10-13 simin. Moses in a tu- ucinhun tengin Pasian a ding kampau ding thuneihna koibang in piathei hiam?`

Moses in a nuntakhun masa kum 40 sungteng Egypt te pil- na sangpen teng sinna in beisak ahihhang; tua pilna in Pasian’ kamtaaipa suakding in kigingzo saklo hi. Tua khit kum 40 sung gamlak ah tuucinna in hunzang in leitung tawh kipelh kilkel in om hi. Kumpi khat’ dinmun panin a kisinsa tampitak mah a hepkhiat kulta hi. Israel a suahtakma in, Moses in aiawh in a kampausak ding Pasian a theihtel masak kul hi.

`Pai. 4:10-16 simin. Pasian leh Moses a kihona uh panin, kamsangte koici kisap a, koici nasep hiam cih bangteng kisin thei hiam?`

Moses in Pasian’ dinmun ah om ding a, Aaron ahihleh Moses a ding kampaupa hi ding hi (Pai. 4:16; Pai. 7:1). Moses leh Aaron tungah ahih mahbang in, Pasian in Ama’ kamsangte’n Ama’ thupuakte a piakkhiat theihna ding lampi vaihawmsak hi.

Moses in amah sumh mahmah phot ahihlam thei hi. A mipih Israel tate tawh a nunungpen a kitalsik hun lungngai kik a, ama’ hihtheihna aana koipan cih dot thuak kha hi (Pai. 2:14). Moses’ launa teng Pasian in telsiamsak mahmah hi. Ama’ launa teng Pa- sian in lungduai takin siansuah masa hi.

Paulam theih ding khempeuh hepkhiatsak masa hinapi Mo- ses in tua sapna nial teitei lai hi. A launa tengin uplohna tung- sukta hi. Nung heineilua kisa Pasian in amah azat theih ding dan khawng dotbawl lai hi. Tua ciangin nungheinei kamsangpa pen Pasian in mainawt dingin thu piata hi (Pai. 3:14-17).

`Nang zong kamsang khat hikhalo mah ding nahihhang, Pasian in bang hihding in nang hong sam hiding hiam? Hih sapna a dingin nangmah a hoihpen ding in koibang in kisak- hol thei ding nahiam?`
