---
title:  Kamsang Zuaute
date:   15/10/2026
---

Pasian in bangpeuh bawl taleh, Satan in atuh bawl pahpah hi. Kamsangte zong tua mahbang ahi uhhi. Israel in kamsang maan leh zuau mu khawm uhhi. A thubulpi in a ki lamdanna theih ding hibek hi.

`Jeremiah 14:1-15 simin. Hitengah bangteng piang a, Topa in Ama’min tawh “kamsang” zuaute koibang in hih hiam?`

Jeremiah 14 sungah, Judah a ding thukhenna; namsau tawh, kialpi tawh, natna tawh ci in Jeremiah kiangah thupia hi. Tuahun a kamsang zuaute in Jeremiah thupuak pen langdo hi. Jeremiah in kiteltak in hilhcian a: “Tua ciangin Topa in, ‘tua kamsangte in Keima’ min suangin zuauthu hong gen uhhi. Amaute kasawl kei a, thu kapia kei a, zong ka hopih kei hi. Note kiangah hong gen thute a maanlo mangmuhna, bangmah in akimang lo aisaanna leh amau’ ngaihsuttawm thute ahi hi” (Jer. 14:14).

Kamsangzuaute pen amau biak pasian zuaute kiangpan kamsanna hi a, siahuai lua hi (Jer. 23:13, 14). Pasian in “Amaute kasawl kei a, thu kapia kei a, zong ka hopih kei hi” (Jer. 14:14) cihi. Amau pen “a phuahtawm uh thu ahilh kamsangte ” (Jer. 23: 26). “Amau lungsim suak bangin kamsanna thu a gen kamsangte” langkhat ah Ezekiel in thugen khin hi (Ezek. 13:2).

Kamsangzuaute in mipi muhdan vive zui uhhi. 1 Kumpite 22 sunga, “kamsang” 400 in kumpi Ahab thumang in a kikaihkho- pna om hi (1 Kum. 22:6). Amauteng un aw kikhat in Ramoth Gil- ead sim dingin thu pia ngeingai uhhi. Ahab in Asherah kamsangte thupiakna a ngahkhit ciangin Judah kumpi Jehoshaphat zong thu dotbawl hi.

Jehoshaphat in, galsim kuantuak maw tuaklo cihthu kam- sang Micaiah dongding in Jehoshaphat in hanthawn hi. Tuaciang in Micaiah kiangah kamtaai a paipa in ‘Thu kikhat in kamsangte’ thugen pen kumpipa a dingin a hoihlam ahi hi. Amaute bangin nang zong gen inla, a hoihlam in genin” (1 Kum. 22:13) aci hi.

Micaiah in zuau gen dingin a kisawlngawhna pen Asher- ah kamsangzuaute thupiak hihtuak a; ahih hang Micaiah in; mipi’ deihna lam zuizawlo in; ama’ gending thu Pasian in a hilh ding ngak ngiungeu hi (1 Kum. 22:14).

`Kamsang nahi a nahi kei zongin, mipi deihna zuih ding baih mahmah hi. Leitung ngeinate pen siahuai mahmah ahih manin, banghang in ngaihsutna hoih hithei ngeilo hiam?`
