---
title:  Kamsang Maante Pulakkhiatna
date:   12/10/2026
---

Israel’ tangthu masalam pawlah kamsangzuau om cih thukip om hi (Thkna. 18: 20). Tua ahih manin Pasian in kamsang maante tawh makaihlo in, Amite mawk nutsiat hetlo hi. Pasian’ pulaakna leh humuapna a sangkha kamsang maante in, mipite lakah amau bangmah hizolo, vang zong neimello mahleh; citaktak, kimtak leh thumaan takin Pasian’ thupuakte tangko uhhi.

`1 Kumpite 22:1-14 simin. Kamsang maante in koibang in nasem uh ci hiam?`

Kumpi khatin “kei’ thu ahoih gen ngeilo in, asia genden ahih manin, amah ka mudah hi” (1 Kumpite 22:8) a cih in kumpi- pa’thu tampi gen a; kamsangpa’ thu tamgen zawlai hi. Amah pen, kumpipa kiang nangawn ah kumpipa maipha ngahna ding hiphial taleh thumaan gen ding a lauhetlo pa khat ahi hi.

Micaiah in kamsangzuau 400 talsik in, ama’ tungah a kipu- laak thumaan genkhia hi. Pasian kiangpan hongpai, amau langkhat pan thupuakte, kheel in, kumpi gilo Ahab leh azii Jezebel tegel maipha ngahna dingin; ki sawlngawh hinapi (1 Kumpite 22: 13, 14), mite’n sangthei/sangtheilo cih ngaihsun selo in Pasian’ thu- maan gen cinten lel hi.

`1 Samuel 9:3-14 simin. Hih mun in, midangte in Pasian’ kamsang maante a mudan uh koibang in gen hiam?`

Laisiangtho sungah a ettehhuai kamsangte nundanhoih tampitak mah om hi. Tua lakah, Saul pen a pa Kish in, a laa mang zongsak hi. Saul khualzinpih a nasempa in tua laa mang zonna lungkiatloh ding leh “hih khuasungah a vanglian mahmah Pasian’ mi khat om” (1 Sam. 9: 6) cihi.

Hiteng ah Samuel’ thu a pulaakna in a kammal zat in Pasian’ kamsangte omzia leh nundan ding pulaakna hipah hi. Laisiangtho sungah kamsangmaan peuhmah, ama’ tatlehhek tawh kizui in “Pa- sian’ mi ” kici ngiat hi. Tua laitak mahin “Pasian’ mi ” lakpan pawlkhat in kamsangte hi leuleu a; tua in zong amaute mawhbaan- glo suahpah tuanlo hi.

`A lianpenpen “Pasian’ mi ” te lakah mawhbaanglo namuh kua om hiam?`
