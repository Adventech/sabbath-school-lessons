### Tukalsung Simding
James 5:17, 18; 1 Kumpite 19:1-16; 1 Kumpite 22:1-14; Pai. 15:20; 2 Kumpite 22:11-14; Jer. 14:1-15.

> <p>Kamngah</p>
> Jehoshaphat dingin, “Judah gam leh Jerusa- lem a tengte aw, ka thugen za un: Topa na Pasian uh um un, tua hileh note nakip ding uhhi; Ama’ kamsangte um un, tua hileh na maavang ding uhhi” aci hi (2 Khang. 20:20).

Leitung bup ommun lahna{The Global Positioning Sys- tem (GPS)} in khualzin mite koiteng tung uh, koilam zuan uh leh mailam ah bang launa om cihkhawng hong theisak hi. Vanlam zuan khalam khualzin mite in zong tu leh mailam ah lamhilh din- gin “lamlah na” kisam uhhi. A beisa hun lai in Pasian hihdan lam- pi khat pen, Laisiangtholui kamsang te nasepna tungtawn ahi hi.

Amau kihona sungah, ngaihsut piakna, genkholna, lakam leh late tungtawn in kam sang tampite in mite tungah Pasian thupuakte mite tungah hawmkhia sawn uhhi.

Tukal sinna sungah Laisiangtholui kamsangte in, Pasian’ nasem kamsangte in kamsaanna silpiakte hong pulak khiatna sungpanin i kisin ding uhhi.

I muhding ahi, amau zong eite mahbang in mihing mah hi a, lauthei, hehthei in lungkia thei hi. Amau a dingin nundan pen haksa mahmah a, tona leh haksatna tawh a ki dim mahbang in, eite a ding zong ahi hi.

Tua ahihleh Pasian’ nasemte a kici tuate tungtawn in bangteng khawng ikisin thei ding hiam?
