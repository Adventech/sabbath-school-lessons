---
title:  Kamsangte’ Nasep
date:   13/10/2026
---

`1 Samuel 8:4-22 leh Siampi. 8:10-13 simin. Kamsangte’ nasep in Kumpite leh Siampite tawh koibang in kizom hiam?`

Kamsangte in hunsiatlai/hunhoihlai in kumpite tawh kizom uhhi. Israel in kumpi khat a nget ciangin (1 Sam. 8:4-22), “minam- dangte mahbang in kote tungah hong thukhen ding kumpi khat” (1 Sam. 8:5) ci uhhi. A dahhuailua lim et-theih ding in Pasian’ mite in akhang akhang in: amau’ kiimkot a mite tawh kibang nuam uhhi.

Hih tungtang ah amau’ ngetna tungtawn in, Pasian in kam- sangpa tungtawnin hilhkhol khinmah taleh, hong piang teitei ding thu ngaihsun zolo uhhi (1 Sam. 8:11-18). A hilhkhol sam hangin amau’ lunggulhna bulphuhzaw in, Israel tangthu sungah kumpi masapen dingin Samuel in Kumpi Saul sathau nilh hi.

Laisiangtholui laibu 1 leh 2 Kumpite leh 1 leh 2 Khang- tangthu in, Israel leh Judah sunga kumpite ukzawhna lamah a dinmun uh ciamteh hi. Laisiangtho tangthu sungah, Pasian’ mai ah apha a gamta sangin asia a gamta tamzaw thampek hi. Kam- sangte’ nasep bulpi khat pen, a citaklo kumpite in Pasian’ thukham mang dingin kilehkik sak ding ahi hi. Tua hangmah in kamsangte in lauhuaina tuak uh a, pawlkhat bangpen, Pasian’ mite a lampi- alpih kumpite in thatlum phial uhhi (1 Kum. 18:4-19, 2 Khang. 24:20-22).

Kamsangte in Siampite tawh zong kizom uhhi. Pasian’ kamsangpa Moses in, Israel tate’ siampi masapente dingin Aaron leh a tapate sathau nilh hi (Siam. 8:10-13). Siampite in kamsangte tungtawn in Pasian’ thuhilhna a saan hun zong om uhhi (Hos. 6:9, Mal. 2:1-3). A kician mahmah et theih dingin, Eli innkuan pen, golhguk nekna hangin thu hilh thuak uhhi (1 Sam. 2:27-36). A hampha tuamdiak mite in, gitlohna a gamtat uh ciangun thuhilhna khauh angahna uh pawlkhat ahi hi.

Kamsangte pen mihingte a thuhilh Siate (2 Khang. 34) ahi uhhi. Pasian kiangpan thupuakte pen siampite, kumpite leh mipite tungah puaksawn uhhi (Jer. 5:31, Ezek. 22: 26). Kamsangte in amau’ kiimlehpam minamte kiangah amau a ding Pasian’ ngiim- na, lai siangtholui kamsangte in zong tamveipi a genphatphat thupuakte genkikkik uhhi (Isa. 60:1-3; 62:1, 2).

Kamsangte in Pasian’ thupuakte citaktak in genkhia bek ding hilo in, khatveivei laibu sungah gelh in kemcing dingin ki- sawl lailai hi (2 Khang. 13:22). Kamsangte zong a thanem mih- ingte vive ahihtei uhhang; amaute un thungen numei/pasal, Pa- sian’ kammal; Pasian leh mihing a ding nasemte ahi uhhi (Jer. 42:4, 2 Kum. 22:15-17).
