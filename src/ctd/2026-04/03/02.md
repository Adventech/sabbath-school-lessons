---
title:  Kamsangte zong Mihing Ahihna
date:   11/10/2026
---

Nungkal sunga i muhsa mahbang in, langdona nasia mah- mah kawmkal ah, Topa in Elijah zangin Israel mite lakah nasep lianpi khat sem hi. Amah tungtawn in Topa’ gual zawhna in nasia mahmah hi.

`James in kamsang Elijah a pulakna ngaihsun pha kik in James 5: 17, 18. Elijah in zong “eite mahbang hi” acihteh bang a cinuam hiding hiam?`

Elijah in Carmel mualtung ah gualzawhna lianpi khat hong ngah hi (1 Kumpi 18). Pasian in amah zangin vanglian takin nasem a, Laisiangtholui tangthu sungah thu lam- dang penpen khat: Elijah kammal tungtawn in vantung panin meikuang hong tuaksuk liang hi (1 Kumpite 18:38). Hih Pasian’ mipa in hibang khit ciangin lungnuam mahmah dingin na umkha ding uhhi.

`1 Kumpite 19:1-16 simin. Pasian’ kamsangpa lungsim bitna ding bangteng hong gen hiam?`

“Phazo hi! Topa aw, tu in ka nuntakna hong lata in. Kapate sangin kei kahoihzaw tuankei hi!” (1 Kumpite 19:4). Hihmahmah zong mikhat in lungkiatna nei cihna ahi hi. Banghang? Carmel mualtung a thupiangte pen zuih ding thu bailam hihetlo hi napi; thukhat bel hi tengah om hi.

A thubulpi in ama’ kammal sungah ompah hi, “Vanglian Pa- sian Topa a dingin ka huaiham mahmah hi. Banghang hiam cihleh Israel mite in na thuciamna nusia in, na biakna taute lawnkhia uh a, namsau tawh na kamsangte that hi. Kei, keibek ka omlai hi. Amaute in keinangawn thah dingin hong zonglai uhhi ” (1 Kumpite 19:10).

Hihthu ah ama’ nasepna sungah guallelhna khat tungah kah- tam hihtuak hi. Ama’ hihtheih zahmah hanciam himah taleh, amah bekin tua tuikhang daalzo ding bang ahih tei hang langdona ahi- hleh omveve hi. Hiteng ah nasia takin bangteng piang taleh, hih Pasian’ mipa’ mihingna leh thanemna kimu thei veve hi. Thudang khat panin Elijah i cih zong mihing mah hi a, “eite khat mahbang” ahi hi.

`Nangma nunna sung leh na maapanna sungah “lelhna” peuh banghang in ombaih mahmah thei hiam? Na lungkiat laitak in bang laisiangtho kammal khawng in nang hong huhthei naci hiam?`
