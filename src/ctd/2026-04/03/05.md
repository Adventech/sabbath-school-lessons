---
title:  Numei Kamsangte
date:   14/10/2026
---

`Paikhiat. 15:20; Thukhen. 4:4; 2 Kum. 22:14 leh Nehe- miah 6:14 simin. Hih munteng in kamsangte nasep bangteng khawng hong hilh hiam?`

Pasian in numei kamsang zong sam ahihna laisiangtho sun- gah tecikip tampi om hi. Hebrew kammal nevi’ah (Pai. 15: 20) leh Greek kammal prophētis (Luka 2: 36) cihte a zatdan enin. Micah 6: 4 in, mipite tungah Moses leh Aaron a kiswl lai in Miriam zong hel hi. Israel mite in Tuipisan a kantan khit uh ciangun gualzawh- na la a sa pen kamsangnu Miriam hi cihi (Pai. 15:20, 21).

Hih kamsangnu in adang kamsangte mahbang in mawh baanglo hilo a; Moses’ zi Ethiopia numei tungtang in a zumhuai mahmah a gamtatna hangin, tua kamsangnu in gawtna thuak kha hi (Gam. 12:1-10).

Kamsangnu dangkhat’ nasepna Deborah’ tangthu sungah om a, Israel sungah thukhen mangnu sem hi (Thkte. 4:1-4). Deb- orah in Pasian kiangpan pulaakna (Thkte. 4:4) ngahkha ahihlam thukipkho om hi (Thkte. 4:4-9, 14-22). A gualzawhna la (Thkte. 5:2-31) zong; Pasian hong lailutna lahna hi a, tualai hun a dingin biaknalam thuthuk upna tawh kidim hi.

`2 Kum. 22:11-14 simin. Kumpi Josiah’ mite in bang- hang in Huldah kiangah pai uh hiam? Tuanu in nasep thupi sem ahihna hihmun in bangteng tawh hong gen hiam?`

Huldah “kamsangnu ” kiangah kumpi Josiah ukna Uliante in thukhamna laibute a simkhit uh ciangun (2 Kum. 22:8) (Thuhilhkikna hihtuak) kumpipa in “a puan balkeek” (2 Kum. 22:11) in, ngaihsutna ngen uh hi (2 Kum. 22:14; 2 Khang. 34:22). Hihpen thu phamawhpi khat hi a; Kumpipa in Huldah kiangah tangtak in paipah vingveng hi.

Tuanu in Judah tungah a tung ding thukhenna thu Pasian ki- angpan thupuak ngah khinzo hi. Huldah kamsaana thupuak tung- tawn in, Josiah’ puahphatna in, Pasian’ mite tungah thukhenna teng lumlet ziau hi.

`Pasal kamsangte, numei kamsangte, Pasian sapsa mah hi taleh, mawhnei vive mah hi uh a, hehpihna ngai veve uhhi. Amau’ tangthu tungpan in bang lam- etna khawng nangah thei diam?`
