Laisiangtholui kamsangte nundan et ciangin, Pasian’ kam- taai hih zong pen thu baih hivetlo cih hong theisak hi. EGWhite in zong tua laisiangtho kamsangte mahbang in tuakkha veve hi. Nung Friday lai i sinsa mahbang in tuanu pen Pasian’ sapna a saanmoilai, maizum in cidam hetlo hi. A nihveina sapna a ngah takciang in, Pasian’ sapna hi ci in kipsak in, hibang in gelh hi; “Topa in mangmuhna a piakkhit kalkhat khit bang ciangin, Topa in mang dang khat hongmusak a, keima kantan ding haksat- na hong lahkhol hi; kei zong paikhia in mi dangte zong hilhsawn ding hileng, langdona nasiatak in tuakkha ding cihi. Hih mangin nakpi takin hong lungkham sak hi. Keilah kum sawmlehsagih pha- pan, zong dammello lailai kahih manin, hih vangikpi midang tun- gah piazaw dingin nakpi takin thu ka ngen hi. Ahihhang kangah khuavak ahihleh ‘nang tungah kong lah khempeuh mite theisak in’ cihi. Leitung ah paikhia dingin bel kakiging hetkei hi. Pianpih upna neucik bek nei hi ing. Hankhuk lam nangawn ka lunggulh zaw liang hi. Hih nasep kasep sang in sih ding peuh ka lunggulh zaw tamai hi.” —Spiritual Gifts, vol. 2, pp. 35, 36.

Ellen Harmon (White) in tua sapna a saan sang in si nua- mzaw cih na phawk hiam? Thungetna tampitak khit nungah, thu ummite’ hanthotna tawh tua sapna sang in a nuntak sungteng Topa sapna peuhpeuh ah zui hi. EGWhite, Spiritual Gifts, vol. 2, p. 38.

Amah pen mailam SDA pawlpi sungah huzaap lianpen a neinu hong suak ding hi. Bangbang ahi zongin EGWhite pen mih- ing mah hi a, mawhna khialhna, taksalam haksat na, innkuan sung haksatna leh mitawh kizopna ah haksatna cihte nei veve hi. Amah zong mihing a hihna pilna lianpi khat mah kingah a: Pasian in mi- poite zong amau un a utnak vua leh teel cihthu ahi hi.

Kha Siangtho makaihna nuai ah EGWhite kamsang nasepna in thu ummite pen Laisiangtho thumaan tawh kizomsak hi. 1860 kum in, a suaktuung SDA pawlpi leh a nasepna ah vanmi thumte thupuak tangkona ah a makailam in hong pang thei hi. Pasian in hih thu ummi pawlkhat teng pen 1863 kum ciangin Seventh-day Adventist Church ci in ding theisak hi.

Tuakhit kum pawlkhat khit ciangin EGWhite kamsaanna aw in Adventistte leitungbup cidamna lamleh pilna lamah hong huzaap zo mahmah ta hi. Tuanu in ama’ nasepna sungah a haw- mkhiat theih a thupi pen in laimai 100,000 phial apha EGWhite kamsaanna laigelhte hi a, tuni in zong leitung bup ah ki tangko lai hi.
