---
title:  "Okwethu Ukululekwa"
date:   12/11/2026
---

Zonke ezi ngxelo zikwiTestamente eNdala, nokuba zimdaka nokuba zilusizi kangakanani, zanikwa ngesizathu esilungileyo: kakhulukazi, ukuze sifunde kuzo izifundo ngoko kwenzekayo xa simvukela uThixo.

`Funda 1 Korinte 10:6-12. Ngokukampostile uPawulos, kwakutheni ukuze uThixo akhuthaze abaprofeti ukuba bakubhale eBhayibhieli oko bakubhalayo?`

Kwezi ndima, uPawulos ukrwela umgca ohlanganisa ixesha eladlulayo nelangoku, ehlanganisa uIsrayeli wakudala nebandla, thina abamamkeleyo uYesu njengeNkosi noMsindisi esiyinxalenye yalo ngoku (Gal. 6:14-16).

UPawulos ukubeka kucace ukuba ingxelo eyadlulayo yamava oluntu noThixo-nokuba alungile, nokuba mabi-akufuneki angajongwa okanye angakhathalelwa. Inyaniso yeyokuba, uThixo wakubona kubalulekile ukuba akubhale kube yimizekelo ukuze “singabi ngabakhanuki bezinto ezimbi, njengokuba bakhanukayo bona abo” (1 Kor. 10:6). Ngamanye amazwi, okwadlulayo kukholisa ukuba yintshayelelo yokuzayo. Okwadlulayo kukholisa ukubeka okungumxholo wokwenzeka ngoku nokuya kwenzeka kwixa elizayo.

Inyaniso, uThixo uxela ukuba asimiselwanga ukuziphinda iimpazamo zezizukulwana ezidlulileyo, ukuba nje siyazimamela iziluleko zesiBhalo nezigidimi zesiprofeto ezithunyelwe nguYe kuthi ngabaprofeti baKhe. Ukuba siyamamela, sifunde sithobele, ubomi bethu buya kwakheka, size siphumelele (2 IziKron. 20:20). Kwabanye, nempumelelo yasemoyeni iye yacaca ngokuba namakhaya amahle, iimoto, impahla, nezinye izinto. Ngexesha uThixo ethembisa ukukhathalela iintswelo zethu, ukwafuna nemiphefumlo yethu ibe nempumelelo, njengoko siphumelela ngezinye iindlela (3 Yohane 2). Kona ukuzala okuyinene okuhlala ixesha elide ebomini bobuKristu kufuna ukuthotyelwa kwezigidimi zikaThixo.

UThixo akadlali ngamazwi. Xa ethetha, aMazwi aKhe angunaphakade. Amandla awo nokusebenza kwawo akuze kuphele (Isa. 40:8). Anikelwe ukululekwa kwethu, “thina aba, bafikelweyo ziziphelo zawo amaphakade” (1 Kor. 10:11). Ukuba oko kwakuyinyaniso ngoko, kunokuba abeke phi ukusebenza kuthi loo mazwi namhlanje?

`Xa ufunda iingxelo eziseBhayibhileni zovukelo nokungakholwa, zifundo zini ongazifumana kuzo ezinokunceda ukuphephe ukwenza izinto ezifanayo?`
