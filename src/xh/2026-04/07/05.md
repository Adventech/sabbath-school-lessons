---
title:  "Ubuhanahanisi"
date:   11/11/2026
---

Ingaba sowakha wakhangela kwizinto ezenziwe ngabanye abantu kwixesha eladlulayo waza wazicingela usithi, Ukuba ibindim, ndandingazange ndiyenze into abayenzayo aba bantu! Sekunjalo, ukuba sinyanisekile kuthi, sisenokungenzi nqo izinto abazenzayo, kodwa sikholisa ukuziphatha ngeendlela ezifanayo, siphinde iimpazamo ezinye lo gama, mhlawumbi sizimgamekisa ngabom koko sikwenzayo. Ngumthetho-siseko awayebhekisa kuwo uYesu xa wayesithi: “ ‘Mhanahanisindini! Wukhuphe kuqala umqadi lowo esweni lakho, wandule ukubona kakuhle ukusikhupha isibi esisesweni lomzalwana wakho’ ” (Mat. 7:5).

`Funda Mateyu 23:30-35. Ibaluleke kangakanani kodwa ibuhlungu le nto ithethwa nguYesu apha? Sithini isilumkiso esingasidinga ukuba sisifumane kumazwi aKhe?`

Kukho ingqondo kwezi ndima yokuba uYesu wayeyithiyile indlela yokuziphatha kwabaFarisi ngenxa yokuhanahanisa kwabo okwenziwa kwathi chatha kukwala kwabo ngokuzimiseleyo ukuguquka. UYesu wayanele mpela yinkolo yabaFarisi. Wayegqibile ngabaphezulu basemoyeni ababevathe iingubo ezirhuqayo phofu bewadlula kancinane amangcwaba aqatyiweyo azele bubugoxo bamathambo abafileyo (Mat. 23:27, 28). Babengafuni ukuzivuma okanye ukuzishiya izono zabo, naxa ziye zatyhilwa kubo, naxa uYesu ephindaphinda ebanika ithuba lokuguquka baze basindiswe.

Ekusebenzeni kwaKhe naba bantu, akumangalisi ukuba uYesu wayebabiza “Bahanahanisindini!” umgca ukholisa ukuqhubeka ngolu hlobo: “ ‘Yeha, nina babhali, baFarisi, bahanahanisindini!’ ” (bona: Mat. 23:14, 15, 23, 25, 27).

Ubomi babo babuphawulwe kukuthetha into babe besenza enye. Babefundisa, umzekelo, ukuba ngokuhombisa amangcwaba abaprofeti, babe phakamisa ukholo; kodwa kwizinto ezibalulekileyo, njengokuguquka kumazwi abaprofeti, babesala ukukwenza oko. Endaweni yoko, babefuna ukuzihlanganisa namandla nemvakalelo yabaprofeti, kodwa kungabi koko kukwizigidimi zabo. Ekuhombiseni amangcwaba abasebenzi bakaThixo, bazixelela ukuba babengasokuze baliphalaze igazi labathunywa bakaThixo. Kwangelo xesha linye kodwa, babeceba ukubulala Lowo ungaphezu kakhulu kunomprofeti-uMesiya ngokwaKhe.

`Siqinisekisa njani ukuba ngokomthetho-siseko, asenzi into enye: ngaphandle sidlala inkolo lo gama ngaphakathi sizele luvukelo nokubawa nentiyo?`
