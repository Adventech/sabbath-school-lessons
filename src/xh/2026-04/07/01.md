### Fundela Isifundo sale Veki
Num. 13:26-30; Yer. 36:20-26; 2 IziKron. 36:15, 16; Mat. 23:30-35; 1 Kor. 10:6-12.

> <p>Indima Yenkumbulo</p>
> “Izinto ezenzeka kubo zingumzekelo, zabhalelwa okwethu ukufundiswa, thina bafikelweyo ziziphelo zawo amaphakade” (1 Korinte 10:11, NASB).

Abanye abantu namhlanje bakhuthazwa ukuba bayi“thethe inyaniso yabo”—amava abo, iingcamango zabo, imida yabo, ukoyiseka kwabo—nokuba zizintoni ezo. Yonke “inyaniso” inexabiso (kucingwa njalo) ngoko akufuneki ithuliswe. Kwenzeka ntoni, phofu, xa ezinye zezi “nyaniso” zibanjwe nzulu ziphikisana neNyaniso, uYesu Kristu, neLizwi laKhe eliBhaliweyo?

Okwenzekayo kukuba abanye abantu bamkela iinyaniso zeBhayibhile basuke kwiimpazamo zabo; phofu abanye babambelela kubuxoki babo. Abanye baguqule ukuziphatha ngokusabela kwinyaniso; abanye bayivukele. Ibinjalo imbali yoluntu.

Abaprofeti beBhayibhile babekholisa ukuhambisa izigidimi abantu ababengafuni ukuziva. Isiphumo, abaninzi babegxekwa, bagxothelwe kude, bade bathenjiswe ngokubulawa okanye ngokwenene babulawe (Heb. 11:32-38). Sekukho konke oku, abaprofeti bakaThixo babezivakalisa ngokuthembeka izigidimi zabo kuba inkokelo yobuprofeti yayingeyonto yokukhethwa kubo. Yayingumyalelo wokusindisa ubomi kubantu bakaThixo. Kungoko izigidimi kwakunyanzelekile ukuba zihanjiswe, nokuba kwakumdla ntoni umprofeti.

_Funda: Isifundo sale veki ulungisela iSabatha kaNovemba 14._
