date:   09/11/2026

`Funda Yeremiya 36:20-26. Kwakusenzeka ntoni kula nkundla, zaza zasabela njani iinkokeli kwisilumkiso somprofeti?`

UYeremiya wayelilizwi likaThixo lokuprofeta kwisizwe sakwaYuda ngexesha lentshabalalo elindelekileyo. Kwaye kuyiminyaka emininzi uThixo ngoYeremiya emlumkisa uYuda ngokunganeliseki kwaKhe ngabo, nokuba iBhabheli iya kuboyisa ibathimbe baye ekubhaceni (Yer. 6:8-19, Yer. 26). UYehova wayefuna ukubasindisa, kodwa kwakufuneka bethobele.

Izono zakwaYuda zazizininzi, ziphandle, zingaguquki. Unqulo lwezithixo, ukubingelelwa ngabantwana, ukukrexeza, nobubhovubhovu kwakuyinto eqheleke kakhulu ngexesha likaYeremiya, zaye izilumkiso nezibheno zomprofeti zingathotyelwa. Kwathi, njengoko ulungiselelo lukaYeremiya lwalusondela ekupheleni, izibheno zikaThixo kubantwana baKhe ababhadule bemka kuYe zaya zikhula ngokuqina, namalinge aKhe ayesoloko engawokubahlangula. KuYeremiya 32, umzekelo, uThixo uxelela uYeremiya ukuba athenge indawana yomhlaba ayigcine kuye imvume yawo, “ ‘Ngokuba utsho uYehova wemikhosi . . .: Kuya kubuya kuthengwe izindlu namasimi, nezidiliya, kweli lizwe” ’ ” (Yer. 32:15).

Yayiluthando olwamenza uThixo ayalele uYeremiya ukuba abhale uhlobo lwesibheno sokugqibela senguquko nohlaziyo. Impendulo kaYehoyiyakim kwesi sigidimi yaba kukumdelela uThixo, nesigidimi saKhe, kwanomthunywa waKhe.

`Funda Yeremiya 36:27-31. UThixo wasabela njani kwisenzo sokudelela sikaYehoyiyakim?`

Ngamanye amaxesha kubakho umkhwa wokukholwa ukuba xa sizigxotha okanye sizitshabalalisa izigidimi zikaThixo okanye abathunywa baKhe, isigidimi ngokwaso siya kuthi shwaka. Ukungakufuni kwethu ukuthobela iLizwi akusicimi isigidimi uThixo anaso esiza kuthi. Ukudelela kwethu kusuka kongeze kwingozi eza kusihlela thina neentsapho zethu ngenxa yokungazi kwethu ngenxa yenkani okanye uvukelo. Isiprofeto sikaYeremiya esingesiphelo sikakumkani uYehoyiyakim sinesilumkiso esinzima kuye wonke umntu (Yer. 36:30, 31): ukungazikhathaleli izilumkiso zikaThixo, kubeka obethu ubomi emngciphekweni, kuba siya kuzivuna iziphumo zesono nobubi bethu.

`Ukuzingisa kukaThixo ukuzama ukufikelela kubantu baKhe sebenendelo abanayo kusixelela ntoni ngesimilo sikaThixo esinothando?`
