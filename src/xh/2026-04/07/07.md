---
title:  "Ukwamkela Nokuhambisa Izigidimi Zakhe"
date:   13/11/2026
---

UArthur White, umzukulwana kaEllen G. White nowakha wangumlawuli welifa iEllen G. White Estate, waqikelela ukuba, ngexesha lokuphila kwakhe, umakhulu wakhe wafumana amakhulu emibono namaphupha obuprofeti. Imibono yayiba yeyemizuzu embalwa kuye kwiiyure eziliqela kwaye maxa wambi oku kwakubonwa ngamaqela amakhulu abantu. Kwezinye iimeko nabo bangakholelwayo babeba nethuba lokumhlola ngexesha lemibonakalaliso esekuhleni.

Njengabaprofeti beBhayibhile, uEllen G. White wayeba neempawu ezingaphezu kwendalo xa esembonweni. Ngowe-1868, uJames White wanika inkcazo yemeko kaEllen White xa esembonweni. Wayengakwazi “ukuyibona yonke into eyenzekayo kufuphi kuye”; wayengaphefumli; izihlunu zakhe zazi“qina”; “imisipha yome”; iintshukumo zakhe nokulinganisa xa ethetha kukhululekile kube nesidima”; amehlo ewavulile. Emva kokuphuma embonweni wayelahlekwa kukubona okomzuzwana, kanti kona ukubona akonakalanga. Kwakungekho unokukulawula ukuba makabe sembonweni xa kutheni, kube kude kangakanani, njengoko imibono yayingalindeleki nakuye ngokwakhe uEllen White. (Bona: James White, Life Incidents, pp. 271-274.) Njengakumaxesha eBhayibhile, ukusabela kwabantu kwizigidimi kwaye kungafani. Abanye bazamkela iingcebiso zikaThixo; abanye bazala. Ngokuyibeka kwakhe uEllen G. White: “UThixo undibeke njengomngxolisi wabantu baKhe; kwaye . . . njengoko ebeke phezu kwam umthwalo omkhulu, uya kubenza abo siya kubo esi sigidimi babe noxanduva ngendlela abasiphatha ngayo. . . . Andiwukhethanga ngokwam lo msebenzi ungakwazi ukonwabisa. Asingumsebenzi oya kuzisa kum ukuthandwa okanye udumo lwabantu. . . . Ngegama namandla oMhlawuleli wam ndiya kukwenza endinakho. Ndiya kubalumkisa ndibacebise, ndibangxolise, ndibakhuthaze njengoko uMoya kaThixo undiyalela, nokuba abantu baya kuva okanye bandinyamezele. Umsebenzi wam asinguwo owokukholisa isiqu sam, kodwa ngowokwenza intando kaBawo wam osezulwini ondinike umsebenzi wam.”—Testimonies for the Church, vol. 4, pp. 231, 232.

Kunika umdla, abaninzi kwabo bazalayo izigidimi zakhe zobuprofeti bakwenza oko kusekwe phezu kokwabo ukungakholwa okanye ukungavumelani kwabo noEllen G. White, kunokuba ngemihlaba yokwazi ngoThixo. UEllen G. White wayebathanda enobubele kwabo babebonisa ukusithandabuza isipho sakhe sobuprofeti. Inyaniso yeyokuba, wakubeka kwacaca ukuba abantu abanjalo kufuneka baphathwe “ngomonde omde nothando lobuzalwaba . . . bade bayifumane indawo yabo baze bazinze ekuxhaseni okanye ekuchaseni.”—Testimonies for the Church, vol. 1, p. 328. UEllen G. White wayoyisakele ukuba okona kuqiniseka okubonisa isipho sakhe sokuprofeta yayizizigidimi uThixo awamnika zona. Ukuba abantu babe nyanisekile ekuphandeni inyaniso, wayekholwa ukuba babeya kufikelela ekuboneni ukuba isipho sakhe sasiluqobo sinyanisekile. Wathembela kuThixo ukubakhokela ekufuneni kwabo ukuqonda okungcono, nangona wabhala ukuba uThixo wasinika ubungqina obaneleyo “ukuze siluzinzise ukholo lwethu kwicala lenyaniso.”—Testimonies for the Church, vol. 4, p. 231.
