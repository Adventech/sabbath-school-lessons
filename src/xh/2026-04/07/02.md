---
title:  "Ingqumbo Ngakumthunywa KaThixo"
date:   08/11/2026
---

UMoses, inkokeli enyuliweyo, wayelwazi uvuyo neentlungu zokukhokela indibandiba enkulu ukucanda intlango kufunwa iZwe leDinga.

`Funda Numeri 14:4-11. Kwenzeka ntoni kwezi ndima? Kutheni abantu becaphuka nje ngakuMoses noAron?`

Ukuzifundisisa ezi ndima kuveza ukuba abantwana bakaIsrayeli babenomsindo ngakuThixo kuba bengamthembanga Yena Thixo. UMoses noAron, abameli abangabantu bobuNkokeli bokugqibela bukaThixo, babengamaxhoba engqumbo yesizwe. Isidingo senkuseleko yomzimba nengqondo iyinkuthazo engumbindi abanayo bonke abantu. Xa enye ithembisa ukuba nengxaki, okanye sibona into eyingozi kumzimba nengqondo yethu, siyachukumiseka side singacingisisi.

`Funda Numeri 13:26-30. Kwakutheni ukuze oyike kakhulu kangaka amaIsrayeli? Lithini ilizwi lenkuthazo awabelana ngalo nawo uKhalebhi?`

AmaIsrayeli agcina uloyiko olufanelekileyo ngemingeni eyayiphambi kwawo ekuzuzeni iZwe leDinga, kodwa “xa abantu benikela iintliziyo zabo ekungakholweni, bazibeka phantsi kolawulo lukaSathana, kwaye akakho ongaxela ukuba angabakhokela ayokubafikisa phi.”—Ellen G. White, Amanyange Nabaprofeti, p. 389. UMoses wajongana nomngeni wobukhokeli obabuphantse ukuba yintaba engakhwelekiyo ngalo mzuzu. Uloyiko lwabantu lwavela lwathi gqi lwangumsindo ngakuAron noMoses lwaba luvukelo oluphandle ngakuThixo. Xa abantu babefuna iinkokeli ezintsha endaweni yomprofeti/nkokeli eyayikhethwe nguThixo (Dut. 34:10-12, Num. 14:4), uYoshuwa noKhalebhi bazikrazula iingubo zabo bababongoza abantu ukuba bamthembe uThixo nokuba sebesoyika: “ ‘Ukuba uYehova usinonelele, wosisa kwelo zwe, asinike; lilizwe elibaleka amasi nobusi.” Musani ukugwilika kuYehova, musani ukuboyika abantu belo zwe’ ” (Num. 14:8, 9).

`Kulula ukuthembela kuThixo xa izinto zihamba kakuhle, akunjalo? Kuthini kodwa xa kufika intlekele? Kuthiwani xa ukuthembela kuThixo kuthetha ukungonwabi okanye ukungaqondi kakuhle? Uziva njani ngelo xesha?`
