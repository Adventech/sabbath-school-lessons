---
title:  "Ukonakalisa Isigidimi: Isiqendu 2"
date:   10/11/2026
---

Ukusabela komntu kwisipho soprofeto kuyakwazi ukuba ziindlela ezininzi. Kwimeko kaMoses, abantwana bakaIsrayeli babanovukelo oluphandle kuMoses noAron, beqhutywa kukoyika iintshaba ababenokuhlangana nazo xa bezama ukungena eZweni leDinga. Kwiminyaka yamva, ukumkani uYehoyiyakim watshisa isigidimi sikaThixo ngethemba lokutshabalalisa izwi lomprofeti kaThixo.

Phofu ezi yayingezo meko zizimele zodwa nakanjani. Imbali kaIsrayeli noYuda bakudala yaye iyeyokungabafuni abaprofeti, nangona zikho imeko ezimbalwa zokubafuna.

`Funda 2 IziKronike 36:15, 16. Isizwe sakwaYuda sasabela njani kwizigidimi ezazithunyelwa nguThixo kubo ngabaprofeti baKhe? Ekugqibeleni, uThixo wathini ngokuziphatha kukaYuda?`

Ulungiselelo lukaYeremiya lokuprofeta lwasebenza ngexesha lokulawula kukaYosiya, Yehowahazi, Yehoyiyazi, Yehoyiyakim, Yehoyiyakin, noZedekiya. Ubukumkani baseBhabheli, obabukhokelwa nguNebhukadenetsare, ngoku babusemnyango kuIsrayeli. Iziprofeto zikaYeremiya zazizaliseke zonke ngexesha uZedekiya owayeminyaka ingama-21 wahlala etroneni (2 IziKro. 36:11). IBhayibhile ithi, “Wenza okubi phambi koYehova uThixo wakhe; akazithoba ebusweni bukaYeremiya umprofeti, obethetha okwasemlonyeni kaYehova (2 IziKro. 36:12).

Qaphela indlela isipho sikaYeremiya soprofeto esichazwa ngayo. Ubonwa ethetha okwasemlonyeni kaThixo. Noko kunjalo, uZedekiya akazange azithobe phambi komprofeti. Kanti ke uYeremiya, naye owayengumntu nje kuphela, umoni njengathi sonke, wayengummeli kaYehova, awayethetha endaweni yaKhe.

UYeremiya wayengomnye phakathi kwabaninzi kuloo ndawo. Kwakuyiminyaka, uThixo ethumela abaprofeti kubantu baKhe (Eks. 3:15, Ind. 105:26, Mal. 3:1, Isa. 6:8, Yer. 25:4, Mat. 11:13, Luka 11:49, njl.), ngokungafuni ukuba kubekho otshabalalayo kodwa bonke bangasinga enguqukweni” (2 Pet. 3:9). Le mbali kaIsrayeli noYuda bakudala ilibali lemizamo kaThixo engaphelendawo yokufikelela kubantu baKhe abahlehlayo ngezithembiso zaKhe zosindiso, ukuba nje bangaguquka.

Ngeli xesha leyure esemva kwimbali kaYuda, ubabalo olunikwe nguThixo kuYuda ngenkokelo nentuthuzelo yelizwi loprofeto alamkelwanga ngentobeko, nokholo, nenguquko, kodwa ngentsini, ukugculela, nokuhlekisa.

`Babagculela abathunywa bakaThixo, bawadela amazwi kaThixo, bahlekisa ngabaprofeti baKhe. Ngokuqinisekileyo, nathi namhlanje asingeke siyenze into efana naleyo—okanye singayenza?`
