---
title:  "Ukugcina UYesu Nelizwi LaKhe Embindini"
date:   23/10/2026
---

Njengoko sifundile, uThixo wabiza abaprofeti ngamaxesha amisiweyo wabanika imisebenzi ebalulekileyo. Owona msebenzi ophambili yayikukuzisa abantu ekumazini uThixo nobudlelwane obuthembekileyo naYe. UEllen G. White wayekhokelwa kukujonga kuloo ndawo kanye. Ulungiselelo lwakhe lwalunemisebenzi emibini: ukukhokelela abantu kuYesu nokubakhombisa eLizwini laKhe-iBhayibhile.

UEllen G. White wayenentshiseko ubomi bakhe bonke ngoYesu nomnqweno wokwabelana nabanye ngothando lukaThixo. Uhlelo lweencwadi zakhe ezintlanu, Imfazwe Yamaphakade, luqala luphele ngamazwi “UThixo uluthando.” Encwadini Amanyathelo Okuya KuKristu, mhlawumbi eyona ncwadi yakhe ithandwa kakhulu, wabhala wenjenje: “Ukukhula kwethu elubabalweni, uvuyo lwethu, ukuba luncedo kwethu - konke kuxhomekeke phezu kokumanyana kwethu noKristu. Kungokuba noghagamshelwano naYe, imihla ngemihla, iyure ngeyure – ngokuhlala kuYe – esithi sikhule elubabalweni. Akanelanga nje ukuba yiMbangi, kodwa unguMgqibelelisi wokholo lwethu.” —Amanyathelo Okuya KuKristu, iph. 69.

Ngeminyaka yama-1890, xa wayesebenza ukubhala incwadi Ulangazelelo Lwamaphakade, wabhala kwidayari yakhe: “Ekubhaleni ngobomi bukaKristu, ndithabatheka ngokunzulu kubo. Ndilibala nokuphefumla njengoko ndifanele ukwenza. Andikwazi nokunyamezela kwemvakalelo endifikelayo xa ndicinga ngokubaleka kukaKristu ngenxa yehlabathi.”—Manuscript 70, 1897. UEllen G. White wayethanda ukuthetha ngoYesu.

IBhayibhile yaye iyindawo yesibini olujonge kuyo ulungiselelo lukaEllen G. White. Wayeyibona imibhalo yakhe “ikukukhanya okunganeno” okukhokelela abantu “kukukhanya okukhulu”-IBhayibhile (Selected Messages, book 3, p. 29). Ngamaxesha onke, wayebakhuthaza abantu ukuba baye esiBhalweni, apho uThixo atyhile khona isimilo sothando lwaKhe necebo loSindiso. Ukuyibeka kwakhe kwasekuqaleni kolungiselelo lwakhe: “Ndilincomela wena, mfundi othandekayo, iLizwi likaThixo njengomgaqo wokholo lwakho nokwenza kwakho.”—Selected Messages, book 3, p. 30.

Sele kuyiminyaka kamva, wabhala wathi: “INkosi inqwenela ukuba nifundisise iiBhayibhile zenu. Ayininikanga okunye ukukhanya emakuthathe indawo yeLizwi laYo. . . . ukuba lithe latyiwa lasetyenzwa sisisu, [iLizwi likaThixo] liligazi lokuphila komphefumlo. Ngoko ke, imisebenzi yothe walitya elungileyo iya kubonakala ikukukhanya okubengezela ebumnyameni.”—Letter 130, 1901.

UEllen G. White wayekwakholelwa ukuba bonke ababanga ingubo yobuprofeti kaKristu kufuneka bafundise “oko uKristu wakufundisayo. Oko wayekuthethile, kungekuphela ngokwaKhe, kodwa nangabaprofeti neetitshala zeTestamente eNdala, konke kuqukiwe apha. Imfundiso yomntu ivalelwe ngaphandle.”—Ulangazelelo Lwamaphakade, iph. 826.

UEllen G. White wamgcina uYesu nesiBhalo embindini wamava ak he obuKristu. NgoLwesihlanu olandelayo siya kuhlola isityhilelo nempefumlelo yakhe.
