---
title:  "Abaprofeti Bobuxoki KwiTestamente ENtsha"
date:   22/10/2026
---

`Funda Mateyu 24:23, 24. Usilumkisa ngantoni apha uYesu? Kutheni le nto kufuneka sisithabathele ingqalelo esi silumkiso?`

UYesu ngokwaKhe wayengeke acacise ngaphezulu: “ ‘Kuba kuya kuvela ooKristu ababuxoki, nabaprofeti ababuxoki, benze imiqondiso emikhulu nezimanga, ngokokude ukuba bekunokwenzeka, balahlekise nabanyuliweyo’ ” (Mat. 24:24).

Ulahlekiso alulmkisa ngalo lunokub lunamandla kakhulu, ukuba ngokwenene, “imiqondiso emikhulu nezimanga” ziya kuba yinxalenye yalo. Iziganeko ezingaphezu kwendalo , kuzo nangokwazo, azibubo ubungqina okusebenza kukaThixo, nokuba sezingaphezu kwendalo. Njengoko esilulmkisile uPawulos: “Umzamo wethu asingowokuzamana negazi nenyama; ngowokuzamana nazo izilawuli, nawo amagunya, nazo iziphatha-hlabathi zobumnyama balo eli phakade, nayo imikhosi yomoya abakhohlakeleyo kwezasezulwini iindawo” (Efese 6:12). La magunya ke asebenza ngabaprofeti bobuxoki.

Inyaniso kukuba, abaprofeti bobuxoki abanti intsha. ITestamente eNtsha ibhekisa kubaprofeti bobuxoki kumaxesha eTestamente eNdala (Luka 6:26). KwiTestamente eNtsha abaprofeti bobuxoki bafaniswa “ ‘neengcuka eziqwengayo’ ” ezambethe “ ‘iingubo zegusha’ ” (Mat. 7:15), kwaye siyaxelelswa ukuba siya “ ‘kubaqonda ngeziqhamo zabo’ ” (Mat. 7:16).

Umsebenzi wabo ophambili kukulahlekisa (Mat. 7:17–20). UYesu ngokwaKhe wathi, “ ‘Kuya kuvela abaprofeti ababuxoki babe baninzi, balahlekise, nabanyuliweyo’ ” (Mat. 24:11); bona nakuMarko 13:22, 1 Yohane 4:1).

`Ngokwabase-Efese 6:10-18, singazikhusela njani kulo naluphi uhlobo lolahlekiso?`

Ukuphila ukholo, ukunxiba “isigcina-sifuba sobulungisa,” ukubhinqa “inyaniso,” kunye nokuzinikela kuMoya Oyingcwele ngomthandazo, nokuzinza eLizwini laKhe-ezi ziindlela esingakhuseleka ngazo kuko konke okungathi kusikhokelele ekulahlekeni ngokwasemoyeni nasekwazini ngoThixo.

`Yintoni ongayenza ukuzixhobisa ngcono ngakuko konke ukulahleka okutshayela ihlabathi lethu?`
