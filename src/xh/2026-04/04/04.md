---
title:  "Imisebenzi Yabaprofeti BeTestamente ENtsha"
date:   20/10/2026
---

`Yintoni esiyixelelwa ku-1 Korinte 14:3, Efese 4:11-13, nesiTyhilelo 22:6 ngendawo yabaprofeti beTestamente eNtsha?`

Ezi ndima zingentla, nokuba ziluncedo kangakanani, aziniki imbono ezeleyo ngako konke okwenziwa ngabaprofeti beTestamente eNtsha. Noko kunjalo, okuninzi kwabakwenzayo, kukwabonisa oko abaprofeti bakwenzileyo kwiTestamente eNdala, njalo kukwanikwa ukuqhubeka kwesipho sobuprofeti.

Kwabase-1 Korinte 12:10, isiprofeto sikhankanywe phakathi kwezinye izipho zokuprofeta. Kumzimba ongumfuziselo kaKristu (1 Kor. 12:27, 28), abaprofeti bakhankanywe emva kwabapostile, nabo ababefumana intyilelo kuThixo. Ngokwabase-1 Korinte 14:3: “Ke yena oprofetayo uthetha ebantwini ulwakheko, noyalo nokhuzo.”

Kwabase-Efese 4:11-13 kudweliswe isiqhu esifanayo sezipho zikaMoya, njengoko kunjalo kwabase-1 Korinte 12. Abaprofeti bakhankanywe Phakathi kwabapostile, nabavangeli, nabafundisi, neetitshala (Efese 4:11). Aba bantu bakho ukuba baxhobise abangcwele, baze “basulungekiselwe umsebenzi wolungiselelo, ukwakhiwa ke komzimba kaKristu, side thina sonke sifike kubo ubunye benkolo, nobokumazi okuzeleyo uNyana kaThixo, ebudodeni obupheleleyo, emlinganisweni wobukhulu bokuzala kukaKristu” (Efese 4:12, 13). Njengoko kubonwa apha nakwenye indawo, uYesu ungumbindi ekujongwe kuwo wesigidimi sabo.

Incwadi yeZenzo yathetha ngabaprofeti ababini, uYuda noSilas (kungekho noyedwa owabhala incwadi yeBhayibhile), “abakhuthaza bomeleza abazalwana ngamazwi amaninzi” IZenzo 15:32). Nangona, phofu, maxa wambi abaprofeti kuye kufuneke bathethe izilumkiso eziqatha ngamanye amaxesha, kuye kuthi, ngokuxhomekeke kwiimeko, banike amazwi enkuthazo, ngakumbi ngamaxesha anzima, amazwi, ngokuqinisekileyo, awaya axatyiswa kakhulu ngabo babedinga ukuwava kwakunye nethemba nesithembiso awayeziphethe.

Umsebenzi wenkuthazo ukwakhankanywe kwimeko yokuprofeta (1 Kor. 14:31). Ngamaxesha embandezelo nentshutshiso, isipho sesiprofeto siye sayinkuthazo nethemba.

KwisiTyhilelo 22:6, uYesu uvezwe eyi “Nkosi, uThixo wabaprofeti abangcwele” abo “ ‘banamazwi athembekileyo, ayinyaniso’ ” (NKJV). Phofu ke, yonke into evela kubakhonzi bakaThixo, xa bethethela uThixo, iza kuba “ithembekile, iyinyaniso,” nayo. Kwihlabathi apho sikwazi ukuthemba okuncinane kakhulu, singakwazi ngokwenene ukuthemba oko abaprofeti beNkosi bayithethayo, ngakumbi eLizwini laYo, igunya lethu lokugqibela neliphezulu.
