---
title:  "Abaprofeti abaziintokazi Nabancedisi abaselula Abangabafundisi"
date:   21/10/2026
---

`Funda Luka 2:36-38 noIZenzo 21:9. Zisixelela ntoni ezi ndawo ngabaprofeti abaziintokazi?`

KwiTestamente eNdala, uThixo wabiza bobabini, amadoda neentokazi ukuba abebe ngabathunywa baKhe. Ngamaxesha eTestamente eNtsha, bukho ubungqina obucacileyo babaprofeti abaziintokazi. UAna wayengowokuqala ukubizwa “mprofetikazi” kwiTestamente eNtsha. Ungumntu owathi, kulo lonke ixesha lolungiselelo lwakhe, “wakhonza uThixo ngokuzila ukudla nangokukhunga ubusuku nemini. wathelela ekuyidumiseni iNkosi; wathetha ngayo” (Luka 2:37, 38).

IZenzo 21:9 uthi uFilipu “wayeneentombi ezine, ziseziintombi, ziprofeta.” Ababhali bembali beTestamente eNtsha, njengo Eusebius, bafumana iindima zakamva ezikhankanya apho banokuba babehlala khona, baza bangcwatywa. Nokuba kunjani, esikubonayo ziintokazi, okucace kakhulu ukuba yayiziintokazi eziselula, ezazinaso isipho sokuprofeta. Asazi ngokuqinisekileyo ukuba zaprofeta ntoni okanye lwalunabe kangakanani ulungiselelo lwazo. Into enye, noko kunjalo, esiyaziyo ngezi ntokazi zilula ezazinesipho sokuprofeta: akukho nelilodwa ilizwi lazo elakwazi ukufakwa kulwamkelo lweBhayibhile. Oko kukuthi, njengabaninzi, bangabaprofeti abaya abafakwa eBhayibhileni, engabaprofeti abanyanisileyo, abamazwi abo, nemibhalo yabo, nokuba yintoni, abangazange bafakwe kwiincwadi zeBhayibhile.

Ukongeza kubaprofeti abaziintokazi, abafundisi abalula babengabantu abasebenza kulwalamano olusondeleyo kakhulu kubapostile. Abapostile babebaqeqeshela ukulungiselela. UPawulos, umzekelo, wayethetha ngokuthi kuTimoti, “Nyana wam kanye waselukholweni” (1 Tim. 1:2; bona naku-1 Tim. 4:12) UPawulos wamfundisa uTimoti iziseko zolungiselelo olululo lobufundisi. Uthi xa ethetha ngokubizwa kukaThimoti: “Olu yalo ndilubeka phambi kwakho, mtwana wam Timoti, ngokweziprofeto ezandulelayo ngawe, ukuze ulwe ngazo ukulwa okuhle” (1 Tim. 1:18).

UPawulos wazisa uTito ngokuthi “umntwana wam wenene ngokwaselukholweni” Tito 1:4, UPawulos wamnika uTito uxanduva lokunyula iinkokeli zamakholwa aseKrete, ibandla elalinzima. Wamnika uTito oselula ingcebiso yasemoyeni ngendlela amakaluphathe ngayo olu xanduva lubalulekileyo, waza waqukumbela ngokuthi: “Ezi zinto zithethe, uvuselele, wohlwaye ngawo wonke ummiselo. Makungabikho namnye ukudelayo” (Titus 2:15).

`Umtsha, umdala, uyindoda, uyintokazi-sonke sinako ukuba nendawo esiyidlalayo emsebenzini kaThixo. Yeyiphi indawo yakho, kwaye ungenza njani nangaphezulu?`
