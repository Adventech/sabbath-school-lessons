---
title:  "Abapostile Nabaprofeti"
date:   19/10/2026
---

ITestamente eNtsha ikhankanya umsebenzi wabaprofeti nabapostile. Abapostile babe“ngabathunywa” bevela kuThixo, njengoko kucaciswa ngegama lesiGrike uapostolos. Kuzo zonke iiVangeli, igama “mpostile” libhekisa kwishumi elinambini labafundi. Okunye kweTestamente eNtsha okuxhasa le ngcamango kukwaquka abanye abantu ababengekho phakathi kwabafundi bukaKristu bokuqala.

UPawulos uzibiza ukuba ungumpostile amaxa ali-12 kwiincwadi zakhe. kwabaseRoma 1:1, 2, uluhlanganisa ubizo lwakhe lobupostile noko kuqulathwe yintshumayelo yakhe, nesigidimi sabaprofeti beTestamente eNdala. UPawulos uziveza engumpostile eziNtlangeni (Roma 11:13). Wayenyulwe “ngentando kaThixo (1 Kor. 1:1).

Ngokungafaniyo nababingeleli ababelandela umnombo wosapho ukusuka kooyise kuye koonyana, uThixo wabanyula abaprofeti nabapostile ngokwentando yaKhe. Xa ubupostile bukaPawulos babuxoxisa, walukhusela ubizo lwakhe (1 Kor. 9:1, 2). UPawulos wabubona ubupostile bakhe njengesakhiwo phezu komsebenzi kaKristu, iSiseko seBandla (Efese 2:20). Njengabamkela isityhilelo sikaThixo, abapostile ngoko banempefumlelo enye njengabaprofeti (Efese 3:1-5).

Ku-2 Petros 3:1, 2, uPetros uthetha ngabaprofeti njengabo babekho ngaphambili nabamazwi abo ayebalulekile, kodwa ngoku abapostile babekwe njengabathwele igunya elinye. Kwezi ndima, kuthethwa ngexesha leTestamente eNdala neNtsha nabapostile abangabathunywa bakaThixo bexesha leTestamente eNtsha. Nangona abaprofeti bengabonakala bengabathunywa bakaThixo bexesha leTestamente eNdala, babe abapostile bengabathunywa bakaThixo bexesha leTestamente eNtsha, babekwakho abantu ababeprofeta okanye abasebenzisa isipho sokuprofeta kodwa bengengabo abapostile. Isipho sokuprofeta sasingaphelelanga kubapostile kumaxesha eTestamente eNtsha, kwaye yayingengabo bonke abaprofeti abangabapostile (IZenzo 15:32).

`Basebenza njani abapostile nabaprofeti ngokwayamana kuKristu nebandla, ngokwabase-Efese 2:20 noEfese 3:5?`

Kwanjengokuba sisazi ngokufunda oko abapostile bakubhalayo kwiTestamente eNtsha, uYesu wayengumbindi phantse wako konke abakubhalayo. Ngandlela ithile, ubomi baKhe, nokufa, nokuvuka, nolungiselelo lobubingeleli obukhulu, nokubuya, kwakusembindini ekujongwe kuwo.

`Ingxelo ekuphela kwayo esemthethweni esinayo yamazwi abapostile nabaprofeti ngoYesu, ubomi baKhe, nemisebenzi, yaKhe, yiBhayibhile. Kumele ukusixelela ntoni oku ngokuba kutheni kufuneka ukuba iBhayibhile ibe ligunya lokugqibela koko sikukholelwayo ngoYesu nangokusifela kwaKhe?`
