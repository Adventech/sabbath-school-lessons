---
title:  "UYesu Njengomprofeti"
date:   18/10/2026
---

UMoses waxela ngenx’ engaphambili ukuba “ ‘uYehova uThixo wakho uya kunivelisela umprofeti ophuma phakathi kwenu, kubazalwana benu, onje ngam; ize nimphulaphule yena’ ” (Dut. 18:15). Yayingubani umprofeti? IsiBhalo sikubeka kucace: sasisalatha kuYesu ngokwaKhe. “ ‘Kuba uMoses okunene wathi koobawo, INkosi uThixo wenu iya kunivelisela umporofeti kubazalwana benu, onjengam nje; ze nimve yena kuzo zonke izinto asukuba eya kuzithetha kuni’ ” (IZenzo 3:22; bona nakuYohane 6:14).

`Iyintoni indawo yobuprofeti kaYesu? Waphendulwa njani lo mbuzo ngexesha likaYesu? (Mat. 16:13-16, Marko 8:27-29, Luka 9:18-20).`

UYesu, njengoMthanjiswa [uKristu], uMesiya, nguYe ababesalatha kuye abaprofeti beTestamene eNdala. KweNtsha iTestamente uYesu ubonwa ekukuzaliseka kweziprofeto zabo zikaMesiya. KuLuka 24:13-35, uziveza enguLowo iziprofeto zikaMesiya zazisalatha kuYe.

Inyaniso yeyokuba, kuYohane 1:1-5, inkcazo enikiweyo kaLizwi apha ayifaneli namnye umntu okanye umprofeti nje ongumntu. ULizwi ubonwa njengoMdali, kuba “izinto zonke zabakho ngaye; engekho yena, akubangakho nanye into ethe yabakho” (Yohane 1:3). Ngoko ke, Wadala konke okwadalwayo, indalo yonke, ngoku eqikelelwa kububanzi bama-93 ebhiliyon zeminyaka. [Olu qikelelo luthi: ukukhanya kungabeleka iminyaka engama-93 ukujikeleza yonke indalo].

Kuye wonke u“Wathi uThixo” kaGenesis 1, siva uYesu ethetha. Lowo wathi, “Makubekho ukukhanya” (Gen. 1:3) ukwa“lukhanyiselo lwabantu” (Yohane 1:4), abe, ngecebo losindiso (Yohane 1:4, 5), wayegxotha ubumnyama bomhlaba.

Amazwi esiprofeto sikaThixo aqinisekile kunjalo ekwaqinisekiswa. Xa ephulaphulwe ngenyameko, “asisibane esikhanyisa endaweni emnyama, kude kuse, liphume ikhwezi ezintliziyweni zenu” (2 Pet. 1:19). Isiprofeto siya kuqhubeka sizisa ukukhanya kwihlabathi lethu lesono ade uYesu, “iKhwezi lokusa,” iMvelaphi yako konke ukukhanya kwesiprofeto, angenise ubukumkani baKhe obungunaphakade.

Kude, kube ngoko, ngobomi baKhe, ukufa, nokuvuka, uKristu usinika sonke Ubomi obungunaphakade. Umbuzo ekuphela kwao ngokuba siyabamkela na okanye asibamkeli.

`Gxila kwinyaniso yokuba uYesu, uMdali (Yohane 1:1-3), ikwa nguYe owafela izono zakho emnqamlezweni. Ifundisa ntoni le nyaniso ikhwankqisayo ngobunyaniso bothando lukaThixo kuthi?`
