### Fundela Isifundo sale Veki
Mat. 16:13-16, Efese 2:20, IsiTy. 22:6, Luka 2:36-38, IZenzo 21:9, Efese 6:10-18.

> <p>Indima Yenkumbulo</p>
> “Ke kaloku kukho izabelo ngezabelo zezibabalo, kodwa ke ikwanguloo Moya mnye. Kukho nezabelo ngezabelo zezilungiselelo, phofu ikwayiloo Nkosi inye. Kukho nezabelo ngezabelo zeentsebenzo, kodwa ke ikwanguloo Thixo mnye uzisebenzayo izinto zonke” (1 Korinte 12:4-6).

Utata wayeqhuba, unyana eqwalasela iimoto ezidlulayo ebala ezifana nekayise. Uyise waya wamalathisa imoto wakhwaza wathi, “Nyana, leya ifana nemoto yam yokuqala!” bathi bakufika ekhaya, uyise Warhola incwadi yokusebenza imoto yakhe yokuqala, kunye neyemoto yabo yangoku. Wambonisa ngomonde unyana wakhe iimpawu zemoto nganye, ephawula izinto ezifana ngazo nokuba zintoni ezaguqulwayo. Noko kunjalo, xa uyise ethelekisa i-injini zombini, yonke into yayifana. Kuninzi okwaguqulwayo, kodwa hayi kwi-injini.

UThixo wathetha ngabaprofeti kwiTestamente eNdala neNtsha, kwaye ukuqhubeka nokuvumelana kwizigidimi zazo kwenziwa kukuba uMoya Oyingcwele, ongumthombo, okanye “Injini” eyimpefumlelo ihleli iyiyo. UMoya Oyingcwele usoloko enika amandla abaprofeti benene ngesipho sobuprofeti. Ukwabeka uKristu embindini wezigidimi zabaprofeti, njengoko incwadi yamaHebhere ikubeka kucace (Heb. 1:1-3).

Kule veki siza kuhlola isipho sesiprofeto kwiTestamente eNtsha, sikhangela koko abaprofeti bakwenzayo, nokuba ngoobani abasebenza njengabaprofeti, bathelekiswa njani abaprofeti nabapostile, kunye nengozi yabaprofeti bobuxoki.

_Funda isifundo sale veki ukulungisela iSabatha kaOkthobha 24._
