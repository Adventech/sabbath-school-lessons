IsiTyhilelo 12 sibonisa imbali ebanzi ebonisa imbambano enkulu, kususela ukuqaleni kwayo ezulwini kude kube yimihla yokugqibela.

`Funda IsiTyhilelo 12:17. Buyintoni ubungqina bukaYesu”? Thelekisa IsiTyhilelo 19:10 nesiTyhilelo 22:8, 9.`

KwisiTyhilelo 12:17, kukho iimpawu ezimbini eziye zakhankanywa ezibonisa ibandla lexesha lokuphela. Ibandla libambelele lwaye luyigcina imithetho eliShumi (equka nomthetho weSabatha) laye lino“bungqina bukaYesu.” Buyintoni ubungqina bukaYesu?

Ezinye iinguqulelo zeBhayibhile zibuchaza “ubungqina bukaYesu” njengobuthetha ukuba thina bakholwayo sivuma uYesu simenze aziwe ngoko sikuthunyiweyo novangelo (bona IsiTy. 19:10, NIV: “bambelela kubungqina bukaYesu,” uYesu eyinjongo yobungqina bokuKristu). Ezinye iinguqulelo zeBhayibhile zinika intsingiselo eyahlukileyo, zixela ukuba uYesu ngokwaKhe unika ubungqina ebandleni laKhe (IsiTy. 19:10, GNT: “inyaniso uYesu awayityhilayo”—uYesu uyintloko enobungqina). Inyaniso yeyokuba, indima yesiGrike semveli inokubekwa nangayiphi indlela. Ngoko ke, wayethetha ukuthini uYohane?

Xa sithelekisa iimpawu zombini zokwazisa, sibona ukufana okukhulu kwindlela ezibekwe ngayo (kude kubonakale kakhulu kwisiGrike semveli kunakwiiBhayibhile zethu). Kanye njengoko “imithetho kaThixo” iphuma ngokucacileyo kuThixo, ngokunjalo “ubungqina bukaYesu” buqala kuYesu. Ngoko ke, nguYesu othwele ubungqina. Ubonisa ibandla lenyaniso. Ukwenza njani oku? Ukwenza ngesipho sesiprofeto.

Iimpendulo zifunyanwa kwisiTyhilelo 19:10 nesiTyhilelo 22:9. Kucacile apha ukuba “ubungqina bukaYesu” bufanekiswa no“Moya wesioprofeto.” UYohane ngokwakhe ungumprofeti kwaye emele ngesiqu sakhe “ubungqina bukaYesu.” Umprofeti ngumntu onobizo olulodwa njengomthunywa kaThixo kubantu baKhe, nokuba abanyanzele okanye ababizele enguqukweni okanye atyhile ixesha elizayo.

IsiTyhilelo 12:17 sibonisa ukuba uYesu ulenza ibandla laKhe libonakale ngesi sipho soprofeto. Oku kuthetha ukuba kanye njengoko uThixo wathumela abaprofeti kubantu beTestamente eNdala bakwaIsrayeli nakwaYuda, okanye umpostile uYohane kwibandla lobuKristu lokuqala, abaprofeti baya kubakho nakwimihla yokugqibela yembali yomhlaba.

`Bungqina buni obubonileyo wena ukubonisa ukuba ulungiselelo lukaEllen G. White lunako ukubonakala ngokufanelekileyo njengomzekelo wokusebenza kwe “sipho sesiprofeto”? (IsiTy. 19:10).`
