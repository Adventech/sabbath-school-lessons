---
title:  "Konke Kungokuthunywa"
date:   18/12/2026
---

Kwabase-Efese 4, uPawulos ubonisa ukuba eyona njongo ephambili yezipho zikaMoya kukunceda amakholwa ahambele phambili koko akutyhunywe nguThixo. Ulungiselelo lokuprofeta kukaEllen G. White lwalunenjono ekwanye. Izigidimi zakhe eziphefumlelweyo ziye zawanceda amakholwa ukuba akhule kubudlelwane obunzulu noThixo, lwawakhuthaza ukuba abelane nabanye ngalo olo thando.

“Kubo bonke abazibiza ngokuba bangamaKristu,” wabhala, “amaSeventh-day Adventists afanele ukuba ngawona aphambili ukuphakamisa uKristu phambi kwehlabathi. Ukuvakalisa isigidimi sengelosi yesithathu kufana nokubekwa kwenyaniso yeSabatha. Le nyaniso, kunye nezinye eziqukwe esigidimini, kufuneka ivakalisiwe; kodwa owona mbindi mkhulu omawutsale, uKristu Yesu, kufuneka angashiywa ngaphandle. Kusemnqamlezweni kaKristu apho inceba nenyaniso zihlangana khona, nobulungisa noxolo apho zangana khona. Umoni kufuneka enziwe akhangele eKhalvari; ngokholo olulula lomntwana omncinane, kufuneka athembele kokuhle koMsindisi, amkele ubulungisa baKhe, ekholelwa kwinceba yaKhe.”—Gospel Workers, pp. 156, 157.

Ngaphezu koko, ikholwa ngalinye, wagxininisa, libizelwe ukuba libe yinxalenye yomsebenzi wokuthunywa nguThixo. “UYehova ufuna umzamo omkhulu kakhulu ngaphaya komntu ukuba wenziwe ngamalungu amabandla ethu.”—Evangelism, p. 113. “Akukho ndilele emsebenzini weNkosi. Abantu abahlukeneyo mabahlangane emsebenzini njengabalobi babantu. Mabazame ukuqokelela imiphefumlo kumonakalo wehlabathi ize kubunyulu bothando lukaKristu.”—Evangelism, p. 115. UEllen G. White yena ngokwakhe wayengumvangeli eYurophu ngeyama-1880, naseAustralia ngeyama-1890.

USathana “unengqumbo” ngenxa yobizelo lukaThixo lobuprofeti kumntu ngamnye ukuba azinikele nasekwenzeni akuthunyiweyo, USathana “unengqumbo” (IsiTy. 12:17) ngakubantu bakaThixo ekupheleni kwexesha. Aba ngabalandeli bakaYesu abanesigidimi sokuphela kwexesha, nenkokelo yesiprofeto, nembono: “UYehova utyhile iingozi ezikhoyo neziphambi kwethu. Ngesenzeli soMoya wesiprofeto, ukuvezile ukulahlekisa okuya kulithimba ihlabathi, waza wathetha kubantu baKhe, esithi, ‘Nantsi indlela; hambani ngayo.’ [Isaya 30:21]. . . . Imbambano Enkulu iyaziveza iinkohliso zikaSathana.”—Ellen G. White, manuscript 31, 1890.

USathana uyaqumba xa abantu benikela ubomi babo kuYesu, kwaye wenza konke ukulenza lingakhathalelwa ilizwi lesiprofeto. Ezinye zokunyelisa isipho sokuprofeta sikaEllen G. White njengesingekho emxholweni. Omnye umzamo kukuzisa intandabuzo ngokuthandabuza amabango akhe obuprofeti nokwalatha kuye njengomprofeti wobuxoki. Phofu, eyona ndlela yokumenza uEllen G. White abe ngungantweni kukuqhawula isizikulwana sangoku ekufundeni imibhalo yakhe ngokwaso.
