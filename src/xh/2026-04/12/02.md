---
title:  "Isithembiso SikaYoweli"
date:   13/12/2026
---

`Funda Yoweli 2:28-32. Uthembisa ukwenza ntoni uThixo ngemihla yokugqibela?`

UYoweli, umporofeti wenkulungwane yesixhenxe b.c., unika imbono ebalulekileyo kwiziganeko zexesha elizayo. Xa sifunda le ndawo, siyamangala ukuba uthetha ngelakhe ixesha lakudala okanye ngezinto kwixesha elizayo elikude.

Abafunda ngoThixo banomkhwa wokwahlula Phakathi kweemo ezimbini zesiprofeto – esiqhelekileyo nesingeziganeko ezizayo. Isiprofeto esiqhelekileyo sithetha ngezizwe eziphathekayo sisebenzise amanani namagama angawo (bona, umzekelo, Yer. 25:1-11). Isiprofeto esingeziganeko lexesha lokuphela, sisebenzisa amanani ayimifuziselo amaxesha amaninzi, sikholise ukuthetha ngemiqondiso yezwe lonke (bona uDaniyeli 7). Ngamanye amaxesha sifumana ukuqabelana kwezi ntlobo zesiprofeto (bona, umzekelo, kuMateyu 24).

Kungokuba esithini yena uYoweli 2:28-31? Ingathi esi isicatshulwa siwa kolu didi lwamva. Oku kuthetha ukuba kufuneka singalindeli ukuzaliseka ngexesha lokuphila komprofeti, okanye nakwiminyaka ekufuphi emva kolungiselelo lwakhe. Kwelinye icala, kufuneka sikhangele ukuzaliseka kwesi siprofeto kwixesha elizayo. Qaphela ukuba kwindawo ekubhalwe kuyo nokuma kwexesha uThixo awanika ngalo isipho sokuprofeta—“ ‘phambi kokufika kwemini enkulu neyoyikekayo kaYehova’ ” (Yoweli 2:31). Yeyiphi loo mini? Ingaba kukuza kokuqala kukaYesu? Okanye kwesibini? Okanye kokubini?

Xa siqaphela ukukhankanywa kwemiqondiso elangeni nasenyangeni kwisiprofeto sikaYoweli, kwangoko sibona yinyaniso yokuba siyakwazi ukuyifumana le ngcamango nakwiqela lezinye izicatshulwa. UYesu wabaxelela abafundi baKhe ngemiqondiso elangeni, enyangeni, nasezinkwenkwezini kwintetho yaKhe yokuphela kwexesha (Mat. 24:29). Ngokufanayo, umtyhilelwa, uYohane, ukhankanya imiqondiso eminye (IsiTy. 6:12, 13). Bobabini, uYesu noYohane baqonde eyona miqondiso yokugqibela yendalo yonke ngaphambi kokuza kwesibini kweNkosi yethu.

Ngoko ke, siqikelela ukuba isiprofeto sikaYoweli, esalatha kwimiqondiso, simele ukuba negalelo kumaxesha okuphela: “ ‘Ndiya kwenza izimanga ezulwini nasehlabathini, igazi nomlilo nemiqulu yomsi. Ilanga liya kusuka libe mnyama, nenyanga ibe ligazi, phambi kokuba ifike imini kaYehova, leyo inkulu yoyikekayo’ ” (Yoweli 2:30, 31).

`Cinga ngazo zonke iziprofeto esezifikile zadlula njengoko bezixeliwe. Kusinceda ngantoni oku ukuba simthembe uThixo nangezo zexesha elizayo—njengokufika kwesibini kukaYesu?`
