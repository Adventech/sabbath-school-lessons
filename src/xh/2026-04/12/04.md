`Funda Efese 4:11-14. Uzinikela ntoni uThixo ezi zipho kwibandla laKhe? Sinceda njani isipho soprofeto ukufeza icebo likaThixo ngebandla?`

Injongo yazo zonke izipho zikaMoya kukondla nokwakha ibandla, nokuvakalisa iindaba ezilungileyo. UPawulos waquka isipho sesiprofeto (elithethwa kwigama “abaprofeti”) kuzo zozine izintlu. Ezinye izipho zenzeka kuphela kube kanye. Xa sikhangela ekuphinda-phindweni koku kuqukwa, siyakwazi ukufikelela kwisigqibo sokuba isiprofeto sesinye sezona zipho zikaMoya ezibaluleke kakhulu.

Makhe siqwalasele ezinye iimpawu ezikholisa ukuba ziphawuleke ngabaprofeti:

1. Abaprofeti babizwa nguThixo (bona: 1 Kumkani 19:16, 19-21 naku-Isaya 6). Bakholisa ukuba bazive bengafanelekanga, nokuba bangakhululeki ukuluthabatha uxanduva lwabo, kodwa bathobele.
2. UThixo uthetha kubaprofeti ngemibono, ngamaphupha, okanye ngokuvakalayo. Ngamanye amaxesha uThixo utyhila iziganeko zexesha elizayo. Maxa wambi ubaxelela ngemiba eqhubekayo yezizwe okanke yabantu (Daniyeli 2).
3. IziBhalo zichaza iimpawu ezibonakalayo: abaprofeti bangaphelelwa ngamandla omzimba okanye bade bayeke ukuphefumla, kodwa basenokuzaliswa ngamandla amatsha (Dan. 10:8–10, 18, 19).
4. Abaprofeti bazigqithisa izigidimi zabo ngeendlela ngeendlela. Kukho ukuzithetha (IZenzo 21:10-12), kukho ukuzenza [bumdlalo] (Hezekile 4), okanye ngokubhala iincwadi [njengeeleta] (IsiTyhilelo 2, 3). Abanye abaprofeti babesebenzisa abhabhali bokubanceda, njengakwimeko kaBharuki, owayenceda uYeremiya (Yer. 36:1-4).
5. Isigidimi somprofeti kufuneka sihambelane nabaprofeti ababengaphambili, kuba uThixo akathethi eziphikisa (Isa. 8:20).
6. Okuqulathwe sisigidimi somprofeti, kwakunye nempembelelo yobomi bomprofeti, konke kuya kunika ubungqina bokuba imvelaphi yaso ikuThixo (Mat. 7:15-20).
7. USathana usoloko ezama ukwenza unomgogwana woko kukaThixo. Oku kubanjalo nangesipho sobuprofeti. Ngexesha lokugqibela, kuya kubakho abaprofeti bobuxoki (Mat. 24:24, 2 Pet. 2:1).
