### Fundela Isifundo sale Veki
Yoweli 2:28-32, IZenzo 2:1-21, Efese 4:11-14, IsiTy. 12:17, IsiTy. 19:10, IZenzo 2:36-42.

> <p>Indima Yenkumbulo</p>
> “Kuya kuthi emveni kokuba njalo, ndithulule uMoya wam; phezu kwenyama yonke, baprofete oonyana benu neentombi zenu; amadoda enu amakhulu aphuphe amaphupha, amadodana enu abone imibono. Kwanaphezu kwabakhonzi naphezu kwabakhonzazana ndothulula uMoya wam ngaloo mihla” (Yoweli 2:28, 29).

Kweminye imithetho yobunye uburhulumente kukho i“sunset Provision (unqunyanyiswa komthetho).” Esi saci somthetho asinanto yakwenza nokutshona kwelanga. Kunoko, sisiqendu esinika ukuphelelwa komthetho ngomhla obekiweyo. Oorhulumente babeka iziqendu ezinjalo ngenxa yezizathu ezahlukeneyo. Umzekelo, basenokwenza eminye imithetho ukufinyeza [okanye ukususa] amalungelo kubantu abangabemi ngamaxesha amabi esizweni. Oorhulumente baye bafake imisindleko yeSUNSET ukuyenza ingasebenzi le mithetho ngomhla othile ukuze kubuyiselwe kubemi babo amalungelo awayesuswe okwexeshana.

Ingaba uThixo waquka unqumamiso lomthetho xa uMoya Oyingcwele wayenika isipho soprofeto ngamaxesha akudala? Ingaba likho ixesha ngaphambi kokubuya kukaYesu xa esi sipho siya kuyeka ukusebenza? Kwiveki ephelileyo sifunde ukuba uThixo wathetha ngabaprofeti kubantu baKhe ngamaxesha abalulekileyo. Kule veki siza kuhlola isipho sokuprofeta ekupheleni kwexesha—umsebenzi waso owodwa nokubakho kwaso umphelo—kunye nendawo yobuprofeti bukaEllen G. White njengoko ukufika kukaYesu kusondela ngakumbi.

_Funda Isifundo sale veki ulungiselela iSabatha kaDisemba 19._
