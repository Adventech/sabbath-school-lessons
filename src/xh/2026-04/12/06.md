title:  "Izibonakalaliso Zexesha Langoku"
date:   17/12/2026
---

Abantu abaninzi banamhlanje bangakuvuma ukuba semthethweni kwabaprofeti beBhayibhile bakudala, kodwa ingaba bakholelwa ukuba kunjalo na ngale mihla yethu? Encwadini kaYoweli 2:28-31 isithembiso esinozuko sesibonakalaliso sesipho soprofeto ekufuneka sikholelwe, sinokukhangeleka njani?

`Funda IZenzo 2:36-42. Wawusithini umongo wentshumayelo kaPetros ngePen-tekoste? Basabela njani ababemphulaphule uPetros?`

Ukuthululwa kukaMoya Oyingcwele ngePentekoste ngo-a.d. 31 ngumfanekiso obalaseleyo wento eya kwenzeka ekuthululweni kukaMoya Oyingcwele ngexesha lokugqibela, nangona kuya kuba ngamandla angaphezulu. IPentekoste yayinezi mpawu: ukuphakanyiswa kukaYesu, inguquko ngokubanzi, kunye nokwenziwa kwabantu abaxhobiselwe umsebenzi kaThixo. Isigidimi sikaPetros esasizinze kuKristu wasinikwa amandla nguMoya Oyingcwele, kwaza oku kwakhokelela imiphefumlo engama-3000 ukuba iguquke ibhaptizwe. Le ntsikelelo ye“mvula yokuqala” yatyala nembewu enexabiso, iBandla – umzimba omanyeneyo wabakholwayo ababangumzekelo walo nababelana ngalo uthando lukaYesu Kristu ngexesha bevakalisa ukubuya kwaKhe.

Njengoko imvula yokuqala yanikwa “ukuze ihlumise imbewu enexabiso, ngokunjalo ‘imvula yamva’ iya kunikwa ekupheleni kwexesha laso ukuze kuvuthwe isivuno.”—Ellen G. White, Imbambano Enkulu, iph. 611. Ngamanye amazwi, izibonakalaliso zangoku zesipho sobuprofeti—imvula yamva—ziya kubonakala zifana kakhulu nezibonakalaliso zemvula yangaphambili kwiPentekoste.

Njengoko sesibonile kuYoweli 2:28-31, isipho sobuprofeti ekupheleni kwexesha siya kuvuleka kwabatsha nakwabadala, kumadoda neentokazi. UThixo uya kuthetha ngabo athe wabanyula uMoya Oyingcwele. Lonke ixesha uMoya Oyingcwele enyula umthunywa wexesha lokuphela, isigidimi sakhe siya kuphakamisa uYesu, sibizele abantu enguqukweni, sibalungise abantu ukuba bazalisekise uthumo lukaThixo. Ezi mpawu zintathu ziluphawu lolungiselelo lokuprofeta kukaEllen G. White. Kubomi bakhe bonke, wamphakamisa uYesu nesiBhalo ekuthetheni nasekubhaleni kwakhe; wawabiza ama-Seventh-day Adventist nabanye ukuba babe nenguquko ephuma entliziyweni; waza wabakhuthaza bonke ukuba bahlangane kumsebenzi wokuthunywa kaThixo wokuba kufunwe abalahlekileyo.
