date:   14/12/2026

`Funda IZenzo 2:1-21. Kwakutheni ukuze uPetros acaphule uYoweli ngokuza kukaKristu kokuqala? Sidlala eyiphi indawo esi siprofeto kwesi sicatshulwa?`

Kwezi ndima zeTestamente eNtsha, sifumana esinye sezona zicatshulwa zide eziphuma kweNdala. Ekufundeni kwethu ukuzathuza kukaPetros, singabuza, “Petros, uthi itheni imiqondiso? Asikwazi ukuyibona kule meko yakho.” UPetros ebengaphendula enjenje, Kwakukho ukuzaliseka kwemiqondiso emnqamlezweni kwiintsuku ezimbalwa ezidlulileyo, ilanga laba mnyama iiyure ezintathu (Marko 15:33).

Oko kwakuyingxoxo eyoyisayo kuPetros ngoko, mhlawumbi ayinamandla kuthi namhlanje. Sisalindele ukuzaliseka okuphelele ngaphezulu kwesiprofeto sikaYoweli. Isitshixo sesisombululo silele kwindima kaYoweli ngokwayo. KuYoweli 2:23, uYoweli ukhankanya imvula yokuqala nemvula yamva. IZenzo 2 kukuzaliseka kokuqala kwesi siprofeto—imvula yokuqala. Ukuzaliseka kokugqibela kuya kuba kukuthululwa kukaMoya kanye phambi kokuphela kwexesha ngemvula yamva.

Abantu bakholisa ukusicacisa isiprofeto ngokuthi “ukuxelwa ngenx’ engaphambili kokuzayo.” Akuphosisi oku, phofu, kodwa abaprofeti beBhayibhile babekwabizela abantu enguqukweni. Ngapha koko, kamva ngokokutsho kukaPetros, abanye kwisininzi, ngokucacileyo bathimbeka.

“Ke kaloku bathe, bakukuva oko, bahlabeka entliziyweni, bathi kuPetros nakwabanye abapostile, ‘Madoda, bazalwana, sithini na?’ ” (IZenzo 2:37).

Waphendula wathini uPetros?

“ ‘Guqukani nibhaptizwe nonke ngabanye egameni likaYesu Kristu, ukuze nixolelwe izono, namkele isipho soMoya Oyingcwele. Kuba idinga likuni, nakubantwana benu, nakubo bonke abakude, esukuba iNkosi uThixo wethu iya kubabiza’ ” (IZenzo 2:38, 39).

UYohane ungomnye umzekelo omhle. Nangona wayevakalisa ukufika kokuqala kukaYesu, wayekwashumayela inguquko ngenxa yezono zabo. “ ‘Guqukani, kuba ubukumkani bamazulu busondele!’ ” (Mat. 3:2).

Ngoko ke, uYoweli 2 uya kufumana inzaliseko kwixesha elizayo. Njengoko uYoweli noPetros benzayo, abaprofeti bexesha lokuphela baya kubiza inguquko, besalatha kuYesu, baze banike inkokelo ibandla likaThixo lexesha lokuphela.

`Ngokubona ezi zibonakalaliso zingaphezu kwendalo, ingcinga eya kuhamba phambili kwabaninzi iya kuba luhlobo oluthile lwesiganeko esiyindalo. Kule meko, bacinga ukuba abantu babenxilile! (Abantu abanxilileyo abathi ngequbuliso bathetha ngeelwimi zasemzini?). Kungaqhelekanga kunjalo ukusabela kwabo, yintoni eyenza ukuba abantu, kuthi nento abayibonayo ukuba ingaphaya kwendalo, basuke bazame ukufuna inkcazelo yemvelo?`
