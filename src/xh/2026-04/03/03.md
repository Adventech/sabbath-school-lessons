---
title:  "Ukuphawula Abaprofeti Benene"
date:   12/10/2026
---

Kwimbali yasekuqaleni kaIsrayeli, bukho ubungqina babaprofeti bobuxoki (Dut. 18:20). UThixo, ke ngoko, akazange abashiye abantu baKhe bengenayo inkokelo yokwazi ukuba ngoobani abaprofeti baKhe benene.

Njengabazuza isityhilelo sikaThixo nempefumlelo yaKhe, abaprofeti benene ngokuthembekileyo, nangokuchanekileyo, kwakunye nangokunyaniseka badlulisa izigidimi zikaThixo, nokuba maxa wambi oko kubenza bangathandwa ngabantu abasemagunyeni.

`Funda 1 Kumkani 22:1-14. Yintoni esiyixelelwayo ngohlobo amabasebenze ngalo ngokuthembeka abaprofeti?`

Ukuba nokumkani othi ngomprofeti, “ ‘[Le ndoda] mna ndiyithiyile ngokuba ayiprofeti okulungileyo ngam, iprofeta okubi’ ” (1 Kumkani 22:8) kuthetha okuninzi ngokumkani, kodwa ngaphezulu ngomprofeti. Ngokuphandle, wayengumntu ongoyikiyo ukuthetha inyaniso, nakukumkani ngokwakhe—kungengandlela yokuba abe nokuthandwa ngukumkani, xa sithethela phantsi.

UMikaya wema yedwa echasene nama-400 abaprofeti bobuxoki ngexesha wayethetha inyaniso ayityhilelweyo. Ejongene nokunyanzelwa ukuba aziguqule izigidimi ezivela kuYehova ukuze athomalalise okanye enze uxolo nokumkani ombi uAhabhi nenkosikazi yakhe uJezebhele (1 Kumkani 22:13, 14), uMikaya wayinika inyaniso kaThixo, nokuba yayiza kwamkeleka kanjani.

`Funda 1 Samuweli 9:3-14. Zisixelela ntoni ezi ndima ngendlela abanye abababona ngayo abaprofeti bakaThixo abathembekileyo?`

Mininzi imizekelo yexabiso lobomi obabuphilwa ngabaprofeti eBhayibhileni. Kule imeko, uSawule wayethunywe nguyise, uKish, ukuba ayokukhangela iidonki zakhe ezilahlekileyo. Isicaka esasihamba noSawule sacebisa ukuba bangakuyeki ukufuna, kuba “ ‘kulo mzi kukho umntu kaThixo; ngumntu ozukileyo’ ” (1 Sam. 9:6). Inkcazo enikwe apha kaSamuweli umprofeti izoba iimpawu ezilindelekileyo kubaprofeti bakaThixo. Abaprofeti benene esiBhalweni babonwa njenga“bantu bakaThixo,” abakhethiweyo ukuze babe ngumzekelo wesimilo sikaThixo. Kwangelo xesha linye, kanye kuba abanye phakathi “kwabantu bakaThixo” banokuba ngabaprofeti, oku akubenzi ukuba babe ngabantu abangenazo iziphako.

`Ngoobani obazi bengenazo iziphako, naphakathi kwabona bahlonitshwa kakhulu “abantu bakaThixo”?`
