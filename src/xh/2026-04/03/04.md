---
title:  "Imisebenzi Yabaprofeti"
date:   13/10/2026
---

`Funda 1 Samuweli 8:4-22 noLevitikus 8:10-13. Abaprofeti babesebenza njani kulwalamano nokumkani nababingeleli?`

Abaprofeti babesebenzisana nokumkani kuzo zombini iimeko, ezintle nezimbi. Xa uIsrayeli wayefuna ukumkani (1 Sam. 8:4-22), bathi, sifuna “ukumkani wokusilawula, njengeentlanga ezi zonke” (1 Sam. 8:5). Hay’ usizi olungako kodwa ikwangumzekelo onamandla wengozi abantu bakaThixo abajongene nayo kuzo zonke izizukulwana: ulangazelelo lokufana nabo babangqongileyo. Kule imeko, babengenayo ingcamango yokuba esi Sicelo siya kubaphathela ntoni, nangona babelunyukiswe kwangaphambili (1 Sam. 8:11-18) nguThixo ngomprofeti waKhe. Ngokuxhomekeke kwiminqweno yabo, nokuba nangona wayebalumkisile, kwakamsinyane uSamuweli waya wabathambisela abakwaIsrayeli ukumkani wokuqala, owaziwa kwimbali yeBhayibhile ngokuba nguKumkani uSawule.

Iincwadi zeTestamente eNdala: eye-1 neye-2 Kumkani kunye neye-1 neye-2 IziKronike zinengxelo yohlobo nexabiso lolawulo olwaboniswa ngukumkani wakwaIsrayeli nakwaYuda. Imbali engcwele ibonisa ukuba ookumkani abenza ububi badlule lee ngobuninzi kwabo benza okulungileyo emehlweni kaThixo. Omnye weyona misebenzi eyayisembindini yabaprofeti yayikukunceda ukukhokelela ookumkani abanganyanisekanga ukuba babuyele ekuthobeleni umthetho kaThixo. Ekugqibeleni, aba baprofeti batyityimbiselwa umnwe, bada abanye babo benzakaliswa babulawa ngukumkani owayekhokelela abantu bakaThixo kude kuYe (1 Kumkani 18:4-19, 2 IziKro. 24:20-22).

Abaprofeti babekwasebenzisana nababingeleli. UMoses, umprofeti kaThixo, wathambisa uAron noonyana bakhe baba ngabokuqala ababingeleli kwaIsrayeli (Lev. 8:10-13). Ayekho amaxesha apho ababingeleli babefumana amazwi okubangxolisa avela kuThixo eziswa ngabaprofeti (Hos. 6:9, Mal. 2:1-3). Umzekelo ochane kakhulu woku yindlu yonke kaEli, abaya bathethiswa ngenxa yezenzo zabo ezikhohlakeleyo (1 Sam. 2:27-36). Ezi yaye ikukungxolisa okuqatha ukulwa nomonakalo owawundlondlobala phakathi kwabantu abanikwe amalungelo akhethekileyo.

Abaprofeti babeziititshala zabantu (2 IziKronike 34). Babedlulisa izigidimi ezivela kuThixo zisiya kubabingeleli, kukumkani, nakwisizwe (Yer. 5:31, Hez. 22:26). Abaprofeti babekwathetha nakwizizwe ezibangqongileyo besandlala icebo likaThixo ngazo, umxholo owawuphindaphindwa amaxesha amaninzi kwizigidimi zabaprofeti beTestamente eNdala (Isa. 60:1-3; Isa. 62:1, 2). Abaprofeti kwakungalindelekanga nje ukuba badlulise ngokuthembeka izigidimi uThixo abanike zona kodwa ngamanye amaxesha babeyalelwa ukuba bazibhale phantsi (2 IziKron. 13:22). Kwakhona, nokokuba abaprofeti babefana nabantu kangakanani (benako bonke ubuthathaka obuhamba nako), babengamadoda neentokazi, abantu bomthandazo, neLizwi, nomsebenzi kuThixo nabantu baKhe (Yer. 42:4, 2 Kumkani 22:15-17).
