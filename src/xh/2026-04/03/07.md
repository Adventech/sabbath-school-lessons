title:  "Ukungagqibeleli Kodwa Kukho Intumekelelo"
date:   16/10/2026
---

Njengoko sesibonile, Ubomi babaprofeti beTestamente eNdala bukwenza kucace ukuba akuzange kwabalula ukuba ngumthunywa kaThixo. UEllen G. White wayenawo la maxhala abaprofeti besiBhalo bawavayo. Njengoko sifundile ngoLwesihlanu, wayemtsha, neentloni zikho, nempilo ingentlanga xa wayefumana ubizo lukaThixo. Nangona umbono wesibini waqinisekisa ubizo lwakhe, uEllen Harmon [White] wahlala enoxinzelelo ngobizo lukaThixo, kangangokuba wabhala: “Kwisithuba seveki emva koku, iNkosi yandinika enye imbono, yandibonisa izilingo ekufuneka ndidlule kuzo; ekufuneka ndiye ndibalise kwabanye oko ithe yakutyhila kum; nokuba kufuneka ndihlangane nenkcaso enkulu, ndifumane ukudandatheka emoyeni. . . . Lo mbono wandihlupha ngokugqithisileyo. Impilo yam yayimbi kakhulu, ndaye ndineshumi elinesixhenxe leminyaka kuphela ubudala. Ndathandaza ngokunyanisekileyo ukuba umthwalo ubekwe phezu komnye. Noko kunjalo, konke ukukhanya endaba nokukufumana kwathi, ‘Yazisa abanye oko ndikutyhilileyo kuwe.’ Ndaye ndingavumelananga [ngaphakathi kum] nokuya ehlabathini. Ngokwendalo ndandinokuzithemba okuncinane. . . . Ndaya ndakhangela ndinomnqweno engcwabeni. Ukufa kwabonakala kum kukhetheka kunoxanduva oluninzi ekwakufuneka ndiluthwele.” —Spiritual Gifts, vol. 2, pp. 35, 36.

Ingaba wakuphawula ukuba Ellen Harmon [White] wayekhetha ukufa kunokwamkela ubizo lwakhe? Emva komthandazo omkhulu nenkuthazo ngamanye amakholwa, wavuma, wazithoba wabunikela ubomi bakhe obuseleyo ekulandeleni “ukuthuma kwaKhe uThixo.”—Ellen G. White, Spiritual Gifts, vol. 2, p. 38.

Wayeza kuba lilizwi elineyona mpembelelo inkulu yalo, nakulo, ibandla leSeventh-day Adventist. Noko kunjalo, uEllen Harmon [White] wayengumntu kwaye wayenazo ezakhe iziphako zobuntu neenzima zasemzimbeni, nemingeni yosapho, namadabi aphakathi kwabantu. Kanti ke, phakathi kobuntu bakhe, sibona isifundo esikhulu: uThixo ukhetha abantu abanentumekelelo ukumsebenzela okanye ukumkhonza.

Phantsi kwenkokelo kaMoya Oyingcwele, ngolungiselelo lobuprofeti lwakhe uEllen G. White wayeza kuwakhuthaza amakholwa, abahlanganise neenyaniso zeBhayibhile. Ngowe-1860, wayeseyidlalile indawo ephambili ekuphuhliseni ibandla elikhulayo iSeventh-day Adventist kunye nokuthunywa kwalo ukuba livakalise izigidimi zeengelosi ezintathu ehlabathini. UThixo wayeza kuwakhokelela la makholwa ukuba aqulunqe iSeventh-day Adventist Church ngowe-1863. Kumashumi eminyaka elandelayo, ilizwi lobuprofeti likaEllen G. White laba nempembelelo ekwakhiweni komsebenzi wama-Adventist wempilo nemfundo emhlabeni wonke. Sekunjalo, elona galelo libaluleke kakhulu lolungiselelo lwakhe yimibhalo yakhe, eqikelelwa kumaphepha alikhulu lamawaka (100,000). Okwabhalwa nguEllen G. White okuphefumlelweyo kusaqhubeka kuthetha kulo lonke ihlabathi nanamhlanje.
