### Fundela Isifundo sale Veki
Yakobi 5:17, 18; 1 Kumkani 19:1- 16; 1 Kumkani 22:1-14; Ekso. 15:20; 2 Kumkani 22:11-14; Yer. 14:1-15.

> <p>Indima Yenkumbulo</p>
> “Wema uYehoshafati, wathi, Kholwani kuYehova uThixo wenu, nozimaseka’ kholwani kubaprofeti bakhe, noba nempumelelo” (2 IZikronike 20:20).

Isixhobo sokubonisa abahambi ukuba bazi ukuba baphi, nalapho baya khona, side sibalumkise ngeengozi. Abahambi basemoyeni kuhambo lwasezulwini nabo badinga inkokelo ngoku nakwixesha elizayo kwisixhobo sasezulwini esibonisa oko. Indlela enye awakwenzayo uThixo oku ngamaxesha awadlulayo kwangolungiselelo lwabaprofeti beTestamente eNdala. Kumabali abo, kwiingcebiso, kwiziprofeto, kwizibongo, neengoma, sifumana okuninzi ngeemfumba zabaprofeti abanika izigidimi zikaThixo ebantwini.

Kule veki siza kuhlola iinkcazo zabaprofeti beTestamente eNdala, iinkcazo ezisinceda ukuba sisiqonde isipho sokuprofeta njengoko sasibonakalalisiwe ngabanye baba bakhonzi bakaThixo.

Njengoko siza kubona, aba yayingabantu abafana nathi, kuba babengabantu ababuthakathaka abanokoyika, nomsindo, nokuphelelwa lithemba. Babungekho lula ubomi kubo, kodwa buzaliswe yimingeni neenzima, njengoko bunjalo nakuthi sonke.

Yintoni, ke ngoko, esinokuyifunda ekukhangeleni kwibathwana laba bakhonzi bakaThixo babizwe ngokukodwa?

_Funda isifundo sale veki ulungiselela iSabatha kaOkthobha 17._
