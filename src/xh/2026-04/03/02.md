---
title:  "Ubuntu Babaprofeti"
date:   11/10/2026
---

Njengoko sibonile kwiveki ephelileyo, uYehova wasebenzisa uEliya umprofeti ukuba enze umsebenzi onzima kakhulu kwaIsrayeli, nangona wayejongene nenkcaso eshushu. Into awayifezayo uYehova ngaye yayingakholeleki. Kanti ke . . . kwenzeka ntoni?

`Cinga ngendlela uYakobi ambeka ngayo uEliya umprofeti, kuYakobi 5:17, 18. Unokuba uthetha ntoni ngokuthi uEliya “wayengumntu oluvo lunjengolwethu” (NKJV)?`

UEliya waba nempumelelo ekhwankqisayo entabeni yeKarmele (1 Kumkani 18); uThixo wayesebenze ngendlela enamandla ngaye, kwaye omnye weyona mimangaliso eyothusayo ezotywe kwimbali yeTestamente eNdala yenzeka: kwathi ngesandi nje samazwi kaEliya, umlilo obonwa ngeliso wehla uvela ezulwini (1 Kumkani 18:38)! Wawungacinga ukuba le ndoda kaThixo yaba novuyo oludlulisileyo emva kwento elolu hlobo.

`Funda 1 Kumkani 19:1-16. Kusixelela ntoni oku ngemeko yemvakalelo yomprofeti kaThixo?`

“ ‘Kwanele! Ngoku, Yehova, wuthabathe umphefumlo wam, ngokuba ndingalungile ngaphezu koobawo!’ ” (1 Kumkani 19:4). Oku kuvakala ngokungathi umntu unengxaki yokuba noxinzelelo. Kwakutheni? Okwenzeka entabeni yeKarmele kwakungakwazi ukuba sisenzo esilula esasiza kulandela, ngokuqinisekileyo; kodwa kunjalo, kuqhubeka enye into apha.

Mhlawumbi isitshixo singafumaneka kumazwi akhe, “ ‘Ndenziwe likhwele ngenxa kaYehova, uThixo wemikhosi; ngokuba bawushiyile umnqophiso wakho oonyana bakaIsrayeli, izibingelelo zakho bazigungxula, abaporofeti bakho bababulala ngekrele; ndasala ndedwa; bafuna umphefumlo wam ke, ukuba bawukhuphe’ ” (1 Kumkani 19:10). Oku kuvakala ngokungathi uyakhala kuba ekholelwa ukusilela kwakhe kulungiselelo lwakhe. Ukreqo lusele, nangona enze eyona mizamo ilunge kakhulu, ngokungathi eyedwa wayenako ukuwuthintela umsinga. Nokuba ekugqibeleni kwenzeka ntoni apha, siyalubona uluntu nobuthakathaka bale ndoda kaThixo. Ngamanye amazwi, uEliya wayengumntu owaye “nendalo efana neyethu.”

`Kwakutheni ukuba kube lula ngamanye amaxesha ukuba nengqondo yo “kusilela’ kobakho Ubomi okanye kwimizamo? Yintoni ethenjiswa yiBhayibhile onokuyibanga ukuze ikuncede usebenzane nokuphelelwa lithemba?`
