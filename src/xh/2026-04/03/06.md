---
title:  "Abaprofeti Bobuxoki"
date:   15/10/2026
---

Akukhathaleki nokuba uThixo wenza ntoni, uSathana unabakhe oonomgogwana. Kunjalo ngabaprofeti. UIsrayeli wayejongene nabanyanisileyo nababuxoki abaprofeti. Isitshixo kukwazi umahluko.

`Funda Yeremiya 14:1-15. Kwakusenzeka ntoni apha, waza uYehova wabathatha njengabantu abanjani nabafanelwe yintoni abo babethetha ngobuxoki njengaba“profeti” egameni laKhe?`

KuYeremiya 14, uThixo wanika uYeremiya isigidimi sesohlwayo uYuda, isohlwayo esasiza kufika sikwimo yekrele (imfazwe), indlala, nendyikitya yokufa. Abaprofeti bobuxoki ngelo xesha basichasa isigidimi sikaYeremiya. UYeremiya ubachaza ngamazwi acacileyo kakhulu: “Wathi uYehova kum, Abaprofeti baprofeta ubuxoki egameni lam; andibathumanga, andibawiselanga mthetho, andithethanga kubo; bona baniprofetela umbono wobuxoki, nokuvumisa, nento engento, nenkohliso yentliziyo yabo’ ” (Yer. 14:14).

Abaprofeti bobuxoki bakhohlakele kuba umthombo wabo wamabango okuprofeta ukholisa ukuba “ngoothixo” abo babanqulayo (Yer. 23:13, 14). UThixo uyalumkisa, “ ‘Andibathumanga, andibawiselanga mthetho, andithethanga kubo’ ” (Yer. 14:14). Bangabo “ ‘abaprofeti benkohliso yentliziyo yabo’ ” (Yer. 23:26). UHezekile wayalelwa ukuba aprofete ngokuchaseneyo “ ‘nabaprofeti . . . abaprofeta okwentliziyo yabo’ ” (Hez. 13:2).

Abaprofeti ababuxoki bakholisa ukulandela iimbono ezithandwa ngabantu. Kwakunjalo kwimeko eku-1 Kumkani 22, xa iqela lama-400 laba“profeti” lalihlangene ngomyalelo kakumkani uAhabhi (1 Kumkani 22:6). Bamcebisa ngazwi-nye ukumkani ukuba alwe neRamoth-Giliyadi. UAhabhi wafuna icebiso kuYehoshafati, ukumkani wakwaYuda, emva kokuba eve icebiso labaprofeti baka-Ashera. UYehoshafati wamkhuthaza uAhabhi ukuba abuze kumprofeti uMikaya ukuba angenyuka na okanye anganyuki ukuya edabini. “Ke obeye kubiza uMikaya, wathetha kuye esithi, Khawubone, amazwi abaprofeti athetha okuhle ngamlomo mnye kukumkani. Nceda, izwi lakho makhe libe linye njengelizwi labo, uthethe okuhle’ ” (1 Kumkani 22:13, NKJV).

Uxinzelelo olwaba phezu koMikaya ukuba axoke lwalufana nolo lwaviwa ngabaprofeti bobuxoki baseAshera, kodwa uMikaya akazange ayilandele loo mbono, koko walindela ukuba uThixo amxelele into amakayithethe (1 Kumkani 22:14).

`Nokuba unguye okanye akunguye umprofeti, kusoloko kulula ukulandela into ethandwayo. Ngokubhekiselele kwindlela inkcubeko ekhohlakele ngayo, kutheni ukuba le ingeyiyo ingcamango elungileyo?`
