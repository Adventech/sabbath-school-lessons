---
title:  "Abaprofeti Abangamabhinqa"
date:   14/10/2026
---

`Funda Eksodus 15:20, Abagwebi 4:4, 2 Kumkani 22:14, noNehemiya 6:14. Zikwasifundisa ntoni ezi ndima ngendawo yobuprofeti?`

Kukho ubungqina obuninzi esiBhalweni bokuba uThixo wawabiza amabhinqa ukuba abe ngabaprofeti. (Bona ukusetyenziswa kwegama lesiHebhere unevi’ah [Ekso. 15:20] negama lesiGrike prophetis [Luka 2:36]). KuMika 6:4, uMiriyam ufakwe kunye noMoses noAron njengabo babethunywe ebantwini. UMiriyam ubekwe njengomprofetikazi owahlabela ingoma yoloyiso akuba amaIsrayeli ewele uLwandle oluBomvu (Ekso. 15:20, 21). Kanti, lo mprofetikazi, njengabanye awayenabo abangamadoda, wayengemntu ungenazo iziphako, njengomoya wakhe okanye ingqondo yakhe ezintloni eyathi yabonakala ngakuMoses nenkosikazi yakhe eyaye ingumTiyopiya, izenzo awaya wohlwaywa ngazo kwangoko ngokufanelekileyo umprofetikazi (bona: Num. 12:1-10).

Siyawubona umsebenzi womnye umprofeti ebalini likaDebhora, owaba ngumgwebi kwaIsrayeli (Abagwebi 4:1-4). Inyaniso yeyokuba, kukho ubungqina obucacileyo bokuba uDebhora (Abagwebi 4:4) wafumana isityhilelo esiphuma kuThixo (Abagwebi 4:4-9, 14-22). Ingoma yakhe yoloyiso (Abagwebi 5:2-31) ikukuvuma ukungenelela kukaThixo kwaye inokubaluleka okunzulu ngokwazi uThixo kwisizwe ngelo xesha kwimbali.

`Funda 2 Kumkani 22:11-14. Kwakutheni ukuze amadoda kakumkani uYosiya aye kuHulda? Kumele ukusixelela ntoni oku ngokubaluleka kwendawo yakhe esizweni?`

UHulda “umprofetikazi,” wahanjelwa ngamadoda aphezulu equmrhu likakumkani uYosiya (2 Kumkani 22:14, 2 IziKron. 34:22) emva kokuba ayefunde amazwi “encwadi yomyalelo” (2 Kumkani 22:8) (ekukholelwa ukuba yayinguDuteronomi) eyayisiya kukumkani, owasuka wathi ukuphendula, “wazikrazula iingubo zakhe” (2 Kumkani 22:11). Kucacile ukuba lo yayingumcimbi onzima, waza ukumkani wawuthabatha ngolo hlobo, into leyo eyenza ukuba amadoda akhe asuke ngoko ukuya kuHulda. Wayesifumene isigidimi esivela kuThixo ngokubhekiselele kwisohlwayo esilindelekileyo sikaYuda. Uhlaziyo lukaYosiya lwaba sisiphumo sezigidimi zokuprofeta zikaHulda nesigwebo esijikiweyo sikaThixo kubantu baKhe.

`Abaprofeti abangamadoda, amabhinqa angabaprofeti, nangona bebiziwe nguThixo, bonke babengaboni abadinga ubabalo. Themba lini ongalifumana kumabali abo?`
