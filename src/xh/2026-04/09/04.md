---
title:  "Ukuzalwa KukaKristu Esenyameni"
date:   24/11/2026
---

Ngokuka-1 Yohane 4:1-3, uvavanyo lwenkolo ekuzalweni kukaYesu esenyameni kwakuya kukhawuleza ukubabekela ecaleni abantu ababanga ukuba ngabaprofeti abaphuma kwezinye iinkolo zehlabathi ezinkulu, okanye abo babanga ukuhlangana kwabo kummango wasemoyeni kodwa bengekho phantsi kwenkolo ethile. Ukuba umntu “akamvumi uYesu Kristu eze esenyameni,” loo mntu “asingokaThixo” (1 Yohane 4:3).

Xa uYohane wayebhala ezi ndima, wayecinga ngeetitshala zobuxoki ezazifundisa ukuba uYesu wayengenguye umntu, kodwa wayebonakala nje ngokungathi ungumntu. Olu vavanyo, phofu, luyadlula apho. Alwaneli ukuthi umprofeti kufuneka avume ukuba uYesu wayengumntu woqobo owayephila emhlabeni. AmaKristu amaninzi ayakholwa koku ngoYesu. UYohane wayephethe kanye lo mba ngelo xesha. Amazwi kaYohane okuprofeta, nangona ayedingeka kwimeko eyodwa, nangoku asenazo iinyaniso ezibalulekileyo nakwizizukulwana zamva.

`Ezi ndima zilandelayo zisixelela ntoni ngoYesu?: Yoh. 3:16; 2 Kor. 5:21; Yoh. 11:25; Heb. 4:14, 15; Yoh. 14:1-3.`

Abaprofeti benyaniso kufuneka bakholwe ukuba uYesu wayenguThixo ezelwe esinyameni ukanti engumntu ngenene. Kufuneka nokuba bayamkele ngokupheleleyo inyaniso ngoYesu—umsebenzi azele wona, Ubomi obungenasono, ukufa kocamagushelo, ukuvuka kwabafileyo, nolungiselelo lobubingeleli obukhulu ezulwini, kwakunye nokubuya kwaKhe kwesibini. Oku kungaphezu kokuvuma nje kwengqondo ukuba uYesu waphila emhlabeni. UYesu kufuneka abe ngumbindi weemfundiso zomprofeti angabi yinto nje eyongeziweyo. Izigidimi zakhe kufuneka zise abantu kulwalamano olusindisayo noYesu.

Ilizwi lobuporofeti eliphefumlelwe nguMoya Oyingcwele lisisikhanyiso sikaThixo, eligxotha ubumnyama ngaphakathi kuthi ade uYesu, iKwezi lomso, liphume ezintliziyweni zethu, kwaye isipho soprofeto siyasimanya naYe sikwaqhubeka sisikhombisa kuYe.

Abaprofeti abaphakamisa uYesu abayi kuzama ukuzenzela ababo abalandeli okanye baziphakamise bona ngokwabo (IZenzo 4:12). Baya kulungela ukukhonza bangabaxhaphazi abo bathunywe kubo. Kwangelo xesha linye, njengoko siza kubona ngomso, abaprofeti bakaThixo babengabantu, kwaye, njengathi sonke, bengaboni abazidingayo nabo iindaba ezilungileyo.

`Kutheni le no kusembindini kangaka wokholo ukuqonda ukuba uYesu, enguThixo ngokupheleleyo, wafa endaweni yethu, ethwele isohlwayo sesono sethu?`
