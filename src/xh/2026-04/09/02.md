---
title:  "Indawo Yongaphezu Kwendalo"
date:   22/11/2026
---

Ngexesha lemfuduko, uYehova wathetha nabantu baKhe ngesipho sokuprofeta phakathi kwabo, esithi, “ ‘Khanive amazwi am. Ukuba nithe nanomprofeti kaYehova, ndozazisa kuye ngombono, ndithethe kuye ephupheni’ ” (Num. 12:6).

Isigidimi sesiprofeto nokubiza abantu kuquka ongaphezu kwendalo; ngokuqinisekileyo kudlula ngaphaya kwembono okanye ingcamango anokuba nayo umntu. Umprofeti ufumana uqhagamshelwano oluvela ngqo kuThixo, kukholisa ukuba ngamaphupha obuprofeti okanye imibono (Dan. 8:1, Isa. 1:1, IZenzo 9:10, njl.) enokukhatshwa kokwenzekayo okubonakalayo emzimbeni.

`Yenza uludwe lweziphumo ezibonakalayo awaba nazo umprofeti uDaniyeli xa wayesembonweni. Bona uDan. 10:7-19.`

UDaniyeli walahlekelwa ngamandla akhe xa wayengena embonweni. Wayexhomekeke kuThixo ngexesha lokuqhuba kwamava lawo. Nangona abo babe kunye naye bengazange bangene embonweni, ubukho bOyingcwele babunamandla kangangokuba babaleka baya kuzifihla besoyika. Noko kunjalo, uThixo usebenzisa okubonakalayo komzimba ukutsalela ingqondo kumprofeti ukuze isigidimi soprofeto sibe nokuqapheleka.

`Yintoni esiyifundiswa nguDuteronomi 13:1-5 ngesipho sokuprofeta?`

Nangona okwenzekayo okubonakalayo kubonisa ukuba Amandla angaphezu kwendalo ayasebenza, oku akuzenzekeli nje ukuba kuthethe ukuthi umntu onoku kwenzekayo uphefumlelwe nguThixo. USathana unako ukubonakalalisa okungaphezu kwendalo. Asiyontwana nje ebonakalayo engqina ulungiselelo loprofeto. Ngaphandle kokwenzekayo emzimbeni, abaprofeti kufuneka bavavanywe. Okubonakalaliswayo kwangaphezu kwendalo akululo uvavanyo lwesipho sokuprofeta esiyinyaniso. Lonke uvavanyo lweBhayibhile lomprofeti kufuneka lubekho ukuze sikwazi ukuqinisekisa ukuba ngumthunywa kaThixo komntu. Imbali, ngelishwa, iye yagcwala ngabaprofeti bobuxoki (bona uMat. 7:15).

`Kwezinye iinkcubeko, ukwenzeka kokungaphezu kwendalo kuqheleke kakhulu kunezinye. Kungasizathu sini ke ngoko le nto kufuneka sibe nokulumka kakhulu xa sifuna ukwazi ukuba into engaphezu kwendalo iyekaThixo ngokwenene na?`
