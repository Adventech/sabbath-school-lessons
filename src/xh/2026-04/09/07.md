---
title:  "“Yivani Nibone”"
date:   27/11/2026
---

Ukwamkela kwebandla iSeventh-day Adventist isipho sesiprofeto sikaEllen G. White kususela ekuqaleni kwaso kude kube ngoku, kuye kwajongana nemingeni efanayo neyabaprofeti beBhayibhile. Emva kombono wokuqala kaEllen G. White ngoDisemba 1844, abanye babanengxaki ngamabango akhe obuprofeti. Oku kwakhokelela ekubeni ama-Adventist agcina iSabatha (kamva amaSeventh-day Adventists) akhulise iingxoxo zokuba asixhase isipho sokuprofeta sikaEllen G. White.

Kuqala, anika ukuthetheleleka kokuqhubeka kwesipho sokuprofeta kude kuphele ixesha. IBhayibhile iyakuxhasa ukuboniswa kwangoku kwesipho sokuprofeta, axoxa atsho. Ngokusekwe kuYoweli 2, IsiTyhilelo 12:17, nezinye izibhalo, isipho sokuprofeta siya kubonakala nge“mihla yokugqibela” sibe “luphawu olwahlulayo” lwabantu bakaThixo bexesha lokuphela. (Bona: Gerhard Pfandl, “The Gift of Prophecy and God’s Remnant Church,” The Gift of Prophecy, chapter 4.)

Okwesibini, ama-Adventist okuqala aphawula ukuba isipho sikaEllen G. White sasinempembelelo entle kumakholwa. Umzekelo, iimbono zakhe zoprofeto zanceda ukumanya amakholwa awaye kwiiNkomfa ezazibonisana ngeSabatha ekupheleni kweminyaka ye-1840, xa ayengavumelani ngeemfundiso zeBhayibhile. Isipho sakhe saqhubeka nokuthwala “iziqhamo ezihle” ngamashumi eminyaka kususela ngoko, nemibhalo yakhe isahleli inempembelelo ngaphakathi koluntu lokholo lwamaSeventh-day Adventist.

Ingxoxo yesithathu ukukhusela uEllen G. White yayamene neziqalelo ezingaphezu kwendalo kumava akhe asemibonweni. Xa esembonweni, uEllen G. White wayebonakala ebonakalalisa okungaphezu kwendalo—exela izenzo neemeko angazaziyo yena, etyhila iziganeko zexesha elizayo, ebonisa amandla angaphezu kwawendalo, nokuba angakwazi ukuyilawula okanye ukuba nempembelelo kwixesha lamava ombono, phakathi kwezinye izinto.

AmaSeventh-day Adventists namhlanje ayaqhubeka nokuzibabaza ezi nkxaso zokuqiniseka kwesipho sikaEllen G. White. Nangona ezi ngxoxo zivakala, “ingqiniseko” enamandla yesipho sikaEllen G. White ngamava omntu nemibhalo yakhe. Njengoko umdumisi uDavide wakhuthaza: “Yivani, nibone ukuba ulungile uYehova” (Indumiso 34:8). Kuphela xa, ngomthandazo, sithi siyifunde imibhalo kaEllen G. White esingathi sikwazi ukuwaxabisa amabango akhe obuprofeti.

Indawo enye ekungaqalwa kuyo ingomqulu wakhe omncinane kodwa nothandwa kakhulu, Amanyathelo Okuya KuKristu. Kulandele oku ngoluhlu lwemiqulu emihlanu, IMFAZWE ENKULU, elanda umxholo wembambano enkulu ukususela kuGenesis kuye kwisiTyhilelo. Imibhalo yakhe ifumaneka ngokubanzi kwiphepha nakwiinguqulelo zombane (bona: EGWWritings.org) kwaye iguqulelwe kwiilwimi ezininzi.
