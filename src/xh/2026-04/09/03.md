---
title:  "Ukuvumelana KweBhayibhile"
date:   23/11/2026
---

Nangona abantu bekholisa ukusiphikisa kwaye benenkohliso, uThixo akaxoki (bona uNum. 23:19). Izigidimi zesiprofeto azinikayo ngabaprofeti ziya kusoloko zikubonisa oku kungaguquguquki. Ukunyaniseka kwaKhe okungaguqukiyo kuya kubonakala kwizigidimi zokuprofeta azinikayo.

`Funda Isaya 8:20. Yingongoma ebalulekileyo ethini le ayenzayo uThixo?`

IBhayibhile isoloko ingumgangatho wokugqibela walo naliphi ibango lempefumlelo. Abaprofeti kufuneka bevavanywe ngokuthelekisa abakuthethayo noko kuthethwa yiBhayibhile. Umprofeti oyinyaniso akasoze aphikise, acuthe, okanye atsalele ingqalelo kude kwinyaniso yeBhayibhile. Yiyo le nto ukuzazi iziBhalo kubaluleke kakhulu kwabo bafuna ukulandela iNkosi bangakhohliswa ziititshala zobuxoki okanye abaprofeti bobuxoki.

KwiiTestamente eNdala neNtsha, abaprofeti bamva babekholisa ukubabaza nokwalatha emva kwimibhalo yokuprofeta edlulileyo ngokomgangatho wolwabo ulungiselelo lokuprofeta. Abaprofeti beBhayibhile bamva batyhila iimbono ezongeziweyo zamacebo kaThixo nendlela yosindiso, kodwa abaze bachase imithetho-siseko esisiseko neembono esele zinikiwe nguThixo.

`Yintoni esiyifundayo ngoThixo nezigidimi zesiprofeto awazinika kwiinkulungwane zonke kwezi ndawo zilandelayo: 2 Petros 1:19-21, Roma 15:4, noYakobi 1:17?`

Isiseko esiyiTestamente eNdala sokuqondwa kwecebo losindiso sabekwa kwisiprofeto sokuqala esanikwa nguThixo ngokwaKhe, kuGenesis 3:15. Ucwangcwiso lwemibingelelo lwaluzekelisa eli cebo, kodwa iinkcukacha zendlela iMvana kaThixo, uYesu Kristu, eyayiza kuza, iphilile, ifele uluntu yayicaciswe inyathelo ngalinye kwiziprofeto ezilandelayo. Ingxelo yeBhayibhile ibonisa ukuba uThixo uyiveza inyaniso xa abantu baKhe bekwazi ukuyiqonda. Oku kuvumela ukuyiqonda inyaniso kuye kwahla nzulu kwisizukulwana esitsha ngasinye. Kuthethwa loo nto ngengcamango yoku “tyhilwa kwenyaniso” okanye “inyaniso eqhubekayo.” Oko kukuthi, isizukulwana samva siya kusoloko sinolwazi olunzulu lwenyaniso kaThixo kunesangaphambili.

Noko kunjalo, ikho yona indawo ekungafuneki ukuba igxininiswe ngokubaxiweyo: izityhilelo ezitsha zenyaniso azisokuze ziyiphikise inyaniso uThixo abeyinike ngaphambili.
