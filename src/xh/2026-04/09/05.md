---
title:  "Iziqhamo Ezihle"
date:   25/11/2026
---

Kwintshumayelo yaseNtabeni (Mateyu 5-7), uYesu wandlala iziseko zebandla lobuKristu. Kungangathi ulungiselelo lobuprofeti lwaluya kuba yinxenye yeso siseko. Ngapha koko, uYesu walumkisa ngabaprofeti bobuxoki esithi, “ ‘Ngoko ke nobaqonda ngeziqhamo zabo’ ” (Mat. 7:20).

Ixabiso lesiqhamo lingabonwa kuphela emva kwexesha. Ngokubona isimilo somprofeti nokujonga iziphumo ezivela kwizigidimi zakhe, singabona ukuba loo mprofeti ubatsalela kuThixo na abanye okanye ubakhokelela kude kuYe.

`Funda Yona 1:1-3, IZenzo 15:36-39, namaGalati 2:11-14. Zisifundisa ntoni ezi ndima ngabaprofeti?`

Kona ukuba ngumprofeti akumenzi umntu ukuba angabi nasono okanye angabi namposiso. Siyazibona kwimizekelo yeBhayibhile kaYona, noPawulos, nekaPetros iimpazamo okanye iimposiso zesimilo. NguThixo yedwa ongenamposiso; bona abathunywa baKhe abaye babenjalo. Abaprofeti, njengathi sonke, banabo ubuthakathaka, kodwa ukuhamba kobomi babo kufuneka kucace ukuba kusinge kuThixo, kwaye abaprofeti kufuneka bangalukhanyeli ulungiselelo lwabo ngendlela yokuphila echaseneyo nesigidimi abasiphetheyo.

Akakho umprofeti obonisa ngokupheleleyo umzekelo kaYesu, kodwa umprofeti wenene uzinikele kwimithetho-siseko nendlela yokuphila yobukumkani bukaThixo (Mat. 7:16-20). Nabani obanga ukuba ungumthunywa kaThixo ibe indlela yokuphila kwakhe ichasana ngokuphandle nesigidimi asithweleyo kufuneka akrokreleke.

Nangona ubizo lobuprofeti lunzima, abaprofeti abaninzi baya babonisa “iziqhamo ezihle” ngexesha lolungiselelo lwabo. Umzekelo, uMoses wabakhokela ngempumelelo abantwana bakaIsrayeli entlango (Dut. 34:10-12). Ukunyaniseka kukaHagayi kwabakhuthazela abemi bakwaYuda ekwakhiweni kwetempile yesibini, eyayiyekwe i-19 leminyaka (Hag. 1:12). Ulungiselelo lukaIsaya olungagungqiyo lwakhonza ookumkani abane kwaYuda, lubakhokela ngexesha lengxushungxushu, lusalatha kuMesiya (Isa. 39:5, 6; Isa. 53:4-6). Asikwazi ukulibala ukuvuma kukaDaniyeli okuphuma entliziyweni (Dan. 9:15), okanye ukuzinikela okuzaliswe ziinyembezi kukaYeremiya (Yer. 9:1). Ingalityalwanga nayo indlela ababetshutshiswa bekwaphethwe kakubi ngayo, abaprofeti bakaThixo basoloko bebonisa uthando lwenene kuYe nabantu baKhe, uphawu olo lolungiselelo oluneziqhamo ezihle.

`Kukangakanani abathe abantu bakaThixo, amadoda neentokazi, bakudanisa ngezenzo zabo okanye izimilo ezineemposiso? Sithini isifundo esibalulekileyo ongasifunda kuloo mava?`
