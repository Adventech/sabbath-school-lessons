### Fundela Isifundo sale Veki
Dan. 10:7-19; Dut. 13:1-5; Isa. 8:20; Yoh. 11:25; IZenzo 15:36-39; Yer. 28:9, 15-17.

> <p>Indima Yenkumbulo</p>
> “Zintanda, musani ukukholwa ngoomoya bonke, bacikideni oomoya ukuba ngabakaThixo na; ngokuba baninzi abapro-feti ababuxoki, abaphume bangena ehlabathini” (1 Yohane 4:1).

Idayimani yoqobo yenye yamagugu anexabiso kakhulu emhlabeni. Inyaniso yeyokuba ezinye izinto ezininzi zingenziwa zikhangeleke zifana nedayimani kodwa zingenalo ixabiso. Umntu angazi njani ukuba idayimani iluqobo okanye ngunomgogwana? Eyona ndlela iyiyo kukuyixilonga. Okunye ukuxilonga kungenziwa ekhaya ngezixhobo nje ezilula. Okunye kudinga iincutshe ezitshatsheleyo. Uninzi loxilongo akufikeleli ekukugqibeni oko ngokwako nje kuphela, kodwa ukuba idayimani iye yaphumelela kuxilongo oluninzi, umntu angathemba ukuba iluqobo.

Idayimani isesinye sezimbiwa ezomeleleyo emhlabeni. Inxenye yokuqina kwedayimani kukukwazi kwayo ukumelana nobona bushushu buphezulu nokuguquka okukhawulezileyo kobushushu. Ukuyvavanya idayimani, ungayigqatsa ngento etshisayo okanye itotshi, uyiphose emanzini abandayo. Eyoqobo ayiyikonakala lo gama ufanakalo unako enokonakala!

Isipho soprofeto sinexabiso ukudlula idayimani, kanti umntu othi unaso akanakusuka akholelwe nje. Kule veki siza kuhlola abaprofeti beBhayibhile sibone imithetho-siseko esetyenzisiweyo ukuhlola ukuqiniseka kwabo.

_Funda isifundo sale veki ulungisela iSabatha kaNovemba 28._
