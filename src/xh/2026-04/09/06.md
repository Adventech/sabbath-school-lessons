---
title:  "Iziprofeto Eziye Zazaliseka"
date:   26/11/2026
---

Abantu baye bacinge ngomprofeti ongumntu oxela into ezayo, kodwa oku kungumba omnye womsebenzi womprofeti. Umsebenzi womprofeti uhlanganise okukhulu. Abaprofeti babuyisela abantu elizwini likaThixo, banike iingcebiso, balumkise, banike nenkuthazo.

`Ngaphandle kokwenza abantu bazi ngeziganeko zexesha elizayo, kutheni le nto abaprofeti bexela kwangaphambili ngexesha elizayo? Bona: Yer. 28:9, 15-17.`

Izinto ezazalisekayo zazixelwe kwangaphambili ziyenye indlela yokuvavanywa komprofeti. Okuxelwe kwangaphambili komprofeti wenene kufuneka kwenzeke njengoko kuprofetiwe: “ ‘Ilizwi, athe walithetha umprofeti egameni likaYehova, alabakho, alehla: lelo ilizwi angalithethanga uYehova. Uthethe ngokukhukhumala umprofeti lowo, uze ungabi nalunxunguphalo ngenxa yakhe’ ” (Dut. 18:22). Olu uvavanyo lunokunika umngeni ukulusebenzisa kuba ezinye iziprofeto ziyaxhomekeka; oko kukuthi, zinokungenzeki njengoko zixelwe kwangaphambili ngumprofeti xa iimeko zingakhange zizivumele okanye zihlangatyezwe.

Siwubona lo mthetho-siseko ngoYona nezilumkiso zakhe ngakwiNineva. Intshumayelo kaYona ibonakala ngathi noko yawa nganeno kodwa yalathe ngqo: “ ‘Zisaya kuba ziintsuku ezimashumi mane, iNineve ibhukuqwe!’ ” (Yona 3:4). Kanti ke oko akuzange kwenzeke. Ngokuba kutheni?

`Funda Yeremiya 18:7-10. Kusifundisa ntoni oku nako, ngesiprofeto esixhomekekileyo?`

Iziprofeto ezixhomekekilelyo ziziprofeto ezixhomekeke kwindlela abantu abasabela ngayo kwizigidimi zikaThixo. Ukuba abantu baye bayitshintsha ingqondo, nokuziphatha kwabo, isiganeko esiprofetiweyo sinokungenzeki. Abantu baseNineve balamkela entliziyweni yabo ilizwi likaYona baguquka kwiindlela zabo, ngoko ke, uThixo akazange abatshabalalise (Yona 3:10). Zathi izizukulwana zamva zakusilibala esi siprofeto sesilumkiso, yaphinda kwakhona iNineve yayindawo enobubi, ekhohlakeleyo, uThixo waya wayitshabalalisa. UThixo uyalazi ikamva, kodwa thina asilazi. Okwethu ukukhetha kugqiba kakhulu ngobunyaniso bekamva lethu.

`Kubaluleke ngantoni ukuyiqonda imbono yesiprofeto esixhomekekileyo? Oku kuqonda kungasinceda njani ukuba siyibambe ngcono inyaniso yeBhayibhile?`
