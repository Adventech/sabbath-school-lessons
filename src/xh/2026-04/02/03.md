`Funda 1 Kumkani 18:19-39. Yintoni esingayibona kweli bali ngendlela isikhundla somprofeti esinokusebenza ngayo?`

Ubizo lukaEliya nohlobo lokuthetha lwayibonakalalisa indawo yakhe njengomthunywa kaThixo. Ukunqanda ukreqo lukaIsrayeli, uThixo wathuma uEliya kukumkani uAhabhi ukuba avakalise ngembalela yeminyaka emininzi. UThixo wasebenzisa imbandezelo ukubuyisa abantu baKhe basuke ekunquleni izithixo beze ekuthobeleni. Ukumkani nelizwe bamgxeka uEliya ngenxa yobubi ababakubo baza bafuna ukufa kwakhe.

Phezu kwentaba yeKarmele, uEliya wayejongene nabaprofeti bakaBhahali noAshera. Umthunywa kaThixo wenza isibheno esizaliswe nguMoya kuIsrayeli owayenqula izithixo ukulahlela phantsi oothixo babo bobuxoki babuyele kuYehova. Abantu babesazi ukuba ukukhetha kwabo kwakhokelela ekubeni kubekho ukreqo lwesizwe sonke. Ngoku babekulungele ukumamkela uThixo kaEliya kwakhona, owazibonisayo ukuba wongame ngaphezu koothixo babo bobuxoki, phofu, ababengekho ngokwenene.

Kanye phambi kokubuya kukaKristu kwesibini, uThixo uyalela ibandla laKhe ukuba livakalise isigidimi sikaEliya kwakhona, nokuba abantu babuye kwi“zithixo” zabo balungele ukuma ngemini enkulu kaYehova. UThixo usathetha nabantu baKhe ngamajelo awakhethileyo. Ilizwi laKhe nesipho sokuprofeta zinika isiyalo, isilumkiso, nenkuthazo.

“Imvuselelo yokuhlonipha uThixo ngenene phakathi kwethu yeyona ntswelo inkulu kakhulu neyona ingxamisekileyo kuzo zonke iintswelo zethu. Ukufuna oku kufuneka kube ngumsebenzi wethu wokuqala okanye ophambili. Kufuneka kubekho ilinge elinyanisileyo lokuzuza intsikelelo kaYehova, kungekuba uThixo akanantumekelelo ukuthulula intsikelelo yaKhe phezu kwethu, kodwa kuba thina asikulungelanga ukuyamkela.”—Ellen G. White, Selected Messages, book 1, p. 121.

`Funda Malaki 4:5, 6. Siya kuba nagalelo lini isigidimi sikaEliya kumakhaya abo balandela uThixo?`

Imvuselelo eyinyaniso iqala entliziyweni nasekhaya. Usapho lulilitye lembombo yebandla nelitye elingenako ukulahlwa kwisakhiwo soluntu ekuhlaleni. Imvuselelo ekhayeni isasaza uthando lwezulu ebandleni, ekuhlaleni nakwisizwe. Yinjongo kaThixo le ngekhaya lobuKristu. Imvuselelo ikwabakhuthaza abantu bakaThixo ukuba basebenze.

`Luhlobo luni lwezithixo, ukuba zikho, ezisemakhayeni ethu, ezintliziyweni, nasemabandleni?`
