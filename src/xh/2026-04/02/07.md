---
title:  "Ukubizelwa Kulungiselelo Lobuprofeti"
date:   09/10/2026
---

UThixo ubabiza abaprofeti ngamaxesha amiselweyo ngeenjongo ezizodwa. Babekho nezigidimi ezazidingeka ngelo xesha. Kule meko, amaSeventh-day Adventist akholelwa ukuba uEllen G. White wayengomnye abanjalo owaba nokubonakalalisa isipho sobuprofeti sisebenza kanye njengomprofeti ongekho eBhayibhileni (siza kufunda ngakumbi ngoku kwizifundo ezizayo). UEllen Harmon [White] walufumana ubizo lwakhe lokuqala ngoDisemba 1844, kwiiveki ezimbalwa emva kokuba ama-Adventist kaMiller ayelindele ukubuya kukaYesu ngokusekwe kuDaniyeli 8:14: “Koda kube kukuhlwa, kusisa, okungamawaka amabini anamakhulu amathathu; yandule ukulungiswa ingcwele.”

Nangona uWilliam Miller engazange wawubeka ngokuthe ngqo umhla, abalandeli bakhe, ekugqibeleni, bakhangela kuOkthobha 22, 1844, ngokusekwe kwixesha leBhayibhile lokubala iMini yoCamagushelo njengomhla uKristu awayeza kubuyela ngawo emhlabeni. Le nkolo yazisa ukulindela okukhulu kakhulu kumawaka-waka kwiNorth America nakwezinye iindawo zehlabathi. Kungekudala, babeza kuhlanganiswa noMsindisi wabo ezulwini. UMiller, naye, wazaliswa kukuvuseleleka nentshiseko enkulu njengoko ukuFika okulindelweyo kusondela.

Noko kunjalo, uKristu akazange abonakale, aza amakholwa kaMiller aba nesona siganeko sityhafisayo kwimbali yazo zonke iziganeko, ukuDana okukhulu.

Yimeko le eyayigquba awathi ngayo uThixo wanika uEllen Harmon [White] umbono wakhe wokuqala. Isigidimi esiphambili kuwo yayikukukhuthaza yena namakholwa awayedanile, nokuba uThixo wayesenabo nangona kwakukho inkcazo eyimpazamo. Umbono walatha kwindlela eyayisuka kulo mhlaba isiya kwiYerusalem yasezulwini. Kule ndlela kwakukho ababekholwa ekufikeni behamba besiya kuMzi Ongcwele. Kunjalo, “ukuba babewamilisele amehlo abo kuYesu, owayephambi kwabo, ebakhokela ukuya eMzini, babekhuselekile.”—Ellen G. White, Spiritual Gifts, vol. 2, p. 31.

Lo mbono wawuziindaba ezilungileyo. UThixo wayeqala ukudala intshukumo ephuma kwisiphithiphithi sokudana. UYesu wayese nabo. Wayesaza kubuya. Bona kwakufuneka bagcine nje amehlo abo “ezinze” kuYe. Babe nezizathu ezikhulu ukuba bangalilahli ithemba, naphakathi kwesiphithiphithi nodaniso olukhulu kangako.

Ngoko ke, uEllen G. Harmon [White], intwazana ene-17 leminyaka ubudala, wabizelwa kulungiselelo lobuprofeti. Okwakhe yayingeyiyo indlela elula, ngakumbi ekuqalekeni.

Nangona esi sipho samkelwa kancinane kancinane ukuba siyinene, Ellen G. White wayesoyika ukuba ngumthunywa kaThixo. Xa kunjalo, wayeya kuthini uEllen White ukuluvuma ubizo lukaThixo?
