---
title:  "UAbraham, Umkhuseli Womnqophiso"
date:   04/10/2026
---

Ukususela kuGenesis ukuya kuIsiTyhilelo, uThixo wayenesisalela esithembekileyo esasamkele ubizo lokuthobela ukusabela kubabalo lwaKhe (bona: Yer. 31:7, 31-34). Umzekelo, sekuyiminyaka emva kokusasazwa kwaseBhabheli, uThixo wanyula uAbram (kamva Abraham) ukuba abe ngumzekelo womnqophiso wokholo, kungekuphela kusapho lwakhe kodwa kuzo zonke izizukulwana zexesha elizayo. Ejikelezwe lukreqo nokungafani kokuziphatha ngokusesikweni, uAbraham wabonisa ukuthembeka kubizo lukaThixo. (Bona: Gen. 12:1-9.)

UThixo wamxelela uAbraham iindlela zomnqophiso waza wambonisa indlela yosindiso loluntu oluya kufezwa ngombingelelo kaKristu (Gen. 15:6-17). Ukuvavanya ukholo luka-Abraham nokwenza umzekelo wombingelelo wexesha elizayo woNyana kaThixo, uAbraham kwathiwa makanikele uIsake, unyana wakhe, njengombingelelo kuThixo (Gen. 22:1-17). Uvavanyo olubuhlungu kangako lomzali wamakholwa. Cinga ngazo zonke izizathu ezazingasetyenziswa nguAbraham ukwala umyalelo ocacileyo kaThixo, noko wathobela ngokuthembeka. UThixo wasibamba isandla sika-Abraham ekumbulaleni uIsake wakhupha imvu endaweni yoko.

Asingabo bonke abaprofeti ababizwa nguThixo ukuba balumkise ngentshabalalo ezayo. Bonke babebiziwe, kodwa, ukubuyisela abantu ekunyanisekeni, nokuba bakholise ukulumkisa ngakukreqo, ngakwingcinezelo yabo babeswele bengamahlwempu, nangasekophulweni komnqophiso nemiyalelo yawo.

Kanti ke, okubaluleke ukudlula konke,abaprofeti babeza kutyhila isithembiso sosindiso olufunyanwa kuMesiya. Ubungqina obuhleliyo babaprofeti beBhayibhile noboMoya waKhe kweli xesha lethu lasemva kwabapostile bufuna ukuba samkele nokuba silumkise abanye ngentlekele ezayo eza kulifikela ihlabathi. Kanti ke, embindini koko kulumkisa kukho iindaba ezilungileyo zokuhlangulwa kwabantu bakaYesu, abathembeke kuYe, nabaye bahlala kubudlelwane bomnqophiso naYe (IsiTy. 12:17).

`Funda Genesis 15:6, Galati 3:6-9, noHebhere 11:8. Kuthiwani ngobomi buka-Abraham nesimilo sakhe esamenza wakufanela ukusebenzela uThixo, nangona, ngokucacileyo, wayengumntu onazo iziphoso?`

Kuninzi ukuhambelana phakathi kwemihla ka-Abraham neyethu. Njengokuba ubuhedeni nonqulo lwezithixo konke kwakundlongondlongo ngemihla ka-Abraham, ngokunjalo namhlanje, uninzi lweengcamango ezifana nezo zijonge ukucima okanye ukubeka ecaleni ubuKristu. Ngokuchasene nokuninzi kwenkcubeko egqubayo, abantu bakaThixo namhlanje babizelwe ukuba babonakalalise umfanekiselo waKhe ekusebenzisaneni kwabo nabanye, kuquka ukuvakalisa kwabo izigidimi zeengelosi ezintathu (IsiTy. 14:6-12).

`IsiBhalo sixela ukuba uAbraham “wakholwa kuYehova; oko wakubalela ebulungiseni” (Gen. 15:6). Usibona njani isithembiso seendaba ezilungileyo ezityhilwe ngamandla kule ndima? Themba lini onokuzizuzela lona wena koku?`
