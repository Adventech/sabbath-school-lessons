---
title:  "UDaniyeli, Imbomi Ethembekileyo"
date:   07/10/2026
---

AmaKristu amaninzi ayalazi ibali likaDaniyeli 1, apho wema waqina kwimithetho-siseko yempilo awayifunda kwasebuntwaneni bakhe. UDaniyeli waye ngumntu ongumzekelo wokunyaniseka (Dan. 1:8). Xa wayenikwa izibiliboco zasebukhosini, wazingisa ekutheni ukutya okwakhelwe kwimifuno nokusela kuphela amanzi kunokuba athabathe ukutya awayekunikwa okusuka etafileni kakumkani (Dan. 1:12-16). Olu yayiluvavanyo oluncinane xa luthelekiswa kokwakuza kulandela kamva, njengakuDaniyeli 6, xa ukunyaniseka kwakhe kwakuthetha, ngokulula, ukufa kuye.

`Funda Daniyeli 2:46-48; Daniyeli 4:18; Daniyeli 5:13, 14; noDaniyeli 6:25-27. Zisixelela ntoni ezi ndima ngohlobo lengqina awayelilo uDaniyeli kubahedeni ababemjikelezile?`

Khangela kubungqina obusuka kwaba bahedeni ngamandla kaThixo kaDaniyeli. Sele engaqhelekanga enjalo la mabali, onke atyhila umthetho-siseko obalulekileyo: Ngobomi bethu, ngokunyaniseka kwethu, nangamandla kaThixo asebenza ngaphakathi kwethu nangathi, abanye banako ukufunda ngoThixo wasezulwini, nokuba ungu “Thixo wooThixo, neNkosi yookumkani.”

Kwakhona, ngenxa yomxholo oqhubekayo weziprofeto zikaDaniyeli kukho ugwebo oluphelela ekubuyeni kukaKristu kwesibini (uloyiso lukaThixo lokugqibela), intsingiselo yegama likaDaniyeli, (“uThixo ungumgwebi [mahluleli] wam,”) lifaneleke kakuhle. Oko akubhalileyo kudinga ukufundisiswa ngononophelo, kunikwe ingqalelo njengoko kufanelekile namhlanje. Iziprofeto zikaDaniyeli zisebenza ukukhumbuza amaxesha onke ukuba kuhlalwe kuphakanyiswe intloko nokuba kugcinwe imithetho kaThixo, nakwizinto ezincinane zobomi.

Kanti ke, njengomprofeti, uDaniyeli waya wakuxela kwangaphambili ukunyuka nokuwa kwezizwe neziphumo zokungamthobeli kwazo uThixo. Ngemibono yakhe yobuprofeti, uDaniyeli watyhila icebo likaThixo nesiphelo esiluloyiso sobukumkani bukaThixo (Dan. 7:13, 14).

Cinga, umzekelo, ngoDaniyeli 2, owabhalwa ngaphezu kwesiqingatha sewaka leminyaka ngaphambi kukaKristu. UDaniyeli wakuzoba ngokuchaneke kakhulu ukuphakama nokuwa kwezi zikumkani zine zehlabathi, kude kuze kwixesha lethu (obo beenyawo neenzwane zomfanekiso oqingqiweyo), kubhekisa ngokucacileyo kwizizwe zeYurophu yeli xesha langoku, ezaziya kuthi, ngokufundisa kwesiprofeto okucacileyo, zizame “ukuzixuba nembewu yento engumntu; kodwa abayi kunamathelana, . . . kwanjengokuba isinyithi singaxubeki nodongwe” (Dan. 2:43). Okuxelwe kwangaphambili okumangalisayo ngeYurophu, nanamhlanje, apho kuthi nangona sekwendiselwene, aza enziwa onke amalinge ukuzisa umanyano, izwekazi lahlala lizizizwe ezahlukene ngemithetho, ngamasiko, kwanezithethe. Kukwathe, kwiminyaka nje embalwa phambi kokuwa kweSoviet Union, akwabakho buchule beentlola bukwaziyo ukukubona ukuwa okuzayo, nekuthe ukuxela kwangaphambili kukaDaniyeli kwasinika izizathu ezinamandla ezenza sithembele kubaprofeti bakaThixo.

`Zonke izikumkani zikaDaniyeli 2 zafika zadlula njengoko kwakuxeliwe. Kumele ukusixelela ntoni oku ngokusithemba isiprofeto esithetha ngobukumkani bukaThixo obungunaphakade obuza kulandela (Dan. 2:44)?`
