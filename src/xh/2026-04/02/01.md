### Fundela Isifundo sale Veki
Gen. 15:6, Gal. 3:6-9, 1 Kumkani 18:19-39, Isaya 53, Dan. 2:46-48, Marko 1:2-5, Mat. 11:1-6.

> <p>Indima Yenkumbulo</p>
> “Ndeva izwi leNkosi, lisithi, Ndothuma bani na? Ngubani na owosiyela? Ndathi, Ndikho, thuma mna!” (Isaya 6:8).

“Ngamaxesha eBhayibhile, abaprofeti babekholisa ukudlala phambili kwimbali yabantu bakaThixo. Maxa wambi babeba ngumgangatho osembindini, bethabatha iindawo ezinamandla, xa zingezizo ezibalulekileyo, kubomi bokuhlala emphakathini.

Kona ukuba ngumprofeti kwakulubizo olunzima. Ubomi babo babukholisa ukungabi lula, kodwa buzaliswe lusizi, ukubukulwa, ukungaqondwa, nokuphelelwa lithemba. Abaprofeti bakaThixo babekholisa ukungakwamkeli lula ukubizelwa kwabo kumsebenzi wobuprofeti. Akumangalisi oko. Abaprofeti abaninzi babechaswa ngamandla ngabo kanye babemele ukuzamkela izigidimi zabo. Abaporofeti bakaThixo babengakhathalelwe, kuhlekiswa ngabo, bebethwa, okanye babanjwe. Abanye babexulutywa ngamatye okanye bazingelwe ngokwezilwanyana zasendle. Abaninzi bafel’ ukholo.

Namhlanje, kwisizukulwana esizele bubundlobongela, ukubawa, ukuziphatha kakubi, ukuxhatshazwa, nazo zonke iimpembelelo ezikhohlakeleyo, izwi lomprofeti likholisa ukwaliwa okanye lingahoywa. Sibizelwe ukuba silithobele ilizwi labaprofeti bakaThixo, sibe ngabathunywa bethemba esilinikwe ngabaprofeti.

Kwiveki ephelileyo sifunde ukuba, emva kokuWa e-Eden, uThixo wasebenza ukumisela ngokutsha uqhagamshelwano nabantu. Kule veki siza kuhlola amanye amaxesha abalulekileyo kwimbali xa uThixo wabiza abaprofeti ukuba alungelelanise abantu baKhe nezigidimi zaKhe zehlabathi eliwileyo.

_Funda isifundo sale veki ulungiselela iSabatha kaOkthobha 10._
