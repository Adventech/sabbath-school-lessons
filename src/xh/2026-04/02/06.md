date:   08/10/2026
---

`Funda Marko 1:2-5. Yayiyintoni injongo yolungiselelo lukaYohane umBhaptizi?`

Ivangeli kaMarko isixelela ukuba ukufika kukaYohane umBhatizi yayikukuzaliseka kwesiprofeto sikaIsaya (Isa. 40:3). Ukukhula kukaYohane ukusuka ebuntwaneni ukuya ebudodeni kwakuphawulwe ngokunyaniseka kuThixo. USathana wakoyika ukulahlekwa bubukumkani bakhe phezu koluntu emva kokuba luve ilizwi likaYohane entlango (bona: Ellen G. White, Ulangazelelo Lwamaphakade, iph. 224). Inkohlakalo yabo babemphulaphule uYohane yayigqunywe ngendlela eyamenza uSathana wangcangcazela. Isandla esiqinileyo sikaSathana kuIsrayeli saya soyikiswa. Waphindaphinda ukuzama kodwa esoyiswa ukumtyhafisa umBhaptizi kwindlela ethembekileyo awayephila ngayo nokuthembela kwakhe kuMesiya.

`Funda Mateyu 11:1-6. UYesu waluqinisekisa njani ukholo lukaYohane ngexesha lo mshumayeli wayesentolongeni?`

USathana akazange akwazi ukumhenda uYesu ukuba angene esonweni, kwaza ukoyiswa kwakhe entlango kwamenza wanengqumbo. Emva koko waziguqula iindlela zakhe ngokwenza ukuba umzala kaYesu, uYohane, aphoswe entolongweni, nangona uSathana engazange akwazi ukumenza one. Le yaye iyindlela kaSathana yokumaphula uYohane, lo gama wayebeka uxinzelelo phezu kwemvakalelo yoMsindisi. Yena uYohane walifumana ilizwi lenkuthazo kuYesu. Kwathi naxa ekwiimeko ezimbi kakhulu, nokuba ngokuqinisekileyo engaqondi ukuba kutheni ukuba ahlale entolongweni kanti uYesu wayenamandla amakhulu kangako, uYohane kodwa sekunjalo, wahlala ethembekile kwada kwasa ekufeni.

Njengabaprofeti bakudala, nathi sibizelwe ukuba sivakalise isigidimi senguquko nokholo nobulungisa bukaKristu obupheleleyo (IsiTyh. 14:6-12). Njengoko wenzayo ngoYohane umBhaptizi, uSathana angasebenzisa ukungaqiniseki ukusenza ukuba sikuthandabuze ukuthunywa kwethu nguThixo kobethu ubomi. Akufuneki siphelelwe lithemba ukuba siyakukhumbula ukukhokela kukaThixo ebomini bethu, ngakumbi xa izinto zingahambi kakuhle kuthi, njengoko kwenzekayo kuYohane, owavalelwa entolongweni waza emva koko wabulawa zizenzo zomfazi okhohlakeleyo nendoda enengqondo ebuthathaka. Nokuba abantu bangenza ntoni kuthi, sinako ukufikelela kuThixo ngokholo, kwaye uya kusiphendula. Usithembisile amandla okuyenza intando yaKhe (IZenzo 1:8). Kwihlabathi lasemva kukaKristu nelingelilo elinobuKristu, kunyanzelekile ukuba sibe ngamangqina athembekileyo kaKristu, naphantsi kwaziphi iimeko.

`NjengoYohane entolongweni, ingaba sowakha wakwindawo apho ungakwaziyo ukuba kutheni izinto ezimbi kangako zisenzeka kuwe? Wakwazi njani ukuqhubeka ulugcinile ukholo lwakho nokuthembela kuThixo?`
