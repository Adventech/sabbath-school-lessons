### Fundela Isifundo sale Veki
Ekso. 4:10-16, Luka 8:22-25, Yohane 3:1-10, IZenzo 16:25-34, 1 Kor. 5:1-5, Luka 7:28.

> <p>Indima Yenkumbulo</p>
> “Kuba ilizwi likaThixo liphilile, lisebenza, libukhali ngaphezu kwamakrele onke antlangothi-mbini; lihlaba liphumele, lide lahlule umphefumlo kwanomoya, amalungu kwanomongo; linokugweba iingcingane nezicamango zentliziyo” (Hebhere 4:12).

NgoNovemba 9, 1989, intetho kaGünter Schabowski kwinkqubo eyayibonwa kwiTV kwiCommunist East Germany yaqala ukushukumisa ukuwa kodonga lwaseBerlin. Esi sithethi soshicilelo lwamaphepha eMpuma Jamani, uSchabowski wenza intetho embi kwinkomfa eyayiboniswa koomabona-kude. Endaweni yokuxela ukuba iMpuma Jamani ingavunyelwa ukuba ityelele iNtshona Yuropu emva kwenkqubo ende nenzima yokugcina iivisa ezizizo, wasuka wathi nje abantu beMpuma Jamani baya kuvunyelwa ukutyelela iNtshona Yuropu. Wathi akubuza ocholachola ingxelo ukuba ukuguqulwa komthetho kuqala ukusebenza nini, uSchabowski wathi, “Ngokokwazi kwam, oku kuza kuqala ukusebenza . . . kwangoku, ngaphandle kokulibazisa.” Ngemizuzwana nje embalwa, baqala abantu ukugaleleka kwimida, bebanga ukuba bavunyelwe bagqithe, njengoko urhulumente evakalisile.

Abantu bakholisile ukunikwa igunya lokuthethela inkampani, okanye ilizwe. Kubekaphi ngabathethela uThixo? Kule veki siza kuvavanya ukuba gunya lini abanalo abaprofeti beBhayibhile, nokuba, ukuba likho, ngunya lini abanalo abo bangazange bayibhale incwadi yeBhayibhile, njengoNathane umprofeti, noYohane umBhaptizi, ewe, kunye noEllen G. White.

_Funda: Isifundo sale veki ulungisela iSabatha kaDisemba 5._
