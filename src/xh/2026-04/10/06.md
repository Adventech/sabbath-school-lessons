---
title:  "Abaprofeti: Ababhalileyo nabangabhalanga eBhayibhileni"
date:   03/12/2026
---

`Funda 1 IziKronike 29:29 ne-2 IziKronike 9:29. Yintoni oyiphawulayo ngabanye babaprofeti abakhankanyiweyo kwezi ndawo zimbini?`

Baninzi abaprofeti njengoNathan, noGadi, noAhija, noIdo ababengabaprofeti abamkelekileyo kodwa abancwadi zabo zingazange zibe yinxalenye yeBhayibhile.

`Funda 1 Kumkani 11:29-39 noLuka 7:28. Zifundisa ntoni ezi ndima ngegunya labaprofeti abangenancwadi iBhalwe eBhayibhileni?`

Nangona singazi ukuba kutheni ukuze uThixo azivumele izigidimi zabanye abaprofeti ukuba zihlanganiswe eBhayibhileni lo gama ezinye zingafakwanga, siyazi ukuba abaprofeti abangaqukwanga eBhayibhileni babephefumlelwe ngokulinganayo nguThixo. IBhayibhile ifundisa ukuba izipho zikaMoya, ziquka isipho sokuprofeta, ziya kuqhuba kude kuphele ixesha. IBhayibhile ikuxela phambi kwexesha ukuba kuya kubakho abaprofeti baphambi kweBhayibhile, nabangekho eBhayibhileni “ukuxhobisela abangcwele umsebenzi wolungiselelo, nokwakhiwa komzimba kaKristu, side sonke sifike kubunye bokholo” (Efese 4:12, 13), kwaye baya kubakho “ ‘phambi kokufika kwemini enkulu yeNkosi’ ” (Yoweli 2:31).

Emva kokuba uDavide ekrexezile wabulala, uThixo wathumela umprofeti uNathan ukuba amngqale (2 Sam. 12:7-14). Nangona uNathan wayengenayo incwadi ebhaliweyo eBhayibhileni, uDavide akazange abe nombuzo ngegunya lakhe uNathan, kwaye oko kungokuba igunya lemibhalo yabaprofeti lixhomekeke kwimpefumlelo yabo, hayi ekubeni ukho na okanye akakho kubabhali abaseBhayibhileni. Noko ke kunjalo, bonke abaprofeti abanyanisileyo abangafakwanga kwababhale eBhayibhileni, ngakumbi abaprofeti basemva kwexesha leBhayibhile—nangokukodwa umprofeti wangoku njengoEllen G. White—bazibona bephantsi kwegunya leBhayibhile. Izigidimi zabo kwafuneka zivavanyiwe kwaye azimi endaweni yeBhayibhile njengethende lokholo nemfundiso.

`UEllen G. White wabhala, “UYehova ulangazelela ukuba nizifundisise iiBhayibhile zenu. Akanikanga nakanye ukukhanya okongeziweyo ukuba kuthabathe indawo yeLizwi laKhe, elithi, xa lityiwe lasetyenzwa esiswini, lifane negazi lobomi emphefumlweni.”—Selected Messages, book 3, p. 29. Siwathobela kakuhle kanjani thina la mazwi akhe?`
