---
title:  "Igunya Lolungiselelo"
date:   02/12/2026
---

UHoseya 12:13 ukwenza kucace ukuba “uYehova wamnyusa uIsrayeli ngomprofeti ke, wagcinwa ngomprofeti ke.” Ukukhusela nokukhokela abantu bakaThixo kwaye kuyinxalenye yokwenza nefunekayo yomsebenzi wabaprofeti beBhayibhile.

Xa sifunda iindima ezinje ngezi: Abagwebi 4:4-8, 2 IziKronike 20:14-17, Eksodus 12:1-3, noDuteronomi 1:9-16, siyakwazi ukubona ukuba abaprofeti babebalulekile ekutyhileni icebo lobukhokeli bukaThixo kubantu baKhe. Ngamaxesha emfazwe nentlekele, abaprofeti babeyindlela yokunika kukaThixo inkokelo, inkuthazo, nethembe kubantu baKhe nakwiinkokeli zabo, njengoko wenzayo kuYehaziyeli noDebhora. Abaprofeti babekwanika inkokelo ngokubhekisele kwimibhiyozo nonqulo (Eksodus. 12:1-3). Ngamanye amaxesha, uThixo wayethetha kubaprofeti ngokubhekiselele kulawulo lwesizwe. Ngenye indlela, ayikho indawo kubomi bukaIsrayeli eyayingaphaya komyalelo nenkokelo kaThixo ngabathunywa baKhe abanyuliweyo.

`Zeziphi ezinye iindawo eziqinisekileyo apho isipho sobuprofeti sasinceda kulun-giselelo nobukhokeli kwibandla lokuqala lobuKristu? IZenzo 6:1-7; 1 Kor. 5:1-5; 1 Kor. 7:10-16, Tito 1:5.`

Yiba nomfanekiso-ngqondweni wolungelelaniso olutsha olungenaso isikhokelo nenkokeli ebonakalayo. Ulungelelaniso olunalo luqinisekile ngokuba nemingeni enzima. Ngendlela ethile okukwakuyimeko kwibandla lobuKristu lokuqala. UYesu wayenyukile engasenabo abafundi baKhe. Babe nomyalelo ocacileyo ophuma kuYe: “ ‘Hambani niye kulo lonke ihlabathi, nizivakalise iindaba ezilungileyo’ ” (Marko 16:15). Nangona abapostile abalishumi elinambini babenyuliwe, kwakungekho lulungiselelo lucacileyo lokubanceda ukuba bawuqale umsebenzi.

Ibandla lokuqala lalijongene nemingeni emininzi, njengoqulunqo lwebandla, imo yomsebenzi wovangelo, nentswelo yokukwazi ukulubona nokulugweba ukreqo nokuziphatha ngokungekho esikweni phakathi kwabo. Ngendlela eyiyo, isipho sokuprofeta esasisebenza kwibandla lokuqala sadlala indawo ekugcinweni kwalo nasekulikhuseleni (IZenzo 13:1, 2). Kunzima, phofu, ukuba nombono wendlela ibandla lokuqala elalinokuwuqala ngayo umsebenzi ngaphandle koncedo lwesipho soprofeto ekuncedeni uqulunqo lwalo nokulenza libheke phambili nomsebenzi eliwuthuyiweyo.

`Abanye abantu babonakala benentandabuzo “ngenkolo equlunqiweyo.” Nokuba ithini na imingeni, kokuphi okuhle ekubeni yinxalenye yebandla eliqulunqiweyo? Yintoni ongayinika, uyizuze, ekubeni yinxalenye yento enkulu kakhulu kunesiqu sakho?`
