---
title:  "Igunya LeBhayibhile"
date:   01/12/2026
---

IBhayibhile izalwa sisiprofeto, isityhilelo esiphefumlelweyo esivela kuThixo. Siphendula imibuzo emikhulu yobomi kwaye siyindlela kaThixo engaqhelekanga yokuzityhila kuhlanga loluntu. ngesiBhalo, sifunda intsingiselo yobukho bomntu, isimilo nentando kaThixo, nenjongo kaThixo ngobomi bethu. IBhayibhile ingumgangatho wegolide wenyaniso, igunya ekufuneka sithi ngalo senze ukukhetha kwethu. Isisiseko sendlela umKristu alibona ngayo ihlabathi, nesiseko sayo yonke inyaniso etyhiliweyo. Ingumgaqo wokholo nokwenza komKristu.

Singalamkela, singangalamkeli iLizwi likaThixo, kodwa asidingi ukuba side sibaxe ngokutshisa iiBhayibhile zethu njengoko wenzayo uYehoyiyakim (bona: Yer. 36:22-27) xa engalifuni. Ukungalikhathaleli, sesisithi siyalikholelwa, kungaba yingxaki nje yodwa. Xa silifumana silamkela iLizwi likaThixo, singathi, njengokumkani uYosiya, senziwe ngokutsha sibakhokelele abanye kuThixo (bona u-2 Kumkani 22:10-13).

Namhlanje abantu bakholisa ukuya esiBhalweni ngokukhangela iziphoso zayo okanye bahlekise ngaso. Abanye bayibona iBhayibhile njengephume ecaleni kwizithethe namasiko eli xesha (ikholisa ukuba njalo, kwaye kufuneka kube njalo, nangoku). Abaninzi bayigculela kuba ichasana neendlela “zesayensi” okanye zenkcubeko.

EbuKristwini, iBhayibhile ikholisa ukusetyenziswa kuphela ukuxhasa indawo ethile ekufundeni ngoThixo kunokuba njengendlela yokukhuliswa komntu emoyeni nasekuhlaziyweni. Abanye bayichaza iBhayibhile ngendlela eyenza ivinjwe naliphi igunya embalini okanye indawo ephathekayo phezu kobomi bethu bemihla ngemihla. Enye yezona zilula kakhulu, mhlawumbi eziqhelekileyo, iindlela zokwala igunya leBhayibhile kukungayikhathaleli iBhayibhile singathathi nexesha siyifunda ukuze sisebenzise isiBhalo ebomini bethu.

`Lada nampembelelo enjani na iLizwi elithethiweyo likaThixo kwezi meko zilandelayo? Bona: Yer. 38:1-4, Yohane 3:1-10, neZenzo 16:25-34.`

Kumaxesha akudala, izinto zokubhala zazinqabile zinexabiso eliphezulu. Ngaphezu koko, abantu abaninzi babengakwazi ukufunda, ngoko ke, iLizwi likaThixo elalithethwa ngabaprofeti baKhe lalifuneka.

Phantsi kwamandla kaMoya Oyingcwele, iLizwi likaThixo, nokuba lithethiwe okanye libhaliwe, liyakwazi ukugqobhoza entliziyweni liveze isono lizise uhlaziyeko nokubuyiselwa. Ilizwi lisibizela enguqukweni nasekuthobeleni (IZenzo 5:29; bona nakuHeb. 4:12). Kwaye oko kukholisa ukuba sisizathu abantu abaninzi ekwakufuneka bazi ngcono babelilandula. Babengawafuni amabango alo ebomini babo.
