---
title:  "Ukuthethela Igunya LikaThixo"
date:   29/11/2026
---

Phambi kokunika umntu imvume yokuba akuthethele, ingaba akungeqiniseki na kuqala ukuba ukhethe umntu omthembileyo na? bekuya kubaluleka ukuba uyayazi indawo yakho aze ayibeke ngokunyanisekileyo.

`Funda Eksodus 2:11-15 noEksodus 3:1-3, 10-13. Ixesha likaMoses engumalusi lamlungisela njani ukuba abe ngumlomo kaThixo?`

UMoses wawachitha ama-40 eminyaka yokuqala yobomi bakhe ekuzuzeni eyona mfundo ilunge kakhulu eyayinokunikwa eYiputa, kodwa le Mfundo yayingakwazi ukumlungisela ukuba abe ngumthunywa kaThixo. Kwanyanzeleka ukuba achithe amanye ama-40 eminyaka entlango engumalusi, kungekho zinto ziphazamisayo zehlabathi. Ngeendlela ezininzi, kwakufuneka akuchithe okuninzi awayekufune njengenkosana. Phambi kokuba asebenzisane noThixo ekukhululeni uIsrayeli, uMoses kwafuneka amazi uThixo awayeza kuthetha endaweni yaKhe.

`Funda Eksodus 4:10-16. Yintoni esingayifunda kule ngxoxo phakathi kukaThixo noMoses ngendlela abaprofeti ababizwa ngayo nabasebenza ngayo?`

UMoses kwathiwa makadlale indawo kaThixo, aze uAron adlale indawo yokuba ngumlomo kaMoses (Eksodus 4:16; bona nakuEksodus 7:1). Njengoko wenzayo ngoMoses, uThixo uyabanceda abaprofeti baKhe ukuba bafumane eyona ndlela ilungileyo yokuhambisa isigidimi abanika sona.

UMoses wazi ukuba wayesilele ngaphambili. Wakhumbula ukuhlangana kwakhe kokugqibela namaIsrayeli akubo, xa igunya lakhe lathandatyuzwa (Eksodus 2:14). UThixo wakuqonda ukuthandabuza kukaMoses. Waluphatha ngomonde uloyiko lwakhe. Yayisusiwe yonke imihlaba yokuzithethelela, kodwa nangoko, uMoses walwala ubizo. Uloyiko lwakhe kwakuwelele ekungakholweni. Ngokuxhathisa kwakhe, wayethandabuza ukukwazi kukaThixo ukumsebenzisa. Kwaba ngelo xesha ke apho uThixo wamnika umyalelo ongenankcazo ukuze amenze asebenze umprofeti othandabuzayo (Eksodus 3:14-17).

`Usenokuba akunguye umprofeti, kodwa yintoni uThixo akubizele ukuba uyenzele Yena ngexesha lokuphila kwakho? Yeyiphi eyona ndlela ilungileyo yokuzilungisela olu bizo?`
