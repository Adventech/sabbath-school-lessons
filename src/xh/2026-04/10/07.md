---
title:  "Ukuthetha Ngegunya"
date:   04/12/2026
---

Abaprofeti bathetha kuthi ngegunya nangokufaneleyo kuba uThixo unyule bona njengabathunywa baKhe. Kukho abaprofeti abaneencwadi eziseBhayibhileni (abo abamibhalo yabo yaba yinxalenye yeBhayibhile), nabaprofeti abancwadi zabo zingekhoyo eBhayibhileni (abo bangazange babhale ncwadi equkwe eBhayibhileni), nabaprofeti bakwixesha lasemva kweBhayibhile (abo baprofeti bavele emva kwamaxesha eTestamente eNtsha). Bonke baphefumlelwa ngokulinganayo, nezigidimi zabo zithwele isitampu segunya likaThixo.

UEllen G. White ukweli qela lasemva kwexesha leBhayibhile, iqela elincwadi zalo ezingafakwanga eBhayibhileni. Kanti ke, amaSeventh-day Adventist awayibaleli imibhalo yakhe njengesongezo kwiBhayibhile, kwanokuba iyalingana nayo. Imibhalo yakhe, sekunjalo, ifanelekile kwaye ithetha ngegunya, nto leyo ayixelileyo yena ngokwakhe uEllen G. White: “ ‘Kwezi leta ndizibhalayo, kwizingqino endizithweleyo, ndininika oko iNkosi indinike kona. Andibhali nenqaku elinye ephepheni ndibeka nje ezam iingcamango. Zizinto athe uThixo wazivula phambi kwam embonweni—imitha enexabiso yokukhanya okubengezela kuphuma etroneni.’ ”—Selected Messages, book 1, p. 29.

Ngokuhambelana neBhayibhile, uEllen G. White walatha ubukho besono kunye nesidingo sethu sikaYesu, ekuphela kwethemba lobomi obungunaphakade, obunikwa ngobabalo kubo bonke abathi ngokholo babubange bube bobabo, omnye umxholo onamandla kwizigidimi zakhe.

Ngezigidimi zakhe (okanye masithi, ngezigidimi zikaThixo ngaye) silibona idabi phakathi kokulungileyo nokubi, kuqalela ekuweni kukaKhwezi ezulwini, kuhle kufikelele kwixesha lokuphela, xa isono nobubi, kunye noSathana neengelosi zakhe ezawayo, beya kucinywa ebusweni bomhlaba ngonaphakade. Imibhalo kaEllen G. White inika imbono kwimithetho-siseko yeBhayibhile kwimeko yexesha lokuphela. Uthetha, umzekelo, ngengozi yokuhlanganisa amabandla nobuzwe nokubaluleka kwenkululeko yenkolo; ulumkisa ngokonakala kokuziphatha; uphakamisa umthetho kaThixo nokubaluleka kwawo; kwaye nokuluqonda kwethu, umzekelo, u“phawu lwerhamncwa” ngokuqinisekileyo akusekwanga phezu kwemibhalo yakhe, amazwi akhe asinika iimbono ezimangalisayo ngeziganeko zokugqibela.

Ukwacinezela ukusetyenziswa okuphathekayo kokuphila ubuKristu. Izigidimi zakhe ngokwasemoyeni, impilo, imfundo, ulwalamano losapho, ubuzali, ubomi bokuziphatha, nangakumbi zincede abaninzi bayiguqula indlela yabo yokuphila baza banobomi obungcono kwimigangatho emininzi.

UThixo umsebenzisile ukukhokela amakholwa aphila kwimihla yokugqibela yembali yomhlaba. Imibhalo yakhe isathetha ngegunya lokuprofeta nangokufanelekileyo. Imibhalo kaEllen G. White ayikho eBhayibhileni, kodwa.
