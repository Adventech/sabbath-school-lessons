---
title:  "Igunya LikaYesu"
date:   30/11/2026
---

`Sasiyintoni isiseko segunya likaYesu? Lalibonakala ngantoni eli gunya? Bona: Mateyu. 21:23-27 noYohane 17:2.`

Ngexesha uYesu wayefundisa, ababingeleli beza kuYe bafuna ukwazi ukuba ngubani owamnika igunya lokwenza olu lungiselelo. Ngapha koko, uYesu wayengafundanga nakwesinye isikolo sasekuhlaleni, engazange waqeqeshwa kwizikolo zezithethe zoorabhi. Endaweni yokuyiphendula imibuzo yabo, uYesu wabolathisa indlela yokuphendula, ebabuza abo babembuza ngegunya elingaphazamiyo elaliboniswe nguYohane umBhaptizi. Ngokwala ukuphendula, baphikisa amandla kaThixo awayongamele ulungiselelo lukaYohane baza abamkela igunya likaYesu owayethunywe nguThixo, ngokufanayo noYohane.

`Zisixelela ntoni ezi ndima zilandelayo nathi ngegunya likaYesu? Mat. 7:28, 29; Marko 1:21-27; Luka 8:22-25; Luka 9:1; Yohane 5:25-27; noYohane 7:38.`

Lonke igunya lelikaYesu njengoMdali woluntu (Yohane 1:3) noMsindisi (Roma 3:24). UliLizwi likaThixo. Igunya likaThixo awayenalo uYesu ngexesha lobomi baKhe emhlabeni nolungiselelo lalingenako ukulinganiswa okanye lizenzekele nangubani. Imfundiso kaYesu yayibakhwankqisa abaphulaphuli kuba wayefundisa njengaLowo unegunya (Mat. 7:29).

Zonke iiVangeli zinike ingxelo yegunya lobuprofeti belinye ilizwe. UYesu wayexolela izono. (Marko 2:10, 11), ekhupha iidemoni (Marko 3:15) ekwazi noko bakucingayo abantu (Yohane 2:24, 25). Wayevusa abafileyo ekwanika ubomi obungunaphakade (Yohane 10:28). Ngexesha esemhlabeni njengoMhlawuleli wethu, uYesu wayesebenzisa igunya alinikwe nguThixo uYise (Yohane 17:2), kwaye wayexhomekeke kuYe ekwasebenzisana naYe uYise (Yohane 5:19) ngamaxesha onke, ekholisa ukubheka eziBhalweni, awayebanga ukuba zingqina ngaYe. UYesu wayekwanalo igunya elipheleleyo ekwazi nokulisebenzisa elo gunya kubafundi baKhe (Marko 6:7). NjengeLizwi likaThixo, ubungqina baKhe nabo beza kubaprofeti awathetha ngabo (IsiTy. 19:10).

`Sebukho bonke ubungqina awabunikayo uYesu, abanye abantu basamkhanyela. Singalumka njani ukuba singawi kumgibe omnye wokungakholwa, sezikho zonke izizathu esinazo zokuba sikholwe?`
