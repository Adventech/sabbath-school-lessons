---
title:  "Izigidimi Ezithethwayo"
date:   27/10/2026
---

`Funda 2 Petros 1:18-21. Yingqiniseko ni esinayo yokuba izigidimi eziziswa ngomlomo zivela kuThixo zaye ziphefumlelwe zithembekile njengezibhaliweyo?`

UThixo wayethetha kuAdam noEva phambi kokuWa (Gen. 2:16, 17) nasemva kwako (Gen. 3:9-19). Kwiimeko zombini, babewaqonda ngokupheleleyo amazwi aKhe. Izwi laKhe lalivakala ngezaci ezicacileyo naseSinayi (Eks. 20:1-20), nasekubhaptizweni nakwiNtaba yokwenziwa kumila kumbi kukaYesu (Mat. 3:17, Mat. 17:5). Sisenokungaqondi kakuhle xa abanye abantu bethetha nathi. Ngokuqinisekileyo kodwa, xa uThixo ethetha naye nabani, akuyi kubakho ukungavani okanye inkohliso.

IBhayibhile ibethelela ukuba “uThixo wathetha kuMoses, wathi kuye, ‘NdinguYehova’ ” (Ekso. 6:2). Ababhali abaninzi beBhayibhile bayakuvuma ukuva ilizwi likaThixo xa ethetha nabo. Kwakunjalo, umzekelo, nakuIsaya (Isa. 8:1, 11), nakuYeremiya (Yer. 1:4, 9, 11), nakuHezekile (Hez. 3:16, Hez. 6:1). Wonke umzamo wokukhanyela la mabango namanye afanayo kuphela kuthandabuza ukunyaniseka kwabaprofeti nokuba nokuxhomekeka kwiBhayibhile ngokwayo.

Njengoko uThixo wayethetha kubaprofeti ngolwimi lomntu oluvakalayo, benza ngokunjalo nabaprofeti kubantu ababephulaphule. Umzekelo, uNowa wayengumprofeti kaThixo no“mshumayeli wobulungisa” (2 Pet. 2:5). Akazange abhale nesuntswana leBhayibhile, kodwa wasihambisa isigidimi sikaThixo ngomlomo wabantu bexesha lakhe, kanti isigidimi sakhe sasingaphefumlelwanga ngokungaphantsi kwemibhalo yabaprofeti eseBhayibhileni.

Abanye abaninzi abaprofeti, bequka uNathan, uEliya, noElisha, noYohane, abazange babhale nentwana yeBhayibhile; endaweni yoko, basihambisa isigidimi sabo ngokusithetha.

Kuthiwani kanti ngoYesu ngokwaKhe? Ingxelo yeGospile ikhankanya ukuba ngexesha lolungiselelo lwasemhlabeni lwaKhe “wabhala emhlabeni ngomnwe waKhe” (Yohane 8:6). Kuphelele apho. Asinayo ingxelo yokubhala kukaYesu nantoni na eyenye. Yena ke wafundisa washumayela ethetha ngomlomo “njengonegunya” (Mat. 7:29). Abanye kwababemphulaphule bada bavuma bathi, “akuzanga kuthethe mntu njengoko athetha ngako lo mntu” (Yohane 7:40, 46). Okuphambili, uYesu wayengumntu ozithetha ngomlomo izigidimi zikaThixo. Izigididimi zaKhe zazingenagunya lingaphantsi okanye liqamangelayo nje kuba Yena ngokwaKhe engazange abhale nayiphi kwiincwadi zeBhayibhile.

`Indlela ethathekisa ngayo into yokuba uThixo enabaprofeti abangenayo imibhalo eBhayibhileni! Kungani ukuba aba baprofeti bangenancwadi eBhayibhileni nabo babe negunya elingaka?`
