---
title:  "UEllen G. White Ngentyilelo Nempefumlelo"
date:   30/10/2026
---

AmaSeventh-day Adventists akholelwa ukuba uEllen G. White wayephefumlelwe lizulu. Imibhalo yakhe, bubomi, nolungiselelo ziyahambelana nemimiselo yomprofeti oyinyaniso. Wayeneempawu ezinye zempembelelo kwakunye nabaprofeti beBhayibhile, nangona engazange wayibona imibhalo yakhe njengekunye nesiBhalo. Kwelinye icala, uEllen G. White wasoloko ephakamisa iBhayibhile. Yena ke phofu, wayeziqonda njani izityhilelo zesiprofeto kuye nakwinkqubo yempefumlelo yakhe? Okokuqala, uEllen G. White wahlala eyihlonipha iBhayibhile njengesityhilelo sikaThixo esinegunya, nesingenazo iziphoso. IBhayibhile ityhila inyaniso kaThixo necebo losindiso kuhlanga loluntu oluwileyo. Kuloo nkqubo, njengoko sifunda, usebenzisa abaprofeti ukudlulisa izigidimi zaKhe.

Okwesibini, uEllen G. White wayekwamkele ukuba inkqubo yempefumlelo yayiquka ukusebenzisana kukaThixo nomntu. UThixo wayedlulisa izigidimi zaKhe, aze athi ngoko umprofeti awuse umyalelo kaThixo esebenzisa awakhe amazwi. Ngokuyibeka kwakhe uEllen G. White: “Asingomazwi la aphefumlelwayo. Impefumlelo yona ayisebenzi emazwini omntu okanye kwizinto azithethayo kodwa emntwini ngokwakhe, othi, phantsi kwempembelelo kaMoya Oyingcwele, azaliswe ziingcinga. . . . Ingqondo nentando kaThixo zihlangana nengqondo nentando yomntu; ngaloo ndlela iintetho zomntu ziba lilizwi likaThixo”—Selected Messages, book 1, p. 21.

Ngokubhekisele kwimibono namaphupha obuprofeti, uEllen G. White wayekuqonda “ukuphefumlelwa kwengcinga” njengokuba lumanyano lwentyilelo kaThixo namava omntu. Waya wabhala wathi, “ ‘IBhayibhile, neenyaniso zayo ezinikwe nguThixo zabekwa ngolwimi lwabantu, inika umanyano lukaThixo nomntu’”—Selected Messages, book 1, p. 25. UThixo wasebenzisa iindlela ezininzi ukuveza ukuqondwa kwesigidimi saKhe engqondweni yomthunywa waKhe. Ezi ziquka ukunikwa komfanekiso, namazwi neengcinga ezithi zizisebenzise zonke izivo zomprofeti. Amava kaEllen G. White ayefana nalawo ababhali beBhayibhile.

Okwesithathu, uEllen G. White waphawula ukuba asingawo onke awakhe amava nokuthetha okwakuphefumlelwe. “Akhona amaxesha xa izinto eziqhelekileyo nje ekufuneka zixeliwe,” watsho, “iingcinga eziqhelekileyo ekufuneka zibe sengqondweni, neeleta ezikwanjalo ekufuneka zibhaliwe nolwazi lunikiwe olusuka komnye luye komnye kubasebenzi. Amazwi anjalo, nolwazi olunjalo, asizinto zinikwa phantsi kwempefumlelo eyodwa yoMoya kaThixo.”—Selected Messages, book 1, p. 39. Kubalulekile ukuwuqonda lo mahluko xa sifunda size sayamanise kwimibhalo yakhe namava akhe, ngokukodwa ukubhala kwakhe needayari zakhe.

Ukukwazi ukuqonda kukaEllen G. White nentyilelo nempefumlelo kusinceda ukuphepha ukubaxa nokuba sizifunde iingcebiso zakhe ngobulumko ngokuziqonda.
