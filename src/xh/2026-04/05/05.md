---
title:  "Izigidimi Ezibhaliweyo"
date:   28/10/2026
---

Ezinye iintetho ezithandwayo zinegalelo elinzulu ebomini babantu zada zaguqula nekhondo lembali. Ukuba ezi ntetho zazingabhalwanga, amazwi ngewaphela okanye atshintshwa ngokuhamba kwexesha. Ngoko ke, ukubhala kunika ukuhlala okude kwegama elithethiweyo, kwaye elo xesha lide lisivumela isigidimi ukuba sigqithiswe njalo ngokuhamba kwexesha.

Kufutshane nje emva kokuba uIsrayeli eshiye iYiputa aza oyisa ama-Amaleki, uThixo wamyalela uMoses wathi, “Kubhale oku kube sisikhumbuzo encwadini” (Ekso. 17:14). Ukuqalela kuMoses, owabhala iincwadi ezintlanu zokuqala zeBhayibhile neNdumiso yama-90, uMoya Oyingcwele wabakhokela abaprofeti ababhala iincwadi zeBhayibhile ukuba baveze imibhalo eya kuthi ngoku ibe zincwadi ezingama-66 zeBhayibhile.

`Funda Eksodus 17:14; Yohane 20:30, 31; 1 Korinte 10:11; nesiTyhilelo 1:19. Zeziphi ezinye izizathu ezenza ukuba uThixo acele abaprofeti ukuba bazibhale phantsi izigidimi awayebanike zona?`

Iincwadi ezahlukeneyo zeBhayibhile zibonisa uhlobo lokubhala nobuchule ngokwababhali bazo njengoko siza kubonisana kwisifundo seveki ezayo, ukwahlukana kwabantu neentlobo ezahlukeneyo zombhali ngamnye azisusinto kwizigidimi zabo, kuba akukho “manqanaba” empefumlelo. Ezinye iindawo zeBhayibhile, ngokucacileyo, zisondele kwaye ziyasebenziseka kubomi bethu nokholo lwethu namhlanje kunezinye iindawo. Sekunjalo, le nyaniso ayenzi enye indawo yeBhayibhile ibe iphefumlelwe ngaphezu kwenye indawo.

Ngamanye amaxesha, ababhali beBhayibhile babebhala izigidimi eziphefumlelweyo ababeqale ukuzinika ngomlomo, ngokwabo, okanye ngeqabane elithenjiweyo. Encwadini yeDuteronomi, uMoses wabhala iinteto zokugqibela awazenzayo kumaIsrayeli phambi kokufa kwakhe. IiVangeli ezine zibhale iintshumayelo ezininzi neemfundiso zikaYesu ngexesha lolungiselelo laKhe lwasemhlabeni.

Zikho kodwa iimeko apho kuthi kuzo abaprofeti baye bacaphule kwimithombo engaphefumlelwanga (IZenzo 17:28, 1 Kor. 15:33, Tito 1:12) kwanamazwi kaSathana (Gen. 3:1-5, Mat. 4:1-11).

Kufuneka sikhumbule ukuba njengoko abaprofeti babebhala iimvakalelo neminqweno yabanye abantu, babekwathetha nokwabo ukuziva bengabantu (bona: Dan. 8:27; IsiTy. 22:8, 9). Ukudlula apho, iBhayibhile ichaza imizekelo emininzi elungileyo emayilinganiswe kodwa nemininzi emibi ekufuneka iphetshwe (1 Kor. 10:1-11).

Lo gama sifumda iBhayibhile, kufuneka sigcine engqondweni ukuba, nangona ingabhalwanga ngolwimi olugqibeleleyo, ithwele isigidimi esingenasiphoso esiya kumelana novavanyo lwexesha.
