---
title:  "Inkqubo Yempefumlelo"
date:   26/10/2026
---

Iincwadi ezininzi nemibhalo, ingeyiyo iBhayibhile kuphela, zehla zisiza kuthi kwizizukulwana. Kanti ke, siyibona iBhayibhile iliLizwi likaThixo. Yintoni eyahlulayo kweminye imibhalo?

`Funda 2 Timoti 3:15-17 noYakobi 1:17. Sithini isigidimi esibalulekileyo esimele sisithabathe kwezi ndima?`

Kukho ingxoxo ekhoyo ngeyona nguqulelo ilunge kakhulu ka-Timoti 3:16, kodwa iinguqulelo ezininzi zeBhayibhile zisibeka esi sicatshulwa ngendlela efanayo, zicinezela ukuba iBhayibhile yonke inemvelaphi enguThixo (2 Pet. 1:20, 21).

Omnye umcacisi weBhayibhile uchaza athi “kubonakala ngathi kuyenzeka ukuba uPawulos ucinga ngesiBhalo sisonke apha nokuba unokutsho ukuba isiBhao Sisonke “sikwancedela ukufundisa, nokohlwaya nokululeka, kwanokuqeqesha okusebulungiseni,’ kunokuthi nje sonke isicatshulwa sesiBhalo siluncedo ngezi ndlela.”—G. W. Knight III, The Pastoral Epistles: A Commentary on the Greek Text (Grand Rapids, MI: Eerdmans, 1992), p. 445.

Phofu, sifanele ukuyiqonda njani inkqubo yempefumlelo eyaqala iBhayibhile? Kufuneka samkele ukuba impefumlelo yesiprofeto nguMoya Oyingcwele enceda abaprofeti benene ngoqhagamshelwano oluthembekileyo lwezigidimi zikaThixo. Olo ncedo alunikwa kuphela kwinkqubo yokufumana izigidimi kodwa nasekuzidluliseni ngokucacileyo izigidimi kwabo bathi bazamkele.

Izigidimi zikaThixo zeza ngemibono namaphupha (Num. 12:6; Isa. 6:1, 8). (1 Yohane 1:1-3), nophando lwembali oluzaliswe nguMoya (Luka 1:1-3), neembono ezisengqondweni (2 Pet. 1:21). Nokuba zinjani na iindlela ezisetyenziswe nguMoya Oyingcwele, abaprofeti nababhali beBhayibhile babenikwe izigidimi zenyaniso emazidluliswe ezijonge ngaphandle, kunokuba zijonge kubo. Oko kukuthi, izigidimi zikaThixo zichukunyiswe ngayo nayiphi indlela, okanye zijikwe ngabathunywa baKhe, nokuba babesebenzise awabo amazwi ukuzibeka ezo zigidimi.

Umsebenzi wokudlulisa izigidimi zesiprofeto ziye kubaphulaphuli bazo bokuqala wawunokwenziwa ngokuthetha (2 Pet. 1:19-21), okanye ngokuzibhala (Yohane 20:30, 31), okanye ngokuzigqithisa ngokuzidlala (Hez. 4:1-8).

`Nangona imininzi imibuzo eseleyo ngendlela iBhayibhile eyaphefumlelwa ngayo, okanye indlela impefumlelo esebenza ngayo jikelele, kutheni le nto kubaluleke kakhulu ukuba siyithembe njengeLizwi likaThixo, nangaphandle koko singakwaziyo ukukuqonda ngokuzeleyo ngendlela eyayibhalwe ngayo?`
