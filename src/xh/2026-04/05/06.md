---
title:  "Umdlalo, Imifuziselo, Imizekeliso"
date:   29/10/2026
---

Ibhayibhile ityebile yimifanekiso yentetho nemizekelo eyahlukeneyo. Umzekelo, lonke ucwangcwiso lweTestamente eNdala lwemibingelelo eyayisenziwa kwizibingelelo ngoomawokhulu, umnquba kaMoses, neTempile yaseYerusalem yayinikelwe ngokomfanekiselo wocamagushelo lukaKristu emnqamlezweni kunye nobubingeleli baKhe obukhulu ezulwini (Heb. 8:1-6).

Kwiziprofeto zikaDaniyeli, izikumkani namagunya ehlabathini ayemelwe ziindawo ezahlukeneyo zomfanekiselo omkhulu (Dan. 2:31-45), izilwanyana neempondo ezahlukeneyo (Daniyeli 7 no-8), nookumkani ababini (Daniyeli 11). Imifanekiso efanayo ikwakho nakwincwadi yesiTyhilelo, njengakwimeko yamarhamncwa amabini esahluko se-13.

Kwiimfundiso zaKhe, uYesu wathetha ngaYe, umzekelo, njengeSonka soBomi (Yohane 6:35, 41-58), ukuKhanya kwehlabathi (Yohane 8:12, Yohane 9:5), noMdiliya weNyaniso (Yohane 15:1-8). Ngomzekeliso womhlwayeli (Mat. 13:1-9, 18-23) neminye emininzi (Mat. 13:24-52, njl.), uYesu wabonisa ngendalo nokukhula kobukumkani baKhe.

`Funda Hezekile 4:1-8. Ucinga ukuba kutheni uThixo enganeli ukuthetha kodwa nokuzenza ziboniswe ngokudlalwa ezinye izigidimi zaKhe?`

Ukunikwa ngomdlalo okucacileyo kubonakala kuHezekile 4:1-8, apho uThixo athi kumprofeti makenze umzekeliso omncinane weYerusalem nokuba naye alale phantsi, kuqala ngecala lakhe lasekhohlo iintsuku ezima-390, emela indlu kaIsrayeli, aze emva koko alale ngelasekunene iintsuku ezingama-40, emela indlu kaYuda. Omnye umzekelo womboniso yayikukutshata kukaHoseya uGomera, ihenyukazi, okwayalelwa nguThixo okwakumele ukunganyaniseki kuThixo kukaIsrayeli ngokwasemoyeni (Hos. 1:2-9).

Amava angumdlalo kaHezekile noHoseya ubonakala usothusa kakhulu namhlanje. Yiloo nto kanye eyayifuneka: yayimele ukothusa, kungabi kuthi kuphela kodwa nakubantu belo xesha. Ngexesha xa izilumkiso zabaprofeti zazisaliwa ekuhleni (bona ku-2 IziKron. 36:15-21), isenzo sokugqibela senceba kaThixo saba kukubavusa abantu ngomdlalo ukuba babone imeko yabo eyinyaniso baze babuyele kwinguquko yenene. Ukunikwa komdlalo onjalo ngokuqinisekileyo kwababonisa indlela ekwakufuneka basamkele ngqongqo ngayo isilumkiso sikaThixo kubo.

`Luhlobo luni lomdlalo anokulenza umntu namhlanje ukusilumkisa, masithi, ngobuLawodike? (Bona IsiTy. 3:14-18.)`
