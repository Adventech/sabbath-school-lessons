---
title:  "IsiTyhilelo Sajikelele Nesisodwa"
date:   25/10/2026
---

`Funda Hebhere 1:1, 2. Kungani ukuba uThixo abe nomdla wokuthetha nathi? Usixelela ntoni ngoYesu nabaprofeti?`

Yintoni eyayinokuthethwa kuthi nguMdali wezwe lonke? Kutheni ethetha nathi? Kungokuba singabantu abawileyo kwihlabathi elizaliswe sisono kwaye sijongene nokufa, imbulaleko, nezihendo; uThixo, ke ngoko, uthetha nathi kuba ufuna ukuba sazi ukuba sikho ngabuni apha, kutheni izinto zimbi kangaka, nokuba kutheni, sekwenzeke konke, sinokuba nethemba kuthando lwaKhe kuthi.

IsiTyhilelo sikaThixo jikelele, njengoko sibonwa kwihlabathi lendalo, sithetha ngobuciko ngamandla aKhe angapheliyo nothando olunenkathalo. Ukumkani uDavide wadanduluka, “Izulu libalisa uzuko lukaThixo, saye isibhakabhaka sixela umsebenzi wezandla zaKhe” (Ndumiso 19:1). Umpostile uPawulos wongeza, “Kuba kwakasekudalweni kwehlabathi, iindawo zakhe ezibe zingenakubonwa zibonwa kakuhle, ziqondeke, ngezinto ezenziweyo, kwamandla aKhe angunaphakade, nobuThixo baKhe, ukuze bangabi nako ukuziphendulela” (Rom. 1:20, NASB).

Kukwakhwankqisa nokukhangela kumandla kaThixo akwazi ukubuyisela [ukuphilisa] ebonakalaliswa kwiinkqubo zokuphilisa ngokumangalisayo ehlabathini lethu. Ngokwenene, “Amandla kaThixo aphilisayo afumaneka kuyo yonke indalo. Ukuba umthi ugawulwe, ukuba umntu ulimele okanye waphuke ithambo, indalo iqala kwangoko ukulungisa ingozi. Nangaphambi kokuba kubekho isidingo, izenzeli eziphilisayo zihleli zilungile; kuthi ngako nje ukuba indawo ilimale, onke amandla asuka axakeke kumsebenzi wokubuyisela [wokuphilisa].”—Ellen G. White, Imfundo, iph. 113.

Lingaba lihle lifundise ihlabathi lwemvelo kangangoko, alaneli nje ukubonisa amandla nothando lukaThixo kodwa likwabonisa nobukho bobubi (bona: Gen. 3:14-19, Mat. 13:24-30). Esi sigidimi simbono-mbini singaqondwa kakuhle kuphela ngokukhanya kwentyilelo eyodwa efumaneka kuko kokubini – kuYesu Kristu, iLizwi likaThixo elaba yinyama, kunye neBhayibhile, iLizwi likaThixo elibhaliweyo elingqina ngaYe (Yohane 5:39).

IziBhalo ziyingxelo yezigidimi zikaThixo eziye zaphefumlelwa eziya kuluntu lonke kuwo onke amaphakade. Zisixelela ngokudalwa kwehlabathi nentlekele yokuwa kwalo, nangecebo likaThixo elimangalisayo lokuhlangula kwanokuba, ekugqibeleni, liyibuyisele iplaneti uMhlaba. Nangona zimfutshane zikwakhethe okuthile zinjalo ezinye iinkcazo, zikholisa ukuba luhlobo ekungaxhonyekekwa kulo ngenxa yokuba nguThixo ethetha ebhekisa kuthi. (Bona: 2 Tim. 3:16, 2 Pet. 1:19-21).

`Nangona indalo ngokuqinisekileyo isalatha kubunyaniso bamandla nothando loMdali, kutheni sisaliswele ngokungenalo ithemba nelizwi eliBhaliweyo? lisixelela ntoni iLizwi le indalo ingasixeleliyo yona kuyo nangokwayo?`
