### Fundela Isifundo sale Veki
Heb. 1:1, 2; 2 Tim. 3:15-17; 2 Pet. 1:18-21; Ekso. 17:14; IsiTy. 1:19; Hez. 4:1-8.

> <p>Indima Yenkumbulo</p>
> “IsiTyhilelo sikaYesu Kristu, awamnika sona uThixo, ukuba ababonise abakhonzi bakhe izinto ezimele ukubakho kamsinya; waqondisa wathumela ngaso isithunywa sakhe sasemazulwini kumkhonzi wakhe uYohane, owalingqinelayo ilizwi likaThixo nobungqina bukaYesu Kristu kwaneento zonke awazibonayo” (IsiTyhilelo 1:1, 2).

Umpostile uYohane wayeyinxenye yeqoqo eliphakathi labapostile abali-12 (Luka 8:51; Mat. 17:1; Mat. 26:36, 37). Emva kokunyuka kukaKristu, waqhubeka nolungiselelo lwakhe eYerusalem. Kanti ke, phambi kokuwa kwesixeko (a.d. 70), waya kuhlala e-Efese ukuze ongamele amabandla esithili saseRoma kwiAsiya Minor. Ngexesha lentshutshiso yamaKristu phantsi kokumkani wamaRoma uDomitan, umpostile wabhacela ePatimo, isiqithi esikulwandle eAegean.

EPatimo, uYohane wafumana izigidimi ezizodwa zoprofeto ezazibhekiswa, kungekuphela kumabandla asixhenxe aseAsiya Minor kodwa kumaKristu kuzo zonke izizukulwana (IsiTy. 1:1-3, 9-11). Ityathanga losasazo lwesigidimi sesiprofeto, awayeyinxenye yalo uYohane, lwalubonakala ngokungathi lunje: Lisuka kuThixo uYise, liye kuYesu Kristu, liye kwingelosi, liye kumpostile uYohane, lize kubafundi nabaphulaphuli. Lo ngumzekelo omhle wesiTyhilelo—inkqubo yempefumlelo athi kuyo uThixo asebenzise abaprofeti ukudlulisela isigidimi saKhe eluntwini.

Kule veki siza kuhlola ngokukodwa indlela exandileyo yesityhilelo—nenkqubo yempefumlelo, nokuqwalasela okukodwa kwindlela yokuthethwayo, nokubhalwayo, nokudlalwayo okanye okwenziwayo, esidluliselwe ngayo isigidimi sikaThixo kuluntu.

_Funda isifundo sale veki ulungiselela iSabatha kaOkthobha 31._
