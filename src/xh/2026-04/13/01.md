### Fundela Isifundo sale Veki
1 Kor. 2:2; Yohane 1:1-5; 2 Tim. 3:16, 17; 2 Tim. 4:1-5; Isaya 40:1-5; Yohane 17:20-23; 1 Kor. 1:10-13.

> <p>Indima Yenkumbulo</p>
> ““Bavuka kusasa ngengomso, baphuma baya entlango yeTekowa. Ekuphumeni kwabo wema uYehoshafati, wathi, Ndiphulaphuleni, nina maYuda, nani bemi baseYerusalem. Kholwani kuYehova uThixo wenu, nozimaseka; kholwani kubaprofeti bakhe, noba nempumelelo.” (2 IZikronike 20:20).

UThixo akasoze athi masikholwe nje ebumnyameni. Kwelinye icala, ubungqina bobukho baKhe bunamandla. Indalo le iyodwa, emva kwama-6,000 eminyaka ibhuqwa sisono, ingqina inyaniso ekungekuphela nje yokuba uThixo onamandla adalayo ekunzima ukuwakholelwa (khangela iifoto kuJames Webb Space Telescope), kodwa ukwanguThixo wothando.

Akuphelelanga apho, kukho okungaphezulu. Nasemva “kwezwi ke saliva thina lisiza liphuma ezulwini, sinaye entabeni leyo ingcwele” uPetros ebesabhala esithi “[si]nalo nelona lizwi liqinisekileyo elilelobuprofeti, enityaphayo ukulinyamekela, linjengesibane esikhanyisa endaweni emnyama, kude kuse, liphume ikhwezi ezintliziyweni zenu” (2 Pet. 1:18, 19). Sisenokungaliva, njengoPetros, ilizwi likaThixo, kodwa nathi sinalo “ilizwi eliqinisekileyo elilelobuprofeti.”

UThixo uthethe kuthi ngabaprofeti baKhe, ngoko kwamkelwe ngokufakwa kwimiBhalo yeBhayibhile nokungakhange kufakwe (kwimiBhalo yeBhayibhile). Sinikwe ubungqina obungaphaya kobuninzi. Umbuzo oseleyo uthi: Sikulungele na ukubamkela?

Imbali engcwele ibonisa ukuba abaninzi abazange bavuleke ekubamkeleni ubungqina obu. Akwaba, ngobabalo lukaThixo, asingenzi kwayona loo mpazamo inye.

_Funda: Isifundo sale veki ulungisela iSabatha kaDisemba 26._
