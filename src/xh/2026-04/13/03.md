---
title:  "Isiprofeto Siqinisekisa IBhayibhile"
date:   21/12/2026
---

IBhayibhile ekugqibeleni iyimveliso kaMoya Oyingcwele, ngesipho sesiprofeto. U-2 Petros 1:21 utyhila inkqubo eyayisetyenziswa nguThixo: “Kuba akukhanga kubekho siprofeto ngokuthanda komntu; bathi abantu abangcwele bakaThixo bahetha beqhutywa nguMoya Oyingcwele.”

`Funda 2 Timoti 3:16, 17. Sikubethelela njani kokubini esi sicatshulwa, igunya nempefumlelo yesiBhalo?`

Ngaphandle kweBhayibhile, uluntu beluya kushiywa ngaphandle kwendlela yokuphepha izigodi [okanye izigingqi] kwihlabathi elizele sisono, ebeziya kulwenza lube ngamaxhoba entshabalalo. Namhlanje iBhayibhile ifumaneka kuyo yonke indawo kwaye ifumaneka ngendlela okanye iimo ezininzi, kodwa kwakungasoloko kunjalo. Umzekelo, ngexesha likaYesu, kwakukho iTestamente eNdala kuphela eyayifundwa izincwadi ezisongwayo, ingababingeleli kuphela okanye oorabhi ababekwazi ukukwenza oko. Abantu nje babekholisa ukungasifumani isiBhalo. Ilizwi elivela kuThixo lalixabisekile ngaloo mihla.

`Funda Luka 24:27. Emva kokuba evukile ekufeni, yintoni awayisebenzisayo uYesu ukusinceda isibini esasiphelelwe lithemba ukuba siyiqonde injongo yaKhe eyodwa?`

UYesu, oyena mprofeti mkhulu owakha wakho—Lowo inguye yedwa uLizwi (Yohane 1:1-3)—waphakamisa isiBhalo (Yohane 7:38). Phakathi kwezona ntsikelelo zixabisekileyo zesipho sesiprofeto kukho esokuba siqinisekisa iBhayibhile.

“Abo bathe bathabatheka kukuba iziBhalo ezingcwele zililizwi likaThixo, baza balangazelela ukulandela iimfundiso zalo, kufuneka bafunde yonke imihla, bafumane yonke imihla ubushushu namandla asemoyeni, alungiselwe wonke okholelwa ngenene kwisipho sikaMoya Oyingcwele.”—Ellen G. White, in Signs of the Times, March 8, 1910. Isipho sesiprofeto senene sisoloko siphakamisa isiBhalo. Sisoloko sibakhombisa abantu [amadoda neentokazi] eLizwini likaThixo njengelikuphela komgaqo wokholo nokuphila [okanye nokwenza].

`Kwakutheni ukuze uYesu aqale abalathise kwiziBhalo phambi kokuba aziveze kubo ukuba ungubani Yena? Kumele ukusixelela ntoni thina oko ngendlela elimele ukuba sisiseko ngayo iLizwi kuthi?`
