---
title:  "Isiprofeto Siphakamisa UKristu"
date:   20/12/2026
---

Isiprofeto yindlela kaThixo yokuthetha nabantu baKhe ebahlanganisa naYe Thixo. Akumangalisi, ke ngoko, ukuba uSathana (inamba) waba nomsindo ngakumfazi (ibandla likaThixo) waya kulwa nabaseleyo bembewu yakhe (umfazi) (bona: Isity. 12:17).

Zithini iimpawu ezibonisa ukwahluka kwesi sisalela sembewu yomfazi? Bagcina imithetho benobungqina bukaYesu. IsiTyhilelo 19:10 sisixelela ukuba ubungqina “ngumoya wesiprofeto.”

`Funda 1 Korinte 2:2 nomaGalati 6:14. UPawulos uyibona injani injongo yobomi nolungiselelo lwakhe—impembelelo enkulu awayephilela yona?`

Ngaphandle kwawabo amava, abapostile bamamkela uYesu phezu kobungqina babaprofeti nabantu abangamalungisa, abamazwi abo anabela ngaphaya kweenkulungwane ezininzi. Ilizwe lamaKristu linetyathanga elizeleyo nelipheleleyo lobungqina obutyhutyha zombini, iTestamente eNdala neNtsha: kweNdala, eyalatha kuMsindisi oza kufika, kweyamva eNtsha, etyhila uYesu njengozalisekisa iimeko zoMsindisi. Konke oku kwanele ukumisa inkolo yabakulungeleyo ukukholwa. “UThixo wayeceba ukulushiya uhlanga nethuba elihle lokukholelwa emandleni kaThixo nawoNyana waKhe, nasemsebenzini kaMoya Oyingcwele.”—Ellen G. White, The Spirit of Prophecy, vol. 3, p. 182.

Ngeendlela ezininzi, umcimbi asingowobuchopho; unento yokwenza nokuziphatha ngokusesikweni. Yinto yentliziyo. Asiyiyo eyentloko. Xa abantu bezivala iintliziyo zabo, iingqondo zabo, ngelishwa, ziya kulandela.

`Funda Yohane 1:1-5. Ezi ndima zisixelela ukuba uYesu ungubani?`

Ukuba uYesu uLizwi likaThixo, wayekho kwangaphambili, wenziwa waba yinyama, wahlala phakathi kwethu, ngoko ke, kuyacaca ukuba uYesu wayenenxaxheba kumazwi okanye izigidimi ezanikwa nguThixo kubaprofeti baKhe. Kanti ke, nabaprofeti ababethwele izigidimi, nangona zazingabhekisi ngokuphandle kuYesu, noko kunjalo zaziphefumlelwe nguMoya Oyingcwele.

`Cinga ngokuba bungakanani ububanzi bendalo yonke, uze ucinge ngaLowo wayidalayo, uYesu, ukuba ikwanguYe owaya wasifela. Lithini ithemba elikhulu elinikwa koku?`
