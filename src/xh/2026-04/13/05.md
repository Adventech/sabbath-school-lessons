---
title:  "Isiprofeto Sithuthuzela Abantu BakaThixo"
date:   23/12/2026
---

`Funda Mateyu 11:28, 29. UYesu uthi masenze ntoni? Uthembisa ntoni kubo bonke abeza kuYe?`

UThixo uhleli enenkathalo ngentlalo entle yabantu. Phambi kokudala uAdam noEva, uThixo walungisa indawo egqibeleleyo yokuba bahlale kuyo eyayiya kubavuyisa ibonwabise (Genesis 1). UThixo wababekela yonke into yokubenza bonwabe. Emva kokuba sithe sawa esi sibini singcwele, uThixo weza ebafuna (Gen. 3:9), waprofeta [waxela kwangaphambili] ngoMsindisi ozayo (Gen. 3:15), wenza impahla ukugquma ubuze babo (Gen. 3:21). UThixo ugcine [okanye ufake] okuninzi kwaKhe ukuze izidalwa zaKhe zihlale kakuhle.

Kunokwenzeka ukuba ayikho intsikelelo yesipho soprofeto engakhathalelwayo njengomsebenzi waso sokuba lithende lentuthuzelo nokuhlaziywa kwabantu bakaThixo kulo lonke ixesha lobukho babo.

`Funda Isaya 40:1-5. Sithni isigidimi uThixo asinika umprofeti waKhe esiya kubantu baKhe abasentlungwini? Singawusebenzisa njani loo mthetho-siseko kuthi namhlanje?`

UThixo wayefuna uYuda azi ukuba nangona kwakufuneka emohlwayile, wayengasayi kubalahla kuphele. Akamniki nje isigidimi uIsaya isigidimi ukuba abakhuthaze abantu baKhe abophukileyo. Uyamyalela uIsaya indlela kanye amakasinike ngayo isigidimi. Kwakudingeka kuba isigidimi sinikwe ngobubele obukhulu, ngothando, nangenkathalo. UThixo wayenenkathalo kakhulu ngesi sigidimi sentuthuzelo ukuba sifikelele kwiintliziyo zabantu baKhe kangangokuba akazange ashiye nto emayizenzekele. Izigidimi ezinjalo zentuthuzelo zaziphinda-phindwa kuyo yonke incwadi kaIsaya (Isa. 12:1, Isa. 49:13, Isa. 51:3, njl.).

Umpostile uPawulos wabalumkisa abo baphila ukumhlonela uKristu Yesu ngokuthi baya kufumana intshutshiso enzima (2 Tim. 3:12). Kumaxesha anzima ngaphambili, siya kuwadinga amazwi entuthuzelo nethemba. Isipho sesiprofeto siyindlela uThixo aya kuyisebenzisa ukupholisa nokomeleza abantu baKhe phambi kokuba uYesu abuye ukubathabathela ekhaya.

`Kukweyiphi indawo ebomini bakho apho udinga ukuva ilizwi lentuthuzelo nethemba? UThixo usinika amandla okuba senze ntoni emva kokuba esithuthuzele? (2 Kor. 1:3, 4).`
