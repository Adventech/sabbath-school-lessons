title:  "Zama Kwakhona"
date:   25/12/2026
---

Kwiminyaka eliqela eyadlulayo iqumrhu lakwaKellogg lenza umboniso wosasazo wesiriyeli yesidlo sakusasa onomdla. Ekuzameni ukubuyisa abathengi abaya kwabanye abathengisa olunye udidi lwesiriyeli, iqumrhu labakhuthaza ukuba “bazingcamle kwakhona okokuqala ngqa.” Ingaba le ntetho ikwasebenza na kumaSeventh-day Adventist abaye bachitha ixesha elininzi befunda ngeBhayibhile nangoEllen G. White kodwa bechitha elincinane kakhulu ngoku“ngcamla” imibhalo yakhe? Ukubakho kwiintlanganiso ezithetha okanye ezifundisa ngoEllen G. White kukunika imbono okanye ingcamango ngobomi bakhe kwanolungiselelo lwakhe, kodwa ungakhange wena “ungcamle” ngokwakho, akusokuze ukwazi ukuyixabisa uyive ngenene intsikelelo yasemoyeni yezigidimi zakhe zobuprofeti bakhe. Kuphela abo bamfundayo, ngakumbi kwimeko awayejongene nayo, abanokukuxabisa ngokupheleleyo oko uThixo asinike kona.

Njengoko siphawule kuyo yonke le kota, uEllen G. White usibophelela kwinyaniso yeBhayibhile asihlanganise noThixo. Zikho iintsikelelo eziqinisekileyo ekufundeni izigidimi zobuprofeti bakhe.

Uphando olwenziwa nguRoger Dudley noDes Cummings, Jr., lubonisa iinyaniso ezinomtsalane. Abo bathi rhoqo befunda okubhalwe nguEllen G. White banolwalamano olunzulu nolusondele ngakumbi noYesu ngaphezu kwabo bangamfundiyo; banokuqiniseka okunamandla ngosindiso, namaxesha azingise ngaphezulu onqulo losapho, nolangazelelo lokungqina kwabanye. “akuxhaphakanga ukuba uphando lufumane okuphathekayo okunzima okuxhasa isigqibo esinye ekufikelelwe kuso,” babhala batsho aba baphandi babini. “Kuphando ngokukhula kwebandla, kwinkcukacha nganye ephethe ingqondo kungenjalo umoya okanye izenzo zomntu, ilungu elizifunda rhoqo iincwadi zikaEllen G. White likholisa ukuba kwinqanaba eliphezulwana kunelungu elizifunda ngamaxesha athile okanye ezingaze lizifunde. Ngokuqinisekileyo, okuthethwa koku kuxela ukuba ukufunda kokubhalwe nguEllen White kuyawenza umahluko omhle ebomini bomKristu nasekungqineni.”—Dudley and Cummings, “Who Reads Ellen White?” in Ministry, Okthobha 1982, p. 12.

Ekuhambeni kwale kota, sifunde ukuba uThixo uthetha ngabaprofeti baKhe. Izigidimi zabo zenza uqhagamshelwano noThixo zizise impembelelo engcwele neyanelisayo kuwo onke amacandelo obomi bomntu, ngakumbi ngokwayamene nesithebiso sosindiso olufunyanwa kuYesu: “Yivani, nibone ukuba ulungile uYehova” (Ndumiso 34:8). Zisibizela ekwenzeni okungcwele, ekwenziweni kutsha kobomi, kuvangelo lomntu ngokwakhe, kwakunye nobomi bokuba luncedo kwabanye. Oku kukusilungisa okufunekayo ukuze siyizalisekise indawo yethu ekuvakalisweni kokugqibela kwesigidimi sokugqibela njengoko sikhangele ekufikeni kukaYesu.
