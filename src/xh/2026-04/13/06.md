---
title:  "Isiprofeto Senza Umanyano Kumzimba KaKristu"
date:   24/12/2026
---

`Funda Yohane 17:20-23 no-1 Korinte 1:10-13. Sithini isimemo okanye isibheno esinguwona mxholo usembindini wezi ndawo zimbini esiBhalweni?`

Ngexesha uYesu wayesondela emnqamlezweni, intliziyo yaKhe yayichukumisekile lulangazelelo olunye. Wayefuna ukuba bonke abalandeli baKhe babe banye, njengoko Yena noYise bebanye eluthandweni, kwinjongo, nokwenza. Ubunye awayethandazela bona uYesu yayingebubo nje obobumnandi. UYesu wayethandaza isiprofeto esinamandla esiya kuzaliseka phambi kokuba abuye-umfanfekiso omhle obubutyebi obenziwe ngabantu abazalwa kwiindawo ezahlukeneyo, iintlanga ngeentlanga, nabaneembono ngeembono baya kumanyana kuYe nakuBawo, ngamandla kaMoya Oyingcwele. Umpostile uPawulos wakha phezu kwalo mxholo xa wayebongoza amakholwa aseKorinte ukuba abenengqondo enye, athethe inyaniso enye, angabi nazantlukwano phakathi kwawo (1 Kor. 1:10). Phofu ke, bubaluleke ngantoni obu bunye buprofetiweyo?

UYesu wathandaza “ ‘ukuze bonke babe banye njengokuba wena, Bawo, ukum, mna ndikuwe, ukuze nabo babe banye kuthi; ukuze ihlabathi likholwe ukuba wena wandithuma’ ” (Yohane 17:21).

UThixo unga bonke abantu, bangasindiswa, kodwa phambi kokuba basindiswe, kufuneka bakholwe kuYesu, bakholwe ukuba uThixo wamthuma ukuba asindise ihlabathi. Xa iqela elahluka-hlukeneyo labantu limbanga uYesu njengeNkosi noMsindisi, bethandana besebenzelana elowo nalowo bengajonge elowo okwakhe, ngoko ke, ihlabathi liya kukholwa ukuba uYesu unguYe lowo wayesithi unguYe. Ubunye uYesu awayebunqwenela babunenjongo bunamandla. Ngelishwa, kwasekuqaleni, iintshaba zazibulwa obo bunye. Bona, umzekelo, IZenzo 6:1. UbuKristu babahlukene kakhulu kwangoko!

Obu bunye ngumsebenzi kaMoya Oyingcwele ngezipho azinikayo. KumaEfese 4:11-15 sixelelwa ukuba izipho zikaMoya Oyingcwele zinikelwe ukuxhobisa amakholwa nokwakha umzimba kaKristu, “side thina sonke sifike kubo ubunye benkolo, nobokumazi okuzeleyo uNyana kaThixo, ebudodeni obupheleleyo, emlinganisweni wobukhulu bokuzala kukaKristu” (Efese 4:13). Isipho soprofeto, esinye esibaliweyo kwizipho zikaMoya, siyafuneka ekumanyaneni abantu bakaThixo, ngakumbi xa besondela ekubuyeni kukaYesu kwesibini, ukuze bakholwe kwiNkosi uYesu Kristu basindiswe (IZenzo 16:31).

`Ngexesha xa ibandla leSeventh-day Adventist mhlawumbi lakhiwe lilelona lahluka-hlukeneyo kumzimba wenkolo yawo onke amaProtestanti ehlabathini, singalubonisa njani uhlobo lobunye obunceda ukuba abantu bakholwe kuYesu? Ntshaba zini ezibuhlaselayo obo bunye?`
