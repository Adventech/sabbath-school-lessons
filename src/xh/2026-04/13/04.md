---
title:  "Isiprofeto Sisixhobo SikaThixo Sokwalatha Inyaniso"
date:   22/12/2026
---

Ezinye izifundiswa ziqikelela ukuba kukho ngaphezu kwama-45000 eenkonzo zobuKristu ehlabathini namhlanje. Kwenzeka njani ukuba inkolo eyaqala ngoYesu Kristu ibe ikhule okweenkowane ezininzi kangaka ezingamahlelo awahlukeneyo enkolo? Impendulo ilele ekuchazweni okuninzi okwahlukeneyo kwesiBhalo okukhule ekuhambeni kwexesha. Ngokucacileyo, azinako ukuba zisemxholweni zonke, kunoko, kwiimeko ezininzi into efundiswa lelinye ibandla ichasana nefundiswa lelinye.

Kususela ekuqaleni (bona kuGen. 2:16, 17; Gen. 3:1-5), isiphithiphithi sesinye zeendlela ezinkulu zikaSathana zokukhokelela abantu kude kwinyaniso kaThixo. Njengoko wenzayo ngoAdam noEva, uSathana uxuba nje intwana yenyaniso nemposiso ukuze abarhutye abantu [amadoda neentokazi] abaphantsi kulawulo lwaKhe uThixo. USathana ubalahlekise okoko ixesha lonke abantu bakaThixo (2 IziKron. 36:14-16). Wada wazama nokulahlekisa uYesu (Mat. 4:1-11), kodwa uYesu waqubisana nezihendo zikaSathana ngocacileyo u“ ‘Kubhaliwe’ ” (Mat. 4:4).

`Funda 2 Timoti 4:1-5. Cebiso lini elibalulekileyo uPawulos alinika uTimoti ngokubhekiselele kwiLizwi likaThixo? Kwakutheni ukuze aqiniseke kangaka ukuba uTimoti akulungele ngokwasemoyeni oko kwakuza kuza?`

Ngeliso lesiprofeto, uPawulos wabona ixesha xa abantu, amadoda neentokazi, bengayi kulithanda iLizwi likaThixo, bengafuni nokulithobela. Endaweyi yoko, babeya kuzifunela iititshala zasemoyeni zokonwabisa abakuthandayo ngeentsomi zobuqhetseba. Kwabo banjalo, uThixo uthumela ulahlekiso olunamandla, ukuze bakholelwe bubuxoki kanye ababufunayo. “Nangenxa yoko, uThixo uya kubathumela ulahlekiso olusebenza ngamandla, ukuze bakholwe bubuxoki, ukuze bagwetywe bonke abangakholwanga yiyo inyaniso, basuka bakholiswa kuko ukungalungisi” (2 Tes. 2:11, 12).

Bona abo babelungele ukwamkela, uYesu wathembisa ukuba babeya “kuyazi inyaniso” (Yohane 8:32). Isipho sokuprofeta yindlela uThixo ahlula ngayo inyaniso kwimposiso, ukukhanya kubumnyama.

Ngenye indlela, singayazi lula inyaniso ngokwaneleyo phakathi kwezi nkonzo zahlukeneyo.

Ukuba akuyi kuyiyeka iSabatha yomhla wesixhenxe (ekhankanywa ngokuqinisekileyo yiBhayibhile), ucime kakuhle kakhulu ama-99.5 eepercent onokukhetha kuwo, njengoko uninzi olukhulu lweenkonzo lungawugcini umthetho wesine. Ukuba akuyi kukholwa ukuba abafileyo baya kwangoko ezulwini ukuba babe noYesu okanye bayatsha ngoku esihogweni kwaye oko nako kuya kuba ngunaphakade,—yeyiphi enye into?

Ususe onke ama-99.5 eepersent kwasala i-0.5 persent.
