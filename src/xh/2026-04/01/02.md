---
title:  "Ukuqhagamshelana  E-Eden"
date:   27/09/2026
---

UGenesis 1 nowe-2 babalisa ibali leNdalo. UThixo Wadala umhlaba omhle, waza wabeka kuwo uAdam noEva, isenzo esiyincopho yokudala. Abantu bahluka kuso sonke esinye isilwanyana emhlabeni kuba benziwa “ngokomfanekiselo kaThixo” (Gen. 1:27), into eyathethwa kuphela ngabo, nengazange ithethwe ngeentaka okanye nezinye izilwanyana. Kangangokuba, babeza kuphila ngokupheleleyo ubomi obude, bathande ngazo zonke iindlela, bahambe benobudlelwane noThixo ngokwaKhe.

Sifunda nakwiGenesis 2:18-23 ukuba uThixo wamdalela uAdam uEva kuba uThixo wathi, “ ‘[akulungile] ukuba umntu abe yedwa’” (Gen. 2:18). Ukususela nje kwasekuqalekeni, abantu badalelwa ukuba basebenzisane njengabantu abakunye. Abafundi ngentlalo yabantu namhlanje bafundisa ukuba ukusebenzisana kwabantu kunika okuhle okuninzi kwimpilo yethu yomzimba nengqondo, lo gama ubulolo bungumbulali.

Akumangalisi ke ngoko, ukuba emva kokudalwa kukaEva, uAdam wadanduluka, “ ‘Eli, ke ngoku lithambo lasemathanjeni am, yinyama yasenyameni yam; lo yena ukubizwa kothiwa ngumfazi, ngokuba ethatyathwe endodeni’ ” (Gen. 2:23). Isimiso sosapho esamiselwa ngumntu oyindoda omnye noyintokazi enye behlanganiswe ngomtshato usisimiso sokuqala uThixo awasinika abantu ukuze kuphume kubo amava obudlelwane obupheleleyo kunye nokuthetha kunye. Akumangalisi ukuba omnye wemigomo ephambili kaSathana usoloko ikukutshabalalisa amandla asisiseko sosapho lwemveli.

`Funda Genesis 1:27, 28; Genesis 2:15-17; noGenesis 3:9. Luhlobo luni lothethwano phakathi kukaThixo nabantu eGadini yase-Eden?`

UAdam noEva babonwabele ukuthetha ubuso ngobuso noThixo. Njengezidalwa ezinobuchopho, babekwazi ukuhlangana noThixo nokuphulaphula imiyalelo yaKhe. Njengamagosa egadi kaThixo emangalisayo, bakhula ekwazini nakubuchule ngexesha bekhathalele iintyatyambo nezilwanyana eziphilileyo. Ngemini nganye babeba nexesha elilodwa kunye noThixo “empepheni yasemini” (Gen. 3:8). ISabatha ngokukodwa, inxenye efunekayo yomsebenzi wokudala, yayiza kuba lixesha elinikelwe ngendlela eyodwa ukuba libe lixesha lokuncokola phakathi kukaThixo nabantwana baKhe (Gen. 2:2, 3; Ekso. 20:8-11). Ngaloo mini abazali bethu bokuqala babe xhamla uthando lukaThixo nenkathalelo yabo ngendlela ekhethekileyo.

`Kunzima kuthi namhlanje, kumawaka eminyaka ukusuka e-Eden, ukuba nombono wokuba kwakunjani ngaphambi kwesono. Kanti ke, nangoku, lalatha njani ihlabathi lendawo emva ekudaleni kukaThixo kwasekuqaleni?`
