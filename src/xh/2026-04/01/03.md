---
title:  "Ukuzimela KuThixo"
date:   28/09/2026
---

Ngenxa yokuba uThixo eluthando (1 Yohane 4:8), akazange adale abantu “abangoomashini abazisebenzelayo.” Uthando lubuka ukukhetha. Ukuze bakwazi ukuthanda, uAdam noEva kwafuneka badalwe babe nentando ekhululekileyo; kungenjalo,uthando, uthando lwenene, lwalungayi kukwazi ukubakho ukuze balunike. Ngoko ke, uAdam noEva babekhululekile ukuba bamthobele okanye bangamthobeli uMdali wabo. Inyaniso yale nkulululeko yokukhetha yayibekiwe emthethweni wokutya kumthi wokwazi okulungileyo nokubi (bona: Gen. 2:15-17).

`Kungani ukuba balunyukiswe ukuba bangatyi kuwo xa babengenayo inkululeko yokwenza oko kwasekuqaleni?`

“Abazali bethu bokuqala, nangona babedalwe bemsulwa, bengcwele, babengenziwanga ukuba babekwimeko yokuba bangaze bayenze imposiso. UThixo wabenza bangabantu abanako ukuzikhethela, babenako ukuthanda ubulumko nobubele besimilo saKhe nobulungisa bemimiselo yaKhe, baze bathi, ngenkululeko epheleleyo, baziyekele okanye bazirhoxise ekuthobeleni. Babeza konwabela ukuxhumana naYe kunye nezithunywa ezingcwele. Ngaphandle kwenkululeko yokuzikhethela, ukuthobela kwakhe kwakungayi kuba kukuzithandela, kodwa kube kukunyanzelwa.”—Ellen G. White, Amanyange Nabaprofeti, amaph. 48, 49.

Kwakutheni ukuze uAdam noEva bazame ukuzimela kuMdali wabo emva kokuba bengamthobelanga? Bona: Genesis 3:1-8 (thelekisa noYer. 23:24 nomaHeb. 4:13).

UGenesis 3 uzoba umboniso ombi kakhulu—ukuwa kukaAdam noEva. Emva kwesiganeko esikhulu sencoko phakathi kwenyoka (uSathana) noEva, abazali bethu bokuqala bahendwa ukuba batye “kumthi wokwazi okulungileyo nokubi,” into ekwakuthiwe bangayenzi. USathana wasebenzisa amandla amazwi, nemvakalelo, kunye nomtsalane onokonwaba ukumoyisa uEva, waza yena woyisa uAdam, ukuba enze ngokuchasene nomyalelo kaThixo. UEva waphawula ukuba umthi “ulungele ukudliwa,” “mhle emehlweni,” nokuba “unqwenelekela ukuqiqisa” (Gen. 3:6). Isithembiso sikaSathana sokuba baya kuba “ ‘njengoThixo, nazi okulungileyo nokubi’ ” (Gen. 3:5) savakala singaphikiseki.

Inyaniso yeyokuba, incoko kaSathana yayizele yimposiso. USathana wazifihla kubo iziphumo zokungathobeli kwabo. Emva kokungamthobeli uThixo, amehlo abo “avuleka” kwangoko, baza babona ukuba “baze.” “Ingubo” kaThixo yokukhanya nobuqaqawuli (Ndumiso 104:1, 2), eyayibonakalalisa “umfanekiselo” kaThixo, yayisukile. Babengasagqunywanga koko uThixo wayebalungisele kona. Akumangalisi ukuba uAdam noEva bazifihla kuMdali wabo (ngokungathi bangakwazi ngokwenene ukuzifihla kuThixo). Bazama ukuzifihla ngenxa yokuba isono sisenza sizive sinobutyala, size sithi kubutyala bethu, sifune ukubaleka ebusweni bukaThixo.

`Athini awakho amava ngehlazo nokuzama ukuzifihla kuThixo emva kokuba wonile?`
