### Fundela Isifundo sale Veki
Gen. 1:27, 28; Gen. 3:1-8; Luka 15:11-32; Gen. 3:14-24; Ekso. 19:16-22; Yoh. 10:27-30.

> <p>Indima Yenkumbulo</p>
> “UThixo wayethethile nje kudala ngeendawo koobawo ngabo abaprofeti, uthethe ngale mihla yokugqibela kuthi ngoNyana; awammisayo wayindlalifa yeento zonke, awathi kwangaye wayenza indalo yonke” (Hebhere 1:1, 2).

Ubuchule boqhagamshelwano lwale mihla bubavumela abaninzi bethu ukuba babone abahlobo nezizalwana kwiindawo ezikude. Yinyaniso le. Phofu, kunjalo, kuhle kangakanani ukuhlangana nabo ubuso ngobuso! Kukho into eyodwa eyenzekayo xa sihlangana kunye, siwolana, sithethe ubuso ngobuso.

Kule veki siza kukhunjuzwa ukuba izidalwa ezingabantu zazidalelwe ubudlelwane bobuso ngobuso noMdali wazo. Sekunjalo, isono saluchitha kudala olo qhagamshelwano. Ukubuyisela olo thethwano nezidalwa zaKhe ezithandekayo. UThixo umise iindlela zokuthetha nathi. Eyona ndlela yaziwa kakhulu yokusebenzisana kwezulu noluntu zizithethi zabantu ezikhethwe ngobunono, ezaziwa ngokuba ngabaprofeti, abatyhila izigidimi zaKhe kuthi. Aba baprofeti bakholisa ukuba nemibhalo eBhayibhileni, kodwa akunjalo onke amaxesha.

Icebo lokugqibela likaThixo kukubuyisela umfanekiselo waKhe ngaphakathi kwethu, umfanekiselo owachithwa sisono. Ngumcimbi eliqulathe wona icebo losindiso: ukubuyiselwa, ukubuyiselwa okuya kugqitywa xa uThixo edala “amazulu amatsha nomhlaba omtsha apho kuhleli ubulungisa” (2 Pet. 3:13).

Koda kube ngoko, uThixo usathetha nathi, kwaye usebenzisa abaprofeti baKhe ukwenza oko.

_Funda isifundo sale veki ulungiselela iSabatha, kaOkthobha 3._
