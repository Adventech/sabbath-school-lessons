---
title:  "Izithethi ZikaThixo"
date:   01/10/2026
---

`Yintoni etyhilwa zezi ndima zilandelayo ngendlela uThixo athethe ngayo nol-untu emva kokuba kungene isono? (Funda: Heb. 1:1, 2; Roma 1:19, 20; Num. 12:6; 2 Tim. 3:16, 17; Yohane 14:7-9; Yohane 10:27-30).`

Sesikho njalo isono, uThixo wenza iindlela ezininzi zokuqhagamshelana nezidalwa ezingabantu eziwileyo. Enye indlela uThixo azityhila ngayo kuthi kungendalo. Indalo isisityhilelo esinamandla esenzo sakhe esimangalisayo. abesayensi bayamangaliswa yimimangaliso yeyunivesi nokuxaka“kobona bulula” ubomi. Kanti ke, indalo ikwabonisa iziphumo zokuwa, ngokufa kwayo yonke into ephilileyo kungowona mzekelo unamandla.

Enye indlela athe uThixo wathetha ngayo noluntu kungabaprofeti—abantu athe uThixo wabakhetha ukuba bathethe endaweni yaKhe. Umzekelo, uThixo wathetha kuNowa, kuAbraham, kuMoses, nakuDaniyeli, etyhila intando yaKhe ngabantu baKhe. Embindini wazo, izigidimi zesiprofeto kukho izikhumbuzi zokuba uThixo akabalibalanga abantwana baKhe kwaye usekunye nabo. Nangona abanye babaprofeti bakubhala oko kuze kuthi njengesiBhalo, abanye abazange babhale nencwadi enye yeBhayibhile.

Sekunjalo, amaninzi kumazwi abaprofeti adluliswe ngesiBhalo (2 Tim. 3:16, 17). IBhayibhile isisityhilelo sikaThixo ngokwaKhe. Kungamazwi aKhe esithi sifunde ukuba ungubani, silubone uthando lwaKhe olungatshitshiyo, sizuze inkokelo yaKhe ngendlela emasiphile ngayo.

Ngaphezu koko, kungeBhayibhile esithi siziqonde ukuba singoobani, apho sivela khona, nalapo siya khona. Apho sifumana injongo kaThixo ngobomi bethu.

Eyona Ndlela inempembelelo enkulu athe kuyo uThixo wathetha nathi nguKristu, owaya, phofu, watyhilwa kuthi eBhayibhileni. Njengoko uThixo wayefuna uAdam noEva eGadini yase-Eden, ngokunjalo uKristu weza “kufuna nokusindisa oko kulahlekileyo” (Luka 19:10). “ ‘Lowo undibonileyo mna, umbonile uBawo,’ ” watsho uYesu (Yohane 14:9). Ngobomi baKhe, ukufa, nokuvuka, uKristu wasizalisekisa isithembiso esanikwa uAdam noEva e-Eden. Weza wafa endaweni yethu ukuze sikwazi ukuba nobomi obungunaphakade ngaYe.

Emva kokunyukela kukaYesu ezulwini, uMoya Oyingcwele uye waqhubeka nokusikhokelela “ ‘kuyo yonke inyaniso’ ” kwanokuthetha kuthi “ngoko kusaza kuza’ ” (Yohane 16:13, NIV).

`Yintuthuzelo engakanani oyifumanayo ekwazini ukuba uYesu, uthando lwaKhe, nesimilo saKhe nokuzincama kwaKhe, kutyhila kuthi oko uThixo uBawo akuko ngokwenene?`
