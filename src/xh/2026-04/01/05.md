---
title:  "Ukugxothwa Emyezweni"
date:   30/09/2026
---

Esinye sezona ziphumo zonakalisayo zesono sikaAdam noEva saba kukwahlukana noThixo. Bagxothwa eGadini ababasakwazi ukuthetha noThixo ubuso ngobuso. Umprofeti uIsaya, ngokufanele ukukhunjulwa, wabhala ngale meko yoluntu enika umngeni, “Bubugwenxa benu obunahlukanisileyo noThixo wenu, zizono zenu ezibusithelisileyo ubuso bakhe kuni” Isa. 59:2).

Ngokwenene, uAdam noEva babesengabo abantwana bakaThixo. Sekunjalo, isiphumo sesono sasingenako ukuphetshwa, kwaza kwanyanzeleka ukuba bayishiye iEden. Izinto zazingayi kuba safana emva kokungathobeli kwabo.

`Isono saluguqula uluntu ngeendlela ezininzi (bona: Gen. 3:14-24). Phofu ke, isono sakuguqula njani ngokukodwa ukuqhagamshelana kwethu noThixo? (Bona nakuEksodus 19:16-22.)`

Isono sabuguqula ngokupheleleyo ubomi bomntu ngendlela esingenako ukuyicinga, noko ngenxa yokuba into esiyaziyo thina lihlabathi lesono. Ngenxa yesono, umhlaba “waqalekiswa” waza waba uza kuveza “ameva namakhakakhaka.” Abantu ngoku babeza kuba nentlungu, baze “babile” ukuze bazifumanele ukudla, baze bafe ekugqibeleni babuyele emhlabeni. Kuko konke okukhumbuza isono, ukufa kusele kukokona kunamandla kakhulu. Kwangelo xesha linye, ngenxa yesono, salahlekwa kukuqhagamshelana noThixo ubuso ngobuso.

Noko kunjalo uAdam noEva bathenjiswa ngesisombululo sokuqubisana nokudlavula kwesono. Nangona abazali bethu bokuqala babengasayi kumbona bathethe naye uThixo ngqo, bayishiya iEden benethemba langoko nexesha elizayo. UThixo wahamba phambili wabachazela icebo laKhe lokuhlangula.

UGenesis 3:15, obizwe ngokuba “sisigidimi sokuqala sethemba,” wayezele lithemba labo (kunye nathi), nangona sekwenzeke konke okunye. Isithembiso soMhlanguli ozayo saye siza kwenza kubekho ubutshaba ngakwisono nethemba losindiso oluzayo loluntu. Ngokucacileyo, umsindleko kaThixo wempahla ka-Adam noEva phambi kokuba bayishiye iGadi sisenzo esasingumfuziselo obonisa uhlangulo lwexesha elizayo. Ngokufa nokuvuka okuzayo kukaYesu, uqhagamshelwano noThixo lwaluza kubuyiselwa (Yohane 14:1-3).

Oko kukuthi, kanye e-Eden apho, kanye emva kokungena kwesono, uluntu lwanikwa ithemba nesithembiso sokuba ingxaki yesono, ekugqibeleni iya kusonjululwa. Ngako nje ukuba kubekho isono, kwabakho isithembiso soMsindisi.

`Waya waziva njani emva kokwenza into engalunganga? Unetyala? Uhlazekile? Ungazithandi? Zaba yintoni iziphumo? Ukholo lwakho kuThixo, nesithembiso seendaba ezilungileyo, zakugcina njani ukuba ungancami ululahle ngokupheleleyo ukholo?`
