title:  "Ellen G. White"
date:   02/10/2026
---

Uqhagamshelwano noThixo aluphelelanga kumaxesha eBhayibhile. Njengoko siya kubona kwiiveki ezizayo, ukuthetha kukaThixo kubantu ngonaphakade, namazwi aKhe aya kubonakalaliswa kwiindlela zangoku ade abuye uKristu.

UEllen Gould Harmon White newele lakhe bazalwa ngoNovemba 26, 1827, eGorham, Maine. Babengabona bancinane kubantwana abasibhozo bakaEunice noRobert Harmon. Kwiminyaka embalwa, usapho lwaya kuhlala kwisixeko sasePortland, Maine, apho uRobert Harmon wayenethemba lokwandisa ishishini lakhe lokwenza iminqwazi. Eneminyaka elithoba, uEllen walinyazwa, nto leyo eyachukumisa impilo yakhe yomzimba, imvakalelo, neyokuhlala nabanye. Uthe esepakeni ekufuphi nekhaya lakhe, owayefunda naye wathi esemsindweni, wamgibisela ngelitye wamchana ebusweni, okwamshiya ebudenga iiveki eziliqela. Le ngozi yakuphelisa kwangoko ukufunda kwasesikolweni yamshiya uEllen enempilo ehesheheshe ixesha elide lobomi bakhe. Ukulimala kwasebusweni nako kwakumshiye uEllen ekhathazekile ngenxa yembonakalo yakhe, yakucutha ukusoloko ephakathi kwabanye.

Kwaye kukule meko yokungaqiniseki apho uEllen Harmon oselula waqala ukucinga ngesiphelo sakhe sasemoyeni. Wayemoyika uThixo eziva engalifanele izulu. Imvakalelo yoloyiko enjalo yaqina ngakumbi xa usapho lwakwaHarmon lwamkela imfundiso kaMiller yokubuya okukhawulezileyo kwesibini kukaYesu. Nasemva kokubhaptizwa kwakhe ngokuntywiliselwa kwinkonzo yeMethodist [yaseWesile] ngowe-1842, uEllen akazange azive elufanele uthando lukaThixo. Wayekholelwa ukuba “ulahleke ngonaphakade” (Life Sketches of Ellen G. White, p. 29; also see pp. 20-31).

Phakathi kwezi ngcamango zibuhlungu, uEllen osemtsha waba namaphupha amabini, awaqala ukuyiguqula ingqondo yakhe ngoThixo. Waya wahlangana noLevi Stockman, umshumayeli wamaMethodist, owenza aluqonde nokuba aluve uthando nobabalo lukaThixo. Kwakungathi uThixo uthethe kuye ngokungabi nathemba. “Ukholo ngoku lwayithimba intliziyo yam,” wabhala watsho kamva. “Ndeva uthando olungathethekiyo ngakuThixo ndanobungqina boMoya waKhe ukuba izono zam zazixolelwe. Iimbono zam ngoBawo zaguquka. Ngoku ndimkhangele njengomzali onobubele nothando, ongenabo ubungqwabalala onyanzela abantu ukuba bathobele nakanjani. Intliziyo yam yaphumela kuYe ngothando olunzulu nolukhulu.”—Life Sketches of Ellen G. White, p. 39. Ngelo xesha linye, uEllen White waya waqonda imfundiso “yemeko yabafileyo” waya wakhula ekubambeni ingcamango yokuba akukho sihogo sivutha umphelo. Ukumqonda kwakhe okutsha uThixo nothando lwaKhe kwaguqula umoya okanye ingqondo yakhe ngokupheleleyo. Njengoko siya kufunda ngooLwesihlanu ngabanye kwiiveki ezizayo, uEllen White wamthanda kakhulu uYesu wamkhonza ubomi bakhe bonke obuseleyo.
