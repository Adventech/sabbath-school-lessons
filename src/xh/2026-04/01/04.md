---
title:  "UThixo Ufuna Uluntu"
date:   29/09/2026
---

Siyabona kuGenesis 3:9, 10 ukuba uThixo wahamba phambili ukufuna abantwana baKhe abawileyo. Umbuzo owawusithi “Uphi” wawuziphendula, kuba uThixo wayeyazi into abayenziwe nguAdam noEva kwanokuba babephi. Isono sona saluqhawula uqhagamshelwano olwalukho phakathi kukaThixo nesibini sabantu sokuqala. Eyona nto yayiphambili kuThixo kwangoko yayikukubuyisela uqhagamshelwano nabantwana baKhe, ukuba ahlangane nabo, nokuba abanike ithemba ekuphelelweni kwabo lilo. Isenzi sesiHebhere ugara’, “ukubiza,” kuGenesis 3:9, sithetha ukuthetha nabantu baKhe.

`Funda Luka 15:11-32. Lo mzekeliso ukubonisa njani oko kwenzekayo kuGenesis 3:9, 10?`

Ukufuna kukaThixo uAdam noEva kutyhila uthando lwaKhe olungaxhomekekanga ntweni kubantwana baKhe (bona: Luka 15). Uhamba indawo ende ukumisela ngokutsha uqhagamshelwano nathi, nokuba singade sizimele. Phambi kokubethelelwa kukaYesu, xa uPetros wamkhanyela kathathu uYesu, uKristu “wajika wamondela” (Luka 22:61). Engathethanga, “[wa]thetha” kuPetros wazama ukufikelela kumhlobo waKhe owonayo.

Qaphela, kuGenesis 3:11-13, indlela isono esakhawuleza sabuguqula ngayo ubudlelwane kuAdam noEva. Endaweni yokuvuma elowo isono sakhe, baqala ukutyholana. UAdam watyhola uEva, waba, ngandlel’ ithile utyhola uThixo, kuba wamnika uEva. “ ‘Umfazi owandinikayo ukuba abe nam, nguye ondinikileyo kuwo umthi, ndadla ke,’ ” watsho (Gen. 3:12). UEva, ngelo xesha, watyhola inyoka ngokungathobeli kwakhe. “ ‘Inyoka indilukuhlile, ndadla ke’ ” (Gen. 3:13), wenza njalo ukuzithethelela. Akukho noyedwa kubo owathi xa bebuzwa okokuqala ngesono sabo, walivuma elakhe ityala. Kuncinci kangakanani okuye kwaguquka kumawaka amathandathu eminyaka!

Inyaniso yeyokuba isono saluchukumisa lonke uluntu. Umprofeti uIsaya uyichaza imeko yethu kakuhle kwabaseRoma 7:15-24, xa enika inkcukacha yentlungu yokunqwenela ukwenza okulungileyo, asuke azifumane esenza okuchaseneyo: “Kuba oko ndikwenzayo andikwazi mna; kuba endikuthandayo, oko andikwenzi; endikuthiyayo, oko ndiyakwenza” (Roma 7:15).

Ngubani ongeke azayamanise?

Oko kuthethwa nguPawulos kuxakile. Sonke singabantu abophukileyo behlala besenza izinto esaziyo ukuba asifanele sizenze. Ngubani ongenawo la mava?

Ithemba lethu ekuphela kwalo, ngokutsho kukaPawulos, kukuhlangulwa “ngoYesu Kristu iNkosi yethu” (Roma 7:25). Yiyo le nto uThixo ehleli esifuna ukuze asise kuYesu, enguye kuphela onokusibumba sibe ngumfanekiselo waKhe aze asisindise kwintshabalalo eyenzekayo.
