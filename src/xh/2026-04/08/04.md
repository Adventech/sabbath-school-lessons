---
title:  "Imeko Ekubhalwe Phantsi Kwayo"
date:   17/11/2026
---

Ibalana elibuncoko elithandwa kakhulu ngabashumayeli lithetha ngendoda eyayijongene nengxaki yaza yafuna ukwazi intando kaThixo ngayo. Yathi nqi iBhayibhile kanti ityhile kuMateyu 27:5, ofundeka ngolu hlobo, “wemka [uYudas] waya wazixhoma.” Yothuswa yile ndima, yaphinda yathi nqi, izama ukufumana isigidimi sokuyiphakamisa, suka umnwe wakhomba kuLuka 10:37: “Wathi ngoko uYesu kuye, Hamba, uye kwenjenjalo nawe.” Zombini ezi zindima eziyinyaniso zesiBhalo, kodwa zidinga ukuqondwa ngokwemeko ezibhalwe kuyo.

Ngokwenene, ukugqwethwa okuninzi kwezigidimi zeBhayibhile zivela ekuzisuseni izicatshulwa kwiimeko ezibhalwe phantsi kwazo. Le ndlela izivulela iziBhalo ekubeni zicaciswe ngazo zonke iindlela ezingezizo.

`Funda Genesis 3:1-5 noMateyu 4:1-11. Wawagqwetha njani uSathana amazwi kaThixo ngokuwasebenzisa ngaphandle kwemeko awayenikwe kuyo?`

Abanye abantu basigqwetha benyanisile isigidimi seBhayibhile ngenxa yokuswela kwabo ulwazi. Abanye bona bakwenza oko ngenjongo, njengoko uSathana wenza kwiziganeko ezibini ezikhankanywe kwiziBhalo ezingentla. Nokuba injongo ithini, isiphumo sisoloko siyingozi.

Emyezweni wase-Eden (Gen. 3:1-5; thelekisa noIsiTy. 12:9), uSathana wabonakala kuEva njengenyoka entle kakhulu enomtsalane nefuna ukwazi nekwaziyo ukuthetha ulwimi lomntu. Ekruna amazwi kaThixo kwimeko yawo yokuthethwa kwawo yemveli, uSathana wawathetha ngokungathi uthetha nje ngento engabalulekanga kangako waza emva koko wanika eyakhe ingcaciso ekhohlisayo.

Xa uYesu wayesiwe nguMoya Oyingcwele entlango, (Mat. 4:1-11, Luka 4:1-13), “uSathana weza njengengelosi yokukhanya entlango ukuba amkhohlise uKristu.”—Ellen G. White, The Advent Review and Sabbath Herald, Disemba 18, 1888. Kwintetho yakhe noKristu, uSathana wacaphula gwenxa iNdumiso 91:11, 12 ukuze inike into ethi, uThixo uya kukhusela nabo bazibeka esichengeni ngabom kwiimeko eziyingozi.

Amava aseGadini yase-Eden nawasentlango okuhendwa aqinisekisa ubukho beMbambano enkulu, equka ukucaciswa kweLizwi likaThixo, elithethiweyo nelibhaliweyo. Kufuneka asincede sikuqonde ukubaluleka kokugcina ukuziqonda kwethu izicatshulwa zeBhayibhile kuthembekile kwiimeko zokubhalwa kwazo.

`Inyaniso ayiyidingi inkohliso ukuba ide ityhilwe. Kungani ke ngoko, kufuneka sikuphephe ukuba isihendo side sizisuse iindima kwimeko yokubhalwa kwazo ukuzama ukubonisa izimvo zethu, nokuba sezilunge kangakanani ezo zimvo zethu?`
