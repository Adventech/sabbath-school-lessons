---
title:  "Intsingiselo Yendima"
date:   18/11/2026
---

Onke amaKristu kufuneka aziqondele ngokwawo iBhayibhile. IBhayibhile icace ngokwaneleyo ukuba wonke umntu aziqondele usindiso, ukanti inzima ngokwaneleyo ukuba ithi rhoqo inika umngeni kwezona ngqondo ziphaphame kakhulu. IsiBhalo siyindyebo engapheliyo yokugcina ulwazi olungasokuze lugqitywe ngokupheleleyo.

`Funda Luka 10:25-28. Abanye abantu babanga ukuba iBhayibhile ayikudingi ukucaciswa. Ukuba oku kunjalo, kungani ukuba uYesu abuze lo mqondisi-mthetho, “ ‘Emthethweni kubhalwe ntoni na? Kanti uwucacisa njani?’ ” (Luka 10:26, Message)?`

Ukuze siyifumane into ethethwa yindima, kufuneka kuqala siqonde amagama asetyenzisiweyo, emva koko ibe yindlela athe asetyenziswa ngayo kwimeko ehlangene nokubhalwa kwawo kunye nokubhalwe kuyo yonke iBhayibhile, kwakunye nemeko yembali nenkcubeko, kangangoko kunokwenzeka. Ekugqibeleni, izigqibo zethu kufuneka zihambelane neemfundiso ezibanzi zesiBhalo. Ukuthelekisa iziBhalo ezihamba kunye –ngokungathi kwenziwe kwiiVangeli zone—kungakutyebisa ukuqonda.

`Thelekisa uMateyu 5:43-48 noLuka 6:27-36. Imeko abhalwe phantsi kwayo uMateyu 5:43-48 kunye nendima ahamba kunye nayo kaLuka 6:36, zisinceda njani ukuba siyiqonde intsingiselo ka“fezeka” kuMateyu 5:48?`

Olunye lwezona mpawu zibaluleke kakhulu zokucacisa isiBhalo ngokunyanisekileyo ngumdibaniso wokwenza oko ngokugqibeleleyo nokuthobeka. Khumbula, akukho noyedwa kuthi onenyaniso; sonke sibona manakanibe ngegilasi.

Kufuneka sisoloko sizama ukufikelela kwimfundiso ephilileyo (2 Tim. 4:1-5). Umthetho-siseko obalulekileyo wokulandelwa, kukuqwalasela koko sikuqondayo, kokucacileyo, nokwakha phezu koko, okuthi kwiimeko ezininzi, ngokuhamba kwexesha kusincede ukuba siziqonde izinto ezingacacanga ngoku. Engekafiki kunaphakade, kuyakuhlala kukho imbizo engaphendulwanga.

`Cinga ngexesha owawunento ongayiqondiyo eyayikuhlupha kakhulu, neyada yacaciswa kamva. Kungakunceda njani oko ukuba umthembe uThixo nakwezinye izinto eziseLizwini laKhe, ongekaziqondi okwangoku?`
