---
title:  "Ukufunda Okubhalwe NguEllen G. White"
date:   20/11/2026
---

Simfunda njani uEllen G. White? Nazi izikhokelo zokumqonda ngokukuko nokukusebenzisa akubhalileyo.

Okokuqala, qala ngomoya onomthandazo, olungileyo nengqondo evulekileyo, kunomoya wentandabuzo. Imo yengqondo yethu iyigilasi enika umbala koko sikuvayo, nesikufundayo, kunye nesikubonayo. Siyamdinga uMoya Oyingcwele ukuba ayikhokele indlela esicinga ngayo. Njengoko uEllen G. White yena ngokwakhe waphawula: “Ukuba niziphengulula iziBhalo nijonge ukuxhasa iimbono zenu, anisokuze nifikelele enyanisweni. Maziphengululwe ukuze kufundwa oko iNkosi ikuthethayo.”—Iimfundiso ZikaKristu Ngemizekeliso, iph. 112. Kunjalo oko ngokubhekiselele kwimibhalo yakhe.

Okwesibini, yiqaphele indawo oyifundayo ukuba ibiluhlobo luni okanye iphethe umcimbi othini. Abantu baya kholisa ukubeka ukukuqonda kwabo okubhalwe nguEllen G. White kwintetho elilolo okanye isiqwenga sesicatshulwa esikude kwintsusa yembali okanye kokuqondwe ngumbhalo. UEllen G. White wabhala esithi, “ixesha nendawo kufuneka ziqatshelwe.”—Selected Messages, book 1, p. 57. Esi senzo singesiso sokungakhathaleli indawo ekucatshulwa kuyo sibangele, kwaye sisaqhubeka ukuba ngunobangela, weenkathazo ezingapheliyo kwamanye amalungu ebandla.

Okwesithathu, bhekisisa kwimithetho-siseko. Ithini ingongoma eyona ngongoma eyintloko (okanye iingongoma eziyintloko) azicinezelayo uEllen G. White kwisicatshulwa esithile? Umzekelo, ngowe-1894 uEllen White wabhalela amalungu ebandla aseBattle Creek, Michigan, egxeka ukuthengwa kweebhayisikili (bona: Testimonies for the Church, vol. 8, pp. 50-53). Umthetho-siseko awayezama ukuwukhuthaza uEllen White bubugosa obububo bezinto [okanye ubutyebi] obuphiwe nguThixo, njengemali (ngelo xesha ibhayisikili yayixabisa kakhulu).

Okwesine, mayifundisiswe yonke into uEllen G. White awayibhalayo ngomxholo othile. Mazihlolwe iintetho ezifihlakeleyo kwimeko yokubhalwa kwazo ekufundiseni jikelele ngomxholo. Ngexesha lokuphila kwakhe uEllen G. White wakhalela ukusetyenziswa gwenxa kwemibhalo yakhe: “okuninzi okunika ingqondo yokuba sisigidimi sikaDade White, kwakusebenza injongo yokumbeka ngendlela engeyiyo uDade White, kumenza ukuba angqine ngokuhambelana nezinto zona ezingahambelani nengqondo okanye nembono yakhe.”—Ellen G. White, Selected Messages, book 1, p. 44.

Ekugqibeleni, makuqinisekiswe ukuba uEllen G. White ukuthethile oko. Kukho izicatshulwa eziliqela ekuthiwa zezikaEllen G. White kuxokwa. Eyona ndlela ekhuseleke kakhulu yokuqiniseka ukuba oko sikufundayo kuvela kuEllen White ikukukhangela isalathiso sendawo ekucatshulwe kuyo. Ngako nje ukuba siqinisekise apho sifunyanwa khona, singakwazi ukusixilonga sisebenzisa imithetho-siseko efanelekileyo.

Sinikwe isipho esimangalisayo kwimibhalo yobuprofeti kaEllen G. White. Kubaluleke kakhulu ukuba siyifunde ngendlela eyiyo loo mibhalo, enokuba, neyiyo, intsikelelo kwizigidi. Kuyingozi xa isetyenziswa gwenxa kwanokuba isetyenziswe ngendlela engeyiyo.
