---
title:  "Ukuchazwa KweBhayibhile"
date:   19/11/2026
---

Ukuyifundisisa kwethu iBhayibhile kufuneka kuqwalasele kwiinjongo ezimbini: ukukhula kwasemoyeni nokukhokelela abanye kubudlelwane obusindisayo noYesu. UKristu wamcacisa umKristu wenene njengalowo ukhokelelwa nguMoya Oyingcwele “kuyo yonke inyaniso” (Yohane 16:13), nophila “ ‘ngamazwi onke aphuma emlonyeni kaThixo’ ” (Mat. 4:4), nofundisa abanye “zonke izinto” asiyalele ngazo (Mat. 28:20).

`Funda Luka 4:16-30 noLuka 24:25-27, 44-49. Yintoni esinokuyifunda kwindlela uKristu awayezichaza ngayo iziBhalo apha?`

UYesu Kristu wayeyiyo, ekwayiyo, eya kuhlala eyiyo “indlela, inyaniso nobomi” (Yohane 14:6). Noko kunjalo, ukushumyela kwaKhe kwakuzinze ngamandla eziBhalweni. Umzekelo, uMarko 1:14, 15 uthi, “Ke kaloku emveni kokufakwa kukaYohane entolongweni, weza uYesu kwelaseGalili, evakalisa iindaba ezilungileyo zobukumkani bukaThixo, esithi, Lizalisekile ixesha, nobukumkani bukaThixo busondele’ ”. Ixesha lalisalatha ngokucacileyo kuDaniyeli 9:24-27. KwiNtshumayelo yaseNtabeni (Mateyu 5-7), wacaphula kuEksodus 20:13, 14 (Mat. 5:21, 27).

KuLuka 4, wacaphula kuIsaya 61:1, 2. UYesu wayengumshumayeli weBhayibhile oqinisekileyo.

Komnye umzekelo, uIZenzo 8 usixelela ukuba ithenwa lomTiyopiya “lalize kunqula eYerusalem” (IZenzo 8:27), into ethetha ukuba laliqhelene nenkolo yobuYuda, mhlawumbi ezinye zeenkonzo zayo. Lalide lanekopi yencwadi kaIsaya, elaliye layifunda ngokunqwenelekayo kuhambo olugodukayo. Kanti ke lalingasiqondi kakuhle isiprofeto soMkhonzi othwele isono, ngoko ke lalifuna umntu onokulicacisela. La mava asifundisa izifundo ezimbalwa ezibalulekileyo. Esokuqala, ukusifunda nje isicatshulwa seBhayibhile akusoloko kukhokelela ekuqondeni okunzulu kwaso. Okwesibini, ekufundisiseni isiBhalo singakwazi, kwaye kufuneka kunjalo, ukufunda komnye nomnye. Okwesithathu, sinoxanduva lokuba thina sabelane ngezicatshulwa ezivakalayo zeBhayibhile nabanye.

Ukuyichaza kukaFilipu incwadi kaIsaya nezinye iindawo zeTestamente eNdala kwithenwa kwalikhokelela ekubhaptizweni (IZenzo 8:35-39; thelekisa neNdumiso 51:12). Ukusicacisa kwethu isiBhalo kufuneka kube nenjongo enye (bona uYohane 17:17).

`Nangona ngamanye amaxesha siludinga uncedo ekuziqondeni iindima, kungani ukuba sihlale soyisakele ezingqondweni zethu ngoko sikukholelwayo?`
