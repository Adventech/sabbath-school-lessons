`Funda Isaya 65:17-20. Kuthetha ukuthini ukuthi kwizulu elitsha umntwana uya “kufa enekhulu leminyaka”? (Isa. 65:20).`

Ukumsusa uIsaya 65:20 kwimeko yembali, kungalula ukuba umntu acinge ukuba kukho ukufa naboni kumazulu amatsha nomhlaba omtsha. Yona loo ngcamango ichasene nesithembiso esikuIsaya sokuba abalungileyo abafileyo baya kuvuswa (Isa. 26:19) nokuba ukufa nosizi ekugqibeleni akuyi kubakho (Isa. 25:8, Isa. 65:19).

UIsaya 65:20, ngokucacileyo, uyintetho engumfuziselo. Ngubani umntwana one-100 leminyaka? Endaweni yoko, xa sifunda uIsaya 65:17-20 kwimeko yakhe yembali, singabona ukuba indima ngokokubonga yayisalatha kwintsikelelo esasiza kuyifumana isizwe sakwaIsrayeli, kodwa kuphela xa bethe bahlala bethembekile kwimiyalelo yomnqophiso (thelekisa noDuteronomi 28). Njengoko wenjenjalo uYesu kuMateyu 24, ukuhlanganisa iziganeko ezisondele kakhulu kwezilandelayo, uIsaya wenza into efanayo apha, ebonisa ukuba oko kuya kwenzeka ngoku kuya kuba yintshayelelo kumazulu amatsha nomhlaba omtsha.

KuIsaya indalo entsha ayikho kwixesha elizayo ngokupheleleyo. Ingene ngokukhawuleza kwixesha langoku ngokusebenza kukaThixo okunendili kokuhlangulwa nokuxolelwa kwezono. Indima yazisa kukuba nangoku, kubukho bangoku bukaIsrayeli, uThixo sele ekoyisile ukufa ngesithembiso sobude nokupheliswa kokufa kweentsana (Isa. 65:20). Konke oku kunika kuphela umfanekiso wokuya kubakho ekugqibeleni.

`Funda 1 Korinte 16:20. Siyisebenzisa njani le ndima kwixesha lethu nakwinkcubeko yethu?`

Kwinkcubeko yamaGrike exesha leTestamente eNtsha, ukubulisana phakathi kwezizalwana nabahlobo abasondeleyo kwakuhamba kunye nokwangana. UPawulos unika imiyalelo yolu thando lufudumeleyo phakathi kwamakholwa angamaKristu kumaRoma 16:16, 1 Korinte 16:20, 2 Korinte 13:12, no-1 Tesalonika 5:26. Ngokwemeko ekhoyo apho, singakhupha umthetho-siseko weBhayibhile wokubulisa amakholwa nokuwenza azive eyinxalenye yosapho lukaThixo. Kwezinye iinkcubeko, ukwanga kunokungafaneleki, kanti ukuxhawula ngesandla, nokuwola ezingalweni, okanye ukugoba kuyamkeleka kakhulu ngokwenkcubeko. Nokuba kunjani na ke ukwenza ngokwenkcubeko, uPawulos ukhuthaza ukuba sibenze abantu bazive bamkelekile, bethandwa kwimihlangano yebandla.
