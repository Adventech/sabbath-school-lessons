---
title:  "IBhayibhile, Umcacisi Wayo Ngokwayo"
date:   15/11/2026
---

Ukusicacisa kwethu isiBhalo kuphenjelelwa kakhulu kukucingela kwethu. Abo bayibona iBhayibhile ifana nengengqokelela nje yamaxwebhu akudala abakwazi ukubona nakuphi ukumanyana kwiincwadi zayo ezininzi. Phofu ngokuqaphelisisa imithetho-siseko esiso isiseko yokuba “sonke isiBhalo siphefumlelwe nguThixo kwaye siluncedo” (2 Tim. 3:16, NASB), singabubona ubunye obukhoyo kuyo size siyivumele iBhayibhile ukuba ibe ngumcacisi wayo ngokwayo.

`Funda Mateyu 5:17-48. Wayicacisa njani uYesu imfundiso yeTestamente eNdala ekhankanywe kwesi sicatshulwa?`

Ukugwengula nje phezulu xa kufundwa uMateyu 5:17-48 kunganika ingqondo yokuba uYesu wayesusa iimfundiso zeTestamente eNdala efaka ezaKhe ezintsha. Kona ukufunda ngononophelo kwenza kucace ukuba uYesu wayetyhila iinzulu zasemoyeni zeTestamente eNdala. Ngale ngqongdo, waya wazalisekisa isithembiso sikaYeremiya 31:31-33 esithi, phantsi komnqophiso omtsha, umthetho kaThixo uya kubekwa ezingqondweni ubhalwe ezintliziyweni zabantu baKhe (thelekisa noHeb. 8:8-10, Heb. 10:16).

Maxa wambi ukucaciswa kwesicatshulwa esinikiweyo kukwakhona kwakwincwadi leyo sikuyo isicatshulwa. Umzekelo, kuDaniyeli 2, sifumana ingcaciso yephupha eliyimfihlakalo likakumkani uNebhukadenetsare (Dan. 2:31-35) lilandelwa yingcaciso yephupha (Dan. 2:36-45). Ngokukwanjalo, kuMateyu 13 sinomzekeliso womhlwayeli (Mat. 13:1-9), emva koko kube yingcaciso yawo (Mat. 13:18-23).

Ngamanye amaxesha ingcaciso yesigidimi inikwa ngumprofeti wexesha lamva. Umzekelo, 2 IziKronike 36:22, 23 uqinisekisa ukuzaliseka kwesiprofeto sama- 70 eminyaka, njengoko sasixelwe ngenx’ engaphambili kuYeremiya 25:11, 12 nakuYeremiya 29:10. Phofu ke, iintlobo ezininzi zeTestamente eNdala zazaliseka kuKristu, zaye zizininzi izicatshulwa ezicaciswe kwiTestamente eNtsha.

“ITestamente eNdala izindaba ezilungileyo ezinikwe ngemifanekiso nemifuziselo. ITestamente eNtsha iluqobo [okanye isiqu ebesifuziselwa]. Enye ifuneka ngokufanayo nenye.”—Ellen G. White, Selected Messages, book 2, p. 104. Ngokwenene, iTestamente eNtsha ingumchazi ophefumlelweyo weTestamente eNdala.

`Kutheni le nto kubalulekile ukuba sibuqonde ubukho bokuqikelela kuthi xa sifundisisa iBhayibhile? Kutheni le nto okona kuqikelela kubalulekileyo ekufuneka sikuthembe kakhulu ibe kukuthemba iLizwi likaThixo nokuba kufuneka ibe lilo elisigwebayo, ingabi sithi abagweba lona?`
