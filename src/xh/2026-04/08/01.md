### Fundela Isifundo sale Veki
Mat. 5:17-48; 1 Kor. 16:20; Mat. 4:1-11; Luka 10:25-28; Luka 24:25-27, 44-49.

> <p>Indima Yenkumbulo</p>
> “Ngoko wathi eqalele ngoMoses nabo bonke abaProfeti, wachaza zonke izinto ezibhaliweyo ngaYe kuzo zonke iziBhalo” (Luka 24:27, NASB).

Okwenzekayo emva kokubethelelwa nokuvuka kukaYesu, uKristu wabonakala kubafundi ababini ababenomoya ophantsi, bobabini bavuma, “ ‘Besithembe ukuba nguye oza kukhulula uIsrayeli ngentlawulelo’ ” (Luka 24:21). Abafundi babelindele uMesiya owayeza kuhlangula amaYuda kwingcinezelo yamaRoma. Oku kulindela kungekho ntweni nesiphumo sokudaniswa kwakuvela kucaciso olunecala, nolucalanye lweziprofeto ezingoMesiya zeTestamente eNdala. UKristu wayisombulula ingxaki yokungaqondi kakuhle kwaba bafundi babini ngokuzicacisa kubo iziBhalo ngokubanzi (Luka 24:25-27). Ukusicacisa kukaKristu isiBhalo kwawavula amehlo abo kwazenza zavutha iintliziyo kubo ngaphakathi (Luka 24:31, 32). Kamva uYesu wabonakala nakwiqela elikhulu labafundi eYerusalem, “wayivula ingqiqo yabo, ukuze baziqonde iziBhalo” (Luka 24:45; bona nakuLuka 24:33, 36, 44-49).

La mava ababaza iziphumo ezihle zokuyiqonda ngendlela eyiyo imibhalo yobuprofeti. Ukusiqonda ngokungekuko isiprofeto kungakhokelela kwintlekele yasemoyeni.

_Funda isifundo sale veki ukulungisela iSabatha kaNovemba 21._
