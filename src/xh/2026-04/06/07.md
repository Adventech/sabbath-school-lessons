---
title:  "Imibhalo KaEllen G. White"
date:   06/11/2026
---

Amava nemibhalo kaEllen G. White hamba kunye kakhulu noko sikufundileyo kule veki ngokubhekiselele kubathunywa bakaThixo eBhayibhileni. Yena, naye wawufumana umyalelo kaThixo ukuba abhale oko kuye kwatyhilwa kuye.

Ekhangela emva kowe-1909, uEllen G. White wabhala, “Ngenxa yengozi eyaphantsa yemka nobomi bam, nandingenawo amandla empilweni, ndingakwazi ukubhala. Ngobunye ubusuku, ingelosi yeNkosi yeza ecaleni lomandlalo wam yathi, ‘Kufuneka uzibhale phantsi zonke izinto endikunika zona.’ Ndathi, ‘Andikwazi ukubhala.’ Waphindwa wanikwa umyalelo, ‘Kubhale konke endikunikayo.’ Ndacinga ukuba ndiya kuzama, ndathabatha into yokubhala etafileni, ndaqala ukubhala, ndafumanisa ukuba ndiyakwazi ukuwenza ngokulula amagama.”—In General Conference Bulletin, May 31, 1909.

Njengababhali abaphefumlelweyo besiBhalo, uEllen G. White wasebenzisa abancedisi bokubhala. Abancedisi bakhe bokubhala benza umsebenzi olindeleke ukuba wenziwe ngabahleli – ukunceda ababhali ekubekeni izigidimi zabo ngokucacileyo kodwa bengafaki ezabo iingcinga. Babengengabo ababhalela omnye incwadi okanye ababhali kunye nombhali. Umsebenzi wabo wawuxhomekeke koko uEllen G.Whte wayekuveza, nokuba kubhaliwe okanye ngokubabizela.

UEllen G. White wayekwacaphula alungise umsebenzi kwamanye amajelo ekubhaleni kwakhe ukunceda ekubekeni izigidimi zakhe. Ngokuncomela abafundi bakhe iincwadi kanye awayezisebenzisa, kuyabonakala ukuba wayengazami kukhohlisa okanye ukubakhohlakalela ababhali bazo, kodwa wafumana kulwini lwabo iintetho ezifanelekileyo ukudlulisa iinyaniso awayenga angazibeka.

Kuthiwani ke ngeendawo ezingafaniyo neempazamo?

“Ngokubhekiselele kukungabi naziphoso,” wabhala wathi, “Andizange ndithi andinazo; nguThixo kuphela ongenazo iziphoso.” Selected Messages, book 1, p. 37. Omnye angahlangana neentetho ezithile kwimibhalo yakhe ekunzima ukuziqonda okanye ukuzenza zivumelani neyabanye. Ubuntu bukaEllen G. White babungoyiswa lubizo lwakhe lukaThixo. Njengathi sonke, uEllen White naye wayenako ukukhula nokuvuthwa, ukukhula nokuvuthwa okuvelayo wkimibhalo yakhe.

Njengoko sesitshilo, wayebalungisa abo bakholelwa ukuba lonke igama awalibhalayo lalivela ngqo ezulwini. “Akho amaxesha xa izinto eziqhelekileyo kufuneka zixeliwe, iingcinga eziqhelekileyo ekufuneka zibekho engqondweni, iileta eziqhelekileyo ekufuneka zibhaliwe luze ulwazi olunikiweyo ludlule komnye luye komnye kubasebenzi. Amagama anjalo, ulwazi olunjalo, awanikwa phantsi kwempefumlelo eyodwa yoMoya kaThixo.”—Selected Messages, book 3, p. 58.
