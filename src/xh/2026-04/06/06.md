---
title:  "Isigidimi Nokungagqibeleli Komntu"
date:   05/11/2026
---

`Uthni uPawulos ngendyebo kaThixo ku-2 Korinte 4:7?`

UPawulos ubhala ngendyebo kaThixo egciniswe kwi“zitya zomdongwe” (abantu), ukuze amandla kaThixo abe nokuboniswa ngokuchasene nobuthakathaka bethu. IsiBhalo siveza ukuba kanye njengokuba abathunywa bakaThixo babengagcinwa ngokwangaphezu kwendalo ekusileleni kubomi beziqu zabo (imizekelo: uMoses, Eliya, Davide, Yona, noPetros), ngokunjalo nemibhalo yabo yayingasindanga kwiingqibelelo zokuqonda komntu nokuthetha kwakhe.

“Umanyano lukaThixo nomntu, olwabonakala kuKristu, lukho naseBhayibhileni. Iinyaniso ezityhiliweyo zonke ‘zinikwe ngempefumlelo kaThixo;’ kanti zibekwe ngamazwi abantu zaye zilungiselwe iintswelo zomntu.”—Ellen G. White, Testimonies for the Church, vol. 5, p. 747.

Ubuntu babathunywa bakaThixo abuye bungananzwa kukubizelwa kwabo ekubeni ngabathunywa bakaThixo ukuba badlulise amagugu enyaniso, njengoko uPawulos ebhala ku-2 Korinte 4:7. Kwaye akubonakali ukuba kuMoya Oyingcwele wabona kufanelekile ukuba abathintele abathunywa baKhe ekwenzeni iintetho nokubhala amazwi esiwafumana namhlanje kunzima ukuwenza avumelane okanye ukuwachaza. Nokuba ke zibalwa kangakanani ezi ndima, kuthabatha ingqondo ecutheke kakhulu ukukhubeka kuzo. NjengamaKristu akholelwa eBhayibhileni, kufuneka sizinikele eLizwini likaThixo kangangoko siliqonda size sithembele eNkosini, naxa sekukho esingakuqondiyo.

Okunye ukuphazama kungabalelwa kwiimpazamo ezenziwayo, nazo ezingabalulekanga, ngabakhupheli bamva, okanye kumajelo aphikisanayo (iingxelo) ababexhomekeke kuwo ababhali abaphefumlelweyo. Xa sijongwa izicatshulwa eziyingxaki, sidinga ukuba sizibuze ukuba singaba siyiqonda ngokuzeleyo na intsingiselo yamazwi ombhali, nokuba mhlawumbi ukubeka phambili ekufundeni kungasisombululela na ingxaki.

Nokuba asikwazi ukuyixazulula yenye into ebonakala iyimpazamo okanye engafaniyo, sifumana ukuba azibalulekanga kwisigidimi esinikwayo. Singanokuthemba ukuba iinyaniso zikaTixo esiBhalweni zityhilwe ngokuthembeka sekukho kunjalo ukungagqibeleli kwabathunywa bakaThixo.

`Ucinga ukuba kutheni ukuze uThixo anyule ukusebenzisa abantu abaneziphoso kuneengelosi njengabathunywa baKhe abaphambili?`
