### Fundela Isifundo sale Veki
Ekso. 17:14; Isa. 13:1; Yer. 36:17, 18; Rom. 16:22; Luka 1:1-4; Yuda 14, 15; 2 Kor. 4:7.

> <p>Indima Yenkumbulo</p>
> “Kuba izinto zonke ezabhalwayo ngenxa engaphambili, zaye zibhalelwa okwethu ukufundiswa, ukuze sithi ngalo unyamezelo novuselelo lweziBhalo sibe nalo ithemba” (Roma 15:4).

Ubungathanda njani ukufundiswa ingoma eqanjwe nguThixo ngokwaKhe? KuDuteronomi 32, amazwi abhalwa phantsi nguMoses ngokukhokelwa nguThixo (Dut. 31:19-22). Asiye sicinge ngeengoma xa sicinga ngemibhalo yobuprofeti, nangona ezininzi kwiindumiso ngokwenene zizingoma ezaya zaphefumlelwa, naxa wona amazwi ayengabizelwa ngqo nguYehova ngokwaKhe.

IBhayibhile ichaza iindlela ezintathu abaprofeti ababeedlulisa ngazo izigidimi zikaThixo: (1) ngokuthetha; (2) ngokubhala; (3) nangezenzo eziyimifuziselo okanye ukuzidlala. Kule veki siza kuqwalasela kwimibhalo esezibhalweni. IBhayibhile inengxelo zeLizwi likaThixo, njengoko libekwe ngamazwi abathunywa baKhe, kuquka iintshumayelo neentetho zabaprofeti.

Cinga ngeentlobo ezahlukeneyo zokubhala neentlobo zemibhalo yabaprofeti: ukubalisa, imbali, izibongo, ukulila neeleta, buncinane. Abantu abanyuliweyo, amadoda nabafazi, babeka kulwimi lomntu oko babekhokelwa nguMoya Oyingcwele ukuba bakudlulise. Kule veki siza kukhangela ekubhaleni kwabo, nokusebenzisa abancedisi bokubhala (oonobhala), nakwizinto zokugcinwa engqondweni xa kufundwa iindawo ezinika umngeni.

_Funda isifundo sale veki ukulungisela iSabatha kaNovemba 7._
