---
title:  "Abancedisi Bokubhala"
date:   03/11/2026
---

`Funda Yeremiya 36:17, 18 nomaRoma 16:22. Zisixelela ntoni ezi ndima ngabab-hali beBhayibhile abasebenzisa abancedisi bokubhala?`

Ngoqikelelo, uYeremiya wayenakho ukuzibhala ngokwakhe izigidimi zakhe, kodwa endaweni yoko, wasuka waxhomekeka kumbhali othembekileyo ukuba azibhale iziprofeto zakhe ngokunyanisekileyo nokuba abelane ngazo neenkokeli zesizwe. UYeremiya asikuphela kwakhe umzekelo eBhayibhileni apho abaprofeti basebenzisa iinkonzo zabancedisi ekusibhaleni kunye nokusidlulisa isigidimi sikaThixo.

KwiTestamente eNtsha, nangona uTertiyo esithi nguye “oyibhalileyo le ncwadi” (Roma 16:22), kuyabonakala ukusuka kwasekuqaleni kwencwadi yabaseRoma ukuba wayebhala kuphela oko uPawulos wayenga angakuthetha. UTertiyo wayengumncedisi wakhe wokubhala, okanye unobhala—umncedisi wokubhala owayebeka amazwi kaPawulos kwimo ebhaliweyo.

Esi senzo sasingeyonto engaqhelekanga kuPawulos. Ekupheleni kweeleta zakhe eziya kwabaseKolose kwaneziya eTesalonika, uPawulos wayeqinisekisa ukuba ufaka umbuliso “ngesam isandla” (Kol. 4:18, 2 Tes. 3:17) njengendlela yokuyinika igunya yonke ileta. Oku kuxela ukuba waxhomekeka komnye umntu ukuba azibhale ezi leta esenzela yena Pawulos. Ngenxa yokuba obu bunyaniso bungenza kubekho ukuthandabuza ukuba ileta imbeka ngokukuko na yena—okanye kakubi, nokuba yayingaveli kuye konke—uPawulos wenza kubekho okubhalwe sisandla sakhe ekupheleni ukuze kungabikho kuthandabuza ekubeni liqobo leleta yakhe.

Abacacisi abaninzi bakubalela ukubakho kokwahlukana kohlobo lokubhala okufumaneka kwiileta zikaPawulos ekusebenziseni kwakhe abancedisi bokubhala. Kukwarhanelwa oko nakwiileta ezimbini zikaPetros: “Nangona iyinyaniso ukuba uhlobo lwentetho ka-2 Petros lwahlukile kwileta yokuqala, oku kungabalelwa ngokufanelekileyo ekuthini uPetros unokuba wasebenzisa umncedisi ekuyibekeni ngolwimi olucacileyo ileta awayeyibhala ngesiGrike. Ukuba uPawulos, owayegodusiwe xa kuthethwa ngesiGrike, wayesebenzise abancedisi, njengoko kucacile ukuba kwakunjalo, bekuya kuvakala ngakumbi ukucinga ukuba uPetros ngewenza njalo, njengoko ulwimi lwakhe lwenkobe lwaluyiArameyiki, nokuba abo bancedisi baba nempembelelo eqinisekileyo kumagama akhoyo esiGrike kwiileta zakhe.”—The SDA Bible Commentary, vol. 5, p. 186.

Oku kunika ingqondo ethi, nangona babesoloko bethembekile kwiingcinga zabathunywa bakaThixo, ulwimi nokwakhiwa kwesivisa-ngcinga kona kungabonisa ukubhala okwahlukeneyo kwabancedisi abasetyenzisiweyo ukubeka ezo ngcamango ziphefumlelweyo.
