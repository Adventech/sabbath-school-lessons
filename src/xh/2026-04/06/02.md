---
title:  "Ubizo Lokubhala"
date:   01/11/2026
---

Eyona ndlela yasekuqaleni uThixo awayisebenzisayo ukudlulisela izigidimi kuluntu yayingokuzithetha ngomlomo, njengamazwi avakalayo, okanye amaphupha, okanye iingelosi. Kwada kwalixesha likaMoses apho isiBhalo sinengxelo yomyalelo wokuqala kaThixo ngokubhekiselele kula mava, okubhala “encwadini” oko uThixo akuthethileyo.

`Kuthini ukukhankanywa kokuqala kokubhala eBhayibhileni: Eksodus 17:14.`

KwiTestamente eNdala, sifumana imizekelo emininzi kaThixo enika imiyalelo kubathunywa baKhe ukuba bawagcine amazwi aKhe aya kubantu baKhe. NjengoMoses, uYeremiya waxelelwa, “ ‘Thabathela kuwe incwadi esongwayo, uwabhale onke amazwi endawathetha kuwe ngamaIsrayeli, nangamaYuda, nangeentlanga zonke, kususela kwimini endathethayo kuwe, uthabathele emihleni kaYosiya, ude uze kule mini’ ” (Yer. 36:2).

Wanikwa nesizathu sokuba kufuneke azibhale iziprofeto: “ ‘Mhlawumbi indlu kaYuda ingabuphulaphula bonke ububi endicinga ukubenza kubo, ukuze babuye elowo endleleni yakhe embi, ndibuxolele ubugwenxa babo nesono sabo’ ” (Yer. 36:3). Okuxelwe kwangaphambili kwanikwa ukuze kukhokelele abantu elusindisweni, kunokuba ngokuba kufunwa ulwazi ngexesha elizayo.

Abathunywa bakaThixo babhala phantsi kweemeko ezahlukene kakhulu. UMoses wabhala iincwadi zakhe ezintlanu ngexesha esentlango. UDavide waziqamba ezininzi kwiindumiso zakhe ngexesha wayezingelwa nguSawule (bona: iindumiso 52, 54, 57, 59). UDaniyeli wayibhala imibono yakhe ngexesha wayesebenza kumabhotwe aseBhabheli naseMedo-Persi. Kwelinye icala, uPawulos wazibhala iileta zakhe eziya kwabase-Efese, kwabaseFilipi, kwabaseKolose, nakuFilemon ngexesha wayesentolongweni, yena uYohane wabhala IsiTyhilelo ngexesha wayesekugxothweni ePatimo. Kuyabonakala ukuba xa kukho kubathunywa bakaThixo into ethi mababhale, babenikwa amandla nguMoya Oyingcwele nokuba babekweziphi iimeko.

Okunika umdla, zonke iimfundiso ezigciniweyo zikaYesu ziza kuthi zibhaliwe ngabantu uThixo awakhetha ukubagcinisa ezo nyaniso zingamagugu. Oku kusibonisa ukuthemba kukaThixo ukuba izigidimi zikaThixo zaziya kubhalwa zigcinwe ngokuthembekileyo ngabathunywa baKhe.

`Ungaphendula uthini kumntu othi asinako ukuyithemba iBhayibhile kuba asinawo amaxwebhu entsusa? Kutheni le, ngokwenene, iyingxoxo efileyo?`
