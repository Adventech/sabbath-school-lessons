---
title:  "Izisele Zokubhaliweyo"
date:   04/11/2026
---

“Phezu koko, ngokuba iNtshumayeli ibisisilumko, yamana ibafundisa abantu ukwazi, yalinganisela yabagocagoca, yayila imizekeliso emininzi. INtshumayeli yafuna, inga ingafumana amazwi anandiphekayo, kuthi nokubhalwayo kulunge, ibe ngamazwi enyaniso” (Intshumayeli 12:9, 10).

Esi sicatshulwa singafundwa ukubonisa ukuba uSolomon akazange abe ngumqali wayo yonke imizekeliso awayibhalayo. Phantsi kwempefumlelo kaThixo, wa“funa” iintetho ezininzi waphengulula ukuba afumane “amazwi afanelekileyo.” Inyaniso yeyokuba, inkcazo ecatshulwa rhoqo yomfazi onesidima kwiMizekeliso 31 ibalelwa kumama wekumkani engaziwayo uLemuweli, hayi uSolomon (Imiz. 31:1). Ubungqina balatha kwimpembelelo yoncwadi lwaseYiputa nakwiMizekeliso ngokwayo.

Ngokufanayo, ababhali bembali ephefumlelweyo yeTestamente eNdala bathi bayifumana koovimba neminye imiqulu yeembali ukuze bachaze ngokuthembeka ibali labantu bakaThixo.

`Funda 1 Kumkani 11:41; Luka 1:1-4; noYuda 14, 15. Yintoni esiyixelelwa zezi ndima ngendlela ababhali abaphefumlelwayo abasebenzisa ngayo izisele ezingekho eBhayibhileni?`

UEzra noNehemiya ngabanye abayimizekelo yababhali abaxhomekeka kwiingxelo zembali ukubhala kwabo iingxelo zabo. UEzra uquka iikopi zeeleta nemithetho yakomkhulu ngokubhekiselele kwiYerusalem nePersi (bona uEzra 4–7), noNehemiya usixelela ngemibhalo yeminombo awayifumana wayihlanganisa kwimbali yakhe (bona uNeh. 7:5-72).

Lo mthetho-siseko ubonakala nakwiTestamente eNtsha. “Kucacile kumava kaLuka ukuba impefumlelo isebenza ngendlela ehambelana nokusebenza kwendalo kwezakhono zengqondo kwaye ayizibeki ecaleni. Apha kukho umbhali ophefumlelweyo owayekhokelwa nguMoya Oyingcwele ukuba anike ukufundisisa okukhutheleyo kwizisele ezifumanekayo zentetho nokubhaliweyo ngobomi bukaKristu, aze ngoko ahlanganise imbali ehlangeneyo yolwazi aluhlanganise ngolo hlobo.”—The SDA Bible Commentary, vol. 5, p. 669.

Kubonakala ngokungathi uYuda ucaphula ku-1 Enoki 1:9—incwadi engekho eBhayibhileni, umsebenzi wexesha laphakathi kweeTestamente. Ngaphandle kokukhankanya amagama abo, noPawulos ukwacaphule kwizazi zamaGrike (bona: IZenzo 17:28 noTito 1:12).

`Nangona ababhali beBhayibhile babekholisa ukukhokelwa yintyilelo ethe ngqo nemibono ekwanjalo, oku akuzange kumbeke ecaleni uMoya Oyingcwele ukuba naye abaphefumlele aba bathunywa ekukhetheni amabakubhale kwezinye izisele. Kutheni le nto oku kuyimbono ebalulekileyo kuthi ukuba siyikhumbule?`
