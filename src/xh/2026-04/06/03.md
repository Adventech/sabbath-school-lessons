---
title:  "Iintlobo Zemibhalo Nezokubhala"
date:   02/11/2026
---

IBhayibile ineentlobo zokubhalwa ezahlukeneyo ezathi zasetyenziswa ngabathunywa bakaThixo abaphefumlelweyo. Ezinye zifana nokubhalwa kwedayari (bona uNum. 33:1-49). Kwelinye icala, iincwadi zikaDaniyeli, noZekariya, noYohane zinemizekelo wokubhalwa kwezinto zokugqibela, okuphawulwa ngemifuziselo ebanzi, imixholo yezwe lonke, neenkcazo zezidalwa ezisezulwini, ezinye nje zeempawu ezimbalwa.

`Funda Isaya 13:1. Ithetha njani apha iBhayibhile ngesigidimi esinikwa nguIsaya?`

Maxa wambi imibono efunyanwa ngabaprofeti ibizwa ngokuthi “yeyosiyazi” - kuthethwa ukuba kuthethwa nabo kwaye intsonkothile okanye zizityhilelo ezivela kuThixo (ezinye iinguqulelo ziliguqule igama ngokuthi “umthwalo” womprofeti. Iinguqulelo ezintsha zeBhayibhile zibonisa ukuba emininzi kule mibono yayibhalwe ngemo yokubonga, kukholiswa ukusetyenziswa ukuhamba kunye. Ukuhamba kunye kuxa imigca elandelelanayo iphinda okanye, maxa wambi, ichasa imigca engaphambili. Umzekelo: “Ngaloo mini umntu uya kondela kuMenzi wakhe, amehlo akhe akhangele kOyingcwele kaIsrayeli” (Isa. 17:7). U“Menzi” no“Oyingcwele” zizaci ezihamba kunye, umfanekiselo ngamnye usinika ulwazi olongezekileyo ngomnye.

Abaprofeti bakwasebenzisa izixhobo eziqhelekileyo zokubhala njengofaniso (imifuziselo) nobaxo (ukwandisa). Ukuze thina siziqonde ngcono izigidimi zabo sitsho sikwazi nokuzisebenzisa kubalulekile ukuba siqonde uhlobo lokubhala abalusebenzisileyo.

Olunye uhlobo lokubhala olufunyanwa esiBhalweni ngulo woku“bambana ngomnqophiso.” Umzekelo, kuYeremiya 2:4-9, uYeremiya unika isikhalazo sikaThixo esisemthethweni ngakubantu baKhe ngokwaphulwa kokufunwa ngumnqophiso. Ngomfanekiselo wenkundla yamatyala, umbhali umzoba uThixo njengomtshutshisi esandlala izizathu zokugweba kwaKhe.

IsiBhalo naso sinokubhalwa kwesiprofeto, kwembali, ingoma, nemizekeliso. UYeremiya kwathiwa makabhale incwadi yazo zonke izigidimi zakhe (Yer. 36:2), ngoku yona imibhalo yeTestamente eNtsha ineeleta eziya kubantu okanye ekuhlaleni. Akukho kwahlukana kubonakalayo kwigunya lohlobo oluthile lokudlulisa isigidimi ngaphezu kolunye. Iileta zikaPawulos zazithatyathwa njengezinesisindo esinye nezabaprofeti bakudala, kuba uPawulos unika ubungqina obucacileyo kwiileta zakhe bokuba wayephefumlelwe nguMoya Oyingcwele (bona u-1 Kor. 7:10, 12, 40).

`Okunye ukufunda okukhulu kucinezela igalelo lomntu esiBhalweni ngaphezu kwelikaThixo. Kutheni loo nto iyindlela eqinisekileyo yokonakalisa okwasemoyeni?`
