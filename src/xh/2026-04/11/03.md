---
title:  "Umama KwaIsrayeli"
date:   07/12/2026
---

Emva kokuwahlangula amaIsrayeli ebukhobokeni eYiputa, uThixo wawanika iZwe laseKanan. Wathembisa ukuwasikelela kakhulu ukuba aye ayilandela imithetho nemiyalelo yaKhe. Waya wawalumkisa ukuba ukwaphulwa komnqophiso owenziwe naYe kuya kuba nesiphumo sokuva ubunzima nentshutshiso (Duteronomi 27–30). Emva kokufa kukaYoshuwa, amaIsrayeli abuya umva asuka ekuwugcineni umnqophiso. Aya acinezelwa, ke ngoko, zizizwe ezimelene nabo baza bakhala kuThixo. Ukuphendula, “uYehova wavelisa abagwebi bawasindisa esandleni sabaphangi bawo” (Abag. 2:16). Waya wathumela abaprofeti ukubakhuthaza ukuba bawuhlaziye umnqophiso noThixo nokubanika Amandla ukuchasa abacinezeli babo.

`Funda Abagwebi 4:4-16 nAbagwebi 5:1-5. Yintoni awayenzela uIsrayeli uDebhora?`

UDebhora wadlala indawo ebaluleke kakhulu yobuprofeti ekuhambiseni isigidimi sikaThixo kuIsrayeli nakuBharaki, umkhokeli womkhosi wesizwe, ngexesha ehlanganisa imikhosi. YayinguDebhora owanika uBharaki umqondiso wokuba kuqalwe ukulwa, esithi, “ ‘Suk’ume! Ngokuba yiyo le imini uYehova amnikele ngayo uSisera esandleni sakho; akaphumi yini na uYehova phambi kwakho?’ ” (Abag. 4:14).

Njengomgwebi kwaIsrayeli, uDebhora wayesisitshixo. Ubukhokeli bakhe nokholo lwakhe kwisithembiso sikaThixo soloyiso kwawanika amandla amaIsrayeli ekulweni kwawo neentshaba zawo. Ubuchule bukaBharaki njengomkhokeli wemfazwe nokuba nentumekelelo kwakhe, nokuba wayethandabuza kangakanani, ukuwukhokela umkhosi kaIsrayeli nako kwakubalulekile, kodwa asikuko oko okoyisayo edabini.

Mhlawumbi esi, sesona sigidimi sibalulekileyo sebali lomprofeti uDebhora nesoldati uBharaki. Embindini wokubhiyozela uloyiso olothusayo, abona bantu banamandla kwaIsrayeli—umgwebi nomlawuli womkhosi owoyisayo—babakhokela abantu ukuzukisa uThixo. Injalo ke imo yobukhokeli basemoyeni ngenene.

Ulungiselelo lobuprofeti bukaDebhora bunika ubungqina ekuthembekeni kukaThixo ngamaxesha oxinzelelo nengcinezelo. Xa uThixo wayenika uDebhora isigidimi esiya kuBharaki, akazange axoxe ngokuba kunokwenzeka na ukubiza imikhosi eyaneleyo, engazange axoxe nokuba amaIsrayeli ayengenawo kakhulu amandla okuba oyise. Endaweni yoko, wasihambisa isigidimi sakhe ngengqiniseko waza wamkhuthaza uBharaki ukuba abeke bucala iintandabuzo zakhe athembele ekuboneleleni kukaThixo.

`Singakufunda njani ukumthemba uThixo xa, ngokwa ngoku, izinto zingakhangeleki zinika ithemba?`
