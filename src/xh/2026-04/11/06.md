date:   10/12/2026
---

`Funda IsiTyhilelo 1:1-3. UYohane wayebizelwe ukuba enze ntoni?`

AmaKristu okuqala ayephila kwimo yokulindela ukubuya okuthenjisiweyo kukaYesu. Umpostile uYohane wayembonile uKristu enyuka engena emafini wayiva nengelosi ichaza ukuba uya kubuya ngangohlobo olunye (IZenzo 1:10, 11). Ngoku ibandla lokuqala lalijongene nokungaziwayo. Kunjalo, uYesu wathi uya kubuya. Umbuzo uthi, Nini?

Ngexesha uYohane wayelahlelwe kwisiqithi sePatimo, uThixo wathetha kuye ngokubhekiselele kwisithembiso sokuza kwesibini: “Yabonani, uza namafu; aye eya kumbona amehlo onke, kwanabo bamhlabayo” (IsiTy. 1:7). UYohane waya wafumana nesityhilelo esibanzi seziganeko ezaziya kuba yimiqondiso yexesha lokuphela. Ngaphandle kwenkuthazo nokungxolisa kumabandla asixhenxe (IsiTyhilelo 2, 3), izityhilelo zaziquka imibono yomgwebo (IsiTy. 14:6-12), eyokuphela kwehlabathi (IsiTyhilelo 20), eyokuza kwesibini kukaKristu (IsiTy. 14:14-16), neyokudalwa ngokutsha kwehlabathi (IsiTyhilelo 21, 22).

UYohane wazazisa njengo“mzalwana wenu, nedlelane nani ngayo imbandezelo, nangabo ubukumkani, nangonyamezelo lukaYesu Kristu” (IsiTy. 1:9). Wayekwazi ukuba kukuthini ukuva ubunzima, ingeyiyo intlungu nje yokulinda, kodwa kwintshutshiso eyayivela ekuvakaliseni iLizwi likaThixo nokuba nobungqina bukaYesu Kristu.

Kuyo yonke incwadi yesiTyhilelo, uYohane wamenywa ukuba abone waza wayalelwa ukuba abhale oko wakubonayo. Kwisahluko sokugqibela wabhala, “Mna, Yohane, ndingulowo ubezibona ezi zinto, eziva” (IsiTy. 22:8). UYohane wayelingqina lesiprofeto kwiziganeko zemihla yokugqibela, elathunywayo ukuba labelane namabandla emihla yakhe oko wayekubonisiwe embonweni (IsiTy. 22:16). Ungumzekelo okhumbuza ngoYohane umBhaptizi, owavakalisa ukufika kukaYesu Kristu kokuqala (Yohane 1:6-8) ngexesha elalibaluleke kakhulu kwimbali yomhlaba. Sikwabiziwe ukuba singqine njengoko wenzayo uYohane. Kufuneka nathi sigcine ubungqina bukaYesu (IsiTy. 19:10)—ubungqina awabunika ngaYe ngabaprofeti. Sisenokungafumani mibono njengoYohane, kodwa singako ukulindela ukubuya kukaKristu ngomonde lo gama sinika ubungqina kumsebenzi waKhe ebomini bethu, silindele nemiqondiso yokuhlangulwa kwethu.

`Cinga ngako konke ukukhanya esikunikiweyo. Mithwalo mini ephezu kwethu nje kuba sinikwe kangaka?`
