---
title:  "Ilizwi Lesiprofeto"
date:   11/12/2026
---

UThixo wasoloko ethetha nabantu ngabaprofeti. Oku kwakuyinyaniso ngokukodwa ngamaxesha abalulekileyo embalini. Akukho ngqondweni na ke ngoko ukulindela, ukuba uThixo ebeya kuthetha kubantu baKhe ekupheleni kwexesha?

Athi ama-Adventist kaMiller akuba “noDaniso oluKhulu” ngo-Okthobha 22, 1844, uThixo wabizela uEllen Harmon (White) kulungiselelo lokuprofeta wamnika isigidimi sakhe esiya kumakholwa awayedanile. Umbono wakhe wokuqala waye uyintuthuzelo kula makholwa ekuphelelweni kwawo lithemba.

Esinye isiganeko esasibalulekile sasikukuqala koshicilelo lweencwadi ngama-Adventist nokuyilwa kweBandla ngokusemthethweni ngama-Adventists angabagcini beSabatha baliBandla eliqingqiweyo. Ngoncwadi lwawo novangelo, lamaSabatha, ayesekhule ngeminyaka ye-1860 angamawaka aliqela. Akudinga ukuqulunqwa ukuze akwazi ukuvakalisa izigidimi zeengelosi ezintathu (IsiTy. 14:6-12) ngempumelelo enkulu. Nangona abanye bayichasa ingcamango yokuqulunqwa babe libandla, uThixo wamsebenzisa uEllen G. White ukulikhuthaza eli nyathelo. NgoMeyi 21, 1863, iBandla iSeventh-day Adventist lazalwa ngokusemthethweni.

Kwakuloo mnyaka mnye, uThixo wasisebenzisa isipho sobuprofeti ukukhuthaza uhlaziyo lwempilo phakathi kwamakholwa. AmaSeventh-day Adventist amaninzi ayengenayo impilo entle okanye ayengaphili ubomi bempilo. Ngenxa yemibono emibini ezeleyo kaEllen G. White ngowe-1863 nowe-1865, le ntlekele yaguqulwa. AmaSeventh-day Adventist aya aba ngabakhuthazi bempilo nokuphila ngokunempilo. Ukususela kweye-1870 ukuya kweye-1890s, uEllen G. White waba sisixhobo esinegalelo ekuphuhliseni uhlelo lwemfundo yama-Adventist. Ubuchule bolwazi lwakhe ngemfundo epheleleyo (ukufundwa kweencwadi, okuphathekayo, nokwasemoyeni) baya busamkelwa baza basetyenziswa. Njengoko ebesa kutsho: “Imfundo yenene iyinto engaphezu kokufundwa nje kwezifundo ezithile. Ingaphezulu kolungiselelo nje lobomi bangoku. Iyinto enokwenza nomntu ephelele, nalo lonke ixesha lokuphila kwakhe umntu. Kukuphuhliswa okundilekileyo komzimba, ingqondo namandla asemoyeni.”—Imfundo, iph. 13.

Ngeyama-1880s, wadlala indawo ebalulekileyo ekuziseni uzinzo ekufundweni kokwazi uThixo kweSeventh-day Adventist ngosindiso. Wacinezela ukuba ngumbindi wobabalo lukaThixo nobulungisa bokholo phakathi kwesakhiwo socinezelo olulodwa lomthetho. Ukususela kowe-1901 ukuya kowe-1903, wancedisa ekulungisweni ngokutsha kokumila kwenkonzo ukuze umsebenzi othunywe ibandla ehlabathini ube nokufezwa ngcono.

Ngokufutshane, ibingayi kubakho inkonzo yeSeventh-day Adventist njengoko siyazi namhlanje lungakhange lubekho ulungiselelo lukaEllen G. White. Wayengumthunywa kaThixo ngamaxesha axakileyo.
