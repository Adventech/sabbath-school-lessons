### Fundela Isifundo sale Veki
2 Pet. 2:5; Abag. 4:4-16; 1 Kumkani 18:21, 38, 39; Isa. 59:1-4; Neh. 9:30; IsiTy. 1:1-3.

> <p>Indima Yenkumbulo</p>
> “Inene, ayikhe yenze into iNkosi uYehova, ingathanga iluhlakaze ucweyo lwayo kubakhonzi bayo abaprofeti” (Amosi 3:7).

Ukususela ekuWeni, uluntu lwajongana nokukhetha iziyolo zexesha, ngokuchasene nobomi obungunaphakade kuYesu. UThixo uyayazi le nyaniso ilusizi, kwaye, seyikho, uyasithanda ekwasebenzela ukusindiswa kwethu nakanjani.

Yiyo le nto, kuthi ngamaxesha anzima embalini, uThixo wasebenza ngeendlela ezimangalisayo ngomsebenzi wobuprofeti ngenxa yabantu baKhe. Phofu ke, uthe yakuphindwa rhoqo ukwaliwa imizamo yaKhe, walumkisa ngomgwebo.

Ngabaprofeti baKhe, uYehova usoloko ezixela izenzo zaKhe phambi kokuba azenze (Amos 3:7). Ukuba abantu bayaguquka, uyakhawuleza ukuxolela.

Uqhubeka ethetha nathi ngendlela enye namhlanje. Kuyaqondakala, ke ngoko, ukuba uThixo kufuneka ethethe kubantu baKhe ngemihla yokugqibela yomhlaba, kanye njengoko wayethetha kwiinkulungwane ezadlulayo.

Kule veki siza kukhangela emva kumaxesha anzima kwimbali yosindiso xa uThixo wasebenzisa abaprofeti ukucacisa intando yaKhe kubantu baKhe nakwihlabathi elibanzi. Siza kusoloko sihlola indawo yolungiselelo lukaEllen G. White ngamaxesha axakileyo ekukhuleni kwentshukumo yeAdventist.

_Funda: Isifundo sale veki ulungiselela iSabatha kaDisemba 12._
