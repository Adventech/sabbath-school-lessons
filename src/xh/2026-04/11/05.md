---
title:  "Ithemba Ekubhaceni"
date:   09/12/2026
---

“Uyehova, uThixo wooyise, wathumela kubo ngabathunywa bakhe, wanyameka ukuthumela; ngokuba wayenemfesane ngabantu bakhe, nekhaya lakhe” (2 IziKron. 36:15). UThixo wathumela abaprofeti abaliqela emva kukaEliya ukuba bathuthuzele, balumkise, bayalele abantu baKhe, kodwa izikumkani, esakwaIsrayeli nesakwaYuda, zazikombi umgxobhozo kangangokuba uThixo wazifanisa neSodom neGomora (Isa. 1:9, 10). Abantu bakaThixo babezaliswe likratshi, nokubawa nokunqula izithixo ngendlela engathethekiyo. Babecinezela abasebenzi babo, nabahlolokazi, neenkedama, nabasemzini ababesiza befuna uncedo. (Bona kuIsaya 58 indlela awasabela ngayo uThixo ekusileleni kukaIsrayeli). Ekugqibeleni, izigwebo zikaThixo zamkhokelela uIsrayeli ekuthinjweni kwaseSiriya (2 Kumkani 17:1-6) yena uYuda wabhacela kwilizwe laseBhabheli (2 Kumkani 24, 25).

Naxa uThixo wayebohlwaya abantu baKhe, akazange abashiye bebodwa elubhacweni. Wayesabathumela abaprofeti, njengoHezekile noDaniyeli, ukuba banikwe ithemba lexesha elizayo.

`Funda Isaya 59:1-4 noNehemiya 9:30. Yintoni le ithethwa apha ethi isebenze kuzo zonke izizukulwana?`

Kuyo yonke imbali yazo, izikumkani, sakwaIsrayeli nesakwaYuda, zabuva ubunzima beziphumo zesono. Ekumkeni kwazo kuThixo, zazishiye inkuseleko yenkathalelo yaKhe zaza zafanelwa zizigwebo zaKhe. UThiixo akayinyamezeli ingcinezelo nonqulo lwezithixo, kodwa akazange abathumele elubhacweni kwangexesha lokuqala besona. UThixo wabasebenza abantu baKhe, ebacenga ngabaprofeti baKhe ukuba baguquke babuyele kuYe. Abantu bala ukuphulaphula. Inyaniso yeyokuba, iyamangalisa yona into yokuba uYehova athabathe ixesha elide ezama ukubabuyisa abantu baKhe ezonweni zabo. Kwaba ziinkulungwane ezinde, umprofeti emva komprofeti, benika isilumkiso emva kwesilumkiso, uThixo ezama ukufikelela kubo.

UYesu wayibeka ngeyona ndlela iyiyo: “ ‘Yerusalem, Yerusalem, wena ubabulalayo abaprofeti, ubagibisele ngamatye abo bathunywe kuwe, kufuthi kangakanani na ndithande ukubabuthela ndawonye abantwana bakho, ngohlobo esithi isikhukukazi siwabuthele ndawonye amathole aso, ngaphantsi kwamaphiko, anavuma!’ ” (Mat. 23:37). Kanti, nangexesha babesekubhaceni, uThixo akazange abashiye abantu baKhe bengenathemba. Sekukhona kunjalo ukuhlala besilela kwabo, uThixo wathembisa, “ ‘UMkhululi uya kuza eZiyon, nakwabo babuyayo elukreqweni kwaYakobi’ ” (Isa. 59:20, NKJV).

`Cinga nokunyaniseka kukaThixo naxa uIsrayeli wasuka wemka kuYe. Ingaba wakha wabubona ubunyani beli cala lesimilo esinothando sikaThixo kobakho Ubomi? Ngowuphi ukuba ebengakhange abe nomonde nothando olungaka kuwe?`
