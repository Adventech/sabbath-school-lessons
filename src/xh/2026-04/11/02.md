---
title:  "Isilumkiso Ngentlekele"
date:   06/12/2026
---

UThixo ufuna ukuluhlangula uluntu. Nasemva kokuWa, wazama ubudlelwane noAdam noEva, bathi okwexesha abemi bomhlaba balibiza “igama likaYehova” (Gen. 4:26). Njengoko izizukulwana zidlula, inani labo bahamba noThixo lancipha. Abo baqhubeka ukulandela uThixo bangqina ngokulunga kwaKhe, bazikhalimela izono zabo babangqongileyo, babalumkisa ngeziphumo zezenzo zabo.

Ngexesha likaNowa abemi bomhlaba babekhohlakele kangangokuba uThixo wakhetha ukubatshabalalisa (Gen. 6:5-7). UNowa, umshumayeli wobulungisa (2 Pet. 2:5), ngaphandle kwentandabuzo, wabalumkisa bonke ababefuna ukuphulaphula ngentshutshiso ezayo. UThixo wamnika uNowa nemiyalelo yokwakha umkhombe wokusindisa ukuze asindise bonke ababeya kuziphulaphula izilumkiso zakhe baguquka.

`Funda Hebhere 11:7, 2 Petros 2:5, noGenesis 6:9. UNowa wayengu“mshumayeli wobulungisa” ngaziphi iindlela?`

UNogumbe yayingumzuzu ongundoqo kwimbali yoluntu, kwaye ingxelo iyanikwa rhoqo kuzo zombini iiTestamente, eNdala neNtsha njengomzekelo wobunzulu kokuhla koluntu nokuphakama kobabalo olumangalisayo lukaThixo. Ubulungisa bukaThixo bubonakalaliswa ekugwebeni kwaKhe ngokuchasene nendalo yesono nasekuhlanguleni bonke abathanda ukwamkela usindiso.

“Phakathi kwenkohlakalo eyayigquba,uMethusela, uNowa, nabanye abaninzi, basebenza nzima ukugcina ulwazi lukaThixo wenene luphilile, nokuthintela umsinga wokuziphaka kakubi. Ikhulu elinamashumi amabini eminyaka phambi koNogumbe, Uyehova, ngesithunywa esingcwele, wavakalisa injongo yaKhe kuNowa, wamyalela ukuba akhe umkhombe. Ngexesha wayesakha umkhombe, kwakufuneka eshumayele ukuba uThixo uza kuzisa isikhukhula samanzi phezu komhlaba ukuba sitshabalalise abakhohlakeleyo. Abo babeya kukholwa sisigidimi, baze balungiselele eso siganeko ngokuguquka nokuphila ngokutsha, bamelwe kukufumana uxolelo baze basindiswe.”—Ellen G. White, Amanyange Nabaprofeti, iph. 92.

UThixo akazange anele ukumxelela uNowa ngoNogumbe waza wahlangula ababengaphakathi emkhombeni, waya wenza umnqophiso noNowa nayo yonke indalo, ethembisa ukuba akasayi kuphinda alitshabalalise ihlabathi ngamanzi (Gen. 9:8–17). Wonke umnyama unesithembiso, nethemba, lokuhlangulwa.

`Ngexesha elizayo ubona umnyama, cinga ngesithembiso esifunyanwa apho. Kutheni kufuneka kukunike intuthuzelo, ukwazi ukuba uThixo uyazigcina izithembiso zaKhe?`
