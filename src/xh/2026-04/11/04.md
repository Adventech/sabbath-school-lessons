`Funda Abagweni 2:16-19. Ngumzekeliso, okanye injongo, ebonakalayo apha?`

Ngokudlula okubalulekileyo kobukumkani, kwanokucela ukumkani kukaIsrayeli (1 Samuweli 8), ixesha labagwebi laphela. Kona oku kuguquka kwezinto kwazisa esona sikhulu Isidingo sabaprofeti bokucebisa, nokulungisa nokumngxolisa ukumkani, neenkokeli zesizwe, kunye nabantu.

Ngexesha lokulawula kukaAhabhi, uThixo wathumela uEliya nesigidimi somgwebo: akuyi kubakho mvula ngenxa yokuba uIsrayeli “ ‘wayishiya imithetho kaYehova, walandela ooBhahali’ ” (1 Kumkani 18:18, NKJV). Kunokuba aguquke, uAhabhi wazama ukumzingela uEliya ukuba ambulale, kodwa uThixo wamkhusela (1 Kumkani 18:10-12). Wathi, ngomnyaka wesithathu wendlala, uEliya ekugqibeleni waya kukumkani, uAhabhi wagxeka yena ngokungabikho kwemvula (1 Kumkani 18:17)!

Ngoko ke, uEliya wambizela ndawonye wonke uIsrayeli ukuze kubekho ukuqubisana ngezigalo phakathi kukaThixo nabaprofeti bakaBhahali (1 Kumkani 18:19).

`Funda okwavakaliswa nguEliya kuIsrayeli kunye nendlela abasabela ngayo ku-1 Kumkani 18:21, 38, 39. Kwaya kwenzeka ntoni?`

Isenzo sikaThixo sokuhla komlilo uphuma ezulwini, kuhlangene nesigidimi sesiprofeto sikaEliya, saba negalelo elibumdlalo kubantu bakaThixo sabenza ukuba baguquke. Kanye njengamaIsrayeli ayengakhululekanga ukuzinikela ngokuzeleyo kuThixo, siphila kwiinkcubeko ezingamamkeliyo uThixo njengoMdali noMhlawuleli. Kunokuba bamkhonze uThixo njengamangqina ukukhuthaza abanye ukuba banyaniseke kuYe, sikholisa ukulubeka ecaleni ubizo lwethu ngokulandela eyethu iminqweno, njengoko wenzayo uIsrayeli.

UThixo ulumkisa ukuba ukulandela eyethu iminqweno kuya kukhokelela ekwaphukeni, kwintlungu, ekugqibeleni ekufeni. Ukuba ke silandela izwi laKhe, ngoko ke, “ ‘Uya kuhlala ekwalathisa uYehova, awuhluthise umphefumlo wakho ekubaleleni kwelanga, omeleze amathambo akho, ube njengomyezo onyakanyiswa yimvula, nanjengendawo ephuma amanzi, emanzi angatshiyo’ ” (Isa. 58:11).

`Iindalo zethu zenyama zisityhalela ukuba sivukele, nokuba kusengqondweni, ngakuThixo. Kuthini ukukhetha esidinga ukukweza ukuze singayivumeli loo ndalo?`
