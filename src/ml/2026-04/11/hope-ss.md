### Prophets of God – Part 11

Welcome to Hope Sabbath School—an in-depth interactive study of the Word of God. We are continuing our series on Prophets of God. Today’s topic: PROPHETIC MANIFESTATION IN CRUCIAL MOMENTS.

**Scripture Song:** Listen to counsel – Proverbs 19:20

- The prophet Noah at a time of impending judgment
	- What was the situation on earth when Noah was called to be a prophet of God? Genesis 6:1-5,12-13
	- Where do you see mercy mixed with judgment in the response of the LORD to the wickedness that was rampant on the earth? Genesis 6:6-8,14
	- The Apostle Peter called Noah “a preacher of righteousness” (2 Peter 2:5). What was the greatest sermon Noah preached as a prophet of God? Genesis 6:22, Hebrews 11:7
	- Noah was 600 years old when the flood came upon the earth. He had been preaching and building for 120 years. How had his family expanded during that time? Genesis 5:32
	- How did the prophetic ministry of Noah, preaching and building, impact his three sons? Genesis 7:7
	- How many people accepted the call to repentance during the prophetic ministry of Noah?
- Prophets of God down through the ages
	- What other prophets of God were sent at crucial moments down through the ages? Moses, Daniel, John the Baptist, etc.
	- What can we learn from these prophetic messages about the character of God? Amos 3:7, John 3:16, 2 Peter 3:9, etc.
- The prophet/apostle John with an end-time message
	- The apostle John is perhaps best known for his inspired writing of the fourth Gospel. What verses in John’s Gospel have been a blessing to your life?
	- What additional prophetic work was given to the Apostle John? Revelation 1:1-6, 22:8-10
	- What important prophetic messages have you discovered in the Book of Revelation? Revelation 1:7, 14:6-7, 21:1-5, 22:1-5, etc.
	- Why are the prophetic messages contained in the Book of Revelation so relevant for our lives today?
	- What solemn warning was given at the conclusion of the Revelation of Jesus Christ? Revelation 22:18-19 (see also Revelation 20:15, 17:8)
	- How can our names remain in the Book of Life? Revelation 3:5 (see also Revelation 12:10-11)
	- What does it mean to overcome by the blood of the Lamb?
	- Reflect on the story of Stephen the deacon who overcame by the blood of the Lamb and the word of his testimony. Acts 6:8-10,15, 7:54-60
	- How can we be free from fear as we prepare for the fulfillment of end-time prophecies? 1 John 4:17-18, Luke 12:22-32, etc.
