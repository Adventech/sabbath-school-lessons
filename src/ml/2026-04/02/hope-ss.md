### Prophets of God – Part 2

Welcome to Hope Sabbath School—an in-depth interactive study of the Word of God. We are continuing our series on Prophets of God. Today’s topic: THE CALL OF A PROPHET.

**Scripture Song:** Listen to counsel – Proverbs 19:20

- The Call of Abram from Ur of the Chaldeans
	- Genesis 12:1-9, 15:1-6 What impresses you about the call of Abram, later called Abraham?
	- What test did Abraham face when his son Isaac was a young man? Genesis 22:1-17, Hebrews 11:17-19
	- What prophetic message did Abraham give through his faithfulness to the call of the LORD?
- The Call of Elijah the Tishbite from Gilead
	- 1 Kings 17:1 What can we learn from this text about the call of Elijah to be a prophet of God?
	- 1 Kings 17:2-10a What impresses you about the response of Elijah to the call of God?
	- How does the confrontation on Mount Carmel demonstrate the prophet Elijah’s confidence in the LORD? 1 Kings 18:21-39
	- What do the events after Mount Carmel reveal about those called to be prophets of God? 1 Kings 19:1-18
- The Call of Isaiah from Jerusalem in Judea
	- Isaiah 6:1-8 What can we learn from this passage about the call of Isaiah to be a prophet of God?
	- Isaiah is called the Gospel Prophet. When were these prophecies of Isaiah fulfilled?
		- Isaiah 40:3
		- Isaiah 9:1-7
		- Isaiah 53:1-12
	- Name some occasions when individuals read from the scroll of Isaiah the prophet of God. (Luke 4:16-19, Acts 8:26-35
- The Call of Daniel, prisoner of war in Babylon
	- Daniel 1:1-19 What can we learn from this passage about the call of Daniel to be a prophet of God?
	- When was Daniel given his first opportunity to speak as a prophet of God? Daniel 2:26-28
	- What was the witness of Daniel’s life as a prophet of God? Daniel 5:11-12, 6:4
	- What do the following verses reveal about Daniel, prophet of God? Daniel 4:19, 8:27
- The Call of John the Baptist from the Judean hill country
	- When was John the Baptist called to be a prophet of God? Luke 1:76-79, Mark 1:1-8
	- What do the following verses reveal about the testimony of John the Baptist’s life as a prophet of God? Luke 3:7-15, John 3:28-30
	- What affirmation did Jesus give concerning John the Baptist, prophet of God? Matthew 11:7-11
- Lessons for our lives today
	- What do the following verses reveal about the call to be a prophet of God? John 15:18-20
	- What lessons can we learn from the call of these prophets of God?
