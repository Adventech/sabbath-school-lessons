### Prophets of God – Part 9

Welcome to Hope Sabbath School—an in-depth interactive study of the Word of God. We are continuing our series on Prophets of God. Today’s topic: TESTING THE PROPHETS.

**Scripture Song:** Listen to counsel – Proverbs 19:20

- The need to test the prophets
	- Matthew 24:24-25 What prophecy did Jesus give about false prophets?
	- What claim do these false prophets make? Matthew 7:22-23
	- What warning did Jesus give about false prophets? Matthew 7:15
	- What additional warning about false prophets was given by the Apostle John? 1 John 4:1 7:22-23
- How to distinguish between prophets of God and false prophets
	- Expect supernatural revelation
		- Numbers 12:6, Daniel 8:1-2, Exodus 3:1-6, etc.
		- What lessons can we learn from the experience of the aged prophet Daniel recorded in Daniel 10:4-21?
		- Why is it dangerous to simply use this one test to distinguish between prophets of God and false prophets? Deuteronomy 13:1-5
	- Expect harmony with the rest of the Scriptures
		- What counsel was given by the prophet Isaiah about testing the prophets? Isaiah 8:19-20
		- How does Psalm 119:105 help us when we are seeking to distinguish between a true prophet of God and a false prophet?
		- Share a time when the Word of God helped you to identify a false prophet.
	- Expect a message that uplifts Jesus Christ
		- 1 John 4:1-3
		- What message did the prophet John the Baptist boldly proclaim? John 1:29-34
		- Why is it dangerous to simply talk about Jesus without a personal surrender to Him as Savior and Lord? Acts 19:13-15
	- Expect positive fruit
		- What test did Jesus give to distinguish between a true prophet of God and a false prophet? Matthew 7:15-20
		- Name a prophet of God who bore good fruit. Daniel 6:4-5, Genesis 5:23-23 (see Jude 14), etc.
		- While true prophets of God bear good fruit, they aren’t perfect. Give some examples.
	- Expect fulfilled prophecies
		- If a prophet gives a prediction about the future, expect it to be fulfilled. Deuteronomy 18:22
		- Give an example of a conditional prophecy. Jonah 3:1-10, etc.
		- What great prophecy given by the prophet Daniel is being fulfilled in every detail? Daniel 2:29-45
