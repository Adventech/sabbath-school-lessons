### Prophets of God – Part 12

Welcome to Hope Sabbath School—an in-depth interactive study of the Word of God. We are continuing our series on Prophets of God. Today’s topic: THE PROMISE OF END-TIME PROPHETIC REVELATIONS.

**Scripture Song:** Listen to counsel – Proverbs 19:20

- The prophecy of the prophet Joel
	- Joel 2:28-32 What important insights can we gain from this prophecy of the prophet Joel?
	- Does the “great and awesome (terrible) day of the LORD” refer to the first coming of Jesus (incarnation) or the second coming of Jesus in glory?
- Fulfillment on the Day of Pentecost
	- How did the Apostle Peter apply this prophecy of the prophet Joel? Acts 2:1-21
	- What signs were manifested on the Day of Pentecost when the Holy Spirit was poured out?
	- What promise did the Apostle Peter give to those who repented of their sins and were baptized in the name of the Jesus Christ? Acts 2:28
- Fulfillment in the early Christian church
	- What evidence of the outpouring of the Holy Spirit do you see in the early Christian church? Acts 4:8, 31, 13:9,52, etc.
	- What gifts of the Holy Spirit are identified by the Apostle Paul? Ephesians 4:11,1 Corinthians 12:28, Romans 12:6-8
	- What gifts of the Holy Spirit are included in all these lists given by the Apostle Paul?
	- What is the purpose of these gifts of the Holy Spirit? Ephesians 4:12-14
	- In this series, we have been studying about prophets of God. Name some prophets of God in the early Christian church. Peter, John, Paul, Judas and Silas (Acts 15:32), Agabus (Acts 21:10-11), the four daughters of Philip the deacon (Acts 22:8-9), etc.
	- Notice some of those prophets of God wrote books of the New Testament. Others did not. Was their inspiration and authority the same or different?
- Fulfillment in the last days of earth’s history
	- Some believe the gift of prophecy ended with the early Christian church. Read Joel 2:28-32 again.
	- Why would prophets of God be needed until the “great and awesome day of the LORD” when Jesus returns in glory?
	- What are some identifying characteristics of faithful followers of Jesus in the last days of earth’s history? Revelation 12:17
	- What is the testimony of Jesus? Revelation 19:10
	- If someone emerges who claims to be a prophet of God, how should we respond? (review some of the tests of a prophet in Lesson #9)
	- Share a testimony of being blessed through the writings of a modern prophet of God.
