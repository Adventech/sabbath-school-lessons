### Prophets of God – Part 3

Welcome to Hope Sabbath School—an in-depth interactive study of the Word of God. We are continuing our series on Prophets of God. Today’s topic: OLD TESTAMENT PROPHETS.

**Scripture Song:** Listen to counsel – Proverbs 19:20

- The prophet Jahaziel
	- What prophecy did Jahaziel give in a time of national crisis for Judah? 2 Chronicles 20:14-17
	- How did King Jehoshaphat respond to the prophecy of Jahaziel? 2 Chronicles 20:18-20
	- Who were some past or present prophets of God the people of Judah would have known at this time? (mid-9th century BC) Moses, Miriam, Deborah, Samuel, Elijah, Elisha, etc.
	- What lessons can we learn from the prophecy of Jahaziel? (see 2 Chronicles 20:21-22)
- The functions of a prophet of God
	- What functions of a prophet of God are revealed in the call and ministry of the prophet Samuel? 1 Samuel 3:1-10, 19-21 8:1-10
	- What functions of a prophet of God are revealed in the call of Jonah? Jonah 1:1-3, 3:1-2
	- What functions of a prophet of God are revealed in the call of the prophet Jeremiah? Jeremiah 1:4-10
	- What functions of a prophet of God are revealed in the ministry of Huldah? 2 Kings 22:12-20
	- What can we learn from the fact that prophets were young and old, men and women? Joel 2:28-29
- The prophets of God were imperfect people
	- As we have seen from the story of Jonah, prophets of God were imperfect people.
	- James 5:17-18 What might James have been referring to when he described the prophet Elijah as a man “with a nature like ours”? (1 Kings 19:2-4)
	- What other stories in the Old Testament remind us that the prophets of God were imperfect people? Genesis 9:20-21, Numbers 12:1-10, 20:7-11, 2 Samuel 11:2-4, etc.
	- What lesson can we learn for our lives today if God can use imperfect people to speak for Him?
	- Share a time when God used you to speak for Him even though you were painfully aware of your limitations and imperfections.
- True prophets of God and false prophets
	- What lessons can we learn from the story of the prophet Micaiah about true prophets of God and false prophets? 1 Kings 22:1-17, 24-28
	- How did the LORD regard the false prophets in the days of the prophet Jeremiah? Jeremiah 14:14-15
	- What prophecy did Jesus give about false prophets? Matthew 24:24, 7:15
	- How can we distinguish between true prophets of God and false prophets? Matthew 7:16-20, 21-23, Isaiah 8:20, etc.
