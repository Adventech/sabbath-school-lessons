### Prophets of God – Part 13

Welcome to Hope Sabbath School—an in-depth interactive study of the Word of God. We are concluding our series on Prophets of God. Today’s topic: BLESSINGS FROM THE PROPHETIC WORD.

**Scripture Song:** Listen to counsel – Proverbs 19:20

- Prophets of God point us to Jesus
	- John 5:39 What testimony did Jesus give about the inspired writings of the prophets of God?
	- What revelation was given to two disciples on the day of Christ’s resurrection? Luke 24:27
	- Under the inspiration of the Holy Spirit as a prophet of God, what revelation did the Apostle John give about Jesus? John 1:1-4,14
	- Where do you see Jesus most clearly revealed in the testimony of the prophets of God?
- Prophets of God affirm the reliability of the Scriptures
	- 2 Peter 1:19-21 What inspired testimony did the Apostle Peter give about the prophets of God?
	- 2 Timothy 3:16-17 What was the testimony of the Apostle Paul about the prophets of God?
	- What other testimonies affirm the reliability of the Scriptures?
- Prophets of God share light and truth
	- Psalm 119 is an inspired testimony about the excellence of the Scriptures. Consider the following verses: Psalm 119:105,130,160, etc.
	- John 17:17 What testimony did Jesus give about the Word of God?
	- What light and truth did Jesus find in the inspired testimony of the prophet Moses when responding to the temptations of Satan? Matthew 4:4,7,10
	- What appeal did the Apostle Paul make to the young preacher Timothy? 2 Timothy 4:1-5
	- Why is the light and truth of the Scriptures so important for our lives today?
	- Share a time when your study of the prophets of God brought light and truth to your life.
- Prophets of God bring comfort and hope
	- What important reminder did the Apostle Paul give to Christians in Rome? Romans 15:4-6
	- Share a time in your life when you needed comfort and hope. What testimonies of the prophets of God were especially meaningful to you?
- Prophets of God call us to unity in Christ
	- What was the prayer of Jesus for all who would believe in Him through the testimony of the prophets and apostles? John 17:20-26
	- Read the appeal of the Apostle Paul in Philippians 2:1-4. Is the call to unity in Christ an appeal or a promise?
- A blessing promised
	- Why is the appeal of King Jehoshaphat so relevant for our lives today? 2 Chronicles 20:20
