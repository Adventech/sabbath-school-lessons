---
title:  Hamjakmani Bagwi Chapmung’-no Slaimani
date:   25/08/2026
---

Chwng nuk kha, Korinthni baksa borok bini hamjamano poito khlaiwi man ya. Tini chwng kaisa nangkukmani karon nai lai anw, abo wng kha: chapmungno slaimani (2 Korinth 1:15–2:4).

`1 Korinth 16:5–7 khúktang di. Pol tamoni bagwi puila kothoma thangnani (travel) khá chapmani tamoni bagwi ?`

Swkango Pol Korinth-o tongmani. 1 Korinth 16:5, 6 bwtango sama hai, bo Makidonia twi thangwi tei uáisa Korinth-o kiphilwi phainani kha chap kha. Masing phuru Korinth-o tongnani hinwi bo uánsugwi tongmani. Aboni ulo khe bo Makidoniani rang khangmani bai Judia thangnani muchungjak kha, aro Jerusalemni biyalo kwlainai poito khlainairokno chubana bagwi. Phiyaba bo bini kha chapmani slai phikha; tamni hwn khe Timothi bini thani Korinth’-ni kóktwma hamya tubuwi phai kha.

Timothi samani hamya koktwmano nainani bagwi Pol kha chap kha bo Epis-o thangwi Korinth-o thang phinai hinwi. Bo kwtal khe ha berainani kha chapmani wng kha Epis → Korinth → Makidonia → Korinth → Judia (2 Korinth 1:15–16). Bo Epis thang kha, aboni ulo Korinth thang kha, phiyaba aboni ulo Epis thang phikha. Bini kha chapmani slaijak kha. Bo kha chapma hai khe Korinth-o thang liya, tamni hwn ba bo aro thangwi kaham wng liya. Hakhe bo Episo phaiwi Korinthsongni bagwi koktun swikha. Bo tei uáisa jwngjal tubuna nai liya (2 Korinth 2:1, 3).

Pol Korinth-o paithak thangmano twiwi Korinthsong bujimani chaya wng kha. Bóhrok uánsuk kha Pol bóhrokno aswk hamjak liya, aswk khasuk liya hinwi (2 Korinth 1:17). Bóhrokno kók phirokmung rwna bagwi Pol bóhrokno Jisuni kothoma kahamno phunugwi rwkha. Khristoni bising twi Kaitor bini kók rwmani narwkma hai kheno jora kaham man khe bóhrokno bo thangwi naina muchungo tei kók narwknai hinwi Pol sakha (2 Korinth 1:18-22).

“ ‘Tamni hwn ba Kaitor bebak kók rwmani abo bini bising tẃi’-no “I’n” wngo. Aboni bagwi’-no chwng Kaitorno chẃngsacha khlaina bagwi Jisu Khristoni bising tẃi “Amen” hwno’ ” (2 Korinth 1:20).

Pol bini kók phirokmungo “Ih” eba “Ihi” hinwi bororokno hoirani khlaina muchung ya. Khristoni bising twi Kaitor samung khlai khe “ih simi hwnma hai” bobo “ih mangno hwnnai” hinwi sakha (2 Korinth 1:19).

Pol Korinth-o thang yaui bóhrokni thani koktun swihor kha; abo bo bóhrokno hamjakma bagwi se, hamjak yani bagwi ya (2 Korinth 2:4). Tumungno bo bóhrokni aro thangwi hamya wngwi phaimani ulo tei uáisa thang phikhe bo aro bóhrokni thani jwngjal kotor snamma se klai anw (2 Korinth 1:24; 2 Korinth 2:3). Bini uánsukmung kahamno buji jadi.
