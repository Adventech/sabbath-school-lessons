---
title:  Khá Dala Tei Khá Sutrang
date:   24/08/2026
---

Miyani swrwngmungo chwng nuk kha, Pol bo tekto birman manwi tong phuru Kaitor bono phan rwma hai khe bobo Korinthsongni birmanni jorao bóhrokno phan rwui chuba kha (2 Korinth 1:1-11). Tini chwng nai lai anw, Pol bóhrokno kwchang kwchang tei bwkha jogojogoni bising twi hamjakma phunuk kha. Hakhe bini tangpalsongbo Korinthni mothani bororokno abo hai khe phunugwi manna twi khe.

`2 Korinth 1:12–14; 2 Korinth 2:17 tei 2 Korinth 4:2 khúktang di. Bahai khe Pol khá dala wngmani abo Korinthsongno bo hamjakmanino phunuk kha?`

2 Korinth 1:12–14, bwtango soi naharmungno bo phunuk kha. Baksa borok Polno swngmung khlaio, bo kwchang kwchang wngmani tei bini phanno (authority) twiwi. Bóhrok uánsugo Pol bini uansukmarokno belai kheno sláio. Abo Chonghorrokni bagwi mwchang ya. Pol sakha, bo tei bini tangpalsong kwchang kwchang tei bwkha jogojogo khe bóhrokni thani tongo hinwi.

Pol tei bini tangpalsongni tongmungno kainwi kókthai bai khe phunukjak kha: khá dala tei khá SUTRANG (2 Korinth 1:12). “Khá dala” abo Grik kókthai _haplotēs_ ni simi phai kha. Aboni kókmang wng kha kók sathani koinene tei kwcham kwchang hwnmani (Epis 6:5; Kolosai 3:22). “Khá sutrang” hwnmani abo (Grik kókthai-o khe _eilikrineia_) uánsukmung kubui tei Kwtharno se sajago.

Pol khá dala wngmani tei khá sutrang wngmani abo Kaitorni aroni se phaio. New English Translation Bible-o abono kwlaisa khe bujina twi khe swijak kha: “kwthar naharmung tei khà sutrang abo Kaitorni aroni se phaio” (2 Korinth 1:12). Abo “Kaitor khairokma bai se wngo” hinwi a kaisa bwtangono Pol sakha.

Polno chajak ya bororok bini koktunno poriui chaya khe saui khna rw khwna (2 Korinth 1:13, 14). Phiya Polni uánsukmung tamo bo bóhrokno swrai swrai buji di hinwi sakha. Pol sakha, bini uansukma, abo “Subrai Jisuni Salo” swrai swrai khe phunukjaknai (2 Korinth 1:14).
