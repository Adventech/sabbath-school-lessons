### O Hatibarsani Swrwngjaknai
2 Korinth 1:3–14, 2 Korinth. 2:17, 2 Korinth 4:2, 1 Korinth 16:5–7, 2 Korinth 7:5–13, 2 Korinth 2:5–17.

> <p>Muitu Bwtang</p>
> “Ang uana twtwi, khá yok twtwi, muktwi klai twtwi se norokno abono swihoro; ang norokno khá hamya khlaina bagwi swi ya, norok bebakno’-no ang bwswk hamjak saui man rwna bagwi se swi-o” (2 Korinth 2:4, KOKBSI).

Chonghor Polni langma le aswk kwlai ya. Alongo tong twtwi tei tong kesebo tong twtwi phano bo swikha: “Judairok bai ang uáibaui phungthamchichuku swprakjak kha; Rumrok bai ang uáitham bujak kha, uáisa holong khitarwi rwjak kha; ang rungtor kwlwk thani uáitham manjak kha, uáisa ang twi-o horsa salsa ma tong kha. Aswk lama-o tháng thani ang twi kotorobo thwi kha mun, dagaitni yagobo thwi kha mun; ang ani bororokni yagobo thwi kha mun, buini bororokni yagobo thwi kha mun; ang taunobo thwi kha mun, bolongobo thwi kha mun, twijlangobo thwi kha mun, takhuk tatalrokni yagobo thwi kha mun. Ang kolomtwi khiklaiwi samungbo ma tang kha, ang thu yauibo kwbángma ma tong kha, ok khuibo ma tong kha, twi kangwibo ma tong kha; ang ok pung yauibo kwbángma ma tong kha, rí kwrwini bai kwchangwibo ma tong kha. Abono karwibo bebak motharokni bagwi khá súgwibo ang sal brúm brúm poja bokjagwi ma tongo” (2 Korinth 11:24–28).

Korinthsongno Pol swihor thani chwng nuk kha motharokni bagwi bini “khá súgwi” tongmani. Bo motharokno hamjakmani abo kwlaiwi thang liya. Abo Khristo chwngno hamjakmani kwlaiwi thang liya wngma hai. Jisuni thani Mothano hamjaknani Pol swrwng kha; abo hai kheno Jisu chwngno hamjakmani ba (2 Korinth 5:14; naidi 1 Korinth 11:1).

`*August 29, Sabat salni bagwi o hatibarsani rwngchapno swrwngwi tiyar wng di.`
