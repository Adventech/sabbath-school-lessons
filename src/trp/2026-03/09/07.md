Ellen G. White swimani _‘The Acts of the Apostles’_ bijapni “The Message Heeded” beremno khúktang di, bwkáng 323–334.

“Jotoni slái birman mannai bororok se buinobo kha-o phan rwui khakchangma rwi mano, bóhrok thang thanio tongthokni pohor pirwi rwo. Birman abo Kaitorni thani bwkha beramungno swkwma ya, tank khe bo hanjagwi phiyokmano se sai man rwo. Abo Kaitor khairokma tei naikolmano se phunugo.”—Ellen G. White, _God’s Amazing Grace,_ p. 122.

“Kaitorni thani sak yaphar súgwi tongnai Khristianni langma abo buini thani pohor, khakchangma tei kha tongthok ma tubuo. Abo hamjakma bai twiduljakma se. Omo hai langma-o Khristo kuplung khe tongo, a langmao buino bagwi pohor rwnai hamari tongo.”—_God’s Amazing Grace,_ p. 122.

“Mothani chayarokno Chonghor Pol charwnani nang kha, phiyaba Mothani chayarokno twiwi bo saphuru kwchang kwchang khe se tongo. Bo hus khlaiwi samung khlaio. Hakhe bororok sai mano, bo borok hamyarokni kiching hinwi. Bo bóhrokno buji rwo. Bo bóhrokno phunugo, bo bóhrokno naikolo, tei bóhrok se bini thani nangkukmani hinwi.”—Ellen G. White Comments, in _The SDA Bible Commentary,_ vol. 6, p. 1094.

**Salaijaknai Swngmungrok**

`2 Korinth 2:1–14 bwtango Pol Kaitorni samungno twiwi bini uánsukmani kókno sakha. Tamoni bagwi Kaitorni samung tang thani-o tongmung/gwrwng belai nangkukmani?`

`Pol bo ha beraimani kha chapmungno slaimani bising twi chwngbo Khristoni samungo koinene wngna nangmani twiwi chwngno tamo phrwng? Tamoni bagwi nangmani jorao khe kha chapmarokno slainani nang?`

`Bo Kaitorni samung tangwi birman tei tektorok man kha. Bo sakha, Mothani twiphangrokbo borokno wngma ba, bóhrokno birman tekto bai ma manjago. Mothani twiphang eba bokhorokrokni pojano heleng rwna bagwi mothani adongrok tamo khlaiwi man?`

`Pol bo Khristoni bising twi kwplai wngmani kók (2 Korinth 2:14) sanani swkang bo uanajakmani kók sakha (2 Korinth 2:13). Kaisa jorao bahai khe bo bini kebel wngmani tei phan manmani kókno sai man kha? Chwng bahai khe abo hai khlai mannai?`
