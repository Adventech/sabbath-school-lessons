---
title:  Hambai Rwmani
date:   23/08/2026
---

`2 Korinth 1:3–7 khúktang di. O bwtango Pol tamoni bagwi hambai rwkha?`

Pol hambai rwmani karonrok wng kha, birmanni jorao Kaitor khá kwchang rwmani eba chubamani bagwi. O bwtango chubao eba khá kwchang rwmani Grik khwlai (verb) kókthai _parakaleō_ tei mung (naun) kókthai _paraklēsis_ uaichi thansa khe núkjak kha. 2 Korinth bijap-o o kókthai uainwichichuku (29) tongo. Phiyaba oro khe khakthamni bere khaksa khobjak kha. Kaitor “wng kha maya gwnang Apha, bebago’-no chubanai Kaitor. Kaitor bai chwng chubajakma hai podrepot tekto manjagwi tongnairokno chwng chubaui manna tẃi khe chwng tekto man brúm brúm bo chwngno chuba-o” hinwi phunukjak kha (2 Korinth 1:3, 4).

Kaitorni thani chubamung eba khá kwchangmung manmani abo chwng chini thani simi tonina bagwi ya (2 Korinth 1:4, 5). Tamo khe bwkha tekto manwi Kaitorni chubamung eba khá kwchang rwmung mannai borok, buinobo abo hai kheno chubathai.

Pol buino chubaui eba khá kwchang rwui man kha; tamni hwn ba bo sak baithang birmanni bisingo Kaitor bai chubajak kha. “Chwng sátokjak khe abo norokno chubana bai mwthangna bagwi se; … phiya chwng sátokjagwi soima hai’-no norokbo khá logwi soiwi manna tẃi khe phan rwjago” (2 Korinth 1:6). Omo wng kha hamjakma!

`2 Korinth 1:8-11 khúktang di. Pol tamono twiwi hambai rwmani phunuk kha?`

Pol bo tektorokni kókno sakha, abo “poja kotor bokjakmani abo panjalwi hilik” hinwi bo sakha. Bini tangpalrok khe uánsuk kha abo se bóhrokni langmani paithak wng khwna hinwi (1 Korinth 1:8). Swrapsa joroani bagwi bóhrok uánsuk kha bóhrokni khatungma kwthwini mwthangwi tisajaknani simi se tong sio. Phiyaba Kaitor bóhrokno chuba kha, tei tong halok slaijak phikha (2 Korinth 1:10). Thwinani kiriui tongmani simi (2 Korinth 1:8) Kaitor bóhrokno phiyoknai hwnmani khatungmao bóhrok sogwi phikha (2 Korinth 1:10). Laijak jorao Kaitor kwplai khlaimani abo chwngno khatungma rwo thinangobo abo hai khe bo chwngno chubanai hinwi. Tektorok chini langmao Kaitor wngnani rwo, chwng bini thani teibo bwkhaui tong manna twi khe. Tektorokni bising twi se chwng teibo pholao torwi eba Kaitor bai sampa kugwi mano. Pol hambai rwmani karonrok wng kha Kaitor bóhrokni surimano khnamani, tei Kaitor chwngno phiyok phuru chwng hambai rwna muchungmani (2 Korinth 1:11).
