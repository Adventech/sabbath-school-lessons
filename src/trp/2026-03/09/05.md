---
title:  Nakarmung Tei Haino Hai Hamjakma Phunukma
date:   26/08/2026
---

Pol Korinth-o uáinwi thang yaui Epis-o kiphilwi thangmani ulo kaisa koktun hor kha, abo ulo “belai kwrak koktun” hinwi sinijak kha (2 Korinth 2:3,4; 2 Korinth 7:8, 12).

`2 Korinth 7:5–13 khúktang di. Pol bóhrokno koktun swimani ulo tamo wng kha, tei bóhrok je wngmano twiwi Polni bwkha tamo wng kha?`

Yagulo Makedonia-o Pol bai Tit malai lai kha. Aro Tit Polno kóktwma kaham sakha, Polni kwrak kókthai abo bwthai kaham tubu kha, hakhe abo bai Pol belai tongthokjak kha. Swkango Korinthni baksa bororok Polno chajak ya wngwi tongmani, phiya tabuk bóhrok Polni bwkhago tong kha. Bokhorok eba twiphangrokno bórom rwmani abo belai nangkukmani! Mothani borok wngwi chwng bóhrokni pojano heleng rwui chubaui mano.

`2 Korinth 2:5–11 khúktang di. Oroni kókbothoma tamo?`

Oroni kókbothoma wng kha Mothani raida tei tongmungno twiwi. Sumsok gwnang borokno bahai khe nainai abono twiwi oro nangkukmani kók sajak kha. Abono twiwi swkango salaimani (1 Korinth 5:1-5).

Oroni kókbothoma-o sajak kha, Mothani raida tei tongmungnj naharmung wng kha nakarmungni bising twi sumsok gwnang borokno tei uáisa hamjaknani, tei bono phiyok phinani (2 Korinth 2:6-8, 10). Baksa Motha-o khe hamjakma tei khairokmano simi uánsugwi Mothani sumsokno twiwi mungsa yapri nahar ya. Baksa mothao khe sumsok khlainairokno nakar ya belai kwrak raida khlaio. Mothao sumsok tong khe abono ma naithai, phiya hamjakma bai khe se abono naina nangnai. Aboni bagwino Pol sakha, chaya khlainaino tei uáisa ma hamjak phinai (2 Korinth 2:8). Tamni hwn ba bo sakphangbo Mothano belai hamjago (2 Korinth 2:8)!

`Sumsok khlainai chaya borokno Korinth motha hamjak phirwi man kha (2 Korinth 2:8). Tamni hwn ba bóhrok Polni hamjakmani bising twi Kaitorni hamjakmano yachagwi man kha. Hamjakmano twiwi oro chwngno tamo phrwng?`
