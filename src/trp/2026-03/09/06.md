---
title:  Khristoni Bising Twi Kwplai
date:   27/08/2026
---

`2 Korinth 2:12, 13 khúktang di. Pol Korinthsongno “belai kwrak koktun” swimani ulo boro thang kha? Bo aro thangwi tamo khlai kha?`

Titno naisingwi tong phuru Pol belai uanajak kha (2 Korinth 7:5, 6). Phiyaba abo hai jorao phano bo Jisuni kókno samani mwthagwi man liya (2 Korinth 2:12). Bo Jisuno belai kheno hamjago. Aphuru phano bo sai man ya kho, bo koktun swimani bwthai tamo wng kha. Bo Tit bai malainani bagwi tei Korinthsong bini koktun manwi tamo wng kha abórok khnana bagwi bwkha muchungjak.

Troa-o Polni samung kaham bwthai man kha, “haiphano bo aro kwbang jora tong langwi man liya. Bebak mothano twiwi bini uánsukma tongo, aroni bisingo Korinthsongni bagwibo. Troa-o Tit bai malainai tei bo Korinthsongno koktun swimani bwthai tamo wng kha bono swngnai hinwi Pol kha kaui tongmani. Phiyaba Tit sók phai liya. Hakhe bo bwkha tongthokjak liya. Bo swikha, ‘Ang bwkha tongthok liya; tamni hwn ba ani takhuk Tit bai malai liya.’ Hakhe bo Troa yakarwi Makidonia thang kha. Aboni ulo Philipi thang khe bo Timothi bai malai kha.”—Ellen G. White, _The Acts of the Apostles,_ p. 323.

`2 Korinth 2:14–17 khúktang di. Makidonia-o Tit bai malaiwi tei Korinthsongni kóktwma kaham manwi Polni bwkha bubo hai ma tong kha?`

Pol tongthokjagwi, bo sakha Kaitor chwngno “sal brúm brúm kwplaisa Khristoni misilo tẃi-o” (2 Korinth 2:14). Bwswk se uanamasingcha kók! Bwkhao Khristo tong khe, abo “báhai motom hai khe Khristoni kók chini yak tẃi ha gwnangwi pírwi tongo” (2 Korinth 2:14).

Pol Khristoni thanio tongthokjak kha; tamni hwn ba bo kha khamma bai khe koktun swimani abo bo khatungma hai kheno bwthai kaham tubu kha (2 Korinth 7:5-9). Abo wng kha kotor kwplai wngmani. A jorao 2 Korinth 2:17 bwtango Pol teibo sakha, bo khoroksa Chonghor wngwi khá sutrang khe Khristoni mungwi kók sa-o hinwi (2 Korinth 2:17; 2 Korinth 1:12). Tatal khe samung tangnaisa hwn khe sakni kahamni bagwi simi kók piro, phiya kubui seleng hwn khe Khristono hamjakmani tei khá sutrang bai khe Kaitorni kók piro.
