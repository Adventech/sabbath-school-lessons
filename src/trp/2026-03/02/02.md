---
title:  Krus’-ni Kothoma Kaham
date:   05/07/2026
---

Pol sakha, Krusni kók wng kha Kaitorni phan chwngno rwmani. “Jisu Khristo bai bini kruso butharjakma” hwnwi bini kók pirmani bisingo abo uanamasingcha kók ya (1 Kor. 2:2).

`1 Korinth 1:17-31 khúktang di. Pol o bwtango tamo nangkukmani kok sakha?`

1 Korinth 1:18–31 bwtango, Pol borokni butua wngma bai Kaitorni simungni bisingo kaisa choba wngmani kókno sakha. Krusni bisingo borokni hamya tei Kaitorni kahamno phunuknani phan tongo. 1 Korinth 1:17 bwtangni bising twi o kókno chengsaui sajak kha. Tamni hwnba, Khristoni Krusni phan kwrwi wngwi tongna nangya (1 Kor. 1:17), aboni bagwino Krusni kok chini kothoma kaham pirmani bisingo kotor jagano narwkna nangnai (1 Kor. 2:2).

Pol sakha, bo baptisma rwna bagwi ya, phiya Krusni kok kaham pirna bagwi se rohorjago. O kokno sana bagwi kainwi nangkukmani naichommung nangnai. Puila, “rohorna” hwnwi swijakmani Grik kokthai wng kha _apostellō_, abo chonghor kokthaini simi phaimani. Hakhe, Polni kotor chonghorrokni samung wng kha Kothoma Kaham pirmani samung. Tei kaisa kok, baptisma wngnani kokno Pol samani abo baptisma wngmani abo belai nangkukmani kok ya abo hai buya, tamo khe kók pirmani tongna nangma kókno se sana naio. Aboni bagwino khoroksa borokno bo kutuk kha. A borok baptisma nakha, phiyaba Jisuno khe kebengwi tongo.

“Elem gwnangrok kók sama” (1 Kor. 1:17) abo hamya hinwi Pol samani ya. Phiyaba krusni kókno borokni elem kebengthai kwrwi. Oro “elem gwnangrokni kók” hwnmani abo wng kha Grik tei Rumni elemni kókno se sajago. Athens-o Pol kók samani wng kha jukti gwnang, soi simung gwnang, tei nukmung (philosophy) gwnang kók. Phiyaba aboni bwthai kisa simi se mano. Hakhe “kubuini tangraida slainani bagwi bo chongwi nakha. Bo aswk kók totra rwlaimungo thang liya. Krusni kothoma kahamno simi bo sakha.”—Ellen G. White, _The Acts of the Apostles,_ p. 244.

`Tamni bagwi Jisu Khristo tei Krus sakao thwimani kokno Korinth-o pirmani bising twi Athens-o juktini kók, soi simung tei nukmung (philosophy ) bai samung khlaimani slai kwbang bwthai man kha? Phiya juktini kók, soi simung tei nukmungni (philosophy) bising twi kothoma kaham pir thanio samung chaui mano de?`
