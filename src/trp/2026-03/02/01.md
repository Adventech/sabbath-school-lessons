### O Hatibarsani Swrwngjaknai
1 Kor. 1:17–31, Kol. 1:20, 1 Pit. 2:24, Samunh 13:16–47, 1 Kor. 2:1–5.

> <p>Muitu Bwtang</p>
> “Khristo kruso thwimani kók abo komorwi tongnairokni thani samung nang ya, tamo khe chwng mwthangjaknairokni thani abo Kaitorni phan” (1 Korinth 1:18, KOKBSI).

Cicero, khoroksa pagan Rumni swikwrwng tei kok sanai borok, Rumni borokrokno sakha, krusni uansukmung wng kha yagulo sujakmani kók se hinwi. Abo bóhrokni uánsuk bai gothok ya. Cicero Jisu achaimani bisira khaksa swkang thwiwi thang phano, bini kokrok Rumni borokrok krusno bahai khe sak nang ya khe romwi tongmani abo phunukgo. Krus abo belai kheno hamya wngma bai bórok abono uansukna samung nangjak ya.

Hakhe, Pol hai hwnwi swi kha: “Krusni kok ... Kaitorni phan” (1 Kor. 1:18). Polni bagwi, Krus wng kha Kaitor bai borokni bisingo kwthalaimani manwi (Epis 2:16, Kol. 1:20), Jisuni koinene wngmani kotorkuk mari (Phil. 2:8), tei chini belai kotor lakai sujakmani jaga (Kol. 2:14).

Krus wng kha Korinthni jwngjalrokni thani Polni kók phirokmung. Motha-o khakjak laiwi tongnai chwng nugwi mano, abono jwngjal kotor. Pol kha kaham yapharmani, tei hambai rwnani ulo belai kheno uanajak chwng nugwi mano (1 Kor. 1:1-3; 1 Kor. 1:4-9). Omono bo puila kok samani (1 Kor. 1:10-17). O hatibaro chwng o jwngjalni tei Korinthni kubun jwngjalrokni kók phirokmung Krusni phan gwnang kokno twiwi uansuknai.

_July 11, Sabat salni bagwi o hatibarsani rwngchapno swrwngwi tiyar wng di._
