---
title:  Masia Kruso Thwi Kha
date:   08/07/2026
---

Pol swi kha, “Judairok soidomni samung nukna nai-o, Grikrok khe kók elemi nai-o” (1 Korinth 1:22). Kaitor naima twi Masia Kruso thwimani kók abo Judairok uánsukmani hai soidomni samung ya. Abo Grikrok uánsukmani hai elem buya. Abo bóhrok uánsukmani bai gothok ya.

Jisu kruso thwimani kókno Sagrirok bahai khe sakha naiwi naidi (Mark 8:31, 32; Mark 9:30–32; and Mark 10:32–34). Judairok uánsugo, tumungno Jisu Masia hwn khe Rumsongno mechen anw hinwi. Phiyaba Jisu swngcharni phan bai bóhrokno “mechen” liya.

Bisi kwbangma romwi Khristianrokni bagwi krus kaisa poitoni mari wngwi phai kha. Phiyaba 21st century ni molo krusni kókno Khristianrok bujinani belai kútuk wngjak kha. Bóhrok 1st century ni molo wngma hai khe uánsuk phikha.

Jisu kruso thwimani abo Kaitor borokno bwswk mwthangna muchungjak abono se phunugo. Krus abo bororok bwswk kirimasingcha sumsok khlai kha abonobo phunugo. Hakhe Jisuno sininai tei bono khulumnai Chúknohani Suarirokni thani a kók bwswk se uanamasingcha!

`Samung 13:16-47 (26, 38, tei 47) khúktang di. O bwtangrok chwngno Krusni kokno tamo phwrwng?`

Pol sao, Khristo bono kothoma kaham pirna bagwi se rohoro. Hakhe Khristo kruso thwimani kók bo pir kha (1 Korinth 1:23). 1 Korinth 2:1-5 bwtango Pol o kókno saui khna rwkha. Chonghor Pol wng kga Khristoni samung bermano sói khe kók narwgwi khlainaisa. Bo “belai sinai hai khe kók kuthuk kuthuk” bai kók saya (1 Korinth 2:1). Tamo khe bo kók samani wng kha “Jisuni kók” tei “bo kruso thwimani kók” simi se (1 Korinth 2:2). Bini kók samani abo “kók kwrwng kók sinai hai khe sa ya, tamo khe Kaitorni Pholani phan bai ang samani a kók sói hwnwi se phunukjak kha” (1 Korinth 2:4). Tamni hwn ba “poito khlaimani abo borokni elem bai wng ya, Kaitorni phan bai se wngo” (1 Korinth 2:5).

`Kruso butharjaknai Mesia wng kha Judairok bai Grikrok uansuk mani hai ya. Chwng uánsukma hai khe máng Kaitorbo samung khlai ya hinwi chwng o kókni bising twi tamo swrwng man?`
