---
title:  Teibo Uánsukmung
date:   10/07/2026
---

Ellen G. White swimani _‘The Desire of Ages’_ bijap ni “Kalbario” rwngchpno khúktang di, bwkang- 741–757.

“Tabukni jorao tongnai lukurokni bwkhao, Kalbari krus abo kwthar muiturok hinwi phunukjak yaksi yagrao tongo. Jisu kruso thwimani bagwi a krusno bóhrok kwthar hinwi borom khlai laio. Tamo khe Paulni jorao krusno khá hamya tei kirima bai se naisik lai-o. Krus sakao thwinai borokno borokrokni mwthangnai hwnwi bóhrok uánsugwi se man ya, abo naturally lachimasingcha tei kirimasingcha hinwi se bóhrok nugwi tongo.

“Korinth kamiyungo tongnai Grik tei Judairok nini kókno khibinai hinwi Pol kaham kheno saui mano. … Phiyaba bini kók khnanai Judairokni bisingo kwbangma bono jolijaknai tongnai. Grikrok khe bini kókno khnaui mwnwi kothok kók wngjaknai. Bóhrok uánsugo krus abo hamya tei lachimasingcha se tubu-o, khatungma, tei bororokno mwthangmani tubu ya.

“Phiyaba Pol ni thani krusni kók pirmani belai tongthok kukmani se. Krusno chẃngsacha khlaimani Pol mwthak liya. Jisu Khristo thwi phuru Kaitorni páiphlai ya hamjakma phunukjakma hai kheno Polbo phunukjak kha. Aphuruni simi bini langma slaijak kha. Bo poito khlaio, sumsok gwnang bororok o hamajakmano buji khe bóhrokbo sláijagwi mannai, tei Jisuni kókno simi uánsuknai.”—Ellen G. White, _The Acts of the Apostles,_ pp. 245, 246.

**Salaijaknai Swngmungrok**

`Getsimani bario Jisu sakha, “Apha, man khe ba o khurino ani oroni nor di” (Mat. 26:39). O bwtangni bising twi Jisu Kruso chini sumsokni lakai sumano twiwi tamo saui man kha?`

`Pol sao, “Kaitor si ya wng phano bo bororokni slái si-o” (1 Korinth 1:25). Bahai lamani bising twi borokni elem bai Kaitorni elem belai juda wng?`

`Judai tei Grikrok Jisu Kruso thwimani kókno samung chuk ya wngjak kha. Tabukni molo chwng baibelni tamo kókno pir khe bororok abono samung chuk ya wngjak? Tamoni bagwi?`

`Pol sao, “Kaitorni Pholano man ya borok bo Kaitorni Phola rwmarokno yachagwi man ya” (1 Korinth 2:14). Bororokni bwkhao nangna twi khe bahai khe chwng Jisuni kókno sanai? Eba chini tongmung bai khe bóhrok khao nangjagwi man?`
