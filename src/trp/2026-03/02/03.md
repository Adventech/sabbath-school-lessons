---
title:  Komorwi Tongnairokni Thani Samung Nang Ya
date:   06/07/2026
---

Kaitorni simung bai borokni samung chukya butua wngmani bisingo choba wngwi tongo. Omono twiwi Pol sakha, “Khristo kruso thwimani kók abo komorwi tongnairokni thani samung nang ya” (1 Korinth 1:18). 1 Korinth 1:18-31 bwtangni bisingo “butua” hwnmani kókthai uaidok sajak tongo. Phiya omo wng kha puilani kókthai. [English Bible naidi, aro samung nang ya hinwi sa yaui foolishness hinwi sao.]

`1 Korinth 1:20, 21, 23, 25, tei 27 khúktang di. Khristo kruso thwimani kók abo komorwi tongnairokni thani samung nang ya hinwi Pol sa thani-o “samung nang ya” [butua] hwnmani bising twi Polni kók bujinani chwngno bahai khe chuba kha?`

1 Korinth 1:18 bwtango “Samung nang ya” [butua] hwnmani Grik kókthai bai _mōria_ hino. A kókthai Swmai Kwtal bijap-o uaiba simi sajak kha. Abórok bebak 1 Korinth-o mano (1 Kor. 1:18, 21, 23; 1 Kor. 2:14; 1 Kor. 3:19).

1 Korinth 1:18, 23 bwtango “samung nang ya” (butua) hinwi Pol samani abo simung kwrwini kókno sajakmani ya. Tamo khe Kaitorno kebengmani tongmungni kókno se sajago.

O kamiyungo Polni tong halokni kókno uansuk di. A kamiyungni bororok belai kheno sakno sinai, rwngnai, tei simung gwnang hinwi uánsugwi kungchuk laio. Aboni bagwino bo Galilini Judai khoroksa, Nazarethni Jisuni kokno sakha, bono Rumsong kruso tisa kha, tei aboni ulo kwthwini thangwi bacha kha—abo bebak borokni sumsokni bagwi. O borok serious wngwi mano de? Bo sabono joke khlai tongkha? O kok khe kaisa kwrak kwtal philosophyni uansukmung ya, eba, philosophyni manwirok bai parse tei analysis khlaiwi mannai; abo wngkha uanamasingcha, uanamasingcha, jesa phano smart tei swrwngnai Korinthsarok belai kheno uansukwi mannai hwnwi uansukjak ya. Phiyaba Pol pirmani kókno bóhrok sakni simung gwnang wngjakma bai sak nang liya.

Polni kókno samung nangjak ya wngmani tei kaisa karon wng kha, aroni bororok takjak mwtaino khulumnairokni ganao tongo. Krusni kók abo Judairokni thani belai kheno hamya. Masia Rumrokni yago butharjagwi da man? Bóhrok uánsugo, Masia Rumsongno se mechenwi rwnai, tei Kruso thwi glak hinwi.

Tamo khe chwng drobni simino Pol Korinth-o belai kebengjak kha. Phiyaba Judai tei buini borok kothoma kahamno yachaknairokbo tong kha.

Oro tamo kók kwcharmung tong?

Chwng kebengmung bai malai phano, aro kubuino yachaknai Kaitorni bororokbo tongo hinwi sina nango.
