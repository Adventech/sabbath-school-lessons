---
title:  Khristo Wng Kha Kaitorni Phan Tei Elem
date:   09/07/2026
---

1 Korinth 1:19, 20, 30, tei 31 bwtango Pol sakha, borokni elem bai Kaitorni elem belaino pher laio. Kaitor bai choba khlainai abo hai borokni elemno Pol samung nang ya khlaiwi kók sakha. Borokni a elem borokno sumsokni mwthangwi man ya. Phiyaba Kaitorni phan tei elem a Khristo simi se khlaiwi mano. Khamao phunukjakno nai jadi:

| Chwng mwthangjaknairokni thani | [Khristo kruso thwimani kók abo] Kaitorni phan. | 1 Korinth 1:18 |
| Tamo khe Kaitor bai ringjaknairokni thani | Khristo se, … Kaitorni phan | 1 Korinth 1:24 |

1 Korinth 1:18 tei 1 Korinth 1:24 o kainwi bwtangono phunugo Khristo wng kha Kaitorni phan. Kók wng kha bororokno sumsokni mwthangnani bini thani phan tongo. “Aboni slái Kaitor chwng pírmani a samung nang ya kók bai poito khlainairokno mwthangna hwnwi khá chong kha” (1 Korinth 1:21). “Mwthangjaknairok” (1 Korinth 1:18), “poito khlainairok” (1 Korinth 1:21), tei “Kaitor bai ringjaknairok” (1 Korinth 1:24) hinwi samani abo kaisa bodolni bororokno se sajago. Bóhrok wng kha poito khlaima bising twi mwthangjakmungno sai mannairok. “Khristoni Kothoma Kaham . . . omo poito khlainai joto borokno mwthangna bagwi Kaitorni phan” (Rum 1:16).

Khristo Kaitorni phan simi ya, phiya Kaitorni elembo kwlaio. Kók wng kha bini bising twi se Kaitor sumsokni jwngjalni yóknani lama snamo. Borokni elem bai a jwngjalni yakni yogwi man ya. Swngcharni elem bai bororok Kaitorno siniui man ya (1 Korinth 1:21). Khristoni bising twi simi se mwthangjakmungno twiwi chwng sinai wngwi mano (2 Timothi 3:15).

`1 Korinth 1:24–29 khúktang di. O bwtango “samung nang ya” [butua], “phan kwrwi,” “phan,” tei “sinai” hinwi sajakmani kókthairokno naichom jadi. Bo tamo kók saui tongmani?`

1 Korinth 1:24–29 bwtango “samung nang ya” [butua], “phan kwrwi” hinwi samani wng kha borokni elemni kókno se sao. Bóhrok krusni kókno buji ya. “Tamni hwn ba Kaitor si ya wng phano bo bororokni slái si-o, phiya Kaitor phan kwrwi wng phano bo bororokni slái phan gwnang” (1 Korinth 1:25). Omo Kaitor phan kwrwi eba siya hwnmani ya. Tamo khe omo bo borokni slái bo bwswk se phan gwnang tei elem gwnang abono se phunugo.

`Naichom jadi: “O swngcharni uansukma hai hwn khe norokni bisingo elem gwnangbo báng tha ya, phan gwnangbo báng tha ya, kolok kotorbo báng tha ya” (1 Korinth 1:26). Omo chwngno tamo samani klai kha?`
