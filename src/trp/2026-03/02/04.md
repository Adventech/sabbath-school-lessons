---
title:  Mwthangjaknairokni Thani Abo Kaitorn Phan
date:   07/07/2026
---

1 Korinth 1:18 bwtangni kók belai sraisrai. Krusni kók abo bororokni thani-o se. Kaitorno kebengnairokni thani khe abo samung chuk ya. Phiyaba mwthangjaknairokni thani abo kaitorn phan.

`Kolosai 1:20 tei 1 Pitor 2:24 pori khe nugo. Jisu chini bagwi kruso thwiwi tamo khlai kha?`

Pol sakha, “Khristo kruso thwimani abo samung nang ya wng ya tẃi khe elem gwnangrok kók sama hai sa yaui Kothoma kaham pír di hwnwi se bo ano rohoro” (1 Korinth 1:17). Krusni kók abo bahai khe borokni elem wng yaui Kaitorni phan chwngno bujinani chubao (1 Korinth 1:18).

“Komornairokni thani” hinwi 1 Korinth 1:18 bwtango sajakmani Grik kókthaini bwtango khe bóhrokni samungni manthai hinwi se sajago. Grik khwlai (verb) kókthai _apollymi_ (“komornairokni thani”) hwnmani kókmang wng kha sokomorjaknairokni thani hwnmani se (Johon 10:10). Hakhe apollymi kókthaino 1 Korinth 1:19 bwtango “sokomornai” hinwi kókslaijak kha.

1 Korinth 1:18 bwtangno Baibelni kubui hinwi buji rwnani bagwi bwtang 19 o Pol Jisaia 29:14 bwtangni kókno khurchaui phunuk kha. Borokni elem bai sakno kungchuknairokno Kaitor sokomornai.

Phiyaba “mwthangjaknairokni thani abo kaitorn phan” (1 Korinth 1:18). A mwthangjakmung Kaitorni aroni phaio. Pol sana nai kha, chwng mwthangjak kha, phiya chwng sak sakno mwthang ya. Chwng sakno mwthangwi se man ya. Poito khlainairokno Kaitor se mwthango (1 Korinth 1:21). Khristo Kruso thwimani bising twi bororokno Kaitor rwmanino khibi khe bóhrok butua wngwi sak baithangno komormungno tubunai (1 Korinth 1:30).

`“Sumsok khlai khe ma thwinai, tamo khe Kaitor hai’-no rwmani abo wng kha chini Subrai Khristo Jisu bai thansa wngma bising tẃi chwngno mwthang phlainai” (Rum 6:23). 1 Korinth 1:18, 19 bwtango Pol samani kók bai o bwtangni kógo tamo gothokma tong?`
