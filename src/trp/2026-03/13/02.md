---
title:  Jisuni Khairokma
date:   20/09/2026
---

Chwng 2 Korinth bijapni paithago, Jisuni khairokma kokno sajakmani kokno nukmani abo bijap cheng phuruni hai kheno nuk kha (2 Kor. 1:2, 2 Kor. 13:14). Pol o kóktunno bini khairokma kókno saui cheng kha tei pái rwkha. Chwng o quarter chwng phuruni kokno nukmani hai, bo Jisuni kokno uansukma tei samani mwthagwi man ya.

“Chini Subrai Jisu Khristo bwswk khairok kha norok abono saui mano; bo kwrwi wngma bai norokno gwnang khlaina bagwi bo gwnang phano norokni bagwi kwrwi wng kha” (2 Korinth 8:9).

Jisuni khairokma bwswk se uanamasingcha tei khao nangmani! Bo Chúknohani mol brúm tongnai gwnangno yakarwi kwrwi wng phai kha. Bo kwcham molni Galilini hadlwi lamano him phai kha. “Bo sakno borok kusu snam kha, hakhe kók khnaui thwina lama himna gosi kha, kruso suda thwina gosi kha” (Philipi 2:8). Bo chwngno “gwnang” khlaina bagwi tei Chúknoha-o bo bai chwng tongwi manna twi khe se abo hairok khlaio. Chwng sumsok, thwimung, tei birman gwnang o swngcharno simi sinimani ba, Jisu Chúknohani chẃngsachano yakarwi chini bagwi bini sakno yapharmani bwswk morok tei mulaja gwnang abono bujinani belai tukjagwi mano.

`Rum 16:20, Galatia 6:18, Philipi 4:23, and 1 Thesaloniki 5:28 khúktang di. O bwtangrogo nangkukmani kók phrwngmani tamo nuk kha?`

Pol bini bebak koktunno Jisuni khairokmani kókno sakha. Abórokni bisingo mulaja gwnang samani kókrok wng kha: “Jisu Khristo hwnwi a borok khoroksa khairokma bising tẃi Kaitor aswk bororokno hai’-no rwmani abobo báng kugo” (Rum 5:15). Khairokmani o kwbangma yachaknairok “bebakno Khristo tẃi kwplai wngwi thangwi tongnai” (Rum 5:17). 2 Korinth hai khe, Pol Jisuni khairokma kók bai se bo kubuni bini koktunrokno chengo, tei mwthago (Rum 1:7, Rum 16:20, 1 Korinth 1:3, 1 Korinth 16:23, Gal. 1:3, Gal. 6:18, Phil. 1:2, Phil. 4:23). Bini o uánsukmung bwswk nangkukmani abo Korinthsongbo uánsuk thung hinwi bo muchungjago.

Abo bebak Mothani bagwino Polni muchungma. Bo Epissongno tamo sakha naichom jadi: “Chini Subrai Jisu Khristono hamjak phlaiwi tongnai bebak bororokno Kaitor khairok thung” (Epis 6:24). Aboni slái kisa khe de chwng Jisuno hamjakna bagwi Pol sakha, eba hamjak phaiwi tongna bagwi? Bini muchungma wng kha, Jisuni khairokma “teibo borok kwbángma saui man rwrwk” thung (2 Korinth 4:15). Kaitorni khairokma bini bagwi wngma haino, buini bagwibo wng wng thung hinwi bo muchungjago (2 Korinth 12:9).
