### O Hatibarsani Swrwngjaknai
2 Korinth 8:9; Rum 16:20; 1 Johon 4:8–11; 2 Korinth 13:11; Philipi 2:1, 2; Galatia 4:4–6.

> <p>Muitu Bwtang</p>
> “Jisu Khristo norokno bebak’-no khairok thung, Kaitor norokno hamjak thung, Phola Kwthar norok bai baksa tong thung” (2 Korinth 13:14, KOKBSI).

Pol bini swijak koktunrokni bisingo sajakmani nangkukmani kokrokno tei uáisa saui 2 Korinthno mwthak kha. Bo kaiba kok kotorni bising twi omo hai khe khlai kha (2 Kor. 13:11).

1. “Tongthok di” (English Bible naidi). Bini puilani koktunni kokno muitu khlai mwi rwkha.
2. “Kwtal khe rak sukna naidi” (English Bible naidi). O kokbwtang Grik kókthai-o khe (_katartizō_). Abo 1 Korinth 1:10 bwtango núkjak kha.
3. “Khoroksa tei khoroksano kha rak rw lai di” (NIV English Bible naidi). 2 Korinth 1:3-7 bwtango sama kókno khurchamani. Kha rak rwmani kók baino Pol bini koktunno cheng kha tei mwthak kha. Chwng buino kha rak rwui manna twi khe chwng Kaitorni kha rak rwmungno yachago (2 Korinth 1:4, 6).
4. “Kók tháisa wng lai di” (2 Korinth 13:11).
5. “Kwchang kwchang khe tong lai di” (2 Korinth 13:11).

4 tei 5 point wng kha thansa khe tongnani bagwi sajakmung. Tongthokma, kwtal khe kha rakma, kha rak rwlaima, kók tháisa wngma, tei kwchang kwchang khe tongma wng kha “hamjaknai tei khá kwchang rwnai Kaitor norok bai tongmani” tong halok se (2 Korinth 13:11). Abo wng kha borokni bwkhao khorokthamsa Kaitorni samungni (triune God’s work) bwthai se.

Chini bagwi khorokthamsa Kaitorni (triune God) samungni bwthai wng kha khairokma, hamjakma, tei thánsa. O kaitham tongdal tong khe abo Kaitor bo bai tongo hinwi sijago.
