---
title:  Phola Kwtharni Thánsa
date:   23/09/2026
---

Jisuni khairokma simi chwngno Kaitorni hamjakmano phunuk ya, phiya Phola Kwthar bai thansa khe a hamjakmani bwthaino tubuwi phaio. A thansani yaphang wng kha hamjaknai Kaitor. Tamni hwn ba hamjakma kwrwi khe sói thansa wngwi man ya. Abo Pol hai hwnwi swimani hai: “Khristo bai tongwi norok phan da man? Bo norokno hamjakmani abo norokno khá da kwchang rw? Phola bai norok thansa da wng? Norok kiching norok khá da khairok lai? Khá da khá súk lai? Hakhe norokno ang koi ja-o, norok khá baksa wng laiwi, baksa khe hamjak laiwi, yak bai khúk bai baksa wng laiwi ano tongthok rw súk di” (Philipi 2:1-2). Baksa borok sao Phola Kwthar abo phan tei kobolnai simi se, phiyaba a kók kubui ya. Tamni bagwi Pol Khorokthamsani (Trinitarian) kókno saui tong phuru khoroknwi Pha tei Sajla bai baksa “phan” eba “kobolnai” hinwi sanai? Abo kókmang se gothok ya. Pha tei Sajlano khoroksa khoroksa khe sama hai kheno (1 Korinth 1:3; 11:31) Phola Kwtharbo bororok bai halok kwrwngnai khoroksa. Kók wng kha bobo khoroksa [person] (Rum 8:15–16; Johon 14:16–17, 26; 15:26).

“Phola bai norok thansa” (Phil. 2:1) hwnmani kokbwtangno kainwi kókmang bai bujiui mano. Abo babaisa phaisingo khe Phola Kwtharni bising twi poito khlainairok khoroksa bai tei khoroksa thansa wng laimano buji rwo. Tei babaisa bwkhak khe, Phola Kwthar sakphang chwng bai thansa wngmani buji rwo. O kainwi kókmangno kaisa bai tei kaisa kókmangno kebeng lai ya. Khoroksa tei khoroksa bai thansa wng laimani wng kha Phola Kwthar bai thansa wngmani bwthai se.

`1 Korinth 2:10, 11; 1 Korinth 3:16; 1 Korinth 12:11; tei 2 Korinth 3:6, 17 khúktang di. Pholano twiwi Korinthsongno Pol tamo phrwng kha?`

Pol Phola Kwtharni samungno twiwi kwbangma sakha. 1 tei 2 Korinth koktuno Phola Kwtharni kók uai brwichini slaibo bo sakha. Phola Kwthar mothano rak rwo tei hamrwo (1 Korinth 14:12), bororokno kothoma kaham pirwi man rwo (1 Korinth 2:4-5), Kaitorni bisingni kók kuthukrokno chwngno phunugo (1 Korinth 2:10-11) abórokno twiwi chwngno phrwngo (1 Korinth 2:13), bo chwng bai tong phaio (1 Korinth 3:16; 6:19), Khristo bai baksa thansa wngwi chwngno borok kaham hinwi romnani a samungno khlaio (1 Korinth 6:11), mothao pholani suarrokno rwo (1 Korinth 12-14), chwngno mwthangjaknani bagwi mari bu-o (2 Korinth 1:22), borokni bwkhao Kaitorni kerongno swiwi rwo (2 Korinth 3:3), tei Khristo-o kwtal langma manwi sumsokni yakni mwthangjaknani rwo (2 Korinth 3:16, 17). Phola Kwthar kwrwi khe chwng tongwi se man ya.

`Tamoni bagwi Phola Kwthar sabo sinani belai nangkukmani?`
