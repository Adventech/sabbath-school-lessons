---
title:  Hamjaknai Kaitor
date:   22/09/2026
---

Kwcham paganni molo mwtairok bororokno hamjago hinwi bóhrok poito khlai ya. Phiyaba mwtairokno kirimasingcha khe simi se bóhrok nugo. Baibel-o hamjaknai Kaitor hinwi chwng nugwi mano. Phiyaba Polni jorao a kók khnaui bororok mwlang chalaio. Pol Kaitorni tongmungno “hamjaknai tei khá kwchang rwnai Kaitor” hinwi sakha (2 Korinth 13:11).

`2 Korinth 13:11 khúktang di. O bwtango tamo phrwng kha nwng bahai khe bujinai?`

“Hamjaknai tei khá kwchang rwnai Kaitor” hinwi samanino kainwi lama twi swbaiwi sai mano. Kaisa khe, Kaitor wng kha hamjakma tei khá kwchangmungni yaphang hai khe núkjago. Tei kaisa khe, Kaitorni tongmung hamjakma tei khá kwchang rwmung bai phunukjago. Kaitorni tongmung wng kha hamjaknai tei khá kwchang rwnai; tamni hwn ba bo chwngno hamjago, tei khá kwchang rwo.

Teibo Pol sakha, Kaitor wng kha “khá lok rwnai tei khá rak rwnai Kaitor” (Rum 15:13), “khatung rwnai Kaitor” (Rum 15:13), “Khá kwchang rwnai Kaitor” (Rum 15:33; Rum 16:20, 1 Korinth 14:33, Phil. 4:9, 1 Thes. 5:23), “maya gwnang Apha, bebago’-no chubanai Kaitor” (2 Korinth 1:3). O bebak hamarini yaphang wng kha Kaitor. Bini páiphlai ya hamjakmani bagwi bo chwngno o bebak hamarirokno rwkha.

“Khá kwchang rwnai Kaitor” abo kwbangma bwtango tong phano, “hamjaknai Kaitor” o bwtango simi nukjak kha (2 Korinth 13:11). Phiya abo belai nangkukmani kókno phunuk kha.

Kwbangma Baibel kók swbaiwi sanairok naichom lai kha, 2 Korinth 13:14 bwtango khorokthamsani hamarini kók (Trinitarian Benediction) sanani swkang Pol “hamjaknai Kaitor” ni kók khurcha phuru, aphuruno bo Kaitorno khoroktham khe tongo hinwi buji kha hinwi. “Tumungno oro bo [Pol] ‘Kaitor’ kókthaino khorokthamni bisingo khoroksano simi sa phano, bini kubuni koktunrogo Jisu tei Phola Kwtharni kók swimani bising twi chwng bujiui mano bo kaisa Kaitorni kóknono saui tongo. Khoroktham kheno yaphangni motharokbo Kaitorno nugo. Bisira molni yagulo Elembakni kókno chernairok a khorokthamsa Kaitorno phunukna bagwi ‘Trinity’ kókthai snam kha. Phiyaba o kókno Pol swkangni simino pirwi tongo.”—Tom Wright, _Paul for Everyone: 2 Corinthians_ (London: Society for Promoting Christian Knowledge, 2004), p. 148.

Chwng poito khlaio Kaitor kaisa simi, hamjakmani halok bai khe mol brúmni simi khoroktham thansa khe tongnaisa. O khorokthamsa Kaitor (triune God) bóhrok khoroktham hamjak laima hai kheno chwngbo buino hamjakma phunuk manna twi khe chwngno hamjago.
