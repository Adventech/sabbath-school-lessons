---
title:  Chini Khorokthamsa Kaitor
date:   24/09/2026
---

2 Korinth 13:14 khúktang khe chwng uánsugwi mano, Khristo simi se khairokmani yaphang, Kaitor simi se hamjakmani yaphang, tei Phola Kwthar simi se thansa khlainai yaphang. Phiyaba abo kubui ya.

`1 Korinth 1:3, 4, 9; 1 Korinth 10:16; 2 Korinth 1:2, 12; Rum 8:35; Rum 15:30; Galatia 2:20; tei Epis 3:19 khúktang di. Khorokthamsa (Trinity) tongnairok bai kwrwngjak khairokma, hamjakma, tei thansano twiwi o bwtangrogo tamo phrwng?`

Pha, Sajla, tei Phola Kwthar chwngno mwthangnani bagwi thansa khe samung khlaio. Khairokma, hamjakma tei thánsa khlaima abo khoroksani thani simi ya, abórok bóhrok khorokthamni thanino tongo. Phiyaba mwthangmani láibuma-o bebaknino samung khakjak tongo. Pol abórokno saui mano, hakhe bini koktunrogo abono phunugo. Phunukmarini bagwi chwng Galatia 4:4-6 bwtangno naiwi mano. Aro mwthangjakmaungni samungno phunuk kha, aro khorokthamno tongo. Apha Kaitor Jisuno rohor kha, hakhe Pha wng kha mwthangnani chapmungni yaphang (Galatia 4:4). Sajla khoroksa bwrwimani bohogo achai kha (Galatia 4:4), abo bo borok wngwi Yaphang 3:15 bwtangni yaphangni swmaino supungwi phunuk kha. Hakhe Sajla chwngno mwthang kha, tei Apha bai halok manjuwi rwphikha, phiya Aphano twiwi Soitan khe tatal saui tongmani (Yaphang 3:5). Phiyaba Phola Kwthar khe chwngno Kaitorni bwsa hinwi sinimung rwo, tei phan rwo (Galatia 4:6).

Polni kotunrogo khoroksano twiwi (Trinity) teibo kók tongo. Pha, Sajla tei Phola Kwthar thansa khe Mothani bagwi samung khlaiwi man rwo (1 Korinth 12:4-6), chwngno pholani phan rwo (Epis 3:14-19), tei poito khlainairokni bisingo thansa wng rwo; a thansa wng kha Kaitor khorokthamsong (members of Godhead) thansa wngmanino se phunugo (Epis 4:4-6). Polni bujimungo Kaitor khorokthamsa (Triune God) simi ya, o khorokthamno (Three Persons of Godhead) chwngno mwthangnani bagwi samung khlai-o (Epis 1:3, 13, 14). Epis koktun bijap-o Pol sakha, chwng Phan (Epis 3:19), Sajla (Epis 4:13) tei Phola Kwtharni (Epis 5:18) bebak tongmung bai kuplung wngna nangnai.

Korinthsongno swihormani koktun mwthak phuru (2 Korinth 13:14) Pol kaisa swmaini bising twi khe mwthak kha: a swmai wng kha Chúknohani khorokthamsa (three Dignitaries of the universe/Heavenly Trio) chwng bai tabukbo tei thinangobo tongo.

`Mothani bororok thansa wngmani bahai khe Kaitor khorokthamsani (Godhead) halokrokno phunuk?`
