---
title:  Kaitorni Hamjakma
date:   21/09/2026
---

“Jisu Khristo norokno bebak’-no khairok thung, Kaitor norokno hamjak thung, Phola Kwthar norok bai baksa tong thung” (2 Korinth 13:14). O bwtang bai khe Pol bini 2 Korinth koktunno mwthak kha. Naichom jadi, o bwtango Pol Khorokthamsani (Trinity) khirokthamrokno khurchaui sakha, abórok wng kha—Sajla, Pha, tei Phola Kwthar. O khoroktham Samung khlaimani bising twi se chwng Kaitor bubo hai kaham khe bujiui mano, tei bo chini bagwi tamo khlai kha kaham khe bujiui mano.

`Johon 3:16, 17; Rum 8:37–39; tei 1 Johon 4:8–11 khúktang di. O bwtangrogo Kaitorni hamjakmano twiwi tamo phrwng?`

1 Johon 4:8 bwtango sao “Kaitor wng kha hamjakmung.” Kaitor bini songdari Sajlano chini sumsokni bagwi thwimani rwmani bising twi chwng hamjakmano sai mano (Johon 3:16). Mwthangnani samungni bagwi Kaitor Jisuno rohor kha (Johon 3:17), abo wng kha mwthangmungni samungo khobjago (Samung 3:20, 21; 1 Johon 4:10, 14). “Ano rohornai” hinwi kothoma kaham bijap-o Jisu kwbangma joraono bini buphani kókno sakha (Mathai 10:40; Mark 9:37).

Pol samani tongo: “Tamo khe Kaitor chwngno bwswk se hamjak abo bo chwngno phunuk kha, tamni hwn khe chwng sumsok gwnang wngwi tong phuru Khristo chini bagwi thwi kha” (Rum 5:8). Kaitorni hamjakmani sampilino chwng bihik bai bwsaini halok kahamo nugwi mano, mapha bai bwsarokni halok kahamo nugwi mano, bayab kahamrokni bisingo nugwi mano. Habarrokno naiwibo chwng Kaitorni hamjakmano nugwi mano. Abono twiwino Ellen G. White sakha, “Bebak bwkhwrwi kiyoksamao tei samphangrok barmao ‘Kaitor wng kha hamjakmung’’ o kok swijak tongo. Naithok tokrok kwtwi khorang bai nobaro sarwi tongthokni rwchapmung rwchap laio, podrepot gapni khumrok nobaro bahai kaham rw laio, bolong gwnangwi buphangni bwlairok surchaui bolongno mwchang rwo—abórok bebakno chini sakao chini bupha Kaitorni naikolma tei bini bwsarokno tong kwchang toninani bini muchungmanino se sakki rwo.”—_Steps to Christ,_ p. 10.

Jephuru Kaitor chwngno hamjakmano chwng sai mano, bo chwngno hamjakma bagwino chini bagwi Jisuno thwinani rohor kha hinwi chwng sai mano, hakhe “chwngbo takhukrokni bagwi chini langmano rwthai klai-o” hinwi muchungma tongnai (1 Johon 3:16).

Korinthsong thansa khe tong lai thung hinwi Pol muchungjago. Phiyaba hamjakma kwrwi khe thansa wngwi man ya. Aboni bagwino bo bóhrokno phrwng kha “hamjakma bai borok kwrak wngo” hinwi (1 Korinth 8:1), phiyaba hamjakma kwrwi khe bebakno chuk ya tei bukcha se (1 Korinth 13:1–3). Aboni babai chwng bebakno hamjakma bai khlaithai (1 Korinth 16:14), a hamjakma Kaitorni hamjakmano se phunugo.

`Tumungno Jisu mol brúmni sói Kaitor ya hwn khe, kothoma kaham-o chwng tamo kwmama klai?`
