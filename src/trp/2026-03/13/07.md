Ellen G. White swimani _‘The Desire of Ages’ bijapni “_ ‘Let Not Your Heart Be Troubled,’ ” beremno khúktang di, bwkáng 662–680.

“Jisuni khairokma simi se bwkha holongno slaiwi mano, tei bwkha kwrakno sobor rwui mano. Hakhe a bwkha Kaitorno hamjaknai, tei bini kók khnanai wngo... Kaitorno karwi bororok sak sak kaham wngwi man ya. Sumsokni hamya sak nainai kotor Jisuni phan bai simi se ham rwui mano. Jisu, Aphani songdari Sajla, khairokma tei kubui bai kuplung, Chúknohani kotor yapharmung, bo simi se komornairokno mwthangwi mano.”—Ellen G. White, in _The Signs of the Times,_ May 2, 1892.

“ ‘Kaitor wng kha hamjakmung’ (1 Johon 4:16). Bini tongmung, bini kerong tei hamjakma. Bo bebak jorao abo haino tongo, tei tong phlainai. Bo ‘kichuk tei chẃngsacha’ abo mol brúmno tongo, bini ‘lamarok mol brúmni’ bo slaijak ya. Bini bisingo ‘mungsa slaijakmung eba slaijakmungni sampili’ kwrwi (Jisaia 57:15; Habakuk 3:6; Jakob 1:17). Kaitor snammani bebakno bini hamjakmano phunugo. Bini naikolmung hamari tubuo. Chúknohani simi chengwi sumsok swkwmajakmani jora kaham bai hamyani chobayung kotorbo Kaitorni hamjakma slaijak ya hinwi phunugo. Jesa jorao phano Kaitor khe hamjaknai, kók narwknai, tei bini hamjakma slaijak ya.”—Ellen G. White, _Patriarchs and Prophets,_ p. 33.

“Kaitor khoroksa wngma hai kheno Phola Kwtharbo khoroksa hinwi chwng ma nainai. Phola Kwthar tumungno khoroksa wng ya khe bo chwng Kaitorni bwsa hinwi chini bwkhano saui man ya. Phola Kwthar tumungno Kaitor wng ya khe, bo Kaitorni bwkha bisingni kóknobo saui man glak.”—Ellen G. White, _The Faith I Live By,_ p. 52.

“Chúknohani khorokthamsa wng kha Pha, Sajla tei Phola Kwthar. Bóhrokni mung bai chwng baptisma wngwi Jisuno yachago. Bóhrok thansa khe Kaitorni bwsarok bai samung tango, bóhrokno chubao, tei Khristo rwmani kwtal langma mannani chubao.”—Ellen G. White, _Evangelism,_ p. 615.

**Salaijaknai Swngmungrok**

`“Uanamasingcha khairokma” (Amazing Grace, Kaisa English Rwchapmung) Khristian rwchapmungno chwng jotono saui mano. Khristoni khairokma-o tamo uanamasingcha tong?`

`Kaitorni aswk naithok hamjakmano chwng Kwmanai Sajlano rwngthoma-o nugo. Bahai khe o rwngthoma-o bupha hamjaknai hinwi chwng nuk kha?`

`Bahai khe motha wngwi “Phola Kwtharni thansa khlaimaungno” kubui-o phunuknai?`
