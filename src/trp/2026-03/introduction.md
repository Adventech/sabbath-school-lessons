Khristian Langma tei Sakki Rwmungni Kókbwkha

Koktun swihor laimani abo belai kwcham jorani samung, phiya abo tabukni molo suda wngwi tong kho. Phiya koktun swimani raida simi kisa slaijak kha. Bwkango koktun swimani sláijagwi tabuk kokduk kwrwngmung sok phai kha. Phiyaba kókmang khe thansano. Emails, tei kubuni Electronic koktun swimungbo thansano samung khlaio: abo bororokni koktunma, uánsukma, tei chongmanirokno tei khoroksa bai thansa khe kwrwng rwo.

Bororok tamoni bagwi koktun swi lai? Kók phirokmung wng kha, bóhrok mungsa swk sajaknai kók tongo. Hakhe Chonghor Polni bagwibo abo haino. Bo kwbangma kók sana nangmani tongo. Haiphano bo kók sana muchungma bororok bai malai phuru bebak kók sanani seb man ya.

Aboni bagwino bo Korinthsongno koktun swikha—aro chwng Elembakni kók kubuirokbo mano. Abórokni bisingo tongo : “Norok bai tong phuru ang Jisu Khristono simi se uansuknai, aboni bere se bo kruso thwimano simi se uansuknai, tei mungsano phano uansuk ya hwnwi ang khá chong kha” (1 Korinth 2:2), tei “Chini Subrai Jisu Khristo bwswk khairok kha norok abono saui mano; bo kwrwi wngma bai norokno gwnang khlaina bagwi bo gwnang phano norokni bagwi kwrwi wng kha” (2 Korinth 8:9). Tei 1 Korinth 13 beremo hamjakmani lobmung tongo, abo le tamo?

Phiya Korinthsongno Pol koktun swihormani khúktang khe, chwng belai ma uananai; tamni hwn ba Korinth mothao motha bisingni kotor kotor jwngjal simi ya—buitali-buitala wngmani hai jwngjalrokno karwibo Mothani adongrokni bisingo khak laimani, tei kak laiwi gut khalaimani, kok banglaimanirok tongo. Tumungno nini mothao kwbangma jwngjal tongo hinwi nwng uánsuk khe, Korinth Mothani jwngjalno Pol bahai khe ma nai kha naiwi naidi. Nwng uánsukma swk nini mothani jwngjal wng yabo wngwi mano. Phiyaba Korinth motha teibo hamya tong halogo kwlaijak.

Korinthni jwngjalrok belai kútuk phano, Polni koktun a jwngjalrokni bagwi nangkukmani ya. Tamo khe Pol bahai khe a jwngjalrokno nai kha abo se nangkukmani. Jisuni kothoma kahamni pohoro Mothani bororok sak sakno amjogwi nai thung, bóhrokni samungno twiwi tei bóhrokni samo tongnai hoda hukumuno twiwi uánsugwi nai thung hinwi Pol bóhrokno kha rak rwkha. Pol teibo samani tongo, abo wng kha kothoma kahamni slai tei kubuni manwirok bebakno aswk nangkukmani ya eba “khibijaknai” (Galatia 1:8-9).

Polni jorao Korinth doli le belai sinijak eba mung gwnang se. A doli rang mai gwnang tei baniya eba bepar thwngwi hamchamani khola. Aro tongo rung bathakmani khola, architecture (nok huk snammani), rungtor snamkhor, tei manwi snammani khor. A doli rang ajimani eba bepar thwngmani thai. Phiya a doliyung buitali-buitala wngmani, Dalbidal tongthar twimungni bodol tongmani, tei kwbangma mwtairokni Nókthar tongmani thai hinwibo sinijago. Sal brúmno Korinth mwtai khulum laimani bai kuplung. O láibuma tei bisingni kók cherwi naikhe chwng sai mano, Pol tamoni bagwi a doliyungni Khristianrokno twiwi aswk uanajak, tei bóhrokno abo hai kókbiti rwkha.

O talthamni bisingo Korinthsongno Pol koktun swihormanino twiwi swrwng lai anw. O kainwi nangkukmani swmai Kwtalni bijapni bising twi Lom phrwng kha, Khristian langma tei sakki rwmungni kók yaphang wng kha kothoma kaham. Abo bai se bebakno amjokjakthai. Chwng sak baithang, eba motha wngwi, eba khe chini Chúknohani lama himmungo jesa jwngjal bai malai phano, Korinthsong hai kheno chwng Jisuno suriui ma tongnai: “Jisu Khristono simi se uansuknai, aboni bere se bo kruso thwimano simi se uansuknai” (1 Korinth 2:2).

Jisu daktino phainai. O joraono chwng teibo Khristo-o thansa wngnani, swkangni slai tabuk teibo chwng Phola Kwtharno nang kha. Phola rwmani suarrokno tabuk teibo samungo phnangna nang kha. Thangwi bachanai chini Subrai bai chwng teibo kuthuk khe halok kwrwngna nang kha. Tabukno jora chwng Kaitorni samung tangnani, tei tatal phrwngmungrokno kebengwi khairokma, hamjakma, tei thansa-o tornani. Tabukno jora Krusni kókno kwtal khe romwi tongnani, tei Korinthsongno Pol koktun swihormani bising twi bahai khe abo khlainai chwngno phrwngo.
