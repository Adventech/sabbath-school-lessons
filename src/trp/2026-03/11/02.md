---
title:  Jisuni Phunukmari
date:   06/09/2026
---

Judiani tongnai kebel motharokno Korinthsong chubaui manna twi khe bóhrokno kha rak rwna bagwi 2 Korinth 8 tei 9 beremo Pol sakha. Bóhrok a samung khlainani tiyar wngwi pai kha (2 Korinth 8:10, 11; 2 Korinth 9:5; teibo naidi 1 Korinth 16:1-4), phiyaba bóhrok bai Polni bisingo halok kisa hamya wngna bagwi a jwngjal bai kisa kwchaui tong kha. Phiya a jwngjal hammani ulo (2 Korinth 1-7) a samungno khlaiwi painani kók Pol saphikha (2 Korinth 8-9).

Puila-o, Pol Makidoniani poito khlainairokni phunukmari rwkha (2 Korinth 8:1-7). “Bóhrok belai kwrwi twtwi phano jai kheno khá tongthokma bai yak kuar khe rw kha” (2 Korinth 8:2). Au, kwrwi bai rwmani thansa khe himwi mano. Makidoniasong yak kuar khe rwmani phunukmari abo Jisuno se phunugo, tamni hwn ba Jisu chini bagwi bini sakno yaphar kha (2 Korinth 8:8-15).

`2 Korinth 8:9 khúktang di. Jisuni phunukmarino twiwi o bwtango tamo sajak kha?`

2 Korinth 8:9 bwtango Pol kók samani abo Baibelni belai phan gwnang kaisa nangkukmani bwtang. Pol Jisuni samungno twiwi kaisa kothoma sakha, phiyaba aro rang-puisani kókno thepajak kha. Oro teibo swrwngjaknai tongo. Omo wng kha kaisa bwtangono Mwthangmani kothoma.

O bwtango rang puilani kókthai thepaui kothoma sajak kha. Au, Jisu wng kha gwnang. Bo gwnang hwnjakmani karon wng kha swkango bo Chúknoha-o tongo (Johon 17:5). Chúknohani chẃngsachano yakarwi tei swngcharni birmano phaiwi bo kwrwi (bigra) wngnani chongwi nakha. Bo sóino kwrwi wng phai kha (Luk 9:58). Bo Kaitor bai baksa wng phano “Aboni slái bo sakno bukcha khlaiwi seleng hai se wng phai si-o. Bo borok wngwi phai kha, hakhe borok hai kheno nukjak phai kha” (Philipi 2:7). “Bo sakno borok kusu snam kha, hakhe kók khnaui thwina lama himna gosi kha, kruso suda thwina gosi kha” (Philipi 2:8).

Chwng bo bai mol brúmno tongwi manna twi khe Jisu bini baithangni langmano rwkha. Bini yapharmani naharmung wng kha chini mwthangjakmung se. Naikolmungni barja bai Kaitorni samung abo thansa khe se thango. 2 Korinth 8-9 bwtango rang yapharmano twiwi sajak kha, phiya a yapharmung Jisuni kothoma bai se sungchajak.

`Jisu achaimani, bini langma, thwima tei thangwi bachamani kókno uánsugwi naidi. Omorok bebak nini bagwi se khlai kha hinwi nwng sai man khe, nwng khatungma manwi tamo khlainai?`
