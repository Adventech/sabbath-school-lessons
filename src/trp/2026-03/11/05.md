---
title:  Tongmung
date:   09/09/2026
---

`2 Korinth 8:1–5 khúktang di. Makidoniasong yapharmung rwna muchungmani uklogo karon tamo wngwi man?`

Makidoniasongni tongmung kaham kwbangma lamani bising twi nukjak kha:

**1. Jai kheno khá tongthokma bai bóhrok rwkha (2 Korinth 8:2).** Pol sakha, Makidoniasong “bórok belai kwrwi twtwi phano jai kheno khá tongthokma bai yak kuar khe rw kha” (2 Korinth 8:2). Aboni ulo bo saphikha, “Kaitor khá srangwi rwnaino se hamjago” (2 Korinth 9:7). Swmai Kwtalni o bwtango simino “khá srangwi” hwnmani kókthai núkjago. Omo bai tei kaisa bwtang núkjakma wng kha: “chubana hwn khe chwng khá srangwi ma chubanai” (Rum 12:8, English Bible naidi). Khá srangwi rwmani kókmang wng kha hamjak ya wng yaui rwmani (2 Korinth 9:7).

**2. Bóhrok yak kuar khe rwkha (2 Korinth 8:2).** Makidoniasongni yak kuarni kókno sanani swkang Pol sakha, “bórok belai kwrwi twtwi phano” rwkha hinwi. “Yak kuar” (Grik kókthai-o _haplotētos_) hwnmani kókthai 2 Korinth 8 -9 beremo uainwi simi núkjago. Pol sakha, “Norok yak kuar khe rw máng tongna tẃi khe bo norokno gwnang khlaiwi tonnai” (2 Korinth 9:11). Aboni kókmang wng kha chwng rwjak kha ba chwng rwui mano. Aboni kisa ulo bo sakha “Norok … yak kuar khe rwmani” (2 Korinth 9:13). Yak kuar khe rwmani wng kha Khristoni kothoma kahamno gosimani lama se.

**3. “Bóhrok rwui mansak rw kha” (2 Korinth 8:3).** Kók wng kha bóhrok muchungma bai khe rwkha. Bóhrok tongmani barano rwliya, tamo khe kisa tongmani bisingni se rwkha. Abo hai kheno Pol Titno sakha, bo muchungma bai khe se Korinthsongni aro beraina thangthai. Bo muchungwino Korinth-o thang kha (2 Korinth 8:17).

**4. Bóhrok rwmani abo seb kaham hinwi uánsugwi se bóhrok rwkha/yaphar kha (2 Korinth 8:4).** Rang khang lai thanio chubanani muchungma bagwi Makidoniasong le koimung khlai kha. “ ‘Chwngnobo Judiani Kaitorni bororokno chuba chru rw di, (seb rwdi)’ hwnwi chini thani koiwi kha” (2 Korinth 8:4).

**5. Kaitorni thani sakno yapharma hai khe se bóhrok yapharmungno uánsugwi rwlai kha (2 Korinth 8:5).** Pol sakha, “Swkang bórok sak sakno Subraini yago yaphar kha, hakhe Kaitor muchungma bai bórok sak sakno chini thanibo yaphar kha” (2 Korinth 8;5). Kaitorni thani sakno yapharmani bwthai wng kha buini thanibo sakno yapharmani. Makidoniasong rang-puisa yapharmani bai simi thak liya. Tamni hwn ba yapharmani, tei chubamani abo rang rwmanino simi hwn ya.
