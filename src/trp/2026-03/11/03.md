`2 Korinth 8:1, 5 tei 2 Korinth 9:7, 9, 13, 15 khúktang di. Oroni kókbothoma tamo?`

2 Korinth 8 tei 9 beremo rwmani kók úaire uai núkjago: “Kaitor khairogwi tamo rw kha” (2 Korinth 8:1, English Bible naidi). “bórok sak sakno ... yaphar kha” (2 Korinth 8:5). “Joto borokno khá chong tẃino rwthai; ... tamni hwn ba Kaitor khá srangwi rwnaino se hamjago” (2 Korinth 9:7). “Bo kwrwirokno yak kuar khe rw-o” (2 Korinth 9:9). “...norok bóroknobo buinobo yak kuar khe rwmani bagwibo bórok Kaitorno lob anw” (2 Korinth 9:13). “Kaitor chwngno rwmani abo khúk bai sa bai ya, aboni bagwi phai di, chwng bono hambai rw lai anw” (2 Korinth 9:15). 2 Korinth 8 tei 9 berem rwmani kókthai bai se chengjago tei mwthakjago (2 Korinth 8:1; 2 Korinth 9:15). Rwna muchungmani bwkha bai khe chwng o beremno khuktangna nangnai. Rwna nangmani kaibrwi karon hai chwng oro mano.

Kaitor khairokma bagwi kha kaham yapharmani (2 Korinth 8:1; 2 Korinth 9:14, 15). 2 Korinth 8 -9 chengmani “Kaitorni khairokma” kokbwtangni bising twi se chengjago (2 Korinth 8:1). Pol teisa sakha: “Chini Subrai Jisu Khristo bwswk khairok kha norok abono saui mano” (2 Korinth 8:9). Chwng Kaitorni thani yapharnani karon wng kha Kaitorni khairokma tei Jisuni khairokma manma bai se. Khristono rwmani bising twi Kaitor chini bagwi kwbangma samung khlai kha. Chwng Kaitorno kiphilwi yapharmani wng kha chini langma-o Kaitorni khairokmano chwng saui manmani se.

2 Korinth 8 -9 berem-o rwmani kók-o, “khairokma” (Grik kók bai, _charis_) kókthai abo uáisa ruai núkjago. _Charis_ (khairokma) kókthai chengphuru tei páithagobo núkjago (2 Korinth 8:1; 2 Korinth 9:14, 15). Pol o kókthai thepamani karon wng kha Khristo chwngno khairokmani bwthai chini langma-o núkjakmani wng kha buino khairokma se, tei hambai rwmani.

Jisuni phunukmari hai khe yapri rwktharnani muchungma (2 Korinth 8:9). Jisu chwngno rwma hai kheno chwng buino rwo.

Kaitorni hamarino rwnani muchungma (2 Korinth 9:10, 11). Chwng Kaitorni thani puila yachak kha ba chwng buino rwo.

Belai hamjakma (2 Korinth 8:8, 24). Rwmani wng kha nini hamjakma bwswk kubui chánaiwi naimani se. Khoroksa swkni bwkhao hamjakma tong khe rwmani bising twi se núkjago.
