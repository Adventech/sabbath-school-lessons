---
title:  Chapmung
date:   08/09/2026
---

`2 Korinth 9:7 khúktang di. Yapharmung rwmani samungno twiwi o bwtango tamo sa?`

Kaitor borokno mwthangnani chapmani wng kha o swngchar-o sumsok habnani swkangni simino. Khristo chini bagwi thwiphainani wng kha kwcham chapmung (Phunukjakmani 13:8). 2 Korinth 8, tei 9 beremo rwmani samungo chapmung belai nangkukmani khe phunukjak kha. Abono kainwi lamani bising twi nugwi mano:

1. Chapmung wng kha swkangni simi chongwi namani. Pol sakha, “Joto borokno khá chong tẃino rwthai” (2 Korinth 9:7). “Chong twi” hwnmanino Grik kókthai-o _proaireō_ hinwi swikha. Aboni kókmang wng kha “swkang baira” eba “swkangni simi” tei _aireō_ hwnmani kókmang wng kha “chongnani.” Phiyaba _proaireō_ hwnmani khe swkangni simino chongwi tonjak. Pol sana nai kha, “jotono” kha chong twino muchungma bai khe rwthai.

2. Chapmungo je chukmani rwmani raidano khobjago. Pol Makidoniasongni kókno sakha “bórok rwui mansak rw kha” (2 Korinth 8:3). Abo hai khlaina bagwi bo Korinthsongnobo sakha. Bo bóhrokno sakha abo tẃi khe’-no o samung khlaiwi páina bagwi! Bóhrokni phan tong tẃi’-no khlaiwi páina bagwi (2 Korinth 8:11). Bo kók mwthak phuru yapharmungno twiwi sakha “Norok rwna muchung khe Kaitor norokni tongmano naiwi se yachaknai, norokni kwrwino naiwi ya” (2 Korinth 8:12). Chi-o kaisa rwmani khe chini bisingo kaisa rwmani, phiya yapharmung (offering) rwmani khe kha chong twi se rwthai. “Joto borokno khá chong tẃino rwthai” (2 Korinth 9:7), jani rwui man twino rwthai.
