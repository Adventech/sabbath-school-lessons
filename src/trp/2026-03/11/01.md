### O Hatibarsani Swrwngjaknai
2 Korinth 8–9; Johon 3:16; Johon 17:5; Luk 9:58; Phunukjakmani 13:8; Rum 12:8; Rum 15:26, 27.

> <p>Muitu Bwtang</p>
> “Chini Subrai Jisu Khristo bwswk khairok kha norok abono saui mano; bo kwrwi wngma bai norokno gwnang khlaina bagwi bo gwnang phano norokni bagwi kwrwi wng kha” (2 Korinth 8:9, KOKBSI).

2 Korinth 8 tei 9 beremo phunuk kha, Korinthsongno Judia-o tongnai takhuk tei bukhuksongno chubana bagwi Pol bóhrokno seb kaham rwmani. Oro phunuk kha, rwmani wng kha seb kaham se, abo Kaitor chwngno chuk rwmani; abo hai khe chwng Khristo sakno yapharmani tongmungno phunugwi mano. Chúknohani kaisa kók wng kha rwmani. O bwtangno Naijadi: “Tamni hwn ba Kaitor swngcharno aswk hamjakmani bagwi bini songdari Sajlano rohor kha, bini a Sajlano poito khlainai borok bo komor yaui mwthangjak phlaiwi manna tẃi khe Kaitor bono rohor kha” (Johon 3:16).

Teibo chwng saui mano Johon 3:16 bwtango sraisrai sao Kaitorni muchungma wng kha Jisuno rwnani, hakhe bono “poito khlainai borok bo komor yaui mwthangjak phlaiwi manna tẃi khe Kaitor bono rohor kha” (Johon 3:16). O bwtango naikolmungni barja tei Kaitorni samung thansa khe thangmani núkjago. Siki beleb babainwi swkakjagwi man ya tongma hai khe abo kainwi samungbo swkakjagwi man ya. O kók belaino sraisrai. Pol bini baithangno tei bini tangpalsongno “Kaitorni bisingni kókni bagwi barja rwjak” hinwi sinimung rwkha (1 Korinth 4:1). Chwngbo abo hai kheno barja manjak borok wng kha.

O hatibaro chwng nai lai anw, barjani samung tei Kaitorni samung abo Jisuni phunukmario se kuthuk khe kaijak. Hakhe naikolmungni barja bai Kaitorni samungno swkakjagwi man ya. Kaitorni samung tangjak bainani bagwi rang puisani chubachu rwnai wng kha naikolmungni barja se.

_September 12, Sabat salni bagwi o hatibarsani rwngchapno swrwngwi tiyar wng di._
