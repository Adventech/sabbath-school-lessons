Ellen G. White swimani _‘The Acts of the Apostles’_ bijapni “A Liberal Church” beremno khúktang di, bwkáng 335–345.

“Bororokni bwkha-o Khristoni hamjakma tong khe, bo Khristoni yaprino rwktharnai. Jisu chini bagwi kwrwi wng phai kha, hakhe chwngno gwnang khlainani lama rwkha. Swkang molni mothani jorao rang, jora, tei Kaitor bai rwjakma suarrokno Kaitorni samungni bagwi simi se bóhrok uánsugo. Abórokni bising twi Khristoni motha tornani se bóhrok uánsugo. Tabukni motharokbo Phola Kwthar bai twijak khe, swngcharni manwirok bóhrokni bwkhao tong glak. Phiya bóhrok kothoma kaham buino khna rwna bagwi yapharnani se muchungnai, hakhe bóhrok kók pirmani abo buini khá-o nangwinai.”—Ellen G. White, _The Acts of the Apostles,_ p. 71.

“Kaitor le chini yapharmungno nangmani ya. Chwng yapharmung bai bono gwnang khlai man ya. Lobmung swinaisa sao: ‘Tamni hwn ba bebak nini aroni se phai-o, tamo khe ninino se chwng nono yapharo.’ Phiyaba chwng kha tongthokmani tei hambai rwmani karonni bagwi yapharwi mano hinwi Kaitor chwngno sakha, abo hai khe buini thanibo a khairokma sógwiwi mano. Yapharmani bising twi chwng Kaitorno hambai rwma tei hamjakma phunuknani lama tongo. Abono karwi kubuni lama bo rwliya.”—Ellen G. White, _Counsels on Stewardship,_ pp. 18, 19.

“Bororokni thani Kaitorni yapharmung bwswk se Kotor, abo chini Kaitor bubo hai khe phunuk! Bo bororokno yaphar kha, hakhe bororok bini Mwthangmano, bini naharmungno, tei hamjakmano saui manna twi. Nwng bini thani kaham ya twtwi se bo ‘bini songdari Sajlano rohor kha, bini a Sajlano poito khlainai borok bo komor yaui mwthangjak phlaiwi manna tẃi khe Kaitor bono rohor kha’?”—Ellen G. White, in _The Advent Review and Sabbath Herald,_ May 15, 1900.

**Salaijaknai Swngmungrok**

`2 Korinth 8:9 khúktang di. Kaitorni manwini barja-o Jisuni phunukmani tamoni bagwi aswk nangkukmani?`

`Johon 3:16 bwtango Chúknohani simi kaisa yapharmungni kók chwng mano. Johon 15:13; Epis 5:2, 25; Galatia 2:19, 20; tei 1 Johon 3:16 khúktang di. O bwtangrok bai Johon 3:16 bwtangni bisingo gothokmung kókbothoma tamo, chwng abórokni bising twi tamo swrwngwi man?`

`2 Korinth 8-9 beremo sajakma hai, yapharmungo baithangni hamkrai tamo tamo tong?`

`Nwng yapharmung rwmano karwibo, Jisu sakno yapharmani phunukmari hai khe chwng tei tamo tamo khlai man?`
