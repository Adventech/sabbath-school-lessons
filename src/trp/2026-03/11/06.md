---
title:  Thansa
date:   10/09/2026
---

Chwng nuk kha Judiani Mothani kwrwirokno chubanani rang khangna bagwi Pol Korinthni bororoknobo sakha. Bini o samani naharmungni bisingo kaisa karon wng kha thansa khlainani. Kaitorni samungo gwdal rwnani bagwi bo bóhrokno chuba thani manjak rwnani muchungjak kha. Bo phunukna nai kha Jerusalemni Judai poito khlainairok hai kheno buini bororokni (Gentile) Mothani bororokbo Kaitorni nukhungni borok hinwi. O bororok swkango kwtha lai ya tongmani, phiyaba Kaitorni swmai kwtal tongrwkmani bising twi tabuk thansa wng lai kha. Judai bai buini borok bebakno Khristian nukhung kaisa khe Pol núknani muchungjak kha.

Tit bai tei khoroknwi takhuksong wng kha rang khang laimanino naikolnai barja gwnangrok. Titni thani mothano naikolnani muchungma bwkha Kaitor rwkha (2 Korinth 8:16). Mothani bising twi Kaitor tei khoroknwi takhuksongno chongwi nakha (2 Korinth 8:18–23). Bóhrokno “motharok bai rohorjaknairok, Khristoni mwkhangno chẃng rwnairok” hwnjak kha (2 Korinth 8:23). Yapharmung rwmani wng kha Mothani bokhorok Khristoni thani kók narwgwi sói khe tongmani mari se (Epis 4:15).

2 Korinth 8-9 beremo sajak kha, Mothani bising twi Kaitor bai chongjaknai bororokni thani se yapharmung rwthai klaio. Bóhrokni kokno hai hwnwi sajagao “Motha brúm brúm” (2 Korinth 8:18), “motharok bai chongjak” (2 Korinth 8:19), tei “motharok bai rohorjaknairok” (2 Korinth 8:23). “Norok bórokno hamjak di, hakhe norok hamjakmano bebak motha saui man suknai” (2 Korinth 8:24).

Ha sakao Kaitor chongwi rwmani bororokni bising twi motha yapharmung rwmanino tubuwi rwmani abo thansa khlaio, tei abo thansani bwthaibo klaio (2 Korinth 8:13, 14). Bororok rang yaphar phuru Kaitorni chẃngsachani bagwi simi naisigwi rwya khe, rangni bising twi mothao kaklaimungni karon snamjagwi mano.

`Rum 15:26, 27 bwtango Pol thansa khlainani muchungmano bahai khe phunuk kha?`

Páithago Pol sakha, yapharmung wng kha Kaitorni samung khlaimani, khairokmani Samung, hamari, surimani samung, tei thansani samung. O kókno uánsuk jadi.
