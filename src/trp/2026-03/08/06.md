---
title:  Páithago Kwthwino Mechenma
date:   20/08/2026
---

`1 Korinth 15:54–57 khúktang di. Páithago kwthwino mechenmano twiwi o bwtango tamo sakha?`

1 Korinth 15 beremni ni kók paithakno kaisa kókmang gwnang kokbwtang bai khe Pol chengsa kha: “Bahan-bwthẃi gwnang abo Kaitorni ha-o ha banta man glak” (1 Korinth 15:50). Kwbangma Baibel pirnairok uánsug kha, Pol o kók samani abo Chúknoha-o bahan bwthwi kwrwi khe nugwi man ya eba phola simi se tongnani kók sao hinwi. Phiyaba Pol samani kókmang abo hai ya. 1 Korinth 15:50 bwtango “bahan-bwthwi” hinwi Pol samani kókmang wng kha “thwblang” hwnmani kókno sama se. Kaitorni hao tei thwiblang kwrwi kha hinmani. Abo hai kheno 1 Korinth 15:42-49 bwtango tabukni mang (thwiblang) bai thangwi bachamani mangno surchaui Pol kók sakha. Phobjakmani mang abo thwiblang, tei thwiwi thangnai mang. Phiya thangwi bachamani mang abo tei dera ya eba thangblang (1 Korinth 15:50, 53, 54). Pol sana naimani wng kha, Chúknoha-o thangnani hwn khe chini bwsakno ma slainai.

Teibo kók bathing rwui sana thang khe, “bahan-bwthwi” hinwi Pol samani wng kha ang kha sumsok gwnang thwiblang mangni kók se. Judairokni swimungo sumsok gwnang borokno khe “bahan-bwthwi” hinwino swio. A sumsokno gwnang mang Jisu phai phuru kwthar eba kaham-o slaijaknani nangnai hinwi Pol samani se.

Aphuru chini sumsokni tongmungno simi se khibiui rwjaknai (1 Korinth 15:58), tei chwng chẃngsacha bai khobjaknai (1 Korinth 15:51-53; 1 Thesaloniki 4:13-17). Hakhe aphuru sakhlaijaknai: “Butharnai mechenjagwi sokomorjak kha!” (1 Korinth 15:54). Aphuru kwplai wngnairok kwplaini rwchapmung rwchap lai anw: “ ‘O Butharnai, nwng pa de pái si? O Butharnai, nini chwknani phan buko?’ ” (1 Korinth 15:55). Abórok bebakno Jisu tei uáisa phai phuru wngnai (1 Korinth 15:51-52).

Uánsuk naidi: chwng thwi phuru mokolo gorobo, phiya chwng mokol phiyok kheno Jisu phaimani se nuk sinai, aphuru bo chwngno kwthwini mwthangwi tisanai. Jeswk bisi kolokma romwi thwiwi thangjak borok phano, Jisuno poito khlaiwi thwi khe “uáisa bai mokol mukphilik kaisa bai’-no” bóhrok thangwi bachanai tei “bebakno mul philjak bainai. A totro pung khe’-no kwthwirok thangblang khe mwthangwi tisajak bainai, phiya chwngbo bebak’-no mul philjak bainai” (1 Korinth 15:52).

`Chini langma bwswk dakti dakti khe kochogwi tong sabo o kókno sana wngnai? Jisu tei uáisa phaimanibo abo haino wngnai. Bo tei uáisa phai phuru chwng hwnwi mano: “Aha! Subrai aswk daktino phaima klai kha!” Chwng bo phainani “lero” hinwi uánsukmano twiwi o kók chwngno tamo bujinani chuba?`
