---
title:  Khristo Thangwi Bacha Kha, Chini Kaisa Susuk Khatungma!
date:   17/08/2026
---

1 Korinth 15:9–19 bwtango Pol sakha, Khristo thangwi bachamano gosi ya hwn khe belai kirimasingcha manjaknai. Khristo thangwi bacha ya khe tabukni bagwi tei thinangni bagwibo mungsa khatungma kwrwi.

`1 Korinth 15:9–19 khúktang di. Khristo thangwi bacha ya khe chini tamo kwmama klai?`

Kwcham jorani mwtai khulumnairok kwthwirok thangwi bachamano poito khlai ya phiyaba Grikrok khe poito khlaio, bóhrok máng tei phola juda juda hinwi poito khlaio. Bóhrok uánsugo thwi khe borokni phola mungsa swk thaio thangwi paio. 1 Korinth 15:12-19 bwtango Pol swngmani belai uanamasingcha: “Norokni bere baksa baksa bahai khe kwthwirok mwthangwi tisajak glak hwnwi sa laiwi man? ” (1 Korinth 15:12). Mokol bai núknairok tongwi suda kwthwirok thangwi bacha ya hwnmano Pol belai uanajak kha (1 Korinth 15:5–8). Phiyaba Jisu thangwi bacha ya khe mungsa khatungma kwrwi. Tatal-o se poito khlaiwi tongma klaio, tei bóhrok bóhrokni sumsogo’-no tong kho.

Bo sana naikha, tumungno kwthwirok thangwi bacha ya khe— (1) Khristobo thangwi bacha ya (1 Korinth 15:13, 16); (2) Chwng kók pirmani mungsa chuk ya (1 Korinth 15:14); (3) Chini poito’-bo chuk ya (1 Korinth 15:14); (4) Chwng pirmani abo tatal (1 Korinth 15:15); (5) chini poito samungo chuk ya (1 Korinth 15:17); (6) chwng tabukbo sumsogo tong kho (1 Korinth 15:17); tei (7) je thwiwi thangnairok bóhrok tei kwrwi kha (1 Korinth 15:18).

Thangwi bachamani kwrwi khe kók pirnanibo mungsa kwrwi tei poitonanibo mungsa kwrwi (1 Korinth 15:14). “Kwrwi” hwhmani Grik kókthai-o khe _kenos_ hino. Aboni kuthuk kókmang tongo. _Kenos_ hwnmani kókmang wng kha kubui kwrwi, eba mungsa bwthai kwrwi, eba khe mungsa naharmung kwrwi hwnmani se (“chuk ya”).

1 Korinth 15:17 bwtango Grik kókthai _mataios_ thepajak kha. Abo _kenos_ kókthai bai gothokjak. Aboni kókmang wng kha, tumungno Jisu thangwi bacha ya khe, chini poito mungsa buya, chwng tatal-o poito khlai tongma se klainai, tamni hwn ba chini sumsok nakarjagwi man ya kho (1 Korinth 15:17). Abo hai hwn khe chwng buino lama koro rwnai, buino tatal sanai, tei sakno bandinai se wngma klaio (1 Korinth 15:15).

`Thwimani ulono bororok Chúknoha-o eba morkeo thang khe, 1 Korinth 15 beremno bahai khe buji kha? Hakhe “kwthwirok thuwi tongo” hwnmani phrwngmung tamoni aswk nangkukmani?`
