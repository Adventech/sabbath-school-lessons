---
title:  Khristo Wng Kha Tháiskangni Bwthái
date:   18/08/2026
---

Tumungno Jisu thangwi bacha ya khe, chini thinang bukcha simi se wngnai (1 Korinth 15:12–19). Phiyaba “Kók kubui khe omo, Khristo kwthwini mwthangwi tisajak kha” (1 Korinth 15:20). Abo láibumani kubui patimung. Hakhe Khristono poito khlai thwinairok bo phai phuru thangwi bachanai hinwi chwng sai man sugo (1 Korinth 15:20–23).

`1 Korinth 15:20–23 khúktang di. Jisu wng kha “tháiskangni bwthái” hinwi sajakmani kókmang tamo?`

Sumsok gwnang swngcharni páithak abo Khristono poito khlai thwinairok thangwi bachamani marini bising twi khe se phunukjaknai (1 Korinth 15:24; Phunukjakmani 20:5, 6). Páithakni Adam hai khe Khristo bini yago tubumani bebakno bini buphano rwmani bising twi hano buphani thani kiphilwi rwnai (1 Korinth 15:25-28). Khristo Kaitorni tolao tongnai (1 Korinth 15:28) hwnmani kók Adam bai Khristono surchamani bising twi ma bujinai. Kaitor borokno mwthangnani chapmungo páithakni Adam hai khe (1 Korinth 15:45) Jisu bebakno Kaitorni muchungmao yapharwi rw bainai,—phiya puilani Adam abo hai khlaiwi man liya.

1 Korinth 15:20-34 bwtango Pol saphikha, Khristo thangwi bachamano poito khlai ya wngmani abo butua wngma klaio hinwi. Phunukmarini bagwi bo Baptismani kók sakha. Baptisma wng kha poito khlainairok Khristo bai baksa thwiwi tei thangwi bachamani mari (Rum 6:3, 4; Kolosai 2:12). Aboni bagwino thangwi bachamano poito khlai ya wngmani abo kókmang kwrwi se. Phiyaba “kwthwirokni mungwi baptisma” hinwi Pol samani abo bo tamo sana naikha o kókno bujinani kutuk se (1 Korinth 15:29).

“O kókni kókmangno twiwi juda juda kók phirokmung rwlai kha. Phiyaba o kókno chwng omo hai khe bujiui mano: baksa borok baptisma nakha, bóhrok thangwi bacha phuru bóhrokni hamjakma borok thwiwi thangnairok bai malaina bagwi. Kók wng kha, Khristono poito khlaiwi thwi khe mwthangjaknai hinwi bóhrok Baptisma nakha. Thwi thangnairokni bodlao baptisma namani kókno Pol samani ya. Phiya Pol sana naikha, thwiwi thangnairok bai malainani muchungma bagwi baptisma namani kók.”—Carl P. Cosaert, “1 Corinthians,” _Andrews Bible Commentary: New Testament_ (Berrien Springs, MI: Andrews University Press, 2022), p. 1652.

Tei kaisa kók khe, tumungno kwthwirok thangwi bacha ya hwn khe, thwi twi thwi twi khe samung khlaimanibo mungsa chuk ya (1 Korinth 15:30-32). Aboni slái swngcharo tongthok-chathok khe tongmano kaham bwla (1 Korinth 15:32).

`2 Timothi 1:12 bwtango Pol kók samano nai jadi. Bahai khe bo thinangno khatung súgwi man? Chwngbo bahai khe mannai?`
