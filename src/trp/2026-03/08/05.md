---
title:  Thangwi Bachamani Máng
date:   19/08/2026
---

1 Korinth 15:35–39 bwtango thangwi bachamani mangno twiwi Pol kisa sakha. Bo kainwi swngmungni bising twi kók cheng kha: “ ‘Kwthwirok bahai khe mwthangwi tisajaknai? Bórokni mang ba bubo tẃi wngnai?’ ” (1 Korinth 15:35). O swngmungni kók phirokmung wng kha 1 Korinth 15:36–49 bwtango mano.

`1 Korinth 15:36–41 bwtangno khúktang jadi. 1 Korinth 15:35 bwtangni swngmungno bahai khe oro kók phirokmung rwjak kha?`

Thangwi bacha phuru tamo wng abono sai manna bagwi Pol kaitham salmung eba phunukmarini (analogies) kókno sakha. Puila kók wng kha (1 Korinth 15:36–38) máng abo bwchlwi hai, puila a bwchlwi (mang) thwina nangnai. Aboni ulo se buphang wngwi achanai. Kók belaino sraisrai, thangwi bachamani wng kha Kaitorni soidomni samung se. Aboni ulo Pol sakha, “Bebak hama gwnangni’-no bahan podsa ya; bororokni bahan podsa, yabrwirokni bahan podsa, toksa-toktẃirokni bahan podsa, phiya árokni bahan tei podsa. Nokhani manwirokbo tongo, swngcharni manwirokbo tongo. Nokhani manwirokni naithokma podsa, swngcharni manwirokni naithokma tei podsa” (1 Korinth 15:39, 40). Phiya chini bwsak eba mang khe Chúknohani tongkhor-o tongwi mannai bwswk eba mang. O kókno teibo phunukjak kha chẃngsacha bwsakni phunukmarini bising twi (1 Korinth 15:40, 41). Thangwi bachamani mang abo chini tabukni mang eba sumsokni mang bai belai juda chẃngsacha wngnai.

O kókno bujina twi khe chini swngcharni mang bai thangwi bachamani mangni bisingo kaibrwi pher laimung tongo hinwi Pol phunuk kha. Tabukni chini mang khe abo thwiblang, kebel, bahan-bwthẃini mang, tei swngcharni mang. Phiya mwthangwi tisajakmani mang khe abo thangblang, kwrak, Pholano mang, tei Chúknohani mang (1 Korinth 15:40-44). Borokni bwsak kainwi tongo hinwi Pol sana naimani ya. Phobjakmani mang bai thangwi bachamani mangno buji rwnani bagwi Pol Grik kókthai _sōma_ (mang) thepa kha. Aboni kókmang wng kha borokni kaisa mangnono sajago. Phiya a kaibrwi pher laimung wng kha, borokni kaisa mangno phobjakmani ulo thangwi bacha phuru bahai wngnai abono phunugo. Chini thinangni bwsak eba mang belai kaham wngnai.

Pol jephuru “pholani mang” hinwi sao, abo bwsak kwrwi mangno samani ya. Bo sana naimani khe, Jisu “Chini o thwiblang bwsak kebelno bo sláiwi bini chẃngsajak bwsak hai khe snamnai” hwnmani se (Philipi 3:21). Chini sói mangno chwng mannai. Tamo khe chini mang abo tei dera glak, eba hamya wng glak. Chwng deramani, hamya wngmani, tei thwimani swngcharo tongmani ba a kókno uánsuknani tukjagwi mano. Phiyaba Jisu chwngno abo hai kheno kók rwkha.
