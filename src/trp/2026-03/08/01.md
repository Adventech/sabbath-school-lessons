### O Hatibarsani Swrwngjaknai
1 Korinth 15; Luk 24:44–47; Phunukjakmani 20:5, 6; Kolosai 2:12; 2 Tim. 1:12; 1 Thes. 4:13–17.

> <p>Muitu Bwtang</p>
> “Phiya Khristo mwthangwi tisajak ya hwn khe chinibo mungsa phano pírna kwrwi, norokbo mungsa phano poito khlaina kwrwi. . . . phiya Khristo mwthangwi tisajak ya hwn khe norok poito khlaimani abo kusubu ya, norok tabukbo sumsokni bagwi komorwi tong lai kho” (1 Korinth 15:14–17, KOKBSI).

Polni jorao baksa bororok kwthwirok thangwi bachamano poito khlai ya, abono twiwi Pol kók ma sakha. Thwi khe tamo wng bororok bororok abono nugo. Bóhrok saui mano, thwi khe borokni bwsak geleui thango, aboni ulo hadlwi khaui thango, tei páithago khe kwmaui thango. Baksa borok jora kolok romwi kwthwi tongmanobo bóhrok saui mano.

Kwthwirok thangwi bachamano twiwi a jorani bororok poito khlai man ya wngma haino tabukni molni bororokbo poito khlaiwi man ya. O jwngjalno twiwino Pol kók ma sakha.

O kók belai nangkukmani. Tumungno Jisu thangwi bacha ya hwn khe, Jisuno twiwi bo samani abo hai se Jisu wngma klai ya. Krusni phanbo tong glak, eba chini sumsokbo nakarjak glak. Chwng uana chinta kheno ma tong khamun, eba khatungma kwrwi wngwi tong khamun. Phiyaba chini Subrai thangwi bacha kha, Chúknoha-o kasaui thang kha, tei chwngno bini tongthai-o tlangna bagwi phai phinai!

O hatibaro chwng 1 Korinth 14 beremno nai lai anw, tei Khristo thangwi bachamano Pol tamo phrwng kha nai lai anw. Ganani mwtai khulumnai bororokni kókrokno khnaui Korinthni bororok uánsuk kha, kwthwirok thangwi bacha ya hinwi. Phiyaba Pol kók phirokmung rwkha, Khristo thangwi bachamani simi se chini mwthangjaknani khatungmani kaisa lama.

_August 22, Sabat salni bagwi o hatibarsani rwngchapno swrwngwi tiyar wng di._
