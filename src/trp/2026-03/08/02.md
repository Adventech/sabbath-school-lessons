---
title:  Khristo Thangwi Bachamano Pirmani
date:   16/08/2026
---

1 Korinth 15 beremno Pol kothoma kahamno phunukma bising twi cheng kha. Kothoma kahamno twiwi bo sakha: (1) A kothoma kahamno bo Korinthsongno pir kha; (2) bóhrok a kothoma kahamno yachak kha; (3) a kothoma kahamno bóhrok poito khlaiwi tongo; tei (4) a kothoma kaham baino bóhrok mwthangjaknai (1 Korinth 15:1, 2). Chwng mwthangjaknani bagwi Khristo thangwi bachamani bwswk se nangkukmani hinwi Pol phunukna bagwi cheng phuruni bo kothoma kahamni bising twi mwthangjakmani kókno sakha (Rum 10:9, 10 naijdi). Kothoma kaham-o Jisu thangwi bachamani kók belai kheno nangkukmani. O kókno khibi khe, bóhrok Jisuno poito khlaimanino se kebengma klaio.

`1 Korinth 15:1–4; Luk 24:44–47; tei Rum 1:1–4 khúktang di. O bwtangrokni bisingo gothokmung tamo?`

1 Korinth 15:1-4 bwtango Pol samani kókno bathong khe chwng mano. “Elembago swijak tongma hai” hinwi Pol samani wng kha Swmai Kwcham Baibel bwtangni kókno se bo sao. Nangkukmani kók wng kha, Jisu thwimani, tei thangwi bachamani abo Swmai Kwcham-o Kaitor swmai rwmani se nang phai baio.

`1 Korinth 15:2, 11 khúktang di. Pirmani tei poito khlaimanino twiwi o bwtango tamo sa? Poito khlaimani tei pirmani o kainwini bisingo tamo kwrwng laimung tong?`

Khristo thangwi bachamano pirnairok jotono bo thwngwi bacha kha hinwi laibumano poito ma khlainai. O kókno twiwi 1 Korinth 15:5-8 bwtango Pol nangkukmani kók sakha. O bwtango sao, Khristo thangwi bachamano kwbangma borokno nuk kha. Jisu thangwi bachamano núknairok Pol koktun swimani jorao’-bo kwthang tong bai kho (1 Korinth 15:6).

Pol samani khe, bo nana naikha, “Norok thang di, a núknairokno tamo nuk kha swngwi di.” Khristo thangwi bachamano twiwi bo poito khlai sugo.

A bororok wng kha mokol baithang Khristo thangwi bachamano núknairok. Bóhrokni kókno Jisu hai hwnwi sakha “Norok’-no omorokno mokol baithang nuknairok” (Luk 24:48).
