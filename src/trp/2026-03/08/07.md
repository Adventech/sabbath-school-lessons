Ellen G. White swimani ‘_The Great Controversy’_ bijapni “Kaitorni bororok phiyókjak kha” beremno khúktang di, bwkang-635–652.

“Khristoni Kaitorni tongmung poito khlainairokno mwthangnai langmani khatungma rwo. Jisu sao, ‘Ano poito khlainai borok bo thwi phano thangnai. [26] Phiya kwthang tongsani ano poito khlainai borok bo thwi phlai glak. Nwng o kókno poito da khlai?’ Oro Jisu bo tei uáisa phai phuruni kókno se saui tongo. Aphuru borok kahamrok tei thwiphlai ya mangno twiwi thangwi bachanai. Tei poito khlainai kwthang tong soknairok khe thwi yauino Chúknoha-o tisai tlangjaknai. Jisu Lasarno mwthangwi tisamani soidomni samung wng kha bebak borok kaham thwinairok thangwi bachanai hinwi phunukmani se.”—Ellen G. White, _The Desire of Ages,_ p. 530.

“Mangkhoro thuwi tongnai poito khlainairokno Kaitorni Sajla ring phai phuru o ha khorang kotor khe chiriknai. Aphuru bini ringmano khorang yachaknai, tei chẃngsacha thangblang mangno chumwi bororok bachanai, tei khorang sotonwi sanai: ‘Butharnai mechenjagwi sokomorjak kha! Butharnai sumsokni bagwi se chwknani phan mano, phiya sumsok Kerongni bagwi se phan mano.’ (1 Korinth 15:55). Aphuru kwthang tong soknai poito khlainairok bai thangwi bachanai poito khlainairok thansa khe tongthokmani tei kwplaini khorang rwnai. ... Subrai bai malaina bagwi kwthang tong soknai poito khlainairok tei thangwi bachanai poito khlainairok nokhao kasaui thangnai. Thwimungni bising twi ma kaklainai, bayab tei hamjakma bororok bai malai phinai, aphuruni simi khe bóhrok tei kaklaina nang glak.”—Ellen G. White, _The Story of Redemption,_ pp. 411, 412.

**Salaijaknai Swngmungrok**

`Jisu thangwi bacha phuru bono mokol baithang núknairokni kókno uánsuk di (Samung 1:22, Samung 2:32, Samung 3:15, Samung 4:33, Samung 5:30–32). Bahai khe chwng bisi nwisaini ulo phano bo thangwi bachamani ”sakki” wngwi man?`

`Khristo thangwi bachamani wng kha kothoma kaham pirmani bisingo kaisa kók bothoma se (1 Korinth 15:1-4). Thangwi bachamani kók kwrwi khe, Khristo thwimani kók pirmani mungsa samungo chuk ya (1 Korinth 15:14). Khristo thwimanibo mungsa chuk ya. Tamoni bagwi? Tamoni bagwi nini kók phirokmungo abono Khristo thangwi bachamani phan hinwi nwng sanai?`

`Pol samani kókno uánsuk di, bo sakha: “Phiya kwthwirok mwthangwi tisajak ya hwn khe phai di, kógo tongma hai’-no ‘Chwng khwna’-no thwinai ba chá lai si anw nung lai si anw’ ” (1 Korinth 15:32). Omoni kókmang tamo?`

`Thwimungno twiwi class-o kók tisa laidi. Kwthwirok thwi phuruno Chúknoha-o kasaui thango hwnmani tamoni bagwi 1 Korinth 15 beremno kók bai kókmang gothok ya?`
