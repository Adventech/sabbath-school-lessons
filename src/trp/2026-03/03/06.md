---
title:  Krus’-no Phunuknai Tongmung
date:   16/07/2026
---

Chwng kwtal bodol snamwi gut khalaimani wng kha borokno twiphang eba bokhorok snamnanino naharwi se. Phiyaba abo chwng borokno twiphang eba bokhorok snam man ya hwnmani ya. Mothani samung tangnai twiphang eba bokhorokrokno chwng bórom khlaina nango tei chubana nango. Ha sakao bini samung khlaijakna bagwi Kaitor se bororokno chongwi nao. Jisu hai khe koinene wngwi sakno yapharwi samung khlainai Mothano twiphang eba bokhorokrokni kók norok khnaui mano, tei bóhrokno rwktharnani chugo.

O kók kubui, tamni hwn ba bororokno sakni kókno uánsukmani mwthaknani tei Kaitorni kók khnanai snamnani phan Kruso simi se tongo. Sakno yaphar súgwi Khristo hai khe samung khlainairok bóhrok samung chamarokno Kaitor se samung cha rwi hinwi sao. Swngcharo samung khlaima jora Jisu borok wngwi bebak chẃngsacha Kaitorno máng se rwo (Johon 17:4).

Krusni kókni bising twi se soi kók nwrwknai Khristianrokni samung wngna nango hinwi Pol sakha. Bororokno mwthangna bagwi Kaitorni phan tei elem phunuknai wng kha krus. Phiya abo borokni elemno butua eba samung chuk ya hinwibo phunugo. 1 Korinth 5:1-13 bwtango Krusni kókno twiwi Pol sraisrai khe phunuk kha. Puila bo sakha, Khristianni twiphang eba bokhorok bahai wngna nangnai Kaitor se bóhrokni tongmungno snamo (1 Korinth 4:1-5). Aboni ulo, sói Khristoni samung khlai khe ma sátokjaknai, abo se kaisa mari (1 Korinth 4:9, 11-13). O ulni kók samano teibo cherwi swbai sukrubwi sanani nangnai.

`2 Korinth 11:23–28, tei Kolosai 1:24 khúktang di. Khristoni bagwi sátokjakmano twiwi o bwtango tamo phrwng kha?`

Khristianni twiphang eba bokhorokrok le Jisuni yaprino se rwktharo. Bóhrokni tangpal takhuk tei bukhuksongni bagwi tekto mannani suda muchungjago. Tumungno Kaitorni samungni bagwi thwina nang khe, bóhrok thwina sudano gosio. Pol bini sakni kókno, tei Apoloni kókno hai hwnwi sakha, “chwng lukuni bwskango butharjakna bagwi marja berjak borok hai se wng si-o” (1 Korinth 4:9). Bóhrok mai machaya tei twi ma nung ya wngmani kókrokno sakha. Teibo sakha, “chwng kichik-kachak kanwi tongo; chwng bujago; chini nok kwrwi” (1 Korinth 4:11). Bóhrok kutukjak kha, sátokjak kha, mung hamya man kha, teibo bo sakha “Chwng wng kha o swngcharni laisrwi bosrwi simi se, tabuk suda chwng o swngcharni simbuk!” hinwi (1 Korinth 4:12, 13).

Korinthni bororokno gwnang, Bugra, elem gwnang tei borijaknai borok hinwi ring kha (1 Korinth 4:8, 10). Phiyaba Khristian twiphang eba bokhorok hwn khe kungchukthai kwrwi hinwi Pol phunuk kha. Tamni hwn ba kungchukmani abo mothao kaklaimung tubuo (1 Korinth 4:6).

`Mothao nini jesa samung phano wng thung, phiyaba Khristoni bagwi nwng bwswk tekto man kha? Nini kók phirokmungo tamo swrwngjaknai tong?`
