Ellen G. White swimani _‘The Acts of the Apostles’_ bijapni “Khorokchirokno Phrwngmani” rwngchpno khúktang di, bwkáng - 17–24.

“Kaitorni bororok kubuino poito khlaiwi abo hai khe tong khe, bohrok thansa wngo. Bóhrok thansa wng khe se swngchar bóhrokno Kaitorni borok hinwi sininai, tei bóhrokni thani Kaitorni kók tongo hinwi saui mannai. Phiyaba o thansano Soitan hamjak ya. Bo dera rwnani naio...”—Ellen G. White, _Testimonies for the Church,_ vol. 1, p. 327.

“Kaitorni kubui-o thansa khe bachanani bagwi se Kaitor bini bororokno twio. Mothao kók bang laimanirokno Kaitor norwi, motha dera twi khe narwknani muchungjago. Kaitor bini bororokno thansa khlaina muchungjago. Jisu bo kruso thwinani swkang surikha, bo bai bupha thansa wngma hai kheno bini bororokbo thansa wngna bagwi. Bóhrok thansa wng khe se swngcharni bororok Kaitor Jisuno rohor kha hinwi poito khlainai. Jisuni a surimung bini Sagrirokni bagwi simi ya, phiya jotoni bagwino..’”—Ellen G. White, _Testimonies for the Church,_ vol. 4, p. 17.

**Salaijaknai Swngmungrok**

`O ha sakao samung khlai lang phuru páithago Jisuni surimung wng kha thansa wngnani bagwi. Bo suri kha, “Nwng ano rohoro hwnwi swngchar poito khlaiwi manna tẃi khe bórok thansa wng thung” (Johon 17:21–23). Kaitor bini Sajlano swngcharno mwthangna bagwi rohor thani Khristo-o thansa abo tamoni aswk nangkukmani? Thansa kwrwi khe tamoni Mothani samungo jwngjal achai?`

`1 Korinth 4:9–13 khúktang di, tei Pol bahai khe oro sakha nai jadi. Swngcharni twiphangrok tei Bugrarok bai oro sajakmani twiphang eba bokhorokrok bahai khe pher lai?`

`1 Korinth 4:16 khúktang di. O bwtango Korinthsongno Pol sakha bo hai khe bóhrokno’-bo tong di hinwi. Borok twiphang eba bokhorok hai khe nwng tongnani da muchung? Twiphang eba bokhorokrokno borom rwui torsa rwmani abo belai kirimasingcha! Phiyaba bahai khe bóhrok hai khe tongmani abo bai pher lai?`
