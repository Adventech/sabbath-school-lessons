---
title:  Khristo Hai Khe Seleng Wngmani
date:   15/07/2026
---

`1 Korinth 4:1, 2 khúktang di. Borokni twiphang eba bokhorokno twiwi sói nukmung bubo hai wngna nangnai? Abono twiwi chwng tamo swrwngwi man?`

1 Korinth 3:1-4 bwtango Pol sakha, Mothao kàklai phuilai wngmani, tei gut khalaimani, abo pholao chakra wng ya wngmani bagwi se. O kókno sana swkang bo sakha, “chwng Khristoni bwkhano man kha” (1 Korinth 2:16). Abo Khristo hai khe uánsukmani, tei samung khlaimanino se sajago. Kók wng kha, Khristo hai khe uánsuk khe, tei samung khlai khe se a poito khlainaisa “Khristoni bwkhano” manma klaio. Phiyaba langmani bebak jorao Khristo hai khe tongnani kwlai de? Greek-Roman hani molo, political twiphang, philosopher, uánsuk kwrwng, tei tongthar twimungni Bokhorokrokni bisingo kotor bataimung wngo. Korinth’-ni mothano swngcharni o tongmungno rwkthar khwna. Tabukni chini molobo mothao abo kirimasingcha wngwi mano.

`Philipi 2:5–8 khúktang di. “Khristoni bwkha” (1 Korinth 2:16) hwnmano twiwi bahai khe chwng bujiui man?`

Korinth Mothani hai kheno Philipi mothao’-bo kàklai phuilai wng lai kha (Phil. 2:1–4). Philipi 2:1–8 bwtango phrwng kha, Khristo hai seleng wng khe sakno tei sakni muchungma-o ma thwinai, tei Jisu hai khe buini hamarino ma rutuknai.

Khristo hai selengno’-no “Khristoni seleng” hwnwi Pol sakha 1 Korinth 4:1). Aboni kókmang wng kha, bóhrok Khristoni bisingo seleng wngwi chubanairok, tei bini bagwi samung khlainairok. Jisu phrwngma hai khe twiphang wng khe se abo se sói twiphang. Jisu buino chubao, tei buini bagwi Samung khlaio. Abórokno “barja rwjaknai borok” hinwibo Pol sakha (1 Korinth 4:1-2). Buini manwino kaham khe naikolnai hinwi kha berajagwi mannai borok wng kha barja rwjaknai borok. Chwng Khristoni manwirokno naikolna bagwi barja rwjak kha, hakhe chini bebak tongmani abo Khristoni máng se.

Surimani bising twi Philipi 2:5–8 naidi. Kaitor sakno butharwi chwngno uanamasingcha khe hamjakmano twiwi o bwtango tamo chwngno phrwng kha? Chwng bahai khe sakno butharwi omo hai khe hamjaknai wngwi mannai?
