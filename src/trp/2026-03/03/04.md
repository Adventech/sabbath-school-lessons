---
title:  Elem Tei Chakra Wngmani
date:   14/07/2026
---

Twiphang eba Mothani bokhorok wngnairokni kókno máng uánsuk kheno Mothani bororokni bisingo kók bang laimung sok phaio. Omo Mothani thansano swbaiwi mano, tei poito khlainairokni pholani langmano dera rwui mano. Omo hai kheno Motha-o kák lai phuilai wng laio. Jephuru bororok Mothani bokhorokrokno simi kwbang khe naisik khe, eba naiwi tong khe abo belai kirimasingcha! Tamni hwn ba abo bóhrok Jisuno bóhrokni langmani soi tomgthaini norwi rohorna klai-o.

`1 Korinth 3:1–4 khúktang di. Korinth-ni bororok pholao chakra wng yano twiwi Pol bahai khe sakha?`

Pol sraisrai khe sakha, pholao chakra wng khe poito khlainairok Kaitorni elemno lobsanai (1 Korinth 2:6, 7), a elem Pholani bising twi chini thani rwjago (1 Korinth 2:13). A elem o swngcharni buya (1 Korinth 2:6), tei borokni elem buya (1 Korinth 2:13). Khristoni krusni bising twi Kaitorni elem phunukjak kha (1 Korinth 2:1–4). Khristo tekto manmani, thwimani, tei thangwi bachamani bising twi Kaitorni elem phunukjak kha. Motha-o thansa wngnani hwn khe (1 Korinth 3:1–17), poito khlainairok sói elem tei Khristo-o chakra wngnani kókno bujina nangnai hinwi Pol muchungjak kha.

Elem gwnang tei chakra wngjak Khristian hwn khe bo pholani borok. Bo tei o swngcharni borok hai liya, tei chwrai hai buya (1 Korinth 3:1). Bóhrok pholani kókno, pholani kók baino naio, tamni hwn ba “Kaitorni Phola phrwngmani kók … Kaitorni Phola bai simi se abórokno bwswk tamo saui mano” (1 Korinth 2:13, 14). Elem gwnang tei chakra wngjak Khristian hwn khe mai chao, dut chaya (1 Korinth 3:2; Hibru 5:12). Poito khlainairok “Abuktwi nungwi tongnai borok bo tabuk phano chwrai kho, bubo cha bubo cha ya abo bo mungsa phano saui man ya kho. Phiya ba mai hwn khe abo okrarokni chámung bórok le chánaiwi manjagwi phaijak, bubo kaham bubo hamya abono saui mano” (Hibru 5:13, 14). Elem gwnang tei chakra wngjak Khristian hwn khe “Ang Polni,” eba “Ang Apoloni,” hinwi sa glak (1 Korinth 3:4), bohrok juda juda bodol snam ya.

Elem gwnang tei chakra wngjak Khristianrok khe “Kaitorni samung tang lainairok” se (1 Korinth 3:9). Chwng Motha, chwng wng kha Kaitorni kheto, nok, tei Nókthar (1 Korinth 3:9, 16, 17). Khristoni bising twi chwng jotono Kaitorni (1 Korinth 3:11).

`Nwng belai bórom khlaimani bororokni bising twi nwng bwkha baimani da tong? Tumungno tongo hwn khe, aboni bising twi nwng tamo swrwng kha?`
