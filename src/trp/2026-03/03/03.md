`1 Korinth 1:10 khúktang di. “Norok khá baksa khe kók tháisa khe thansa wng súk di” hinwi Pol samani kókmang tamo?`

Kwtal bodol khagwi gut khalaimani tei Motha-o bodolni bisingo jwngjal achaimani wng kha Khristoni koinene tongmungno khibimani se klaio (1 Korinth 1:10). Kaitor chwngno ringo, “Bini Sajla Jisu Khristo bai thansa wngna bagwi” (1 Korinth 1:9). Chini Subrai wng kha Jisu Khristo. Hakhe chwng Jisu-o se yaphang ma snamnai. Aboni bagwino o swngmungni kók phirokmung wng kha ahai: “Khristo ba omo hai khe kha se khajak si bla! Hai da? Pol da norokni bagwi kruso thwi? Polni sagri wngna bagwi da norok baptisma wng?” (1 Korinth 1:13). Kók phirokmung wng kha: “Ihi!” Khristo khakjak ya. Khristo chini bagwi kruso thwi kha. Tei chwng “Pha, Sajla, tei Phola Kwtharni mung bai baptisma nakha” (Mathai 28:19).

Pol saui khna rwkha chwng wng kha “Khristoni bwsak, khoroksa khoroksa wng kha bini bedek” (1 Korinth 12:27). Bwsakni kwbangma manwi tongo. Joto manwinino janija samung tongo. Abo hai khe se kaisa bwsak wngo. Bwsak kaham khe cholina hwn khe, bwsakni manwirok janija manma samungno kaham khe khlaina nangnai. Omo hai khe se Pol thansa wngmungno nuk kha. Khoroksa bai tei khoroksa juda juda phano thansa wngwi mano—Pol abono nuk kha.

tamo khe bebakno kók tháisa khe thansa wng súgwi Subrai Khristono yapharna nango. Khristo chini Subrai, a nangkukmani uánsukmung kók bai se Pol 1 Korinth bijapno chengsa kha (1 Korinth 1:2, 7, 8, 9, 10). Twiphangrok, tei Mothani bororokni bisingo kók bang laina swkango chwng jotono chini thanio Jisuno tonina nango hinwi Pol sakha. Motha-ni yaphang twiphang ya. Khristianrokni yaphang Khristo Jisu se.

“Norok khá baksa khe kók tháisa khe thansa wng súk di” (1 Korinth 1:10) hinwi Pol samani bujina bagwi 1 Korinth puilani bwtang nai kheno chwng nugo, abo Jisu se Subrai hinwi phunugo. “Thansa” hinwi oro swijakmani abo Grik kókthai _katartizō_ (khwlai kókthai). Aboni kókmang wng kha teisa kaham khlai phirmani. Motha-o kók bang laiwi tong phano teisa kaham khlai phirnani. Khristo-o thansa wngma bising twi simi se abo hai wngwi mannai.
