---
title:  Motha-o Bodolni Bisingo Jwngjal
date:   12/07/2026
---

Pol koimung khlai kha, “norok bebak’-no kók tháisa wng di, kak lai-phui lai ta wng di. Norok khá baksa khe kók tháisa khe thansa wng súk di” (1 Korinth 1:10). 1 Korinth 1 ni simi 4 jora beremni kókbothoma wng kha thánsa tong lainani kok. Baibel scholars rok poito khlaio, thánsa wng kha bebak Polni koktunrokni kók yarwng, eba kókbothoma. Abo bebak bini koktunrokno thansa khlaio.

`1 Korinth 1:12–17 khúktang di. Motha-o kak laiwi gut khalaiwimani, eba janija twiphang snam laimani abo bwswk hamya bujinani bagwi o bwtango chwngno bahai khe chuba kha? Pol a jwngjalni yóknani lama rwmani tamo?`

Korinth Mothani bororok thansa kwrwi wngmano twiwi Pol kwrak khe kók sakha. Bo Grik kókthai _schisma_ (“kak lai-phui lai” 1 Korinth 1:10) tei _eris_ (“uálai-suklai” 1 Korinth 1:11) o kainwi kókthaino thepaui swikha. _Schisma_ (“kak lai-phui lai”) hwnmani juda juda kheno swijak Swmai Kwtal bijap-o mano. Abo wng kha Motha-o kwtal khe bodol snam laimani, eba kak laiwi gut khlai lamani se. Tei _eris_ (“uálai-suklai”) hwnmani kókmang wng kha Khristianrok khlaithai ya abo hai sumsok khlaimanirokno se sajago.

`Rum 1:29, Rum 13:13, 1 Korinth 3:3, 2 Korinth 12:20, tei Galatia 5:20 khúktang di. Eris (“uálai-suklai”) hwnmani kókthai-o tongmani sumsokrok tamo tamo? A sumsok bwswk hamya?`

Korinth Mothao kók bang laimani, tei khoroksa bai tei khoroksa kebeng laimani, páithago bóhrok kes rwlaimani jorao sogwi kha (1 Korinth 6:1-3). Abo hai wngmanino Pol hai hwnwi sakha, “Abo tẃi wng khe norokni bwswk lachimasingcha!” (1 Korinth 6:5). Subraini panda khlai phuru sudano bóhrok a jwngjalno mwthagwi man lai ya, khoroksa bai tei khoroksa swkak laiwi kók bang laio (1 Korinth 11:17-22).

Motha-ni bororokni bisingo thansa kwrwi wngnani jwngjal abo belai kheno kirimasingcha. Pol o kókno twiwi belai uanajago. Bini koktunni puila kókbothoma khe omono twiwino chengsao.

`1 Korinth 1:12–27 khúktang phidi. Mothani thansa-o o bodolni bisingo jwngjal núkjakmani bwswk kirimasingcha? O bwtangrokni bising twi chwng tamo bujiui man? Nini mothao-o phano omo hai wng khe o jwngjalni bahai khe yogwi mannai?`
