### O Hatibarsani Swrwngjaknai
1 Kor. 1:12–17, Rum 1:29, 1 Kor. 1:10, 1 Kor. 3:1–4, Phil. 2:5–8, 2 Kor. 11:23–28, Kol. 1:24.

> <p>Muitu Bwtang</p>
> “Takhukrok, chini Subrai Jisu Khristoni mungwi ang norokno sa ja-o; norok bebak’-no kók tháisa wng di, kak lai-phui lai ta wng di. Norok khá baksa khe kók tháisa khe thansa wng súk di” (1 Korinth 1:10, KOKBSI).

Bolongni malmatarokno amjogwi nainai bororok hwn khe saui man anw, baksa bolongni malmatarok belai kheno thánsa khe tong laio. Geguma, dolphin 🐬 ah, tei muisrumrok belai kheno thánsa khe tong laio. Chimpanjirok (dalsa mwkhra) teibo halok kaham khe somaj snamwi tong laio. Jora jorao 15 ni simi 150 chimpanjirok kotor bodol khe tong laio. Phiyaba abo hai khe thánsa khe tong laimani belai kheno tongthothok. Haiphano jora jorao khe chimpanjirok belai khe ualai laio.

Bororokbo kisa omo hai kheno tong laio. Bororok bodol kaisa khe mángbo tong ya. Uáisa uisu khe bodolni bisingo ualai laio. Omo hai chini Motha bisingo’-bo núkjago! Twiphangni bising twi jora jorao baksa chikonsa bodol snamjago. Phiyaba abo hai bodol uaisa uisu thansa tong lai man ya wngwi, kák laio, tei choba wng laio.

Omo hai wng laimarokno nwng Mothao nude nuk kha?

Tumungno omo hai nini Motha-o jwngjal wngmani tong khe, nwng saui mannai Korinth Motha-o Polbo omo haino wng kha. O hatibaro 1 Korinth 1–4 no cherai lai nai anw. Motha-o jwngjal wngmanino bahai khe nainai, tei Motha-o thansa wngnai, abono twiwi Pol bahai khe kók ma bang lai kha, abórokno nai lai anw.
