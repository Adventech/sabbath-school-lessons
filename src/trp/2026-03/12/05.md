---
title:  Kothoma Kahamni Bagwi Sátokjakmani
date:   16/09/2026
---

Tatal phrwngmungrok wng kha Soitanni samung tangnairok se hinwi phunukmani ulo (2 Korinth 11:1-15) Pol kisisa sakno siya hai khe “kok Kotor” saui phunuk kha (2 Korinth 11:16-21). Tatal phrwngnairokni kók khnamani bwswk chuk ya phunukna bagwi se Pol abo hai khlaio. Korinthsong tatal phrwngnairokno se aswk bórom rwui tong khe, Pol teibo bórom mannani chúkmung tongo. Tamni hwn ba kothoma kahamni bagwi bo sátokjak kha, abo hai khe bo Khristoni samung tangnai khoroksa (2 Korinth 11:22, 23).

`2 Korinth 11:22–28 khúktang di. Oro tamo kók sajak kha?`

Tumungno Pol judai wngwi phaimani, tei bini chúkmungno tatal phrwngnairok hai khe uánsuk khe (2 Korinth 11:22) Khristoni samungo bini yak bóhrokni slái phano kwbang tongo (2 Korinth 11:23). Bo swngmung khlai kha, “Bórok Khristoni seleng da?” Bini kók phirokmung khe, “Ang kobor hai khe sa-o, ang se bórokni slái seleng kaham kuk.” Tamni hwn ba Pol bóhrokni slái teibo samung khlai kuk kha; bo alongrogo ma tong kuk kha; bo teibo kirimasingcha jora kutuk tei sátokjakmung bai manjak kuk kha.

Bo sátokjakmani aswk bai thak ya kho. Bo Judairok bai ang uáibaui phungthamchichuku swprakjak kha (2 Korinth 11:24), Rumrok bai uáitham bujak kha, uáisa holong khitarwi rwjak kha; rungtor kwlwk thani uáitham manjak kha, uáisa twi-o horsa salsa ma tong kha (2 Korinth 11:25), Aswk lama-o tháng thani bo twi kotorobo thwi kha mun, dagaitni yagobo thwi kha mun; bini bororokni yagobo thwi kha mun, buini bororokni yagobo thwi kha mun; taunobo thwi kha mun, bolongobo thwi kha mun, twijlangobo thwi kha mun, takhuk tatalrokni yagobo thwi kha mun (2 Korinth 11:26), bo kolomtwi khiklaiwi samungbo ma tang kha, bo thu yauibo kwbángma ma tong kha, ok khuibo ma tong kha, twi kangwibo ma tong kha; ok pung yauibo kwbángma ma tong kha, rí kwrwini bai kwchangwibo ma tong kha (2 Korinth 11:27), Abono karwibo bebak motharokni bagwi khá súgwibo bo sal brúm brúm poja bokjagwi ma tongo (2 Korinth 11:28).

Khristoni kubui seleng wng khe simi se kothoma kahamni bagwi omo hai khe sátokjaknani muchungwi mano. Tumungno Pol bo sátokjakmarokno twiwi kók kotor sana khe, bini kwbangma sajaknai tongo. Phiyaba bo Khristoni bagwi tamo samung khlai kha abono twiwi Pol kók kotor saya, tamo khe Khristo bini bagwi tamo samung khlai kha abono twiwi se bo kók kotor sao. Pol sai mano, Kaitorni phan borokni kebelni bising twi se teibo sraisrai khe phunukjago (2 Korinth 11:9-10). Hakhe bo sakni khlai manmarokno twiwi kók kotor saui man ya twi khe Kaitor bono bwsago “chor pa rwkha” (2 Korinth 12:7). Aboni bagwi bo koinene wngo, bini bwsak kebelno twiwi bo hus wngo, Kaitorni phanno simi bwkha berao, hakhe Kaitorni khairokma teibo manna bagwi bo bwkha berao.
