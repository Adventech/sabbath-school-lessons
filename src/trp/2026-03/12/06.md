---
title:  Khá Kiphilyarokno Koimung
date:   17/09/2026
---

2 Korinth 12:14–13:10 bwtango Pol Korinthsongni thani uáitham thangna hwnmano twiwi saui man rwkha (2 Korinth 12:14, 2 Korinth 13:1). Bo bóhrokno phunuk kha, bono tatal Chonghorrok bai thopsa phano suthai kwrwi. Tabuk khe Korinth Mothani baksa kha kiphil ya bororokno chubanani bagwi tei uáisa Korinth-o thangnani kók sakha. Abo wng kha bo Korinth-o thangna nangmani kaisa karon. Motha tornani tei hamnani bagwi máng se Pol bebak samungno khlai-o (2 Korinth 12:19).

`2 Korinth 12:20, 21 khúktang di. Korinth Mothani pholani tong halogo hamya-o khiklainai sumsokrok tamo tamo?`

2 Korinth 12:20, 21 bwtango a sumsokrokno khurchaui sajakmani abo Polni kubuni koktunrogo sajakma bai gothokjak (Rum 1:29–31; Galatia 5:19–21). Puilani kainwi sumsok khe 1 Korinth 3:3 bwtangobo tongo. A kainwi sumsok wng kha Korinth Mothani bisingo músú laiwi tongmani, tei uálai laiwi tongmani. Pol uanajak kha, bo Korinthsongni aro uáitham phai phuru bude bóhrokni tongmung slaijak ya wngno hinwi. Aboni bagwi bo sakha, “Ang phaiwi norokno nukna muchung tẃi nuk phai glak, norokbo ano nukma muchung tẃi nuk glak hwnwi ang uana-o” (2 Korinth 12:20). Kók wng kha bo bóhrokno “Khristo kwchang kwchang khe tongma bai” naiwi man glak kha (2 Korinth 10:1), tamo khe “kók khna yarokno akol rwna tiyar” wngna nang sinai (2 Korinth 10:6).

Polni uanamung kotor wng kha a bororok “tongmung sitra tongmani, buitali-buitala wngmani, tei sak tongthok tongmani” yakarwi khá kiphil glak (2 Korinth 12:21). Phiyaba omo hai sumrokrokno Motha-o káklai phuilai khlaio.

Aboni ulo Pol sakha, sumsok khlaiwi thangnai bororokno bahai khe kiphilwi tubunai, abono twiwi Mothani phan tei samung tamo (2 Korinth 13:1-4). Kebel wngwi sumsok khlaina nang kha hinwi samani abo kók pherna naimani klai ya. Tamni hwn ba kwplai khe tongnani bagwi Kaitor phan rwnani swmai rwkha (2 Korinth 13:4). Korinthni baksa bororok buitali-buitala tongwi bóhrok phunugo, bóhrokni langmao Kaitorni phan samung khlai phai ya hinwi. Phiyaba Pol bóhrokno khá kiphilwi Kaitorni phanno sai man rwnani muchungjak kha, abo se borokno kók khnanai khlaio. Pol bóhrokno yagul suyaui bóhrokni bagwi suri kha. Bo sakha, “Norok samung hamya ta khlai thung hwnwi chwng Kaitorni thani suri sano, ... norok samung kaham khlai thung hwnwi chwng omo hai khe suri sano. ... Aboni bagwi’-no norok rak súk thung [kwtal snamjak phi thung] hwnwi chwng suri sano” (2 Korinth 13:7-9). Bwswk se kaham surimung! Bóhrokno sakno uánsugwi nai thung, bóhrok poito khlaiwi de tong eba tong ya si thung hinwi bo sakha.

`2 Korinth 13:5 khúktang di. Poito khlaiwi tongmani kókmang tamo? Chwng poito khlaiwi de tong kha bahai khe saui mannai?`
