### O Hatibarsani Swrwngjaknai
2 Korinth 10:1–17; Jirimia 9:24; 2 Korinth 11:1–15, 22–28; 2 Korinth 12:20, 21; 2 Korinth 13:5.

> <p>Muitu Bwtang</p>
> “Chwng choba khlai thani o swngcharni da-seng tẃi ya, chini da-seng wng kha bera kwrak swbaina bagwi Kaitorni phankrak da-seng se” (2 Korinth 10:4, KOKBSI).

Polni jwngjal pai ya kho hai ma tong phano, aboni bisingo kwtal jwngjal nukjak phikha, bo a kwtal jwngjalno ma naikha: mothao baksa tatal phrwngnairok bacha kha. Bóhrok Polni samungno kebengo. Teibo hamya kók wng kha, a tatal phrwngmungrok Korinthni bororokno bóhrokni bwkhak twlang man kha. Pol o jwngjal bai bini chobano pholani choba hinwi sakha.

Abo samung kwbang wngma da klai? Ihi. Pol sai mano, a bororok bono kebengma klai ya, phiya Khristono se kebengma klaio. Bo (Pol) sakni phan tei bóromno narwgwi mannai, abo hai kungchuknai twiphang ya. Bo sai mano, Kaitorni kók pirnani rwjakmani abo thwi phano eba thang phano pirna nangmani se belai nangkukmani hinwi. A samungni bagwino Kaitor bono chong kha hinwi Pol sai mano. “Pol ... Kaitorni morji bai Khristo Jisuni chonghor wngna hwnwi ringjak” (1 Korinth 1:1).

Mothao tatal phrwngmung nukjak khe, hamjakma bai se abono ma nainai, tei Elembakni sakao phan rwui ma nainai. Kothoma kahamno dera ya twi khe mwthangwi narwkna nangnai. Tamni hwn ba kothoma kaham se bororok mwthangjaknani khatungma tongo.

_September 19, Sabat salni bagwi o hatibarsani rwngchapno swrwngwi tiyar wng di._
