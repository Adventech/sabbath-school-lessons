Ellen G. White swimani _‘The Advent Review and Sabbath Herald,_ September 30, 1873’ kókrwbaini “Ludikiani Motha” beremno khúktang di, bwkáng 125.

“Kaitor bini bororokno laijak jorao úaire uai chaya khlaimani simi mwthang kha. Tatal phrwngnairok kók chaya eba tatal mángno phrwngo, tei kwthang ya tongmung tongo. Bóhrok belai kaham hai khe núkjago, bóhrokni kók kubui hai khe núkjago, phiyaba tatal raida bai khe bororokno lama koro rwnani naio. Man khe Kaitorni chongjak bororokno suda lama koro rwo.”—Ellen G. White, in _The Advent Review and Sabbath Herald,_ January 7, 1904.

“Chini uánsukma tei poitono potaliui nai thung hinwi Subrai muchungjago. Tamni hwn ba abo chwngno Elembak swrwngnani bwswk nangkukmani chuba-o. Aboni bising twi chwng sraisrai bujui mano chwng poito khlaiwi da tong tong ya. Kwbangma kubuino poito khlaiwi tongo hinwi sanai bororok khá kochomwi tongthothok khe tong laio. Bóhrok uánsugo, ‘Ang gwnang, ani sompotirok tongo, tei ani mungsani biyal kwrwi.’ ”—Ellen G. White, _Counsels to Writers and Editors,_ p. 36.

“Kubuino simani ulo phano bororok tatal phrwngmungno yachago. Tumungno bóhrok bóhrokni poitoni Baibel bai khe chánaiwi nai khe, bóhrok sakni uánsukmano Baibel bai khapi rwui sana nai ya hwn khe, hakhe bóhrok mwnakma tei tatal-o tongna nang glak. Kwbangma borok sakni bujima twi baibelni kókno swbaiwi sao. Omo hai khlaiwi bóhrok saknobo lama koro rwo, tei buinobo lama koro rwo. Chwng Baibel pori phuru koinene ma wngnai. Chwng kungchukmani tei sakno juda khe nukmani khibina nangnai. Jora kolokma romwi chwng twiwi phaimani kókrokno kubui hinwi máng uánsuk yaui, Kaitorni kók bai se chánaiwi naina nango.”—Ellen G. White, _Counsels to Writers and Editors,_ pp. 36, 37.

**Salaijaknai Swngmungrok**

`2 Korinth 10:1–6 khúktang phidi. Kaitorni kubuini bagwi pholani “choba” bai malai phuru Polni tangraida bubo hai? Chwngbo bahai khe chini pholani chobao abo hai khlaiwi mannai?`

`Baibel-o sao, mol painani swkango kwbangma tatal phrwngnairok phaiwi bororokno kubui bai hakchal khlainani nainai. Mothani bororok bahai khe aboni katiui mannai? Mothani samungno khlai bainani tamoni bagwi aswk nangkukmani?`

`Pol tamoni bagwi bo sátokjakmarokno kok kotor sanani nangkukmani hinwi uansuk kha (2 Korinth 11:16-33)? “Kaitorno twiwi kók kotor samani” kókmang tamo?`

`Mothani bororok sak sakni poitono potaliui naimani tamoni bagwi nangkukmani (2 Korinth 13:5)? Aro tamo judakuk tong?`
