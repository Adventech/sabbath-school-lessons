---
title:  Tatal Phrwngnairok Sinijago
date:   15/09/2026
---

Khristianrokni bisingo juda juda tatal phrwngnairok hab phaimano twiwi hus khlaiwi rwjakmani swmai Kwtal-o chwng nugo. Abono twiwi Jisu suda bini Sagrirokno hus khlaiwi rwkha (Mathai 7:15-20). Chonghorrokbo abono twiwi hus wngnani kók sakha (Gal. 1:6–9, 1 Tim. 6:3–5, 2 Pit. 2:1–3).

`2 Korinth 11:1–15 khúktang di. Tatal phrwngmungrok bai malaiwi Pol tamo khlai kha?`

Pol tatal phrwngnairokni soi samungno khulogwi phunuk kha. Tei bo phunuk kha, bini samung wng kha Khristoni bagwi samung. Korinthsongno hamjwk kwtal bai khe Pol surchaui kha, tei bo baithangno khe bupha bai surcha kha. Bupha wngwi Khristoni thani hamjwk kwtal yapharnani hinwi bo surchaui sakha (2 Korinth 11:2). Bo [Pol] mothano hamjagwi kwbangma samung khlai kha (2 Korinth 11:11). Bo Korinth Mothani rangri chubachu suda na liya, tamni hwn ba bini manthai tong phano bo liya (2 Korinth 11:7-12).

Phiyaba bono kebengnai tatal Chonghorrok khe (tatal phrwngnairok) Ebino phemloknai chibuk bai khe surchajak kha (2 Korinth 11:3). Eden thaiblwngo Soitan hai kheno Korinth mothao tatal phrwngnairokni tongmung wng kha phemlokmani tei rang chanani (2 Korinth 11:3, 4). Tatal phrwngmungrok Korinthsongno Khristono suriui tongmani hakchal suda khlai mano hinwi Pol sói ma uánsukmani.

Tatal phrwngmungrok Pol pirmani hai khe pir ya—bóhrok kubuni Jisu tei kubuni kothoma kahamno se piro (2 Korinth 11:4). Orono sraisrai, Jisuni kókno pirnai jotono Kaitor dagimani kók pirmungni samungno khlai ya. Abono twiwino Jisu sama tongo: “Ano ‘Subrai, Subrai!’ hwnnairok bórok bebak’-no Kaitorni ha-o habwi man glak, tamo khe chuknohani Aphani muchung tẃi tongnairok simi se habwi man anw” (Mathai 7:21). Galatia 1:6–9 bwtango sao, tei kaisa (juda/kubuni) kothoma kaham pirnairok sakni bagwi swraimung se tubu-o, phiyaba Korinthni baksa bororok a tatalrokno se nai laiwi tongo.

Pol tatal Chonghorrokni tongmungno phunugwi sakha, “A bororok sói sói chonghor ya, bórok wng kha phatrarok se, bórokni samungno tẃiwi tatal saui Khristoni chonghor hai khe sakno mul philo” (2 Korinth 11:13). Bóhrok Khristoni Chonghor hai khe sakno mul philo, abo “Soitan pohorni suari hai khe sakno mul philmani” hai, tei “bini [Soitanni] selengrok seleng kaham hai khe mul philmani” hai (2 Korinth 11:14, 15). Kók paithago Pol hai hwnwi sakha: “bórok samung tangma tẃi’-no bórokni manthaino ma se mannai” (2 Korinth 11:15).
