---
title:  Pholani Choba
date:   13/09/2026
---

`2 Korinth 10:1-11 khúktang di. Korinthsong bai Pol tongmani jorao Pol kwchang kwchang khe koinene wngmani jora jorao kebel wngmani hinwi chaya khe bujijago. Korinth-o tatal phrwngmung habmano twiwi Polni kók oro tamo sajak kha?`

Pol baithang phunugwi 2 Korinth 10 beremno cheng kha: “Ang Pol sakphang’-no norokno sa ja-o” (2 Korinth 10:1). Mothao tatal phrwngmung hab phaiwi Pol bwswk se uanajak kha o bwtango chwng sai mano. 2 Korinth 10:1 bwtango samani abo bono kebengnairokno sama se; tamni hwn ba bóhrok uánsugo Pol swihor phuru khe kók kwrak kwrak swi-o, bóhrok bai tong phuru khe bo kebel se, bini kókrokbo mungsa samung nang ya hinwi (2 Korinth 10:10, 11). Phiyaba kók phirokmungo Pol sakha, bóhrok kebel hinwi uánsukmani abo bo phan gwnang khe koinene wngmani se, tei Khristoni kwchang kwchang tongmungno phunukmani se.

Tatal phrwngnairok bai bwkha kwrak tei khá kotor khe choba ma khlainai (2 Korinth 10:2), phiyaba bóhrok bai choba khlai phuru Jisuni koinene hai khe chwng ma tongnai (2 Korinth 10:1). Jisu sama tongo, “Ang borok kwbo kwchang, kobo koinene” (Mathai 11:29). Phiyaba Nóktharo bepar thwngnairokno dagait hinwi ringwi tei songphang philwi Jisu kha kwrak tei khá kotor khe bóhrokno sakha (Mathai 21:12, 13). Jisu Pharisairokno dadak didik kheno Phatrarok tei chwndwi phuljak holongdapni mangkhor hai hinwi bóhrokno hin kha (Mathai 23:23-27). Jisu hai kheno chwngbo Kaitorni da seng bai pholani choba khlaina nangnai hinwi Pol sai mano (Epis 6:12-17).

2 Korinth 10 beremo Pol choba khlainaini kók sakha (2 Korinth 10:3-6). O choba borok bai borokni choba ya. Phiyaba bororokno Khristoni thani tubuna bagwi pholani choba. Aboni bagwi tatal phrwngmungrokno Kaitorni kók bai se choba khlaiwi ma mechennai. Abo hai khe se “bebak uansukmarokno khaui Khristoni kók khna rw-o” (2 Korinth 10:5).

Pholani chobao Khristo rwmani phan bai se Pol samung khlaio. O phan rwjakma abo bororokno sokomorna bagwi ya; tamo khe bororokno poito-o tisanani tei phan rwnani bagwi se (2 Korinth 10:8). Kaitor rwmani phan bai samung khlainai Mothani bokhorok o kók sanani kwlai. Phiyaba a phan Khristoni aroni se phaio hinwi bóhrok muitu ma khlainai. Khristo hai kheno bóhrok koinene tei kwchang kwchang ma wngnai. Korinthsong tatal phrwngnairokni kók khnaui Khristoni kók khnaya wngmani Pol nuk kha.
