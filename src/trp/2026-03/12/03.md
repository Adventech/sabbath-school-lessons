---
title:  Kaitorno Twiwi Kók Kotor Samani
date:   14/09/2026
---

Miya chwng nuk kha, Pol tei bini tangpalsong bóhrok Kaitorni samung tangmano pholani choba hinwi sakha, tei Kaitorni da seng bai a choba khlaimani kókbo sakha. Tini chwng nai lai anw, tatal phrwngnairok bororokni tong gwrwng hai khe samung khlaio. Bóhrok kók kotor sao. Bo swikha, “Khoroksa swk kók kotor sana muchung khe Kaitor khlaima samungni bagwi kók kotor sa thung” (2 Korinth 10:17).

`2 Korinth 10:13–17 khúktang di. Bahai khe bataimungni bwkha tongmani abo kothoma kaham pirmano dera rwui man?`

Pol sakni bagwi kók kotor samani abo Elembak swbai sukrubwi sanairokni thani belai kaham nangjagwi tongmani. Kwcham molo sakni bagwi kók kotor samani abo kaisa raida hai se, kók khnanairokno khá bai ya khlaina twi khe abo hai ma khlaio. Pol a raidarokno sai manwino bo sak baithang abo hai khlai-o. Phiyaba Pol sraisrai khe sakha, bo sakni bagwi kók kotor samani bai tatal phrwngnairok kók kotor samani bisingo pher laimung tongo. Bo Kaitorni chẃngsacha khlaiwi se Kaitorni bagwi kók kotor sao (2 Korinth 10:17). Abo Swmai Kwchamni kaisa kókbiti: “Kung chuknai borok bórok ang’-no Subraiyung hwnwi sini thung tei saui man thung; ang sói khe hamjago, tei swngcharo bujsói tei kubui bai khe hamjago, tamni hwn ba ang abórokno hamjago” (Jirimia 9:24). Jirimiani swijak bwtangno phunugwi Pol sakha, bo Khristono simi se naio,—Khristoni hamjakma, bujsoi, tei kahamno.

Kók wng kha, Pol kók kotor samani wng kha Khristoni bising twi Kaitor samung charwmano twiwi se. Hakhe bo kók kotor samani abo Elembak bai kwrwngjak se, kebono kebengnai bagwi ya. Phiyaba bono kebengnairok khe sakno bui bai surchaui bataimungni bwkha snam lai kha. Phiyaba abo siya (butua) wngma se klaio! (2 Korinth 10:12).

2 Korinth 10:14-16 bwtango Pol sakha, kothoma kaham pirmani wng kha bini samungni kotor naharmung, abo Korinth Motha-o simi ya, Korinthni phatarni bagwibo. Jisuno hamjakmani bising twi Pol kothoma kaham pirnani muchungma tong kha. Tamni hwn ba Khristo thwimani tei thangwi bachamani bising twi se mwthangjakmung mano.

Korinthni tatal phrwngnairok hai khe Pol sakni bagwi kók kotor saya eba sakno bolai ya. Tamni hwn ba Subrai bono kaham hwnwi hwn khe se bo kaham wngo (2 Korinth 10:12, 18). Bo wng kha “Kaitorni morji bai Khristo Jisuni chonghor wngna hwnwi ringjak” (1 Korinth 1:1). Pol bini langmao tong sak jora twino bo ringjakma hai kheno kwrak khe tongo (2 Timothi 4:7).

`2 Korinth 10:12-18 bwtangno khúktang jadi. Bahai khe Mothani bokhorokrok tei adongrok bataimungni bwkhano khibinai?`
