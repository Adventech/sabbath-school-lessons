Ellen G. White swimani _‘Patriarchs and Prophets’_ bijapni “Sinai-o Takjak mwtai” beremno khúktang di, bwkang 315–330.

“Chwng bui bai halok kaham snam khe, chwng kwbangma kaham samung khlai mano. Kaitorni thani hamari yachaknai bororok buinobo pohor rwna nango, tei chubana nango. ... Kaitorno kubui khe hamjaknairok sakni kók uánsukmano mwthakna nangnai, buini bagwi simi uánsukna nangnai.”—Ellen G. White, in _The Advent Review and Sabbath Herald,_ November 18, 1884, p. 730.

“Poito khlainairokno Pol sakha, chwng kók samani, tei samung khlaimani buino tamo khlaiwi man. Buino dera rwnai abo hai samungno khlai yaui tongna nangnai. Abo takjak mwtaino khulummani se klaio. Chwng poito kebelrokno ma chubanai. ‘Hakhe norok mungsa swk samung khlai phano, chá phano nung phano, norok bebak samung Kaitor lobjakna tẃi khe khlai di….’

“Pol Korinthsongno hus khlaimani abo bebak molo joto borokno bagwino. Abo chini bagwibo. Takjak mwtaino khulummani abo takjak mwtaino khulum kha abo simi ya. Sakni kók simi uánsukmani, tongthok simi naimani, sakni muchung twi simi tongmani abórokbo takjak mwtaino khulummani.”—Ellen G. White, _The Acts of the Apostles,_ pp. 316, 317.

“Jesa samung phano bui kubuino yakarwi thangnai abo hai samungno ta khlai di. Nini kók samani tei samungni bising twi bui bwkha bainai, tei Kaitorno yakarwi thangnai abo hai ta khlai di. Bebak khlai manmani samungno bebak jorao samungo chukmani ya.”—Ellen G. White, _Testimonies for the Church,_ vol. 9, p. 215.

**Salaijaknai Swngmungrok**

`Pol sama hai khe, jora jorao Phola-o torjak eba chakra wngjak Khristian poito kebel bororokno bwkha dukhu nang rwui mano. Buphuru omo hai wng uánsugwi naidi. Abo hai jwngjalrokno nainani bagwi bebakno slái Kaitorno hamjakmani, tei sakno hamjak twi buino hamjakmani le tamoni bagwi aswk nangkukmani?`

`Khristianrok phano takjak mwtaino khulumwi mano, abórok bahai khe eba tamo tamo? Chini langmao takjak mwtai wngwi mano abo hai kaham manwirok tamo? Bahai khe nwng abono sai mannai?`

`1 Korinth 9:27 bwtango Pol sakha, “Buino ‘Phai di, batai di,’ hwn phano ang khibijak ya tẃi khe ang ani sago yamusung kwrak khlaiwi ani muchung tẃi khlaiwi tono.” O kókrokno uánsuk naidi.`

`1 Korinth 10 beremo Takjak mwtairok bai hus wngnani tei a kirimasingchani kókno Pol sakha, “takjak mwtairokno surimano khibi di” (1 Korinth 10:14). Tamoni bagwi takjak mwtai aswk hamya?`
