### O Hatibarsani Swrwngjaknai
1 Korinth 8; Samung 15:20; 1 Kor. 9:1–6; 1 Kor. 10:5–22; Uáinwi Saphima 6:4, 5; Mark 12:28–31.

> <p>Muitu Bwtang</p>
> “Hakhe norok mungsa swk samung khlai phano, chá phano nung phano, norok bebak samung Kaitor lobjakna tẃi khe khlai di” (1 Korinth 10:31, KOKBSI).

1 Korinth 8-10 beremo buitali-buitala wngmani kókno twiwi (abo 5-6 beremo tongo) chwng mwthak kha. Koktunni bising twi Korinthsong bono swngmanirokno’-bo bo kók phirokmung rwbai kha (1 Korinth 7:1). Bini kók phirokmung wng kha 1 Korinth bijapni tei tong rwkma nangkukmani kókrok.

1 Korinth 7 berem-o phunuk kha, buitali-buitala wngmani sumsok (5-7 berem) bai takjak mwtaino khulummani sumsok (8-9 berem) kwrwng laijak se. Swmai Kwtal-o o kainwi sumsokno kwbangma thaiono thansa khe swijago (Naidi, Samung 15:20, 29; Samung 21:25; 1 Korinth 6:9; Epis 5:5; Kolosai 3:5; Phunukjakmani 21:8; 22:15).

1 Korinth 5-7 beremo buitali-buitala wngmani sumsokni kókno Pol kók sakha. 1 Korinth 8-10 beremo khe takjak mwtaino twiwi Pol kók sakha. O kainwi sumsok bai Khristianrok hakchal khe ma tongnai hinwi bo sakha (1 Korinth 6:18; 1 Korinth 10:14).

Thangnai hatibaro chwng nugwi man kha, buitali-buitala wngmani sumsok bai kagwi manna hwn khe Phola Kwtharni Nókthar ma wngnai (1 Korinth 6:19-20). O hatibaro khe chwng nai lai anw “bebak samung Kaitor lobjakna tẃi khe khlai khe” (1 Korinth 10:31) takjak mwtairokni yakni yogwi mannai.
