---
title:  Takjak Mwtairokno Twiwi Hus Khlairwmung
date:   29/07/2026
---

`1 Korinth 10:5-22 khúktang di. Chwng tamoni bagwi takjak mwtairok bai hakchal tongnani nang?`

1 Korinth 10:14–22 bwtango takjak mwtairokni thani barokmani kókno Pol tei uáisa saphikha. Agini molo takjak mwtairokni thani malmatarokno barok khe, ochairokni banta tongmano ochairok hatio phalwi rwo. O muihanrok hatio phaljago. A muihanrok Khristianrok paiwi chai mano hinwi Pol kókbiti rwui sakha.

Takjak mwtairokni thani barokmani muihanno Khristianrok nogo paiwi chaui man phano (1 Korinth 8:1-13), phiya takjak mwtairokni Nóktharo thangwi bóhrok bai baksa chalaimani nung laimani khe Khristianrokni bagwi chaya hinwi Pol sakha. Kók belai kheno sraisrai, takjak mwtairokno barokmani muihanno Khristianrok nogo chaui mano; tamni hwn ba takjak mwtairok mungsa buya (1 Korinth 8:4). Phiyaba takjak mwtairokni pandao thang khe, abo takjak mwtaino khulumma bai baksa (1 Korinth 10:20, 21). Takjak mwtairokni pandao thangmani wng kha bóhrok bai baksa pal wngmani hai (1 Korinth 10:20). Romdi, Subrai pandao manjakmani abo Khristo bai thansa wngmani hai (1 Korinth 10:16).

Pol sakha, “Norok Subraini khuri bai mwtai hamyarokni khuri tháinwi nwi-o’-no nungwi man ya; norok Subraini tebul bai mwtai hamyarokni tebul tháinwi nwi-o’-no achugwi cháui man ya” (1 Korinth 10:21). Abo wng kha Jisu sama hai: “Khoroksa phano nokphang khoroknwini seleng wngwi man ya” (Mathai 6:24).

Kaitor khe chini kók narwkmungno se naio hinwi Pol phrwng kha. Bo sakha, takjak mwtai abo Subraino musu rwo (1 Korinth 10:22). Poito khlainairokno takjak mwtairok bai hakchal tongna bagwi Pol Uáinwi Saphima 6:4-5 bwtangno yasi surwi phunuk kha: “O Israil khna di: Subraiyung chini Kaitor, bo simi se Subraiyung; tei nwng Subraiyung nini Kaitorno nini bebak khá bai khúk bai tei phan bai khe hamjak súk di.” Chwng Kaitorno belai kheno hamjakna nangnai. Aboni ulo Jisu saphikha: “Nwng sakno hamjak tẃi’-no buinobo hamjak di” (Mark 12:31, Lebi 19:18). Kubui khulummung wng kha Kaitorno hamjak sukmani, tei buino hamjak sukmani.

`Takjak mwtai a holong mungsa buya. Chwng mangthong snamwi mano. Phiyaba chwng bubo hai takjak mwtai bai hakchal wngthai klai?`
