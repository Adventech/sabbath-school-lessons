---
title:  Takjak Mwtaino Mechenmani
date:   30/07/2026
---

1 Korinth 8:1-3 bwtango Pol sakha, Hamjakma abo takjak mwtai bai bororokno kati rwo. 1 Korinth 10:23–11:1 bwtango o kókno Pol teibo saui khna rwkha. 1 Korinth 8:3 bwtango Kaitorno hamjaknano twiwi Pol sakha. Bo sakha, “Khoroksa phano sakni kahamno ta nai thung, buini kaham se nai thung” (1 Korinth 10:24). Omo wng kha buino hamjakmani!

`Mark 10:17–22 tei Mark 12:28–31 khúktang di. 1 Korinth 10 beremno bujina bagwi o kainwi bwtango tamo man?`

1 Korinth 10 beremo Mark 12:28–31 bwtango Jisu khlaima haino Polbo khlai kha. Bo kainwi kotor dagimungno sakha: bebakni slái Kaitorno hamjak di, tei buino hamjak di. Sikla gwnangsani kothomao Jisu o kainwi dagimungno thansa khe khlai kha (Mark 10:17-22). Bo Uáinwi Saphima 6:4 bwtangni kókno phunuk kha. Tei buino hamjaknani dagimungbo sakha. Sikla gwnangsani kothomao a siklasa bo Kaitorno hamjakmani tei buino hamjakmani slai bini rang tei sompotino hamjak kuk kha (Mark 10:22). Chúknohani manwini slái swngcharni manwino bo hamjak kuk kha. Bo kwrwirokno chubamani slai rang maino se nangkukmani wngjak kha (Mark 10:21). Omo hai kheno bo takjak mwtaino khilumnai wngmano klai kha.

Jisu sama hai kheno 1 Korinth 10:27–28 bwtango Pol samani. Takjak mwtairokni thani barokmani chaui man phano, khoroksa borokni bagwi abo nono nugwi dukhu nangjakmani wngwi tong khe, nwng chamani abo kaham ya (1 Korinth 10:23). O kókno bathong khe Pol sakha: “Bebak samung Kaitor lobjakna tẃi khe khlai di” (1 Korinth 10:23). O kók samani bising twi Pol sana nai kha, takjak mwtaino khulummani abo kwbangma lamani bising twi wngwi mano. Kaitorni chẃngsacha wngnanino chwng sakni bagwi nakhe abono takjak mwtaino khulummani wngwi mano (Jisaia 22:8).

1 Korinth 10:31-11:1 wng kha 1 Korinth 8-10 beremno mwthakmani Polni kókthai. Pol sakha, bo bini kahamni bagwi nai ya. Phiya bui mwthangjagwi manna twi khe buini kahamni bagwi se Pol samung khlaio (1 Korinth 10:33). Abo hai kheno Pol Jisuno rwktharo (1 Korinth 11:1).

`Nwng sakno hamjak twi bahai khe buino hamjaknani swrwngwi man?`
