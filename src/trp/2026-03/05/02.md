---
title:  Elem Bai Hamjakmungni Bisingo Choba
date:   26/07/2026
---

`1 Korinth 8:1–13 khúktang di. Elem bai hamjakmungni bisingo chobani kók Pol tamoni bagwi sakha?`

Takjak mwtairokni thani barokmani kókno Pol sana nangmani karon wng kha kuthuk kaisa jwngjalni kókno sana bagwi tei hamjakmung kwrwi wngwi tongmani kókno sana bagwi (1 Korinth 8). Takjak mwtairokni thani barokmani chámano twiwi Korinth mothao bodol kainwi khe bakjagwi thang kha. Baksa khe uánsugo, takjak mwtairok kwthang ya, eba abo mungsa buya—o elemni bising twi bóhrok takjak mwtaino barokmani manwirokno chao (1 Korinth 8:4). Omo hai bororokno “phan gwnang” hwnjago (1 Korinth 4:10). Phiyaba bóhrokno kebengnairokno khe “phan kwrwi” hwnjago (1 Korinth 8:9-12). Pol o mung rwmani uklogo karon tongmani wng kha bóhrok agini mwtaino khulummani mwnak puiturokno khibi sugwi man ya kho. Jephuru “phan gwnang” bororokno takjak mwtaino barokmanino chámani nugo, aphuru bóhrokno kebengnairok uánsugo Khristian tongthar bai takjak mwtaino khulummani thansa wngo hinwi. Phiyaba “phan gwnangrok” a “phan kwrwirokni” kebengmung wngwi bacha khe abo Pol hamjak ya.

Takjak mwtaino barokmanino chámani twiwi Baibel-o hamya khe sajak kha (Samung 15:20, 29; Samung 21:25; nai phidi, Phunukjakmani 2:14, 20). Phiya Pol o kókno aswk kwrak khe saliya. Tamni hwn ba bini sói naharmung wng kha, baksa bororok elemno chaya lama twi khe samungo thepaui thansa kwrwi wngwi mano, abono twiwi se. Pol elemno hamya hinwi saya. Phiyaba bo sana naimani khe, baksa elem Motha-o kungchukmung tubuo tei thansano swkago. Hamjakmung kwrwi khe a elem sói elem wngwi man ya (1 Korinth 8:2). Je borok Kaitorno hamjago, tei Kaitor bai sinijago, hakhe se a borok sói elemno mano (1 Korinth 8:3)

Uáinwi Saphima 6:4 bwtangni kókno khurchaui Pol phunuk kha, poito khlainairok sina nangnai kubui Kaitor khoroksa simi se (1 Korinth 8:4-6). Uáinwi Saphima 6:4-5 bwtango sao, “Subraiyung nini Kaitorno nini bebak khá bai khúk bai tei phan bai khe hamjak súk di.” Musi bai Pol, bóhrok khoroknwini thanino hamjakma kwrwi khe a elem mungsa buya.

Sakni elemo bwkha beranai “phan gwnang” poito khlainairok khe uánsugo takjak mwtairokno barokmanino cha phano mungsa wng ya. Phiyaba tumungno “phan kwrwi” poito khlainairokni bagwi abo chaya wngwi tong khe (1 Korinth 8:9) chwng takjak mwtairokni thani barokmanino chayau tongnani nango. Tamni hwn ba Khristono tei buino hamjakma tong khe abo hai khe se Khristianrok le ma tongnai.

`Pol sakha, hamjakma kwrwi khe elem hamya wngwi mano (1 Korinth 8). Bubo hai jorao abo hai wngwi man?`
