---
title:  Sakni Kókno Uánsuk Yaui Hamjakma
date:   27/07/2026
---

`#1 Korinth 9:1–6 khúktang di. Sakni kókno uánsuk yaui hamjakmano twiwi o bwtango tamo sakha?`

1 Korinth 9 berem khúktang khe, norok uánsugwi mano, swkango Pol sama hai khe elem bai hamjakmani bisingo mungsa halok kwrwi. Phiyaba chwng saui manna nango Baibel swijakmani jorao berem khakjagwi swijak ya. 1 Korinth 9 beremo Pol phrwngmani abo swkangni kók bai khakjak ya. 1 Korinth 9 beremo sakni kókno uánsuk yaui Khristono tei buino hamjakmano twiwi sóini phunukmari rwkha. Hamjakma bagwi Pol bini manthairok suda yakar kha.

“Cha phano nung phano” (1 Korinth 9:4) hinwi o bwtango sajakmani wng kha rang chubajakmani kók se. Chonghor wngwi Pol le bo chubamani bororokni thani manwirok mannani eba yachaknani manthai tongo. Abo hai kheno kubuni tongtharni bokhorokrok khlaio. Phiyaba Pol bini manthaino na liya. Sakno chubana bagwi bo rini tamu snamnai wngwi samung khlai kha (Samung 18:3).

“...ang ba Khristan hikno ani logi tlangwi man ya diba?” (1 Korinth 9:5) hinwi Pol samani kókmang wng kha khoroksa kaijak Chonghor wngwi bini logio bihikno tlangwibo Mothani rang naui Kaitorni samung tangnani ha beraiwi mano. Káijak hik-sai Chonghorrokni bisingo tongo: Priskila bai Akula (Rum 16:3), Andronik bai Junia (Rum 16:7). Phiyaba Pol káijak ya (1 Korinth 7:8). Bo muchung khe káijagwi bihikno logio twiwi mothani rang naui tong man khamun.

“Ang bai Barnaba simi da samung tangwi ma chánai?” (1 Korinth 9:6) hinwi Pol samani kókmang wng kha, Pol bai Barnaba Kaitorni samungno leng sugwi tangmani bagwi rang mannani bóhrokni manthai tongo (1 Korinth 9:4-6). Phiyaba Pol kwthang tongnani bagwi rini tamu snamo (Samung 18:3). Phiya Barnaba khe tamo samung khlai chwng si liya. Phiyaba Barnaba belai borok kosong tei kha phurnai borok hinwi chwng saui mano (Samung 4:36-37). Kók khe bo sak sakno samung khlaiwi sakno mwthangwi tongna muchungnai borok.

1 Korinth 9:6 bwtangni kókno 1 Korinth 9:7-11 bwtango teibo swbai sukrubwi phunuk kha. Bo sakha, bo bai Barnaba belai khe Kaitorni samung tangmani bagwi chubamung manthai tongo, abo bujsoino kwlaio (1 Korinth 9:11-12). Subrai khúkphang sakha, “Kothoma Kaham pírnairok bórok abo bai’-no chá thung” (1 Korinth 9:14; 1 Timothi 5:18). Phiyaba Pol sakha, “Tamo khe chwng abo tẃi manthai tongma tẃi nai li ya” (1 Korinth 9:12). Bo sakha, bini manthaino khibiui rwma bising twi Korinth-o kothoma kaham pirnani samung chaui mano (1 Korinth 9:19–23).

`Kaitorni samung cha thung hinwi nwng le tamo tamo nini manthairokno khibi kha?`
