---
title:  Laijak Jorani Bising Twi Swrwngmani
date:   28/07/2026
---

Pol bini baithangni langma-o sakni kókno uánsuk ya wngnai hinwi phunukmari rwmani ulo, takjak mwtairokni kókno sana cheng kha.1 Korinth 9:27 bwtangni kókno 1 Korinth 10 beremo sana chengjak phikha. Korinthni bororok Polno swrwng thung hinwi bo naio. Phiyaba Jisu se jotoni slái kotor chini swrwngjaknai (1 Korinth 11:1).

`1 Korinth 10:7–11 khúktang di. Hasing panthoro tong phuru Israilrok tamo sumsok khlai lai kha? Bóhrok seb kaham manmani ulo tamoni bagwi teibo hamya wng kuk kha?`

1 Korinth 10:1-5 bwtango Pol samani wng kha láibuma-o Kaitorni bororok hasing panthoro tong phuruni kók se. Chumui tei twijlang abo Kaitorni naikolma, bóhrok bai Kaitor tongma, tei mwthangwi tongmano se muitu khlaiwi rwo. Tei chamung bai nungmung khe Kaitor bóhrokno rwui tongmano se phunugo. Pol sakha, Israilrokni jorao bóhrok chumui tei twijlangno saui manmani wng kha Khristianrokni bagwi khe baptisma namani hai se. Tei Israilrokni jorao chamung tei nungmung khe Subraini Páithakni Chamung (Subraini Panda) hai se.

1 Korinth 10 beremo Pol phrwng kha, Khristianrok wng kha Israilrok hai. Bóhrok haino manjak bai kha. Israilrokni kókno Pol muitu khlai rwmani wng kha, Khristianrok bóhrok hai chaya ta khlai phi thung hinwi muchungma bagwi se. Israilrok aswk Kaitorni hamari manwi phano se takjak mwtaino khulummani (1 Korinth 10:7) tei buitali-buitala wngmani (1 Korinth 10:8)sumsokno khlai kha (1 Korinth 10:6). Aboni bagwino “bórokni bere kwbángmano’-no Kaitor hamjak ya” (1 Korinth 10:5).

Israilrokno sumsok khlaio chaya hinwi majra bernani belaino kwlai. Phiyaba Pol sakha, Khristianrok Khristono sini phano, eba Baibelni láibuma saui man phano, bóhrok hai sumsogo kwlaiwi mano. Aboni bagwi Pol hus khlaiwi rwkha: “ ‘Ang kaham khe bachaui tongo,’ hwnwi uansuknai borok bo klai ya tẃi khe hus wng thung” (1 Korinth 10:12).

Chwng soiwi man ya abo hai belokjakmungo Kaitor chwngno khiklai ya. “Norok soiwi manmani bára bo belok rw glak; norok belokjak phuru bo norokno soiwi manna tẃi khe rak rwnai, hakhe yóknani lama khlaiwi rwnai” (1 Korinth 10:13).
