### O Hatibarsani Swrwngjaknai
1 Korinth 12, Epis 4:11–13, 1 Korinth 13, 1 Pit. 4:8–11, 1 Korinth 14:27, Amos 3:7.

> <p>Muitu Bwtang</p>
> “Hakhe buino kaham khe hamjakna nai di. Phola rwmani suarrokni bagwi se norok khatung di, aboni bere se Kaitorni kók diyari sanani suarni bagwi se khatung di” (1 Korinth 14:1, ESV).

Borokni bwswk hai kheno mothabo kaisa se, phiya kwbangma borok tongo, khoroksa khoroksa borokni samung, khlaimung, tei suar juda juda. Hamjakmani bising twi o phola rwmani suarrok thansa wngnani uansukmungno tubuo, tei abo Kaitor khorokthamsani (triune God) tongmungno phunugo.

O hatibaro chwng 1 Korinth 12–14 tei aboni phwrwngmung phola rwmani suarrokni kokno naiwi nai lai anw. O becheb wng kha Pol kók kwbang samani khotol. Mothao Khristianrok bahai khe tongnai abono twiwi Pol sakha (1 Korinth 11-14). Motha-o kuthum lai thani naithok ya mwchang ya wngmano twiwi Pol belai ma uánsuk kha. O jwngjalni kókno phirokmung rwphuru kheno Pol sakha, Motha wng kha kaisa bwsak hai. Bwsakni kwbangma manwi juda juda khe samung khlaio. A samungrok “Khristoni bwsakno rak rwna bagwi se” (Epis 4:12). Juda juda samungni bising twi motha-o thansa tubunani bagwi Kaitor mothao phola rwmani suarrok rwkha.

1 Korinth 4 beremo swimani a mothao kaklai phuilai wngmani kók Polni uánsukmungo tong kho. Mothao kók baksa wng lai ya wngwi tong thani bini kók phirokmung wng kha Khristo-o thansa. O kókno bo teibo swbai sukrubwi buji rwna bagwino Phola rwmani suarrokni kókno sana nang kha. Pol sama hai khe, a kàklai phuilai wngmanino mwthaknani kaisa simi lama wng kha Khristo tei Phola-o thánsa wngmani se.

_August 8, Sabat salni bagwi o hatibarsani rwngchapno swrwngwi tiyar wng di._
