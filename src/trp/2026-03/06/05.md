---
title:  Kók Sanani Suar
date:   05/08/2026
---

Kók samani suar tamo? 1 Korinth-o sajakmani kók sanani suar abo wng kha buini kók bai kók sanani phan Phola Kwthar bai rwjakmani (Mark 16:17; Samung 2:1-13; Samung 10:44-48; Samung 19:6).

1 Korinth 12:8–10 bwtango phola rwmani suarrokni bisingo kók sanani suarni kók Pol sakha (teibo naidi, 1 Korinth 12:28, 30; 1 Korinth 13:1, 8). O kókno 1 Korinth 14 beremo bo haino hai saphikha. Kók samani suarno Grik kókthai bai _glōssa_ hino. 1 Korinth 12–14 beremo uainwichi Pol o kókno khurcha kha. 1 Korinth 14 beremo simino uaichiba khurcha kha. Abono karwibo Grik kókthai bai _heteroglōssos_ nukjak kha, aboni kókmang wng kha “buini kók” (1 Korinth 14:21). Korinth mothao o suarno chaya lama twi khe samungo thepajagwi tong kha. Aboni bagwino Pol o kókno belai ma uánsuk sio. Tamni hwn ba o suarno Korinth mothao chaya lama twi khe samungo thepajagwi tongmani bagwi mothao suri lai thani buji lai ya (confused) wng laiwi tong kha (1 Korinth 14:23, 27, 33, 40).

`1 Korinth 14:5, 13, 26, 27 tei 1 Korinth 12:10, 30 khúktang di. Kók sanani suarno twiwi Pol tamo sakha?`

Kók sanani suar rwjakmani karon wng kha bororok sraisrai khe kók bujiui manna bagwi se (1 Korinth 14:9). Kebo sraisrai khe buji man ya khe kók sanai suar abo mungsa samungo chuk ya (1 Korinth 14:6). Swbai sukrubwi samani tei bujinanino twiwi se Pol o kókno kaham khe ma sao. Korinthsong kók sanani suarno simi nangkukmani wngjak laio; kók diyari samani suarno khe nangkukmani wngjak lai ya.

Korinthsongno buini kók bai kók saui man thung hinwi Pol muchungjago (1 Korinth 14:5). Phiyaba jotoni thani o suar simi tong thung hinwi Pol muchungjak ya (1 Korinth 12:10). O kókno bujinani belai nangkukmani. “Phola Kwthar bai baptisma nana swkang joto borokno buini kók bai kók saui manna nangnai hinwi phrwngmani abo chaya, tei abo 1 Korinth 12 tei 14 beremo Pol phrwngmani bai gothok ya.”—Raoul Dederen, _Handbook of Seventh-day Adventist Theology,_ electronic ed., vol. 12 of Commentary Reference Series (Hagerstown, MD: Review and Herald Publishing Association, 2001), p. 620.

`Nini Mothao buini kók bai kók saui mannai da tong? Bóhrok bahai khe buini thani Khristoni kókno twlangwi man? Bahai khe omo hai khe kók sanai suarni sói kókmang hinwi chwng bujiui man?`
