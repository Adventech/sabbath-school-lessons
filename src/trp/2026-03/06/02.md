---
title:  Podrepot Suarrok
date:   02/08/2026
---

1 Korinth 12:1 bwtango “tabuk…. si rwna nai-o” hinwi saui Pol kwtal kókno sini rwkha. Abo wng kha “phola rwmani suarrok” eba “pholani bororok.” Tamni hwn ba Grik kókthai _tōn pneumatikōn_ hwnmani o kainwi kókmangno kwlaio.

“Phola rwmani suarrokni” kók 1 Korinth 12:4 bwtango Pol samani wng kha Phola bai rwjakma suarirokni kók se. 1 Korinth 12:2, 3 bwtango Pol sakha, Pholani puila suar wng kha kha kotor khe Jisuno Subrai hinwi gosimani (1 Korinth 12:2, 3). Swmai Kwtalni molo Jisuno Subrai hinwi gosi khe, Hani Bugra kotorno Bugra hinwi gosi ya wngma hai khe uánsukjago (Samung 17:7; Johon 19:12, 15). Hani Bugrano Bugra hinwi gosiya wngmani abo Bugrano kebengmani bai baksa. Bugra kotorno kebeng khe bo butharjakna se nangnai.

Jisu tei Pol sakha, Kaitorno poito khlaimani abo abo sátokjagwi tei butharjagwi phano mannai—abono wng kha phola rwmani suar. Poito wng kha kaisa kotor kuk rwjakmani suar se. Phola rwmani suarrokni bisingo puilani suar wng kha poito (1 Korinth 13:13). Pol sraisrai kheno o kókno sakha (1 Korinth 12:9). Abono karwi teibo phola rwmani suarrok tongo. Phola Kwthar juda juda suarrokno rwkha “bo muchung tẃi khoroksa khoroksano podrepot suar rw-o” (1 Korinth 12:11).

`1 Korinth 12:1–6 khúktang di. O bwtango tamo nangkukmani kók sajak kha?`

Uaira uai “podrepot” hinwi samani kókmang khe Phola rwmani suarrok juda juda tongo hwnmani se. Abórokno 1 Korinth 12:1 bwtango “phola rwmani suarrok” hwnwi sakha. Phiya 1 Korinth 12:4-6 bwtango abóno kaitham khe swbai sukrubwi sakha: “suarrok” (_charisma_), “Samung” (_diakonia_), “Samung khlaijaknai” (_energēma_). O kaitham kókthaini juda juda kókmang tongo. Phiyaba Pol sana naimani khe omorok bebakno thansa khe samung khlaio. Phola rwmani suarrokni sói samung wng kha thansa snamnani; tamni hwn ba Phola rwmani suarrok abo khorokthamsa Kaitorni (Triune God) aroni se phaio (Epis 4:8–11). Phola Kwthar poito khlainairokno suarrok rwo, tei mothao bóhrok Khristoni samung tangwi manna twi khe Kaitor bóhrokno phan rwo (1 Korinth 12:5, 6; 1 Korinth 12:11). Phiyaba bebak Suarirok abo Mothao rak rwnani bagwi se.

`Pol thansa wngnani kókno samani naidi. Tamoni bagwi Motha-o abo nangkukmani?`
