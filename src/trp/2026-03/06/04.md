“Abórokni bisingo hamjak abo kaisa suar ya. Phiya hamjakmani bising twi se bebeak suarrokno samungo thepaui sói naharmungo ma twlangnai.”—Carl P. Cosaert, “1 Corinthians,” _Andrews Bible Commentary: New Testament (Berrien Springs, MI: Andrews University Press, 2022), p. 1643._

`1 Korinth 13:1–7 tei 1 Pitor 4:8–11 khúktang di. Phola rwmani suarrogo hamjakmani samung tamo?`

1 Korinth 13 bwtango phrwng kha, hamjakmani bising twi simi se Phola rwmani suarrokno kaham khe samungo thepaui mano. 1 Korinth 12 beremo tongmani kók sana rwngmani suarno khurchaui Pol 1 Korinth 13 beremno chengsa kha. Bo sana naimani wng kha hamjakma kwrwi khe a suar mungsa buya. Elem eba simung (1 Korinth 12:8), tei poito (1 Korinth 12:9) tong phano, eba “hatairokno norwi mannani” abo hai poito tong phano (1 Korinth 13:2), hamjakma kwrwi khe abo mungsa buya (1 Korinth 13:2). Hamjakma kwrwi khe kók sanani suar tong phano (1 Korinth 12:10, 28, 30) abo “kók samani abo keche-keche pungnai bel eba khe uang uang pungnai cheuang hai se klai-o” (1 Korinth 13:1). Abo hai kheno kók diyari sanani suar tong phano hamjakma kwrwi khe abo mungsa chuk ya (1 Korinth 13:2).

1 Korinth 13:4-7 bwtango hamjakma tamono hwn tei tamono hamjakma hwn ya, tei hamjakma tamo khlai, tei hamjakma tamo khlai ya abórokno swbai sukrubwi Pol saui khna rwkha. Hamjakmno saui khna rwnani bagwi Pol khwlai (verbs) thepaui swikha. Abo wng kha hamjakma abo ahaino kók simi ya, abo samung khlaio hinwi phunugo. Pol sakha, Hamjakma wng kha: 1.	Hamjaknai borok bo khábo logo, 2. Khábo khairogo; 3. kubui bai se tongthokjago; 4. Sak soi máng tongo 5.Poito khlai máng tongo; 6. Khatung máng tongo 7. Khá lok máng tongo. Phiyaba hamjakmani abo: 1.Músú ya; 2.	Kung chuk ya; 3. Hamjaknai borok hamya khlaijakmarokno bwkha tẃi ya; 4.	Joliui phunuk ya; 5. Sakni kaham simi nai ya; 6. Kisa bai joli ya; 7. Hamya khlaijakmarokno bwkha tẃi ya; 8. Samung hamyani bagwi tongthokjak ya

1 Korinth 12 tei 14 ni kwcharo hamjakmani kókno Pol toni kha. Tamni hwn ba hamjakma kwrwi phola rwmani suarrokno samungo thepamano twiwi Pol sana naimani wng kha bebak Suarirok hamjakmani bising twi se samungo thepana nango. Hamjakmani bising twi simi se Phola rwmani suarrokno kaham kuk lama twi samungo thepaui mano. Aboni bagwino Pol sakha, “Poito khlaimani, khatungmani, hamjakmani, o mungtham tong phlainai, tamo khe abórokni bere hamjakma’-no tor kugo” (1 Korinth 13:13).
