---
title:  Podrepot’-ni Bising Twi Thansa
date:   03/08/2026
---

1 Korinth 12:4–6 bwtango “kaisano” kókthai thepa kha (“Phola a kaisa’-no,” “Subrai a kaisa’-no,” “Kaitor a kaisa’-no.” Pol omo hai khe samani wng kha “a Phola kaisa’-no” (1 Korinth 12:11), “bwsak kaisa se” (1 Korinth 12:12), “bwsak kaisa” (1 Korinth 12:12, 13, 20), “Phola kaisa” (1 Korinth 12:13 [uáinwi khurchajak kha]), “Omo tẃi khe bwsakni bisingo phér laima kwrwi, tamo khe bebak bedekrok’-no kaisa tei kaisano baksa khe nai lai-o” (1 Korinth 12:25).

Thansani kókno samani bai baksa Pol sakha, Khristoni bwsago podrepot bedekrok tongo, abórok wng kha “kwbangma Mothani bororok” (1 Korinth 12:12, 20), “chwng bebak’-no … baptisma wng bai kha” (1 Korinth 12:13), “Bwsak wng kha bedek kaisa bai snamjak ya, bedek kwbángma bai snamjak” (1 Korinth 12:14), “Bebak bwsak” (1 Korinth 12:17), “Tamo khe Kaitor bo muchung tẃi’-no bebak bedekno kaisa kaisa khe bwsago thepaui rw kha” (1 Korinth 12:18), “Bwsakni bedek” (1 Korinth 12:22, 23), “bebak bedek’-no” (1 Korinth 12:26). Podrepotni baking twino thansa khlainani wng kha Phola rwmani suarrokni naharmung hinwi Pol sakha.

Juda judani bisingo thansa wngmani abo Kaitorni tongmungno se phunugo. Apha khoroksa, Sajla tei khoroksa, tei Phola Kwthar tei khoroksa. O khoroktham juda juda, phiyaba Mothani samung khlai thani tei Kaitorni samungni bagwi phan rw thani bóhrok thansa khe samung khlaio (1 Korinth 12:4–6, Epis 4:11–13).

`1 Korinth 12:12–31 khúktang di. Bwsak tei bwsakni juda juda manwirok tamoni bagwi Motha tei Mothani bororokno phunuk kha?`

1 Korinth 12 beremni kókbokhiri wng kha bwsakni manwirok kaisa bai tei kaisa juda juda wng phano (1 Korinth 12:15-20), kaisa bai tei kaisa jotono samung nang laio (1 Korinth 12:21-26). Yakungbo biyango himnai núknani bagwi mokolno samung nango. Mokolbo núk phano mungsa tangwi man ya, bobo yakno samung nango. Bwsakni baksa manwi kubuni slai kebel hamjagwi mano (1 Korinth 12:22), eba samung nang ya hwnjagwi mano (1 Korinth 12:23), phiyaba bebakno samung nangmani máng se (1 Korinth 12:22).

Phiyaba Korinthsong baksa phola rwmani suarrokno khe samung nangmani hinwi uánsugo, phiya baksa phola rwmani suarrokno khe samung nangmani ya hinwi uánsugo.

O uánsukmungno mwthaknani bagwi Pol bóhrokno hamjakmungno phunuk kha. Abo se “lama kaham kuk” (1 Korinth 12:31). Kók wng kha jesa suar phano elem tei hamjakma bai khe samungo thepa khe Kaitor hamjago.

`1 Korinth 12:8–10, 28; Rum 12:6–8; tei Epis 4:11 bwtangrogo phunukjakmani phola rwmani suarrokno naidi. Nini suar khe tamo? Khristoni bwsakno rak rwna bagwi nwng abono bahai khe samungo thepaui man?`
