---
title:  Kók Diyari’-ni Suar
date:   06/08/2026
---

Phola rwmani suarrokni kók bisingo Kók diyarini suar belai nangkukmani. Kók sanani suarni kók sanani swkang Pol kwbangma jorao’-no kók diyarini kókno khurchao (1 Korinth 12:10, 28; 13:8). Jephuru Pol puila kók sanani suarni kókno sao, bo phunukna naikha kók diyari abo kók sanani suarni slai teibo nangkukmani (1 Korinth 14:4, 5, 6, 22).

`Epis 4:11–13 tei 1 Korinth 14:3, 4 khúktang di. Phola rwmani suarrokni naharmungno twiwi o bwtango tamo sa? Tei kók diyarini suarno twiwi nangkukmani tamo sakha?`

Kók diyarini suar rwmung wng kha khá rak rwnani, khá srang rwnani tei khá kwchang rwnani (1 Korinth 14:3; Samung 15:32). Kók diyari hwn kheno abo thinango bahai khe tongnai abono twiwi swkang kók sasokmani máng buya. Grik kók bai abono _prophēteuō_ hino. Aboni kókmang wng kha “swkang mungsa sanani,” eba “khoroksa swkni bagwi mungsa swk sanani.” Phunukmarini bagwi puila o kókno nugwi mano Samung 2:29-31 bwtango (Amos 3:7 nai phidi). Aro Daudno diyari hwnjak kha; tamni hwn ba bo thinango tamo wngnai nugwi mano. Phiyaba tei kaisa diyarini kókmang chwng Samung 15:32 bwtango mano. Aro Juda bai Silano diyari hwnjak kha; tamni hwn ba bóhrok kók diyari sao hwnmani khe abo poito khlainairokno bóhrokni kók bai kha rak rwmani tei phan rwmani se.

Epis 4:11–13 bwtangni bising twi chwng swrwngwi mano phola rwmani suarrok Chonghorrokni mol jora simi bai paiwi thang ya. Mol páithak jorano abo tongnai (Samung 2:39). Phiyaba khoroksa swk sakno diyari hinwi gosi khe, bo Elembak bai gothokjak wngna nangnai. Kaibrwi gothokmung bini thani núkjakna nangnai.

1. Bini kók diyari samarok kubui wngwi nangna nangnai (Uáinwi Saphima 18:22; Jirimia 28:8, 9).
2. Elembakni diyarirokni kók bai bini kók gothokjak wngna nangnai (Uáinwi Saphima 13:1–3, Jisaia 8:20).
3. Bo sal brúmno Khristoni thani bini langmano yapharnai wngna nangnai (1 Johon 4:1–3).
4. Jisu sakha, diyari phatrarok bóhrokni bwthai baino sinijaknai (Mathai 7:15–20). Omo hai kheno kubui diyarino’-bo sininani lama.

Phunukjakmani bijap-o sao tongrwknai Mothani (remnant church) thani kók diyarini suar tongnai (Phunukjakmani 12:17, Phunukjakmani 19:10, English Baibel naidi, aro “the gift of prophecy” hinwi sajago). Chwng Seventh-day Adventists wngwi Ellen G. White’-ni thani tei bini swimungo kók diyarini suarno nuk kha.
