Ellen G. White swimani Spiritual Gifts, vol. 1, ni Roswell F. Cottrell, _“Spiritual Gifts,_” khúktang di, bwkáng 5–16.

“Bororok Kaitorno poito khlaiwi se samungo nongkhorna nango. Khoroksa samung tangnai kók sana kwrwngwi wngwi mano, tei khoroksa samung tangnai khe swina kwrwng wngwi mano. Khoroksa borok bwkha jogojogo gwnang wngwi mano, eba surinani phan gwnang wngwi mano. Phiya tei khoroksa borok rwchapmani suar gwnang wngwi mano. Baksa khe ba Kaitorni kókno swbai sukrubwi sana kwrwng wngwi mano. Bebak Suarirokno Kaitorni bagwi phan gwnang. Tamni hwn ba samung tangnairok bai baksa khe bo samung khlaio. Kaitor khoroksano elem rwo, tei khoroksano simung rwo, phiya tei khoroksano poito rwo. Phiyaba rwnaisa Subrai kaisano. Juda juda suar abo juda juda samung khlaio, ‘tamo khe jotono’-no samung khlainani phan rwnai Kaitor a kaisa’-no’ (1 Korinth 12:6).

“Phola rwmani suarrokno kaisano phano kebel khe ta nuk di. Bebak borokno Kaitorni bagwi se samung khlainani chengna nango. Nwng samung kotor khlai man ya hinwi achugwi ta tong di. Nwng sakni bagwi uánsukmano mwthak di. Nini Subraino naisik di. Nwng khlai manma samungno koinene, kwchang kwchang tei hamjakma bai khe khlai di.”—Ellen G. White, in _The Advent Review and Sabbath Herald,_ April 12, 1906.

“Chwng jotoni buini chubachu tei uánsukmung mannani nango. Kaitor buini bising twi kheno samung khlaio, chini bising twi máng ya. Juda juda borokno juda juda suar rwo. Hakhe bororok thansa khe samung khlaina nango, ‘Kaitorni samung khlaina hwnwi Kaitorni bebak bororokno tiyar khlaina bagwi, phiya Khristoni bwsakno rak rwna bagwi se bo omono khlai-o’ (Epis 4:12). . . .

“Chini bwskango kebengmungrok tongnai, phiyaba chwng chini Subraino rwkthar khe, bo bai yak romwi thansa wng khe, chwng a jwngjalrok bai choba khlaiwi mannai.”—Ellen G. White, _The Upward Look,_ p. 141.

**Salaijaknai Swngmungrok**

`Kók diyarini suarni kókno teisa uánsuk di. Tamoni bagwi kók sanani suarni slái phano kók diyarini suar teibo nangkukmani? 1 Korinth 14 khúktangwi nai jadi.`

`Chwng motha tamoni bagwi Ellen G. White’-ni aro kók diyarini suar tongo hinwi poito khlai kha? O suarni bising twi mothao tamo hamari kotor man?`

`Nono belai khe hamjaknai khorokbrwi eba khorokba borokni kókno uánsuk di. Nwng bahai khe sai man kha bóhrok nono hamjakmani kubui hinwi? Hakhe Phola rwmani suarrokni kókno sa thanio Pol tamoni bagwi hamjakmani kókno belai kwbang khe sakha? Nwng tamo buji kha?`

`Hamjakmani belai nangkukmani wng phano, khoroksa borokni kók kubui de eba bini kók chwng khnanani nangnai de amjok thani tamoni bagwi a hamjakmani bai khe amjokjak ya?`
