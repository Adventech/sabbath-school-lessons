---
title:  Hamjakma Belai Nangkukmani
date:   09/08/2026
---

Thangnai hatibaro chwng 1 Korinth 13 beremni hamjakmani kókbothomano nai lai kha. Phiya oro Pol samani kókrokno chwng teibo kuthuk khe ma nainai.

`1 Korinth 13 beremno khúktang di. Hamjakmano twiwi Pol tamo sakha?`

Kók sanani rwngmani (1 Korinth 13:1), kók diyari samani, simani, elem, poito (1 Korinth 13:2), chubamung (1 Korinth 13:3) abórok mungsa chuk ya hinwi Pol samani ya. Bo sana naikha, hamjakmani bising twi abóhrokno samungo thepajak ya hwn khe se abo mungsa chuk ya.

Pol je hamjakmani kók samani abo “Ang strawberry hamjago,” eba “Ang ani bayabno hamjago,” eba khe “Ang ani logisong tei bwsano hamjago” hwnwi samani abo hai hamjakmani kók ya. Movie-o núkmani hamjakmani kókno twiwibo Pol oro samani ya. Eba omo chwla bai bwrwini hamjakmungni kók buya. Chwng kók pir phuru o hamjakmungni kókno samungo thepajak phano omo chwla bai bwrwini hamjakmungni kók buya.

O hamjakmungo khairokma, chubama, gwrwng kaham, eba yak kuar khe chubama simi ya. Abórok o hamjakmano phunuknani chuk sugwi man ya. O hamjakma wng kha juda kuk khairokmanai bising twi Phola Kwthar chwngno rwmani se. 1 Korinth 13 beremo sajakma o hamjakma wng kha Phola bai twiduljagwi tongmani hamjakma. A hamjakma chwngno khairoknai, chubanai, kaham khlainai, tei yak kuar khe yapharnai wngnani phan rwo. O hamjakmao Khristoni bagwi tei buini bagwi simino uánsugo.

`Mathai 24:12 khúktang di. O bwtango Jisu tamo hus khlaiwi rwkha?`

Aboni bagwino _agapē_ hamjakma belai nangkukmani, tei norok kukmani. Khristoni phan bai chwng chini nok, motha, tei yaksi yagra-ni somaj-o hamjakmano telakar khe toniui man ya. Khristo chini bagwi krus-o thwimani phunukmung chini bwskango phang phang. Aboni slái kaham kuk tei phan gwnang hamjakma tei tamo wngwi man? Chwng a hamjakmano buji sugwi man ya phano, Kaitor chwngno khairogwi rwmani a hamjakmano chwng man sak jora twi chini langma-o phunukna nango.
