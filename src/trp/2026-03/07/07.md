Ellen G. White swimani _‘Review and Herald,_ August 28, 1888’ bijapni “Hamjakma nango” hwnmani bechebno khúktang di, bwkáng: 545, 546.

“Jeswk Mothani bokhorok phano wng thung, bini bwkhao Kaitorni hamjakma bai supungjak ya khe, eba bo buino hamjakma kwrwi khe, bo Khristono sói rwktharnai wng ya. Bo poito gwnang wng phano, soidomni samung khlai man phano, hamjakma kwrwi khe a poito mungsa buya. Bo kwrwirokno bebak rwbai phano, phiyaba hamjakma bai khe bo abo hai khlai ya khe, Kaitor abono yachak ya. Khoroksa swk borok butharjak phano, eba thwi phano, bini thani hamjakma kwrwi khe, a borok chaya khlai kha, eba sakni kaham naio hinwi se Kaitor bono nugo.”—Ellen G. White, _)The Acts of the Apostles,_ pp. 318, 319.

“Chwng kwbangma kók pir kha. Phiyaba bororok nangmani khe kwmajak bororokni bagwi hamjakma se. A hamjakma Kaitorni achukthaiyungni phaimani. Kubui Khristian hwn khe hamjakmano bini lamgnani bising twi pirna nangnai….”—Ellen G. White, _Lift Him Up,_ p. 134.

“Khristoni bwkhani phaimani hamjakma simi se bororokno ham rwui mano. Bwkha baijak bororokno chubaui mannai wng kha je borokni bisingo a hamjakma tongo. Abo buphango buphangni tos tongma hai, eba bwsak bisingo thwi tongma hai.”—Ellen G. White, _Education,_ p. 114.

**Salaijaknai Swngmungrok**

`Hamjakma tamo tamo khlai ya Pol saui khna rwmani bebakno thwk bai kha de? Teibo aro point thepaui da man?`

`“Buino kaham khe hamjakna nai di” (1 Korinth 14:1) hinwi Pol samani kókmang tamo? 1 Korinth 13:4–7 bai oro sajakma tamo gothok lai?`

`Hamjakmani bo tongmungno nini sal brumni langmao thepana nangwi tong kha? Nini Mothao hamjakmani bo tongmung nangkukmani? Pol tamoni bagwi hamjakmano kók diyari samani saur, kók sana rwngmani suar, tei simani suarrok bai surchaui sakha )1 Korinth 13:8)?`

`Korinth mothao bororokni bisingo thansa kwrwini bagwi hamjakma se nangnai hinwi Pol sakha. Tamoni bagwi? Chini motha-o abono bahai khe thepanai?`
