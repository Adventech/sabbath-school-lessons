---
title:  Poito, Khatungma, Tei Hamjakma
date:   13/08/2026
---

Chwng sai man kha hamjakma tamo khlai? Hamjakmao khá logo, khairogo, tongthokjago, sói-o, poito khlaio, khatungo, khá lok máng tongo (eba sak soi máng tongo); tamni hwn ba omorok bebakno Jisuni aro núkjago. O tongmungrok Jisuni thani nuk khe, chwng bono swrwngna nango. Abo hai kheno Pol Korinthsongno muchungjakmani. “Phiyaba hamjakma tamo tamo khlai ya kaichar point nai khe, abo haino Korinth mothao khlai laiwi tongo: músúmani, kungchukmani, hamya khlaimani, sakni kaham simi naimani, dakti khe jolimani, tei buini hamyarokno bwkhao tonmani akorok. O hamya tongmungrok Korinth motha bai gothokjak máng, haini bagwino o kókthairokno Pol swikha.”—Verlyn D. Verbrugge, “1 Corinthians,” in _The Expositor’s Bible Commentary: Romans–Galatians,_ revised edition (Grand Rapids, MI: Zondervan, 2008), p. 372.

Korinthsong kwbangma swrwngthai tongo, tei chwngbo. Hamjakma tamo tamo khlai tei tamo tamo khlai ya saui khna rwbaimani ulo Pol kók mwthak kha hamjakma abo mol brúmno tongnai hinwi samani bising twi. Bororok kubui hamjakmano khlai thung hinwi kha rak rwna bagwi Pol o kók sakha.

Salsa kók diyari samani samungo nangmani ya wngwi thangnai; chwng kók kaisa simi salainai; tei borokni elem sláijagwi Kaitorni kwtal elem rwjaknai (1 Korinth 13:12). Phola rwmani suarrok samungo chuk rwjakmani ulo páiwi thangnai (1 Korinth 13:10). “Hamjakmani abo thak ya [mol brúmno tongnai] (1 Korinth 13:8).

Abo hai kheno Khristo tei uáisa phai phuru poito abo sraisrai nugwi mannai (2 Korinth 5:7), chwng aswk sal khatungmani abo kubui wngnai (Rum 8:24). Phiyaba chini khorokthamsa Kaitorni (triune God) tongmung a hamjakma tong phlainai. Poito tei khatungma mol brúmno tongnai. Phiyaba poito abo mwthangjakmungno simani se (Rum 4:3). Geh khatungma khe kwtal swngcharo tongthokma khe tongnani kha kamani. Mwthangjak bororokni langmao abóhrok tongnai. Phiyaba hamjakma, Kaitorni hamjakma mol brúmno tongnai.

Daktino chwng chini Subraino dadak-didik khe nuknai (1 Korinth 13:12). A sal phaiya sak chini langmao kaitham manwi tongna nangnai: poito, khatungma, tei hamjakma. O kaitham wng kha Pholani bising twi Khristian langmano phunugo. Aboni bagwino abo Khristianrokni bisingo phunukjago (Rum 5:1–5; Gal. 5:5, 6; Epis 1:15, 18; 4:1–5). Phiyaba hamjakmano tor kugo. Hamjakma wng kha Kaitorni tongmung hinwi se phunukjago (1 Johon 4:8).

`“Kaitor wng kha hamjakmung” hwnmani kókno uánsuk di. Bahai khe chwng abono bujinai?`
