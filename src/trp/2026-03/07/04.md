---
title:  Hamjakma Tamo Khlai Ya
date:   11/08/2026
---

`1 Korinth 13:4–7 khúktang di. Hamjakma tamo tamo khlai ya abórokno twiwi Pol tamoni sakha?`

Hamjakma tamo tamo khlai chwng miyani swrwngmungo nuk kha. Hamjakma tamo tamo khlai ya, abo hai kaichar point chwng tini nai lainai. Hamjakma . . .

**1. Músú ya (_zeloō_).** _Zeloō_ kókthai abo kahamno (positive). Aboni kókmang “kaham suarno manna khatungma [_zeloō_] (1 Korinth 12:32). “Pholani suarrokno manna khatungma [_zeloō_] (1 Korinth 14:1). “Kók diyari sana khatungma [_zeloō_] (1 Korinth 14:39). Samung 7:9 bwtango sama hai oro sajakmani khe hamyano (negative) sajak kha. Pholani suarrokno khatungma [_zeloō_] abo kahamno, phiyaba pholani suar mannai borokno ta músú di. Abo hai khe músú lai khe kaklaiai thango (1 Korinth 3:3).

**2. Kók kotorbo sa ya (_perpereuomai_).** _Perpereuomai_ hwnmani kókmang wng kha kungchukmani tei bui bai bolaijakna muchungmani. Hamjakmao sakno bolaimani eba sakni bagwi naimani kwrwi.

**3. Kung chuk ya (_physioō_).** _Physioō_ hwnmani kókmang chwng 1 Korinth 8:1 bwtango mano. Aro sao, “ ‘Chwng jotono si-o,’ hwnwi borok samani o kók kubui’-no. Hai phano omo tẃi sima bai borok tor-rere wngwi kung chugo” (1 Korinth 8:1). Kók wng kha Physioō hwnmani kókmang borok sakno nangkukmani hinwi uánsukmani se.

**4. Lachimasingcha khlai ya (_aschēmoneo_).** _Aschēmoneo_ kókthaini kwbangma kókmang wngwi mano. Abo borokno lachimasingcha khlaimani, bórom rwya wngmani, hamya khlaimani, somaj tei kahamrokno kasumani. Korinthni “phan gwnang” poito khlainairok, “phan kwrwi” poito khlainairokno abo hai khlai kha hinwi Pol oro sakha (1 Korinth 4:10; 1 Korinth 8).

**5. Sakni kaham [manthai] simi nai ya (_zēteo_).** 1 Korinth 10:24 bwtango Pol samani bai o kók belaino gothokjak. Aro Pol sakha: “Khoroksa phano sakni kahamno [manthaino] ta nai thung, buini kaham se nai thung” (1 Korinth 10:24). Hamjakmao buini bagwi sakni manthai suda yakarwi rwo (Rwngchp-5 naidi). Buini kaham naidi.

**6. Kisa bai joli ya (_paroxynō_).** _Paroxynō_ hwnmani khe jesa borokno phano dakti dakti kheno jolijakmani. Phiyaba hamjakmao thamchi kutung, dakti jolinai wng ya.

**7. Hamya khlaijakmarokno bwkha tẃi ya (_logizomai_).** Hamjakmao hamya khlaijakmarokno bwkha-o narwk ya. Hamjakmao nakarwi rwo.

**8. Samung hamyani bagwi tongthokjak ya (_chairō_).** Hamjakmao hamya khlaijakmarokno bwkha-o narwk ya aswk simi ya, abo hai samung bai tobo tongthokjak ya. Chwng kubui khe buino hamjak khe, bóhrok chaya khlai khe chwng tongthokjak ya, phiya chwng bóhrokno chubana muchungnai.
