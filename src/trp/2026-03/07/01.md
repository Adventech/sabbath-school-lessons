### O Hatibarsani Swrwngjaknai
1 Korinth 13; Mathai 24:12; Galatia 5:22, 23; 1 Timothi 1:14; 1 Johon 4:8.

> <p>Muitu Bwtang</p>
> “Poito khlaimani, khatungmani, hamjakmani, o mungtham tong phlainai, tamo khe abórokni bere hamjakma’-no tor kugo” (1 Korinth 13:13, KOKBSI).

Hamjakma bebaknono mechenwi mano. Aboni bagwino hamjakmano twiwi Pol kwbangma kók sana muchungjago. Swmai Kwtal-o hamjakmano phunukna bagwi Grik kókthai _agapaō_ belai kheno thepajak kha. Pol a kókthaino bini koktuno uai 135 swikha. Korinthsongno Pol swihormani koktun-o o hamjakma belai nangkukmani.

Hamjakmano twiwi sajakmani kók Swmai Kwtal-o teibo tongo. Naiwi mano—Rum 8:35–39, 1 Korinth 2:9, 1 Korinth 8:3, Galatia 2:20, Kolosai 1:13, 1 Thesaloniki 3:12, akorok.

Phiyaba abórok 1 Korinth 13 beremno hamjakma kókthaini kókmang bai gothok ya.

Thangnai hatibaro chwng swrwng kha, hamjakma kwrwi khe bebakno mungsa chuk ya. Phola rwmani suarrok sudano hamjakma kwrwi khe mungsa chuk ya. O hatibaro khe 1 Korinth 13 beremno teibo kuthuk khe chwng nai lai anw.

Hamjakma abo phunuk mamang simi ya. Abo langma-o thepajakna nangnai. Samungo, tei kókthai-o a hamjakma tongna nangnai. Haiya khe abo mungsa chuk glak.

Hamjakma tamo, hamjakma tamo khlai, abo Jisuni langma-o bebakno phunukjak kha.

_August 15, Sabat salni bagwi o hatibarsani rwngchapno swrwngwi tiyar wng di._
