---
title:  Jisuno Phunukmung
date:   12/08/2026
---

1 Korinth 13:4–7 bwtangno khúktang khe chwng khá baiwi mano; tamni hwn kha aro hamjakmani bebak tongmungno chwngno phunukjak ya. Pol o kókrokno swiwi tong phuru Jisuni kókno muitu manjak khwna. Jisu simi se a hamjakmarokno phunuk sugwi mano. Hakhe hamjakmano twiwi Pol samani abo Jisuno phunukma se klaio.

`Khúktang di: Johon 13:1, 34; Johon 15:9, 12; 1 Timothi 1:14; 2 Timothi 1:7, 13; 1 Johon 3:16; tei 1 Johon 4:7–12, 19–21. Hamjakmano twiwi chwng tamo swrwngwi man?`

Kaitor wng kha hamjakmung (1 Johon 4:8). Bo chwngno belai hamjakmani bagwi bini songdari Sajlano rwkha (Johon 3:16). Jisuni aro se hamjakma nukjak baio (Hibru 1:3). Hamjakmano twiwi chwng sina muchung khe Jisuno naiwi swrwngwi mano. Swmai Kwtal-o Jisuno phunukmano chwng nai khe, 1 Korinth 13 beremo sajakmani hamjakmani kaham tongmungrok Jisuni aro se núkjak baio.

**1. Jisu wng kha khá loknai.** “Hai phano ang sumsok gwnang kukni bising tẃi Khristo Jisu khá lok súgo [_makrothymia_] hwnwi phunukna bagwi se Kaitor ano khairogo, omo tẃi khe ulo Khristo Jisuno poito khlaiwi tháng phlainairok Kaitor bwswk khairok abono nuknai” (1 Timothi 1:16).

**2. Jisu wng kha khairoknai.** Baibel sao, “Subraini khairokmano norok chánaiwi kha” (1 Pitor 2:3). O bwtango “Subrai” hwnmani abo Jisuno se sajago. “Khairokma” abo Grik kókthai-o _chrēstos_. 1 Korinth 13:4 bwtangno _chrēsteuomai_ (khairokma phunuknani) hinwi sajakma kókthai bai gothokjak.

**3. Jisu kubui bai se tongthokjago.** Jisu buphani muchung twi tong khe, tei buphano hamjakma bai se tongthokjago (Johon 15:9–11, Johon 17:12–14).

**4. Jisu bwkha bai phlai ya/khá lok máng tongo.** Hibru 12:2, 3 bwtango sao, Jisu “bo krusno soi-o…sumsok gwnang bororok bai bo bwswk se naislerjak kha, phiya abórokno bo sói kha” (NKJV English Bible naidi). Jisu hai sak sóinai kebo kwrwi (Phil. 2:8). Bini bwskango tongthok tongmani bagwi se bo omo hai khlaio!

**5. Jisu poito khlai máng tongo.** Pol bai kók salaimano twiwi Anania swng phuru (Samung 9:13, 14), Jisu kók phirokmung rwkha: “Ang bono chong kha” (Samung 9:15). Bororok bahai tongmani bisi abo hai khe bóhrokno nuk ya. Phiyaba bini phan bai bóhrok tamo wngnai abo bo sio.

`Kubui hamjakma tamo, abono twiwi Jisu teibo phunukmani lamarok tamo tamo?`
