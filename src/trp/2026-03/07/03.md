---
title:  Hamjakma Tamo Khlai
date:   10/08/2026
---

1 Korinth 13:4-7 bwtang wng kha beremni bwkhathai. Pol hamjakmungni tongmungno phunukma bising twi phunugwi rwkha—hamjakma tamo, hamjakma tamono hwn ya, tei hamjakma tamo khlai, phiya hamjakma tamo khlai ya.

Pol hamjakmano khoroksa borok hai khe phunuk kha; tamni hwn ba Phola bai twiduljaknai borok bubo hai wng nukmani bising twi a hamjakmano saui manna bagwi. Hamjakma abo bwkha tei uánsukmani slái samung bai se sinijago hinwi Pol phunuk kha.

Hakhe hamjakma tamo khlai?

**1. Khá lokmano phunugo (_makrothymeō_).** _Makrothymeō_ hwnmani khe tong halok kebengmungni bisingo phano khá lokmano phunugo. Khá lokmani abo khoroksa tei khoroksano hama logwi tongnani phannobo phunugo (Epis 4:2).

**2. Khairokmano phunugo (_chrēsteuomai_).** Swmai Kwtal-o _Chrēsteuomai_ kókthai oro simino núkjago. Swmai Kwcham Grik version-o o kókthai núkjakmani wng kha Lobmung 145:9 bwtango. Abo Kaitorni khairokma bai khe phunukjak kha. Pol sana naikha, o hamjakmao khairokma tongo. Abo Kaitor chwngno khairokma hai kheno chwngbo buino khairokna nangmani.

**3. Kubui bai tongthokjago (_synchairō_).** _Synchairō_ hwnmani kókmang wng kha bui bai baksa tongthokma khe tong manmani (Luk 1:58; Luk 15:6, 9; 1 Korinth 12:26; Phil. 2:17, 18).

**4. Bwkha bái phlai ya/ Sal soimang tongo (_stegō_).** Amjoknairok sao, stegō hwnmani kókmang wng kha mungsa swkno “kholobwi tonnani.” Kók wng kha kwrak khe kha ragwi tongnani. 1 Korinth 9:12 bwtango abono twiwi sraisrai khe sajakma tongo. Kwbangkuk bororokno poito khlaio, o kókni soi kókmang wng kha khá lokmani.

**5. Poito khlai máng tongo (_pisteuō_).** _Pisteuō_ abo Grik kókthai poito khlaimani (_pistis_) hai. 1 Korinth 13 beremo poito khlai máng tongo hwnmani kókmang wng kha bui bai poito khlaijak man ya wng ya twi khe tongnani.

**6. Khatung máng tongo (_elpizō_).** Swmai Kwtal-o _elpizō_ hwnmani khwlai (verb) kókthai abo mungsa swk kaham wngnai hinwi poito khlaimanino se sajago.

**7. Khá lok máng tongo (_hypomenō_).** 1 Korinth 13:7 bwtangni _stegō_ (verb) tei _hypomenō_ kókthaini bisingo pher laimung kwrwi. O kainwi kókthaini kókmang gothokjak. Tong keseb jorao khá loknani. Poito tei khatungmani bising twi khá loknani kókno Pol oro sakha.

`1 Korinth 13:4–7 bwtang bai Galatia 5:22, 23 bwtangno naidi. O kainwi bwtango gothokmani kókbothoma tamo? Bahai khe abo hai hamjakmano chini langma-o phunuknai?`
