---
title:  “O Taun Bisingo Ani Borok Máng Se”
date:   01/07/2026
---

`Samung 18:4-8 khúktang di. Polni kók pírmani bising tẃi tamo wng kha?`

Pol Korinthni Judairokni bisingo khlaima samungrok bo muchungma hai bwthai man liya. Bo naikoljakmung man liya tei naislerjakmung khe man kha. Baibel-o hai hwnwi sajak kha, “bórok bono kebengwi bini mungwi kók hamya sa kha” (Samung 18:6). Grik kok bai _blasphēmeō_ (hamya sama) hwnmani wng kha, borokno hamya khlaima se. Aboni kókmang khe, bohrok Polni mungno hamya khlaina nai kha tei bo Kaitorni samung khlaimano dera rwna naikha.

Phiyaba Korinth’-ni thumnokrogo Pol samungrok khlaimani magwna wng liya. Aboni ulo Kaitor bini kók pirmani samungni mwrwknai ang kha. Bo swmai tang kha, “Ani búkhúkni ongkhorma kóktháirokbo abo tẃi’-no wngnai; abo nang ya tei bukcha wngwi ani thani kiphilwi sók phai glak” (Jisaia 55:11). Baksa Judairok, thumnokni tẃiphang Krispo bai bini nukhungni borokrok Jisuno Mesia hwnwi yachagwi baptisma wngnai hwnwi uansuk phano man liya (Samung 18:8). Bórok simi ya, “Korinthsongni bere teibo borok kwbángma Kothoma Kaham khnaui poito khlai kha, hakhe baptisma wng bai kha” (Samung 18:8). Abo Krispo’-no nugwi bini bising twibo borok ermani wngwi mano.

`Samung 18:9, 10 khúktang di. Korinth kamiyungo jwngjalrok bai manjakmani jorao Polni khá-o tongmani kokno chwng tamo saimanwi mano? Kaitor bini selengno bahai khe khá rak rw kha?`

Pol thumnokni ongkhorwi thangmani ulo’-no bo kaisa samung khlai kha, abo bono khá rak rw kha. Khristo sak baithangno horni jorao bini thani nukjak phai kha. Jisiano sama hai khe bono sa phai kha: “Ta kiri di, ang nwng bai tongo” (Jisaia 41:10). Puila, Pol bo Korinth-o tong phuru “phan kwrwi kirima gwnang khe thango” (1 Kor. 2:3). Bo belai kheno kebengjakma bai Beriani Athens-o thángna nang kha. Bo uansukmani khe ahai, bo Korinth yakarwi thangna nangnai, tamni hwn ba abo kaisa belai kheno bini bagwi kebengmung karonni bagwi. Tamo khe o jora-o abo hai wngnai ya. Jisu bono hai hwnwi sakha: “O kamio ani borok kwbángma tongo” (Samung 18:10). Tei Pol bohrokni thani mwthangjakmani koktwma tubuna bagwi bini samung tangnai wng kha.

`Jisaia 41:10 khúktang di. O kókni bisingtwi chwngno tamo uanamasingcha swmai rwjak kha? Abórok nini sal brumni langmano bahai khe swnamnai?`
