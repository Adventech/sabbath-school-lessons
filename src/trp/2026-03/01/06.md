---
title:  Korinthsongni Thani Polni Koktun
date:   02/07/2026
---

1 Korinth 1:11-13; 1 Korinth 4:14; 1 Korinth 5:11; 1 Korinth 7:1; tei 1 Korinth 14:37, 40 khúktang di. Tei 2 Korinth 1:12, 2 Korinth 2:9, 2 Korinth 11:3, tei 2 Korinth 13:10 khúktang di. Pol tamoni bagwi Korinthsarokno swimani? O bwtangrokni bising twi bahai khe chwng bujiui man?

Pol 1 Korinth swimani jorao Ephiso tongmani (1 Kor. 16:5-9). Kloini nukhungni borokrok bini thani thangwi, Korinth kamiyungo samungrok belai kaham wngya hwnwi sakha (1 Kor. 1:11). Korinth 1–6 bwtango Pol Kloi bai sajakmani kokrokno sakha. Aroni jwngjalrok wng kha dopha bai dopha kwtha lai ya wngmani, buitali-buitala wngmani, kerong rwksaui majra ber laimani, tei uaija laimani. Polbo kaisa swijak koktunbo man kha, aro swngmungrok tongo (1 Kor. 7:1). O swngmungrokni kók phirokmungno bo 7 berem jora swiwi rwkha. Swngmungrokni kókbothomarok wng kha: kaijakma, kak laima, kaijak yaui tongma, takjak mwtairokni thani barokmanino chama, surimung panda khlaima, phola rwmaungrokno samungo thepama, tei kwthwirok thangwi bachama abórokno twiwi. Korinthni mothani borokrok belai kheno jwngjal gwnang tei Motha chakra wngwi man ya kho. Phiyaba nini mothao’-bo kwbangma jwngjal tongwi mano. Tamo khe Korinthni mothani bororok khe teibo hamya wngwi tongmani.

Polni puila Korinthsarokno swimani koktun chini jora baibo belai kheno nangkukmani. Tabukni molo chini motharogo’-bo abo hai jwngjalrok nugwi man ya diba? Bini kóktunni kók kwbángma chwngno sajaknai tongo. Abo “bini bebak swijakmarokni bisingo kaisa kotorkuk, phwrwngmung gwnang, phan gwnang koktunrokni bisingo kaisa.”—Ellen G. White, _The Acts of the Apostles,_ p. 301.

Pol Korinthsarokno kaitham eba kaibrwi swk koktun swihor kha (2 Kor. 10:9). Bo 1 Korinth’-ni swkang kaisa koktun swikha, phiya abo kwmawi thang kha (1 Korinth 5:9). 2 Korinth’-ni swkang, bo kaisa tei kaisa koktun swi kha, abono amjokgwi rutuknairok “nangkukmani koktun” hwnwi sa kha (2 Kor. 2:3, 4, 9; 2 Kor. 7:8), phiya abobo kwmawi thang kha. Baksa baksa uansugo bo 1 Korinthno a kókrokno sa-o hwnwi, eba o swijakma 2 Korinthno baksa rwjak kha hwnwi.

2 Korinth’-ni bising twi chwng saiman kha, Korinthni borokrok bohrokni gana-o tongnai hukumu bai kolopjagwi tong kha. Bórok bataimung, phan, tei rang-ri hairokno belai kheno morokmung eba nangkukmani khlaio, abo bebakno tinibo chini mothani thani kebengmung wngwi tongo. Phohaba Pol khe Khristono twiwi uansukma hukumu swnamna bagwi naisigo; abo wng kha swngcharno kothoma kahamni naisikmung bai bai nukmani lama. Chwngbo, chini tabukni swngcharno kothoma kahamni naisikmung baino nuknani belai nango.

`2 Korinth 2:4 khúktang phi di. Pol o borokrokno bwswk naikolwi tong abono twiwi chwngno oro tamo sakha? Chini bwkhabo bahai khe chwng buini bagwi rwnai?`
