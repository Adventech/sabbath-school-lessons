---
title:  Jisuni Chonghor Wngna Bagwi Kaitor Bau Ringjaknai Pol
date:   28/06/2026
---

Pol Korinthsarokno swimani koktun’-no Jisuni chonghor hwnwi sakno phunugwi cheng kha, bo wng kha “Kaitorni morji bai Khristo Jisuni chonghor wngna hwnwi ringjak” (1 Korinth 1:1; 2 Korinth 1:1). Jisu bai kwrwngwi tongmani kokno twiwi bini poito belai kheno kwrak wngma bai, bo bini koktunrokno bebakno omo hai kheno cheng kha.

`1 Korinth 1:1 tei Rum 1:1 khúktang di. O bwtango Pol’-ni samungni kainwi nangkukmani kók tamo? (Galatia 1:1 bwtangno nai jadi.)`

Pol bini ringjakma tei chonghor wngmani samungno Kaitorni muchung twi khlaimani hwnwi sakha. Bo bororok bai ringjagwi phaimani ya, phiyaba Kaitor bai ringjagwi phaimani (Galatia 1:1). Jirimia hai kheno, Polbo bo bini bumani oksingo tong phuruni simino ringjak kha (Jirimia 1:5). Omo Kaitorni khairokma bai samung bai se (Galatia 1:15), tei abo buini bororokni bisingo Khristoni Kothoma Kaham pirna bagwi se.

1 Korinth 15:8 bwtango, Pol sakno Khristo thangwi bachamani ulo nukjaknairokni bisingo bo khoroksa hinwi sakha (1 Korinth 15:5–7). Bini chonghor wngna bagwi ringjakmani abo Jisu bai malaimani bising twi wng kha hinwi bo phunuk kha (1 Korinth 15:9-11).

“Jisuni chonghor” hwnwi mung rwjakmani abo kwbangma uansukmungrokno khobjago. Puila khe, Jisu bai rohorjak bororokno sa-o. Tamokhe, Pol o kokno bo sakno Khristoni seleng hwnwi phunukna bagwibo sakha (Rum 1:1, Tit 1:1, Galatia 1:10), abo hai kheno bo sakno pirnai tei phwrwngnai hwnwi phunuk kha (1 Timothi 2:7, 2 Timothi 1:11). Bo kok pirnai eba phwrwngnai phano wng thung, Khristo bini samungni bisingo tongwi tongo. Bathing khe sakhe, Pol Jisuni chonghor se.

Jisu Polni chonghor wngmani bisingo simi ya, phiya Polni langmani bisingobo tongo. Polni uansukmung tei khá-o tongmani abo Jisu bini bisingo tongmani se. O kokno 1 Korinth bijapni chengsamungni tei hambai rwmani bechebo Jisuni kokno (kaichuku bwtango uaichuku jora) sajakmani bising twi phunukjak kha. Pol Jisuno belai khe hamjakmani bagwi bini kok uansukma tei kok samanino bo mwthagwi man ya. Bo bini samungno naikolna bagwi samung tangnairoknobo Jisuno bohrokni langmani bisingo swnamna bagwi muchungjak kha. Pol chonghor wngna bagwi ringjak phuru, Subrai bohrokno ringmani samungno khlaiwi Jisuno poito khlainai borok wngna bagwibo ringjak kha.

`Pol chonghor wngna bagwi ringjak kha. Nini ringjakma tamo, tei bahai khe saimannai abo nini ringjakma hwnwi? Nwngbo ringjak kha hinwi tumungno uansuk ya khe, Kaitor bai hinmani lama-o nini chaya tamo wngnai?`
