---
title:  Athens’-ni Simi Korinth
date:   29/06/2026
---

`Samung 17:16–34 khúktang di. Korinth-o thangna swkang Pol boro tongmani? Bo aro tamo khlaiwi tongmani?`

Samung 17:16–34 bwtango sao, Pol Korinth kamio thangmani swkang Athensni bororokno kók pírwi tongo. Bo a jora-o Athens-o thangna bagwi uansuk ya, phiya ba Beria-o bono kebengmani bagwi bini baksa bayabrokni chubamung bai bo aro ma tháng kha (Samung 17:13–15).

Pol bai baksa Athens-o thangnairok Timothi bai Silano dakti dakti khe bo bai baksa tongna bagwi dagiui Beria-o kiphilwi phaikha (Samung 17:15). Pol bórokno naisingwi tong phuru tamo khlai kha Samung 17:16–34 bwtango swijak tongo. Bo thumnokrogo, hatirogo tei Ariopago Jisuni kokno sa-o. Bo kubui’-no Jisuni kokno samani tei seb kaham manmanino mwthawi manliya.

`Samung 18:1-11 khúktang di. Pol Korinth-o sók phai khe tei a kami-o tong phuru tamo khlai kha?`

Pol bini uáinwi samung tangma jorao Korinth-o tháng kha. Luk chwngno sakha, Pol aro bisa tei khaksa tongwi kha.

Pol bini samungno thumnokni simk cheng kha (Samung 18:4-6). Samung 17:1, 2 bwtango swijak tongo, omo bini tongmung hwnwi. Bo “Juidirokno swkang” hwnwi sajakmani tangremno rwkthar kha, (Rum 1:16, Samung 13:46) abo wng kha Jisu bini chonghorrokno dagimani hai (Samung 1:8 no naidi).

Paithago Sila bai Timothi Korinth-o bo bai baksa phai phuru, bo “Jisu’-no a Masia hwnwi Pol Judairokno dak-dak khe phunuk twtwi Kothoma Kaham pír máng tong kha” (Samung 18:5). Korinth-o tong phuru bo “Kaitorni kokno phwrwngwi” samung khlaiwi tongkha (Samung 18:11, NKJV). Korinthsongni thani Pol kok sana naimani abo “Jisu Khristono simi se uansuknai, aboni bere se bo kruso thwimano simi se uansuknai, tei mungsano phano uansuk ya” (1 Kor. 2:2).

`Kothoma Kaham pirna bagwi seb kaham swnammani kokno twiwi Athens tei Korinth-o Pol kók pirmani bising twi chwng tamo swrwngwi mano? Jisuni kókno buino saui manna bagwi seb kahamni kókno uansukdi tei abohai khe bahai khe samung khlaiwi mannai uánsuk di.`
