### O Hatibarsani Swrwngjaknai
1 Kor. 1:1, Gal. 1:1, Samung 17:16–34, 1 Kor. 5:9–11, Samung 18:4–10, 2 Kor. 2:4.

> <p>Muitu Bwtang</p>
> “Horsa Subrai imango Polno hai hwnwi sa kha, ‘Ta kiri di, kók sa máng tong di, sama ta cho di. Ang nwng bai tongo. Khoroksa phano nono dera rwui man glak, tamni hwn khe o taun bisingo ani borok máng se kwbáng’ ” (Samung 18:9, 10, KOKBSI).

Inglishni missionary kotor William Carey kwbangma jorao sakha, bo sakno mwthangna bagwi juta swnamnai samung khlaio, phiya bini kubui samung wng kha Kaitorni thani pholarokno tubumani.

Abo hai kheno, chonghor Polbo sakni langmani bagwi ríni tamu snamnai wngwi samung khlai kha (Samung 18:1–3), phiya bini samung kubui khe borokrokno Khristoni thani tubunani.

O hatibaro chwng Korinthni mothani borokrokni thani Pol samung khlaimani kokno naiwi nai anw. Chwng nuknai, a mothani borokrok belai kheno jwngjal bai kupulung wng kha—bisi nwisaini ulo abo hai kwbangma jwngjalrok tini chini mothani borokrok bai manjagwi tongo. Phiya khristan eba mothani samungno kwbangma jora khlaiwi tongnairok kaham khe swngwi mano: Khoroksa swk khristan mothao jwngjalni yakni phiyogwi tongnai borok tongkha de? O swngmungni phirokmung khe “ih!”

Chonghor Pol Korinth kamio kwbangma jwngjal bai malai kha, phiya krusni kók bai abórokno choba khlai kha (1 Korinth 2:2). O kokno poito khlaiwi tongmani abo se tini chwngni bwskango tongnai jwngjalrokno swnamnani lama. Chwng o hatibaro tei quarter-o nai lai anw 1 tei 2 Korinthni kókrok chini langmao belai nangmani.

_July 4, Sabat salni bagwi o hatibarsani rwngchapno swrwngwi tiyar wng di._
