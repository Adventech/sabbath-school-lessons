---
title:  Korinth’-ni Kamiyung
date:   30/06/2026
---

`Samung 18:1-3, 1 Korinth 5:9-11, tei 1 Korinth 8:4 khúktang di. Korinthni rang-ri, tongmung, tei tongtharni langmani kokno chwng tamo saimanwi mano?`

Korinth khe kwcham swngcharni molo kaisa nangkukmani kamiyung, abo bini rang-rirokni bagwi mung gwnang. A kamiyungno 146 b.c. molo Rum bai sokomorjak kha tei Julius Caesar bai 44 b.c. a kamiyung Rumni ha snamjak kha. O Rumni Korinth wng kha Swmai Kwtalni Korinth. Polni jora-o Korinth wng kha Athensni kaisa phan gwnang ha tei kwbángma lama-o bono swkang wngwi tongmani. Korinth kamiyungo kainwi nangkukmani rungtorbathakthai (harbor) tongo. Hakhe aro kaisa hani simi tei kaisa hao tlangmani manwirok slai rwo, abo hai kheno a thai wng kha baniya eba bepar thwngmani khola kotor wngwi bacha kha.

Pol Korinth kamiyungno chongwi kha; tamni hwnba abo belai nangkukmani ha. “Omo hai khe Kothoma Kaham pirna bagwi kaisa seb kaham man kha, abo wng kha Korinth-ni ha, aroni simino swngcharni bebak thaio kothoma kaham pirnani seb kaham tongo.”—Ellen G. White, _Sketches From the Life of Paul,_ p. 99.

Tei sana kwlaio, Korinth kamiyungo belai bepar thwngmani hamchaui tongma bagwi Pol a kamio Kothoma Kaham pírwi tong phuru ríni tamurok snamwi tei phalwi sakno chubana bagwi seb man kha (Samung 18:2, 3). Phiyaba aswk kotor kamiyungo Kaitorni samung tangnani aswk kwlai ya. Korinth kamiyungo kwbangma tongthar twinai bodolrok tongo (1 Kor. 8:5) Aro Apollo, Athena, tei Aphrodite hai mwtairokno khulumna bagwi snamjak kwbángma noktharrok tongo. Tei Sarapis tei Isis hai Ijipni mwtairokno khulummanirokbo aro núkjago.

Kwbangma tongthar twimung sanul nabul tongmani bagwi Korinth kamiyungo chwla bai bwrwi uaijamanibo belai núkjago. Strabo mungwi Grikni geographer tei laibuma rutuknai borok tongo. Bo sakha, Korinthni mwtai noktharo Aphroditeno khulumna bagwi 1,000 kwthar khe uaijanai bwrwirok tongo. Athensrok Korinth kamiyungno kebengwi auaimung khlai phano, kwcham hayungni molo uaija laimani abo kaisa kerong se hinwi sajago. Omo laibuma amjoknairok samani kók. Korinth kamiyungi kaisa jwngjal kotor wng kha uaija laimani. Takjak mwtaino khulummani tei tongmung sitra tong laimani wng kha bóhrokni sal brúmni tongmung. O kókno puila tei philnwi Korinth-o swbai sukrubwi sajak kha.

`Pol Korinth kamiyungo Kaitorni samung tangwi tong phuru, mwtairokno khulumnai tei hamya khlainai dophani borokrokni jwngjal bai ma manjak kha. Tabukni molo kothoma kaham pirnairok tamo tamo jwngjal bai manjagwi tong? Chwng bahai khe abórokno mechennai? Tini Korinth bai chini kamiyungrokni bisingo bwswk pher laimung tong?`
