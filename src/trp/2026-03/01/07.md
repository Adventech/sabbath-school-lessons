Ellen G. White swimani ‘_The Acts of the Apostles’_ bijapni “Korinth” rwngchpano khúktang di, pp. 243–254 l.

“Korinth-o Kothoma Kaham pír phuru, chonghor Pol Athens-o bini samungni slái juda kwrwng khe kaisa lama tẃiwi tháng kha. ... Bo khá khlai kha, Korinthsarokni bisingo ‘Jisu Khristo, tei bono kruso butharjak kha’ hwnwi simi se saiman ya hwnwi.”—Ellen G. White, _The Acts of the Apostles,_ p. 244.

“Pol kisa swk samung chakha”, phiya “bo aro motha songchaui mannai de” uánsugwi man liya. Korinth belai kheno jwngjal tei swngmungni jaga, aboni bagwi bo a kamiyungno yakarna uánsuk kha…

“Pol Korinth yakarwi kubun jagao thángna bagwi uansuk kha, abo se kaham hwnwi bo uansukjak. Tamo khe, salsa hor jorao, Subraiyung bono imango hai hwnwi sakha, ‘Ta kiri di, Kaitorni kók pírwi tong di, tamni hwn ba o kamio ani borok kwbángma tongo.’ Pol buji kha Kaitor bono Korintho tongna bagwi muchungmani. Tei bini samungrokno Kaitor hamari rwnai tei borok kwbangmano Jisuno yachakna chubanai hwnwi bo poito khlaio. Abo bai’-no borok kwbángma Khristono rwkthar kha, hakhe aro kaisa motha kotor chengjak kha.”—Ellen G. White, _Sketches from the Life of Paul,_ pp. 106, 107.

“Pol Korinth kamio nokha bisi kaisa tei taldok samung tang kha. Tamo khe bo a kamiyungo simi samung tang ya. Bo Korinthno bini samung khlainani bagwi kotor jaga snam kha. Aroni simi bo juda juda jagao kwbangma motha swnamna bagwi samung khlai kha. Pol sak baithang mothani borokrokno naiwi man liya jorao, bo bórokno kwrak tei nangkukma koktunrok rohorwi bórok bai kwrwngwi tong kha. Poito khlainairok o koktunrokno Kaitorni kók hwnwi yachak lai kha. O kóktunrokno motharogo khorang kotor khe porijak kha.”—Ellen G. White, _Sketches from the Life of Paul,_ p. 109.

**Salaijaknai Swngmungrok**

`Pol, bo Jisuni chonghor hwnwi poito khlai kha, tei Kaitor bai se bo ringjago hinwi poito khlai kha. Chwng sabo tei chini ringmani tamo saimannani bagwi tamoni belai nangkukmani?`

`Kaisa jorao Pol Korinthni Kaitorni samungno yakarwi, eba a kamiyungno yakarwi thangnani muchungjak kha. Tamo wngwi bo bini kháno swlai kha? Kaitorni samungno yakarna muchung phuru omo bahai khe chwngno chubachu khlaiwi mano? Tamo khe, chwng khlaina nangmani jora tongwi mano de?`

`Korinthni mothani borokrok bohrokni gana-o tongnairokni hukumu bai belai kheno koboljagwi tong kha. O kokbo tini chini bisingo kaisa belai kheno kubui kok. Chwng bahai khe swngcharo tong twtwi (Johon 17:11, 15) tei “swngcharo tongmani--- bwsakni muchungmarok, mokolni muchungmarok tei langmani uanamarok” (1Jon 2:16) bai koboljagwi tong? Chini mothani borokrok tamo hukumurok bai hamya wngwi tong kha?`
