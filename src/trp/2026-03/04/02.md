---
title:  Lachimasingcha Tongmungrokno Naiwi Tomgmani
date:   19/07/2026
---

Khristianrokni láibuma-o theologians, pastors, tei mothani adongrok motha bahai wngna nangnai sina bagwi Swmai Kwtal’-no kaham khe swrwng kha. Phunukmarini bagwi Chonghorrokni Samung bijap’-ni mothano chwng nai khe ma uanao. Phiyaba chwng kaisa nangkukmani kókno daktino pokjago: abo wng kha bororokni thani jwngjal tongmani. Motha bahai wngthai kwrwi, abono twiwi Baibel tamo sa, chwng sina hwn khe Swmai Kwtal bijap ma nainai. Korinthsongno Pol swihormani le belai nangkukmani kókni bising twino cheng kha.

`1 Korinth 5:1–13 khúktang di. O bwtango bubo hai tong halokni kókno Pol saui khna rwkha? Tei abo tamoni bagwi aswk kebengmung wng kha?`

Pol sakha baksa baksa bini matoi bai suda buitali-buitala tongmung tongo (1 Korinth 5:1). Pol o kók khnamani wngwi mano “Khloini nokni” bororokni bising twi (1 Korinth 1:11). Buitali-buitala wngmani abo belai sitra sumsok, “poito kwrwirokni bisingo suda abo tẃi tongmung sitra kwrwi” (1 Korinth 5:1). Phiyaba yaphangni Khristian mothao se omo hai jwngjalrok núkjak kha! Mothani borok khoroksa omo hai sitra sumsok khlai tongo hinwi khnaui Pol belai uanajak kha tei kha khamjak kha (1 Korinth 5:1-2).

Phiyaba o tong halok teibo hamya wng kha. Pol teibo uanajak kha; tamni hwn ba Korinthni bororok abo hai sumsok khlaiwi sakno chaya uánsuk ya karwi se teibo kungchuk laimani bagwi (1 Korinth 5:1-2). Hakhe Pol a sumsok khlainai borokno simi phrwngna nai liya, tamo khe Mothani joto bororokno bo phrwngna nai kha; tamni hwn ba bóhrokni poito bai tang baithang samungni bisingo phermung tongo. Pol sraisrai khe úaire uaino sakha, a sumsok khlainai borok mothao phrwngjakna tongmani, phiya motha abono samung nangjak ya khe tong kha. Abo hai sumsok samungno twiwi se kungchuk laiwi tong kha (1 Korinth 5:2, 6). Omo Polni thani joboino laikuk laha! A bororokni tamo chaya wngma klai kha?

A borokno Mothani bororok tamoni bagwi aswk naiwi phai kha, chwng kaham khe saui man suk ya. Wngwi mano bo Mothano chubanai gwnang borok. Eba wngwi mano “bebak samungno khlai mano” hinwi mothao kerong gwnang (1 Korinth 6:12). Aboni bagwino bóhrok a sumsokno belai dorokuk khe nuk liya wngwi mano! Chwng o kókno twiwi aswk sraisrai wng suk ya.

Jesa phano karon wng si thung, phiyaba bóhrok Elembakni kókno swbai tongmano nugwi man liya (Lebi 18;7-8). Phiyaba tamoni bagwi bóhrok abono twiwi kungchuk?

`Elembago ta khlai di hinwi majra bermani sumsokno chini mothaobo khlaiwi tong phano “hamjakmani” tei “yachakmani” bising twi hinwi motha a sumsokno twiwi phimanirok tamo tamo?`
