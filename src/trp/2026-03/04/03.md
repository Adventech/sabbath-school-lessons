---
title:  Mothani Sinimungno Mwthangmani
date:   20/07/2026
---

Buitali-buitala wngmani sumsokno naiwi simi tongmani abo belai hamya! Abo Polni thanibo hamya, tei chini bagwibo hamya! Omo bai tong halogo khe chwng Elembakni kókno twiwi tongna nango, tei surimani bai hamjakmani bising twi omo hairokno ma nainai. Chini naharmung kwtal ma khlainai (restoration) hwnmano pogwi thangthai kwrwi.

`1 Korinth 5:1–13 khúktang phidi. Abo hai tong halokni twiwi tongnano twiwi Pol bahai khe bóhrokno sakha?`

1 Korinth 5 beremo Pol sakha, abo hai khe buitali-buitala wngmanino Motharok phrwngna nango. Abo hai borokno Mothani tikhlaiwi rwna nangnai (1 Korinth 5:2), buj thumna nangnai (1 Korinth 5:3), Soitanni yago yapharwi rwna nangnai (1 Korinth 5:5), tei motha bisingni “khagwi rohorna” nangnai (1 Korinth 5:13). “Abo tẃi borok bai ta tong di” (1 Korinth 5:9, 11) tei “Abo tẃi borok bai achugwi mai suda ta chá di” hinwi Mothani bororokno sajak kha (1 Korinth 5:11). Polni kókno khnaui tabukni molni bororok jobor kók kwrak hinwi uánsugwi mano. Phiyaba bini kókno kaham khe bujina hwn khe bóhrokni molni láibuma saui manna nangnai. Tei chwng o kókbo sina nango, Pol oro kók saui tongmani abo luku bai sijagwi dakdak khe buma matoi bai tongnai borokni kókno se saui tongo. Abo belai tong halok sitra. O kók kisa bathong khe sama baino chwng bujina lama tongo.

“Omo tẃi tongmung tongnaino norokni bisingni khagwi rohorthai” (1 Korinth Cor. 5:2, 1 Korinth 5:13). Omono’ hwno Mothani phrwngmung.

“O borokno Soitanni yago yaphar di” (1 Korinth 5:5). Tamni hwn ba o borok Kaitorni kók khnaui Kaitorni katirwmung bisingo tongnani chongwi na liya. Hakhe bo Soitanni yago romjagwi painai. O kókni kókmang wng kha ahai, “Bo chongwi nama twi kheno bini manthaino bo man si thung” hwnmani.

“Abo tẃi borok bai ta tong di” (1 Korinth 5:9, 11) “Abo tẃi borok bai achugwi mai suda ta chá di” (1 Korinth 5:11). Bóhrokni molo buitali-buitala wngnai bororok bai halok kwrwngmani belai kirimasingcha hinwi lukurok bai uánsukjago. Tamni hwn ba bóhrok buinobo bóhrokni tongmung hamyano phrwngwi rwi mano. Kwcham jorani molo thansa khe achugwi mai chalaimani wng kha bóhrokni tongmung-chamung thansa hwnmano se phunugo. Chwngbo chini nok arini bororokni tongmung hamyarok bai hus khe ma tongthai.

“Bini phola mwthangjakna tẃi khe” (1 Korinth 5:5). Mothani phrwngmungni naharmung khe borokno kaham snamnani se. Kók wng kha sumsok gwnang bororokno tei uáisa sói lama kiphilwi tubunani, tei bóhrokno sumsokni langmani khibi thani chubanani. Aboni bagwino Pol “mangno komor rwna” kók sakha (1 Korinth 5:5). Bini kókni kókmang wng kha a borok bini sumsokni manthaino saui man thung, phiya kha kiphil phai thung hwnmani. Omo haibo wngwi mano, 1 Korinth 5 beremo sajakmani a uaijanai borok yagulo kha kiphil kha, bini kókno ulo sajak kha (2 Korinth 2:5-10 naidi). Sumsok khlainai bóhrok kha kiphilwi mothao tei uáisa kiphilwi phai khe, aphuru se Mothani samung chakha hwnjago.
