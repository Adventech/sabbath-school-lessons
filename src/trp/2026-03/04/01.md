### O Hatibarsani Swrwngjaknai
1 Kor. 5:1–13, 2 Kor 2:5–10, 1 Kor. 6:1–13, 1 Thes. 4:1–8, 1 Kor. 6:19–7.

> <p>Muitu Bwtang</p>
> “Norok saui man ya da, norokni bwsak wng kha norokni bisingo tongnai Phola Kwtharni nokthar, a Phola Kaitor bai rwjakma se. Norok tei sak sakni li ya, norok Kaitorni se; bo norokno pai kha. Aboni bagwi Kaitor lobjakna tẃi khe norokni bwsakno samungo phnang di” (1 Korinth 6:19, 20, KOKBSI).

Chini khorok bomtom wng kha tharuk hai. Chwng khokchaui jesa phano aro tubu khe abo kwchaui tong rwgo. Abórok tamo tamo chwng joto muitu man ya phano wngwi mano. Phiyaba abórok chini bokhorogo’-no tong rwk baio, hakhe abo chini uánsukma, chongma, tei khlaimarogo samung khlaio.

Aboni bagwino chwng Khristian phano, chini sampa kúgo tongnai hamya manwirok bai koboljaknani belai kheno kwlai se. Yaphangni simino Khristian mothao jwngjal tongo. Phunukmari, Kóktisal-o surimani buroni simi phai kha? Abo ahaino de motha bai tubujak kha? Phiya abo hai ya. Abo sampa kúgo tongnai buini tongmungno swrwngwi sók phaimani se.

Korinth Mothao’-bo chwng abo hai nugwi man kha. Mothao kàklai phuilai wngmani kók samani ulo (1 Korinth 1-4), Pol sana cheng kha, Mothao buitali-buitala wngmani, kes rwlaimani, uaija laimani, káijakmani, tei kaijak yaui saichung tongmani akorokno twiwi jwngjal (1 Korinth 5-7). Swngcharni tongmungno swrwngwino bóhrok abo hai tongmungrok bai koboljak kha. 1 Korinth 1-4 bwtango kak laiwi gut khalaimani jwngjal núkjakmani abo ulni beremo khe tongmung hamyani duglamno se phiyogwi rwkha. Motha bisingni sumsokrokno Pol bahai khe twina nai kha, tei bini koktun swimungni bising twi chwng tamo swrwngwi man?

_July 25, Sabat salni bagwi o hatibarsani rwngchapno swrwngwi tiyar wng di._
