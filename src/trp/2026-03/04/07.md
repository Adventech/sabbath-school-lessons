Ellen G. White swimani _‘The Acts of the Apostles’_ bijapni “A Message of Warning and Entreaty” beremno khúktang di, bwkáng 298–308.

1 Korinth 5:10, 11 tei 1 Korinth 6:9, 10 bwtango buitali-buitala wngmani sumsok bai baksa takjak mwtaino khulummani tei chuak kephek wngmani kóknobo Pol khurchaui sakha. 1 Korinth 10:7 bwtango Pol chwngno muitu khlai rwna muchungjak kha (Lamrwma 32:1-6) takjak mwtaino khulummani-o chalai nunglaibo tongo. Abo hai kheno buitali-buitala wngmani lamao sogwio (1 Korinth 10:8). Ellen G. White sakha:

“Kungchuknai tei belai khe chakrak wngnai bororok kwthar khe tongthogwi tongwi man ya ... Borokni bwsak belai phan gwnang. O bwsak hamya manwirokno mwthagwi mano. Phiyaba belai khe chamani tei nungmani tongmung loi khlai khe abo bwsakno kebel snamo. Sumsokni tongmungno máng khlai khe Khristianrok phano bóhrokni bwsakno dera rwo, hakhe abo uánsukma phanno dera rwo”—Ellen G. White, _The Sanctified Life,_ pp. 25, 26.

Khoroksa swk phano bini bwkhao kungchukmani kwrwi wng khe, tei takjak mwtaino bwkhano khibiui rwkhe, hakhe bini bwkha bukchao Khristoni Phola bai supungjago. A borok sw poito bai baksa hamjakmani bising twi samung khlaio. Tei bebak hamyano bo khibui rohoro.”—Ellen G. White, _The Home Missionary,_ November, 1889.

“Kaitor le chwngno kuchugo tisana muchungjago, kwthar khlaina muchungjago, tei Chúknohani tongmung snamna muchungjago. Aboni bagwino Phola Kwthar chini bisingo samung khlai máng tongo. . . . Tumungno Phola Kwtharni bising twi slaijak ya hwn khe chwng komornai.”—Ellen G. White, _Manuscript_ 12, 1888.

**Salaijaknai Swngmungrok**

`Kwbangma borok uánsugo Korinthsong dophani hukumu mukumuno kuchugo tisanani muchungma tongo. Tamoni bagwi Khristianrokni sinimungo abo kirimasingcha? Chwngbo abo hai chayani phiyokjakna bagwi chwng tamo khlai man?`

`Polni swngmung “Norok saui man ya da, norokni bwsak wng kha norokni bisingo tongnai Phola Kwtharni nokthar?” (1 Korinth 6:19), hai khe 1 Korinth 5-6 beremni kaisni swngmungp páithago “norok saui man ya da?” hwnmani máng chwng nugo (1 Korinth 5:6; 6:2, 3, 9, 15, 16, 19). O bebak swngmungrokni kók phirokmung wng kha “Ih, norok saui mano.” O swngmungrok Pol mothano twiwi uánsukmano twiwi chwngno bahai khe bujinani chuba? Tamoni bagwi chwng tabukni molobo abórokno twiwi uánsuknani nang?`

`Káijakmung abo Kaitor bai rwjakma se (Yaphang 1:27, 28; Yaphang 2:18-24), tei káijakmani abo bórom khlaijakna nango (Hibru 13:4). Kwbangma borok uánsugo abo kwcham raida se hinwi. Bahai khe chwng a káijakmungno Eden thaiblwngo Kaitor bai rwjakma se hinwi buino phunuknai?`
