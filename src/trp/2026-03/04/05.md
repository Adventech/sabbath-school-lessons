---
title:  Káijakmani Bai Káijak Ya Tomgmani
date:   22/07/2026
---

`1 Thesaloniki 4:1–8 khúktang di. Kwthar wngmani tei buitali-buitala wngmani yakni katimani bisingo kwrwngmung tamo tong?`

Pol sakani kók abo khorok swkno swihormani wng tong phano, o kok bebak khristanrokni bagwi wngwi mano.

Korinth mothao tamo wngwi tongmani? Tamoni bagwi a jwngjalrok wng kha?

Korinthni baksa bororok uánsugo, kothoma kaham bóhrokno phiyok kha ba, bóhrok sakni muchung twi bebakno khlai man kha hinwi. Bóhrok sakha, bohok chámungni bagwi snamjakma haino, borokni bwsak buitali-buitala wngna babai snamjak kha hinwi (1 Korinth 6:13). Phiyaba Pok sakha, abo Khristianrok phiyokjak kha hinwi samani sói kók swbai sukrubwi samani kwlai ya. Abo wng kha kothoma kahamni bising twi Kaitor phiyókjakmungo rwmano chaya khe samungo thepamani se (Rum 8:2; Galatia 5:13). Sumsok khlai máng tong man kha hinwi Kaitor chwngno phiyokmani ya. Sumsokni se bo chwngno phiyogo (Rum 8:2! Rum 6:18, 22). Kubui kók wng kha “Borokni bwsak … Subraini bagwi se; phiya Subrai bwsakni bagwi se” (1 Korinth 6:13). Chwng Khristoni se (1 Korinth 6:15). Hakhe chwng jesa samung phano khlai khe abo chini sinimung bai gothokjak wngna nangnai. 1 Korinth 6 beremo abono kaitham lamani bising twi phunuk kha:

1. Chwng “Subrai Jisu Khristoni mung bai tei chini Kaitorni Phola bai” suchajak, kwthar khlaijak, tei Kaitor bai kaham hinwi romjak hinwi sinijak kha (1 Korinth 6:11). Hakhe 1 Korinth 6:9, 10 bwtango je sumsokrokni kókno khurchajak kha, tei 1 Korinth 6:12–20 bwtango buitali-buitala wngmani sumsokno twiwi sajak kha, abórok suchajak, kwthar khlaijak, tei Kaitor bai kaham hinwi romjak bororokni langmao gothok ya, mwchang ya.
2. Chwng wng kha Khristoni borok (1 Korinth 6:15). Kók khe chwng Khristo bai thansa ma wngnai (1 Korinth 6:17). Buitali-buitala wng kha o thansano swbainai se (1 Korinth 6:13). Jesa borok phano bui sakni logisong wng yaui bai buitali-buitala wng khe, bo a borok bai baksa “bwsak kaisa” wngo (1 Korinth 6:16). Tamni hwn ba Phola Kwtharni bising twi Khristianrok Khristo bai thansa wng kha. Hakhe Khristo bai o halok le bahai khe tongnai, tei bahai khe buitali-buitalano twiwi uánsuknai eba tongnai buji rwjago.
3. Chini bwsak wng kha “Phola Kwtharni Nókthar” (1 Korinth 6:19, 20). Kwthar khe tongnani, tei buitali-buitala tongmungno kasunani kaisa simi lama wng kha Phola Kwtharni bising twi Jisu bai halok kwrwngnani. Chini bwsakno Phola Kwtharni Nókthar hinwi phunugwi Pol sakha, “Kaitorni thani pun tok ratharwi yapharma hai norok kwthangwi’-no sakno Kaitorni thani yaphar di, bini yago yapharjakna tẃi khe, phiya bo bai hamjakna tẃi khe sakno yaphar di” (Rum 12:1).

`Buitali-buitala wngmani sumsok Khristianrokni bagwi abo bwswk kirimasingcha? Abono twiwi chwng bwswk nangkukmani khe uánsukthai?`
