title:  Bebak Samung Kaitor Lobjakna Tẃi Khe
date:   23/07/2026
---

Buitali-buitalano hus khlaiwi rwna bagwi Pol sakha, chini bwsak wng kha “Phola Kwtharni nokthar” (1 Korinth 6:19). Phola Kwtharni Nókthar hwnmani kókmang wng kha chwng kwthar khe tongnani nangnai hwnmani se. Motha wng kha kaisa Khristian bodol, abo yaksi yagrani tongmung bai juda. Phola Kwthar tong khe abo hai wngnani lama tongo.

`1 Korinth 6:19–7:9 khúktang di. “Buitali-buitala tongmungno khibi di” (1 Korinth 6:18) hwnmano twiwi o bwtango tamo sakha?`

1 Korinth 7 berem-o belai nangkukmani kók tongo. O beremo kainwi kókbothoma khurchajak kha: (1) Káijakmano twiwi phrwngmani (1 Korinth 7:1-24), tei (2) Kaijak yaui tongmano twiwi phrwngmani (1 Korinth 7:25-40).

1 Korinth 7 berem khúktang phuru chwng muitu ma khlainai abo Korinth Motha bai gothokjak abo hai swngmungrokno Pol kók phirokmung rwmani hinwi. Wng ya khe chwng bini kókno buji glak eba uánsugwi phano mano Pol káijakmungno aswk nangkukmani wngjak ya hinwi. Phiyaba Pol káijakmungno nangkukmani wngjak ya abo hai ya (1 Timothi 4:1–3, 1 Timothi 5:14, tei Hibru 13:4).

Khristo bai thansa wngnani (1 Korinth 6:17) tei Phola Kwtharni Nókthar (1 Korinth 6:19) hinwi Pol samani kwcharo bo kaisa dagimung rwkha, “Buitali-buitala tongmungno khibi di” (1 Korinth 6:18). Buitali-buitala tongmung bai hakchal wngmani slái tei kaham lama da tong? Ihi kwrwi.

Kaitor le sex snam kha. Phiya abo káijaknairokni bagwi simi se. Sex abo chwla bai bwrwini bagwi simi se.

“Buitali-buitala tongmungno khibi di” hinwi Pol sa phuru bo Josep’-ni kothoma muitu manjagwibo mano (Yaphang 36:6-18). Baibel sao, Potioharni bihik bai tongmung sitra tongna swkangno Joseb “nokni nongkhorwi khár kha” (Yaphang 39:18). Yaphang 36:6-18 bwtango o kók uaibtwini slaibo kwbang khurchajak kha. Baibel-o sraisrai sajak ya phano, o kók chwng saui mano, Josep kaijak phuru simi sex khlainai hinwi naisingwi tongo (Yaphang 41:45). Bo wng kha Kaitorni Phola bai kuplung (Yaphang 41:38), tei Kaitorni mokolo chajakma samung simi khlaina muchungjago.

`Bahai khe chwng Motha wngwi abo hai chini arini bororokni tongmung buitali-buitala wngmani bai kati rwnai?`
