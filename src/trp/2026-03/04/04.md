---
title:  Buitali-buitala Wngmani Yakni Katinani Lama
date:   21/07/2026
---

1 Korinth 6:1–11 bwtango, Khristianrok bahai khe tamo khlainai abórokno twiwi Pol teibo sakha.

`1 Korinth 5:3, 12, 13; tei 1 Korinth 6:1–13 khúktang di. Korinthsongno tei chwngno Pol tamo phrwngnani chaitok?`

1 Korinth 6:1 bwtango Grik kókthai _pragma_ hinwi swijakmani, abo NKJV Baibel-o khe “matter” hinwi sajak kha. Aboni kókmang wng kha “mungsa swk kók” eba “patimung.” Abo kes rwlaimani kókno sajago. O kókno sina nango, 1 Korinth 6:1-11 bwtango criminal case eba bulai-toklai eba belai samung sitra tangwi kes khlaimani kókno sajak ya. Haphang simi se abo hai kes no naiwi mano (Rum 13:1–5). Buitali-buitala wngmani kókno samani ulo kheno Pol kes khlai laimani kókno sakha. Abo Uáinwi Saphima 22:22-24 bwtango Musi khlaima hai. Mothani jwngjalrokno kaham khlaina hwn khe Elembakni bising twi se khlaina nango hinwi Pol phunuk kha.

1 Korinth 6:1-11 ni kók wng kha kainwini kwcharo buitali-buitala wngmani sumsokni kókno sajago (1 Korinth 5 tei 1 Korinth 6:12-20). Hakhe 1 Korinth 6:1 bwtangni kók abo buitali-buitala wngmani sumsokni kókno twiwino sajakma klaiwi mano. Haiphano abo tamo chwng aswk sraisrai khe siya.

Mothani bororok Mothani jesa jwngjalno twiwi phano kes rwlaimani bagwi Pol hamjak liya. Pol sakha, Khristoni takhuk bukhuk hinwi bóhrok a jwngjalno mothaono kaham khlaiwi man liya. Phiya “poito kwrwirokni thani kes khlaina hinwi bahai phairak” (1 Korinth 6:1)?

Baksa borok uánsugo, 1 Korinth 61: bwtangni patimung abo 1 Korinth 5:1 bwtango sajakma a bupha bai bwslano bisingo wngwi mano. Phiyaba tamono twiwi wng kha abo aswk nangkukmani; nangkukmani khe sói kók yarwngno bujinani se. Pol Mothani sinimungno kaham narwkna naio; tamni hwn ba Khristian bodol phatarni bororok bai naijago. Hakhe buini mokolo Khristian bodol sitra hinwi nukjak rwthai kwrwi (1 Korinth 6:6). Bisingni jwngjalno mwthakna bagwi phataro a jwngjalno twlangthaibo kwrwi. Rumni molo, bóhrokni buj thum nogo gwnang tei borok kotorrok khe yóknani lama tongo. Abo bujsoi wng ya. Aboni bagwi Khristianrok swngcharni buj thum twi khe thum yaui Khristo hai khe se buj thumthai kwlaio.

`1 Korinth 5:10, 11 tei 1 Korinth 6:9, 10 bwtango Pol bai sajakma sumsokrokno twiwi uánsuk di. Tamoni bagwi buitali-buitala wngmani sumsok bai baksa takjak mwtaino khulummani, khokmani, khamaimani, tei bandimani abo hai sumsokroknobo khurchaui sakha?`
