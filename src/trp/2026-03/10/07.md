Ellen G. White swimani _‘The Acts of the Apostles’_ bijapni “The Message Heeded” beremno khúktang di, bwkáng 323–334.

Thangnai hatibaro chwng The Acts of the Apostles bijapni sakao sajakma kókrok khúktang di. Tabuk khe chwng Polni kwrak koktunno nai lai anw, bo a koktun swiphuru bini bwkha bubo hai, tei Korinthsong khá kiphilmani khnaui bo bwswk tongthokjak kha. Tei Pol bwswk sói khe Kaitorni samung tang kha uánsuk di, tei bo Khristoni bagwi samung tangmani bising twi chwng tamo swrwngwi man uánsuk di.

“Kaitor le bororokno nakaro hinwi chwng bebak swngcharno, sumsok khlainai, tei sumsok khlai ya swngcharrokno ma phunuknai. Kaitorni hamjakma bising twi chwng bo bai kwtha rwjak kha. Khoroksa swk kha kiphil khe, sumsokni bagwi kha kham khe, tei Khristo bini sumsokni bagwi thwi kha hinwi poito khlai khe, hakhe Kaitor bai a borok kwtha rwjak kha hinwi sidi.”—Ellen G. White, _Special Testimonies On Education,_ p. 223.

“Motha wngwi chwng pohor kotorno yachak kha. O pohor swngcharni kaham tei hamari wngwi mannai twi khe Kaitor chwngno rwkha. Kwtha rwnani a samung chwngno rwjak kha. Kaitorni phan bai chwng buino Kaitor bai kwtha rwnani samung ma khlainai.”—Ellen G. White, Letter 32, 1903.

“Kaitor bai kwtha kheno a borok kwthar langma tongnani ma rutuknai. “Kaitorno kiriui sói sói kwthar khe tong lai anw” (2 Korinth 7:1) hinwi Pol samani kókno Ellen G. White samani tongo. Bo sakha, Pol kwtal Khristian wngnairokno chubanani naikha “kwrak wngnani, tornai Khristian wngnani, poito-o raknani, jotojogo khe muchungnai wngnani, tei Kaitorno kha yaphar súknai wngnani bai bini hani samungno tangnai wngnani.”—Ellen G. White, _The Acts of the Apostles,_ p. 201.

**Salaijaknai Swngmungrok**

`Pol chwngno “hani manwi” bai khe surchaui sakha (2 Korinth 4:7). Kothoma kaham pir thanio borokni tong halok le kebel hinwi chwng bahai khe bujiui mannai?`

`“Borok kwtal” (2 Korinth 5:17) hwnmani kókmang tamo? Bahai khe sal brúm abo wngwi mannai? Bahai khe Khristo nono borok kwtal snam kha?`

`2 Korinth 6:4 5 bwtango kothoma kahamni bagwi birman manmani kókrokno sakha. A tektok birmanrokno bo bahai khe nuk kha (2 Korinth 6:6, 7 naidi)? Bahai khe bini kók nini uánsukmano chubaui man?`

`Swngcharni kha khammani bai Kaitor bai kha kham rwjakmanino twiwi Pol sakha (2 Korinth 7:10). Bubo hai kha khammung abo kha kiphilmungo twlang? Swngcharni kha khammani bai Kaitor bai kha kham rwjakmanino twiwi nwng bahai khe sanai?`
