---
title:  Birmanrok Tei Chẃngsacha
date:   31/08/2026
---

`2 Korinth 4:7–18 khúktang di. Polni birmanrok tamo tamo? Bahai khe bo bini birmanrokno sak soi kha?`

Kwcham Bohemia’-ni great reformer John Huss, Jisuno twiwi bini samani kók tongo: “Bo swngcharni Subrai, tei chwng khe thwiblang borok—haiphano bo tektorok man kha! Hakhe chwngno kwthar khlainani bagwi birman phai khe chwngbo tamoni bagwi a birmanrokno man yaui tongnai ba?”—Ellen G. White, _The Great Controversy,_ p. 105.

Chonghor Pol bisi kwbang swkangni simino Khristoni bagwi birman mannani muchungjak kha. Bo sai mano, bo wng kha hani takjak manwi hai se (2 Korinth 4:7). Bo tekto man manẃi tongo, bwkha udru mudru wngo, sátokjago, belai khe bujago; hai phano bái ya; bwkha bái ya; khibijak ya; tei thwi ya (2 Korinth 4:8, 9). Pol bini langmao Jisuno phunukna bagwi Jisu thwimanino suda bini sakao rujuna muchungjago (2 Korinth 4:10, 11).

“Jisu thwimani” hinwi oro sajakmani abo swkangni bwtango sajak kha, abo birman tektokrokni kókno Pol samani se. Tei “Jisuni langma” hinwi bo samani khe, thwimani yakni yogwi pholani phan sal brúmno yachakmanino se bo sao. Phiyaba páithago abo mwthangwi tisajakmanino se phunugo (2 Korinth 4:12).

2 Korinth 4:10-12 bwtango “thwimani tei thangmani” o kainwi kók thansa khe uaitham nukjak kha (English Bible naidi). Bo chwngno muitu khlai rwna naikha, chini o langma thwimung bai kwrwngjak. Phiyaba thinangni langma abo tei thwina nang glak (Phun.20:14; Phun. 21:4).

Nangkukmani kók wng kha 2 Korinth 4:7-18 bwtango phunugo, kothoma kaham abo borok kebelrokni bising twino pirjago, hakhe Kaitor simi chẃngsacha wngwi manna twi khe (2 Korinth 4:15). Kwbangma jora Kaitorni kók pirnairok bóhrokni langmao tekto birmanrok bai manjago. Phiyaba chwng naisingmani chẃngsacha bai surchaui nai khe chwng tabuk tekto birman manmarok kisisa se (2 Korinth 4:17). Poito khlainai borok mokol bai núkmani simi bai tong ya, poito bai se tongo (2 Korinth 4:18; 5:7).

Thinangni langmani khatungma abo Chonghor Polni bwkhano kuthuk khe nang rwkha, aboni bagwino bo o kókno pholwi ma sakha (2 Korinth 5:1-10). Bo bini thwiblang bwsakno swngcharni nok hai khe phunuk kha. Phiya “Kaitor chuknoha-o chwngno tongnani nok rw kha” hinwi bo samani abo khe mwthangwi twlangjakmani mangni kókno bo phunuk kha (2 Korinth 5:1). Abono bebak molni poito khlainairokni khatungma.

`Jesa jwngjal phano phai si thung, phiya chwng mwthangwi twlangjaknani khatungma man kha (1 Korinth 15:52), abo tamoni bagwi aswk nangkukmani?`
