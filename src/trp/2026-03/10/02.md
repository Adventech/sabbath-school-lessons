---
title:  Sói Khe Samung Tangmani Bwthai
date:   30/08/2026
---

`2 Korinth 3:1–9 khúktang di. Bahai khe chwng Khristoni koktun wngwi man?`

Grik tei Rumni molo, sinimungni koktun wng kha belai nangkukmani. Phiyaba Chonghor Pol abo hai koktun logio twi ya. Korinthsong Phola Kwtharni bising twi slaijakmani wng kha bo sói khe samung tangmani bwthaini mari. Phiyaba Chonghor Pol sai mano, bini elem tei samung kwrak tangmani bising twi Korinth motha songchajak ya (2 Korinth 3:4–6). Bo sakno borok torjagwi phunukna muchungjak ya (2 Korinth 3:5; 1 Korinth 2:2).

Pol bini samung tangmani kókno sana bagwi kainwi swmaini kókno phunuk kha: Musini bising twi twiduljak Swmai Kwcham, tei bo bai bini yangpalsong bai twiduljak Swmai Kwtal. Dosi dophra khe khúktang khe nwng uánsugwi mano swmai Kwchamni molo mwthangjakmani khatungma kwrwi hinwi. Phiya abo kubui kók ya. Swmai Kwtal-o mwthangjaknani lama tongma hai, swmai Kwchamo’-bo tongo. Swmai Kwcham wng kha swkangni simino kothoma kaham rwjakmani se. “Kaitor buini bororokno poito khlaima bising tẃi se kaham hwnwi romnai, abo elembago swkang baira sajak kha. Hakhe elembago swkang baira’-no Abrahamno hai hwnwi Kothoma Kaham pírjak kha, ‘Nini bising tẃi Kaitor bebak dopharokno hammari rwnai’ ” (Galatia 3:8).

2 Korinth 3:1-4:6 bwtango chwng nuk kha, swmai Kwcham wng kha abo Kaitorni kók khnaui tei samung khlaiwi Kaitorno tongthok rwmani kerongni mari hai khe phunukjago. Phiyaba Swmai Kwtal-o khe chwng nuk kha, Kaitor bororokni langmao khlainani kók rwmano twiwi Kaitorni khairokma sakao kha bera sukmanino phunukmani.

Poito khlainai tei poito khlaiyarokni tyani kothoma kahamno twiwi Pol kainwi kók phirokmungni kók sakha. Pol juda juda kothoma kahamni kók samani ya. Swmai Kwcham bai Swmai Kwtalni kothoma kahamni kókno se bo sao. Phiyaba Kaitor bai rwjakmani kothoma kaham kaisa simino. “Bo [Kaitor] chwngno mwthangwi bini borok wngna bagwi ring kha, abo chini samung khlaima bai ya, bo khá chabwi khairokma bai se. O swngchar wng ya sini’-no bo chwngno Khristo Jisuni bising tẃi omo tẃi khe khairok kha” (2 Timothi 1:9).

2 Korinth 2:14-4:6 bwtango láibumani kainwi kókno abo sana naimani ya. Pol láibumani kók khurchamani wng kha, bo phunukna naikha, baksa “mwthangjaknairok” tongo tei baksa “komornairok” tongo (2 Korinth 2:15). Tamni hwn ba láibuma-o baksa borok Musini samungo poito khlai yaui yachak kha,—hakhe Musini samungno majra bernani tei butharnai hai khe nugwi mano. Phiyaba Korinthni bororok kothoma kahamno poito khlai kha. Hakhe Polni samungno kahamni Samung, tei langma rwnai phola hai khe nuk kha.

Korinthsong mwthangjakmungno sai manmani wng kha Pol samung khlaimani sói tei kubui wngmani mari.
