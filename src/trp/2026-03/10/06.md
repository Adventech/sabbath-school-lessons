---
title:  Khá Rakma Tei Tongthokma
date:   03/09/2026
---

`2 Korinth 7 berem-no khúktang di. Korinthsong khá kiphilmani khnaui Polni bwkha bubo hai wng kha?`

“Chwng norokno aswk hamjago” (2 Korinth 7:3) hinwi samani kokbwtango hamjakma bwswk se kuthuk khe phunukjak! Pol bo hamjakmani hai kheno hamjakma manna bagwi bo teibo sakha: “Norokni bwkha-o chwngno ton di” (2 Korinth 7:2).

Kubuino Korinthsong Pol tei bini tangpalsongni bagwi bóhrokni bwkhano phiyogwi rwkha. Aboni bagwino Pol tongthokjagwi sakha: “Ang norokno belai poito khlai-o, ang norokni bagwi aswk kung chugo. Chini bebak tekto-o ang khá rak súgwi tongo, ang belai kheno tongthogwi tongo” (2 Korinth 7:4). Pol khá rak sugwi tongthokma bai kuplung wng kha. Mothani bororok Khristono sak yaphar súgwi tong khe, aphuru Mothani twiphang eba bokhorokrok tei samung tangnairok le khá rak sugwi tongthokma bai kuplung wngo.

2 Korinth 7:5–16 bwtango khá rakrwma tei tongthokmano twiwi swbai sukrubwi sakha. O beremo “khà rakrwma” tei “tongthokma” — o kainwi kókno belai nangkukmani khe phunukjak kha. “Khá rakrwmano” Grik kókthai-o _parakaleō_ (verb) hwnjago, tei “khá rakrwmani” no khe Grik kókthai bai _paraklēsis_ (noun) hwnjago. O kainwi kókthai thansa khe 2 Korinth 7 berem-o uaisni nukjak kha. O koktun cheng phuruni hai kheno paithakgo mwthakjago Kaitorni khá rak rwmani kókni bising twi (2 Korinth 1:3–7). 2 Korinth 7 berem-o Pol khá ragwi tongthokjakmani wng kha bo “belai kwrak koktun” swimani bwthai se.

Tit’-ni thani kóktwma kaham manwi Pol kha tongthokjak phano, bono sói kha tongthok rwnai wng kha Kaitor se (2 Korinth 7:6). Kubuino Kaitor wng kha “bebago’-no chubanai Kaitor. ... chwng tekto man brúm brúm bo chwngno chuba-o” (2 Korinth 1:3-4).

Pol “khá ragwi” tei “tongthokma bai kuplung” wng kha (2 Korinth 7:4, 7, 13). Bo sakha kha samani koktun swimani bwthai manwi hai hwnwi sakha: “Tabuk khe ang tongthok kha, norokno khá kham rwmani bagwi ya, norok khá khamwi khá kiphilmani bagwi se. Kaitor bai se norok khá kham rwjago, hakhe norokno chwng mungsa phano hamya khlai ya. Norok Kaitor bai khá kham rwjakmani abo’-no norokno khá kiphil rwui mwthango, aboni bagwi khá hamya wngna kwrwi. Phiya ba o swngcharni bororok hai khá khammani abo borokno butharo. Norok o khá khamma bai Kaitor tamo khlai kha nai di; aboni bagwi norok bwswk khá súk kha, aboni bagwi norok sakno bwswk majra kwrwi khe phunukna nai kha! Norok bwswk jolijak kha, bwswk uanajak kha, bwswk uansuk kha, bwswk khá srang kha, samung hamyani bagwi norok bwswk akol rwna nai kha! Norok bebago’-no sakno majra kwrwi khe phunuk kha” (2 Korinth 7:10–11).
