### O Hatibarsani Swrwngjaknai
2 Korinth 3:1–9, 2 Korinth 4:7–18, 2 Korinth 5:11–15, Kolosai 1:19–23, Epis 2:13–16, 2 Korinth 6:11–7:1, 2 Korinth 7.

> <p>Muitu Bwtang</p>
> “Chwng tekto man manẃi tongo, hai phano chwng bái ya; chwng bwkha udru mudru wngo, hai phano chwng bwkha bái ya; chwng sátokjago, hai phano chwng khibijak ya; chwng belai khe bujago, hai phano chwng thwi ya. Chini bisingo Jisu tongmano sijak rwna bagwi chwng sal brúm brúm’-no chini bwsak thwiblango Jisu thwimano tẃiwi tongo” (2 Korinth 4:8–10, KOKBSI).

Thangnai hatibaro chwng nuk kha Chonghor Pol bini khá dala tei khá sutrangno phunugwi Korinthsongni hamya uánsukmani yakni yókmani. Korinthsong uánsuk kha, Chonghor Pol kók narwk ya tei bóhrokno hamjak ya hinwi. Phiyaba bo bini Pholani bwsarokni kahamni bagwi se samung tango hinwi phunuk kha. Bo 2 Korinth 2:12–17 bwtangni simi phrwngna chengwi berem 7 jora bóhrokno phrwng kha. O bwtangrogo bo phrwng kha, Khristoni bagwi sói khe samung tangnai bubo hai wngna nangnai. O kókno twiwi Chonghor Polni uánsukmungni bising twi kwbangma chwng swrwngwi mano.

O hatibaro khe 2 Korinth 3-7 beremni kókno twiwi cherwi nai lai anw. O beremo sói Khristian’-ni samungno twiwi Chonghor Pol sakha. Ellen G. White sao, “Jephuru sumsok gwnang borok khá kiphilo, tei kubuino manwi kwthar khe slaijago, abo wng kha Kaitor bono bini samungni bagwi ringmani kaisa phan gwnang sakki se. Bwkha slaijaknai borokni bwkhao Kaitorni samungni mari núkjago, abo bóhrokni langmani bising twi no phunukjago. Khristo bóhrokni bisingo tongwi chẃngsacha khatungma rwo. Omoni bwthai Kaitorni samung tangnairok o phan rwo tei khá rak rwo.”—Ellen G. White, _The Acts of the Apostles,_ p. 328.
