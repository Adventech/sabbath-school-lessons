---
title:  Kwtharwmungno Twiwi Khristoni Bagwi Samung
date:   01/09/2026
---

`2 Korinth 5:11–15 khúktang di. Bahai khe o bwtangni bising twi chwng saui man Polni samung wng kha Khristono twiwi hinwi?`

Chonghor Pol sai mano, bo samung khlaimani hisab salsa bo Khristono ma rwnai (2 Korinth 5:10). “Subraino kirinano” bo saui mano, tei Khristoni kothoma kahamno buinobo yachak rwna bagwi khá hab rwna naio (2 Korinth 5:11). Bo Subraino kirio hwnmani kókmang wng kha Subraino bórom khlaimani tei borimani se. Aboni bagwino Pol Khristono hamjakmani tei Khristo bono hamjakmao bini bwkha berammani kwrwng laijak. Swmai Kwchamni molo, Kaitorno kirimani kókmang wng kha, bini lama-o himmani, bono hamjakmani, tei bebak bwkha bai tei uansukma bai Subraiyung Kaitorno monmani (Uáinwi Saphima 10:12).

Pol samung khlaimani abo sakni bagwi ya, Khristoni bagwi se. Bo sakni bagwi kók saya. Bo kók kotor samani abo Khristoni bagwi se (2 Korinth 12:9). Bo sao, “Ang le chini Subrai Jisu Khristoni krusni bagwi simi se kók kotor sanai” (Galatia 6:14). Abo hai kheno Korinthsongbo bono twiwi kók kotor sanani seb man kha (2 Korinth 5:12). Kók wng kha bo sakni bagwi samung tang yaui Khristoni bagwi samung tangmano twiwi kók kotor saui mano hwnmani.

`2 Korinth 5:16–21; Kolosai 1:19–23 tei Epis 2:13–16 khúktang di. “Kwtha rwnani samung” hinwi Pol samani kókmang tamo?`

Khristo wng kha kwtha rwnani samung tangnai phunukmari. Aboni bagwi bo “kwtha rwnani samung chwngno ber kha” (2 Korinth 5:18). 2 Korinth 5:16-21 bwtango “kwtha rwmani” kók úaire uai sajak kha. Tamni hwn ba a kók Polni bagwi tei chini bagwibo belai kheno nangkukmani.

Kaitor bini Sajlano yapharwi butharmani bising twi borok bosongno bo bai kwtha rwkha. Kaitor bai kwthanairok khe borok kwtal wng kha (2 Korinth 5:17). Tabuk Khristoni kothoma kaham pirmani bising twi “kwtha ramani a kókno” buino sana nangnai (2 Korinth 5:19). Abo hai khe “Chwng aboni bagwi Khristoni mungwi kók sa-o, abo chini khúk tẃi Kaitor ... kók sama hai se” (2 Korinth 5:20).
