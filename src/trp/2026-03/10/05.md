---
title:  Kwthar Khe Tongnani Bagwi Ringjakma
date:   02/09/2026
---

2 Korinth 6:3-10 bwtango Pol Korinthsongno Kaitor bai kwthamano twiwi kha rak rwui kók sakha. Bo kolokma birman manmani tei kwplai wngmanino phunuk kha, tamni hwn ba khoroksa Khristono rwktharnai, tei Kaitorni samung tangnai bubo hai bo sirwna naio. Bathong rwui sana thang khe, bo tong kesebni kók sakha (2 Korinth 6:4–5), tongmungno twiwi sakha (2 Korinth 6:6), Kaitorni samung tangnani dasengni kók sakha (2 Korinth 6:7), tei Kaitorni samung tangwi uaisa tola tei uáisa saka ma wngmani kók sakha (2 Korinth 6:8-10). Korinthsongno Kaitor bai kwtha nani kók samani ulo bóhrokno kwthar khe tongnani bagwi Pol sakha. Bo sakha, poito khlai yarokni tongmung swrwng yaui tei kwthar yarok bai kaklaiwi tongnani (2 Korinth 6:14–17).

`2 Korinth 6:11–7:1 khúktang di. Kwthar langma tamono hwn?`

Pol o bwtango Motha bisingo khairokma tei hamjakmani nangkukmani kókno twiwi sakha (2 Korinth 6:11-13). Kaitor bai kwthamani mari wng kha mothao khoroksa bai tei khoroksa kwtha laiwi tongwi manmani se. Abo hai khe se bóhrok kwthar rwnaini samung tangnai wngwi mano.

Aboni ulo kaidok dagimungni bising twi kwthar khe tongnani koimung khlai kha:

1. “Poito kwrwirok bai jual thansa ta wng di” (2 Korinth 6:14)
2. “Norok bórokni bisingni ongkhorwi … phai di” (2 Korinth 6:17)
3. “Kagwi phai di” (2 Korinth 6:17)
4. “Kwthar ya bai sak ta nang di” (2 Korinth 6:17)
5. “Hakhe phai di, chwng bwsak bai bwkhani bebak kosomno sú lai anw” (2 Korinth 7:1)
6. “Kaitorno kiriui sói sói kwthar khe tong lai anw” (2 Korinth 7:1)

Omorok hai khe tongmani bising twi se kwthar langma tongwi mano, tei takjak mwtairokrok bai kaklaio hinwi Kaitor dagikha.

Abono karwi, oro-o kaisni swmaibo rwjak kha, abo wng kha Khristoni motha Kaitorni kwthar Nókthar wngwi bahai khe samung khlai abono phunugo:

1. “Ani bororok bai ang tongnai”
2. “Ang bórok bai tháng-phai khe tongnai”
3. “Ang bórokni Kaitor wngnai”
4. “Bórok ani borok wngnai”
5. “Ang norokno yachaknai”
6. “Ang norokni pha wngnai”
7. “Norok ani sajwk sajla wngnai”

Omorok naidi: 2 Korinth 6:16, 17, 18 bwtango.

Naichom jadi, 2 Korinth 6:16 bwtangni kaibrwi swmai wng kha 2 Korinth 6:17) bwtangni dagimungni kók yaphang [kókthai cheng phuru “aboni bagwi” hwnmani kókthai naidi]. Abo phunuk kha, kwthar borok wngmani abo sakni chaitokmungni bwthai ya, tamo khe Phola Kwtharni samung se. Phiya kwthar langma Kaitorni aroni phai phano, poito khlainairok sakni khlaithaino ma khlainai, tei takjak mwtai bai bebak kwthar yarokno ma khibinai.

`2 Korinth 6:16–18 bwtangni swmairok kwthar langmano twiwi chwngno tamo sa?`
