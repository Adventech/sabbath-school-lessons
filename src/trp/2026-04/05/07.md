---
title:  Ellen G. White Phunukjakmani tei Koboljakmani
date:   30/10/2026
---

Seventh-day Adventists poito khlaio, Ellen G. White Kaitorni Phola bai koboljak kha. Bini swimungrok, bini langma, tei bini samung bebakno kubui Diyari hinwi bono phunuk kha. Bo bini swimungno Elembak bai baksa hinwi saya phano Baibelni diyarirok hai kheno bo Kaitorni Phola bai koboljak kha. Ellen G. White Baibel’-no mangno tisao. Phiya Kaitor bono kok diyari phunuk kha tei Kaitorni Phola bai bo koboljak kha hinwi bo sak sak bahai khe buji kha?

(1) Baibel se kubui phunuknai tei Kaitorni kubui phunukmung hinwi Ellen G. White jephuru phano gosio. Baibelo Kaitorni kubui tei sumsok borokno bini mwthangmani chapmungno phunugo. Abo hai kheno Kaitor bororokno bini kok sana bagwi diyarirokno samungo thepao.

(2) Ellen G. White saui man kha Kaitorni phola bai koboljakmani-o Kaitor bai borokni kwrwngmung tongo. Kaitor bini kok diyarirokno rwo, phiya diyarirok abo sakni kokthai bai buino saui khna rwo. Ellen G. White sao: “Baibelni kokthairok abo Kaitor bai koboljakmani ya, phiya Baibel swinai bororok se Kaitor bai koboljago. Kaitorni phola borokni kokthaio kobol phai ya, phiya borokni sakao se kobol phaio. Phola Kwthar borokno kwbangma uansukmungrok rwo. Hakhe Kaitorni bwkha bai borokni bwkha thansa wngo, aphuru khe a borokni kokno Kaitorni kok wngo.”—Selected Messages, book 1, p. 21.

Kaitor bai phunukjakmani tei kok diyarini imang phunukjakmanino Ellen G. White bujimani khe “borokni uansukmung simi se Kaitorni Phola bai koboljago” hinwi, hakhe Kaitorni phunukmung bai borokni bujimung thansa wngo. Bo swikha, “ ‘Baibel wng kha borokni kokthaio Kaitorni kubui rwjakmani phunukjakma se, abo Kaitor bai borokno thansa khlaima se. ’ ”—Selected Messages, book 1, p. 25. Kaitorni kok sanairokni bwkhao bini kokno buji rwnani Kaitorni kwbangma lama tongo. Phunukmung, kokthai, tei uansukmungni bising twi Kaitor diyarirokno buji rwo. Baibel swinairok hai kheno Ellen G. White’-bo wng kha.

(3) Ellen G. White sakha, bini bebak simung tei samani abo bebakno Kaitorni Phola koboljagwi manmani ya. “Nangmani kok sana nangmani tongo, nangmani uansukmungrok bwkhao phaio, nangmani koktunrok swina nangma jora tongo, khoroksa tei khoroksa tangpalni thani nangmani kok ma rwmanirok tongo. A kokthairok, a kokrok Kaitorni Phola bai koboljagwi rwjakmani ya.”—Selected Messages, book 1, p. 39. O phermungno bujina khe chwng bini koktun tei diary pori kheno saui mano.

Phunukjakmani tei koboljakmanino twiwi Ellen G. White ni bujimungno sai man khe chwng kwbangma chayani katiui mano tei bini kokbitirok pori khe bujimung mano.
