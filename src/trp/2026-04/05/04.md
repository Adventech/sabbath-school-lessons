---
title:  Khuk bai Samani Kok
date:   27/10/2026
---

`Khuktang di 2 Pitor 1:18–21. Kaitorni diyarirok Kaitorni kokno khuk bai sa khe abo swijak Elembak hai kheno Kaitor bai koboljagwi rwjakmani tei poito khlaina chukjak hinwi chini tamo khatungma tongnai?`

Sumsokni swkang tei sumsokni ulobo Kaitor Adam tei Ebi bai kok sakha (Yap. 2:16, 17; Yap. 3:9–19). Bohrok kaham kheno bini kokno bujio. Sinai hathaio’-bo bororok Kaitorni khorangno sraisrai kheno bujiui mano (Lamrwma 20:1–20), Jisu baptisma naphu tei bo mul phil phurubo bororok Kaitorni khorangno sraisrai kheno bujio (Mat. 3:17, Mat. 17:5). Phiya bui chwngno kok saphai khe chwng abono chaya khe bujiui mano. Phiya Kaitor khoroksa swk bai kok sakhe aro bujiui man ya eba lama koronani mungsabo kwrwi.

Baibelo sao “Kaitor Musino hai hwnwi sa kha, ‘Ang se Subraiyung’ ” (Lamrwma 6:2, KOKBSI). Kwbangma Baibel swinairokbo omo hai khe Kaitorni khorangno khna kha. Phunukmari wng kha Jisaia (Jis. 8:1, 11), Jirimia (Jir. 1:4, 9, 11), tei Esekiel (Esek. 3:16, Esek. 6:1). Omorokno gosi ya wng khe a borok diyarirokni tongmung kubuino tei Baibelni kok kubuirokno se gosiya wngma klaio.

Borokni kok bai Kaitor diyarirokni thani kok sama hai kheno diyarirokbo buini thani kok sao. Romdi, Noa wng kha Kaitorni kubui diyari tei poito gwnang “kaham khe tong di hwnwi pírnai” khoroksa (2 Pit. 2:5). Phiyaba Noa Baibelni kaisa bijap phano swimani kwrwi; hai phano Kaitorni kokno khuk bai khe bo buino sakha, bini khukni kok abo Baibel canon-o sitokjak diyarirokni swijak bijap bai baksano Phola Kwthar bai koboljagwi Kaitorni kok samani se.

Baibel swinai wng ya phano Kaitorni kokno khuk bai buino sanai abo hai teibo diyarirok tongo, aborok wng kha Nathan, Elijah, Elisha, tei baptisma rwnai Johon.

Jisubo ha sakao samung khlaimani bini jorao, bo “yasi bai khe ha-o rik kha” (Johon 8:6, KOKBSI). Abono karwi Jisu swimani kaisa bijap phano kwrwi. Phiyaba “suar gwnang khe” bo khuk baino bororokno phrwng kha tei kok pir kha (Mat. 7:29, KOKBSI). Bini kok khnanairok baksa ma gosikha, “O borok kók sama hai khoroksa phano sa ya kho” (Johon 7:40, 46, KOKBSI). Baibel-o Jisuni kaisa swk swijak bijap kwrwi phano, bo khuk bai buino Kaitorni kok samani abo Baibel swijak bai baksano kwlaio.

`Baibel-ni bijap swi ya phano Kaitorni diyarirok tongo. Tamoni bagwi bohrokni suarbo Baibel swinairokni bai baksa?`
