---
title:  Swijak Kok
date:   28/10/2026
---

Baksa nangmani kokrok borokni langmano slaiwi rwo tei laibumano suda slaiwi mano. Phiya omo hai kokrokno swiwi narwk yak he jora bai baksa a nangmani kokrok kwmaui thangnai. Aboni bagwi swiwi narwkjak tong khe abo tei kwma ya, phiya jora bai baksa abo bwskango yapharwi twlangjaknai.

Israilrok Ijip yakarwi tei Amalekrokno mechen bai kheno Kaitor Musino sakha “Omono muituni bagwi bijap kangsa-o swi di” (Lamrwma 17:14, KOKBSI). Musi khe Baibelni puilani kaiba bijap, Lobmung 90, tei Job bijapno swikha. Bono puila Baibel swinaisa. Bini simi chengsaui Phola Kwthar le diyarirokno’-bo Baibel swinani chuba kha. Tabuk bebagwi Baibel canon-o 66 bijap tongo.

`Khuktang di Lamrwma 17:14; Johon 20:30, 31; 1 Korinth 10:11; tei Phunukjakmani 1:19. Kaitor tamoni bagwi bini diyarirokni bok ok samanirokno swiwi narwkna bagwi dagi kha?`

Baibelni juda juda bijap swijakma swinairokni baithangni swimung raida bai khe swijak kha. Phiya swimung raida juda juda phano Kaitorni kok le abo bai chaya wngma klai ya; tamni hwn ba swimungni “raida” hinwi Kaitor bai koboljakmungo kwrwi. Baibelni baksa kok belai poito khlaina chukjak tei chini langmao nangkukmani. Phiyaba Baibelni baksa kok aswk nangkukmani ya hai wng phano aborokbo Phola Kwthar bai kobolwi swijakmanino, tei nangkukmanino.

Baksa Baibel swinairok bohrok Phola Kwthar bai koboljagwi sakphang khuk bai Kaitorni kok samarokno swiwi narwgo, eba poito khlaiwi manmani bororokni kokno swiwi narwgo. Uainwi Saphima bijap-o Musi bo thwina swkang Israilrokno paithak samani kokrokno swiwi narwk kha. Kaibrwi Kothoma Kaham bijap-o khe Jisu swngcharo tong phuru Kaitorni kok samani tei phrwng langmanirokno swiwi narwkjak kha.

Phiya diyarirok Kaitor bai rwjakmani ya kokroknobo swiwi narwkma tongo (Samung 17:28, 1 Kor. 15:33, Tit 1:12) tei Soitan kok samani suda swiwi narwk kha (Yap. 3:1–5, Mat. 4:1–11).

Diyarirok buini bwkhani kok tei muchungmarokno swimani hai khe sakni bwkhani uansukmarokno’-bo swikha (Dan. 8:27; Phun. 22:8, 9). Baibel-o swrwngjaknai kahambo tongo, phiya swrwngthai kwrwi hamyabo tongo (1 Kor. 10:1–11).

Baibel pori phuru chwng muitu ma narwknai, Baibel kaham kuk kokthai bai swijak ya phano aro Kaitorni hao kwlaiwi man ya kubui kok tongo, abo mol brumno tongnai.
