### Hatibarsani Bagwi Swrwngjaknai
Hib. 1:1, 2; 2 Tim. 3:15–17; 2 Pit. 1:18–21; Lamrwma 17:14; Phun. 1:19; Esekiel 4:1–8.

> <p>Muitu Bwtang</p>
> “Omo wng kha Jisu Khristo phunukmani abo se; omo wng kha dakti’-no tamo wngno abo Kaitorni selengrokno phunukna bagwi Jisu Khristono Kaitor rwmani abo se. Khristo bini suarino bini seleng Johonni thani rohorwi abono si rw kha, hakhe Johon bo nukmano bebak sa bai kha. Bo Kaitorni aroni kóknobo sa kha, Jisu Khristo si rwmanobo sa kha” (Phun. 1:1, 2, KOKBSI).

Tabuk, Chonghor Johon wng kha Chonghor chiknwirokni bere khoroksa (Luk 8:51; Mat. 17:1; Mat. 26:36, 37). Jisu Chuknohao thangmani ulo phan obo Jerusalemo Kaitorni samung tangwi tong kha. Phiya Jerusalem bainani swkang (AD 70) bo Asia angkhorni Rumni motharokno naina bagwi Epis-o thang kha. Rumni Bugra Kotor bai Khristianrok satokjagwi tong phuru Chonghor Johon’-no khe Aegean twijlang kwcharni ha Patmos-o tunwi rwijak kha.

Patmos-o tong phuru Johon kok diyarirok rwjak kha, abo Asia angkhorni kaisni motharokni bagwi simi ya, tamo khe bebak molni Khristianrokni bagwibo rwjakmani (Phun. 1:1–3, 9–11). Kok diyari chain hai khe yaphar lai yaphar lai wng kha. Aro Johonbo khoroksa tongo; abo wng kha omo hai: Apha Kaitor khe Jisu Khristono rwkha, bo khe suarino rwkha, bo khe Chonghor Johonno rwkha, bo khe porinai tei khnanairokno rwkha. Omo hai kheno diyarirokni bising twi Kaitor bini phunukmanino bororokno sao.

O hatibaro Kaitor phunukmani tei koboljakmungni bising twi bororok bai kok samano twiwi swrwng lai anw.

_October 31 Sabat salni bagwi o hatibarsani rwngchapno swrwngwi di._
