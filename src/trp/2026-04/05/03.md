---
title:  Kaitor bai Koboljakmani Samung-berem
date:   26/10/2026
---

Baibelno karwibo kwbang bijap tei swimungrok chini thani tongo. Phiya Baibel simi se Kaitorni kok. Kubuni bijaprok bai Baibelni phermung tamo?

`Khuktang di 2 Timothi 3:15–17 tei Jakob 1:17. Oro nangkukmani kok tamo?`

2 Timothi 3:16 bwtangno kaham khe kokslaijakmani da tong abono twiwi koklam salai thani kwbangkuk Baibel kokslaijakmanino o bwtango sol laimung nukjak kha, abo wng kha Baibel Kaitor bai rwjakmani se (2 Pit. 1:20, 21).

Khoroksa Baibelni kokno cherwi sakha “Pol bebak Elembakno pai rwuino naikha, tei sakha bebak Elembakno ‘abo kubuino phrwng thani chúgo, tatalno tatal hwnwi phunuk thani chúgo, samung cha yano cha rw thani chúgo, phiya kaham khe tongnani phrwng thani chúgo’ ”—G. W. Knight III, The Pastoral Epistles: A Commentary on the Greek Text (Grand Rapids, MI: Eerdmans, 1992), p. 445.

Phiya chwng bahai khe abono bujiui mannai? Chwng saui manna nangnai Kaitor bini kokno kubui diyarirokni bising twi khe Phola Kwthar bai bohrokno kobolwi rwkha. Bohrok Kaitorni kokno rwjagwi yachaknani simi ya, tamo khe abono buini thani ma sok rwnai.

Kaitorni kok khe phunukjakmung tei bo imang phunukmani bising twi se sok phaio (Lekhamung 12:6; Jis. 6:1, 8). Kaitor mokol baithang nuknairokno samungo thepa kha (1 Johon 1:1–3), laibuma rutukna bagwi Phola khe borokno kobol kha (1 Johon 1:1–3), tei borokni bwkhao Phola Kwthar khe kobolwi samung khlai kha (2 Pit. 1:21). Phola Kwthar bai koboljagwi se diyarirok tei Baibel swinairok buino kubui kok sanani Kaitor bai rwjak kha. Baibel swinairok swi phuru sakni kokthai tei raida bai swi phano, Kaitorni kok kubui le chaya eba derama nukjak ya.

Kok diyari buino sana bagwi rwjakma abo bukhuk bai buino sama phano wngwi mano (2 Pit. 1:19–21), swijak phano wngwi mano (Johon 20:30, 31), khlaimung bai khe buino phunugwi Kaitorni kok yapharmani phano wngwi mano (Esek. 4:1–8).

`Baibelni kok buji bai ya phano bahai khe chwng Baibelno bwkha bera sugwi man?`
