title:  Jotoni Bagwi tei Judakuk Phunukmung
date:   25/10/2026
---

`Khuktang di Hibru 1:1, 2. Tamoni bagwi Kaitor chwng bai kok sana`

muchung? Jisu tei diyarirokni bising twi bo chwng tamo sakha?

Hayungno Snamnaisa se chwngno kok sana hwn khe abo tamo wngno? Tamoni bagwi bo chwng bai kok sa? Tamni hwn khe chwng sumsok kwlainai borok wng kha, aboni bagwi thwimung, tekto, tei birman mannani nang kha; phiya Kaitor chwngno kok sakha, tamni hwn khe bo chwngno saui man rwna naio tamni bagwi chwng o hao, tamni bagwi ha sakao hamya kuplung, tei bo chwngno hamjakmani-o tamo khatungma tong akorok.

Kaitor jotoni bagwi phunukmani swngcharni habaro nukjago, aboni bising twi bini paiphlai ya phan tei bini hamjagwi naikolmanino chwngno sao. Habugra Daud sakha, “Nokharok Kaitorni chẃngsachano sakhlai-o; nokha bini yakni samungrokno phunugo” (Lobmung 19:1, KOKBSI). Chonghor Pol saphikha, “Kaitor mol brúm brúm kwplai wngwi tongo, phiya bo sói’-no Kaitor, bororok o swngchar snamjak phuruni simi abono saui mano, mokol bai nuk ya phano abono kaham khe saui mano. Kaitor aswk snammani manwirokno nugwi bororok abono saui mano. Abo bai’-no bórokni búkhúk tẃi kupulung!” (Rum 1:20, KOKBSI).

Kaitor kwtal khe snam phimani tei ham rwmani bisingni kókrokno uánsuk khe abo belai uanamasingcha. “Kaitor le habarni bising twi se ham rwo. Tumungno buphang tanjak khe, eba khoroksa swk kwsa chakhe, eba bekreng bai khe, habarni bising twi abo ham rwjago. Kwsa chaya swkang se ham rwnani bithi snamjagwi paikha; bwsakni manwi hamya wng khe, bwsakni bebak manwino abono ham rwnani tei kaham khlai phinani bagwi samung khlaio.”—Ellen G. White, Education, p. 113.

Swngcharni habar khe Kaitorni phan tei hamjakmano simi phunuk ya, swngcharo hamya tongmano’-bo phunugo (Yap. 3:14–19, Mat. 13:24–30). Phiya o kokno chwng sraisrai khe buji sukna khe Kaitorni judakuk phunukmungni pohoro simi se—abo Kaitorni kok mang wngnai Jisu Khristo tei Kaitorni kokthai bono sakki rwna bagwi swijak Baibel o kainwio’-no (Johon 5:39).

Baibel wng kha bebak molo bororokni bagwi Kaitor bai koboljagwi swijakmani se. Aro swngcharo sumsok chengmani simi Kaitor swngcharno kwtal khlai phimani kok tongo. Baibelno chwng poito khlai sugwi mano, aboni bising twi Kaitor chwngno kok sao (2 Tim. 3:16; 2 Pit. 1:19–21).

`Habar saui man ya tamorokno Kaitorni kokthai chwngno sakha?`
