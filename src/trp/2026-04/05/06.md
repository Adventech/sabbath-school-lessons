---
title:  Phunukmani, Marirok, Rwngthomarok
date:   29/10/2026
---

Baibelo belai kheno rwngthoma tei phunukmarirok tongo. Romdi, Swmai Kwchamni chuchusong khlai langmani hayarok, Musini rini nokthar, Jerusalemni Nokthar akorok bebakno Khristo kruso sakno yapharmani tei Chuknohani Ochai wngwi bo samung khlaimanino phunukmanirok mang se (Hib. 8:1–6).

Daniel’-ni kok-diyarirogo swngcharni harok tei habugrarokni phanno kaisa mangthong kotorni manwi bai (Dan. 2:31–45), tei juda juda yabrwirok tei bokorongrok bai (Daniel 7 tei 8), tei khoroknwi Habugrarok bai khe phunukjak kha (Daniel 11). Abo hai sol laimung Phunukjakmani bijapo’-bo nukjak kha, Phunukjakmani 13 beremo yabrwi maknwi chwng nugwi mano.

Jisu bini kokno phrwng thani sakno mwthangnai chamung (Johon 6:35, 41–58), swngcharni pohor (Johon 8:12, Johon 9:5), tei soini angur buphang hinwi sakha (Johon 15:1–8). Maichwlwi sarmani rwngthoma (Mat. 13:1–9, 18–23) tei kwbangma rwngthomarokni bising twi (Mat. 13:24–52, etc.), Jisu bini hani tongmungno phunuk kha.

`Khuktang di Esekiel 4:1–8. Tamoni bagwi Kaitor phang phang sayaui phunukmani bising twi khe kokrokno sa?`

Esekiel 4:1–8 bwtangobo tail bai phunukmani tongo. Kaitor diyarino sakha Jerusalem kamiyungni marino rigwi phunukna bagwi, tei rokonani bagwi, puila yaksi bwkhak 390 sal ma rokonai, abo khe Israil dophano phunukmani; tei aboni ulo yagra bwkhak 40 sal ma rokonai, abo khe Juda dophano phunukmani. Tei kaisa Kaitor phunukmani khe Hoseano buitali Gomer bai kaijaknani dagimani, abo wng kha Israil dopharok pholao Kaitorni kok narwk ya wngmano se phunugo (Hos. 1:2–9).

Esekiel tei Hoseani bising twi tail bai phunukmani tabukni molni bagwi abo belaino uanamasingcha. Phiyaba abo chini bagwi simi uanamasingcha ya; bohrokni jorani bororokni bagwibo uanamasingcha. A jorao diyarirok hus khlaimano bororok khna liya (2 Bosong 36:15–21), aboni bagwi Kaitor bororokno khairokma bising twi bohrok kha kiphil thung hinwi tail bai khe phunuk phikha. Abo Kaitor hus khlaiwi rwmano bohrok jogojogo khe uansukna nang abono se phunugo.

`Lodikiano hus khlaimani phunukmungni bising twi abo chwngno tamo hus khlaiwi rwna naimani? (Phun. 3:14–18.)`
