---
title:  Swmai Kwtal’-ni Diyarirokni Samung
date:   20/10/2026
---

`Khuktang di 1 Korinth 14:3, Epis 4:11–13, tei Phunukjakmani 22:6. Swmai Kwtal’-ni diyarirokni samung tamo?`

Sakani bwtangrogo Swmai Kwtalni diyarirokni bebak samungrokno sajak ya. Phiya Swmai Kwchamni diyarirokni samung bai bohrokni samung kwbangkukno gothokmani tongo. Kok diyari sanani suarbo Swmai Kwtalni diyarirok rwjagwino tong kho.

1 Korinth 12:10 bwtango Phola rwmani suarrokni bisingo kok diyari samanibo kaisa khurchajak kha. Khristoni bwsak bai phunuk thani (1 Kor. 12:27, 28) Kaitorni phunukmungno yachaknai Chonghorrokni ulo diyarirokni kok sajak kha. “Phiya ba Kok diyari [Kaitorni kók] sanai borok bo bororokno sama klai-o, bo abo bai khá rak rw-o, khá srang rw-o tei khá kwchang rw-o” (1 Korinth 14:3, NKJV English Bible naidi).

1 Korinth 12 beremo sama hai kheno Epis 4:11–13 bwtango pholani suarrokni bisingo kok diyari samanino rwkha. Chonghorrok, pirnairok, mwrwknairok, tei phrwngnairokni bisingo diyariroknobo khurcha kha (Epis 4:11). Omorok ma tongmani wng kha “Kaitorni samung khlaina hwnwi Kaitorni bebak bororokno tiyar khlaina bagwi, phiya Khristoni bwsakno rak rwna bagwi se bo omono khlai-o. Abo baino chwng bebak’-no poito khlai thanibo, Kaitorni Sajlano saui man thanibo thansa wngnai; chwng chakra wngnai, Khristo torma lokma tẃi chwngbo torwi logwi chakra wngnai” (Epis 4:12, 13, KOKBSI). Phiya bohrokni naisikmung khe Jisu se.

Samung bijap-o Judas bai Sila khoroknwi diyarini kokno sajak kha (Phiya bohrokni bijap swijak Baibel-o kwrwi), bohrok “kók sa laima bai takhukrokno khábo rak rw kha, phanbo rw kha” (Samung 15:32, KOKBSI). Hus khlaimungni jorao diyarirok tongna nango o kok soino; phiya diyarirokni teibo samung wng kha kha rak rwma kok sanani, tei bororokno khatung kok sanani. Phiya tong halok bujiui diyarirokni samung nukjago.

Kha rak rwmani kok samanibo kok diyari samanino kwlaio (1 Kor. 14:31, NKJV English Bible naidi). Tong kesep tei digisigini jorao kha rak rwmani tei khatungma rwmani kok diyarini suarni samung nango.

Phunukjakmani 22:6 bwtango sao, Jisu wng kha “Diyarirokni thani bini Pholano rwnai Subrai Kaitor” tei bini kokrok wng kha “kubui, poitobo khlaiwi mano” (KOKBSI). Kaitorni selengrok Kaitorni kok samani le kubui tei poito khlaina chukjakno. Phiya poito khlai man ya swngcharo phano diyarirokni kokno chwng teibo bwkha bera sugwi mano.

`Sikla-sikli, chakra, chwla, bwrwi—chwng jotonino Kaitorni samungo khlaithai barja tongwi mano. Nini samung khe tamo, tei babai khe nwng a samungno teibo khlai mannai?`
