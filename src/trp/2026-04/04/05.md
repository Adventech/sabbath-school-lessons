---
title:  Bwrwi Diyarirok tei Siklirokni Pastor Wngwi Chubachuni Samung
date:   21/10/2026
---

`Khuktang di Luk 2:36–38 tei Samung 21:9. Bwrwi diyarirokno twiwi oro tamo sajak kha?`

Swmai Kwchamni molo chwla tei bwrwi Kaitorni kok sanairok tongo. Abo haino Swmai Kwtalo bwrwi diyarirok tongo. Swmai Kwtalni puila “diyarijwk” wng kha Hanna (Anna). Bo wng kha “ubas khe suri sanwi Kaitorno hor sal suri máng tongo. Bo … Kaitorno hambai rw kha, phiya Kaitorni … kókno saui khna rw kha” (Luk 2:37, 38, KOKBSI).

Samung 21:9 bwtango sao, Philipni “bwsajwksong káijk ya khorokbrwi tongo, bórok kók diyari sanai” (NKJV English Bible naidi). Swmai Kwtalni laibuma rutuknai Eusebius yagulo rutugwi sai man kha bóhrok boro-o tong tei phobjak kha. Phiya chwng sai mano, bwrwirok, phiya sikli bwrwirok sudano kok diyarini suar man kha. Phiyaba bóhrok tamono twiwi kók diyari sakha, eba bóhrokni samung bwswk Kotor abo chwng saui man suk ya. Phiya a sikli bwrwi diyarirokni kók Baibel canon-o sitokjak liya. Bóhrokni kókrok Baibel-o manthok ya phano bihrokbo kubui diyarirok se.

Bwrwi diyarirok tei sikli Pastorrok Chonghorrok bai thansa khe samung khlaio. Sikla-siklirokno Kaitorni samung tangnani phrwngnai wng kha Chonghorrok. Aboni bagwino Pol le Timothino “poito khlaima bising tẃi ani sói sói angsla” hinwi nuk kha (1 Tim. 1:2, KOKBSI; nai phidi 1 Tim. 4:12). Pastorni samungni bagwi Pol Timothino phrwng kha. Aboni bagwi bo bono sakha: “Angsla Timothi, swkang baira nini bagwi kók diyari sajakma tẃino ang nono hai hwnwi dagi-o. Poito kwrak khe khá sutrang khe kahamni bagwi choba nang laina bagwi a kókrokno da-seng hai khe tẃi di” (1 Tim. 1:18, KOKBSI).

Tit 1:4 bwtango, Pol Titno “Ang hai khe poito khlainai angsla” hinwi sinimung rwkha. Belai khe kebengmung gwnang Motha Krit’-ni poito khlainairokni bagwi twiphangrok chongnani barja Pol Tit’-no yaphar kha. Bahai khe o barjano khlainai Pol Titno kokbiti rwui kok mwthak kha: “O kókrokno phrwng di, bebak nini phan bai bororokno khábo rak rw di, khobo khorai di; khoroksa bai phano sak nang ya ta wngjak di” (Tit 2:15, KOKBSI).
