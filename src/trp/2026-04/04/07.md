Chwng swrwng kha, Kaitor nangmani jorao diyarirokno ringo tei nangkukmani samung yapharo. Diyarirokni soi samung wng kha bororokno Kaitorno saui man rwnani tei bo bai kaham snam rwnani. Omo haino Ellen G. White’-bo. Bini kainwi puilani samung wng kha: bororokno Jisuni thani tei bini kokthai Baibel-o twlangnani.

Ellen G. White bini langma-o Jisuno twiwi mangno muchungma tongo, tei buini thani Kaitorni hamjakma kokno sana muchungjago. Choba kotor chengmani simi paimani jora “Kaitor wng kha hamjakmung” kokthai tongo. Steps to Christ bini swijak bijap-o bo swikha: “Chini khairokma bisingo tornani loknani, chini tongthokmung, chini samung nangmanirok,—abo bebakno chini Khristo bai thansa wngmani bising twi simi se wngo. Sal brumno, bebak jora-o no, bo bai kwrwng mani bising twi,—bo bai thansa tong khe—hakhe chwng khairokmani-o torwi logwi mannai. Bo chini swikwrwng eba swinaisa simi hinjakya, tamo khe bo chini poitoni paithakbo wngo.”—Steps to Christ, p. 69.

1890 bisio jephuru bo The Desire of Ages bijap swiwi tongo, aphuru bini diary-o bo swikha: “Khristoni langmano twiwi ang swiwi tong phuru ang belai kuthugo kwlwk kha. Hama sochana ang pok kha. Jephuru Khristo o swngcharo chini bagwi tekto manmani kókno ang uánsugo, aphuru ang belai kha kham kha, khano bujogwi man liya.”—Manuscript 70, 1897. Ellen G. White Jisuni kok sanani belai hamjago.

Baibel wng kha Ellen G. White’-ni samungo tei kaisa kha rwmani samung. Bini swimung khe “kisa pohor” abo “kotor pohor” Baibelo bororokno twlangnai hinwi bo nuk kha (Selected Messages, book 3, p. 29). Bo jephuru phano bororokno Elembago thangnani bagwi sao; tamni hwn ba Elembago se Kaitorni hamjakma tongmung tei mwthangnani chapmung phunukjak kha. Bini swkangni samungo’-bo bo sao: “Khuktangnairokno ang sao, Kaitorni kokthai se nini poito tei samungni yaphang.”—Selected Messages, book 3, p. 30. Aboni kisa bisini ulo bo swikha: “Subai naio norok Baibel swrwng di. Bini kokthaio bo tei kwtal kokthai thepa liya. …Mangni langmani-thwi hai khe [bini kokthaino] chaui bohogo gele khe…mwnakma-o samung kaham se pohor wngwi chwngsanai.”—Letter 130, 1901.

Ellen G. White poito khlaio, Khistoni kok diyari sanairok le “Khristo phrwngmani haino ma phrwngnai. Abo bo baithang kok samani simi ya, phiya Swmai Kwchamni diyarirok tei phrwngnairokni bising twibo bo kok samani aro khobjago. Phiya borokni phrwngmung khe aro khobjak ya.” —The Desire of Ages, p. 826.

Khristianrokni bagwi Ellen G. White le Jisuno tei Elembakno se yaphango ton kha. Bwskang hatibarni Phasalo khe chwng bo phunukjakmani tei koboljakmanino (rwjakmani) kokno twiwi nai lai anw. Teibo sina bagwi visit khlai di: https://whiteestate.org/absg/4.
