---
title:  Swmai Kwtal’-ni Tatal Diyarirok
date:   22/10/2026
---

`Khuktang di Mathai 24:23, 24. Jisu oro hus khlaimani tamo? Abo tamoni bagwi nangkukmani?`

Jisu sraisrai khe sakha: “ ‘Masia phatrarok bai diyari phatrarok bacha anw. Bórok soidom bai uanamasingcha samungrok phunuk anw, wng khe ba Kaitorni chongjak bororokno suda lama koró rw anw’ ” (Mat. 24:24, KOKBSI).

“Soidom bai uanamasingcha samungrok” ni bising twi lama koro rwjaknano twiwi Jisu hus khlaiwi rwkha. Phiya soidomni samung abo Kaitorni samung simi ya. Chini pholani kena arirok tong kho. Pol hus khlai rwui sakha: “Chwng borok bai choba nang lai ya, tamo khe nokha sakani mwtai hamyarok bai, phiya o mwnakjak mol hamyani bugrarok, bokhorokrok tei swngcharni phan gwnangrok bai se choba nang lai-o” (Epis 6:12, KOKBSI). Tatal diyarirokbo omo hai phan gwnang samungrok khlaio.

Phiya tatal diyarirok le mungsabo kwtal ya. Swmai Kwtalo Swmai Kwchamni tatal diyarirokno’-bo phunugo (Luk 6:26). Swmai Kwtalni tatal diyarirok wng kha “saka saka mera hai khe” “khá bisingo le bórok thamchi kutung geguma hai se” (Mat. 7:15, KOKBSI), phiya chwngno sajak kha chwng “bórokni samung bai’-no norok bórokno sini anw” (Mat. 7:16, KOKBSI).

Soi bohrokni samung khe lama koro rwnani (Mat. 7:17–20). Jisu sakphang sakha, “ ‘Aphuru diyari phatrarok kwbángma bachaui baksa baksano lama koró rw anw’ ” (Mat. 24:11, KOKBSI), tei “ ‘wng khe ba Kaitorni chongjak bororokno suda lama koró rw anw’ ” (Mat. 24:24, KOKBSI; teibo naidi Mark 13:22, 1 Johon 4:1).

`Khuktang di Epis 6:10–18. Bahai khe chwng belokjakmungni yakni katiui mannai?`

Poito bai khe tongwi “kahamno khaklap katijaknai” hai khe naui, kubuino romwi tong khe, tei surimani bising twi Phola Kwtharni thani sakno yapharwi Kaitorni kok-o yarwng sur khe simi se belokjakmani yakni katiui mannai.

`Belokjakmung bai choba khlaina bagwi bahai khe nwng sakno sengkrak snamnai?`
