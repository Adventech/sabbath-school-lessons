---
title:  Chonghorrok tei Diyarirok
date:   19/10/2026
---

Swmai Kwtalo Chonghorrok tei diyarirokni samungni kokno sajak kha. Kaitor bai “rohorjaknairok” eba Kaitorni “kok sanairokno” Chonghor hwnjago, phiya Grik kokthai bai bohorkno apostolos hwnjago. Kothoma kaham bijap-o “Chonghor” abo khorokchinwi sagrirokno se hwnjago. O kokno Swmai Kwtalni tei bijaprokbo gosio, phiya Khristoni sagri tongnairok ya phano bohrokni kokbo oro sajago.

Pol bini koktun-o uai chinwi sakno Chonghor hinwi sakha. Swmai Kwchamni diyarirok Kaitorni kok sana bagwi ringjakma hai kheno Polbo Chonghor wngna bagwi ringjak kha hinwi Rum 1:1, 2 bwtango sakha. Buini bororokni (Gentiles) thani Chonghor hinwi Pol sakno gosio (Rum 11:13). Bo “Kaitorni morji bai Khristo Jisuni chonghor wngna hwnwi” chongjak kha (1 Kor. 1:1).

Ochairok khe bosongni bwtang sotroui buphani ulo bwsa wngo, phiya Chonghorrok khe abo hai ya; tamo khe Kaitorni morji twi se bohrok chongjago. Bui bono Chonghor hwn ya phan obo Kaitor bai ringjakmano Pol saui mano (1 Kor. 9:1, 2). Pol bini Chonghorni samungno abo Mothani nok busuni holongthai Jisuni samung khlaina bagwi se hinwi saui mano (Epis 2:20). Kaitorni phunukmungno yachaknai wngmani bagwi diyarirok hai kheno Chonghorrokbo Phola Kwtharni bising twi se wngo (Epis 3:1–5).

2 Pitor 3:1, 2 bwtango Pitor sakha swkangni diyarirokni kok belai nangkukmani wngma hai Chonghorrokni kokbo abo haino. Oro Swmai Kwcham tei Swmai Kwtal’-ni jorani kokno sajak kha. Swmai Kwchamo Diyarirok Kaitorni kok sanai wngma haino Chonghorrokbo Swmai Kwtalni molo Kaitorni kok sanairok. Phiya Chonghorrokno karwi kok diyarini suar mannai Swmai Kwtalni molo’-bo tongo. Swmai Kwtalni Chonghorrokni molo’-bo kok Diyari rwjakma thak ya. Phiya joto diyarirokno Chonghorrok ya (Samung 15:32).

`Khuktang di Epis 2:20 tei Epis 3:5. Khristo tei Mothani bagwi Chonghorrok tei Diyarirok bahai khe samung khlai?`

Swmai Kwtalni Chonghorrok Jisuno twiwi mang se swio. Jisuni langma, thwimani, thangwi bachamani, ochai wngwi bo samung khlai mani, tei bo phai phinani se bohrokni bwkha rwmani kokrok.

`Baibelo simi se Jisuni twiwi Chonghor rok tei diyarirok Samani kok tongo. Jisu tei chini sumsokni bagwi bo thwimani kokno twiwi poito khlaijaknai tamoni bagwi Baibel simi wngthai?`
