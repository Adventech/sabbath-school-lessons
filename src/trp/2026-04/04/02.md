---
title:  Jisu Wng Kha Diyari hai
date:   18/10/2026
---

Musi kok Diyari sakha “ ‘Subraiyung norokni Kaitor norokni bisingnino norokni bagwi ang hai khoroksa diyari snam anw, bini kókno se norok khna di’ ” (Uainwi Saphima 18:15, KOKBSI). A diyari le sabo? Elembag sao abo Jisu se. “ ‘Musi hai hwnwi sa kha, ‘Norokni Kaitor Subrai ano rohorma hai’-no norokno diyari khoroksa rohor anw, bo norokni borokni bisingo wngnai. Bo samani joto kókno norok ma khnathai klai-o’ ” (Samung 3:22, KOKBSI; Johon 6:14).

`Jisu Diyari hwnjakma le tamo? Jisuni jorao bahai khe o swngmungni kok phirokmung man kha? (Mat. 16:13–16, Mark 8:27–29, Luk 9:18–20).`

Swmai Kwchamni diyarirok yasi surwi Khristo eba Masia hinwi Jisuno simi se phunugo. Swmai Kwtalo khe Masiani kok Diyari sajakma Jisuni sakao se nangphai bai kha. Luk 24:13–35 bwtango Jisu sakphang sakha kok Diyari sajaknai Masia bono hinwi.

Johon 1:1–5 bwtango sajakmani a kok hwnjaknai abo borok eba borok Diyari wngwi se man ya. A Kok se snamnaisa hinwi sajak kha, “ini yak tẃino Kaitor bebak’-no snam bai kha; aswk snamjakmani bisingo bono karwi mungsa phano snamjak ya” (Johon 1:3, KOKBSI). Bono bebakno snamnaisa, tabuk bai bebak hayungni estimate 93 billion light-years hai wng khwna.

Yaphang 1 beremo “Kaitor sakha” hinwi sajakma abo Jisu kok samani se. “Pohor wng di” hinwi sanaino (Yap. 1:3) “bororokni pohor” (Johon 1:4), tei mwthangmani chapmungni bising twi (Johon 1:4, 5), bo swngcharni mwnakmano swkwma kha.

Kaitorni kok Diyari soino nang phai kha. “Tamni hwn ba a Sal kasa ya sak, phiya a aitorma norokni khá-o ka ya sak abo wng kha mwnakma-o chẃngwi tongnai chati hai” (2 Pit. 1:19, KOKBSI). “Aitorma” a Jisu phai y asak kok Diyari sumsok swngcharo mwnakma-o chẃngwi tongnai chati hai khe phai kha. Khristoni aroni se kok diyarini pohor phaio, phiya a pohor Kaitorni mol brumni hano se phunugwi twio.

A jora sok phai ya sak, Khristo bini langma, thwimung, tei thangwi bachamani bising twi chwng mol brumni mwthangjakmung yaphar kha.

`Jisu wng kha snamnaisa o kokno uansuk di (Johon 1:1–3), phiya bono nini sumsokni bagwi kruso thwinaisa. Kaitor chwngno hamjakmano twiwi omo chwngno tamo phrwng?`
