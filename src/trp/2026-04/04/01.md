### Hatibarsani Bagwi Swrwngjaknai
Mat. 16:13–16, Eps 2:20, Phun. 22:6, Luk 2:36–38, Samung 21:9, Epis 6:10–18.

> <p>Muitu Bwtang</p>
> “Phola rwmani suar podrepot tongo, tamo khe rwnaisa Phola a kaisa’-no. Kaitorni samung khlainani podrepot lama tongo, tamo khe samung khlaijaknai Subrai a kaisa’-no. Kaitorni samung khlainani phan podrepot, tamo khe jotono’-no samung khlainani phan rwnai Kaitor a kaisa’-no” (1 Korinth 12:4–6, KOKBSI).

Aphuru lama-o bupha khe malkhung chalok tongo, tei bwsla khe lamani malkhungrokno buphani malkhung bai solmani da tong

naiwi tongo. Bupha kaisa malkhungno phunugwi bwslano sakha, “Sajla, iko omo ani puilani malkhung hai!” Bohrok nogo sogwi khe bupha bini malkhungni owner’s manual sokok kha. Tei bini tabukni malkhungnibo kari kha. Bo bwlsano phunuk kha malkhung kainwini bisingo sol laimung tamo tei phermung tamo. Phiya kwbangma sol laikha, tei baksa phermungbo tong kha, tamo khe malkhungni engin le slaijakmung nuk liya.

Kaitor le Swmai Kwcham tei Swmai Kwtalni diyarirok bai kok sakha. Phiyaba bohrokno kobolnai “engine” wng kha Phola Kwthar kaisano. Phola Kwthar kok diyarini suarni bising twi kubui diyarirokno phan rwo. Hibru sama hai khe kok diyarini kokbothoma khe Khristo’-no se bo ton kha (Hibru 1:1–3).

O hatibaro Swmai Kwtalni kok diyarini suarrokno cherwi nai lai anw; tei tatal diyarirokno Chonghorrok bahai khe phunuk kha nai lai anw.

_October 24 Sabat salni bagwi o hatibarsani rwngchapno swrwngwi tiyar wng di._
