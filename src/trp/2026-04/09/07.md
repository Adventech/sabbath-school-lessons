---
title:  “Chanaiwi Naidi”
date:   27/11/2026
---

Ellen G. White wng kha bo kók diyari yachak phuruni simi tabuk jora jorabo Baibelni diyarirok haino khoroksa diyari se hinwi Seventh-day Adventists yachagwi nao. 1844 bisini December talo Ellen G. White puila Kaitor bai phunukjago, phiya baksa bororok bini kók diyari manmano twiwi swngmung khlai kha. Hakhe Sabat sal twinai Adventists bodol (yagulo bóhrokno Seventh-day Adventists) Ellen G. White ni kók diyari manmano chajakma phunukma lai kha.

(1) Bóhrok sakha, mol páithak jora Kaitor le kók diyarino rwnai. Bóhrok sakha, tabukni kwtal molobo kók diyarini samung nang kho hinwi Baibel sao. Joel 2, tei Phunukjakmani 12:17, tei kubuni bwtangrogo sajakma hai, “mol páithak” jora kók diyari núkjaknai, tei Kaitorni mol páithakni bororokni bagwi abo kaisa “sinimungni mari” wngnai. (Gerhard Pfandl, “The Gift of Prophecy and God’s Remnant Church,” in The Gift of Prophecy, chapter 4.)

(2) Puilani Adventists bodolrok naichom kha, Ellen G. White ni kók diyari yapharmung poito khlainairokni sakao kaham bwthai tubu kha. Rom di, 1840s ni paithak bisio jephuru bororok Sabbath luku kuthum pandao Baibel Doctrine no twiwi kók totra rwlaiwi tongo bini kók diyarini samungni bising twi se bóhrok thansa wng laiwi mano. Bini kók diyari le “bwthai kaham” rwkha, tei Seventh-day Adventists poito khlainairokni thani bini swimung kaham bwthai rwkha.

(3) Ellen G. White no kubui hinwi chajakma phunuknani tei kaisa lama wng kha bo Kaitor bai phunukjakmani abo uanamasingcha samung nukjak kha. Ellen G. White bororok saui man ya abo hai kókrokno sakha, abo thinango kubuino phunukjak kha, bo Kaitor bai phunukjak phuru borokni phan wng ya abo hai soidomni phan bo man kha, tei bo sakphangbo sai man ya buphuru tei bwswk jora kolok Kaitor bai phunukjaknai, omorokno bo kubui diyari hinwi sakki rwo.

Tabukbo Seventh-day Adventists o sakkirokno nugwi Ellen G. White ni kók diyarino yachago. Ellen G. White swi phuru tamo wng kha abórokno bo Kaitor bai rwjakmani “sakki.” Lobmung swinai Daud samani hai: “Chánaiwi nai di Subraiyung bwswk se kaham” (Lob. 34:8, KOKBSI). Surimani bising twi Ellen G. White ni swimungno pori khe bo diyari se hinwi nwngbo saui mannai..

Chengnani bagwi bini swijak chikonsa bijap Steps to Christ poridi. Aboni ulo bini kaiba berem gwnang Conflict of the Ages poridi. O beremo Yaphangni simi Phunukjakmani jora Chobayung kotorni kokbothomano twiwi swijak kha. Bini swijak kókrok bwkango, tei electronic version-o manthogo (EGWWritings.org) tei bini swijakrok kwbangma kók bai kókslaijak kha.
