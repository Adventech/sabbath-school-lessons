---
title:  Khristo Borok Wng Phaimani
date:   24/11/2026
---

1 Johon 4:1–3 bwtango sao, kubui diyari hwn khe Jisu borok wngwi phaikha hinwi de gosi kha bono ma amjoknai. Tamni hwn ba kwbangma kotor kotor tongthar bodolo sakno diyari gosinairok tongo, tei tongtharni hodarok tongo bohrok Jisuno borok wngwi phaikha hinwi gosi ya. “Jisu Khristono borok wngwi Kaitorni aroni phai kha hwnwi gosi ya borok bo Kaitorni aroni … ya” (1 Johon 4:3, KOKBSI).

Johon o kok swi phuru bo tatal phrwngnairokni kokno muitu khlai kha, tamni hwn ba tatal phrwngnairok phrwngo, Jisu borok ya, phiya borok hai khe nukjak phaio hinwi. O amjokmung teibo kuthugo se thango. Jisu ha sakao borok wngwi phaikha o kokno diyari poito ma khlainai hinwi simi oro sajakmani ya. Johon bini molo abo hai kaisa jwngjalno twiwi choba khlai ma tongmani; tamo khe Johonni kok diyari le phainai bosongrokni bagwibo nangkukmani.

`Jisuni kokno twiwi phunukjak bwtangrok chwngno tamo sa? Johon 3:16; 2 Kor. 5:21; Johon 11:25; Hib. 4:14, 15; Johon 14:1–3.`

Kubui diyari hwn khe Jisu khe Kaitor borok wngmani tei soi borok wng phai kha hinwi poito khlaina nangnai. Jisuni samung, bo sumsok kwrwi wngmani, bo sakno yapharwi thwimani, thangwi bachamani, Chuknohao bo ochaini samung khlaimani, tei bo tei uaisa phai phinani—Jisuni kok, eba o kubui kokrokno kubui diyarirok le saui manna nangnai. Jisu ha sakao phaikha o kokni slaibo omorokno sinani nangsugo. Diyarirokni phrwngmungo Jisuno twiwi se wngna nangnai. Diyarini kok le bororokni Jisuni mwthangma halogo twlangna nangnai. Phola Kwthar bai koboljagwi kok diyari rwjakmani abo “aitorma” a Jisu chini bwkhao kasa phai ya sak chini bisingni mwnakmano hakchal khlaiwi tongo. Kok diyari le chwngno Jisu bai thansa khlaio, tei bini phaisingo mang twlango.

Diyarirok Jisuno se tisao, bohrok sakno tisa ya eba sak baithang borom mannani nai ya (Samung 4:12). Bohrok je bororokni thani rohorjago, bohrokni thani mannani nai yaui bohrokno se naina-nuknani tiyar tongo. Phiya diyarirokbo chwng haino sumsok gwnang borok ba bohrokbo kothoma kahamno nango.

`Jisu wng kha Kaitor wng twtwi phano chini sumsokni lakaini bagwi thwi kha. Tamni bagwi omo chini potion yaphang hinwi bujinani nangkukmani? O phrwngmung krwi khe chwng tamo kwmajaknai?`
