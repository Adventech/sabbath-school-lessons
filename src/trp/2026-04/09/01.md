### Hatibarsani Bagwi Swrwngjaknai
Dan. 10:7–19; Uai. 13:1–5; Jis. 8:20; Johon 11:25; Samung 15:36–39; Jir. 28:9, 15–17.

> <p>Muitu Bwtang</p>
> “Khájani takhukrok, bebak pholano’-no poito ta khlai di, tamo khe pholarok abo Kaitorni aroni da potaliui nai di; tamni hwn ba diyari phatra kwbángma swngchar gwnangwi ongkhor kha” (1 Johon 4:1, KOKBSI).

Au, ha sakao soi diamond le belaino morok. Phiyaba diamond no kosol kubuni manwi wng khe abo diamon hai morok wng glak. Hakhe bahai khe chwng tatal bai soi diamond no sininai?

Soi kubuino sininani lama khe amjogwi nainani se. Baksa manwi khe chwng nogo’-no amojogwi eba chanaiwi nai mano. Baksa khe nogo manthok ya manwi bai amjokna nango. Abo hai kheno diamond no chwng belai chanaimani ulo se kubuino siniui mannai.

Diamond wng kha ha sakani belai phan gwnang kaisa manwi. Diamond phan tongmani kaisa karon wng kha belai tungmanino soinani bini phan tongo. Diamond no amjoknani hwn khe nwng hor bai abono sogwi twi kwchango drop ma khiklainai. Soi diamond hwn khe abo hamya wng glak, phiya tatal diamond wng khe abo hamya wngwi thangnai!

Kok diyari rwjakmani abo diamond ni slaibo morok kuk, phiya kok diyarino man kha hinwi kebo ahaino gosiui man ya. Abo hai gosinai borok bo amjokjakna nangnai. O hatibaro khe Elembakni raidani bising twi Baibel’-ni diyarirokno chwng amjogwi nai lai anw.

_November 28, Sabat salni bagwi o hatibarsani rwngchapno swrwngwi tiyar wng di._
