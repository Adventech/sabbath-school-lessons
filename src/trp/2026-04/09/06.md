---
title:  Kok Diyari Nang Phaima
date:   26/11/2026
---

Bororok diyari hwn khe thinangni kók sanai se hinwi uánsugo. Phiyaba diyarini samung aboni slái teibo kwbang. Diyarirok bororokno Kaitorni kókthaini bwkhak kiphilwi tubu-o, tei bororokno kókbiti rwo, hus khlai rwo, tei kha rak rwo.

`Thinangni kókno twiwi bororokno saui man rwmani tei hus wngnani chubamanino karwibo, diyarirok tamoni kók diyari sa? Jir. 28:9, 15–17.`

Diyarirokno amjoknani tei kaisa lama wng kha bóhrokni kók diyari samani nada nang amjokmani bising twi. Kubui diyari kók diyari sakhe abo nang phaio: “ ‘Tumungno khoroksa swk diyari Subraiyungni mungwi kók sa khe, tamo khe a kók nang ya hwn khe tei kubui wng ya khe, hakhe a kókthái Subraiyung sama ya; a diyari khá chongwi sakni kók samani se, norok aboni bagwi bono kirithai kwrwi’ ” (Uai. 18:22, KOKBSI). Phiyaba o amjokmung khlainani kisa kók tugwi mano; tamni hwn ba tong halokno rwksaui se abo nang phaio. Tumungno je kók diyari sajakmani naharmung supungjak khe a kók diyari samani nang ya phano wngwi mano.

Phunukmari naidi, Jona Ninebini bagwi kók diyari sakha: “ ‘Nokha salbrwi chini ulo Ninobi sokomorjaknai!’ ” (Jon. 3:4, NKJV). Phiyaba bini kók diyari samani nang phai liya. Tamoni bagwi?

`Khuktang di Jirimia 18:7–10. Tong halokni kók diyarino twiwi o bwtango tamo sajak kha?`

Tong halokni kók diyari sajakmani khe bororok Kaitorni kókno bubo hai khe yachaknai abono naiwi se a kók diyari samung khlainai. Tumungno bororok tongmung slai khe, a kók diyari sajakma nang phai glak. Ninebini bororok Jonani kók diyari khnaui bwkha slai lai kha, aboni bagwi Kaitor bóhrokno sokomor liya (Jona 3:10). Phiyaba ulni bororok a kók diyarini hus khlaimungno pokjak lai kha, hakhe Nineni tongmung hamya bai kuplung wng phikha, aphuru khe Kaitor bóhrokno sokomor kha. Kaitor le thinangno saui mano, phiya chwng khe saui man ya. Chwng tamono chongwi nanai abo se chini thinango bwthai mannai.
