---
title:  Bwthai Kaham
date:   25/11/2026
---

Hathai sakao Jisu phrwng phuru (Mathai 5–7), Khristian Motha songchajakmani hani kok sakha. Aboni ulo khe bo diyari phatrarokno twiwi hus khe rwui sakha, “ ‘Hakhe aboni bagwino norokbo diyari phatrarokno bórokni samung [bwthai] bai’-no sini anw’ ” (Mat. 7:20, KOKBSI).

Bwthai eba samung bai chwng chanaiwi mano. Diyarirokni tongmung nai khe tei bohrokni kokni bwthai tamo nai kheno chwng sai mano a diyari bororokno Kaitorni thani de tlangwi tong eba Kaitor bai de hakchal khlai rwui tong.

`Khuktang di Jona 1:1–3, Samung 15:36–39, tei Galatia 2:11–14. Diyarirokni kokno twiwi o bwtangrogo chwng tamo phrwng?`

Diyari hwn kheno chaya khlai ya eba sumsok khlai ya wngnai a kok ya. Jona, Pol, tei Pitor songbo chaya khlaio. Kaitor simi se majra kwrwi; phiya bini kok sanairok jotono chaya khlainai mangno. Diyarirokbo chwng hai kebel borok ja se. Phiyaba bóhrokni langma bebak phaisingono Kaitorni bwkhak wngna nangnai. Diyarirok bóhrok kók samani hai khe tong ya wngthai kwrwi.

Kebo diyari phano Jisu hai wng sugwi man ya, phiyaba kubui diyari khe Kaitorni hani tongmung chamung bai khe tongo (Mathai 7:16-20). Kebo sakno Kaitorni diyari hinwi gosiui, phiya bo kók sama hai khe bini langmao tong ya khe bono poito khlaithai wng ya.

Diyarirokni samung belai kutuk wng phano, kwbangma diyarirok bóhrokni samungo “bwthai kaham” núkjago. Rom di, Musi Israilrokno kaham kheno hasing panthoro twlangwi man kha (Uáinwi Saphima 34:10-12). Haggai kók khnanai Judairokno bisi chichuku bathagwi tongmani Nóktharno tei uáisa tisanani bagwi kha rak rwkha (Haggai 1:12). Jisaia khorokbrwi Judiani Habugrarokni bagwi soi khe samung khlai kha, tei bóhrokno Masia phainanino phunuk kha (Jisaia 38:5, 6; Jisaia 53:4-6). Chwng pogwi man ya Daniel bwkha bai bini chaya gosimani (Daniel 9:15) eba Jirimia muktwi kuplung khe kapmani (Jir. 9:1). Kaitorni diyarirok kwbangma jorao sátokjago, hamya hwnjago, haiphano bóhrok kubui hamjakma phunugo. Omorokno wng kha bwthai kaham rwnai diyarirokni samungni tongmung.

`Kaitorni bororokni tongmung eba chaya wngmani samungrok nono uai bwswk bwkha swbai kha? Bóhrokni langmani bising twi tamo swrwngwi man?`
