---
title:  Baibel bai Gothokmung
date:   23/11/2026
---

Chwng bororok se sakni kokno sak baithangno kebengnai wngwi mano, tei sakno phemloknai wngwi mano, phiya Kaitor le tatal ya (Lekhamung 23:19). Kaitor diyarirokni bising twi rwmani kok diyarirok le bo kubui wngmano se phunugo. Kaitorni kok diyario bini slaijak ya kubui eba kok narwkmung le nukjago.

`Khuktang di Jisaia 8:20. Oro tamo nangmani kok tong?`

Mungsa swk gosimungno phano Baibel bai simi se gothokmung naina nango. Diyarirokno’-bo amjoknani hwn khe bohrok kok samani Baibel samani bai de gothokjak naina nango. Kubui diyari hwn khe bini kok Baibelni kubuirokno kebeng glak. Aboni bagwino Kaitorno poito khlainairok tatal phrwngnai tei diyari phatrarok bai lama koro rwjak ya twi khe tongna khe Elembakno Sinani nangsugo.

Swmai Kwcham tei Swmai Kwtalni molo diyarirok kok sakhe bohrokni swkangni diyarirokni swimungrokno se bohrok phunugo, abo hai khe bohrokni diyarini samung le swkangni diyarirokni samung bai gothokjak hinwi phunugo. Ulni phainai Baibelni diyarirok Kaitorni chapmung tei mwthangmani lamano se teibo bororokno saui man rwo; bohrok Kaitor rwmani kerong tei raidarokno swbai ya.

`Khuktang di 2 Pitor 1:19–21, Rum 15:4, tei Jakob 1:17. Kaitor agini simi kok diyari rwui phaimano twiwi o bwtangrogo tamo phrwng?`

Swmai Kwchamni jorao mwthangjaknani chapmungni kokno bujina bagwi naijaknai yaphang wng kha Kaitor puila kok diyari rwmani Yaphang 3:15 bwtango. Ratharmung barokmungni raida wng kha a chapmungni sampili eba mari. Tamo khe Kaitorni mera bwsa Jisu Khristo phainai, tong phainai, tei bororokni bagwi thwi phainai. Bwskangni kok diyarino abono teibo srai srai khe phunukjak kha. Kaitorni bororok o kokno twrwk twrwk buji man khe Kaitor teibo bini kubuirokno phunugo. Aboni bagwi kwtal bosongrokni thani kubuino bujinani teibo kuthuk wngo. Abono “bwskangni kubui” eba “twrwk twrwk khe phunukjakma kubui” hwnjago. Swkangni bororokni slai ulni bosongrok kubuino teibo kuthuk khe bujiui mano. Aboni bagwi swkang bai ulni kok kuburokni bisingo kebengmung kwrwi.
