---
title:  Soidomni Samung Nukjakmung
date:   22/11/2026
---

Lamrwma (Exodus) ni jorao kok diyari rwmungno twiwi Kaitor bini bororokno sakha, “ ‘Ani kókno khna di, ‘Norokni bisingo khoroksa swk diyari tong khe ang Subraiyung baithang bo bai nukjagwi sinijaknai ang bono imango kók sanai’ ” (Lekhamung. 12:6, KOKBSI).

Kok diyarini kok manmao tei kok diyarini bagwi ringjakmao soidomni samungbo nukjago; abo bororok uansuk man ya soidom nukjakmani se. Diyari hwn khe baithangno Kaitor bai kok saui mano, phiya uaisa uisu khe imang eba Kaitor bai phunukjakmani bising twi kok diyari mano (Dan. 8:1, Jis. 1:1, Samung 9:10), phiya abo hai jorao diyarirokni bwsago mari nukjago.

`Daniel Kaitor bai phunukjak phuru bini bwsago tamo tamo mari nukjak kha list khlai di. Dan. 10:7–19.`

Daniel Kaitor bai phunukjak phuru bini bwsago phan kwrwi wng kha. A jorao bo Kaitorni sakao simi bwkha beraui ma tongo. Phiya Danielni logio tongnai bororok khe Daniel phunukjakmanino nuk ya, tei Kaitor Danielni bwskango phai phuru aswk phan gwnang khe nukjakmani bagwi Danielni ganani bororok kiriui huijaknani khar lai kha. Phiyaba Kaitor diyarini bwsakno samungo thepao bo bini kok diyarirokno nwrwgwi manna twi khe sinani bagwi.

`Kok diyarini rwmungno twiwi Uainwi Saphima 13:1–5 bwtango chwngno tamo phrwng?`

Imang eba vision nukmani jorao bwsago soidomni samung nukjak kheno abo Kaitor bai phunukjakmani wng ya kho. Soitanbo soidomni samung khlaiwi mano. Bwsakni marino karwibo diyarino sinina bagwi amjokthai tong kho. Soidomni samung phunukjakma simi bai kubui kok diyarino siniui man ya. Kubui diyarino sinina hwn khe Baibelni bebak amjokmung point bai khe chanaiwi naina nangnai. Laibumao nai khe diyari phatrarok mang se kuplung (Mat. 7:15).

`Baksa bororokni thani khe soidomni samung se belai nangkukmani khlai laio. Phiya soidomni samung bebakno de Kaitorni aroni phai o kokno twiwi tamoni bagwi chwng hus wngthai?`
