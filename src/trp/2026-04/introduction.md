### Kok Diyarini Suar

Neuroscientists bujiui mannani cheng kha, chwrai bwsarok kók (language) swrwngmani phan tongmani abo uanamasingcha. Umor bisi dok phuru se chwrairok 7,000 ni slái kwbang English kókthai swrwngwi mano.

Bóhrok tei kaisa kwtal kók khna khe abo suda dakti kheno swrwngwi mano. Omo hai jorao chwrairokni bokhorogo judakuk slaijakmung wngo. Abo hai jorao bohrok achai phuru 800 kókthai swk khnamani bisingo 40 kókthai khorang swk chongwi naui mano. Phiyaba aphuru chwrairok bai kók sanani nangnai. Kok khnamani bising twi se chwrairok kok swrwngo.

Kaitor chwng bai kok se saya hwn khe tamoni bagwi aswk kók swrwngna kwrwng khe chwngno snam kha ba? Swngmung belai nangmani—phiya Baibel sao, “Agi Kaitor chini chuchusongno diyarirokni bising tẃi kwbángma’-no podrepot khe kók sa kha, phiya tabukni o mol páinanogo bo bini Sajlani bising tẃi se chwngno kók sa si-o. Kaitor a Sajlani yak tẃi’-no bebak swngcharno snamo, mol pái phuru bebak’-no bini yago rwna bagwi Kaitor bono’-no chong kha” (Hib. 1:1, 2, KOKBSI).

Sumsok jephuru Adam tei Ebino Kaitor bai kók salaiwi man ya khlai kha, aphuru phano Kaitor le bini yak suwi borokno komormungo yakar kwlang ya. Bo phaiwi bóhrok khoronwino rutuk phai kha, tei Adamno swng phai kha, “ ‘Nwng buro wng kha?’ ” hinwi (Yaphang 3:9, KOKBSI). Sumsokni simi swngcharno mwthangnaisa phainani kók diyari bo bóhrokno rwkha (Yaphang 3:15). Hakhe aphuru Eden’-ni simino Jisu jora kók diyari sajagwi sumsok gwnangrokno Jisuni bwkhak simi kaisa susuk khatungma phunukjak kha.

O hatibaro Kaitor sumsok gwnang bororok Kaitor bai malaiwi man ya phano Kaitor bini muchungma tei lama phunugwi kók samani kók diyarini suarno twiwi nai lai anw. Phola rwmani suarrokni bisingo judakuk suarni kók chwng manmani beremrok wng kha 1 Korinth 12, 1 Korinth 14, Epis 4, tei Rum 12. Phola Kwthar wng kha podrepot suar rwnaisa, “bo muchung tẃi khoroksa khoroksano podrepot suar rw-o” (1 Korinth 12:11, KOKBSI).

Kwbangma mol romwi pholani suar rwkha Kaitorni wngwi kók sanairokno, bóhrok wng kha sumsok habnani swkango Eden-p tong phuruni hai khe Kaitor borok bai halok snam phinani muchungma kókno sao. Kaitor bahai khe diyarirokno ring, tei bahai khe chwng diyarirokno chánaiwi nainai swrwng lai anw. Tei chwng nai lai anw Swmai Kwchamni diyarirok bai swmai Kwtal-ni diyarirokni bisingo gothokmungrokno. Baksa diyari khe kók sao, baksa khe swio, phiya baksa khe o kainwino khlaio. Chwng bóhrokni kókrokno nainai, tei diyarirokni kokni bising twi hamari manmarokno nainai.

Kók diyarini suar rwmao Kaitorni kaiba naharmung tongo: (1) sumsok gwnang bororokno bono phunukna bagwi, tei bini kubui bai bini muchungmano phunukna bagwi; (2) nangkukmani jorao tei patimungni jorao kókbiti rwna bagwi; (3) Khristoni bwsak Mothani samung khlainani bagwi; (4) Jisuno rwktharnairokni pholani kha rak rwna bagwi; (5) sumsok gwnangrokno sicha rwui kha kiphil rwnani ringna bagwi. Phiya “kók diyarini suar” wng kha Kaitorni mol páithakni tongrwknai Mothani kaisa mari se (Phun. 12:17; Phun. 19:10, English Baibel naidi).

Kaitor bini bororokno kaham kwrwng khe tongthai-o tlang manna twi khe mol páithakni bagwi Kaitor kók diyarini suar rwkha (Joel 2:28-31). Seventh-day Adventists poito khlai-o Ellen G. White, Adventist mothano songchanai tangpal, bo kók diyarini suar man kha hinwi.
