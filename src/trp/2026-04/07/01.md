### Hatibarsani Bagwi Swrwngjaknai
Lekhamung 13:26–30; Jir. 36:20–26; 2 Bosong 36:15, 16; Mat. 23:30–35; 1 Kor. 10:6–12.

> <p>Muitu Bwtang</p>
> “Buino phunukna bagwi se omorok bórokni sago klai-o, hakhe chwngno hus khlaiwi rwna bagwi se omorok swijago. Tamni hwn ba o mol pái twtwi-o se chwng tong lai-o” (1 Korinth 10:11, KOKBSI).

Sana kwlaio, tabukni molo sakni simani, uánsukma, nangmani, eba jesa phano wng thung, abo hai “bóhrokni kubui kókno”

sana bagwi seb kaham rwjago. Kók khe bebak “kubuino” mwthagwi” eba siring khe tonina kók ya. Phiya Jisu Khristo kubui bai bini kókthai ni bisingo gothok ya tumungno wng khe bubo hai wngno?

Baksa borok baibelni kubuino yachagwi bóhrokni samung hamyani kiphilo; phiya baksa bóhrokni tatalno romwi tongo. Baksa khe kubuino manwi tongmung slaijago; baksa khe kubuino kebengwi mang tongo. Abono bororokni láibumani kaisa tongmung.

Baibelni diyarirok Kaitorni kók sana bagwi ringjak phano bororok bóhrokni kók khnana nai ya. Aboni bagwi bororok diyarirokno majra ber laio, butharna naio, phiya baksano khe butharwibo paikha (Hibru 11:32-38). Phiyaba Kaitorni diyarirok Kaitorni kókno sauino thango, tamni hwnba abo bóhrokni thani khlaithai se (option ya). Abo se Kaitorni bororokno mwthangnai kók. Diyarirok jesa phano wng thung, tamo khe Kaitorni kókno sana se nangnai.

_November 14, Sabat salni bagwi o hatibarsani rwngchapno swrwngwi tiyar wng di._
