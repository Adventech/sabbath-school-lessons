---
title:  Kaitorni Kók Sanaino Jolima
date:   08/11/2026
---

Swamini hano rutugwi hasing panthor twi bororokno twlang phuru Kaitorni chongjak twiphang Musi le tongthok tei kosto bebakno saui man kha.

`Khúktang di Lekhamung 14:4–11. Bororok tamoni bagwi Musi tei Aronno chajak ya?`

Israilsarok Kaitorno jolijak kha; tamni hwn ba bóhrok bono bwkha bera ya. Musi bai Aron khe Kaitorni twithermung khlainai twiphang wngma bagwi lukurok bóhrokno belai jolijago. Bororokni thani bahan-bwthwini nangmung, tei bwkhani muchungmani nangmung belai kheno doro. Jephuru chini mungsa swk kaham wng ya hai ma tong khe aphuru jolimani phaio.

Khúktang di Lekhamung 13:26–30. Israilrok tamoni aswk kirijak? Kaleb bahai khe bóhrokno kha rak rwkha?

Swmaini hao sogwina swkang Israilrokni langmao kwbangma jwngjal phai kha, abono bóhrok kiri laimani, “phiyaba jephuru bororok Kaitorno poito khlai ya wngo, phuru bóhrokni bwkha Soitanno yapharwi rwo, abo bóhrokno bwswk hakchalo twlangnai kebo siya.”—Ellen G. White, Patriarchs and Prophets, p. 389. Musi twiphangni samung khlai thani belai jwngjal man kha. Bororok jephuru jwngjalrok manjak khe, bóhrok Aron bai Musino jolijago, tei Kaitorno kebeng laio. Bororok Kaitorni chongjak diyari tei twiphangno sláiwi kwtal twiphang rutuk lai kheno (Uáinwi Saphima 34:10–12, Lekhamung 14:4), Josua bai Kaleb bóhrokni rino chir kha, tei bóhrok kiriui tong yaui Kaitorno bwkha beranani kók sakha: “ ‘Subraiyung chwngno kubui’-no hamjak khe chwngno o ha-o tubunai tei dut bai piya bwtwi yorjak hano chwngno yapharnai. Norok Subraiyungno ta kebeng lai si di’ ” (Lekhamung 14:8, 9, KOKBSI).

`Kaham jorao Kaitorno chwng poito khlaio, hai ya da? Hamya jorao bono poito khlaiwi da mano?`
