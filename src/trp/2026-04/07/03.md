date:   09/11/2026

`Khúktang di Jirimia 36:20–26. Kók diyarini hus khlaimungno dophani bokhorokrok bahai khe naikha?`

Bwskangni birman sok phainani swkango Judani dopharokni thani Jirimia wng kha Kaitorni kók diyari sanai. Bisi kwbangma romwi Jirimiani bising twi Kaitor Judani chayarok bono bwkha hamya khlaimano twiwi hus khlaiwi phai kha, tei saui man rwkha Babilon bóhrokno mechenwi bini yago bóhrokno chobwi nanai hinwi (Jirimia 6:8-19, Jirimia 26). Kaitor bóhrokno mwthangnani bagwi kók sao, phiya bóhrok bini kók khnana nangnai. Judani sumsok belai kheno kwbang, phang phang, tei slaijak ya. Jirimiani jorao takjak mwtaino khulummani, chwrai bwsarokno barokmani, buitali-buitala wngmani, tei hamya samung khlaimani abórok; haiphano diyarini hus khlaimung tei koimungno khna liya. Jirimia diyarini samung khlaimani paithak wng rwrwk, Kaitor teibo bini kók khnaya bwsarokno koimung khlai rwrwk. Kaitor bóhrokno mwthangna bagwi se aswk chaitok kha. Phunukmarini bagwi Jirimia 32 beremo, Kaitor Jirimiano sakha, kaisa ha paiwi, narwgwi tonnani, “ ‘Tamni hwn khe sengkrakrokni Subraiyung Israilni Kaitor hai hwnwi sa-o: ha-o haino hai nokrok, hapharrok, angur bwlwngrok pai phirwi man anw’ ” (Jirimia 32:15, KOKBSI).

Kaitor hamjakmani bagwi Jirimiani bising twi kha kiphilnani tei tongmung kwtal wngnani páithakni koimung swi rwkha. Phiyaba Jihoiakim khe a kókno bórom rwliya, tei Kaitorno khibi kha, bini kók tei diyarinobo khibi kha.

`Khúktang di Jirimia 36:27–31. Jihoiakim’-ni chaya samungno Kaitor bahai khlai kha?`

Chwng uánsugo, Kaitorni kók eba diyarirokno sokomor kheno a kók kwmaui thangnai hinwi. Phiya diyarirokni bising twi rwjakmani Kaitorni kók kwmaui thang ya. Kaitorni kók khnaya wng khe chini nukhungo se teibo birman tubuwi phaima klaio; tamni hwn ba abo Kaitorno kebengmani klaio. Habugra Jihoiakim manjakmani abo diyari Jirimiani bagwi buino hus khlaiwi rwnani kók wng kha (Jirimia 36:30, 31): chini sakao hamya sok phaina bagwi se chwng Kaitorni hus khlaimungrokno khnaya wngo. Sumsok tei chaya khlai khe abono chwng ma ranai.
