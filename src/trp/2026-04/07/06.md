---
title:  Chwngno Hus Khlai Rwna Bagwi
date:   12/11/2026
---

Swmai kwchamni bebak patimungrok kaham tei hamya bebakno kaham naharmungni bagwi se rwjak kha: Kaitorno kebeng khe tamo wngnai chwng swrwngwi manna twi khe abórok rwjak kha.

`Khuktang di 1 Korinth 10:6–12. Kaitor tamoni bagwi diyarirokno Baibel swi rwna bagwi bini Phola bai bóhrokno kobol kha hinwi Pol sakha?`

O bwtango laijak jora bai tabukni jorani bisingo kwrwng laimungno Pol phunuk kha—kwcham Israil bai mothano thansa khe phunuk kha. Jisuno Subrai tei Mwthangnaisa hinwi chwng gosiui yachak khe, bóhrokno Mothani borok wng kha (Gal. 6:14–16).

Pol sraisrai khe sakha, Kaitor bai borok bosongni kwcham laibuma abo kaham eba hamya jesa phano wng thung, abo nangkukmani hinwi uánsukthai ya. Tamo khe Kaitor abono aswk nangkukmani wngjak kha, tamni hwn ba a kók swijakmarok chwngno phunukna bagwi se “Bórok muchung tẃi chwngbo samung hamya khlaina ta muchung thung” hinwi (1 Korinth 10:6, KOKBSI). Kók wng kha laijak jorao wngmanirok thinango wng phinani chengsamung se. Laijak jorao wngmanirok wngtong joraobo núkjago, phiya thinangobo wngnai..

Tamo khe Kaitor saui tongo tumungno chini thani hus khlaina bagwi Elembak tong khe, tei Kaitorni diyarirokni bising twi chini thani kók diyari tong khe chwng laijak jorani hai khe kha kiphil yaui chaya khlai ya wngna nangnai. Tumungno chwng diyarirokni kókno khna khe, swrwng khe, tei bóhrokni kókno poito chakhe, hakhe chwng songchajaknai tei chini hamchanai (2 Bosong 20:20). Pholani hamchamung abo wngwi mano nok kaham tongmani, malkhung tongmani, ri chum twi kubuni manwi khwnwi tongmani akorok. Kaitor chini nangmani manwirokno rwnai hinwi sao, phiya chini bwkha tei chwngno sak kaham wng thung hinwibo bo naio (3 Johon 2). Phiya Khristian langmao pholani kuplung wng sukna khe Kaitorni kókno khnana nangnai.

Kaitorni Kók le magwna wng ya. Bo kók sakhe bini kókthai le mol brúmno tong phlainai. Bini kókthaini phan paiwi thang ya (Jis. 40:8). Bini kókthairok chwngno hus khlai rwna bagwi se rwjago, “ Tamni hwn ba o mol pái twtwi-o se chwng tong lai-o” (1 Korinth 10:11, KOKBSI). Chwngbo laijak jorani hai wng khe chini sakao’-bo bóhrok manjakma hai wng glak diba?

`Baibelni láibuma-o Kaitorno kebengnai tei poito khlai yarokno naikhe, chwng bahai khe bóhrok hai wng yaui tongnani swrwngmung man?`
