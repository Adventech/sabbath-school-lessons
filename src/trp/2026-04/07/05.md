---
title:  Phatra!
date:   11/11/2026
---

Bui khlaimani hai nwng sakno Tumungno ang wng khe ang khlai glak khamun hwn khwna! Tamo khe chwng saksakno koinene wngwi man khe chwng bui hai khlai yaui tongwi mano, phiya jora jorao khe chwng bui haino chaya khlaio, si twtwino chwng jesa phano khlaio. Omo haino Jisu hai hwnwi sakha: “ ‘O phatra, nini mokolni buphang bokocho tikhlai gra di, hakhe nini takhukni mokolni buphang biphiring kaham khe nuk anw; aphuru nwng tikhlai man anw’ ” (Mat. 7:5, KOKBSI).

`Khuktang di Mathai 23:30–35. Oro tamo nang kukmani tei kha khammani kokno Jisu sakha? Bini kokthaio tamo hus khlaimung tong?`

Jisu Pharisairokni tongmungno chajak ya; tamni hwn ba bóhrok sakni tongmung slaina nai ya wngwi teibo kirimasingcha hamya wngwi phaio. Pharisairokni tongtharno Jisu belai naithokjak liya. Ri lolang jilik-jalak kan laijak thumnokni hodarokno Jisu kisa phano mokol gothokjak liya—tamni hwn ba bóhrok chwndwi phuljak holongdapni mangkhor hai, saka nai khe kaham, bisingo khe bekreng kurung karang (Mathai 23: 27, 28). Bóhrokni sumsok phunugwi rwjak phano bóhrok sakni sumsokno gosimabo kwrwi, khibimabo kwrwi. Phiya bóhrok kha kiphilwi mwthangjak thung hinwi Jisu úaire uai bóhrokno seb kaham rwkha.

Aboni bagwino Jisu omo hai bororokno “Phatra!” hwn kha. Omo hai khe bo sakha: “ ‘Kerong phrwngnairok bai Pharisairok, phatrarok! Norok bwswk se hamya sitra no!’ ” (Mat. 23:14, 15, 23, 25, 27).

Bóhrok wng kha kok sama juda tei khlaima juda. Diyarirokno’-bo bóhrok abo haino khlaio. Rom di, bóhrok uánsugo, diyarirokni mangkhorno naithok khe sajok kha, bóhrok poito khlaimano bórom rwma klaio hinwi. Aile diyarirok bóhrokno kha kiphilna kók samano khe bóhrok gosi ya. Bóhrok diyarirok hai phan manna muchungjago, phiya bóhrokni kokno khe khnaya. Kaitorni seleng tei diyarirokni mangkhorno naithok khe sajokma bising twi bóhrok Kaitorni seleng tei diyarirokno buthar ya kho hinwi sakno phunukna naio. Phiyaba a jorao bóhrok diyarini slaibo Kotor Masiano se butharnani chap laiwi tongo.

`Saka saka simi tongthar twinai hai, phiya bisingo khe hamya, abo hai wng ya twi khe bahai khe chwng tongnai?`
