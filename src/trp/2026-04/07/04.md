---
title:  "Kaitorni Kókno Sokomormani: Bwkhak 2"
date:   10/11/2026
---

Juda juda lamani bising twi bororok kók diyarini suarno tamo khlai naidi. Musini phuru khe, Swmaini hao sogwina phuru kena arirok bai ma nang lai kheno, kena arirokno kirima bagwi Israilsarok Musi bai Aronno kebeng laio. Kwbangma bisi ulo, Kaitorni kók diyarino sokomorna bagwi Habugra Jihoiakim Kaitorni kókno sók kha.

Phiyaba abórok khakjak patimung ya. Kwcham Israil tei Judani láibuma-o, diyarirokno khibijakmani patimung úaire uai núkjago.

`Khúktang di 2 Bosong 36:15, 16. Kaitor bini diyarirokni bising twi bini kók rohormarokno Judani dopharok tamo khlai kha? Judani tongmungno Kaitor tamo khlai kha?`

Jirimia diyarini samung khlaimani khe Josia, Jehoahaj, Jihoiakim, Jihoiakin, tei Jedekia habugra chamani mol jora. Aboni ulo Judani samo sok phai kha Nebukadnejarni ha Babilon. Jedekia nokha bisi 21 phuruni simi habugra chána cheng phuruni simi kheno Jirimiani kók diyari sama nang phaikha (2 Bosong 36:11). Elembago sao, “Bo Subraiyung bini Kaitorni mokolo samung hamya khlai kha, bo Subraiyungni kók sanai diyari Jirimiani bwskango bini sakno koinene wng li ya” (2 Bosong 36:12, KOKBSI).

Jirimiani kók diyarino bahai khe sajak kha naidi. Bo kók samani abo Kaitorni bukhukni kók se hwnjago. Phiyaba Jedekia diyarini bwskango bini sakno koinene wng li ya. Jirimia chwng hai sumsok gwnang borok wng phano Kaitorni borok wngwi kók sanai wng kha.

Kwbangma diyarirokni bisingo Jirimiabo khoroksa. Saisai biri romwi Kaitor bini bororokni thani diyari rohoro (Lam. 3:15, Lob. 105:26, Mal. 3:1, Jis. 6:8, Jir. 25:4, Mat. 11:13, Luk 11:49), tamni hwn ba Kaitor le “khoroksa phano komor thung hwnwi bo nai ya, bebakno’-no bórokni sumsok yakar thung hwnwi se bo nai-o” (2 Pitor 3:9, KOKBSI). Kwcham Israil tei Judani láibuma wng kha lama phernai bororokno Kaitor mwthangnani chaitokmani, tumungno bóhrok kha kiphil khe, bini mwthangmani swmai bóhrokni thani sogwi khamun.

Phiyaba Judani láibumani paithago Jirimia diyarini khorangni bising twi Kaitor Judano khairokmani, chubamani, tei khakchangmani kók rwkha, tamo khe Juda Kaitorni kókno koinene khe, poito khlaiwi, tei kha kiphilwi yachak liya; phiya Kaitorni kókno mwnwi kha, thesra kari kha, tei khibi kha.

`Bóhrok Kaitorni kók sanairokno mwnai kkha, Kaitorni kókno khibi kha, tei diyarirokno thesra kari kha. Chwng le bubo hai wngnai?`
