---
title:  Bini Kókno Yachakmani tei Buino Samani
date:   13/11/2026
---

Arthur White wng kha Ellen G. White ni busuk, tei Ellen G. White Estate ni kwcham dagiphang, bo uánsugwi man kha bini bwchwi Kaitor bai rasa hai (vision) phunukjak kha tei kók diyarini imang phunukjak kha. Baksa bini kók diyarini phunukjakmung swrapsani bagwi, baksa khe jora kolok romwi, phiya baksa khe lukuni bwskango. Baksa bini phunukjakmungno poito chaya wngnairok bono amjogwibo nai lai kha.

Baibelni molni diyarirok hai kheno Ellen G. White phunukjakmani gothokmung núkjago. Ellen G. White Kaitor bai phunukjak phuru bubo hai wngwi tong 1868 bisio James White saui khna rwkha. Bo swikha, Ellen G. White Kaitor bai phunukjak phuru “bini yaksi yagra tamo wngwi tong bo saui man ya”; bo hama naya; bini bwsakrok “raksaui thango” tei bini “jura ragwi thango”; phiya bini bwsak “ahaino kwlaijak” tamo khe bini mokol khe phiyokjak. Kaitor bai phunukjakmani ulo bo swrapsani bagwi mokol nugwi man ya wngo, haiphano bini mokol mungsa hamya wng ya. Bo bwswk jora kolok tei buphuru Kaitor bai phunukjaknai kebo sai man ya; Ellen G. White sakphangbo o kókno siya. (James White, Life Incidents, pp. 271–274.)

Baibelni molni hai kheno baksa ba o kókrokno yachak kha; phiya baksa ba yachak liya. Ellen G. White hai hwnwi sakha: “Kaitor ano bini bororokno naina bagwi chong kha; tei ... O kók hilik barja ano rwjakma hai khe, o kók yachakma hai kheno, je bororokni thani o kók rwjak kha, bini bagwi abo barja wngnai. O muchung ya samungno ang chongwi namani ya. O samung ang bororok bai kaham hwnjakna bagwi eba lobjakna bagwi ya. Ano Mwthangnaisani mung tei phan bai khe ang manmani samungno simi se khlainai. Ang hus khlaio, kókbiti rwo, tei kha rak rwui sao, Kaitorni phola rwmanino bororok khna thung eba khnaya wng thung, yachak thung eba yachak ya wng thung. Ani khlaithai sakno kha kochom rwnani ya; tamo khe ano o samung rwnai Chúknohani Aphani muchungmano khlainani se.”—Testimonies for the Church, vol. 4, pp. 231, 232.

Ellen G. White ni langmano naiwi, phiya theological naimung wng yaui kwbangma borok Ellen G. White ni kók diyarino yachak ya wng kha. Ellen G. White bini kók diyarino yachak ya wngnai bororokno belai khairokjago. Abo hai bororokno twiwi bo sraisrai khe sakha, “bohrok bóhrokni soi thaino rutugwi man ya sak eba aboni bwkhak haiya khe kebengo bachaya sak bóhrok bai kha logwi hamjagwi tongthai.”—Testimonies for the Church, vol. 1, p. 328.

Ellen G. White sai mano bini kók diyari rwjakmani soi sakki wng kha bini thani Kaitor bini kókthairok rwmani. Tumungno bororok koinene khe kubuino rutuk khe bini kók diyarirokno kubui tei soi hinwi saui mannai. Kaitor bóhrokno kaham bujimungo tubunai hinwi bo Kaitorni sakao poito khlaio, tei “bohrokni poitoni kubuio tubunani bagwi Kaitor nangmani jora sakki rwmano Kaitor bóhrokno phunuknai.”—Testimonies for the Church, vol. 4, p. 231.
