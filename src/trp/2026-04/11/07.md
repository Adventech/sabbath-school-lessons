date:   11/12/2026
---

Kaitor bini diyarirokni bising twi mángno borok bai kók sao. Laibumani nangkukmani jorao abo hai teibo wng kugo. Hakhe mol paithago Kaitor bini bororok bai kók sanai, o kók kubui ya da?

October 22, 1844 bisio Millerite Adventists bodolrok jephuru “kha baimung kotor” man kha, aphuru Kaitor Ellen Harmon’-no (White) diyari wngna bagwi ring kha, tei kha baijak poito khlainairokni bagwi kók sanani bagwi bini kók rwkha. Ellen G. White puila Kaitor bai phunukjakmani wng kha kha baijakrokno kha rwnani se.

Tei kaisa nangkukmani jora wng kha Adventists bijap karimani samung chengmani, tei Sabat sal twinai Adventists motha bodol official khe songchajakmani. Sabat twinai bodolrok bijap kariui tei kók pirmani bising twi 1860s ni molo saisai borokno man kha. Suari khorokthamni kok kaham khe pirjaknani bagwi bóhrok bodol snamna nang kha (Phun. 14:6-12). Baksa bodol snamnani chajak ya phano Kaitor le bodol snamnani yapri senai bagwi Ellen G. White’-no samungo thepa kha. May 21, 1863 salmario Seventh-day Adventists motha officially achai kha.

A bisio’-no poito khlainairokni bisingo sak kaham tongnani kók sana bagwi Kaitor kók diyarino rwkha. Kwbang kuk Seventh-day Adventists sak kaham tongnani sinai kwrwi eba sak kaham wng ya tongmani. 1863 tei 1865 bisio uáinwi sak kaham tongnani kók Ellen G. White Kaitor bai phunukjakmani bising twi Seventh-day Adventists motha-o sak kaham tongnani kók songchajak kha.

1870s ni simi 1890s ni molo Ellen G. White Adventist Educational System snam kha. Education’-no twiwi bini uánsukmung Education, practical, tei spiritual phaising twi develove wng kha. Bo sao, “Kubui swrwngmung abo mungsa swk lekhporani course khlai baimani ya. Abo o langmao tiyar wngmani slaibo bwskango tongo. Abo borok kwthang tong sak tiyar wngmani se. Abo bahan-bwthwio, uánsukmungo, tei pholao bwskang thangmani se.”—Education, p. 13.

1880s molo khe Mwthangjakmani kókno twiwi Seventh-day Adventists mothao theology snamjak kha. Adventists rok kerongni kok phrwngmao mwthangjakmani khe poito khlaiwi Kaitorni khairokma bai se kaham hinwi romjago hinwi bo sakha. Motha hayung gwnangwi teibo kaham khe kok pirwi manna twi khe 1901 ni simi 1903 jorao motha bodolni tangraidarokno snamnani bo teibo chuba kha.

Ellen G. White kwrwi khe chwng tini Seventh-day Adventists denomination wngwi man glak khamun. Bo wng kha nangkukmani jorao Kaitorni kók sanai diyarijwk.
