---
title:  Israilrokni Buma eba Diyarijwk
date:   07/12/2026
---

Kaitor Israilrokno ha Ijipni phiyokmani ulo bóhrokno Kanan hano rwkha. Tumungno bóhrok bini kerong tei dagimung twiwi tong khe hamari kwbang rwnai hinwi bo bóhrokno swmai rwkha. Phiya bo bai swmai tangmanino bóhrok swbai khe birman tei deramung mannai hinwibo bo bóhrokno hus khlaiwi rwkha (Uai. 27-30). Josua thwimani ulo kheno Israilrok a swmaino narwk lai liya. Hakhe bóhrok bóhrokni arini bororok bai satokjak khe, aphuru khe bóhrok Kaitorni thani kap lai kha. Phiya kók phirokmung man kha, “Subraiyung buj thumnairokno bacha rwui bórokni sekpronairokni yakni mwthang kha” (Buj. 2:16, KOKBSI). Israilrok Kaitor bai swmai tangmano kwtal khe songchaui mannani tei bóhrokno satoknairokni slái phan gwnang wngnani twi khe bóhrokno kha rak rwnani bagwi Kaitor diyarirokno rohor kha.

`Khúktang di Buj thumnairok 4:4–16 tei Buj thumnairok 5:1–5. Israilrokni bagwi Debora tamo khlai kha?`

Israil, tei dophani bokhorok Barak’-ni thani Kaitorni kók twlangnani bagwi Debora nangkukmani diyarini samungno khlai kha. Bo Barakno sengrakrokno thansa khlainani bagwi sakha. Choba chengsanani maribo Barakno rwkha. Debora sakha, “Tháng di, tini’-no Subraiyung Siserano nini yago yaphar kha. Hakhe nini bwskang bwskang Subraiyung thang glak diba?” (Buj. 4:14, English Bible naidi).

Debora wng kha diyarijwk. Israilrokni buj thumnai wngwi Debora belai nangkukmani. Bo twidulmani tei Kaitor kwplai khlainai hwnmani kokno bo poito khlaimani abo kena arirok bai choba khlai thani Israilrokno phan rwkha. Chobani bokhorok wngwi Barak rwngmani-simani, tei Israilni sengrakrokno twithernani bini muchungma abórok belai nangkukmani, phiya aswk bai simi bóhrok choba kwplai wng ya.

Diyarijwk Debora bai Chobani bokhorok Barakni kothomao nangkukmani swrwngjaknai wng kha—bóhrok choba kwplaini tongthok laimani bisingo phano Israilrokni phan gwnang khoroknwi—buji thumnai, bai kwplai chobani twiphang—o khoroknwi borok Kaitorno chẃngsacha khlainani bagwi dopharokno twi kha.

Debora diyarini samung khlaimani le birman tei sátokjakmani jorao phano Kaitor kók narwkmano se sakki rwo. Barakno sana bagwi Kaitor Deborano kók rwphuru chobani sengkrakrokno tiyar khlaiwi de mano eba man ya bo Kaitorno swngmung khlai liya. Israilrok kebel twtwi kwplai wngwi de mano abono twiwibo swngmung khlai liya. Tamo khe bo poito khlaimani bising twi Barakni thani Kaitorni kókno sakha, tei Barakno udru-mudru wng yaui Kaitorno bwkha bernani kha rak rwkha.
