---
title:  Rwkhlaijakmungo Khatungma
date:   09/12/2026
---

“Subraiyung bórokni chuchusongni Kaitor bini tẃitunrokno thak phlai ya khe bórokni thani khoraina rohoro, tamni hwn ba bo bini bororokno tei bini noktharno belai khe khairogo” (2 Bos. 36:15, KOKBSI). Elijani ulo Kaitor bini bororokno khakchangma, hus khlaima, tei phrwngmung rwna bagwi kwbangma diyarirokno rohor kha. Phiyaba Israil tei Judani harok belai tongmung hamya le Sodom tei Gomra bai surchajak kha (Jis. 1:9, 10). Kaitorni bororok kungchuknai, khamainai, tei takjak mwtaino khulumnai wng kha. Chubamung sannai samung tangnai, randijwk, mapha kwrwi, tei buini hani bororokno bóhrok satok kha. (Israilrokni chayano Kaitor tamo sakha Jisaia 58 beremo naidi). Páithago khe Kaitorni buj thummani Israilrok sakao sok phai kha, hakhe bóhrok Assyriarokni yago yapharjak kha (2 Habugra 17:1-6) tei Judani khe Babilon hao rwkhlaijak kha (2 Habugra 24, 25).

Kaitor bini bororokno rwkhlai rohorwi yagulo su phano bóhrokno yakarwi rwya. Bóhrokni thinangni khatungma rwna bagwi Kaitor le bóhrokno Esekiel tei Daniel hai diyarirok rohorwi tongo.

`Khúktang di Jisaia 59:1–4 tei Nehemia 9:30. Mol brúmni bagwi wngwi thangnai abo hai tamo kók o bwtango sajak kha?`

Israil tei Judani láibuma-o bóhrok sumsok khlaimani manthai manwi birman manmani chwng nugo. Kaitor bai hakchal wngwi bóhrok Kaitorni naikolmung bai kak laio, hakhe Kaitor bai buj thumjago. Satokmani tei takjak mwtaino khulummanino Kaitor naiwi tong man ya. Phiyaba bororok puila sumsok khlai phuru le Kaitor bóhrokno rwkhlai ya. Bóhrok kha kiphilwi bini bwkhak phai thung hinwi Kaitor diyarirokni bising twi samung khlaiwi bóhrokno koimung khlaio. Phiyaba bororok bini kók khna ya. Sal bwswk Kaitor le bini bororokno sumsokni kiphilnani naiwi tongnai. Sirisitini simino kwbangma diyarini bising twi Kaitor le bini bororokno hus khlaiwi máng rwi tongo.

Jisubo sakha: “ ‘O Jerusalem, Jerusalem, diyarirokno nwng butharo, nini thani Kaitor bai rohorjaknairokno holong khitaro! Tokma bini bwkrang tola toksarokno koroui tonma hai ang bwswk se nini borokno yak kwbagwi tonna nai kha; nwng abo hai khlai rw li ya’ ” (Mat. 23:37, KOKBSI).

Kaitor bini bororokno rwkhlai phano khatungma kwrwi khe bóhrokno yakarwi rwya. Tamo khe Kaitor bóhrokno swmai rwkha: “ ‘Jakobni bororok sumsokni bwkha kiphilnairokni bagwi bo Siyono Phiyóknaisa wngwi phainai’ ” (Jis. 59:20, KOKBSI).

`Chwng je pohor (kubui) rwjakmarokni kókno uánsuk di. Chwng rwjakmanino tamo khlaina bagwi chini barja wng kha?`
