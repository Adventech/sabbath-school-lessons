---
title:  Kha Kiphilnani Bagwi Ringjakmung
date:   08/12/2026
---

`Khuktang di Buj. 2:16–19. Oro tamo wngmani nukjak kha?`

Habugra nangjakmani tei Israilrok Habugra san laimani (1 Samuel 8) bising twi buj thumnairokni jora paikha. Phiyaba a jora slaijakmani ulo Habugrano, dophani twiphangrokno, tei bororokno kókbiti rwnani, samung kaham khlai rwnani, tei chayano hwnnani bagwi teibo se diyarini samung nang kuk sio.

Habugra Ahabni molo Kaitor bini buj thummani kók sana bagwi Elijano rohor kha; A ha watwi kwlai ya wng sinai, tamni hwn khe aphuru Israilrok “Subraiyungni dagimarokno khna yaui Bal mwtaino khulumo” (1 Habugra 18:18, KOKBSI). Phiya Ahab kha kiphil yaui Elijano se butharnani rutuk kha, phiya Kaitor bono mwthang kha (1 Habugra 18:10-12). Asari nangmani bisikthamni molo khe Elija Ahabni thani thangwi watwi kwrwini bono majra berwi kha (1 Habugra 18:17)! Aboni ulo khe Elijani Kaitor bai Bal mwtaini diyarirokni bisingo batai lainani bagwi bebak Israilrokno kwrwngsa khe thum kha (1 Habugra 18:19).

`Elija Israilrokno sakhlaimani kók tei Israilrok kók phirokmung rwmano twiwi 1 Habugra 18:21, 38, 39 bwtangrok khúktang di. Tamo wng kha?`

Chúknohani simi hor tiklaimani Kaitorni a samung tei Elija kók diyari samani Kaitorni bororokni khao belai nang kha, tei bóhrokno kha kiphil rwkha. Israilrok hai khe chwngbo Kaitor se Snamnaisa tei Mwthangnaisa hinwi saui man ya abo bororok bai tongwi ma tong laio. Buino chwng Kaitorni kók saya karwi, chwngbo Israilrok hai kheno Kaitorni ringmano khna yaui sakni muchungma twi tong laio.

Sakni muchungma twi tong khe chwng baikhrubnai, birman mannai, tei páithago khe ma thwinai hinwi Kaitor hus khe rwkha. Phiya chwng Kaitorni khorangno rwkthar khe “Subraiyung thak yaui nono lama soui tẃinai tei kaham nangma manwi bai nono bwkha kochom rwnai, abo bai nini bekreng raknai; tei nwng twi kochomjak ha hai wngnai, twimuk gwnang twisa hai twi ran phlai ya hai wngnai” (Jisaia 58:11, KOKBSI).
