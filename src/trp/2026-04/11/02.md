---
title:  Deramungno Twiwi Hus Khlaimaung
date:   06/12/2026
---

Kaitor le bororokno mwthangma muchungjago. Sumsok hab phaimani ulo phano bo Adom tei Ebi bai halok manjunani chaitogwi tongo, tei swrapsani bagwi ha sakani bororok “Subraiyungni mungno” ring kha (Yaphang 4:26, KOKBSI). Jora lai rwrwk kheno Kaitorno hamjaknai bororok kisa rwrwk kha. Phiya Kaitorno rwktharwi tongnairok khe bini kahamni kók buino sao, tei bóhrokni arini bororokni sumsokno bóhrok hamjak ya, tei sumsokni manthaini kóknobo twiwibo buino hus khlaiwi rwo.

Noani molo ha sakani bororok belai hamya wngma bagwi Kaitor bóhrokno sokomornani uánsuk kha (Yaphang 6:5-7). Phiya kaham khe tong di hwnwi pírnai wng kha Noa (2 Pitor 2:5). Bo kha udru mudru kwrwi kheno bini kók khnanairokno bwskangni salo swngchar sokomorjaknani kókno twiwi pir kha. Bini hus khlaimungno khnaui kha kiphilnairok mwthangjakna twi khe rung snamnani bagwibo Kaitor Noano dagi kha.

`Khuktang di Hibru 11:7, 2 Pitor 2:5, tei Yaphang 6:9. Bahai khe Noa le “kaham khe tong di hinwi kók pirnai” wng kha?`

Twi tormani abo láibumani kaisa nangkukmani patimung. Aboni bagwi o kók Swmai Kwcham tei Swmai kwtal o kainwi-o’-no úaire uai sajakmani tongo. Abo bororokni sumsok bangmani tei Kaitorni uanamasingcha khairokma bangmani phunukmari se. Kaitor sumsokno kebengwi buj thummani tei mwthangmungno yachaknairokno bo mwthangmani-o bini kaham le phunukjak kha. “Yaksi yagra sumsok kuplung wngwi tongmani jorao Methusela, Noa, tei teibo borok kahamrok Kaitor se kubui hinwi sinimung narwknani bagwi twi hamyani kobolmung bai hakchal tongnani bagwi belai samung khlai kha. Twi tornani nokha bisi 120 swkango Kaitor bini chapmungno kwthar suarini bising twi se Noano sakhlai kha, tei rung tangnani dagi kha. Rung snamwi tongmani jorao borok hamyarok ha sakao twi tormani bai khe sokomorjaknai hinwi Noa kók pir kha. Bini kókno poito khlaiwi, kha kiphilwi tei tongmung sláijagwi sakno tiyar wngnairok khe khairokjaknai tei mwthangjaknai.”—Ellen G. White, Patriarchs and Prophets, p. 92.

Kaitor Noano twi kotorno twiwi saui man rwkha tei rung bisingni bororokno mwthang kha aswk simi ya; bo Noa tei bebak langma gwnangrok bai kaisa swmai songcha kha. Bo tei twi bai swngcharno sokomor liya hinwi swmai rang kha (Yaphang 9:8-17). Chokhreng núkjakma-o mwthangmani a swmai, tei khatungmano se phunugo.
