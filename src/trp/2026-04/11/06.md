---
title:  Naisingma Jorani Bagwi Diyari
date:   10/12/2026
---

`Khúktang di Phun. 1:1-3. Johon tamo khlaina bagwi ringjak kha?`

Puilani Khristianrok Jisu tei uáisa phai phinani swmaino khatungwi tong laio. Jisuno chumuio khe Chúknoha-o kasaui thangmano nukma hai kheno bo kiphil phinai hinwi Suari samanino Chonghor Johon khna kha (Samung 1:10, 11). Puilani Mothani kaisa jwngjal nukjak kha. Abo khe ih, Jisu sakha bo kiphilwi phainai. Phiya buphuru?

Twi kwcharni ha Patmos-o tong phuru Jisu tei uáisa phai phinani swmaini kókno Johon swikha: “Nai di, bo chumui bai khe phainai! Joto borok bono nuknai, bono yórnairokbo nuknai” (Phun. 1:7, KOKBSI). Mol páithakni kókrokno’-bo Johon kwbangmano phunukjak kha. Motha kaisnino kha rak rwma tei khoraimano karwibo (Phun. 2, 3) Johon teibo phunukjakmanirok wng kha, buj thummani (Phun. 14:6-12), mol páithakni kók (Phun. 20), Jisu tei uáisa phai phinani (Phun. 14:14-16), tei swngchar kwtal snamjakmani (Phun. 21, 22).

Johon sakno “Jisu bai thansa wngma bising tẃi ang norok bai baksa sátokjak kha, ang norok bai baksa bini hani borok, ang norok bai baksa soi kha” hinwi sinimung rwkha (Phun. 1:9, KOKBSI). Bo saui mano, naisingwi tongmani simi kha khammani ya, tamo khe Kaitorni kók pir khe tei Jisu Khristo sirwmani twiwi tong khebo sátokjakna ma nangnai, tei sak soina nangnai.

Phunukjakmani bijap-o Johon bo phunukjakmarokno naiwi tei swiwi narwina nang kha. Páithago bo swikha, “Ang Johon omorokno bebakno nubo nuk kha, khwbo khna kha” (Phun. 22:8, KOKBSI). Johon wng kha mol páithakni bagwi kók diyarini sakki rwnai, tei bo nukmanirokno bini molni motharokno saui khna rwkha (Phun. 22:16). Laibumani kaisa nangkukmani jorao Jisu puila hao phainani kókno pirnai baptisma rwnai Johonni kóknobo bo muitu khlaiwi rwkha (Johon 1:6-8).

Johon hai khe Jisuni kók buino sana bagwi chwngnobo sajak kha. Chini thanibo Jisu sirwmani tongna nangnai (Phun. 19:10)—a Jisu sirwmani wng kha diyarirokni bising twi bo kók rwmani se. Johon hai khe chwng Kaitor bai phunukjak ya phano sak soiwi Jisu tei uáisa phai phinani kókno chwng buino saui mano, tei chini langmao bini sakki rwui mano, tei mwthangjaknani mariroknobo chwng naiwi mano.
