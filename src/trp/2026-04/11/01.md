### Hatibarsani Bagwi Swrwngjaknai
2 Pit. 2:5; Buj. 4:4–16; 1 Habugra 18:21, 38, 39; Jis. 59:1–4; Neh. 9:30; Phun. 1:1–3.

> <p>Muitu Bwtang</p>
> “Kubui’-no, Subraiyung Kaitor bini diyarirokni thani bini chongmarokno phunuk yaui mungsabo khlai ya” (Amos 3:7, KOKBSI).

Sana thang khe, o ha sakao sumsok hab phuruni simi bororok Jisuni bisingo mwthangjakmani lamgmano hamjak yaui swrapsani bagwi sumsokni tongthokmungno se hamjak si-o. Hakhe o kha

khamjaknai kók kubuino Kaitor sio, haiphano bo chwngno hamjago, tei chwngno mwthangnani lama khlaiwi samung khlai tongo.

Aboni bagwino láibumani nangkukmani jorao khe Kaitor bini bororokni thani kók diyarini bising twi Samung khlaio. Phiyaba bini samungno úaire uai khibijak khe, bini buj thummungno twiwi bo hus khlaiwi rwo.

Kaitor mungsa swk khlainani swkang bini diyarirokni bising twi jephuru phano bini samungni chapmungno phunugo (Amos 3:7). Tumungno bororok kha kiphil khe, bo daktino bóhrokno nakaro.

Tabukni molo’-bo abo hai kheno Kaitor chini thani kók saui tongo. Agini molo Kaitor bini bororokni thani kók sama hai kheno mol páithago’-bo Kaitor bini bororokni thani kók sanai.

O hatibaro chwng swrwng anw Kaitorni mwthangmani láibuma-o nangkukmani jorani kókrokno, tamni hwn ba nangkukmani jorao Kaitor bini bororokno sraisrai khe saui man rwnani bagwi diyarirokno samungo thepa kha. Adventist Movement’-no hamcha rwnani bagwi nangkukmani jorao Ellen G. White nangkukmani samung khlaimanobo chwng nai lai anw.

_December 12 Sabat salni bagwi o hatibarsani rwngchapno swrwngwi tiyar wng di._
