---
title:  Kók Diyari Baibelno se Soi Hinwi Phunugo
date:   21/12/2026
---

Baibel wng kha kók diyarini bising twi Phola Kwthar rwmani se. 2 Pitor 1:21 bwtango sao: “Kók diyari tháisa phano borokni muchung tẃi sajakma ya, tamo khe borok Phola Kwthar bai koboljagwi se Kaitorni aroni kók sa-o” (KOKBSI).

`Khúktang di 2 Timothi 3:16, 17. Elembakni phan (authority) tei abo Phola Kwthar bai rwjakmano twiwi oro tamo sakha?`

Baibel kwrwi khe bororok hamya tei kirimasingcha swngcharo lama korojago yakarwi rwjakma hai se, páithago khe abo komorna nangnai. Tabukni molo Baibel bebak thaio’-no manthok kha. Phiyaba joto jorao omo hai ya. Jisuni molo Swmai Kwcham doljak bwchap simi porinani bagwi manthogo, tei Ochairok eba phrwngnairok simi se abo porinani seb mano. Ahaino lukurokni thani Baibel manthok ya. A molo Kaitorni kók le belai morok.

`Khúktang di Luk 24:27. Jisu thangwi bachamani ulo tamo khlaimani bising twi khoroknwi sagri bini judakuk naharmungno bujiui man kha?`

Jisu se kotor kuk diyari—bono a kók (Johon 1:1-3)—Elembakno chẃngsacha khlainai (Johon 7:37). Kók diyarini suarni bisingo morok kuk hamarirok khe Baibelno kubui hinwi phunukmani se.

“Elembak Kwthar abo Kaitorni khorang se hinwi yachaknairok, tei aroni phrwngma twi khe tongnairok, bóhrok sal brúmno Phola Kwtharnj bising twi pholani simung tei khairokma rwjakmano yachakna nangnai.”—Ellen G. White, Signs of the Times, March 8, 1910. Kók diyari khe Elembaknno máng se chẃngsacha khlaio. Kók diyari khe poito khlaiwi kaham khe tongna bagwi Kaitorni kókthai-o se bororokno twlango.

`Emao lamao Jisu tamoni bagwi sagri khoroknwino bo kók swbai sana swkang Elembakno phunugwi kók sakha?`
