---
title:  Kók Diyari le Kaitorni Kubuino Rutuknaisa
date:   22/12/2026
---

Baksa rutuknairok saui man kha ha sakao 45,000 ni sláibo kwbang Khristian denomination bang kha. Jisuno poito khlaina chengmani le bahai khe aswk juda juda wng laikha? Tamni hwn khe tatal khe Elembakni kók swbaiwi sajakmani juda juda bang kha. Motha bai mothani phrwng laima se gothok lai liya.

Yaphangni simino (Yaphang 2:16, 17; 3:1-5) Soitan Kaitorni kubui bai bororokno hakchal khlaimani kotorkuk lama wng kha bororokno buji man ya (confusion) khlaimani se. Adam bai Ebino Soitan kubui tei tatal dalogwi kók sama bai bini bwkhak twlangna naikha. Piri mol romwi Soitan Kaitorni bororokno lama koro rwnani naikha (2 Bosong 36:14-16). Jisuno suda bo lama koro rwna naikha (Mathai 4:1-11). Phiyaba Jisu Soitanno kók phirok phuru “Elembago o kók tongo” hinwi sraisrai khe phunuk kha (Mat. 4:4, KOKBSI).

`Khúktang di 2 Timothi 4:1-5. Kaitorni kókno twiwi Pol Timothino tamo sakha? Thinango wngnani kókno twiwi Timothino tiyar wngnani bagwi kwrak khe Pol tamoni bono hus khlaiwi rwkha?`

Omo hai jora phai anw aphuru bororok Kaitorni kókno hamjak glak, tei Kaitorni kókbo khna glak, abo Pol diyarini mokol bai thinangno nuk kha. Phiyaba bóhrok khano muchungmano supungna bagwi kerang kothoma sanai hai phrwngnairok rutuk lai anw. Hakhe bóhrok tatalno rutukmani a tatalnono poito khlaiwi tongna twi khe Kaitor bóhrokno belai pok rwnai. “Aboni bagwi’-no bórok tatalno poito khlaiwi rwna tẃi khe Kaitor bórokno belai khe pok rw-o. Aboni bagwi’-no kubuino poito khlai yaui sumsok khlaiwi tongthokjaknairok bórok bebak’-no majra berjaknai” (2 Thes. 2:11, 12 KOKBSI).

Phiya kubuino yachakna muchungnairokno Jisu swmai rwkha, bohrok “kubuino saui mannai” (Johon 8:32, KOKBSI). Kók diyari Kaitor rwmani khe kubui bai tatal, tei pohor bai mwnakmano swkakna bagwi se.

Kwbangma Khristian Denomination-o kubuirokbo tongo. Phiyaba 99.5% denomination thaibrwini dagimung Sabat salno twi ya.

Thwi kheno Jisu bai tongna bagwi Chúknoha-o thango, eba mol brúm khamna bagwi morkeo thango hinwi nwng tumungno poito khlai ya khe tamo wngnai eba buro thangnai? 5% Denomination tong rwkma bisingo Seventh-day Adventists motha’-no karwi tei kubuini denomination tongo diba?

`Nini langmao bubo thai-o nwng khakchangma tei khatungmani kók nang? Khakchangma rwmani ulo Kaitor chwngno tamo khlainani phan rw? (2 Kor. 1:3, 4).`
