Bisi kisa swkango Kellogg Company kaisa kók awaimungni slogan snam kha, tei abo bororokni khao nangjak kha. Bóhrokni manwino bororok belai hamjagwi “puila chanaiwi simi phano naidi” hinwi bóhrokni manwino ma salai kha. O kók Seventh-day Adventists baibo gothogo. Nwng Ellen G. White’-ni langma tei samungno twiwi seminar khlai khe, kwbangmano bini kókno nwng saui mannai, phiyaba nwng bono “chanaiwi” nai ya khe bini kók diyario pholani hamari tongo hinwi nwng saui man glak. Context bujiui bini swijak kókno porinairok simi se bujiui mannai bo Kaitor bai kók diyari rwjak kha hinwi.

O talthamni bisingo chwng nugwi man kha, Ellen G. White khe chwngno Baibel-o se twlango, tei Kaitor bai kwrwng rwo. Bini kók diyari swijak pori khebo hamarirok mannai.

Roger Dudley bai Des Cummings Jr. khe amjogwi nai kha. Ellen G. White ni swijak pori yarokni slái porinairok le Jisu bai teibo halok kuthuk khe tongo; bóhrok teibo mwthangmungno khatungwi mano; nukhung surimung choya wngo; tei buino Jisuni kók sanani muchungjago. Amjoknairok sakha, “Motha tor thanio Ellen G. White ni kók porinairok le Khristian langmao belai slaijakmung núkjago.”—Dudley and Cummings, “Who Reads Ellen White?” Ministry, October 1982, p. 12.

Kaitor le diyarirokni bising twi se kók sao hinwi chwng o talthamni bisingo swrwng laikha. Bóhrokni kókrok Kaitor bai chwngno kwrwng rwo, borokni langmao kwthar tubu-o, tei Jisuni thani mwthangjakmani swmaio chwngno sotono: “Chánaiwi nai di Subraiyung bwswk se kaham” (Lobmung 34:8). Kwthar langma tongwi, tei langma slaijagwi Kaitorni samung khlaina bagwi se diyarirok le chwngno ringo. Omo hai kheno Jisu phainanino nahar twtwi swngcharni paithak sakhlaimungo gwdalnani bagwi sakno tiyar wngwi mano.
