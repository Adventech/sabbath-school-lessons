---
title:  Kók diyari khe Kha Kwchang Rwo
date:   23/12/2026
---

`Khúktang di Mathai 11:28, 29. Chwngno tamo khlai di hinwi Jisu oro sakha? Bini thani phainairokno bo tamo swmai rwkha?`

Bororokni kaham máng se Kaitor le naio. Adam bai Ebino snamna swkang, bóhrok tongthogwi tong manna twi khe Kaitor kaham swngchar snam kha (Yaphang 1). Bóhrokno kha kwchang rwna bagwi Kaitor bóhrokno bebakno rwkha. Phiya kwthar hiksai sumsok khlaimani ulo khe Kaitor bóhrokno rutukna phai kha (Yap. 3:9), Mwthangnaisa phainani kók diyarino rwkha (Yap. 3:15), twi bóhrokni sak langa kholopna twi khe kanmung-chummung snamwi rwkha (Yap. 3:21). Kaitor bini snamjak langma gwnangrokni hamkrai mángno naio.

Khakchangma bai kwtal phanni yaphang wng kha kók diyari se, abo Kaitor bini bororokni bagwi piri mol romwi snammani. Kók diyari-o belai hamari tongo.

`Khúktang di Jisaia 40:1-5. Kaitor bini diyarirokni bising twi birman-o tongnai bini bororokno tamo kók rwkha? Tabuk chwng bahai khe a kókrokno chini langmao thepanai?`

Kaitor Judano saui man rwna muchungjago, bóhrok yagul sujakna nangnai, haiphano Kaitor bóhrokno khibi ya hinwi. Kha baijakrokni bagwi kha raknani kók simi Kaitor Jisaiano rwmani ya; phiya bahai khe a kók yapharna nangnai abobo bo Jisaiano sakha. Belai khairokma, hamjakma, twi naikolmung bai khe bóhrokni thani kók ma yapharnai. Kaitor bini bororokni thani khakchangma kók sogwi rwna muchungjago. Jisaiani bijap-o kha kwchang ramani kók úaire uai núkjago (Jis. 12:1; Jis. 49:13; Jis. 51:3).

Pol hus khe rwkha, Khristo Jisu-o tongmung kaham khe tongnairok ma satokjaknai (2 Tim. 3:12). Tong keseb jorao kha kwchang rwma tei khatungma kók belai nango. Kók diyari khe Jisu phaiwi chwngno nogo twlangna swkango bini bororokno kha kwchang rwnani tei phan rwnani bagwi se.
