---
title:  Kok Diyari Khristoni Bwsakno Thansa Khlaio
date:   24/12/2026
---

`Khúktang di Johon 17:20-23 tei 1 Korinth 1:10-13. O kainwi bwtango nangkukmani kók tamo?`

Jisu Kruso thwinani sampa kheno bini bwkhao kaisa kuthuk muchungma phai kha. Bo bai bupha hamjakmao, naharmungo, tei samungo thansa wngma haino bono rwktharnairok thansa wng thung hinwi bo muchungjago. Jisu bóhrokno thansa khlainani bagwi suri kha, phiya abo bororokno kaisa tongmung snamnani kók ya. Jisu tei uáisa phai phinani nang phainai abo hai kók diyari Jisu sakha—juda juda lama, dopha, tei naharmungni phainairok Phola Kwtharni phanni bising twi Jisu tei bupha bai thansa wngnai. Polbo o kókno nangkukmani wngjagwi sakha. Bo Korinth Mothani bororokno koi kha, khá baksa, kók thansa wngwi tei kak lai-phui lai wngya twi khe tongna bagwi (1 Korinth 1:10). Phiya o kók diyarini thansa khlaimung tamoni bagwi nangkukmani?

Jisu surikha, “Bórok thansa wng thung hwnwi ang hwno. O Apha, nwng ani bisingo, angbo nini bisingo tongma hai bórokbo chini bisingo tong thung. Nwng ano rohoro hwnwi swngchar poito khlaiwi manna tẃi khe bórok thansa wng thung” (Johon 17:21, KOKBSI).

Joto borokno mwthangjak thung abo Kaitor naio. Phiya mwthangjakma mannani swkang bóhrok Jisuno poito khlaina nangnai, tei Kaitor se swngcharno mwthangna bagwi bono rohor hinwi poito khlaina nangnai. Jephuru juda juda hani bororok Jisuno bóhrokni Subrai tei Mwthangnaisa hinwi yachago, tei koinene khe khoroksa tei khoroksano hamjagwi nai laio nuk laio, hakhe se swngchar saui mannai Jisu wng kha bo sama haino soi kubui hinwi. Jisu thansa khlaina muchungmani aboni naharmung tongo tei phan tongo. Phiyaba yaphangni simino thansano swbaina naijagwi phaikha. Phunukmarini bagwi Samung 6:1 naidi. Khristianrok kak laimani dakti dakti kheno bangwi phai kha!

Phola rwmani suarirokni bising twi khe thansa khlainani samung khe Pholani samung se. Epis 4:11-15 bwtango sao, Phola rwmani suarrokni samung wng kha poito khlainairokno tiyar khlaina bagwi, tei Khristoni bwsakno rak rwna bagwi “Abo baino chwng bebak’-no poito khlai thanibo, Kaitorni Sajlano saui man thanibo thansa wngnai; chwng chakra wngnai, Khristo torma lokma tẃi chwngbo torwi logwi chakra wngna” (Epis 4:13, KOKBSI). Phola rwmani suarrokni bisingo kók diyarini suar khe Jisu tei uáisa phai phinani swkang Kaitorni bororokno thansa khlai thani belai nangkukmani. Aboni naharmung wng kha bohrok Jisuno poito khlaiwi mwthangjakna bagwi (Samung 16:31).
