### Hatibarsani Bagwi Swrwngjaknai
1 Kor. 2:2; Johon 1:1–5; 2 Tim. 3:16, 17; 2 Tim. 4:1–5; Jis. 40:1–5; Johon 17:20–23; 1 Kor. 1:10–13.

> <p>Muitu Bwtang</p>
> “Elembakni bebak kókno Kaitor bai koboljagwi swimani; abo kubuino phrwng thani chúgo, tatalno tatal hwnwi phunuk thani chúgo, samung cha yano cha rw thani chúgo, phiya kaham khe tongnani phrwng thani chúgo; abo tẃi khe Kaitorni borok chúk súgwi mano, joto samung kahamni bagwi bini nangmani manwi bebakno mano” (2 Timothi 3:16, 17, KOKBSI).

Au, Kaitor le mokol nuk ya hai khe chwngno poito khlai di hwn ya . Bo kwthang tongo aboni sakki le belai phan gwnang se. Habarrok 6000 bisi romwi Kaitor tongo hinwi simi sakki rw

ya (James Webb Space Telescope ni mamgpili naidi), phiya Kaitor khe hamjaknaisa hinwibo sakki rwo.

Pitor le kwthar hathaio tong phuru “Chuknohani khorang” khna kha. Haiphano Pitor swikha “Chwng bo bai a hathái kwtharo tong phuru khunju baithang chuknohani o kók samano khna kha. Aboni bagwi diyarirok sa langmani a kókno chwng teibo poito khlai kugo. Norok a kókno khunju kai khe teibo hamo, tamni hwn ba a Sal kasa ya sak, phiya a aitorma norokni khá-o ka ya sak abo wng kha mwnakma-o chẃngwi tongnai chati hai” (2 Pitor 1:18, 19). Chwng Pitor hai khe Kaitorni khorang khna ya phano, “kók diyari” samarokno chwng teibo poito khlai sugwi mano. Baibelni diyari tei Baibel phatarni diyarirokni bising twi Kaitor le chwng bai kók sao. Phiyaba chwng abono yachakna tiyar de wng kha? Kwbangma bororok diyarirokni kókno yachakna tiyar wng liya abo chwng láibuma-o nugo. Phiya Kaitorni khairokma bai chwng abo hai chaya khlaithai kwrwi.
