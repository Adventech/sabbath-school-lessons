---
title:  Kók Diyari Khristono Chẃngsacha Khlaio
date:   20/12/2026
---

Kók diyari khe Kaitor bororok bai kwrwngmani tei bororokno bo bai kwrwng rwmani se. Phiyaba Soitan (dragon) bwrwimano (Kaitorni Mothano) jolijak kha, hakhe bwrwimani tei bosong tongmarok bai nang laina thang kha (Phun. 12:17).

O bwrwimani tei bosong tongmarokni sinimung tamo? Bóhrok Kaitor dagima twi tongwi Jisu sirwmano twiwi tongnairok. Phunukjakmani 19:10 bwtango sao, Jisu sirwmani wng kha “diyarini kók” se.

`Khúktang di 1 Korinth 2:2 tei Galatia 6:14. Pol bini langma tei samungni naharmungno bahai khe sakha?`

Sakni simung bai baksa Chonghorrok Jisuno yachakmani wng kha diyarirok tei borok kwtharrok sirwmani bising twi se. Khristianrokni thani Swmai Kwcham tei Swmai kwtalni bisingo aboni sakki tongo. Swmai Kwcham-o Jisuno yasi surwi naharmani, phiya Swmai kwtal-o khe Jisuno phunukjak kha, hakhe Jisu le Mwthangnaisa wngwi Kaitorni chapmungno supung kha. Poito khlaina muchungnairoknk bagwi sakki nangmani jora twino tongo. “Kaitorni phan, bini Sajlani phan, tei Phola Kwtharni phan bai poitono tisaui manna twi khe bororokno kaham seb rwnani se Kaitorni naharmung.”—Ellen G. White, The Spirit of Prophecy, vol. 3, p. 182.

Mwthangjaknani bagwi poito khlaimani le simung bai simi ya, tongmung kaham baibo wngo. Abo khorom bai samung khlaimani slai bwkha bai se teibo nangkukmani. Jephuru bóhrokni bwkhano muthubjago, aphuru bóhrokni bwkha ayang phaisingno twlangjago.

`Khúktang di Johon 1:1-5. Jisu sabo? O bwtango tamo sakha?`

Tumungno Jisu le Kaitorni kók, sirisitini simi tongo, tei máng wngwi chwng bai tong phai kha hwn khe, diyarirokno Kaitor kók rwmani kók le Jisu bai halok kwrwngjak se. Jisuni kókno sraisrai khe saya diyarirok phano phola Kwthar bai koboljak se.

`Uánsuk di, aswk nokhayung bai hayung kotormano Snamnaisa Jisu se chini bagwi thwi phai kha. Abo nono tamo khatungma rwkha?`
