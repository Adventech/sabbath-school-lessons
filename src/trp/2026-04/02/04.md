---
title:  Kothoma Kahamni Diyari, Jisaia
date:   06/10/2026
---

Jisaia wng kha Swmai Kwchamni Diyari, diyarini samung khlaina bagwi Kaitor bai ringjak kha (Jis. 6:1–8). Kaitorni kwthar tei chwngsachano nugwi Jisaia bini chukyano saui man kha. A jorao Jisaiani thani Chuknohani hayani hoki bothria tubuwi seraphim birwi phai kha, tei bini sumsokrokno “khibi kha.” Hakhe Kaitor swng kha, “Ang sabono rohornai tei chini bagwi sabo thángnai?” Hakhe Jisaia kok yachakma hai khe chwng kok yachakthai, “Iko ang oro se! ano rohor di” (Jis. 6:8).

Israilrokni laibumani nangkukmani jorao Jisaia ringjak kha. Israilrok tei bohrokni bokhorokrokni sumsok belai nangkukmano twiwi hus khlainani samung berjak kha. Jisaiani kok diyarirogo Elembakni kokmungrokno chwng mano, Kaitorni tongmungno twiwi, Borokni tongmungno twiwi, tei Kaitorni mwthangnani chapmungno twiwi. Phiya teibo phan gwnang bini kok Diyari Samani bisingo Jisu thwimano twiwi simi ya, tamo khe bini thwimung chini bagwi tamo khlai kha a kokbo chwng Jisaia 53 beremo mano.

`Khuktang di Jisaia 53. Bahai khe Khristo thwinani bisi kwbangma swkango bini thwimung chini bagwi tamo khlai kha kok Diyari swijak kha? Kok diyarini suarno twiwi o kok Diyari chwngno tamo phrwng?`

Masiani naharmung tei samungno twiwi Jisaia kok Diyari sakha (Jis. 53:3–5). Bo swbaiwi sakha, tamoni bagwi Khristo “Mera bwsarok ratharjakna bagwi tlangjakma hai” (Jis. 53:7) tei bahai khe bo sakno yapharmani abo bini bororokno sumsokni mwthang kha. Khristo thwimani wng kha bebak bororokni bagwi bo sakno yapharmano se phunugo.

“Chwng bebakno merarok hai lama koró bai kha; chwng bebakno chini muchung tẃi khe him lai kha; phiya, Subraiyung chini bebak sumsokno bini sago ruju-ui na kha” (Jis. 53:6). Kruso hayungni sumsokni tongmungno phunukjak kha, tei sumsokni yoknani lamabo phunukjak kha. O beremo Swmai Kwcham tei Swmai Kwtalni kwbangma beremrokni slai mwthangmani chapmungno phunukjak kuk kha.

Diyarini samungni bagwi Jisaia ringjakmani le aswk kwlai ya. Sumsokni manthaino twiwi bo hus khlaiwi rwkha (Rum. 6:23). Bini mol tei chini molni bagwi Jisaini kok Diyari le srai srai (Jis. 2:4, Jis. 5:20). Bini kok twi bujsoi tei kwtal khlainani kokno yachak ya wngwino Jerusalem dera kha. Abo hai kheno Jisu phai phuru swngcharni borok hamyarokni tongthai deranai.

`Jisai 53 beremno naidi. Kok diyarini bising twi Khristo chini bagwi thwimani kok nono tamo khatungmung rwkha?`
