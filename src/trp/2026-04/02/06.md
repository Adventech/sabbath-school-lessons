---
title:  Baptisma Rwnai Johon, Bororokno Tiyar Khlaio
date:   08/10/2026
---

`Khuktang di Mark 1:2–5. Baptisma rwnai Johonni samungni naharmung tamo?`

Jisaiani kok diyarino supungna bagwi se Baptisma rwnai Johon phaikha hinwi Mark Kothoma Kahamo chwng nugo (Jis. 40:3). Johon chwraini simi tormani jora Kaitorni thani poito krak khe tong kha. Hasing panthoro Johonni khorang khnaui bororokno Soitan bini yago mang tonui man glak kha hinwi kirijak kha (Ellen G. White, The Desire of Ages, p. 224). Johon bini kok khnanairokni samung chyano phunuk kha. Hakhe Israilrokno Soitan bini yago romwi ton man glak kha hinwi kirijak kha. Aboni bagwi bo baptisma rwnai Johonno uaire uai khiklaina naikha, tei Masiano poito khlai rwya khlaina nai kha. Haiphano bo man liya.

`Khuktang di Mathai 11:1–6. Johon alongo tong phuru Jisu bahai khe bini poitono rak rwkha?`

Hasing panthoro Soitan Jisuno belogwi man liya. Hakhe bo Jisuni sandai Johonni thani thang kha, tei bono alongo khiklai kha. Johon alongo kwlaimani Johon kha khamjak kha. Jisuni bwkhao poja rwnani bagwi Soitan Johonno dera rwkha. Phiya Johon Jisuni kha rak rwma kokno khna kha. Hakhe bo tamni bagwi alongo kwlai kha sai man ya phano, bo thwi ya sakno poito krak khe tong kha.

Agini diyarirok hai khe Khristoni kahamno twiwi kha kiphilnani tei poitoni kok pirnani bagwi chwngno ringjak kha (Phun. 14:6–12). Kaitorni samungno yakar rwna bagwi baptisma rwnai Johonno khlaima hai khe Soitan chwngnobo khlainai. Brwi hamya tei borok hamya bai khe Johon buthar rwjakma hai chwngnobo khlaijagwi mano. Phiyaba jesa wng phano chwng Kaitorno poito khlaiwi tongna nangnai, tei bo chwngnio kok phirokmung rwnai. Kaitorni muchung twi khlai manna bagwi bo chwngno phan rwnai hinwi kok rwkha (Samung 1:8). Jesa tong halogo phano poito krak khe tongna nangnai.

`Johon hai khe nwng alongo de klai kha? Tamoni bagwi abo hai ninin thani wng kha buji man ya de wng kha? Bahai khe nwng Kaitorno poito khlaiwi tongwi mannai?`
