title:  Elijani Kok Pirmung
date:   05/10/2026
---

`Khuktang di 1 Habugra 18:19–39. O kothomao chwng tamo nugwi man?`

Elija ringjakmani, tei kok samani abo Kaitorni kok sanaini tongmungno phunugo. Israilrokni tatalno mwthakna bagwi Kaitor Habugra Ahabni thani Elijano rohor kha. Kaitor bini bororokno takjak mwtaini yakni kati rwnani bagwi tei bini kok khnanai snamnani bagwi Kaitor o samung khlai kha. Habugra tei hani bororok Diyari Elijano bohrokni jwngjalni bagwi majra ber kha, tei butharnani nai kha.

Karmel hathaio Baal tei Asherani diyarirok bai Elija malai kha. Phola bai koboljagwi Kaitorni kok sanaisa Israilrokno tatal mwtairokno khibui Kaitorni thani kiphil di hinwi koimung khlai kha. Hakhe aphuru bororok saui man kha bohrok chongwi namani abo tatal-o sogwi kha hinwi. Aboni bagwi bohrok Elijani Kaitorno tei uaisa yachaknani chong lai kha. Tatal mwtairok Elijani Kaitorni slai phan kwrwi, tei kwbo kwrwi hinwi bohrok saui man kha.

Khristo tei uaisa phainani kokno uansuknani, tei Subraini a sal kotoro bachanani tiyar wngwi manna twi khe, tei “takjak mwtairokno” khibui manna twi khe Elijani kok pirmung khlaina bagwi Kaitor bini mothano dagi kha. Kaitor bini chongjak bororokni bising twi khe tabukbo bororok bai kok saui tongo. Bini kok tei Phola kwtharni suar rwmani abo bororokno kha sicha rwo, hus khlai rwo, kokbiti rwo, tei kha rak rwo. “Chini bisingo nang kukmani kotor wng kha soi tongmung kahamni pholani sichamung se. Omoni bagwi puila chwng samung khlaina nangnai. Kaitorni hamari manna bagwi chwng samung belai khlaina nangnai, abo Kaitor bini hamari chwngno rwna muchung ya wngmani ya; tamo khe chwng hamari yachaknani tiyar kwrwi wngmani bagwi se.”—Ellen G. White, Selected Messages, book 1, p. 121.

`Khuktang di Malaki 4:5, 6. Kaitorno poito khlainairokni nogo Elijani kok pirmung bahai khe samung khlai?`

Soi pholani sichamung bwkha tei nukhungni simi chengjago. Nukhung khe mothani nok busubni holongthai. Pholao sichamung bai se Kaitorni bororok bini bagwi samung khlainai wngo.

`Bubo hai takjak mwtai tabukni molo chini nukhungo, bwkhao, tei mothao achaiwi tong?`
