### Hatibarsani Bagwi Swrwngjaknai
Gen. 15:6, Gal. 3:6–9, 1 Kings 18:19–39, Isaiah 53, Dan. 2:46–48, Mark 1:2–5, Matt. 11:1–6.

> <p>Muitu Bwtang</p>
> “Hakhe ang Subraiyungni khorang bai hai hwnwi sama khna kha, ‘Ang sabono rohornai tei chini bagwi sabo thángnai?’ Hakhe ang hai hwnwi sa kha, ‘Iko ang oro se! ano rohor di’ ” (Jisaia 6:8, KOKBSI).

Imo hai khe Baibelni jorao, Kaitorni bororokni laibumao Diyarirok samung kwbangma joraono nukjak kha. Bóhrok jora jorao. bororokni thani belai nangkukmani wngwi samung khlaio. Bóhrokni thani phan gwnang tei nangkukmani barja tongo.

Tamo khe diyarirokni ringjakma aswk kwlai ya. Bohrokni langma jora jorao aswk kwlai ya, phiya kha khamma, bui bai khibijakma, bui bai bujijak ya wngma, tei kha swbaijakma bai se kuplung.

Puila phuru Diyarirok a samungno yachakna muchung ya wngmabo tongo. Tamni hwn ba kwbangma borok bohrokni kokno yachak ya, phiya kebengo. Kaitorni diyarirok khibijago, butok khlaijago, tei alongrogo chobjago. Baksaba holong bai khitarjago, eba bolongni malmatarok bai uathar rwjago. Baksa khe butharjago.

Tabukni molni bororok Soitanni tongmung hamya bai koboljagwi Diyarirni kokrokno khibi lai kha. Diyarirokni bising twi khatungma rwjakma kok buino saui manna twi khe Kaitorni diyarirokni kok khna di hinwi chwngno dagijak kha.

Eden thaiblwngo bororok sumsok khlaimani ulo phano Kaitor borok bai kok sanani lama snam phinani samung khlaimano twiwi thangnai hatibaro chwng swrwng kha. O hatibaro khe diyarirokni bising twi Kaitor chwngno kok Samano twiwi chwng swrwngnai.

_October 10 Sabat salni bagwi o hatibarsani rwngchapno swrwngwi tiyar wng di._
