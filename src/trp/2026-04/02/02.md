---
title:  Abraham Khe Swmaino Mwthangnaisa
date:   04/10/2026
---

Yaphangni simi Phunukjakmani jora Kaitorni khairokmano bini kok khnaui yachaknani ringjakmano yachaknai tongrwknai Kaitorni bororok tongo (Jir. 31:7, 31–34). Rom di, Babelni simi bororok koprokmani ulo Kaitor Abramno (ulo khe Abraham) bini swmaini poitono narwknai swrwngjaknai kaham wngnani bagwi chong kha, abo bini nukhungni bagwi simi ya, tamo khe thinangni bosongrokni bagwibo. Kubui kwmajak tei hamya bororokni tongkhoro tong phano Abram Kaitorni ringmano yachak kha (Yap. 12:1–9.)

Kaitor Abraham bai kok saui swami rwmani bising twi bororokno mwthangna bagwi Khristo yapharjaknanino phunuk kha (Yap. 15:6–17). Abrahamno amjokna bagwi tei thinango Kaitorni Sajla yapharjakmano phunukna bagwi Abrahamno bini Sajla Isakno Kaitorni thani yaphar di hinwi ringjak kha (Yap. 22:1–17). Uansuk di Abraham kok khnanai wngmani kokno. Phiyaba Abraham Isakno butharna nai phuru Kaitor Abrahamni yakno narwk kha, phiya Isakni sak slaio mera bwsa rwkha.

Hus khlaimung rwna bagwi simi bebak diyarirok ringjakmani ya. Phiyaba poito gwnang bororokno kwtal snam phinani, tatalno twiwi hus khlainani, bigra tei kwrwirok kusupjakmano twiwi hus khlainani, swmaino swbaijakmano twiwi hus khlainani bagwi se bebak diyarirok ringjago.

Jotoni slai nangkukmani kok wng kha, diyarirok Maisani bisingo mwthangjakmung manmano se phunugo. Baibelni diyarirokni sakki tei Phola kwtharni chini molo chwngno bwskangni mol hamyano twiwi saui tongo. Phiyaba kothoma kaham wng kha Jisuno poito khlaiwi bo bai swmaini halok nwrwgwi tongnairokno Jisu le mwthangnai (Phun. 12:17).

`Khuktang di Yaphang 15:6, Galatia 3:6–9, tei Hib. 11:8. Abrahamno twiwi oro tamo sajak kha?`

Abraham bai chini molni bisingo kwbangma gothokmung tongo. Abrahamni molo paganism tei takjak mwtaino khulumnairok tongma haino chini molobo Khristian-o suda tatal phrwngmung habwi thang kha. Kaitorni bororok aborokno kebengwi Kaitorno kosol khe tongna bagwi ringjak kha, tei suari khorokthamni kok pirnani bagwi ringjak kha (Phun. 14:6–12).

`Elembago sakha, Abram “Subraiyungno bwkha bera kha; tei aboni bagwi Subraiyung bono bwkha phurjak kha” (Yap. 15:6). Kothoma kahamni swmaino bahai khe o bwtango nuk kha? Oro tamo khatungmung man kha?`
