#### Drug Phalnai Tongmani . . .

By Mission Editor

Marek Poland ni Khristian nukhungo tor kha. Bini 18 umor phuru bini hamjakma bwrwi bono yakar thang kha. Bo khatungma kwmajak kha. Bo suri kha, phiya Kaitor bini surimano khnaya wngjagwi bo Atheist wng kha.

Matthew bai Martin wng kha bini bayab. Martin bono drug phalnani lama phunuk kha. Bo drug phalnai wng kha. Phiya salsa bini bini achwini nogo beraina thang phuru Jisuni mamgpili nugwi chwrai jorani kokno bo muitu man kha. Aphuru Bo chwrai phuru Jisuni kók swrwngmani muitu man kha.

Salsa Yapha nainai Marekno sakha, bisi kaisa kheno bo thwinai. Aphuru kheno bo Phunukjakmani bijap porina muchungjak kha. Abono ulo kothoma kaham poriui swrwng kha. Salsa bo Signs of the Times bijapkhor beraina thang kha. Aro bo Ellen G. White swimani The Great Controversy bijap man kha. Bo abono Baibel bai khe naiwi kaham khe swrwng kha. Hakhe Poland-o Seventh-day Adventists mothao hab kha.
