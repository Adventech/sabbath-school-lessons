---
title:  Diyarini Samungni Bagwi Ringjakma
date:   09/10/2026
---

Kaitor judakuk naharmungni bagwi judakuk jorao Diyarirokno ringo. Ellen G. White wng kha mol kwtalni jorao khoroksa diyarini bagwi ringjaknai hinwi Seventh-day Adventists poito khlaio. Daniel 8:14 bwtangni “Nokha nwisai tei thamra phung tei sanja jora abo hai khe tongnai. Aboni yagulo khe noktharno tei uáisa kwthar khlaijakna” hinwi sajakma kok diyarino rwksaui Jisu tei uaisa phainano khatungmani Millerite Adventists’-ni ulo 1844 bisini December talo Ellen Harmon (White) puila Kaitor bai ringjak kha.

William Miller salmari chongwi rw ya, bini logi rwktharnairok se Elembakni Kwthar khlaimani salno rwk saui October 22, 1844 salmarino Jisu phainani sal hinwi chongwi rwkha. O kok North America tei swngcharni juda juda hani saisai bororokni khao poito habwi thang kha. Daktino Chuknohao mwthangnaisa bai malainani khatung lai kha. Miller’-bo Jisu phai phinanino belai tongthogwi khatung kha.

Phiyaba a salo Jisu phai liya, hakhe Millerite poito khlainairok laibumani kha baimung kotor (Great Disappointment) man kha.

Omo wng kha Kaitor Ellen Harmon (White) no puila phunukjakmung (vision) rwnani lama snamma se. Bujimung chaya khe swbaiwi samanino twiwi kha rak rwnani, abo bini basgwibo tei bohrok Kaitor bohrok bai tongo hinwi poito khlainairokni bagwibo. Bini phunukjakmungo bono swngcharni simi Chuknohani Jerusalem jora lama phunukjak kha. O kwthar taunni lama-o Jisuno phainano poito khlainairok him laijak. “Swkangni simi Jisuno simi naharnairok khe taun kwtharo sogwi kha, tei mwthangjak kha.”—Ellen G. White, Spiritual Gifts, vol. 2, p. 31.

Bini o phunukjakmani (vision) wng kha kothoma kaham. Kha baimungni bising twino Kaitor kwtal movement cheng kha. Jisu bohrok baino tong kho. Bo phainai. Bo bohrokno mwthangnani lamao tlangwi tong kho. Phiya bohrok Jisuno “naisikna” nangnai. Bohrok kha baimungni bising twi phano khatungma khibi yaui tongnani karon tongo.

Abo hai kheno bisi chisni umorni sikli Ellen G. Harmon (White) diyarini samungni bagwi Kaitor bai ringjak kha. Puilao bini bagwi abo aswk kwlai wng liya.

Tamo khe bo a suarno kha sutrang khe yachak kha. Ellen G. White wng kha Kaitorno kirinai wngwi bini kok sanaisa. Kaitorni Diyari wngnani samung le aswk kwlai ya. Bahai khe bo Kaitorni ringmao bini sakno yaphar kha?
