---
title:  Poito Krak khe Tongnai Diyari Daniel
date:   07/10/2026
---

Kwbangma Khristianno Daniel 1 beremni kothoma saui mano, Daniel chwraini simino sak kaham tongnaani raidarokno swrwngmani kok (Dan. 1:8). Habugrani songphango bono habugrani chamung bedokjak phuru dalo sampra chaui tei twi simi nungwi tongnani bagwi bo chong kha (Dan. 1:12–16). Abo wng kha thinangni bagwi amjokjakmungo, romdi Daniel 6 beremo bo poito krak khe tongmani bagwi butharna suda naijak kha.

`Khuktang di Daniel 2:46–48; Daniel 4:18; Daniel 5:13, 14; tei Daniel 6:25–27. Takjak mwtaino khulumma hao Daniel Kaitorni sakki rwmano twiwi o bwtangrogo tamo phunuk?`

Takjak mwtairokni thani Danielni Kaitorni sakki rwjakmani phanno naidi. Oroni kothoma wng kha judakuk tei nangkukmani raidano phunugo. Chini tongmung, chwng poito rakmani, tei chini bising twi Kaitor samung khlaimani phan abo Chuknohani Kaitorno twiwi tei Bo se “Mwtairokni Kaitor, tei Bugrarokni Bugra” hinwi buino swrwngwi man rwo.

Danielni kok diyarirok wng kha buj thummano twiwi, Khristo tei uaisa phainano twiwi (Kaitorni paithak kwplai wngmung), aboni bagwino Danielni mungni kokmang wng kha “Kaitor ano buj thumnai.” Bini swimungrok swrwngmani abo tabukni molo chini bagwi belai nangkukmani. Danielni kok Diyari wng kha hus khlaimung rwo, tei Kaitorni dagimung twinano twiwi muitu khlai rwo. Dopharok bachanai tei kwlainai, tei bohrok Kaitorni kok khnaya wngmani manthai mannanno twiwi Diyari Daniel kok diyari sakha. Daniel kok diyari phunukjakmani bising twi Kaitorni chapmung tei Kaitorni ha kwplai wngmano phunugo (Dan. 7:13, 14).

Daniel 2 Khristo phainani bisi swkangma swkang swijak kha, o kokno uansuk naidi. Kaibrwi ha bachanani tei chini molni kokno twiwibo (mangthongni yakung bai yapha tola) bo kok diyari sakha. Abo wng kha modern Europe hano phunukma se, kok diyario sajak kha “hani bororok kubun dophani bororok bai kái lai anw, phiya ba sor bai ha kwpha laiwi tong lai ya hai khe, bórok thansa khe tongwi man lai glak” (Dan. 2:43). Europe’-no twiwi kok diyarirok sajakmarok bwswk se nang phai kha, kok diyari sajakma hai khe bohrok kai lai kha (intermarriage), haiphano bohrokni hukumu, somaj tei raida thansa yani bagwi kak laio. Soviet Union bisi kisa swkang baimani abo Danielni kok diyari se nang phaio. Danielni kok diyari wng kha Kaitorni diyarirokno bwkha berana bagwi chwngno chubao eba karon rwo.

`Daniel 2 beremo kok diyari sajakmani harok bachakha tei thang kha. Chwngno oro tamo sa Kaitorni mol brumni hano twiwi kok Diyari samarokno poito khlainani?`
