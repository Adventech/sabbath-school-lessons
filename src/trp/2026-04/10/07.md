Phan (authority) bai tei poito khlaina chukjak kheno chini thani diyarirok kok sao, tamni hwn ba Kaitor bohrokno bini kok sanani bagwi chongwi nakha. Baksa ba Baibel swinai diyari tongo, tei baksa ba Baibel swiya diyari tongo, phiya baksa Baibel-o mung gwnang diyari tongo, tei baksa ba Baibel-o mung kwrwi diyari tongo. Phiya bóhrok bebakno baksa se, bóhrok bebakno Kaitorni phan (authority) bai tei Kaitorni phola bai rwjakma se.

Ellen G. White wng kha Baibel-o mung kwrwi tei Baibel swiya diyari. Bini swimung Baibel bai baksa eba Baibel-o thepaui mano hinwi Seventh-day Adventists poito khlai ya. Haiphano bini swimungrok poito khlaina chukjak, tei phan (authority) bai se bo kók sao. Ellen G. White sao: “ ‘Ang o koktun sirwmani kók swithani Subrai ano samano se ang norokno sao. Ani sakni uánsukma kókno ang swibwlaio kókbwkhal swiya. Kaitorni achukthayaungni simi pohor chwngwi Kaitor ano phunukmano se ang norokno sao.’ ”—Selected Messages, book 1, p. 29.

Baibel bai gothokjak kheno Ellen G. White sumsokno phunuk kha, chini kaisa susuk mwthangjaknani khatungma Jisuno nangmano phunuk kha, abo poito khlaima bising twi khairokma bai se mwthangjago hinwi bo sakha, tamni hwn ba abo bini swimungni kokbothoma se.

Bini swimungo chwng nuk kha kaham bai hamyani chobani kók. A choba Lucifer Chúknoha-o sumsok khlai phuruni simi cheng kha, tei Soitan bai bini sumsok khlainai Suarirok, phiya bebak sumsok bai hamya sokomorjakma bising twi a choba kholopjaknai. Ellen G. White ni swimungo mol páithakni bagwi Baibelni raidarok rwjak kha. Bo kók samanirok wng kha, rom di, salsa Motha bai haphang thansa wngnai abono twiwi hus khlai kha; tongmung hamyarokno twiwi hus khlai kha; Kaitorni kerongno nangkukmani khe phunuk kha; phiya rom di, “Yabrwini mari” abo chwng bini swimungo manmani ya, tamo khe bini swimungo mol páithakno twiwi bujimung rwkha.

Khristianrok bahai khe tongnai abono twiwibo bo phrwngo. Pholani langma, sak kaham tongnani, lekhapora swrwngmung, nukhung, maphani samung, tongmung kaham abo hairokno twiwi bo samani kókrok bororokni langmao belai slaijakmung tubu kha.

Ellen G. White ni swimung Baibel-o kwrwi phano mol páithago tongnai poito khlainairokno chubanani bagwi Kaitor bono samungo thepa kha. Bini swimurok kók diyarini phan (authority) bai se tei poito khlaina chukjak.
