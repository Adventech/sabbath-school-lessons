---
title:  Bodol Snamjakmani Phan (Authority)
date:   02/12/2026
---

Hosea 12:13 bwtango sraisrai khe sajak kha “Subraiyung khoroksa diyarini yak tẃi Israilno ha Ijipni phiyógwi tubu kha tei diyari bai bo mwrwkjak kha” (KOKBSI). Kaitorni bororokno phiyoknani tei mwrwknani wng kha Baibelni diyarirokni nangkukmani samung.

Buj thumnairok 4:4-8, 2 Bosong 20:14-17, Lamrwma 12:1-3, tei Uáinwi Saphima 1:9-16 bwtangrokno khúktang khe chwng saui mano, Kaitor bini bororokno mwrwkmani samungno bini bororokno phunukna bagwi diyarirokni se belai nangkukmani samung. Choba tei tong keseb jorao Kaitor bini bororok tei bóhrokni twiphangrokno lama phunukmani, kókbiti rwmani, tei kha rak rwmani wng kha diyarirokni bising twi se. Omo hai kheno Kaitor Jehajial tei Debora’-no samung ber kha. Ter tei surimungno twiwibo diyarirok khe lam phunukmung rwo (Lamrwma 12:1-3). Baksa jorao khe, dopharokno naikolna bagwibo Kaitor le diyarirok bai kók sao. Sana kwlaio, Israilsarokni langmao diyarirok bai mwrwkjak ya eba twiduljak ya wngmani jora se kwrwi kho.

`Puila Khristian Motha tei bodolo diyarirok bahai bahai khe chuba kha? Samung 6:1-7, 1 Kor. 5:1–5, 1 Kor. 7:10–16, Tit 1:5.`

Uánsuk di, tumungno twidulnani raida kwrwi eba nukmung (visible) gwnang twiphang kwrwi khe kaisa bodol snamjak kha. A bodol le belai jwngjal bai manjaknai. Abo haino puilani Khristian mothani tong halok nukjak kha. Jisu Chúknoha-o kasaui thang kha, hakhe Sagrirok bai bo tei kwrwi kha. Bo bóhrokno dagiui klang kha, “ ‘Tháng di, swngchar gwnangwi bebak borokni thani Kothoma Kaham pírwi di’ ” (Mark 16:15, KOKBSI). Sagri chiknwirok le chongjak wng phano, bohrok samung chengnani bagwi bodol eba Mothani kaham jai khlaijakmung (structure) se kwrwi kho.

Puilani Motha-o belai jwngjal nukjak kha. A jwngjalrok wng kha bahai khe motha bodol songchanai, samung tangnani raida bubo hai khlainai, tei bóhrokni bisingni tatal phrwngnai tei tongmung hamya tongnairokno siniui tikhlainani akorok. Phiyaba diyarirok puilani mothano mwrwgwi tei hamya bai kati rwnani samung khlai kha (Samung 13:1, 2). Mothano kaham bodol snamnani, tei kók pirnani samungo bwskango twlangnani hwn khe diyarirokni samungno karwi bahai khe samung chengwi mannai, abo wngwi se man ya.
