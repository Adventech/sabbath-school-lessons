---
title:  Baibel Canon tei Canon Phatarni Diyarirok
date:   03/12/2026
---

`Khúktang di 1 Bosong 29:29 tei 2 Bosong 9:29. O kainwi bwtango sajakma baksa diyarirokno twiwi nwng tamo naichom man?`

Nathan, Gad, Ahija, tei Iddo hai diyarirok tong phano, bóhrokni swijak bijap chwng Baibel-o man ya.

`Khúktang di 1 Habugra 11:29-39 tei Luk 7:28. Baibel bwchap eba canon-o kwrwi diyarirokni phanno (authority) twiwi o bwtangrogo tamo phrwng?`

Kaitor tamoni bagwi baksa diyarirokni kók khe Baibel-o sitok kha, phiya baksa diyarirokni kókno khe Baibel-o sitok liya abo chwng saui man ya phano Baibel-o swimung kwrwi (canon-o kwrwi) diyarirokbo Elembakni diyarirok bai baksano Kaitor bai rwjak kha hinwi chwng sai mano. Kók diyarini suarno khobwi bebak phola rwmani suarrok mol páithak jora tongnai hinwi Baibel-o sao. Baibel-o kók diyari sajakmabo tongo, baksa Baibel-o mung gwnang diyari tongnai, phiya baksa Baibel-o mung kwrwi diyarirokbo tongnai, tamni hwn khe “Kaitorni samung khlaina hwnwi Kaitorni bebak bororokno tiyar khlaina bagwi, phiya Khristoni bwsakno rak rwna bagwi ... Abo baino chwng bebak’-no poito khlai thanibo” (Epis 4:12, 13, KOKBSI), tei “Subraiyungni a kirimasingcha sal phaina swkang” diyarirok tongnai (Joel 2:31, KOKBSI).

Daud uaijaui tei borok butharwi sumsok khlaimani ulo bono kók sana bagwi Kaitor diyari Nathanno rohor kha (2 Sam. 12:7-14). Nathan Baibel swiya diyari wng phano Daud Nathan saboni phan (authority) bai bini thani phaikha hinwi bo bono swng liya, tamni hwn ba diyarirokni phan (authority) wng kha Kaitor bai rohorjakma se, abo bo Baibel swikha eba swiya abo bai ya. Baibel swinai diyari phano wng thung, eba Baibelni ulni diyari phano wng thung, eba khe kwtal molni Ellen G. White diyari phano wng thung, bóhrok Baibelni phan (authority) bai ma tongnai hinwi sakno nugo. Bóhrokni kókrokno Baibel bai se amjogwi chanaiwi naithai, phiya Baibel bai slainani ya.

`Ellen G. White sakha, “Kaitor naio nwng Baibel swrwng di. Baibelni pohor le khibijak ya. ..”—Selected Messages, book 3, p. 29. Bahai khe chwng motha o kókno bujithai?`
