---
title:  Baibel’-ni Phan (Authority)
date:   01/12/2026
---

Baibel wng kha Kaitor rwmani kók diyari tei Kaitor bai kobolwi swijakma bwchap. Baibel-o kotor swngmung kók phirokmungrok tongo, tei Baibel wng kha bororokni thani Kaitor juda khe bo sakno phunukmani se. Elembakni bising twi borok ha sakao phaimani kókmang, Kaitorni tongmung tei muchungma, chini langmani bagwi bini chapmung abórokno chwng swrwngo. Baibel wng kha kubui, hakhe kubuino nai thani Baibelni sakao phan (authority) chwng tonna nangnai. Khristianrokno kubui phunukmani yaphang khe Baibel se. Baibelni sama twi khe se Khristianrok poito khlaiwi tongthai.

Chwng Kaitorni kókno khibiui eba yachagwi mano, phiya baibelni kókno khibina bagwi Habugra Jihoiakim hai khe chwng baibelni sókthai kwrwi (Jir. 36:22-27). Phiya baibelno swrwngwi chwng baibelni kókrokno yachak khe, Habugra Josia hai khe sláijagwi mano, tei buinobo Kaitorni thani twlangnai (2 Habugra 22:10-13).

Tabukni molo bororok Elembago chaya tamo tong simi rutuk laio. Baksa borok uánsugo Baibel abo kwtal molni raida kerong bai gothok ya hinwi. Baksa khe Baibel abo “Science” tei culture bai kebengmung gwnang hinwi phunugo.

Khristian bisingo baksa khe baithang phola tornani tei slaijaknani bagwi baibelno samungo thepa ya, tamo khe tongtharni kókno phunukna bagwi simi samungo thepao. Baksa khe sao, baibelni laibuma tei tangbaithangni phan (authority) abo chini sal brúmni langmao thepana nang ya. Bororok baibelni sakao phan (authority) tongmani khibi laimani kwlaikuk lama wng kha bohrok baibelno samung nangjak ya, Baibel porinani jora rwya, tei Elembakni kókno langmao thepa ya.

`Phunukjak Elembak bwtangni tong halogo Kaitorni kók sajakma tamo khlai kha? Jir. 38:1–4, Johon 3:1–10, tei Samung 16:25–34.`

Agini molo swijaknai manwirok manthok ya tei painani belai morok. Aboni bagwi bororok porinani seb man ya. Hakhe diyarirokni bising twi Kaitorni kók sakhlainani belai nangkukmani wng kha. Phola Kwtharni phan bai Kaitorni kók swijak phano eba bukhuk bai sajakma phano wng thung—abo bwkhani sumsokno phunugwi mano, borokno kwtal khe sláiwi mano. Kaitorni a kók khe chwngno kha kiphilwi kók khnanani bagwi ringo (Samung 5:29; Hibru 4:12).
