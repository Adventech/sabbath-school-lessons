---
title:  Jisuni Phan (Authority)
date:   30/11/2026
---

`Jisuni phanni (authority) yaphang tamo? Bini phanni (authority) sakki tamo? Mat. 21:23–27 tei Johon 17:2.`

Jisu phrwngwi tong phuru ochairok bini thani phaiwi swng phai kha omo hai samung khlainani sabo bono phan rwkha hinwi. Tamni hwn ba Jisu rwngnogo lekhapora khlaijak buya, eba thumnokni hodarokni thani swrwngjak buya. Phiya bohrokni swngmungni kok phirokmung rw yaui Jisu bohrokno swngmung khlai phikha baptisma rwnai Johon khe saboni phan bai baptisma rwui tong hinwi; phiya Jisuni o kiphilwi swngmungo bini kok phirokmung tongo. Hakhe bohrok kok phirokmung rw liya, Kaitorni phan bai Johon samung khlaimano bohrok gosiya wngma klai kha, tei Johon hai khe Kaitor bai rohorjaknai Jisuni phanno’-bo (authority) bohrok gosi liya.

`Jisuni phanno (authority) twiwi phunukjak bwtangrok chwngno tamo sa? Mat. 7:28, 29; Mark 1:21–27; Luk 8:22–25; Luk 9:1; Johon 5:25–27; tei Johon 7:38.`

Jisu le borokno snamnaisa (Johon 1:3) tei mwthangnais (Rum 3:24) wngwi bebak phan (authority) bini se. Bo wng kha Kaitorni kok. Jisu ha sakao tong phuru Kaitorni phanni samung khlaimani hai kebo khlaiwi man ya. Jisu phrwngmano khnanairok uanama chajak bai kha; tamni hwn b abo phan (authority) gwnang hai khe se phrwngo (Mat. 7:29).

Bebak kothoma kaham bwchap-o o swngcharni diyarini phan wng ya abo hai Jisuni phanni kokrok swiwi narwkjak kha. Jisu sumsok nakar kha (Mark 2:10, 11), mwtai hamya rwkhlai kha (Mark 3:15), tei bo bororokni uansukmano’-bo sai mano (Johon 2:24, 25). Bo kwthwino mwthangwi mwthangnai langma rwo (Johon 10:28). Chini mwthangnaisa wngwi ha sakao tong phuru Jisu Apha Kaitor bai rwjakmani phanno samungo thepa kha (Johon 17:2), tei bo bebak jorao buphano kha beraui thansa khe tongo (Johon 5:19), tei bini sinimungno bo gosimano phunukna bagwi jora jorao bo Elembakno phunugo. Jisuni thani phan tongmani abo bo bini sagriroknobo rwui mano (Mark 6:7). Bo Kaitorni kok wngma ba, bono sirwmani kok diyarirokni thanibo phai kha, tamni hwnba diyarirokni bising twi se bok ok sao ba (Phun. 19:10).
