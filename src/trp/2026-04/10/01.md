### Hatibarsani Bagwi Swrwngjaknai
Lamrwma 4:10–16, Luk 8:22–25, Johon 3:1–10, Samung 16:25–34, 1 Kor. 5:1–5, Luk 7:28.

> <p>Muitu Bwtang</p>
> “Kaitorni kók wng kha kwthang, abo wng kha nangwi tongo. Babai nwnwi seng kubukni slái phano omo buk kugo. Omo bwkha bai phola thansa wng thani, jora bai pecha thansa wng thani aro’-no swkago. Omo borokni bwkhani muchungma bai uansukmano se romo” (Hibru 4:12, KOKBSI).

Omo hai salsa November 9, 1989 salmario Communist East Germany ni TV live phaiwi Günter Schabowski kok samani le Berlin Wall bainani karon snam kha. East Germany ni press

ni khukphang wngwi Schabowski haphangni kwtal raidani kók sana thang thani live press conference ni jorao koroui bo chaya kók sajak twi wngwi thang kha. Phiya bo sana nangmani kók khe East Germany ni bororok jora kolok tei visa khlai baimani ulo khe West Germany-o habwi mannai hinwi. Tamo khe bo saui rwkha, East Germany ni bororok West Germany habwi mannai hinwi. Khoroksa kóktwma thumnai Schabowski’-no raida slaijakmani kokno twiwi swng phuru khe bo sakha, “Ang saui manma jora twi o raida ... ler yaui dakti wngnai.” Hakhe swrapsa ulo kheno bororok border-o kuthumwi phai lai kha, haphang raida sakhlaima twino East Germany ni bororok West Germany-o habnani hinwi.

Jora jorao bororok company eba haktorni bagwi kók sanani phan rwjago. Phiya Kaitorni kók sanairokni eba Baibelni diyarirok bubo hai phan (authority) rwjak abono twiwi o hatibaro nai lai anw. Phiya Kaitorni kók swiya diyari Nathan, tei baptisma rwnai Johon, phiya Ellen G White’-nibo hai diyarirokni phan (authority) bubo hai?

_Dec. 5 Sabat bagwi o hatibarsani rwngchapno swrwngwi tiyar wng di._
