---
title:  Kaitorni Phan (Authority) bai Kwrwngma
date:   29/11/2026
---

Nini bagwi khoroksa swk borokno nwng kók sarinani hwn khe, nwng poito khlaimani bororoknono gosimung rwnai, hai ya da? Phiya a borok nwng sabo, tamo khlai sina nangnai.

`Khuktang di Lamrwma 2:11–15 tei Lamrwma 3:1–3, 10–13. Musi mera mwrwgwi tong phuru Kaitor bahai khe bono bini kok Sanai snamnani tiyar khlai kha?`

Musi nokha bisi brwichi romwi bini langmao Ejipni lekhapora swrwng kha. Phiya a lekhapora bono Kaitorni kok Sanai wngnani tiyar khlai man liya. Bo tei nokha bisi brwichi hasing panthoro mera mwrwknai wngwi jora katokna nang kha, aro bo swngcharni boyar bai hakchalwi tong kha. Bo Habugrani bwsla wngwi kwbangma swrwngmanirok pogwi thangna nang kha. Israilsarokno phiyokna bagwi Kaitorni samungo gwdalnani swkang Musi Kaitorno kaham khe sinina nang kha; tamni hwn ba bo Kaitorni bagwi kók sana nangnai.

`Khuktang di Lamrwma 4:10–16. Diyarirok bahai khe ringjak tei bahai khe samung khlai abo Kaitor bai Musi Kók salaima bising twi chwng tamo swrwngwi man?`

Musi khe Kaitor hai wngnani bagwi dagijak kha, tei Aron khe Musini khukphang wngna bagwi dagijak kha (Lamrwma 4:16; Lamrwma 7:1). Musi bai Aronno khlaima hai kheno Kaitor le diyarirokno bini kok rwmani buino sana bagwi khlaio.

Swkango le Musi khlaiwi man liya. Musi muitu manjak kha, jephuru puila bo Israilrok bai malaina thango, aphuru saboni phan (authority) bai phaikha hinwi bono swngjak kha (Lamrwma 2:14). Musi bubo hai wng kha Kaitor sai mano. Hakhe Kaitor kha logwi Musini kirimungno swkwma kha. Musi kwbangma sakno katinani lama phunukmarokno Kaitor lama phunuk kha, haiphano se Musi a samungno khlainani muchungjak ya. Bini kirima khe poito khlai ya wngmani-o sogwi kha. Kaitor bono samungo thepanani phan da tong hinwi bo Kaitorno swng kha. Aphuru khe Kaitor a muchung ya wngnai diyarino thangnani bagwi paithakni dagimung rwkha (Lamrwma 3:14–17).
