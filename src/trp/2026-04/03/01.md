### Hatibarsani Bagwi Swrwngjaknai
Jakob 5:17, 18; 1 Ha. 19:1–16; 1 Ha. 22:1–14; Lamrwma 15:20; 2 Ha. 22:11–14; Jir. 14:1–15.

> <p>Muitu Bwtang</p>
> “Bórok phung aidrobo bachaui Tekoani hasing panthoro tháng lai kha, bórok tháng lai phuru khe Jihosapat bachaui sa kha, ‘Juda tei Jerusalemo tongnairok ani kókno khna di, norok Subraiyung Kaitorno poito chá di, norok songchajaknai, bini diyarirokno poito chá di, norokni samung chanai’ ” (2 Bosong 20:20, KOKBSI).

The Global Positioning System (GPS) wng kha bororokno biyang twi himnai lama phunukna bagwi snamjak kha. Hakhe Kaitorni bororokbo tabuk tei thinango Chuknohani “lama phunukmung” bai twiduljakna nangnai. Agini molo Kaitor bini bororokno Swmai Kwchamni Diyarirokni bising twi lama phunuk kha. Diyarirok kok samani, kokbiti rwmani, lobmung swimani, tei rwchapmung swimani akorokni bising twi Kaitorni bororokno Kaitorni kok sakha.

O hatibaro swmai kwchamni molni diyarirokno nai lai anw, aboni bising twi chwng buji mannai Kaitor baksa bini selengrokni bising twi kok diyarini suar rwmani kokno.

Phiya a brorokbo chwng hai kirinai, jolinai, tei kha bainai tongmani. Chwng haino bohrokni langmao jwngjal tongo.

A Kaitorni selengrokni bising twi chwng kisa swk tamo swrwngwi mano?

_October 17 Sabat salni bagwi o hatibarsani rwngchapno swrwngwi tiyar wng di._
