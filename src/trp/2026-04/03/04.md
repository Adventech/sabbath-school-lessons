---
title:  Diyarirokni Samungrok
date:   13/10/2026
---

`Khuktang di 1 Samuel 8:4–22 tei Lebi 8:10–13. Habugrarok tei Ochairokni thanio diyarirok bahai khe samung khlai?`

Diyarirok Habugrarokni kaham tei hamya kainwi joraono bohrok bai malaio. Jephuru Israilrok Habugra san lai kha (1 Sam. 8:4–22), bohrok salai kha, “buini dopharok hai khe chwngnobo khoroksa habugra chongwi rw di” (1 Sam. 8:5). Bebak molo’-no Kaitorni bororok kirimasingcha hamya-o yapri seo: bohrokni arini bororokni tongmung hai tongna muchungjakmani. Kaitorni diyarirok bai bohrok thinango tamo wngnai hus khlaiwi rwjak phano aphuru bohrok romwi man liya bohrokni sanmung tamo bwthai tubunai (1 Sam. 8:11–18). Bohrokni sanmungno hus khlaiwi rwjak phano, bohrok muchungma bagwi Diyari Samuel Dakti kheno puilani Habugra chongwi ma rwkha. Laibumao Israilrokni puila Habugra wng kha Habugra Sol.

Swmai Kwchamni 1 tei 2 Habugra tei 1 tei 2 Bosong Bijap-o Israil tei Judani Habugrarokni tongmungrokno swijak kha. Phiyaba laibumao phunukjak kha kahamni slai hamya Habugrarok se kwbang. Hakhe Diyarirokni kaisa samung wng kha kok khnaya Habugrarokni Kaitorni kerongni kok khnnai khlaina bagwi bohrokno kiphilwi tubunani. Omo hai samungni bagwi diyarirok baksa butharjak kha, tei baksa satokjak kha (1 Habugra 18:4–19, 2 Bosong 24:20–22).

Diyarirok Ochairok baibo malaio. Kaitorni Diyari Musi bini takhuk Aron bai Aronni bwslasongno Israilrokni puila Ochairok chong kha (Lebi 8:10–13). Phiyaba diyarirokni bising twi ochairok Kaitor bai hwnjakmani jorabo tongo (Hos. 6:9, Mal. 2:1–3). Phunukmarini bagwi Elini nukhungno naidi, bohrok samung chaya khlaima bagwi hwnjak kha (1 Sam. 2:27–36). Samung chayarok omo hai khe diyarirok bai phunugwi hwnjak kha.

Diyarirok khe bororokni phrwngnai se (2 Bosong 34). Bohrok Kaitorni kokno Habugrarok, Ochairok, tei dopharokni thani sao(Jir. 5:31, Esek. 22:26). Diyarirok bini ssmo tongnai bororokno Kaitorni chapmung tamo saui khna rwo, omo hai khe Swmai Kwchamni diyarirok uaire uai Kaitorni kok saui tongo (Jis. 60:1–3; Jis. 62:1, 2). Diyarirok Kaitor bai rwjakma kokno buino sanani bagwi simi ya, tamo khe jora jorao abohrokno swiwi narwknani tei khatinani bagwibo bohrokni samung (2 Bosong 13:22). Diyarirok khe belai surinai, Kaitorni kok Sanai, Kaitorni samung tangnai, tei boroknobo nainai nuknairok se (Jir. 42:4; 2 Habugra 22:15–17).
