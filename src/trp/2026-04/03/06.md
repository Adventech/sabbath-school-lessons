---
title:  Tatal Diyarirok
date:   15/10/2026
---

Kaitor jesa phano khlai khe Soitan le aboni tatal snamo. Soitan tatal diyarirokbo snam kha. Israilrok tatal tei kubui diyarirok bai malai kha.

`Khuktang di Jirimia 14:1–15. Tatal diyarirokno twiwi Kaitor tamo sakha?`

Jirimia 14 beremo Kaitor Jirimiano Juda buj thumjaknani kok rwkha, sengg (choba), asari, tei chacharara bai khe abo buj thumjaknai. A jorao tatal diyarirok Jirimiani kokno chaya hwn kha. Phiya Jirimia sraisrai khe sakha: “Subraiyung ano ahai hwnwi sa kha: ‘Kók diyari sanairok ani mungwi tatal khe thinangni kókrok sa-o; ang bórokno robo rohor ya tei sa di hwnwibo dagi ya. Bórok norokno tatal khe imang-mukthangni kók se sa-o, nang ya-sók ya phunukjakmani kókrok se sa-o, bórok uansuk tẃi kókrok tagwi saui bandimung kókrok sa-o’ ” (Jir. 14:14, KOKBSI).

Tatal diyarirok khe samung hamya khlaio; bohrok “tatal” mwtaino khulummani phan bai kok Diyari salaio (Jir. 23:13, 14). Kaito hus khe rwkha, “ ‘Ang bórokno robo rohor ya tei sa di hwnwibo dagi ya’ ” (Jir. 14:14, KOKBSI).Bohrok wng kha “ ‘tatal kók diyari sanai diyarirokni bwkha bisingni tatal kók diyarirok’ ” sao (Jir. 23:26, NKJV). “ ‘Sakni bwkhani muchung tẃi khe kók sanai … diyari tatalrok’-no’ ” kebengwi Kok Diyari sanani bagwi Esekiel dagijak kha (Esek. 13:2, KOKBSI). Tatal diyarirok khe kwbang bororokni uansukmungno rwktharo. 1 Habugra 22 beremo Habugra Ahab khorok brwira (400) “diyarirokno” ringwi malai phuru abo haino wng kha (1 Habugra 22:6). A diyarirok khorang thansa khe Habugrano salai kha Ramot-giliad bai choba khlai di hinwi. Aserni diyarirokni kok khna baiwi Judani Habugra Jihosapat bai Ahab kokbiti chab kha. Chobao thangnai da thang ya Diyari Mikaiano swng di hinwi Jihosapat Habugra Ahabno sakha. “A jora-o Mikaiano naharna thángnai tẃiphang bono hai hwnwi sa kha, ‘Bebak diyarirok’-no bugrano páinai hwnwi sa kha ba nwngbo haino bwkha bacha rwui sa khe hamnai’ ” (1 Habugra 22:13, KOKBSI).

Aserni diyarirok hai khe Mikaia tatal sana muchungjak ya, borok kwbangmani uansukmungno bo rwkthar liya. Tamo sanai Kaitor bono saui rw thung abono se bo naisingwi tong kha (1 Habugra 22:14).

`Nwng Diyari wng ya phano kwbang bororokni uansukmungno rwktharnani belaino kwlai. Phiya abo tamoni bagwi kaham ya?`
