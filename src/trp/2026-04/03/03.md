---
title:  Kubui Diyarirokno Sinimani
date:   12/10/2026
---

Swkango Israilrokni laibumao tatal diyarirok tongo (Uainwi Saphima 18:20). Phiyaba Kaitor bini bororokno kubui diyarirokni bising twi khe twimani yakarwi rw ya.

Kaitorni kubui diyarirok bebak jorao’-no kok nawgwi, kubui khe, tei koinene khe Kaitorni kokno bororokno yapharo.

`Khuktang di 1 Habugra 22:1–14. Kok narwknai diyarirok bahai khe samung khlai?`

Khoroksa Habugra diyarini kokno hai hwnwi sa kha, “ ‘Tamo khe ang bono naislero, tamni hwn ba bo uáisa phano ani bagwi mungsa kaham kók diyari sa ya kho; ani bagwi hamya máng se sa-o’ ” (1 Habugra 22:8, KOKBSI). Diyari bo borokno, phiya bo kubui kokno sanani kirijak ya, Habugra bai kaham hwnjaknano uansuk yaui kiri ya khe Habugrano suda bok ok kubui sao.

Mikaia bini thani bini jorao kok kubui phunukjak phuru khorok brwira (400) tatal diyarirokno kebengwi bacha kha. Habugra hamya Ahab tei bini bihik Jesebel ni hamya koktwmabo bo Kaitorni thani manma twi kok kubuino saui khna rwkha, bui bono tamo hwnnai abono bo thopsa phano uansuk liya.

`Khuktang di 1 Samuel 9:3–14. Bui bahai khe Kaitorni diyarirokno nuk?`

Baibelo diyarirokni langmani tongmungni phunukmari kwbangma tongo. Romdi, Sol bini bupha Kish bai bini korai chirka naina bagwi rohorjak kha. Lama him lai tong phuru Solno seleng sakha, korai chirka rutukma ta thak di hwnwi, tamni hwn ba “ ‘o kamiyungo khoroksa Kaitorni borok tongo, bo kaham khe’-no bórom khlaijago’ ” (1 Sam. 9:6, KOKBSI). Omo Samuel diyarini kok sajagwi tongo, bini bising twi Kaitorni diyarini tongmung phunukjak kha. Elembago kubui Diyari hwn khe Kaitorni borok” hinwi sinijago. Phiya “Kaitorni borok” wng kheno bini chaya thopsa phano kwrwi kha abo hai ya.
