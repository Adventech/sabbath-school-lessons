---
title:  Diyarirokni Tongmung
date:   11/10/2026
---

Thangnai hatibaro chwng nuk kha Diyari Elija aswk kebengjak phano Israil-o samung kotor khlai kha. Kaitor bini bising twi samung khlaimani abo uanamasingcha. Tei… tamo tong kho?

`Jakob bahai khe Elijani kokno sakha Jakob 5:17 bwtango naidi. “Eliabo chwng hai bahan-bwthẃi gwnang borokno” hinwi bo samani kokmang tamo?`

Elijah Karmel hathaio samung kotor khlai kha (1 Habugra 18); Swmai Kwchamni laibumao Kaitor bini bising twi soidomni samung khlai kha: Elijah ni khorang tei kokthai bai Chuknohani hor nongkhorwi phai kha (1 Habugra 18:38)! Abo hai samung khlai baiwi a borok belai tongthokjak khna hinwi nwng uansugwi mano.

`Khuktang di 1 Habugra 19:1–16. Kaitorni Diyari kha khamjakmano twiwi o bwtango tamo sakha?`

“ ‘Wng kha tei nang li ya! Subraiyung tabuk ano tlang si di, angbo ani chuchusongni slái kaham ya!’ ” (1 Habugra 19:4, KOKBSI). Omo wng kha belai kha khammani kok. Tamoni bagwi?

Bini kokthaio chwng man kha, “ ‘Subraiyung kwplaisa Kaitor, ang nini bagwi bwswk se bwkha nangjak; hai phano Israilni bororok nwng bai swmai narwk li ya, nini háyárok swbai kha tei nini diyarirokno seng bai tanwi buthar bai kha. Ang simi se tong rwk si-o tei bórok ano butharna nai tong lai kha’ ” (1 Habugra 19:10, NKJV). Omo wng kha bo uansukma hai bini samung cha liya wngwi bo kapmani hai. Oro Kaitorni bororokni tongmungno chwng nugwi mano. Kok wng kha “Eliabo chwng hai bahan-bwthẃi gwnang borokno.”

`Nini samungo “kwlainani” abo tamoni bagwi aswk kwlai? Nini kha baimani jora nono chubanani tamo Baibelni kok swmai tong?`
