Swmai Kwchamni diyarirokni bagwibo Kaitorni kok Sanai wngnani aswk kwlai ya. Ellen G. White’-bo abo haino wng kha. Thangnai hatibarni Phasal-o nukmani hai, Ellen G. White Kaitor bai ringjak phuru lachima gwnang tei sak hamya se. Uainwi Kaitor bai bo phunukjakmani (vision) khe bo Kaitor bai ringjakmano sraisrai wng kha, bo hai hwnwi swikha:

“Aboni hatibarsani ulo Subrai ano phunuk phikha, ang satokjakma bising twi thangna nangnai abo phunukjak kha; ang bui bai khibijakna nangnai, birman manna nangnai, bwkha tekto manna nangnai. . . .

O phunukjakmani ano belai uana rwkha. Ani sak belai hamya tei ang bisi chisni umor si kho. . . . Ang jogojogo khe suri kha a poja khoroksa swkni thani yapharjak thung hinwi. Phiya je pohorno ang man kha, ‘Nono phunukmani abo buino saui man rwdi.’ Phiya o kokno swngcharo twlangnani ang bwkhao uansuk man ya. Ang aswk bwkhao puitu kwrwi. Ang mangkhoro naisik kha. Je samung rwjak kha, abo twlangnani slai ang thwimungno se hamjakma hai ma tongo.”—Spiritual Gifts, vol. 2, pp. 35, 36.

Ellen Harmon (White) Kaitor bai ringjakmano yachaknani slai thwinani se muchungjak sio, nwng abo nugwi da man kha? Bini tangpalsong surimani tei kha rakrwmani bising twi se bo bini langmani samungno “yachak kha”—Ellen G. White, Spiritual Gifts, vol. 2, p. 38. Bini khorang belai pirjak kha—thinangni Seventh-day Adventist Church jorano bini khorang sok kha. Ellen Harmon (White) borok wngma ba binibo chaya khlaima, bahan-bwthwini potalijakma, nukhungni birman, akorok tongnai. Bini tongmungo chwng swrwngwi mano: Kaitor le kaham wng ya phano bini samung khlaina muchungnairokno se chongwi nao.

Phola Kwtharni chubamung bai Ellen G. White’-ni kok diyarini samung poito khlainairokno kha rak rwkha, tei Baibelni kubui bai kwrwng rwkha. 1860 bisio Seventh-day Adventist denomination tisa thani tei swngcharo suari khorokthamni kok pir thanio bo samung khlai kha. Kaitorni chubamung bai 1863 bisio Seventh-day Adventist Church songchajak kha. Ellen G. White’-ni kok diyarini khorangni bising twi hayung gwnangwi Adventist health and educational systems songchajak kha tei pirjak kha. Bini teibo yapharmung khe 100,000 ni slai kwbang bwkang bini swimungrok. Ellen G. White bini swimungni bising twi tabukni molni swngcharni thani kok sakha.
