`Khuktang di Lamrwma 15:20, Buj. 4:4, 2 Habugra 22:14, tei Nehemia 6:14. Kok diyarino twiwi oro tamo sajak kha?`

Bwrwirokbo Diyari tongo. (Hebrew kokthai nevi'ah bahai khe sajak kha naidi [Lamrwma 15:20] tei Greek kokthai prophētis bahai khe sajak kha naidi [Luk 2:36].) Mika 6:4 bwtango sao, Musi tei Aron bai baksa bororokni thani rohorjaknairokni bisingo Miriambo tongo. Israilrok twjlang kwchak basumani ulo kwplaini rwchapmung rwchapnai wng kha Miriam, bono diyarijwk hinw sinijago (Lamrwma 15:20, 21). Chwla diyarirok hai Miriam diyarijwkbo chaya kwrwi wng ya. Musi tei Ithiopiani bihikni thani bobo lachimasingcha samung khlai kha. Aboni bagwi Miriam le satokjak kha (Naidi, Lekhamung 12:1–10).

Deborani kothomao chwng tei khoroksa diyarini samungno nugwi mano, Israil-o buj thumnai wngwi bo samung khlai kha (Buj. 4:1–4).

Hakhe chwng sraisrai sai mano Debora wng kha (Buj. 4:4) Kaitor bai phunukjak kha (Buj. 4:4–9, 14–22). Bini kwplai wngmani rwchapmung (Buj. 5:2–31) wng kha a jorani bagwi Kaitor dopharokno bohrokni bagwi kasuwi mwthangmanino saui manmani.

`Khuktang di 2 Habugra 22:11–14. Tamoni bagwi Habugra Josiani borok Hulda-ni thani thang kha?`

“Kerongni bijap” porimani ulo (2 Habugra 22:8, KOKBSI) (Uainwi Saphima Bijap) jephuru Habugra kok yachagwi “bini rino sichigo” (2 Habugra 22:11, KOKBSI) aphuru Habugra Josiani kokbiti najaknai khoroksa borok wng kha Hulda “Diyarijwk” (2 Habugra 22:14, 2 Bosong 34:22). Jephuru nangkukmani jora wng kha aphuru Habugrani borok Huldani thani thang kha. Juda buj thumjaknano twiwi Hulda Kaitorni aroni kok man kha. Huldani kok Diyari manwi Josia samung kaham khlai kha tei Kaitor bini bororokni sakao buj thumnani yakni kati kha.
