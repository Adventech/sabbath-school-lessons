date:   17/12/2026
---

Tabukni molni kwbangkuk bororok Baibelni diyarirokno simi se poito khlaio, phiya chini molo le diyarirok tongo hinwi bohrok poito da khlai? Tumungno Joel 2:28–31 bwtango mol paithago kok diyarini suar rwjaknai hwnwi swmai rwjak khe, abo bubo hai wngnai?

`Khuktang di Samung 2:36–42. Pentikos salo Pitor tamo hinwi kok pir kha? Pitorni kok khnanairok le tamo salai kha?`

31 AD bisini Pentikos salo Phola Kwthar lukhlaijakmani wng kha mol paithago Phola Kwthar lukhlaijak phinai aboni sampili se. Pentikos wng kha Jisuno tisamani, kwbangma bororok kha kiphilmani, bororok tiyar wngwi Kaitorni samungno charwmanino se phunuk kha. Pitor Khristoni kok samani abo Phola Kwtharni phan bai se. Aboni bagwi khorok thamsai (3000) kha kiphilwi baptisma nakha. “Swkangni uatwi” Phola Kwtharni hamari bai bwchlwi kaham kaijak kha, abo wng kha Motha—abo Jisu phaiya sak poito khlainairokni bwsakno thansa khlainani tei Jisuni hamjakmano pirnani.

Swkangni uatwi “bwchlwi kaham sarnani bagwi rwjakma haino, ‘ulni uatwi’ khe mai kumunno raui thumna phuru rwjaknai.”—Ellen G. White, The Great Controversy, p. 611. Kok khe ahai, kwtal molo kok diyari phunukjakmani wng kha ulni uatwi—abo Pentikosni salo swkangni uatwi phai phuru phunukjakmani bai kwbang kukno gothokmung tongo.

Joel 2:28–31 bwtango sao, mol paithago sikla-sikli, umor chakra, tei chwla bwrwi kok diyarini suar rwjaknai. Phola Kwthar chongwi namani bororokni bising twi Kaitor kok Sanai. Phola Kwthar bai chongjagwi mol paithakni Kaitorni kok sanairok le Jisuni kok simi se Sanai, sumsok yakarna bagwi bororokno ringnai, tei Kaitorni samung khlai baina bagwi bororokno ringnai. Ellen G. White’-ni diyarini samungo’-bo o kaitham sinimung tongo. Bini langma tei swimungni bising twi Jisu tei Elembakno bo tisa kha. Kha kiphilnani tei kwmanairokno rutukna bagwi Kaitorni samungo gwdalnani bagwi bo Seventh-day Adventists rokno tei buinobo ringwi tongo.

`Ellen G. White’-ni samungni bising twi uanamsingcha kok diyarini suar rwjakmao nini bwswk kaham wng kha?`
