---
title:  Joel’-ni Swmai
date:   13/12/2026
---

`Khuktang di Joel 2:28–32. Mol páithago tamo khlaina bagwi Kaitor kók rwkha?`

Joel wng kha around 700 B.C. ni diyari, bo thinangni bagwi nangkukmani kók rwkha. O bwtango khúktang khe chwng swngmung khlaio bo agini molni a jorani kókno de saui tong eba thinangni kókno.

Baibel swrwngnairok kók diyarino kainwi khe bak kha—Phang-phang kók diyari bai Mari phunugwi kók diyari. Phang-phang kók diyari khe kaisa swk dopha, eba soi mung eba number khurchauino kók diyari samani (Phunukjakmarini bagwi naidi, Jir. 25:1-11). Mari phunugwi kók diyari samani khe mari gwnang kók diyari se, abo jorani bagwibo marini number bai se phunukjago, tei aro uanamasingcha marirokno twiwi mangno kók diyari sajago (Phunukjakmarini bagwi naidi, Mathai 24).

Joel 2:28–31 bwtang khe tamo khlai? O kók diyari mari phunugwi kók diyari sajakma thinangni kókno se phunukma klaio. Thinango se chwng o kók diyarino nang phaimani nuknani rutukna nangnai. Kaham khe naichom di, oro sajakma Kaitor bini kók diyarino rwnai abo jora wng kha “Subraiyungni a kirimasingcha sal phaina swkang” (Joel 2:31, KOKBSI). Abo bo jorani kók? Jisu puila ha sakao phaimani jora de? Jisu tei uáisa phai phinani jorani kók de? Eba o kainwi jorani kókno?

Joel ni kók diyario sal, tei talni kók sajakmano nai khe Baibelni tei kaisa jorani kókno chwng bujiui mano. Jisu bini Sagrirokno sakha mol páithago sal, tal, tei athukurirokni bising twi mari phunukjaknai (Mat. 24:29). Johonbo Phunukjakmani bijap-o omo hai marirokni kókno sakha (Phun. 6:12, 13). Jisu bai Johon khoroknwino Jisu tei uáisa phai phinani swkang mari phunukjakmani kókno se saui tongo.

Hakhe Joel’-ni kók diyari abo mol páithago mari phunukjakmani jorani kókno se sao hinwi chwng bujiui mano. “Nokharogo tei ha saka-o ang uanamasingcha samungrok phunuknai, thẃi bai hór bai hoku duphung saui kasanai. Subraiyungni a kirimasingcha sal phaina swkang sal mwnaknai, tal thẃi hai chaksanai” (Joel 2:30, 31, KOKBSI).

`Kók diyari sajakma bebakno nang phaimani kókrokno uánsuk naidi. Jisu phaina swkangni kók diyarirokbo nang phainani tong kho. Hakhe omorok bahai khe chwng Kaitorno poito khlai tong mannani-o chuba?`
