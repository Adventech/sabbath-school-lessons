`Khuktang di Samung 2:1–21. Jisu puila hao phai phuruni jorao Pitor tamoni bagwi Joel ni a kók diyarino nahar kha?`

Swmai kwtalni bwchap-o o bwtangono Swmai Kwchamni kók kolok khe thepajak kha. Phiya Pitor tamoni bagwi omo hai khe kók sakha? Chwng o kókno pori phuru Pitorno swngwi mano, “Pitor, nwng bubo tamo mari nuk kha? Nini jorao’-o mungsa mari nuk ya bwle.” Phiya Pitor omo hai khe kók phirokmung rwnai, “Ih, marirok nukjak kha. Jisu Kruso khachijagwi tong phuru dam kwtham sal mwnagwi thang kha (Mark 15:33).

Pitorni thani abo belai gothokjak kókno, wngwi mano chwng bini jorao a kók diyarino gothokjak ya. Phiya Joel’-ni kók diyari thinango’-bo nang phainai hinwi chwng khatungwi mano. Joel 2:23 bwtango swkangni uatwi tei ulni uatwi o kainwi kókno khurchajak kha. Samung 2 berem wng kha swkangni uatwi wngwi o kók diyari nang phaimani se. Phiya o kók diyari nang phainani páithak tong kho, abo wng kha mol painani swkang ulni uatwini bising twi Kaitorni phola lukhlaiwi rwjakmani jorao.

Jora jorao bororok kók diyarino “thinangni kók” hinwi salaio. O kók chaya kwrwi. Phiyaba Baibel-o kók diyarirok khe bororokno kha kiphilnani bagwi ringo. Pitor kók sa phurubo bororok kha kiphil lai kha.

“Bororok omono khnaui bwkha nangjak bai kha, hakhe Pitor bai tei chonghorrokno hai hwnwi sẃng kha, ‘Takhukrok, chwng tamo khlainai?’ ” (Samung 2:37, KOKBSI).

Pitor tamo kók phirokmung rwkha?

“ ‘Norok jotono sumsok nakarjakna tẃi khe norokni sumsok yakar di tei Jisu Khristoni mungwi baptisma wng di, hakhe Kaitor rwmani a Phola Kwtharno norok mannai. Tamni hwn khe chini Kaitor Subrai bini aro phaina tẃi khe ringmani jotoni thani bo kók rwjak norokni thani, nwsa-nwtẃirokni thani, phiya hakchalo tongnai jotoni thani’-no bo kók rwjak’ ” (Samung 2:38, 39, KOKBSI). Baptisma rwnai Johonbo diyari wngma bagwi kha kiphilnani bagwi bororokno ringo. Jisu puila hao phainani kókno bo sakhlai phano bororokno sumsokni kha kiphilnanibo bo pir kha. “ ‘Sumsok yakar di, Kaitorni ha sampha kha!’ ” (Mat.3:2, KOKBSI).

Hakhe Joel 2 ni kók diyari páithak nang phainani khe thinango se. Joel tei Pitor hai kheno mol páithakni diyarirok le bororokno kha kiphil di hinwi ringnai, Jisuno phunuknai, tei Kaitorni mol páithakni mothano chubanai.
