### Hatibarsani Bagwi Swrwngjaknai
Joel 2:28–32, Samung 2:1–21, Epis 4:11–14, Phun. 12:17, Phun. 19:10, Samung 2:36–42.

> <p>Muitu Bwtang</p>
> “ ‘Páithago khe ang bebak bororokni thani ani pholano lukhlai rwnai; norokni sajwk sajlasong kók diyari sanai, norokni chakrarok imangrok nuknai, siklarokbo imang-mukthangrok nuknai. Bwrwi tei chwla selengrokno suda ang a salo ani pholano lukhlai rwna’ ” (Joel 2:28, 29, KOKBSI).

Ih, baksa haphangni kerongo, “sunset provision” mungwi kerong togo. Sal hab khe a kerong mungsa chuk ya wngwi thango. Aboni kókmang wng kha a kerong ahaino kaisa swk salmario paiwi

thangnai. Kwbangma karonni bagwi haphang a kerongno snam kha. Rom di, nangmani jorao haphang kerong snam khe abo swrapsani bagwi baksa hani bororokni bagwi manthai (rights) tongnai. Phiya ulo khe salmari chongwi a kerongno swrapsani bagwi rosaui nai mano.

Agini molo Phola Kwthar kók diyarini suar yaphar phuru a “sunset provision” hai kerongno de thepa khwna? Jisu phaina swkang kók diyarini suarno rosaui najakmani jora eba salmari da tongno? Thangnai hatibaro chwng swrwng kha, nangkukmani jorao Kaitor diyarirokni bising twi bini bororokni thani kók sao. O hatibaro khe chwng mol páithakni kók diyarini suarno twiwi swrwng lai anw—aboni naharmung tei bwswk jora a suar tongnai. Tei Jisu phaina swkang Ellen G. White’-ni diyarini samungno’-bo chwng nai lai anw.
