---
title:  Mol Paithakni Tongrwknai Bosong
date:   16/12/2026
---

Phunukjakmani 12 beremo Chúknohani simi chengwi mol páithak jora choba kotor tongmanino phunuk kha.

`Khúktang di Phunukjakmani 12:17. “Jisu sirwmani” tamono hwn? Phunukjakmani 19:10 tei Phunukjakmani 22:8, 9 bwtangrokno nai phidi.`

Mol paithakni mothani kainwi sinimung Phunukjakmani 12:17 bwtango sajak kha. Mol paithakni Motha hwn khe dagimung kaichi twi-o (Sabat salbo twio) tei bohrokni thani “Jisu sirwmani” tongo. A Jisu sirwmani le tamono hwn?

Baksa borok sao “Jisu sirwmani” hwnmani abo chwng Jisuni kók buino samani tei kothoma kaham pirwi Jisuno buino sini rwmani se hinwi (naidi Phun. 19:10 KOKBSI/NIV: “Jisu si rwmano kwrak khe poito khlaiwi tongnai,” Khristianrok Jisuni kókno samani). Kubuni Baibel bwtango khe kókmang juda khe sao, Jisu se Motha-o sirwmani rwo (Phun. 19:10, GNT: “Jisu phunukmani kubuirok,” Jisu se sirwmani). Grik kókthaini omo hai kainwi kókmang khe kók slaijak kha. Phiya Johon tamo sana naikha?

Mol páithakni Kaitorni Mothani kainwi sinimungno nai khe o kainwino gothokmung tongmani núkjago. “Kaitorni dagimung” khe Kaitorni aroni phaima haino “Jisu sirwmani” khe Jisuni aroni phaio. Jisu se sakki rwnai wngo. Bo bini kubui mothano sinio. Bahai khe bo abo khlai? Kók diyarini suarni bising twi khlaio.

Phunukjakmani 19:10 tei Phunukjakmani 22:9 bwtango chwng kók phirokmung mano. Oro “Jisu sirwmani” wng kha “diyarini kók” se hinwi sajago. Johon sakphangno diyari, hakhe bini thani buino phunukna bagwi “Jisu sirwmani” tongo. Diyari khe buino Kaitorni kók sana bagwi se ringjago. Buino kókbiti rwnani, kha kiphilnani kók sanani, kha rak rwnani eba thinangno phunuknani.

Phunukjakmani 12:17 bwtango sao, Jisu bini mothano sini rwmani khe kók diyarini suarni bising twi se. Swmai Kwchamni molo Israil tei Judani thani Kaitor diyarirokno rohorma hai, tei puilani mothao Chonghor Johonno rohorma hai, mol páithago’-bo diyarirok bachanai.

`Ellen G. White ni samungo “diyarini kók” nwng nugwi da man (Phun. 19:10)?`
