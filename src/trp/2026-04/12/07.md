---
title:  Kaitorni Samungni Bagwi Máng se
date:   18/12/2026
---

Epis 4 beremo sao, phola rwmani suarrokni soi naharmung wng kha Kaitorni samung khlai thani poito khlainairokno chubana bagwi se. Ellen G. White’-ni diyarini samungni naharmungbo abo haino. Bini kókni poito khlainairokno Kaitor bai kaham kuthuk tongnani chubao, tei Kaitorni hamjakma buino sanani chubao.

“Christianrokni bebak samungno,” bo sakha, “Seventh-day Adventists rok swngcharni bwskango Jisuno se tisana nangnai. Suari khorokthamni sakhlaimung le Sabat kubuino phunukna bagwi sajak kha. Sabat salni kók tei kubuni kók kubuirokbo buino sana nangnai; phiyaba yaphang khe Jisu se wngna nangnai, Jisuno khibijakthai kwrwi. Khristoni Kruso se khairokma bai kubui thansa khe malaio, tei kaham bai kha kwchang rwma thansa khe motom sulaio. Sumsok gwnangrokno Kalbario se naisik rwna nangnai; chwrai bwsani poito hai khe Mwthangnaisani chukmungo se bwkha berana nangnai, tei bini kahamno yachagwi bini khairokmano poito ma khlainai.”—Gospel Workers, pp. 156, 157.

Bebak poito khlainairok le Kaitorni samungo ma gwdalnai hinwi bo teibo sakha. “Chini motharokni adongrok bebakno bini samungo ma gwdalnai hinwi Subrai naio.”—Evangelism, p. 113.

“Kaitorni samungo kebo selerwi tongthai kwrwi. Bebakno borok rommani samungo thansa khe gwdal di. Khristoni hamjakmani kaham mwthangmungo tubuna bagwi hamya swngcharni bororokno bóhrok thumwi rutuk thung.”—Evangelism, p. 115. Ellen G. White sak baithang Kaitorni kók kha, 1880s bisi khe Europe hao, tei 1890s bisi khe Australia hao.

Kaitorni Kók diyari khe bini samung tangna bagwi bororokno ringmani ba Soitan khe mol páithakni Kaitorni bororokno “jolijak kha” (Phun. 12:17, KOKBSI). Tamni hwn ba bóhrok Jisuno rwktharo, tei bóhrokni thani Khristo rwmani mol páithakni kók tongo, diyarini chubamung tongo. “Chini yaksi-yagra tei bwskango kirimasingcha tongmarokno Kaitor chwngno phunuk kha. Phiyaba kók diyarini bising twi swngcharno lama koro rwnai phemlokmungno chwngno phunuk kha, tei bini bororokno hai hwnwi sakha, ‘Omo se lama, o lama tẃi’-no him di.’ [Jisaia 30:21] . . . Chobayung kotor khe Soitanni phemlokmungno phunuk kha.”—Ellen G. White, Manuscript 31, 1890.

Bororok Jisuno sak yaphar khe Soitan le jolijago, tei Kaitorni kók diyarini khorangno mansak jora twi poito khlai man ya khlainani chaitogo. Soitan le Ellen G. White’-ni kók diyarirokno’-bo “dera rwna” naio. Tamni hwn ba Ellen G. White’-ni swimung wng kha chini jorani slaibo teibo swkangjak jorani kókrok. Soitanni tei kaisa samung khe Ellen G. White’-no tatal diyari hinwi phunuknani. Ellen G. White’-no kebengnani Soitanni kaham kuk lama wng kha tabukni molni bororokno bini swijakrokno pori rw ya khlainani.
