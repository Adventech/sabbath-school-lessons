`Khuktang di Epis 4:11–14. Kaitor tamoni bagwi bini mothao o suarrokno rwkha? Bahai khe kók diyarini suar abo Mothani bagwi Kaitorni chapmungno chubaui tong?`

Phola rwmani suarrok wng kha kothoma kaham pir thanio mothano tisa thani chubanani. O chat ni kaibrwi list nai khe Pol kaibrwi thaiono phola rwmani suarrokni bisingo kók diyarini suarno (eba diyari) ton kha. Kubuni phola rwmani suarrok uáisa simi se khurchajago. Hakhe chwng sai mano kók diyari wng kha nangkukmani phola rwmani suarrokni bisingo kaisa.

Phaidi diyarirokni sinimung eba tongmungrokno nai lai anw:

[1] Diyarirok Kaitor bai se ringjago (1 Habugra 19:16, 19–21 tei Jisaia 6). Bohrok sakno chukya wngjago tei ringjakmano khlaina muchung ya wngo, hai phano Kaitorni kokno khnao. [2] Kaitor diyarirokno phunukmani tei imangni bising twi kok sao. Jora jorao khe Kaitor thinangno phunugo. Tei baksa jorao khe bo bohrokni jorani dopha eba bororokno twiwi kok sao (Daniel 2). [3] Diyarirok Kaitor bai phunukjak phuru bwsago phan kwrwi wngo, hama thagwi thango, phiya ulo phan man phiro (Dan. 10:8–10, 18, 19). [4] Diyarirok buino Kaitorni kok samani lamarok wng kha—kok saui (Samung 21:10–12), eba tail bai phunugwi (Esekiel 4), eba swimung bai (Phun. 2, 3). Diyarirok swi kwrwngrokni chubachubo nao (Jir. 36:1–4). [5] Diyarirok swkangni diyarirokni kokno khibi ya (Jis. 8:20). [6] Diyarirokni langmao bwthai kaham nukjago (Mat. 7:15–20). [7] Soitan kok diyarini suarno’-bo tatal snam kha. Mol paithago tatal diyarirok tongnai (Mat. 24:24, 2 Pit. 2:1).
