---
title:  Swimungno Bujithai
date:   17/11/2026
---

Khoroksa mung gwnang kok pirnaisa kothoma saui khna rwkha, khoroksa borok belai jwngjal wngwi bini langmani bagwi Kaitorni muchungma tam obo sina muchungjak kha. Bo Baibel phiyogwi ahaino yasi kaiwi rwkha, abo khe Mathai 27:5, aro swijak tongo, “Judas a rangno noktharo sarwi klang kha, hakhe thángwi kotok sorwi thwi kha” (KOKBSI). O kokno poriui bo bwkha tongthokjak ya wngwi kubuni bwtang phiyogwi yasi kaiwi rw phikha Luk 10:37 bwtango, aro swijak tongo: “ ‘Tháng di, nwngbo abo hai’-no khlai di,’ hwnwi Jisu bono sa kha” (KOKBSI). O kainwino soi Elembakni bwtang, tamo khe sajakmani kokno eba context se bujithai.

Baibelni swijakmani kokno buji ya wngwi kwbangma nangmani kokrok khibijak kha eba norjak kha. Aboni bagwi kwbangma Elembakni kok swbaiwi samanirok juda juda bang kha.

`Khuktang di Yaphang 3:1–5 tei Mathai 4:1–11. Soitan bahai khe Elembakni swijakmani context norwi Kaitorni kokno swbaiwi sakha?`

Baksa borok sakni simung kwrwini bagwi Babielni kokno chaya khe sao. Phiyaba baksa khe Soitan hai kheno Baibelni kok thepaui chaya khe swbaiwi sao. Phiyaba aboni bwthai chaya se wngo.

Eden thaiblwngo (Yap. 3:1–5; Phun. 12:9), Soitan naithok goron bai borokni kok saui mannai wngwi Ebini bwskango nukjak phai kha. Soitan Kaitorni kokthaini soi context norwi lama koro rwna bagwi chaya khe Kaitorni kokno kok swbaiwi sakha.

Jisu Phola Kwthar bai hasing panthoro twlangjak phuru (Mat. 4:1–11, Luk 4:1–13), “Soitan pohorni suari hai khe Khirtono belokna bagwi hasing panthoro nukjak phai kha.” .”—Ellen G. White, in The Advent Review and Sabbath Herald, December 18, 1888. Jisuno Soitan Lobmung 91:11, 12 bwtangni kokno phunugwi sakha, si twtwi phano sakno kirimasingchao khiklai khe Kaitor mwthang phai anw hinwi.

Eden thaiblwngo tei Hasing panthoro belokmani abo Chobayung Kotor-o tongwi thangnai hinwi phunugo. Kaitorni kok tei swijakrokno chaya khe swibaiwi sajakmani tongnai. Phiyaba Baibelni context no bujiui lama koro ya twi khe tongna bagwi omo chwngno hus khlaiwi rwo.

`Baibel bwtang phunukjakma simi bai, tei context buji yaui lama koro ya twi khe chanaiwi nai ya sak poito khlai ya wngnani tamoni nangmani?`
