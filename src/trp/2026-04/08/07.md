Bahai khe chwng Ellen G. White’-no sai mannai? Ellen G. White’-ni swimungno bujinani tei Sinani bagwi kisa raida naidi.

(1) Surimani, bwkha kaham, tei uansukmung kaham bai khe cheng di, poito khlai ya bwkha bai ta cheng di. Chwng khna thani, pori thani, tei nuk thani chini uansukmung se samung khlai kugo. Chini uansukmungno chubana bagwi chwng Phola Kwthar nango. Ellen G. White sakha: “Tumungno Elembakno sakni sima rwngma bai bujina nai khe jephuru phano kubuino nwng man glak. Subrai sama hai khe swrwngna bagwi se rutuk di.”—Christ’s Object Lessons, p. 112. Omo hai kheno Ellen G. White ni swimungno’-bo pori di.

(2) Nwng pori phuru context’-no naidi. Baksa bororok laibuma tei swijakmani context nai yaui Ellen G. White’-ni swimungno sakni bujimung bai simi naio. Ellen G. White sao, “jora tei thaino naina nangnai.” —Selected Messages, book 1, p. 57. Context buji yaui naimani abo baksa mothani bororokno buji man ya jwngjalo khiklaio.

(3) Tamono sajakmani kokbothomano naidi. Ellen G. White tamono twiwi kwbang jur rwui tong point naidi. Romdi, 1894 bisio Michigan ni Battle Creek-o tong phuru Ellen G. White mothani bororokno bicycle paimani chaya hinwi sakha (Testimonies for the Church, vol. 8, pp. 50–53). Phiya oro Ellen G. White sana naimani khe Kaitor bai rwjakma rang phano wng khe abono kaham khe naiwi samungo thepa di hwnmani se (Tamni hwn ba a jora-o bicycle belai morok eba expensive).

(4) Ellen G. White swimani kaisa kaisa kokbothomano swrwng di. Bo phrwngmani context rokno kaham khe amjok naidi. Ellen G. White kwthang tong phuru bini swijak kokno chaya khe samung thepajakmani bagwi bo kap kha: “Bukhuk White ni kok hinwi kwbangma kok pirjak phano abo tatal khe se bukhuk White ni mung bai chaya kok pirwi tong laio; baksa sakni uansukma kok sathani se bukhuk White kok Samani hinwi bini mungno toksaui rwlaio.”—Selected Messages, book 1, p. 44.

(5) Ellen G. White samani kok de kok ya sraisrai khe buji na gradi. Ellen G. White samani kok se hinwi bini mungno toksaui kwbangma tatal kokrok auaijagwi tongo. Ellen G. White samani kok de kok ya sinani hwn khe bini bijapni swimuk (reference) naina nangnai.

Ellen G. White ni kok diyari uanamasingcha yapharmung le chwngno rwjak kha. Bini swijakrokno kaham khe porinani belai nangkukamani—tamni hwn ba bini swijak kokno poriui rasai rasai bororok hamari man kha, tei chwngbo hamari manwi mannai. Phiya bini swijak kokno chaya khe samungo thepa khe abo le hamya.
