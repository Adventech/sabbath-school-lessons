---
title:  Swijakni Kokmang
date:   18/11/2026
---

Joto Khristianno Baibel sak baithang bujithai. Mwthangjaknani lamano bujinani joto borokni bagwi sraisrai khe rwjak kha; tamo khe Elembakni simungrokno rutugwi paiwi man ya.

`Khuktang di Luk 10:25–28. Baksa borok sao kok swbaiwi sana bagwi Baibel nang ya hinwi. Hakhe tamoni bagwi Kerong phrwngnaino Jisu sakha, “ ‘Kerongo tamo swijak tong? Nwng tamo saui man kha’ ” (Luk 10:26, KOKBSI)?`

Swijakni kokmangno bujina hwn khe puila chwng kokthai thepajakmano ma bujinai. Aboni ulo swijakmani raida, Baibelni context, tei laibuma-culture akorok bahai khe thepajak kha chwng sina nangnai. Paithago khe bebak Elembakni phrwngmung bai khe ma gothok rwnai. Elembak bebak kaisa bai tei kaisa nai khe se bujiui man nai, rom di kaibrwi kothoma kaham nai khe se Jisuni kothoma bujimani hai.

`Naidi Mathai 5:43–48 tei Luk 6:27–36. Mathai 5:43–48 bai Luk 6:36 bwtangno naimani bahai khe chwngno Mathai 5:48 bwtango “kaham” hinwi sajakmani kokmangno bujinani chuba?`

Elembakno swbaiwi Sanai kahamni kaisa tongmung wng kha Elembakni bebak kokrokno thansa khe cherwi naimani tei koinene wngmani. Muitu narwk di, chwng khoroksa phano kubui kókno man ya; chwng jotono aina hamya bai naiwi tongma hai mang se.

Kaham phrwngmungno mangno chwng naina nangnai (2 Tim. 4:1–5). Nangkukmani khe chwng bujimani eba sraisrai wngmanirokno teibo bwkha bai ma nainai; tamni hwn ba jora bai baksa chwng tabuk buji ya kokrok thinango teibo bujiui mannai. Kok khe baksa chini swngmungni kok phirokmung man ya phano tongwi mano.

`Kaitorni kokno buji man ya wngwi tong phuru, kok swbaiwi sajakna nangwi tongo abo hai jorani kokno uansuk naidi. Bahai khe nwng buji man ya phano Kaitorni kokthaio bwkha beraui man?`
