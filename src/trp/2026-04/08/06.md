---
title:  Baibel’-ni Kok Swbai Samung
date:   19/11/2026
---

Chwng Baibel swrwng khe kainwi naharmung tongna nango: baithangni pholao tormung tei buino Jisuni mwthangma halogo twlangmani. Jisu sakha kubui Khristian hwn khe Phola Kwthar bai “bebak kubui-o” twijaknai (Johon 16:13), tei “ ‘Kaitorni búkhúkni bebak kók bai’ ” tongnai borok (Mat. 4:4, KOKBSI), tei buino Jisu chwngno dagimani “joto kok” phrwngnai borok (Mat. 28:20).

`Khuktang di Luk 4:16–30 tei Luk 24:25–27, 44–49. Jisu Elembakni kokno swbaiwi Samani bising twi chwng tamo swrwngwi man?`

Jisu Khristo khe jephuru phano “lama, kubui, tei langma” (Johon 14:6, KOKBSI). Haiphano bo phrwng khe Elembakni kokno naharwi se phrwngo. Phunukmari naidi, Mark 1:14, 15 bwtango sao, “Johon alongo klai bai khe Jisu Galili-o thángwi Kaitorni aroni Kothoma Kaham hai hwnwi pír kha, ‘Mol sók phai kha, Kaitorni ha sampha kha. Sumsok yakar di, hakhe Kothoma kahamno poito khlai di!’ ” (KOKBSI). A jora eba mol sok phaimani wng kha Daniel 9:24–27 bwtangno phunukmani se. Hathai sakao Jisu phrwng phuru (Mathai 5–7) bo Lamrwma 20:13, 14 (Mat. 5:21, 27)bwtangni kokbo nakha. Luk 4 beremo Jisaia 61:1, 2 bwtangni kok bai se Jisu phrwng kha.

Teibo phunukmari naiwi mano Samung 8 beremno, oro sao Ithiopiani tangphang “Kaitorno surina hwnwi Jerusalemo phaimani” (Samung 8:27, KOKBSI), kok wng kha a borok khoroksa swk judai tongtharni borok bai sinijak eba ter palaimungni borok bai sinijak. Bini thani Jisaiani bwchap’-bo tongo, lama thang twiwi bo poriui thangmani. Phiya somsok rujunai seleng ni kok diyarino bo bujiwi man liya; hakhe khoroksa swk bono swbaiwi sanani bagwi bo naikha.

O kothoma-o chwng nangkukmani swrwngjaknai tongo. (1) Ahaino Elembak khuktangmani simi bai chwng kokmangno kuthuk khe bujiui man ya. (2) Elembak swrwng phuru chwng khoroksa tei khoroksani thanibo swrwngthai. (3) Baibelni soi kok buino saui man rwnani chini khlaithai tongo.

Swmai Kwchamni swijak kokrok tei Jisaiani bwchapno Philip swbaiwi sakheno a tangphangsa baptisma ma nakha (Samung 8:35–39; Lob. 51:12). Chwng Baibelni kok swbai sa phurubo abo hai naharmung tongna nango (Johon 17:17).

`Jora jorao Elembakni kok bujina bagwi buini chubachu nagwi phano, phiya chwng je poito khlaimano khe chwng tamoni bagwi kwrak khe romwi gosithai?`
