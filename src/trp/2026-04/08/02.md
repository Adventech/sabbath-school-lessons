---
title:  Baibel, Bono Baithangni Kok Swbaiwi sao
date:   15/11/2026
---

Elembakni kok swbaimungno chwng chini bujima twi khe mangno khlai kugo. Baibelno kwcham swijak kokrok thumjak hinwi uansuknairok juda juda Baibelni bijaprokni bisingo thansa wngmungno bohrok nugwi man ya. Phiyaba “Elembakni bebak kókno Kaitor bai koboljagwi swimani; abo … chúgo” (2 Tim. 3:16, KOKBSI), hakhe Baibel ni bebak bijaprokni bisingo thansa wngma nukjakma bagwi chwng Baibelno se baithang bini swijak kokrokno swbaiwi sanai khlaina nango.

`Khuktang di Mathai 5:17–48. O bwtango Swmai Kwchamni swijak kokrokno Jisu bahai khe swbaiwi sakha?`

Mathai 5:17–48 bwtangno khuktang khe Jisu Swmai Kwchamni kokrokno bini baithangni kokthai-o slai kha hinwi uansugwi mano. Phiya kuthuk khe swrwng khe Jisu a slai ya, teibo se Swmai Kwchamni kokrokno kuthuk khe swbaiwi phunugo. Omo hai khe Jirimia 31:31–33 bwtangni swmaino se bo supungma klaio, Swmai Kwtalo Kaitorni kerong bini bororokno uansukmao tonjaknai tei bwkhao swijaknai (Hib. 8:8–10, Hib. 10:16).

Baksa kok swbaiwi sajakmani kaisa bijap bisingono tongmani nukjago. Rom di, Daniel 2 beremo Habugra Nebudkadnejarni uanamasingcha imangni kok sajak kha (Dan. 2:31–35) phiya aboni ulo bini imangni kokno swbaiwi sajak kha (Dan. 2:36–45). Omo hai kheno Mathai 13 beremo maichwlwi sarmani rwngthoma chwng nugo (Mat. 13:1–9) tei aboni ulo khe a rwngthomani kok swbaiwi sajago (Mat. 13:18–23).

Baksa kok swbaiwi sajakmani khe ulni diyarini bising twi. Rom di, Jirimia 25:11, 12 tei Jirimia 29:10 bwtango kok diyari sajakmani nokha bisi 70 ni kok diyari nang phai kha hinwi 2 Bosong 36:22, 23 bwtango sajak kha. Swmai Kwchamni kwbangma kok diyari sajakmarok Khristoni sakao nang phai bai kha, tei kwbangma Swmai Kwchamni kokrok Swmai Kwtalo swbaiwi sajak kha.

“Swmai Kwcham wng kha mari tei goron-o kothoma kaham. Swmai Kwtal khe soi goron phunukjakma. Kaisa bai tei khoroksa hai kheno nangkukmani kok wngo.”—Ellen G. White, Selected Messages, book 2, p. 104. Swmai Kwtal wng kha Swmai Kwchamni kokrokno swbaiwi Samani se.

`Baibel swrwng phuru tamoni bagwi borokni uansukma twi kok swbaiwi Samani bai chwng hus wngna nangnai? Tamoni bagwi chwng Kaitorni kokno simi bwkha berathai?`
