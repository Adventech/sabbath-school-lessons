`Khuktang di Jisaia 65:17–20. Ha kwtal bai nokha kwtalo “khoroksa swk nokha bisi sa ra-o thwi phano” hwnmani kokmang tamo? (Jis. 65:20, KOKBSI).`

Laibumano rwk saui nai ya khe baksa borok Jisaia 65:20 bwtangno phunugwi saui mano Ha kwtal bai nokha kwtalo thwimung tei sumsok gwnangrok tongnai hinwi. Phiyaba tumungno abo hai wng khe Jisaia swmai rwmani bai abo gothok ya se wngnai; tamni hwn ba bo sakha borok kahamrok khe kwthwini thangwi bachanai (Jis. 26:19) hakhe thwimung tei kha khammani tei tong phlai glak (Jis. 25:8, Isa. 65:19).

Jisaia 65:20 bwtang wng kha mari phunukmani kok se. Bo chwraino eba siklasa umor bisi sa ra wngnai ba? Chwng Jisaia 65:17–20 bwtangno khuktang khe aro chwng laibumani kok se mano, abo Israil dopharok tumungno Kaitorni swmaino narwgwi tong khe hamari yachaknani kokno se koklop hai khe swijakmani (Uainwi Saphima 28). Mathai 24 beremo samo wngwi tongmanirok thinango wngnai hinwi Jisu Samani hai kheno Jisaia sakha. Abo ha kwtal bai nokha kwtalni sampili wngnai hinwi Jisaia sakha.

Jisaia-o kwtal snamjakmani kok abo thinangni kokno bebakno sajakmani ya. Abo a jorani Kaitorni mwthangmani tei nakarmani chwngsachani samungno se sajago. A bwtango Samani tabukbo nagwino tongo; Israilrokni bwskango Kaitor kwthwino mechen kha. Hakhe Jisaia wngtong jorani swmaini kokno Samani bagwino chwrairok thwinani kokno sakha (Jis. 65:20). Phiyaba abohrok bebakno thinangni sampili se.

`Khuktang di 1 Korinth 16:20. O bwtangno bahai khe chwng chini jorao tei culture-o samungo thepanai?`

Swmai Kwtal molni Grik culture-o sandai tei bayab kahamrok kha kaham yaphar phuru motom sulaio. Khristianrokbo abo hai khlai thung hinwi Pol sakha (Rum 16:16, 1 Korinth 16:20, 2 Korinth 13:12, tei 1 Thesaloniki 5:26). Baksa culture-o motom sulaiwi man ya; chibai rwmani, kwbangmani, khulum kha hwnmanirok se yachakjak kugo. Aboni bagwi je yachakjakmanino Mothao khlai lai thung hinwi Pol sao.
