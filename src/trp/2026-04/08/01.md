### Hatibarsani Bagwi Swrwngjaknai
Mat. 5:17–48; 1 Kor. 16:20; Mat. 4:1–11; Luk 10:25–28; Luk 24:25–27, 44–49.

> <p>Muitu Bwtang</p>
> “Hakhe Musini simi tei bebak diyarirok bono tẃi elembakni bebago aswk saui klangmarokno Jisu bórokno swbaiwi saui khna rw kha” (Luk 24:27, KOKBSI).

Jisu Khristo thwiwi tei thangwi bachamani ulo Sagri khoroknwirok bai nukjak kha, bohrok khoroknwino sakha, “ ‘Israilrokno phiyóknai hwnwi chwng bono’-no khatungwi tongmani’ ” (Luk 24:21, KOKBSI). Sagrirok uansugo, Masia khe Judairokno Rumsongni yakni phiyoknai se wng anw hinwi. Omo hai uansukmung bohrokno kha baimungo se twlango; tamni hwn ba abo Swmai Kwchamni Masiano twiwi kok swbaiwi sajakmani pherjakni bagwi.

Khristo a sagri khoroknwini chaya bujimungno Elembakni bising twi khe swbaiwi sakha (Luk 24:25–27). Elembakni kokno Khristo bohrokno swbaiwi sakheno bohrokni mokol kiyok kha, tei bohrokni bwkhao belai nangjak kha (Luk 24:31, 32). Aboni ulo Jerusalemo Jisu teibo Sagri palrok bai nukjak kha, tei “Elembakni kókrokno bórok romwi manna tẃi khe Jisu aphuru bórokni bwkha kiyok rwkha” (Luk 24:45, KOKBSI; teibo naidi Luk 24:33, 36, 44–49).

Kok diyarino kaham khe buji khe aboni bwthai kaham manmanino o kothomao phunukjak kha. Phiya kok diyarino chaya khe buji khe abo pholani deramungno twlangwi mano.

_November 21 Sabat salni bagwi o hatibarsani rwngchapno swrwngwi tiyar wng di._
