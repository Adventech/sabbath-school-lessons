---
title:  Kokrwbai Swimungni Chubanairok
date:   03/11/2026
---

`Khuktang di Jirimia 36:17, 18 tei Rum 16:22. Baibel swinairok kokrwbai swimungni chubanairokno samungo thepamano twiwi oro bwtangrogo tamo sakha?`

Jirimia bo saichungno Kaitorni kokno swina khe swiwi mano, phiyaba bini kok diyarirokno thiti khe swiwi narwknani bagwi tei a kokrokno dophani twiphangrokno saui manna bagwi bo khoroksa swi kwrwngno se bwkha bera kha. Phiyaba Jirimia simi abo hai khlai ya, tamo khe teibo diyarirok tongo, bohrokni Kaitorni kokno pirwi manna twi khe bohrokni swimungo swi kwrwngrokni chubachu nakha.

Swmai kwtalo’-bo omo hai nukjak kha. Rumsongno swihornai Pol se hinwi yaphangni simi chwng sio; phiyaba Terti sakha “Ang Terti, o kóktun swinai” (Rum 16:22. Terti wng kha Polni kokthaino swiwi rwthani chubanai.

Polni bagwi abo ahaino samung ya. Kolosai tei Thesalonikisongno swimani koktunni paithago Pol sakha “Ang yak baithang omono swi-o” (Kol. 4:18, 2 Thes. 3:17, KOKBSI). Koktun swithani-o bo khoroksa swkni swithani chubachu nao. Aboni bagwi bui uansugwi mano, bini koktun poito khlaijakna chukjak wngwi da man hinwi; tamni hwn ba bini koktun bo buino swi rwthanio bui chaya khe phano swiwi mano—phiyaba paithago Pol bini yak baithang bai swiwi sakha a bini koktunrok poito khlaina chukjak, buini yak twi swi rwmani phano bo naiwi akoino paithakni bwkango bini yak bai swiwi hormani. Buini yak bai swi rwmani phano bini koktunno bwkha udru mudru wngna kwrwi hinwi hinwi Pol sana naikha.

Kwbangma amojoknairok sao, Polni koktunno juda juda kokrwbai swimungni raida nukjago; abo khe Pol bini koktun swithani swi kwrwngrokni chubachu namanino se phunugo. Pitorni kainwi koktuno’-bo abo haino nukjago: “Pitorni puilani koktun bai philnwini koktunni kokni raida juda juda khe swijak, abo wngwi mano Pitorbo . . . Grik kok bai bo bini koktun swithani-o khoroksa swk swi kwrwngni chubachu naui mano. Grik kokthai kwrwngkuk Pol se swi kwrwngrokni chubachu naui tong khe Pitorbo swi kwrwngrokni chubachu naui mano. Tamni hwn ba Pitoni amani kok khe Aramaic se, phiya bini koktunrok khe Grik kok bai swijak. Abo wngwi mano bo bini koktun swina bagwi khoroksa swk swi kwrwngni chubachu na khwna.”—The SDA Bible Commentary, vol. 5, p. 186.

Kaitorni diyarirok se buini chubachu naui swiwi tongo hwn khe, chwng orono bujiui mano, Kaitorni kok swithani Phola Kwthar borokni bwkhao (uansukmungo) simi se kobol phaio, swimungni raida-o ya.
