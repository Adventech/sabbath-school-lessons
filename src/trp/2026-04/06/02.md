---
title:  Swinani Bagwi Ringjakma
date:   01/11/2026
---

Swkangni molo Kaitor bororokno kok samani lama wng kha bini khorang bai khe kok samani bising twi eba imang phunukma bising twi eba khe suarirokni bising twi. Phiyaba Musini simi khe puila Elembak swinani Kaitor bai dagijak kha, abaorok Kaitor kok Samani “bijap.”

`Baibel swinani bagwi puila sajakma tamo? Khuktang di Lam. 17:14.`

Swmai Kwchamni molo Kaitor bini bororokni bagwi bini kokthaino khati narwknani bagwi bini diyarirokno dagimani chwng kwbangma nugwi mano. Musino sama hai kheno Jirimia’-bo sajak kha, “ ‘Nwng dolwi tonmani bijap kangsa na di tei Josia habugra wng phuruni simi tini jora-o Israilrokno tei Judarokno tei bebak dopharokno kebengwi ang nono samani a salni simi aswk samarokno nwng aro swi di’ ” (Jir. 36:2, KOKBSI).

Tamni bagwi a kok diyarirokno swinani nangnai Jirimiano sajak kha: “ ‘Ang Judani bororokni saka-o aswk hamyarok tubuna hwnwi uansugwi tongmano bórok khnaui bórokni lam hamyani kiphilwi phano phaimano, hakhe ang bórokni cha ya tei sumsokno nakarnai’ ” (Jir. 36:3, KOKBSI). Kok diyari rwjakmani abo thinangni kokno saui man rwna bagwi ya, tamo khe bororokno mwthangma-o twlangnani bagwi se.

Juda juda tong halogo Kaitorni bororok ma swikha. Hasing panthoro tong phuru Musi khe Baibelni puilani kaiba bijap’-no ma swikha. Sol bai rutuk jagwi tongmani jorao Dauid kwbangma bini lobmungrok swikha (Lobmung 52, 54, 57, 59). Babilon tei Medo-Parsiani noyungo samung khlaimani jorao Daniel bini Kaitor bai phunukjakmani kokrokno swikha. Alongo tong phuru Pol khe Epis, Philipi, Kolosai tei Philimonni thani koktunrok swikha. Patmos tong phuru Johon khe Phunukjakmani bijap’-no swikha. Kaitorni kok sanairok jesa tong halogo phano tong thung; tamo khe Phola Kwthar se bohrokno swinani phano rwui chubao.

Kaitor bini kok kubuino khatui narwkna bagwi bwkha beraui manmani bororokni bising twi khe Jisuni phrwngmarokno swiwi narwknani bo a bororokno chong kha. Hakhe Kaitorni kok le bini bororok kaham kheno swiwi khatui narwk kha hinwi chwng sai mano.
