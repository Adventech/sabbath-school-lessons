---
title:  Kaitorni Kok tei Borokni Kaham Wngsuk ya
date:   05/11/2026
---

`2 Korinth 4:7 bwtango Kaitorni manwino twiwi Pol tamo sakha?`

Pol swikha, Kaitorni rangchak hai a manwi kaham “hani manwio” (borokni thanio) tonjak kha, tamni hwn khe chwng kebelni bising twi Kaitorni phan nukjakna bagwi. Elembak chwngno phunugo Kaitorni diyarirokbo bohrokni langmao chaya chukya kwrwi wng man ya (Phunukmari, Musi, Elijah, Daud, Jona, Pitor akosong), aboni bagwi bohrokni swimungbo borokni bujimung tei borokni kokthai bai swijak abo bebakno chasukjak wngwi man suk ya, derajak korojak tongnai.

“Khristoni bisingo Kaitor tei borok thansa nukjakma haino Baibelo’-bo abo hai. Phiya kok kubui khe Elembakni bebak kokno ‘Kaitor bai kobolwi swijakmani;’ abo borokni kokthai bai swijak, tei borokni nangmarok thepajak.”—Ellen G. White, Testimonies for the Church, vol. 5, p. 747.

Kaitor bini chẃngsacha kok kubuino pirnani bagwi ringjak phano, bini kókno sanairok le borok ja se hinwi 2 Korinth 4:7 bwtango Pol sakha. Phola Kwthar le chwng bujiui man ya kókrokno ta swidi hinwi saya hai khe chwng ma tongo. Kisisa baibelni bwtangrok tongo, abórokno chwng bujiui man ya; phiya chwng buji man ya phano chini poito khibina kók ya. Chwng Baibel poito khlainai Khristian hwn khe chwng buji man ya phano Kaitorno bwkha beraui, tei chwng buji manma jora twi bini kókno chwng yachakna nango.

Baibel copy khlai phuru kisa misa koromani chaya nukjagwi mano. Phiya jephuru chwng buji man ya bwtangno mano, aphuru chwng sak sakno ma swngnai, swinaisa tamo sana naimani chwng bujiui da man kha? A buji yano bujina bagwi chwng teibo de kaham khe swrwngna nangnai.

Chwng Baibelni kaisa swk kokno chaya hinwi uansugwi tong phano abo soi sana naimani kokno chaya snam ya. Borok kaham wng suk yani bising twi Kaitor bini kubuirokno phunuk phano, chwng Elembakno bwkha bera sugwi mano.

`Kaitor bororokni thani bini kok sana bagwi tamoni sumsok kwrwi suarirokno chong yaui chaya chukya bororokno chong kha? Nwng tamo hinwi uansuk?`
