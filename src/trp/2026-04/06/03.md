---
title:  Kokrwbaini Goron tei Swimung-raida
date:   02/11/2026
---

Baibel-o juda juda swimung-raida nukjago, phiya aborok bebakno Kaitorni selengrok Kaitorni Phola bai koboljagwi swimani. Baksa khe diary hai khe swio (Lekhamung 33:1–49). Phiya Daniel, Sakaria tei Johonni swimung khe Phunukmung gwnang swimung (apocalyptic writing), abo marirok tei mol paithakno phunugo, tei Chuknohani borororok bai kubuni tongmungrokbo oro phunugo.

`Khuktang di Jisaia 13:1. Jisaia rwmani kokno Biabel-o tamo hinwi sa?`

Diyarirok Kaitor bai phunukjakmanino (visions) jora jorao “kok diyari” hwnjago—aboni kokmang wng kha Kaitorni aroni kok eba phunukjakmani. Kwtal Baibel kok slaijago khe a kokrok lobmung (koklop) hai khe swijak kha. Phiya bohrokni swimungo kokmang gothokjak juda juda khebo swijakma tongo. Phunukmarini bagwi naidi: “A salo bororok bórokno taknairokno samung nangjaknai tei Israilni kwtharsano bórok mokol bai naituk lainai” (Jis. 17:7, KOKBSI). Oro “taknai” tei “kwtharsa” wng kha kaisa kokmang, phiya kaisa bai tei kaisa teibo a kokmangno kwbang khe cherwi phunugo.

Diyarirok swithani-o joto borok buji manmani kokrwbai raida bai khe swio, aborok wng kha marirok tei kok bang rwui samani akorok. Chwng bohrokni swijak kokrokno bujina khe bubo hai raida bai bohrok swikha abo se swkang ma nainai.

Elembago tei kaisa kokrwbai swimungni raida nujakma wng kha “swmaini kerong swbaimani” phunukmung bai. Phunukmarini bagwi naidi Jirimia 2:4–9, oro Kaitor bini bororokno bohrok swmai swbaima bagwi bujsoini silchamung rwkha. Oro buj thummani nokni mangpili phunukjak kha. Kaitor khe bini bujni bagwi chayano phunuknai borok hai khe swinaisa oro swikha.

Elembago kok diyarini swimung, laibuma, rwchapmung, tei rwngthomarok tong baio. Jirimia khe Kaitorni kokrokno bijap snamnani bagwi dagijak kha (Jir. 36:2). Phiya Swmai kwtal-o khe koktunrok mangno kwbang. Phiyaba swimungni raida simi se pher phano Swmai Kwchamni diyarirokni swimung bai Pol swimani koktunrok baksa se, aborok bebakno Phola Kwthar bai koboljagwi swimani (1 Kor. 7:10, 12, 40).

`Baksa amjoknairok Elembakno borok swimani raida-o se kwbangkuk naisigo, Kaitor bai koboljagwi swimanino aswk nai ya. Abo hai wngmani tamoni bagwi chaya wngwi man?`
