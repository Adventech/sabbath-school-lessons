### Hatibarsani Bagwi Swrwngjaknai
Lamrwma 17:14; Jis. 13:1; Jir. 36:17, 18; Rum 16:22; Luk 1:1–4; Juda 14, 15; 2 Kor. 4:7.

> <p>Muitu Bwtang</p>
> “Elembak chwngno khá lok rwmani tei khá rak rwmani tẃi chwng khatungwi manna tẃi khe elembago swijakmani abo jotono chwngno phrwngna se” (Rum 15:4, KOKBSI).

Hakhe Kaitor swimani rwchapmung nono phrwngjak khe nwng bahai ma tongno? Uainwi Saphima 32 beremo Kaitor dagimani bising twi Musi rwchapmung swikha (Uai. 31:19–22). Hakhe diyarirokni swijak kokrokno jephuru chwng uansugo, phiya aborokno chwng rwchapmung hai ma tong ya; phiya Lobmungrok abo Kaitor khukphang kok samani ya phano bebakno Kaitorni Phola bai kobolwi swijakmani se.

Baibel-o sao, Kaitorni kokno diyarirok kaitham lamani bising twi buini thani yapharo: (1) kokni bising twi, (2) swijakni bising twi, tei (3) mari phunukmani eba tail phunukmani bising twi. O hatibaro khe chwng Elembakni swimungno twiwi nai lai anw. Baibel-o diyarirokni kokni bising twi Kaitorni kokno swiwi narwkjak kha, aro diyarirokni kokbo khobjago.

Diyarirok swimani juda juda swimungni raidarokno twiwi uansuk naidi, aborok wng kha: kothoma, laibuma, lobmung, khapsimang, tei koktunrok. Phola Kwthar bai twiduljagwi se borokni kokthai bai Baibel swijak kha. O hatibaro chwng nai lai anw bohrokni swijak kokrokno, aro chwng swrwng thani kutuk wngmarokbo bwkhao narwknani nangmarok tongo.

_November 7 Sabat salni bagwi o hatibarsani rwngchapno swrwngwi tiyar wng di._
