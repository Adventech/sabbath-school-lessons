title:  Ellen G. White’-ni Swimungrok
date:   06/11/2026
---

Ellen G. White’-ni swimungrokbo chwng o hatibaro swrwngmani Kaitorni Baibel swinairokni haino. Kaitor bono phunukmarokno swiwi narwkna bagwi bo Kaitor bai dagijak kha.

1909 bisio Ellen G. White swimani tongo, “Ani langmao uaisa sak deramani bagwi ang sak hamya wngwi swiwi man yaw ng kha. Phiya horsa Subraini Suari ani samo phaiwi saphai kha, ‘Ang nono phunukmarokno nwng ma swinai.’ Ang sakha, ‘Ang swiwi man ya.’ Phiya bo saphikha, ‘Ang nono samarokno swiwi narwk di.’ Ang swinani chaitok nai anw hinwi songphangni lapboard nakha, tei swinani cheng kha, phiyaba kwlaisa kheno kokthairokno ang swiwi man kha.”—The General Conference Bulletin, May 31, 1909.

Elembak swinairok hai khe Ellen G. White’-bo bini swimungo swi kwrwngrokni chubachu nakha. Bini tang yakchurok komthingnai hai khe swithani bono chuba kha. Phiyaba bóhrok bóhrokni sakni uánsukma twi kókrok thepa ya. Bohrok Ellen G. White swiwi tonimani, tei khuk bai samanino se swithani chubao.

Ellen G. White’-bo bini swimungo buini swijak bijaprokni kokrok thumwi nahar kha. Porinairokni bagwi kaham wngnai abo hai kokrokno se bo buini bijapni naharo. Abo bui swinairokno bo chaya khlaima klai ya. Phiya bohrokni bijap-o kok kubui buino sajaknai tongmano se bo manwi bini swimungo thepao.

Bini swimungo derama koroma le tongo de?

Bo sakha, “Ani swimungobo chaya korojak derajak tongnai, chaya kwrwi hinwi ang gosi ya; Kaitor simi se chaya wngwi man ya.”—Selected Messages, book 1, p. 37.

Bini swimungni baksa kok bujinani kutukbo tongwi mano. Kaitor bai ringjak phano le Ellen G. White’-bo borok ya wngwi thang ya. Chwng hai kheno bo toro tei chakra wngo, abo hai kheno bini swimungbo tormani tei chakra wngmani phunukjago.

Ellen G. White swimani bebak kokno Kaitorni aroni phaimani ya hinwi Ellen G. White sraisrai khe o uansukmungno twiwi sakha, “Nangmani kok sana nangmani tongo, nangmani uansukmungrok bwkhao phaio, nangmani koktunrok swina nangma jora tongo, khoroksa tei khoroksa tangpalni thani nangmani kok ma rwmanirok tongo. A kokthairok, a kokrok Kaitorni Phola bai koboljagwi rwjakmani ya.”—Selected Messages, book 3, p. 58.
