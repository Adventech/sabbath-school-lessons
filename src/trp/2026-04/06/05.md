---
title:  Swimung Naharjakmanirok
date:   04/11/2026
---

“Phrwngnaisa sak-sak sinai wng twtwi buinobo siyan phrwng kha, kaichomwi uansugwi kwbángma phrwngmung kóktháirokno chap kha. Phrwngnaisa sói kókthái kholwi kók kubui tei srai-srai khe swi kha” (Phrwngnaini 12:9, 10, KOKBSI).

O bwtangni bising twi chwng bujiui man kha Phrwngmung Kaham Bijano bebak swinaisa Solomon ya. Kaitorni Phola bai koboljagwi bo “sói kókthái kholwi” “kóktháirokno chap kha.” Phrwngmung 31 beremo bwrwima kahamni kokno sajakmani abo Solomonni kok ya, tamo khe Habugra Lemuel bini bumani bagwi Samani kok se (Phrwngmung 31:1). Abono karwibo Phrwngmung Kaham bijap-o Ijipni kokrwbai swimungbo nukjago.

Omo hai kheno Kaitor bai koboljagwi Swmai Kwchamni laibuma swinairokbo Kaitorni bororokni kothoma swina bagwi juda juda bijap tei laibuma bwchaprokni swimuk (reference) nahar kha.

`Khuktang di 1 Habugra 11:41; Luk 1:1–4; tei Juda 14, 15. Baibelni wng ya abo hai kubuni bijaprokni swimuk naharmano twiwi o bwtangrogo tamo sakha?`

Ejra bai Nehemia Baibel swinani bagwi laibuma bwchaprokni rutugwi swimung nahar kha. Ejra Jerusalem tei Parsiano twiwi swijak koktunrok tei decree rokno bini swimungo thepa kha(Ejra 4–7), tei Nehemiabo mung-rem swithanio kubuni bijaprokni rutugwi ma nahar kha (Neh. 7:5–72).

Swmai Kwtalo’-bo abo hai nukjak kha. “Phola Kwthar bai koboljagwi swimungni kok man ya, tamo khe baithang se ma rutuknai, abo chwng Lukni bising twino saui man kha. Swinaisa Phola Kwthar bai twiduljagwi Khristoni langmani kok buini khugo ma khwna kha, tei swijak kokrokno ma swrwng kha, aboni ulo khe aborokno thansa khe chabwi Jisuni kothoma swikha.”—The SDA Bible Commentary, vol. 5, p. 669.

Juda’-bo Baibel Canon-o kwrwi Judairokni kokrwbai bijap 1 Henok 1:9 bwtangni kokthai bini swimungo thepa kha. Mung khurchajak kwrwi phano Polbo Grik Philosopher rokni swijak kokrok bini swimungo thepao (Samung 17:28 tei Tit 1:12).

`Baibel swinairok baksaba Imang tei Kaitor bai phunukjakmani bising chubajak kha, abo haino buini bijapni kokthai naharmanibo Phola Kwthar bai chubajak ya wng ya. O kok tamni bagwi chwng muitu narwkthai?`
