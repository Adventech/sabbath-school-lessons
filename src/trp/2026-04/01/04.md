---
title:  Kaitor Borokno Rutukma
date:   29/09/2026
---

Yaphang 3:9, 10 bwtango chwng nuk kha Kaitor bini sumsok kwlainai bwsarokno rutukmani. Kaitor swng kha “Nwng buro wng kha?” Tamni hwn ba Kaitor sai mano Adam bai Ebi tamo khlai kha. Sumsokni bagwi puilani borok bai Kaitor kok salaiwi man liya. Kaitorni muchungma wng kha bini bwsarok bai kok saphinani, malainani, tei khatungma rwnani. Yaphang 3:9 bwtango Kaitor “ringmani” abo Hibru kokthaio qara hino. Aboni kokmang wng kha Kaitor bini bwsarok bai tei uaisa kok sanani lama snam phinani ringmani.

`Khuktang di Luk 15:11–32. O bwtangni rwngthomao bahai khe Yaphang 3:9, 10 bwtangni patimungno phunuk kha?`

Kaitor Adam bai Ebino rutukma abo bini bwsarokni bagwi bini paiphlaiya hamjakmano phunugo. Kaitor chwngno yakar ya (Luk 15). Chwng huijak phano Kaitor chwng bai kok salai phinani muchungjago. Jisu kruso thwinani swkang Pitor Jisuno uaitham gosi yaw ng kha, Phoyaba Khristo bono “dadak-didik nahar kha” (Luk 22:61). Jisu khuk per ya phano abo hai khe Pitorno kok sakha.

Yaphang 3:11–13 bwtango naidi, sumsok bahai khe Adam bai Ebini halokno swbai rwkha. Bohrok khoroksa tei khoroksano majra ber lai kha. Adam Ebino phang phang ya phano majra berma klai kha; tamni hwn ba Kaitor Ebino bono rwkha. Bo sakha, “ ‘ng bai tong di hwnwi nwng ano rwmani a bwrwi se ano a bwtháino rw-o, hakhe ang ma chá kha (Yap 3:12, NKJV). Ebi khe chibukno majra ber kha’ ” (Yap. 3:13). Bohrok sakni sumsok gosi lai liya. Bisi doksaio bwswk kisa swk slaijak kha.

Sumsok bai joto borokno manjak kha. Rum 7:15-24 bwtango sumsokni tongmungno Pol phunuk kha. Bo samung kaham khlaina muchung phano khlaiwi man ya: “Ang khlaimano ang saui man ya, tamni hwn khe khlaina muchungmano ang khlai ya, phiya ba ang selengmano se ang khlai-o” (RUm 7:15, KOKBSI).

Chwng khlai thai ya si twtwino chwngbo khlaiwi tong laio. Sabo omo hai wng ya tong kha?

Pol sakha “hini Subrai Jisu Khristo tẃi” simi se chini khatungma (Rum 7:25). Aboni bagwino Kaitor khe chwngno bono kosol snam phinani bagwi Jisuni thani twlangnani chwngno rutuk tongo.
