---
title:  Kaitorni Wngwi Kok Sanairok
date:   01/10/2026
---

`Sumsok hab phaimani ulo Kaitor bororok bai bahai khe kok sa? (Khuktang di: Hib. 1:1, 2; Rum 1:19, 20; Lekhamung 12:6; 2 Tim. 3:16, 17; Johon 14:7–9; Johon 10:27–30.)`

Sumsokni ulo phano sumsok gwnang bororok bai kok sanani Kaitorni thani kwbangma lama tongo.

Kaitorno bororok nuknani kaisa lama Kaitor rwmani wng kha bo snammani manwirok. Habarrok Kaitorni uanamasingcha yakni samungno phunugo. Scientists-rok chini swngcharno nugwi uanajago, tei chini langmano twiwi mwlang chajago.

Kaitor bororok bai kok Samani tei kaisa lama wng kha diyarirokni bising twi. Diyarirok wng kha Kaitorni wngwi kok sanairok. Noa, Abraham, Musi, tei Daniel diyarirokni bising twi Kaitor kok sakha, tei bororokni bagwi bini muchungmano phunuk kha. Kaitor bini bwsarokno poky a kho hinwi phunukmani wng kha diyarirokni swimung. Baksa diyarirokni swimung Elembago tongo, phiya baksani khe Baibelo kwrwi.

Phiyaba diyarirokni kok Elembak bai kwrwngjak se (2 Tim. 3:16, 17). Baibel wng kha Kaitor sakno phunukmani se. Baibelni bising twi chwng swrwngo Kaitor sabo, tei bini hamjakma bai chubamarokno.

Chwng sabo tei chwng buroni phai tei chwng buro thangnai o kokrokbo chwng Baibelni bising twino sai mano. Baibelo se chwng Kaitorni naharmung tamo sai mano.

Phiya teibo samungo nangkukmani abo hai khe Kaitor chwng bai kok Samani wng kha Jisuni bising twi, abo chwng Baibelni bising twino mano. Edeno Kaitor Adam bai Ebino sakha, Khristo phaimani khe “kwmanairokno rutugwi mwthangna bagwi” (Luk 19:10). “ ‘no nuknai borok bo Aphano nuk kha’ ” hinwi Jisu sakha (John 14:9, KOKBSI). Khristoni langma, thwima, tei thangwi bachamani bising twi Eden-o Adam bai Ebino swami rwmani Khristo phnang kha. Bini bising twi mwthangjagwi manna twi khe bo chini bagwi thwi phai kha.

Jisu Chuknohao thangmani ulo Phola kwthar chwng chubaui “bebak kubui-o” twio, tei “phiya tamo tamo wngno” aborokno chwngno sao” (Johon 16:13,KOKBSI).

`Jisuni kok, bini hamjakma, bini tongmung, tei Apha Kaitorno kosol khe bo chini bagwi sakno yapharma kokno saui manwi nwng bwswk khakchangma man?`
