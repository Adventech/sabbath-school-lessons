Kaitor bororok bai kok samani Baibelni jorao simi ya. Khristo phaina swkang kwtalni molobo Kaitor bororok bai kok sauino tongo.

Ellen Gould Harmon (White) tei bini apher bukhuk Elizabeth, bohrok achai kha November 26, 1827 salmario, thai khe Gorham, Maine. Bohrok wng kha Robert bai Eunice Harmon-ni khorokcharni thaithak bwsarok. Sal kisani ulo bepar thwngna bagwi Robert Harmon bini nukhungno twiwi kutulwi thang kha Portland, Maine-o. Phiya Ellen bini umor chuku phuru sak hamya kwlai kha. Ellen bini nok samo tongmani beraikhirio tong phuru bini classmate khoroksa bono jolijagwi holong bai khitar kha, hakhe Ellen kisa hatibar romwi lengwi tong kha. Hakhe aboni bagwi bo lekhapora khlaiwi man liya, tei bini sak aswk hamya wngwi thang kha.

Abo hai jorao sikli Ellen pholani phaisingo uansukna cheng kha. Bo Kaitorno kiri kha, tei Chuknohani bagwi sakno chuk ya hai nuk kha. Abo hai uansukmungni bising twi Harmonni nukhung Jisu dakti phaina naikha hwnmani Millerite doctrine-no yapchakna cheng kha. Twi bisingo sitokjagwi 1842 bisio Methodist Church-o baptisma namani ulo phano Ellen Kaitorni hamjakma mannani sakno chukya hai ma tongjak kha. Bo uansuk kha bo “mol brumni bagwi komorjak” hinwi (Life Sketches of Ellen G. White, p. 29; also see pp. 20–31).

Omo hai uansukmungni jorao bo kainwi imang nuk kha, hakhe Kaitorno twiwi bini uansukma slaijak kha. Phiyaba bo Methodist preacher Levi Stockman bai malai y asak Kaitorni hamjakma tei khairokmano buji ya khe tong kha. Yagulo bo sakha, “Ani bwkhao tabuk poito phai kha. Tabuk ang Kaitorni bagwi hamjakma phai kha, tei bini Phola ani sumsokno nakarmani sakki man kha. Aphano ang nukmani slaijakmung rwkha. Ang tabuk Kaitorno Bugra tei khairoknai mapha hai khe nai kha, siyaui kok khnayarokno jolinai hai khe nuk liya. Ani bwkha tabuk bono belai hamjak kha.”—Life Sketches of Ellen G. White, p. 39. A jorao “thwimungno twiwi” Ellen bujinani cheng kha. Bo sai man kha mol brum khammani morke kwrwi. Bo Kaitorni hamjakmano kwtal khe bujimani le bini tongmung tei bwkhnao slai sugwi rwkha. Ellen bini langmao Jisunobo belai hamjagwi bini samung khlai kha, o kok chwng bwskang hatibarni Phasalni kokbwkhal-o nai lai anw.
