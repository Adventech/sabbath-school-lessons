---
title:  Eden Thaiblwngo Kok Salaima
date:   27/09/2026
---

Yaphang 1 tei 2 beremo Kaitor swngchar snammani kok tongo. Bo naithok swngcharo Adam bai Ebino ton kha. Bororok wng kha malmatarok bai juda. “Kaitorno kosol khe snamjak kha” (Yap. 1:27), hakhe bohrokno twiwi simino mungsa swk sajak kha, tok tei malmatarokno twiwi sajak ya. Bororok Kaitorni hamjakmano nugwi siui tong kha, tei Kaitor bai baithang himwi tei kok saui tongo.

Tei Yaphang 2:18-23 bwtango Adamni bagwi Kaitor Ebino snam kha, bo sakha, “ ‘Borok saichung susuk khe tongmani hamya’ ” (Yap. 2:18, KOKBSI). Yaphangni simino bororok wng kha thansa tongnaijat khe sma,jak kha. Sociologistrok sao, bororok somaj bai thansa tong khe bahan bwthwi tei bokhorokni bagwi kaham wngo, saichung tongmani hamya.

Ebino snam baimani ulo Adam sakha, “ ‘Páithago omo ani bekrengni bekreng tei ani bahanni bahan; bini mung Bwrwi wngnai,tamni hwn ba bo chwlani aroni karijakma se’ ” (Yap.2:23). Chwla bai bwrwino thepamani bising twi Kaitor bororokno puila thansa tongnani phrwngmung rwkha. Phiyaba Soitanni soi naharmung wng kha soi nukhungni yaphangno dera rwnani.

`Khuktang di Yaphang 1:27, 28; Yaphang 2:15–17; Yaphang 3:9. Eden thaiblwngo Kaitor bai borok bahai khe kok salai?`

Adam bai Ebi dadak didik kheno Kaitor bai kok salaiwi tongmani. Bohrok Kaitor bai malai mano tei bini kok khnaui mano. Kaitor bohrokno thaiblwng mwrwknani barja rwkha ba bohrokni elembo bang kha. Sal brum bohrok Kaitor bai malaimani jora wng kha “sarik sal kwchang dodo-o” (Yap. 3:8). Sabat sal khe judakuk khe snamjak kha. A salo Kaitor bini bwsarok bai kok salaina bagwi snam kha (Yap. 2:2, 3; Lamrwma 20:8–11). A salono chini puilani buam buphasong Kaitorni hamjakma tei naikolmano judakuk khe sai mano.

`Sumsokni swkang bahai tongmani o kok chwng tabuk uansuknani tuk kha. Phiyaba chini habar bahai khe Kaitorni yaphangni snammano phunugwi tong?`
