---
title:  Kaitorni Thani Huijakmani
date:   28/09/2026
---

Tamni hwn ba Kaitor wng kha hamjakmung (1 Johon 4:8), aboni bagwi bo borokno “robots” khlaiwi snam ya. Hamjakmungo chongmung tongo. Hamjagwi manna twi khe Adam bai Ebino sakni muchungmao yoksamung gwnang khe snamjak kha; abo hai wng ya khe kubui hamjakma wngwi man ya.

Adam bai Ebi wng kha Snamnaisani kok khnanani eba khnaya wngnani yoksamung man kha. A yoksamungni bagwi bohrok dagijak kha, kaham hamya si rwnai buphangni bwthaino ta chadi hinwi (Yap. 2:15–17). Tamni bwgwi abo ta chadi hwnwi hus khlaijak kha?

“Chini puilani buma bupha uansuk kha, Kaitorni snamjak borok wng kha kwthar, tei kaham, bohrok samung hamya khlaiwi man glak. Kaitor bini bujsoino si rwnani bagwi bohrokno yoksamung rwkha. Bororok Kaitor tei suarirok bai kok saui mano. Yoksamung kwrwi khe, bohrok phan bai twiduljakma se klainai.”—Ellen G. White, Patriarchs and Prophets, pp. 48, 49.

`Tamni bagwi Adam bai Ebi Snamnaisani kok khnaya wngmani ulo huijaknani nang kha? Yaphang 3:1-8 naidi. (Nai phidi Jir. 23:24 tei Hib. 4:13).`

Yaphang 3 berem wng kha Adam bai Ebi sumsok khlaimani kothoma. Ebi Chibuk (Soitan) bai kok salaimani ulo kheno Kaitor ta chadi hwnmani kaham hamya si rwnai bwthai chakha. Kokni phan bai se Soitan bwkha nang rwui kok khna rwkha. Ebibo kok saui se Adamno kok khna rwkha, tei Kaitorni dagimungno kebeng kha. Ebi nuk kha a buphang wng kha “naithok” “bini bwthái chá khe bwswk thokno tei siyan gwnang wng khe bwswk hamno” (Yap. 3:6, KOKBSI). Soitan bohrokno kok rwmani wng kha bohrok “ ‘Kaitor hai wngnai tei bubo kaham bubo hamya saui mannai’ ” (Yap. 3:5, NKJV), o kok bwswk se khnathok.

Phiyaba Soitan bohrokno tatal sakha. Kok khnaya wngmani manthaino khe bo bohrokno sayaui huiwi ton kha. Kok khnaya wngmani ulo bohrokni mokol “kiyok kha” tei bohrokni aroni chwngsacha tei pohorni Kaitorni “richum” kwmaui “sak langa” nukjak kha (Lobmung 104:1, 2), aphuruno Kaitorni “kosol” kwmaui thang kha. Bohrokni chayano nugwi bohrok Kaitorni thani ma huijak kha. Chwngbo chini chayani bagwi Kaitorni aroni kharna muchungo.

`Nwng tamo sumsok khlaimani ulo Kaitorni aroni kharna muchung kha?`
