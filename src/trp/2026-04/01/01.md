### Hatibarsani Bagwi Swrwngjaknai
Yap. 1:27, 28; Yap. 3:1–8; Luk 15:11–32; Yap. 3:14–24; Lamrwma 19:16–22; Johon 10:27–30.

> <p>Muitu Bwtang</p>
> “Agi Kaitor chini chuchusongno diyarirokni bising tẃi kwbángma’-no podrepot khe kók sa kha, phiya tabukni o mol páinanogo bo bini Sajlani bising tẃi se chwngno kók sa si-o. Kaitor a Sajlani yak tẃi’-no bebak swngcharno snamo, mol pái phuru bebak’-no bini yago rwna bagwi Kaitor bono’-no chong kha” (Hibru 1:1, 2, KOKBSI).

Digital technology ni bising twi chwng tabuk chini bayab tei sandairok bai hakchal tong twtwi phano nuk laiwi mano. Phiyaba chwng dadak didik malaiwi kok salai man khe abo se teibo kaham.

O hatibaro chwng nai lai anw, bororok Snamnaisa bai malaiwi halok kwrwngwi manna twi khe snamjak kha. Phiyaba sumsok abono dera rwkha. Phiyaba a halokno snam phinanani bagwi Kaitor chwng bai kok sanani lama rwkha. Diyarirokni bising twi bo chwng bai kok sakha. Diyarirokni swimungrok baksa Elembago tongo, Phiya baksa khe kwrwi.

Kaitorni soi naharmung wng kha chwngno bini kosol snam phinani. Abono bo mwthangnani chapmani: snamphinani, Kaitor snam baimani jora wng kha “ha kwtal bai nokha kwtal … aro se borok kahamrok tong bainai” (2 Pit. 3:13, KOKBSI).

A Jora sok phai ya sak Kaitor chwng bai kok saui tongo, tei bini diyarirokni bising twi bo abo hai khlaio.

_October 3 Sabat salni bagwi o hatibarsani rwngchapno swrwngwi tiyar wng di ._
