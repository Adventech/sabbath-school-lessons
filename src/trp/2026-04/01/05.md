---
title:  Thaiblwngni Rwkhlaijakma
date:   30/09/2026
---

Adam bai Ebi sumsok khlaiwi manthai manmani bisingo kaisa kotor wng kha Kaitor bai ma kakmani. Bohrok Eden thaiblwngni rwkhlaijak kha, hakhe Kaitor bai malaiwi tei kok sai man liya. O kokno twiwi Jisaia Diyari swikha, “Norokni cha yarok norokno Kaitor bai swkak kha, norokni sumsokrokni bagwi bini mwkhang norokno huiwi ton kha” (Jis. 59:2, KOKBSI).

Phiya kubui kok wng kha Adam bai Ebi Kaitorni bwsano wngwi tong kho. Phiya sumsokni manthaini bagwi bohrok Eden ma yakar kha. Kok khnaya wngmani ulo agini hai wng liya.

`Sumsok borokno bebagono slaiwi rwkha (Yaphang 3:14–24). Phiyaba bahai khe sumsok le chwng Kaitor bai kok salaiwi tongmano slaiwi rwkha? (Lam. 19:16–22.)`

Sumsok slaiwi rwmarokno chwng uansuk phano man ya. Sumsokni bagwi ha “swraimung nang kha,” tei “busurok achai kha.” Hakhe bororok “tekto” tamo sai man kha; borok “kolomtwi kwlaiwi” tangwi ma chakha. Paithago khe thwinani tei hao kiphilnani nang kha. Sumsokni manthaini bisingo thwimung jotoni slai kirimasingcha. Sumsokni bagwino chwng Kaitor bai kok salaiwi man liya.

Tamo khe Adam bai Ebi sumsokni yagul sujakmani yakni yoknani lama rwjak kha. Bohrok Kaitor bai kok salaiwi man liya phano thinangni khatungma manwi Eden yakar kha. Kaitor bohrokno mwthangnani chapmano swbaiwi saui khna rwkha.

Yaphang 3:15 bwtang wng kha “puila kothoma kahamni swami.” Abo Adam bai Ebino khatungma rwkha. Thinango mwthangnaisa phainai, tei sumsokno kebengwi borokno mwthangnani khatungma rwnai. Eden thaiblwngo Adam bai Ebi kwmajakmani ri abo rwjak phinai khatungma wng kha mari abo hinango mwthangjakmani. Jisu thwimani tei thangwi bachamani bising twi Kaitor bai borok kok salaiwi man phinai (Johon 14:1–3).

`Mungsa swk nwng chaya khlaimani ulo bubo hai uansuk? Chaya de uansuk? Lachijago de? Tamo wng? Phiya Kaitorni kothoma kahamno poito khlaiwi bahai ma tong?`
