#### Bwkha Sichajak: Poland-o Adventism Chengsamani

1888 bisio Poland-o puila Adventist ni kothoma sok phai kha. Aphuru Herman Szkubowiec puila Seventh-day Adventist wng kha. Puila aroni SDA Church wng kha Zambrów-o. 1893 bisio khe Karol Fendel tei Józef Słodziński, Łódź-o Adventist ni kok pir kha. Hakhe bororok Adventist ni kók kubuino yachak lai kha. 1895 bisio aro Adventist motha bacha kha.

Hakhe motha twrwk twrwk tor kheno 1912 bisio khe Polish Mission hinwi Organize khlaijak kha. Tei 1921 bisio khe khorok 1,080 adong twiwi Poland-o Seventh-day Adventist Union songchajak kha. 1988 bisio khe Poland-o Kaitorni samung tangmano bisira kamani panda khlaijak kha.
