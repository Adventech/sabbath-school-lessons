---
title:  Thuthlung Thara zâwlnei derte
date:   22/10/2026
---

`Matthaia 24:23, 24 chhiar la. Isua’n vaukhânna min pêk hi eng chungchângah nge ni? He vaukhânna hi eng vângin nge kan lo ngaih pawimawh êm êm a tûl?`

Amah Isua ngei pawh khân hei âia chiang hi chuan a sawi thei tawh bîk kher lo maithei: “Krista derte leh zâwlnei derte an lo chhuak dâwn si a, chûng chuan thei ang sela, thlante meuh pawh an bum theih nân chhinchhiahna ropui tak takte lantîrin, thilmakte pawh an ti bawk ngei ang” (Mat. 24:24, NKJV).

Inbumna a sawite hian mi a hneh thei viau dâwn niin a lang lehnghâl a, a chhan pawh “chhinchhiahna ropui tak tak leh thilmak chi hrang hrang” a ken tel tlat dâwn vâng a ni. Thilmak, leilung dân pângngâi anga thleng ni lote hi, eng anga âwihawm pawh ni mah se, anmahni ngau ngau chuan Pathianin hna a rawn thawk a ni tih entîrtu a ni hran rêng rêng lo va. A chhan pawh chutiang thilte ti theitu chu hmêlma kan nei tlat alâwm. Paula chuan: “Kan buante hi tisa leh thisen an ni si lo, lalnate leh, thuneihnate leh, he thim chunga khawvêl rorêltute leh, vânn hmunahte thlarau sualho awmte an ni zâwk e” (Eph. 6:12) tiin a lo sawi a. Hêng thiltihtheihna hrang hrang a sawite hian zâwlnei derte hmangin hna an thawk thîn a ni.

Dik tak chuan, zâwlnei derte hi tûn hnua lo chhuak ta chauh an ni rêng rêng lo. Thuthlung Thar ziaktute pawhin Thuthlung Hlui hunah khân zâwlnei derte an awm thin thu an sawi a (Luka 6:26). Zâwlnei derte chu “chinghne koham tak,” nimahsela, berâm emaw tih palh mai theih tûra “berâm vun sin” nên tehkhinin” (Mat. 7:15, NKJV), chûng mite chu an rah atanga an nihna dik tak hriat theih tûr an nih thu pawh an sawi bawk (Mat. 7:16, NKJV).

An hna pui ber chu mite bum a ni a (Mat. 7:17–20). Isua ngei pawh khân zâwlnei der tam tak lo la chhuakin, mi tam tak an la bum dâwn tih a lo sawi a (Mat. 24:11). Thei phei se chu, thlante meuh pawh bum an tum dâwn a ni a ti bawk (Mat.

24:24, NKJV; Marka 13:22, 1 Joh. 4:1 pawh en bawk ang che).

`Ephesi 6:10–18 chhiar la. Inbumna eng chi pawh lak atanga kan lo invên theih dân tûr chu eng nge ni?`

Rinnaa nung a, “fêlna âwmphâw” inbel a, kan kâwng chu “thutaka” hrêng a, tawngtâina hmanga Thlarau Thianghlim hnêna intulût a, Pathian Thua inthlung nghet tlatin—hêngte hi thlarau lam leh thurin lama mi hruai bo thei thil chi hrang hrang laka kan lo invên theih dân tûr kawngte chu a ni.

`Kan khawvêl tuam mêktu inbumna thil chi hrang hrangte do lêt tûr hian engtin nge tha zâwkin râlthuam i inbel theih ang?`
