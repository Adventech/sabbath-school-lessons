### Chhiar tûrte
Mat. 16:13–16, Eph. 2:20, Thup. 22:6, Luka 2:36–38, Tirh. 21:9, Eph. 6:10–18.

> <p>Châng vawn</p>
> “Tin, thilpêk hrang hrang a awm a, Thlarau erawh chu hmun khat a ni. Rawngbâwl dân hrang hrang pawh a awm a; nimahsela, Lalpa chu hmun khat a ni. Hnathawh dân hrang hrang pawh a awm a, Pathian erawh chu hmun khat a ni—mi zawng zawng chunga eng kim thawktu chu” (1 Korinth 12:4–6).

Pa pakhat hian kawngpuiah car a khalh a, chutia a khalh lâi chuan a sîra thu a fapa chuan an câr tlân pelh leh tawhte chu ngun taka lo thlîrin, a pa car khalh ang a nih leh nih loh hriat a lo tum a. Chutah a pa chuan an piaha car ding chu kâwkin, “Mama en teh saw, ka car neih hmasak ber ang chiah kha!” a ti a. In an thlen chuan, a car neih hmasak ber chungchâng inziahna lehkhabu chu a hâi chhuak leh a, tin, tûna a khalh mêk car chungchâng inziahna lehkhabu pawh a phawrh bawk a. A fapa hnênah chuan a car neih hmasak ber leh tûna a khalh mêk nihna hrang hrangte chu entîrin, an inan dân leh inan loh dân zawng zawng a hrilhfiah vek a. Amaherawhchu, car pahnihte khâwlpui ber (engine) a han khâikhin chuan, engkim a lo inang vek tlat mai. Thil tam tak thlâk danglam a ni tawh a; mahse, a khâwlpui ber erawh chu a la ngâi reng a nih chu.

Pathian chuan Thuthlung Hluia zâwlneite leh Thuthlung Thar lama tirhkohte kal tlang khân thu a lo sawi thîn a. Khâng zâwlneite leh tirhkohte thuchah puan kha an inzawm kual vek chhan leh, an inrem tlân êm êm bawk chhan chu anmahni rawn thâwk khumtu/uaptu Thlarau Thianghlim—an thu dawnna hnâr emaw, an “Khâwlpui” emaw tiin sawi ta mai ila—hi hmân ata tawh tûn thlenga a pângngâi reng a la nih tlat vâng a ni. Thlarau Thianghlim chuan hril lâwkna thilpêk hmangin zâwlnei dik takte kha a lo thuam thîn a. Hebrai Lehkhathawn ziaktu’n chiang taka a lo sawi ang hian, Krista chu zâwlneite thuchah laimu berah a dah zêl thîn bawk a ni (Heb. 1:1–3).

Tûn kârah hian Thuthlung Thar lama hril lâwkna thilpêk a lo lan dân kan zir dâwn a. Zâwlneite thil tih te, tute nge zâwlnei nia hna thawk tih te, zâwlneite leh tirhkohte inanna leh danglamna tih te leh zâwlnei derte hlauhawm dân tih te kan en ho ang.
