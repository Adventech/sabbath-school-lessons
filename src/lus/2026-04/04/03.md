---
title:  Tirhkohte leh zâwlneite
date:   19/10/2026
---

Thuthlung Thar lehkhabute hian tirhkohte leh zâwlneite hnathawh an sawi hlawm a. Tirhkohte tih hi a tawng bul lamah (Grik) chuan apostolos niin, chu chuan “thuchah puangtute” tih emaw, “Pathianin a tirhte” tih emaw a kâwk. Chanchin Tha bu pali-ahte hian zirtîr 12-te sawina atân “tirhkoh” tih hman a ni fo thîn a. Thuthlung Thar lehkhabu dangte pawhin chu chu an tâwmpui ve bâkah, a tîra Isua Krista zirtîr ni ve lo mi dangte pawh an huam teltîr ta zêl a ni.

Paula hian a lehkhathawn hrang hrangah tirhkoh tiin a insawi thîn a, chutianga tirhkoh a nih thu a sawina Bible châng chu 12 zet a awm nghe nghe. Rom 1:1, 2-ah chuan tirhkoh a nihna mai bâkah, a thu hril ber pawh Thuthlung Hlui zâwlneite thuchah lo puan thin nên khân a sawi zawm a. Jentail-te tirhkoh an nih thu sawiin (Rom 11:13), chumi tûr chuan Pathian duh zâwng ang taka ruat niin a insawi bawk (1 Kor. 1:1).

Eng hnam emaw leh tute chhûngkua emaw te’n an inrochun chhoh zêl thin puithiamte ang lo takin, tirhkohte leh zâwlneite hi chu Pathianin ama duh ang leh duhzâwng ang zêlin a lo ruat thîn a. Tute’n emaw tirhkoh ni tak tak lo anga Paula an lo sawi khân amah Paula ngei hian chhângin, tirhkoh a nih ngeizia huai takin hrilh a nih kha (1 Kor. 9:1, 2). A tirhkoh nihna chu Krista, kohhranho innghahna lungphûm chunga sak niin a ngâi bawk a (Eph. 2:20). Chutiang chuan, tirhkohte khân, Pathian inpuan chhuahna lo dawngtu an nih angin, zâwlneite’n Pathian Thlarau uapna an chan ang tho kha an chang a ni (Eph. 3:1–5).

Petera chuan zâwlneite kha tirkohte lo chhuah hmaa lo awm tawh, an thuchah puante pawh pawimawh tak tak ni hlawm niin a sawi a, chutiangin tirhkohte pawh zâwlneite ang bawka thuneihna nei ve tho an ni a ti (2 Pet. 3:1, 2). Hêng châng pahnih lekah hian Thuthlung Hlui hun leh Thuthlung Thar hun a sawi ve ve a. Zâwlneite kha Thuthlung Hlui hun lâia Pathian thuchah puangtute niin, tirhkohte erawh chu Thuthlung Thar hun lâia Pathian thuchah puangtute an ni ve thung ni âwmin lang mah se, tirhkoh ni lo, hril lâwkna thu puang thîn emaw, hril lâwkna thilpêk kawl emaw tlat si an awm ve tho bawk. Hril lâwkna thilpêk hi Thuthlung Thar hun lâia tirhkohte chauh khân an kawl a ni lo va. Tin, zâwlnei zawng zawngte kha tirhkoh an ni vek chuang lo bawk (Tirh. 15:32).

`Ephesi 2:20 leh Ephesi 3:5 chhiar la. Tirhkohte leh zâwlneite khân engtiangin nge Krista tân leh kohhran tân hna an thawh thin?`

Tirhkohte’n Thuthlung Thar lehkhabua an lo ziahte kan chhiar atanga kan hriat ang hian, a thu sawi leh ziah zawng zawng tih theih deuhthawin an kawh ber Isua a ni a. Eng emaw ti zâwng talin Isua nun, a thihna, a thawhlehna, puithiam lalber nia vân biak bûka rawng a han bâwlna leh a lo kal lehna chungchâng an sawi zêl thîn.

`Tirhkohte leh zâwlneite’n Isua chanchin—a nun leh a hnathawhte—an lo ziahna bu rin tlâk kan neih chhun chu Bible hi a ni a. Hei hian Bible hi Isua chanchin leh kan tâna a thihna chungchâng kan rin dâna thuneitu sâng ber a nih a tûl chhan min hriatthiamtîr em?`
