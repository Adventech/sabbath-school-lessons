---
title:  Thuthlung Thar zâwlneite hnathawh
date:   20/10/2026
---

`1 Korinth14:3, Ephesi 4:11–13 leh Thupuan 22:6 te’n Thuthlung Thar zâwlneite hnathawh min hrilh chu eng nge ni?`

Hêng Bible chângte hi bengvârthlâk viau tho mah se, Thuthlung Thar zâwlneite’n thil an tih hrang hrangte kha an sawi kim lo hle mai. Eng pawh ni se, an thil tih tam tak kha Thuthlung Hlui hun lâia zâwlneite pawhin an lo tih thin tho a ni a, Thuthlung Thar hunah pawh hril lâwkna thilpêk dawng an la awm chhunzawm zêl a ni tih kan hriat theih phah bawk.

1 Korinth 12:10-ah chuan hril lâwkna thilpêk hi thlarau lam thilpêk hrang hrang zîngah chhiar tel ve a ni a. Tehkhin nâna Krista taksa tih tawngkam hman a nihnaah pawh zâwlneite chu Pathian hnên ata thuchah dawngtu ni ve tho tirhkohte dawtah dah an ni bawk (1 Kor. 12:27, 28). 1 Korinth 14:3-a kan hmuh dânin, “Hril lâwkna thu puangtu chuan mite hnênah siamthatna thu te, fuihna thu te leh thlamuanna thu te a sawi thîn a ni” (NKJV).

Ephesi 4:11–13-ah pawh 1 Korinth 12-a a lo sawi ang thlarau lam thilpêk hrang hrangte bawk kha a sawi leh a.

Zâwlneite chu tirhkohte, chanchin tha hriltute, kohhran enkawltute (pastor-te) leh zirtîrtute zîngah a chhiar tel (Eph. 4:11). Hêng mite hi “rinna leh Pathian Fapa hriatna kawngah chuan thu khat vuaa, puitlinga, Krista famkimna lenzia tluka kan zaa kan lo awm hma loh zawng rawngbâwl hna thawk tûra mi thianghlimte tihfamkim nân leh Krista taksa siam tha tûra” awm an ni a (Eph. 4:12, 13). Hetah leh hmun dangah pawh kan hmuh angin, an thuchahin a tinzâwn ber chu Isua a ni zêl thîn.

Tirhkohte bu hian zâwlnei pahnih, Juda leh Sila te (anni pahnih hian Bible-a dah tel ni ta lehkhabu an ziak lo ve ve) chanchin a sawi a, “thu tam tak sawiin unaute chu fuiha an tihchak” thin thu pawh min hrilh bawk (Tirh. 15:32, NKJV). Ni e, zâwlneite khân vaukhânna thu an puan chhuah châng pawh a awm thîn a, thu rum leh dêngkhâwng deuh hlek an sawi tum a awm bawk. Nimahsela, mite’n an mamawh dân ang zêlin fuihna thu pawh an sawi ve thîn tho va, a bîk takin, hun khirh tak an tawh lâi phei chuan an mamawh tak thute bâkah, beiseina thar pe thei tûrte pawh an lâwm tlângpui thîn.

Hril lâwkna chu mite fuih phûrna atâna pêk a nih thin thu pawh sawi lan a ni bawk a (1 Kor. 14:31, NKJV). Ringtute’n tihduhdahna leh fiahna an hmachhawn lâi chuan hril lâwkna thilpêk hi chhelna leh beiseina neihtîrtu a ni thîn.

Thupuan 22:6-ah chuan Isua hi “zâwlnei thianghlimte Lalpa Pathian,” ani’n thu a sawite chu a rinawmin a dik êm êm zêl nia târ lan ni a (NKJV). Pathian bâwihte’n Pathian âiawha thu an sawi rêng rêng pawh a dikin a rinawm thîn ang. Rin ngam alawi kan neih tawh lohna khawvêlah hian Lalpa zâwlneite’n an sawi erawh kan ring ngam thei a, a Thu Bible phei hi chu thuneihna tâwp nei leh chungnung ber a ni tih kan hre reng tûr a ni.
