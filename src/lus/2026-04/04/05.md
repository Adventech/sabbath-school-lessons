---
title:  Zâwlnei hmeichhiate leh tirhkohte tha chhangdâwltute
date:   21/10/2026
---

`Luka 2:36–38 leh Tirhkohte 21:9 chhiar la. Hêng chângte hian zâwlnei hmeichhiate chungchâng engtin nge an sawi?`

Thuthlung Hlui hunah khân Pathianin a thuchah puang chhuak tûrin hmeichhia leh mipa a lo ko thîn a. Thuthlung Thar hunah pawh hian zâwlneite zîngah hmeichhia an awm ngei a ni tih tichiangtu a awm nual mai. Anni kha Thuthlung Thar lama “hmeichhe zâwlnei” tia sawi hmasak ber a ni a. Hun puma rawngbâwlin, “a chhûn a zânin, chaw nghei tea tawngtâiin Pathian a be thîn. Tin, . . . Pathian hnênah lâwmthu a sawi fo a, mite hnênah Isua chanchin a sawi thîn bawk” (Luka 2:37, 38).

Tirhkohte 21:9 hian Philipa’n hril lâwkna thu puang thîn fanu pali, mipa la ngâi miah lo thiau lehnghâl a neih thu min hrilh a. Thuthlung Thar chanchin zirtu mi thiam, entîr nân, Eusebius-a te pawh khân Thuthlung Thar lehkhabute ziah a nih hnu daiha ziah lehkhabu eng eng emaw hâi chhuakin, chûngahte chuan Philipa fanute chênna khua leh an thiha phûm an nihna hmun pawh ziah a nih thu an lo sawi a ni âwm e. Eng pawh ni se, hmeichhia, chu pawh nula la ni lehnghâlte’n hril lâwkna thilpêk an dawng tih kan hmu a. Eng thu nge an hril a, tu tute hnênah nge an hril tih erawh kan hre lo. Thil pakhat kan hriat chian êm êm awm chu: he mite unau za thu hril hi tûna Thuthlung Thar (Bible) kan neihah hian telh ve a ni lo tih hi a ni. A awmzia chu, mi dangte ang bawkin, anni pawh hi Bible-a an thuziak awm ve lêm lo zâwlneite an ni a. An thu sawi emaw, an thu ziah emaw kha Bible-ah hian dah ve ni ta lo mah se, zâwlnei dik tak an ni tho si a ni.

Zâwlnei hmeichhiate bâkah pastor patuai deuhte pawh khân tirhkohte puiin rawng an lo bâwl ve thîn a. Tirhkohte khân chûng hruaitu la naupang deuhte chu rawngbâwl tak tak thei tûrin an chher hriam thîn a ni. Entîr nân, Paula chuan Timothea kha “rinnaa ka fa tak tak” tiin ko a (1 Tim. 1:2, NKJV; 1 Tim. 4:12 pawh en bawk ang che). Kohhran enkawlna kawnga a hriat ngei tûl thilte a zirtîr thîn. A hna thawh tûr chungchâng pawh heti hian a hrilh a: “Ka fapa Timothe, hmâna i awm dân tûr an lo hril lâwk tawh ang ngei khân, indona tha chu i beih theih nân he mawhphurhna hi i kovah ka nghat ta a ni” (1 Tim. 1:18, NKJV).

Tita pawh kha “rinna thuhmun ka neihpui ka fapa tak tak” tiin a sawi bawk a (Tita 1:4, NKJV). A hnênah Kret thliarkâra ringtu awmte tân hruaitu ruat hna thawk tûrin mawhphurhna hlânin, chu hna chu a awlsam dâwn lo hle nghe nghe. He mawhphurhna pawimawh tak hi thiam leh tha taka a hlen chhuah theih nân thlarau lam thurâwn pawh a pe nghâl a: “Chûng thute chu thu nei takin sawi la, fuih an tûlnaah fuihin, zilh an ngaihnaah zilh bawk rawh. Tu ma’n lo ngâinêp che suh se” (Tita 2:15, NKJV).

`Kan la naupang deuh a ni emaw, kan upa deuh tawh a ni emaw, a hmei a pa hian Pathian hnaah chuan thawh tûr kan nei theuh a. Nang eng nge i thawh tûr ni ve a, engtin nge nasa zâwk leh hlâwk zâwkin i thawh theih ang?`
