### Chhiar tûrte
Jak. 5:17, 18; 1 Lalte 19:1–16; 1 Lalte 22:1– 14; Exod. 15:20; 2 Lalte 22:11–14; Jer. 14:1–15.

> <p>Châng vawn</p>
> “Jehosaphata chu a ding a, ‘Aw, Juda mite leh Jerusalem mite u, ka thu hi ngâithla rawh u; LALPA in Pathian chu ring ula, nghet takin in awm dâwn nia; a zâwlneite chu ring ula, hmuinghîl takin in awm dâwn nia’ a ti a” (2 Chronicles 20:20).

GPS tia kan sawi mai thin, Global Positioning System hian khualzin vâkvêlte chu khawi hmunah nge an awm tih te, khawi lam hawiin nge an kal mêk tih te a hrilh bâkah, an awmna hmun leh kalna tûra thil hlauhawm awmte pawh a hriattîr thei bawk. Thlarau lam zin kawng zawh, vân lam pana kal mêkte pawh hian tûnah leh nakîn zêlah pawh vân lam GPS atanga kaihhruaina kan mamawh a. Tûn hma lama Pathianin chutiang a lo tih thin dân kawng pakhat chu Thuthlung Hlui zâwlneite rawngbâwlna hmang khân niin, an thawnthu ziah, thurâwn pêk, hril lâwkna thu an puan, leh hla chham mi an phuahte atang hian anmahni chanchin tam tak kan lo hre thei a ni.

Tûn kârah hian Thuthlung Hlui lehkhabu ziaktute’n zâwlnei nihna leh an chanchin an sawi dân kan zir ho ang a. Chûng kan thil zirte chuan Pathian chhiahhlawhte’n an lo lantîr thin hril lâwkna thilpêk awmzia hrethiam tûrin min tanpui dâwn a ni.

Hêng mite hi keimahni ang bawka mihring ni ve tho—thil eng emaw hlau thei, thinur thei, beidawng leh hnual ve thei an ni tih kan hre dâwn a. Harsatna leh buaina chi hrang hrang an tawh fo avângin an nun pawh a hrehawm thîn hle.

Chuti a nih chuan, hêng Pathian chhiahhlawhte zînga tlêm azâwngte chanchin kan zir atang hian eng thil tha nge kan lo chhar chhuah theih ang?
