---
title:  Zâwlneite hnathawh
date:   13/10/2026
---

`1 Samuela 8:4–22 leh Leviticus 8:10–13 chhiar la. Zâwlneite khân engtiangin nge anmahni hun lâi zêla lalte leh puithiamte nên rawng an bâwl ho thin?`

Zâwlneite khân, a nuamah leh a hrehawmah pawh an hun lâia lalthutthlêng luahtu lalte kha thawhpui an tum tlângpui thîn a. Israel-te’n Samuela hnêna lal neih an dîl (1 Sam. 8:4– 22) tuma an sawi chu, “hnam dang zawng zawngte ang bawkin kan chunga rorêl tûr lal neih kan duh” (1 Sam. 8:5) tih a ni. Hei hi Pathian mite’n chhuan tina hlauhawm an lo hmachhawn tâk thin entîrtu lungchhiatthlâk tak leh pawimawh êm êm ni bawk si a ni a. Keini pawh hian kan chhehvêla awmte ang nih ve kan châk fo thîn a ni lo’m ni? Hemi thuah hian an thil dîl chuan—Pathianin a zâwlnei hmanga a hrilh lâwk sa vek chung pawhin (1 Sam. 8:11–18)—eng nge ken tel a neih dâwn tih lam an ngaihtuah thleng phâk lo rêng rêng a. Samuela chuan, an dîl ang ngei leh, vaukhânna a pe chung pawha a dîl luih tlat tho avângin Israel-te lal hmasa ber, Bible-a Lal Saula tia hriat ni zui ta hi hriak a lo thih ta a ni.

Thuthlung Hlui lehkhabu—1 leh 2 Lalte leh 1 leh 2 Chronicles te hian Israel lalte leh Juda lalte’n anmahni hun theuha an ram ve ve an enkawl dân leh an nungchang min hrilh a. Ngun taka han chhiar hian, an zîngah Pathian mit hmuha thil tha ti thîntute âiin thil tha lo ti thîntute an tam zâwk hle tih kan hria ang. Zâwlneite hnathawh pawimawh tak pakhat chu Pathian thupêkte zâwm a, a hnêna kîr leh tûra lal sual tak takte hriattîr a ni a. Chutiang thu an sawi avâng chuan anmahni vauna thu rum pui pui dawngin, thenkhat chuan Pathian hnên ata Pathian mi hruai botu lalte kut an tawrh bâkah, tihlum hial pawh an tâwk nghe nghe (1 Lalte 18:4–19, 2 Chron. 24:20–22).

Tin, puithiamte nên an thawk ho thîn bawk a. Pathian zâwlnei Mosia pawhin Arona leh Arona fapate chu Israel puithiam hmasa ber atân hriak a lo thih a nih kha (Lev. 8:10– 13). Puithiamte hian zâwlneite kal tlanga Pathian zilhhauna thu an dawn châng pawh a awm thîn a (Hosea 6:9, Mal. 2:1– 3). Entîrna tha tak pakhat chu, Elia te pafa za, dik lo taka thil an tih thin avânga mi pakhatin a rawn hmachhawnte kha an ni (1 Sam. 2:27–36). Pathianin hamthatna a pêkte ngeiin thil sual nasa tak an han tih hian a huatthlala zual êm êm a; chu vâng chuan an hnêna zilhhauna thu lo thleng pawh a khauh bîk thîn rêng bawk.

Zâwlneite kha mipuite zirtîrtu an ni bawk a (2 Chronicles 34). Pathian hnên ata thuchah an dawnte chu puithiamte hnênah te, lalte hnênah te leh mipuite hnênah te an hrilh chhâwng ve leh thîn (Jer. 5:31, Ezek. 22:26). Tin, Israel ram chhehvêla chêng hnam hrang hrangte hnênah pawh thu sawiin, an tâna Pathianin ruahmanna a siamte chu an hrilh châng a awm thîn bawk a, chutiang thu chu Thuthlung Hlui zâwlneite thuchah hrang hrangah hian kan hmu fo mai (Isaia 60:1–3; Isaia 62:1, 2). Zâwlneite kha an hnêna Pathianin thuchah a pêkte rinawm taka sawi chhuak leh tûra beisei an nih bâkah, an thuchah dawnte chu ziaka daha vawng tha tûra hriattîr an nih châng pawh a awm thîn bawk a (2 Chron. 13:22). Zâwlneite kha mihring ve tho chu ni mah se, tawngtâi thîn mi, Pathian Thu zâwm tlat thîn, Pathian leh a mite rawngbâwlsak duh êm êmtu an ni (Jer. 42:4; 2 Lalte 22:15–17).
