---
title:  Zâwlneite kha mihring ve tho an ni
date:   11/10/2026
---

Kâr hmasa lama kan lo sawi tawh ang khân, zâwlnei Elija kha dodâlna nasat tâwk mah se, Lalpa’n hna ropui tak thawk tûra a hman a ni a. Amah hmanga Lalpa’n a hlen chhuah tâk kha a ropui lutuk a, a âwihawm loh hial zâwk. Nimahsela. . . eng thil nge lo thleng ta si le?

`Jakoba’n zâwlnei Elija chanchin a sawi dân hi ngun takin chhiar la (Jak. 5:17, 18). Elija pawh khân “keimahni nihphung ang” tho hi a nei ve tia a sawi hian, eng nge a sawi tum tak ni ang?`

Elija khân Karmel tlângah hlawhtlinna mak leh ropui tak a chang a (1 Lalte 18); amah kal tlang khân Pathianin hna ropui tak a rawn thawk a ni tih kan hria. Khami tum khân Thuthlung Hluia kan hmuh Pathian thilmak tih ropui ber pakhat chu a lo thleng a: Elija dîlna chu chhâna awmin vân ata mei a lo tla a nih kha (1 Lalte 18:38). Khatiang thil ropui tak a thlen hnu kha chuan he Pathian mi pawh hi a hlim ve hle tûrah i lo ngâi ngei ang.

`1 Lalte 19:1–16 chhiar la. He lâi thu hian Pathian zâwlnei Elija rilru awm dân eng nge min hrilh?`

“A tâwk tawh e! Tûnah chuan LALPA ka nunna hi la mai tawh teh, min thlahtute âi khân ka tha bîk chuang si lo va” (1 Lalte 19:4, NKJV). Hetiang thu hi chu beidawnna nasa tak neite’n an sawi ni âwm tak a ni. Eng vângin maw? Karmel tlânga thil thleng kha thil tih awl tak a ni lo vang tih a chiang a; mahse, chuti chung pawh chuan thil dang lo thleng tel ve a awm tlat.

Chu thil thleng chu eng nge ni tih kan hriat theih nân amah Elija tawngka chhuak ngei hi lo chîk ila: “LALPA, sipaihote Pathian tân thahnem ka ngâi êm êm thîn a; Israela thlahte chuan i thuthlung an hawisan a, i mâichâmte an tichim a, i zâwlneite pawh khandaihin a sât hlum zo vek tawh bawk. Kei hi a la awm chhun chu ka ni a; keima nunna pawh hi rawn lâk ve leh an tum a ni” (1 Lalte 19:10, NKJV). A han sawi dânah hian rawngbâwlnaah hlawhchham nia inhriain, chu chuan a tilungngâi ta êm êm mai bawk niin a lang. Theihtâwpa a bei chung pawhin milem biakna a la hluar êm êm reng tho a, chumi lo dodâl kawngah chuan a mal tan niin a inhre bawk. A enga pawh chu lo ni se, he Pathian mi hi mihring ve tho, derthâwng êm êm bawk si a ni tih kan hmu thei a. Tawngkam danga sawi chuan, Elija hi keimahni nihphung ang tho nei ve a ni.

`Keimahni nun emaw, kan hnathawhna kawngah emaw pawh ni se, enga ti nge “hlawhchham” nia inngaihna hi neih a awlsam viau mai thin le? Beidawn deuh ram ram châng ka neih pawha kan thawh leh thuai zêl theih nân eng Bible chângte hi nge kan rinchhan theih ang?`
