---
title:  Zâwlnei dik hriat theih dân
date:   12/10/2026
---

Israel-te chanchin hmasa lamah khân zâwlnei der/suak an lo awm tawh thîn a ni tih kan hria a (Deut. 18:20). Chu vâng chuan, Pathian pawhin a mite hnênah khân zâwlnei dik chu tute nge ni tih an lo hriat theih nân kaihruaina tha tak a lo pe rêng a ni.

Zâwlnei dikte chuan, Pathian inpuanna dawngtu leh awmpuina chang an nih angin, rinawm tak, dik tak leh rilru ngîl tak pu chungin Pathian hnên ata thuchah an dawnte chu mite hnênah an puang chhâwng leh thîn a. An thu puan avâng chuan lalte leh rorêltute lawm hlawh loh phah dâwn pawh ni se, tîm miah lovin an puang chhuak tho thîn.

`1 Lalte 22:1–14 chhiar la. He lâi thu hian zâwlnei rinawmte hna thawh dân eng nge ni min hrilh?`

Lal Ahaba hian zâwlnei Mikaia chungchâng sawiin, “Ani hi chu ka huat zâwng tak a ni, ka chungchâng thu hi a chhe lam ngawt a sawi zêl a, a tha lem erawh a sawi duh ngâi rêng rêng lo” (1 Lalte 22:8, NKJV) a ti a. He a thil sawi hian amah Ahaba chungchâng tam tak a tihlan rualin, zâwlnei Mikaia chungchâng phei chu a tilang tam lehzual. Mikaia hi thu dik sawi chhuah tîm ngâi miah lo, lal hnênah pawh ni se, sawi hmiah mai thîn a ni tih a chiang a. Lal leh rorêltute duhsak thlahlela thu dik a hriat ang pawh sawi ngam lo nih chu a duh ngâi lo.

Mikaia hi zâwlnei der 400 rual zet hmaah mal tanin, a hnêna thuchah lo thleng chu a puang chhuak a. Lalpa hnên ata thuchah a dawn chu Lal sual tak Ahaba leh a nupui Jezebeli te tihlâwm nâna sawi lo va, a âiah thu dang sawi zâwk tûra an hrilh pawhin (1 Lalte 22:13, 14)—a lo dawngsawngtute chuan engtin paw’n lo ngâi dâwn mah se—a sawi ta tho a ni.

`1 Samuela 9:3–14 chhiar la. He lâi thu hian mi dangte’n Pathian zâwlnei rinawm takte an hmuh dân engtin nge min hrilh?`

Bible-ah hian an nun pawh entawn tlâk tak tak ni hlawm zâwlneite chanchin kan hmu tam hle. He lâi thu-ah pawh hian Saula chu a pa Kisa’n a sabengtung bote zawng tûrin a tîr a. Saula kalpui chhiahhlawh pakhat chuan, Saula’n haw leh mai a han rawt chuan a lo duh ve hauh lo. “He khuaah hian Pathian mi a awm a, amah chu mi zahawm tak a ni nghe nghe asin” (1 Sam. 9:6, NKJV) tiin a hrilh a. A mi sawi hi zâwlnei Samuela niin, a sawi dân atang hian mite’n Pathian zâwlneite nungchang ni tûra an beisei chu kan lo hre thei mai âwm e. Pathian Lehkhabua kan hmuh zâwlnei dik takte chu “Pathian mite” tia sawi thin niin, Pathian nungchang phur chhuaktu ni tûra thlan an ni a. Chutih rualin “Pathian mite” zînga thenkhat erawh hi chu zâwlnei an nih vâng maiin mi tha famkim erawh an ni kher chuang lo.

`“Pathian mi” kan ngaihsân ber berte zîngah meuh pawh hian mi tha famkim tia sawi tûr hriat i nei rêng rêng em?`
