---
title:  Zawlnei hmeichhiate
date:   14/10/2026
---

`Exodus 15:20, Rorêltute 4:4, 2 Lalte 22:14 leh Nehemia 6:14 chhiar la. Hêng Bible chângte’na zâwlneite hna thawh chungchâng an sawi chu eng nge?`

Pathian Lehkhabuah hian Pathianin zâwlnei ni tûra hmeichhiate pawh a koh thin thu kan hmu tam hle (Hebrai thumal, nevi’ah [Exod. 15:20] tih hman a nih dân leh Grik thumal, prophetis [Luka 2:36] hman a nih dân te en la.) Mika 6:4-ah chuan Miriami pawh Mosia leh Arona ang bawkin Pathian mite hnêna thu sawi tûra thlan a nih thu kan hmu a. Tin, Israel-te’n Tuipui Sen an kân tuma hnehna hlado satu zâwlnei nia sawi a ni bawk (Exod. 15:20, 21). Nimahsela, he zâwlnei hmeichhia pawh hi, a zâwlnei nihpui mipate ang bawkin thil tih dik loh nei ngâi miah lo chu a ni bîk hauh lo tih chu Mosia leh a nupui, Ethiopia mi lak a rilru puthmang pawh khân a tilang a, kha a thiltih zahawmpui tak avâng khân chawplehchilha hrem nghâl a ni ta nghe nghe a ni (Num. 12:1–10 en ang che).

Israel mite chunga rorêltu Debori pawh kha zâwlnei hna lo thawk tawh thîn a ni tih kan hria a (Ror. 4:1–4). Dik tak chuan, Debori khân Pathian hnên ata thu a dawng thîn ngei a ni tih tichiangtu thil thlengte pawh a awm (Ror. 4:4–9, 14–22). A hnehna hlado sak (Ror. 5:2–31) hi Pathian rawn inrawlh avânga hnehna chang an ni tih puan chhuahna a ni a, khatih lâi huna Israel hnam tân khân pawimawhna thûk tak a nei nghe nghe.

`2 Lalte 22:11–14 chhiar la. Enga ti nge Lal Josia mi tirhte hi Huldi hnênah an va kal? Hei hian Israel ramah khân hna pawimawh tak a thawk a ni tih min hriattîr em?`

“Zâwlnei” Huldi hi Lal Josia sawrkâra mi pawimawh tak tak pangate’n lal hmaa “Dân Lehkhabu” an chhiara (2 Lalte 22:8, NKJV) (hemi tuma an chhiar hi Deuteronomi bu a nih rin a ni), lalin chu thu a lo hriata “puante a pawh thlêr” (2 Lalte 22:11, NKJV) hnua an va râwn kha a ni a (2 Lalte 22:14, 2 Chron. 34:22). Hemi tuma an thil tih hi Lal Josia khân a la thutak hle a ni tih a lanna chu, Huldi hnênah a khâwnbâwl upate a tîr nghâl tlat alâwm. Huldi hian Juda ram chunga hremna lo thleng tûr chunchângah Pathian hnên ata thuchah a dawng a. Josia hova siamthatna lo thleng pawh kha Huldi hril lâwkna thupuan rah niin, Pathianin a mite chunga rorêlna thlentîr a lo tum pawh chu a thulh leh ta rih hial a ni.

`Zâwlneite kha—hmeichhia an ni emaw, mipa an ni emaw—Pathianin a koh ni mah se, khawngaihna mamawh mi sual ni ve tho an ni a. An chanchin atang hian eng ang beiseina nge kan lo chhar chhuah theih ang?`
