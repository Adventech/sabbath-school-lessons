### Chhiar tûrte
1 Kor. 2:2; Joh. 1:1–5; 2 Tim. 3:16, 17; 2 Tim. 4:1–5; Isaia 40:1–5; Joh. 17:20–23; 1 Kor. 1:10–13.

> <p>Châng vawn</p>
> “Pathian Lehkha Thu zawng zawng hi Pathian thâwk khuma pêk a ni a, zirtîr kawngah te, thiam loh chantîr kawngah te, zilh kawngah te, felna zirtîr kawngah te a sâwt bawk a ni; Pathian mi chu hna tha tinrêng thawk tura kim taka inthuamin a famkim theih nân” (2 Timothea 3:16, 17).

Pathian hian amah ring mâwl tawp mai tûrin min ti ngâi lo.

A awm ngei a ni tih kan hriat theihna tûr thil tam tak a awm a. Thilsiamte chauh pawh hian, suala kum 6,000 vêl zet an awm hnuah pawh Pathian thiltihtheihzia leh a thilsiamte ropuizia bâkah (James Webb Space Telescope atanga an lâk thlalâkte en la), hmangaihnaa khat a nihzia pawh chiang takin an lantîr a nih hi.

Hei lo pawh hi thil dang a la awm. Petera chuan ama hova tlâng thianghlima an awm lâiin, vân atanga lo chhuak âw chu an hriat thu a sawi a. Chumi hnuah pawh chuan hril lâwkna thu chiang lehzual kan neih thu sawiin, “chu chu khua a lo vâr a, in thinlunga vârparh arsi a lo chhuah hma loh zawng hmun thim chhun êngtu in ngaihsak anga in ngaihsak chuan in ti tha ang” (2 Pet. 1:18, 19) tiin a lo ziak bawk. Petera ang hian kan beng ngei hian Pathian âw chu kan lo hre ve kher lo a ni thei e; nimahsela, “hril lâwkna thu chiang lehzual” chu kan nei ve tho si a ni.

Pathian chuan a zâwlneite—Bible-a thuziak awmte leh awm ve lêm lote pawh—kal tlangin kan hnênah thu a lo sawi tawh a. Kan hnênah thu a lo sawi ngei a ni tih hi a va chiang êm! Zawhna pawimawh tak kan inzawh tûr ni ta chu: Hêng thute hi pawm tûrin kan lo inhawng em? tih hi a ni.

Pathian mite chanchin kal tawhte kha thlîr kîr ila, mi tam tak chu pawm tûrin an inhawng miah lo tih a chiang a. Keini hi chuan, Pathian khawngaihna azârah, an lo tih sual ang khân i ti sual ve lul lo ang u.
