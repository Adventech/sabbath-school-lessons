---
title:  Hril lâwkna thu chuan Krista a châwisâng
date:   20/12/2026
---

Hril lâwkna thu hi Pathianin a mite a rawn biak pawhna hmanraw pawimawh tak a ni a, chu bâkah, amah nêna min thlun zawmtu ni bawk. Chutiang chu a nih avângin hmeichhe (Pathian kohhran) laka Setana (drakon) a thinur êm êm mai pawh kha a mak vak lo rêng a, a thinur êm avâng chuan a thlah la awm chhunte do tûrin a chhuak ta nghe nghe a ni (Thup. 12:17 en la).

He hmeichhe thlah la awm chhunte hriat theihna tûr chhinchhiahna pawimawh tak takte chu eng nge ni? Pathian thupêkte an zâwm a, Isua hriattîrna thu an nei bawk. Thupuan 19:10 kan chhiar chuan, he hriattîrna thu hi “hril lâwkna thlarau” a ni tih kan hria.

`1 Korinth 2:2 leh Galatia 6:14 chhiar la. Paula’n a dam chhan leh Pathian rawng a bâwl chhan ber nia a sawi chu eng nge ni?`

Tirhkohte’n Isua an pawm chhan kha anmahni ngeiin an tawn hriat a nih bâkah, hun hrang hranga rawngbâwl zâwlneite leh mi fel tak takte hriattîrna thu an pawm vâng a ni. Kristiante hian Thuthlung Thar mai ni lovin, Thuthlung Hlui lamah pawh Isua chanchin sawina thu kan nei tam hle a: Thuthlung Hlui lehkhabute hian Chhandamtu lo kal tûr chu min kawhhmuhin, Thuthlung Thar lehkhabute hian Isua kha Chhandamtu lo kal tûra chu a ni tih min hriattîr bawk. Hêng zawng zawng hi a ring duh tân chuan rin chhan tûr tling vek a ni a. “Pathian thiltum chu mihringte hian Pathian leh a Fapa thiltihtheihna leh, Thlarau Thianghlim hnathawh lo rin theihna hun tha nei vek se tih hi a ni.”—Ellen G. White, The Spirit of Prophecy, vol. 3, p. 182.

Chhandamna thlen thei rinna tih hi a taka finfiah theih loh, rilrua pawm mai tûr chi a nihna lâi pawh a awm a. Ngaihtuahna fîm tak hman âi mahin, thinlunga a thawh dân lo ngaihchân mai a pawimawh zâwk. A awmzia chu, kan thinlung kan hawn loh na na na chuan, kan rilru pawh kan khâr ve chhawm mai bawk dâwn tihna a ni.

`Johana 1:1–5 chhiar la. Hêng Bible chângte’n Isua nihna min hrilh chu eng nge ni?`

Isua, Pathian Thu, khawvêl siam a nih hma pawha lo awm tawh chu tisaah lo changin, kan zîngah a lo chêng ve ta a nih chuan, Pathianin a zâwlneite hmanga min lo pêk thute emaw, thuchahte emaw laimu ber a ni tihna a ni a. Hetih rual hian, Isua chanchin chiang taka lo sawi ve lêm lotu zâwlneite pawh kha Thlarau Thianghlim thâwk khum an ni ve tho tih erawh hria ila.

`Vân thengreng zauzia leh, chuta thil awm zawng zawngte chu ngaihtuah la, tin, chûngte siamtu Isua chu kan tâna lo thi kha a ni tih pawh ngaihtuah bawk ang che. Chu chuan eng beiseina ropui tak nge a pêk che?`
