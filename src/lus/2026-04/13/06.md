---
title:  Hril lâwkna thu chuan Krista taksa a tipumkhat
date:   24/12/2026
---

`Johana 17:20–23 leh 1 Korinth 1:10–13 chhiar la. Hêng Pathian Lehkha thu pahnihte hian engti tûrin nge ringtute an sâwm?`

Isua khân kraws a hnaih telh telh lâi khân a thinlung luah rengtu thil pakhat a awm tlat a. Amah zuitu zawng zawngte chu hmangaihna, thil tih tum leh thil tiha amah leh a Pa pumkhat an ni zêl anga pumkhat an lo nih ve a duh a ni. Isua’n nei tûra a duhsak êm êm inpumkhatna hi inan tlân thapna, Mizovin “arpui mei ang that” kan tih ang hi a ni lêm lo. A lo kîr leh hma ngeia lo thleng tûr thu pawimawh tak a sawi a—tobul leh lo chawr chhuahna hmun inang lo tak tak, hnam hrang hrang leh thil thlîr dân, rilru sûkthlêk pawh inang lo êm êmte chu Thlarau Thianghlim thiltihtheihna azârah, amahah leh a Paa an lo inpumkhat vek a duh êm êm a ni. Tirhkoh Paula pawhin rilru hmun khat pu a, thu khat vaw tlâng a, thu thuhmun sawi a, an zîngah inthenna rêng awmtîr lo tûra Korinth khaw mite a ngen pawh khân he thu tho hi tan chhanah a rawn hmang ve leh a (1 Kor. 1:10). Hril lâwknain a phuar khâwm inpumkhatna chu eng vângin nge neih a pawimawh êm em le?

Isua kha “Nang, ka Pa, keimaha i awm ang leh kei pawh nangmaha ka awm ang bawk ang hian an zain pumkhat nihtîr la; nangin kei mi tîr tih khawvêlin an rin theih nân anni pawh keimahniah pumkhatin awmtîr ang che” (Joh. 17:21, NKJV) tiin a lo tawngtâi a.

Pathianin a duh ber chu mi zawng zawng chhandama an lo awm vek a ni a; nimahsela, chhandama an awm theih nân chuan Isua an rin leh, ani chu khawvêl chhandam tûra Pathianin a rawn tirh a ni tih an rin phawt a tûl thung. Mi chi hrang hrang inang lo tak takte’n an Lalpa leh Chhandamtua Isua an pawm a, an inhmangaih tlân a, mahni hmasialna awm miah lova rawng an inbâwl tawnsak theuh hunah khawvêl hian Isua chu nia a insawi Pathian Fapa a ni ngei a ni tih an lo ring tawh ang. Isua’n lo nei se a tih êm êm inpumkhatna hi tum tha tak nei leh thiltithei êm êm ni bawk a ni a. Nimahsela, vânduaithlâk takin, a tîr atang rêng khân Setana leh a tirhkohte chuan tihphel tumin tan an la nasa hle thîn a nih kha. Entîr nân, Tirhkohte 6:1 hi chhiar mah teh. Khami atang khân Kristian kohhranah inthen darhna a lo awm chho chhunzawm ta zêl a nih hi!

He inpumkhatna hi Thlarau Thianghlimin kohhranho hnêna thilpêk hrang hrang a pêkte hmanga a thlentîr a ni a. Ephesi 4:11–15 kan chhiar chuan hêng Thlarau Thianghlim thilpêkte hi “rinna leh Pathian Fapa hriatna kawngah chuan thu khat vua a, puitling a, Krista famkimna lenzia tluka kan zaa kan lo awm hma loh zawng” (Eph. 4:13, NKJV) ringtute tichak leh tifamkimtu atân pêk a ni tih kan hmu. Thlarau Thianghlim thilpêkte zînga pawimawh ber pakhat hril lâwkna thilpêk hi Pathian mite pumkhata an lo awm theih nâna thil neih tûl tak a ni a. A bîk takin Isua lo kal lehna a hnaih tawh bawk si avângin Lalpa Isua Krista an lo rin a, chhandama an awm theih nân (Tirh. 16:31) phei chuan a tûl lehzual hle.

`Seventh-day Adventist Kohhran hi Protestant kohhrante zîngah chuan hnam leh chi hrang hrang inpawlhsawp nasatna ber pakhat a ni maithei. Chutah tak chuan engtin nge mite’n Isua an lo rin phah theih nân kan zîngah inpumkhatna kan lan chhuahtîr theih ang? Chu kan inpumkhat chu eng thilin nge tiderthâwnga tichhe hial thei?`
