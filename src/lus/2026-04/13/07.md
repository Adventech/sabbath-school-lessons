title:  Han tan tha leh teh
date:   25/12/2026
---

Kum eng emaw ti liam ta khân Kellogg company chuan an thil siam (breakfast cereal) fakna tûrin video clip tâwi tê an siam a. An thil siam lei thîn, mahse, mi dangte thil siam leisan tawhtute hîp lêt leh nân tiin, an thil siam chu “vawi khat tal han tem chhin leh tûrin” an sâwm a. He an tawngkam hman hi Seventh-day Adventist, Bible leh Ellen G. White-i chungchâng zirna atâna hun tam tak lo sêng tawh, Bible leh Pi White-i thuziakte chhiar nân erawh hun sêng tlêm êm êm lawi site tân pawh lo tangkâipui theih hle tûr a ni ang em? Ellen G. White-i chungchâng zir hona an buatsaih fo thîn a, chûngahte i tel ve a nih chuan a nun leh a rawngbâwlna chanchin bengvârthlâk tak tak i va hriat belh ngei ang. Amaherawhchu, nangmah ngeiin i “tem” (a thuziakte i chhiar) ve a nih loh chuan a thuchah kente’n malsâwmna an pâi hi a hlutzia i hrethiam tak tak thei dâwn lo va, chu bâkah a takin i lo chang ve thei hek lo ang. A thuziakte chhiar thîn, chutah pawh a ziah lâi hun leh hmun, a ziah chhante ngaihtuah tel chunga chhiarte chauhin Pathianin kan hnêna a lo pêk zâwlnei thuchahte hi a hlutzia an hrethiam thei dâwn a ni.

Tûn kuartar chhûng zawnga kan zir tâk ang khân Ellen G. White-i thuziakte hian Bible thutakah min phunin Pathian nên min inzawmtîr a. Pathian hnên ata a dawn thuchah hrang hrangte han chhiar hian malsâwmna tam tak kan chhar chhuak thei.

Hmân deuh khân Roger Dudley leh Des Cummings, Jr., te’n zir chianna an lo nei tawh a, an thil hmuhchhuah pawh a ngaihnawmin a bengvârthlâk hle nghe nghe. Ellen G. Whitei thuziak chhiar thînte chuan chhiar ve ngâi lote âiin Isua nêna inlaichînna thûk zâwk an nei tlângpui a; tin, chhandam nih inhriatna, chhûng inkhâwm leh mi dangte hnêna Isua chanchin hrilh ve tûl tihna rilru pawh an nei deuh zêl bawk. Heti hian an sawi a: “Hetiang rênga thutlûkna felfâi leh chiang hi zir chianna hrang hrang kan neih tawhah kan la nei ngâi lo. Kohhran than dân chungchâng zir chianna kan neiah, mi mal rilru sûkthlêk emaw, thil tih thin emaw chungchânga zawhna hrang hrang kan siamte atanga kan hmuh chhuah chu: kohhran member, Ellen G. White-i thuziak ngun taka zir thînte chu a châng châng chauha chhiar thînte emaw, chhiar ngâi miah lohte emaw âiin thlarau nunah an sâng zâwk tlângpui a. . . . A awmzia chu, Ellen White-i thuziakte chhiar thinna hian Kristian nun leh rawngbâwlna kawngah a tha zâwngin nghawng a nei tihna a ni.”—Dudley and Cummings, “Who Reads Ellen White?” in Ministry, October 1982, p. 12.

Tûn kuartar chhûng khân Pathian chuan a zâwlneite hmangin kan hnêna a sawi duhte a rawn sawi thîn tih kan lo hre ta a. An thuchah puante chuan Pathian nên min inzawmtîr bâkah, mihring nun pêng hrang hrang, a bîk takin Isuaa awm chhandamna thutiam nêna inzawm chungchângah phei chuan rah tha leh thianghlim tak tak a chhuah thîn bawk: “Aw LALPA thatzia chu tem chhin the rêng u” (Sam 34:8). Tin, Pathian thutak hril darh a, mite nun siam tha a, thil tha ti leh mi dangte rawngbâwlsak tûrin min sâwm bawk a. Hei hi Isua lo kal lehna kan lo nghah paha kan lo tih ve tûr chanchin tha puan darh hna thawk tûra kan lo inbuatsaihna kawng pawimawh tak a ni.
