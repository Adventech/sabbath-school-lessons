---
title:  Hril lâwkna thute’n Bible an nemnghet
date:   21/12/2026
---

A nihna takah chuan Bible hi hril lâwkna thilpêk kal tlanga Thlarau Thianghlim kutchhuak a ni a. 2 Petera 1:21 chuan heti hian Pathianin a zâwlneite a hman dân a lo sawi: “Thu hril lâwk rêng rêng hi mihring thua chhuak a ni ngâi si lo a, miten Thlarau Thianghlim tirhin Pathian hnên ata an sawi zâwk thîn a ni.”

`2 Timothea 3:16, 17 chhiar la. He lâi thu hian engtin nge Pathian Lehkhabu thuneihna leh thâwk khum a nih dân a sawi?`

Bible hi awm ta lo se, khawvêl sual taka thil tha lo leh hlauhawm chi hrang hrang awmte hi a pawmpelh dân kawng kan hre miah dâwn lo tihna a ni a, boral mai loh chu hmabâk kan nei lo ang. Tûnah chuan hmun tinah Bible hmuh tûr a awm tawh a, lehkhabu ang mai bâkah, computer leh cellphoneahte pawh a duh tâna hmeh chhuah mai theih tûrin dah a ni ta bawk. Hetiang hi tûn hma chuan a ni ngâi hauh lo va, entîr nân, Isua hun lâia chhiar tûr an neih awm chhun chu Thuthlung Hlui bu zial chauh niin, chu pawh puithiamte emaw, Juda sakhaw zirtîrtute (rabbis) emaw chauhin an chhiar thei. Mi pângngâi ve tân chuan chhiar ve theih loh tluk a ni a. Chutiang chuan, Pathian atanga chhuak thute hi a lo hlu êm êm thîn a ni.

`Luka 24:27 chhiar la. Isua’n thlân atanga a thawh leh hnua zirtîr beidawng tak tak pahnihte’n a hnathawh an lo hriatthiam theih nâna a tanpui dân chu eng nge ni?`

Isua, zâwlnei ropui ber—amah chauh Thu nia—chuan Pathian Lehkhabu hi a châwisâng hle a (Joh. 7:38). Hril lâwkna thilpêkin malsâwmna a thlente zînga a hlu ber pakhat pawh Bible a rawn nemghet hi a ni.

“Pathian aw nia Pathian Lehkhabu Thianghlim pawmte leh, a zirtîrna thute zawm duhte chuan ni tin zirin, ni tin thlarau lama phûrna leh thiltihtheihna an dawng thar ziah thîn tûr a ni a. Chûngte chu Thlarau Thianghlim thilpêk hmangin ringtu dik takte hnênah pêk a ni thîn.”—Ellen G. White, in Signs of the Times, March 8, 1910. Hril lâwkna thilpêk dik tak chuan Pathian Lehkhabu a châwisâng thîn a. Mite chu an rinna leh an thiltih tehfung awm chhuna Pathian Thu hmang tûrin a hrilh thîn bawk.

`Isua’n tu nge a nih tih a hriattîr hmaa Pathian Lehkhabuin a sawi a kawhhmuh hmasak phawt chhan hi eng nge ni? Hei hian kan rinna innghahna chu Pathian Lehkhabu a ni tûr a ni tih engtin nge min hrilh?`
