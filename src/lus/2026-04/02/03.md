`1 Lalte 18:19–39 chhiar la. He thawnthu atanga zâwlneite hnathawh tûr kan hmuh chu eng nge ni?`

Elija thil tih leh thu a han sawi vêl dân hian Pathianin a thuchah puang chhuak tûra a koh a ni tih a pho lang chiang hle. Israel-te kalsualna titâwp tûra Pathianin a tirh niin, Lal Ahaba hnênah a va kal a, kum eng emaw ti chhûng chu Israel ramah khua a khêng dâwn a ni tih thu a hrilh a. Pathian chuan he chhiatna thleng hi a mite’n milem biakna an lo hawisan a, a thupêkte an lo âwih leh theih nân hman a tum a. Nimahsela, Lal leh a ram mipuite lah chuan he thil thleng hi Elija vâng niah ngâiin, tihhlum an tum ta hlauh si a ni.

Elija chuan Karmel tlângah Baala leh Aseri zâwlneite a hmachhawn ta a. He Pathian zâwlnei hian milem pathian betu Israel mipuite hnênah Thlarau Thianghlima khat ngenna siamin, an milem pathiante chu paih a, Lalpa hnênah kîr leh zâwk tûrin sâwm a. Mipuite pawh chuan an duhthlanna siam dik lo tak khân a hnam pum anga an kalsualna a thlen a ni tih an lo hrethiam ta. Tûnah chuan Elija’n a biak Pathian chu pawm leh tûrin rilru an lo siam fel ta a. Chu Pathian chu an milem pathiante âi chuan chungnung zâwk daih a nih bâkah, an lo biak thin pathiante kha chu nunna nei pawh a ni lo hrim hrim tih pawh an lo hre ta a ni.

“Kan zîngah hian Pathian tih tak takna kawnga harhtharna lo thleng se, chu chu kan mamawh ber leh hmanhmawhthlâk êm êm ni bawk a ni. Nimahsela chu chu Pathianin kan chunga malsâwmna rawn vûr a tum loh vânga dîl lo tûl ta a ni hauh lo va, a dawng tûra keimahni kan lo inbuatsaih loh vâng a ni zâwk.”—Ellen G. White, Selected Messages, book 1, p. 121.

`Malakia 4:5, 6 chhiar la. Elija thu sawi hian engtiangin nge Pathian zuitute chhûngkuaah nghawng a neih thin?`

Harhtharna dik tak chu thinlungah leh in chhûngkhurah a intan thîn. Chhûngkua hi kohhran innghahna, a kila lung pawimawh tak a ni a. In chhûngkhura harhtharna thleng chuan kohhranah, khawtlângah leh ram pum thlengin vân lam chhuak hmangaihna a pu darh thîn. Hei hi Kristian chhûngkaw tâna Pathianin tih a tum pawh a ni a. Tin, harhtharna chuan Pathian mite hi chanchin tha hril darh hna thawk tûrin a chawk tho thîn bawk.

`Kan in chhûngkhurah, kan thinlungah leh kan kohhranahte hian eng ang pathian lemte nge lo awm ve le? Chûngte chu engtin nge kan paih chhuah theih ang?`
