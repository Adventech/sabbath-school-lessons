---
title:  Zâwlnei rawngbâwl hna thawk tûra koh
date:   09/10/2026
---

Pathian chuan zâwlneite kha hna bîk eng emaw thawk tûrin a ko thîn a. Koh a nih hun lâia mite mamawh tak thuchah an puang chhuak thîn. Seventh-day Adventist-te chuan Ellen G. White-i kha tûnlâi huna hril lâwkna thilpêk dawngtu niin an pawm a, Bible-a telh ve ni ta lo lehkhabu ziaktu zâwlneite ang bawk khân (hemi chungchâng hi zirlâi dangah chipchiar zâwkin ka la sawi leh ang) a hunlâi mite hnênah Pathian hnên ata thuchah a dawngte chu a puang chhuak thîn niin an ngâi bawk. Ellen Harmon [White-i] khân kum 1844, December thlaah a tum khatna atân inlârna hmuin, zâwlnei ni tûra kohna a lo dawng a. Chu chu Miller-a hnung zuitu Adventist-te’n Daniela 8:14 thu: “Ni sânghnih leh zathum thlengin; tichuan, biak bûk chu tlen fâi a ni ang” tih hi an zirna atanga Isua lo kal an lo nghah thlawn hnu deuh lawk a ni.

William Miller-a tak kha chuan a nî leh thla leh kum bituk lo mah se, amah zuitute khân Bible calendar anga Tlanna Ni/ Remna Ni an hman thin hun beh chhanin October 22, 1844 chu khawvêla Isua Krista a lo kîr leh hun tûr niin an beisei a.

He thurin hi North America ramah chauh ni lovin, khawvêl hmun dang danga mite pawhin an pawm nasa hle. Vânah khian an Chhandamtu hovin an chêng tawh mai dâwn nia rinna an nei a. Amah Miller-a ber pawh hi an nî leh thla tuk a lo thlen hnaih tak tak chuan a phûr ber berte zîngah mi a ni ve leh tho a ni an ti.

Nimahsela, Krista chu a rawn lang ta rêng rêng si lo va, Miller-a hnung zuitute chuan mihringte chanchina thil thleng lungchhiatthlâk ber mai pakhat, “Beidawnna Râpthlâk” an tih mai chu an tâwk ta a ni.

Hemi tuma thil thleng hi Pathianin Ellen Harmon [White-i] hnêna a inlârna hmuh hmasak ber a pêk phahna a lo ni ta bawk a. A thuchah bulpui ber pawh amah Ellen-i leh ringtu beidawng tak takte fuih phûrna tûr niin, an Bible châng hrilhfiah dân chu dik tâwk chiah lo mah se, an hnênah Pathian a awm tlat tho a ni tih a rawn hriattîr bawk. Inlârnaah chuan lei atanga vân Jerusalem han pawhtu kawng zîm tak mai hmuhtîr a ni a. Chu kawng chu Khaw Thianghlim lam pantu Adventist ringtute’n an lo zawh a. “An hmaa kal Isua, khawpui lam pana anmahni hruaitu chu an thlîr reng a nih chuan him takin an thleng dâwn a ni.”—Ellen G. White, Spiritual Gifts, vol. 2, p. 31.

He a inlârna hmuh hi an tân chuan thil lâwmawm tak a ni a. An rilru buaina leh beidawnna atang chuan Pathianin chêt lâkna thar a siam chhuak dâwn a ni. Isua’n a la awmpui reng a. An hnênah hian a la rawn kal ngei dâwn. Chhandamna kawngah a la hruai zêl bawk a. An tih tûr awm chhun chu amah “thlîr tlat” reng a ni. An beiseina neih chu bânsan loh tûr chhan tha tak tak an nei a, buaina leh beidawnna kârah pawh an nghîng chuang tûr a ni lo.

Tichuan, tleirâwl kum 17 mi, Ellen G. Harmon [White-i] chu zâwlnei rawngbâwl hna thawk tûra koh a ni ta a. A kawng zawh tûr hi, a tîrah phei chuan a awlâi lo hle nghe nghe.

He a thilpêk dawn hi a dik ngei a ni tih zâwi zâwia lo pawm chhoh tâk chu ni tho mah se, amah Ellen G. White-i kha chuan Pathian thuchah puang chhuaktu nih a hreh êm êm thîn a. Pathian zâwlnei nih hi thil âwlâi tak leh hming that hlawhna tûr chi a ni ngâi lo hrim hrim a ni âwm e. Chu vâng chuan, Ellen G. White-i pawh hian Pathian kohna a dawn chu engtin nge a lo chhân ang?

Kâr leh lamah hian Thuthlung Hlui zâwlneite’n hna bîk an thawhte leh harsatna an tawhte chîk takin kan zir ho ang a. Zîrtâwpni lo awm leh tûrah hian an zînga thenkhatte thil tawn kha Ellen G. White-i nun leh rawngbâwlnaah pawh a rawn thleng ve leh chiah a ni tih kan sawi dâwn a ni.
