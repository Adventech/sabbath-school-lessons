---
title:  Ziaka dah thute
date:   28/10/2026
---

Thu sawi tha leh hmingthang eng emaw zât a awm a, chûngte chuan mi tam tak nun hnehin, khawvêl chânchin pawh an her danglam daih châng a awm thîn. Mahse, chûngte chu ziaka dah lo ni ta lo se, an thu sawi tha tak takte chu theihnghilh zui a nih loh vêk pawhin, hun lo kal zêlah lo tihdanglam zeuh zeuh a awm thei dâwn a. Chu vâng chuan, ziaka kan lo dah hian an thu sawite kan hriat reng theih nâna min puih bâkah, chhuan loh awm leh zêlte hnênah pawh kan hlan chhâwn theih phah bawk a ni.

Israel fate’n Aigupta ram an chhuahsan a, Amalek mite an hneh hnu deuh lawk khân Pathianin Mosia hnênah, “Hemi thu hi hriat rengna atân lehkhabuah ziak la” (Exod. 17:14) tiin a hrilh a. Thlarau Thianghlim chuan, Bible-a lehkhabu hmasa pangate leh Sam 90 ziaktu Mosia atanga tanin, kan Bible hman mêka lehkhabu 66 awm ziaktu zâwlneite kha a lo kâihruai ta zêl a ni.

`Exodus 17:14; Joh. 20:30, 31; 1 Korinth 10:11; leh Thupuan 1:19 chhiar la. Pathianin zâwlneite hnêna thuchah a pêkte ziaka dah tûra a hrilh chhan thenkhat chu eng nge ni?`

Bible-a lehkhabu chi hrang hrang awmte hian a ziaktute thu ziah dân hmang bâkah, an thu thiam dân emaw, thiam loh dân emaw pawh an pho lang a. Nimahsela, kâr leh lamah chipchiar zâwkin kan la sawi dâwn tho a, thâwk khum nasat bîk leh thâwk khum nêp deuh bîk a awm hran chuan loh avângin a ziaktute mizia leh thu ziah dân hmang bîk an neihte chuan an thu ziah a tihlu sawt emaw, tihlu lo deuh emaw hran chuang lo. Chutih rualin, Bible-a eng lâite emaw hi chu a dangte âia tangkâi zâwk leh kan tûnlâi nun leh rinna kawngah pawh pawimawh zâwk erawh chu a awm tehmeuh mai a. Nimahsela, chumi a nih avâng chuan Bible-a a then lâi hi a dangte âiin thâwk khum nasat bîk a ni e tihna chu a ni chuang lo.

A châng chuan, Bible ziaktute hian a sawia an lo sawi tawh Pathian thâwk khum thu an dawn chu anmahni ngeiin emaw, anmahni tanpuitu tuin emaw ziakin an dah leh hnuhnawh thîn bawk. Deuteronomy bu-ah hian Mosia’n a thih hmaa Israel mite hnêna thuchah hnuhnûng a sawi chu ziakin a lo dah a. Tin, Chanchin Tha bu pali-ahte hian Isua’n leia rawng a bâwl chhûnga thu a sawite leh a thu zirtîr tam takte chu ziaka dah a lo ni bawk.

Hetih lâi hian zâwlneite’n Pathianin a thâwk khum thu ni lo thu dang an sawi chhâwnte pawh a awm nual bawk a; chu mai a ni lo, Setana thu sawi ngei pawh kan hmu a ni.

Tin, zâwlneite hian an thil hmuh leh hriatte’n anmahnia nghawng a neih dân leh chumi an lo chhân lêt dân pawh an lo ziak tel bawk a (Dan. 8:27; Thup. 22:8, 9 en ang che). Chu bâkah, kan entawn atâna tha tak takte min kawhhmuhin kan inthiar fihlîmna tûr thilte pawh min hriattîr bawk (1 Kor. 10:1–11).

Bible kan chhiarin, tawngkam bungraw tha leh nalh ber ber hmanga ziah chu ni miah lo mah se, hun lo kal zêlah pawh dingchang tûr thuchah dik lo thei lo a pâi a ni tih kan hre reng tûr a ni.
