### Chhiar tûrte
Heb. 1:1, 2; 2 Tim. 3:15–17; 2 Pet. 1:18–21; Exod. 17:14; Thup. 1:19; Ezek. 4:1–8.

> <p>Châng vawn</p>
> “Isua Krista thu puan chhuah, Pathianin a bâwihte hriattîr atâna a hnêna a pêk, thil lo thleng thuai tûr thu chu. Tin, chu chu a vântirhkoh hmangin a bâwih Johana a rawn hriattîr a, chu Johana chuan Pathian thu leh Isua hriattîrna thu, a thil hmuh zawng zawng thu chu a hriattîr a” (Thupuan 1:1, 2).

Tirhkoh Johana kha tirhkoh sâwm leh pahnihte zîngah pawh Isua hnaih zual pathum zînga tel phâk a ni (Luka 8:51; Mat. 17:1; Mat. 26:36, 37). Vâna Krista hruai chhoh a nih tâk hnu khân Jerusalemah rawng a bâwl chhunzawm ta zêl a. Mahse, Rom sipai laka Jerusalem khawpui a tlûk (A.D. 70) hma khân, Rom sawrkârin a awp ram Asia Minor-a kohhranhote enkawl tûrin Ephesi khuaah a pêm ta a ni. Rom lal Domitian-a hnuaia Kristiante tihduhdahna a thlen lâi khân Aegean tuipuia thliarkâr pakhat, Patmos-ah tântîr a ni a. Patmos thliarkâra a tân lâi hian Asia Minor-a awm kohhran pasarihte tân bîk chauh ni lovin, hun lo kal zêla Kristian zawng zawngte tân pawha pawimawh hril lâwkna thuchah a dawng ta a ni (Thup. 1:1–3, 9–11).

Chu hril lâwkna thu inhlan chhâwn dân, Johana ngei pawh a tel ve-na chu hetiang deuh hi a ni: Pa Pathianin Isua Krista hnênah a puang chhuak a, Isua Krista’n vântirkoh hnênah a puang chhuak ve leh a, vântirhkohin tirhkoh Johana hnênah a puang chhuak ve leh a, tichuan, tirhkoh Johana’n a lehkhabu lo chhiartute leh mi dang chhiar lo ngâithlatute hnênah a puang chhuak ve leh ta a ni. Hei hi Pathian rawn inpuan chhuah dân leh zâwlneite a lo thâwk khum thin dân entîrtu tha tak a ni a, Pathian chuan mihringte hnêna a thuchah sawi chhâwng tûrin a zâwlneite a lo hmang thîn rêng a ni.

Tûn kârah hian mihringte hnêna Pathian a rawn inpuan chhuah dân leh a zâwlneite a thâwk khum dân hrang hrang kan zir ho ang a. Mihringte hnênah Pathian thuchah hriattîr a nih dân thlîr kualin, tawngka, ziak leh mak danglam deuh taka an dawn dânte chu kan en ngun zual deuh bîk dâwn a ni.
