---
title:  Pathian thâwk khumna hna thawh dân
date:   26/10/2026
---

Hmasâng ata Bible chauh ni lovin, lehkhabu tam tak leh thuziak chi hrang hrang pawh kan lo nei tawh thîn a. Mahse, Bible hi chu Pathian Thu niin kan ngâi bîk a ni lo’m ni? Lehkhabu dangte laka Bible a danglam bîkna chu eng nge ni?

`2 Timothea 3:15–17 leh Jakoba 1:17 chhiar la. Hêng thute atanga thuchah pawimawh tak kan hmuh chu eng nge ni?`

Bible lehlin (version) hrang hrangte’n 2 Timothea 3:16 thu an lo dah dân hi a inang diak diak lêm lo va; mahse, Bible pum pui hi Pathian atanga lo chhuak a nih thu (2 Pet. 1:20, 21) erawh an sawi theuh hlawm tho a ni.

Bible hrilhfiahna lehkhabu siamtu pakhat chuan heti hian a ziak a: “Paula hian Pathian Lehkhabu hi a pum pui thuin a thlîr niin a lang a, a sawi awmzia pawh Bible thu tinte hi ‘inzirtîr nân te, inzilh nân te, insiam that nân te, leh felna kawng inhrilh nân te a tangkâi a ni’ tih niin a lang.”—G. W. Knight III, The Pastoral Epistles: A Commentary on the Greek Text (Grand Rapids, MI: Eerdmans, 1992), p. 445.

Nimahsela, Bible lo hring chhuaktu Pathian thâwk khumna hnathawh dân (Pathianin Bible ziaktute a awmpui dân) hi engtin nge kan hriatthiam ang? Pathian thâwk khum tih awmzia chu zâwlnei dik takte’n Pathian hnên ata thu dawng thei tûra Thlarau Thianghlimin a puihna (awmpuina) a ni tih kan hre tûr a ni a. Chu puihna chu thuchah an dawn kawngah chauh pawh ni lovin, an thuchah puan lo dawng tûrte hnênah chiang taka an hrilh chhâwn ve lehna kawngah pawh pêk an ni thîn bawk.

Pathian thuchah chu inlârna leh mumang hmangin a lo thleng thîn a (Num. 12:6; Isaia 6:1, 8). Tin, Pathian chuan mahni ngeiin a taka an hmuhna (1 Joh. 1:1–3) te, Thlarau Thianghlim uapna changa thil thlen dân ngun taka an chhuina (Luka 1:1–3) te leh an rilru leh ngaihtuahna a rawn chênchilhna te pawh a hmang thîn bawk (2 Pet. 1:21).

Thlarau Thianghlimin hmanrua a hmante chu eng pawh lo ni se, zâwlneite leh Bible ziaktute hnênah khân a nihna ang anga mite hnêna an hrilh chhâwn ve tûr thuchah pêk a ni thîn a. A awmzia chu, a ziaktute leh a sawi chhâwngtute hian anmahni tawngkauchheh bîk an lo hmang a nih pawhin Pathian thuchah an dawnte erawh an lo tidanglam thiang lo tihna a ni.

Pathian hnên ata thuchah an dawn chu a lo dawngtu tûrte hnênah tawngkain an sawi chhâwng thei a (2 Pet. 1:19–21), tin, a ziaka ziakin (Joh. 20:30, 31) emaw, a lem chang (zia khêl) emaw pawhin an hriattîr thei bawk (Ezek. 4:1–8).

`Bible thute thâwk khum a nih dânah leh, thâwk khumnain hna a thawh dân hrim hrimah pawh zawhna tam tak la awm mah se, Pathian thâwk khum thu a nihna anga Bible rin mai hi—ziak a nih dân dik tak kan hrethiam vek lo a nih pawhin—eng vângin nge a pawimawh êm êm?`
