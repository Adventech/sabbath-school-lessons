---
title:  A tlângpui leh a bîk taka Pathian inpuanna
date:   25/10/2026
---

`Hebrai 1:1, 2 chhiar la. Pathianin kan hnêna thu rawn sawi a duh êm êm chhan hi eng nge ni ang? Isua leh zâwlneite kal tlanga thu a lo sawi chu eng nge ni?`

Lei leh vân leh a chhûnga thil awm zawng zawngte Siamtu Pathian chuan kan hnênah eng thu nge sawi tûr a neih ang? Mi a rawn biak thin chhan hi eng nge ni? Suala tlu tawh mihring, suala khat khawvêla chêng, thihna, hrehawm tawrhna leh fiahna hrang hrang hmachhawn reng mai kan nih vâng a ni a. He khawvêla kan lo awm chhan leh thil tha lo tak tak a lo thlen thin chhan bâkah, chuti chung pawh chuan mi a hmangaihnaa beiseina kan neih theih dân pawh hrethiam tûra min duh avângin kan hnênah thu a rawn sâwi thîn a ni.

A tlângpuia Pathian rawn inpuanna, a thilsiam hrang hranga kan hmuhte hian a thiltihtheihzia leh mi a hmangaihzia fiah leh thiam takin a târ lang a. Lal Davida chuan heti hian a lo sawi: “Vânte khian Pathian ropuizia an puang chhuak a, vân boruak zau tak khian a kutchhuak chu an lantîr a ni” (Sam 19:1, NKJV). Tirhkoh Paula pawhin, “A zepui, mihringte’n kan hmuh theih si lohte (a chatuan thiltihtheihna leh Pathian nihna) chu khawvêl siam a nih atang khân fiah taka hmuh a lo ni tawh a, a thilsiamte hian a hriatthiamtîr vek bawk avângin chhuanlam siam tûr an nei lo” (Rom 1:20, NASB) tiin a lo sawi bawk.

Kan khawvêla nunna nei thil chi hrangte an lo nat emaw, hliam emaw-a a lo dam ve leh chhawm mai theih dân tûr a lo duan te hi a makin a ropui a nia aw! Heti hian Ellen G. Whitei chuan a lo sawi a: “Pathian atang chhuak damna chu thilsiam zawng zawngah hian a inbilh tel vek a. Entîr nân, thing hi kih emaw, a zâr thlâksak emaw ni ta se, chutiangin mihringte pawh hian kan tisaah hliam nei ila emaw, kan ruh tliak emaw ta se, a hliam lâi chu a lo dam theih nân Pathianin a lo dah damna chuan hna a thawk ve nghâl vat thîn. Tihdam hna thawk tûra Pathianin a lo dah sate chu, mamawh an nih hma hauh pawhin an lo inpeih sa diam tawh a; kan taksaa hliam a lo awm ve leh a tihdam hna thawkin a bûr nghâl char char zêl thîn a ni.”—Ellen G. White, Education, p. 113.

Pathian thilsiamte hi mâwiin, Pathian thiltihtheihna leh hmangngaihna lantîrtu chu ni mah se, khawvêlah hian sual pawh a awm ve a ni tih min hriattîrtu a ni ve tho bawk a (Gen. 3:14–19, Mat. 13:24–30 en la). He thu inkâwlkalh tak mai—thil tha leh mâwi tak chuan thil tha lo pawh a awm ve tho a ni tih a lantîr bawk tih awmzia hi a bîk taka Pathian a rawn inpuanna êngah chauh hriatthiam theih a ni. A bîk taka Pathian rawn inpuanna kan tih chu Pathian Thu tisaa lo chang Isua Krista leh Isua chanchin min hriattîrtu, ziaka dah Pathian Thu, Bible te hi a ni (Joh. 5:39).

Pathian Lehkhabu hi hun hrang hranga lo awm mi zawng zawng hnêna Pathian thâwk khum thuchah ziaka lo dah a ni a. Hêng thute hian khawvêl siam a lo nih dân leh suala a tlûk tâk hlauh dân te, Pathian tlanna ruahman ropui tak chanchin leh khawvêl siam thar leh a nih dân tûr te min hrilh a. Biblea kan hmuh chanchin thenkhat hi chu tâwiin, kimchang lo viau tho mah se, an sawi chin erawh a rin tlâk êm êm a, a chhan pawh Pathianin kan hnêna a rawn sawi a nih vâng a ni (2 Tim. 3:16, 2 Pet. 1:19–21 en la).

`Pathian thilsiamte’n Siamtu thiltihtheihzia leh hmangaihnaa khat a nihzia min hriattîr reng chunga ziaka dah Pathian Thu hi kan la mamawh êm êm tho bawk si hi eng nge ni a chhan? Pathian thilsiamte’n anmahni ngâwng ngâwnga min hrilh ve theih loh ziaka dah Pathian Thu Biblein min hrilh si chu engte nge ni?`
