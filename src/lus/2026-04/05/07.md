---
title:  Ellen G. White-i’n Pathian inpuanna leh thâwk khum chungchâng a lo sawi dân
date:   30/10/2026
---

Seventh-day Adventist-te chuan Ellen G. White-i kha Pathian zâwlnei niin an ngâi a. A thuziak, a nun leh a rawngbâwlna te an han zir chianin, zâwlnei dik a ni ngei tih an pawm. Bible-a kan hmuh zâwlneite’n an dawn ang Pathian thâwk khumna tho kha ani pawhin a dawng ve a; mahse, amah kha chuan a thuziakte hi Pathian Lehkhabu nên thuhmun reng niah a ngâi lo. Ngaih ahnehin Bible châwisângtu a ni ngar ngar zâwk a. Chuti an nih chuan a hnêna Pathianin thu a rawn hriattîrte leh Thlarau Thianghlim thâwk khumna chang a nih dân kha engtiangin nge a ngaih?

A hmasa berin, Ellen G. White-i khân Bible hi Pathian inpuanna dik lo thei lo leh thuneihna nei niin a ngâi. Bible hian Pathian thutak leh suala tlu ta mihringte chhandamna atâna ruahmanna a lo siam chu a puang chhuak a, chumi atâna a hmanraw hmante pawh zâwlneite an ni.

Pahnihnaah chuan, Ellen G. White-i khân Pathianin Bible ziaktute a rawn thâwk khumna kawngah hian mihringte thawhpuiah a rawn hmang tih a pawm. Pathian chuan zâwlneite hnênah a thuchah a hriattîr a, chutah zâwlneite chuan anmahni tangkauchheh hmangin an thu dawn chu an puang chhuak leh ta thîn a ni. Heti hian a lo sawi a: “Pathianin a thâwk khum chu Bible thumal tê tê-te hi ni lovin, a ziaktu mihringte zâwk kha an ni. Thâwk khumna chuan a ziaktute thumal emaw, tawngkam emaw hmanah ni lovin, a ziaktu mihringteah zâwk hna a thawk a, anni chuan Thlarau Thianghlim awmpuina changin, an ngaihtuahnate pawh rawn luahsak an ni bawk. . . . Pathian ngaihtuahna leh duhzâwngte chu mihring ngaihtuahna leh duhzâwng nên a lo infin a; tichuan, zâwlneite’n an sawi chu Pathian thu a lo ni ta a ni.”— Selected Messages, book 1, p. 21.

Inlârna hmuh leh mang nung man chungchângah chuan “ngaihtuahna thâwk khum” tih hi Pathian rawn inpuanna leh mihringte thil tawn inkawp niin a ngâi a. “Bible hi Pathian thutak, mihringte tawnga sawi chhuah niin, he lehkhabu hi Pathian leh mihring tang kawp kutchhuak a ni” tiin a lo ziak— Selected Messages, book 1, p. 25. Pathian chuan a thuchah puangtute’n a thuchah an lo hriatthiam theih dân tûr kawng hrang hrang a hmang thîn a. Chumi zingah chuan thil eng emaw lem hmuhtîr te, tawngkaa hrilh te, leh an ngaihtuahna rawn luahsak te pawh a tel. Bible ziaktute’n an lo tawn ang tho kha Ellen G. White-i pawhin a rawn tawng ve a ni.

A tâwp berah chuan, Ellen G. White-i khân mihring a nihna anga thil a tawn zawng zawng leh a sawi zawng zawngte chu Pathian thâwk khum a ni vek chuang lo tih a sawi. “Thil pângngâi sawi ve hun pawh a awm thîn a, ngaihtuahna pângngâiin rilru a luah ve hun a awm bawk. Lehkhathawn pângngâi ziah ve hun pawh a awm thîn a, kan hna thawktu hrang hrangte hnênah an hriat tûr awm thilte hriattîr ve châng pawh a awm tûr a ni. Chûnga ka thil sawi, ka thu hriattîrte chu Pathian Thlarau awmpuina bîk ka chan laia ka sawi emaw, ka ziah emaw a ni lo.”—Selected Messages, book 1, p. 39. Hêngte hi a thuziakte kan chhiara a thil tawnte kan sawi chhuah ni khuain kan hriat reng a pawimawh a, a bîk takin tute emaw nêna lehkha an inthawn tawnnate leh ama diaryte kan chhiar phei chuan a pawimawh lehzual hle.

Ellen G. White-i’n Pathian inpuanna leh Pathian thâwk khumna chang tih a pawm dân hi kan hrethiam a nih chuan, fawr lutuka kan kal palh theihna tûr lakah kan invêng thei ang a, a thurâwn ziahte ngei pawh fîmkhur taka chhiarin a sawi tum tak kan lo man thiam thei dâwn a ni.
