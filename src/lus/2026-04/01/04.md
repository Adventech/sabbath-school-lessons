---
title:  Pathianin mihringte a rawn zawng thîn
date:   29/09/2026
---

Genesis 3:9, 10 kan chhiar chuan, Pathianin suala tlu ta a fate zawng chhuak tûra hma a rawn lâk dân kan hmu a. A zawhna, “Khawiah nge in awm?” tih hi an awmna a hriat loh vânga zâwt ni lovin, thiam taka a thil sawi duh tak a sawi chhuahna kawng a ni zâwk. A chhan chu Evi leh Adama te thil tih kha a lo hre vek a, khawiah nge an awm tih pawh hrilh hran a ngâi rêng rêng lo. Nimahsela, sual chuan Pathian leh nupa hmasa berte inkâra inbiak pawh tawnna awm reng thîn kha a lo titâwp ta daih a. Pathianin a duh ber chu a fate nên hmâi chhana inbiak leh a, anmahni rawn kawm a, an beidawn lâi taka beiseina rawn pêk a ni. Dik tak phei chuan, Genesis 3:9-a kan hmuh, “ko” tih hi Hebrai Bible-ah chuan qara’ a ni a, he thumal hi Pathianin thil sawi dunpui/hopui tûra a mite a rawn sâwm thu sawina atân hman a ni thîn nghe nghe.

`Luka 15:11–32 chhiar la. He tehkhin thu hian engtiangin nge Genesis 3:9, 10-a thil thleng hi a awmzia a târ lan?`

Pathianin Evi leh Adama te a rawn zawng hian, thu delh kilh engmah awm lova a fate a hmangaihzia a pho lang. Pathian chuan min thlauh thla ngâi lo va (Luka 15 en la). Amah nêna kan lo inzawm leh theihna tûr a nih dâwn phawt chuan— amah kan birûksan lâi ngei pawhin—eng kim tih a huam thîn. Isua khenbeh a nih hma, Petera’n vawi thum ngawt a phat lâi khân Krista chuan Petera lam va “hawiin,” amah a “en” a (Luka 22:61). Chutiang chuan, thu kam khat mah chhâk chhuak si lovin, Petera hnênah khân thu a sawi a, thil sual titu a thianpa chu a hnêna hruai kîr leh hrâm a tum a ni.

Genesis 3:11–13 chhiar la, sualin Evi leh Adama te inkâr thu a tihchhiat chawpchilh nghâl dân chu i lo hmu thiam em? Anmahni thil sual tih chu pawm ta mai lovin an inmawhpuh tawn ve ve zâwk a. Adama hian Evi a mawhpuh a, a awmzia chu Evi hi Pathianin a pêk a nih avângin ama thil sual tiha mawhphurtu tak chu Pathian niah a ngâi tihna a ni. “Ka bula awm tûra min pêk hmeichhia khân thing rah chu min pe a, ka lo ei ve ta mai a ni” (Gen. 3:12, NKJV) a ti a nih kha. Hetih lâi hian, Evi pawhin Pathian thu a zawm loh chhanah rûl a lo puh ve tho bawk a. “Rûlin min tihder a, ka ei ta mai alâwm maw le” (Gen. 3:13, NKJV) tih chu a chhuanlam siam a ni. A tu mah mah hian—an thil sual tih hriat chhuah a nih khân—anmahni duh thu rênga ti niah an inpawm lo va. He thil a thlen atanga kum sâng ruk vêl zet a liam leh hnu-ah pawh thil a la danglam vak chuang lo chu a ni lo’m ni?

Thil nihna dik tak chu mihring zawng zawng hi sualin min nghawng ta vek tih hi a ni. Tirhkoh Paula chuan Rom 7:15– 24-ah chiang takin kan dinhmun dik tak chu a lo sawi a. Thil dik leh tha tih duhna a neih ve châng pawhin, chumi letling chiah chu a tih leh zâwk tlat thin si avânga hrehawm a tih thu a sawi chhuak hial a ni. “Ka thil tih mêk hi a awmzia pawh ka hrethiam lo. Tih ka duh êm êm thilte chu ka ti thîn lo va; chutih lâiin, tih ka duh miah loh thilte chu ka ti fo zâwk lawi si” (Rom 7:15, NKJV) a ti.

Keini pawh Paula ang tho hi kan ni ve lo maw?

Paula’n a sawi hi a ril khawp mai. Kan zavâi hian mi beidawng tawh, kan tih loh tawp tûr a ni tih kan hriat reng thilte la ti chhunzawm zêl thîn kan ni a. Chutiang thilte ti ve miah lo chu tu nge awm ang?

Kan beisei awm chhun chu “kan Lalpa Isua Krista kal tlanga” chhanchhuahna a nih thu Paula’n a lo sawi a (Rom 7:25). Chu vâng chuan alâwm Pathian pawhin Isua hnêna hruai thlen kan nih theih nân min la rawn zawn chhunzawm zêl ni. Isua chauh hi Pathian anpui ni leh tûra min siam theitu a ni a, ani chuan sualin a thlen boralna lak ata min chhandam thei bawk.
