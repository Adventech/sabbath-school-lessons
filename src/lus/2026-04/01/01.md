### Chhiar tûrte
Gen. 1:27, 28; Gen. 3:1–8; Luka 15:11–32; Gen. 3:14–24; Exod. 19:16–22; Joh. 10:27–30.

> <p>Châng vawn</p>
> “Pathian, hmasânga thlahtute hnêna bung tam tak leh kawng tinrênga zâwlneitea thu lo sawitu khân, hêng ni tâwpah hian keimahni hnênah a Fapaah chuan thu a sawi ta. Chu mi chu engkim rochungtuah a ruat a, chu mi pawh chuan khawvêlte chu a siam a ni” (Hebrai 1:1, 2).

Tûnlâi thiamnain a siam chhuah khâwl thil chi hrang hrangte hian hmun hla taka awm kan chhûngkhatte leh thian thate pawh min hmuhtîr thei ta a. Thil tha tak chu a ni e. Hmun leh hmun ngeiah hmu thei phei ila chuan a va tha leh zual dâwn êm! A chhan pawh kan han inhmuh khâwm a, kan han inchibai tawn a, hmâi chhan ngeia han inbiak hi chuan thil danglam bîk tak a thlen thin vâng a ni.

Tûn kârah hian mihringte hi an Siamtu Pathian nên hmâi chhana inbiaa, inlaichînna thûk tak nei tûra siam an nih dân kan hre thar leh dâwn a. Nimahsela, chu an inzawmna ril tak chu kum sâng tam tak liam taah khân tihchhiat a lo ni tawh a nih kha. Pathian chuan duh taka a siam mihringte nên hmâi chhan ngeia an lo inbiak leh theih nân kawng hrang hrang a rawn buatsaih ta a. Mihringte hnêna thu a rawn sawi dân kawng hriat tlânglâwn ber chu amah ngeiin fîmkhur taka a thlan, atâna thu sawitu tûr, zâwlnei tia kan hriat tâkte hi niin, anni hian Pathianin kan hnêna sawi duh a neihte chu min hrilh chhâwng thîn a ni. Hêng zâwlneite hian Bible-a lehkhabu hrang hrang awmte hi an lo ziak a; mahse, lehkhabu emaw, lehkhathawn emaw ziak ve tho, an thuziakte erawh Bible-a dah ni ve ta lo pawh eng emaw zât an awm bawk.

Pathianin a duh leh a tum ber ni bawk chu sualin a tihkhawloh tawh, keimahnia ama anpui nihna awm thîn kha din thar leh a ni, chu chu tlanna ruahman tiin kan sawi mai thîn. Chu din thar lehna chu Pathianin “lei thar leh vân thar, a chhûnga felna awmna chu” (2 Pet. 3:13, NKJV) a siam hunah a famkimin a thleng tawh ang.

Chumi a thlen hma chuan kan hnênah thu a la sawi chhunzawm zêl rih dâwn a, chumi atâna an hmanraw hman ber pawh a zâwlneite a la ni zêl bawk dâwn a ni.
