---
title:  Eden huana Pathian leh mihringte inbiak dân
date:   27/09/2026
---

Genesis 1 leh 2 hian Pathianin khawvêl leh a chhûnga thil awmte siam an nih dân min hrilh a. Pathian chuan khawvêl mâwi leh tha tak siamin, chutah chuan a thilsiamte zînga chungnung ber Evi leh Adama te kha a dah a nih kha. Mihringte chu leia Pathian thilsiam dang zawng zawng lakah an danglam bîk hle a, a chhan pawh “Pathian anpuia” siam an nih bîk vâng a ni (Gen. 1:27). Anni chauh kha chutianga siam chu niin a sawi a, thil dang, entîr nân, savate emaw, ramsate leh rante chu Pathian anpuia siam an nih thu sawi a ni ve lo. Chutiang chu a nih avângin an tân dam man a awm bîk êm êm a, hmangaihna pawh a famkima changin, Pathian nên ni tin an lêng ho thîn a ni.

Tin, Genesis 2:18–23 kan chhiar chuan Pathianin a mihring siam Adama kha amah chauha a awm tha a tih loh (Gen. 2:18) avângin a awmpui atân Evi a siamsak tâk thu pawh kan hmu bawk a. Chutiang chuan, a tîr atang rêng khân mihringte hi a huhova chêng za tûra siam kan lo ni. Mihring nun ho dân zir mite (sociologists) chuan a hova kan chên a, mi dangte nêna thil kan han tih ho hian kan taksa leh rilru pawhin a thatpui êm êm tih an sawi thîn a, chutih lâiin, mahni chauha awm leh mal riauva inhriatna erawh thil hlauhawm tak a ni thung.

Chutiang chu a nih avângin khatia Pathianin Evi a siam hnu a, Adama’n, “Hei zet chu ka ruh ang ruh nei a, ka tisa ang tisa nei ngei chu a ni; mipa laka lâk chhuah a nih avângin ‘hmeichhia’ tih tûr a ni ang” (Gen. 2:23) tia a lo sawi pawh kha sawi âwm tak rêng a lo ni e. Chhûngkaw ruhrel, inneihnaa hmeichhe pakhat leh mipa pakhat te pumkhata an insiamnain a din hi mihringte tâna Pathianin a huhova an awmna tûr a lo buatsaih hmasak ber a ni a, hemi hmang hian inlaichînna leh inzawmna famkim chu an lo chang thei thîn bawk. Chutiang chu a nih avângin Setana’n a tum ber pakhat ni fo pawh Pathianin a lo duan dân anga din inneihna leh chhûngkua tihchhiat a ni.

`Genesis 1:27, 28; Genesis 2:15–17; leh Genesis 3:9 chhiar la. Pathian leh Eden huana chêng mihringte inkâra inbiak pawhna awm kha eng ang nge ni?`

Evi leh Adama te kha Pathian nên hmâi chhanah an inbe thîn a. Ngaihtuahna hmang thei a nih angin Pathian an hmu thei a, an hnêna thu a rawn sawite pawh an lo ngâithla thei bawk. Pathianin a siam huan mâwi leh ropui tak enkawltu an nih angin thiamna leh hriatna kawngah pawh an thang chho zêl a, duat tak leh awmzei neiin thing leh mau, thlâi chi hrang hrang leh ramsate kha an enkawl thîn. Ni tinin, “tlâi ni nêmah” (Gen. 3:8) Pathian nên an lêng ho ziah thîn a. Pathianin a thil siam hna a chawlhsan tâk ni Sabbath phei chu Pathian leh a fate’n hun hlimawm leh nuam tak an hman hona atân a bîk taka lo serh hran sa thlap a ni nghe nghe (Gen. 2:2, 3; Exod. 20:8–11). Chumi ni chuan, kan nu leh pa hmasa berte khân Pathianin anmahni a hmangaihzia leh a enkawlna an dawn nasatzia a hre thar ziah thîn a ni.

`Keini tûnlâi huna mi, Eden huan atanga Evi leh Adama te hnawhchhuah a nih atanga kum sâng tam tak liam hnua lo piang ve chauh tân hi chuan khawvêla sual a lo luh hmaa thil awm dân kha eng ang tak nge ni ang tih suangtuah thiam pawh a harsa tawh hle a. Chutiang chu ni tho mah se, engtiangin nge thilsiamte hian a tîra Pathianin a siam a lo ropui hle ang tih rin theihna min neihtîr le?`
