title:  Ellen G. White-i
date:   02/10/2026
---

Pathianin mihringte a rawn biak pawh thinna hi Bible hun lâiah chauh khân a ni bîk chuang lo. Kâr lo awm leh tûrahte pawh kan hmu chho zêl ang a, Pathian chuan mihringte hi la be chhunzawm zêlin, Krista lo kîr leh hma chuan kan hun lâi nêna inrem zêlin kan hnênah a thute a rawn puang thîn dâwn a ni.

Ellen Gould Harmon White-i leh a phîrpui Elizabeth-i te chu November 26, 1827 khân America ram state pakhat, Maine-a Gorham khuaah an lo piang a. Eunice-i leh Robert Harmon-a te fa hrin pariat zînga a naupang ber an ni. Naupang tê an la nih lâiin Maine state-a awm tho, Portland-ah an pêm a, an pêm chhan pawh Robert Harmon-a hian lukhum siamna dâwr lian zâwk hawn theih a beisei vâng a ni. Ellen-i hian kum 9 a nihin a taksa, rilru leh nunphung zawng zawng nghawng buai vektu tawh sual a tâwk hlauh mai a. An in bul hnâi maia awm park-a a awm lâiin an thiannu, school lama a pâwlpui hmeichhe naupang pakhat thinrim chuan a lam hawia lung vawm pheiin, chu lung chuan a hmâiah tak a fuh a, chawlhkâr eng emaw zât chhûng chu nikhaw hre lo titihin a awm zui ta nghe nghe. He a tawhsual tawh avâng hian school pawh a kal chhunzawm thei ta lo va, a dam chhûng zawng chu bawrhsâwmna eng engin emaw a tlâkbuak deuh reng thîn bawk. A hliam tawrh chuan a hmâi awm dân a tihmâwi loh deuh tâk avângin hrehawm a ti êm êm thîn a, mite zînga awm ve pawh nuam ti lovin a inkiltawih deuh tlat zêl tawh a ni.

Hetiang dinhmuna a din lâi hian, tleirâwl mai la ni mah se, a thlarau lam dinhmun tûr ngaihtuahna a nei tan ta a. Pathian chu hlauvin vânram kâi tlâk pawh niin a inhre lo hial thîn. Chutianga hlauthâwng taka a awm lâi chuan an chhûngkua hian Miller-a leh a hnung zuitute’n Isua a lo kal têp tawh a ni tih thu an sawi chu an lo pawm ve ta a. Methodist Kohhranah hnim phûm ngeia baptisma pawh lo chang tawh ni mah se, Pathian hmangaihna dawng tlâk niah a inngâi chuang lo. “Kumkhua atâna boral” tûr nia inngaihna a nei tlat thîn a ni (Life Sketches of Ellen G. White, p. 29; pp. 20–31 pawh en bawk rawh).

Chutiang ngaihtuahna tha lo tak tak a neih thin lâi chuan Pathian a hmuh dân thlâk danglamsak vektu mumang pahnih a nei ta a. Nimahsela, Methodist thu hriltu, Levi Stockman-a nên an inkawm hma zawng chuan Pathian hmangaihna leh khawngaihna awmzia hrethiamin a la chang rih chuang lo thung. A beidawn lâi takin Pathianin a hnênah thu a rawn sawi niin a ngâi a. “Tûnah chuan rinnain ka thinlung a luah khat ta. Sawi fiah thiam zawh rual loha chiangin Pathian hmangaihna ka chang tih inhriatna ka nei a, ka sualte pawh ngaihdam a ni ta tih Thlarau Thianghimin min rawn hriattîr bawk. Pa Pathian ka hmuh dân pawh a lo dang ta daih a. Tûnah chuan mi khirh tak, a thupêk chu ihe lova zâwm mai tûra mi tilui thîn niah ka ngâi tawh lo; pa zâidam leh ngilnei tak a ni tih ka lo hre ta a. Ka thinlung hi amah hmangaihna thûk takin a lo khat ta a ni ber mai.”—Life Sketches of Ellen G. White, p. 39.

Hetih lâi hian mihringte kan lo thih hnua kan awm dân pawh a lo hrethiam ta a, kumkhuaa kâng reng hremhmun pawh a awm lo tih ngaihdân pawh zâwi zâwiin a lo pawm ve ta bawk. Pathian mizia leh a hmangaihna a hriatthiam dân thar chuan ama ngaihtuahna pawh a thlâk danglamsak ta vek a. A dam chhûng zawngin Isua hmangaihna thûk tak neiin a rawng a bâwl chhunzawm ta zêl a ni.
