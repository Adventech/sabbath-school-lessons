---
title:  Pathian rawngbâwl nân chauh a ni
date:   18/12/2026
---

Ephesi 4-ah chuan thlarau lam thilpêkte pêk a nih chhan ber chu ringtute’n Pathian rawngbâwl hna an thawh theih nân a nih thu Paula’n a sawi a. Chu ngei chu Ellen G. Whitei rawngbâwlna pawhin thupui bera a neih thin a ni. Pathian awmpuina changa a dawn thuchah hrang hrangte hian ringtute’n Pathian nên inlaichînna thûk zâwk an lo neih theih nân a tanpui thîn a; tin, chu hmangaihna chu mi dangte hnênah pawh pe chhâwng ve tûrin a fuih thîn bawk.

“Kristian nia inchhâlte zîngah rau rau pawh Seventh-day Adventist-te chu khawvêl hmaa Krista châwisâng nasa bertu an ni tûr a ni. Vântirhkoh pathumna thuchah hian Sabbath thu dik puang chhuak tûrin min hrilh a. He thu hi, he thuchaha inbilh tel thil dang zawng zawng nên puan chhuah vek tûr niin, a laimu leh pawimawh ber ni bawk Krista Isua hi kan puan hmaih tûr a ni lo. Krawsah khân khawngaihna leh thutak a lo intâwk a, felna leh remna pawh a infâwp tawn bawk. Mi sual chu Kalvari thlîr tûra hrilh tûr a ni a; naupang tê-in rinna mâwlmang tak a neih ang hi neiin, Chhandamtu thil tha tihte chu a rin chhan ang a, a felna chu ama felnaah lo pawmin, a khawngaihna chu a ring ngei bawk tûr a ni.”—Gospel Workers, pp. 156, 157.

Tin, ringtu tinte chu Pathian chhandamna hna thawhnaa tel theuh tûr an nih thu pawh a sawi bawk a. “Lalpa chuan kan kohhran member-te hi tûna an thawh dân ang âia nasa zâwk daiha thawk tûrin a phût a ni” a ti.— Evangelism, p. 113.

“Lalpa hna kan thawhna kawngah hian tu mah zawmthaw kan awm tûr a ni lo. Mi chi hrang hrangte hi mihring mantu theuh kan nih angin kan thawk ho tûr a ni a. Mite chu khawvêl sualna ata chhanchhuakin, Krista hmangaih chhandamna thianghlim takah chuan i hruai lût zêl ang u.”—Evangelism, p. 115. Amah Ellen G. White-i ngei pawh hi kum 1880 chho vêl khân Europe ramah-ah missionary-in a lo awm ve reng tawh thîn a; tin, kum 1890 chho vêlah khân Australia ramah a lo awm tawh thîn bawk.

Tâwpna huna Pathian mite chuan Pathianin amah pawm tûr leh, a chhandama thu hril darh tûra thu a pêk chu an zawm avângin an lakah Setana a “thinur” hle a. Anni hi tâwpna hun atâna thuchah nei, zâwlnei kaihhruaina dawng, leh tâwpna huna thil thlengte awmzia hrethiama Isua zuitute chu a ni. “Lalpa chuan kan hma leh kan vêla hlauhawm awmte chu min hmuhtîr tawh a. Hril lâwkna thlarau hmanrua hmangin khawvêl sala mantu tûr bumna thute pawh lo pho lang vek tawh bawkin, a mite hnênah, ‘Hei hi kawng chu a ni, hetah hian kal rawh u’ [Isaiah 30:21] tiin a hrilh a. Ka lehkhabu ziah, Indona Ropui hian Setana bumna thu hrang hrangte chu a hâi lang vek a ni.”—Ellen G. White, manuscript 31, 1890.

Satana chu miten Isua hnêna an nun an hlan hian a thinur êm êm thîn a, Pathianin a thu puan chhuah nâna a mi hman pawhin rin loh a hlawh theih nân a tih theih ang apiang tih a tum thîn bawk. Ellen G. White-i’n hril lâwkna thilpêk a kawl hi mite’n tûnlâi hun atân “pawimawhna nei tawh lo” tia an lo ngaih theih nân kawng hrang hrangin tan a la thîn a. A lehkhabute hi kan tûnlâi hun nêna inhlat taka a ziakte a lo ni ve ngei bawk si. Tin, a hril lâwkna thu puante hi mite’n an rinhlelha, zâwlnei dera an ngaih theih nân nasa takin a bei reng bawk a. Mahse, Ellen G. White-i thuziakte hian pawimawhna a nei lo ni anga mi a ngaihtîr dân kawng hlawhtling ber erawh chu chhiar lo tûra tûnlâi chhuante a hmin ta deuh duak hi a ni âwm e.
