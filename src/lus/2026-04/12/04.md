title:  Thlarau thilpêkte
date:   15/12/2026
---

Krista kohhran hi tha taka a kal zêl theih nân Pathian chuan a rawngbâwlna tanpui tûrin thilpêk hrang hrang a lo pe a. Chûng thilpêkte ziah chhuahna pawh chu hmun eng emaw zâtah kan hmu nghe nghe. Thlarau thilpêk hrang hrang an ziahte pawh inang tlâng tak chu ni hlawm mah se, danglam tlêm tlêm pawh a awm tho bawk a. Chûng a inan lohna takte chu an intihfamkim tawnna a ni hlauh zâwk a ni.

`Ephesi 4:11–14 chhiar la. Eng vângin nge hêng thilpêkte hi Pathianin a kohhran hnênah a lo pêk? Hril lâwkna thilpêk hian kohhran tâna Pathianin ruahmanna a lo neihte chu engtiangin nge a hlen chhuah?`

Thlarau lam thilpêk eng pawh hi pêk a nih chhan chu kohhranho châwm nân leh siam that nân a ni a, tin, a zavâi hian chanchin tha hril darhna hmanrua atâna hman vek tûr a ni bawk. Paula’n thlarau lam thilpêkte a ziah chhuahna hrang hrang paliahte hian hril lâwkna thilpêk (ani chuan “zâwlnei” tiin a sawi mai a) a tel zêl a. Chutih lâiin thilpêk dangte erawh chu hmun khatah chauh zêl a lang hlawm thung. Hetia hril lâwkna thilpêk a sawi tel zêl avâng hian hril lâwkna hi thlarau lam thilpêkte zînga a pawimawh ber pakhat a ni tiin kan sawi thei ang.

Zâwlneite nihna tlângpui lo sawi ila:

1. Zâwlneite chu Pathianin a kohte an ni (1 Lalte 19:16, 19– 21 leh Isaia 6 en la). Anmahni hi chuan zâwlnei ni tlâkah pawh an inngâi lo tlângpui a, an kova Pathianin mawhphurhna a nghah pawh phur an zuam lo thîn hle. Nimahsela, a kotu an zah avângin an pawm mai thîn thung a ni.
2. Pathian chuan inlârna hmang te, mumang hmang te lehbenga a ri hriat theih aw hmang te’n anmahni a rawn be thîn a. A châng chuan nakîna thil lo la thleng tûrte pawh a lo hrilh lâwk thîn bawk. Tin, hnam thenkhatte emaw, mi thenkhatte emaw thil tawn mêk pawh a hriattîr châng pawh a awm thîn (Daniela 2).
3. Pathian Lehkhabu hian zâwlneite’n inlârna an hmuhlâia an taksa awm dân min hrilh a: tha leh chakna an nei lo va, tin, an thâwk pawh a tâwp rih châng pawh a awm fo bawk; chutih rual chuan, chakna thar pêk ni taa inhriatna pawh an nei bawk thîn (Dan. 10:8–10, 18, 19).
4. An thuchah dawnte chu kawng hrang hrang—tawngkahmang (Tirh. 21:10–12) te, a chana chang (Ezekiel 4) te leh lehkhathawn ziak hmang (Thupuan 2, 3) te’n an sawi chhâwng ve leh thîn a. Zâwlnei thenkhat chuan an thu sawi duh lo ziahsaktute pawh an nei bawk, entîr nân, Jeremia pawh khân amah puitu Baruka a nei a (Jer. 36:1–4).
5. Zâwlneite thuchah puan chu anmahni hmaa rawng lobâwl tawh zâwlneite thuchah puan nên a inrem tûr a ni a, a chhan pawh Pathian chu amah leh amah a inkalh ngâi loh vâng a ni (Isaia 8:20).
6. An thuchah ken leh anmahni mi mal nunin nghawng aneihte chuan Pathian hnên ata lo chhuak a ni tih a hriattîr ang (Mat. 7:15–20).
7. Setana chuan Pathianin hna a thawh rêng rêngin a lememaw, a suak emaw lo siam chhuah ve a tum nghâl zêl thîn a. Chutiang chu hril lâwkna thilpêk chungchângah pawh hian a rawn ti leh chat rêng bawk. Tâwpna hunah chuan zâwlnei der, zâwlnei suak an lo la chhuak dâwn a ni (Mat. 24:24, 2 Pet. 2:1).
