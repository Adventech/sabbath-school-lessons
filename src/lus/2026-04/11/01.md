### Chhiar tûrte
2 Pet. 2:5; Ror. 4:4–16; 1 Lalte 18:21, 38, 39; Isaia 59:1–4; Neh. 9:30; Thup. 1:1–3.

> <p>Châng vawn</p>
> “Dik takin Lalpa PATHIAN chuan a rawngbâwltu, a zâwlneite hnênah a thurûk hrilh lovin engmah a ti lo vang” (Amosa 3:7).

Suala Evi leh Adama te an tlûk tâk chinah khân mihringte hian Isuaa awm chatuana nunna âi chuan rei lo te chhûng chauh sual nawmnate thlan zâwk kan châk ta tlat mai hlawm a nih hi! He thu lungchhiatthlâk tak hi Pathian chuan hre vek mah se, mi a hmangaih êm êm tho a; chu vângin, chhandama kan lo awm theih nân hna a thawk reng thîn a ni.

Mihringte chanchina hun pawimawh tak tak lo thlengahte khân Pathian chuan a mite tân, zâwlneite rawngbâwlna kal tlangin thil ropui leh chhinchhiah tlâk tak tak a lo ti tawh thîn a. Nimahsela, a hmalâkna chu mite’n an lo ngaihsak lo lui tlat a nih erawh chuan a chungah hremna a chelek dâwn tih a hrilh ta mai thîn bawk.

Lalpa chuan a zâwlneite kal tlangin a thil tih tumte a lo sawi lâwk hmasa phawt thîn a (Amosa 3:7). A mite’n inchhîra an sualte an lo sim a nih phawt chuan a ngâidam leh nghâl mai thîn bawk.

Tûnlâi hunah hian, keini hnênah pawh thu a la rawn sawi chhunzawm zêl tho a. Chu vângin, Pathian chuan, kum za tam tak liam taa a mite hnêna thu a lo sawi thîn ang chiah khân, ni hnuhnûnga chêng a mite hnênah pawh thu a sawi leh ang tih hi a rin ngam êm êm a ni.

Tûn kârah hian chhandamna chanchina hun pawimawh tak tak lo thlengah khân Pathian chuan a mite hnênah leh khawvêl hnênah pawh a duhzâwng leh thiltumte chiang taka hriattîr nân zâwlneite a lo hmang thîn a ni tih kan zir dâwn a. Tin, Adventist kohhran a than chhoh zêlna lama hun pawimawh tak tak a tawnahte khân Pathian thâwk khumna chang Ellen G. White-i rawngbâwlnain hna a thawh dân pawh kan en tel bawk ang.
