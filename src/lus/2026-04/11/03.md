---
title:  Israel rama nu pakhat
date:   07/12/2026
---

Pathian chuan khatia Aigupta bâwih nihna atanga Israel fate a hruai chhuah hnu khân an hnênah Kanan ram a pe ta a. A dânte leh a thupêkte an zâwm a nih phawt chuan malsâwmna tam tak vûr a tiam bawk a nih kha. Chutih rualin, amah nêna thu an lo thlun tawh chu an bawhchhia a nih erawh chuan hrehawm nasa tak tuarin, an boral hial dâwn a ni tih pawh a hriattîr bawk (Deuteronomi 27–30). Nimahsela, Israel-te chuan Josua a thih hnu khân Lalpa nêna thu an thlun chu an hawisan ta daih a. chhehvêla chêng hnam dangte’n rap betin, an mangang lutuk chuan Pathian an au va. An au thâwm chu Lalpa’n lo chhângin, “rorêltute a siamsak a, chûngte chuan anmahni rawktute kut ata chu an rawn chhan chhuak ta a” (Ror. 2:16). Tin, Pathian nêna thu an thlun kha thar thawh leh tûra fuih tûr leh, anmahni rap betute do lêt ve tûra huaisenna pe tûrin zâwlneite pawh a tîr bawk.

`Rorêltute 4:4–16 leh Rorêltute 5:1–5 chhiar la. Israel-te tâna Debori’n thil a tih chu eng nge ni?`

Debori hian Israel mite leh Israel sipai hotu Baraka hnênah Pathian thuchah hlân chhâwngin, an hmêlmate an hnehna kawngah chanvo pawimawh tak a chang a. Baraka hnênah

Sisera sipaite beih hun tûr hriattîrin, “‘Khai le, vawiin hi LALPAN Sisera chu a pêk ni che a ni e: I hma hruaitu chu LALPA a ni lo’m ni?” (Ror. 4:14) a ti a nih kha.

Amah hi Israel rama rorêltu a nih avângin mi pawimawh tak a ni a. A kaihhruaina leh Pathianin hnehna a chantîr dâwn a ni tih a rin ngamna chuan Israelte kha an hmêlmate an beihna kawngah huaisenna leh chakna a pe thîn. Chutih lâiin, Israel sipai hotu ber leh indo dân phung pawh hre hneh êm êm tawh Baraka erawh kha chu a zâm deuh tlat thung a, hlau chung chunga sipaite a kâihruai ta tho kha thil pawimawh tak chu ni mah se, hnehna an chan chhan tak erawh a ni lo.

Hei hi zâwlnei Debori chanchin leh sipai hotu Baraka chanchin atanga zirlâi kan zir chhuah theih pawimawh ber pawh a ni hial âwm e. Khatia hnehna ropui tak an chan hnu khân hêng Israel rama pawimawh leh thil ti thei ber ber pahnihte—rorêltu leh sipai hotu—hian hmahruaiin, Israel mipuite chu Pathian châwimâwina hun an neihpui ta a. Hetiang hi hruaitu dik takte’n an tih thin a ni rêng bawk.

Debori’n zâwlnei a nih anga thil a tih hian kan mangan êm êm lâi leh, rah beha awm lâi pawhin Pathian chuan min ngaihsak reng tho a ni tih a lantîr a. Baraka hnêna sawi tûr thuchah Pathianin a pêk khân, chutiang zâta tam sipai chu koh khâwm theih a rinawm loh thu a sawi lo va, Israel-te chu hnehna chang tûr êm chuan an chak tâwk niin a ngâi hek lo. A thuchah dawn chu ring tak chungin Baraka hnênah a hrilh chhâwng mai a, Baraka chu a rinhlelhna leh hlauhna rilru putte dah thaa, Pathian hruainaa innghat hmiah mai tûrin a hrilh ta a ni.

`Beisei tûr rêng a awm lo nia a lan hunah pawh engtin nge Pathiana rinna nghah ngam dân kan lo hriat theih ang?`
