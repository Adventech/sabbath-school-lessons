---
title:  Saltân lâia beiseina
date:   09/12/2026
---

“Tin, LALPA, an pi leh pute Pathian chuan an hnênah a mi tirhte hmangin theihpatâwp leh thahnemngâi takin vaukhânna thu a hriattîr thîn a, a chhan pawh a mite leh an chênna hmun chu a lainat êm atin ni” (2 Chron. 36:15).

Pathian chuan Elija thih hnu pawh hian a mite thlamuana, vaukhânna thu hrilha, kawng dik kawhhmuh tûrin an hnênah zâwlnei tam tak a tîr leh a. Chuti chung pawh chuan Israel ram leh Juda ram kha sual khur thûk takah an tlu lût ta tho a. Chutiang taka hnam suaksual a nih avâng chuan Sodom leh Gomorra nên a tehkhin hial nghe nghe (Isaia 1:9, 10). Pathian mite chu an lo chapo va, an duhâm hle a, tin, milem pathiante hial an be bawk. An hlawhfa rawihte, hmeithâite, nu leh pa nei lote leh anmahni tanpuina beiseia rawn pantu hnam dangte chu an hnehchhiah thîn a (Isaia 58 chhiar la, hetianga an awm avânga Pathianin a zilhhau tâk dân chu i hmu ang.) A tâwpah phei chuan Israel mite hi Assuria salah tântîr niin (2 Lalte 17:1–6), Juda ram pawh Babulonin a rawn la ta daih bawk (2 Lalte 24, 25).

Pathian chuan a mite hi hrem mah se, an va tânna ramah chuan anmahni awm awma awm mai tûrin a hnutchhiah chuang lo va. An saltânna atang chuan chhanchhuah an la ni dâwn tih hrilh tûrin an hnênah zâwlnei hrang hrang, entîr nân, Ezekiela te, Daniela te kha a tîr thîn a ni.

`Isaia 59:1–4 leh Nehemia 9:30 chhiar la. Hêngahte hian Israel-te tân chauh ni bîk lovin, Pathian mi eng huna mite tân pawh thurâwn tha leh tangkâi tak tak eng nge awm?`

Israel ram leh Juda ram khân an thil sual tihin nghawng tha lo leh pawi tak tak a neihte chu an tuar fo va. Khatianga Pathian an hawisan khân a enkawlna leh vênhimna chu kalsanin, a hremna an cho chhuak ta thîn a ni. Pathian chuan inhnehchhiahna leh milem bikna te hi a en liam mai mai ngâi lo va; chuti chungin, hêng thil sual leh runthlâk tak takte hi an tih rual rualin ram dang mite kutah a pe nghâl mai ngâi chuang lo thung. A mite chu a thawhpui hrâm hrâm reng tho a, a zâwlneite kal tlangin an sualte siama, a hnêna kîr leh tûrin a thlêm thîn. Nimahsela, anni lah chuan an ngâithla duh bawk si lo va. An laka Lalpa dawhtheihzia leh, an sualte hawisan tûra a sâwm rei peihzia hi a makin a va ropui êm! Kum za tam tak chhûng chu, khâng a zâwlnei hrang hrangte hmang khân an hnênah vaukhânna thu eng emaw zât a lo puang chhuak tawh thîn a ni.

Isua ngei pawh khân heti hian a lo sawi a: “Aw Jerusalem, Jerusalem, zâwlneiho thata, i hnêna an rawn tirhte lo dêng hlum thîntu. Arpuiin a thla hnuaia note-te awp khâwm a tum angin i fate hi vawi eng zât nge awp khâwm ka tum tawh kha? Nimahsela i duh tlat si lo!” (Mat. 23:37, NKJV).

Nimahsela, ram danga sal an tân chhûng ngei pawh hian Pathian chuan beisei nei miah tawh lo tûrin a kalsan ngâi chuang lo va. A duh loh zâwng thilte an ti fo thîn chungin, “Zion-ah chuan Tlantu a lo kal ang a, Jakob-ho zînga bawhchhiatna hawisantute hnênah pawh a lo kal ang” (Isaia 59:20, NKJV) tiin a lo tiam tho a ni.

`Pathian chu Israel-te’n an hawisân châng pawhin an lakah a rinawm tlat tho a ni tih hi ngun takin ngaihtuah la. He Pathian nungchang leh hmangaihna ngahzia hi nangma nun ngeiah pawh engtin nge a takin i lo hmuh ve tawh le? Khati tak khân dawhtheiin hmangaihna lo ngah lo ta se, eng nge i chan tûr ni ang?`
