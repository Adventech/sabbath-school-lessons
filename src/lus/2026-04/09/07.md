---
title:  “Tem chhin tehrêng u”
date:   27/11/2026
---

Seventh-day Adventist-te’n Ellen G. White-i’n hril lâwkna thilpêk a kawl tih an pawmna hian, an kohhran din a nih tirh atanga vawiin thlengin Bible-a zâwlnei kan hmuhte pawhin an lo tawn ve thin tho chona hrang hrang a lo hmachhawn tawh thîn a. Kum 1844, December thlaah khân a tum khatna atân inlârna hmuin, khami hnu khân Adventist-te zîngah ngei ngei pawh zâwlnei tak tak a nih ringhlel deuh tlat an lo awm tan pang tawh a. Chumi avâng chuan Sabbath serhtu Adventist-te (a hnuah chuan Seventh-day Adventist-te tih an lo ni ta a) khân Ellen G. White-i’n hril lâwkna thilpêk a dawng tih hi lo pawm nghet zual sauh zâwkin, an pawm chhante pawh an sawi chhuak thîn ta nghe nghe a ni.

An pawm chhan hrang hrang zînga a hmasa ber chu Bible pawh hian hril lâwkna thilpêk hi tâwpna hun thlenga awm chhunzawm zêl tûr niin a lo sawi a. Joela 2 leh Thupuan 12:17 bâkah, châng dang dangte pawh tanchhan atân hmangin, hril lâwkna thilpêk chu “ni hnuhnûngahte” lo lang chhuakin, tâwpna huna chêng Pathian mite “chhinchhiahna pawimawh tak” a lo ni dâwn a ni an ti (Gerhard Pfandl, “The Gift of Prophecy and God’s Remnant Church,” The Gift of Prophecy, chapter 4 chhiar ang che.)

Tin, Adventist hmasate khân Ellen G. White-i thilpêk dawn hian a tha zâwngin ringtute nunah nghawng a nei tih an hre chiang a. Entîr nân, a thu sawi leh ziakte hian kum 1840 chho vêla Sabbath Conference-a tel ringtu, Bible-in a zirtîr nia an pawm dân inang lo tak takte kha lo inremtîrin, an zîngah inpumkhatna a lo pian phah ta a ni. A thilpêk dawn hian a hnu zêlah pawh “rah tha” a chhuah chhunzawm zêl a, a thuziakte hi Seventh-day Adventist-te zîngah phei chuan huhâng nei lian tak a la ni chhunzawm zêl a ni.

A tâwp berah chuan, Ellen G. White-i hnêna Pathianin thuchah a puan hian thil mak danglam tak tak a thleng thîn a. A hma lamah pawh kan lo sawi tawh ang khân, inlârna a hmuh chhûng hian thil mak tak tak tiin—a la hriat ngâi miah lo thilte a sawi chhuak a, thil lo thleng tûrte a puang a, mihring pângngâi chak dân âia nasa daihin a lo chak a, chu bâkah, a inlârna hmuh hun tûrah pawh duh a thlang thei miah lo bawk.

Seventh-day Adventist-te chuan vawiin thlengin hêng Ellen G. White-i thilpêk dawn thlavâng huahsakna hrang hrangte hi kan sawi chhuak chhunzawm zêl thîn a. Hêng kan tanchhan neihte hi âwihawm tak tak chu ni hlawm tho mah se, a “finfiahna” chak tak erawh chu keimahni’n a thuziakte kan lo nunpui dân hi a ni. Sam ziaktu Davida’n, “LALPa thatzia hi tem chhin tehrêng u” (Sam 34:8) a lo tih ang khân Ellen G.

White-i thuziakte hi chhiarin kan nunah seng luh tum ila. Ngun taka, tawngtâina nên kan chhiar thîn a nih chauhin zâwlnei a lo ni ngei tih kan hrethiam thei dâwn a ni.

Bul tanna atân kan hman theih chu a lehkhabu ziak zînga pan ber, mite chhiar hlawh ber ni bawk, Krista Panna Kâilâwn hi a ni. He lehkhabu hi i chhiar zawhah a lehkhabu bu ziah hrang hrang panga—Thlahtu Bulte leh Zâwlneite, Zâwlneite leh Lalte, Chatuan Nghahfâk, Tirhkohte Thiltih leh Indona Ropui dah khâwmna, Conflict of the Ages an tih chu chhiar zui ve leh nghâl la. Hêng lehkhabute hian Genesis atanga Thupuan thleng tuamin, Setana leh Krista indo tawn dân min chhuipui a ni. A thuziakte hi a bua chhuah a nih bâkah, electronic version pawha siam a ni hlawm (EGWWritings.org en la) bawk a, tin tawng chi hrang hrang tam taka lehlin a ni tawh bawk.
