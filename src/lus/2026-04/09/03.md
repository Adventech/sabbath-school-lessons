---
title:  Bible nêna inrem
date:   23/11/2026
---

Mihringte hi chu mahni leh mahni inkalh leh thîn, dâwt pawh sawi fo mai kan ni a; Pathian erawh chuan dâwt a sawi ngâi lo (Num. 23:19 en la). Zâwlneite hmanga hril lâwkna thuchah min pêkte pawh hian thu dik chauh a sawi thîn a ni tih a rawn nemnghet a. Thu dik bâk a sawi ngâi lo tih chu zâwlneite hnêna a pêk thuchah hrang hrangahte pawh hian kan hmu ang.

`Isaiah 8:20 chhiar la. Hetah hian eng thu pawimawh tak nge a sawi?`

Bible hi miin Pathian hnên ata thu dawng nia an insawia a fiahna chungnung ber ni zêl tûr a ni a. An thu sawite chu Bible thute nên khâikhina fiah thin tûr a ni. Zâwlnei dik tak chuan Bible thu a kalh emaw, a sawi hnâwm emaw ngâi dâwn lo va, Bible thu atangin mi a hruai pêng ngâi hek lo vang. Chu vângin, Lalpa zui duhte tân chuan Pathian Lehkhabu thute hi hriat bel hneh hle a tûl a, zirtîrtu derte leh zâwlnei derte’n min bum thlûk mai loh nân ngun takin Bible kan zir thîn tûr a ni.

Thuthlung Hluia kan hmuh zâwlneite leh Thuthlung Thara kan hmuh tirhkohte hian anmahni rawngbâwlna dikzia tilangtu atân anni’n rawng an bâwl ve hma daiha rawng lo bâwl tawh zâwlneite thuziak an sawi chhuak fo thîn a. Hêng zâwlnei lo lang hnuhnûng zâwkte hian Pathian ruahmanna siam leh chhandamna kawng a lo buatsaih chu chiang zâwkin rawn sawi mah se, an thil sawi dân chu zâwlnei hmasate hmanga Pathianin a lo puan chhuah tawh a thu bulpuite leh laimute nên khân a inkâlh chuang rêng rêng lo.

`2 Petera 1:19–21, Rom 15:4, leh Jakoba 1:17 te hian Pathian mizia leh kum za tam tak chhûnga hril lâwkna thu a lo puan chhuah dân chungchâng engtin nge an sawi?`

Thuthlung Hlui lama chhandamna ruahman kan hriatthiam theihna tûr lungphûm pawimawh tak kan hmuh chu Genesis 3:15-a amah Pathian ngeiin lo puan hi a ni a— hei hi Bible-a hril lâwkna thu kan hmuh hmasak ber pawh a ni nghe nghe. He ruahmanna hi ran hmanga inthâwina an hlan thin khân a lo entîr a; nimahsela, kimchang zâwka Pathian Berâm No, Isua Krista lo kal dân tûr, a nun a hman dân tûr leh mihringte tâna a thih dân tûr sawina erawh chu a zâwlneite hnênah khân a tahtawlin a rawn puang chho ta zêl a ni. Bible hian Pathian mite’n an hriatthiam theih hun zêla Pathianin a thutak a hriattîr thin dân a târ lang a. Chumi avâng chuan chhuan thar lo awm leh zêlte’n thutak hriatthiam dân thûk zâwk leh chiang zâwk an lo neih theih phah thîn. Hei hi “thutak lo lang chho zêl” ti emaw, “thutak thang chho zêl” ti emaw-a kan sawi thin pawh a ni a. A awmzia chu, chhuan thar zâwkte hian tûn hma lama mite âi khân Pathian thutak hriatthiamna thûk zâwk an nei tlângpui thîn tihna a ni.

Amaherawhchu, sawi uar luat awm thei lo hial tûr khawp thu pawimawh êm êm a awm a: thutak puan chhuah tharte chu tûn hmaa Pathianin thutak a lo puan chhuah tawhte nên khân a inkalh tûr a ni lo.
