### Chhiar tûrte
Exod. 17:14; Isaia 13:1; Jer. 36:17, 18; Rom 16:22; Luka 1:1–4; Juda 14, 15; 2 Kor. 4:7.

> <p>Châng vawn</p>
> “Pathian Lehkha Thua chhelna leh thlamuanna hmua beiseina kan neih theihna tûrin, hmânlâi ziak apiangte chu keimahni min zirtîrna tûrin ziak a ni si a” (Rom 15:4, NKJV).

Pathian ngeiina a phuah hla eng emaw zir i châk em?

Deuteronomy 32-a hla hi Mosia’n Pathianin a hrilh ang zêla a lo phuah chhuah a ni tlat mai (Deut. 31:19–22). Sam tam tak hi chu Pathian thâwk khumna changa phuah chhuah hla chham mi a ni tih kan hre tlângpui ngei ang a; chutih lâiin, zâwlneite thuziak kan han chhiar erawh hi chuan kan rilruah hla a lo lang ngâi meuh âwm lo ve. Mahse, zâwlneite thuchah eng emaw zât pawh hi hla chham mi anga ziah chhuah a ni hlawm a, a hla thu ngau ngau hi chu Pathianin a rawn thlan ni lo mah se, ama uapna changa an lo ziah a nih avângin thâwk khum an ni ve tho a ni.

Bible hian zâwlneite’n Pathian thuchah an puan chhuah dân kawng thum a sawi a, chûngte chu: (1) tawngkain; (2) thuziak hmangin; (3) a lem changin. Tûn kârah hian a pahnihna, Pathian Lehkhabua thuziak hrang hrang awmte tih hi kan thlûr bing deuh dâwn a. Bible hi Pathian Thu, a zâwlneite tawngkam hmanga ziah chhuah niin, chûngahte chuan zâwlneite thu hril leh thu sawite pawh a tel.

Zâwlneite thuziakah hian ziah dân phung chi hrang hrang leh kalhmang pawh inang lo tak tak kan hmu a. Thenkhat chu thawnthu a ni a, thenkhat chu chanchin kal tawh chhuina a ni a, hla chham mi te, tah hla hlam chhuah te leh lehkhathawn te pawh a awm bawk. Pathian mi thlante’n Thlarau Thianghlimin sawi tûra a rawn hrilhte chu mihring tawngin an lo ziak chhuak a, chûngte chu Bible-a kan hmuh Pathian Thu tia kan lo pawm tâkte hi a ni. Tûn kârah hian chûng an thuziakte chu kan en ho ang a, tanpuitu (lo ziaksaktu) an neih dân leh thu harsa deuh kan zir tuma kan hriat reng tûr thilte pawh kan sawi tel bawk dâwn a ni.
