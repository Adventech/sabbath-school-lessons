---
title:  Thu lâkna hnârte
date:   04/11/2026
---

“Tin, Thuhriltu chu a fing chauh pawh ni lovin, mite hnênah hriatna thu a zirtîr bawk thîn a; thufing tam tak a ngaihtuah a, a zawng chhuak a, a rem fel thîn. Thuhriltu chuan thu pawm tlâkte hmuh tumin a zawng thîn a; a ziak chhuahte chu a dik a—chûngte chu thutak a ni” (Thuh. 12:9, 10, NKJV).

He thu atang hian Solomona kha a thufing sawite nia kan ngaih zawng zawng phuah chhuaktu a lo ni bîk chuang lo tih kan hre thei a. Pathian uapna hnuaiah thufing tam tak a zawng chhuak a, “thu pawm tlâk” hrang hrang pawh a dap khâwm thîn bawk. Dik tak phei chuan, Thufing 31-a kan hmuh nupui fel/hmeichhe khawsak tha chanchin pawh hi Solomona ziah ni lovin, tu nge a nih tih pawh kan hriat lêm loh Lal Lemuela nu sawi daih a ni a (Thuf. 31:1). Heta tanga thil lo lang ta chu, Thufingte buah ngei pawh Aigupta mite thu leh hla ziah chhâwn a awm nual tih hi a ni.

Chutiang chiah chuan, Thuthlung Hlui lama kan hmuh chanchin ziaktu tam takte pawh hian dik tak leh fiah taka Pathian mite chanchin an ziah theih nân, anni’n an ziah ve hmaa lo awm tawh chanchin ziahna bu dangte leh lehkha pawimawh eng eng emaw an lo hmang tangkâi thîn bawk.

`1 Lalte 11:41; Luka 1:1–4; leh Juda 14, 15 chhiar la. Hêng châng hrang hrangte hian Pathian awmpuina changa Bible ziaktute’n Bible-a mi ni lo thu dang an ziah chhâwn ve thin dân engtin nge an sawi?`

Ezra leh Nehemia te hi Bible ziaktute’n an lehkhabu an ziah dâwnin ziaka lo awm tawh lehkha pawimawh eng eng emaw an hmang tângkâi ve thîn a ni tih tichiangtu an ni. Ezra khân Jerusalem khawpui leh Persia ram chungchânga lehkhathawn copy hrang hrang leh thupêk lo chhuah tawhte pawh a lo ziak tel ve a (Ezra 4–7 en la). Tin, Nehemia khân an thlahtute ang zêla chhûngkaw hrang hrang hming ziahna bute a awm thu min hrilh bâkah, chûngte chu a lehkhabuah hian a lo telh ve nghe nghe bawk (Neh. 7:5–72 en ang che).

Hetiang hi Thuthlung Thar lamah pawh kan hmu a. “Luka thil tawn atanga lang chiang tak chu [Thlarau Thianghlim] thâwk khumna tih hian kan rilru ngaihtuahna hnathawh dân pângngâi remin hna a rawn thawk ve mai a, ngaihtuahna fîm kan hman leh chîk taka thil kan lo chhui ve pawh min khapsak hran hek lo. Hetah hian lehkhabu ziaktu hi Krista chanchin ziahna lehkhabu hrang hrang awmte leh, tawngkaa an lo inhlan chhawn mai thinte pawh chîk taka zir thei tûrin Thlarau Thianghlimin a kâihruai a. Chûng a thil chhui chhuahte chu a chîn chhuaka a zir hnuah a indawt zêlin a lo ziak chhuak ta a ni.”—The SDA Bible Commentary, vol. 5, p. 669.

Juda khân Thuthlung Hlui leh Thuthlung Thar inkâra Judate kutchhuak, Bible-a seng luh ni ve ta lo lehkhabu pakhat, 1 Enoka 1:9 thu a ziak chhâwng niin a lang a. Tin, Paula ngei pawhin an hming sawi lang lêm lovin Grik mi fingte thuziak a sawi chhâwng nual bawk (Tirh. 17:28 leh Tita 1:12).

`Bible ziaktute kha inlârna leh mumang an hmuhte hmanga Pathian inpuanna an dawnin a kaihhruai châng chu awm thîn ngei mah se, Thlarau Thianghlim chuan thu ziak lo awm tawh sate hman tangkâi thiam dân tûr pawh a rawn hrilh thîn tho bawk a. Enga ti nge hemi chungchâng hi kan hriat reng a pawimawh?`
