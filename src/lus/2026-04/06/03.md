---
title:  Ziah dân phung leh kalhmangte
date:   02/11/2026
---

Bible-ah hian Pathian thâwk khumna chang zâwlneite’n an lo hman thu ziah dân kalhmang chi hrang hrang kan hmu a. Thenkhat hi chuan ni tina kan tihte kan ziahna (diary) ang hi a pâwl hle (entîr nân, Num. 33:1–49 en la). Chutih lâiin, Daniela lehkhabu te, Zakaria lehkhabu te leh Chanchin Tha Johana ziak te erawh hi chu tâwpna huna thil thleng tûr chanchin a ni deuh ber ve thung a. Hetiangah hi chuan chhinchhiahna leh entîr lam chi te, vântirhkohte leh khawvêl tâwpna lam hawi tawngkam a tam thîn hle.

`Isaiah 13:1 chhiar la. Hetah hian Isaia thu puan tûr chu engtin nge a sawi?`

Zâwlneite’n inlârna an hmuhte hi a châng chuan “Pathian thu chhuakte” ti pawha sawi thin a ni bawk a—a awmzia chu, Pathian hnên ata lo thleng a ni tihna a ni (Saptawng Bible lehlin thenkhatah “burden” tih a ni a, chutiangin Mizo tawng Bible-ah pawh “phurrit” tia dah a ni). Tûnlâi Bible lehlin thar tam takah hi chuan hla chham mi, chu pawh parallelism (para-lel-li-zim) an tih ang chi-a dah a ni ta hlawm a. Parallelism an tih chu a hma chiah tlara a lo sawi tawh kha tawngkam dang deuh hmanga sawi nawn leh emaw, chumi kalh zâwng chiah thu sawi ve thung emaw ang chi hi a ni. Entîr nân, “Chumi niah chuan miin a Siamtu lam a thlîr ang a, a mitte chuan Israel-ho Mi Thianghlima chu zah takin a en tawh ang” (Isaia 17:7, NKJV) tihah hian “Siamtu” tih leh “Mi Thianghlima” tih hi thu inthlâwp tawn a ni a, a khawi ve ve pawh hian a dang zâwk nihna min hrilh ve ve a ni.

Tin, zâwlneite khân thu ziak dân tlânglâwn tak tak, entîr nân, tehkhinna (entîrna) leh thu uar uchuakna tawngkam te pawh an hmang thîn bawk a. An thu sawite hi kan lo hriatthiam theih nân leh, kan nuna kan lo hmeh bel ve theih nân eng ang thu ziah dân kalhmang nge an hman tih hriat a pawimawh ta thîn rêng a ni.

Pathian Lehkhabua kan hmuh tam angreng tak thu ziak dân kalhmang dang pakhat leh chu “thuthlung an bawhchhiat avânga hêkna thu” a ni a. Entîr nân, Jeremia 2:4–9-ah chuan, Israel fate’n Pathian nêna thu an thlun an bawhchhiat avânga Pathianin a hêk thu kan hmu a. Rorêlna pindana thu buai ngaihtuah a nih dân chu tehkhin nâna hmangin, Pathian chu Israel fate hêktu ni angah a chan a. Chumi hmang chuan an chunga rorêlna lo thlen chhan pawh kan lo hrethiam thei ta a ni.

Bible-ah hian hril lâwkna thu te, chanchin kal tawh chhuina lam te, hla chham mi te leh tehkhin thu te kan hmu bawk a. Jeremia kha a thu dawn zawng zawngte ziaka dah vek tûra hrilh a nih lâiin (Jer. 36:2), Thuthlung Thar lehkhabu hrang hrang zînga eng emaw zât hi chu mi mal tute emaw leh, khawi kohhran emaw lo chhiar atâna lehkhathawn ziahte a ni thung. Hêng thu ziah dân kalhmang hrang hrang zînga a enga mah hi a dangte âia chungnung zâwk emaw, hnuaihnung zâwk emaw a awm hran chuang lo va. Paula lehkhathawn ziahte hi—amah Paula ngei hian Thlarau Thianghlim thâwk khumna changa a ziah tih chiang taka a lo sawi avângin (1 Kor. 7:10, 12, 40 en la)—hmasâng zâwlneite lo ziah lehkhabute nên hian a pawimawh dân a inchen reng a ni.

`Bible zir mite zînga thenkhat chuan Pathian Lehkhabu lo pian chhuah dâna mihringte inrawlhna lam hi Pathian rawn chêt ve-na lam hlamchhiah khawpin an sawi uar ta thîn a. Enga ti nge chutiang ngaihdân neih chu thlarau lam chhiatna min thlen ngeitu tûr a nih?`
