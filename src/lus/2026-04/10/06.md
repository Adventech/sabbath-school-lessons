---
title:  Bible-a an lehkhabu ziak awm leh awm lo zâwlneite
date:   03/12/2026
---

`1 Chronicles 29:29 leh 2 Chronicles 9:29 chhiar la. Hêng châng pahniha zâwlnei hming sawi lante chungchângah hian eng thil nge hriat i lo neih?`

Zâwlnei thenkhat, entîr nân, Nathana te, Gada te, Ahija te leh Idova te kha zâwlnei nia pawm chu an ni vek a; mahse, Bible-ah hian an lehkhabu ziah pakhat mah a awm bawk si lo.

`1 Lalte 11:29–39 leh Luka 7:28 chhiar la. Hêng thute hian Bible-a an lehkhabu ziak awm ve lêm lo zâwlneite khân thuneihna an nei ve tho a ni tih engtin nge an sawi?`

Pathianin zâwlnei thenkhatte thu ziah Bible-a dah tel tûra a phal lâia thenkhat dang thu ziah erawh dah tel loh a remtih lawi bawk si chhan hi keini chuan kan hre hauh lo va, kan hriat chian êm êm erawh chu, Pathian Lehkhabua an thu ziak awm ve lo zâwlneite pawh hi an thu ziak awm zâwlneite thâwk khum an nih bawka thâwk khum ni ve an ti tih hi a ni. Bible hian thlarau lam thilpêkte hi, hril lâwkna thilpêk pawh telin, tâwpna hun thlenga awm chhunzawm zêl tûr niin a sawi a. Bible-a lehkhabu awm tûrte thlan fel a nih vek hnuah pawh “rinnaa kan zaa kan lo inpumkhat hma loh zawng rawngbâwl hna thawk tûra mi thianghlimte buatsaih nân leh, Krista taksa tihhmasâwn zêl nân” (Eph. 4:12, 13, NKJV) zâwlnei an la awm zêl dâwn tih lo sawi lâwkin, chûng zâwlneite chuan “LALPA ni ropui tak leh râpthlâk tak chu (Joela 2:31) a lo thlen hma zawng rawng an bâwl chhuzawm zêl dâwn a ni.

Davida’n mi nu a uire a, a uire nu pasal a tihhlum hnu khân Pathian chuan a hnêna thu sawi tûrin Nathana a tîr a (2 Sam. 12:7–14). Nathana hian Bible-a lehkhabu awmte hi pakhat mah ziak ve lo mah se, Davida khân zâwlnei tak tak ni lovah a ngâi miah chuang lo. A ngaih loh chhan pawh zâwlneite thuziakin thuneihna a neih leh neih loh chu Biblea an lehkhabu ziakte a awm ve leh awm ve lohah ni lovin, thâwk khum an nihnaah zâwk a innghah vâng a ni. Chutih rual chuan, Bible-a an lehkhabu ziah awm ve lo zâwlneite zâwng zawng, a bîk takin Bible-a awm tûr lehkhabute thliar fel a nih hnua rawngbâwl zâwlneite—tûnlâi huna zâwlnei, entîr nân, Ellen G. White te ang phei kha chuan Bible thuneihna hnuaia awm an ni tih an inhre chiang hle a. An thuchah puante pawh rinna leh thurin chher chhuah kawnga Bible luahlântu tûr ni lovin, Bible hmanga a dik leh dik lo fiah zêl tûr a ni zâwk.

Ellen G. White-i khân heti hian a lo ziak a: “Lalpa’n i Bible kha ngun taka zir tûrin a duh che a. A Thu (Bible) luahlân tûrin eng êng dang mah min pe lo. He êng (ama thuziakte) hi a ngaihna hre lo mihringte hnêna a Thu hriatthiamtîrtu tûr a ni a, he Thu hi eia pâi tawih a nih bawk chuan thlarau tân damna thlentu chaw tha tak a ni ang.”— Selected Messages, book 3, p. 29.

`He thu hi engtiangin nge a kohhran angin kan lo ngaih thutak le?`
