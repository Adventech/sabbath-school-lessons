---
title:  Thuneihna neia thu sawiin
date:   04/12/2026
---

Zâwlneite chuan thuneihna neiin thu tangkâi tak tak an sawi thîn a, a chhan pawh Pathianin a thuchah puangtu atâna a thlante an nih vâng a ni. Bible-a an lehkhabu ziah awm zâwlneite (canonical prophets) an awm a, Bible-a an lehkhabu ziah awm lêm lo zâwlneite (non-canonical prophets) pawh an awm bawk. Tin, Bible-a lehkhabu awm tûrte thliar fel a nih hnu daiha zâwlnei hna thawk (post-biblical, non-canical prophets) pawh an awm leh bawk a. Mahse, an zavâiin Pathian thâwk khum an nih vek bâkah, an thuchah puante hian Pathian atanga thuneihna dawng an ni tih a tilang.

Ellen G. White-i kha Bible-a lehkhabu lawm tûrte thliar fel a nih hnu daiha zâwlnei hna thawk kan tih zîngah hian a tel a. Mahse, Seventh-day Adventist-te chuan a thuziakte hi Bible belhchhahna angah emaw, Bible nên intluk reng angin emaw an ngâi lo. A thuziakte erawh thu tangkâi tak tak a nih hlawm bâkah, thuneihna neia thu sawi niin an pawm a. Amah Ellen G. White-i ngei khân heti hian a lo sawi:

“Hêng lehkhathawn ka ziahahte leh hriattîrna thu ka sawiahte hian Lalpa’n ka hnêna a rawn puante chu ka ziak chhuak a. Keima ngaih dân târ lan nân mai mai chuan thu pakhat pawh ka ziak lo. Ka thuziahte hi inlârnaa Pathianin min rawn hmuhtîr a ni a—êng zung mâwi leh hlu tak chu [vân] lalthutphah atangin a rawn êng chhuak a ni.”—Selected Messages, book 1, p. 29.

Bible nên hian inrem thlap niin, sual nihna dik tak leh chatuan nunna kan neih theih nâna kan beisei theih awm chhun Isua kan mamawhzia min hriattîr a, chu chu rinnaa lo pawm duhtu zawng zawngte hnênah chuan khawngaihnaa pêk vek a ni bawk. Hei hi a thuchah laimu leh thupui ber pakhat a ni nghe nghe.

A thuchahte (Pathianin amah hmanga a rawn puan chhuah thuchahte ti ila a dik zâwk maithei) kan chhiar atangin sual leh tha an indo tawn reng thu kan hmu a. Chu indona chu suala Lucifera a tlûk khân vânah lo intanin, chatuan atâna sual leh thil tha lo zawng zawng, Setana leh a vântirhkohte nên lam tihboral vek an nih hun tûr tâwpna hun thlengin a kal zêl dâwn a ni. Ellen G. White-i thuziakte hian Bible-in tâwpna huna thil thleng tûr a lo sawite hi hrethiam tûrin min pui a. Entîr nân, kohhran leh sawrkâr kal kawp a hlauhawmzia leh sakhaw zalênna vawn nun zêl a pawimawhzia a lo sawi a; suahsualna a sosân zêl tûr thu a lo sawi a; Pathian dân châwisângin tûnlâi hunah pawh a la pawimawh reng tih a lo sawi a; tin, entîr nân, “sakawlh chhinchhiahna” awmzia kan hrilhfiah dân hi ama thuziah beh chhan chu ni chuang hauh lo mah se, a thuziahte hian tâwpna huna thil thlengte hriatthiamna kawngah nasa takin min pui bawk a ni.

Kristiante awm dân tûr pawh a sawi tam khawp mai. Thlarau mi-na chungchâng te, hrilsêlna chungchâng te, zirna chungchâng te, chhûngkaw inkungkaihna leh fanau enkawl chungchâng bâkah, thil dang chi hrang hrang a sawite hian mi tam tak chu an nun dân an thlâk theih nân a tanpui a. Chûng mite chuan nun dân phung dik leh tha zâwk an loh neih phah thîn a ni.

Ellen G. White-i thuziakte hi Bible-a awm ve ni lo mah se, Pathianin khawvêl chanchin tâwp dâwn hnaih lama chêng ringtute kaihhruaina atân a hmang nasa êm êm a. Hêng a thuziakte hian thuneihna nêna thu la sawi chhunzawm rengin, kan hriat ngei ngei tûr thu tangkâi min hriattîr thîn a ni.
