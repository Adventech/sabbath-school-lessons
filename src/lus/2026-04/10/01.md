### Chhiar tûrte
Exod. 4:10–16, Luka 8:22–25, Joh. 3:1–10, Tirh. 16:25–34, 1 Kor. 5:1–5, Luka 7:28.

> <p>Châng vawn</p>
> “Pathian thu hi a nung a, thil a ti thei a, khandaih hriam tawn eng ang âi pawhin a hriam a, nunna leh thlarau, ruhtuah leh thlîng phel hrang khawp hialin a chhun thei a, thinlunga ngaihtuah leh tumte hi a hre nghâl thei a ni” (Hebrai 4:12).

November 9, 1989 khân Günter Schabowski-a’n Communist lamin an awp East Germany-a thu a sawi, a sawi lâi la laa TV-a an pêk chhuah nghâl chuan a tum loh deuhin East Germany leh West Germany dâidangtu atâna an siam Berlin Wall chhut chhiat vekna kha a thlen tlat mai. Amah hi East Germany lamin sawrkâr thu pawimawh puang zârtu atâna an hman a ni a, hemi tuma press conference an neih lâi hian a phîlî buai deuh nge ni, thu a puang sual tlat mai. East German mite chuan hun rei tak awh leh buaipui fêt ngâi visa an neih hnuah Western Europe an tlawh thei tawh dâwn tia sawi a tum chu sawi sual palhin, East German-ho chu Western Europe tlawh phalsak an ni tawh ang a ti ta mai pek a. Thu thar theh darhtute’n an dân thar siam chu eng tik atanga hman tan tûr nge tih an han zawh lah chuan, “Ka hriat dân chuan, tûn atang hian, tihkhawtlâi awm lovin hman tan nghâl a ni tawh mai ang” a ti liam leh mai bawk si. Chu veleh mi tam takin ramri-a checkpoint awmte chu an pan nghâl nguah nguah mai a, sawrkâr berin a puan tawh miau avângin Western Europe lamah luh phei chu phalsak an nih ngei an phût ta a ni.

Mi tu tute emaw chu company tân emaw, ram tân emaw pawh thu sawi tûra tirh leh mawhphurhna pêk an ni thîn a. Pathian tâna thu sawitute hi Pathianin a tirh leh mawhphurhna a pêk an ni ve rêng em? Tûn kârah hian Bible-a kan hmuh zâwlneite hian eng thuneihna nge an neih tih kan zir ho ang a. Chu bâkah, Bible-a an lehkhabu ziak telh ve ni ta lo zâwlnei, entîr nân, zâwlnei Nathana te, Baptistu Johana te leh Ellen G. White-i ngei pawh hian eng thuneihna nge an neih ve tih pawh kan en tel bawk dâwn a ni.
