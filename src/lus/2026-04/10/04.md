---
title:  Bible thuneihna
date:   01/12/2026
---

Bible hi zâwlneite kutchhuak, Pathian inpuanna rah chhuah a ni a. Mihring nunin zawhna pawimawh leh harsa ber ber a neihte chu chhângin, mihringte hnêna Pathian rawn inpuan chhuahna kawng pawimawh tak a ni bawk. Pathian Lehkhabu atang hian he khawvêla mihringte kan lo awm chhan kan hre thei a, Pathian nungchang leh duhzâwng bâkah, kan tâna Pathianin ruahmanna a lo neih leh tih a tumte pawh kan hre thei bawk. Bible hi thutak kan hriat theih nâna tehfung tha elkhên a ni a, kan nuna duhthlanna pawimawh kan siam dâwn rêng rêngin kan râwn zêl tûr a ni. Kristiante khawvêl thlîr dân innghahna lungphûm leh thutak puan chhuaha awm tawh zawng zawngte tobul a ni a. Kristiante rinna leh thiltih tehfung chungnung ber a ni bawk.

Pathian Thu hi kan duh chuan kan pawmin kan pawm lo thei a; mahse a lova luata duh loha, Lal Jehoakima’n a lo tih anga hâl ral kher erawh a tûl lo vang (Jer. 36:22–27en la). Chutih rualin, ring nia inchhâl sia zawm duh chuan miah si loh erawh thil tha a ni chuang lo va. A lehlamah, Pathian thutak kan hmu chhuak thar a, chûngte chu kan pawm nghâl mai a nih erawh chuan Lal Josia ang khân siam thara lo awmin, mi dangte pawh Pathian hnênah kan hruai ve thei dâwn a ni (2 Lalte 22:10–13 en ang che).

Mi tam tak chuan Pathian Lehkhabu hi an chhiar pawhin a dik lo lâi hmuh chhuah an tum deuh ber thîn a, nuihzata lâk tum pawh eng emaw zât an awm bawk. Mi thenkhat dang chuan tûnlâi khawvêl nun dân leh sûkthlêk nên inrem tawh lo niin an ngâi bawk a (inrem lo tûr pawh a nihna chen a awm rêng a). Tin, science dân nên a inkalh emaw, mi nâwlpuite pawm dân nên a inkalh emaw avânga lo diriam tâwk pawh an awm thîn bawk.

Kristian thenkhat chuan Bible hi an thlarau nun thanna tûr leh an insiam thatna atâna hmang lovin, anmahni’n ngaih dân an lo neih sa sawh nghehna hmanrua atân an hmang ta zâwk hlauh thîn a. Mi dang thenkhat lahin Bible-a thil thlengte leh an chanchin kan hmuh mi hrang hrangte hi a taka thleng lo leh a taka awm miah hlawm lo niin an lo hrilhfiah thûl. Tin, an ni tin nuna thuneihna nei tak tak lo nia ngâi pawh thahnem tak an awm bawk a. Hêng zawng zawng hi a nihna takah chuan Bible pawm lohna vek a ni.

Bible thuneihna hnâwl dân kawng hrang hrang zînga a awlsam ber leh tlânglâwn ber ni maithei nghe nghe bawk chu ngaihthah a, a chhiarna hun pawh insiam loh leh, a thil sawite nunpui loh a ni.

`Hetiang dinhmuna Pathian Thu sawi chhuahin nghawng a neih dân chu eng nge ni? Jer. 38:1–4, Joh. 3:1–10, leh Tirh. 16:25–34 te hi chhiar la.`

Hmânlâi chuan thu ziakna hmanrua a vâng êm êm a, a man a to hle bawk. Chu mai a la ni lo, mi tam zâwk kha ziak leh chhiar thiam lo an ni a; chu vâng chuan, zâwlneite kal tlanga Pathian Thu puan chhuah mai thin kha thil tih loh theih loh ni.

Thlarau Thianghlim hnathawh zârah, Pathian Thu—a sawia sawi chhuah emaw, ziaka dah emaw pawh—hian sualna lantîra, siam thatna leh din thar lehna thlentîr tûrin kan thinlung a rawn luh chilh thei a. Pathian Thu chuan kan sualte sima, Pathian thupêkte zâwm tûrin mi a sâwm reng thîn (Tirh. 5:29; Heb. 4:12 pawh en la). Chu chiah chu mi tam takin, a hre ngei ngei tûr ni lehnghâl site’n an hnâwl chhan a ni hlauh zâwk a. Bible-in an nun a rawn thunun tûr chu an phal tlat lo.
