---
title:  Pathian mite kaihhruai kawnga thuneihna
date:   02/12/2026
---

Hosea 12:13-ah chuan zâwlneite hmanga Lalpa’n Aigupta ram ata Israel fate a hruai chhuah thu leh, zâwlneite hmang vêka anmahni a vênhim bawk thu kan hmu a. Pathian mite vênhim leh kaihhruai kha Bible-a kan hmuh zâwlneite hna thawh pawimawh tak pakhat a ni thîn rêng a ni.

Tin, Rorêltute 4:4–8, 2 Chronicles 20:14–17, Exodus 12:1– 3, leh Deuteronomy 1:9–16 te hi kan chhiar chuan zâwlneite kha Pathianin a mite inrêlbâwl dân tûr a lo duan dân puan chhuah kawngah an pawimawh hle a ni tih kan hmu thei a. Indona leh buaina an hmachhâwn lâi te khân Pathianin a mite leh an hruaitute hnêna kaihhruaina, fuihna thu leh beiseina a pêkna hmanrua ber ni thîn chu zâwlneite an ni. Tin, zâwlneite khân kût ni vângthla hman dân tûr leh Pathian chibai bûkna kalpui dân tûrah pawh kaihhruaina an lo pe thîn a (Exod. 12:1– 3). A châng chuan, zâwlneite kha Pathianin rawn biain ram hruaitute’n ram an hruai dân tûr a rawn hriattîr thîn bawk. A tâwi zâwngin sawi ta mai ila, Israel hnam nun pêng hrang hrangah khân Pathian chuan a thuchah puangtu atâna a thlan zâwlneite kal tlangin kaihhruaihna a lo pe zêl thîn a ni.

`Kristian kohhran hmasate kha, an inrêl bâwl dân tûrah emaw, an inkaihhruaina kawngah emaw pawh engtiangin nge hril lâwkna thilpêk hian a lo tanpui thin? Tirh. 6:1–7, 1 Kor. 5:1–5, 1 Kor. 7:10–16, Tita 1:5.`

Pâwl thar in din a; mahse, kalphung mumal leh inkaihhruaina dân zawm tûr bâkah, hruaitu ber pawh nei chuang bawk si lo ta ula, engtin nge ni ang? Buaina leh harsatna in tâwk thuai dâwn tih a chiang reng mai. Eng emaw chenah chutiang chiah chu Kristian kohhran hmasate dinhmun tawn pawh kha a ni. Isua kha vânah hruai chhoh a ni tawh a, a zirtîrte zîngah a awm ve tawh lo. A zirtîrte hnênah khân, “Khawvêl zawng zawngah kal ula. . . . Chanchin Tha hi hril rawh u” (Marka 16:15) tiin thu a pe a. Khâng tirhkoh sâwm leh pahnihte kha Isua’n a thlante ngei ni mah se, an thawh tûra a tuksak hna chu thawk tan nghâl thei tûrin inrêlbâwlna fel leh inkaihhruaina mumal an la nei tlat lo.

Kristian hmasate khân kohhran inkaihhruai dân leh inrêlbâwl dân tûrah te, chanchin tha hril darh dân tûr kawngah te, an zînga thurin dik lo zirtîrtute hriat chhuah leh hrem dân tûr thuah te, dik lo taka mipat-hmeichhiatna lo hmangte chunga thununna lek dân tûr chungchângah te chona hautak tak tak an hmachhawn thîn a. Chutiangah chuan, kohhran zahawmna humhalh a niha, a keh darh maina tûr laka vênhim tlat a nih theih nân hril lâwkna thilpêk hian hna pawimawh tak a lo thawk thîn a ni (Tirh. 13:1, 2). Dik taka sawi chuan, kohhran hmasa tân khân pâwla insiam a, chanchin tha hril darh tûra ke han pên tan te kha hril lâwkna thilpêk puihna tel lo chuan tih theih rual niin a lang lo.

`Mi thenkhat chuan hotu nei kohhrana insiam hi an duh lo va, duh lo mai pawh ni lova hlau deuh titih tlat pawh eng emaw zât an awm. Kohhrana awm hi—chona chi hrang hrang hmachhawn tûr chu awm tho bawk mah se—a thatna leh tangkâi zâwkna hrang hrang eng nge awm? Nangmah ngawt âia lian fê zâwk pâwl eng emawa i tel ve avâng khân eng thilte nge i pêk chhuah theiha eng thilte nge i dawn theih awm?`
