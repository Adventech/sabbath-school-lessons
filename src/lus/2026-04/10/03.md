---
title:  Isua thuneihna
date:   30/11/2026
---

`Isua’n thuneihna a neih chhan kha eng nge ni? Chu thuneihna chu a nei ngei a ni tih tichiangtu eng nge awm? Mat. 21:23–27 leh Joh. 17:2 en ang che.`

Isua’n mite a zirtîr lâiin a hnênah puithiamho an lo kal a, chu thil chu tu thua ti nge a nih tih an rawn zâwt a. Isua kha khawi khaw sikul chhuak mah a ni lo va, dân zirtîrtu (rabbi) ni tûra naupangte an chherna hmunah lah chawhma êm pawh zir lo a ni bawk si. An zawhna zawh chu chhâng tha ta mai lovin, Baptistu Johana’n mite a baptis kha tu thua ti nge a nih tih a zâwt ta khauh zâwk a. A zawhna an chhâng duh lo hian Johana rawngbâwlna rawn pawmsaktu Pathian thiltihtheihna an hnâwl bâkah, Johana ang bawka Pathianin a tirh ama thuneihna pawh an hnâwl bawk tih an tilang.

`Hêng Bible chângte hian engtin nge Isua thuneihna chungchâng an sawi hlawm? Mat. 7:28, 29; Marka 1:21–27; Luka 8:22–25; Luka 9:1; Joh. 5:25–27; leh Joh. 7:38.`

Thuneihna zawng zawng hi Isua ta vek a ni a, a chhan pawh mihringte Siamtu (Joh. 1:3) leh Chhandamtu (Rom 3:24) a nih vâng a ni. Amah hi Pathian Thu chu a ni a. Isua’n leia a awm chhûng leh rawng a bâwl lâia thuneihna a kawl kha tu ma tân lo entawn ve theih emaw, mahni thua lo neih ve ngawt theih emaw a ni lo. A zirtîrna khân a ngâithlatute mak tih a hlawh a, a chhan chu thuneihna nei tih hriat taka a zirtîr thin vâng a ni (Mat. 7:29).

Chanchin Tha bu ziaktute hian Isua’n thu hril tûra thuneihna a neih kha khawvêla mi a ni lo tih an sawi theuh a. Sual a ngâidam a (Marka 2:10, 11), ramhuai a hnawtchhuak a (Marka 3:15), mite’n an thinlunga an ngaihtuahte pawh a hriatsak thei a (Joh. 2:24, 25). Mitthi kâi thovin, chatuana nunna hial a pe bawk (Joh. 10:28). Kan Tlantu nia leia a rawn awm chhûng khân Pa Pathianin thuneihna a pêk chu a hmang thîn a (Joh. 17:2), eng tikah pawh a Paa innghatin, amah nên chuan thil an ti dûn zêl thîn (Joh. 5:19). Pathian Lehkha Thu (Thuthlung Hlui lehkhabute) a sawi chhâwng fo va, chûngte chuan ama chungchâng an lo puang chhuak niin a sawi thîn bawk. Isua khân thuneihna tâwp a nei a, chu thuneihna chu a zirtîrte hnênah pawh a pe chhâwng ve thei nghe nghe (Marka 6:7). Pathian Thu [mihringa lo chang ta] a ni angin, thu a rawn sawi thinna zâwlneite hnênah khân ama chanchin a lo hriattîr tawh bawk a ni (Thup. 19:10).

`Mi thenkhat chuan, Isua’n finfiahna hrang hrang a pe chung pawhin an la hnâwl a. Keini pawh hian rin chhan tûr heti zât kan nei chung pawhin kan ring duh tlat lo ve thei tho mai. Chumi thangah chuan kan awh loh nân engtin nge kan invên theih ang?`
