---
title:  Pathian hnên ata thuneihna dawng
date:   29/11/2026
---

I tâna thu sawi tûrin tu emaw thlang dâwn ta la, i mi rin zâwng tak leh rin tlâk nia i ngaih ngei chauh i thlang lo vang em ni? Chu mi chuan i dinna leh i ngaih dân a hriat chian hle a tûl dâwn a, chu mai ni lovin, rinawm tak leh dik takin a sawi chhuak tûr a ni bawk ang.

`Exodus 2:11–15 leh Exodus 3:1–3, 10–13 chhiar la. Mosia kha engtiangin nge berâm vênga hun a hman lâi khân Pathian tâna thu puang chhuaktu ni tûrin a lo buatsaih?`

Mosia khân a dam chhûng hun hmasa lam kum 40 vêl zet chu Aigupta ramin zirna a pêk that theih ber berte dawn nân a lo hmang tawh a. Nimahsela, chûngte chuan Pathian thuchah puang chhuaktu ni thei tûrin a buatsaih hran rêng rêng lo thung. Kum 40 dang kha thlalêrah berâm vêngin, khawvêl thil rêng rêngin a tihbuai phâk loha a awm leh rih phawt a tûl a. Kawng tam takah Aigupta lal fapa a nihna anga a thil lo zir leh pawm tâk tam takte chu a theihnghilh leh emaw, a thlauhthlâk leh emaw phawt a ngâi bawk. Israel-te chhuah zalên tûra Pathian a thawhpui hmain a âiawha thu a sawi tûr Pathian chu a hriat chian hmasak phawt a tûl a ni.

`Exodus 4:10–16 chhiar la. Pathian leh Mosia inbiakna atang hian zâwlneite koh an nih dân leh Pathian tâna hna an thawh dân kan lo hre thei em?`

Mosia kha Pathian âiawha ding tûra koh a ni a, tin, Arona kha Mosia thu sawi mipuite hnêna sawi chhâwng lehtu tûr a ni bawk (Exod. 4:16; Exod. 7:1 pawh chhiar ang che). Chutiangin Pathian chuan Mosia leh Arona te a lo tanpui tawh ang bawk khân a zâwlneite pawh an hnêna thuchah a pêkte a tha thei bera an puan chhuah theih dân kawng a lo ngaihtuahsak thîn a ni.

Mosia khân thil a lo ti sual tawh a ni tih a hre chiang a. A Israel hnam nihpui pahnih nêna an intawn a, chu mite’n an chunga rorêltu nia an pawm loh dân (Exod. 2:14) te kha a la theihnghilh rêng rêng lo. Pathian pawhin Mosia thil hlauhthâwn chu a hriatthiampui a; chu vâng chuan zahngâi tak chungin a dâwr thîn rêng a ni. Mosia kha Pathianin a chhuanlam siam zawng zawng a pheh thlâksak vek hnuah pawh a la tang ta fan a. A hlauhthâwnnate chu rinhlelhnaah a lo chang ta hial mai. Khatia Pathian kohna a dawn a pawm mai duh tlat loh chhan kha Pathianin a hmang thei a ni tih a rin zawh loh vâng a ni ber a. Khatah chiah khân alâwm Pathianin zâwlnei nih hreh êm êm chu hnial rual loh thupêk khauh tak a pêk phah tâk rêng ni (Exod. 3:14–17).

`Zâwlnei chu i ni ve kher lo vang a, mahse, Pathianin a tâna ti tûra a hrilh che chu eng nge ni ang? Chumi ti tûra i inbuatsaih dân tûr tha ber chu eng nge ni?`
