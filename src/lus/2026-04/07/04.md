---
title:  "Thuchah tiboralin: Then 2"
date:   10/11/2026
---

Mihringin hril lâwkna thilpêk a lo dawnsawn dân chu kawng hrang hrang a ni thei. Mosia chungchângah khân Israel fate chu Mosia leh Arona laka a lang a pau takin an hel a, Ram Tiam a va luah huna an hmêlmate’n an lo hneh zâwk dâwn nia an ngaih avângin an hlauthâwng êm êm ringawt mai a ni. A hnu kum eng emaw zâtah Lal Jehoiakima khân Pathian thu puan chhuah ziahna lehkhabu zial chu a hâl ral a, a chhan pawh chutia a tih chuan kumkhua atân a reh zui ve mai tawh dâwn niah a ngâi a ni ngei ang. Anni kha Pathian thuchah ngâithla duh lo leh tihboral tum awm chhun a ni lo va. Hmasâng Israel leh Juda ram chanchin kha thlîr lêt ila, Pathian zâwlneite’n dodâlna an tawh nasat thinzia chu tu hrilh hranpa kan ngâi chuang âwm lo ve.

`2 Chronicles 36:15, 16 chhiar la. Juda hnamte khân Pathianin a zâwlneite hmanga thuchah a pêkte chu engtin nge an lo chhân lêt thîn?`

Jeremia khân Josia rorêl lâi te, Jehoahaza rorêl lâi te, Jehoiakima rorêl lâi te, Jehoakina rorêl lâi te leh Zedekia rorêl lâi te khân zâwlnei hna a lo thawk thîn a. Nebukadnezzara kaihruai Babulon ram chu Juda ram rawn rûn tûrin a rawn inchhawp hnâi tawh hle mai. Jeremia’n a lo sawi zawng zawngte chu kum 21 leka upa Zedekia’n lalthuthlêng a rawn luah khân a zavâiin a lo thleng vek tawh a (2 Chron. 36:11).

“LALPA a Pathian mit hmuha thil tha lo tak tak ti thînin, LALPA kâ atanga thu sawi thîntu zâwlnei Jeremia lakah pawh a inphah hniam duh ngâi miah lo bawk” (2 Chron. 36:12, NKJV).

He lâi thua Jeremia’n hril lâwkna thilpêk a dawn thu a sawi dân hi lo chhinchhiah ila. Pathian kâ ngei atanga thu sawi thîn niin a sawi a. Chutiang a ni chung pawhin, Zedekia kha zâwlnei hmaah chuan a inphah hniam duh chuang miah lo nia! Jeremia kha mihring ve mai, keimahni ang bawka mi sual ni ve tho ni mah se, a âiawha thu a sawi thin Lalpa palâi a ni tlat.

Jeremia kha zâwlnei hna thawk tam tak zînga pakhat a ni a. Israel hnam chanchin kum za tam tak chhûng khân Pathian chuan, “tu ma boral a duh lohva, mi zawng zawngin an sualte sim vek se” (2 Pet. 3:9, NKJV) a tih êm avângin an hnênah zâwlnei emaw eng zât a lo tîr thîn a ni (Exod. 3:15, Sam 105:26, Mal. 3:1, Isaia 6:8, Jer. 25:4, Mat. 11:13, Luka 11:49, leh a dangte pawh.) Hmasâng Israel-te leh Juda-te chanchin hi Pathianin amah vah bosan thîn a mite hruai kîr leh ngei a tum thin dân târ langtu a ni a. An sualte an sim a nih phawt chuan ngâidamin chhandamna chantîr ngei a tiam thîn.

Nimahsela, Juda hnam chanchina a tâwp lamah khân Pathianin a zâwlnei Jeremia hmanga kaihhruaina leh thlamuana thuchah a rawn hriattîr chu inngaihtlâwmna leh rinna nên dawnsawn a lo ni lo va. An thil sual tiha inchhîr leh sim duhna neih tâk aruangin, an lo nuihsawh ta hial zâwk a ni.

`Juda lal leh hruaitute khân Pathian zâwlneite chu nuihsawhin, Pathian thuchahte pawh an lo hmusit êm êm mai a. Keini tûnlâi huna mite hi chuan an tih ang kha kan ti ve lo vang ti r’u? Nge ni a, kan ti ve tho zâwk dâwn ni le?`
