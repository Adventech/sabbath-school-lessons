---
title:  Bible hi amah leh amah a inhrilhfiah thîn
date:   15/11/2026
---

Pathian Lehkhabu thute kan hrilhfiah dân hi keimahni ngaihdân lo neih sain a hruaina chin thui tak a awm thîn a. Bible hi hmâsâng mite thuziak eng eng emaw fawm khâwm ni maia ngâitute phei chuan Bible-a lehkhabu hrang hrang awmte hi inpumkhat niin an hmu thei lo rêng rêng. Nimahsela, “Pathian Lehkha Thu zawng zawng hi Pathian thâwk khuma pêk” (2 Tim. 3:16) vek niin, tangkâina kawng eng emaw tak nei theuh an ni bawk tih kan pawm a nih erawh chuan, hêng lehkhabute hi chi hrang hrang ni mah se, inpumkhat tha tak an ni tih kan lo hrethiam ang a. Chu bâkah, Bible hi amah leh amah a inhrilhfiah tûr a ni tih thu pawh hi kan pawm thei bawk dâwn a ni.

`Matthaia 5:17–48 chhiar la. He lâi thua Thuthlung Hlui zirtîrna lo lang hi engtin nge Isua’n a hrilhfiah?`

Matthaia 5:17–48 hi kan chhiar liam deuh puat ringawt a nih chuan, Isua’n ama tihchhuah zirtîrna thar hmangin Thuthlung Hlui zirtîrnate hi a thlâk ta emaw kan lo ti mai thei dâwn a. Nimahsela, ngun taka kan chhiar a nih erawh chuan, Thuthlung Hlui lehkhabute’n thlarau lam thu an lo sawite hi a awmze ril zâwk a rawn phawrh chhuak ta a ni tih kan lo hre thei ang. Jeremia 31:31–33-ah chuan Pathianin thuthlung thar siamin, chumi hnuaiah chuan a mite thinlungah a thupêkte ziakin, an chhûngrila a dânte a dah tûr thu kan hmu a, he thutiam hi Isua khân a rawn tifamkim ta a ni (Heb. 8:8–10, Heb. 10:16 te nên hian khâkhin ang che).

A châng chuan kan thu chhiar mêk awmze hrilhfiahna chu a awmna lehkhabuah vêk a awm nghâl mai thîn a. Entîr nân, Daniela 2-ah chuan Lal Nebukadnezzara’n mumang mak danglam tak a hmuh thu sawiin (Dan. 2:31–35), chumi bungah vêk chuan chu a mumang hrilhfiahna pawh kan hmu nghâl bawk (Dan. 2:36–45). Chutiangin, Matthaia 13-ah chuan buh chi thehtu tehkhin thu kan hmu a (Mat. 13:1–9), tichuan, a hrilhfiahna pawh bung 13-ah vêk sawi leh nghâl a ni (Mat. 13:18–23).

Thu thenkhat erawh hi chu a hnu kum tam taka rawn chhuak zâwlneite’n a hrilhfiahna an rawn sawi tâk chauh pawh a ni thung a. Chumi entîrna tha tak pakhat chu, Jeremia hmanga Lalpa’n a lo sawi (Jer. 25:11, 12 leh Jer. 29:10) kum 70 hril lâwkna kha a sawi ang ngeiin a lo thleng ta tih 2 Chronicles 36:22, 23-ah kan hmu hi a ni âwm e. Amaherawhchu, Thuthlung Hluia chhinchhiahna leh entîrna tam takte hi Kristaah khân a lo thleng famkim ta hlawm a, chutiangin, Thuthlung Hlui thu tam tak pawh Thuthlung Tharah hian a awmzia sawi fiah a ni thîn bawk.

“Thuthlung Hlui hi entîrnate leh chhinchhiahnate hmanga puan chhuah chanchin tha a ni a. Thuthluang Thar hi chûng entîrnate leh chhinchhiahnate’n an lo entîr a tak chu a ni. A khawi ve ve pawh hi a dang ang bawka pawimawh a ni a, thlauh thlâk bîk tûr a awm chuang lo.”—Ellen G. White, Selected Messages, book 2, p. 104. A awmzia chu, Thuthlung Thar lehkhabute hi Thlarau Thianghlim thâwk khumna chang zâwlneite leh tirhkohte’n Thuthlung Hlui thu an hrilhfiahna a ni.

`Bible kan chhiar leh kan zir hian ngaihdân nghet sa kan lo neih chu hriat a tûl thîn a ni tih hi enga ti nge a pawimawh? Bible hi Pathian Thu a ni tih rin leh, keimahni’n Bible thute a dik leh dik loh thua thutlûkna siam lovin, Bible thute hi kan nuna thu neitu a ni tûr a ni tih pawm hmak mai hi eng vânga pawimawh êm êm nge a nih bawk?`
