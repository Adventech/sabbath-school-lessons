---
title:  Bible thu awmzia hrilhfiahna
date:   19/11/2026
---

Bible kan zira kan tum ber tûr thil pawimawh tak pahnih awmte chu: keimahni thlarau nun thanna tûr leh mi dangte pawhin Pathian nêna chhandamna thlen thei inzawmna an lo neih ve theih nâna hruai a ni a. Krista’n Kristian dik takte nihna a sawi fiah dân pawh Thlarau Thianghlimin “thutak zawng zawnga a “hruai luh (Joh. 16:13), “Pathian kâ atanga lo chhuak thu hrang hrang rin chhana” nung (Mat. 4:4, NKJV), leh ama’n thu min pêk “zawng zawngte” pawm tûra mi dangte pawh zirtîr ve thîntute an ni (Mat. 28:20).

`Luka 4:16–30 leh Luka 24:25–27, 44–49 chhiar la. Krista’n Pathian Lehkhabu thu a hrilhfiah dân atang hian eng nge zir chhuah theih kan neih?`

Isua hi “kawng leh thutak leh nunna” an lo ni tawh thîn a, tûnah pawh la ni rengin, a ni chhunzawm zêl bawk ang (Joh. 14:6, NKJV). Chutiang chu ni mah se, a thu hrilte kha Pathian Lehkhabu thua (Thuthlung Hlui) innghat a ni zêl thîn. Entîr nân, Marka 1:14, 15-ah chuan, “Tin, Johana an man hnuin, Isua chu Pathian Chanchin Tha hrilin Galili ramah a lo kal a, ‘Hun chu a lo thleng ta a, Pathian ram pawh a lo hnâi ta’ a ti a” (NKJV) tih thu kan hmu a. Heta hun a sawi hian Daniela 9:24– 27 a kâwk a ni tih a chiang hle. Tin, tlâng chunga thu a sawiah (Matthaia 5–7) pawh khân Exodus 20:13 leh 14 thu a sawi chhâwng a (Mat. 5:21, 27). Chutiangin, Luka 4-ah pawh Isaia 61:1, 2 thu a sawi chhâwng leh bawk. Isua khân Bible thu a hre chiang hle a, Bible thu behchhan zêlin thu a sawi thîn a ni.

Entîrna dang pakhat lo sawi leh ila, Tirhkoh 8-ah chuan Ethiopia ram mi, mi tilreh pakhat, Jerusalem-a Pathian be tûra a han kal thu kan hmu a (Tirh. 8:27, NKJV). A awmzia chu, he mi tilrehpa hian Juda-te sakhaw biak dân eng eng emaw a lo hre ve tawh tihna a ni. Isaia lehkhabu pawh a kawl bawk a, chu lehkhabu chu Jerusalem atanga a haw kawngah ngaihnawm ti takin a chhiar nghe nghe. Nimahsela, a mite sual phurtu Chhiahhlawh chungchâng hril lâwkna thu a chhiar chu a awmzia a hrethiam hauh lo mai a; chu vâng chuan, hrilhfiahsaktu tûr a zawng ta rêng a ni.

Ethiopia mi thil tawn hian zirlâi pawimawh tlêm azâwng min zirtîr a. A hmasa berin, Pathian Lehkhabu hi chhiar satliaha kan chhiar ve ringawt a nih chuan kan thu chhiar awmzia kan hre thei lo vang. Pahnihna chu, Pathian Lehkhabu hi mi dangte nên zir dûn/ho ila, tichuan, kan hriatthiam loh lâite kan inhrilhfiahsak tawn thei ang. Pathumna chu, Bible thuchah dik leh rintlâk kan chhar chhuah thar chu mi dangte hnênah pawh kan hrilh chhâwng ve tûr a ni.

Philipa’n Isaia lehkhabu leh Thuthlung Hlui thu dangte awmzia a hrilhfiahna chuan Ethiopia mi tilreh kha baptisma chan duhna thinlung a lo neihtîr ta a (Tirh. 8:35–39; Sam 51:12 pawh en la). Keini pawh Pathian Lehkhabu thu awmzia kan hrilhfiahnate hian mi dangte a tipiangthar ve thîn ngei tûr a ni ang (Joh. 17:17 en ang che).

`Bible thute hi awmzia hrethiam lovin, min hrilhfiahsaktu tûr kan mamawh châng pawh awm thîn mah se, eng vângin nge dik nia kan rin chu kan rilruah kan vawn ngheh tlat a pawimawh?`
