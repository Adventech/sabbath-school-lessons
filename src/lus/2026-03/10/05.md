---
title:  Thianghlim tûra koh
date:   02/09/2026
---

2 Korinth 6:3–10-ah hian Paula’n Pathian nêna inrema awm tûrin Korinth khuaa ringtu awmte chu a fuih thu kan hmu a. Krista zuitu nih leh Pathian rawngbâwltu nih awmzia tilangtu tûr harsatna hrang hrangte leh hnehna an chan tûrte pawh a sawi bawk. A tâwi zâwngin sawi ta mai ila, harsatna an tawrh tûr te (2 Kor. 6:4, 5), nungchang tha an put tûr te (2 Kor. 6:6), rawngbâwlna atâna an mamawh hriamhrei te (2 Kor. 6:7) leh, rawngbâwlna kawnga thil inkâwlkalh tak tak an tawn tûr te (2 Kor. 6:8–10) chu a hrilh ta a ni. Pathian nêna inrema awm tûra a hrilh hnu hian thianghlim taka nung tûrin a sâwm zui nghâl a, thianghlim tâka an nun theih dân tûr chu ring lo mite huhâng hlauhawm tak atang leh, thil thianghlim lote lak atanga inthiar fihlîm a ni tih thu pawh a hrilh bawk (2 Kor. 6:14–17).

`2 Korinth 6:11–7:1 chhiar la. Nun dân thianghlim chu eng ang nge ni?`

Paula hian kohhran chhûnga inngainatna leh inhmangaihna a awm a pawimawhzia uar takin a sawi a (1 Kor. 6:11–13). Mi dangte nêna inrem dân kawng an zawnna chu Pathian nên an lo inrem ta a ni tih tilangtu niin, chumi avâng chuan remna siamtute tia hriat an lo nih phah bawk ang.

Tichuan, thianghlim taka an nun theih dân tûr kawng hrang hrang paruk a hrilh ta a, chûngte chu: “Ring lo mite nên rual lo takin nghâwngkawl bât dûn suh u” (2 Kor. 6:14, NKJV) tih te; (2) “An zîng ata lo chhuak rawh u” (2 Kor. 6:17, NKJV) tih te; (3) “A hrangin awm rawh u” (2 Kor. 6:17, NKJV) tih te; (4) “Thil bawlhhlawh rêng rêng chu khawih suh u” (2 Kor. 6:17, NKJV) tih te; (5) “taksa leh thlarau bawlhhlawhna rêng rêng lak atang chuan i intifâi ang u” (2 Kor. 7:1, ESV) tih te leh; (6) “Pathian tih dêk chungin thianghlim famkim ni rawh u” (2 Kor. 7:1, RSV) tih te hi a ni. Hêng fuihna thu hlu tak takte hian Pathian thianghlim chuan thianghlim taka nung tûrin min phût tih min hriattîr a; tin, milem biakna thil rêng rêng lakah chuan inthiar fihlîm hlauh tûrin min duh a ni tih a sawi bawk.

Hetih rual hian, Kristiante chu biak in thianghlim an nihna anga an mawhphurhna hriattîrtu tûr thutiam pasarih ngawt kan hmu bawk a, chûngte chu: 1) “Anmahniah ka awm ang” tih te; (2) “Anmahniah ka lêng bawk ang” tih te; (3) “An Pathian ka ni ang a” tih te; (4) “Anni pawh ka mite an ni ang” tih te; (5) “Ka lâwm ang che u a” tih te; (6) “In tân Pa ka ni ang a” tih te leh; (7) “Nangni pawh ka fanute leh ka fapate in ni ang” tih te hi a ni (2 Kor. 6:16, 17, 18, NKJV).

2 Korinth 6:16-a kan hmuh thutiam palite hi 2 Korinth 6:17-a kan hmuh thupêk pathumte innghahna a ni tih i lo hria em? (2 Korinth 6:17 tîr lamah hian “chu vângin”tih tawngkam hman ni). Hei hian a thianghlimna chu keimahni’n kan thawh chhuah ni lovin, kan thinlunga Thlarau Thianghlim hnathawh zâwk a ni tih a lantîr a. Chutih rualin, thianghlimna chu Pathian hnên ata kan dawn ni mah se, keimahni’n kan tih ve tûrte kan tih a, milem biakna leh thil thianghlim lo rêng rêng chu kan hnâwl vek a ngâi ve tho bawk.

`2 Korinth 6:16–18-a kan hmuh Pathian thutiamte hian thianghlimna leh mi thianghlim nih chungchâng eng nge min hrilh?`
