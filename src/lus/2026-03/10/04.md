---
title:  Inremna rawngbâwl hna
date:   01/09/2026
---

`2 Korinth 5:11–15 chhiar la. He thu hian engtiangin nge Paula rawngbâwlna hi a pawimawh bera Krista dahna a ni tih a târ lan?`

Paula hian a rawngbâwl dân chu Krista hmaah tihlanin a la awm dâwn a ni tih a hre chiang hle a (2 Kor. 5:10). Chu vâng chuan, Lalpa tihin, mite chu Krista chanchin tha lo pawm ve tûrin a thlêm thîn (2 Kor. 5:11). Heta “tih” a tih hi Krista zahna leh hlauhna a ni a, chu chu Krista a hmangaihna leh Krista’n amah a hmangaih a ni tih a rin ngamna nên a kal kawp tlat bawk. Thuthlung Hlui lehkhabute’n an sawi dânin Lalpa tih awmzia chu Lalpa hmangaih leh a kawngte zawh tihna a ni a, amah hmangaih tak taktute chuan an thinlung leh nunna zawng zawngin a rawng an bâwl thîn (Deut. 10:12).

Paula’n rawng a bâwla a ngaih pawimawh ber ni fo thîn chu amah ni lovin, Krista zâwk a ni a. Amah hi chu a infak ngâi rêng rêng lo. A chak lohnate a chhuan chhan pawh Krista tho a ni a (2 Kor. 12:9). “Kei erawh chuan kan Lalpa Isua Krista kraws lo chu engmah ka chhuang dâwn lo” (Gal. 6:14, ESV) a ti thlâwt mai a ni. Chu vângin, Korinth khua ringtu awmte hnêna amah an chhuan chhan (2 Kor. 5:12) tûr a hrilh pawh hi a pawimawh bera Krista dah chunga rawng a bâwlna a ni a, amah dodâltute’n anmahni ngawt inngaihtuaha rawng an bâwl dân nên chuan a inang lo hle a ni tih pawh a sawi lang bawk.

`2 Korinth 5:16–21, Kolossa 1:19–23 leh Ephesi 2:13–16 te chhiar la. Paula’n “inremna rawngbâwl hna” a tih hi eng nge ni a awmzia?`

Inremna rawngbâwl hna thawh kawnga chhuanawm ber leh entawn tlâk êm êm ni bawk chu Krista a ni a. Chutiang chu a nih avângin keini hnênah pawh hian “inremna rawngbâwl hna” min lo pe ve ta rêng bawk a ni (2 Kor. 5:18, NKJV). 2 Korinth 5:16–21-ah hian inrem lehna chungchâng a sawi ngun angreng khawp mai a. He thu hi Paula’n a ngaih pawimawh êm êm niin, keini pawhin ngaih pawimawh ber pakhatah kan neih a tûl hle.

Pathian chuan mihringte hi a Fapa thihna azârah amah nên inremin min lo siam leh ta a. Chutianga Pathian nêna lo inrem tawhte chu thilsiam thar (mi thar) an ni (2 Kor. 5:17). Chu “inrem lehna thuchah” chu mi dangte hnênah pawh Krista chanchin tha hril chunga an hlan chhâwn ve zêl a ngâi ta a (2 Kor. 5:19, ESV). Chutiang chuan, keini hi “Krista tâna palâi, Pathianin amah nên inrem tûra mite a sâwmna hmanrua kan ni” (2 Kor. 5:20, NKJV).

`I tâna Krista’n a lo tih tawh kha ngaihtuah la. Krawsah khân i tân lo thi lo ta se mi sual thiam loh chang ngei ngei tûr i ni tih pawh hre bawk ang che. Chumi i hriatna chuan engtiangin nge mi dang, a bîk takin Lalpa pawh la hre ve lote chunga i rilru put dânah nghawng a neih ang?`
