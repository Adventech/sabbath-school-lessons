Ellen G. White-i lehkhabu, Tirhkohte Thiltih bung 31, “Thuchah Chu Ngaihven A Ni” (phêk 323–334) chhiar ang che.

Kâr hmasa lamah khân a chunga kan târ lan Tirhkohte Thiltih bu khi kan chhiar a. Chhiar manhla tak a nih nghe nghe kha. Tûnah hian Paula’n rilru na tak, thinrim deuh tûn chunga lehkhathawn a ziah thu Pi White-i’n a sawina lâi vêl kha ngunthlûk takin chhiar leh la, a ziah lâia a rilru awm dân tûr te, a lehkhathawn an lo chhiar hnua an nun dân tha lote an sim tâk thu a dawna a hlimzia te chu ngaihtuah ang che. Tichuan, chumiin Paula rawngbâwlna kha a der mai a nih lohzia min hriattîr dân pawh ngaihtuah bawk la, Krista tâna hna kan thawhna kawnga kan tangkâipui theih tûr zirlâi tha tak tak awmte chu hriat i tum bawk dâwn nia.

“Lei leh vâna mite hnênah, suala tlu tawh khawvêla chêngte hnênah leh suala tlu ve lo khawvêl danga chêngte hnênah pawh Pathian chu mi ngâidam thîn a ni tih thu hi kan puang chhuak tûr a ni a, Pathian hmangaihna avângin amah Pathian nên kan lo inrem leh thei a ni tih kan hriattîr ngei ngei tûr a ni. Kan thil sual tihte kan sim a, thinlungah inchhîrna kan nei a, kan sualte thâwi nâna Krista thihna chu kan ring a, amah avângin Pathian nên kan lo inrem ta a ni—Ellen G. White, Special Testimonies On Education, p. 223.

“A kohhran ang hian êng ropui tak kan dawng a. Chu êng chu khawvêl thatna tûr leh malsâwmsakna tûra Lalpa’n min kawltîr a ni. Kan hnênah hian inremna rawngbâwlna hna chu dah a ni a. Vân lam chhuak thiltihtheihna tang kawpin mihringte chu Pathian nên inrem tûrin kan thlêm tûr a ni.”—Ellen G. White, Letter 32, 1903.

Pathian nêna kan lo inrem tawh hnu chuan thianghlim taka nun hman kan tum ngei ngei tûr a ni. Ellen G. White-i pawhin 2 Korinth 7:1 thu a sawi zaunaah Paula’n “Pathian tih dêk chunga thianghlimna famkimin” (2 Kor. 7:1) tih thu a lo sawi hi a awmzia a lo sawi a. Paula hian ring thar tê tê-te chu “mahni inring tawk tûr te, rinna kawnga chak leh thang chho zêl tûr te, thahnemngâi leh phûr taka awm tûr te, Pathian hnênah leh a ram zauh zêlna atân inserh hrang tûrin a sâwm a ni” a ti.—Ellen G. White, Tirhkohte Thiltih, p. 201.

**Sawi ho tûrte:**

`Paula chuan chanchin tha rohlu kawltu “hlum belte” kan nih thu a sawi a (2 Kor. 4:7). Mi chak lo, derthâwng tak leh tlâkchham hrang hrang nei kan nihna hian engtin nge chanchin tha hril darh hna tithuanawp lovin a tihchak zâwk hlauh theih ang?`

`“Thilsiam thar” (“mi thar”) nih awmzia chu eng nge ni (2 Kor. 5:17)? Chu chuan engtin nge kan ni tin nun hman dânah nghawng a neih ang? Krista’n thilsiam thar (mi thar) a nihtîr dân che chu eng nge ni?`

`2 Korinth 6:4, 5-ah hian Paula’n chanchin tha hril hna a thawh avânga harsatna a tawh hrang hrangte a sawi a. Chûng a harsatna tawhte chu engtin nge a lo dawnsawn hlawm? A dawnsawn dân atang hian i harsatna tawhte dawnsawn dân tûr i lo hriat phah em?`

`Paula hian Pathian duhzâwng anga lungngaihna leh khawvêl mite tih dân anga lungngaihna inan lohzia a sawi a (2 Kor. 7:10). Lungngaihnain sual sim duhna thinlung min puttîr theih dân chu eng nge ni? Pathian duhzâwng anga lungngaihna leh khawvêl mite tih dân anga lungngaihna inan loh dân chu engtin nge i sawifiah ang?`
