---
title:  Rawngbâwlna dik rahte
date:   30/08/2026
---

`2 Korinth 3:1–9 chhiar la. Krista lehkha kan lo nih theih dân kawng chu eng nge ni?`

Hmânlâi Grik mite leh Rom-hote khawvêlah khân mi pawimawh tak tute’n emaw a tha zâwnga an chanchin an ziahsakna lehkha ken an chîng hle a ni âwm e. Paula erawh hi chuan chutiang lehkha chu pakhat mah a nei ve lo va. A rawngbâwlna dikzia tilangtu awm chhun chu Korinth khuaa awm ringtute nuna Thlarau Thianghlim hnathawh lo lang kha a ni tih a pawm hmak mai a ni. Korinth khuaa kohhranho hetiang an lo nih theih chhan hi ama fin leh fel vâng emaw, a thawhrim êm vâng emaw a ni miah lo tih a hre chiang hle a (2 Kor. 3:4–6). Chu vângin, mahni insawimâwi emaw, infak emaw lam chu a ti ve ngâi rêng rêng lo (2 Kor. 3:5, 1 Kor. 2:2).

Paula hian thuthlung pahnih, Mosia’n a lo hriattîr thuthlung hlui leh amah leh a thawhpuite’n an hril thuthlung thar chungchâng tâwi têa sawi fiahin a rawngbâwlna chungchâng a sawi a. A thil sawi hi kan chhiar liam puat ringawt a nih chuan thuthlung hlui khân chhandam nih inbeiseina mi a pe miah lo niin kan lo ngâi ngei ang. Mahse, chutiang chu a sawi lo rêng rêng a, chhandamna chu Thuthlung Tharah a awm ang bawkin Thuthlung Hluiah pawh khân an a lo awm thîn a ni zâwk. Thuthlung Hlui khân chanchin tha chu a lo thlen hmain a lo puang lâwk a. “Tin, Pathian Lehkha thu chuan, Pathianin rinna avangin Jentailte thiam a chantîr dâwn tih lo hre lâwkin, Abrahama hnênah khân, ‘Nangmahah chuan hnam tin malsâwmsakin an awm ang’ tiin chanchin tha chu a lo hril lâwk tawh a ni” (Gal. 3:8, NKJV).

2 Korinth 3:1–4:6-ah hian Thuthlung Hlui chu Pathian tihlâwmna atâna mahni thil tha tih rinchhantute awm dân entîrna atân hman a ni tih kan hmu a. Chutih lâiin, Thuthlung Thar erawh chuan anmahni tân leh anmahni nuna tih a lo tiam zawng zawngte ti tûra Pathian khawngaihna rinchhan maitute awm dân a entîr ve thung.

Paula hian ringtute’n chanchin tha an lo dawnsawn dân leh ring lote’n chanchin tha an lo dawnsawn dân inan lohzia a sawi a. Chanchin tha chi hnih, Thuthlung Hluia chanchin tha leh Thuthlung Thara chanchin tha inan loh thu lam a sawi lo. A chhan pawh chanchin tha chu pakhat chauh a awm a, chu chanchin tha chu “kan thiltih angte ni lovin, ama ruat ang leh, leilung pian hma daiha Krista Isuaa min lo pêk tawh khawngaihna ang zâwka min chhandamtu leh kohna thianghlima min kotu” (2 Tim. 1:9, NKJV) Pathianin min pêk a ni.

2 Korinth 2:14–4:6-ah hian a taka thil lo thleng tawh ngei eng emaw a sawi lang a ni tih erawh kan pha chuang lo va. Nimahsela, chu thil thleng chu an zînga mi thenkhatte “chhandam mêk” an nih lâia, thenkhat dang erawh an “boral mêk” thung thu sawina atân a hmang a ni tih lo hria ila (2 Kor. 2:15, NKJV). Mosia rawngbâwlna an rin zawh loh avângin ama rawngbâwlna ngei pawh anmahni thiam loh chantîrtu tûr leh an thihna thlentu tûr niin an lo ngâi hlauh thei a. Nimahsela, Korinth khuaa kohhranho hian an lo rin avângin an zînga Paula rawngbâwlna pawh felna rawngbâwlna, nunna petu Thlarau Thianghlim rawngbâwlna a ni tih chiang takin an lo hre thei ta a ni.

Hetianga ringtute’n chhandamna an lo chang ta hi Paula rawngbâwlna dikzia tilangtu pawh a ni bawk.
