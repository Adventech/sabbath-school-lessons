### Chhiar tûrte
2 Kor. 3:1–9, 2 Kor. 4:7–18, 2 Kor. 5:11–15, Kol. 1:19–23, Eph. 2:13–16, 2 Kor. 6:11–7:1, 2 Korinth 7.

> <p>Châng vawn</p>
> “Lehlam lehlama nêk sâwrin kan awm a, nimahsela kan thaw a ipik si lo; mangangin kan awm a, nimahsela kan bei a dawng si lo; tihduhdahin kan awm a, nimahsela kalsanin kan awm si lo; vuak thlûkin kan awm a, nimahsela tihboralin kan awm si lo; kan taksaah hian kumkhuain Isua thihna chu kan pu zêl thîn a, Isua nunna pawh kan taksaah hian a lan theih nân” (2 Korinth 4:8–10).

Kâr hmasa khân Paula’n a thianghlimzia leh dikzia sawiin, amah dotute’n mi rin tlâk lo leh Korinth kohhran pawh ngaihsak tak tak lo nia an puhnate chu a chhâng lêt tih kan sawi tawh a. Thlarau lama a fate thatna tûr a nih phawt chuan eng thil pawh a ti zêl mai thîn tih a hrilh a nih kha. 2 Korinth 2:12–17-ah hian 2 Korinth 7-a a sawi chhunzawm leh chauh thu pawimawh tak a sawi a. Hetianga a sawi chhan hi Krista tâna rawngbâwlna dik tak chu eng ang nge ni tih an lo hriat a duh vâng a ni ngei ang. Hemi chungchânga Paula thil sawi atang hian keini pawhin zirlâi pawimawh tak tak zir chhuah theih kan nei âwm e.

Tûn kârah hian Paula’n Krista tâna thlarau seng khâwm hna a thawh thu a sawina 2 Korinth 3–7 kan en ho dâwn a. Ellen G. White-i chuan heti hian a lo sawi: “Mi sualte piantharna leh thutaka tihthianghlim an nihna hi rawngbâwltu’n rawngbâwl tûrin Pathian kohna a dawng tih finfiahna tha ber a ni. Tirhkoh Paula tirhkoh nihna pawh hi a rawngbâwlna kal tlanga piantharna lo chang tate thinlungah ziah lan a ni a, nunna thar an lo neih tâkte chu a hriattîrtu an ni bawk. Kan thinlunga Krista a lo chên hunah ropuina beiseina chu kan lo nei ang a. Rawngbâwltu chu hêng a rawngbâwlna rahte hian nasa takin a tichak dâwn a ni.”—Ellen G. White, Tirhkohte Thiltih, p. 328.
