---
title:  Hrehawm tawrhna leh châwimâwina
date:   31/08/2026
---

`2 Korinth 4:7–18 chhiar la. Paula hrehawm tawrh hrang hrangte chu eng nge ni? Hêng a hrehawm tawrh hrang hrangte hi engtin nge a lo tuar chhuah?`

Hmânlâi Bohemia rama kohhran siamthatu ropui tak John Huss-a khân heti hian Isua chungchâng a lo sawi a: “Ani chu khawvêl Hotupa a ni a, keini erawh mihring hmuhsitawm tak mai chauh kan ni—chuti chung pawhin ani chu kan tân a lo tuar a. Tuarna chu kan lo thianghlim theihna tûr a nih chuan enga ti nge kan tuar loh ang ni?” Indona Ropui, p. 105.

John Huss-a’n he thu a sawi hma kum za eng emaw zâtah khân tirhkoh Paula chuan Krista tâna tawrh a huam thu a lo sawi daih tawh bawk a. Keh mai thei hlum bêl ang lek a ni tih pawh a hre chiang hle (2 Kor. 4:7). Leh lam leh lama nêk sâwr, mangang taka awmtîr, tihduhdah leh vuak thlûkin awm thîn mah sela, thaw ipik takin emaw, beidawng êm êmin emaw chuan a awm ngâi rêng rêng lo va, kalsan emaw, tihchhiat vek emaw ni taa inhriatna pawh a nei ngâi miah lo bawk (2 Kor. 4:8, 9). A “taksaah khân Isua thihna put zêl” a tum tlat a, a chhan pawh chumi hmang chuan amahah Isua nunna lantîr a lo nih theih dâwn vâng a ni (2 Kor. 4:10, 11).

Paula’n “Isua thihna” tih tawngkam a hman hian, châng hmasa lama a lo târ lan tawh hrehawm a tawrh hrang hrangte kha sawi a tum a ni maithei a. Chuti a nih chuan, “Isua nunna” tih tawngkam a hman pawh hian, thihna atanga chhanchhuahna emaw, tûn dam chhûng atâna thlarau lam thiltihtheihna min pêk emaw hi a kawh ngei a rinawm. Eng pawh ni se, thil chiang tak chu Isua nunna tih hian thawhlehna a kâwk a tih hi a ni a (2 Kor. 4:12). Isua lo kal leh hunah amah ringtute zawng zawng chu kaihthawhin an la awm ang.

Thil mak leh ngaihnawm angreng tak ni bawk chu 2 Korinth 4:10–12-a vawi thum ngawt mai “thihna leh nunna” tih tawngkam a lo hmang hi a ni. He tawngkam hian tûna kan khawvêl nun hi chu nunna leh thihna inchawhpawlh a ni tih min hriat chhuah thartîr thîn a. Nimahsela, nakîna tihropui kan nih hunah chuan thihna tel lo nunna kan chang tawh dâwn a ni (Thup. 20:14, Thup. 21:4).

Thil pawimawh ber mai chu 2 Korinth 4:7–18 hian chanchin tha chu Pathian chauhin ropuina a chan theih nân hlum bêl ang maia dawrâwm mihringte hmanga hril a ni tih a târ lang hi a ni tih lo hria ila (2 Kor. 4:15). Ni e, ram thima chanchin tha hril darhtu missionary-te pawhin chanchin tha hril hna an thawhna kawngah hrehawm chi hrang hrang an tuar thîn ngei alâwm. Amaherawhchu, he leia kan hrehawm tawrhte hi, min lo hmuak rengtu chatuan ropuina nêna tehkhin râlah chuan ho tham tak leh rei lo te chhûng atân chauh a ni a (2 Kor. 4:17). Chu vângin, ringtute chu an mita an hmuh theih thilah ni lovin, rinna zâwkin an nung tûr a ni (2 Kor. 4:18, 2 Kor. 5:7).

He beiseina, nakîna kan la chan tûr ropuina hian Paula rilru pawh a luah hneh êm êm thîn a; chu vâng chuan a sawi pawh a sawi chhuak fo thîn rêng bawk (2 Kor. 5:1–10). Thi thei a taksa chu leia kan chênna in nên hian a tehkhin a. Chutih lâiin, thlân atanga kaihthawh taksa erawh chu “Pathianin a sak in” tia sawiin (2 Kor. 5:1), chu chu chhuan tina ringtute’n an nghahfâk ber a ni.

`Eng pawh tâwkin, eng thil pawh lo pal tlang mah ila, eng vângin nge thawhlehna, keimahni thawhlehna (1 Kor. 15:52) ngei chu kan beisei tlat reng a pawimawh êm êm?`
