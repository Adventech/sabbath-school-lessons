---
title:  Thlamuanna leh hlimna
date:   03/09/2026
---

`2 Korinth 7 chhiar la. Paula’n Korinth khuaa ringtu awmte’n an awm dân tha lote an sim ta a ni tih a hriat khân engtin nge a awm?`

“Kan thinlungah hian in awm tlat asin” (2 Kor. 7:3, NKJV; 2 Kor. 6:11 pawh en la) tih tawngkam a hmang hian anmahni a hmangaihzia a tilang a. Anmahni a hmangaihna chu hmangaihna vêka an lo chhân lêt ve beiseiin, “In thinlungah khân kan tân hmun lo zuah ve rawh u” tiin a ngên nghâl bawk (2 Kor. 7:2, ESV). “In thinlungah” tih tawngkam tak hi chu a Grik tawng lamah a awm chiah lo niin an sawi a; nimahsela, Saptawng Bible hrang hrangte leh Mizo Bible pawhin an lo dah hi, a chheh vêl thu ngaihtuahin a dik thlap zâwk tlat.

Korinth khua ringtu awmte hian Paula leh a thawhpuite lakah an thinlung an hawng êm êm a. Chu chu Paula hlim êm êm chhan pawh a ni rêng bawk. Paula tawngkam hman hian thinlung taka a hlimzia a târ lang a: “Nangni lama ka tawng ngamzia hi a nasa ngawt mai, ka chhuanzia che u pawh hi ropui tak a ni. Thlamuannain ka khat a, kan hrehawmna zawng zawngah chuan ka hlim êm êm zâwk a ni” a ti (2 Kor. 7:4, NKJV). Paula hi thlamuanna leh hlimnain a khat a. Kan kohhrante hian Krista hnêna an inpêkna hmangin an rawngbâwltu pastor-te thinlungah thlamuanna leh hlimna nasa tak an thlen thei rêng a ni.

Paula hian a thlamuan chhan leh a hlim chhan chu chiang deuh zâwkin a sawifiah nghâl a. Hêng thil pahnih, thlamuanna leh hlimna thute hi 2 Korinth 7:5–16 luah khattu ber pawh a ni nghe nghe. 2 Korinth 7-ah hian “thlamuan” (Grik tawngin, parakaleo) tih emaw, “thlamuanna” ” (Grik tawngin, paraklesis) tih emaw kan hmu nual a, a belh khâwm phei chuan vawi sarih zet a tling. Tin, he lâi thu hi 2 Korinth intanna, Pathiana thlamuanna thu (2 Kor. 1:3–7) nên bawk khân tihtâwp a ni a. 2 Korinth 7-a kan hmuh Paula thlamuanna hi thinrim deuh tûn, rilru na tak chunga a ziah lehkhathawn khân rah tha tak a chhuah ta ngei a ni tih a hriat avânga rilru hahdamna a neihin a thlen a ni.

Rilru hahdam taka a awm theih tâk chhan hi Tita’n thu lâwmawm tak a rawn thlen vâng chu ni tho bawk mah se, thlamuanna neihtîrtu tak erawh Pathian a ni a (2 Kor. 7:6). Pathian chu “thlamuanna zawng zawng thlentu Pathian, kan hrehawm tawrh zawng zawnga min thlamuan thîntu” a ni rêng a ni (2 Kor. 1:3, 4, NKJV).

Thil ngaihnawm deuh chu, “thlamuannaa a khah” mai bâkah, a “hlim êm êm” bawk hi a ni a (2 Kor. 7:4, 7, 13). Thinrim tûn chunga a ziah a lehkhathawn khân anmahni tilungngâi mah se, chu an lungngaihna chu Pathian duh dân anga an lo sim phah theihna tûr a ni zâwk tih a hriat vâng a ni (2 Kor. 7:9–11, NASB). Korinth khuaa ringtu awmte hi “Pathian duhzâwng ang takin an lungngâi” a (2 Kor. 7:11, NKJV), chutiang lungngaihna chuan “chhandam nih theihna tûra sual simna” a thlen thîn (2 Kor. 7:10, NKJV). Chu bâka Pathian rawngbâwltu dik takte lâwmna thlentu tûr thil dang a awm thei tawh dâwn chuang em ni?

`Pathian duhzâwng anga lungngaihna chu i lo chang ve tawh em? Chu lungngaihna chu i sualte simtîrtu tûr che Pathian duhzâwng ang lungngaihna a ni tih engtin nge i hriat theih ang?`
