date:   17/07/2026

Ellen G. White-i lehkhabu, Tirhkohte Thiltih bung, “Sâwm leh Pahnihte Zirtîr An Ni” (phêk 17–24) chhiar ang che.

“Pathian thutak ringtu kohhran la bângte hian an inpumkhatna leh an tan rualna hmangin khawvêl hnênah hnial thlâk rual lovin thutak kawltu, mi danglam bîk leh Pathian mi thlante an nihzia an lantîr a. He inpumkhatna leh tan rualna hian hmêlmapa chu awm hle hletîr thei lovin, chumi chu a thlen loh nân theihtâwp chhuah a tum thîn. Tûn hun atâna thu dik hian—miin a thinlunga a rin a, a nunpui a nih bawk chuan—Pathian mite a tipumkhat sawt thei a, mi dangte hneh thei tûrin thiltihtheihna a pe bawk thîn.”—Ellen G. White, Testimonies for the Church, vol. 1, p. 328.

“Pathian chuan a mite hi chatuan thutak chungah pheikhâi rual taka ding tlâng tûrin a hruai a. Amah Krista ngei pawh ‘chi thlan bîk, thil tha tih kawnga thahnem ngâi, ama tâna a tihthianghlim’ an lo nih theih nân khawvêl hnênah a lo inpe tawh a nih kha. He tihthianghlimna kawng hi fel lohna zawng zawng leh inrem lohna leh inhmuh mawhna tinrêng lak ata kohhran tlen fâi a niha, a member-te pawh inpawt hniam lova an indâwm kân zâwk a, an hmaa awm hna ropui tak chu hlen chhuak tûra an thawh tlân theih nâna ruahman a ni. Pathian chuan a mite hi rinnaa inpumkhat tlat ni tûrin a duh hle a. Khenbeh a nih hma chiaha Krista tawngtâina pawh kha amah chu Pa tirh a ni tih khawvêlin an lo âwih theih nân, amah leh Pa chu pumkhat an ni anga a zirtîrte pawh pumkhat an lo nih ve a ni. He tawngtâina thu ropui leh rilru khawih tak mai hian hmân ata tawh tûn thlengin thu a la sawi reng a; a tawngtâi thu chham chu: ‘Hêng mite tân chauh hian dîl ka ni lo, an thu sawi atanga keimah min la ring tûrte tân pawh ka dîl nghâl bawk a ni’ tih hi a ni.”—Ellen G. White, Testimonies for the Church, vol. 4, pp. 23, 24.

**Sawi ho tûrte:**

`Isua kha khawvêla a rawngbâwl hna a thawh zawh dâwn hnaiah “an zaa pumkhat an nihna tûrin. . . nangin kei mi tîr tih khawvêlin an lo hriat theihna tûrin” tiin a tawngtâi a (Joh. 17:21–23, NASB). Enga ti nge Kristaa inpumkhatna hi khawvêl chhandam tûrin Pathianin a Fapa a rawn tîr a ni tih hriat theihna pawimawh tak a nih? Chutih rual chuan, enga ti nge inpumkhat lohna erawh kohhran rawngbâwlna tithuanawptu lian tak a nih thung bawk?`

`1 Korinth 4:9–13 chhiar la, heti lâia tirhkohte nihna a târ lan dân hi ngun takin lo chîk ang che. Tirhkohte nihna a târ lan dân leh kan tûnlâi khawvêla kan hmuh hruaitu tam takte awm dân hi engtiang chiahin nge a danglam? Heta tang hian Pathian tehfung leh khawvêl tehfung inan loh theihzia kan lo hmu thiam em?`

`1 Korinth 4:16-ah hian Paula’n amah entawn tûra Korinth mite a sâwm thu kan hmu a. Kohhran hruaitu mihring ni ve thote hi entawna neih i duh ang em? Hruaitute entawn tih leh a dik a dawk ngaihtuah miah loha anmahni fak leh rîkngawt ang hi engtiangin nge a inan loh?`
