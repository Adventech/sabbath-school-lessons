---
title:  Finna leh puitlinna
date:   14/07/2026
---

Pâwl pâwla inthen hranna lo thleng thîn hi kohhran hruaitute zînga tu emaw ngaihsân bîk kan neih vâng a ni tlângpui a. Hei hian kohhran inpumkhatna a tihderthâwn theih bâkah, kohhran member-te thlarau lam hrisêlna pawh a nghawng chhe vek thei bawk. Miin Kristian rawngbâwlna a hmuh dân a dik tâwk loh chuan kohhran hruaitute zîngah ngaihsân bîk tak a nei ang a, hruaitu dangte erawh a hmu nêp ngawih ngawih mai dâwn a ni. Chu chuan kohhran hruaitute zîngah inelna leh inbeih tawnna a hring chhuak ang a, kohhran phel hialna pawh thleng thei a ni. Chu âi pawha la hlauhawm zâwk chu, mihring ni ve tho kohhran hruaitute a lova luata kan ngaihsân hian Krista âi pawha ngaih pawimawh zâwk hialna rilru kan pu thei a, kan nuna a lailum ber luahtu tûr chu Krista a ni tih hi kan lo hre chang lo mai ang tih a hlauhawm hle.

`1 Korinth 3:1–4 chhiar la. Paula’n thlarau lama Korinth kohhran member-te an puitlin lohzia a sawi dân chu eng nge ni?`

Paula hian thlarau lama puitlingte chuan Pathian finna an lawm thiam thin thu chiang takin a sawi a (1 Kor. 2:6, 7), chu finna chu Thlarau Thianghlimin mi a zirtîr niin (1 Kor. 2:13), he khawvêl finna (1 Kor. 2:6) leh mihring finna (1 Kor. 2:13) nêna inang lo tak a ni tih pawh a hrilh bawk. Pathian finna chu Krista Krawsah khân târ lanin a lo awm tawh tih a sawi a (1 Kor. 2:1–4). Hrehawm chi hrang hrang a tawrh te, a thihna leh a thawhlehna te khân a finzia chu chiang takin a lo lantîr tawh a ni a ti. Chutiang chuan, inpumkhata awm tûra a sâwm leh hma hian (1 Kor. 3:1–17) finna dik tak leh Kristaa puitlinna an neih a tûlzia lo hre hmasa phawt se a duh thu a hrilh a ni.

Mi fing leh rinna kawnga puitling takte chu thlarau mite an ni a, tisa mite emaw, nausên ang emaw an ni ve lo (1 Kor. 3:1). Anni chuan thlarau lam thilte chu thlarau lam thilte vêk hmangin an khâikhin thîn a, a chhan pawh Pathian Thlarau thilte chu . . . thlarauva hriatthiam theih chauh a nih vâng a ni (1 Kor. 2:13, 14, NKJV). Ringtu fing leh puitlingte chu hnute tui ni lovin, chaw taka châwm an ni a (1 Kor. 3:2; Heb. 5:12 pawh chhiar ang che). Ringtu ni si, “hnute tui ring tlat chu nausên mai a nih avângin felna thu-ah hian hriatna nei lo a ni. Chaw tak erawh chu puitling tân a ni a, chûng mite chuan an chhia leh tha hriatna an hman fo avângin thil tha leh tha lo hriat theihna an nei tawh bawk” (Heb. 5:13, 14, ESV). Chutiang chu a nih avângin, ringtu fing leh puitling chin chuan, “Kei chu Paula pâwl ka ni” tiin emaw, “Kei chu Apollova pâwl ka ni” tiin an sawi ngâi lo vang (1 Kor. 3:4).

Paula te, Apollova te pawh hi anmahni ang bawka “Pathian hna thawhpuitute” ni ve tho an ni a (1 Kor. 3:9, ESV). A kohhran ang hian Pathian thlawhhma, a in leh a biak bûk kan ni bawk (1 Kor. 3:9, 16, 17). Kan za hian Krista zâra Pathian tâ vek kan ni (1 Kor. 3:11).

`Kohhran hruaitu i ngaihsân tak tu emawin a lo tihnual rawih tawh che em? Chutiang chu i lo tawng tawh a nih chuan eng zirlâi nge zir chhuah i neih?`
