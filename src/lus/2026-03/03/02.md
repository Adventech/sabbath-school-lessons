---
title:  Kohhrana inthen hranna awm
date:   12/07/2026
---

1 Korinth tîr lam bung pali-a thupui langsâr ber chu Paula’n an zînga inthen hranna rêng a awm loha, rilru hmun khat leh thu khat vaw tlânga awm tlat tûra a ngen thu (1 Kor. 1:10, ESV) hi a ni a. Thuthlung Thar zirna lama mi thiam tam zâwk pawhin inpumkhat thu hi 1 Korinth-a a thil sawi hrang hrangte puar khâwmtu a ni tih an pawm tlâng vek bawk.

`1 Korinth 1:12–17 chhiar la. He lâi thu hian kohhran chhûnga inthen hran leh tu pâwl pâwl emawa insiam ang chi hi thil tih loh tawp tûr a nih dân hrethiam tûrin engtin nge min tanpui? Chutiang chu a lo awm palh a nih pawha a chin fel dân tûr Paula’n min hrilh chu eng nge ni?`

Paula hian Korinth kohhran member-te zîngah inthen hranna a awm thu hi tawngkam na deuh hlek hmangin a târ lang a. A Grik tawng lama thumal a hmante chu _schisma_ (“inthen hranna,” 1 Kor. 1:10, NKJV) tih leh _eris_ (“inhnial buaina,” 1 Kor. 1:11, NKJV) tih te a ni. _Schisma_ (noun) tih leh _schizô_ (verb, “then hrang”) tih te hi Thuthlung Thar hmun dang dangah pawh ngaihdân inang lovin inthen hranna a hrin chhuah thin thu sawina atân hman a ni a. Chutiangin, _eris_ (noun, “inhnial buaina”) tih pawh hi Kristiante’n an tih ve loh tûr chîn tha lo chi hrang hrang zîngah chhiar tel a ni ve deuh ziah bawk.

`Rom 1:29, Rom 13:13, 1 Korinth 3:3, 2 Korinth 12:20, leh Galatia 5:20 te hi chhiar la. Eris (“inhnial buaina,” “innghirnghona”) tih nên huang khata dah tlân sual dang chi hrang hragte chu eng nge ni? Chu chuan inhnial buaina that lohzia engtin nge min hrilh?`

Korinth kohhrana inrem lohna awmte hi kawng hrang hranga lo lang chhuakin, mi thenkhat phei chu rorêltute hmaah an inkhing hial a ni âwm e (1 Kor. 6:1–3). Hetianga kohhran member ni ve ve si an inkhing ta mai hi Paula’n tha a ti lo êm êm a, “In zahna tûrin ka sawi a ni” (1 Kor. 6:5) ti hialin a sawi. Thil runthlâk êm êm ni lehnghâl chu, an inhuat tawnna te, an inei loh tawnna te hi Lalpa Zanriah kîl ho tûra an rawn kal khâwm lâi ngei pawha an thlah chuang rêng rêng lo hi a ni (1 Kor. 11:17–22).

Kohhran member-te zînga inthen hranna awm thîn hi thil pawi tak a ni a, Paula pawh hian a vei ngang a ni ang, Korinth khuaa a lehkhathawn hmasa zâwkah pawh hian sawi chhuah hmasak berah a nei chat rêng a ni.

`1 Korinth 1:12–27 hi chhiar leh la. He lâi thu hian tu pâwl pâwl emaw nia inngaih hi kohhran inpumkhatna tiderthâwng theitu a nihzia hrethiam tûra min puih dân ngun takin ngaihtuah ang che. Chutiang buaina chu in pumpelh theih nân eng nge tih theih in neih?`
