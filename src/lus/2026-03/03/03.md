---
title:  Ngaih pawimawh bera Isua neih
date:   13/07/2026
---

`1 Korinth 1:10 chhiar la. Paula’n “rilru hmunkhat leh thukhat vaw tlâng” (ESV) a tih hian eng nge a sawi tum ni ang?`

Tu pâwl pâwl emaw nia an insawi thîn hi hotua Krista an pawm lohzia tilangtu a ni tih a hrilh a (1 Kor. 1:10). Pathianin min kohna chu “a Fapa Isua Krista kan Lalpa pâwlnaa luh” a ni a ti (1 Kor. 1:9). Kan Lalpa chu Krista a ni a, amah chu ngaih pawimawh berah kan nei tûr a ni bawk. Tichuan, a zawhna siam awmze nei tak tak, “Krista chu then hranin a awm em ni? Paula hi in âia khenbehin a awm em ni? A nih loh leh, Paula hmingin em ni baptisma in chan?” (1 Kor. 1:13, NKJV) tih chhânna chu “Ni nâng aw!” tih a ni ang. Krista chu then hranin a awm lo va. Krista chu kan tân khenbeha awm a ni a. Tin, “Pa leh Fapa leh Thlarau Thianghlim hmingah” chuan baptis-in kan awm bawk (Mat. 28:19).

Paula hian “Krista taksa” chu kan ni a, tin, chumi pêngte chu ka nih theuh bawk thu min hrilh a (1 Kor. 12:27). Taksa hian bung/pêng hrang hrang—mahni thawh tûr bîk nei theuh—eng emaw zât nei mah se, taksa pakhat a ni tho. Taksa hian tha taka hna a thawh theih nân a pêng tinte’n anmahni theihna zâwn zêlah an tih tûr tha taka an tih theuh a ngâi a. He tehkhin thu hmang hian kohhran member-te chu inang tlâng thap tûr ni lovin, inpumkhat tlata awm tûr a nihzia a hriattîr a ni. A hmuh duh chu danglamna kâra inpumkhatna a ni a. Chu âi pawha a la hmuh châk zâwk phei chu eng anga inang lo tak tak pawh ni hlawm mah se, an inpumkhat tlat tho a ni.

Chutih rualin, kan rilru leh ngaihdân zawng zawngte chu kan Lalpa Krista hnênah kan tu lût theuh tûr a ni a. Krista chu kan Lalpa a nih thu hi Paula ngaihah chuan thu pawimawh tak niin, 1 Korinth tîr lamah pawh hian vawi eng emaw zât a sawi nawn nghe nghe (1 Kor. 1:2, 7, 8, 9, 10). Chu vâng chuan, kohhrana inthen hranna leh tu pâwl pâwl emaw ni anga insiam chungchâng a sawi hma hian, Isua chu kan Lalpa theuh a nih thu uar takin a sawi phawt a. Kohhran hian a ngaih pawimawh leh a zui ber tûr chu an hruaitute ni lovin, Isua Krista zâwk a ni.

1 Korinth tîr lama Isua Krista chu Lalpa a nih thu a lo sawi hian, “Rilru hmun khat leh thu khat vaw tlângin awm rawh u” (1 Kor. 1:10, ESV) tia lo sawi chhan hrethiam tûrin min tanpui a. “Hmunkhat,” “thukhat, “pumkhat” tia an lo lehlin Grik thumal chu _katartizô_ niin, chu chuan thil eng emaw a nihna dik taka dah lêt leh tih a kâwk. Kohhran hruaitu tu emaw ngaihsân bîk kan neih avânga pâwl pâwla kan inthen hran hian kohhran chhûnga inpumkhatna awm thîn chu siam that ngâi khawpin a lo chhe tawh tihna a ni a. Chumi siam that lehna chu Kristaa inpumkhatna leh chumiina a ken tel mahni inthihtîrna (mahni ngaihdân thlauh thlâk phalna) hmangin a lo thleng thei ang.

`Tûn hnâi kum sawmhnih vêl liam ta chhûng khân Seventh-dayAdventist Kohhran member thenkhat chuan Bible zir ho pâwl lian vak lo siamin rawngbâwlna an nei thîn a. Hetiang hi kohhran hruaitu tu emaw ngaihsân bîk kan neihte hnung zuia kan inthen hrang thîn nên hian eng nge a danglamna awm? Bible zir ho pâwla kan insiam hi kohhrana inthen hranna thlentu a nih loh nân engtin nge kan invên theih ang?`
