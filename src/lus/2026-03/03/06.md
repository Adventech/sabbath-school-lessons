---
title:  Kraws lantîrtu nun dân
date:   16/07/2026
---

Kohhran hruaitu tu emaw ngaihsân bîk neih avanga pâwl pâwla inthen hran a hlauhawm thu kan sawi hian, kan hruaitute thu kan zâwm tûr a ni lo kan tihna erawh a ni lo tih lo hria ila. Kohhran rawngbâwlna kawnga a sûlsutu leh hruaitute chu kan ngâisângin kan theihna apiangah kan tanpui bawk tûr a ni zâwk. Pathian chuan leia a rawngbâwl hna thawk tûrin mihring ni ve thote a ruat thîn a. Krawsin a lo entîr mahni inphatna nun neitu kohhran hruaitute chu an hnung kan zuiin, an thu leh hla pawh kan ngâichâng thîn ngei tûr a ni ang.

Chutiang nun an lo neih theih chhan chu Kraws hian mi dangte thunun châkna rilru an put kha thlauh thlâktîrin, Pathian Thu ang zêla thil ti thîn zâwk tûra a siam thar thin vâng a ni a. Krista rilru ang pu vetu kohhran hruaitute chuan rawngbâwlna kawnga hlawhtlinna an chanah Pathian an châwimâwi thîn. Isua pawhin leia rawng a bâwl lâi khân hlawhtlinna a chanah Pathian a châwimâwi zêl thîn a nih kha (Joh. 17:4).

Paula ngaihdân chuan, Kristian rawngbâwlna hi Kraws thua zung kaih nghet tlat a ni tûr a ni a. A chhan pawh Kraws hi Pathianin a finzia leh min chhandam tûra thil a tih theihzia a puan chhuahna a nih vâng a ni. Chutih rualin, mihring finna erawh âtna mai a nih dân a târ lang bawk. 1 Korinth 4:1–13-ah hian Kraws thu awmzia leh nihna chu chiang takin a lo sawi fiah a. A hmasa berin, Kristian kohhran hruaitute nihna tûr hrang hrang tuktu chu Pathian a ni a ti (1 Kor. 4:1–5). Tin, hrehawm tawrhna hi Kristian rawngbâwlnain a ken tel ve rêng a ni tih pawh a sawi bawk a (1 Kor. 4:9, 11–13). A hnuhnûng zâwk hi i han sawi zau hlek dâwn teh ang.

`2 Korinth 11:23–28 leh Kolossa 1:24 chhiar la. Krista vânga hrehawm tuar tih awmzia chu eng nge ni?`

Kristian kohhran hruaitute chuan Isua hniakhnung zuiin, an û leh naute (an ringtu nihpuite) tâna hrehawm tawrh leh, a tûl a nih phei chuan Krista avânga thih hial pawh an huam tûr a ni. Paula chuan amah leh Apollova te kha Pathianin “thi tûra a lo ruat sa tawh” an nih thu a sawi a (1 Kor. 4:9, NKJV). Chaw khaw puar leh tuisik in tûr tak ngial pawh hmu ngâi lo, silhfên tha râi pawh nei pha lo, mi kut tuar fo mai leh chênna in takngial pawh nei zo lo an ni (1 Kor. 4:11, NKJV). Tin, mahni intum chawp, tihduhdah tâwk reng, mite’n an sawi hmingchhiat thin leh, “tun (he lehkhathawn a ziah lâi) thleng pawha khawvêl hnâwmhnawk, bawlhhlawh tinrêng vawm khâwmna ang maia siam” a ni bawk a (1 Kor. 4:12, 13, NKJV). Chu mai a la ni lo, hausa, rorêltu, mi fing leh châwimâwia awm ni anga Korinth mite a han sawi vêl (1 Kor. 4:8, 10) chhan pawh hi eng dang vâng ni lovin, kohhran hruaitu tha leh dik takte chuan chapona leh mahni tha intihna rilru an pu miah tûr a ni lo tih hriattîr a duh vâng a ni. Chapona leh uanthuanna te hi kohhrana inthen darhna thlentu a ni si a (1 Kor. 4:6).

`Kohhrana i mawhphurhna chelh kha eng pawh lo ni se, Krista avângin engtianga nasa nge i lo tawrh ve tawh le? I chhânna atang chuan eng zirlâite nge i zir chhuah theih ang?`
