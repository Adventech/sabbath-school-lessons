---
title:  Isua nihna dik tak târ lanna
date:   12/08/2026
---

1 Korinth 13:4–7 kan han chhiar hian, hetiang hmangaihna hi kan neih loh châng a awm fo a ni tih hriain kan lo hnual rawih pawh a ni maithei. Thil ni ngeia lang chu, Paula’n he thu a ziah lâi hian a rilruah Isua a awm a. Hêng hmangaihna nihphung hrang hrangte hi tha famkima lan chhuahtîrtu awm chhun chu Isua Krista chauh a ni rêng bawk. Chu vângin, Paula’n hmangaihna nihphung a târ lan pawh hi a nihna takah chuan Isua nihphung zâwk a ni.

`Johana 13:1, 34; Johana 15:9, 12; 1 Timothea 1:14; 2 Timothea 1:7, 13; 1 Johana 3:16; leh 1 Johana 4:7–12, 19–21 te hi chhiar la. Hêng thute atanga hmangaihna chungchâng zir chhuah theih kan neihte chu eng nge ni?`

Pathian chu hmangaihna a ni a (1 Joh. 4:8). Mi a hmangaih êm avângin a Fapa neih chhun pawh min lo pe hial tawh nghe nghe (Joh. 3:16). Isua hi hmangaihna lo lan chhuahna famkim chu a ni a (Heb. 1:3). Hmangaihna chu engtiangin nge a lan chhuah thin tih kan hre duh a nih chuan ngun takin Isua kan thlîr mai tûr a ni. Thuthlung Thar lehkhabute’n Isua nihna leh a chanchin an sawite hi ngun taka kan chhiar a nih chuan 1 Korinth-a hmangaihnain a tih thin hrang hrang a târ lante hi amah a lang a ni tih kan hre ngei ang.

**Isua chu dawhthei tak a ni.** “Nimahsela, hemi avâng ngei hian a ni zahngaihna ka hmuh ni, Krista Isua chuan mi sual ber keimahah hian a dawhtheihna [Grik tawngin, makrothymia]” nasa tak chu a lantîr theihna tûrin” (1 Tim. 1:16, NIV).

**Isua chu mi khawngaih thei tak a ni.** Bible hian “Lalpa chu mi khawngaih thei tak a ni” (1 Pet. 2:3) tiin a lo sawi a. He lâia “Lalpa” a tih hi Isua Krista a ni. “Mi khawngaih thei” tia an lo lehlin Grik thumal chu chrêstos a ni a, he thumal hi 1 Korinth 13:4-a “ngilnei” tia an lo lehlin, chrêsteuomai nên hian a tobul chu thuhmun reng a ni.

**Isua chuan thutak a lâwm thîn.** Isua kha a Pa duh zâwng thilte a tihin a lâwm thîn a, a Pa’n amah a hmangaihzia pawh a hre reng thîn bawk (Joh. 15:9–11, Joh. 17:12–14).

**Isua chuan engkim a tuar hrâm hrâm thîn.** Hebrai 12:2, 3 chuan Isua khân kraws a tuar thu a sawi a, mi sualte’n amah an huatna zozâite kha a lo tuar chhuak vek tawh tih min hrilh bawk. Isua tuar anga nasa kha tuar ve mi dang tu mah an awm lo va (Phil. 2:8). Khâng a thil tawrh zawng zawng te kha a hmaa hlimna awm avâng chauha tuar chhuak thei a ni bawk.

**Isua chuan engkim a ring thîn.** Paula a lo pianthar a, Anania’n a pianthar tak tak a rin loh deuh thu a sawi khân (Tirh. 9:13, 14) Isua’n rawn chhângin a hmanrua ni tûra ama’n a thlan ngei a nih thu a sawi a (Tirh. 9:15). Isua chuan kan nihna ang anga min en mai lovin, a thiltihtheihna zâra kan lo nih theih tûr chu a thlîr zâwk thîn.

`Isua’n hmangaihna dik tak nihna mi a hriattîr dân kawng dangte chu eng nge ni?`
