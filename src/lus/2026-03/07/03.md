---
title:  Hmangaihnain a tih thinte
date:   10/08/2026
---

1 Korinth 13:4–7 hi bung 13 laimu ber a ni kan ti thei ang. Hetah hian hmangaihna nihphung sawi chhuah niin—hmangaihna chu eng nge a niha eng nge a nih loh tih leh, eng nge a tih thina eng nge a tih ngâi loh tih te min hrilh a. Thlarau Thianghlimin hmangaihnaa a chhûn khahte nun dân kan lo hriat theih nân hmangaihna chu mihring angah a chan bawk. Hmangaihna nihphung a sawinaah hian thumal eng emaw zât a hmang a. Ani ngaih dân chuan hmangaihna hi thil eng emaw hriatna leh ngaihtuahna âi mahin thil tih a ni ber zâwk.

A nih leh, hmangaihna chuan eng nge ni a tih thin?

**1. A dawhthei thîn (Grik tawngin, _makrothymeô_).** Grik thumal, _makrothymeô_ awmzia chu harsatna kârah pawh dawhtheihna lantîr tihna a ni a. Tin, dawhtheihna tih chuan mi dangte nêna indawh tawn theihna pawh a kawk bawk (Eph. 4:2).

**2. Ngil a nei thîn (Grik tawngin, _chrêsteuomai_).** 1 Korinth 13:4 hi Thuthlung Thara Grik thumal, _chrêsteuomai_ a lo lanna awm chhun a ni a; nimahsela, a laichîn hnâi tak thumal dang erawh chu hmun danah pawh kan hmu nual thung. Grik tawnga Thuthlung Hlui an lehin, Septuagint an tihaa Sam bu-ah chuan Pathian ngilneihna leh a khawngaihna sawina nân _chrêsteuomai_ nêna a tobul inang thumal eng eng emaw hman a ni a (Sam 145:9). Paula’n hmangaihnain ngil a nei thîn tih thu a sawi hian mi dangte kan hmangaihna chuan Pathianin keimahni min khawngaihna leh lainatna ang kha a lan chhuahtîr ve ang a tihna a ni.

**3. Thutak a lâwm thîn (Grik tawngin, _synchairô_).** Grik thumal, _synchairô_ chuan mi dangte lâwmna tâwmpui ve thei tih a kâwk (Luka 1:58; Luka 15:6, 9; 1 Kor. 12:26; Phil. 2:17, 18).

**4. Engkim a tuar hrâm hrâm thîn (Grik tawngin, _stegô_).** Thuthlung Thar zirna lama mi thiamte chu heta Paula thumal hman, _stegô_ hi “khuh,” entîr nân, thil eng emaw a tlâng than hluai loh nâna thup (vêng himtihna pawh a ni thei ang) tihna nge ni a, “tuar,” fân thei tih sawina tih thu-ah an ngaihdân a rual chiah lo va. Mahse, tuarna thu hi chiang takin 1 Korinth 9:12-ah pawh a lo lan tawh avângin, hrilhfiahtu leh Bible letlingtu tam zâwk daihte chuan a hnuhnûng zâwk, “tuar” tih hi a sawi tum niin an ngâi.

**5. Eng kim a ring thîn (Grik tawngin, _pisteuô_).** Grik thumal, _pisteuô_ hi “rinna” tih sawina thumal, pistis nên tobul thuhmun a ni a. 1 Korinth 13-ah hian engkim ring tih chuan rinhlelhna tûr chhan awm reng mah se,ring lui hrâm tho tih a kâwk.

**6. Engkim a beisei thîn (Grik tawngin, _elpizô_).** Thuthlung Tharah hian Grik thumal, _elpizô_ tih chuan thil tha eng emaw a lo thleng dâwn nia rinna emaw, beiseina emaw a kâwk tlângpui thîn.

**7. Engkim a tuar chhuak thîn (Grik tawngin, _hypomenô_).** 1 Korinth 13:7-ah hian Grik thumal pahnih, _stegô_ leh _hypomenô_ te hi a awmzia a danglam lêm lo va, thumal hrang, a sawi tum erawh inang reng si niin, harsatna kârah pawh chhel taka awm tihna ve ve a ni. 1 Korinth 13:7 tâwp lamah hian _stegô_ sawi nawn leh âiah a tlukpui tho thumal dang, _hypomenô_ a hmang ta thung a. Hetianga thumal hrang, a awmzia erawh thuhmun reng ni si a hman chhan chu rinna leh beiseina neih a pawimawhzia hriattîr a tum vâng a ni. Tawngkam danga sawi chuan, hmangaihna hi kan rina kan beisei tlat bawk chuan a chakin a daih rei thîn.

`1 Korinth 13:4–7 hi Galatia 5:22, 23 nên khâikhin la. Eng thu thuhmun nge i hmuh? Engtin nge hetiang hmangaihna ang hi keimahni nun theuhah kan lan chhuahtîr theih ang`
