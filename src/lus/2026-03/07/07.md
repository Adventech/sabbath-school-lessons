date:   14/08/2026

Ellen G. White-i’n Review and Herald chanchin bu August 28, 1888 chhuaka thu a lo ziah, “The Need of Love” (pp. 545, 546) tih kha chhiar ang che.

“Eng anga hna sâng pawh thawk sela, Pathian leh a mihringpuite hmangaihna a neih si loh chuan Krista zirtîr dik tak a ni lo tihna a ni a. Rinna ropui tak neiin, thilmak tih theihna pawh nei hial mah se, hmangaihna a neih si loh chuan a rinna pawh hlutna engmah nei lo mai a ni ang. Mi thilphal tak pawh a ni maithei e; nimahsela, hmangaihna dik tak atanga lo chhuak ni lo, chhan dang eng emaw vâng maia ti a nih chuan mi retheite châwm nân a neih zawng zawng pe vek mah sela, a thil tih chuan Pathian lawm a hlawhtîr chuang dâwn lo. Thahnem a ngaih êm avângin a martar hial pawh a ni maithei e; nimahsela, chu pawh chu hmangaihna vâng a nih si loh chuan Pathian ngaihah phûrna dik lo leh lemchanna mai a ni ang. .”—Ellen G. White, Tirhkohte Thiltih, pp. 318, 319.

“Sermon tha tak tak kan ngâithla tam hle mai. Kan mamawh ber erawh chu . . . thlarau bo mêkte hmangaihna, Pathian lalthutthlêng atang ngeia lo chhuak hmangaihna ngei chu a ni. Kristian zirtîrna dik tak chuan kan nihna pum pui a fan chhuak thîn a. Kan taksa pêng pawimawh tak tak: kan rilru, kan thinlung, kan kut leh ke te khawihin, Pathianin ding tûra min duhna hmunah nghet takin kan din theih phah thîn. Hmangaihna chuan kan ke thate a tichak a, nun kawng dik lo kan zawhna tûr lakah min vêng thîn bawk. Thlarau boral tûrte lainattu Krista hmangaihna chu Kristian nun tichaktu leh kâihruaitu ber a ni.”—Ellen G. White, Lift Him Up, p. 134.

“Krista thinlung atanga lo chhuak hmangaihna chauhin damna a thlen thei a. Chu hmangaihna neitu chauh chuan thlarau hliam a tidam thei a ni.”—Ellen G. White, Education, p. 114.

**Sawi ho tûrte:**

`Paula hian hmangaihnain a tih thin thil hrang hrangte chu a sawi kim em? A sawi kim lo a nih chuan, eng engte nge sawi hmaih a neih? A sawi hmaih nia i hriat chu han sawi la.`

`Puala’n, “Hmangaihna hi ûm zêl rawh u” (1 Kor. 14:1) tia min lo hrilh hian eng nge a sawi tum ni ang? Heta a sawi hian 1 Korinth 13:4–7-a a sawi kha a awmzia kan lo hriatthiam theih nân engtin nge min tanpui?`

`Hmangaihnain a tih thin hrang hrangte zînga eng ber hi nge i ni tin nunah i tihlan ve thin ngâi ang? In kohhranin in mamawhte chu eng eng nge ni? Paula’n hmangaihna leh thilpêk dang, entîr nân, thu hril theihna, tawng hriat loha tawng theihna leh hriatna a khâikhin chhan hi eng nge ni (1 Kor. 13:8)?`

`Paula chuan hmangaihna hi Korinth kohhran member-te zînga inpumkhatna thlen theitu tûr a nih thu a sawi a. Eng vângin nge a nih? Kan tûnlâi kohhranah pawh hian a ni ve tho em?`
