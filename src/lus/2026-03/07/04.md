---
title:  Hmangaihnain a tih ngâi lohte
date:   11/08/2026
---

`1 Korinth 13:4–7 chhiar leh la. Paula hian enga ti nge hmangaihnain a tih thin chauh ni lovin, a tih ngâi lohte pawh a sawi tel ve?`

Niminah khân hmangaihnain a tih thin thil pasarih kan sawi tawh a; vawiinah chuan a tih ngâi loh thil pariat kan sawi ve thung dâwn a ni. Hmangaihna chuan. . .

**1. A îtsîk (Grik tawngin, _zeloô_) ngâi lo.** Grik thumal, _zeloô_ hi a tha lam hawi zâwng pawhin hman theih ve tho a ni a, entîr nân, “thilpêk ropui zâwkte chu châk [_zeloô_] hle zâwk rawh u” (1 Kor. 12:31, ESV) tihah te, “thlarau lam thilpêkte pawh châk [_zeloô_] hle rawh u” (1 Kor. 14:1, ESV) tihah te leh, “thu hril hi châk [_zeloô_] hle ula” (1 Kor. 14:39, ESV) tihah te hian a tha lam hawi zâwng veka hman a ni. Nimahsela, 1 Korinth 13:4-ah hi chuan, Tirhkohte 7:9-ah pawh hman a lo nih ang khân a tha lo lam hawi zâwnga hman a ni ve thung a. Thlarau lam thilpêkte dawn châkna neih chu thil tha tak a ni ngei alâwm; mahse, chu thilpêk dawngte chu îtsîk kher tûr a ni lo. In îtsîkna chuan inthen hranna a thlen thîn si a (1 Kor. 3:3).

**2. A infak (Grik tawngin, _perpereuomai_) ngâi lo.** Grik thumal, _perpereuomai_ tihin a kawh chu chapona leh mi dangte fak hlawh nih châkna te a ni a. Hmangaihna erawh chuan mahni hma ngaihna lam a hawi ve lo. Chutiang a nihzia chu a dawt leh chiah thu-ah hian a chiang lehzual ang.

**3. A uangthung (Grik tawngin, _physioô_) ngâi lo.** Grik thumal, _physio_ hi 1 Korinth 8:1-a Paula thu sawi pawimawh tak, “Hriatna zawngin mi a uanthuantîr thîn a, hmangaihna erawh chuan mi a siam tha thîn” tihah pawh khân kan lo hmu tawh a. He thumal hian mi dangte âia pawimawh bîk nia inhriat avânga indah sâng tih a kâwk.

**4. Mâwi lo takin a che (Grik tawngin, _aschêmoneo_) ngâi lo.** Grik thumal, _aschêmoneo_ hian awmze hrang hrang a nei a. Mahse, dân tlângpuiin, khawtlâng nunin a pawmpui loh leh mahni chhia leh tha hriatna nên ngei pawha inrem lo thil zahpuiawm leh dik lo tak tak thilte ti tihna a ni thîn. Paula’n a sawisêl pawh hi Korinth kohhrana “mi chak tak takte’n” inlulung leh chapo taka “mi chak lo zâwkte” an diriam leh a hnuaichhiah thîn kha a ni ang (1 Kor. 4:10, 1 Korinth 8).

**5. Ama hma a ngâi (Grik tawngin, _zêteo_) ngâi lo.** He thu hi 1 Korinth 10:24-a a lo sawi tawh, “Tu pawhin mahni thatna tûr zawng lovin, mi dang thatna tûr zawng zâwk rawh se” tih nên khân awmze thuhmun reng a ni a. Hmangaihna chu mi dangte thatna tûr a nih dâwn phawt chuan amah a inhre chang ngâi lo (Zirlâi 5 kha en leh rawh). Chutianga mi tinin mi dangte thatna tûr an ngaihtuah theuhnaah chuan an zavâia hlâwk tlâng a thleng thîn.

**6. A thinrim mai mai (Grik tawngin, _paroxynô_) duh ngâi lo.** Grik thumal, _paroxynô_ hian awlsam taka tihthinrim mai theihte rilru puthmang a kâwk a. Hmangaihna chu a thin a ram lo, a thin a awrh lo tihna a ni.

**7. Mi thil tha lo tihte a hriatsak reng (Grik tawngin, _logizomai_) ngâi.** Heta a Grik thumal hman, _logizomai_ hian sum leh pâi hman dân vawnna, chhinchhiahna lam a kâwk a. Hmangaihna chuan mite thil sual tihte a hriatsak reng ngâi lo va, a lo chhinchhiahsak kauh kauh ngâi lo a tihna a ni. Tawngkam dangin sawi ta ila, hmangaih tih awmzia chu ngâidam tihna a ni pawh kan ti thei ang.

**8. Thil sual tihah a lâwm (Grik tawngin, _chairô_) ngâi lo.** Hmangaihna chuan mite thil sual tihte a hriatsak reng ngâi lo ngei mai tak a; chutih rualin, an thil sual tihah erawh a lâwm ngâi lo thung. Mite kan hmangaih tak tak a nih chuan, an thil sual tihah kan lâwm ngâi lo va, lâwm chu sawi loh, an lo sim theih nân tanpui kan tum thîn zâwk a ni.
