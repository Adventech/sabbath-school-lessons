### Chhiar tûrte
1 Korinth 13; Mat. 24:12; Gal. 5:22, 23; 1 Tim. 1:14; 1 Joh. 4:8.

> <p>Châng vawn</p>
> “Chutichuan, hêng pathum—rinna te, beiseina te, hmangaihna te hi a awm reng a; tin, hêngahte hian a ropui ber chu hmangaihna hi a ni” (1 Korinth 13:13).

Hmangaih chuan chuan engkim a hneh thei a. Chu vâng chu a ni ngei ang, Paula pawh hian hmangaihna chungchâng hi a lo sawi tam hle mai. A lehkhathawn hrang hrangah hian Thuthlung Thara hmangaihna thu sawi chhuahna thumal hman tlânglâwn ber, _agapaô_ tih chu vawi 135 chuang langin, chu chu Thuthlung Thar lehkhabu danga lang zawng zawng belhkhâwm zât thuak a ni a. Hei hian Korinth kohhran hnêna lehkha a thawna a thupui ber chu hmangaihna a nihzia min hrilh chiang hle.

Thuthlung Tharah hian hmangaihna chungchâng sawina châng chhinchhiah tlâk tak tak—entîr nân, Rom 8:35–39, 1 Korinth 2:9, 1 Korinth 8:3, Galatia 2:20, Kolossa 1:13, 1 Thessalonika 3:12 leh a dangte pawh a awm a. Nimahsela, 1 Korinth 13 han el pha tûr erawh pakhat mah a awm lo.

Kâr liam ta khân hmangaihna tel lo chuan eng mah hian, thlarau thilpêkte meuh pawhin tangkâina a nei lo tih kan lo sawi tawh kha a ni a. Tûn kârah hi chuan thûk zâwka 1 Korinth 13 luh chilhin, mâwi leh ropui taka hmangaihna a sawi fiah dân zir chhuah kan tum ve thung dâwn a ni.

Hmangaihna hi rilrua awm hriatna mai ni lovin, kan nun, kan thil tih leh thil sawia lan chhuahtîr tûr ngaihtuahna a ni a; chutiang a nih loh chuan engmah lo mai a ni ang.

Hmangaihna hi eng nge a nih chiah tih leh, eng thil nge a tih thin tih chu Isua nunah khân a famkimin puan chhuah a lo ni reng tawh a ni.
