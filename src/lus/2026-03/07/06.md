---
title:  Rinna, beiseina leh hmangaihna
date:   13/08/2026
---

Hmangaihna chuan a dawhthei a, ngil a nei a, a lâwm thîn a, a tuar hrâm hrâm thîn a, a ring thîn a, a beisei thîn a, tin, a tuar chhuak nge nge thîn bawk tih kan lo hre ta a, a chhan pawh Isua chu hêng zawng zawng hi a nih vâng a ni tih pawh kan sawi tawh bawk a nih kha. Hêngte hi Isuaah a awm a ni tih kan hmuh huna kan tih nghâl ngei ngei tûr chu amah ang lo nih ve tum a ni dâwn a. Chu ngei chu Paula’n Korinth khuaa mite a duhsak pawh a ni rêng bawk. Amaherawhchu, hmangaihnain a tih ngâi lo hrang hrang pariatte hi Korinth khuaa ringtu awmte’n kohhran huang chhûnga an ti thin a ni tlat mai pek a: “an inîtsîk tawn thâm mai a, mahni an infak fo va, an uangthuang êm êm a, an che mâwi lo thîn hle a, mahni hma an sial tlâng êm êm a, an thin a ram tlâng hlawm viau mai a, tin, mi dangte thil sual tih an hmu thei hle bawk. Hêng thumal hrang hrangte hi mâwi a tih êm avânga hmang a ni ngawt lo va, Korinth khuaa ringtu awmte nihna dik tak târ langtu a nih avâng zâwka hmang a ni.”—Verlyn D. Verbrugge, “1 Corinthians,” in The Expositor’s Bible Commentary: Romans–Galatians, revised edition (Grand Rapids, MI: Zondervan, 2008), p. 372.

Korinth khuaa ringtu awmte hian thil tam tak zir tûr an nei a. Chutiangin, keini pawhin zir tûr kan ngah hle mai. Paula hian hmangaihnain a tih thin thilte leh a tih ngâi loh thilte a sawi fiah hnuah hmangaihna chu chatuana awm zêl tûr a nihzia uar taka sawiin a thu sawi tlâng a kâwm ta a, hmangaihna dik tak chu keimahni nuna lan chhuahtîr theuh tûrin min fuih nghâl bawk.

Hrilhlâwknate hi kan thil mamawh a nih loh hun a la awm dâwn a; tawng thuhmun kan la hman tlâng vek hun a la thleng dâwn a; tin, mihring hriatna tawngchham tak hi Pathian hriatna thar diaiin a rawn luahlan hun pawh a la thleng bawk ang (1 Kor. 13:12). Thlarau thilpêkte hi pêk kan nih chhan hlen chhuah a nih hunah chuan kan kawl reng a tûl hran dâwn tawh lo va, chutih hunah chuan a tâwp zui ve nghâl mai ang (1 Kor. 13:10). “Hmangaihna erawh chu chatuanin a awm zêl dâwn a ni” (1 Kor. 13:8, NLT).

Chutiang bawkin, Krista lo kal leh hunah chuan rinna pawhin hmuh theihna kawng a kian tawh ang a, hmâsâng ata kan lo beisei thin chuan a tak ram a lo chang tawh ang (Rom 8:24). Chûng zawng zawng âi pawha pawimawh zâwk chu eng tikah maha hmangaihna reh ngâi lo tûr hi a ni a, chatuan chatuanin Pathiana minung pathum awmte nungchâng entîrtu a ni chhunzawm zêl dâwn a ni. Hetih rual hian, rinna leh beiseina te pawh kumkhuaa a awm ve zêlna tûr chen a awm ve tho bawk a. Rinna chu chhandamte’n chhandam an ni tih an inhriatna (Rom 4:3) a nihna ang leh, beiseina pawh lei thara lâwmna thar leh hriatna thar an la neih tûr an lo beiseina a nihna angin kumkhuain awmin a awm reng ang. Mahse, hmangaihna zawnga a ropui ber chu Pathian hmangaihna a ni a, chu hmangaihna chu chatuan chatuanin a awm zêl dâwn a ni.

Hmâi chhan ngeia kan Lalpa kan hmuh hun chu a thleng têp tawh a (1 Kor. 13:12). Chumi ni a lo thlen hma zawng chu hêng thil pathum: rinna, beiseina leh hmangaihna te hi kan nunah kan lantîr thîn tûr a ni. Hêng thil pathumte hi Thlarau Thianghlimin a luah khah nun neitute chhinchhiahna a ni a. Chu vâng chuan a ni Kristiante zîngah khân a lo sawi fo thin rêng ni. Hêng zînga a ropui ber chu hmangaihna a ni a; hei hi Pathian nihna sawi fiahna atâna hman awm chhun pawh a ni bawk (1 Joh. 4:8).

`“Pathian chu hmangaihna a ni” tih thu hi ngun takin ngaihtuah la. Eng nge ni a awmzia ni chiah le? A then a zâr chauh la hrethiam rih tho mah ila, enga ti nge he thu hi kan tân chanchin lâwmawm tak a nih?`
