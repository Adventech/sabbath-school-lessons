---
title:  Hmangaihna pawimawhzia
date:   09/08/2026
---

Kâr liam ta khân 1 Korinth 13-a kan hmuh dân angin hmangaihna chungchâng kan lo sawi tawh a. Tûn kârah hian hemi bunga Paula thil sawite hi thûk leh zuala luh chilh kan tum dâwn a ni.

`1 Korinth 13 chhiar la. Hmangaihna chungchâng min hrilh hi a tlângpui han khâikhâwm teh.`

Paula hian tawng hriat loha thil sawi theihna (1 Kor. 13:1) te, thu hril theihna te, hriatthiamna te, hriatna te, rinna te (1 Kor. 13:2) leh mi retheite tanpui duhna (1 Kor. 13:3) te hi thil tangkâi lo a ni e a ti rêng rêng lo va. Hmangaihna avânga tih a nih loh chauhin tangkâina nei lo a ni a ti a ni zâwk.

Paula’n a sawi hmangaihna hi chu tawngkam hrang hrang, entîr nân “Strawberry hi ka ngaina a nia aw” tih te, “Ka thiante ka hmangaih” tih te leh “Ka nupui/pasal fanaute ka hmangaih” tih te hmanga sawi chhuah theih a ni lo va. Lemchan (movie/film) hrang hranga kan hmuh thin hmangaihna ang lah hi a ni hek lo. Tin, he lâi thu hi inneih sermon-ah te sawi lan ve fo chu ni tho mah se, nupa inhmangaih tawnna pawh a ni chuang lo bawk.

He hmangaihna hi chu tu emaw kan hmangaihna, kan khawngaihna, lainatna leh a laka lan mâwi kan tumna ang chi mai a ni lo va. Chutih rual chuan, hêngte hian a nihna then a zâr chu an lantîr ve tho thung. He hmangaihna hi Thlarau Thianghlimin mi a khawngaih êm avânga mi a neihtîr a ni a. Dik taka sawi chuan, 1 Korinth 13-a Paula’n a sawi hmangaihna hi Thlarau Thianghlimin a thlâwna min pêk, hmangaihna, khawngaihna, thilphalna nêna thil min tihtîr thîntu a ni. Krista leh kan thenawm khawvêngte hnêna kan thil tih, kan rilru leh kan ngaihtuahna zawng zawng hlanna pawh a ni bawk.

`Matthaia 24:12 chhiar la. Isua’n vaukhânna thu min pêk chu eng nge ni?`

He lâi thuin a sawi ang hian _agapê_ hi a pawimawh mai bâkah, neih ngei ngei ngâi a ni bawk a. Krista thiltihtheihna zârah kan in chhûngkhurah te, kan kohhranah te leh kan inthenawm khawvênna kawngah te pawh hmangaihna kan tihdaih loh a tûl hle. Chumi kawngah chuan kan tâna krawsa lo thi tawh Krista hi kan entawn thei a. A thil lo tih tâk âia hmangaihna târ lanna tha zâwk leh chiang zâwk thil dang a awm thei tawh dâwn chuang em ni? Khatiang ang hmangaihna kha chu keini tân târ lan zawh rual ni lo mah se, Pathian khawngaihna azârah kan theih dân ang angin keimahni nun ngeiah hian puan chhuah kan tum ve ngei tûr a ni ang.

`Thil dang zawng âia Isua’n min hmangaihna ang kha mamawh zâwk tam tak a awm a. Chutiang mite hnêna Isua’n min hmangaihna ang kha kan lan chhuahtîr hun tûrte chu eng nge ni?`
