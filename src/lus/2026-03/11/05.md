---
title:  Pêkna rilru
date:   09/09/2026
---

`2 Korinth 8:1–5 chhiar la. Makedonia rama mite’n phal taka an thawhlâwm an thawh theih chhan hi eng nge ni ang?`

Makedonia rama mite’n phal taka thilpêk an pêk chhan ni thei âwm hrang hrangte chu:

A hmasa berin, **hlim tak chungin an thawh** (2 Kor. 8:2). Paula chuan Makedonia rama mite hlimna nasa tak leh retheih bakberha an awmna chu thilphalnaah a lo chang ta hlauh zâwk niin a sawi a (2 Kor. 8:2, ESV). A hnuah pawh Pathian chuan hlim taka petu a lawm thu a sawi leh bawk (2 Kor. 9:7, NKJV). Hemi châng hi Thuthlung Thar pum puia “hlim tak” tia an lo lehlin Grik thumal a lo lanna awm chhun a ni nghe nghe a. A tlukpui thumal dang erawh chu hmun dangah pawh kan hmu nual thung: “Mite khawngaihtu chuan lâwm takin khawngaih rawh se” (Rom 12:8, ESV). Tin, he thumal nêna inlaichîn hnâi tak thumal eng eng emaw pawh Bible ni lo lehkhabu dang hrang hrangah hman a ni bawk a, chûngte pawh chuan lâwmna leh hlimna lam a kâwk vek. 2 Korinth 9:7-a rilru hlim tak pu chunga petu tih hi mahni duh thu rênga pe, uina nei miah lova pe tihna a ni.

Pahnihnaah chuan, **phal takin an pe** (2 Kor. 8:2). Paula hian Makedonia rama mite thilphalzia a sawi hmain rethei bakberh an nih thu a sawi hmasa phawt tih lo hria ila. “Thilphalna” (Grik tawngin, haplotêtos) tih thumal hi 2 Korinth 8-ah leh 9-ah te pawh hman a ni a. “Engkima thilphal tak in lo nih theih nân kawng tinrênga tihhauhsakin in awm ang” (2 Kor. 9:11, NIV) tia a sawi hian, a sawi tum tak chu mi dangte tanpuitu kan lo nih theih nân kan thil neihte hi Pathianin min lo pe a ni tihna a ni. A hnu deuhah pawh “thilpêk pêk kawnga in thilphalzia” tih thu a sawi leh bawk a (2 Kor. 9:13, ESV). Thilpêk pêk kawnga pawk tak nihna hi Krista chanchin tha lan chhuahtîrna kawng pawimawh tak a ni a tihna a ni âwm e.

Pathumnaah chuan, **anmahni duh thu ngeiin an pe** (2 Kor. 8:3). A awmzia chu tu ma tihluihna ni lovin, anmahni rilru puak chhuak ve rêngin an pe tihna a ni a. Retheih bakberh tak ni ve tho chunga, an neih chuang liam pawh ni lo an pe hi an ngaihsânawm zual êm êm chhan pawh a ni bawk. He thu vêk hi Tita’n Korinth kohhran tlawh a duh chhan sawi nân pawh a hmang bawk a. Korinth khuaah hian ama duh thlan ngeiin a va kal a ni (2 Kor. 8:17).

Palinaah chuan, **mi dangte hnêna tanpuina pêk theih chu hamthatna nia an ngaih avângin an pe** (2 Kor. 8:4). Hetiang rilru an pu a ni tih kan hriat theih chhan chu anmahni zâwkin thawhlâwm thawh an ngên hi a ni a. “He rawngbâwlnaa tel ve hi hamthatnaah ngâiin, an thilpêkte chu dawng tûrin nasa takin min ngên zâwk a ni” (2 Kor. 8:4, NIV).

A tâwp berah chuan, **tanpuina pêk chu Pathian hnêna inhlanna pumhlûm niah ngâiin an pe** (2 Kor. 8:5). Paula chuan, “Lalpa hnênah an inhlân hmasa phawt a, tichuan, Pathian duhzâwng angin keimahni hnênah pawh an inhlân ve leh ta a ni” (2 Kor. 8:5, ESV) tiin a sawi a. Lalpa hnêna inhlanna chuan mi dangte tâna inhlan duhna thinlung min neihtîr thîn rêng a ni. Makedonia rama mite hian an rawngbâwlna chu sum leh pâia tanpuina pêkah duh tâwk mai lovin, anmahni nun hial pawh hlan an huam a. A awmzia chu, thilpêk pêk leh mi thilphal nih hi sum leh pâi theh chhuah kawngah chauh a ni chuang lo tihna a ni.
