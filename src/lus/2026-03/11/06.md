---
title:  Inpumkhatna
date:   10/09/2026
---

Judai rama ringtu rethei tak tak awmte tanpuina thilpêk thawh khâwm tûra Paula’n Korinth kohhran member-te a ngen thu kan sawi tawh a. Chumi a tih chhan pakhat chu inpumkhatna rilru an lo put phah a beisei vâng a ni. Chanchin tha hrilh hna thawhna kawngah hian tel ve se, tha thawh ve se a duh a. Jentail-te lâwi khâwmna kohhrante pawh Jerusalema Juda ringtute ang bawkin Pathian chhûngte an ni ve tho tih lantîr a duh bawk. A awmzia chu, hêng mi, tûn hmaa anmahni lo dodâl thîntute kha tûnah chuan Pathian Thuthlung Thar neitu an nihpuite an lo ni ta tihna a ni a. Kristian chhûngkaw pum pui, Juda-te leh Jentail-te chu chhuan lo awm leh zêl tûrte hnênah pawh finfiahna ropui tak a lo nih theih nân inpumkhatin awm se a duh êm êm a ni.

Thilpêk lâk khâwm hna thawk tûr hian Tita leh mi dang pahnih ruat an ni a. Pathian chuan Tita thinlungah hian thahnemngaihna leh Judai rama ringtu rethei te te-te hmangaihna a dah a (2 Kor. 8:16). Ani bâkah hian kohhranho kal tlangin Pathian chuan mi dang pahnih a thlang tel bawk (2 Kor. 8:18–23). Hêng mi pathumte hi “kohhranho tirhkoh, Krista ropuina” tia sawi an ni (2 Kor. 8:23, NKJV). “Krista ropuina” a tih hian hêng ringtu pahnihte hi emaw, anmahni tîrtu kohhrano zâwk emaw a kâwk thei ve ve a. A eng zâwk zâwk pawh chu lo ni se, thilpêk pêk hi kohhran Lu Krista laka an/kan rinawmzia kan lantîrna kawng pawimawh tak a ni (Eph. 4:15).

2 Korinth 8–9 hian thawhlâwmte chu kohhran kal tlanga Pathian mi thlante hnênah pêk tûr a ni tih min kawhhmuh a. Hêng tawngkam a hman hrang hrang: “kohhranho zawng zawng” (2 Kor. 8:18, NKJV) tih te, “kohhranhote’n an thlan” (2 Kor. 8:19, NKJV) tih te leh, “kohhranhote tirhkoh” (2 Kor. 8:23, NKJV) tih te hian chumi a nihzia chu a rawn a nemnghet bawk. Chu vâng chuan, “Kohhranho hmaah in hmangaihna lo lantîr rawh” tih thu a hrilh hi thil âwm tak a ni rêng a. Chutia an tih chuan kohhranho pawhin chhuan tlâk rêng an nihzia an lo hre thei dâwn a ni (2 Kor. 8:24, ESV).

Pathianin leia a hmanrua ni tûra a lo ruat kohhran hnêna thawhlâwm thawh hian kohhranho zîngah inpumkhatna a thlen thîn a, chu bâkah, thawhlâwm thawh hi an zînga inpumkhatna lo awm tawh sain rah a chhuah pawh a ni bawk (2 Kor. 8:13, 14). Sum leh pâi hi mi tipumkhattu ropui tak a ni thei a. Chutih rualin, Pathian ropuina tûr ngawt kan ngaihtuah a nih loh erawh chuan inthurual lohna thlentu pawh a ni zâwk daih thei a ni.

`Engtiangin nge Rom 15:26, 27 hian Paula’n kohhranhote inpumkhatna a ngaih pawimawhzia a tihlan?`

Paula hian thawhlâwm thawh leh a khawn khâwm hna thawh chu rawngbâwlna, khawngaihna thiltih, malsâwmna, chibai bûkna leh inpâwl hona niin a sawi a. Hêng zawng zawng hi thawhlâwm atanga lo chhuak vek a ni em? Ngun takin ngaihtuah ang che.

`Kan member-puite hnênah leh ram danga chanchin tha hril hna thawhna atâna thawhlâwm thawh hian engtiangin nge khawvêl puma kan kohhranah inpumkhatna a thlentîr thin?`
