---
title:  Thawhlâwm thawh chhan tûr
date:   07/09/2026
---

`2 Korinth 8:1, 5 leh 2 Korinth 9:7, 9, 13, 15 chhiar la. Hêng châng hrang hrangte’n thu pawimawh tak an sawi theuh chu eng nge ni?`

2 Korinth 8 leh 9 hi thil eng emaw pêk thu sawinain a luah khat a ni ber a: “Pathian khawngaihna chu. . . pêk an ni tawh” (2 Kor. 8:1, ESV); “Anmahni ngei . . . an inpe” (2 Kor. 8:5, ESV); “Mi tinin . . . in thinlunga in tum ang zêlin pe theuh rawh u, Pathianin a lawm chu lâwm taka petute a ni si a” (2 Kor. 9:7, NIV); “Mi retheite hnênah a pe a” (2 Kor. 9:9, ESV); “Thawhlâwm thawh kawnga in thilphalna azârah Pathian an lo châwimâwi ang” (2 Kor. 9:13, ESV); “Pathian hnênah chuan a hlutzia sawi fiah zawh loh khawp thilpêk ropui min pêk avângin lâwmthu awm rawh se” (2 Kor. 9:15, ESV). Hetiang hian 2 Korinth 8 leh 9 hi thilpêk thu sawina tawngkam nên bultan niin, chumi tawngkam vêk chuan khâr a ni a. Chu vângin, hêng bung hnihte hi rilrua thilpêk pêk duhna thinlung pu chungin kan chhiar thîn tûr a ni.

Thilpêk kan pêk thin chhan tûr pali emaw a sawi a, chûngte chu:

**Pathian khawngaihna kan chan achunga kan lâwm êm avângin** (2 Kor. 8:1; 2 Kor. 9:14, 15). 2 Korinth 8–9 hi “Pathian khawngaihna” tih tawngkam nên a intan a. Chumi hnu deuh lawkah pawh “kan Lalpa Isua Krista khawngaihna chu . . . in hre si a” tih thu a sawi leh bawk (2 Kor. 8:9, ESV). Hetah hian Pathian khawngaihna leh Krista khawngaihna chu thilpêk pêk kan chîn chhan tûr bulpui ber a ni tih hriattîr kan ni. Pathian chuan Krista min lo pêkna hmang khân kan tân thil tam tak a lo ti tawh a. A chhân lêtna atâna thilpêk kan pêk hian Pathian khawngaihna kan chang a ni tih kan lantîr thîn a ni.

“Khawngaihna” (Grik tawngin, _charis_) tih thumal pawh hi thilpêk pêk thu ang bawkin 2 Korinth 8–9-ah hian vawi eng emaw zât sawi lan a ni a. A tîr lama a lan bâkah a tâwp lamah pawh a lang leh bawk (2 Kor. 8:1; 2 Kor. 9:14, 15). He tawngkam hi kawh chi hrang hrang nei angin a hmang a, a sawi tum ber erawh mi dangte khawngaihna rilru min puttîrtu leh lâwmthu sawi nachâng min hriattîrtu kan nuna Krista khawngaihna kan chan hi a ni.

**Isua hniakhung zui châk avângin** (2 Kor. 8:9). Isua chu hausa hle mah se mi rethei tak a lo ni ta a (heta “hausa” tih hian mihringa a lo chan hma, chatuan mi a nihna kha entîrin, “rethei” tih hian mihringa a lo channa kha a entîr bawk tih lo hria ila). Hetiang a lo nih theihna chhan hi pakhat chiah a awm: a nihna zawng zawng leh a thil neih engkim a thlauh thlâk vâng a ni a. Keini pawh hian, kan thawhlâwm thawhte hmangin mi dangte’n Krista an lo hriat theih nân kawng kan sial a lo ni.

**Mi dangte hnêna Pathian malsâwmna kan dâwn pêk ve châk avângin** (2 Kor. 9:10, 11). Mi dangte hnêna tanpuina kan pêk theih chhan chu Pathian hnên ata malsâwmna kan lo dawn tawh vâng chauh a ni a. Ani chuan thilpêk kawnga thilphal tak kan nih theih nân malsâwmna hrang hrang min vûr thîn a ni.

**Pathian leh mi dangte kan hmangaih avângin** (2 Kor. 8:8, 24). Thilpêk pêk hi hmangaihna dik leh belh chian dâwl kan nei nge nei lo tih hriat theihna tehfung tha tak a ni a. Kan thinlungah hmangaihna a awm ni tih tilangtu pawimawh ber a ni bawk.

`Engtiang taka thilphal nge i nih le? Kraws êngah han inzâwt la, i pêk theih ang tâwk tûra tam i lo pe chhuak meuh em?`
