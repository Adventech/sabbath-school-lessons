Ellen G. White-i lehkhabu, Tirhkohte Thiltih bung 12, “Kohhran Thilphal” (pp. 335–345) chhiar ang che.

“Krista hmangaihnain a luah khah thinlung neitute chuan a tih dân ang kha an ti ve ang a, ani chu a retheihna avânga kan lo hausak theih nân keimahni avângin a lo rethei ta a nih kha. Sum leh pâi te, hun leh mi dangte hneh theihna bâkah, Pathian hnên ata thil pêk dang an dawn zawng zawngte chu chanchin tha hril hna tihhmasâwn zêlna atân chauh hman an duh ang. Chutiang chu kohhran hmasate pawh khân an lo ti tawh a; tûnlâi kohhranah pawh hian Thlarau Thianghlim hnathawh azârah member-te chuan khawvêl thilte an duhna an kalsan a, an mihringpuite’n chanchin tha an lo hriat ve theih nâna an sum leh pâite an thawh phal hunah an thutak puan chuan a lo ngâithlatute thinlungah nasa takin hna a thawk dâwn a ni.”—Ellen G. White, Tirhkohte Thiltih, p. 71.

“Lalpa chuan kan thawhlâwmte hi a mamawh hran rêng rêng lo va. Kan thilpêkte hmang hian amah chu kan tihausa thei hek lo. Sam ziaktu pawhin, ‘Thil zawng zawng hi i hnên atanga lo chhuak a ni a, i ta chu kan rawn pe leh mai che a ni si a’ tiin a lo sawi a nih kha. Chutih rualin, Pathian chuan kan chunga zahngaihna a lo lantîrte chu kan ngâihlu a ni tih kan tihlan nân mahni inphat meuha mi dangte hnêna tanpuina kan hlan erawh min phalsak a. Hei hi Pathian chunga kan lâwmna leh amah kan hmangaihzia kan tihlan theihna kawng awm chhun niin, chu lo kawng dang min siamsak lo.”—Ellen G. White, Enkawltute Tâna Thurâwna, pp. 18, 19.

“Pathianin mihringte hnêna thilpêk a pêk ropuizia, chumi tih chu nuam a tih êm êm dân hi a mak zâwk ngawt mai! A khûm zo awm ngâi lo tûr thilphalna rilru nên a pe a, chu pawh hel hmang mihring fate a chhandam theih nân leh a thiltum an lo hmuha, a hmangaihna an lo hriatthiam phah theih nâna tih a la ni zui bawk. I thilpêkte leh i thawhlâwmte hmang khân ‘amah chu tu pawh a ring apiang an boral loha, chatuana nunna an lo neih zâwk theihna nâna’ a Fapa mal neih chhun min lo petu tân chuan engmah ui i nei lo a ni tih i tilang ve dâwn lo’m ni?”—Ellen G. White, in The Advent Review and Sabbath Herald, May 15, 1900.

**Sawi ho tûrte:**

`2 Korinth 8:9 thu hi ngun lehzualin han ngaihtuah leh la. Enkawltu nih chungchângah hian enga ti nge Isua tih dân hi kan entawn ber tûr a nih?`

`Johana 3:16 thu hian vâna chêngte lawm zâwng chu mi dangte hnêna tanpuina pêk a ni tih min hriattîr a. Johana 15:13; Ephesi 5:2, 25; Galatia 2:19, 20; leh 1 Johana 3:16 te hi chhiar la. Hêng chângte leh Johana 3:16 thu hian eng nge inanna an neih a, hêng chângte atang hian eng zirlâi nge zir chhuah theih kan neih bawk?`

`Korinth 8–9 i chhiar atanga thilpêk pêk atanga hlâwkna awm thei i hriat chhuahte chu eng nge ni?`

`Thawhlâwm thawh bâka Isua’n mahni inphata thil a tih dang eng engte nge entawn tûr awm?`
