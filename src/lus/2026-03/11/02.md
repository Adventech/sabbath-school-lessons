---
title:  Isua tih dân
date:   06/09/2026
---

2 Korinth 8 leh 9 thute hi Judai rama ringtu rethei tak tak awmte tanpuina thawhlâwm lâk khâwm hna thawk zo tûra Puala’n Korinth kohhran member-te a fuih thu a ni ber a. Anni pawh hian chumi tih chu lo tum ve reng tho mah se (2 Kor. 8:10, 11; 2 Kor. 9:5; 1 Kor. 16:1–4), anmahni leh Paula inkârah inhriatthiam lohna eng eng emaw a lo awm tâk avângin thil a sukuk zo ta niin a lang. Chûng an inhriatthiam lohna hrang hrangte chu a ching fel ta vek a (2 Korinth 1–7); tichuan, thawhlawm lâk khâwm hna kha thar thawh leha thawk zo tawh tûrin a ngên ta a ni (2 Korinth 8–9).

A hmasa berin Makedonia rama kohhranho tih dân chu a han hrilh phawt a (2 Kor. 8:1–7), anni pawh rethei leh khawsak harsa tê tê ni ve tho hlawm mah se, an retheihna chuan “thilphalna nasa” an neihna kawng a dâlsak chuang lo tih pawh a sawi bawk (2 Kor. 8:2, ESV). Ni e, retheihna leh thilphalna hi inrem takin a kal dûn thei rêng a. Nimahsela, Makedonia rama kohhranho thilphalna ngaihsânawm tak erawh hi chu Isua’n phal taka kan tâna amah a lo inpêkna ang chiah kha a ni (2 Kor. 8:8–15).

`2 Korinth 8:9 chhiar la. He thu hian Isua thil lo tih thin dân eng nge min hrilh?`

2 Korinth 8:9-a Paula thil sawi hi Bible-a thu mak, ropui leh pawimawh ber pakhat a ni a. Paula hian Isua rawngbâwl thin dân sawiin, a tawngkam hmante erawh sum leh pâi thawh chhuah chungchâng sawina lam a ni daih mai. Hetah hian Pathian thuril tak kan hmu a. Isua’n tlanna hna a thawh dân chu chang khat leka khâikhâwm a ni a.

Thil ngaihnawm zual êm êm chu sum leh pâi chungchâng sawina tawngkam hmanga Isua hnathawh chanchin a sawi hi a ni. Ni e, Isua kha a hausa rêng alâwm. Hausa kan tihna erawh vâna lo chêng thîn a nihna kha a ni (Joh. 17:5). A vân ropuina chu a hlîp a, lungngainaa khat he khawvêlah hian rethei tak anga lo kal a thlang ta a ni. A khawsak a harsa thei êm êm a. Pathian nêna intluk ni mah se, “bâwih anga insiamin mihringte angin a lo piang a, amah leh amah a intiruak ta zâwk a” (Phil. 2:7, NASB). “Tichuan, mihring anga lo awmin, krawsa thi hial khawpin amah leh amah a lo intitlâwm ta a ni” (Phil. 2:8, ESV).

Isua khân amah nên chatuana kan chên ho theih nân a nun a lo hlân tawh a. A inhlannain a tum ber chu chhandamna mi chantîr a ni. Enkawltu nihna leh chanchin tha hril hna thawh hi thil kal dûn tlat a ni tih pawh kan sawi tawh kha. 2 Korinth 8–9 hian eng emaw bîk atâna hman tûr thawhlâwm khawn chungchâng min hrilh a; chu chu Isuaa innghat a ni tih pawh a sawi bawk. Tûn kârah hian Krista inhlanna beh chhana thawhlâwm thawhna nêna inzawmna nei thu pawimawh tak kan zir ho dâwn a ni.

`Isua pian thu, a nun, a thihna leh a thawhlehna te kha ngun takin ngaihtuah la. Khâng zawng zawng kha he khawvêl piah lama beisei i neih theih nân i tâna a lo tih vek a ni tih i hrethiam em? I hrethiam a nih chuan engtin nge i lo chhân lêt ang?`
