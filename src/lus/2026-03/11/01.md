### Chhiar tûrte
2 Kor. 8–9; Joh. 3:16; Joh. 17:5; Luka 9:58; Thup. 13:8; Rom 12:8; Rom 15:26, 27.

> <p>Châng vawn</p>
> “Kan Lalpa Isua Krista khawngaihna chu, a retheihna avânga in lo hausak theihna tûrin, hausa mah sela nangmahni avâng ngeiin a lo retheih tâk kha in hre si a” (2 Korinth 8:9).

2 Korinth 8 leh 9 hian Korinth khuaa ringtute kha Paula’n Judai rama an unaute rawng an bâwlsak theih nân remchâng a siamsak thu min hrilh a. Mi dangte tanpui leh an hnêna thilpêk hlan hi Pathianin hamthatna min pêk, Krista nungchang ang kan lantîr theihna kawng pawimawh tak a ni tih min kawhhmuh bawk. Vân mite khian mi dangte hnêna thil tha pêk leh tihsak nuam an ti hle a. He thu ropui tak hi han chhiar mah teh: “Pathianin khawvêl a hmangaih êm êm êm a, chutichuan, a Fapa mal neih chhun a pe ta a” (Joh. 3:16, NKJV).

Hei mai a ni lo, Johana 3:16 hian Pathianin a Fapa Isua min pêk chhan pawh chiang takin a lo sawi bawk a. “Amah chu tu pawh a ring apiang an boral loha, chatuana nunna a neih zâwk nân” a ti. He lâi thu-ah hian enkawltu nihna leh chanchin tha hril hna chu thil kal kawp zêl a ni tih kan hmu a. Pawisa thîr hmâi pahnihte ang deuh hian then hran theih miah loh a ni. Chu vângin, “Pathian thurûk enkawltute” (1 Kor. 4:1) tia amah leh a rawngbâwlpuite a lo sawi pawh hi thil âwm tak a ni rêng a. Keini pawh hi chu thurûk enkawltute chu kan ni ve tho tih kan hriat a pawimawh hle mai.

Tûn kârah hian enkawltu nihna leh chanchin tha hril hna thawh hi Isua rawngbâwlnaah khân a pawimawh lâi ber pahnih a nih dân kan en ho dâwn a. Enkawltu nihna leh chanchin tha hril hna thawh hi then hran theih miah loh khawpa inzawm nghet a ni. Enkawltu nihna chuan kohhran hi Pathian hna a thawh theih nân sum leh pâia tanpuina a pe a, chu hna thawktu tûr mihringte pawh a chher chhuak thîn bawk.
