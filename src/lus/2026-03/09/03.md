---
title:  Thianghlimna leh dikna
date:   24/08/2026
---

Niminah khân Paula’n Korinth khuaa mite a hmangaihzia chu a hrehawm tawrha Pathian thlamuanna a lo chan dân ang chiaha anni pawh an hrehawm tawrha a thlamuan dânah a lo lang a ni tih kan sawi tawh a (1 Kor. 1:1–11). Vawiinah hian anmahni a hmangaihna chu anmahni lama amah leh a rawngbâwlpuite’n that an chhuahna hmanga lantîr a nih dân kan sawi ve leh dâwn a ni.

`2 Korinth 2:17 leh 2 Korinth 4:2 chhiar la, chumi zawhah 2 Korinth 1:12–14 chhiar leh ang che. Paula tih tak takna hian engtin nge Korinth khuaa mite a hmangaihzia a tihlan?`

2 Korinth 1:12–14 hian he lehkhathawn pum puia a thu khel tûr chu a rawn sawi hawng a. Korinth khuaa mite hian a tirhkoh nihna an pawm chiah loh bâkah, mi rinawm tak a nih pawh a ring chiah lo a ni âwm e. Ngaihdân nghet pawh nei hran lova thle tawn vêl mai mai thîn, tirhkoh hna thawk tûr phei chuan chhawr tlâk miah lo niin an ngâi a. Chûng mite chhân lêt nân chuan an laka amah leh a thawhpuite’n nungchang dik tak an pu thîn tih uar takin a sawi ta a ni.

Paula hian amah leh a thawhpuite awm dân sawi nân thumal pahnih a hmang a, chûngte chu: “thianghlimna” tih leh “dikna” tih te hi a ni (2 Kor. 1:12). “Thianghlimna” tih thumal hi Grik thumal, haplotês atanga lo kal niin, hetah hi chuan thu sawi emaw, nun dân emawa thianghlim leh rinawm tih sawina atân hman a ni a; a tâwi zâwnga sawi chuan, rilru thianghlim pu tihna a ni mai âwm e (Eph. 6:5, Kol. 3:22). Chutiangin, “dikna” tih pawh hi Grik thumal, eilikrineia atang lo kal a ni a, hei pawh hian rilru thianghlimna leh rinawmna tih tho a kâwk.

Korinth khuaa mite hian Paula awm dân leh rawngbâwlna an ringhlel tûr a ni lo va. Anmahni lama a rilru put hmang thianghlimzia leh dikzia sawiin, chûngte chu Pathian hnên ata a dawn a ni tiin a hrilh hial nghe nghe. Chumi a nihzia chu Saptawng Bible lehlin lâr tak pakhat, New English Translation-in, “rilru thianghlim leh dik tak, Pathian hnên atanga kan neih chu” (2 Kor. 1:12) tia a lo dah hian a phawk chhuak tha khawp mai a. Hemi chângah vêk hian hêng rawngbâwltu nungchângte hi “Pathian khawngaihna zâra” pêk kan nih thu pawh a sawi bawk.

Thil nia lang chu, amah dotute hian a lehkhathawn hmasa zâwk kha dik lo takin an lo hrilh fiah pek a (2 Kor. 1:13, 14). Chu vâng chuan, kha lehkhathawn a ziah lâi khân rilru dik leh thianghlim tak a pu a ni tih a hrilh a. A thu sawi dikzia te, a rilru thianghlimzia te leh a thil tihte pawh pawm tlâk a nihzia chu “Lalpa Isua nî” a lo thlen hunah chiang taka pho lan a ni dâwn tih pawh a hre chiang êm êm bawk a ni (2 Kor. 1:14, NKJV).

`Rilru dik leh thianghlim tak i pu chunga rinhlelh leh sawichhiat i hlawh tho tum i tawng tawh em? Chu chuan mi dangte rilru leh thil tih dân lo rinhlelh leh sawi chhiat mai a pawi theihzia a hriatthiamtîr che em?`
