---
title:  Ngaihdamna leh hmangaihna
date:   26/08/2026
---

Paula hian a tum hnihna atâna Korinth tlawh ta lova, Ephesi lamah a kîr leh hnu khân “rilru na taka ziah lehkhathawn” tia hriat ni ta hi a ziak ta a (2 Kor. 2:3, 4; 2 Kor. 7:8, 12 te pawh hi en ang che).

`2 Korinth 7:5–13 chhiar la. A hnêna lehkha a thawnin nghawng a neih chu eng nge ni a, chumi chu engtin nge Paula’n a lo chhân lêt ve?`

Paula leh Tita te hi Makedonia ramah an inhmu a, Tita hnên atang chuan a lehkhathawna tawngkam hriam tak tak a hmante khân rah tha a chhuah a ni tih thu dawngin, chu chuan a tilâwm hle nghe nghe. A hma kha chuan, Korinth khuaa mi thenkhat hian amah an duh lo thîn a; tûnah erawh a lamah an tang tlat tawh niin a lang. Kan kohhran hruaitute lama tan hi thil pawimawh tak a ni a. An lama kan tan hian an rawngbâwlna kawngah kan pui tihna a ni.

`2 Korinth 2:5–11 chhiar la. He lâi thua sawi a tum tak chu eng nge ni?`

He lâi thu hi kohhran inthununna lek chungchâng a ni a. Bawhchhetu a tih hi 1 Korinth 5:1–5-a kan hmuh a pa nupui lo mutpuitu kha nge ni a, mi dang, kohhrana huhâng nei lian tak, Paula hi a thu lo sawi tawh anga awm leh lo thîn leh anmahni ngaihsak tak tak lo nia sawitute kha ni zâwk ang tih thu-ah Thuthlung Thar zirna lama mi thiamte pawh an ngaihdân a rual bîk lo va. A hma lam thute han chhiar chian hi chuan a hnuhnûng zâwk hi an nih a rinawm mah. Eng pawh chu lo ni se, he lâi thuin min hrilh chu thil sual titute chunga kohhranin hma a lâk dân tûr a ni.

Kohhran inthunnunnain a tum ber chu thil sual titu ngaihdamna leh amah kan hmangaih a ni tih lantîrna hmanga Pathian hnêna hruai kîr leh a ni tih min hrilh a (2 Kor. 2:6–8, 10). Tin, kohhran inthunna chu a lo na deuh a nih pawhin tih ngei ngâi si a ni tih pawh min kawhhmuh bawk. Mi thenkhat hi chuan—chhan tha tak an neih vâng leh, thil sual titu’n “khawngaihna” nei nia an hmuh an thlâkhlelh vâng pawh a ni maithei—sual lian leh langsâr tak titu pawh hmachhawn loh emaw, zilh loh emaw an duh zâwk thîn maithei. Chutih lâiin, mi thenkhat dang erawh chu a khawng hle a, inngaihdam mai âi chuan na deuh taka thununna lek hrâm an duh maithei thung. Thil sual titu chungah chuan thununna chelek tûr a ni ngei mai tak a; mahse, khawngaihna nêna hmachhawn thîn tûr a ni. Chu vângin, Paula pawh hian kohhran chu bawhchhetu lama an hmangaihna tinghet sauh tûrin a fuih a (2 Kor. 2:8), a chhan pawh amah hi kohhran hmangaihtu a nih miau vâng a ni (2 Kor. 2:4)

`Korinth khuaa kohhranho hian bawhchhetu chu an hmangaih thei a (2 Kor. 2:8), a chhan pawh anni hi Paula kal tlanga Pathianin a hmangaihte a nih vâng a ni. Hei hian hmangaihna chungchâng engtin nge min hrilhfiahsak?`
