---
title:  Kristaa hnehna
date:   27/08/2026
---

`2 Korinth 2:12, 13 chhiar la. An hnêna “rilru na tak chunga a ziah lehkhathawn” a ziah hnu hian khawiah nge a kal tâk? Chutah chuan eng nge ni a tih?`

Paula hi Tita a lo nghah chhhûng hian rilru nuamin a awm lo va (2 Kor. 7:5, 6). Chuti chung pawh chuan Isua chanchin sawi erawh a tâwpsan thei chuang lo (2 Kor. 2:12). A chhan pawh Isua a hmangaih êm atin ni. Hetih lâi tak hi chuan a lehkhathawn khân engtiangin nge nghawng a neih dâwn tih a la hre lo va. Chu vâng chuan, Tita nên inhmuh a, Korinth khuaa mite’n a lehkhathawn an lo dawnsawn dân chu hriat thuai a duh a ni.

Troas khuaa Paula rawngbâwlna hi hlawhtling viau mah se, “rei erawh a châm thei lo. ‘Kohhran zawng zawng a a ngaihtuahna,’ a bîk takin Korinth kohhran a ngaihtuahna chuan a rilru a delh rit êm êm a. Troas khuaah hian Tita va hmuh a, ani hnên atang chuan Korinth khuaa mite hnêna thurâwn leh zilhhauna thu a va thawnte kha engtin nge an lo dawnsawn tih hriat a duh a; nimahsela, a beisei angin thil a thleng ta tlat lo. Heti hian a sawi a, ‘Ka unaupa Tita chu ka hmuh loh avângin ka rilru a hah hle mai’ tiin. Chu vâng chuan, Troas khua hi chhuahsanin Makedonia ram lamah a kal ta daih a, chumi rama khawpui pakhat, Philippi-ah chuan Timothea nên an va inhmu ta a ni.”—Ellen G. White, Tirhkohte Thiltih, p. 323.

`2 Korinth 2:14–17 chhiar la. Timothea nên an inhmuh a, Korinth khuaa mite’n a lehkhathawn ziah an lo dawnsan dân a han hriat khân engtin nge a rilru a awm?`

Lâwm êm êmin Pathian chuan “Kristaa hnehnaah a hruai zêl thîn” (2 Kor. 2:14) tih a puang chhuak a. A va han ropui êm! Krista awmpuina kan chan hian amah hriatna chu rimtui angin hmun tinah a lo zâm darh thîn rêng a ni (2 Kor. 2:14, NKJV).

Paula hi rilru na deuh tak chunga lehkhathawn a ziah khân a beisei ang taka rah tha a chhuah tâk avângin Krista chungah a lâwm hle a. Hei hi hnehna ropui tak a ni rêng a ni (2 Kor. 7:5–9). Hetih lâi hian 2 Korinth 2:17-ah chuan tih tak zeta Krista rawngbâwltu a nihzia bawk kha a sawi nawn leh a (2 Kor. 2:17, 2 Kor. 1:12). He lâi thu atang hian, Krista rawngbâwltu rinawm takte leh rawngbâwltu derte danglamna chu, rawngbâwltu derte chuan anmahni hlâwkna atân chanchin tha an hril lâiin, Krista rawngbâwltu rinawm takte erawh chuan thinlung zawng zawnga Krista hmangaihna nên Pathian Thu an hril thîn tih kan hre thei a ni.

`I thil tih hrang hrang, a bîk takin Isua hminga i tihte kha eng ang rilru puin nge i tih thin le?`
