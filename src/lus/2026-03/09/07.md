Ellen G. White-i lehkhabu, Tirhkohte Thiltih bung 31, “Thuchah Chu Ngaihven A Ni” (phêk 323–334) chhiar ang che.

“Lungngaihna tâwk nasa apiangte hi mi dangte thlamuantu ropui ber berte an ni thei a, an kalna piangah beiseina êng chhitu a ni thîn bawk. Chutiang mite chu an hrehawm tawrhte’n chher hrimin, zirlâi pawimawh tak tak a zir chhuahtîr thîn a; buainate’n an tlâkbuak hunah pawh Pathian an rinna thlah mai lovin, a hmangaihna an vuan nghet sauh zâwk thîn. Chutiang mite chu Pathianin a mite a ngaihsakzia lantîrtute an ni.”—Ellen G. White, God’s Amazing Grace, p. 122.

“Krista tâna inserh thianghlimte nun chuan mi dangte hnênah beiseina, thlamuanna leh remna a thlen thîn a. Thianghlimna, remhriatna, dikna leh tangkâinain a thuam thîn bawk. An huhâng tithianghlimtu mahni hmasial lo hmangaihna chuan a kâihruai tlat a. Krista chên chilh nun neiin, an kalna apiangah beiseina êng an hnut chhiah zêl thîn a ni.”—God’s Amazing Grace, p. 122.

“Tirhkoh Paula hian kohhrana thil sual titute chu zilh an tûl a ni tih a hria a; nimahsela, mahni inthunun zo lo khawp chuan a ti ngâi lo. A zilh chhan chu tha takin a hrilh fiah vek thîn a. A zilhate chuan an thian tha tak a ni tih an hriat theih nân a fîmkhur êm êm thîn. Anmahni a zilhna lamah a rilru a hrehawm hle thîn a ni tih a hriatthiamtîr a. Ama duhzâwng leh anmahni duhzâwng chu thuhmun a ni tih an hriat theih nân theihtâwp a chhuah thîn.”—Ellen G. White Comments, in The SDA Bible Commentary, vol. 6, p. 1094.

**Sawi ho tûrte:**

`2 Korinth 2:1–14-ah hian Paula’n rawngbâwlna kawnga a rinawmzia leh a tih takzia a sawi a. Hetiang rilru put hi enga ti nge a pawimawh êm êm?`

`Paula’n a zin dân tûra ruahmanna a lo siam tawh a thlâk danglam leh hian rawngbâwlna kawnga fân thei nih a tûlzia engtin nge min hrilh? A tûl huna insiam rem thei leh rilru thlâk theih nih hi enga ti nge thil pawimawh tak a nih?`

`Paula hian a rawngbâwlna kawngah rilru hang châk leh han thinrim ve deuh tum pawh a nei thîn a. Hei hian kohhran hruaitute pawh mihring ve tho, mi dangte ang bawka rilru hrehawm ve thei an ni tih a tilang chiang hle. Kohhran member-te’n rawngbâwltute kan chhâwk zângkhâi theih dân kawng awmte chu eng nge ni?`

`Paula hian Kristaa hnehna a chan thu a sawi (2 Kor. 2:14) hma chiahin rilru hah taka a awm thu a sawi a (2 Kor. 2:13). Engtin nge a chak loh thu leh a chak thu hi tum khatah a sawi rual theih? Engtin nge keini pawhin kan sawi rual ve theih ang?`
