---
title:  Lâwmthu sawina
date:   23/08/2026
---

`2 Korinth 1:3–7 chhiar la. Paula lâwm êm êm chhan hi eng nge ni?`

Paula lâwm chhan chu hrehawm tuar mêkte hnêna Pathianin thlamuanna a pêk vâng a ni. He lâi thuah hian vawi sâwm zet thlamuan (Grik tawngin, _parakaleô_) tih thumal leh thlamuanna (Grik tawngin, paraklêsis) tih thumal te hman a ni a. A awmzia chu, 2 Korinth-a hêng thumalte lo lanna zawng zawng (vâwi 29) hmun thuma then hmun khat chu he lâi thua mi hi a ni tihna a ni. Pathian chu “khawngaihnate Pa leh thlamuanna zawng zawng Pathian” niin a sawi a, “kan hrehawm zawng zawnga min thlamuan thîntu a ni” (2 Kor. 1:3, 4, NKJV) a ti bawk.

Pathian hnên ata kan dawn thlamuanna chu keimahnia pum bilh mai tûr kan ni lo va (2 Kor. 1:4, 5). Pathian thlamuanna dawngtu thinlung hliamte chauhin anmahni ang bawka hrehawm tuarte hnênah thlamuanna awmze nei an pe chhâwng ve thei ang.

Paula pawh hian a hrehawm tawrhahte Pathian hnên ata thlamuanna a lo dawn tawh avângin hrehawm tuar ve tho mi dangte a tanpui thei ta a. “Hrehawm kan lo tuar a nih pawhin, nangmahni thlamuanna tûr leh chhandamna tûr a ni a. . . . Thlamuana kan awm a nih pawhin, nangmahni thlamuanna leh chhandamna tûr bawk a ni” (2 Kor. 1:6, NKJV) a ti. Hei ngei hi a ni hmangaihna chu!

`Paula’n 2 Korinth 1:8–11-a a lawmna a sawite chu eng nge ni?`

Paula hian amah leh a rawngbâwlpuite chu an thi ngei tawh dâwn nia rinna neihtîrtu hrehawm “nasa tak, tuar zawh rual loh tûr khawp” an tawrh thu a sawi a (2 Kor. 1:8). Hetih lâi hi chuan, mitthi thawhlehna bâk chu beisei theih dang an nei tawh lo niin an ngâi hman hial nghe nghe. Nimahsela, Pathianin rawn chhanchhuakin, thil awm dân pawh a lo inthlâk ta a (2 Kor. 1:10). Dam khaw chhuah inbeisei ngam tawh miah lo kha Pathianin a la chhanchhuak leh dâwn a ni tih beiseina an lo nei leh ta a ni. Hun kal tawha Pathianin hnehna a lo chan tâkte khân hun lo la awm tûrah pawh hnehna a chang leh zêl dâwn a ni tih rin ngamna min neihtîr thîn a. Pathian chuan kan hrehawm tawrhte hi amaha rinna nghah nachâng kan lo hriat theih nân hmanruaah a hmang thîn. Kan harsatna tawhte pawh hian thlarau lama mi puitling ni tûrin min chher hriam a, keimahni’n Pathian hnaih phahna atâna kan lo hman phal dân ang zêlin hna a thawk mai thîn. Paula lâwmthu sawina hian intawngtâisak tawn pawimawhzia a târ lang a, Pathian chhanchhuahna kan chan avânga kan lâwmna chuan thil a ti thei êm êm a ni tih min hrilh bawk (2 Kor. 1:11).

`Tute pawhin harsatna leh hrehawm tawrh châng kan nei theuh a, chutiang huna beiseina leh tanpuina i lo chhar chhuah dân chu han sawi teh.`
