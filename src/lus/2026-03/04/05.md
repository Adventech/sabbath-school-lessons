---
title:  Dik lo taka Mipat-hmeichhiatna hman loh
date:   22/07/2026
---

`1 Thessalonika 4:1–8 chhiar la. He lâi thu hian thianghlimna leh dik lo taka mipat-hmeichhiatna hman laka inthiarfihlîmna inzawm dân engtin nge a sawi?`

He thu hi Thessalonika khuaa mite lo chhiar atâna a ziah chu ni mah se, a thu zirtîr hi Kristian zawng zawng tâna pawimawh leh zawm ngei tûr chi a ni a.

Zawhna la awm ta fo tho erawh chu: Korinth kohhranah hian eng thil nge thleng? Enga ti nge hêng buaina hrang hrangte hi an tawh? tih hi a ni.

Korinth khuaa mi thenkhat hian chanchin thain zalênna a pêk tawh avângin eng pawh an duh apiang chu tih phalsak an ni ta niin an ngâi a. Pumpui hi ei tûrte tâna siam a ni ang chiahin taksa pawh hi mipat-hmeichhiatna hmanna atâna siam a ni a, chutiangin mipat-hmeichhiatna hmanna pawh taksa tân vêk duan a ni an ti (1 Kor. 6:13). He ngaihdân hi Paula chuan Kristiante’n an lo neih tâk zalênna dik lo taka hmanna a nih tih thu a sawi a. Mipat-hmeichhiatna hman chungchânga rinawm lohna rêng rêng chu Kristiante nihna nêna inrem lo a nih mai bâkah, chanchin tha kal tlanga kan lo chan tâk zalênna (Rom 8:2, Gal. 5:13) hman sualna pawh a ni bawk. Thil sual ti tûr ni lovin, thil sual lak ata “zalênna” chantîr tawh kan ni a (Rom 8:2; Rom 6:18, 22). Dik tak phei chuan, “taksa hi . . . Lalpa tân niin, Lalpa pawh taksa tân a ni zâwk” (1 Kor. 6:13). Krista tâ kan ni a (1 Kor. 6:15), chu kan nihna chuan kan awm dân leh thil tihah nghawng a nei ngei tûr a ni bawk. Hêng thil pahnihte hi inzawm tawn tlat a ni a. A nihzia pawh 1 Korinth 6-ah hian kawng hrang hrang pathumin târ lan a ni nghe nghe.

Pakhatnaah chuan, “Lalpa Isua hmingah leh kan Pathian Thlarauva” **tlen fâi tawh, tihthianghlim tawh** leh **thiam chantîr tawh** kan ni tih a sawi a (1 Kor. 6:11, NKJV). 1 Korinth 6, 9, 10-a sual chi hrang hrang a sawi leh 1 Korinth 6:12–20-a dem êm êm dik lo taka mipat-hmeichiatna hmanna te hian tlen fâi tawh, tihthianghlim tawh leh thiam chantîr tawhte nunah chuan hmun an chang lo vang.

Pahnihnaah chuan **Krista pêngte** kan ni tih a sawi a (1 Kor. 6:15). Chumi awmzia chu Krista nên pumkhat kan ni tûr a ni tihna a ni (1 Kor. 6:17). Dik lo taka mipat-hmeichhiatna hman hi Krista nêna pumkhat kan nihna tihchhiatna a ni a (1 Kor. 6:13, 15). Mi, a nupui emaw, a pasal emaw ni lo nêna mipat-hmeichhiatna hmang chu a hmanpuinu emaw, hmanpuipa emaw nên “pumkhat” an ni (1 Kor. 6:16). Thlarau Thianghlim zâra Krista nên pumkhat kan nihna chuan mipat-hmeichhiatna hman kawngah pawh thianghlim taka awm thei tûrin min vêng ang a, Kristian nun dân mâwi leh tha chu kan lan chhuahtîr thei ang.

Pathumnaah chuan, kan taksa hi **Thlarau Thianghlim** in a ni a (1 Kor. 6:19, 20, NIV). Mipat-hmeichhiatna hman kawnga rinawm leh thianghlim taka nun theih dân kawng awm chhun chu Thlarau Thianghlim zâra Krista nên inzawmna nghet tak neih a ni. Hmun dangah pawh Thlarau Thianghlim in nih chu “inthâwina nung, thianghlim leh Pathian lawm tlâk” ni tûra taksa hlan a ni tiin a lo sawi bawk (Rom 12:1).

`Dik lo taka mipat-hmeichhiatna hmannain mihringte chunga chhiatna a lo thlen tawh dân ngaihtuah la. Chu chuan hemi kawngah hian Kristiante chu kan invêng fîmkhur hle tûr a ni tih a hriattîr che em?`
