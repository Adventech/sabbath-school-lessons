---
title:  Nupui pasal thu leh lên tûl chungchâng
date:   23/07/2026
---

Paula’n kan taksa chu “Thlarau Thianghlim in” (1 Kor. 6:19) a nih thu a sawi hi dik lo taka mipat-hmeichhiatna hmanna laka inthiar fihlîm tûra mi a hrilhnaah a lo lang a. Thlarau Thianghlim in nih chu thianghlim taka nun theihna kawng awm chhun a ni. Kohhran chu a chheh vêla awm ring lo mite laka hrang Kristian inpâwl khâwmte an ni a. Chu chu Thlarau Thianghlim awmpuina kan chang a nih chuan thil tih theih a ni bawk.

`1 Korinth 6:19–7:9 chhiar la. He thu hian “dik lo taka mipat-hmeichhiatna hmanna hi tlânchhiatsan rawh u” (1 Kor. 6:18, ESV) tih thupêk hi a taka kan nunpui theih dân tûr engtin nge a lo sawi?`

1 Korinth 7-ah hian mipat-hmeichhiatna hman chungchânga kan hriat tûr pawimawh tak tak kan hmu a. Hemi bung hi hlâwm hnihah: (1) nupui pasal neih thu (1 Kor. 7:1–24) tiin leh (2) nupui pasal nei lova len tûl chungchâng (1 Kor. 7:25–40) tiin a then theih ang.

1 Korinth 7 hian mipat-hmeichhiatna hman chungchâng sawi hi thil pawimawh leh tûl tak a ni tih hrethiam tûrin min tanpui a. Amaherawhchu, he lâi thu kan chhiar hian Korinth kohhrana harsatna an tawh chin felna tûra a ziah a ni tih kan hre reng tûr a ni. A nih loh chuan, a thil sawi thenkhat hian inneihna, nupui pasala insiam hi a ngâisâng lo hle emaw min tihtîr daih thei dâwn a; mahse, chu chu a sawi tum a ni hauh si lo (1 Tim. 4:1–3, 1 Tim. 5:14 leh Heb. 13:4 te hi chhiar ang che).

Thil pawimawh tak kan hriat tûr chu 1 Korinth 6:18 (ESV)-a kan hmuh “dik lo taka mipat-hmeichhiatna hmanna hi tlânchhiatsan rawh u” tih thupêk hi Krista nên pumkhat nih thu (1 Kor. 6:17) leh Thlarau Thianghlim in nih thu (1 Kor. 6:19) te nên huang khatah a dah tih hi a ni a. Hêngte âia dik lo taka mipat-hmeichhiatna hmanna tlânchhiatsan dân kawng tha zâwk a awm thei dâwn em ni? Awm hauh lo.

Mipat-hmeichhiatna hi Pathianin hman tûra a siam chu ni tho mah se, nupaa insiamte inkârah chauh hman tûr a ni a. Tin, nupaa insiam tih pawh hi Bible-in a phal awm chhun hmeichhia leh mipa inneihna niin, mipat-hmeichhiatna pawh chu mite inkârah chauh chuan hman tûr a ni bawk.

Paula’n, “Dik lo taka mipat-hmeichiatna hmanna (inngaihna sual) hi tlânchhiatsan rawh u” tih thu a sawi lâi hian a rilruah Josefa kha a rawn lang pawh a ni maithei (Gen. 39:6–18). Potiphera nupuiin mutpui ngei tuma a silhfên a lo mansak pawh khân pâwn lamah a tlân chhuak nghâl daih mai a nih kha (Gen. 39:18). He thu hi Genesis 49:6–18-ah ngawt pawh vawi li âia tlêm lo sawi lan a ni a. Bible hian chiang takin a sawi lo naa a, mipat-hmeichhiatna hman chu nupui a neih hun atân chauh a khêk tih erawh a hriat theih (Gen. 41:45). Josepha hi Thlarau Thianghlima khat a ni a (Gen. 41:38); chu vâng chuan, Pathian mit hmuha thil dik ni chauh tih a duh thîn a ni.

`Kan tûnlâi khawvêlin mipat-hmeichhiatna hman chungchânga ngaihdân dik lo tak tak a neihte lak atang hian engtin nge a kohhran ang zâwng pawhin kan invên theih ang?`
