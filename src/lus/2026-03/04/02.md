---
title:  Rinna leh thil tih inrem lo
date:   19/07/2026
---

Kristian kohhran chanchin kal tawh kan thlîr kîr chuan, Pathian thu thiam (theologian) te, pastor te leh kohhran mimir te pawhin kohhran nihna leh thil tih tûr hriat tumin Thuthlung Thar lehkhabu hrang hrangte hi an lo zir nasa tawh thîn a ni tih kan hria a. Keini pawhin Tirhkohte Thiltih bua kan hmuh kohhran hi eng ang chiah nge maw a nih le tiin ngaihtuahna kan sêng fo thîn. Nimahsela, thil pawimawh tak pakhat kan hriat hmaih fo thin chu mi tu pawh hian harsatna eng emaw kan nei vek tih hi ni. Tin, Bible-in kohhran nihna ni lo tûr leh a tih loh tûr a lo sawite chu hre tûrin Thuthlung Thar lehkhabute tho hi kan chhiar thîn bawk a. Chumi atâna tha ber pakhat chu Korinth khuaa mite hnêna Paula lehkhathawn hi a ni.

`1 Korinth 5:1–13 chhiar la. He lâi thua Paula’n an thil tih thin zahpuiawm tak a sawi chhuah chu eng nge ni a, chu an thil tih chu eng vânga ngaihmawhawm êm êm nge a nih?`

“A pa nupui” (1 Kor. 5:1) tih tawngkam hian chhûngkhat hnâi lutuk inngaihna kâwkin, tlângvâl pakhat chu a nuhrawn nên an inngâizâwng niin a lang a. Hemi thu hi “Kloii chhûngte” (1 Kor. 1:11) hnên atangin a lo hria a ni maithei. Chhûngkhat hnâi lutuk inngaih hi sual râpthlâk tak, “milem be mite pawhin a ngaihtheih hauh loh” a ni a (1 Kor. 5:1, ESV). Nimahsela, Kristian hmasate zîngah khân a lo thleng mauh mai bawk si. 1 Korinth 5:1, 2-a Paula tawngkam hman hian hemi thu a lo hriata mak a tihzia leh a rilru natzia a tilang.

He an thil tih hi a zahpuiawm tehrêng nên, kohhran lah chuan pawi tih tâk aruangin, a thil sual tih lo en liam mai thei an ni chu an uanpui zâwk za mah a (1 Kor. 5:1, 2), chu chu Paula rilru tina zualtu pawh a ni bawk. Chutichuan, thil sual titu chu a zilh mai bâkah, kohhranho pawh an thurin vawn nêna inrem lo thil an tih avângin a khak hlak bawk a. Kohhran chu a nuhrawn ngei pawh lo mutpui duhtu a ngaihtheih avângin siam that ngei ngâi nia a hriat thu a hrilh ta a ni. Hetiang thil âwm ang lo lutuk titu an ngaithei leh, ngaithei nih chu a la uanpui (1 Kor. 5:2, 6) leh ta lâi hi Paula tân ngaihthiam theih a ni lo va. Hêng mite hian eng thil nge maw an tih tâk mai le?

Korinth kohhranin he mi leplêrhpa an ngaihtheih tlat mai chhan hi eng nge ni tih kan hre lo va. Kohhranin a tangkâipui thin mi hausa tak a lo ni reng pawh a ni maithei. A nih loh pawhin, he kohhranah hian “thil engkim tih a thiang a,” chu vâng chuan keini’n ngaihmawhawm tak nia kan lo ngaih pawh an ngaimawh ve lêm lo a ni maithei bawk.

A chhan chu eng pawh lo ni se, Pathian Lehkha thu-in a sawi bawhchhiatna chiang tak (Lev. 18:7, 8) pawh hmu thei lo khawp hialin an mit a del a ni tih a chiang a. Chu chu an la uanpui zui nghe nghe bawk si a ni.

`Pathian Lehkha thuin chiang taka a khap, mahse “hmangaihnaa en” tih thu leh “hrethiamtu nih” changchâwia kan lo ngaihtheih reng theih eng thilte nge awm?`
