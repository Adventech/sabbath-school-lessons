---
title:  Pathian hmangaihna
date:   22/09/2026
---

Hmâsâng milem be mite khawvêlah khân mihringte hi pathiante’n an hmangaih an ring ngâi lo va. Pathiante chu khirh tak leh mihringte chhiatna tûr zawng reng mai, tlawn deuh heuh heuh reng ngâi niin an hria a ni ber mai. Bible-a kan hmuh dân ang, Pathian chu hmangaihna a ni tih thu hi chu a rim a ra pawh an hre ngâi lo hrim hrim a. Chutiang khawvêlah chuan Paula hian kan Pathian chu “hmangaihna leh remna Pathian” tiin a rawn puang chhuak ta a ni (2 Kor. 13:11).

`2 Korinth 13:11 chhiar la. He lâi thu atanga beiseina kan lo neih theih chu eng nge ni? Hemi chângin min hrilh hi engtin nge a takin i lo chan ve theih ang?`

“Hmangaihna leh remna Pathian” tih thu hi kawng hniha hrilhfiah theih a ni. Kawng khatah chuan Pathian hi hmangaihna leh remna lo chhuahna hnâr a ni tihna a ni thei a. Kawng leh lamah chuan, hmangaihna leh remna hi Pathian nihphung ve rêng a ni tihna pawh a ni thei bawk. A khawi ve ve pawh hi a dik a, a dik zâwk thlan chhuah tum kher a tûl hran lo. Hmangaihna leh remna te chu Pathian zepui pawimawh tak a nih avângin keini mihringte hnênah pawh hmangaihna leh remna min lo pe ve a ni.

Paula hian hmun dangah pawh Pathian chu “chhelna leh thlamuanna Pathian” (Rom 15:5, ESV) ti te; “beiseina Pathian” (Rom 15:13, ESV) ti te; “remna Pathian” (Rom 15:33, Rom 16:20, 1 Kor. 14:33, Phil. 4:9, 1 Thes. 5:23) ti te; “khawngaihnate Pa” (2 Kor. 1:3) ti te leh; “thlamuanna zawng zawng Pathian” (2 Kor. 1:3) ti tein a lo sawi bawk a. Pathian chu hêng malsâwmna zawng zawng hnâr hi niin, hêngte hi a hmangaihna chuai ve ngâi lo avânga min pêk a ni.

Tin, “remna Pathian” tih tawngkam hi Bible-a tawngkam hman tlânglâwn tak ni mah se, “hmangaihna Pathian” tih tak erawh hi chu hemi chângah (2 Kor. 13:11) chiah hian a lang a; chu vângin, ngunthlûk deuha a awmzia han ngaihtuah pawh a phû rêng hle.

Bible thu hrilhfiahtu tam takte’n an lo sawi angin, Paula’n 2 Korinth 13:14-a malsâwmna thu a lo sawiah hian Pathian chu Minung hrang hrang pathumte pumkhata a awmna niin a ngâi tih a hriat theih a. “He chângah hian ‘Pathian’ tih chu Minung pathumte zînga pakhat sawina angin hmang tho mah se, a lehkhathawn danga Isua leh Thlarau Thianghlim nihna a lo sawi dânte hian he lâi thu hi Pathian pakhat, kohhran hmasate’n nihna pathum nei nia an ngaih kha a sawi niah min ngaihtîr lo thei lo ta a ni. Pathian thu zir mite’n ‘trinity’ tih tawngkam an hman tanna tak chu tlâi khaw hnu daih a ni tawh a; nimahsela, anni’n he tawngkam an chher chhuah hma kum za eng maw zâtah khân Paula chuan Pathiana Minung pathum an awm thu hi a lo sawi daih tawh a ni.”—Tom Wright, Paul for Everyone: 2 Corinthians (London: Society for Promoting Christian Knowledge, 2004), p. 148.

Keini pawh hian Pathian pakhat, Minung pathumte pumkhata an awmna, chatuan ata hmangaihnaa lo inzawm reng tawh chu kan ring a. Chu Pathian chuan mi a hmangaih êm êm bâkah, anmahni zînga inhmangaihna awm chu kan lan chhuahtîr ve theih nân inhmangaih tlâng vek tûrin min sâwm bawk a ni.
