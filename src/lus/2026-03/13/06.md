---
title:  Pathiana Minung pathum awmte
date:   24/09/2026
---

2 Korinth 13:14 kan han chhiar hian Krista chauh hi khawngaihna hnâr a ni a, Pathian chauh hi hmangaihna hnâr a ni a, tin, Thlarau Thianghlim chauh hi inpâwl hona hnâr a ni emaw kan lo ti maithei a. Mahse, chutiang chu ngaihdân dik a ni lo.

`1 Korinth 1:3, 4, 9; 1 Korinth 10:16; 2 Korinth 1:2, 12; Rom 8:35; Rom 15:30; Galatia 2:20; leh Ephesi 3:19 te hi chhiar la. Hêng chângte hian khawngaihna, hmangaihna leh awmpuina te hi Pathiana pathum awmte nêna an inzawm theuh dân engtin nge a sawi?`

Pa, Fapa leh Thlarau Thianghlim te hi kan chhandamna atân hna an thawk ho vek a. Khawngaihna, hmangaihna leh in pâwl hona te hi tu emaw ber hnên atang chauh ni lovin, an zavâia hnên atanga lo thleng zâwk a ni. Chutih rualin, chhandamna chanchinah hian chanpual leh tih tûr bîk an nei theuh bawk a. Chu chu Paula hian hriain, an hnathawh theuhte chu a lehkhathawn hrang hrangah hian a sawi fiah fo rêng a ni. Entîr nân, chhandamna ruahman chu Galatia 4:4–6-a kan hmuh tawngkam mâwi leh ril tak takte hmang hian a lo puang chhuak a, chu ruahmannaa Pathiana Minung pathum awmte’n hna chanpual an neih theuhte chu a târ lang. Pa Pathian chuan Fapa a rawn tîr a, a awmzia chu, Pa hi chhandamna ruahmanaa a bul tumtu ber a ni tihna a ni (Gal. 4:4). Fapa chu hmeichhe lakah a lo piang a tih hian mihringa Pathian a rawn channa kha sawiin, hmâsânga thutiam lo awm tawh kha (Gen. 3:15) a rawn thleng famkim ta tih a kâwk. Fapa chuan min tlan chhuakin, Setana’n dâwta a lo puh chhiat thîn Pa nên inzawmna dik min neih thartîr leh ta a. Tin, Thlarau Thianghlim chuan Pathian fate kan ni ngei tih a rawn nemnghet thîn bawk (Gal. 4:6).

Paula lehkhathawn hrang hrangah hian Trinity sawina châng tam tak a awm a. Pathiana Minung pathum awmte hian hna thawk hovin, ringtute chu rawng an bâwl theih nân an tanpui a (1 Kor. 12:4–6), thlarau lam chakna an pe a (Eph. 3:14–19), kohhran member-te zîngah inpumkhatna thlentîrin, chu inpumkhatna chu anmahni pathum nihphung (Eph. 4:4–6) pho langtu a ni nghâl bawk ang (Eph. 4:4–6). Paula ngaihdân chuan Pathianah Minung pathum a awm a ni mai lo va, chûng Minung pathumte chuan kan chhandamna atân hna an thawk ho vek bawk (Eph. 1:3, 13, 14). Ephesi khuaa mite hnêna lehkha a thawnah phei chuan Pa (Eph. 3:19), Fapa (Eph. 4:13) leh Thlarau Thianghlim (Eph. 5:18) famkimnaa kan lo khah ngei a tûl thu a sawi nghe nghe a ni.

Korinth khuaa mite hnêna a lehkhathawn pahnihna khâr nân hian thutiam mâwi êm êm mai a hmang a (2 Kor. 13:14)—vân lam mi ropui pathum, Pathiana Minung pathum awmte chuan tûnah leh chatuan thleng pawhin min awmpui zêl dâwn a ni a ti.

`Kohhran member-te zînga inpâwl hona awm hian engtin nge Pathiana Minung pathumte inpâwl hona duhawm tak chu an entîr theih ang?`
