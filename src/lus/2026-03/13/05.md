---
title:  Thlarau Thianghlim pâwlna
date:   23/09/2026
---

Isua khawngaihna chuan Pathianin mi a hmangaihzia a târ lang a ni mai lo va, chu hmangaihnain a nghawng chhuah Thlarau Thianghlim pâwlna/awmpuina pawh min chantîr thîn bawk. Chutih rual chuan, he awmpuina hi Pathian hmangaihna atanga lo chhuak ni tih kan hre tûr a ni a. Chu vângin, hmangaihna tel lo chuan Thlarau Thianghlim awmpuina chan theih a ni lo. Heti hian Paula chuan a lo ziak a: “Chutichuan, Kristaah tanpuina rêng rêng te, hmangaih thlamuanna rêng rêng te, Thlarau Thianghlim pâwlna/awmpuina rêng rêng te, lainatna leh khawngaihna rêng rêng te a awm chuan, rilru hmunkhata awmin, hmangaihna hmunkhat neiin, inthuruala rilru hmunkhata awmin, ka lâwmna tifamkim rawh u” (Phil. 2:1, 2) a ti.

Mi thenkhat chuan, Thlarau Thianghlim hi minung hrang ni lovin, Pathian huhâng emaw, Pathian tha chakna emaw ni maiin an ngâi a; mahse, chutiang ngaihdân chu a dik hauh lo. Lo dik ta ang se, Paula hian eng vângin nge minung pahnihte—Pa leh Fapa—hi minung ni ve lo, “huhâng” mai ni nên intluktlâng ni angin a sawi ang? Awmzia a awm miah dâwn lo tihna a ni. Pa leh Fapa te chu mi mal taka inlaichîn tawnna nei nia târ lan a ni a (2 Kor. 1:3, 2 Kor. 11:31), chutiang chiah chuan, Thlarau Thianghlim leh keini ringtute inlaichînna pawh hian Thlarau Thianghlim chu minung a ni ngei a ni tih hriatthiamna min neihtîr thîn a ni (Rom 8:15, 16; Joh. 14:16, 17, 26; Joh. 15:26 te pawh hi en bawk rawh).

“Thlarau Thianghlim pâwlna” (Phil. 2:1) tih thu hi kawng hniha hrilhfiah theih a ni a. Thlarau Thianghlim zâra ringtute kan inpâwl hona tihna a nih theih rualin, amah Thlarau Thianghlim nêna kan inpâwlna tihna pawh a ni thei tho bawk. Bible thu hrilhfiahtu tam tak chuan hêng hrilhfiahna pahnihte hi a dik thei ve ve niin a ngâi a. Ringtute kan inpâwl hona hi Thlarau Thianghlim nêna inpâwlna kan neih rah a ni thin.

`1 Korinth 2:10, 11; 1 Korinth 3:16; 1 Korinth 12:11; leh 2 Korinth 3:6, 17 te hi chhiar la. Korinth khuaa mite hnêna Paula’n Thlarau Thianghlim chungchâng a sawi dân chu eng nge ni?`

Paula hian Thlarau Thianghlim hnathawh chungchâng sawi tûr a nei tam khawp mai a. 1 leh 2 Korinth-ah ngawt pawh hian Thlarau Thianghlim sawina châng sawmli chuang zet a awm. Thlarau Thianghlim chuan kohhranho a siam tha a (1 Kor. 14:12), ringtute chu chanchin tha hril tûrin a tanpui a (1 Kor. 2:4, 5), Pathian thu ril tak takte min hriattîr a (1 Kor. 2:10, 11) chûngte chu a awmzia min hrilhfiahsak a (1 Kor. 2:13), keimahniah a chêng a (1 Kor. 3:16, 1 Kor. 6:19), thiam kan lo chan theih nân Krista a thawhpui a (1 Kor. 6:11), kohhran hnênah thlarau lam thilpêkte a pe a (1 Korinth 12–14), chhandama kan lo awm theih nân min chhinchhiah a (2 Kor. 1:22), kan thinlungah Pathian dân a nem kâi a (2 Kor. 3:3), Kristaah chuan nun thar min pe-in (2 Kor. 3:6) sual laka zalênna min chantîr thîn bawk a ni (2 Kor. 3:17).

`Enga ti nge Thlarau Thianghlim chu Pathian a ni tih pawm hi Pathianin min hmangaihzia a famkima kan lo hriatthiam theih nân thil pawimawh tak a nih?`
