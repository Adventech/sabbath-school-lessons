---
title:  Pathian hmangaihna
date:   21/09/2026
---

“Lalpa Isua Krista khawngaihna te, Pathian hmangaihna te, Thlarau Thianghlim pâwlna te chu in zaa hnênah awm rawh se” (2 Kor. 13:14). He thu nên hian Korinth khuaa mite hnêna lehkha a thawn pahnihna hi a titâwp a. Hetiang hian Pathiana Minung pathum awmte chu a sawi lang theuh a ni tih lo chhinchhiah ang che—Fapa, Pa, leh Thlarau Thianghlim. An pathuma hna an thawh hona azârah Pathian chu eng ang nge a nih tih leh, kan tân eng hna nge a thawh tih te kan lo hrethiam thei a ni.

`Johana 3:16, 17; Rom 8:37–39; leh 1 Johana 4:8–11 te hi chhiar la. Hêng thute’n Pathian hmangaihna chungchâng min hrilh chu eng nge ni?`

1 Johana 4:8 hian Pathian chu hmangaihna a nih thu min hrilh a. Pathian zepui pawimawh tak pakhat chu hmangaihna a ni. Johana hian kan tâna thi tûra Pathianin a Fapa neih chhun a pêknaah khân hmangaihna awmzia chu kan lo hrethiam thei a ni tih a sawi a (Joh. 3:16). Isua kha Pathianin mihringte chhanchhuah hna thawk tûra a rawn tirh niin (Joh. 3:17), chu chu chhandamna ruahmana tel ve sa rêng a ni bawk (Tirh. 3:20, 21; 1 Joh. 4:10, 14). Amah Isua pawh khân Chanchin Tha bu pali-ahte hian Pa chu amah rawn tîrtu a nih thu vawi eng emaw zât a sawi (Mat. 10:40, Marka 9:37).

Paula chuan, “Nimahsela, Pathian chuan hemiah hian mi a hmangaihzia chu a lantîr a: mi sual kan la nih lâi reng khân Krista chu kan tân a lo thi a nih kha” (Rom 5:8, NIV) tiin a sawi a. A thil sawi hi a pawimawh hle mai. Pathian hmangaihna chu nupa inkâra inlaichînna duhawm tak awmah hian târ lanin a awm a, chutiangin, nu leh pate leh an fate inkâra inlaichînna awmah te, thian tha tak inkâra inlaichînna awmah te leh inlaichînna tha dang dangah te pawh hmuh theih a ni. Tin, ama thilsiam hrang hrangahte pawh kan hmu thei bawk a. Heti hian Ellen G. White-i chuan a lo sawi: “‘Pathian chu hmangaihna a ni’ tih thu hi pangpâr lo pâr chhuak mâi kuhmûm tinah te, hlobet lo chawr chhuak hlim hlawt tinah te hian ziah kâi vek a ni. Sava mâwi tak takte chuan hlim taka thlâwk kual vêl chung hla an sa a, pângpâr no nghîl nghial, rawng mâwi tak takte pawhin boruak an tirimtui chem chem bawk a, thing lian tak takte chuan hna hring dup mai chhuahin ramngaw an luah khat hmuk mai bawk—hêng zawng zawng hian kan Pathian chu min ngaihsak êm êmtu a ni tih a lantîr a, ani chuan a fate hi hlim takin awm se min duh êm êm a ni.”—Krista Panna Kâilâwn, p. 6

Amaherawhchu, kan sualte thâwi nâna Isua a rawn tirhna kha a hmangaihna thûkzia târ lang chiang bertu a ni a. Pathian chuan mi a hmangaih êm avângin kan tâna a nun hlân tûrin a Fapa Isua ngei pawh a rawn tîr hial a ni tih kan lo hriata kan chhân lêt dân tûr chu “kan unaute tâna kan nun hlan ve” a ni” (1 Joh. 3:16).

Paula hian Korinth kohhan member-te kha inpumkhata awm tûrin a duh êm êm a. Amaherawhchu, hmangaihna tel lo chuan inpumkhatna a awm ngawt thei lo. Hei hi “hmangaihna chuan mi a siam tha thîn” tih thu a hrilh chhan pawh a ni rêng a (1 Kor. 8:1), hmangaihna tel lo chuan eng pawh hi tangkâina nei lo leh ruak mai a ni a ti bawk (1 Kor. 13:1–3). Chu vângin, kan thil tih engkim hi hmangaihna, Pathian hmangaihna atanga lo chhuak avânga tih ni zêl rawh se (1 Kor. 16:14).

`Amah Isua hi Pathian famkim leh chatuan mi lo ni lo ta se, chanchin thaah hian eng thil nge kan chân ang?`
