Ellen G. White-i lehkhabu, Chatuan Nghahfâk bung 73, “In Thinlung Mangang Suh Se” (phêk 747–769) chhiar ang che.

“Isua Krista khawngaihna chauhin lunga sak kan thinlung hi tisa anga nêm thinlungah a chantîr thei a, Pathian lamah a tinung thar leh thei bawk. . . . Mihringte hian thiam inchantîr theihna pakhat mah an nei lo va, thinlung lah an tithianghlim thei hek lo. An natna vei sual chu Tidamtu ropui thiltihtheihna chauh lo chuan a tidam thei lo va. Vân lam thilphêk ropui ber, Pa Fapa mal neih chhun, khawngaihna leh thutaka khat chauh hi boralte tlan chhuak theitu chu a ni.”—Ellen G. White, in The Signs of the Times, May 2, 1892.

“‘Pathian chu hmangaihna a ni’ 1 Johana 4:16. A nih phung mai bâkah, a dân pawh hi hmangaihna a ni a. Chutiang chu a lo ni tawh thin dân niin, a ni zêl bawk ang. ‘Mi chungnung leh chawisâna awm, chatuan mi, a kawngte pawh chatuana a awm reng tûra’ chu a danglam ngâi lo va. Amahah chuan danglamna leh herna hlim rêng rêng a awm thei hek lo.’ Isaia 57:15; Habakkuka 3:6; Jakoba 1:17. A thilsiam engkim mai hian hmangaihna tâwp ngâi lo neitu a nihzia an lantîr a. Pathian lalzia leh chungnunzia chu a thil siam mihringte hnêna malsâwmna chhiar sên loh a rawn vûr pawh hian a tilang chiang hle. . . . Sual leh tha inkâra indona nasa tak chanchin, vâna a lo intan tirh atanga helna paih thlâk hlen a nih hun tûr, sual leh a zungzâm zawng zawng pawh tihboral vek a nih hun tûr thleng pawh hian Pathian hmangaihna danglam ngâi lohzia târ lanna vek tho a ni ang.”—Ellen G. White, Thlahtu Bulte leh Zâwlneite, p. 9.

“Thlarau Thianghlim hi . . . [Pa] Pathian ang bawka minung ni ve tho a ni. . . . Thlarau Thianghlim hian minung nihna zia nei lo ni ta se, kan rilru-ah thu a rawn sawi thei dâwn lo va, tin, Pathian fate kan nih tih pawh min hrilh thei hek lo vang. Pathiana minung pakhat lo ni lo ta ang se chuan Pathian rilrua inthup thu ril tak takte chu a hre thei dâwn lo va, keini hnênah hian a puang chhuak thei lo bawk ang.”—Ellen G. White, The Faith I Live By, p. 52.

“Pathianah hian minung pathum an awm. Hêng thiltihtheihna ropui tak tak pathumte—Pa, Fapa leh Thlarau Thianghlim hmingah hian rinna nung neia Krista pawmtute chuan baptisma an chang a, tin, hêng thiltihtheihna pathumte hian vân khua leh tui thuâwih tak takte chu rawn thawhpuiin, Kristaa nun thar an lo neihna kawngah a tanpui thîn a ni.”—Ellen G. White, Evangelism, p. 615.

**Sawi ho tûrte:**

`Kristian hla lâr leh sak hlawh tak pakhat chu, “Khawngaihna Mak, Mâwi leh Duhawm” tih hi a ni. Isua khawngaihna hi eng vânga mak, mâwi leh duhawm êm êm nge ni?`

`Pathian hmangaihna thûkzia leh duhawmzia lo lan chianna êm êm pakhat chu fapa tlân bo tehkhin thu-ah khân a ni. He tehkhin thua fapa tlân bo pa hi engtin nge hmangaihna ngah tak a ni tih kan hriat theih?`

`Tual chhûng kohhrante hian “Thlarau Thianghlim pâwlna” chu a takin an chang a ni tih engtin nge an lantîr theih ang?`
