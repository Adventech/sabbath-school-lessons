### Chhiar tûrte
2 Kor. 8:9; Rom 16:20; 1 Joh. 4:8–11; 2 Kor. 13:11; Phil. 2:1, 2; Gal. 4:4–6.

> <p>Châng vawn</p>
> “Lalpa Isua Krista khawngaihna te, Pathian hmangaihna te, Thlarau Thianghlim pâwlna te chu in zaa hnênah awm rawh se” (2 Korinth 13:14).

Paula hian Korinth khuaa mite hnêna lehkha a thawn pahnihna hi a lehkhathawn pahnihtea thil pawimawh tak tak panga a sawi tawhte sawi nawn lehin a khâr a (2 Kor. 13:11). Chûngte chu:

A hmasa ber, “Lâwm takin awm rawh u” tih hian a hma lama a lo sawi tawhte kha min hriat chhuahtîr leh a.

A pahnihna, “Tihthat famkimin awm ula” tih hi Grik tawngah chuan thumal pakhat (_katartizô_) chauh niin, hemi chângah leh 1 Korinth 1:10-ahte hian hman a ni.

A pathumna, “inthlamuan tawn thîn ula” (NIV) tih hi 2 Korinth 1:3–7-ah pawh a lo sawi tawh a nih kha. Chutiang chuan, Paula hian inthlamuanna leh infuih tawnna thuin a lehkhathawn pahnihna hi bul a tan bâkah a khâr leh thlap bawk a. Pathian thlamuanna kan chan chhan pawh hi mi dangte kan thlamuan ve theih nân a ni (2 Kor. 1:4, 6).

A palina leh a pangana, “Rilru hmun khat pu ula, lungrual takin awm rawh u” (2 Kor. 13:11) tih hian inpumkhata awm tûrin min fuih a. Lâwmna, tihthatna, thlamuanna, inpumkhatna leh remna te hi Pathian, “hmangaihna leh remna Pathian” awmpuina kan chan theih nâna kan neih ngei ngei tûr niin, mihringte thinlunga Pathian hnathawh lo lan chhuahna a ni bawk (2 Kor. 13:14).

Khawngaihna, hmangaihna leh inpâwl hona te pawh hi kan tâna Pathian hnathawh rah tho a ni a. Hêng Kristiante ze ropui tak tak pathumte hian Pathian awmpuina kan lo chan theih nân boruak tha a siam thîn.
