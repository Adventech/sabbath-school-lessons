---
title:  Krista khawngaihna
date:   20/09/2026
---

2 Korinth tâwp lamah hian, a tîr lamah pawh kan lo hmuh tawh Isua khawngaihna thu bawk kha kan hmu leh a (2 Kor. 1:2, 2 Kor. 13:14). Chutiang chuan, Paula hian he a lehkhathawn hi Isua khawngaihna thu sawiin bul a tan bâkah a tâwp a lo khâr leh bawk a nih chu. Tûn kuartar tîr lama kan sawi tâk khân, Paula hian Isua chanchin a sawi bâng thei lo va, ngaihtuah lovin a awm thei hek lo.

“Kan Lalpa Isua Krista khawngaihna chu, a retheihna avânga in lo hausak theihna tûrin, hausa mah sela nangmahni avâng ngeiin a lo retheih tâk kha in hre si a” (2 Kor. 8:9).

Isua khawngaihna thûkzia hi a ngaihsânawmin, hnar phal chi a va ni lo tehlul êm! Keimahni avânga a lo retheih theih nân vâna a hausakna zawng zawngte a rawn kalsan vek a. Hmânlâi Galili ram khaw kâr kawng khu leh phut tak takte chu a lo zawh chip dep dup tawh thîn a nih kha. “Mihring anga lo awmin a inngaitlâwm a, thi khawp hiala thu zâwmin a lo awm a, krawsa thihna ngei chu” (Phil. 2:8). Kha thil kha keini kan lo “hausak” theih nân chauha ti a ni a, a awmzia chu, amah nên vânrama kan chên ho theihna tûrin tihna a ni. Sualna, thihna leh hrehawm tawrhnaa khat khawvêl chauh hre phâk keini tân hi chuan Isua tâna vân rorêlna hmun rawn kalsan leh, he leia rawn kal a, kan tâna thih a rawn thlan ngawt mai chhan hi hriatthiam a har êm êm a ni.

`Rom 16:20, Galatia 6:18, Philippi 4:23 leh 1 Thessalonika 5:28 te hi chhiar la. Hêng châng hrang hrangahte hian eng thu pawimawh tak nge i lo hmuh?`

Paula hian a lehkhathawn ziah hrang hrangah Isua khawngaihna thu a sawi tam hle thîn a. Chûng zînga pakhat chu, “Isua Krista khawngaihna avânga thilthlâwnpêk chu mi tam tak chungah vûrin a awm ngei ang” (Rom 5:15) tih hi a ni. 2 Korinth-ah pawh kan hmuh ang hian a lehkhathawn dangte pawh Isua khawngaihna thu sawia bul tanin, chumi thu vêk chuan a khâr thîn a (Rom 1:7, Rom 16:20, 1 Kor. 1:3, 1 Kor. 16:23, Gal. 1:3, Gal. 6:18, Phil. 1:2, Phil. 4:23). He thu hian a ngaihtuahna a luah hneh êm avângin Korinth khuaa mite ngaihtuahna pawh luah khat ve se a duh a ni.

Hei hi Korinth kohhran tân chauh ni lovin, kohhran zawng zawng tâna a duh a ni nghe nghe zâwk a. Ephesi khuaa mite hnêna a sawi hi lo chhiar mah teh: “Kan Lalpa Isua Krista chuai lova hmangaihtute zawng zawng hnênah khawngaihna awm rawh se” (Eph. 6:24) a ti. Paula hian hei âia nêp zâwk hian Isua hmangaih tûrin a duh ang em? Duh hauh lo vang. A duh chu Isua khawngaihnain “mi tam zâwk” a thlen a ni a (2 Kor. 4:15, NIV), ama tâna a tâwk zo ang bawkin anni tân pawh lo tâwk ve se a ti êm êm a ni (2 Kor. 12:9).

`Hetiang taka Pathianin a lo khawngaih thin dân che chu ngaihtuah la. I thil sawi tawhte leh i tih tawhte avâng khân eng thil nge dawn i phu? Chumi âia Pathian khawngaihnain a pêk tâk zâwk che chu eng nge ni?`
