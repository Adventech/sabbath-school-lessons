Ellen G. White-i lehkhabu Indona Ropui bung 40, “Pathian Mite Chhanchhuah An Ni” (phêk 635–652) chhiar ang che.

“Krista Pathianna hi ringtute’n chatuana nunna an nei a ni tih hriat chianna an lo neih theih chhan a ni a. Amah Isua pawhin, ‘Tu pawh mi ring chu thi mah sela, a nung reng ang; mi ringa nung apiang chu kumkhuain a thi lo vang. Chu chu i ring em?’ tiin a lo sawi. Hêng thute hi a lo kal leh hun thlîra a sawi a ni a. Chutih hunah chuan mi fel thi tate chu tawih thei lova kaihthawh niin, mi fel nung dama la awmte pawh thihna tem miah lovin vânah hruai chhoh an ni bawk ang. Isua’n thilmak a tih mai tûr, thlân atanga Lazara a kaihthawhna hmang hian mi fel thi ta zawng zawngte thawlehna chu a entîr dâwn a ni.”—Ellen G. White, Chatuan Nghahfâk, pp. 592, 593.

“Pathian Fapa âwin mi thianghlim muhîlte a han koh thawh chuan lei chu nasa takin a lo nghîng a. Anni chuan a kohna chu chhângin, thih theih lohna ropui tak nên inthuama lo tho chhuakin, ‘Hnehna, hnehna, thihna leh thlân laka hnehna! Aw thihna khawiah nge i tûr chu a awm a? Aw, thlân, khawiah nge i hnehna chu a awm tâk?’ ’ (1 Kor. 15:55 en la) an ti a. Tichuan, mi thianghlim nung dama la awmte leh thlân atanga lo tho lehte chuan ring takin hnehna hla do an chham rual ta thup thup mai a ni. Natna leh chhan hrang hrang vânga thlâna zalh tâk taksate chu hrisêl leh pian to pawh tha êm êmin an lo tho chhuak a. Mi thianghlim nung dama la awmte pawh, rei lo te chhûngin, mit khap kâr lekah tihdanglam an ni bawk. Tichuan, an zavâiin boruakah an Lalpa hmu tûrin hruai chhoh an ni ta a. Aw, intawh khâwmna ropui tak a va ni dâwn êm! Thihna avânga inthen tawh kan thiante nên chatuana inthen ngâi rêng rêng tawh lovin kan la inhmu khâwm leh dâwn a ni.”—Ellen G. White, Tlanna Thu, pp. 411, 412.

**Sawi ho tûrte:**

`Tho leh Isua hmutute kha ngaihtuah la (Tirh. 1:22, Tirh. 2:32, Tirh. 3:15, Tirh. 4:33, Tirh. 5:30–32). Keini, kha thil a thlen atanga kum sânghnih vêl zet liam hnua mite hi engtin nge a thawhlehna thu hretute kan lo nih ve theih ang?`

`Krista thawhlehna hi chanchin tha thuchah laimu, a pêng pawimawh êm êm a ni a (1 Kor. 15:1–4). Thawhlehna hi awm ta lo se, Krista thih thu puan rîk ngawt hi chuan tangkâina a nei lo ang (1 Kor. 15:14). Chu mai a ni lo va, Krista thihna ngei pawh kha engmah lo mai a ni bawk ang. Enga ti maw? He zawhna i chhânna hian Krista thawhlehna thiltihtheihzia engtin nge a tihlan?`

`Paula thu sawi mak danglam tak, “Mitthite kaihthawh an nih dâwn chuan hleih loh te chuan, ‘I eiin i in ang u hmiang, naktûk lawkah kan thi dâwn tho alâwm!’” (1 Kor. 15:32, NKJV) tih hi ngun takin ngaihtuah la. Eng thu nge sawi a tum?`

`In class-ah kan thih hnua kan awm zui dân chungchâng sawi ho ula. Kan thih rual rualin mi felte chu vânramah hruai chhuah ni nghâl zêl dâwn ta ila, 1 Korinth 15 thu hi enga ti nge awmze nei lo a nih ang?`
