---
title:  Krista thawh leh thu puang darhin
date:   16/08/2026
---

Paula hian Krista’n min chhandam thu uar taka sawiin 1 Korinth 15 hi a ziak tan a. Chanchin tha chu (1) Korinth khuaa mite hnênah hian a lo hril tawh a; (2) chu chu anni pawhin lâwm takin an lo dawngsawng a; (3) chutah chuan an ding a; tin, (4) chumi avâng chuan chhandam mêkin an awm bawk (1 Kor. 15:1, 2). He a thuhmaruai sawi tâwi tê hian a sawi leh zêl tûr chu a awmzia a lo hriatthiam lâwktîr a, kan chhandamna atâna Krista thawhlehna pawimawhzia pawh a târ lang nghâl bawk (Rom 10:9, 10 pawh en bawk ang che). Krista thawhlehna hi chanchin tha laimu pawimawh tak a nih avângin a tho leh a ni tih ring lotu chuan Krista a hnâwl tihna a ni.

`1 Korinth 15:1–4; Luka 24:44–47; leh Rom 1:1–4 te hi chhiar la. Hêng thute hian eng nge inan tlânna an neih?`

1 Korinth 15:1–40-ah hian Paula thuchah tlângpui khâikhâwmna kan hmu a. A tawngkam hman, “Pathian Lehkha thu ang zêl a” tih hian Thuthlung Hlui thu khawi lâi emaw, Thuthlung Hlui pum pui emaw kâwk pawh ni se a danglam chuang lo. A sawi tum chu Isua thihna leh thawhlehna khân Thuthlung Hluia kan hmuh Pathian thutiamte hi a rawn tifamkim ta tih hi a ni.

`1 Korinth 15:2, 11 chhiar la. Hêng châng pahnihte hian enga ti nge Isua thawhlehna rin leh, chumi chanchin hril chu thil kal dûn tlat ni angin a sawi? Hêng thil pahnihte inzawm dân chu eng nge ni?`

Krista chu a tho leh a ni tih puangtu zawng zawngte chuan a thawhlehna hi a taka thleng ngei a ni tih an ring hmasa phawt tûr a ni a. Chumi kawngah chuan 1 Korinth 15:5–8-a kan hmuh hi a pawimawh ta êm êm rêng a ni. He lâi thu hian tho leh Krista kha mi tam takin an hmuh thu chiang takin a sawi a, chûng a hmutute zînga eng emaw zât chu Paula’n Korinth khuaa mite lo chhiar tûr lehkhathawn a ziah lâi pawh hian an la dam hlawm nghe nghe (1 Kor. 15:6).

Paula’n a sawi awmzia tak chu hetiang deuh hi ni âwm e: Nangmahni ngeiin chûng mite chu va hmu ula, an thil hmuhte chu zâwt rawh u. Krista kha a tho leh ngei a ni tih a va han hre chiang tehrêng êm!

Hêng mite hi a tho leh a ni tih hmutute ngei an ni a. Isua’n “Chûng thil zawng zawngte chu a hretu in ni e” (Luka 24:48) a tihte kha an ni.

`Krista kha a tho leh a ni tih rin chhân tûr kan neihte chu eng nge ni? Thil dang, sakhaw lam emaw, sakhua lam ni lêm lo emaw pawh ni se, keimahni ngeiin kan hmuh ni lo rin tlat si eng engte nge kan neih?`
