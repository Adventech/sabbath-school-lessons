---
title:  Krista, rah hmasa ber chu
date:   18/08/2026
---

Krista chu lo nung leh lo ta ang se, hma lam huna beisei kan neih zawng zawng pawh inbumna mai a ni tihna a ni dâwn a (1 Kor. 15:12–19). “Nimahsela, Krista chu mitthi zîng ata kaihthawhin a awm ngei a ni” (1 Kor. 15:20, ESV). A thawhlehna hi a taka thleng ngei a ni a. Chu vâng chuan, Kristaa thi ta zawng zawngte chu a lo kal leh hunah kaihthawhin an awm vek dâwn a ni tih chiang takin kan hria (1 Kor. 15:20–23).

`1 Korinth 15:20–23 chhiar la. Isua chu “rah hmasa ber” a ni tia sawi a ni hi eng nge ni a awmzia?`

Kan chênna khawvêl hun hi taksa pûa Kristaa thi tate an thawhlehna a lo thlen hunah a tâwp ta tih chiang takin a lang dâwn a (1 Kor. 15:24; Thup. 20:5, 6). Krista chuan Adama hnuhnûng zâwk a ni angin Pa hnênah a lalram a pe lêt leh ang a, a hnênah he khawvêl rorêltu nihna chu a hlân tawh ang (1 Kor. 15:25–28). Amah Isua ngei pawh Pathian hnuaiah a indah dâwn a, hemi awmzia hi Adama leh Krista inzawmna târ lan a nih dân atang hian a hriatthiam theih âwm e. Tlanna ruahmana Adama hnuhnûng zâwk a nihna angin (1 Kor. 15:45) Pathian duhzâwng apiang ti zêl tûrin amah a inpe tawp thîn a, Adam hmasa zâwk erawh kha chuan chutiangin a ti ve thei ta lo a nih kha.

1 Korinth 15:29–34-ah hian Krista thawhlehna lo hnâwl ve ngawt âtthlâkzia bawk kha a sawi chhunzawm leh a. Baptisma hi ringtute’n Krista thihna leh a thawhlehna an tâwmpuizia entîrtu a nih avângin (Rom 6:3, 4; Kol. 2:12) a tho leh ngei a ni tih lo âwih duh loh tlat chu thil âwm ang lo tak a ni. Thil hriatthiam harsa angreng tak erawh chu “mitthite puala baptisma chang” (1 Kor. 15:29) tih awmzia hi a ni âwm e.

“A awmzia ni thei hrang hrang chhawp chhuah a ni a, ni âwm bera lang ta chu mi thenkhat, an hmangaih kal tate nên thawhlehna a thlen huna an inhmuh khâwm leh theih beiseia baptisma chan tumte chungchâng sawina a ni ang tih hi a ni. Kristaa thi tate kha an ngaihsân êm avângin anmahni anga baptisma chan ve an duh pawh a ni maithei bawk a. Chutiang a nih chuan, mitthite tâna/puala baptisma chang ni lovin, mitthite avâng zâwka chang an ni tihna a ni ang.”—Carl P. Cosaert, “1 Corinthians,” Andrews Bible Commentary: New Testament (Berrien Springs, MI: Andrews University Press, 2022), p. 1652.

Tin, mittthite kaih thawha an awm dâwn chuan si loh chuan Isua avânga thih pawh awmze awm miah lo tihna a ni dâwn bawk a (1 Kor. 15:30–32). Chutianga nun thâp lo chuan, he khawvêlah hian nuam kan tih zâwng apiang tih hmiah hmiah mai tûr a ni ang (1 Kor. 15:32).

`Paula’n 2 Timothea 1:12-a a sawi hi ngun takin ngaihtuah la. Nakîna thil lo la thleng tûrte a hriat chian êm êm theih chhan hi eng nge ni? Engtin nge keini pawhin kan hriat chian ve theih ang?`
