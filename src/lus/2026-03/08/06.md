---
title:  Thihna chunga hnehna famkim
date:   20/08/2026
---

`1 Korinth 15:54–57 chhiar la. He lâi thu hian thihna chungah hnehna famkim kan la chang dâwn a ni tih engtin nge min hrilh?`

1 Korinth 15-a paragraph hnuhnûng ber hi thu mak danglam deuh hlek, “Tisa leh thisen hian Pathian ram chu a luah thei lo vang” tih nên bul a tan a (1 Kor. 15:50). Mi tam tak chuan he thu hi tan chhana hmangin, vânah chuan taksa tel lovin kan la awm dâwn tih Paula pawhin a sawi alâwm an ti thîn. Nimahsela, a hma lam thute hi han en chiang ila, chutiang chu Paula sawi tum a ni hauh lo tih a hriat theih a. 1 Korinth 15:50 thu inchheh dân atang hian “tisa leh thisen” tih chu—“Pathian ram” tih leh “tawih thei lo” tih pawh thil thuhmun reng a ni ang bawkin—“tawih thei” tih nên inang reng a ni tih kan hrethiam mai âwm e. 1 Korinth 15:42–49-ah pawh a lo sawi tawh angin, Paula hian tûna kan put mêk taksa (kan ruang pawh a ni bawk ang chu) leh tho leh taksa a khâikhin tawn vêl a. Thlâna phûm taksa chu chhe thei leh boral thei a nih lâiin, tho leh taska erawh chhe thei lo leh boral thei lo a ni ve thung (1 Kor. 15:50, 53, 54). Heti hian han sawi ila, kan taksa hian vânram a chan theih nân a pumhlûma siam thatna chan phawt a mamawh a tihna a ni.

A tâwi zâwnga sawi chuan, Paula hian chhe thei tih leh thi thei tih tawngkamte hi kan nihna sual kâwkin a hmang a. Juda-te thuziak hrang hrangah pawh “tisa leh thisen” tih tawngkam hi suala tlu tawh mihring taksa sawina atân hman thin a lo ni ve tho mai. Chu tak chu Isua Krista lo kal leh huna siam that a ngaih chhan pawh a ni rêng bawk a, a bawlhhlawhna zawng zawng hi tlen fâi vek a la ni ang.

Kan nihphung sual hi lâk kian a nih a (1 Kor. 15:54), tihropuina kan chan hunah (1 Kor. 15:54) chauh “Thihna chu hnehnaa lem zawh a ni ta” (1 Kor. 15:54, NKJV) tih thu hi lo thleng famkim tawh dâwn a. Tichuan, he hla huaisâr leh tuang chhah tak, “Aw thihna, khawihah nge i tûr chu a awm a? Aw thlân, khawihah nge i hnehna chu a awm tâk?” (1 Kor. 15:55, NKJV) tih hi sak a ni tawh ang. Hêng zawng zawng hi Krista lo kal leh huna thleng vek tûr a ni (1 Kor. 15:51, 52).

Hei hi han ngaihtuah teh: Kan hnuk a chhah rualin kan mit kan chhîng nghâl a, chumi hnua kan thil hriat leh hmasak ber tûr chu Isua lo kal lehna, thlân atanga mi a kaihthawh hun tûr a ni. Ringtute chu kum za tam tak liam taa thi tawh pawh ni se, “tâwtawrâwt hnuhnûng ber a lo rîk hunah chuan, rei lo tê-ah, mit khap kâr lek chhûngin” kaih thawh an ni ang. “Tâwtawrâwt chu a ri ngei dâwn si a; chutichuan mitthite chu tawih thei tawh lo tûra kaihthawh an ni ang a, keini [nung dama lo la awmte] pawh hi tihdanglamin kan awm ve nghâl dâwn a ni” (1 Kor. 15:52, NKJV).

`Mihring nun tâwizia leh liam chakzia hi hre lo kan awm âwm lo ve. Hetiang chiah hian Isua lo kal lehna pawh kan rin âia hmain a lo thleng mai dâwn a. A lo kal huna kan rilrua lo awm hmasa ber tûr chu, “Lalpa, i lo va kal hma êm êm ve!” tih a ni ang. Hei hian a lo kal hun “tihkhawtlâi” nia lang pawh hi hrethiam tûrin engtin nge min tanpui?`
