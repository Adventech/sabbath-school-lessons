---
title:  Chanchin tha avânga hrehawm a tawrhte
date:   16/09/2026
---

Setana tirhkohte tia zirtîrtu derte a sawi hnu hian, Korinth khuaa ringtu awmte’n zirtîrtu derte thu sawi lo bengkhawn chu thil âtthlâk tak a ni tih an lo hriat theih nân mi â-te tih âwm tâwk nia ngaih chu tiin a han infak ve deuh ta deuh hlak a (2 Kor. 11:16–21). Zirtîrtu derte mai mai pawh an ngâisâng a nih chuan ani Paula phei hi chuan ngaihsân lehzual a phu hle tûr a ni ang. Chanchin tha avânga hrehawm a tawrh zawhzâite pawh hian Krista rawngbâwltu rinawm tak a nihzia a tilang chiang hle (2 Kor. 11:22, 23).

`2 Korinth 11:22–28 chhiar la. Heta Paula’n a sawi hi eng nge ni?`

Paula hi zirtîrtu derte ang bawka Hebrai mi, Abrahama thlah ve tho (2 Kor. 11:22) ni mah se, Krista tâna rawng a bâwl dânah erawh anni tluk phâk rual a ni lo (2 Kor. 11:23). “Krista rawngbâwltute an ni maw?” tiin zawhna a han siam a. Chu zawhna chu ama’n chhângin, “[Anmahni âiin] ka ni zâwk mah” a ti. Anni âiin a thawk rim zâwk hle a; anni âiin tân inah a awm ngun zâwk hle a; tin, anni âiin vuak a tuar tam zâwk hle bawk.

Hêngte chauh hi a thil tawrh a la ni lo leh ta nghâl a. Hrehawm chi hrang hrang a tawrh zîngah hian tum nga ngawt mai hruihruala vawi sawmthum leh pakua vuak (2 Kor. 11:24) te, tianga vuak te, lunga den te, lawng chhiatna tawh te, zan khat leh ni khat chhûng tuifinriata awm (2 Kor. 11:25) te, zin kualna lama thil hlauhawm tak tak tawn te, luia thil hlauhawm tawn te, suamhmangte mai bâkah, ama chipuite ngei laka hlauhawm tawn te, Jentail-te laka hlauhawm tawh te, khawpuia hlauhawm a tawh te, ram palailênga hlauhawm tawh te, tuipuia hlauhawm tawh te, ringtu derte lak hlauhawm tawh (2 Kor. 11:26) te, thawh hahna leh thawh rimna te, zâna mutmu pawh tuah hleih theih loh tum te, riltâm, tuihâl, ei tûr tlâkchham, khawsik leh saruaka awm te pawh a tel (2 Kor. 11:27). Hêngte hi a la hrehawm tâwk lo emaw ni dâwn tehrêng ni, kohhranho a ngaihtuahna khân a delh rit êm êm reng bawk a, chu chuan rilru hahdam tak pawhin a awmtîr thei thîn lo (2 Kor. 11:28).

Hetiang zozâi hi chu Krista rawngbâwltu dik takte chauhin a tuar peih dâwn a. A hrehawm tawrhte hi uanpui duh ni ta se, sawi tûr a ngah ngawt ang le. Amaherawhchu, a thu dawt leh chiahah hian a chhuan chu Krista tâna thil a tihte ni lovin, ama tâna Krista’n a lo tihte zâwk a ni tih a sawi a. Pathian thiltihtheihna chu mihringte chak lohnaah hian chiang takin târ lan a ni thîn tih a hre chiang hle (2 Kor. 12:9, 10). Khatia Pathianin a tisaa hlîng a lo dah khân (2 Kor. 12:7) hlawhtlinna a chante a uanpuina tûr lakah a vêng reng a lo ni zâwk a. Kha khân inngâitlâwm takin a awmtîr a, a chak lohna a hriattîr a, Pathian thiltihtheihnaa innghahtîrin, Pathian khawngaihna leh lainatna dawng nasa deuh deuh thei tûrin a lo buatsaih reng a lo ni.

`Chanchin tha avângin hrehawm i lo tuar ve tawh em? Chu i hrehawm tawrh atang chuan eng thil nge i zir chhuah? Paula’n a hrehawm tawrhte a dawnsawn dân hian i hrehawm tawrhte i dawnsawn dân tûrah engtin nge a tanpui theih ang che?`
