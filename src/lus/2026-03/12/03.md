---
title:  Lalpa chauh chhuang
date:   14/09/2026
---

Nimin lamah khân Paula leh a rawngbâwlpuite’n an rawngbâwlna kha thlarau lam indona nia an ngaih thu leh, chu indonaah chuan Pathian râlthuamte an hman thu an sawi dân kan lo hmu tawh a nih kha. Vawiinah chuan zirtîrtu derte’n mihringte tih dân anga thil an tih thin thu kan en ho ve leh thung dâwn a. An thiam thilte an chhuanzia pawh kan hmu bawk ang. Chutih lâiin, Paula hi chuan Lalpa chiah a chhuang thîn a. “A chhuang apiangin Lalpa chu chhuang rawh se” (2 Kor. 10:17) tiin a sawi hial.

`2 Korinth 10:13–17 chhiar la. Rawngbâwltute zînga inelna rilru awm hian engtiangin nge chanchin tha hril kawngah harsatna a siam thin?`

Paula’n mahni infakna tawngkam a hmang ve khauh hian hmân ata tawh mi tam tak a tihrilhhai deuh hlek thîn a. Nimahsela, hmânlâi khawvêlah khân mahni han infak vêl hi thil pângngâi taka ngaih niin, an thu sawi lo ngâithlatute tâna tawngkam huathlala an chhâk chhuah loh nân erawh an fîmkhur thîn hle thung. An khawtlâng nun khân pawm theih chin leh pawm theih loh chin a nei vek a, chûngte chu Paula hian a hriat chian bâkah, a zâwm tha hle bawk. Chu bâkah, amah a infak dân leh zirtîrtu derte an infak dân kha a inang lo hle tih chiang takin a sawi bawk a. Ani chhuan chu amah ni lovin, Lalpa zâwk a ni (2 Kor. 10:17). He thu hi Thuthlung Hlui atanga a lâk chhuah a ni nghe nghe a: “A chhuang apiang erawh chuan hei hi chhuang rawh se, kei LALPA hi ngilneihna te, rêldikna te, felna leia hmang thîntu chu ka ni tih mi hriat a, a hriatthiamna chu” (Jer. 9:24). Thil eng pawh ti ila, kan thil tih chhan ber ni zêl tûr chu Krista—Krista hmangaihna, dikna leh felna a ni tih lantîr a tum a ni.

Tawngkam dangin sawi ta ila, Paula’n a chhuan hi chu Kristaa Pathianin thil a lo hlen chhuah tâkte kha a ni a. Chu vângin, a chhuan hi Bible thu nêna inrem leh mi dangte tân pawha huatthlala ni miah lo tûr a ni. Chutih lâiin, amah dodâltute erawh hi chu mi dangte nên inkhâikhin ta rêng rênga thil ti thîn niin, inerna rilru an pu deuh rân thîn bawk a. Paula chuan hetiang hi thil âtthlâk tak niin a ngâi (2 Kor. 10:12).

2 Korinth 10:14–16-ah hian a rawngbâwlnain a tinzâwn ber chu Korinth khuaah leh hmun hrang hranga chanchin tha hril a ni tih a sawi a. Isua a hmangaihna chuan Krista thihna leh thawhlehna azâra kan lo chan tâk chhandamna chanchin tha hi sawi lo thei lovah a siam a ni.

Korinth khuaa awm zirtîrtu der, anmahni infak vak vak thînte ang lo takin Paula hi chu Pathianin a fak leh a rawngbâwlna pawh a pawmsak thlap a ni a (2 Kor. 10:12, 18). “Pathian duh zâwng anga Isua Krista tirhkoh ni tûra koh” (1 Kor. 1:1, NKJV) niin, he kohna a dawnah hian a thih ni thlengin a rinawm tluan ta parh bawk (2 Tim. 4:7).

`2 Korinth 10:12–18 hi chhiar leh la. Kohhran hruaitute mai bâkah, member-te pawh hian engtin nge inelna rilru kan put loh nân kan lo invên ang? Thil pawimawh ni lêm lo lo buaipui chiam mai hi enga ti nge thil awl tak a nih thin?`
