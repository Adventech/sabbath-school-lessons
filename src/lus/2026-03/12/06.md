---
title:  Sim lote hnêna ngenna
date:   17/09/2026
---

2 Korinth 12:14–13:10-ah hian Paula’n a tum thumna atâna anmahni tlawh leh a tum thu a hrilh tih kan hmu a (2 Kor. 12:14, 2 Kor. 13:1). Zirtîrtu derte âiin a hnuaihnung bîk chuang lo tih pawh chiang taka lo sawi tawhin, tûnah chuan an hnêna va zin leh a, kohhran member-te zînga an thil sual tih sim lote din thar leh tûrin a inring tâwk ta hle mai. Dik taka sawi chuan, chumi ti tûra zin tum pawh chu a ni rêng bawk a. Paula’n thil a tih leh a sawite rêng rêng hi kohhran siam thatna tûr a ni zêl thîn (2 Kor. 12:19).

`2 Korinth 12:20, 21 chhiar la. Korinth kohhran thlarau lam dinhmun tiderthâwng thei sualte chu eng nge ni?`

2 Korinth 12:20, 21-a sual chi hrang hrang a sawite hi Paula lehkhathawn danga sual kan hmuhte nên pawh a inang hlawm hle mai (Rom 1:29–31, Gal. 5:19–21). A hmasa pahnihte hi 1 Korinth 3:3-ah pawh a lo lang tawh a, Korinth kohhran member-te zîngah inîtsîkna leh inhnialna a awm thin thu a sawi a nih kha. A vawi thumna atâna a va tlawh leh pawh hian thil a lo la danglam vak chuan loh a hlau ru angreng hle a. “Ka lo kal hunah hmuh che u ka duh dân angin ka rawn hmuh loh che u ka hlau a. Chutiang bawkin, kei pawh hi hmuh min duh angin min lo hmu lo mai ang tih ka hlau êm êm bawk a ni” (2 Kor. 12:20, ESV) a ti. A awmzia chu, an hnêna a va kal hunah hian “Krista zâidamna leh nunnêmna nên” (2 Kor. 10:1) a zilh dâwn lo va, thu âwih lohna zawng zawng chu hrem zêl tûrin a inpeih sa reng zâwk tihna a ni (2 Kor. 10:6, ESV).

A ngaihmawh ber mai chu kohhran member-te zînga thil bawlhhlawh tak tak titute, dik lo taka mipat-hmeichhiatna hmangtute leh mi hur te kha niin, chûng mite chuan an sual an sim loh mawlh a hlau a (2 Kor. 12:21, ESV). Hêng ang suahsualnate hi kohhrana inthen hranna thlen thîntu pawh a ni rêng thîn.

Paula rilru luahtu dang leh chu thil sual titute siam that nâna kohhran inthununna chelek dân tûr chungchâng a ni (2 Kor. 13:1–4). Chak lohna hi sual kawng zawh phahna atâna chhuanlamah hman theih a ni lo va. Hnehna nun nei duhtute tân chuan thiltihtheihna a awm reng zâwk a ni (2 Kor. 13:4). Korinth kohhran member thenkhatte’n dik lo taka mipat-hmeichhiatna an lo hmang thîn pawh hi an nuna Pathian thiltihtheihna a awm tak tak lohzia tilangtu mai a ni a. An sualte chu sim a, thuâwihna kawnga hruaitu tûr thiltihtheihna chu lo chang ve tûrin a duhsak êm êm a ni. Anmahni zilh leh, an chunga thununna lek hi a thil châk zâwng a ni rêng rêng lo va. “Thil sual rêng rêng in tih loh a, . . . thil dik leh mâwi in tih zâwk theih nân Pathian hnênah kan dîl thîn a. . . . Kan dîl ber chu ngâi in lo awh leh a ni” (2 Kor. 13:7–9, ESV) tiin a sawi. Tawngtâi thu chham mâwi tak a va ni êm! Rinnaah chuan an awm em tih an hriat theih nân anmahni inen fiah fo tûrin a chah bawk.

`2 Korinth 13:5 chhiar la. Rinnaa awm tih chu eng nge ni a awmzia? Rinnaah chuan i awm a ni tih engtin nge in hriat theih ang?`
