---
title:  Thlarau lam indona
date:   13/09/2026
---

`2 Korinth 10:1–11 chhiar la. Paula’n Korinth khuaa mite a dâwrna kawnga a inngaihtlâwmna hi a chak lohnaa lo ngaihsak mai tâwk an awm thîn a. Mahse, he lâi thua a tawngkam hmante hian engtiangin nge Korinth kohhran member-te tibuaitu zirtîrtu derte a hmachhawn dânah khân a huaisenzia a tihlan?`

2 Korinth 10 bultan nâna a tawngkam hman hi a mi mal thil angreng khawp mai: “Tûnah kei Paula hian mi mal takin ka ngên tak meuh meuh a che u” (2 Kor. 10:1, HCSB) a ti a. He a tawngkam hman hian kohhrana kua rawn hreuh mêk zirtîrtu derte chu a lo ngaimawh êm êm a ni tih a tilang. 2 Korinth 10:1-a a tawngkam hman hian amah dodâltute’n râlkhat atanga lehkha a thawn lâi chuan huaisen êm êm, hmâichhan ngeia han inchhân râlah erawh chuan dâwizep leh êm êm bawk si nia an puhna kha mei nei deuh takin a chhâng lêt a (2 Kor. 10:10, 11). A chak lohna nia lang hi zâwldawhna thiltithei tak leh Krista zâidamna ang kha a ni zâwk tih a hriattîr ve ta ngat a ni.

Zirtîrtu derte chu huaisen tak leh mahni inrintâwkna nên, Krista zâidamna ang kha nei chung bawk sia hmachhawn tûr a ni a (2 Kor. 10:1). Isua khân tum khat chu, “Thuhnuairawlh leh rilrua inngâitlâwm tak ka nih hi” (Mat. 11:29) tiin a sawi ve tho mai. Amaherawhchu, Pathian biak ina pawisa thlengtute kha huaisen taka hmachhawnin, an pawisa thlengna dawhkânte chu a nam thlûksak a, “rûkruho” tiin a hau hrep mai a nih kha (Mat. 21:12, 13).Tin, Pharisaite pawh hmâichhan ngeiah chapchârin, mi vervêkho tiin a ko phiar phiar mai a, thlânlung hnawih vâr mai mai ang lek chauh a nih thu pawh a hrilh bawk (Mat. 23:23–27). Isua ang khân, Paula pawh hian thlarau lam indona bei mêk kan ni tih a hria a; chu vâng chuan, Pathian râlthuam famkima inthuam tûrin thu min râwn rêng a ni (Eph. 6:12–17).

Paula 2 Korinth 10-a tawngkam a hman hi sipaihovin an hman thin tawngkama ni a, hetiang tawngkam a hman chhan chu boral mai hlauhawm dinhmunah kan ding nia a hriat vâng a ni (2 Kor. 10:3–6). He indona hi mihring inbeihna mai a ni lo va, Krista hnêna mi tam tak hruai thlen an nih theihna tûra Pathian indona a ni zâwk. Chutiang chu a nih avângin hnialkhân dik lo leh ngaihdân ropui tak takte chu Pathian Thu behchhana hmachhawn leh chhân thlâk vek a tûl a. Chutichuan, ngaihtuahna tinrêng hi Krista thu âwih salah hruai an lo ni thei dâwn a ni (2 Kor. 10:5).

He thlarau lam indonaah hian Paula hi Krista thuneihna chantîr a ni a. Amaherawhchu, he thuneihna hi mite tihboral nân ni lovin, mite siam thatna atân zâwk a hmang ang (2 Kor. 10:8). Thlarau lama hruaitute tân hian Pathian thuneihnaa thil ti nia inchhâl mai a awl thîn hle a. Hetih lâi hian, an thuneihna chu Krista’n a pêk a ni tih hriain, amah Krista ang khân zâwldawh tak leh rilrua inngâitlâwm tak chungin thil an ti thîn tûr a ni. Paula’n Krista’n a hnênah thuneihna a hlân nia sawi chhan hi Korinth khuaa ringtu awmte’n zirtîrtu derte thu leh hla an lo bengkhawn riau nia a hriat vâng a ni a. Chu chu an tâwpsan thuai a nih loh chuan Krista an la phatsan nge nge dâwn tih a hmu tlang vek bawk.

`Zirtîrtu derte kan hmachhawn hunah engtin nge zâwidawh tak, huaisen êm êm bawk si kan nih theih ang? Enga ti nge chumi kan nih kawp chu a pawimawh?`
