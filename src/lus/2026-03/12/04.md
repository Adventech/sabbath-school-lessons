---
title:  Zirtîrtu derte chu
date:   15/09/2026
---

Thuthlung Thar lehkhabu hrang hrangah hian Kristiante zîngah ngei pawh zirtîrtu derte an awm theih thu leh, chûng mite lakah chuan fîmkhur a tûl thu pawh kan hmu hnem angreng khawp mai. Isua ngei pawhin hemi chungchâng hi a zirtîrte hnênah khân a lo sawi a (Mat. 7:15–20). Chutiangin, tirhkohte pawh khân ngaihven ngun hle tûrin min chah lawm lawm bawk (Gal. 1:6–9, 1 Tim. 6:3–5, 2 Pet. 2:1–3).

`2 Korinth 11:1–15 chhiar la. Hêng zirtîrtu derte nêna harsatna an neihte chu engtin nge a sawi chhuah?`

Paula hian zirtîrtu derte’n a rûka an tih thin chu mite hriat theih tûrin a sawi chhuak a. Chutih rual chuan, ama rawngbâwlna erawh a pawimawh bera Krista dahna a ni tih pawh a hriattîr nghâl bawk. Korinth khuaa kohhranho hi mo nêna tehkhinin, amah chu an pa, Krista hnêna mo (chu chu kohhranho) petu tûr niin a intehkhin bawk a (2 Kor. 11:2). Hetianga a tehkhin chhan hi anmahni a hmangaih êm vâng a ni (2 Kor. 11:11). Anmahni chhawmdâwla awm thei hi a ni reng a; mahse, an tân phurrit niha, anmahni tihhautak chu a duh miah lo thung (2 Kor. 11:7–12).

Hetih lâi hian, “tirhkoh challang leh hmingthang ber berte” (zirtîrtu derte a sawina a ni maithei) erawh chu Evi bumtu rûl nên khân a tehkhin a (2 Kor. 11:3). Eden huana Evi bumtu Setana ang khân Korinth khuaa zirtîrtu der awmte pawh hi mi bum chîng leh dik lo taka thil ti thîn niin a sawi (2 Kor. 11:3, 4). Hêng zirtîrtu derte hian Korinth khuaa ringtu awmte chu Krista hnên ata an hruai bo daih mai a hlau hle a ni.

Hêng kohhrana rawn inzep lût luite hian Paula’n a hril ang ni lo thu dang an hril a—mite hnênah Isua dang leh chanchin tha dang an zirtîr thîn (2 Kor. 11:4). Hei hian Isua chanchin hriltu nazawng hi Pathianin a tirh, Pathianin a hmante an ni kher chuang lo thei tih a tilang chiang hle a. Hemi chungchângah hian Isua ngei pawh khân he thu hi a lo sawi a nih kha: “‘Lalpa, Lalpa’ mi ti nazawng chu vânram an lût lo vang a; ka Pa vâna mi duhzâwng titute erawh chu an lût ang?” (Mat. 7:21) tiin. Galatia 1:6–9-ah chuan chanchin tha dang hriltu chuan ama chungah ânchhia a chhêk khâwl thu a lo sawi a; nimahsela, vânduaithlâk takin, Korinth khuaa kohhran member thenkhat hi chuan chutiang chanchin tha dang hriltute chu an lo ngaithei tlat mai si a ni.

Paula hian zirtîrtu derte nihna pho langin, chutiang mite chu “tirhkoh der, hnathawktu ni ve si, mahse mi bum hmang tak, Krista tirhkoha lo insiam chawp ve ngawtte” an nih thu a sawi a (2 Kor. 11:13, NKJV). Hêng mite hian êng tirhkoha Setana a insiam chawp ang chiah khân Krista tirhkohah insiam chawpin, Setana rawngbâwltu ni reng si kha felnaa rawngbâwltuah an lo inchhâl ve ringawt bawk (2 Kor. 11:14, 15, ESV). An va runthlâk tak êm! Krista rawngbâwltu nia inchhâl siin, Setana tân hna an lo thawk reng zâwk a. “An tâwpna chu a thiltihte ang zêl a ni ang” tiin thu tlâng a kâwm ta nghe nghe a ni (2 Kor. 11:15, NKJV).

`Paula’n kohhran chhunga zirtîrna dik lovin kua a hreuh palh a hlauhzia hi hria ila. Hei hian kan hnênah pawh eng vaukhânna thu nge min pêk le?`
