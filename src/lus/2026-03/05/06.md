---
title:  Milem biakna hnehin
date:   30/07/2026
---

1 Korinth 8:1–3-ah chuan Paula’n hmangaihnain milem biakna lak ata mi a humhim thu a sawi a. Chu thu tho chu chipchiar deuh zâwkin 1 Korinth 10:23–11:1-ah pawh a sawi leh bawk. 1 Korinth 8:3-ah chuan Pathian kan hmangaihna chungchâng a sawi a. 1 Korinth 10-ah erawh mi dangte kan hmangaihna chungchâng a sawi ve thung. “Tu pawhin mahni thatna tûr zawng lovin, mi dang thatna tûr zawng zâwk rawh se” tiin min hrilh a (1 Kor. 10:24). Chutiang chu mi dangte hmangaihtu chauhin an ti thei thîn.

`Marka 10:17–22 leh Marka 12:28–31 chhiar la. Hêng thu pahnihte hian eng nge inanna an neih a, engtiangin nge 1 Korinth 10-a Paula thu sawi hi hrethiam tûrin min tanpui?`

Paula’n 1 Korinth 10-a a sawi hi Isua’n Marka 12:28–31-a dân thu ropui ber ber pahnih: Pathian kan hmangaihna leh mi dangte kan hmangaihna te a lo suih zawm ang chiah tho kha a ni. Tlangvâl hausa tak pakhat chanchinah (Marka 10:17–22) khân hêng hmangaihna chi hnihte hi a suih zawm a, a suih zawm dân pawh Deuteronomi 6:4 thu (Marka 10:18 en la) leh Thu Sâwm Pêka a lungphêk pahnihnaa chuangte (Marka 10:19 en la) sawi chhâwngin a ni nghe nghe. He tlangvâl hausa takin harsatna a neih chu Pathian leh a thenawm hnâi maia chêngte âi pawha a thil neihte a hmangaih zâwk tlat kha a ni a (Marka 10:22). Vâna a chan tûr rote âiin, leia a sum neih tam takte chu a hmangaih zâwk tih a chiang reng mai. Mi retheite âiin a sum leh pâi a ngaina zâwk a (Marka 10:21). Milem (sum) chibai bûktu dik tak a ni.

Isua’n a lo sawi ang hian Paula pawhin thil dang zawng âia Pathian hmangaih tûr leh, keimahni kan inhmangaih ang bawka kan vêngte pawh hmangaih ve chu 1 Korinth 10:27, 28-a a sawi lan dinhmuna kan din huna kan tih tûr niin min hrilh a. A awmzia chu, thil tih thiang ngeite pawh—tu emaw tâna huatthla la a nih theih avângin—tih kher a tha chuang lo thei tihna a ni (1 Kor. 10:23). He thu hi “Chutichuan. . . engkim Pathian ropuina tûr zêlin ti thîn ang che u” (1 Kor. 10:31, NKJV) tih hian khâikhâwm a ni a. A awmzia chu, ropuina leh châwimâwina zawng zawng changtu tûr chu Pathian a ni tihna a ni. Milem biakna hi kawng hrang hrangin a lang chhuak thei a, Pathian chauhin a chan tûr ropuina lo chuhpuitu chu eng thil pawh ni se milem biakna vek a ni (Isaia 42:8).

1 Korinth 10:31–11:1-a Paula thil sawi hi bung 8–10-a sawite khâikhâwmna ang deuh a ni âwm e. Ama hamthatna tûr zawng lovin, mi tam takin chhandamna an lo chan theih mawlh chu a ngaihtuah ber a nih thu a sawi a (1 Kor. 10:33, ESV). Chu chu Isua zirtu a nih dân pawh a ni bawk (1 Kor. 11:1).

`Engtin nge nangmah i inhmangaih angin i vêngte pawh i lo hmangaih lehzual theih ang?`
