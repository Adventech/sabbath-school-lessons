---
title:  Thil thleng tawh atanga zirlâi zirin
date:   28/07/2026
---

Paula hian ama thil tawn ngei atanga mahni inphatna nun neih a tûlzia a sawi hnuah, milem biak chungchâng bawk kha chipchiar deuh zâwkin a sawi tha leh ta a. Kawng khatah chuan, 1 Korinth 10-ah hian paih thlâka a awm palh hlauh loh nâna a thu hnuaia a taksa a dah tawh thu a sawi kha (1 Kor. 9:27) chipchiar zâwkin a rawn tui hnih leh a ni pawh kan ti thei ang. Korinth khuaa mite hi ama tih dân anga ti ve tûrin a duh êm êm a; mahse, entawn atâna tha ber erawh Isua Krista a ni (1 Kor. 11:1).

`1 Korinth 10:7–11 chhiar la. Israel-te’n thlalêra thil sual an tih kha eng nge ni a, enga ti nge Pathianin hamthatna a pêkte kha an thil sual tihte tirunthlâk zualtu a nih bawk?`

1 Korinth 10:1–5-ah hian Pathian mite’n thlalêra an awm lâia thil an tawn kha a sawi a. Chhûm tih leh tuifinriat tih a sawi hian kan rilru-ah Pathian kaihhruaina, awmpuina, leh vênhimna an chanzia min hriattîr âwm e. Chutiangin, ei tûr leh in tûr tih hian Pathianin an taksa mamawh a pêk zêlzia a entîr bawk. Israel fate’n chhûmah leh tuifinriata thil an tawn kha Kristiante’n kan chan thin baptisma entîrtu a ni a. Chutiangin, ei tûr leh in tûr tih pawh hian Lalpa Zanriah a kâwk.

Tawngkam danga sawi chuan, 1 Korinth 10 hian Kristiante chu hmasâng Israel fate’n thlalêra an lo tawn ang kha tawng ve niin a sawi a. Israel-te chanchin kal tawh a sawi chhuah chhan erawh khatiang thil kha lo thleng nawn leh se a tih vâng a ni lo. Israel fate khân hamthatna tam tak an nei chungin an zînga eng emaw zât khân thil sual tih (1 Kor. 10:6), entîr nân, milem pathian biak (1 Kor. 10:7) leh dik lo taka mipat-hmeichhiatna hman (1 Kor. 10:8) te an châk tlat a. Mi tam zâwk chunga Pathian a lungni ta lo (1 Kor. 10:5) pawh kha a mak hran lo rêng a ni.

Hmasâng Israel fate kha dema, thil sual râpthlâk tak ti nia sawi a awl angreng khawp mai. Nimahsela, Paula hi chuan Kristiante pawh hian hmânlâi mite’n an hriat ve loh Krista chanchin kan hre vek chungin, an thil sual tih ang tho kha kan ti ve thei a ni tih a sawi a. “Ding nia inngâi chu fîmkhur hle rawh se; chuti lo chuan a tlu dah hlauh ang e” (1 Kor. 10:12, NKJV) tiin vaukhânna thuchah chiang tak min lo pe a ni. “Nia inngâi” tih tawngkam hian kohhran member-te zînga thenkhat hi chuan Israel fate’n thil sual an lo tih ang kha ti ve thei reng dinhmunah an ding a ni tih rêng an hre lo tih a kâwk a. Vawiinah hian eng dinhmunah nge kan din hlawm le?

“Ding nia inngâi chu fîmkhur hle rawh se; chuti lo chuan a tlu dah hlauh ang e.” Kan zînga tu hian nge he vaukhânna thuchah hi a taka thleng thei reng a nihzia la hre lo le?

`Bible hian kan tuar theih tâwk aliam chu Pathianin thlêmna kan tawh a phal dâwn lo va, thlêmna a lo thlen rualin a tlân chhuahna kawng pawh a siam nghâl zêl zâwk dâwn tih a lo sawi (1 Kor. 10:13). Chuti ni si enga ti nge suala tlûk mai hi kan la awlsam viau mai le?`
