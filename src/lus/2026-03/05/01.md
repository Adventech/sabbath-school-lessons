### Chhiar tûrte
1 Korinth 8; Tirh. 15:20; 1 Kor. 9:1–6; 1 Kor. 10:5–22; Deut. 6:4, 5; Marka 12:28–31.

> <p>Châng vawn</p>
> “Chutichuan, in ei pawhin, in in pawhin, in tih apiangah pawh Pathian ropuina tûr hlîrin ti rawh u” (1 Korinth 10:31).

1 Korinth 8–10-ah hian mipat-hmeichhiatna hman chungchâng (bung 5–6) a sawi kha a hmâwr bâwk tain, hêng bung pathumah vêk hian Korinth khuaa mite hnên atanga zawhna eng eng emaw a dawnte (1 Kor. 7:1) pawh a chhâng tan ta bawk a. Chûng a chhânnate chuan 1 Korinth 8 atanga a tâwp thleng hi a luah nghe nghe.

1 Korinth 7 hian dik lo taka mipat-hmeichhiatna hmanna (bung 5–7) leh milem biakna (bung 8–10) te hi thil inzawm tlat a nih thu a sawi a. Chutiang a nih avâng pawh chu a ni ang, Thuthlung Thar lehkhabu hrang hrangah hian sawi kawp a ni fo rêng a ni.

1 Korinth 5–7-a sawi ber hi dik lo taka mipat-hmeichhiatna hman chungchâng a nih lâiin, 1 Korinth 8–10-a luh chilh ber erawh milem biakna chungchâng a ni ve thung a. Kristiante chuan hêng thil sual pahnihte hi an tlânsan ngei ngei tûr a ni a ti (1 Kor. 6:18, 1 Kor. 10:14).

Kâr hmasa khân dik lo taka mipat-hmeichhiatna hmanna lak atanga kan fihlîm theihna kawng pawimawh tak chu Thlarau Thianghlim in nih a ni tih kan sawi a (1 Kor. 6:19, 20). Tûn kârah hi chuan, “Pathian ropuina tûr zêla” thil tihna (1 Kor. 10:31, NKJV) hmangin milem biakna kan tlânsan thei a ni tih kan zir ho ve thung dâwn a ni.
