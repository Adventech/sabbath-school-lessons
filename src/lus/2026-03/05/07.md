date:   31/07/2026
---

Ellen G. White-i lehkhabu Thlahtu Bulte leh Zâwlneite bung 28, “Sinai Tlânga Milem Biakna” (phêk 318–334) chhiar ang che.

“Mi dangte thatna tûr leh an sâwtna tûr hi inngaihtuahsak tlâng ta theuh ila thil tha tak tak kan va ti tam dâwn êm aw! Pathianin hamthatna a pêk dawngtu zawng zawngte chuan eng emaw ti tala mi dangte kalna tûr kawng chhun ênsaktu (anmahni tanpuitu) nih an bâ a. . . . Pathian hmangaih tak taktute hian chutiang chu an ti a nih chuan anmahni rawng an bâwl hi an bânsan ngei ang.”—Ellen G. White, in The Advent Review and Sabbath Herald, November 18, 1884, p. 730.

“Paula hian a unaute chu an tawngka chhuak leh an thil tihte’n mi dangte chunga nghawng a neih dân tûr ngaihtuah thîn tûrin a chah a. Eng thil pawh ni se—amah ngau ngau chuan pawina nei hran lo pawh a ni maithei—milem biakna nia mite’n an lo ngaihsak theih tûr leh, rinna kawnga chak lo zâwkte an tlûk phahna tûr ang chi chu ti hauh lo tûrin a ngên bawk. A thurâwn pêk chu, ‘Chutichuan, in ei pawhin, in in pawhin, in tih apiangah pawh Pathian ropuina tûr hlîrin ti rawh u’ tih hi a ni. . . .”

“Korinth kohhran hnêna Paula vaukhânna thuchah sawite hi eng hun atân pawh lo hman ve theih vek a ni a, keini hun atân hlei hlei hian a tûl lehzual. Milem biakna a tih hian mite’n milem pathian an siam chawp chibai an bûk thin ang chauh hi a kâwk lo va, mahni chauh inngaihtuahna te, awm âwl thlâkhlelhna te, kâ châkna leh tisa châkna te pawh bânsak vek tûrin a ngên a ni.”—Ellen G. White, Tirhkohte Thiltih, pp. 316, 317.

“I tih atâna thiang ngei pawh ni se, chûng thilte i tih chuan Pathian ramin hma a sâwn zêl tûr hi a tibahlah a nih si chuan ti lo zâwk mai la. Mi dangte’n thutak an pawm loh phah theihna tûr hil rêng rêng chu eng mah ti suh. . . . Thil eng kim tih a thiang a; nimahsela, thil engkim tih chu a tha kher chuang lo.”—Ellen G. White, Testimonies for the Church, vol. 9, p. 215.

**Sawi ho tûrte:**

`Paula ngaihdân chuan Kristian puitling tawh nungchang hian Kristian puitling tawh than a tihthutsak theih châng a awm a. Chutiang a thlen theih hun âwm chu han ngaihtuah teh. Thil dang zawng âia Pathian hmangaih leh mahni inhmangaih anga mi dangte pawh hmangaih ve thu hi enga ti nge he chona lo chhân dân tûr kawng awm chhun a nih?`

`Kristiante ngei pawhin, kan fîmkhur hle a nih loh chuan chibai kan lo bûk ve mai theih tûr milem eng engte nge awm? Tin, i ngaihhlut êm êm thil eng emaw kha milem pathian a lo ni daih thei tih engtin nge i hriat ang?`

`Paula chuan chanchin tha a hril hunah hril tlâk lo nia mite’n an lo ngaih palh loh nân tiin a taksa a thunun a, ama thu hnuaiah a dah tawh thu a sawi a (1 Kor. 9:27). Tûn kâr zirlâi beh chhan hian chanchin tha hriltu ni tlâk lo nia mi siam thei eng thilte nge awm ngaihtuah ang che.`

`1 Korinth 10-ah chuan Paula’n milem biak a hlauhawmzia sawiin, “Milem biakna chu tlânchhiatsan rawh u” a lo ti a (1 Kor. 10:14). Milem biakna that loh êm êm chhan chu eng nge ni?`
