---
title:  Mahni hma sial lo hmangaihna
date:   27/07/2026
---

`1 Korinth 9:1–6 chhiar la. He thu hian engtin nge hmangaihna atanga lo chhuak mahni inphatna nun chu kan neih theih dân tûr a sawi?`

Han chhiar thuak ringawt chuan, Paula’n tirhkoh a nih ngei thu a sawi hi a hma chiaha hriatna leh hmangaihna chungchâng a lo sawi tawh nên khân inzawmna nei hran âwm pawhin a lang lo. Mahse, thil pakhat kan hriat reng tûr chu, a tîrah chuan Bible hi bung hrang hranga then a ni lo va. 1 Korinth 9-a Paula’n a sawi pawh hi a hma chiah bunga (bung 8) a sawi nên inzawmna nei hran lo a ni lo. Dik tak phei chuan, 1 Korinth 9-ah hian Krista tân leh an ringtupuite tâna mahni hmasialna tel lo hmangaihna an lan chhuahtîr theih dân tûr a hrilh zâwk a. Amah ngei hian chutiang hmangaihna chu a neih avângin a hamthatna chan theih hrang hrang pawh a chân nual nghe nghe:

**“Ei leh in tûrin”** (1 Kor. 9:4). Heta ei leh in a tih hian sum leh pâia tanpuina dawng thei a nihna a kâwk a. Paula hian tirhkoh a nih avângin an rawng a bâwlsakte hnên atangin sum leh pâia tanpuina leh duhsakna dangte pawh a dawng thei reng a ni. Chutiang chu a hun lâia sakhaw hruaitu dangte kha chuan an dawng thîn rêng bawk a. Mahse, ani hi chuan a dawng ve lo; puan in siamin a khawsakna mamawhte chu ama’n a thawk chhuak ve mai thîn a ni (Tirh. 18:3).

**“Pathian ringtu ni ve tho nupui hruai tûrin”** (1 Kor. 9:5, NKJV). Hetih hun lâi hian nupui nei tirhkohte chu chanchin tha hrila an zin chhuah nikhuain an sênso kohhranin a tumsak tûra an nupuite pawh an hruai ve phalsak an ni a. Chutianga nupaa zin chhuak thînte zîngah chuan Priskili leh Akuila te nupa (Rom 16:3) te, Andronika leh Junia te nupa (Rom 16:7) te pawh kha an tel. Paula erawh hi chu nupui nei lo a ni a (1 Kor. 7:8). Duh ni se nupui neiin, a nupui chu rawngbâwla a zin kual vêlnaahte pawh a hruai thei reng a ni.

**“Hna thawk lova awm tûrin”** (1 Kor. 9:6, NKJV). Paula leh Barnaba te hian duh ni se chanchin tha hril hna an thawhah khân hlawh an phût thei reng a; mahse, an phût lêm lo. Paula hian puan in siamin a khawsakna mamawhte chu a thawk chhuak ve mai thîn a (Tirh. 18:3), Barnaba erawh hi chu eng hna thawk nge ni tih kan hre lo. Mi thilphal tak mai a ni a (Tirh. 4:36, 37), chutiang a nih avâng chuan mahni intum chawp mai a pawi ti lo a ni ang.

1 Korinth 9:7–11-ah hian amah leh Barnaba’n kohhran hnên ata chhawmdâwlna dawn chu lo duh pawh ni se âwm lo a nih hran lohzia a sawi ta a (1 Kor. 9:11, 12). Lalpa ngei pawhin, “Chanchin tha hriltute chuan chanchin tha atangin ei an hmu tûr a ni” tiin a lo sawi nghe nghe bawk (1 Kor. 9:14, NKJV; 1 Tim. 5:18). Nimahsela, Paula hi chuan “chu chanvo chu chan kher kan tum lo” tiin a sawi a (1 Kor. 9:12, ESV). Chutiang chuan, mahni inphat kawnga mi dangte entawn tlâk niah insiamin (1 Kor. 9:1–18), chu chuan Korinth khuaa chanchin tha hril hna thawh pawh a tisâwt zawt bawk niin a sawi (1 Kor. 9:19–23).

`I dawn theih reng ni tho mah se, Lalpa tâna thuhretu thawh hlâwk zâwk i lo nih theih nâna i dawn loh mai tha zâwk eng thilte nge awm?`
