---
title:  Milem be lo tûra vaukhânna thu
date:   29/07/2026
---

`1 Korinth 10:5–22 chhiar la. Eng vângin nge milem biakna chu kan tlânchhiatsan ang?`

1 Korinth 10:14–22-ah hian milem pathiante hnêna ei tûr hlan chungchâng bawk kha a sawi leh a. Kan tûnlâi ngaihtuahnaah milem pathiante hnêna ei tûr hlan chu thil mak leh âtthlâk tak niin lang mah se, Bible hun lâi kha chuan an thil chîn tlânglâwn tak a ni tlat. Milem pathiante biak ina inthâwina ran an hlan khân a sa thenkhat chu a lo buaipuitu puithiamte’n changin, anni chuan bazaar hmunahte an zuar chhuak leh thîn a. Chûng sate chu an pathiante hnêna hlan ni ve lo sa pangngâite nên an chhawp pawlh mai thin avângin Kristiante tân pawh hre lova lo lei palh mai awl tak a ni âwm e. Chutiang thila Paula thurâwn pêk chu: chutiang sa chu Kristiante tân lei a pawina a awm hran lo tih a ni.

Chutih rualin, milem pathian biak ina hlan hnu sa an lei chu anmahni chênna inah ei thei mah se (1 Kor. 8:1–13), milem pathian biak ina kala, an kût ni vâng thla lo va hmanpui ve erawh phal loh bur a ni thung a. A tehkhâwng zam hi a chiang khawp mai: Kristiante chu anmahni ina inthâwina sa ei phalsak a ni a, a chhan pawh milem pathiante chu eng mah lo mai an nih vâng a ni (1 Kor. 8:4); nimahsela, milem be mite kût ni vângthla hmannaa lo tel ve erawh chu ramhuaiho hnêna inthâwina hlan ang (1 Kor. 10:20, 21) a nih avângin an ti miah tûr a ni lo. Lalpa Zanriah kîl honaa tel ve hi Krista nêna inpâwl hona nei tihna a ni (1 Kor. 10:16) ang chiahin, milem be mite kût leh sakhaw ni serh hmannaa lo tel ve chu ramhuaite nêna inpâwl hona nei tihna a ni (1 Kor. 10:20).

Chu vângin, Paula chuan, “Lalpa no leh ramhuaiho no in dawm kawp thei lo vang a; Lalpa dawhkâna mi leh ramhuaiho dawhkâna mi pawh in ei kawp thei hek lo vang” tiin a sawi a. Isua pawhin, “Tu ma’n hotu pahnih rawng an bâwl kawp thei lo” (Mat. 6:24, NKJV) tiin a lo sawi bawk a nih kha.

Paula hian Pathianin kan thinlung zawng zawnga a rawngbâwl tûra min beisei thu a sawi a. Milem biakna hian Lalpa thîk thu chhiatna a chawk tho thîn tih pawh min hrilh bawk (1 Kor. 10:22). Chumi chu a thlen loh nân, Deuteronomi 6:4, 5 thu, “Aw Israel mite u, ngâithla teh u, LALPA kan Pathian, LALPA chu pakhat chauh a ni e! LALPA i Pathian chu i thinlung zawng zawngin, i thlarau zawng zawngin, i chakna zawng zawngin i hmangaih tûr a ni” tih hi beh chhana hmangin milem pathian biaknaa kan tlûk luh loh nân dân dik lo thei lo a lo zam nghe nghe a (1 Kor. 8:4–6). Thil dang zawng zawng âia Pathian hmangaih tûr kan nih thu (Deut. 6:5) beh chhan vêk hian Isua pawhin, “Nangmah i inhmangaih ang bawkin i thenawm hnâia awmte pawh kha hmangaih ve ang che” tiin a lo belh ve bawk” (Marka 12:31, NKJV; Lev. 19:18 pawh en ang che).

`Milem pathian han tih hian lung emaw, thing emaw hmanga siam milim chauh a kâwk chuang lo va. Thil eng atang pawhin milem pathian chu kan siam thei vek a ni. I nunah khân i tlânchhiatsan ngâi milem pathian eng engte nge awm?`
