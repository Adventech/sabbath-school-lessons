---
title:  Hriatna nge hmangaihna
date:   26/07/2026
---

`1 Korinth 8:1–13 chhiar la. Paula’n hriatna leh hmangaihna a khâikhin chhan hi eng nge ni? A sawi tum tak chu eng nge ni?`

Paula hian thu pawimawh tak, Korinth kohhran member-te’n mi dangte hmangaihna an tlâkchham thu (1 Korinth 8) sawi chhuahna atân milemte hnêna ei tûr hlan chungchâng a hmang a. Milemte hnêna ei tûr hlan hnu ei a thian leh thian loh thuah hian Korinth kohhran hi pâwl hnihah an inthen phawk niin a lang. Khawvêlah hian pathian dang rêng rêng an awm loh tih an hriatna chuan eng pawh ei thei tûrin dikna chanvo a pe nia ngâi (1 Kor. 8:4) eng emaw zât awmin, chûng mite chu “mi chakte” tiin a sawi a (1 Kor. 4:10). Hetih lâi hian an ngâihdân tawmpui lotu tam tak an awm ve tho bawk a, chûng mite chu “mi chak lote” tiin a sawi ve thung (1 Kor. 8:9–12). “Mi chak lote” tia a sawi chhan chu milem be mi an nih lâia an rin dân leh puithuna thil eng eng emaw kha an la thlah loh vâng a ni a. “Mi chakte’n” milem hnênah hlan hnu sa an ei lâi an han hmuh khân Kristian sakhua leh milem biakna te chu kal dûn/kawp thei nia ngaihna an lo neih phah theih avângin “mi chakte” chu “mi chak lote” dâltu lungpui ni lo tûrin a chah ta a ni.

Bible hian milemte hnêna hlan hnu sa ei hi a sawi hnâwm angreng khawp mai a (Tirh. 15:20, 29; Tirh.21:25; Thup. 2:14, 20). Paula erawh chuan hêng châng hrang hrangte’n tawngkam na deuh tak an hman ang hi a hmang ve lêm lo. A duh ber chu kohhranhote an inpumkhat a ni a, mi thenkhatin hriatna changchâwia thil an ti bawrh bawrh mai a nih chuan kohhranah inrem lohna a thlen mai a hlau a ni. Chutih rual chuan, hriatna hi sual a ni e chu a ti hran chuang miah lo bawk a; chapona rilru puttîrtu leh kohhrana inthen hranna thlentu hriatna erawh a do dâl tlat thung. Hmangaihna tel lo hriatna ngawt chu hriatna dik leh tha a ni lo hrim hrim a (1 Kor. 8:2). Hriatna dik chu miin Pathian a hmangaih a, amah pawh Pathianin a rawn hriatna atanga lo chhuak a ni thîn (1 Kor. 8:3).

Paula hian Deuteronomi 6:4 thu sawi chhâwngin ringtute chuan Pathian pakhat chauh a awm a ni tih an hriat a tûl thu a târ lang a (1 Kor. 8:4–6). Deuteronomi 6:4, 5-a kan hmuh kan Pathian chu pakhat niin, “LALPA i Pathian chu . . . i hmangaih tûr a ni” tih thupêkin a zui nghâl bawk. Paula leh Mosia te ngaihdân chuan hmangaihna tel lo hriatna ngawt chu hlutna engmah nei lo a ni.

“Mi chakte” hian an hriatna chu ring tâwkin milem hnêna inthâwina sa ei chu thil pawi leh hlauhawmah an ngâi lo rêng rêng a. Nilaini zirlâi leh Ningani zirlâiah te pawh kan la hmu ang a, Paula hian chumi anga an tih chu phalsakna chin a nei rêng bawk. Nimahsela, chu chu “mi chak lote” tân dâltu lungpui a nih erawh chuan (1 Kor. 8:9) an ti bîk kher lo mai tûr a ni a. Kristiante chu Krista leh mi dangte an hmangaihna atanga lo chhuak mahni inphat nun nei theuh tûra beisei leh phût an ni.

`Paula hian hmangaihna tel lo hriatna ngawt chu thil tha lo tak a ni theih thu a sawi a (1 Korinth 8). Eng kawngahte hian nge hmangaihna tel lo hriatna ngawt chu thil tha lo tak a nih theih?`
