---
title:  Krista, Pathian thiltihtheihna leh finna chu
date:   09/07/2026
---

1 Korinth 1:19, 20, 30, leh 31-ahte hian Pathian finna leh mihring finna inan lohzia a sawi a, thil kal dûn thei miah lo, inkâwlkalh tlat niin a ngâi a ni ber mai. Chutih rual chuan, mihring finna hi a pâwngpaw hnâwl ngawt chuang rêng rêng lo va, Pathian finna hliahkhuh tum tlattu mihring finna erawh a hnâwl fithla thak thîn. Mihring finna hi chuan sual lak ata mi a chhuah zalên thei lo va. Chu hna thawk theitu chu Pathian finna, Krista chauh a ni. A hnuaia kan târ lan hi lo thlîr ila.

| keini chhandam mêkte tân erawh chuan | [Kraws thu hi] Pathian thiltihtheihna a ni | 1 Kor. 1:18 (NKJV) |
| kohte tân zawng | Krista hi Pathian thiltihtheihna a ni | 1 Kor. 1:24 (NKJV) |

1 Korinth 1:18 leh1 Korinth 1:24 te hian Krista chu Pathian thiltihtheihna a nih thu an sawi a, a awmzia chu, Krista’n mihringte hi an sual lak ata chhandam tûrin thiltihtheihna a nei tihna a ni. “Thu âtthlâk tak hrilna hmanga a ring apiangte chhandam chu Pathian tha tih zâwng tak a ni si a” (1 Kor. 1:21, NKJV). Hêng Paula tawngkam hman hrang hrang—“Keini chhândam mêkte” (1 Kor. 1:18) tih te, “a ring apiangte” (1 Kor. 1:21) tih te leh “kohte” (1 Kor. 1:24) tih te hian mi thuhmun vek an kâwk a, chûng mite chu rinna avânga chhandamna lo chang tate an ni. “Krista chanchin tha chu . . . tu pawh a ring apiangte tân chhandamna tûra Pathian thiltihtheihna a ni si a” (Rom 1:16).

Krista chu Pathian thiltihtheihna chauh pawh ni lovin, Pathian finna pawh a ni tel bawk a. A awmzia chu, amah hmang hian Pathianin sual avânga harsatna, mihring finnate’n an chin fel theih rêng rêng loh tûr chu hmachhawnin a lo ching fel ta a ni. He khawvêl finna hi chuan Pathian min hriattîr thei lo va (1 Kor. 1:21). Chutih lâiin, Krista avâng erawh hi chuan chhandamna hmu thei khawp hiala tihfinin kan lo awm ta a nih hi (2 Tim. 3:15).

`1 Korinth 1:24–29 chhiar la. Paula tawngkam hman—“atna” tih te, “chak lohna” tih te, “thiltihtheihna” tih te leh “finna” tih te hi chîk takin lo en ang che. A sawi tum tak hi eng nge ni?`

1 Korinth 1:24–29 i chhiar lâi khân Paula tawngkam hman pahnih—â (âtna) tih leh chak lo (chak lohna) tih te hi ngun takin lo chhinchhiah la. A sawi awmzia chu, mihring finna chuan Kraws thu hi âtna leh chak lohnaah a ngâi a ni maithei e; nimahsela, “Pathian âtna chu mihringte âia a fin zâwk bâkah, Pathian chak lohna pawh mihringte âia chak zâwk a ni bawk” (1 Kor. 1:25, ESV). Hetia kan sawi hian Pathian chu a chak lo emaw, a â emaw kan tihna a ni hauh lo va, Pathian chakna leh finna chu mihringte el phâk rual a nih loh dân kan târ lang mai zâwk a ni.

`“Tisa lama mi fing tam tak te, mi chak tam tak te, mi zahawm tam tak te koh a nih lohzia” tih thu hi ngun takin ngaihtuah la. Hetah hian kan tân eng thuchah pawimawh tak nge awm?`
