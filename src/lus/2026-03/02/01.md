### Chhiar tûrte
1 Kor. 1:17–31, Kol. 1:20, 1 Pet. 2:24, Tirh. 13:16–47, 1 Kor. 2:1–5.

> <p>Châng vawn</p>
> “Kraws thu chu boral mêkte tân chuan âtna a ni si a; keini chhandam mêkte tân erawh chuan Pathian thiltihtheihna a ni zâwk e” (1 Korinth 1:18).

Hmâsâng Rom mi thu sawi thiam leh lehkhabu ziaktu hmingthang tak ni bawk Cicero-a khân tum khat chu an thil tih sual hremna atâna krawsa khenbeh hlau miah lo tûrin Rom ram mipuite a hrilh a ni âwm e. Cicero-a kha Isua pian hma kum 50 vêla lo thi daih tawh ni mah se, a thu sawi hian Rom mite’n kraws an hmuh dân chiang takin a târ lang a. Krawsa thih chu thil mualphothlâk tak, bâwih te, mi sual tâwpthang te leh an ram hmêlma te chan tûr chauh nia ngaih a nih avângin an tân lo hlauh ve chhan tûr a awm lo.

Chutih lâiin, Paula erawh chuan “kraws thu hi . . . Pathian thiltihtheihna a ni” (1 Kor. 1:18, NIV) tiin a lo sawi ve thung a. Ani ngaihdân chuan Isua Kraws hi Pathian leh mihring inkâra inremna lehna thlentîrtu (Eph. 2:16, Kol. 1:20), Isua inngaihtlâwmzia tilangtu (Phil. 2:8) leh kan leiba rit tak tlâksak kan nihna hmun a ni (Kol. 2:14).

Kraws hi Korinth kohhrana buaina hrang hrang thlengte chin fêl nâna Paula hmanraw hman ber a ni a. A rilru kaptu lian ber pakhat chu kohhrana inthen hranna thleng tlat mai kha a ni tih hre tûrin 1 Korinth hi i chhiar thui vak pawh a ngâi lo vang. Chibai bûkna (1 Kor. 1:1–3) leh lâwmthu (1 Kor. 1:4–9) a sawi zawh veleh hemi chungchâng hi a sawi chhunzawm nghâl a (1 Kor. 1:10–17). Tûn kârah hian Kraws thu ropui tak hi Korinth kohhrana inthen hranna thleng leh buaina dang thlengte chin felna hmanrua a nih dân kan thlîr ho dâwn a ni.
