---
title:  Chhandam mêkte tân chuan thiltihtheihna a ni
date:   07/07/2026
---

1 Korinth 1:18 thu hi a awmzia hriat thiam loh theih miah loh tûr khawpa chiang a ni a. Kraws chu a lo thlîrtu rilru azirin a awmzia pawh a danglam thei hle ang. Pathian hel khum tlattute tân chuan âtna mai a ni ang a, chutih lâiin, a chhandamna thlahlel êm êmtute tân erawh thiltihtheihna a ni ve thung si ang.

`Kolossa 1:20 leh 1 Petera 2:24 chhiar la. Kristan kan tân krawsa a lo hlen chhuah tâk chu eng nge ni?`

Kan hmuh tâk ang khân, chanchin tha kan hril hian thu fing leh thu ril pui pui hman loh hrâm kan tum tûr a ni a, a chhan pawh chutiang kan hman chuan “Krista kraws hi eng mah tih theih nei lova kan chhuah daih theih vâng a ni (1 Kor. 1:17). 1 Korinth 1:17-in a sawi êng atang hian âtna letling chiah chu mihring finna ni lovin, Pathian thiltihtheihna zâwk a nih chhan a lo hriatthiam theih ta ruak a (1 Kor. 1:18). Mihring finna nêna inkalh êm êm Kraws thu hian mihring finna âtthlâkzia a târ lang chiang hle rêng a ni.

1 Korinth 1:18 hi a Grik tawng lam kan en chuan “boral mêkte” hian an thil tih rah an seng tih a sawi niin a lang a. Heti hian i hah dah chhin teh ang: “Kraws thu hi anmahni intichhe mêkte, intiboral mêkte tân chuan âtna a ni.” Grik tawnga “tiboral” tih sawina thumal, _apollymi_ chu “tichhia” (Joh. 10:10) tihna pawh a ni ve thei tho bawk a. 1 Korinth 1:19-ah pawh “tichhia” tia lehlin a ni rêng bawk (NKJV).

Paula hian châng 18-a a sawi, boral mêkte nihna a sawi hi Bible thu beh chhan a nihzia târ langin, châng 19-ah hian Isaia 29:14 thu a sawi chhâwng nghe nghe a. Chutah chuan boral mêkte tiboraltu chu Pathian a ni tih sawiin, chu chu a hma chiaha anmahni an intihboral/intihchhiat thu a lo sawi nên khân a inkalh emaw tih mai pawh a awl hle. Amaherawhchu, a inkalh hran miah lo va. A sawi awmzia chu, Pathian hian anmahni intiboral tawh mêkte chu a tiboral nge nge dâwn tihna a ni ang.

“Keini chhandam mêkte tân” (1 Kor. 1:18) tih tawngkam erawh hi chuan chhandamna chu Pathian hnên ata chauh lo thleng a nihzia a sawi ve thung a. Paula’n chhandam mêk kan nih thu a sawi awmzia pawh hi keimahni inchhandam kan nih lohzia min hriattthiamtîr a tumna a ni ang. Ni e, keimahni inchhandam thei kan ni lo rêng a. Kan chhandamna chu keimahni ni lo mi dang thil tih a ni tih kan hriat reng a pawimawh khawp mai. Kan boralna chu keimahni’n kan inthlen a nih lâiin, chhandamna erawh mi dangin mi a pêk theih chauh a ni a, mi sualte hnêna khawngaih thilthlâwnpêk a ni. Chutiang a nihzia chu 1 Korinth 1:21-ah pawh chiang takin kan hmu a, amah ringtu apiangte chhandam thîntu chu Pathian a ni a ti. Chuti a nih chuan, âtna chu Krista kraws kal tlanga Pathianin chhandama min pêk a tum lo hnar hi a ni tih a chiang a (1 Kor. 1:30), chumi lo hnar tlattute chuan anmahni boralna an inthlen nge nge dâwn a ni.

`“Sual man chu thihna a ni si a; Pathian thilthlâwnpêk erawh chu, kan Lalpa Krista Isua zârah chatuana nunna a ni” (Rom 6:23). He châng hian engtiangin nge 1 Korinth 1:18, 19-a Paula’n a sawi tho kha a rawn sawi ve leh?`
