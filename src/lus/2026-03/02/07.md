Ellen G. White-i lehkhabu, Chatuan Nghahfâk bung 78, “Kalvari” (phêk 837–857) chhiar ang che.

“Tûn huna chêng mi tam takte rilru-ah hian Kalvari kraws chu hriat reng tlâk thil thianghlim tak takte’n a hual vêl a ni a. Isua khenbeh a nih lâia thil thlengte kha a thianghlimin a zahawm êm êm vek niin an ngâi bawk. Nimahsela, Paula te hun lâi kha chuan kraws hi mite thinhrik leh thil râpthlâk tak anga ngaih a ni thîn a. Krawsa thi ni si, mihringte chhandamtu anga lantîr tumna chuan mite nuihzat leh dodâl a hlawh lo thei lo.

“Paula pawh hian a thuchah ken chu Korinth khuaa chêng Juda-te leh Grik mite’n eng angin nge an lo ngaih dâwn tih a hre lâwk sa vek a. . . . A thu sawi ngâithlatu Juda-te zîngah chuan a thu sawi tûrin a tihthinrim tûr tam tak an awm. Tin, Grik mite ngaihdânah lah a thu sawite chu thu âtthlâk tak a ni dâwn bawk a. Kraws leh mihring dinhmun châwisânna emaw, chhandamna emaw inzawm theih dân lantîr tum chu mi â tih tâwkah a ngâi ngei ang.

“Nimahsela, Paula ngaihdân chuan kraws hi ngaih pawimawh bera neih tûr a ni tlat mai a. Khenbeha awm Nazareth mi zuitute a tihduhdah lâia man a nih atang khân kraws châwisân a la bâng chhin ngâi lo. Khami tum khân Krista thihnaa lo lang Pathian hmangaihna tâwp chin nei lo chu chiang taka hmuhtîr a ni a; a nunah danglamna ropui tak lo thlengin, a thil ruahmante leh thil tih tum zawng zawngte pawh vânram duh dân nên inremin a lo awm ta vek a ni. . . . Ama thil tawn atang ngei hian mi sualin Pathian Fapa inhlannaa lo lang Pathian hmangaihna chu a thlîr a, Pathian hnêna a tlûk luh rual rualin a thinlungah thil inthlâkthleng a lo awm thîn a ni tih a hria a; chumi hnu chuan Krista hi a tân eng kima eng kim a lo ni ta a ni.”—Ellen G. White, Tirhkohte Thiltih, phêk 245, 246.

**Sawi ho tûrte:**

`Gethsemani huanah khân Isua’n, “Aw ka Pa, a theih chuan he no hian mi pêl lul teh se” tiin a lo sawi a (Mat. 26:39). He a thu sawi hian krawsa kan sual man a lo tlâk kha a sâng hle a ni tih a hriattîr che em?`

`Paula chuan, “Pathian âtna chu mihringte finna âiin a fing zâwk” (1 Kor. 1:25) tiin a lo sawi a. Pathian finna leh mihring finna inthlauh dân chu eng nge ni?`

`Krista khenbeha a awm thu hi Juda-te tân chuan dâltu lungpui lian tak ang a ni a, Grik mite tân lah thu âtthlâk mai a ni bawk. Vawiina kan hril Bible thu eng engte hi nge a lo ngâithlatute ngaiha huatthlala leh âtthlâk mai ni ve ang? Eng vânga chutiang chu ni nge ni?`

`Paula chuan, “khawvêl mi chuan Pathian Thlarau lam thilte a lâwm thîn lo” (1 Kor. 2:14) tih a lo sawi a. Chuti a nih chuan, hêng mite hnênah hian an thinlung khawih tûr zâwngin engtin nge Isua chanchin kan sawi theih ang? Nge ni a, kan nun dân leh thil tihte chauh hian a pawh thei dâwn zâwk?`
