---
title:  Kraws Chanchin Tha
date:   05/07/2026
---

Paula hian Kraws thu chu kan tâna Pathian thiltihtheihna a nih thu a sawi a. Chu vângin, a thu hril laimu ber pawh “Isua Krista chanchin leh amah ngei chu khenbeha a awm thu” (1 Kor. 2:2) a ni hi a mak hran lo rêng a ni.

`1 Korinth 1:17–31 chhiar la. He lâi thua Paula thil sawi pawimawh tak chu eng nge ni?`

1 Korinth 1:18–31-ah hian mihringte âtna leh Pathian finna inthlauhzia a sawi a. Kraws chuan mihringte sualzia leh Pathian erawh a felzia chiang takin a târ lang thei. 1 Korinth-a he lâi thu, “Krista Pathian thiltihtheihna leh finna” tih hi 1 Korinth 1:17-a kan hmuh thu tâwite hmangin sawi hawn a ni a. Krista Kraws chu thiltihtheihna nei miah lova chantîr tûr a nih loh avângin (1 Kor. 1:17), Kraws thu hian kan thu hrilah hmun pawimawh ber a chang ngei tûr a ni (1 Kor. 2:2 pawh chhiar ang che).

Paula hian mite baptisma chantîr tûr ni lovin, Kraws chanchin tha hril tûr zâwka tirh a nih thu a sawi a. He a thu sawiah hian ngun taka ngaihtuah ngâi thil pawimawh tak tak pahnih a awm. Pakhat chu “tir” tia an lo lehlin Grik thumal _apostellõ_ hi niin, he thumal hi Saptawnga “tirhkoh” sawina, apostle tih nên hian a tobul a inang a. Chu vângin, tirhkoh a nihna anga Paula hnathawh pawimawh leh bulpui ber ni bawk chu chanchin tha hril a ni. Pakhat dang chu, Paula’n baptisma chantîr tûra tirh a nih loh thu a sawi hi a ni a; a sawi awmzia erawh baptisma a pawimawh lo a tihna emaw, thu hril ang êm chuan a pawimawh lo a tihna emaw a ni lo. Baptisma an channa tûr Isua âia anmahni baptisma chantîrtu rawngbâwltute lo châwimâwi chiam tâwk an awm niin a lang a, chûng mite chu a zilhhau a ni mah zâwk.

“Thu fing tak tak” (1 Kor. 1:17) tih tawngkam a hman avâng hian thiam taka thu sawite hi thil tha lo tak a ni e a tihna a ni lo bawk a. A sawi tum chu, mihringte finna hian Kraws thu a hliah tûr a ni lo a tihna a ni zâwk. He tawngkam hian hmânlâi Grik mite leh Rom-hote’n tawnghmang thiam taka thil an sawi thin dân kha a kâwk a. Amah Paula ngei pawh hian Atheni khuaah khân thil chhût thiamna te, science thu te leh finna thuril tak takte pawh a lo hmang ve tawh tho a nih kha. Nimahsela, chu chuan rah tha a chhuah tam lo hle a. Chu vângin, “Korinth khuaah hi chuan mi inthlahdah tak takte leh ngaihdân nghet pawh nei lêm lote a hîp khâwm theih nân ruahmanna dang a siam ta a ni. Hnialkhân fing tak tak vawrh chhuah leh titi hona satliah lam chu hawisan hmak a tum tawh a, Isua Krista, amah ngei khenbeha a awm thu chauh lo chu thil dang engmah hriat a tum tawh lo”¬—Ellen G. White, Tirhkohte Thiltih, p. 244.

`Engtiang kawngtein nge thu fing pui pui hman leh tawngkam thiam taka thu sawite hian Kraws thu a lo hliah hlauh theih? Atheni khuaa mihring thil chhût thiamna te, science thu te leh finna thu ril pui pui te a hman âia Korinth khuaa Isua Krista chanchin leh, amah chu krawsah khenbeha a awm thu a hril maiin rah tha a chhuah tam zâwk daih chhan hi eng nge ni ang? Amaherawhchu, mihringte thil chhût thiamna, science thu leh finna thu ril tak takte hi chanchin tha hril darhna hmanrua atân hman ve theih a nih hun a awm thei tho ang em?`
