### Chhiar tûrte
1 Korinth 12, Eph. 4:11–13, 1 Korinth 13, 1 Pet. 4:8–11, 1 Kor. 14:27, Amoas 3:7.

> <p>Châng vawn</p>
> “Hmangaihna hi ûm zêl ula; thlarau lam thilpêkte pawh châk cheu rawh u; amaherawhchu, thu in hril theihna tûr lek phei chu châk bîk rawh u” (1 Korinth 14:1).

Kohhran hi mihringte taksa ang deuh a ni a, pumkhat ni mah se, pêng tam tak a nei a, chûng pêng hrang hrangte chuan tih tûr, pawimawhna leh thiam zâwng bîk eng emaw tak an nei theuh bawk. Chûng thlarau thilpêkte chu hmangaihna nêna an hmang a nih chuan kohhranah inpumkhatna rilru a lo awm ang a, Pathiana minung pathum awmte thinlunga inremna awm chu an nun chhuahpui thei dâwn a ni.

Tûn kârah hian 1 Korinth 12–14 kan thlîr ho ang a, thlarau thilpêk chungchâng a lo sawite chu hriatthiam ngei tumin kan zir dâwn a ni. Hêng bung pathuma a sawite hi kohhran huang chhûnga Kristiante nun dân tûr a sawinaa mi tho a ni a (1 Korinth 11–14). Paula ngaihmawh ber pawh an inkhâwm dân phung mumal lo lutuk thîn kha a ni. Chumi chin felna atâna a chhawp chhuah chu kohhran hi “Krista taksa siam that” (Eph. 4:12) nâna tih theih hrang hrang nei member-te awm khâwmna a ni tih hriat a ni a. A tâwi zâwnga sawi chuan, kohhran hnênah hian member inang lo tak takte zînga inpumkhatna a lo awm theih nân Pathianin thlarau thilpêkte a lo pe a ni kan ti thei ang.

Thil lang chiang tak chu, Paula hian 1 Korinth bung 1–4-a a lo sawi kohhran chhûnga inthen hranna awm chungchâng bawk kha a la vei reng a, chumi tih tihrehna tûr atâna damdâwi a chawh chu Kristaa inpumkhatna a ni tih pawh kan hriat tawh kha. Tûnah hian chu thu tho chu thlarau thilpêkte hnathawh a hriatthiam dân târ langin a rawn sawi chhuak leh ta a. A ngaihdân chuan, Kristaah leh Thlarau Thianghlima inpumkhatna hi inthen hranna rilru kan putna tûr pumpelhna kawng awm chhun a ni.
