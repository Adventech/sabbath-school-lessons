---
title:  Tawng hriat loh
date:   05/08/2026
---

Tawng hriat loh chungchâng hi engtin nge a lo sawi ve thung le? Bible hmun danga thlarau thilpêk hrang hrangte sawinaah pawh kan hmuh angin (Marka 16:17, Tirh. 2:1–13, Tirh. 10:44–48, Tirh. 19:6), 1 Korinth 13-a tawng hriat loh pawh hi Thlarau Thianghlimin mi tute emaw hnêna anmahni pianpui tawng ni lo tawng danga thu sawi theihna a pêk ang kha a nih a rinawm hle.

Paula’n 1 Korinth 12:8–10-a thlarau thilpêk hrang hrang a sawi zîngah hian tawng hriat loh pawh a tel ve a (1 Kor. 12:28, 30; 1 Kor. 13:1, 8 te pawh hi chhiar rawh). Amaherawhchu, 1 Korinth-a 14-a thlarau lam thilpêkte a sawi zawmnaa hian tawng hriat loh chungchâng chauh a thlûr bing ta a ni. 1 Korinth 12 atanga 14 chhûngah hian Grik tawnga “tawng” tih sawina thumal, _glôssa_ vawi 20 chuang langin, chûng zînga 14 zet chu 1 Korinth 14-a mi hi a ni nghe nghe. Chu bâkah, 1 Korinth 14:21-ah hian Grik thumal dang, _heteroglôssos_ (“tawng dang”) tih pawh kan hmu bawk a. Hetianga tam a lo sawi hian hemi chungchâng hi Paula tân pawh sawi fel a tûl hle a ni tih a tilang a. Dik lo tak leh a hmanna tûr ni hauh lova he thilpêk hmanna hian kohhranhote Pathian chibai bûkna hmunah buaina zakhua a thlen thîn a ni tih a chiang a, inkhâwm lâi lâia lo hman tâwk te an awm thin avângin inkhâwm dân phung mumal pawh an nei hlei thei lo thîn ni âwm tak a ni (1 Kor. 14:23, 27, 33, 40).

`1 Korinth 14:5, 13, 26, 27 leh 1 Korinth 12:10, 30 chhiar la. Paula tawng hriat loh chungchânga thurâwn a pêk chu eng nge ni?`

Tawng hriat loha tawng theihna leh, chûng tawngte hrilh fiah theihna chu thil kal dûn tlat tûr a ni a, a chhan pawh kan leia thil kan sawite chu awmze nei a nih a ngaih vâng niin, (1 Kor. 14:9), awmze nei lo a nih chuan sâwtna pawh a nei miah dâwn lo tihna a ni bawk ang (1 Kor. 14:6). Chu vâng chuan, Paula pawh hian tawng hriat loh chu hrilhfiah ngâi leh a lo hretute’n a awmzia an hriatthiam ngei a tûl thu uar takin a sawi rêng a. Tawng hriat loh hrim hrim hi a sawisêl ni lovin, Korinth khuaa mite’n an ngâisâng lutuk leh, thu hril lam an ngaihthah phah hial mai chu tha a ti lo a ni.

Hetah hian thil pawimawh tak kan lo chhinchhiah tûr awm chu: Paula hian Korinth kohhran member zawng zawng hi tawng hriat loha thu sawi thei vek tûrin duhsak hle mah se (1 Kor. 14:5), an sawi theih tak tak erawh a beisei chuang lo va (1 Kor. 12:10). Chu vângin, “Thlarau Thianghlima baptisma chang tûr chuan tawng hriat loha tawng hmasak phawt tûr a ni tih ang ngaihdân hi 1 Korinth 12 leh 14-a Paula thu sawi lo lâk kawihna a ni.”—Raoul Dederen, Handbook of Seventh-day Adventist Theology, electronic ed., vol. 12 of Commentary Reference Series (Hagerstown, MD: Review and Herald Publishing Association, 2001), p. 620.

`In kohhranah khân tawng danga (tawng hriat loha) tawng thei/thiam an awm em? Chutiang mite chuan an thilpêk kawl chu Krista hnêna mite hruai thlen nân engtin nge an hman theih ang? Tawng hriat loh thilpêk hi Krista hnêna mite hruai thlenna hmanrua tûr a ni tih hian Paula’n a sawi tawng hriat loh nihphung dik tak hi hrethiam tûrin engtin nge min tanpui?`
