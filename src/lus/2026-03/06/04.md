---
title:  “Kawng tha lehzual”
date:   04/08/2026
---

“Hmangaihna hi thilpêk tam tak zînga pakhat ve mai a ni lo va. Thilpêk dang zawng zawngte’n pêk an nih chhan ang tak hna an thawh chhuah theihna nâna hmanraw pawimawh tak pawh a ni bawk.”—Carl P. Cosaert, “1 Corinthians,” Andrews Bible Commentary: New Testament (Berrien Springs, MI: Andrews University Press, 2022), p. 1643.

`1 Korinth 13:1–7 leh 1 Petera 4:8–11 chhiar la. Hmangaihna leh thlarau thilpêkte inzawm dân chu eng nge ni?`

1 Korinth 13 hian dik taka thlarau thilpêkte hman theih dân kawng awm chhun chu hmangaihna neih a ni tih thu min hrilh a. Hemi bung tîr lamah hian 1 Korinth 12-a lo sawi tawh thlarau thilpêk hrang hrangte kha a thâwitham deuha sawi chhuak lehin, chûng thilpêk hrang hrangte chu hmangaihna nêna hman a nih loh chuan awmzia a awm tak tak loh tûr thu pawh a sawi bawk. Chu vângin, hriatna (1 Kor. 12:8) leh rinna (1 Kor. 12:9), “tlâng sawn theihna khawp hial” rinna (1 Kor. 13:2) pawh ni se, hmangaihna a tel loh chuan engmah lo mai tho a ni dâwn a (1 Kor. 13:2). Hmangaihna nên a nih loh chuan tawng chi hrang hranga thu sawi theihna (1 Kor. 12:10, 28, 30) pawh “dâr ri mai mai emaw, dârbenthek ri mai mai emaw” chauh a ni ang (1 Kor. 13:1). Chutiangin, thilpêk pawimawh tak, thu hril theihna (hrilhlâwkna thilpêk) meuh pawh hi hmangaihna tel lo chuan engmah a ni chuang dâwn lo (1 Kor. 13:2).

1 Korinth 13:4–7-ah hian hmangaihna chu eng nge a niha eng nge a nih loh tih te, hmangaihna chuan eng nge a tiha eng nge a tih loh tih te a sawi a. Hmangaihna nihna sawina atâna tawngkam hrang hrang a hmante hian hmangaihna chu a hriata hriatna âi mahin, a taka thil eng emaw tihna lam a kâwk zâwk tih a lantîr. Chu vâng chuan, heti hian a sawi ta rêng bawk a: Hmangaihnain (1) a dawh thei a; (2) ngil a nei a; (3) thutak a lâwm thîn a; (4) engkim a tuar hrâm hrâm thîn a; (5) engkim a ring a; (6) engkim a beisei a; (7) engkim a tuar chhuak thîn. A leh lamah chuan, hmangaihnain (1) a îtsîk lo va; (2) mahni a infak lo va; (3) a uangthuang lo va; (4) mâwi lo takin a che lo va; (5) mahni hma a sial lo va; (6) a thinur mai mai duh lo va; (7) thil tha lo lamah ngaihtuahna a sêng duh lo va; (8) thil dik lo tihah a lâwm ngâi lo bawk.

Hêng a tawngkam hrang hrang hmante hian thlarau thilpêkte dik taka kan hman theih nân kaihhruaina khal leh hlâwm tha tak min pe a. Thil chhinchhiah tlâk tak mai chu, thlarau thilpêkte chungchânga inhnialna awm thu a sawina 1 Korinth 12 leh 14 inkâra hmangaihna nihphung dik tak a sawi hi a ni. Ni e, hmangaihna hi fing leh thiam taka thlarau thilpêkte kan hman theih nân thil pawimawh tak a ni rêng a. Tin, hmangaihna hi rinna leh beiseina te nên huang khata dah niin, “hêngahte hian a ropui ber chu hmangaihna hi a ni” (1 Kor. 13:13) a ti bawk.

`Enga ti nge hmangaihna hi kan rinnaa thil pawimawh tak a nih? Pathian hmangaihna hi mi dangte hnêna lantîr ve âia a taka nunpui theih dân kawng tha zâwk a awm em?`
