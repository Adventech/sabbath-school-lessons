---
title:  Hrilhlâwkna thilpêk
date:   06/08/2026
---

Hrilhlâwkna thilpêk hian Paula’n thlarau lam thilpêkte a sawinaah hian hmun pawimawh tak a chang a. Thil ngaihnawm angreng tak chu, hrilhlâwkna thilpêk hi tawng hriat loh hmaa a sawi tlângpui thîn hi a ni (1 Kor. 12:10, 28; 1 Kor. 13:8). Tawng hriat loh thilpêk a sawi hmasak zâwk tum pawh a awm ve tho bawk a; mahse, chûngte pawh chu hrilhlâwkna thilpêk âia pawimawh lo zawk a nihzia târ lanna a ni tlângpui (Kor. 14:4, 5, 6, 22).

`Ephesi 4:11–13 leh 1 Korinth 14:3, 4 chhiar la. Hêng thute hian thlarau thilpêk hrang hrangte pêk a nih chhan leh hrilhlâwkna thilpêk bîk pêk a nih chhan engtin nge an sawi?`

Hrilhlâwkna thilpêk hi mite siam that nân te, fuih nân te leh thlamuan nân te pêk a ni a (1 Kor. 14:3; Tirh. 15:32 pawh chhiar ang che). Hei hian a tihlan chiang tak chu, hrilhlâwkna kan tih hi nakîna thil lo thleng tûrte lo sawi lâwkna lam âi mahin, tûn huna kan nun dân tûr inhriattîrna lam a ni zâwk. Grik thumal, _prophêteuô_ tih chu “thil eng emaw a thlen hmaa lo sawi lâwk” tihna emaw, “tu emaw âi awha thil eng emaw sawi” tihna emaw a ni a. Entîr nân, Tirhkohte 2:29–31-ah chuan (Amosa 3:7 pawh chhiar la) a hmasa zâwk ang hi lo langin, zâwlnei tia Davida a sawina chu “lo hre lâwk” tia hrilhfiah a ni. A hnuhnûng zâwk ang hi zâwlnei tia Juda leh Sila te sawi an nihna Tirhkohte 15:32-ah kan hmu ve bawk a. An “thu hril” erawh thil lo thleng tûr lo sawi lâwk lam ni lovin, “thu tam taka unaute” thlamuan leh tihngheh a ni ve thung.

Ephesis 4:11–13 thu atang hian thlarau thilpêkte hi a tirhkohte (Thuthlung Thar) hunah khân a tâwp zui ve nghâl mai lo tih kan hre thei a, tâwp chu sawi loh, khawvêl tâwp thlenga awm chhunzawm zêl tûr a ni zâwk (Tirh. 2:39). Amaherawhchu, mi tu emawin zâwlnei niah lo inchhâl ta se, chu mi chu Pathian Lehkha thu beh chhanin fiah ngei tûr a ni ang. Dân pali ka zui tûr a awm a. A hmasa berin, a thu hrilhlâwkte chu a lo thleng dik ngei tûr a ni (Deut. 18:22; Jer. 28:8, 9). Pahnihnaah chuan, a thuchah ken chu Bible-a kan hmuh zâwlneite thuchah puan nên hian a inrem tûr a ni (Deut. 13:1–3, Isaia 8:20). Pathumnaah chuan, a ni tin nun dânin Krista neitu a nihzia a tilang tûr a ni (1 Joh. 4:1–3). Palinaah chuan, an rah kan lo en tûr a ni (Mat. 7:15–20). Isua chuan zâwlnei derte chu an rahah a hriat theih mai tûr thu a lo sawi a. Chutiang bawkin zâwlnei dik takte pawh an rahah tho hriat theih a ni ang.

Thupuan bu hian kohhran la bângte’n an neih tûr thil pawimawh tak pakhat chu hrilhlâwkna thilpêk hi a ni tih a lo sawi a (Thup. 12:17, Thup. 19:10). Seventh-day Adventist-te chuan hrilhlâwkna thilpêk hi Ellen G. White-i hnênah khân pêk niin, chûng a thu dawnte chu thuziak hrang hrangahte hian pho chhuahin a awm niin kan ring.

`Ellen G. White-i khân hrilhlâwkna thilpêk a kawl a ni tih kan rin chhan hrang hrangte chu eng nge ni? A hnathawh leh thuneihna neih chungchânga zawhna dang la awm theite chu eng nge ni?`
