---
title:  Thilpêk hrang hrangte
date:   02/08/2026
---

Paula hian “tûnah chuan . . . chungchâng chu” tih tawngkam hmangin thu dang a sawi leh ta a. Heta a sawi hi “thlarau thilpêkte” nge ni a, “thlarau mite” zâwk tih thu-ah a zir mite ngaihdân a rual chiah lo a ni âwm e. A chhan pawh a Grik tawngkam hman, _tôn pneumatikôn_ tih hian a khawi ve ve pawh a kawh theih vâng a ni. Nimahsela, Paula’n chiang taka thlarau thilpêkte a sawina 1 Korinth 12:54 han chhiar ila, “thlarau thilpêkte” tih hi a thlanawm deuh zâwk âwm e. 1 Korinth 12:2, 3 kan chhiar chuan, Thlarau Thianghlim thilpêk hmasa ber chu huaisen taka Isua hi Lalpa a ni tih puan a nih thu kan hmu a (1 Kor. 12:2, 3). Thuthlung Thar hunah khân Isua hi Lalpa a ni tih sawi chu Kaisara hi Lalpa a ni lo tia sawi nên thuhmun reng anga ngaih a nih avângin thil tih ngamawm loh tak a ni ang (Tirh. 17:7; Joh. 19:12, 15 pawh en bawk ang che). Rom ram lalte kha pathian anga ngaih niin chibai an bûk thîn a, amah ni lo mi dang tu emaw lal anga lo sawi chu a laka hel tûra infuihpawrhna a nih avângin a sawitu pawh tihhlum ngei tûr a ni.

Isua leh Paula te chuan Pathian rinna hi Thlarau Thianghlim thilpêk a ni tih uar takin an lo sawi a, tihduhdahna kârah leh tihhlum hiala vauna hnuaiah pawh rinna vuan tlat tûrin min fuih bawk. Dik taka sawi phei chuan, rinna hi thilpêk hrang hrang zîngah pawh a pawimawh ber a ni a. Chu vâng chu a ni ang e, 1 Korinth 13:13-a thil pathum a sawi zîngah pawh rinna tih hi a sawi hmasa ber a ni. Rinna hi thlarau thilpêk a ni tih chu 1 Korinth 12:9-a Paula thil sawi atang pawh hian a chiang mai a. Amaherawhchu, thilpêk dang tam tak pawh a awm tho bawk. Thlarau Thianghlim chuan “mimal tin hnênah, ama duh dân ang zêlin” thilpêk chi hrang hrang a sem thîn a (1 Kor. 12:11, NKJV); chu vâng chuan, thilpêk zawng zawng hi a tangkâi vek a tih a chiang hle.

`1 Korinth 12:1–6 chhiar la. He lâi thuin uar taka a sawi chu eng nge ni?`

“Hrang hrang” tih tawngkam an hmang nawn fo hian thilpêk chi hrang hrang tam tak a awm ni tih min hriattîr a. 1 Korinth 12:1-a “thlarau lam thilpêkte” tia a lo sawi kha châng 4–6-ah kil hrang hrang pathum: “thilpêk” (Grik tawngin, _charisma_) tih te, “rawngbâwlna” (Grik tawngin, _diakonia_) tih te leh “hna thawh dân” (Grik tawngin, _energêma_) tih te hmangin a sawi fiah ta a ni. Hêng a tawngkam hman hrang hrangte hian awmze hran theuh nei tho mah se, a thil sawi hi inzûl tak vek a nih hlawm avângin a inan lohna lâi lo zawn phêt tum loh a tha ang a. Hêng thlarau thilpêk hrang hrangte hi Pathiana minung pathum awmte an inpumkhat tlâng tlat anga kohhran member-te pawh an lo inpumkhat phah theihna tûra duan a tih kan hriat reng a pawimawh hle (Eph. 4:8–11 pawh chhiar ang che). Ringtute hnêna thilpêk petu chu Thlarau Thianghlimi ni mah se, ringtute zînga Krista rawngbâwltîr theitu erawh Pathian a ni a (1 Kor. 12:5, 6). Ringtu mal tin hnênah thilpêk eng emaw ber hi pêk theuh niin (1 Kor. 12:11), chûng thilpêk an dawnte chu ringtu pum pui sâwt tlânna atân an hmang thîn tûr a ni.

`Paula’n inpumkhatna a ngaih pawimawhzia hi lo hria la. Eng vângin inpumkhatna hi kohhran tân thil pawimawh tak a nih?`
