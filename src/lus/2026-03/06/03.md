---
title:  Mi hrang hrang kâra inpumkhatna
date:   03/08/2026
---

1 Korinth 12:4–6-a inpumkhatna thu sawina tawngkam hrang hrang a hman (“Thlarau thuhmun” tih te, “Lalpa thuhmun” tih te leh, “Pathian thuhmun” tih te) hi 1 Korinth 12 hmun dangah pawh a hmang zui ta zêl a. Chumi a nihzia chu a tawngkam hman hrang hrang, entîr nân, “Thlarau hmunkhat leh thuhmun” (1 Kor. 12:11, NKJV) tih te, “taksa hi pakhat a ni” (1 Kor. 12:12) tih te, “taksa pakhat” (1 Kor. 12:12, 13, 20) tih te, “Thlarau pakhat” (1 Kor. 12:13 [vawi hnih]) tih te leh, “[pêngte chu] an inngaihsak tlâng theuh” (1 Kor. 12:25, NKJV) tih te hian chiang takin a tilang.

Paula hian inpumkhatna thu a sawi rualin, tawngkam hrang hrang, hêng—“pêng tam tak” (1 Kor. 12:12, 20) tih te, “baptis-in kan awm theuh” (1 Kor. 12:13, NKJV) tih te, “pêng khat chauh ni lovin, pêng tak tam a ni zâwk” (1 Kor. 12:14, NKJV) tih te, “taksa pum pui” (1 Kor. 12:17) tih te, “pêng tinte” (1 Kor. 12:18, NKJV) tih te, “taksa pêngte” (1 Kor. 12:22, 23) tih te, “pêng zawng zawngin” (1 Kor. 12:26) tih te hmangin Krista taksaa member inang lo tak tak an awm thu pawh a sawi tho bawk a. Hetianga inpumkhat pawimawhzia leh mi hrang hrang inang lo tak tak awm khâwm kan nih thu a sawi hian, thlarau thilpêkte hi mize inang lo tak tak zînga inpumkhatna thlentîrtu tûr atân pêk a ni tih uar takin a târ lang tih kan hrethiam tûr a ni.

He danglamna kâra inpumkhatna hian hian Pathian nungchang a târ lang ngei ngei tûr a ni a. Pa chu minung pakhat a ni a, Fapa pawh minung dang pakhat a ni a, tin, Thlarau Thianghlim pawh minung dang pakhat a ni bawk. An pathum hian anmahni nihna theuh an vawng nung reng a, kohhran thatna atâna hna thawk tlângin, rawngbâwl hna a thawhna kawngah pawh thiltihtheihnain an thuam bawk thîn (1 Kor. 12:4–6, Eph. 4:11–13).

`1 Korinth 12:12–31 chhiar la. Enga ti nge tehkhinna atâna taksa leh a pêng hrang hrangte hman hi kohhran leh a member-te entîrna remchâng tak a nih?`

1 Korinth 12-a thupui pawimawh tak pakhat awm chu kohhran member tinte hi mi inang lo tak tak ni hlawm mah se (1 Kor. 12:15–20), an zavâiin inmamawh tawn theuh an ni tih hi a ni (1 Kor. 12:21–26). Ke hian a kalna tûr hmuh theih nân mit a mamawh a; chutiangin, mit hian engmah a khawih thei lo—kut chauhin a khawih thei. Tin, member thenkhat chu an chak lo va (1 Kor. 12:22), thenkhat chu châwimâwi tlâk an ni lo (1 Kor. 12:23) a tih pawh hi tehkhin thu mai a ni a, a nihna takah chuan a pawimawh ber berte an ni zâwk (1 Kor. 12:22).

Thil pawi tak chu, Korinth khuaa ringtute zîngah hian thilpêk thenkhat ngaihsân bîk riauna rilru a awm tlat mai pek a. Chu ngaihdân dik lo leh pawi tak ni bawk chu an thlah theih nân “kawng tha lehzual zâwk” (1 Kor. 12:31), hmangaihna nei tûrin a chah a. Tawngkam danga sawi chuan, eng thilpêk pawh dawng la, fing tak leh hmangaihna nêna i hmang a nih phawt chuan Pathian tân lâwm tlâk a ni tho ang.

`1 Korinth 12:8–10, 28; Rom 12:6–8; leh Ephesi 4:11-a thilpêk hrang hrang a sawite hi chhiar la. Eng thilpêk ber nge i dawn ve? Chu chu Krista taksa thatna atân engtin nge i hman theih ang?`
