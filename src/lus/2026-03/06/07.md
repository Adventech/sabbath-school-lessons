Roswell F. Cottrell-a’n Ellen G. White-i lehkhabu, Spiritual Gifts, vol. 1-a thuhmahruai a ziah, “Spiritual Gifts” (pp. 5–16) tih kha chhiar ang che.

“Kohhran member-te hi Lalpa rin chhan hna thawk tûrin kal chhuak rawh se. Tichuan, ani’n a lo kalpui ang a, mite chu an sualna hriattîrin piantharna a chantîr dâwn a ni. Hna thawktu pakhat chu thu hril thiam tak a ni maithei a, pakhat dang chu thu ziak mi a ni maithei a, a dang leh chu tih tak zet leh bâng lova tawngtâi thîn mi a ni maithei a, zâi thiam tak pawh an awm maithei bawk. Tin, mi dang chu chiang leh fiah taka Pathian thu hrilhfiah thiam a ni maithei bawk a. Thilpêk tinte hi, Pathianin a kawltute a rawn thawhpui thin avângin Pathian thiltihtheihna hmanrua a ni thei vek a ni. Mi pakhat hnênah chuan Pathianin finna a pe a, pakhat dang hnênah chuan hriatna a pe a, pakhat dang leh hnênah chuan rinna a pe bawk. Nimahsela, an zavâiin hotu pakhat hnuaia thawk an ni. Thilpêk hrang hrang a lo awm avâng hian kawng hrang hrangin hna kan thawh theih phah a, ‘nimahsela, mi zawng zawng engkima hna thawktu chu Pathian tho a ni’ (1 Kor. 12:6).

“Tu mahin thilpêk tê tham deuh nia ngaihte pawh hi an ngâinêp chuang tûr a ni lo. Kohhran member zawng zawngte chu hna thawk tûrin feh chhuak se la. Mi tu mah hi hna ropui tak thawk thei ve lo nia an inhriat avângin ring lovin an kut kuangkuah mai mai suh se. Mahni inen bânsan la. In Hruaitu chu thlîr zâwk rawh. I thawh theih apiang chu inngâitlâwm tak, tih tak zetin leh hmangaihna nên thawk ang che.”—Ellen G. White, in The Advent Review and Sabbath Herald, April 12, 1906.

“Mi tin hian mi dangte hnên atanga kan dawn theih tanpuina kan mamawh theuh a. Pathian chuan keimahni rilrua hna a thawh dân âia nasa mahin mi dangte rilruah pawh hna a thawk thei. Mi hran hran hnêna thilpêk chi hrang hrang pêk a nih chhan chu ‘mi thianghlimte tih famkim nân te, rawngbâwl hna thawh nân te leh Krista taksa siam that nân te a ni a (Eph. 4:12) . . .

“Kan hmaah hian harsatna eng eng a awm reng dâwn. Nimahsela, kan Hruaitu chu zui mai tûr kan ni a, kan harsatnate chu inpumkhatin, kut insuihin kan hmachhawn tlâng zêl ang.”—Ellen G. White, The Upward Look, p. 141.

**Sawi ho tûrte:**

`Hrilhlâwkna thilpêk chungchâng hi ngun takin han ngaihtuah leh la. Eng vângin nge thu hril hi, a awmzia hrilh fiah loh tawng hriat loh âi chuan a pawimawh zâwk? Paula thi sawi awmzia hi i lo hriattheih nân, a tûl a nih chuan 1 Korinth 14 chhiar leh mai ang che.`

`In class-ah Ellen G. White-i nun leh a rawngbâwlna chungchâng sawi ho ula, kan kohhranin hrilhlâwkna thilpêk kawl nia kan ngaih chhan pawh sawi bawk ang che u. He thilpêk a dawn atanga kohhrinin malsâwmna ropui tak tak a dawnte chu eng nge ni? He thilpêk hi a tha thei ang bera hman dân tûr kan hriatna kawng dâl thei thilte chu eng nge ni?`

`Nangmah hmangaih tak taktu che mi pathum atanga panga te kha ngaihtuah la. Engtin nge an hmangaih tak tak che a ni tih i hriat theih? Chu chuan Paula’n thlarau thilpêk chungchâng a sawinaa hmangaihna a sawi tam viau chhan hi a hriatthiamtîr che em?`

`Hmangaihna chu pawimawh êm êm tho mah se, enga ti nge mi chuan thu dik a sawi nge sawi lo tih leh, a thu sawi chu ngaihthlâk tlâk a nih leh nih loh hriatna atâna tehfung awm chhun erawh a nih chuan loh?`
