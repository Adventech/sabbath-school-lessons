---
title:  Atheni atangin Korinth khuaah
date:   29/06/2026
---

`Tirhkohte 17:16–34 chhiar la. Paula hi Korinth khuaa a lo kal hma khân khawiah nge a awm? Chutah chuan eng nge ni a tih?`

Tirhkohte 17:16–34 kan chhiar chuan Korinth khuaa a lo kal hma hian Atheni khuaa mite hnênah thu a hril thu kan hmu a. Thil lang chiang tak chu, hemi tum hian Atheni khua tlawh a lo tum hran lo va, Beroia khuaah khân dodâlna a tawh tâk avângin Atheni khuaah hian a thiante’n an hruai ta daih a ni âwm e (Tirh. 17:13–15).

Atheni khuaa Paula hruaitute chu an theih anga hmaa a hnêna rawn kal ve tûra Timothea leh Sila te a chahna thu nên Beroia lamah an kîr leh ta a (Tirh. 17:15). Tirhkohte 17:16–34-ah hian Timothea leh Sila te a lo nghah chhûnga thil a tih chu kan hmu. Inkhâwmna inah te, dâwr hmunah te leh Areopagi-ah te Isua chanchin a sawi thîn a. Isua chanchin sawi lova a awm theih miau loh avângin a sawi hrâm theih dân kawng a zawng ngat ngat mai thîn a ni.

`Tirhkohte 18:1–11 chhiar la. Paula’n Korinth khua a lo thlen leh a châm chhûng zawnga a tih chu eng nge ni?`

Paula hian chanchin tha hrila a zin chhuah tum hnihnaah Korinth khua hi a tlawh a. Luka sawi dânin he khawpuiah hian kum khat leh a chanve vêl a châm niin a lang.

A dân pângngâi angin chanchin tha hril hna chu Juda-te inkhâwmna inah bawk a han tan leh a (Tirh. 18:4–6). Hetiang hi tum dangah pawh a lo tih thin dân rêng a ni (Tirh. 17:1, 2). Chanchin tha chu Juda-te hnênah hril hmasak phawt tûr niin a ngâi a, chutianga ti tûr chuan Isua pawhin a tirhkohte kha thu a lo pe a nih kha (Tirh. 1:8 en ang che).

Korinth khuaa Sila leh Timothea te an lo thlen hnu chuan thu hril hnaah a insâwr bing ta ngar ngar a, Juda-te hnênah Isua kha Krista a nihzia thahnemngâi takin a sawi fiah thîn (Tirh. 18:5). Korinth khuaa a châm chhûng hian “Pathian thu zirtîrin” a buai char char mai a (Tirh. 18:11). Chutiang taka a thawhrim avâng chuan he thu lâr tak, Korinth mite zînga “Isua Krista, amah ngei khenbeha a awm thu chauh lo chu eng mah rêng hriat ka tum loh kha” (1 Kor. 2:2, NKJV) tih thu hi a lo sawi chhuak hial a ni.

`Paula’n chanchin tha hrilna tûr remchâng awm apiang hmang tangkâia Atheni khawpui leh Korinth khawpuia hna a thawh dân atanga zirlâi zir chhuah theih kan neih chu eng nge ni? Mi dangte hnêna Isua chanchin hrilh theihna remchâng hrang hrangte kha ngaihtuah la, chûngte chu engtin nge i lo hman tangkâi thin?`
