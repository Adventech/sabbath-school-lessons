---
title:  “He khawpuiah hian mi tam tak ka nei si a”
date:   01/07/2026
---

`Tirhkohte 18:4–8 chhiar la. Paula thu hrilin rah a chhuahte chu eng nge ni?`

Paula hi Korinth khuaa chêng Juda-te zînga rawng a bâwlnaah hian duh angin a hlawhtling tâwk lo deuh a. Dodâlna leh tihdudahna eng eng emaw pawh a tawng thîn bawk. Bible chuan, “[Juda-te hian] lo dodâlin an lo sawi chhe thîn” tih min hrilh a (Tirh. 18:6). Grik tawnga “sawi chhia” tih an sawina thumal, _blasphemeô_ hi mihring laka hman a nih chuan “deusawh” tihna emaw, “tihmingchhia” tihna emaw a ni tlângpui thîn. Tawngkam danga sawi chuan, Paula hming tihchhiat tum rânin thil an ti a, chanchin tha hril hna a thawhna kawngah hian a hlawhchham ngei theih nân tan a la nasa hle a ni ang.

Nimahsela, vânneihthlâk takin inkhâwmna ina Paula thu hril hi a lo thlâwn mai chu a lo ni bîk lo va. Pathian chu ama hna viltu leh enkawltu ber a ni tlat alâwm. “[Ka kâ atanga ka thu chhuak chu] eng mah tih nei lovin ka hnênah a lo lêt leh lo vang” tiin a lo sawi tawh a si a (Isaia 55:11). Juda-te zînga thenkhat kha chuan inkhâwmna in hotu Krispa leh a chhûngte hian Messia nia Isua pawma baptisma an chan (Tirh. 18:8) an lo ring miah lo. Anni chauh ni lovin, “Korinth khuaa mi tam tak pawhin [Paula thu hril] hi ringin baptisma an lo chang ta bawk a” (Tirh. 18:8), Krispa’n a a chan avânga chang ve an nih ngei a rinawm.

`Tirhkohte 18:9, 10 chhiar la. Paula’n Korinth khuaa harsatna hrang hrang a tawhte chu engtin nge a lo dawnsawn? Pathianin a fuih dân chu eng nge ni?`

Paula hian inkhâwmna in a chhuahsan hnu lâwk khân phûr leh huaisen phah sawtna tûr thil a tawng a. A zân mumangah Krista rawn inlârin, Isaia 40:10 thu, “Hlau suh la. . . kei i hnênah ka awm alâwm” tih hi a rawn hrilh ta a. Korinth khuaah hian “chak lo leh hlauvin, nasa taka khûr chunga” a awm thu pawh a lo sawi avângin, hetiang thlamuanna thu hi a mamawh êm êm ang tih a chiang. Dodâlna nasa tak a tawh avângin Beroia khua ata Atheni lamah a lo tlânchhe tawh a. Korinth khua pawh hi dodâlna namên lo a tawh avâng bawkin a chhuahsan a tûl ve leh ngei dâwn niin a ngâi hman ngei ang. Nimahsela, chutiang chu tûn tumah chuan a thleng dâwn lo. “He khawpuia hian mi tam tak ka nei si a” (Tirh. 18:10) tiin Isua’n a rawn hrilh a. Chûng mite hnêna chhandamna chanchin tha hriltu atân chuan Paula hi a hmang dâwn a ni.

`Isaia 41:10 chhiar la. He châng tâwi tê-a thutiam ropui tak tak kan hmuhte chu eng nge ni? Chûng thutiamte chuan engtin nge i nunah a tha zâwngin nghawng a neih ang?`
