---
title:  Paula, Pathianin a koh Isua tirhkoh chu
date:   28/06/2026
---

Paula hian Korinth khuaa mite hnêna lehkha a thawn hi “Pathian duhzâwng anga” koh Isua tirhkoh a nih thu nên bul a tan a (1 Kor. 1:1; 2 Kor. 1:1 pawh chhiar bawk la). Isuaah chuan tu nge a nih tih an inhriat dân hi a chian êm avângin chumi chu lehkhathawn hrang hrang bultanna atân pawh a hmang ta thîn rêng a ni.

`1 Korinth 1:1 leh Rom 1:1 chhiar la. Paula rawngbâwlnaa thil pawimawh tak tak pahnih awmte chu eng nge ni? (Gal. 1:1 pawh en bawk ang che.)`

Paula hian koh a nihna leh tirhkoh a nihna te chu Pathian duhzâwng ang tihpuitlinna a nih thu a sawi a. Kohna a dawn chu mihringte hnên ata ni lovin, Pathian hnên ata ngei a ni tih a ring nghet hle (Gal. 1:1). Jeremia ang bawk khân a nu pum chhûnga a la awm lâia koh tawh niin (Jer. 1:5), chu chu Pathian khawngaihna vâng liau liau a ni a (Gal. 1:15), Jentail-te zînga Krista chanchin tha hril tûra Pathianin a lo ruat sa rêng a ni.

1 Korinth 15:8-ah chuan amah pawh tho leh Krista inlârna hmutute zînga mi a nih ve thu a sawi a (1 Kor. 15:5–7). A châng dawt leh maiah pawh tirhkoh ni tûra kohna a dawn chu Krista nên an intawnna rah chhuah a ni ni âwmin a sawi nghe nghe (1 Kor. 15:9–11).

“Isua tirhkoh” tih hian thil pawimawh tak tak entîr a nei a. A hmasa ber leh pawimawh ber ni bawk chu Isua ngeiin a tirh a ni tihna a ni. Amaherawhchu, he tawngkam vêk hi Krista bâwih a nih thu sawina atân pawh a lo hmang ve tho bawk a (Rom 1:1, Titus 1:1, Gal. 1:10), chutiangin, thuhriltu leh zirtîrtu a nih thu sawina atân pawh a hmang bawk (1 Tim. 2:7, 2 Tim. 1:11). Thu a hril emaw, mite a zirtîr emaw pawh ni se, a hril leh a zirtîr chu Krista a ni zêl thîn a. A tâwi zâwnga sawi chuan, Paula hi Isua’n a koh leh leh Isua tâna thawk tirhkoh a ni.

Paula tirhkoh nihnaa hmun pawimawh ber changtu chu Isua a ni a, chu mai lovin, a nuna hmun pawimawh ber luahtu pawh Isua tho a ni. Paula ngaihtuahna leh rilru zawng zawng hi Isua’n a luah hneh hle thîn a. Chumi a nihzia chu 1 Korinth tîr lam leh lâwmthu a sawina chângahte hian Isua hming vawi eng emaw zât a lam nawn hian chiang takin a tilang (châng kua lekah hian vawi kua zet a lam a ni). Paula hian Isua a hmangaih êm avângin a chanchin sawi a bâng thei lo va. A enkawl kohhranhote chuan a pawimawh bera Krista an lo dah ve theih nân a chanchin hrilh zêl mai a duh thîn. Amah hi tirhkoh ni tûra koh a nih lâiin ringtute erawh Isua zuitu rinawm takte ni tûra koh an ni ve thung a, Lalpa’n a kohna zâwn apiangah rinawm takin Lalpa tân an thawk thîn ang.

Paula kha tirhkoh ni tûra koh a ni a. Nang eng ni tûrin nge koh i nih ve le?

`Chumi chu koh i nihna a ni tih engtin nge i hriat theih? Koh i nihna i hre lo a nih chuan Pathian nên in lên dunna kawngah thil dik lo a awm a ni thei ang em? Awm ta se, eng nge ni ang?`
