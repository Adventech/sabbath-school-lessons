date:   03/07/2026

Ellen G. White-i lehkhabu ziah, Tirhkohte Thiltih bung 24, “Korinth Khuaah” (phêk 243–254) chhiar ang che.

“Tirhkoh Paula’n Korinth khuaa chanchin tha a hril dân hi Atheni khuaa rawng a lo bâwl tawh dân nên kha chuan a inang lo hle. . . . Inhnialna leh thuril sawi hona lam chu pumpêlh hrâm zêl a tum tawh a, Korinth khuaa mite zîngah hi chuan Isua Krista, amah ngei khenbeha a awm thu chauh lo chu eng mah dang hriat a tum ta lo a ni”—Ellen G. White, Tirhkohte Thiltih, p. 244.

“Paula hian hlawhtlinna eng emaw chen chu a nei tho na a, chutih rual chuan, hlauhthâwn rûkna lian tak a nei deuh reng bawk. Korinth khua hi kohhran phunna atâna hmun tha ber niah a ngâi lêm lo va; chu vâng chuan, chhuahsan leh mai a rilrûk ta a. . . .

“Chutia rawngbâwlna atâna hmun îtawm zâwk pan tûra chhuah leh mai a tum mêk lâi chuan . . . . zân inlârna hmangin a hnênah Pathian a rawn inlan a, ‘Hlau suh la, ngâwi lovin thu sawi zêl zâwk rawh; . . . . he khuaah hian mi tam tak ka nei si a’ tiin a hrilh a. Hei hi Korinth khuaa la châm rih tûra Pathianin a rawn hrilhna a ni tih a lo hrethiam mai a, a chi lo tuh tawh kha Pathianin a rawn to chhuahtîr ngei dâwn a ni tih pawh a ring nghet ta hle bawk. . . . tichuan, Isua Krista zirtîrna zui duh kohhran lian tak din a lo ni ta a ni.”—Ellen G. White, Sketches from the Life of Paul, pp. 106, 107.

“Paula hi Korinth khuaah hian kum khat leh thla ruk zet rawngbâwlin a awm a. Amaherawhchu, Korinth khuaah chauh rawng a bâwl thîn tihna chu a ni lo. . . . Korinth khawpui hi hmunpui bera hmangin, hmun dang dangah pawh chanchin tha hrilin a vâk kual thîn a. . . . Chutichuan, kohhran eng emaw zât a lo ding ta a ni. . . . Hêng a kohhran enkawl hrang hrangte hnênah hian anmahni a zin chhuahsan hlâna an lo chhiar tûr lehkhathawnte a ziak thîn a, chûng a lehkhathawn ziah zînga thenkhat chu a thu pawh a rum hlawm angreng viau nghe nghe. Amaherawhchu, Pathian thu ni angin an lo pawm hlawm tlângpui a. . . . Kohhran hruaitute’n anmahni kohhranah theuh an lo chhiar thîn a ni.”—Ellen G. White, Sketches from the Life of Paul, p. 109.

**Sawi ho tûrte:**

`Paula hian Isua tirhkoh a ni tih a pawm thlap a, chumi ni tûra kotu chu Pathian a ni tih pawh a hre chiang hle bawk. Enga ti nge kan nihna leh Pathianin ti tûra min hrilhte hi kan hriat chian a pawimawh êm êm?`

`Thawk khat lâi chu Paula hian Korinth khuaa chanchin tha hril hna a thawh chu bânsana, Korinth khua pawh chhuahsan daih a châk rum rum mai a. Chutiang ti lo tûra a rilru thlâktîr lehtu chu eng nge ni? Hei hian chanchin tha hril hna thawh bânsan mai duhna rilru kan put hunah engtin nge min tanpui theih ang?`

`Korinth khuaa ringtu awmte hi an chheh vêla awm ve-te nun dân leh thil thlîr dânin a hneh vena chen thui tak a awm a. Hetiang hi keini tûnlâi huna mite zîngah pawh a ni ve tho mai. Engtin nge khawvêla awm (Joh. 17:11, 15) ni chung hian “khawvêla thil awm—tisa châkna te, mit châkna te, dam chhûng ropuina lem te” (1 Joh. 2:16) hneh theih loh kan nih theih ang? Kan chheh vêla mite thil tih dân leh nun dânin kan kohhran a tha zâwnga a hneh mêk dân kawng dangte chu eng nge ni?`
