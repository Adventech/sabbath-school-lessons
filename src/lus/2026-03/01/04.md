---
title:  Korinth khawpui
date:   30/06/2026
---

`Tirhkohte 18:1–3, 1 Korinth 5:9–11 leh 1 Korinth 8:4 te hi chhiar la. Hêng chângte atang hian Korinth khawpuia chêngte sum leh pâi dinhmun, a rilru sûkthlêk leh an sakhaw nun eng engte nge hriat theih kan neih?`

Korinth hi hmânlâi khawvêla khawpui pawimawh tak pakhat a ni a, sumdâwnna lama an hmuingîl êm avângin a hmingthang hle thîn. Kum 146 B.C. khân Rom-hovin hnehin an chhu chhe vek a; mahse, kum 44 B.C. khân Julius Caeser-a’n Roman colony atân a din thar leh ta a. Chu Korinth, Julius Caesar-a’n a din thar leh tâk chu Thuthlung Thara kan hmuh hi a ni. Paula rawngbâwl hun lâi vêl khân Atheni khawpui elpui ber pakhat a ni a, Atheni a khûmna lâi pawh kawng tam tak a awm nghe nghe. Korinth khawpui hian lawng chawlhna hmun pawimawh tak tak pahnih a nei a, chûngte chuan sumdâwngtute tân kawng zau tak hawnsakin, mipuite tân pawh thil lei leh hralh vêl a awlsam phah hle.

Paula’n rawngbâwlna atâna Korinth a thlan chhan pawh hi khawpui pawimawh tak a nih vâng leh, a awmna hmun a fuh êm vâng a ni. “Paula hi chanchin tha theh darh theihna kawng remchâng tha tak hawnsak a ni a. Korinth khawpuiah hian kohhran a din thei a nih ngat chuan khawvêl hmun hrang hrangah a darh thuai dâwn tih a chiang reng mai.”—Ellen G. White, Sketches From the Life of Paul, p. 99.

Chu bâkah, sumdâwnna hmunpui a nihna hi Paula tân pawh mahni intum chawp nih a awlsam phah sawt dâwn bawk a, chanchin tha hril pahin puan in a siam ang a, chûngte chu hralhin a mamawh zualpui chu a phuhrûk zêl thei ang (Tirh. 18:2, 3). Thil chiang chu, hetiang khawpui lian leh hausa bawk sia chanchin tha hril hna thawh chu hautak êm êm dâwn a, harsatna chi hrang hrang hmachhawn tûr pawh a tam hle ang. Korinth khawpui hi sakhaw hrang hrang betute fuan khâwmna hmun a ni a, Rom-ho pathian—Apollo te, Atheni te leh Aphrodite te chibai bûkna atâna an sak biak in mâwi tak tak awmin, Aigupta mite pathian—Sarapis leh Isis betute pawh an awm teuh bawk.

Chutianga sakhaw hrang hrang inpawlh sawpna hmun chu a nih bâkah, mipat-hmeichhiatna hman kawnga an thiang zau êm avângin khawvêl hriat an hlawh bawk a. Grik mi chanchin ziaktu hmingthang tak Strabo-a chuan Korinth khuaa awm Aphrodite biak inah amah chibai bûkna tûra hmeichhe inzuar 1,000 zet an awm thin thu a sawi. A thil sawi hi mi thiam tam tak chuan âwih lovin, Atheni khuaa mite’n Korinth khua an er avânga an puh hmingchhiatna mai niah an ngaihsak a. Eng pawh chu lo ni se, hmâsâng khawvêlah khân sakhaw hmun thianghlima nawhchizuarte khâwi khâwm hi chu an thil chîn than tak a ni ve hrim hrim tih kan hria. Chu vâng chuan, Korinth khawpuiah pawh hian hmun dang ang thovin mipat-hmeichhiatna hman khaw lohna a hluar hle tih a chiang a. Milem biakna leh dik lo taka mipat-hmeichhiatna hman kha a ni tin nuna bet tlat a nih avângin, chumi chungchâng sawina chu Korith khuaa mite hnêna Paula lehkhathawn pahnihahte pawh hian kan hmu tam hle rêng a ni. 	

`Paula hian milem be mite leh mipat-hmeichhiatna hman kawnga thiang zau tak takte chênna khawpui Korinth-ah chanchin tha hril hna a thawk a ni a, a hautak hle ang tih pawh a rinawm. Kan tûnlâi khawvêl nun dânah hian chanchin tha hril darhna tûr dâl thei eng thilte nge awm? Chûngte chu engtin nge kan hneh theih ang? Korinth khawpui nun leh kan tûnlâi khawpuite nun hian danglamna a nei em? A nei a nih chuan engtiang taka danglam nasa nge ni?`
