---
title:  Korinth khuaa mite hnêna Paula lehkhathawnte
date:   02/07/2026
---

`1 Korinth 1:11–13; 1 Korinth 4:14; 1 Korinth 5:11; 1 Korinth 7:1; leh 1 Korinth 14:37, 40 te hi chhiar la. 2 Korinth 1:12, 2 Korinth 2:9, 2 Korinth 11:3, leh 2 Korinth 13:10 te pawh hi chhiar bawk ang che. Hêng thute hian Paula’n Korinth khuaa mite hnêna lehkha a thawn chhan kan hriat theih nân engtin nge min tanpui?`

Korinth khuaa mite hnêna lehkha a thawn pakhatna hi Ephesi khuaa a awm lâia a ziah a ni a (1 Kor. 16:5–9). A hnênah hian Kloii chhûngte chu lo kalin, Korinth kohhranah buaina eng eng emaw a thlen thu an rawn hrilh a (1 Kor. 1:11). 1 Korinth 1–6-ah hian Kloii chhûngte’n an hrilh an buaina hrang hrangte chu sawiin, chûng zînga thenkhat chu an zînga inthen hranna awm te, dik lo taka mipat-hmeichhiatna hmanna awm te, rorêltute hmaa an inkhing tawn te leh nawhchizawrhna te a ni. Tin, an hnên atang vêk hian zawhna eng eng emaw a dawng bawk a (1 Kor. 7:1). Chûng an zawhnate chu bung 7 atang hian chhâng tanin, chûngte chu inneih leh inthen chungchâng te, nupui pasal nei lova lêng tûlte chungchâng te, milem hnêna hlan tawh sa ei a thian leh thian loh thu te, Pathian biak inkhâwm lâia awm dân tûr te, thlarau thilpêkte hman hman dân tûr chungchâng te leh thawhlehna chungchâng te a ni. Korinth khuaa kohhranhote hi tih an har bâkah, an rilru a puitling lo hlawm êm êm bawk a. In kohhran pawh khân harsatna tam tak in nei ve tho a ni maithei; mahse, Korinth kohhranin harsatna an neih hi chu a la khirh fe zâwk tho ang.

Korinth khuaa mite hnêna Paula lehkhathawn pakhatna hi kan tûnlâi hun atân pawh lehkhabu pawimawh tak a ni ve tho a. Eng vângin maw? E le, Korinth kohhranin buaina a tawh thenkhat hi kan tûnlâi kohhan tam tak pawhin an tawh ve mêk tho a ni lo vem ni? He lehkhathawn hian thil min hriattîr duh tam tak a nei a. “Paula lehkhathawnte zînga hausa ber, hman tangkâi theih ber leh thiltithei ber pakhat a ni.”—Ellen G. White, Tirhkohte Thiltih, p. 301.

Korinth khuaa mite hnêna Paula lehkhathawn zât hi a vâi chuan pathum emaw, pali emaw lâi a ni maithei (2 Kor. 10:9 chhiar teh). 1 Korinth a ziah hma hian lehkhathawn pakhat emaw chu a lo ziak tawh niin a lang a (1 Kor. 5:9); mahse, chu chu a lo bo thlau hman tawh a ni ang. Tin, 2 Korinth a ziah hma pawh hian, mithiamte’n “thinrim lehkha” tia an sawi mai thin chu a lo ziak bawk a (2 Kor. 2:3, 4, 9; 2 Kor. 7:8); mahse, chu pawh chu hâi chhuah zawh a ni tawh lo. Mi thenkhat chuan 1 Korinth hi a sawina niin an ring a; tin, 2 Korinth-a châng thenkhat hi nia ngâi pawh an awm nual bawk.

2 Korinth atang hian Korinth kohhran member-te khân an chheh vêla chêngte nun dân an entawnna chin thui tak a awm tih kan hre thei a. Tûnlâi hunah pawh kan kohhran tibuai êm êmtu la ni ta zêl—inelna te, thiltihtheihna te, hausakna te hi an thlahlel êm êm thîn. Chutiang chu a nih lâiin, Paula hian a pawimawh bera Krista dahna nun nei tûrin a sâwm a, khawvêl hi chanchin tha tarmit nên thlîr thîn tûrin a chah bawk. Keini pawh hian chanchin tha tarmit hmanga kan khawvêl kan thlîr thin a pawimawh hle a ni.

`2 Korinth 2:4 chhiar leh la. He thu hian Paula’n Korinth khuaa mite a ngaihsakzia engtin nge min hrilh? Mi dangte ngaihsak chu sawi loh, an lakah rilru tha lo tak kan lo pu zâwk reng lo maw?`
