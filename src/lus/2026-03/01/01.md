### Chhiar tûrte
1 Kor. 1:1, Gal. 1:1, Tirh. 17:16–34, 1 Kor. 5:9–11, Tirh. 18:4–10, 2 Kor. 2:4.

> <p>Châng vawn</p>
> “Zan khat chu Paula hnênah inlârna hmangin Lalpa’n heti hian a sawi a: ‘Hlau suh la, ngâwi lovin thu sawi zêl zâwk rawh; ka awmpui tlat dâwn che a, tina tûr chein tu ma’n i chungah kut an thlâk lo vang, he khuaah hian ka mite tam tak an awm si a’ a ti a” (Tirh. 18:9, 10, NRSV).

England ram atanga lo kal missionary ropui tak William Carey-a khân a mamawhte leina atân pheikhawk chhe siam that hna a thawk thîn a, a hna thawh tak erawh chu thlarau bo seng khâwm a nih thu a sawi thîn an ti.

Chutiang deuh chuan, Paula pawh hian a khawsak sênso phuhrûkna atân puan in siam hna a thawk thîn a (Tirh. 18:1–3); mahse, a hna thawh tak chu Krista hnêna mite hruai thlen tho a ni.

Tûn kârah hian Korinth khawpuia awm kohhran hnêna Paula rawngbâwl dân tlângpui kan thlîr ho thuak thuak ang a. He kohhran hi harsatna hrang hrangin a bâwm a ni ber mai. Chûng an harsatna tawhte zînga tam tak chu, kum 2,000 dâwn liam taa mi daih ni mah se, tûnlâi huna kan kohhranhote pawhin kan tawh ve mêk tho a ni hlawm a. Kum eng emaw zât chhûng Kristian lo ni ve tawh emaw, a nih loh pawhin kohhran rawngbâwlna kawnga lo inhmang ve hrep tawh emaw i nih chuan, “Kristian kohhran pâwl hrang hrang zîngah hian harsatna nei miah lo a awm ang em le” tiin i ngaihtuah ngeiin a rinawm. A chhânna chiang tak chu, awm hauh lo tih ang.

Paula hian Korinth khuaah khân chona/harsatna hrang hrang a hmachhawn a, a hmachhawn dân pawh Kraws thuchah nên a ni zêl thîn (1 Kor. 2:2). Vawiina chona/harsatna hrang hrang kan neihte pawh hi rinawm taka Kraws thuchah pawmna leh hrilna hmanga kan lo hmachhawn ve zêl a pawimawh hle a. Tûn kârah leh tûn kuartar chhûng zawng hian 1 leh 2 Korinth thuchah hi keimahni tân ngei ziah a lo ni tih kan zir chhuak dâwn a ni.
