### Kristian Nun leh Thuhretu Nihna

Lehkhathawn ziah hi hmasâng ata tawh kan la chîn chhunzawm tâk zêl a ni a, kan ziah dân hmang erawh a danglam ta deuh thung. Lehkhaphêk-a ziak tawh lovin, social media hrang hrang kan hmang ta deuh ber hlawm a. Nimahsela, a nihna takah chuan email leh WhatsApp te pawh hian hmânlâia an lo hman thin lehkhathawnin a thawh ang tho kha an la thawk a ni tih kan hre theuh âwm e. Mi dangte hnênah an hriat atâna tûl kan tihte hrilhin, kan ngaihdân leh ngaihtuahna te kan hriattîr a, chutiang chuan mi hrang hrangte kan inthlun zawm kual vek a nih hi.

Eng vângin nge lehkhathawn kan ziah thin? A chhânna ni thei pakhat chu mi dangte hnêna thil eng emaw hriattîr duh kan neih vâng a ni. Chu chu tirhkoh Paula’n a lehkhathawnte a ziah chhan pawh a ni rêng a. An hnênah sawi duh tam tak nei mah se, hmâi chhana hrilhna hun tha a nei reng thîn lo.

Chu vâng chuan, an lo chhiar atân lehkhathawn a ziak ta mai thîn a, hêng a lehkhathawn ziah hrang hrang—entîr nân, Korinth khuaa mite hnêna lehkha a thawnahte pawh hian Pathian thu pawimawh tak tak kan hmu a. Chûng zînga thenkhat chu: “Isua Krista chanchin leh amah chu khenbeha a awm tâk thu chauh lo chu in zîngah eng mah rêng hriat ka tum loh kha” (1 Kor. 2:2, ESV) tih leh, “Kan Lalpa Isua Krista khawngaihna chu, a retheihna avanga in lo hausak theihna tûrin, hausa mah sela nangmahni avâng ngeiin a lo retheih tâk kha in hre si a” (2 Kor. 8:9, ESV) tih te hi a ni. Tin, 1 Korinth 13-a hmangaihna chanchin a phuah chhuahna hla pawh hi a ril teh a nia!

Chutih rualin, Korinth khuaa mite hnêna Paula lehkhathawn ziahte kan han chhiar hian mak kan tih deuh deuh tûr eng eng emaw kan hmu dâwn a, a chhan pawh kohhran chhûnga buaina lian tham tak tak awm, entîr nân, dik lo taka mipat-hmeichhiatna hmanna te mai bâkah, kohhran member-te zînga pâwl pâwla inthen hrannain a hrin chhuah buaina ho tham tê tê te pawh a ziah tel nual vâng a ni. In kohhran khân harsatna chi hrang hrang in hmachhawn nia i hria a nih chuan, Paula’n Korinth khuaa chin fel ngâi buaina chi hrang hrang a hmachhawnte hi hre tûrin lo inpeih sa tawp mai la. In kohhranin buaina in tawhte kha ani buaina tawhte âi hi chuan a lo tê tham hle maithei a nia. An hnêna Paula’n lehkha a thawn lâi vêla an awm dân pawh hetiang hi a ni tawh nâka lâiin, a thawn hmaa an awm dân phei chu a la tawp lehzual zâwk ta lâi a! Chutiang a nihzia chu a lehkhawthawnte hi i chhiar anga i hmu mai ang.

Korinth kohhranin buaina leh harsatna a tawhte hi chu eng pawh lo ni se, an hnêna Paula’n lehkha a thawn hi chhiar a phûrawm angreng viau lawi a, a phûrawm chhan erawh an buaina tawhte hi lovin, thiam taka Paula’n a chin fel dân hi a ni zâwk. Kohhran member-te chu an anmahni inen fiah a, an nungchang leh an chheh vêla awm ve milem be mite nungchang chu Isua Krista chanchin tha hmanga khâikhin tûrin a chah a, chutianga a tih chhan chu Kraws thu châwisân a tum vâng a ni. Amah Paula’n a lehkhathawn danga tawngkam a lo hmante hi han hawh lawk ila, chanchin tha thuchah ni lo thuchah dang rêng rêng chu “ânchhedawng” nia ngaih vek tûr a ni tawp mai (Gal. 1:8, 9).

Paula rawngbâwl hun lâi vêl hian Korinth chu a hausak avângin a hmingthang hle a, lawngchawlhna hmun lian tak tak, in tha leh ropui tak tak a awm bâkah, lawng siam leh bêl vuak lam pawh an ti nasa hle bawk. Tin, sumdâwnna hmunpui pawimawh tak a ni a. Nimahsela, dik lo taka mipat-hmeichhiatna an hman nasat avâng te, sakhaw hrang hrang betute fuan khâwmna hmun a nih avâng te leh pathian hrang hrangte puala biak in an sak te avâng pawhin hriat an hlawh viau bawk. Dik taka sawi chuan an ni tin nun dân kha milem biakna thil tenawm tak takte’n a tikhaw lo a ni ber a. Hêngte kan han sawi kual deuh duah chhan chu Paula’n he khawpuia chêng Kristiante a ngaihtuah êm êm chhan leh, anmahni a zilhna thute hi eng vânga sawi nge ni tih kan lo hriatthiam theih nân a ni.

Tûn kuartar-ah hian Korinth khuaa mite hnêna Paula lehkhathawn pahnihte kan zir dâwn a. Hêng Thuthlung Thara lehkhabu pawimawh tak tak pahnihah hian chanchin tha chu Kristian nun leh thuhretu nihna laimu nia sawi niin, chumi hmang chuan engkim hi teh vek tûr a ni a ti. Vânram pana kan kal mêk lâi hian a mimal ang leh a kohhran ang pawha harsatna kan hmachhawnte chu eng pawh lo ni se, Krista tâna hna kan thawhna kawnga zawhna harsa tak tak kan tawnte chhânna ni tûr chu Korinth khuaa mite tân pawha chhânna lo ni tawh tho kha a ni a. Chu chu, “Isua Krista leh amah chu khenbehin a awm” tih hi a ni (1 Kor. 2:2, NKJV).

Isua chu a lo kal têp tawh a. Tûn hi Kristaa kan inpumkhat lehzual hun, tûn hma zawng âia Thlarau Thianghlim lo thlen theih nâna kan inhawn zau lehzual hun, thahnemngâi taka thlarau thilpêk kan dawnte kan hman hun leh tho leh kan Lalpa awmpuina nasa lehzuala kan chan hun tûr a ni a. Kristian rawngbâwl hna dik taka kan thawh hun tûr pawh a ni bawk. Chu vâng chuan, enkawltu niin chanchin tha hril hna thawk ila, zirtîrna dik lote dovin thlarau lam indona chu bei ila, khawngaihna, hmangaihna leh inpâwl hona lamah i thang lian zêl bawk ang u. Tûn hi nghet taka din hun leh rinawm taka Kraws thuchah kan vawn tlat hun a ni a, Korinth khuaa mite hnêna Paula lehkhathawnte hian chumi ti tûr chuan min zirtîr a ni.
