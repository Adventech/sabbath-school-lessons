Soma Igitabo cyitwa _Ibyakozwe n'Intumwa_ cyanditswe na Ellen G. White, ku rupapuro rwa 199-206 [ingeri ya 2010], icyigisho cyitwa "Ubutumwa Bwakiriwe“.

"Abantu bihanganiye imibabaro ikomeye kurusha iyindi yose, ni bo bageza ku bandi bantu ihumure rikomeye cyane, bakageza imirasire y'izuba aho bajya hose. Abantu nk'abo baba baracishijwe bugufi kandi bakagirwa beza n'imibabaro banyuzemo; ntibatakaza icyizere bafitiye Imana igihe amakuba abagezeho, ahubwo bikomereza ku rukundo ibakunda. Abantu nk'abo ni igihamya kizima cyo kwitabwaho n'Imana kuje urukundo.“-Ellen G. White, _God's Amazing Grace_, p. 122.

"Imibereho y'umukristo wiyeguriye Imana ihora ikwirakwiza hose umucyo n'ihumure n'amahoro. Irangwa no kubonera, ubwenge, kwiyoroshya, no kuba ingirakamaro. Iyoborwa n'urukundo rutihugiraho rweza imbaraga ihindura abandi. Yuzuyemo Kristo, kandi aho nyirayo agiye hose ihasiga ikimenyetso cy'umucyo."-Ellen G. White, _Ubuntu bw'Imana Butangaje`, p.122.

"Intumwa Pawulo yabonye ko ari ngombwa gucyaha ikibi mu itorero, ariko ntiyananiwe kwitegeka igihe yarimo acyaha ikosa. Yashishikariye gusobanura impamvu y'ibyo yakoze. Mbega ukuntu yakoranye ubwitonzi kugira ngo atere abantu kumutekereza nk'inshuti y'uwari waguye mu ikosa! Yabafashije gusobanukirwa ko kubababaza na we byamusabye kubabara. Yatumye batekereza ko inyungu ze zari zo nyungu zabo.“-Ellen G. White, in _The SDA Bible Commentary_, vol. 6. p. 1094.
 **IBIBAZO:**

`Mu 2 Abakorinto 2:1-14, Pawulo ashimangira ubunyangamugayo bwe mu murimo w'ubugabura akora. Kuki iyi miterere ari ingenzi cyane mu murimo wo kugabura iby'Imana?`

`Kuba Pawulo yarahinduye gahunda y'urugendo rwe ni iki bitubwira ku birebana no kuba umuntu wemera ko habaho impinduka mu murimo w'ubugabura wa Gikristo? Ni ukubera iki ari ingenzi kwemera ko habaho impinduka igihe zikenewe?`

`Pawulo yagize umubabaro no guhagarika umutima mu murimo we. Ibi byerekana neza ko abayobozi b'itorero ari abantu bashobora kugerwaho n'umubabaro nk'uko bigenda no ku bandi bantu. Ni iki abagize itorero bashobora gukora kugira ngo baborohereze mu murimo wabo?`

`Pawulo arengurira ku kutaruhuka kwe (2 Abakorinto2:13) mbere gato yuko avuga ibyo kuneshereza muri Kristo (2 Abakorinto 2:14). Ni mu buhe buryo yashoboraga kuvuga ku ntege nke ze ndetse no ku mbaraga ze icyarimwe? Twebwe dushobora kubivugaho dute?`
