---
title:  Kwiyorosha no Kuba Umunyakuri
date:   24/08/2026
---

Ejo hashize twize ko urukundo Pawulo yakundaga Abakorinto rwagarajwe n'uko yabahumurizaga mu bibarushya, nk'uko na we yari yarahawe n'Imana ihumure mu mibabaro ye (2 Abakorinto 1:1-11). Uyu munsi turabona ko urukundo yabakundaga na none rwagaragariye mu bunyangamugayo we n'abakozi bagenzi be bagaragarije abagize itorero ry'i Korinto.

`Soma 2 Abokorinto 1: 12-14 ushingiye ku bivugwa mu 2 Abakorinto 2:17 no mu 2 Abakorinto 4:2. Ni mu buhe buryo kuba umunyakuri kwa Pawulo guhishura urukundo yakundaga Abakorinto?

Mu 2 Abakorinto 1:12-14 hatangira icyigwa Pawulo azakomeza kwimbikamo mu bice bisigaye by'urwandiko rwe. Ubunyangamugayo bwe no kuba intumwa kwe byagiye bikemangwa na bamwe mu bantu b'i Korinto. Batekerezaga ko Pawulo afite imyifatire yo kujarajara no kudafata icyemezo, ibyo bikaba bitari bikwiriye mu murimo w'intumwa. Nk'igisubizo cye, Pawulo ashimangira ko we na bagenzi be babereye abo bantu inyangamugayo ku rwego rukomeye cyane.

Aya magambo abiri yerekana imyifatire ya Pawulo na bagenzi be: kwiyoroshya no kuba abanyakuri (2 Abakorinto 1:12). Ijambo "kwiyoroshya" rikomoka ku ijambo ry'ikigiriki _haplotēs_. Hano rikoreshwa berekana ubunyangamugayo umuntı agira mu magambo no mu myifatire; muri make, ryerekana kwera mu migambi (Abefeso 6:5; Abakolosayi 3:22). Mu rundi ruhande, amagambo "kuba umunyakuri“ (akomoka ku ijambo ry'ikigiriki eilikrineia) na ryo ryerekana ubunyangamugayo no kwera mu migambi.

Abakorinto ntibari bakwiriye gushidikanya ku kuba imigambi ya Pawulo isobanutse. Asobanura ko ukwiyoroshya kwe no kuba umunyakuri bifite inkomoko ku Mana. Iki gitekerezo cyomvikana neza mu "Ngeri Nshya ya Bibiliya y'Abongereza" ivuga iti "imigambi iboneye no kuba umunyakuri nahawe n'Imana" (2 Abakorinto 1:12). Muri uyu murongo, Pawulo yongeraho ko iyi mico y'umugabura tuyihabwa "ku bw'ubuntu bw'Imana."

Bisa n'aho abarwanyaga Pawulo basobanuraga nabi amagambo ye, mu byo yari yaranditse mbere (2 Abakorinto 1:13. 14). Pawulo abahamiriza ko ibyo yari agambiriye byari bisobanutse kandi byumvikana. Yari azi adashidikanya ko gutungana kw'amagambo ye, imigambi, ndetse n'ibikorwa bye byajyaga kuzasobanuka"ku munsi w'Umwami wacu Yesu“

`Mbese ni ibiki byakubaycho wowe ubwawe mu bijyanye no gukemangwa no kurwanywa kw'imigambi n'intego byawe, nubwo byabaga bisobanutse neza kandi nta buryarya bubirimo? Ni iki ibyo bik wigisha ku birebana n'uko ukwiriye kwitonda igihe ukemanga imigambi y'undi muntu?`
