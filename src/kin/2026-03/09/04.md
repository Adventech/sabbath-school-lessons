---
title:  Guhindira Gahunda Kubwo Urukundo
date:   25/08/2026
---

Twabonye ko bamwe mu Bakorinto bashidikanyaga ku migambi ya Pawulo n'urukundo rwe. Uyu munsi, turareba impamvu imwe yihariye: guhindura gahunda z'ingendo ze (2 Abakorinto 1:15-2:4).

`Soma 1 Abakorinto 16:5-7. Gahunda y'urugendo Pawulo yari yabanje kugira yari iyihe?`

Pawulo yari yarigeze kujya i Korinto mbere. Dukurikije ibyanditswe mu 1 Abakorinto 16:5, 6, yari yaragambiriye kunyura i Makedoniya igihe yari kuba agaruka i Korinto, maze wenda akamara i Korinto igihe cy'imbeho. Igihe yari kuba avuye i Korinto, yari kujya i Yudeya ajyanye amaturo yo gufasha abakene b'i Yerusalemu yari yasonzoranyirijwe adakuwe muri Makedoniya gusa ahubwo akuwe no muri Akaya, muri Garatiya no muri Asiya. Nyamara, yahinduye gahunda ze kubera amakuru mabi yari yazaniwe na Timoteyo ayavanye i Korinto (1 Abakorinto 4:17; 1 Abakorinto 16:10; 2 Abakorinto 1:1).

Pawulo yari afite umugambi wo kuva muri Efeso agahita ajya i Korinto atagize ahandi anyura maze agakemura ibibazo yari yabwiwe na Timoteyo byari bihari. Inzira nshya yajyaga kunyuramo yabaye kuva muri Efeso akajya i Korinto, agakomeza i Makedoniya, akagaruka i Korinto, noneho akajya i Yudeya (2 Abakorinto 1:15, 16). Yavuye Efeso ajya i Korinto, ariko nyuma yaho agaruka muri Efeso. Gahunda ze zarahindutse. Ntiyasubiye i Korinto nk'uko yari yabigambiriye, nibura muri uwo mwanya, kuko urugendo yari aherutsemo rutari rwabaye rwiza. Bityo rero, yagarutse muri Efeso ahubwo arabandikira. Yahisemo kuboherereza urwandiko aho kurushaho kuzambya ibintu binyuze mu kongera kubasura (2 Abakorinto 2:1, 3).

Impamvu z'uruzinduko Pawulo yari aherutsemo zasobanuwe nabi. Bamwe mu bantu b'i Korinto bavuze ko atari umuntu wo kwiringirwa kandi ko atabakundaga bihagije (2 Abakorinto  1:17). Nk'igisubizo yatanze ku byo bamuregaga, yerekeje amaso y'Abakorinto ku butumwa bwiza bwa Kristo. Yabaye indahemuka ku migambi ye yo gusura Abakorinto igihe hari kuba habonetse umwanya mwiza, nk'uko Imana yabaye indahemuka mu gusohoza amasezerano yari yarabahereye muri Kristo (2 Abakorinto 1:18-22).

"Ibyo Imana yasezeranije byose, muri we ni mo " Yee" iri. Ni cyo gituma ari we udutera kuvuga ngo "Amen," ngo Imana ihimbazwe natwe" (2 Abakorinto 1:20)

Bityo rero, igisubizo yatanze nticyari uruvange ruteye urujijo rwa  "Yee“ na "oya" bitewe n'uko ibintu bimeze, nk'uko babivugaga, ahubwo cyari "yee buri gihe," mbese nk'uko umurimo Imana ikorera muri Kristo ari "yee buri gihe" (2 Abakorinto 1:19).

Ku bw'iyi mpamvu rero. icyatumye Pawulo yandikira Abakorinto urwandiko mu cyimbo cyo kubasura rwari urukundo rutaryarya yari abafitiye, si ikinyuranyo cyarwo (2 Abakorinto 2:4). Urundi ruzinduko rwari kubaho nyuma y'urwo rwari rubabaje rwajyaga kubongerera umubabaro, ntirubazanire ibyishimo yari yagambiriye kubatera kugira ku bwo kubana na bo kwe (2 Abakorinto 1:24; 2 Abakorinto 2:3). Mbega ukuntu byaboroheye gusobanura nabi imigambi ye!
