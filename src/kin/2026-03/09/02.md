title:  Gushima
date:   23/08/2026
---

`Soma 2 Abakorinto 1:3-7. Ni iyihe mpamvu y'inyifato ya ,Pawulo yo gushima kugaragara hano?`

Gushima kwa Pawulo kwibanda ku guhumurizwa Imana iha abari mu mubabaro. Muri iyi mirongo, inshinga guhumuriza (_parakaleō_) n' izina ihumure (_paraklēsis_) bikoreshwa inshuro icumi. Ibi bihagarariye kimwe cya gatatu cy'inshuro ayo magambo akoreshwa mu 2 Abakorinto (akoreshwa inshuro 29). Imana igaragazwa nka"Data wa twese w'imbabazi, Imana Nyirihumure ryose, iduhumuriza mu makuba yacu yose" (2 Abakorinto 1:3, 4).

Ihumure umuntu ahabwa n'Imana ntabwo agomba kuryigumanira (2 Abakorinto 1:4, 5). Umutima ubabaye maze ukakira guhumurizwa kuvuye ku Mana, ni wo wonyine ushobora gutanga neza iryo humure rikagera ku bandi bari mu mibabaro.

Pawulo yashoboraga guhumuriza abandi bitewe n'uko we ubwe, mu mibabaro ye, yari yarakiriye ihumure riva ku Mana. "Iyo tubabazwa tuba tubabarizwa kugira ngo muhumurizwe no gukizwa: kandi iyo duhumurizwa, tuba duhumurijwe no kugira ngo muhumurizwe namwe“ (2 Abakorinto 1:6). Uru ni urukundo!

`Ni iki Pawulo arimo gushimira mu rwa 2 Abakorinto 1:8-11?`

Pawulo avuga iby'amakuba yabaremereye cyane "kuruta ibyo bashobora" bigatuma we n'abakozi bagenzi be biheba ko bazapfa (2 Abak.1:8). Bamaze umwanya bibwira ko umuzuko ari wo byiringiro rukumbi bari basigaranye. Nyamara Imana yarabatabaye, maze ibintu birahinduka (2 Abakorinto 1:10). Bavuye mu bwoba bwo gutinya urupfu (2 Abakorinto 1:8), maze bahagurukira mu byiringiro byo mu mitima by'uko Imana yari kongera ikabarokora (2  Abakorinto 1:10). Gutsinda kw'Imana mu gihe cyashize kuduha icyizere cy'uko izakora ibimeze nk'ibyo mu gihe kizaza. Imana ikoresha imibabaro kugira ngo itwigishe kuyiringira. Ibirushya bishobora kutugeza ku kigero cy'ubukure mu bya Mwuka, nibura bikatugeza ku rugero tubyemerera kutwegereza Imana. Na none ishimwe rya Pawulo ryerekana imbaraga ziri mu isengesho ryo gusabira abandi no kunyurwa kuba mu mitima yacu bitewe no gutabarwa n'Imana (2 Abakorinto 1:11).
