---
title:  Umurimo Ukomotse ku Rukundo
date:   22/08/2026
---

### Aho icyigisho cy'iki cyumweru gishingiye:
2 Abakorinto 1:3-14; 2 Abakorinto 2:17; 2 Abakorinto 4:2; 1 Abakorinto 16:5-7; 2 Abakorinto 7:5-13; 2 Abakorinto 2:5-17.

> <p>Icyo kwibukwa:</p>
> "Nabandikiye mfite agahinda kenshi n'umubabaro mwinshi wo mu mutima, ndira amarira menshi; icyakora si ukugira ngo mbatere agahinda, ahubwo ni ukugira ngo mumenye uburyo urukundo mbakunda ruhebuje. (2 Abakorinto 2:4).

Intumwa Pawulo buri gihe ntibyabaga bimworoheye. Hejuru yo gufungwa ndetse n'ibihe biruhije yari yanyuzemo, yongeyeho kwandika ati:"Ibihe bitatu Abayuda bankubise inkoni mirongo itatu n'icyenda. Ibihe bitatu nakubiswe inga: igihe kimwe natewe amabuye: ibihe bitanu inkuge zaramenetse, naraye imuhengeri nirirwamo: nari mu ngendo kenshi, mu kaga gatewe n'inzuzi, mu kaga gatewe n'abambuzi, mu kaga gatewe na bene wacu, mu kaga gatewe n'abapagani, mu kaga ko mu midugudu, mu kaga ko mu butayu, mu kaga ko mu nyanja, mu kaga ko muri bene Data b'ibinyoma, mu miruho n'imihati; mba maso kenshi, ngira inzara n'inyota, nirirwa ubusa kenshi, , nicwa n'imbeho, nambara ubusa. Ariko ne kuvuga ibindi, keretse ko hariho ikindemerera iminsi yose: ni uguhagarikira umutima amatorero yose." (2 Abakorinto 11:24-28).

Icyo tubona mu nzandiko ze yandikiye Abakorinto ni ibintu bimwe byatumaga "ahagarikira umutima" itorero rye. Nyamara kandi muri ibyo byose, urukundo yabakundaga ntirwacogoraga, kimwe n'uko urukundo Kristo adukunda rutajya rucogora. Mu by'ukuri, ni kuri Yesu Pawulo yigiye gukunda amatorero urukundo rugaragaza urwo Yesu adukunda (2 Abakorinto 5:14; soma no mu 1 Abakorinto 11:1).
