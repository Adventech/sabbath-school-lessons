Soma Igitabo cyanditswe na Ellen G. White cyitwa Spiritual Gifts, vol. 1, pp.5-17, Igice cyitwa "Spiritual Gifts."

Nimureke abantu bajye gukora, biringiye Uwiteka, na We azajyana na bo, yemeze imitima kandi ayihindure. Umukozi umwe ashobora kuba ashoboye kuvuga, undi ashoboye kwandika, undi ashobora kugira impano yo gusengana umwete ataryarya kandi atagamburura, undi afite impano yo kuririmba. Undi ashobora kugira ububasha budasanzwe bwo gusobanura ijambo ry'Imana mu buryo bwumvikana. Kandi buri mpano yose ihinduka imbaraga Imana ikoresha kuko ikorana n'uwo mukozi. Umwe Imana imuha ijambo ry'ubwenge, undi agahabwa kumenya, undi agahabwa kwizera. Nyamara bose bagomba kugira umuyobozi umwe. Impano z'uburyo bwinshi zigeza ku mikorere y'uburyo bwinshi, "ariko Imana ikorera byose muri bo ni imwe" (1 Abakorinto 12:6).

"Nimureke he kugira umuntu n'umwe usuzugura impano zifatwa nk'izoroheje. Bose nibajye gukora. Nimureke he kugira uwipfumbata mu kutizera bitewe n'uko atekereza ko atakora umurimo ukomeye. Nimureke kwirebaho. Nimuhange amaso ku Muyobozi. Mu bugwaneza, kuba abanyakuri, no mu rukundo mukore ibyo mushoboye." Ellen G. White, _in The Adventist Review and Sabbath herald_, April 12, 1906.

"Buri wese muri twe akeneye ubufasha dushobora guhabwa bukomotse ku bundi bwenge. Imana izakorera mu bindi bitekerezo bitari ibyacu. Impano zitandukanye zahawe abantu batandukanye zigomba guhurizwa hamwe kugira ngo 'zitunganyirize abera gukora umurimo wo kugabura iby'Imana, kugira ngo zungure umubiri wa Kristo' (Abefeso 4:12). "Buri gihe imbere yacu hazahora inzitizi, ariko tugomba gukurikira Umuyobozi wacu, maze lugahangana n'ibiturushya twunze ubumwe, dufatanye mu biganza" Ellen G. White, _The Upward Look_, p.141.

**IBIBAZO:**

`Mara umwanya muto utekereza ku mpano yo guhanura. Kuki impano yo guhanura ari ingenzi kurusha impano yo kuvuga indimi igihe cyose izo ndimi zidasobanuwe? Nibiba ngombwa wongere usome 1 Abakorinto 14 kugira ngo ubone inshamake y'ibyo Pawulo avuga.`

`Mu itsinda ry'ishuri ryo ku Isabato mubarizwamo, nimuganire ku mibereho no ku murimo bya Ellen G. White n'impamvu twebwe nk'itorero, twizera mu by'ukuri ko yagaragaje rwose impano yo guhanura. Ni iyihe migisha ikomeye igera ku itorero iturutse kuri iyi mpano? Kandi se, ni ibihe bibazo biri mu kumenya uburyo bwiza bwo gukoresha iyi mpano?`

`Tekereza kuva ku bantu batatu kugeza kuri batanu bagukunda mu buryo bw'ukuri. Umenya ute ko urukundo bagukunda ari urw'ukuri? Ni iki ibyo bikubwira ku mpamvu yatumye Pawulo atinda cyane ku rukundo mu magambo yavuze ku mpano za Mwuka? Nubwo urukundo ari ingenzi, kubera iki icyo atari cyo cyonyine kigomba kugenderwaho mu kwemeza ko umuntu runaka avuga ukuri kandi ko ukwiye kumutega amatwi cyangwa kutamutega amatwi?`
