---
title:  Impano yo Kuvuga Indimi
date:   05/08/2026
---

Bimeze bite ku mpano yo kuvuga izindi ndimi? Dukurikije uko iyi mpano igaragazwa ahandi muri Bibiliya (Mariko 16:17; Ibyakozwe n'Intumwa 2:1-13; Ibyakozwe n'Intumwa 10:44-48; Ibyakozwe n'Intumwa 19:6), impano yo kuvuga izindi ndimi ivugwa mu 1 Abakorinto, birashoboka cyane ko bwaba ari ubushobozi butangwa na Mwuka bwo kuvuga izindi ndimi.

Pawulo ashyira impano yo kuvuga izindi ndimi ku rutonde rw'impano ziboneka mu 1 Abakorinto 12:8-10 (soma no mu 1 Abakorinto 12:28, 30; 1 Abakorinto 13:1, 8). Nyamara kandi arengurira kenshi kuri yo mu 1 Abakorinto 14. Mu by'ukuri, ijambo ry'Ikigiriki _glōssa_ ("ururimi") rigaragara inshuro zirenga 20 mu 1 Abakorinto 12-14, aho rikoreshwa inshuro cumi n'eshanu mu gice cya 14 cyonyine. Icyiyongera kuri ibi, ni uko ijambo ry'Ikigiriki _heteloglōssos_ ("urundi rurimi") na ryo rikoreshwa mu 1 Abakorinto 14:21. Uyu mubare munini w'amasomo avuga iby'impano yo kuvuga izindi ndimi werekana ko iki kintu cyihariye cyari gishishikaje Pawulo. Gukoresha nabi no guhindanya iyi mpano kw'abo mu itorero ry'i Korinto byatumye habaho imivurungano n'urujijo mu misengere y'abantu benshi bateraniye hamwe (1 Abakorinto 14:23, 27, 33, 40).

`Soma 1 Abakorinto 14:5, 13, 26, 27 n' 1 Abakorinto 12:10, 30. Ni iyihe nyigisho Pawulo yatanze ku birebana n'impano yo kuvuga izindi ndimi?`

Impamvu ituma impano yo kuvuga indimi idasigana n'iyo kuzisobanura ni uko indimi zigomba kuba zumvikana (1 Abakorinto 14:9); bitabaye bityo, nta cyo gukoresha iyo mpano byaba bimaze (1 Abakorinto 14:6). Ibi bisobanura impamvu Pawulo atinda cyane ku gusobanura indimi no kuzisobanukirwa. Biragaragara rwose ko atanegura impano yo kuvuga indimi ubwayo, ahubwo nk'uko tuzabireba ejo, ugukabya mu kuyirutisha izindi kw'Abakorinto, kwabateye gusuzugura impano y'ubuhanuzi.

Kugeza ubu, ni ingenzi kubona ko nubwo Pawulo yifuzaga ko Abakorinto bose bavuga izindi ndimi (1 Abakorinto 14:5), ntiyari yiteze ko ibyo bizabaho (1 Abakorinto 12:10). Bityo rero, igitekerezo cy'uko "abantu bose bagomba kuvuga indimi mbere yuko basaba kubatizwa na Mwuka Wera ni uguhindura inyigisho ya Pawulo iboneka mu 1 Abakorinto 12 na 14." _Raoul Dederen, Handbook of Senenth-Day Adventis Theology_, electronic ed., vol. 12 of Commentary Reference Series (Hagerstown, MD: Review and Herald Publishing Association, 2001), p. 620.

`Mbese mu itorero ryawe hari abantu bavuga izindi ndimi? Ni mu buhe buryo bashobora gukoresha ubwo bushobozi kugira ngo barehereze abantu kuri Kristo? Ni mu buhe buryo ibi bidufasha gusobanukirwa imiterere nyakuri y'indimi Pawulo ari kuvugaho?`
