---
title:  Ubumwe mu Maharakwinshi
date:   03/08/2026
---

Imvugo y'Ubumwe ikoreshwa mu 1 Abakorinto 12:4-6 ("Umwuka umwe," "Umwami umwe," n' "Imana imwe") isobanurwa mu yindi mirongo isigaye yo mu 1 Abakorinto 12. Ibi bihamywa n'uburyo Pawulo akoreshamo itsinda ry'amagambo nk'aya ngo "uwo Mwuka umwe" (1 Abakorinto 12:11), "nk'uko umubiri ari umwe" (1 Abakorinto 12:12), "umubiri ni umwe" (1 Abakorinto 12:12), "umubiri umwe" (1 Abakorinto 12, 13, 20), "Umwuka umwe" (1 Abakorinto 12:13 [inshuro ebyiri]), "ahubwo ingingo zigirirane" (1 Abakorinto 12:25).

Hamwe n'iyi nyito y'ubumwe, Pawulo agaragaza neza uburyo ingingo zinyuranye mu mubiri wa Kristo abinyujije mu mvugo nk'izi ngo "ingingo nyinshi" (1 Abakorinto 12:12, 20), "twese ari mo twabatirijwe" (1 Abakorinto 12:13), "si urugingo rumwe, ahubwo ni nyinshi" (1 Abakorinto 12:13). "umubiri wose" (1 Abakorinto 12:17), "zose uko zingana" (1 Abakorinto 12:18), "ingingo z'umubiri" (1 Abakorinto 12:22, 23) "ingingo zose" (1 Abakorinto 12:26). Uku gushimangira ubumwe n'amaharakwinshi kugaragaza ko impano za Mwuka zigambiriye guteza imbere ubumwe binyuze mu maharakwinshi.

Ubu bumwe mu maharakwinshi bugomba kugaragaza imico y'Imana. Data wa twese ni umwe, Umwana ni undi, kandi na Mwuka Wera ni undi. Buri wese muri abo batatu afite umwihariko we nubwo bakorera hamwe kugira ngo bateze imbere itorero no kuriha imbaraga ngo rikore umurimo (1 Abakorinto 12:4-6; Abefeso 4:11-13).

`Soma 1 Abakorinto 12:12-31. Kubera iki iri gereranya ryifashisha umubiri n'ingingo nyinshi ziwugize ari ishusho ikwiriye yerekana itorero n'abizera barigize?`

Igitekerezo gikuru kiboneka mu 1 Abakorinto 12 ni iki: nubwo ingingo z'umubiri zitandukanye rwose (1 Abakorinto 12:15-20), zose ni magirirane (1 Abakorinto 12: 21-26). Ibirenge byishingikiriza ku maso kugira ngo bibone aho bikandagira; na none kandi, amaso ntashobora kugira icyo afata-ibiganza byonyine ni byo bifata. Kandi, igitekerezo cy'uko ingingo zimwe ari inyantegenke (1 Abakorinto 12:22) cyangwa iz'icyubahiro gike (1 Abakorinto 12:23) Kurusha izindi, ni ibyo abantu bibwira, kubera ko ingingo zose zikenewe (1 Abakorinto 12:22).

Ikibabaje ni uko mu Bakorinto abantu basaga n'abaha agaciro impano zimwe maze bakirengagiza izindi. Kugira ngo akosore iryo kosa, Pawulo yakanguriraga ibitekerezo byabo gukunda, ikaba ari yo "nzira irushaho kuba nziza " (1 Abakorinto 12:31). Mu yandi magambo, mpano iyo ikoreshejwe mu bwenge no mu rukundo, uko yaba imeze kose ishimisha Imana.

`Keba urutonde rw'impano ziboneka mu 1 Abakorinto 12:8-10, 28; Abaroma 12:6-8; n' Abefeso 4:11. Mbese impano yawe ni iyihe? Ni mu buhe buryo ushobora kuyikoresha kugira ngo ukomeze umubiri wa Kristo?`
