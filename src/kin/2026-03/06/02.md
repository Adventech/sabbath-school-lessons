---
title:  Impano z'Uburyo Bwinshi
date:   02/08/2026
---

Pawulo atangira insanganyamatsiko nshya mu 1 Abakorinto 12:1 akoresheje aya magambo ngo "ibyerekeye." Abahanga bajya impaka bibaza niba ari kuvuga ku "mpano za Mwuka" cyangwa "abantu bayoborwa na Mwuka" bitewe n'uko amagambo yo mu Kigiriki _ton pneumatikōn_ ashobora gusobanura ibyo byombi. "Impano za Mwuka" ni yo umuntu yahitamo dukurikije ibivugwa mu 1 Abakorinto 12:4 aho Pawulo avuga mu buryo busobanutse ku mpano za Mwuka. Mu 1 Abakorinto 12: 2, 3, Pawulo avuga ko impano ya mbere ya Mwuka ari ukwatura ushize amanga ko Yesu ari Umwami (1 Abakorinto 12:2, 3). Mu bihe by'Isezerano Rishya, kuvuga ko Yesu ari Umwami ni ukuvuga ko Kayisari atari umwami (Ibyakozwe n'Intumwa 17:7; na Yohana 19:12, 15). Ibi byafatwaga nko gutera abantu kwigomeka ku butegetsi bw'umwami mukuru, bityo bikaba byaragombaga guhanishwa igihano cy'urupfu.

Yesu na Pawulo bashimangiye ko kwizera Imana-ndetse no mu gihe waba ubirenganyirizwa cyangwa ukangishwa urupfu-ari impano ya Mwuka. Mu by'ukuri, kwizera ni impano ibanziriza izindi zose. Si ibintu bitangaje, kwizera ni impano ya mbere ku rutonde ruboneka mu 1 Abakorinto 13:13. Kuba kwizera ari impano ya Mwuka bigaragara neza mu magambo Pawulo avuga mu 1 Abakorinto 12:9. Nyamara kandi, hariho n'izindi mpano nyinshi. Kuba Mwuka Wera "agabira buri muntu wese uko ashaka" impano zitandukanye (1 Abakorinto 12:11), bihamya ko izo [mpano] zose ari ngombwa.

`Soma 1 Abakorinto 12:1-6. Ni iki iyi mirongo ishimangira?`

Kwisubiramo kw'amagambo "uburyo bwinshi" bishimangira ko hariho impano nyinshi. Icyo Pawulo yita "impano za Mwuka" mu 1 Abakorinto 12: 1, kirushaho kwaguka mu murongo wa 4-6, binyuze mu byerekezo bitatu bitandukanye: "impano" (_charisma_), "kugabura iby'Imana" cyangwa "ibyiciro by'imikorere" (_diakonia_), no "gukora" (_energēma_). Nubwo aya magambo asobanura ibintu bitandukanye, ni ingenzi kudashyira itandukaniro hagati yayo kubera isano iri hagati y'ibivugwa muri iyo mirongo. Na none kandi umuntu akwiriye kubona ko impano za Mwuka zateguriwe guteza imbere ubumwe hashingiwe ku mico y'Imana yigaragariza mu butatu bugize Imana (reba no mu Abefeso 4:8-11). Nubwo Mwuka ari we uha abizera impano, Imana ni Yo ibashoboza gukorera Kristo mu muryango mugari w'abizera (1 Abakorinto 12:5, 6). Buri mwizera ahabwa impano nk'umuntu ku giti cye (1 Abakorinto 12:11), nyamara zose zigomba kugirira umumaro umuryango mugari wose w'abizera.

`Ongera wite ku buryo Pawulo ashimangira ubumwe. Kuki iki kintu ari ingenzi cyane ku itorero?`
