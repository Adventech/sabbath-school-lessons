---
title:  "Inzira Irushaho kuba Nziza"
date:   04/08/2026
---

"Urukundo si imwe mu mpano nyinshi. Ni inzira izindi mpano zose zinyuramo kugira ngo zigera ku mugambi wazo."-Carl P. Cosaert, "1 Abakorinto," _Andrews Bible Commentary: New Testament_ (Berrien Springs, MI: Andrews University Press, 2022), p. 1643.

`Soma 1 Abakorinto 13:1-7 na 1 Petero 4: 8-11. Ni akahe kamaro k'urukundo ku byerekeranye n'impano za Mwuka?`

Mu rwandiko rwa mbere rw'Abakorinto 13 higisha ko impano za Mwuka zishobora gusa gukoreshwa uko bikwiriye ari uko zikoreshejwe mu rukundo. Pawulo atangira urwandiko rwa 1 Abakorinto 13 arengurira ku mpano zari zavuzwe mu 1 Abakorinto 12, bikaba bishimangira ko nta gaciro ziba zifite igihe zidakoreshwa n'urukundo. Bityo rero, kumenya (1 Abakorinto 12:8) no kwizera (1Abakorinto 12:9), nta cyo biri cyo igihe hatari urukundo (1 Abakorinto 13:2). Hatari urukundo, ubushobozi bwo kuvuga indimi (1 Abakorinto 12:10, 28, 30) buhinduka nk' "umuringa uvuga cyangwa icyuma kirenga" (1 Abakorinto 13:1). Mu buryo nk'ubwo, impano y'ubuhanuzi ihinduka ubusa igihe itajyanye n'urukundo (1 Abakorinto 13:2).

Mu 1 Abakorinto 13:4-7, Pawulo yibanda ku cyo urukundo ruri cyo n'icyo rutari cyo; by'umwihariko, icyo urukundo rukora n'icyo rudakora. Inshinga yahisemo kugira ngo yerekane urukundo zigaragaza ko urukundo atari ikintu twiyumvamo kurusha ko ari icyo dushyira mu bikorwa. Muri ubwo buryo, Pawulo avuga ko urukundo (1) rwerekana kwihangana (2) rugira neza; (3) rwishimira ukuri; (4) rubabarira byose; (5) rwizera byose; (6) rwiringira byose; (7) rwihanganira byose. Tubivuze mu bundi buryo, urukundo (1) ntirugira ishyari; (2) ntirwirarira; (3) ntirwihımbaza; (4) ntirukora ibiteye isoni; (5) ntirushaka ibyarwo; (6) ntiruhutiraho; (7) ntirutekereza ikibi ku bantu; (8) ntirwishimira gukiranirwa kw'abandi.

Uru rutonde rw'inshinga cumi n'eshanu rutuyobora neza ku myifatire ikwiriye mu byo gukoresha impano. Biragaragara ko iki kiganiro kirebana n'imiterere nyakuri y'urukundo kiri mu 1 Abakorinto igice cya 12 kugeza ku gice cya 14, aho Pawulo avuga iby'intambara ishingiye ku mpano za Mwuka. Mu by'ukuri, urukundo ni urufunguzo rutuma impano za Mwuka zikoreshwa neza. Na none kandi, urukundo ntirusigana no kwizera no kwiringira, "ariko ikiruta ibindi ni urukundo" (1 Abakorinto 13:13).
