---
title:  Ubumenyi n'Urukundo
date:   26/07/2026
---

`Soma urwa 1 Abakorinto 8:1-13. Kuki Pawulo atandukanya ubumenyi n'urukundo, kandi ni mu buhe buryo? Arashaka kuvuga iki?`

Pawulo akoresha insanganyamatsiko y'ibyokurya byaterekerejwe ibigirwamana kugira ngo akemure ikibazo cyimbitse: kutagirira abandi urukundo (1 Abakorinto 8). Ikibazo cyerekeranye n'ibyo kurya byaterekerejwe ibigirwamana cyaciyemo ibice bibiri itorero ry'i Korinto. Bamwe bizeraga ko kuba bazi ko nta zindi mana zibaho bibaha uburenganzira bwo kurya ikintu icyo ari cyo cyose ( Abakorinto 8:4). Abo bagaragazwa nk' "abanyambaraga" (1 Abakorinto 4:10, BII). Abavuguruza iyo myitwarire bitwa "abanyantege nke" (1 Abakorinto 8:9-12). Pawulo akoresha iryo zina kuko batatsinze imyizerere y'ibinyoma, imyizerere yaranze imibereho yabo ya gipagani ya mbere. Igihe babona "abanyambaraga' barya ibyokurya byaterekerejwe ibigirwamana, bashobora kwanzura bavuga ko Ubukristo no gusenga ibigirwamana bishobora kubangikanywa. Bityo, Pawulo ntabwo yashakaga ko "abanyambaraga" babera abanyantege nke igisitaza.

Bibiliya ibona igikorwa cyo kurya ibyokurya byaterekerejwe ibigirwamana nk'igikorwa kibi cyane (Ibyakozwe 15:20, 29; Ibyakozwe 21:25; gereranya n'Ibyahishuwe 2:14, 20). Nyamara, ntabwo Pawulo akoresha amagambo akakaye nk'uko umuntu abibona muri iyi mirongo. Ibi biterwa nuko ikibazo cy'ibanze cyari kimuhangayikishije cyari icyo kubura ubumwe kwashoboraga guterwa no gukoresha ubumenyi nabi. Ntabwo Pawulo anenga ubumenyi nkaho ubwabwo ari ikintu kibi; ibiri amambu, ntabwo ashyigikiye ubumenyi butuma habaho ubwirasi ndetse n'amacakubiri mu itorero. Ubumenyi nyakuri bubaho iyo umuntu akunda Imana kandi akaba azwi na Yo (1 Abakorinto 8:3).

Akoresha amagambo ari mu Gutegeka kwa Kabiri 6:4, Pawulo agaragaza ko abizera bagomba kumenya ko hariho Imana imwe yonyine (1 Abakorinto 8:4-6). Igishimishije, akurikiza igitekerezo kiboneka mu Gutekega kwa Kabiri 6:4, 5, havuga ko Imana yacu ari imwe hakurikirwa n'itegeko "Ukundishe Uwiteka Imana yawe umutima wawe wose ... " Pawulo na Mose bavuga ko ubumenyi butagira urukundo ntacyo buba bumaze.

Mu kugirira icyizere ubumenyi bwabo, "abanyambaraga" bizeraga ko kurya ibyokurya byaterekerejwe ibigirwamana ari ntacyo bitwaye. Nk'uko tuzabibona ku wa Kane no ku wa Gatanu, Pawulo yabemereye ubwo burenganzira ariko agira ibyo abasaba kubanza kuzuza. Nyamara, niba ibyo bihindutse igisitaza ku "banyantege nke" (1 Abakorinto 8:9), bigomba kwirindwa. Abakristo bakwiriye kwiyanga babitewe n'urukundo bakunda Kristo n'abandi.

`Pawulo avuga ko, ahatari urukundo, ubumenyi bushobora guhinduka bubi (1 Abakorinto 8). Ni ryari ubumenyi butarimo urukundo bushobora kuba bubi?`
