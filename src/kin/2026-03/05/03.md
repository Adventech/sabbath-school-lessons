---
title:  Urukundo Rutikanyiza
date:   27/07/2026
---

`Soma mu 1 Abakorinto 9:1-6. Ni mu buhe buryo iyi mirongo itanga urugero rufatika rw'icyo kwizinukwa bitewe n'urukundo bisobanura?`

Ubwa mbere, bisa naho amagambo yo mu rwa 1 Abakorinto 9 Pawulo yavuze yisobanura ko ari intumwa ntaho ahurira n'ikiganiro cyari cyabanje cyerekeranye n'ubumenyi buhanganye n'urukundo. Nyamara kandi, umuntu ntashobora kwibagirwa yuko Bibiliya ubwo yandikwaga mu mizo ya mbere itanditswe mu bice. Icyo Pawulo yigisha mu 1 Abakorinto 9 ntabwo gitandukanye n'ibyakurikiyeho. Mu by'ukuri, mu 1 Abakorinto 9 hatanga urugero rufatika rw'urukundo rutikanyiza dukwiriye gukunda Kristo ndetse na bene Data. Kubw'urukundo, Pawulo yigomwe bumwe mu burenganzira bwe.

**"Kurya no kunywa"** (1 Abakorinto 9:4). Hano, ibyokurya n'ibyo kunywa bihagarariye ubufasha bw'amafaranga muri rusange. Nk'intumwa, Pawulo yari afite uburenganzira bwo kwakira ubufasha bw'ibikoresho buturutse ku bo yari yarabwirije. Abandi bayobozi mu by'idini bo mu gihe cye bagenzaga batyo [bahabwaga ubwo bufasha]. Ariko Pawulo ntabwo yigeze abikora, ahubwo yifashaga binyuze mu kuboha amahema (Ibyakozwe n'Intumwa 18:3).

**"Kugira umugore wizera"** (1 Abakorinto 9:5). Intumwa yashyingiwe yari yemerewe kujyana n'umugore wayo mu rugendo rw'ivugabutumwa kandi itorero ni ryo ryagombaga kubishyurira. Ingero z'ababwirizabutumwa bari barashyingiranwe zirimo Purisikila na Akwila (Abaroma 16:3), Andironiko na Yuniya (Abaroma 16:7). Ariko Pawulo ntiyari yarashatse (1 Abakorinto 7:8). Yashoboraga gushyingirwa maze akabona inyungu zituruka ku burenganzira bwo guherekezwa n'umugore we, kandi bombi bagahabwa ubufasha bw'amafaranga.

**"Kuruhuka gukorera ibibatunga"** (1 Abakorinto 9:6). Pawulo na Barinaba bari bafite uburenganzira bwo gukorera ibihembo by'umurimo wabo w'ivugabutumwa (1 Abakorinto 9:4- 6). Pawulo yari umuboshyi w'amahema kugira ngo abone ibimutunga (Ibyakozwe n'Intumwa 18:3), ariko ntabwo tuzi umwuga Barinaba yakoraga. Icyo tuzi ni uko yagiraga ubuntu cyane (Ibyakozwe n'Intumwa 4:36, 37), bityo yari ashoboye kwifasha ubwe.

Mu 1 Abakorinto 9:7-11, Pawulo asesengura igitekerezo cyo mu 1 Abakorinto 9:6 kugira ngo agaragaze ko byari bikwiriye ko we na Barinaba itorero ribaha ibibatunga (1 Abakorinto 9:11,12). Umwami ubwe yarategetse ati: "Abavuga ubutumwa bwiza batungwa n'ubutumwa" (1 Abakorinto 9: 14, gereranya na 1 Timoteyo 5:18). Nyamara Pawulo aravuga ati: "Ntituragakoresha ubwo butware" (1 Abakorinto 9:12). Bityo, Pawulo yigaragaza nk'urugero rwo kwigomwa (1 Abakorinto 9:1-18) kandi avuga ko ibyo bihesha inyungu kubwiriza ubutumwa bwiza i Korinto (1 Abakorinto 9:19-23).

`Ni ibihe bintu, nubwo ubifitiye uburenganzira, ushobora kwigomwa kugira ngo urusheho kuba umuhamya mwiza w'Uwiteka?`
