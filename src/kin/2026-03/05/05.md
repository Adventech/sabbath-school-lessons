---
title:  Umuburo wo Kwirinda Gusenga Ibishushanyo
date:   29/07/2026
---

`Soma mu 1 Abakorinto 10:5-22. Kuki tugomba kuzibukira gusenga ibishushanyo?`

Mu 1 Abakorinto 10:14-22, Pawulo agaruka ku kibazo cy'ibyokurya byaterekerejwe ibigirwamana. Guterekereza ibyokurya ibigirwamana bishobora gufatwa nk'ibidasanzwe mu mico myinshi y'iki gihe, ariko byari gikwira mu bihe bya Bibiliya. Igihe amatungo yaterekerezwaga ibigirwamana mu nsengero z'abapagani, umugabane w'iryo tungo wahabwaga abatambyi bakoraga uwo muhango, maze bakagurishaga inyama. Zimwe muri izo nyama zagezwaga mu masoko rusange. Kubera ko izo nyama zitabikwaga ahantu hatandukanye n'izindi nyama na zo zabaga zije kugurishwa mu isoko, Umukristo yashoboraga kugura izo nyama zaterekerejwe ibigirwamana atabizi. Inama y'intumwa ni iyi ngo: inyama nk'izo zishobora kugurwa n'Abakristo.

Nyamara, mu gihe inyama zatambiwe mu rusengero zashoboraga kuribwa n'Abakristo mu rugo (1 Abakorinto 8:1-13), kujya mu nsengero z'abapagani ukitabira ibirori byabo byari bibujijwe ku Bakristo. Icyagenderwagaho kiragaragara: Abakristo bemerewe kurya inyama bari mu rugo kuko ibishushanyo ntacyo ari cyo (1 Abakorinto 8:4), nyamara, Abakristo ntabwo bagombaga kujya mu birori by'ababapagani kuko ibyo bifitanye isano no kuramya abadayimoni (1 Abakorinto 10:20, 21). Kugira uruhare mu mihango y'abapagani bihwanye no kugirana ubumwe n'abadayimoni (1 Abakorinto 10:20), nk'uko kujya ku meza y'Umwami bihwanye no kugirana ubumwe na Kristo (1 Abakorinto 10:16).

Bityo, Pawulo aravuga ati: "Ntibishoboka ko munywera ku gikombe cy'Umwami wacu kandi ngo munywere ku gikombe cy'abadayimoni. Ntimushobora gusangira ibyo ku meza y'Umwami wacu n'ibyo ku meza y'abadayimoni" (1 Abakorinto 10:21). Nk'uko Yesu yabivuze: "Nta wucyeza abami babiri" (Matayo 6:24).

Pawulo avuga ko Imana idusaba kuyiyegurira n'imitima yacu yose. Avuga ko gusenga ibishushanyo bitera "Umwami ishyari" (1 Abakorinto 10:22). Kugira ngo ibyo bitabaho, Pawulo mu 1 Abakorinto 8:4-6, atanga itegeko ritibeshya rirwanya gusenga ibishushanyo, arengurira ku Gutegeka kwa Kabiri 6:4, 5: "Umva wa bwoko bw'Abisirayeli we, Uwiteka Imana yacu ni we Uwiteka wenyine. Ukundishe Uwiteka Imana yawe umutima wawe wose n'ubugingo bwawe bwose n'imbaraga zawe zose" (Gutegeka kwa Kabiri 6:4,5). Kuri iki gitekerezo cyo gukunda Imana ukayirutisha ibintu byose cyo mu Gutegeka kwa Kabiri, Yesu yongeyeho ati: "Ukunde mugenzi wawe nk'uko wikunda" (Mariko 12:31, soma n' Abalewi 19:18).

`Ikigirwamana si ngombwa ko kiba ikibumbano cy'ibuye. Ikintu cyose gishobora kutubera ikigirwamana. Ni ibihe bigirwamana, ibyo aribyo byose, ukeneye kuzibukira mu mibereho yawe?`
