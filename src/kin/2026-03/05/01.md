---
title:  Mukorere Byose Guhimbaza Imana
date:   25/07/2026
---


### Aho icyigisho cy'iki cyumweru gishingiye:
1 Abakorinto 8; Ibyakozwe n'Intumwa 15:20; 1 Abakorinto 9:1-6; 1 Abakorinto 10:5-22; Gutegeka kwa Kabiri 6:4, 5; Mariko 12:28-31.

> <p>Icyo kwibukwa:</p>
> "Namwe iyo murya cyangwa munywa cyangwa mukora ikindi kintu cyose, mujye mukorera byose guhimbaza Imana." (1 Abakorinto 10:31).

Mu Rwandiko rwa  1 Abakorinto 8-11 hasoza ikiganiro cyerekeranye n'ubusambanyi (kivugwa mu gice cya 5-6) kandi hagatangiza ibisubizo Pawulo yatanze ku bibazo byabajijwe muri urwo rwandiko (1 Abakorinto 7:1) bibajijwe n'Abakorinto. Ibyo bisubizo ni byo byiganje mu mugabane usigaye w'urwa 1 Abakorinto.

Imiterere y'uguhererekanya kw'ibivugwa mu rwa 1 Abakorinto 7 igaragaza ko ubusambanyi (igice cya 5-7) no gusenga ibishushyanyo (igice cya 8) ari ingingo zifitanye isano. Mu byukuri, izo ngingo zombi zikunze kuvugirwa hamwe mu Isezerano Rishya (soma Ibyakozwe n'Intumwa 15:20, 29; Ibyakozwe n'Intumwa 21:25; 1 Abakorinto 6:9; Abefeso 5:5: Abakolosayi 3:5; Ibyahishuwe 21:8; Ibyahishuwe 22:15).

Muri rusange, mu gihe mu 1 Abakorinto 5-7 Pawulo ahanganye n'ikibazo cy'ubusambanyi, mu 1 Abakorinto 8-10 ikibazo cy'ingenzi ahanganye na cyo ni ikibazo cyo gusenga ibishushanyo. Avuga ko Abakristo bakwiriye kuzibukira ibyo byombi (1 Abakorinto 6:18, 1 Abakorinto 10:14).

Mu cyumweru gishize, twabonye yuko kubwo kuba urusengero rwa Mwuka Wera 1 Abakorinto (6:19, 20), umuntu ashobora kuzibukira ubusambanyi. Muri iki cyumweru, turabona ko umuntu ashobora kuzibukira gusenga ibishushanyo binyuze mu "gukorera byose guhimbaza Imana" (1 Abakorinto 10:31).
