---
title:  Ibindi byo Kwigwa no Kuzirikanwa
date:   31/07/2026
---

Soma igitabo cyanditswe na Ellen G. White cyitwa, *Abakurambere n'Abahanuzi*, ingingo ivuga ibyo "Gusenga Inyana y'Izahabu"

"Mbega uburyo byari kuba byiza iyo dukoresha neza imibanire yacu n'abandi! Buri muntu wese wahawe imigisha iturutse mu ijuru afite inshingano yo kumurikira inzira z'abandi .... Maze abakunda Imana bose by'ukuri bazareka gusenga ibishushanyo"-Ellen G.White, in *The Advent Review and Sabbath Herald*, November 18,1884, p.730.

"Pawulo yingingiye abavandimwe be mu kwizera kwibaza icyo amagambo n'ibikorwa byabo bishobora gukora ku bandi no kutagira icyo bakora, kabone nubwo cyaba ari cyiza, cyasa n'aho gishyigikira gusenga ibigirwamana cyangwa kigakomeretsa ugushidikanya kw'abashobora kuba ari abanyantege nke mu kwizera. Yaravuze ati: "Namwe iyo murya cyangwa munywa cyangwa mukora ikindi kintu cyose, mujye mukorera byose guhimbaza Imana .... '

"Amagambo y'imbuzi y'intumwa Pawulo yabwiye Itorero ry'i Korinto ni ay'ibihe byose kandi by'umwihariko aratureba muri iki gihe cyacu. Igihe yavugaga ibyo gusenga ibigirwamana, nti byari bisobanuye kubiramya gusa, kwiyitaho gusa, gukunda ibinezeza, gutwarwa n'umururumba n'irari."-Ellen G. White, *Ibyakozwe n'Intumwa*, p.195.

"Niba mubona ko gukora ibintu bimwe na bimwe ufitiye uburenganzira bwuzuye bwo gukora bizabangamira iterambere ry'umurimo w'Imana, mureke gukora ibyo bintu. Ntimukagire ikintu na kimwe mukora kizatuma intekerezo z'abandi zirwanya ukuri. Ibintu byose bishobora kuba byemewe, ariko si ko byose bikwiriye kandi ngo bibe bigira icyo byungura."-Ellen G.White,_Testimonies for the Church_, vol. 9, p. 215.

**IBIBAZO:**

`Dukurikije uko Pawulo abivuga, rimwe na rimwe imico y'Umukristo umaze igihe kirekire mu itorero ishobora kubangamira iterambere mu bya Mwuka ry'Umukristo ukiri mushya. Tekereza ku bihe ibi bishobora kubaho. Kuki ihame ryo gukunda Imana kuyirutisha ibintu byose, ndetse no gukunda abandi nk'uko wikunda, ari bwo buryo bwonyire rukumbi bwo guhangana n'iki kibazo?`

`Ni ibihe bigirwamana n'Abakristo bashobora gusenga baramutse batabaye maso? Ni ibihe bintu bisanzwe ari byiza bishobora kuduhindukira ibigirwamana? Nanone kandi, wamenya ute ko ikintu runaka uha agaciro cyane cyahindutse ikigirwamana?`

`Pawulo avuga ko ababaza umubiri we, awukoza uburetwa ngo ahari ataboneka ko atemewe igihe abwiriza ubutumwa bwiza (1 Abakorinto 9:27). Ushingiye ku cyigisho cy'iki cyumweru, tekereza ikintu gishobora gutuma umuntu aboneka ko atemewe nk'umubwiriza w'ubutumwa bwiza.`

`Mu 1 Abakorinto 10, Pawulo avuga akaga ko gusenga ibigirwamana kandi akavuga ati: "Nimuzibukire kuramya ibishushanyo" (1 Abakorinto 10:14). Ni ukubera iki gusenga ibigirwamana ari ibintu bibi cyane?`
