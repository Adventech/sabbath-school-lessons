---
title:  Gutsinda Gusenga Ibishushanyo
date:   30/07/2026
---

Mu 1 Abakorinto 8:1-3, Pawulo avuga ko urukundo rurinda umuntu gusenga ibishushanyo Iki gitekerezo gisubirwamo kandi kigasesengurwa mu 1 Abakorinto 10:23-11:1. Mu 1 Abakorinto 8:3, avuga ku rukundo dukunda Imana. Aravuga ati: "Ntihakagire umuntu wizirikana ubwe, ahubwo azirikane na mugenzi we" (1 Abakorinto 10:24). Uku ni ugukunda abandi.

`Soma muri Mariko 10:17-22 na Mariko 12:28-31. Ni iki iyi mirongo yombi ihuriyeho, kandi ni buryo ki ihuje n'ibivugwa mu 1 Abakorinto 10?`

Mu 1 Abakorinto 10, Pawulo akora nk' ibyo Yesu yakoze muri Mariko 12:28-31; ari byo ibi bikurikira: yahuje amategeko abiri akomeye y'urukundo; gukunda Imana no gukunda mugenzi wawe. Mu nkuru y'umusore w'umutunzi (Mariko 10:17-22), Yesu ahuza ubwo buryo bubiri bw'urukundo, kandi akora ibyo arengurira mu Gutegeka kwa Kabiri 6:4 (soma muri Mariko 10:18) ndetse n'igisate cya kabiri cyari cyanditsweho amategeko (soma Mariko 10:19). Ikibazo uwo musore w'umutunzi yari afite ni uko yakundaga ubutunzi bwe kurusha uko akunda Imana na bagenzi be (Mariko 10:22). Yahaga ubutunzi bwo ku isi agaciro karuta ako yahaga ubutunzi bwo mu ijuru. Yahaga amafaranga agaciro karuta ako yahaga abakene (Mariko 10:21). Yasengaga ibishushanyo.

Akurikije inyigisho za Yesu, Pawulo agaragaza ko ihame ryo gukunda Imana ukayirutisha ibintu byose ndetse no gukunda bagenzi bawe nk'uko wikunda rigora gukoreshwa ku bivugwa mu 1 Abakorinto 10:27, 28. Ibi bisobanuye ko n'ibintu byemewe n'amategeko bishobora kutagira akamaro cyangwa kutagira icyo byigisha kuko bishobora kuba bibangamiye umutimanama w'abandi (1 Abakorinto 10:23). Iri hame rikubiye mu magambo avuga ngo: "Mujye mukorera byose guhimbaza Imana" (1 Abakorinto 10:31). Avuga ngo mujye mukorera byose guhimbaza Imana, Pawulo agaragaza ko gusenga ibigirwamana bishobora kwigaragaza mu buryo butandukanye, kuko ikintu cyose gihabwa icyubahiro kigenewe Imana yonyine ari ugusenga ibigirwamana (Yesaya 42:8).

Amagambo ya Pawulo yo mu 1 Abakorinto 10:31-11:1 ni umwanzuro w'igice cya 8-10. Agaragaza ko ataharaniraga inyungu ze bwite, "keretse icyungura benshi kugira ngo bakizwe" (1 Abakorinto 10:33). Ni ko yigannye Kristo (1 Abakorinto 11:1).

`Ni mu buhe buryo ushobora kwiga kurushaho gukunda mugenzi wawe nk'uko wikunda?`
