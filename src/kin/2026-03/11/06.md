---
title:  Ubumwe
date:   10/09/2026
---

Twabonye ko Pawulo ashishikariza abizera b'i Korinto kugira uruhare mu gusonzoranya impiya zo gufasha amatorero y'i Yudeya. Imwe mu ntego ze kwari ugukangura ubumwe muri bo. Ashaka ko bagira uruhare mu murimo w'ivugabutumwa. Ashaka kugaragaza ko amatorero y'abanyamahanga ari umugabane w'umuryango w'Imana nk'uko byari bimeze ku bizera b'Abayuda bari muri Yerusalemu. Ni ukuvuga ko abo bantu, bahoze bahanganye. ubu noneho, basangiye kuba abasigaye bo mu Isezerano Rishya ry'Imana. Pawulo ashaka kubona umuryango wose wa Gikristo, Abayuda n'abanyamahanga, bunze ubumwe mu buryo bufite imbaraga bushobora guhamiriza itorero ryo mu bisekuru bizaza.

Tito ndetse n'abandi bavandimwe babiri ni bo bari bashinzwe gukusanya inkunga. Imana yashyize uku kwita ku itorero mu mutima wa Tito (2 Abakorinto 8:16). Binyuze muri ayo matorero, Imana yatoranyije abandi bavandimwe babiri (2 Abakorinto 8:18-23). Bitwa "Intumwa z'amatorero zihimbaza Kristo" (2 Abakorinto 8:23). "Icyubahiro cya Kristo" niba kigaragaza abo bavandimwe babiri, cyangwa kikaba kigaragaza amatorero ubwayo, ntacyo bitwaye. Gutanga amaturo byanze bikunze ni ikimenyetso cy'icyubahiro cya Kristo, umutwe w'Itorero (Abefeso 4:15).

Urwandiko Rwa Kabiri rw'Abakorinto igice cya 8 n'icya 9 rugaragaza ko amaturo akwiriye guhabwa abantu batoranyijwe n'Imana binyuze mu Itorero. Interuro "amatorero yose" (2 Abakorinto 8:12), "batoranyijwe n'amatorero" (2 Abakorinto 8:19), n "Intumwa z'amatorero" (2 Abakorinto 8:23) zigaragaza ibyo neza. Bityo, aya magambo ntabwo atangaje, "Nuko muberekere imbere y'amatorero ibihamya urukundo rwanyu" (2 Abakorinto  8:24).

Kuzana andi maturo ku itorero-igikoresho cyashyizweho n'Imana ku isi-bituma habaho ubumwe kandi na none bikaba ingaruka z'ubumwe (2 Abakorinto 8:13,14). Amafaranga ashobora gutuma habaho ubumwe. Ku rundi ruhande, iyo amaso y'abantu b'Imana adahanzwe ku cyubahiro cy'Imana, amafaranga ashobora gutuma habaho amacakubiri.

`Ni mu buhe buryo Abaroma 15:26, 27 hagaragaza icyifuzo cya Pawulo cy'ubumwe?`

Amaherezo, Pawulo agaragaza igikorwa cyo gusonzoranya impiya nk'umurimo, nk'igikorwa cy'ubuntu, nk'umugisha, nk'igikorwa cyo kuramya, ndetse nko gushyikirana. Mbese ibyo byose bikomoka ku maturo? Bitekerezeho.

`Ni gute gutanga amaturo kwacu tuyaha amatorero ndetse n'umurimo wo hakurya y'inyanja, inshuro nyinshi hakaba ari ahantu kure, bisufasha kuzana ubumwe mu itorero ryaco ku isi hose?`
