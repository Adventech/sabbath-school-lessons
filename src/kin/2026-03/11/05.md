date:   09/09/2026
---

`Soma 2 Abakorinto 8:1-5. Ni iyihe mpamvu ishoboka ishobora kuba yari inyuma y'ubushake bw'Abanyamakedoniya bwo gutangana ubuntu nk'ubwo?`

Inyifato nziza y'Abanyamakedoniya ishobora kubonwa mu buryo bwinshi.

Ubwa mbere: _**batanganye umunezero mwinshi (2 Abakorinto 8:2).**_ Pawulo avuga ko Abanyamakedoniya "umunezero wabo uhebuje n'ubukene bwabo bwinshi byasesekariyemo ubutunzi, kubw'iby'ubuntu batanze" (2 Abakorinto 8:2). Nyuma yaho, yaje kuvuga ko "Imana ikunda utanga anezerewe" (2 Abakorinto 9:7). Ijambo ry'Ikigiriki ryasobanuwe nko "kunezerwa" rigaragara hano mu Isezerano Rishya gusa. Irindi jambo rimeze nka ryo rigaragara ahandi: "Ugira imbabazi azigire anezerewe" (Abaroma 12:8). Amagambo afitanye isano n'iri jambo, rimwe na rimwe agaragara mu nyandiko zitari iza Bibiliya, afite ubusobanuro bw'ibyishimo n'umunezero. Mu 2 Abakorinto 9:7, gutanga unezerewe bisobanuye gutanga udaseta ibirenge.

Ubwa kabiri: _**batanganye ubushake (2 Abakorinto 8:2).**_ Mbere yuko Pawulo agaragaza gutangana ubuntu kw'Abanyamakedoniya, yabanje kuvuga ku "bukene bwabo bwinshi." Ijambo "gutangana ubuntu" (mu Kigiriki, _haplotētos_) rigaragara izindi nshuro ebyiri mu 2 Abakorinto igice cya 8 n'icya 9. Pawulo aravuga ati: "Namwe muzatungishwa muri byose ngo mugire ubuntu bwose" (2 Abakorinto 8:11), bisobanuye ko twahawe kugira ngo natwe dutange. Nyuma gato, yavuze ku "buntu bwinshi bwo gufasha" (2 Abakorinto 9:13). Muri uyu murongo, kugira ubuntu bwo gufasha abandi ni uburyo bwo guhamya ubutumwa bwiza bwa Kristo.

Ubwa gatatu: _**batanze ku "bwende bwabo" (2 Abakorinto 8:3).**_ Ibi bisobanuye ko batanze ku bushake bwabo. Ibi birushaho gutangaza iyo umuntu abonye ko batatanze ibibasagutse, kuko bari batunze bike cyane. Pawulo akoresha igitekerezo kimeze kimwe n'icyo agaragaza ubushake bwa Tito bwo gusura Abanyakorinto. Yagiye i Korinto ku bushake bwe (2 Abakorinto 8:17).

Ubwa kane: _**batanze bazi neza ko gutanga ari amahirwe (2 Abakorinto 8:4).**_ Iyi nyifato igaragarira mu busabe bw'Abanyamakedoniya ubwo basabaga kugira uruhare mu gusonzoranya ubutunzi. "Batwingingira cyane kugira ngo twakire ubuntu bwabo batanze" (2 Abakorinto 8:4).

Ubwa nyuma: _**bagize uruhare mu gusonzoranya impiya nk'igikorwa cyo kwiyegurira Imana burundu (2 Abakorinto 8:5).**_ Pawulo avuga ko "babanje kwitanga ubwabo biha Umwami wacu, kandi biha natwe nk'uko Imana yashatse" (2 Abakorinto 8:5). Kwiha Umwami bituma umuntu yiha n'abandi. Abanyamakedoniya baguye uruhare rwabo mu murimo, bakora ibirenze gutanga ubufasha bw'amafaranga. Ni ukuvuga ko gutanga no kugira ubuntu bitagarukira ku mafaranga gusa.
