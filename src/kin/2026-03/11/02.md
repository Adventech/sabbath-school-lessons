---
title:  Icyitegererezo cya Yesu
date:   06/09/2026
---

Ibivugwa mu 2 Abakorinto igice cya 8 n'icya 9 bifitanye isano n'ibyo Pawulo yavugaga atera abizera b'i Korinto umwete wo kutangiza gahunda yo gusonzoranya inkunga yari igenewe amatorero yari akennye yo muri Yudeya.

Mu bigaragara, bari baramaze kugambirira kubikora (2 Abakorinto 8:10,11; 2 Abakorinto 9:5; na 1 Abakorinto 16:1-4), ariko ibibazo by'imibanire byari hagati ye na bo byari byatumye ibintu birushaho kugorana. Ubwo yari amaze guhangana n'ibyo bibazo (2 Abakorinto 1-7), Pawulo yagarutse ku mwanzuro w'uwo murimo (2 Abakorinto 8-9).

Bigitangira, Pawulo ararikira abantu kwitegereza urugero rw'Abanyamakedoniya (2 Abakorinto 8:1-7), bari bafite ubukene bukabije ariko ntibubabuze "gusesekaramo ubutunzi kubw' iby'ubuntu batanze" (2 Abakorinto 8:2). Ubukene n'ubuntu bishobora kugendana. Nyamara, ubu buntu butangarirwa bw'Abanyamakedoniya ni ishusho y'ubuntu bwa Yesu bwagaragariye mu kutwitangira (2 Abakorinto 8:8-15).

`Soma 2 Abakorinto 8:9. Ni iki uyu murongo utubwira cyerekeranye n'icyitegererezo Yesu yaduhaye ?`

Amagambo ya Pawulo ari mu 2 Abakorinto 8:9 ni bumwe mu butumwa butangaje, bufite imbaraga kandi bwimbitse muri Bibiliya yose. Pawulo avuga inkuru y'umurimo wa Yesu, ariko ayivuga mu magambo make cyane. Hano hari inyigisho nyinshi cyane z'iby'iyobokamana. Iyi ni inkuru yo gucungurwa, ariko ivugwa mu murongo umwe gusa.

Ikirushijeho gutangaza ni uko iyi nkuru ivugwa hakoreshejwe imvugo yo mu by'ubutunzi. Yego, Yesu yari umutunzi. Ubutunzi bwe burengurira ku buryo yari ameze mu ijuru (Yohana 17:5). Yafashe icyemezo cyo guhinduka umukene binyuze mu gusiga icyubahiro yari afite mu ijuru akaza muri iyi si y'imibabaro. Yahindutse umukene (Luka 9:58). Nubwo yari ahwanye n'Imana, "Yisize ubusa, ajyana akamero k'umugaragu w'imbata, agira ishusho y'umuntu" (Abafilipi 2:7). "Yicisha bugufi, araganduka ntiyanga no gupfa ndetse urupfu rwo ku musaraba" (Abafilipi 2:8).

Yesu yatanze ubugingo bwe kugira ngo tuzabashe kubana na we iteka ryose. Impano ye yari ifite intego: Agakiza kacu. Ubusonga n'umurimo w'ivugabutumwa birajyana. Mu 2 Abakorinto 8-9 hatubwira igitekerezo cy'ituro-faranga ryihariye, ariko iki gitekerezo gishingiye kuri Yesu. Muri iki cyumweru, tuzarebera hamwe amahame y'iyobokamana arebana no gutanga impano z'amaturo ashingiye ku kuba Kristo yaritanze ubwe. 

`Ibande ku kuvuka, kubaho, urupfu ndetse no ku kuzuka bya Yesu. Igihe usobanukiwe ko ibyo byose byakorewe kugira ngo ushobore kugira ibyiringiro by'ikintu kirenze iyi mibereho ibabaje ya hano ku isi, ni uba ukwiriye gukora?`
