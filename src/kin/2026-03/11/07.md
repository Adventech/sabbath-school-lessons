title:  Ibindi byo Kwigwa no Kuzirikanwaa
date:   11/09/2026
---

Soma igitabo cyanditswe na Ellen G. White cyitwa, _Ibyakozwe n'Intumwa_, pp.207-213, igice kivuga "Itorero Ritangana Ubuntu".

"Abantu bose bafite imitima yuzuye urukundo rwa Kristo bazagera ikirenge mu cye, We wahindutse umukene ku bwacu kugira ngo muri ubwo bukene duhinduke abatunzi. Impano zose bahawe n'Imana, (amafaranga, igihe, no kumenyekana) bazabifata gusa nk'uburyo bwo gutuma umurimo wo kwamamaza ubutumwa bwiza utera imbere. Uko ni ko byari bimeze mu itorero rya mbere; kandi mu itorero ry'iki gihe, iyo bigaragaye ko kubw'imbaraga ya Mwuka Muziranenge abizera bazinutswe iby'isi kandi bakemera kwitanga kugira ngo bagenzi babo bashobore kumva ubutumwa bwiza, ukuri kwamamazwa kuzatera impinduka ikomeye ku bakumva." -Ellen G. White, _Ibyakozwe n'Intumwa_, p. 48.

"Imana ntikeneye amaturo yacu. Ntabwo twashobora kuyikungahaza binyuze mu mpano zacu. Umunyazaburi yaravuze ati: 'Ibintu byose ni ibyawe, kandi ibyo tuguhaye bivuye mu byawe.' Nyamara Imana iduha amahirwe yo kugaragaza ko twanyuzwe n'ibyo yadukoreye, tukabigaragarisha umwete wo kwitanga tukagirira abandi neza nk'uko twagiriwe. Ubwo ni bwo buryo bwonyine dushobora kugaragariza Imana ko twanyuzwe kandi ko tuyikunda. Nta bundi buryo yaduhaye butari ubu."-Ellen G. White, _Inama Ku Busonga_, p.18.

"Mbega uburyo impano Imana yahaye umuntu ari impano ikomeye, kandi mbega uburyo Imana yacu ishaka ko iyo mpano imera! Kubw'ubuntu butagereranywa, [Imana] yaratanze, kugira ngo ibashe gukiza bene muntu bigometse maze ibafashe kubona umugambi n'urukundo byayo. Mbese binyuze mu mpano zawe n'amaturo yawe, uzagaragaza ko nta kintu cyiza cyane utekereza utaha 'Uwatanze Umwana we w'ikinege kugira ngo umwizera wese atarimbuka ahubwo ahabwe ubugingo buhoraho?' "-Ellen G. White, _in The Advent Review and Sabbath Herald_, May 15, 1900.

IBIBAZO:

`Ibande ku Rwandiko rwa Kabiri rw'Abakorinto igice cya 8 n'icya 9. Kuki urugero rwa Yesu ari ingenzi cyane mu byerekeranye n'ubusonga?`
`Muri Yohana 3:16 hagaragaza ko imvugo yo mu ijuru ari ugutanga. Soma Yohana 15:13; Abefeso 5:2, 25; Abagalatiya 2:19, 20 na 1 Yohana 3:16. Ni iki iyi mirongo ihuriyeho n'ibivugwa muri Yohana 3:16, kandi ni ubuhe butumwa dushobora gukuramo?`
`Ushingiye ku byo wasomye mu 2 Abakorinto 8-9, ni izihe nyungu z'umuntu ku giti cye zo gutanga?`
`Nk'ibyiyongera ku gutanga amaturo mu buryo buhoraho, ni ikihe kintu kindi ushobora gukora mu kwigana urugero rwa Yesu rwo kwitanga?`
