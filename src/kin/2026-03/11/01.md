---
title:  Ubusonga n'Umurimo w'Ivugabutumwa
date:   05/09/2026
---

### Aho icyigisho cy'iki cyumweru gishingiye:
2 Abakorinto 8-9; Yohana 3:16; Yohana 17:5; Luka 9:58; Ibyahishuwe 13:8; Abaroma 12:8; Abaroma 15:26, 27.

> <p>Icyo kwibukwa:</p>
> "Kuko muzi ubuntu bw'Umwami wacu Yesu Kristo uko yari umutunzi, maze agahinduka umukene ku bwanyu kugira ngo ubukene bwe bubatungishe (2 Abakorinto 8:9).

Urwandiko rwa Kabiri rw'Abakorinto igice cya 8 n'icya 9 bigaragaza ko Pawulo yahaye Abakorinto amahirwe yo gufasha bene se na bashiki babo b'i Yudeya. Ayo magambo agaragaza ko gutanga ari amahirwe Imana iba iduhaye, kugira ngo tubashe kwigana imico ya Kristo yo kwitanga. Imvugo yo mu ijuru ni iyo gutanga. Zirikana aya magambo y'ingenzi, "Kuko Imana yakunze abari mu isi cyane, byatumye itanga umwana wayo w'ikinege" (Yohana 3:16).

Nanone kandi, muri Yohana 3:16 hagaragaza neza umugambi Imana yari ifite ubwo yatangaga Yesu, kugira ngo "Umwizera wese atarimbuka ahubwo ahabwe ubugingo buhoraho." Ubusonga n'umurimo w'ivugabutuma birajyana muri uyu murongo. Ntabwo bishobora gutandukana nk'uko bimeze ku mpande ebyiri z'igiceri kimwe. Ntibitangaje kuba Pawulo avuga ko we n'abo bari bafatanyije umurimo bari "ibisonga byeguriwe ubwiru bw'Imana". (1 Abakorinto 4:1). Natwe turi ibisonga muri ubwo buryo.

Muri iki cyumweru, tuzarebera hamwe uburyo ubusonga ndetse n'umurimo w'ivugabutumwa bishingiye ku rugero rwa Yesu. Mu by'ukuri, ubusonga n'umurimo w'ivugabutumwa ntibishobora gutandukana. Ubusonga bufasha itorero kubona amafaranga ndetse n'abakozi bo gukora umurimo w'Imana.
