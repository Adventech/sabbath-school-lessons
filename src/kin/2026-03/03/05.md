---
title:  Kuba Umugaragu nka Kristo
date:   15/07/2026
---

`Soma 1 Abakorinto 4:1, 2. Ni iki uyu murongo wigisha ku byerekeye uburyo bukwiriye umuntu agomba kubonamo abayobozi bayobora abantu?`

Murwa 1 Abakorinto 3:1-4, Pawulo abona ko udutsiko duterwa no kudakura mu bya Mwuka. Nyamara, mbere yo kuvuga kuri iyo ngingo, ahamya ko, "twahawe gutekereza kimwe na Kristo" (1 Abanyakorinti 2:16, BII). Iyi nteruro yerekeza ku buryo bwo gutekereza no gukora byarangaga Kristo. Mu yandi magambo, umwizera aba agomba kugira "imitekerereze ya Kristo" igihe atekereza kandi agakora nka Kristo. Nyamara, gushyira mu bikorwa uburyo utekereza mubyo ukora byose mu mibereho yawe, ntabwo ari ikintu cyoroshye, siko biri? Mu gihe isi yategekwaga n'Abagiriki n'Abaromani, hariho ukurwanira isumbwe kwinshi mu banyapolitiki, abacurabwenge, abahanga mu mitekerereze, n'abayobozi b'idini. Gushaka cyane gukurikiza ibyemewe mu muco wabo, byashoye itorero ry'i Korinto ku gukurikiza urugero rugenderwaho n'ab'isi. Ibyo bishobora ndetse no kubera akaga itorero ryo muri iki gihe.

`Soma Abafilipi 2:5-8. Ni mu buhe buryo iryo somo ridufasha gusobanukirwa n'icyo amagambo "gutekereza kwa Kristo" asobanuye" (1 Abakorinto 2:16)?`

Nk'uko byari bimeze i Korinto, kwirema ibice byari biri no mu itorero ry'i Filipi (Abafilipi 2:1-4), ahari ku rugero ruto rutameze nk'uko byari mu itorero ry'i Korinto. Mu Abafilipi 2:1-8 hatwigisha ko kuba umugaragu nka Kristo bisaba gupfa kuri kamere, no kudashyira inyungu zacu imbere, ahubwo tugashakisha icyatuma abandi bamererwa neza, kurenza uko twitekerezaho, nk'uko Kristo yabigenzaga.

Kuba umugaragu nka Kristo nicyo Pawulo yasobanuraga akoresha iyi mvugo yo "kuba abakozi ba Kristo" (1 Abakorinto 4:1). Iyo mvugo ishobora gusobanura ko bakorera Kristo nk'abamwungirije cyangwa nk'abagaragu be. Birasobanutse neza ko uburyo bwiza abayobozi bayobora abantu bakwiriye gushingiraho ari ugukurikiza urugero rw'ubuyobozi bwa Kristo. Abagaragu bongeye kwerekanwa nk' "ibisonga" (1 Abakorinto 4:1, 2). Igisonga ni umuntu washinzwe gucunga umutungo w'undi muntu. Kandi burya ibyo dufite byose, ni ibya Kristo.

`Tekereza kandi usenga wite kur'ubu butumwa bwo mu Abafilipi 2:5-8. Ni mu buhe buryo twakwakira icyo butubwira cyerekeye urukundo rw'Imana rwo kwisiga ubusa ku bwacu? Ni kuki kandi dukeneye natwe gupfa ku narijye, mu buryo dushobora natwe kwerekana urukundo nk'urwo aho turi?`
