---
title:  Guhurizwa muri Yesu
date:   13/07/2026
---

`Soma 1 Abakorinto 1:10. Uratekereza ko ari iki Pawulo yasobanuraga avuga aya magambo: "mwumvikane, mwe kwicamo ibice, ahubwo mushyire hamwe muhuje ibitekerezo n'imigambi" (BII) ?`

Kurema udutsiko biba bivuze ko nta sano muba mugifitanye na Kristo (1 Abakorinto 1:10). Imana iduhamagarira "kugirana ubumwe n'Umwana wayo, Yesu Kristo Umwami wacu" (1 Abakorinto 1:9). Umwami wacu ni Kristo, kandi tugomba guhurizwa muri We. Bityo, igisubizo cy'ikibazo Pawulo yababajije ngo: "Mbese Kristo yagabanyijwemo ibice? Pawulo ni we wababambiwe? Cyangwa mwabatirijwe mu izina rya Pawulo?" (1 Abakorinto 1:13), cyumvikana ko ari "Oya!" Kristo ntabwo yaciwemo ibice. Ahubwo ni Kristo watubambiwe ku musaraba. Twabatirijwe "mu izina rya Data wa twese, n'Umwana n'Umwuka Wera" (Matayo 28:19).

Pawulo avuga ko turi "umubiri wa Kristo, kandi umuntu wese wo muri mwe ni urugingo rwawo" (1 Abakorinto 12:27). Mu gihe umubiri ugizwe n'ingingo nyinshi, buri rugingo rufite inshingano yarwo, ntibiwubuza gukomeza kuba umubiri umwe. Kugira ngo umubiri ubashe gukora neza, ni uko buri rugingo rukora inshingano zarwo nk'uko bikwiye kandi mu bushobozi bwarwo. Iyi mvugoshusho igaragaza ko icyo Pawulo ashaka ari ubumwe, atari ibintu bisa. Ashaka ubumwe mu maharakwinshi. Ikirenze ibyo, [Pawulo] arashaka ubumwe nubwo hari ubudasa.

Nyamara kandi, ibitekerezo byose n'imigambi yose bigomba kwegurirwa Kristo, Umwami wacu. Ukuri k'uko Kristo ari Umwami wacu ni ingingo y'ingenzi Pawulo yagiye asubiramo kenshi, mu gihe yatangiraga igice cya mbere cy'urwandiko yandikiraga Abanyakorinto (1 Abakorinto 1:2, 7, 8, 9, 10). Niyo mpamvu, mbere yuko Pawulo akemura ikibazo cy'udutsiko n'icy'abayobozi b'itorero, yabanje gushimangira ko twese tugomba kwakira Yesu nk'Umwami wacu. Itorero ntabwo rishingiye ku bayobozi b'abantu. Abakristo bashingiye kuri Yesu.

Ugushimangira ko kugira Yesu akaba Umwami wawe aricyo kigomba kubanziriza ibindi byose nk uko tubisanga mu rwa 1 Abakorinto, bidufasha gusobanukirwa n'icyo Pawulo yashakaga gusobanura muri aya magambo: "mushyire hamwe muhuje ibitekerezo n'imigambi" (1 Abakorinto 1:10, BII). Imvugo y'Ikigiriki yasobanuwemo "gushyira hamwe" ikomoka ku nshinga _katartizō_, ivuga ko ikintu kigomba kuvugururwa kigasubirana umwimerere wacyo. Igihe mu bayobozi b'itorero yajemo gucikamo ibice, umubano mwiza hagati y'abarigize ugomba kuvugururwa, ugasubira uko wari umeze mbere, kandi ibyo bishoboka igihe bavuguruye ubumwe bafitanye na Kristo, bakunga ubumwe nawe ndetse no mu rupfu rwe.

`Mu gihe cy'imyaka itari myinshi ishize, hamwe mu matorero y'Abadiventisiti b'Umunsi wa Karindwi hadutse gahunda yo gushyiraho amatsinda mato y'abiga ibyigisho bya Bibiliya. Ni irihe tandukaniro riri hagati y'udutsiko n'amatsinda mato? Ni mu buhe buryo twashishoza neza ayo matsinda mato ntahinduke udutsiko?`
