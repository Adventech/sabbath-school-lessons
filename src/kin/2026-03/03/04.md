---
title:  Ubwenge no Gukura
date:   14/07/2026
---

Muri rusange, udutsiko tubaho bitewe n'uko abantu babona abayobozi babo nk'abafite isumbwe rikomeye. Icyo ni ikintu giteza akaga ku bumwe bw'itorero no ku buzima bw'imibereho y'ibya mwuka y'abizera, kuko habamo kugoreka uburyo umurimo wa Gikristo ugaragara, bikaba byatuma itorero riha isumbwe abayobozi bamwe, bigakorwa hirengagijwe ko ibyo bihutaza bagenzi babo. Ingaruka z'imyitwarire nk'iyo ni umwuka utari mwiza wo kurwanira imyanya uvuka mu itorero, ariwo ushobora gutuma itorero ricikamo ibice, Ibirenze ibyo, niba dufata abayobozi b'abantu nk'aho aribo Ubukristo bwacu bushingiyeho, tuba turi mu kaga ko kwimura Kristo mu mwanya w'ibanze agomba kugira mu mibereho yacu.

`Soma 1 Abakorinto 3:1-4. Ni mu buhe buryo ahangaha Pawulo asobanura ukugwingira mu bya mwuka kw'Abanyakorinto?`

Pawulo asobanura neza ko gukura mu bya Mwuka biyobora umwizera kunyurwa no kugira ubwenge bw'Imana (1 Abakorinto 2:6, 7), bukaba butugeraho binyuze mu kwigishwa na Mwuka w'Imana (1 Abakorinto 2:13), bukaba buhabanye n'ubwenge bw'iki gihe aribwo bwenge bw'iyi isi (1 Abakorinto 2:6), ubwenge bw'abantu (1 Abakorinto 2:13). Ubwenge bw'Imana bwahishuriwe mu Musaraba wa Kristo (1 Abakorinto 2:1-4). Mu buryo bw'umwihariko ubwenge bw'Imana bwahishuriwe mu mubabaro .wa Kristo, mu rupfu rwe, no mu kuzuka kwe. Bityo rero, mbere yuko dusubira ku kurarikirwa kuba umwe (1 Abakorinto 3:1-17), Pawulo ashaka kò abasomyi b'urwandiko rwe bamenya igikenerwa kugira ngo agire ubwenge nyakuri no gukurira muri Kristo.

Abakristo b'abanyabwenge kandi bakuze baba ari abantu bayoborwa na Mwuka w'Imana, ntabwo baba ari aba kamere, ntabwo baba bameze nk'abana b'impinja (1 Abakorinto 3:1). Bagereranya iby'umwuka n'iby'umwuka bindi, kubera ko "dusobanuza iby'Umwuka iby'umwuka bindi" (1 Abakorinto 2:13, 14). Abakristo b'abanyabwenge kandi bakuze bagaburirwa ibyokurya bikomeye, si amata (1 Abakorinto 3:2; bigereranye n'ibivugwa mu Abaheburayo 5:12). Umwizera "unywa amata aba ataraca akenge mu by'ijambo ryo gukiranuka, kuko akiri uruhinja; ariko ibyokurya bikomeye ni iby'abakuru bafite ubwenge, kandi bamenyereye gutandukanya ikibi n'icyiza" (Abaheburayo 5:13, 14). Abakristo baciye akenge kandi b'abanyabwenge ntabwo bavuga ibintu nk'ibi: "Jyewe ndi uwa Pawulo" cyangwa ngo bavuge bati, "jyeweho ndi uwa Apolo" (1 Abakorinto 3:4), bagenda bavuga abantu batandukanye. N'ubundi kandi, abo bantu na bo ni "abakozi bakorera Imana" (I Abakorinto 3:9). Natwe, nk'abagize itorero, turi abakozi bo mu ruzabibu rw'Imana, inzu y'Imana n'urusengero rwayo (1 Abakorinto 3:9, 16, 17). Twese turi ab'Imana binyuze muri Kristo. (1 Abakorinto 3:11).

`Mbese hari ubwo wigeze uhemukirwa n'umuntu wemeraga mu buryo bukomeye? Niba ibyo byarakubayeho, ni ayahe masomo byakwigishije?`
