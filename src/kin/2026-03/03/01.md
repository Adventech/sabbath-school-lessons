---
title:  Ubumwe muri Kristo
date:   11/07/2026
---

### Aho icyigisho cy'iki cyumweru gishingiye:
1 Abakorinto 1:12-17; Abaroma 1:29; 1 Abakorinto 1:10; 1 Abakorinto 3:1-4; Abafilipi 2:5-8; 2 Abakorinto 11:23-28; Abokolosayi 1:24.

> <p>Icyo kwibukwa:</p>
> "Ariko bene Data, ndabingingira mu izina ry'Umwami wacu Yesu Kristo, kugira ngo mwese muvuge kumwe; kandi he kugira ibice biremwa muri mwe, ahubwo muhurize hamwe, muhuje imitima n'inama" (1 Abakorinto 1:10).

Abakunda kwitegereza imibereho y'inyamaswa zo mu gasozi bazi ko ibyo biremwa, biba biri hamwe mu masenga, mu mashyo, mu matsinda, uko bigenda bitandukana mu bwoko no mu bunini cyangwa mu buto. Birumvikana ko ibyo biremwa byose, guhera ku birura (nk'uko bisanzwe), ukageza ku bifi binini, ndetse no ku kiguri cy'intozi, birahorana, bigakorera hamwe. Inguge zizwi ku bumwe zigirana n'umurunga ukomeye uzihuza zigira, rimwe na rimwe ziba mu matsinda ava ku nguge 15-150. Nyamara, ubwo bumwe zigirana ntabwo buri gihe burangwa no kuzuzanya, kuko rimwe na rimwe zirwana hagati zazo.

Abantu nabo ni uko bameze mu rugero runaka; ni ukuvuga ko, usibye no kuba bakunze kuba mu matsinda, nabo rimwe na rimwe usanga barwana hagati yabo mur'ayo matsinda babamo. Ibyo bibaho cyane ndetse no mu itorero! Abantu barema udutsiko, akenshi bafite umuyobozi runaka babogamiyeho. Kandi aho bibera bibi, ni uko agatsiko kamwe gashobora kutumvikana n'akandi.

Mbese ibyo wigeze ubibona mu itorero ryawe?

Niba bihari, noneho ushobora kumva ikibazo Pawulo yari ahanganye nacyo mu itorero ry' i Korinto. Muri iki cyumweru tuzareba ibivugwa mu rwandiko Pawulo yandikiye ab'i Korinto, igice 1-4 (1 Abakorinto 1-4), aho intumwa Pawulo yahanganye n'ikibazo cy'intonganya za hato na hato zari muri iryo torero n'uburyo yagikemuye, binyuze mu kubararikira kugira ubumwe muri Kristo.
