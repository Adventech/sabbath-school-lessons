---
title:  Ikibazo cy'Udutsiko mu Itorero
date:   12/07/2026
---

Pawulo ahamagarira abagize itorero ry'i Korinto agira ati "mwese mwumvikane, kandi mwe kwicamo ibice, ahubwo mushyire hamwe muhuje ibitekerezo n'imigambi" (1 Abanyakorinti 1:10, BII), iyo ngingo akaba ariyo yibanzeho mu bice bine bibanza by'urwandiko rwa mbere yandikiye Abanyakorinto. Muby'ukuri, abahanga mu bumenyi bahuriza ku kintu cyo gushyira hamwe, bavuga ko ari ingingo ihurizahamwe imigabane yose igize urwo rwandiko.

`Soma 1 Abakorinto 1:12-17. Ni mu buhe buryo uwo murongo udufasha gusobanukirwa uburyo ari bibi kwicamo ibice mu bayobozi b'itorero? Umuti Pawulo yatanze kuri icyo kibazo ni uwuhe?`

Pawulo yakoresheje amagambo akomeye asobanura ikibazo cyo kudashyira hamwe kw'abizera b'itorero ry'i Korinto. Yakoresheje ijambo ry'Ikigiriki _schisma_ ("ibice", 1 Abakorinto 1:10) n'irindi jambo _eris_ ("amakimbirane," 1 Abakorinto 1:11). Izina _schisma_ (kimwe n'inshinga schizō, bisobanura "gucamo ibice") yakoreshejwe n'ahandi mu Isezerano Rishya mu gusobanura kugira ibitekerezo bitandukanya bivamo kwirema ibice. Niyo mpamvu, izina _eris_ ("amakimbirane") akenshi riboneka mu rutonde rw'imico mibi idakwiriye kuranga Abakristo.

`Soma mu Abaroma 1:29, Abaroma 13:13, 1 Abakorinto 3:3, 2Abakorinto 12:20, n'Abagalatiya 5:20. Ni ibihe byaha bindi biri mu rutonde rw'ibyaha bikururwa na eris ("amakimbirane," "amahane")? Ni iki ibyo bitubwira kerekeranye n'ububi bw'ibyo bintu?`

Amakimbirane yari mu itorero ry'i Korinto yareruye ajya ku mugaragaro kugeza nubwo abo mu itorero bajya kuburanira mu nkiko umuntu ahanganye na mugenzi we (1 Abakorinto 6:1- 3). Pawulo yarababwiye ati, "Ibyo mbivugiye kubakoza isoni," abitewe n'uko kuregana mu nkiko (kuregana ku bacamanza) hagati y'abizera b'itorero (1 Abakorinto 6:5). Mu by'ukuri, ntibigeze birengagiza ko bari bafite ibyo batandukaniyeho, ndetse n'igihe bizihizaga Ifunguro ry'Umwami (1 Abakorinto 11:17-22).

Ikibazo cyo kutagira ubumwe mu bizera b'itorero ni ikibazo giteye inkeke, kikaba cyari gihagaritse umutima Pawulo, akaba ari nacyo kibazo cya mbere yibanzeho cyane muri uru rwandiko yandikiye Abanyakorinto.

`Ongera usome ibyanditswe mu 1 Abakorinto 1:12-27. Tekereza uburyo iri somo ridufasha kumva neza impamvu udutsiko ari tubi cyane ku bumwe bw'itorero. Ni iki itorero ryawe rishobora gukora kugira ngo rikumire ikibazo nk'icyo?`
