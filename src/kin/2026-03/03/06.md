---
title:  Imibereho Igaragaza Umusaraba
date:   16/07/2026
---

Kuba tutagomba kurema udutsiko, cyane cyane mu buryo bwo kubogamira ku ruhande rw'abayobozi b'abantu, ntibishatse kuvuga ko tutagomba gushyigikira abayobozi bacu. Tugomba gushima no gufasha abayobozi mu murimo w'itorero. Imana yahaye abantu inshingano zo gukomeza umurimo wayo hano ku isi. Abayobozi b'itorero bagaragaza imibereho iganduka no kwicisha bugufi kubera Umusaraba bagomba gutegwa amatwi kandi inama zabo zigakurikizwa.

Kandi ibyo ni ukubera ko Umusaraba wonyine ufite ububasha bwo guhindura imbaraga y'uburyo ubwo aribwo bwose bwo gushaka ubutegetsi mu buryo bunyuranyije no gucishwa bugufi n'ljambo ry'Imana. Abayobozi bafite imico nk'iya Kristo bavuga ko intsinzi yabo mu murimo bayikesha Imana yonyine. Mu murimo we wa hano ku isi, na Yesu nk'umuntu yaheshaga Imana icyubahiro (Yohana 17:4).

Dukurikije ibyo Pawulo avuga, umurimo wa Gikristo w'ubudahemuka ugomba gushingira ku cyo tubasha kwita inyigisho y'lyobokamana ry'Umusaraba. Umusaraba ni uguhishurwa k'ubwenge bw'Imana n'ububasha bwayo bwo gukiza. Na none kandi, unagaragaza ubwenge bwa muntu nk'ubupfapfa. Mu 1 Abakorinto 4:1-13, Pawulo asobanura neza uko iyo nyigisho y'Umusaraba iteye. Icya mbere, abanza kugaragaza ko ari Imana ishyiraho amahame agenga ubuyobozi bwa Gikristo (1 Abakorinto 4:1-5). Icya kabiri, anavuga ko kubabazwa ari ikimenyetso kiranga umurimo nyakuri wa Gikristo nyakuri (1 Abakorinto 4:9, 11-13). Iyi ngingo ya kabiri ikeneye gusobanurwa biruseho.

`Soma 2 Abakorinto 11:23-28 no mu Abakolosayi 1:24. Ni iki ibyo bitwigisha ku byerekeye n'icyo bivuze kubabazwa uhorwa Kristo?`

Abayobozi b'Abakristo bagera ikirenge mu cya Yesu binyuze mu kuba biteguye kubabazwa ku bwa bene se na bashiki babo, ndetse n'igihe byaba ngombwa gupfa bazira umurimo bakora. Pawulo yivuga ubwe hamwe na Apolo nk' "abaciriwe urwo gupfa" (1 Abakorinto 4:9). Bagaragajwe nk'abari bahanganye n'ibibazo byo kubura ibyokurya n'amazi yo kunywa, kwambara ubusa no gukubitwa ibipfunsi, no kutagira aho kurambika umusaya kuko bari inzererezi" (1 Abakorinto 4:11). Ibyiyongera kuri ibyo, baranatutswe, baratotezwa, barabasebya, yemwe "baduhinduye nk'icyavu cy'iyi si, mbese batugize nk'ibishingwe bose bajugunye" (1 Abanyakorinti 4:12, 13, BII). Ibirenze ibyo, mu buryo bwo kuninura Abanyakorinto babita, abakire, abami. abanyabwenge, n'abafite itandukaniro n'abandi (1 Abakorinto 4:8, 10), Pawulo yagaragaje ko uko kwishyira hejuru kudakwiriye guhabwa umwanya mu buyobozi nyakuri bwa Gikristo, kuko ari inkomoko yo gucikamo ibice kw'itorero (1 Abakorinto 4:6).

`Mbese wababajwe bingana iki uzira Kristo, mu nshingano waba ufite mu itorero iyo ariyo yose? Ni ayahe masomo ashobora kuba ari mu gisubizo cyawe?`
