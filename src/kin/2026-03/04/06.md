---
title:  Gushyingiranwa no Kuba Ingaragu
date:   23/07/2026
---

Uguhamya kwa Pawulo ko imibiri yacu "ari insengero z'Umwuka Wera" (1 Abakorinto 6:19) bigaragara mu cyerekezo cy'umuburo aha abantu abihanangiriza ku kibazo kijyanye n'ubusambanyi. Kuba urusengero rwa Mwuka Wera nibwo buryo bwonyine bwo kubaho imibereho izira inenge. Itorero ni umuryango w'Abakristo witandukanya ubwawo n'ibiwuzengurutse. Ibyo bishoboka iyo Mwuka Wera aririmo.

`Soma mu 1 Abakorinto 6:19-7:9. Ni mu buhe buryo iyo mirongo itanga umucyo ku buryo umuntu ashobora gushyira mu bikorwa itegeko ryo "kuzibukira gusambana" (1 Abakorinto 6:18)?`

Hari amasomo y'ingenzi yerekeranye no guhuza ibitsina dusanga mu rwa 1 Abakorinto 7. Tubivuze mu ncamake, iki gice gishobora kugabanwamo ibika bibiri: (1) amabwiriza arebana no gushyingiranwa (1 Abakorinto 7:1-24) na (2) amabwiriza arebana no kudashaka (guhitamo kuba ingaragu) (1 Abakorinto 7:25-40). Mu rwandiko rwa mbere Pawulo yandikiye 1 Abakorinto 7 hadufasha gusobanukirwa ko kuvuga ku bijyanye n'imibonano mpuzabitsina ari ikintu cy'ingenzi cyane kandi ari ngombwa kuko bikenewe ko abantu babimenya.

Nyamara, iyo dusoma igice cya 7 cy'urwandiko rwa mbere Pawulo yandikiye Abakorinto, tugomba kwibuka ko Pawulo yatangaga ibisubizo by'ibibazo by'ingutu byari mu itorero ry'i Korinto. Naho ubundi, ingingo zimwe zishobora kugaragaza ko ahari yari afite imyumvire mike mu buryo yabonaga ibyo gushyingiranwa, kandi ntabwo ari uko byari bimeze (1 Timoteyo 4:1-3, 1 Timoteyo 5:14; Abaheburayo 13:4).

Mu byo twitaho, itegeko "muzibukire gusambana" riboneka mu (1 Abakorinto 6:18) rihuzwa n'igitekerezo cyo kwifatanya na Kristo (1 Abakorinto 6:17) no kuba urusengero rwa Mwuka Wera (1 Abakorinto 6:19). Mbese hari indi nzira yarushaho kuba nziza yo kuzibukira ubusambanyi? Birumvikana ntayo.

Na none ariko, Imana niyo yaremye ibitsina, ariko kubihuza bikaba bigomba kwishimirwa n'abashakanye gusa. Imibonano mpuzabitsina ni amahirwe yishimirwa n'umugabo n'umugore bashyingiranwe, bukaba aribwo buryo bwemewe gusa na Bibiliya.

Igihe yavugaga ku "kuzibukira ubusambanyi," Pawulo ashobora kuba yaratekerezaga inkuru ya Yosefu (Itangiriro 39:6-18). Bibiliya ivuga ko mbere y'uko umugore wa Potifaro agaragaza irari yari afitiye Yosefu, Yosefu "yasohotse mu nzu arahunga" (Itangiriro 39:18). Inshuro nke ibyo byavuzwe mu Itangiriro 39:6-18, ni inshuro enye. Bibiliya ntivuga neza uko byose byagenze, ariko bigaragara ko Yosefu yategereje gukora igikorwa cy'imibonano mpuzabitsina ari uko amaze gushaka (Itangiriro 41:45). Yari umugabo wuzuye Mwuka Wera (Itangiriro 41:38) kandi yashakaga gukora igikwiriye imbere y'amaso y'Imana.

`Nk'abagize itorero, ni mu buhe buryo dushobora kwirinda twebwe ubwacu kugira imyumvire yabaye gikwira y'uburyo bwo guhuza ibitsina bwamaze gukwira mu muco?`
