---
title:  Guhangana n'Ibyaha by'Urukozasoni
date:   20/07/2026
---

Guhangana n'ibibazo birebana n'ubusambanyi buri gihe biba bigoye. Byari bigoye kuri Pawulo, kandi natwe biratugora. Mu bihe nk'ibyo, tugomba kuba indahemuka ku Byanditswe kandi tugakemura icyo kibazo mu rukundo kandi tukagisengera. Ntitugomba na rimwe kwibagirwa ko intego yacu ari ugusubiza ibintu mu buryo.

`Ongera usome 1 Abakorinto 5:1-13. Ni mu buhe buryo Pawulo ababwira uko bakwiriye gukemura ikibazo nk'icyo?`

Mu 1 Abakorinto igice cya 5, Pawulo agaragaza neza ko ibyaha by'urukozasoni by'ubusambanyi bwakozwe ku mugaragaro bisaba igihano cy'itorero. Avuga ko umuntu wakoze icyaha cy'ubusambanyi bukabije akwiriye gukurwa mu bantu (1 Abakorinto 5:2), agacirwa urubanza (1 Abakorinto 5:3), agahabwa Satani (1 Abakorinto 5:5), kandi "ashyirwe kure yabo" (1 Abakorinto 5:13). Abizera b'itorero babwiwe ko "batagomba kwifatanya nawe" (1 Abakorinto 5:9, 11), "ndetse badakwiriye no gusangira nawe" (1 Abakorinto 5:11). Pawulo yakoresheje amagambo akomeye ashobora gusa n'aho akomeretsa amatwi y'abantu bo muri iki gihe, ariko amagambo ye agomba kumvikana mu mujyo w'amateka y'igihe yavugiwe. Ikindi, uyumva agomba kwibuka ko arimo guhangana n'ikibazo cy'imibereho y'icyaha cyabaye gikwira. Ubusanzwe, igihe ibintu bikabije kuba bibi, ni ngombwa gukoresha amagambo akomeye. Uko byaba biri kose, ibisobanuro bigufi by'amagambo amwe n'amwe bishobora kuba ingirakamaro.

**"Uwakoze icyaha akurwe muri mwe"** (1 Abakorinto 5:2; 1 Abakorinto 5:13). Ibyo birebana no guhabwa igihano cy'itorero.

**"Mushyikirize uwo muntu Satani"** (1 Abakorinto 5:5). Kubera ko uwo muntu atahisemo kuba munsi y'uburinzi bw'Imana, ngo abeho imibereho iyumvira, agomba kurekwa, akegurirwa Satani yahisemo gukorera. Iyo mvugo ishobora kuba ivuga ikintu nk'iki: "Nimumureke asarure imbuto z'ibyo yakoze."

**"Ntimwifatanye nawe"** (1 Abakorinto 5:9, 11), **"ndetse ntimusangire n'umuntu nk'uwo"** (1 Abakorinto 5:11). Kwifatanya n'abasambanyi byafatwaga nk'ikintu kibi cyane cyateza akaga, kubera ko bashobora guhindura abandi bagakurikiza imico yabo. Mu bihe bya kera, gusangira n'umuntu byasobanuraga kuba muhuje n'indangagaciro. Twese turi abantu bashobora guhindurwa n'abari hafi yacu, tukaba tugomba kwirinda mu buryo bwose bushoboka, cyane cyane igihe dusakiranye n'ikibazo nk'icyo.

**"Umwuka we ubone kuzakira ku munsi w'Umwami Yesu"** (1 Abakorinto 5:5). Igihano cy'Itorero gisubiza umuntu ku murongo. Kiba kigamije gutuma abanyabyaha bisubiraho bakareka imibereho yabo y'ubunyacyaha. Ibyo bishoboka ko aribyo Pawulo yavugaga "kugira ngo umubiri we urimbuke" (1 Abakorinto 5:5). Ikindi gishoboka ni uko uwo muntu wakoze icyaha gikomeye gutyo uvugwa mu 1 Abakorinto 5 ari umuntu hanyuma ushobora kwihana (soma 2 Abakorinto 2:5-10). Igihano cy'itorero

kigera ku ntego zacyo, iyo uwagihawe akomorewe akagaruka mu muryango w'itorero yarahindutse.
