---
title:  Urukingo Rukumira Ubusambanyi
date:   22/07/2026
---

`Soma mu 1 Abakorinto 4:1-8. Ni iki iyi mirongo ivuga ku byerekeye isano iri hagati yo kwezwa no guhitamo kwirinda ubusambanyi?`

Nubwo mur'ayo masomo Pawulo yandikiraga undi muntu, iri hame rishobora gukoreshwa n'ahandi, ku Bakristo bose.

`Ni iki cyari gihari cyatuma umuntu yibaza iki kibazo: Ni ikihe kibazo muby'ukuri cyari i Korinto? Kuki hari ibyo bibazo byose?`

Bisa nk'aho bamwe mu Bakristo b'i Korinto bizeraga ko, kuva ubutumwa bwiza bwarababatuye, bemerewe gukora ikintu cyose bashaka. Bavugaga ko, nk'uko inda yagenewe ibyokurya, umubiri nawo wagenewe guhuza ibitsina, kandi guhuza ibitsina bikaba byaragenewe umubiri (1 Abakorinto 6:13). Pawulo yasubije avuga ko ibyo ari ugusobanura nabi umudendezo wa Gikristo. Kubura ukwiyubaha mu bijyanye no guhuza ibitsina binyuranyije n'ikigira Umukristo uwo ariwe kandi bikaba ari ugukoresha nabi umudendezo wahawe umuntu binyuze mu butumwa bwiza (Abaroma 8:2, Abagalatiya 5:13). Twabatuwe ku cyaha ntabwo "twahawe umudendezo" wo kugikora (Abaroma 8:2; Abaroma 6:18, 22). Muby'ukuri, "umubiri ni uw'Umwami, kandi Umwami na we ni uw'umubiri" (1 Abakorinto 6:13). Turi aba Kristo (1 Abakorinto 6:15), kandi abo turi bigomba kugira icyo bihindura kubyo dukora. Kimwe gishinze imizi mu kindi. Ibi byasobanuwe mu 1 Abakorinto 6 mu buryo butatu butandukanye.

Uburyo bwa mbere, dufatwa nka abuhagiwe, tukezwa, kandi tugatsindishirizwa "n'Umwuka w'Imana yacu mu izina ry'Umwami Yesu Kristo" (1 Abakorinto 6:11). Urutonde rw'ibyaha rwanditswe mu 1Abakorinto 6:9,10, hamwe n'icyaha cy'ubusambanyi cyavuzwe 1Abakorinto 6:12-20, nta mwanya bifite mu mibereho y'abuhagiwe, bakezwa, kandi bagatsindishirizwa.

Ubwa kabiri, turi ingingo za Kristo (1 Abakorinto 6:15). Ibyo bisobanuye ko tugomba kuba umwe na Kristo (1 Abakorinto 6:17). Ubusambanyi bwica ubwo bumwe dufitanye na we (1 Abakorinto 6:13, 15). Uwifatanya na maraya aba abaye "umubiri umwe" na we (1 Abakorinto 6:16). Ubumwe tugirana na Kristo binyuze muri Mwuka w'Imana bigena imico ya Gikristo ikwiriye kuturanga mu bijyanye n'imibonano mpuzabitsina.

Ubwa gatatu, imibiri yacu ni "insengero za Mwuka Wera" (1 Abakorinto 6:19, 20). Uburyo rukumbi dushobora kubaho imibereho yejejwe twirinda mu bijyanye no guhuza ibitsina ni ukugirana umushyikirano ushyitse na Kristo binyuze muri Mwuka Wera. Ahandi Pawulo avuga ibyo kuba insengero za Mwuka Wera ni mu buryo bwo gutanga imibiri yacu ngo ibe "ibitambo bizima byera bishimwa n'Imana" (Abaroma 12:1).

`Tekereza ku kaga ibyaha bijyanye n'ubusambanyi byazaniye inyokomuntu. Ni iki ibyo bitubwira eyerekeye uburyo tudakwiriye kujenjekera icyo kibazo n'ingorane gishobora gukururira Abakristo?`
