---
title:  Imbuto z'Umurimo Nyakuri
date:   30/08/2026
---

`Soma 2 Abakorinto 3:1-9. Ni mu huhe buryo dushobora kuba urwandiko rwa Kristo?`

Inzandiko zogeza umuntu zari zisanzwe zihabo mu gihe cy'Abagiriki n'Abaroma. Nyamara, ntabwo Pawulo yigeze yitwaza inzandiko nk'izo. Imbaraga ihindura ya Mwuka Węra yari mu mibereho y'Abakorinto yari igihamya cy'umurimo we nyakuri. Ariko Pawulo yari asobanukiwe ko itorero ry'i Korinto ritabayeho bitewe n'ubwenge bwe cyangwa imbaraga ze (2 Abakorinto 3:4-6). Ntabwo yigeze yishyira hejuru (2 Abakorinto 3:5; 1 Abakorinto 2:2).

Pawulo avuga ibyerekeye umurimo we binyuze mu kuvuga muri make amasezerano abiri: Irya kera rihagarariwe na Mose, naho Irishya rihagarariwe na Pawulo ndetse na bagenzi be. Umusomyi usoma amajyejuru ashobora gutekereza ko Isezerano Rya Kera ridatanga ibyiringiro by'agakiza, ariko si ko biri. Agakiza kariho mu Isezerano Rya Kera nk'uko kariho no mu Isezerano Rishya. Isezerano Rya Kera ryari Ubutumwa Bwiza bwatanzwe mbere y'igihe. Kandi Ibyanditswe byamenye bitaraba yuko Imana izatsindishiriza abanyamahanga kuko bizeye, bibwira Aburahamu ubutumwa bwiza bw'ibitaraba biti: "Muri wowe ni mo amahanga yose azaherwa umugisha." (Abagalatiya 3:8).

Mu 2 Abakorinto 3:1-4:8, dushobora kubona ko Isezerano Rya Kera rikoreshwa mu gushushanya imibereho yo gutwarwa n'amategeko y'abantu bishingikiriza ku mirimo yabo bwite yo kumvira nk'uburyo bwo kunezeza Imana. Nyamara, Isezerano Rishya rihagarariye imibereho y'abantu bishingikiriza rwose ku buntu bw'Imana kugira ngo babashe gukora ibyo Imana yasezeranye kubakorera byose kandi ikabikorera muri bo.

Pawulo avuga ku nyifato ebyiri zitandukanye, abizera n'abatizera bagira ku Butumwa Bwiza. Ntabwo avuga Ubutumwa Bwiza butandukanye, ngo bumwe bube ubwo mu Isezerano Rya Kera, ngo ubundi bube ubwo mu Isezerano Rishya-kuko hariho Ubutumwa Bwiza bumwe, bwatanzwe n'Imana. "yadukijije, ikaduhamagara guhamagara kwera itabitewe n'imirimo yacu, ahubwo ibitewe n'uko yabigambiriye ubwayo, no ku bw'ubuntu bwayo twaherewe muri Kristo Yesu uhereye kera kose" (2 Timoteyo 1:9).

Ibi ntabwo ari uguhakana ko mu 2 Timoteyo 2:14-4:6 hakubiyemo imigabane y'amateka imwe n'imwe. Ariko Pawulo akoresha ayo mateka ashaka kugaragaza ko muri bo hari "abakira" n'"abarimbuka"(2 Abakorinto 2:15). Kubw'iyo myitwarire yo gushidikanya no kutizera umurimo wa Mose, umurimo we ushobora gufatwa nk'umurimo wo guciraho abandi iteka ndetse n'urupfu. Kubera yuko itorero ry'i Korinto ryizeye, umurimo wa Pawulo muri bo wagaragaye ko ari umurimo wo gukiranuka, umurimo wa Mwuka utanga ubugingo.

`Uku kugira imibereho y'abakijijwe kw'itorero ry'i Korinto ni igihamya cy'uko umurimo wa Pawulo wari umurimo nyawo.`
