---
title:  Ihumure n'Ibyishimo
date:   03/09/2026
---

`Soma 2 Abakorinto 7. Pawulo yumvise ameze ate ubwo yumvaga ko Abakorinto bihannye?`

Ni urukondo rungana iki ruva muri aya magambo: "Muri mu mitima yacu" (2 Abakorinto 7:3; soma na 2 Abakorinto 6:11). Mu cyifuzo cye gikomeye cyo kugarukirwa n'urukundo rwe, Pawulo aravuga ati: Nimutwakire mu mitima yanyu" (2 Abakorinto 7:2). Nubwo interuro "mu mitima yanyu" itari mu isomo ry'Ikigiriki, ingeri z'Icyongereza zirayikoresha, kandi ibyo ni byo kuko icyerekezo ryakoreshejwemo kibishyigikira.

Mu by'ukuri, Abakorinto bakinguriye Pawulo na bagenzi be imitima yabo. Ni yo mpamvu umurongo wa 4 wuzuyemo umunezero. Amagambo ya Pawulo agaragaza uburyo yari anezerewe muri iki gihe: "Mbashiraho amanga cyane, kandi ndabirata cyane. Nuzuye ihumure, ndetse no mu makuba yacu yose ngira umunezero usesekaye" (2 Abakorinto7:4). Pawulo yuzuwe n'ihumure ndetse n'ibyishimo. Ni ihumure ndetse n'ibyishimo bingana iki amatorero yacu ashobora kuzana mu mitima y'ababwiriza bayo binyuze mu kwiyegurira Kristo kwayo?

Mu 2 Abakorinto 7:5-16, Pawulo akomeza gusobanura impamvu y'ihumure rye ndetse n'ibyishimo bye. Ayo magambo yombi yiganje muri iryo somo. Inshinga _parakaleō_ ("guhumuriza") cyangwa izina _paraklēsis_ ("ihumure") byombi bigaragara inshuro zirindwi mu 2 Abakorinto 7. Uyu mugabane w'urwandiko urangira nk'uko watangiye, ni ukuvuga, ko urimo ihumure ryinshi mu Mana (2 Abakorosayi 1:3-7). Ihumure rya Pawulo ryo mu 2 Abakorinto7 rituruka ku butabazi yahawe kuko urwandiko rwatumye habaho ibyo yari arutegerejeho.

Nubwo uku koroherwa ari umusaruro w'amakuru meza yari azanywe na Tito, mu buryo budasubirwaho, Imana ni yo nkomoko yo guhumurizwa kwa Pawulo (2 Abakorinto 7:6). Imana ni "Imana nyir'ihumure ryose, iduhumuriza mu makuba yacu yose" (2 Abakorinto 1:3,4).

Igitangaje, mu gihe Pawulo yuzuwe "n'ihumure," aba "afite umunezero usesekaye" (2 Abakorinto 7:4,7,13). Nubwo urwandiko rwe rubabaje rwari rwarateje agahinda kenshi, kari agahinda ko mu buryo bw'Imana ndetse no kwihana kwashak waga (2 Abakorinto 7:9-11). Abakorinto "bagize agahinda ko mu buryo bw'Imana" (2 Abakorinto 7:11), agahinda gatera "kwihana kuzana agakiza" (2 Abakorinto 7:10). Ni ikihe kindi cyashoboraga kuzana ibyishimo mu mutima w'umukozi nyakuri w'Imana?

`Mbese wigeze ugira agahinda ko mu buryo bw'Imana mu mibereho yawe? Wamenye ute ko ari agahinda ko mu buryo bw'Imana kazana kwihana?`
