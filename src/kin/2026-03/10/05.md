---
title:  Guhamagarirwa Kwera
date:   02/09/2026
---

Mu 2 Abakorinto 6:3-10, Pawulo akomeza gutera abanyakorinto umwete wo kwiyunga n'Imana. Agaragaza urutonde rurerure rw'ibirushya ndetse n'insinzi ashaka kugaragaza icyo kuba umuyoboke wa Kristo ndetse n'umukozi w'Imana bisobanuye. Muri make, akora irutonde rw'ibihe biruhije (2 Abakorinto 6:4, 5), kubonera kw'imico (2 Abakorinto 6:6), ibikenewe ngo umurimo ukorwe (2 Abakorinto 6:7), ingorane ziboneka mu murimo (2 Abakorinto 6:8-10). Ubwo yari amaze kwigisha abizera b'i Korinto kwiyunga n'Imana, Pawulo yabararikiye kubaho imibereyo yejejwe, no gukora ibyo binyuze mu kwitandukanye n'imbaraga mbi ihindura ituruka mu batizera no mu bintu bitejejwe (2 Abakorinto 6:14-17).

`Soma 2 Abakorinto 6:11-7:1. Ukurikije ibivugwa muri iyi mirongo, imibereho yejejwe iba imeze ite?`

Muri iyi mirongo Pawulo ashimangira akamaro ko gusabana ndetse n'urukundo mu itorero (1 Abakorinto 6:11-13). Igihamya kigaragaza ko abantu biyunze n' Imana ni uko bashaka kwiyunga na bagenzi babo. Ni ukuvuga ko bahinduka ibikoresho bizana ubwiyunge mu buryo bw'intambike (hagati y'abantu n'abandi).

Icya kabiri, kurarikirwa kwera gutangwa binyuze mu kwinginga k'uburyo butandatu, (1) "Ntimwifatanye n'abatizera mudahwanye" (2 Abakorinto 6:14); (2) "Muve hagati ya ba bandi" (2 Abakorinto 6:17); (3) "Mwitandukanye" (2 Abakorinto 6:17); (4) "Ntimugakore ku kintu gihumanye" (2 Abakorinto 6:17); (5) "Twiyezeho imyanda yose y'umubiri n'umutima"(2 Abakorinto7:1); (6) "Tugende twiyejesha rwose kubaha Imana"(2 Abakorinto 7:1). Izi mpuguro zigaragaza ko Imana yera isaba abantu kugira imibereho yejejwe ndetse no kwitandukanye n'imigirire yo gusenga ibigirwamana.

Ku rundi ruhande, iryo somo rigaragaza amasezerano arindwi ashimangira akamaro k'itorero rya Gikristo nk'urusengero rwera: (1) "Nzatura muri bo" ; (2) "Ngendere muri bo"; (3) "Nzaba Imana yabo"; (4) "Na bo bazaba ubwoko bwanjye";(5) "Nzabakira"; (6) "Nzababera so";(7)"Namwe muzambere abahungu n'abakobwa" (2 Abakorinto 6:16,17,18).

Zirikana ko amasezerano ane yo mu 2 Abakorinto 6:16 ari ishingiro ry'amasezerano atatu yo mu 2 Abakorinto 6:17(reba ijambo _Nuko_, riri ku ntangiriro ya 2 Abakorinto 6:17). Ibi bigaragaza ko kwera atari umusaruro w'imbaraga z'umuntu ahubwo ni umurimo wa Mwuka wera uri mu mutima. Nubwo kwera guturuka ku Mana, abizera bagomba gukora uruhare rwabo bakazibukira gusenga ibigirwamana ndetse n'imirimo yo gukiranirwa.

`Ni iki isezerano ry'Imana riri mu 2 Abakorinto 6:16-18 ritubwira cyerekeranye n'icyo kwera ari cyo?`
