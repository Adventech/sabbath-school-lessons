Soma igitabo cyanditswe na Ellen G.White cyitwa _Ibyakozwe n'Intumwa_, pp.199-206, igice cyitwa "Ubutumwa bwakiriwe",

Mu cyumweru gishize, twasomye amagambo ari haruguru mu gitabo cy'Ibyakozwe n'Intumwa. Byari ngombwa ko tuyasoma. Muri uyu mwanya, mwibande ku migabane igaragaza urwandiko rwa Pawulo, uko yumvaga ameze ubwo yarwandikaga, ndetse n'ibyishimo yagize ubwo yakiraga inkuru nziza yerekeranye no kwihana kw'abo yabwiraga. Noneho, tekereza ku cyo ibi bikubwira cyerekeranye n'ukuri k'umurimo wa Pawulo ndetse n'amasomo dushobora gukoresha mu murimo wacu dukorera Kristo.

"Dukwiriye kugaragariza isanzure, isi yacumuye ndetse n'indi mibumbe itaracumuye" yuko Imana ifite imbabazi, yuko binyuze mu rukundo rw'Imana dushobora kungwa na yo. Umuntu arihana, agashenjagurika umutima we, akizera Kristo nk'igitambo cye cy'impongano, maze agasobanukirwa ko yiyunze na Yo."- Ellen G. White, _Special Testimonies on Education_, p. 223.

"Nk'itorero, twahawe umucyo mwinshi. Uyu mucyo Uwiteka yawuduhaye kugira ngo ugirire umumaro abari mu isi kandi ubabere umugisha. Twahawe umurimo wo kuzana ubwiyunge. Kubw'imbaraga mvajuru, tugomba kwingingira abantu kwiyunga n'Imana."- Ellen G. White, _Letter_ 32,1903.

Iyo abantu bunzwe n'Imana, baba bagomba gushaka ukwera. Avuga ku magambo yanditswe mu 2 Abakorinto, Ellen G. White agaruka ku cyo Pawulo yashakaga kuvuga igihe yavugaga ibyo "kwiyejesha kubaha Imana" (2 Abakorinto 7:1). Aravuga ati: "Pawulo yashakaga gufasha abizera bashya "ngo bahinduke Abakristo bashobora kwifasha ubwabo, Abakristo bakura bakomereye mu kwizera bafite ishyaka kandi bihaye Imana n'umutimanama wabo wose ndetse biyeguriye umurimo wo kwamamaza ubwami bwayo."- Ellen G. White, _Ibyakozwe n'Intumwa_, p. 122.

**IBIBAZO:**

`Pawulo atugereranya n' "'inzabya z'ibumba" zirimo ubutunzi bw'ubutumwa bwiza (2 Abakorinto 4:7). Ni mu buhe buryo kuba umuntu ari umunyantege nke, kandi imbaraga ze zifite aho zigarukira bitera umwete ivugabutumwa aho kurica intege?`

`Mbese kuba "icyaremwe gishya" bisobanuye iki? (2 Abakorinto 5:17)? Mbese ibyo bigira ngaruka ki ku mibereho yacu? Ni mu buhe buryo Kristo yakugize icyaremwe gishya?`

`Mu 2 Abakorinto 6:4, 5, Pawulo atanga urutonde rurerure rw'ibirushya duhura na byo kubw'ubutumwa bwiza. Yifashe ate mu mibabaro ye (reba 2 Abakorinto 6:6,7)? Ni mu buhe buryo ibyo bigufasha mu nyifato ugira mu mu mibabaro yawe?`

`Pawulo atandukanya agahinda ko mu buryo bw'Imana n'agahinda ko mu buryo bw' isi (2 Abakorinto7:10). Ni mu buhe buryo agahinda gashobora guhuzwa no kwihana? Ni gute wasobanura agahinda ko mu buryo bw'Imana ukagatandukanya n'agahunda ko mu buryo bw' isi?`
