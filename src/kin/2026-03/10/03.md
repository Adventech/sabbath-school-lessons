---
title:  Imibabaro n'Ubwiza
date:   31/08/2026
---

`Soma 2 Abakorinto 4:7-18. Kora urutonde rw'imibabaro ya Pawulo. Pawulo yihanganiye ate imibabaro ye?`

Yohana Huse, umugorozi ukomeye w' i Boheme ya kera, yigeze kugira icyo avuga kuri Yesu ati: "Ni Umwami w'isi, twe tukaba abantu bapfa ndetse b'insuzugurwa-nyamara yarababajwe! Nonese kuki twe tutagomba kubabazwa, by'umwihariko igihe uwo mubabaro ugendereye kudutunganya?" - Ellen G. White, _Intambara Ikomeye_ p. 86.

Mu myaka myinshi yabanjirije icyo gihe, intumwa Pawulo yari yaragaragaje ubushake nk'ubwo bwo kubabazwa ahowe Kristo.Yari azi ko nta kindi ari cyo kirenze kuba urwabya rworoshye rw'ibumba (2 Abakorinto 4:7).

Buri gihe yumvaga afite amakuba impande zose, ashobewe, arenganywa, akubitwa hasi, ariko ntabwo yigeze akuka umutima, ngo yumve yihebye, atereranwe, cyangwa ngo yumve atsinzwe rwose (2 Abakorinto 4:8,9). Yari afite ubushake bwo "kugendana mu mubiri iteka urupfu rwa Yesu, ngo ubugingo bwa Yesu bugaragarire mu mubiri we" (2 Abakorinto 4:10,11).

Igihe Pawulo yavuvuga "urupfu rwa Yesu," birashoboka ko yaba yarashakaga kuvuga imibabaro yari yavuze mu mirongo yabanje. Kubw'iyo mpamvu, mu buryo buhita bwumvikana, interuro "ubugingo bwa Yesu" igaragaza gucungurwa uva mu rupfu cyangwa imbaraga za Mwuka zidushoboza kubaho muri iki gihe. Ariko, amaherezo, ibi bigaragaza umuzuko (2 Abakorinto
4:12).

Igitangaje, amagambo "urupfu n'ubugingo" agaragara inshuro eshatu mu 2 Abakorinto 4:10-12. Ibi bitwibutsa ko muri iki gihe, ubugingo buvanze n'urupfu. Nyamara, mu gihe kizaza, tuzahabwa ubugingo butarimo urupfu (Ibyahishuwe 20:14; Ibyahishuwe 21:4).

Icy'ingenzi kurutaho, mu 2 Abakorinto 4:7-18 hagaragaza ko Ubutumwa Bwiza bubwirizwa bunyuze mu bantu boroheje kugira ngo icyubahiro kibe icy'Imana yonyine (2 Abakorinto 4:15). Si inshuro nke ko abavugabutumwa bababazwa mu rugendo rwabo rw'umurimo w'ivugabutumwa. Nyamara, umubabaro wacu hano uroroheje kandi ni uw'akanya gato iyo ugereranyijwe n'ubwiza bw'iteka ryose budutegereje (2 Abakorinto 4:17). Uwizera abeshwaho no kwizera, ntabwo abeshwaho n'ibigaragarira amaso (2 Abakorinto 4:18, 2 Abakorinto 5:7).

Ibi byiringiro by'imibereho yo mu gihe kizaza byakoze Pawulo ku mutima cyane ku buryo akomeza kubivugaho muri iyi mirongo (2 Abakorinto 5:1-10). Avuga umubiri we upfa akoresheje imvugoshusho y'inzu yo ku isi. Nyamara, "inyubako y'Imana"ni imvugoshusho ihagarariye umubiri wazutse (2 Abakorinto 5:1), ibyiringiro bikomeye by'abizera b'ibihe byose.

`Kuki ari ingenzi cyane ko mu byo tunyuramo byose muri iki gihe, duhoza imbere yacu ibyiringiro by'umuzuko, umuzuko wacu (1 Abakorinto 15:52)?`
