---
title:  Imana Yuje Urukundo
date:   22/09/2026
---

Mu isi ya gipagani ya kera, abantu ntabwo bizeraga ko imana zikunda abantu. Ahubwo bizeraga ko imana zagiraga nabi kandi zikarakara, ndetse ko abantu bagombaga kuzigusha neza. Igitekerezo cy'Imana y'urukundo, nk'uko tubibona muri Bibiliya, cyari igitekerezo kitamenyerewe. Nk'uko aya magambo yari atangaje mu gihe cye, Pawulo agaragaza Imana yacu nk "Imana y'urukundo n'amahoro" (2 Abakorinto 13:11).

`Soma 2 Abakorinto 13:11. Ni mu buhe buryo ushobora gukura ibyiringiro mu bivugwa hano? Ni mu buhe buryo ushobora kugira imibereho ihuye n'ibyo uyu murongo
wigisha?`

Interuro "Imana y'urukundo n'amahoro" ishobora gusobanurwa mu buryo bubiri butandukanye. Ku ruhande rumwe, Imana ibonwa nk'isoko y'urukundo n'amahoro. Ku rundi ruhande, Imana irangwa n'urukundo n'amahoro. Nyamara ntabwo ari ngombwa guhitamo hagati y'ibyo byombi. Kuko urukundo n'amahoro ari imico ihishwe mu Mana, iduha urukundo n'amahoro.

Ahandi, Pawulo agaragaza Imana nk "Imana nyir'ukwihangana no guhumurizwa" (Abaroma15:5)"Nyir'ibyiringiro" (Abaroma 15:13); "Nyir'amahoro 15:33"(Abaroma15:33; Abaroma16:20; 1 Abakorinto14:33; Abafilipi4:9; 1 Abatesalonike 5:23), "Data wa twese w'imbabazi" (2 Abakorinto 1:3). Imana ni isoko y'iyo migisha yose. Iyiduha yose kubw'urukundo rwayo rutagajuka.

Nanone kandi, nubwo iyi nteruro "Imana y'amahoro" isa naho iri rusange muri Bibiliya, interuro "Imana y'urukundo" igaragara rimwe gusa hano (2 Abakorinto 13:11), kandi tugomba kuyitekerezaho cyane.

Nk'uko abasesenguzi benshi babibonye, kuba Pawulo avuga ku Mana y'uruk undo mu mirongo mike ibanziriza kuvuga iby'imigisha ikomoka mu Butatu Bwera yo mu 2 Abakorinto 13:14, bigaragaza ko atekereza Imana nk'igizwe n'Ubutatu. "Nubwo hano akoresha ijambo 'Imana' nk'umwe mu bagize ubutatu bwera, uburyo asobanukiwe Yesu ndetse na Mwuka wera ahandi mu nzandiko ze ... biduhatira kubona iyo nteruro yose nk'igaragaza Imana itorero rya mbere ryabonaga mu butatu bwera. Bishoboka ko hashize imyaka irenga ijana mbere yuko abahanga mu by'lyobokamana ... batangira gukoresha amagambo nk "Ubutatu" nk'uburyo bwo kuvuga muri make ibyo Pawulo yari yaravuze."-Tom Wright, Paul for Everyone:2 Corinthians (London: Society for promoting Christian Knowledge, 2004), p.148.

`Twizera Imana imwe, ubumwe bw'Abatatu babaho iteka ryose mu isano n'urukundo. Iyi Mana, imwe igizwe n'Ubutatu iradukunda kandi iturarikira gukundana mu buryo bugaragaza urukundo ruri hagati y'abagize Ubutatu Bwera.`
