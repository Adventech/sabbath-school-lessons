---
title:  Ubuntu, Urukundo, n'Ubusabane
date:   19/09/2026
---

### Aho icyigisho cy'iki cyumweru gishingiye:
2 Abakorinto 8:9; Abaroma 16:20; 1 Yohana 4:8-11; 2 Abakorinto 13:11; Abafilipi 2:1, 2; Abagalatiya 4:4-6.

> <p>Icyo kwibukwa:</p>
> "Ubuntu bw'Umwami wacu Yesu Kristo, n'urukundo rw'Imana, no kubana n'Umwuka Wera bibane namwe mwese" (2 Abakorinto 13:14).

Pawulo asoza 2 Abakorinto ashimangira ingingo z'ingenzi zivugwa mu nzandiko ze. Ibyo abikora binyuze mu bintu bitanu yasabye abizera kugira (2 Abakorinto 13:11).

Icya mbere: "Mwishime," byibutsa amagambo yabanje mu nzandiko ze.

Icya kabiri: "Mutungane rwose," bisobanuwe mu ijambo rimwe ry'Ikigiriki (_katartizō_), rigaragara hano ndetse no mu 2 Abakorinto 1:10.

Icya gatatu: "Muterane inkunga" bigaruka ku bivugwa mu 2 Abakorinto 1:3-7. Pawulo atangira kandi agasoza urwandoko rwe atera umwete. Imana iraduhumuriza kugira ngo natwe duhumurize abandi (2 Abakorinto 1:4, 6).

Icya kane n'icya gatanu: "muhuze imitima, mubane amahoro" (2 Abakorinto 13:11), ni uguhamagarirwa kunga ubumwe. Uyu mwuka w'ibyishimo, gutungana, guterana umwete, ubumwe, ndetse n'amahoro ni byo basabwa ngo Imana ibe iri kumwe na bo "Imana y'urukundo n'amahoro" (2 Abakorinto 13:11). Bikomoka ku murimo Imana igizwe n'Ubutatu ikorera mu mitima yacu (2 Abakorinto 13:14).

Ubuntu, urukundo no gusabana bikomoka ku murimo Imana igizwe n'Ubutatu Bwera ikorera muri twe. Ibyo birango bitatu by'Umukristo ni byo bituma haba umwuka ugaragaza ko Imana ihari.
