---
title:  Urukundo rw'Imana
date:   21/09/2026
---

Ubuntu bw'Umwami wacu Yesu Kristo, n'urukundo rw'Imana, no kubana n'Umwuka Wera bibane namwe mwese" (2 Abakorinto 13:14). Uyu murongo, Pawulo awukoresha asoza urwandiko rwe rwa kabiri. Zirikana ko avuga ba batatu bagize Imana imwe muri ubu buryo - Umwana, Data, n'Umwuka wera. Dushobora kurushaho gusobanukirwa uko Imana imeze ndetse n'Icyo yadukoreye binyuze mu murimo abagize ubutatu bose bakora.

`Soma Yohana 3:16; Abaroma 8:37-39; na 1 Yohana 4:8-11. Ni iki iyi mirongo ikubwira cyerekeranye n'urukundo rw'Imana?`

Mu 1 Yohana 4:8 havuga ko "Imana ari urukundo." Urukundo ni kimwe mu bintu by'ingenzi biranga Imana. Yohana ashimangira ko dushobora kumenyera urukundo rw'Imana mu kuba yaratanze umwana wayo w'ikinege kugira ngo adupfire (Yohana 3:16). Imana yohereje Yesu ku murimo wo gukiza (Yohana 3:17), kandi ibi byari umugabane w'umugambi w'agakiza (Ibyakozwe n'Intumwa 3:20, 21; 1 Yohana 4:10, 14). Mu Butumwa bwiza, Yesu yavuze inshuro nyinshi ko Data ari we wamutumye (Matayo 10:40; Mariko 9:37).

Mu magambo atangaje, Pawulo avuga ko "Imana yerekanye urukundo rwayo idukunda, ubwo Kristo yadupfiraga tukiri abanyabyaha" (Abaroma 5:8). Dushobora kurabukwa urukundo rw'Imana mu isano nziza iri hagati y'umugabo n'umugore, ndetse n'iba hagati y'ababyeyi n'abana babo, mu bucuti nyakuri, n'ibindi. Dushobora na none kubonera urukundo rw'Imana mu byaremwe. Muri ubwo buryo, Ellen G. White aravuga ati: "Imana ni urukundo" ryanditswe ku mugengararo w'ururabyo rwose, no ku katsi kose. Utunyoni twiza turirimba mu kirere, n'uburabyo bw'amabara meza buhumuza umwuka neza, n'ibiti by'inganzamarumbo byo mu ishyamba bihora bitoshye, uko bingana byose bigaragaza uko Imana itugenzereza neza, nk'umubyeyi w'impuhwe wifuriza abana be kunezerwa."- Ellen G. White, _Kugana Yesu_, p.9.

Nyamara, nta cyemeza umutima nko kuba Imana yaratanze Yesu nk'igitambo cy'ibyaha byacu. Iyo dusobanukiwe ko Imana iyadukunze kugeza ubwo yohereje Yesu kugira ngo atange ubungingo bwe ku bwacu, igisubizo cyacu kiba kugira ubushake bwo "gutanga ubuzima bwacu ku bwa bene Data" (1 Yohana 3:16).

Pawulo yashakaga ko Abakorinto babaho mu bumwe. Nyamara, hatari urukundo, nta bumwe bwabaho. Ni yo mpamvu yabigishije ko "urukundo rukomeza" (1 Abakorinto 8:1) kandi ko hatari urukundo, ibintu byose byaba ntacyo bimaze kandi byaba ari ubusa (1 Abakorinto 13:1-3). Bityo, ibyo dukora byose bigomba gukorwa mu rukundo (1 Abakorinto 16:14), rwa rukundo ruturuka mu kwaguka k'urukundo rw'Imana.

`Mbese twari gutakaza iki mu butumwa bwiza iyo Yesu ubwe ataba Imana mu buryo bwuzuye kandi mu buryo buhoraho?`
