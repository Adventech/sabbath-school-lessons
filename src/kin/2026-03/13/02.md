---
title:  Ubuntu Bwa Yesu
date:   20/09/2026
---

Birashishikaje kubona ko ku iherezo ry'urwandiko rwa 2 Abakorinto , havuga iby'ubuntu bwa Yesu, nk'uko bimeze mu ntangiriro yarwo (2 Abakorinto 1:2; 2 Abakorinto 13:14). Pawulo atangira kandi agasoza uru rwandiko agaragaza ubuntu bwa Yesu. Nk'uko twabibonye mu ntangiriro z'iki gihembwe, ntabwo yashoboraga kureka gutekereza no kuvuga ibya Yesu.

"Kuko muzi ubuntu bw'Umwami wacu Yesu Kristo uko yari umutunzi, maze agahinduka umukene ku bwanyu kugira ngo ubukene bwe bubatungishe" (2 Abakorinto 8:9).

Mbega uburyo ubuntu bwa Yesu bushimishije kandi bugatsinda imitima! Yasize ubutunzi bwe bwo kuba mu ijuru iteka ryose ahinduka umukene. Yagendeye mu mihanda yarimo ivumbi yo muri Galileya ya kera. "Yicisha bugufi, araganduka ntiyanga no gupfa ndetse urupfu rwo ku musaraba" (Abafilipi 2:8). Ibyo yabikoreye kugira ngo tube "abatunzi," ni ukuvuga, tubone amahirwe yo kuzabana na we mu ijuru. Kuri twebwe, tutagira ikindi tuzi uretse isi y'icyaha, urupfu, n'imibabaro, biradukomereye kumenya icyo bisobanuye kuba Yesu yaravuye mu bikari byo mu ijuru kugira ngo aze hano ku isi kandi atange ubugingo bwe ku bwacu.

`Soma Abaroma 16:20, Abagalatiya 6:18, Abafilipi 4:23 na 1 Abatesalonike 5:28. Ni ikihe cyigisho cy'ingenzi ubona muri iyi mirongo?`

Pawulo agenda agaruka ku buntu bwa Yesu inshuro nyinshi mu nzandiko ze. Aha ni hamwe mu ho abugarukaho: "Impano y'ubuntu bw'Imana ... ubuntu bw'Imana n'impano y'ubuntu ... byarushijeho gusaga kuri benshi" (Abaroma 5:15). Abakira ubu buntu busesekaye "bazarushaho kwimikanwa ubugingo ... na Yesu" (Abaroma 5:17). Nk'uko bigaragara mu 2 Abakorinto , na none Pawulo atangiza kandi agasoza izindi nzandiko avuga ubuntu bwa Yesu (Abaroma 1:7; Abaroma 16:20; 1 Abakorinto 1:3; 1 Abakorinto 16:23; Abagalatiya1:3, Abagalatiya 6:18; Abafilipi 1:2; Abafilipi 4:23). Iyi ngingo ni yo yari yiganje mu bitekerezo bye, kandi yashakaga ko yuzura no mu ntekerezo z'Abakorinto.

Iki ni cyo yifurizaga amatorero yose. Zirikana ibyo yabwiye Abanyefeso: "Ubuntu bw'Imana bubane n'abakunda Umwami wacu Yesu Kristo bose bataryarya" (Abefeso 6:24). Mbese yaba atwifuriza gukunda Yesu urukundo ruri munsi ya ruriya rukundo rudapfa? Oya rwose. N'ubundi kandi, icyifuzo cye cyari icy'uko ubuntu bwa Yesu bugera kuri "benshi" (2 Abakorinto 4:15) kandi bukaba buhagije kuri bo nk'uko byari bimeze kuri we (2 Abakorinto 12:9).

`Tekereza ku buntu bw'Imana wagiriwe muri ubu buryo. Ni iki ukwiriye ukurikije ibyo wavuze ndetse n'ibyo wakoze? Ni iki ubuntu bw'Imana bugukorera aho kugukorera ibyo wari ukwiriye?`
