---
title:  Imana Yacu y'Ubutatu
date:   24/09/2026
---



Iyo dusomye 2 Abakorinto 13:14, umuntu ashobora gutekereza ko Kristo ari we soko rukumbi y'ubuntu, yuko Imana ari yo soko rukumbi y'urukundo, kandi ko Mwuka Wera ari we soko rukumyi y'umushyikirano, ariko nta na kimwe muri byo gishobora kuba kure
y'ukuri.

`Soma 1 Abakorinto 1:3, 4, 9, 1 Abakorinto 10:16; 2 Abakorinto 1:2, 12; Abaroma 8:35; Abaroma 15:30; Abagalatiya 2:20; Abefeso 3:19. Ni iki iyi mirongo ivuga cyerekeranye n'ubuntu, urukundo, gusabana tubihuje n'abagize Ubutatu Bwera?`

Data wa twese, Umwana na Mwuka wera bakorera hamwe ku bw'agakiza kacu. Ubuntu, urukundo no gusabana ntabwo bikomoka kuri umwe mu bagize Ubutatu, ahubwo bikomoka kuri abo Batatu bose bagize Imana imwe. Ariko buri wese afite inshingano zihariye mu mateka y'agakiza. Ibyo Pawulo arabizi kandi ashimangira iyi nyigisho mu nzandiko ze. Urugero: inama y'agakiza ivugwa muri make mu Abagalatiya 4:4-6, agaragaramo uruhare rwa buri wese mu bagize Ubutatu bwera. Imana Data yohereje Yesu, ari byo bigaragaza ko Imana Data ari yo nkomoko y'uwo mugambi (Abagalatiya 4:4). Uwo mwana yabyawe n'umugore (Abagalatiya 4:4), ari byo bigaragaza ukwigira umuntu kandi bikagaragaza no gusohora kw'isezerano ryatanzwe kera (Itangiriro 3:15). Umwana yaraducunguye kandi atugarura mu kugirana umushyikirano utunganye na Data Satani yari yarabeshyeye (Itangiriro 3:5). Kandi Mwuka Wera na we agahamya abo turi bo nk'abana b'Imana (Abagalatiya 4:6).

Mu nzandiko za Pawulo, hari ahandi henshi hagaragaza Ubutatu Bwera. Abo uko ari batatu bakorera hamwe, bagashoboza itorero gusohoza umurimo waryo (1 Abakorinto 12:4-6). bakaduha imbaraga zo mu bya Mwuka (Abefeso 3:14-19), kandi bagatuma habaho ubumwe bukomeye mu bizera b'itorero, bugaragaza ishusho y'ubumwe buranga isano iri hagati y'abagize Ubutatu bwera (Abefeso 4:4-6). Mu myumvire ya Pawulo, Imana si Ubutatu gusa, ahubwo abo batatu bagize Ubumana bakorera hamwe ku bw'agakiza kacu (Abefeso 1:3,13, 14). Mu Befeso, Pawulo akomeza avuga ko tugomba kuzuzwa kugeza ku kuzura kw'Imana (Abefeso 3:19), Umwana (Abefeso 4:13), na Mwuka Wera (Abefeso 5:18).

Mu gusoza urwandiko yandikiye Abakorinto (2 Abakorinto 13:14), Pawulo nta kindi yashoboraga gusorezaho kiruta-isezerano ry'uko Abanyacyubahiro batatu bo mu ijuru. Ubutatu Bwera bwo mu ijuru, bazabana natwe muri iki gihe no mu gihe kizaza.

`Ni mu buhe buryo ugusabana hagati y'abagize itorero gukwiriye kugaragaza umushyikirano mwiza uri hagati y'abagize Ubutatu Bwera?`
