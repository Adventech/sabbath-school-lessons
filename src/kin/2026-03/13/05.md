---
title:  Gusabana na Mwuka Wera
date:   23/09/2026
---

Ubuntu bwa Yesu ntabwo bugaragaza urukundo Imana idufitiye gusa ahubwo bunazana gusabana na Mwuka wera nk'ingaruka zimbitse z'urwo rukundo. Na none kandi, gusabana tikomoka mu rukundo rw'Imana. Kandi ibyo biterwa n'uko iyo hatari urukundo, nta gusabana kubaho. Nk'uko Pawulo yabyanditse: "Nuko niba hariho gukomezwa kuri muri Kristo, kandi niba hariho guhumurizwa kuzanwa n'urukundo, niba hariho no gusangira Umwuka, niba hariho imbabazi n'impuhwe, musohoreshe umunezero wanjye guhuriza imitima mu rukundo, mwibwira kumwe muhuje imitima" (Abafilipi 2:1, 2).

Abantu bamwe bavuga ko Mwuka wera ari ububasha cyangwa imbaraga ihindura gusa, nyamara ibyo ntibishobora kuba ukuri. Ubundi se, kuki Pawulo yagombaga kuvuga babiri mu Butatu Bwera-Data wa twese n'umwana-akababangikanya n' "imbaraga" ngo Ubutatu buboneke? Ibyo nta busobanuro byari kuba bifite. Nk'uko Data n'Umwana bagaragazwa mu mushyikirano wabo wihariye (2 Abakorinto 1:3; 2 Abakorinto 11:31), umushyikirano wa Mwuka wera n'abantu utuma twanzura tuvuga ko na we ari nk'umuntu wihariye (Abaroma 8:15,16; soma na Yohana 14:16, 17, 26; Yohana 15:26).

Interuro "Gusangira Umwuka" (Abafilipi 2:1) ishobora kumvikana mu buryo bubiri. Ishobora gusobanura gusabana hagati y'abantu gutangwa na Mwuka, cyangwa gusabana na Mwuka ubwe. Abasobanuzi benshi ba Bibiliya bavuga ko ubwo busobanuro butavuguruzanya. Bityo, gusabana hagati y'abantu ni umusaruro uturuka mu gusabana na Mwuka.

`Soma 1 Abakorinto 2:10,11; 1 Abakorinto 3:16; 1 Abakorinto12:11; 2 Abakorinto 3:6,17. Ni iki Pawulo yigishije Abakorinto cyerekeranye na Mwuka Wera?`

Pawulo afite byinshi byo kuvuga byerekeranye n'umurimo wa Mwuka wera. Mu Rwandiko rwa mbere n'urwa kabiri z'Abakorinto, hari ahantu harenga 40 havugwa Mwuka wera. Mwuka Wera atuma habaho kunguka kw'itorero (1 Abakorinto 14:12), agashoboza abantu gukora umurimo (1 Abakorinto 2:4,5), akaduhishurira ibintu byimbitse byerekeranye n'Imana (1 Abakorinto 2:10, 11) kandi akabitwigisha (1 Abakorinto 2:13), akaba muri twe (1 Abakorinto 3:16;1 Abakorinto 6:19), agakorana na Kristo kugira ngo dutsindishirizwe (1 Abakorinto 6:11), aha itorero impano za Mwuka (1 Abakorinto 12-14), adushyiraho ikimenyetso cy'agakiza (2 Abakorinto 1:22), yandika amategeko mu mitima y'abantu (2 Abakorinto 3:3), kandi agatanga ubugingo bushya muri Kristo (2 Abakorinto 3:6) ndetse no kubaturwa ku cyaha (2 Abakorinto 3:17). Mu by'ukuri, ntidushobora kubaho Mwuka Wera
adahari.

`Kuki na none gusobanukirwa ubumana bwa Mwuka wera ari ingenzi cyane kugira ngo dusobanukirwe byuzuye urukundo Imana idukunda?`
