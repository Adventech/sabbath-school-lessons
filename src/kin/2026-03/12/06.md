---
title:  Irarika ku Banze Kwihana
date:   17/09/2026
---

Mu 2 Abakorinto 12:14-13:10, Pawulo amenyesha itorero ibyerekeranye no kubasura kwe ku nshuro ya gatatu (2 Abakorinto 12:14; 2 Abakorinto 13:1). Yari yaragaragaje ko atari afite agaciro kari munsi y'ak'intumwa z'ibinyoma, kandi ko ubu afite icyizere cyo kongera kugaruka i Korinto akagerageza kugarura mu murongo abizera batihana. Mu by'ukuri, iyi yari imwe mu ntego z'uruzinduko rwe. Ibyo Pawulo yakoze byose ndetse n'ibyo yavuze byose byari bigamije kubaka itorero (2 Abakorinto 12:9).

`Soma 2 Abakorinto 12:20, 21. Ni ibihe byaha byari bibangamiye ibya Mwuka mu itorero ry'i Korinto?`

Urutonde rw'ibyaha ruri mu 2 Abakorinto 12:20, 21 rumeze kimwe n'uruboneka ahandi mu nzandiko za Pawulo (Abaroma 1:29-31; Abagalatiya 5:19-21). Ibyaha bibiri bibanza biboneka mu 1 Abakorinto 3:3, aho Pawulo avuga ishyari n'amahane byari mu bizera b'i Korinto. Pawulo yari afite ubwoba bw'uko mu ruzinduko rwe rwa gatatu azasanga ibintu bidatandukanye cyane n'uko yabisize. Aravuga ati: "Ntinya yuko ubwo nzaza ahari nzasanga mumeze uko ntashaka" kandi "nanjye mugasanga meze uko mudashaka" (2 Abakorinto 12:20). Ibi bisobanuye yuko aho guhangana na bo akoresheje ubugwaneza n'ineza bya Kristo (2 Abakorinto 10:1), yari yiteguye guhora kutumvira kose (2 Abakorinto 10:6).

Icyari kimuhagaritse umutima cyane ni uko abo bizera bari baragize uruhare mu "gukora ibyonona, ubusambanyi, n'iby'isoni nke" bashobora kuba bari batarihana (2 Abakorinto 12:21). Kandi ibyaha nk'ibyo ni byo bituma mu itorero habamo amacakubiri.

Na none kandi, Pawulo yibanda ku kamaro k'igihano cy'itorero mu kugarura mu murongo abari mu byaha (2 Abakorinto 13:1-4). Ubunyantege nke ntabwo ari urwitwazo bw'imibereho irangwa n'ibyaha. Hari imbaraga zihabwa abashaka kubaho imibereho inesha (2 Abakorinto 13:4). Kuba bamwe mu banyakorinto barakoraga icyaha cy'ubusambanyi ni igihamya kigaragaza ko imbaraga y'Imana itari ukuri kw'impamo mu mibereho yabo.

Pawulo yashakaga ko bihana kandi bagahabwa imbaraga ibayobora ku kumvira. Kubahana cyari ikintu cya nyuma yashakaga gukora. Aravuga ati: "Nuko ndasaba Imana ngo mutagira ikibi mukora ... ahubwo ni ukugira ngo mukore neza ... kandi icyo dusabira ni iki:ni uko mutunganywa rwose" (2 Abakorinto 13:7-9). Mbega isengesho ryiza! Yabasabye kwisuzuma kugira ngo bamenye ko bakiri mu byo twizera.

`Soma 2 Abakorinto 13:5. Mbese kuba mu byo twizera bisobanuye iki? Mbese wamenya ko ukiri mu byo twizera ute?`
