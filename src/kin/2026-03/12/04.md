---
title:  Abigisha b'Ibinyoma Bagaragajwe
date:   15/09/2026
---

Isezerano Rishya rikubiyemo imiburo myinshi yo kwirinda abigisha b'ibinyoma mu miryango ya Gikristo. Yesu ubwe yaburiye abigishwa ibyerekeranye n'iki kintu (Matayo 7:15-20). Abigishwa na bo bitaye kuri uwo muburo (Abagalatiya 1:6-9,1 Timoteyo 6:3-5, 2 Petero 2:1-3).

`Soma 2 Abakorinto11:1-15. Ni mu buhe buryo Pawulo agaragaza ingorane yahuye na zo zerekeranye n'aba bigisha b'ibinyoma?`

Pawulo ahishura umurimo w'abigisha b'ibinyoma. Na none kandi, agaragaza ko umurimo we ushingiye kuri Kristo. Agereranya itorero ry'i Korinto n'umugeni kandi akigaragaza nka se uribyara, ufite inshingano zo kurishyingira Kristo (2 Abakorinto11:2). Ibyo abikora kuko akunda itorero (2 Abakorinto 11:11). Bityo, yari anafite ubushake bwo kutaribera umutwaro ku byerekeranye n'amafaranga, nubwo yari afite uburenganzira bwo gufashwa na ryo (2 Abakorinto 11:7-12).

Ku rundi ruhande, "intumwa zamenyekanye cyane" (ibi bigaragaza abigisha b'ibinyoma mu buryo bw'ininura) zigereranywa n'inzoka yashutse Eva (2 Abakorinto 11:3). Kimwe na Satani mu ngobyi ya Edeni, abigisha b'ibinyoma b'i Korinto barangwa n'uburyarya ndetse no kuyobya (2 Abakorinto 11:3, 4). Impungenge Pawulo yari afite ni uko abo bigisha b'ibinyoma bashoboraga kuyobya Abakorinto bakabakura mu kuyoboka Kristo bataryarya no mu kumwumvira.

Abigisha b'ibinyoma babwirizaga ubutumwa butandukanye n'ubwo Pawulo yabwirije- Bababwirizaga undi Yesu ndetse n'ubundi butumwa (2 Abakorinto 11:4). Ibi bigaragaza ko abantu bose babwiriza atari ko bose baba ari ibikoresho by'Imana. Kuri ibyo, Yesu ubwe yaravuze ati: "Umuntu wese umbwira ati: "Mwami, Mwami"si we uzinjira mu bwami bwo mu ijuru, keretse ukora ibyo Data wo mu ijuru ashaka"(Matayo7:21). Mu Bagalatiya1:6-9, umuntu wese ubwiriza ubutumwa butandukanye n'ubwatanzwe aba yikururira umuvumo, ariko bamwe mu b'i Korinto bihanganiraga ikinyoma nk'iki.

Pawulo agaragaza intumwa z'ibinyoma avuga ko ari "abakozi bariganya bigira nk'intumwa za Kristo" (2 Abakorinto 11:13). Bariyoberanya bakigira nk'intumwa za Kristo, nk'uko "Satani yiyoberanya akigira nka marayika w'umucyo" n'abakozi be na bo bariyoberanya bakigira nk'abakozi bakiranuka" (2 Abakorinto 11:14,15). Mbega ibintu biteye ubwoba: Kuvuga ko uri umukozi wa Kristo nyamara ukorera Satani! Pawulo asoza igitekerezo cye avuga ko "iherezo ryabo rizahwana n'imirimo yabo" (2 Abakorinto11:15).

`Reba uburyo yahanganye n'ikinyoma cyari mu itorere afite imbaraga! Ni iki ibyo byari bikwiriye kutubwira natwe?`
