---
title:  Ibindi byo Kwigwa no Kuzirikanwa 
date:   18/09/2026
---

Soma amagambo yanditswe na Ellen G. White ari mu ngingo ivuga iby' "Itorero rya Lawodokiya" yasohotse mu kinyamakuru cyitwa _The Advent Review and Sabbath Herald_ cyasohotse ku wa 30 Nzeri, 1873.

"Imana irinda ubwoko bwayo gusubira mu makosa yo mu gihe cyatambutse. Hagiye habaho sbigisha benshi b'ibinyoma, bagiye bashyigikira inyigisho z'ibinyoma ndetse n'imigenzereze idatunganye, kandi bagakurikiza amahame y'ibinyoma mu buryo budafashije, bwihishe, kandi bushukana. Bagerageje cyane kuyobya n'intore niba bishoboka."-Ellen G. White, in _The Advent Review and Sabbath Herald_, January 7, 1904.

"Uwiteka yagennye ko ibitekerezo byacu bisuzumwa, kugira ngo tubashe kubona ko ari ngombwa gusuzumana ubwitonzi ibyanditswe kugira ngo tumenye niba tukiri mu byo twizera cyangwa tutakibirimo. Abantu benshi bavuga ko bizera ukuri biyicariye mu mutuzo bavuga bati: "Ndi umukire, ndatunze kandi ndatunganiwe, ntacyo nkennye"-Ellen G. White, _Counsels to Writers and Editors_, p.36.

"Abantu banezezwa n'ibinyoma kandi ukuri kwarashyizwe ahagaragara; kandi iyo bazana amahame yabo bakayasuzuma bifashishije ijambo ry'Imana, ndetse ntibasome ijambo ry'Imana mu mucyo w'inyigisho zabo, bagamije gushyigikira ibitekerezo byabo, ntabwo bari kugendera mu mwijima ndetse no mu buhumyi cyangwa ngo bashyigikire ibinyoma. Abenshi baha amagambo y'ibyanditswe byera ubusobanuro buhuje n'ibitekerezo byabo, maze bakayoba kandi bakayobya n'abandi binyuze mu gusobanura nabi ijambo ry'Imana. Igihe twiga ijambo ry'Imana, tuba dukwiriye kubikora dufite imitima iciye bugufi. Inarijye yose, gukunda ibyo dusanganywe kose bigomba gushyirwa ku ruhande. Ibitekerezo byimakajwe igihe kirekire ntabwo bigomba gufatwa nk'ibitibeshya."-Ellen G. White, _Councils to Writers and Editors_, pp.36,37.

**IBIBAZO:**

`Ongera usome 2 Abakorinto 10:1-6. Ni ubuhe buryo Pawulo yakoresheje arwana "intambara" zo mu bya Mwuka arwanirira ukuri kw'Imana, kandi dushobora kubukoresha dute mu ntambara zacu z'ibya Mwuka?`

`Bibiliya ivuga ko mbere yuko iherezo riza, abigisha benshi b'ibinyoma bazagerageza kuyobya abantu babavana mu kuri. Ni iki itorero ryawe ry'ibanze rishobora gukora ngo ririnde abizera kuyobywa n'abigisha b'ibinyoma bashobora kuba bari no mu itorero ryawe? Kuki ibyo ari ingenzi cyane mu kurangiza inshingano y'itorero?`

`Kuki Pawulo yabonye ko ari ingenzi kwirata urutonde rurerure rw'imibabaro (2 Abakorinto 11:16-33)? Na none kandi, bisobanuye iki "kwirata Uwiteka"?`

`Kuki ari ingenzi ku bizera b'itorero kwisuzuma kugira ngo bamenye ko bakiri mu byo twizera (2 Abakorinto 13:5)? Mbese ibyo bituma habaho irihe tandukaniro?`
