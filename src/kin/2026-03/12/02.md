---
title:  Intambara y'ibya Mwuka
date:   13/09/2026
---

`Soma 2 Abakorinto 10:1-11. Ubugwaneza bwa Pawulo mu kuvugana n'Abakorinto rimwe na rimwe byitiranywaga n'intege nke. Ni ayahe magambo cyangwa interuro biri muri iyi mirongo bigaragaza umwete Pawulo yari afite mu guhangana n'ikibazo cy'abigisha b'ibinyoma i Korinto?`

Pawulo atangira 2 Abakorinto 10 avuga yiyerekezaho cyane: "Njyewe Pawulo ubwanjye ndabiginga" (2 Abakorinto 10:1). Ibi bigaragaza uburyo Pawulo yari ababajwe n'inyigisho z'ibinyoma zacengezwaga mu itorero. Amagambo ye mu 2 Abakorinto 10:1 mu buryo bw'ininura agaragaza ikirego cy'abamurwanyaga bavugaga ko yari umunyagitugu utinyitse igihe yabaga yandika inzandiko ari kure, nyamara akaba ikigwari igihe yabaga ahanganye n'abantu imbona nkubone (2 Abakorinto 10:10,11). Asubiza avuga ko icyagaragaraga nk'intege nke gikwiriye gufatwa nk'ubugwaneza bukomeye no kwiyoroshya nka Kristo.

Tugomba guhangana n'abigisha b'ibinyoma dufite ubushizi bw'amanga ndetse n'icyizere (2 Abakorinto 10:2) ariko bivanze no kwiyoroshya kwa Kristo (2 Abakorinto 10:1). Yesu yigeze kuvuga ati: "Ndi umugwaneza kandi noroheje mu mutima" (Matayo11:29). Nyamara kandi, Yesu na we yahanganye n'abavunjaga amafaranga mu rusengero ashize amanga, yubika ameza yabo kandi abita abambuzi (Matayo 21:12,13). Yanise Abafarisayo indyarya ndetse n'ibituro byasizwe ingwa abivugira imbere yabo (Matayo 23:23-27). Kimwe na Yesu, Pawulo na we azi ko turi mu ntambara y'ibya Mwuka idusaba gukoresha intwaro zose z'Imana (Abefeso 6:12-17).

Imvugo ya Pawulo mu 2 Abakorinto 10 ni imvugo ya gisirikare kuko ubuzima buri mu kaga (2 Abakorinto 10:3-6). Uru ntabwo ari urugamba rwa kimuntu ahubwo ni urugamba rw'ijuru rugamije kugarura abantu kuri Kristo. Muri uwo mujyo, ingingo yose y'ikinyoma n'igitekerezo cyose cyishyize hejuru kigomba kurwanywa kandi kigakubitwa hasi, hashingiwe ku ijambo ry'Imana, kugira ngo buri gitekerezo cyose gifatwe mpiri ngo "kigomorerwe Kristo" (2 Abakorinto 10:5).

Muri uru rugamba rw'ibya Mwuka, Pawulo akorera mu butware bwa Kristo. Nubwo bimeze bityo, ubu butware bugamije kubaka ntabwo ari ugusenya (2 Abakorinto 10:8). Byoroheye abayobozi b'ibya Mwuka gushimangira ko bakorera mu butware bw'imana. Nyamara, bagomba kwibuka ko ubutware bwabo butangwa na Kristo, kandi ko kimwe na we, bagomba kuba abagwaneza kandi bakiyoroshya mu mitima. Kuba Pawulo avuga ko ubutware yabuhawe na Kristo, abiterwa n'ikibazo yari afite cy'uko Abakorinto bari bari gutega amatwi abantu babi, bityo bigatuma bareka kuba indahemuka kuri Kristo.

`Ni mu buhe buryo dushobora kuba abagwaneza kandi tugashira amanga igihe duhanganye n'abigisha b'ibinyoma? Kuki ibi byombi bigomba kujyana?`
