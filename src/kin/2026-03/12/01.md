---
title:  Guhangana n'Abigisha b'Ibinyoma
date:   12/09/2026
---

### Aho icyigisho cy'iki cyumweru gishingiye:
2 Abakorinto 10:1-17; Yeremiya 9:24; 2 Abakorinto11:1-15, 22-28; 2 Abakorinto 12:20, 21; 2 Abakorinto13:5.

> <p>Icyo kwibukwa:</p>
> "Kuko intwaro z'intambara yacu atari iz'abantu, ahubwo imbere y'Imana zigira imbaraga zo gusenya ibihome no kubikubita hasi" (2 Abakorinto 10:4).

Nk'aho Pawulo atari asanzwe afite ibibazo bihagije, hari ikindi kibazo cyavutse kandi yagombaga guhangana na cyo: Abigisha b'ibinyoma mu itorero. Abo bantu baramurwanyije kandi barwanya n'umurimo we. Icyabaye kibi kurushaho, abo bigisha b'ibinyoma bari baramaze gushuka n'abizera b'i Korinto. Pawulo avuga ko urugamba rwe rwo kurwanya iki kibazo ari urugamba rw'ibya Mwuka.

Mbese uko kwari ugukabya? Oya rwose. Pawulo yari azi ko byanze bikunze abo bantu atari we babaga barwanya ahubwo ko babaga barwanya Kristo. Ntabwo yari umuyobozi w'umwibone uharanira gukomeza kugira izina ryiza nk'uburyo bwo kugaragaza imbaraga ze ndetse n'ubutware yari afite ku bari bamwungirije. Yari azi ko ubutumwa yahawe ngo abwirize cyari ikibazo cy'ubugingo cyangwa urupfu, [ubutumwa] bwari bufite ingaruka z'iteka ryose. Kandi yari azi ko Imana ubwayo ari yo yamwohereje kubwiriza ubwo butumwa. "Pawulo wahamagariwe kuba intumwa ya Yesu Kristo nk'uko Imana yashatse" (1 Abakorinto 1:1).

Ku byerekeranye n'inyisho z'ibinyoma, itorero rigomba gukorera mu rukundo ariko rikaba rishikamye, rishingiye ku butware bw'Ibyanditswe Byera. Ubutumwa bwiza bugomba kubungabungwa, ntibwangirike kandi bugakomeza kuba bwera, kugira ngo buhe ubugingo ibyiringiro by'iteka ryose.
