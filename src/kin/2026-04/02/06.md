---
title:  Yohana Umubatiza, Gutegura Ubwoko bw'Imana
date:   08/10/2026
---



`Soma muri Mariko 1:2-5. Umugambi w'umurimo wa Yohana Umubatiza wari uwuhe?`

ubutumwa bwanditswe na Mariko butubwira ko kuza kwa Yohana Umubatiza kwabaye ugusohora k'ubuhanuzi bwa Yesaya (Yesaya 40:3). Imikurire ya Yohana Umubatiza kuva akiri umwana kugeza abaye mukuru yaranzwe no gukiranukira Imana. Satani yagize ubwoba bwo gutakaza ubutware bwe ku bantu nyuma yo kumva ijwi rya Yohana ryavugiraga mu butayu (soma mu gitabo cya Ellen G. White, _Uwifuzwa Ibihe Byose_, p. 167, 2018). Ububi bw'abari bateze amatwi Yohana bwashyizwe ahagaragara mu buryo bwatumye Satani ahinda umushyitsi. Icyo gihe noneho, ubutegetsi bwa Satani kuri Isirayeli bwari burimo guhungabanywa. Incuro nyinshi yagerageje kubuza Yohana Umubatiza kureka imibereho ye y'ubudahemuka no kwizera Mesiya, ariko ntiyabishobora.

`Soma muri Matayo 11:1-6. Ni mu buhe buryo Yesu yakomeje ukwizera kwa Yohana mu gihe uwo mubwiriza yari ari muri gereza?`

Satani ntiyashoboye gushuka Yesu ngo akore icyaha, kandi kuba yaratsinzwe mu butayu byaramurakaje cyane. Hanyuma yahinduye amayeri, afata Yohana, mubyara wa Yesu, amushyira mu nzu y'imbohe. Kuba Yohana yarafunzwe byababaje Yesu, n'ubwo Satani atashoboye kumushuka ngo akore icyaha. Ubwo ni bwo buryo Satani yakoreshaga kugira ngo amuce intege, mu gihe yari arimo atesha umutwe Umukiza. Ariko kandi, Yohana yabonye ijambo rimukomeza rivuye kuri Yesu. Kandi n'ubwo Yohana yari mu buzima buteye ubwoba, kandi mu by'ukuri adasobanukiwe impamvu yari akomeje kuba muri gereza mu gihe Yesu yari afite ububasha bwinshi, n'ubwo byari bimeze bityo, yakomeje gukiranuka, ndetse kugeza ku rupfu. Kimwe n'abahanuzi ba kera, natwe twahamagariwe kwamamaza ubutumwa bwo kwihana no kwizera no gukiranuka kwa Kristo mu buryo bwuzuye (Ibyahishuwe 14:6-12). Nk'uko yabigenje kuri Yohana Umubatiza, Satani ashobora gukoresha ibihe by'amage kugira ngo atume dushidikanya ku nshingano Imana yadushinze mu mibereho yacu. Ntitugomba gucika intege niba twibuka ko Imana ituyobora mu mibereho yacu, cyane cyane iyo ibintu bitatugendekera neza, nk'uko byagenze kuri Yohana, wafunzwe kandi akicwa n'ibikorwa by'umugore mubi n'umugabo w'umunyantege nke. Ibyo abantu badukorera byose, dushobora gusenga Imana dufite kwizera, kandi izadusubiza. Yadusezeranyije kuduha imbaraga zo gukora ibyo Ishaka (Ibyakozwe n'Intumwa 1:8). Mu isi ya nyuma y'Ubukristo n'isi itari iya Gikristo, ni ngombwa ko tuba abahamya b'indahemuka ba Kristo, uko ibihe twanyuramo byaba bimeze kose.

`Mbese, waba warigeze kuba mu mibereho nk'iyo Yohana yarimo igihe yari muri gereza, aho utashoboraga gusobanukirwa impamvu ibintu bibi nk'ibyo byakugeragaho? Ni iki cyaguteye gukomeza kwizera Imana kandi ukayiringira?`
