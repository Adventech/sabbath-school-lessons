---
title:  Umuhamagaro w'Umuhanuzi
date:   03/10/2026
---

### Aho icyigisho cy'iki cyumweru gishingiye:
Itangiriro 15:6; Abagalatiya 3:6-9; 1 Abami 18:19-39; Yesaya 53; Daniyeli 2:46-48; Mariko 1:2-5; Matayo 11:1-6.

> <p>Icyo kwibukwa:</p>
> "Numva ijwi ry'Umwami Imana riti, 'Ndatuma nde, ni nde watugendera?' Maze ndavuga nti, 'Ni jye: ba arijye utuma." (Yesaya 6:8).

Mu bihe bya Bibiliya, abahanuzi bakunze kugira uruhare rw'ingenzi mu mateka y'ubwoko bw'Imana. Rimwe na rimwe, babaga ari abantu bakomeye cyane, babaga bafite umwanya ukomeye mu mibereho y'abaturage.

Nyamara kandi, kuba umuhanuzi byari umuhamagaro utoroshye. Incuro nyinshi, imibereho yabo nti yari yoroshye, ahubwo yari yuzuyemo agahinda, kwangwa, kudasobanukirwa, no kwiheba.

Incuro nyinshi, abahanuzi b'Imana ntibabaga biteguye kwemera umuhamagaro ngo bajye mu murimo w'ubuhanuzi. Kandi ibyo ntibitangaje. Abahanuzi benshi barwanyijwe cyane n'abantu bagombye kuba baremeye ubutumwa bwabo. Abahanuzi b'Imana barirengagijwe, barasuzugurwa, barakubitwa kandi barafungwa. Abandi batewe amabuye cyangwa bahigwa nk'inyamaswa z'inkazi. Hari benshi bishwe bazira ukwizera kwabo.

Muri iki gihe, mu gihe cy'abantu buzuwemo n'urugomo, umururumba, ukwiyandarika, gukandamiza abandi, hamwe n'uburyo bwose bw'ibikorwa bya Satani, iryo jwi ry'ubuhanuzi akenshi rirangwa no kwirengagizwa cyangwa ntiryitabweho na mba. Twahamagariwe kumvira ijwi ry'abahanuzi b'Imana no kuba intumwa z'ibyiringiro twahawe binyuze ku bahanuzi.

Mu cyumweru gishize, twize ko nyuma yo Kugwa kwa Adamu na Eva muri Edeni, Imana yakoze ibishoboka byose kugira ngo abantu bongere gushyikirana na yo. Muri iki cyumweru tuzasuzuma ibihe bimwe na bimwe by'ingenzi mu mateka, ubwo Imana yahamagaraga abahanuzi kugira ngo bongere guhuza ubwoko bwayo n'ubutumwa bwayo ku isi yaguye.
