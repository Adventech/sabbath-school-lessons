---
title:  Daniyeli, Umuhanuzi Ukiranuka
date:   07/10/2026
---

Abakristo benshi bazi inkuru yo muri Daniyeli igice cya 1, aho yashyigikiye amaham. arebana n'ubuzima yari yarigishijwe akiri umwana. Daniyeli yabaye intangarugero birebana no kuba indahemuka (Daniyeli 1:8). Igihe bamuteguriraga ibyokurya by'ibwami biryoshye, yahisemo akomeje kurya ibyokurya by'ibimera no kunywa amazi gusa, aho kurya ibyokurya bamuzaniraga bivuye ku meza y'umwami (Daniyeli 1:12-16). Icyo cyari ikigeragezo gito ugereranyije n'icyo yari kuzahura na cyo nyuma y'aho, nk'icyo dusanga muri Daniyeli 6, igihe gukiranuka kwe kwari gutuma yicwa mu buryo bworoshye.

`Soma muri Daniyeli 2:46-48; Daniyeli 4:18; Daniyeli 5:13, 14; na Daniyeli 6:25-27. Ni ibiki aya masomo atubwira cyerekeranye n'ubuhamya Daniyeli yahaga abapagani bari bamukikije?`

Reba ubuhamya bwatanzwe n'abo bapagani ku mbaraga z'Imana ya Daniyeli. N'ubwo izo nkuru zihariye, zose zihishura ihame ry'ingenzi: binyuze mu mibereho yacu, mu gukiranuka kwacu, no mu mbaraga z'Imana zikorera muri twe kandi zikanyura muri twe, abandi bashobora kandi bagombye kumenya ibyerekeranye n'Imana yo mu ijuru, ko ari Yo "Mana nyamana, ni Umwami w'abami."

Nanone kandi, kubera ko insanganyamatsiko y'ingenzi y'ubuhanuzi bwa Daniyeli ari urubanza, ruzarangirana no kugaruka kwa Kristo (intsinzi y'ikirenga y'Imana), ubusobanuro bw'izina rya Daniyeli, "Imana ni umucamanza wanjye," rirakwiriye rwose. Inyandiko ze zikwiriye kwiganwa ubushishozi no kwitabwaho cyane, kubera ko zifite agaciro muri iki gihe. Ubuhanuzi bwa Daniyeli butwibutsa ko tugomba guhora turi maso kandi tukitondera amategeko y'Imana, ndetse no mu bintu "bito cyane" byo mu buzima. Nanone kandi, kubera ko Daniyeli yari umuhanuzi, yahanuye iby'ukuntu ingoma z'ubwami n'ibihugu bizima bigakura n'ukuntu bizahanguka bikavaho, ndetse n'ingaruka zo kutumvira Imana kwabyo. Binyuze mu iyerekwa ry'ubuhanuzi yagize, Daniyeli yahishuye umugambi w'Imana n'ukuntu ubwami bw'Imana buzatsinda burundu (Daniyeli 7:13, 14).

Nk'urugero, reka tuzirikane ibivugwa muri Daniyeli 2, byanditswe muri kimwe cya kabiri cy'ikinyagihumbi mbere ya Kristo. Daniyeli yagaragaje mu buryo budashidikanywaho uko ubwami bune bukomeye bw'isi buzazamuka bukima ingoma n'uko buzagwa bugahanguka, bikaza ndetse kugeza no mu gihe cyacu (igihe cy'ibirenge n'amano by'igishushanyo), yerekeza mu buryo bugaragara ku mahanga yo mu Burayi bwo muri iki gihe, ayo mahanga akaba, nk'uko ubwo buhanuzi bubyigisha neza, "bazivanga n'urubyaro rw'abantu, ariko ntibazafatana nk'uko ibyuma bitavanga n'ibumba" (Daniyeli 2:43). Mbega ubuhanuzi butangaje bwerekeranye n'u Burayi, ndetse kugeza no muri iki gihe, aho, n'ubwo ibihugu bizagerageza kwihuza binyuze mu gushyingiranwa gutandukanye, ndetse n'imbaraga zose bazakoresha
ngo habeho ubumwe, uwo mugabane wakomeje (uzakomeza) kuba ibihugu bitandukanye ifite amategeko, imigenzo n'imico yihariye. N'ubwo imyaka mike mbere y'uko Repubulika Zunze Ubumwe z'Abasoviyeti zisenyuka, nta kigo cy'ubutasi cyigeze kibona ko ibyo byari kuzaba, ubuhanuzi bwa Daniyeli butuma tugira impamvu zifatika zo kwiringira abahanuzi b'Imana.

`Ubwami bwose buvugwa muri Daniyeli igice cya 2 bwagiyeho nk'uko byari yarahanuwe. Ni iki ibyo byagombye kutwigisha ku byerekeranye no kwiringira buhanuzi buvuga ko ubwami bw'Imana buzahoraho buzimikwa nyuma y'ubwo Daniyeli 2:44)?`
