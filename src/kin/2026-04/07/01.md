### Aho icyigisho cy'iki cyumweru gishingiye:
Kubara 13:26-30; Yeremiya 36:20-26; 2Ngoma 36:15, 16; Matayo 23:30-35; 1 Abakorinto 10:6-12.

> <p>Icyo kwibukwa:</p>
> "Ibyo byababereyeho kutubera akabarore, kandi byandikiwe kuduhugura, twebwe abasohoreweho n'imperuka y'ibihe" (1 Abakorinto 10:11).

Abantu bamwe na bamwe muri iki gihe bashishikarizwa kuvuga "ukuri kwabo"-ibyababayeho, ibitekerezo byabo, ibyo bakeneye, imbibi bagarukiraho, ibyo bemera-uko byaba bimeze kose. Buri "kuri" gufite agaciro (ni ko batekereza) kandi ntigukwiriye gucecekwa. Ariko se bigenda bite iyo kumwe muri uku "kuri" kugundirwa cyane kugonganye n'Ukuri ari We Yesu Kristo ndetse n'Ijambo rye yandikishije?

Ikibaho nuko abantu bamwe bazemera ukuri kwa Bibiliya maze bakava mu kwibeshya kwabo; nyamara abandi bazihambira ku binyoma byabo. Bamwe bahindura imyifatire yabo bitewe no kumvira ukuri; abandi bakakwigomekaho. Uko ni ko amateka ya muntu yagiye amera.

Abahanuzi ba Bibiliya akenshi bagiye bahamagarirwa gutanga ubutumwa abantu batashakaga kumva. Ingaruka yabaga ko bamwe muri bo banengwaga cyane, bagacibwa, ndetse bagakangishwa kwicwa cyangwa hakaba nubwo bicwa (Abaheburayo 11:32-38). Nubwo ibintu byabaga bimeze bityo, abahanuzi b'Imana batangaga ubutumwa bwabo bakiranutse kuko ubuyobozi bwa gihanuzi bw'Imana butari amahitamo kuri bo. Yabaga ari inyigisho yo gukiza ubugingo ku bwoko bw'Imana. Bityo rero ubutumwa bwagombaga gutangwa uko ikiguzi bwajyaga gusaba umuhanuzi cyari kuba kingana kose.
