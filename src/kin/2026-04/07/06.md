---
title:  Ngo Bitubere Akabarore
date:   12/11/2026
---



Hinkuru zose zo mu Isezerano rya Kera, nubwo ari mbi kandi zikaba zibabaje, zatanzwe ku bw'impamvu nziza: icyifuzwa kuruta ibindi ni uko dukwiriye kuzigiramo amasomo y'uko bitugendekera igihe twigometse ku Mana.

`Soma 1 Abakorinto 10:6-12. Dukurikije ibyo intumwa Pawulo ivuga, ni iyihe mpamvu yatumye Imana ihumekera mu bahanuzi ngo bandike ibyo banditse muri Bibiliya?`

Muri iyi mirongo Pawulo ahuza neza ahashize n'iki gihe turimo, agahuza Isirayeli ya kera n'itorero, ari ryo twebwe abemeye Yesu nk'Umwami n'Umukiza tubarirwamo (Abagalatiya 6:14-16).

Pawulo agaragaza neza ko ibyanditswe kera ku byagiye biba hagati y'inyokomuntu n'Imana- byaba byiza cyangwa se bibi-bidakwiye kwiregagizwa cyangwa ngo bye kwitabwaho. Mu by'ukuri, Imana yatekereje ko ari ingenzi cyane kubyandikisha nk'akabarore "kugira ngo tutifuza ibibi nk'uko bo babyifuje" (1 Abakorinto 10:6). Mu yandi magambo, ibyo mu gihe cyashize biba ari intangiriro y'ibyo mu gihe kizaza. Ahashize hakunze kwerekana ibibaho muri iki gihe ndetse n'ibizaba mu gihe kizaza.

Mu bigaragara, Imana iri kuvuga ko tutagusha ishyano ryo gusubira mu makosa yakozwe n'abo mu bisekuru byashize, turamutse twumviye imiburo iri mu Byanditswe Byera n'ubutumwa bwa gihanuzi yatwoherereje binyuze mu bahanuzi. Nidutega amatwi, tukiga kandi tukumvira, imibereho yacu izakomezwa kandi igubwe neza (2 Ngoma 20:20). Kuri bamwe, kugubwa neza mu bya Mwuka byasobanuwe nko kugira ingo nziza, imodoka, imyambaro, n'ubundi butunzi bw'ibifatika. Nubwo Imana isezerana kuduha ibyo dukeneye, na none ishaka ko imitima yacu igubwa neza, nubwo twaba tunaguwe neza mu bundi buryo (3 Yohana 2). Nyamara kuzura nyakuri kandi kurambye mu mibereho ya Gikristo bisaba kumvira ubutumwa bw'Imana.

Imana ntipfusha ubusa amagambo yayo. Igihe ivuze, amagambo yayo aba ari ay'iteka ryose. Ububasha bwayo n'imbaraga ntibizagira iherezo (Yesaya 40:8). Byaratanzwe ngo biduhugure "twebwe abasohoreweho n'imperuka y'ibihe" (1 Abakorinto 10:11). Niba ibyo byari ukuri muri icyo gihe, ni mu buhe buryo aya magambo atarushaho guhuzwa n'ibyacu muri iki gihe?

`Igihe usomye inkuru zivuga ibyo kwigomeka no kutizera ziboneka muri Bibiliya, ni ayahe masomo ushobora kuzikuramo ashobora kugufasha kwirinda gukora ibimeze nk'ibyo bakoze?`
