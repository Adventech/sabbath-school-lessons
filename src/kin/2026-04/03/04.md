---
title:  Inshingano z'Abahanuzi
date:   13/10/2026
---

`Soma igitabo cya 1 Samweli 8:4-22 n'Abalewi 8:10-13. Ni mu buhe buryo abahanuzi bakoragamo iyo babaga batumwe ku bami n'abatambyi?`

Abahanuzi bakoranaga n'abami mu bihe byiza no mu bihe bibi. Igihe Isiraheli yasaba umwami (1 Abami 8:4-22), abantu baravuze bati: "turashaka umwami, ajye aducira imanza nk'ayandi mahanga yose". (1Samweli 8:5). Mbega urugero rubabaje, nyamara rukomeye rw'akaga ubwoko bw'Imana bwagiye bugeramo igihe cyose: gushaka kumera nk'ababazengurutse. Muri ubwo buryo, ntabwo bari bazi icyo basaba kizabazanira, nubwo bari baraburiwe mbere y'ighe akaga bizabazanira (1Samweli 8:11-18), kuko Imana yari yarabibabwiye mbere ibinyujije mu muhanuzi. Ashingiye ku byifuzo byabo, nubwo yari yarababuriye hakiri kare, Samweli yahise yimika umwami wa mbere wa Isiraheli amwimikishije amavuta, mu mateka ya Bibiliya uwo mwami akaba ari Sawuli.

Ibitabo bya Bibiliya byo mu Isezerano rya Kera, igitabo cya 1 n'icya 2 by'Abami n'igitabo cya 1 n'icya 2 cy'Ibyo ku Ngoma bivuga indangagaciro n'imiterere y'ubutegetsi bw'abami ba Isiraheli n'Ubuyuda. Amateka yera agaragaza ko abami bakoze ibibi baruta abakoze ibyiza mu maso y'Imana. Umwe mu mirimo y'ingenzi y'abahanuzi wari uwo gufasha abami bakiranirwa kugaruka ku mategeko y'Imana bakayumvira. Icyakurikiragaho ni uko aba bahanuzi bahuraga n'akaga ko gúterwa ubwoba, ndetse bamwe bakagirirwa nabi n'abo bami babaga baratumye abantu bimura Imana, ndetse bakanicwa (1 Abami 18:4-19, 2 Ngoma 
24:20-22).

Abandi bagiraga aho bahurira n'abahanuzi ni abatambyi. Mose, umuhanuzi w'Imana yasutse ku mutwe w'Aroni n'uw'abahungu be amavuta nk'abatambyi ba mbere b'Abisiraheli (Abalewi 8:10-13). Hari ibihe abatambyi bahabwaga amagambo yo gucyaha avuye ku Mana anyujijwe mu bahanuzi (Hoseya 6:9, Malaki 2:1-3). Urugero ruteye ubwoba rwa bene uwo muburo ni uwahawe ab'inzu ya Eli, baburiwe kubw'imyitwarire n'ibikorwa byabo bibi (1Samweli 2:27-36). Uko kwari ugukeburwa gukomeye kubera ibikorwa byabo bibi byari byarabaye akamenyero bakoreraga mu bwoko bw'Imana kubera inshingano z'umwihariko bari barahawe.

Abahanuzi bari abigisha b'abantu (2 Ngoma 34). Batangaga ubutumwa bw'Imana bakabugeza ku batambyi, ku bami, no ku baturage b'igihugu (Yeremiya 5:31, Ezekiyeli 22:26). Abahanuzi bahabwaga n'ubutumwa bugenewe andi mahanga yabaga abazengurutse ibyo bihugu bikagezwaho umugambi Imana ibafitiye, ikintu cyasubiwemo inshuro nyinshi mu butumwa bwahabwaga abahanuzi bo mu Isezerano rya Kera (Yesaya 60:1-3; Yesaya 62:1, 2). Abahanuzi ntabwo basohozaga ubutumwa bahawe gutanga gusa, hari n'ubwo bategekwaga kubwandika mu buryo bwo kububika (2 Ngoma 13:22). Na none kandi, uko abahanuzi bari bameze kose, ari abantu nkatwe (bakaba bakora amakosa bakagira n'ibibananira), bari abagabo n'abagore b'abanyamasengesho, biga Ijambo ry'Imana, kandi bakorera Imana n'abantu bayo (Yeremiya 42:4; 2 Abami 22:15-17).
