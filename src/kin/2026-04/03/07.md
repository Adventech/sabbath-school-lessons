---
title:  Ntiyari Atunganye Ariko Yari Afite Ubushake
date:   16/10/2026
---

Nk'uko twabibonye, imibereho y'abahanuzi bavugwa mu Isezerano rya Kera igaragaza ko bitigeze byoroha kuba intumwa y'Imana. Ellen G. White yagize guhagarika umutima nk'ibyo abahanuzi bo muri Bibiliya bagiraga. Nk'uko twabyize ku wa Gatandatu ushize. yari akiri muto, agira amasonisoni, kandi adafite n'ubuzima buzira umuze mu gihe yahamagarwaga n'Imana. Mu gihe iyerekwa rye rya kabiri ryashimangiye ihamagarwa rye, Elina Harmon [waje kwitwa izina ry'umugabo we rya White] yakomeje kumva atewe ubwoba no guhamagarwa n'Imana, no kwandika ibyo imubwiye:

Mu gitabo cye cyitwa '_Spiritual Gifts_' [Impano za Mwuka] vol. 2, pp. 35, 36, yanditse aya magambo: "Nyuma y'icyumweru kimwe ibyo bibaye, Umwami Imana yampaye irindi yerekwa, maze anyereka ibigeragezo ngomba kuzanyuramo; kandi ko ngomba kugenda nkereka abandi ibyo yampishuriye; ikindi cyari uko nzahangana n'abandwanya, nkagira n'umubabaro mwinshi mu mutima .... Iryo yerekwa ryanteye ubwoba mu buryo bukabije. Ubuzima bwanjye bwagerwaga ku mashyi, kandi icyo gihe nari mfite imyaka cumi n'irindwi gusa y'ubukuru .... Nasenze nshimikiriye ngo uwo mutwaro ube wagira undi uhabwa. Ariko umucyo nahawe ni uyu ngo: "Genda ubwire abandi ibyo naguhishuriye." Ntabwo nari niteguye kuzenguruka isi mbivuga. Nta cyizere na gito nari nifitiye .... Numvaga nifuza gupfa. Nkumva urupfu arirwo nahitamo kurenza guhabwa izo nshingano."- _Spiritual Gifts_, vol. 2, pp. 35, 36.

Wumvise ko Elina Harmon yifuzaga gupfa kuruta kwemera umuhamagaro w'Imana? Nyuma y'amasengesho menshi no gukomezwa n'abizera bagenzi be, yahise yemera umuhamagaro nta yandi mahane ateye, ibisigaye birebana n'ubuzima bwe abyegurira Imana."- _Spiritual Gifts*_*, vol. 2, p. 38. Yari kuzaba ijwi rirangurura kuri bose kandi akagira uruhare rukomeye ku hazaza h'Itorero ry'Abadiventisiti b'Umunsi wa Karindwi. Nyamara kandi, Elina Harmon [White] yari umuntu kandi na we yari afite amakosa ye bwite n'ibigeragezo by'umubiri, ibibazo by'umuryango we, n'ibibazo yari afitanye n'abandi. Nyamara, muri ibyo bibazo yari afite nk'umuntu, dukuramo isomo rikomeye: Imana ihitamo abantu badatunganye bafite ubushake bwo kuyikorera.

Kubw'ubuyobozi bwa Mwuka Wera, umurimo wa gihanuzi wa Ellen G. White wagombaga guha imbaraga ukanatera umwete abizera kandi ukabayobora ku kuri kwa Bibiliya. Kuva mu mwaka 1860, yagize uruhare rukomeye mu buyobozi bw'Itorero ry'Abadiventisiti b'Umunsi wa Kandwi ryari rikeneye gutera imbere mu murimo ryahamagariwe wo kwamamaza ubutumwa bw'abamalayika batatu mu isi. Imana yagombaga kuyobora abo bizera bagashinga Itorero ry'Abadiventisiti b'Umunsi wa Karindwi mu mwaka wa 1863. Mu myaka mirongo yakurikiyeho, ijwi rya gihanuzi rya Ellen G. White ryemezaga benshi ryatumye hatangizwa gahunda y'ubuvuzi n'uburezi muri iryo torero ku isi yose. Nyamara, umusanzu munini w'umurimo we wabaye inyandiko ze, ubu zigeze ku mpapuro ibihumbi 100,000. Inyandiko za Ellen G. White yanditse ayobowe na Mwuka Wera zikomeje kuvuga hirya no hino ku isi kugeza n'uyu munsi.
