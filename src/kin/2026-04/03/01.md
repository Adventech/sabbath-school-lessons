---
title:  Abahanuzi bo mu Isezerano rya Kera
date:   10/10/2026
---

### Aho icyigisho cy'iki cyumweru gishingiye:
Yakobo 5:17, 18; 1 Abami 19:1-16; 1 Abami 22:1-14; Kuva 15:20; 2 Abami 22:11-14; Yeremiya 14:1-15.

> <p>Icyo kwibukwa:</p>
> "Yehoshafati arahagarara aravuga ati: 'Nimunyumve yemwe Bayuda, namwe abatuye i Yerusalemu, mwizere Uwiteka Imana yanyu, mubone gukomezwa; mwizere n'abahanuzi bayo, mubone kugubwa neza" (2 Ngoma 20:20).

Hari ikoranabuhanga (ryitwa GPS) rifasha abari mu rugendo kumenya aho bageze, aho bagiye, no kumenya aho akaga gashobora guturuka, rikababurira. Abagenzi mu bya Mwuka bari mu rugendo rujya mu ijuru nabo bakeneye ikibayobora muri iki gihe no mu gihe kizaza. Ni ukuvuga ko bakeneye iryo koranabuhanga. Uburyo bumwe Imana yakoze ibyo mu bihe byahise yabikoze ibinyujije mu murimo w'abahanuzi bo mu Isezerano rya Kera. Mu nkuru zabo, inama, ubuhanuzi, ubusizi, n'indirimbo, tubonamo amatsinda menshi y'abahanuzi benshi bagejeje ubutumwa bw'Imana ku bantu.

Mu cyigisho cy'iki cyumweru tuzahamenyera ubusobanuro bw'icyo abahanuzi ari bo, ubusobanuro budufasha gusobanukirwa impano yo guhanura nk'uko yagaragajwe na bamwe muri abo bagaragu b'Imana.

Nk'uko tuzabibona, abo bari abantu nkatwe, b'abanyantege nke, ibiremwa muntu bigira ubwoba nkatwe, bigira uburakari, bahura n'ibibaca intege. Ubuzima bari babayeho ntabwo bwari bworoshye, bwari bwuzuyemo imbogamizi n'ibirushya, nk'uko natwe twese duhura nabyo.

None se ni iki dushobora kwigira kuri bimwe mu byaranze abo bantu biswe abagaragu b'Imana?
