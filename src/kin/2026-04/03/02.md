---
title:  Ubumuntu bw'Abahanuzi
date:   11/10/2026
---

Nk'uko twabibonye mu cyumweru gishize, Imana yakoresheje umuhanuzi Eliya gukora umurimo w'indashyikirwa muri Isiraheli, nubwo yari afite abamurwanyaga benshi. Iby'Imana yamukoresheje ni ntagereranwa. Ariko se, ni iki cyakurikiyeho?

`Reka turebe uburyo Yakobo avuga umuhanuzi Eliya muri Yakobo 5:17, 18. Ni iki yashakaga gusobanura igihe yavugaga ko Eliya yari "umuntu umeze nkatwe"?`

Eliya yageze ku ntsinzi ihambaye ku Musozi Karumeli (1 Abami 18); Imana yamukoresheje ibintu bikomeye, kandi kimwe mu bitangaza biheranije akaba ari icyavuzwe mu mateka yo mu Isezerano rya Kera, igitangaza gikomeye cyabayeho nyuma y'amagambo Eliya avuze, maze umuriro uramanuka uva mu ijuru (1 Abami 18:38)! Ushobora gutekereza ko uwo muntu w'Imana yasabwe n'umunezero urenze urugero nyuma y'ikintu nk'icyo cyari kibayeho.

`Soma 1 Abami 19:1-16. Mbese ibyo dusomye bitubwira iki cyerekeye uko uwo muhanuzi w'Imana yumvise amerewe muri icyo gihe?`

"Uwiteka ndarambiwe, icyabimara ni uko ubu wakuraho ubugingo bwanjye, kuko ntaruta ba sogokuruza ubwiza!" (1 Abami 19:4). Ibyo bisa nk'aho ari umuntu urwaye indwara imeze nko kwiheba. Kuki? Nta gushidikanya, ibyabereye ku Musozi Karumeli ntibyari igikorwa cyoroheje gukurikiza; ariko kandi, hari ikindi kintu kirimo kuba ahangaha.

Ahari urufunguzo rushobora kuba muri aya magambo: "Nagize ishyaka ryinshi ry'Uwiteka Imana nyiringabo; kuko Abisiraheli bavuye mu isezerano ryawe, bagasenya ibicaniro byawe, kandi bicishije abahanuzi bawe inkota, ni jye jyenyine usigaye; nanjye baragenza ubugingo bwanjye ngo banyice" (1 Abami 19:10). Ibi bisa nk'aho [Eliya] yumvikanisha ko yari ababajwe n'ibyo yumvaga ko ari amakosa yakoze mu murimo we. N'ubwo yagize ishyaka ryinshi, ubuhakanyi buracyariho, nk'aho ari we wenyine washoboraga kubuhagarika. Uko byamera kose, dushobora kubona ko uyu muntu w'Imana yari umuntu kandi ko yari afite intege nke za kimuntu. Mu yandi magambo, Eliya yari umuntu "umeze nkatwe."

`Ni ukubera iki rimwe na rimwe byoroha kugira igitekerezo cyo "gutsindwa/gucika intege" mu mibereho yawe no mubyo ukora? Ni ayahe masezerano yo muri Bibiliya ushobora kwifashisha kugira ngo wigobotore icyo kibazo cyo gucika intege?`
