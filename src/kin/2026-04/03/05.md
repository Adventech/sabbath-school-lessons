---
title:  Abahanuzi b'Abagore
date:   14/10/2026
---

`Soma mu Kuva 15:20, Abacamanza 4:4, 2 Abami 22:14, no muri Nehemiya 6:14. Ni iki kindi ayo masomo yigisha cyerekeye uruhare rw'ubuhanuzi?`

Ibyanditswe bitanga ibihamya byinshi byerekana ko Imana yahamagariye abagore kuba abahanuzi. (Reba uburyo ijambo ry'Igiheburayo _nevi'ah_ [Kuva 15:20] n'ijambo ry'Ikigiriki _prophētis_ yakoreshejwe [Luka 2:36].) Muri Mika 6:4, Miriyamu yashyizwe ku rutonde hamwe na Mose na Aroni nk'aboherejwe kugira icyo babwira abantu. Miriyamu avugwa nk'umuhanuzikazi waririmbye indirimbo yo kunesha igihe Abisiraheli bambukaga Inyanja Itukura (Kuva 15:20, 21). Nyamara, uwo muhanuzikazi, kimwe n'abahanuzi b'abagabo bagenzi be, ntabwo yafatwaga nk'utagira amakosa, nk'uko inyifato ye igayitse ku mugore wa Mose w'Umunyetiyopiyakazi ibihishura, ibikorwa uwo muhanuzikazi yaje no guhanirwa bikomeye (soma mu gitabo cyo Kubara 12:1-10).

Dushobora kubona umurimo w'undi muhanuzi mu nkuru ya Debora, wakoze umurimo w'ubucamanza muri Isiraheli (Abacamanza 4:1-4). Muby'ukuri, hari igihamya kigaragara cy'uko Debora (Abacamanza 4:4) yahawe iyerekwa rivuye ku Mana (Abacamanza 4:4-9, 14- 22). Indirimbo ye yo kunesha (Abacamanza 5:2-31) ni uguhamya ku mugaragaro ugutabara kw'Imana kandi yari ifite ubusobanuro bwimbitse mu by'iyobokamana ku ishyanga muri icyo gihe cy'amateka.

`Soma mu 2 Abami 22:11-14. Ni iki cyateye abantu boherejwe n'Umwami Yosiya kujya kureba Hulida? Ni iki ibyo bishobora kutubwira cyerekeye akamaro yari afite mu gihugu?`

Hulida "umuhanuzikazi" yohererejwe n'Umwami Yosiya abagabo bo mu nzego zo hejuru mu gihugu ngo bajye kumureba bagire ibyo bamuhanuza (2 Abami 22:14, 2 Ngoma 34:22) nyuma yo gusoma amagambo yo mu "Gitabo cy'Amategeko" (2 Abami 22:8), (byari byizewe ko bishobora kuba ari ubutumwa bwari bugenewe umwami), kuko amaze kubwumva "yashishimuye imyambaro ye" (2 Abami 22:11). Ibyo bigaragaza ko ibyari muri icyo gitabo byari ibintu bikomeye byo kwitabwaho, akaba ariyo mpamvu umwami yabifashe nk'ibidakwiriye kujenjekerwa, akaba ari nayo mpamvu yahise yohereza intumwa ze kureba Hulida atazuyaje. Hulida yari yaramaze guhabwa ubutumwa buvuye ku Mana burebana n'ibihano byari bitegerejwe kugera ku Buyuda. Ivugurura rya Yosiya ryavuyemo ko igihano cy'Imana cyari cyarabwiwe Hulida ko kizagera ku bwoko bwayo gikurwaho.

`Abahanuzi b'abagabo, n'abahanuzi b'abagore, nubwo bari barahamagawe n'Imana, nabo bari abanyabyaha bakeneye ubuntu bw'Imana. Ni ibihe byiringiro ushobora gukura mu nkuru zabo?`
