---
title:  Bibiliya Irisobanuro Ubwayo
date:   15/11/2026
---

Uburyo dusobanuramo Ibyanditswe Byera ahanini bushingira ku byo twibwira. Abafata Bibiliya nk'igitabo gihurijwemo gusa inyandiko za kera ntibashobora kubona ubumwe buri hagati y'ibitabo byayo bitandukanye. Nyamara igihe twitaye ku ihame ry'ibanze rivuga ko "Ibyanditswe Byera byose byahumetswe n'Imana kandi bigira umumaro" (2 Timoteyo 3:16), dushobora kwishimira ubumwe buyirimo maze tukareka Bibiliya ikaba ari yo ubwayo yisobanura.

`Soma Matayo 5:17-48. Yesu yasobanuye ate inyigisho zo mu Isezerano rya Kera zivugwa muri iyi mirongo?`

Gusoma amajyejuru ibyanditswe muri Matayo 5:17-48 bishobora gutuma umuntu yibwira ko Yesu yari ari gusimbuza inyigisho zo mu Isezerano rya Kera inyigisho nshya ze bwite. Ariko iyo usomye witonze urushaho kubona neza ko Yesu yari ari guhishura ubwaguke bwimbitse bwo mu bya Mwuka bw'Isezerano rya Kera. Muri ubu buryo, yari ari gusohoza isezerano riri muri Yeremiya 31:31-33 rivuga ko mu gihe cy'isezerano rishya amategeko y'Imana yagombaga gushyirwa mu bwenge bw'abantu be kandi akandikwa mu mitima yabo (gereranya no mu Baheburayo 8:8-10; Abaheburayo 10:16).

Rimwe na rimwe ubusobanuro bw'imirongo runaka buba buboneka mu gitabo iyo mirongo igaragaramo. Nk'urugero: muri Daniyeli 2, tuhabona hagaragazwa inzozi z'amayobera z'umwami Nebukadineza (Daniyeli 2:31-35) zakurikiwe no gusobanurwa kw'inzozi (Daniyeli 2:36-45). Mu buryo bumwe n'ubwo, muri Matayo 13 tuhafite umugani w'umubibyi (Matayo 13:1-9), noneho hakaza ubusobanuro bwawo (Matayo 13:18-23).

Rimwe na rimwe ubusobanuro bw'ubutumwa runaka butangwa n'umuhanuzi wakurikiyeho. Nk'urugero: 2 Ngoma 36:22, 23 hashimangira ugusohora k'ubuhanuzi bw'imyaka 70, nk'uko bwari bwaravuzwe muri Yeremiya 25:11, 12 na Yeremiya 29:10. Nubwo bimeze bityo, ibyinshi mu byasuraga ibizaba byo mu Isezerano rya Kera byagiye bisohorezwa muri Kristo, kandi imirongo myinshi yo mu Isezerano rya Kera isobanurwa mu Isezerano Rishya.

"Isezerano rya Kera rivuga ubutumwa bwiza mu buryo bw'ishushanyamvugo n'ibimenyetso, naho Isezerano Rishya ni ubutumwa bwiza bufatika (butavugwa mu buryo bw'ibishushanyo). Buri sezerano rikenewe nk'irindi."-Ellen G. White, _Ubutumwa Bwatoranyijwe_, igitabo cya 2, p. 118. Mu by'ukuri, Isezerano Rishya ni umusobanuzi wahumekewe w'Isezerano rya Kera.

`Kuki ari ingenzi cyane kwitondera ibyo twibwira mu gihe twiga Bibiliya? Kubera iki icyo twibwira kiruta ibindi byose kigomba kuba kwiringira ko ari Ijambo ry'Imana, kandi ko ari ryo rigomba kuturondora aho kuba ari twe turirondora?`
