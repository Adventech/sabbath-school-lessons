---
title:  Icyerekezo Gishingiye ku Myandikire
date:   17/11/2026
---



Umugani ukundwa n'ababwiriza benshi uvuga iby'umuntu wari uhanganye n'ibibazo hikomeye akaba yarashakaga kumenya ubushake bw'Imana kuri we. Yabumbuye Bibiliya maze ashyira urutoki kuri Matayo 27:5 atagombye gutoranya; aho hantu haranditswe ngo "Yuda] aragenda ariyahura". Akura urutoki kuri uwo murongo, maze arongera asubira muri ubwo buryo yari yakoresheje, agerageza kubona ubutumwa bwamusubizamo agatege, nuko urutoki rwe rukora kuri Luka 10:37, havuga ngo "Yesu aramubwira ati: 'Genda, nawe ugire utyo." Iyi mirongo yombi ni iyo mu Byanditswe, nyamara ikwiriye kumvikana mu cyerekezo irimo.

Mu by'ukuri, kugoreka ubutumwa bwa Bibiliya kwinshi gukomoka mu migenzereze rusange yo gufata imirongo ya Bibiliya bakayitandukanya n'icyerekezo yanditswemo. Ubu buryo butuma Ibyanditswe byinjirwamo n'ubwoko bwose bw'ubusobanuro buhimbano. Soma Itangiriro 3:1-5 na Matayo 4:1-11. Ni mu buhe buryo Satani yagoretse amagambo y'Imana agashaka kuyakoresha mu cyerekezo kinyuranye n'icyo yanditswemo?

Abantu bamwe bagoreka ubutumwa bwa Bibiliya bataryarya bitewe n'ubumenyi buke bafite. Ariko abandi babikora babigambiriye, nk'uko Satani yabikoze inshuro ebyiri zavuzwe mu mirongo twabonye. Tutitaye ku cyabibateye, buri gihe cyose ingaruka iba mbi. Mu murima wa Edeni (Itangiriro 3:1-5; gereranya n' Ibyahishuwe 12:9), Satani yiyeretse Eva anyuze mu nzoka yari ibereye ijisho kandi iteye amatsiko kandi ishobora kuvuga nk'umuntu. Satani yakuye amagambo y'Imana mu cyerekezo cy'umwimerere yarimo mbere, maze abanza kuyashyira mu buryo rusange, hanyuma ayaha ubusobanuro bwe buyobya.

Igihe Yesu yajyanwaga mu butayu na Mwuka Wera (Matayo 4:1-11; Luka 4:1-13), "Satani yaje ameze nka malayika w'umucyo mu butayu bw'ibishuko kugira ngo ashuke Kristo."- Ellen G. White, in The Advent Review and Sabbath Herald_, December 18, 1888. Igihe yavuganaga na Kristo, Satani yasubiyemo nabi amagambo ari muri Zaburi 91:11, 12 kugira ngo yerekane ko Imana yajyaga kurinda n'abishora mu kaga babigambiriye.

Ibyabereye mu murima wa Edeni ndetse no mu butayu by'ubushukanyi bishimangira ko intambara ikomeye iriho, ikubiyemo no gusobanura nabi Ijambo ry'Imana, ryaba iryavuzwe ndetse n'iryanditswe. Bikwiriye kudufasha kumenya akamaro ko gukomeza ubusobanuro dufite bw'amagambo ya Bibiliya tukabugumisha mu cyerekezo yanditswemo.

`Ni ukubera iki tugomba kwirinda igishuko kitubwira gukura imirongo mu cyerekezo cyayo kugira ngo tugerageze kwerekana ko turi mu kuri, bona nubwo ibyo tuvuga byaba ari ukuri koko?`
