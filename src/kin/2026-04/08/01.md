---
title:  Gusobanuro Inyandiko za Gihanuzi
date:   14/11/2026
---

### Aho icyigisho cy'iki cyumweru gishingiye:
Matayo 5:17-48; 1 Abakorinto 16:20; Matayo 4:1-11; Luka 10:25-28; Luka 24:25-27, 44-49.

> <p>Icyo kwibukwa:</p>
> "Atangirira kuri Mose no ku bahanuzi bose, abasobanurira mu byanditswe byose ibyanditswe kuri we" (Luka 24:27).

Nyuma yo kubambwa kwa Yesu no kuzuka kwe, Kristo yabonekeye abigishwa be babiri bari bafite agahinda, kandi bombi barivugiye bati: "Twiringiraga ko ari we uzacungura Abisirayeli" (Luka 24:21). Abigishwa bari biteze Mesiya wari gukiza Abayuda gutegekwa n'Abaroma. Uko kwitega ibitari byo kandi koko bakaza kubibura byakomokaga ku busobanuro bubogamiye ku ruhande rumwe bw'ubuhanuzi bwerekeye Mesiya wavugwaga mu Isezerano rya Kera.

Kristo yakemuye ikibazo cyo kudasobanukirwa neza kw'aba bigishwa babiri binyuze mu kubasobanurira Ibyanditswe mu buryo burushijeho kwaguka (Luka 24:325-27). Ubusobanuro bw'Ibyanditswe Byera Kristo yatanze bwatumye imitima yabo igurumanira muri bo (Luka 24;31, 32). Nyuma yaho Yesu yanabonekeye itsinda rigari ry'abigishwa bari i Yerusalemu, kandi "abungura ubwenge ngo basobanukirwe n'Ibyanditswe" (Luka 24:45; soma na Luka 24:33, 36, 44-49).

Ibi bintu byabaye bishimangira umusaruro mwiza uturuka mu gusobanukirwa neza inyandiko za gihanuzi. Gusobanukirwa nabi ubuhanuzi bishobora kugeza umuntu mu kaga ko mu bya Mwuka.
