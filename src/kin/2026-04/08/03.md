---
title:  Icyerekezo Gishingiye ku Mateka n'Umuco
date:   16/11/2026
---



`Soma Yesaya 65:17-20. Mbese bisobanuye iki kuba mu ijuru rishya n'isi nshya umwana "azapfa amaze imyaka ijana"? (Yesaya 65:20).`

Ufashe ibivugwa muri Yesaya 65:20 ukabikura mu cyerekezo gishingiye ku mateka bivugirwamo, umuntu yakwibwira mu buryo bworoshye ko urupfu n'abanyabyaha bizaba mu ijuru rishya n'isi nshya. Nyamara icyo gitekerezo kivuguruza amasezerano aboneka muri Yesaya cy'uko abapfuye bakiranuka bazazuka (Yesaya 26:19) kandi ko urupfu n'umubabaro amaherezo bizakurwaho (Yesaya 25:8; Yesaya 65:19).

Biragaragara rwose ko ibivugwa muri Yesaya 65:20 ari imvugo irengurira ku kindi kintu. Mbese ni uwuhe mwana waba afite imyaka 100 y'ubukuru? Ibiri amambu, iyo dusomye Yesaya 65:20 mu cyerekezo gishingiye ku mateka, dushobora kubona ko uyu murongo, mu buryo bwa gisizi, watungaga agatoki ku mugisha ishyanga rya Isirayeli ryajyaga guhabwa, ariko bakawuhabwa ari uko bakomeje kwitondera ibivugwa muri iryo sezerano (gereranya no mu Gutegeka kwa kabiri 28). Nk'uko Yesu yabikoze muri Matayo 24, agahuza ibyariho muri icyo gihe n'ibyajyaga kuzaza nyuma yaho, hano Yesaya na we abigenza atyo, akerekana ko ibyabaho muri iki gihe bishobora kuba integuza y'ibizaba mu ijuru Rishya n'isi nshya.

Muri Yesaya irema rishya ntabwo ryose uko ryakabaye riri mu gihe kizaza. Mu buryo bwihuse ryinjiye mu gihe turimo binyuze mu murimo w'icyubahiro Imana yakoze wo gucungura no kubabarira. Uyu murongo utangaza ko ndetse n'uyu mwanya, mu mibereho ya Isirayeli yo muri iki gihe, Imana iri kunesha urupfu. Iki gitekerezo kigaragarira mu isezerano ryo kubaho igihe kirekire no gukurwaho kw'imfu z'abana (Yesaya 65:20). Ibi icyo bikora gusa ni ukuba igicucu cy'ibitazabura kubaho mu gihe kizaza.

`Soma 1 Abakorinto 16:20. Ni mu buhe buryo duhuza iyi mirongo n'igihe turimo ndetse n'umuco uriho muri iki gihe?`

Mu muco w'Abagiriki bo mu bihe by'Isezerano Rishya, gusuhuzanya kw'abafitanye isano ndetse n'ukw'inshuti magara kwaherekezwaga no gusomana. Pawulo atanga amabwiriza arebana n'uku gusuhuzanya kurimo ubwuzu n'urukundo mu bizera b'Abakristo mu Abaroma 16:16; 1 Abakorinto 16:20; 2 Abakorinto 13:12, ndetse na 1 Abatesalonike 5:26. Dukurikije iki cyerekezo, dushobora gukuramo ihame rya Bibiliya ryo kuramutsa abizera ufite ubwuzu maze ukabatera kwiyumvamo ko ari bamwe mu bagize umuryango w'Imana. Mu mico imwe n'imwe, gusomana bishobora kugaragara ko bidakwiriye; bityo guhana ikiganza, guhoberana cyangwa gucisha bugufi umutwe ukunama bikaba byarushaho kwemerwa. Nyamara atitaye ku byo kugaragaza umuco, Pawulo adutera umwete wo gutuma abantu biyumvamo ko bemewe kandi bakunzwe igihe bari mu materaniro y'abizera.
