---
title:  Gusobanura Bibiliya
date:   19/11/2026
---



Kwiga Bibiliya kwacu gukwiriye kwibanda ku ntego ebyiri: gukura mu bya Mwuka Lımuntu ku giti cye no kuyobora abandi ku gushyikirana na Yesu gukiza. Kristo yasobanuye umukristo nyakuri nk'umuntu Mwuka Wera ayobora mu "kuri kose" (Yohana 16:13), umuntu ubeshwaho n' "ijambo ryose riva mu kanwa k'Imana" (Matayo 4:4), kandi akigisha abandi "ibyo" yadutegetse byose (Matayo 28:20).

`Soma Luka 4:16-30 na Luka 24:25-27, 44-49. Ni iki dushobora kwigira mu buryo Kristo yakoresheje hano asobanura Ibyanditswe?`

Yesu yahoze ari "inzira, ukuri n'ubugingo" (Yohana 14:6) n'uyu munsi ni ko ari, kandi ni ko azahora. Nubwo bimeze bityo, inyigisho ze zabaga zishinze imizi mu Byanditswe. Nk'urugero: muri Mariko 1:14, 15 haravuga hati: "Nuko bamaze kubohesha Yohana, Yesu ajya i Galilaya, avuga ubutumwa bwiza bw'Imana, ati 'Igihe kirasohoye, ubwami bw'Imana buri hafi'" Igihe cyarenguriraga rwose ku magambo yanditswe muri Daniyeli 9:24-27. Mu kibwirizwa cyo ku musozi (Matayo 5-7), akoresha amagambo yanditswe mu Kuva 20:13, 14 (Matayo 5:21, 27). Muri Luka 4, akoresha amagambo aboneka muri Yesaya 61:1, 2. Yesu yari umubwiriza wigisha Bibiliya atagize icyo yirengagiza.

Mu rundi rugero, Mu Byakozwe n'Intumwa 8 hatubwira ko Umunyetiyopiya w'inkone "yari yaraje i Yerusalemu gusenga" (Ibyakozwe n'Intumwa 8:27, bisobanuye ko yari asanzwe azi idini y'Abayuda, ndetse nibura akaba yari azi imwe mu minsi mikuru yabo. Ndetse yari afite kopi y'igitabo cya Yesaya yari ashishikariye gusoma igihe yari mu nzira asubira iwe. Nyamara ntiyashoboraga gusobanukirwa ubuhanuzi bw'umugaragu wishyizeho ibyaha, bityo akenera umuntu wo kubimusobanurira.

Ibyo bintu byabaye hari amasomo bitwigisha. Irya mbere: gusoma gusa imirongo runaka yo mu Byanditswe Byera ntabwo ari ko buri gihe bitugeza ku gusobanukirwa iyo mirongo. Irya kabiri: igihe twiga Ibyanditswe Byera buri wese ashobora, kandi akwiriye kugira icyo yigira kuri mugenzi we. Irya gatatu: buri wese afite inshingano yo kumenyesha abandi ubwutumwa bwiza bwo muri Bibiliya.

Kuba Filipo yaramenyekanishije ibyanditswe mu gitabo cya Yesaya ndetse n'indi mirongo yo mu Isezerano rya Kera byagejeje inkone ku mubatizo (Ibyakozwe n'Intumwa 8:35-39; gereranya na Zaburi 51:12). Igihe tumenyesha abandi Ibyanditswe dukwiriye kuba dufite intego isa n'iyi (reba Yohana 17:17).

`Nubwo rimwe na rimwe dushobora gukenera ubufasha ngo dusobanukirwe imirongo runaka, ni ukubera iki buri gihe tugomba kwemezwa mu mitima yacu bwite ibyo twizera?`
