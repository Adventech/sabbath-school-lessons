---
title:  Kwihisha Imana
date:   28/09/2026
---

Kubera ko Imana ari urukundo (1 Yohana 4:8), ntiyarema abantu bameze nk'imashini "robots." Urukundo rusaba guhitamo. Kugira ngo Adamu na Eva bashobore kugira urukundo, bagombaga kuremwa bafite umudendezo wo kwihitiramo; bitabaye ibyo, urukundo, urukundo nyakuri, ntirwashoboraga kuboneka. Bityo rero, Adamu na Eva bari bafite umudendezo wo guhitamo kumvira Umuremyi wabo cyangwa kutamwumvira. Kuba ubwo burenganzira bwo kwihitiramo bwari ikintu nyakuri, byagaragajwe n'itegeko ryo kutarya ku giti cy'ubwenge bumenyesha icyiza n'ikibi (reba Itangiriro 2:15-17). Kuki byabaye ngombwa kubaha umuburo ubabuza kurya kuri icyo giti, niba koko batari bafite uburenganzira bwo kukirya mbere na mbere?

« Ababyeyi bacu ba mbere n'ubwo baremwe ari intungane kandi ari abaziranenge, ntibari ku rugero rudashobora gukora ikibi. Imana yabaremye bafite umudendezo, bashobora kugenzura ubuhanga n'ubugwaneza by'imico mbonera y'Imana n'ubutabera bw'ibyo ibasaba, kandi bafite ubwigenge busesuye bwo kwihitiramo kumvira cyangwa kutumvira. Bagombaga kwishimira gushyikirana n'Imana hamwe n'abamarayika batunganye ... Hatabayeho umudendezo wo kwihitiramo, kumvira kwe kwajyaga kuba ari ukw'agahato aho kuba uk'ubushake. " - Ellen G. White, _Abakurambere n'Abahanuzi_, p. 31 (2016).

`Kuki Adamu na Eva bagerageje kwihisha Umuremyi wabo nyuma y'uko baretse kumwumvira ? Soma mu Itangiriro 3:1-8 (hagereranye no muri Yeremiya 23:24 n' Abaheburayo 4:13).`

Igice cya 3 cy'Itangiriro cyerekana ipica iteye ubwoba - gucumura no kugwa mu cyaha kwa Adamu na Eva. Nyuma y'ikiganiro kidasanzwe cyabaye hagati y'inzoka (Satani) na Eva, ababyeyi bacu ba mbere bageragereshejwe kurya ku mbuto z' «igiti cy'ubwenge bumenyesha icyiza n'ikibi," ibyo bakaba barabibujijwe kubikora. Satani yakoresheje imbaraga z'amagambo, ibyiyumvo n'ibishuko bishimishije, kugira ngo yemeze Eva, na we aherako yemeza Adamu, kurenga ku itegeko ry'Imana. Eva yabonye ko icyo giti cyari «gifite ibyokurya byiza, «ko ari icy'igikundiro," kandi ko cyari «icyo kwifuriza kumenyesha umuntu ubwenge." (Itangiriro 3:6). Isezerano Satani yabasezeranyije ko bari kuzaba "nk'Imana, bakamenya icyiza n'ikibi" ryasaga nk'aho bataryikura (Itangiriro 3:5). Mu by'ukuri, amagambo ya Satani yari yuzuyemo ibinyoma. Satani yabahishe ingaruka zo kutumvira kwabo. Bamaze gusuzugura Imana, bahise "bahumuka," babona ko "bari bambaye ubusa." "Imyambaro" y'Imana y'umucyo n'icyubahiro (Zaburi 104:1, 2), yagaragazaga "ishusho" y'Imana, yari yagiye. Ntibari bagitwikiriwe n'ibyo Imana yari yarabateganyirije. Ntibitangaje rero kuba Adamu na Eva barihishe Umuremyi wabo (nk'aho mu by'ukuri bishoboka kwihisha Imana). Bagerageje kwihisha kubera ko icyaha gituma twumva twicira urubanza, kandi ikidodo cy'icyaha cyacu, tuba dushaka guhunga ubwiza bw'Imana.

`Ni ibihe bintu wowe ubwawe wigeze uhura na byo, byatuma wumva ufite ikimwaro (isoni) kandi ukagerageza kwihisha Imana nyuma yo gukora icyaha?`
