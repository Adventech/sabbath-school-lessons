---
title:  Imana Ishakisha Umuntu
date:   29/09/2026
---

Mu Itangiriro 3:9, 10 tubona ko Imana yafashe iya mbere mu gushakisha abana bayo bacumuye. Ikibazo ngo, "Uri he?" cyari ikibazo gisobanutse, kuko Imana yari izi ibyo Adamu na Eva bakoze n'aho bari bari. Icyakora, icyaha cyatumye umushyikirano usanzwe wari hagati y'Imana n'umugabo n'umugore ba mbere uhagarara. Icyo Imana yari jhangayikishijwe na cyo mu buryo bwihutirwa kwari ugusubizaho umushyikirano yari ifitanye n'abana bayo, guhura na bo no kubaha ibyiringiro mu bwihebe bari bafite. Mu by'ukuri, inshinga y'Igiheburayo qara', "guhamagara," mu Itangiriro 3:9, ikoreshwa kenshi mu gusobanura uguhamagara kw'Imana kugira ngo ishyireho uburyo bwo gushyikirana n'ubwoko bwayo.

`Soma muri Luka 15:11-32. Uyu mugani ugaragaza ute ibyabereye mu Itangiriro 3:9, 10?`

Kuba Imana yarashakishije Adamu na Eva, bigaragaza urukundo rutagira ikigombero ikunda abana bayo. Imana ntiyigera na rimwe idutererana (soma muri Luka 15). Ikora ibishoboka byose kugira ngo yongere kugirana natwe umushyikirano, ndetse n'iyo twaba twihishe. Mbere y'uko Yesu abambwa ku musaraba, ubwo Petero yihakanaga Yesu incuro eshatu, Kristo 'yarahindukiye aramureba' (Luka 22:61). Ndetse n'ubwo atavuze ijambo, "yavuganye" na Petero kandi agerageza kwegera incuti ye yari yaguye mu ikosa. Zirikana ukuntu mu Itangiriro 3:11-13, icyaha cyahinduye mu buryo butunguranye umushyikirano Adamu na Eva bari bafitanye. Aho kugira ngo bicuze ibyaha byabo, batangiye kubiryoza abandi. Adamu yashinje Eva, kandi mu buryo butaziguye, yashinje Imana, kubera ko ari Yo yamuhaye Eva. Yaravuze ati: "Umugore wampaye ngo tubane, ni we wampaye ku mbuto z'icyo giti, ndazirya," (Itangiriro 3:12). Eva we yaherereje ukutumvira kwe ku nzoka, atanga impamvu yamubeshye agira ati: "Inzoka ni yo yanshutse maze ndazirya" (Itangiriro 3:13). Nta n'umwe muri bo wemeye icyaha cye, nibura mu gihe yabaga ahanganye na cyo ngo yemere igicumuro cye bwite. Mbega ukuntu ibintu byagiye bihinduka ni ruto ni ruto mu myaka ibihumbi bitandatu. Ukuri ni uko icyaha cyageze ku bantu bose uko bangana. Intumwa Pawulo avuga iby'imiterere yacu neza mu rwandiko yandikiye Abaroma 7:15-24, ubwo yasobanuraga akaga ke ko kwifuza gukora icyiza, maze akisanga ubwe yakoze ikinyuranye na cyo, agira ati: "Kuko ibyo nshaka, atari byo nkora; ahubwo ibyo nanga, akaba ari byo nkora" (Abaroma 7:15).

Ni nde wabihunga? Ibyo Pawulo yavuze bifite ubusobanuro bwimbitse. Twese turi abantu bononekaye kandi dukomeza gukora ibintu tuzi neza ko tutagombye gukora. Ni nde utigeze ahura n'icyo kibazo?


`Pawulo yavuze ko ibyiringiro byacu rukumbi ari ugucungurwa "kubwa Yesu Kristo Umwami wacu" (Abaroma 7:25). Ni yo mpamvu Imana ikomeza kudushaka kugira ngo itugeze kuri Yesu, we wenyine ushobora kutubumba mu ishusho yayo kandi akadukiza ibyago byatewe n'icyaha.`
