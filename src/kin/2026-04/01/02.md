---
title:  Imana Ivugana n'Abantu muri Edeni
date:   27/09/2026
---

Mu Itangiriro igice cya 1 n'icya 2 tubwirwamo inkuru y'Irema. Imana yaremye isi nziza cyane, maze iyishyiramo Adamu na Eva, ari na bo kamba ryo kurema kwayo. Abantu bari batandukanye n'ibindi biremwa byose byo ku isi, kuko baremwe "mu ishusho y'Imana" (Itangiriro 1:27), iki kintu kikaba cyaravuzwe kuri bo gusa, aho kuba, urugero, ku nyoni cyangwa ku zindi nyamaswa. Kubera iyo mpamvu, bagombaga kugira imibereho yuzuye, bakagaragarizwa urukundo mu buryo bwuzuye, kandi bakagendana n'Imana ubwayo bashyize hamwe.

Nanone kandi, mu Itangiriro 2:18-23 dusoma ko Imana yaremeye Adamu umugore, ari we Eva, kubera ko Imana yavuze iti: "si byiza ko umuntu aba wenyine" (Itangiriro 2:18). Ku bw'ibyo rero, kuva mu ntangiriro abantu baremwe ari ibiremwa bigirana imishyikirano n'abandi. Muri iki gihe, abahanga mu by'imibanire y'abantu bigisha ko kugirana umushyikirano n'abandi bigira akamaro kenshi ku buzima bwacu bwo mu buryo bw'umubiri no mu bwenge, mu gihe ubwigunge bwo butuma twicwa n'irungu.

Ntibitangaje rero kuba igihe Eva yari amaze kuremwa, Adamu yamubonye akavuga aya magambo atangaye ati: "Uyu ni igufwa ryo mu magufwa yanjye, ni akara ko mu mara yanjye: Azitwa Umugore, kuko yakuwe mu Mugabo" (Itangiriro 2:23). Urugo rwubatswe n'umugabo n'umugore bashyingiranywe, ni rwo rwego rwa mbere rw'imibanire Imana yashyiriyeho abantu, kugira ngo bagirane umushyikirano n'ubumwe byuzuye. Ntibitangaje rero kuba imwe mu ntego z'ibanze za Satani buri gihe ari iyo gusenya imishyikirano y'ibanze y'umuryango washyizweho katanga ka mbere.

`Soma mu Itangiriro 1:27, 28; Itangiriro 2:15-17; n'Itangiriro 3:9. Ni ubuhe buryo Imana yakoreshaga ivugana n'abantu mu Ngobyi ya Edeni?`

Adamu na Eva bishimiraga kugirana umushyikirano imbone nkubone n'Imana. Nk'ibiremwa bifite ubuhanga, bashoboraga guhura n'Imana maze bagategera amatwi amabwiriza yabahaga. Nk'ibisonga byarindaga ubusitani butagira uko busa, bagendaga bongera ubuhanga bwabo n'ubumenyingiro uko bagendaga bita ku bimera n'inyamaswa. Buri munsi kandi, bagiranaga ibihe bidasanzwe n'Imana "mu mafu ya nimunsi" (Itangiriro 3:8). By'umwihariko, umunsi w'Isabato, ugize umugabane w'ingenzi w'umurimo w'Irema ry'Imana, wagombaga kuba igihe kidasanzwe cyihariye cyo gukomeza umubano hagati y'Imana n'abana bayo (Itangiriro 2:2, 3; Kuva 20:8-11). Kur'uwo munsi, ababyeyi bacu ba mbere bishimiye kubona urukundo Imana ibakunda n'ukuntu ibitaho mu buryo bwihariye.

`Muri iki gihe, nyuma y'imyaka ibarirwa mu bihumbi Adamu na Eva bavuye muri Edeni, biratugora kwiyumvisha uko ubuzima bwari bumeze mbere y'uko bakora icyaha. Nyamara kandi, no muri iki gihe, ni buryo ki ibiremwa biri ku isi bigaragaza ibyaremwe byashyizweho n'Imana katanga ka mbere?`
