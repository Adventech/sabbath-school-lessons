---
title:  Ellen G. White
date:   02/10/2026
---

Kuvugana n'abantu kw'Imana ntikugarukira .mu bihe Bibiliya yandikiwemo. Nkuko tuzabibona mu byumweru biri imbere, Imana ivugana n'abantu ibihe byose, kandi amagambo yayo azagaragazwa no mur'iki gihe tugezemo kugeza Kristo agarutse.


Ellen Gould Harmon White na Elizabeth w'impanga ye bavutse ku itariki ya 26 Ugushyingo 1827, i Gorham muri Leta ya Maine. Ni bo bari bato mu bana umunani ba Eunice na  Robert Harmon [Yunisi na Roberi Harumoni]. Mu myaka mike, umuryango wimukiye mu  mujyi wa Portland, Maine, aho Robert Harmon yari yiringiye kwagura ubucuruzi bwe bwo  kuboha ingofero. Igihe Ellen yari agize imyaka icyenda, yagize igikomere cyamugizeho  ingaruka mu buryo bw'umubiri, mu byiyumvo no mu mibanire ye n'abandi. Igihe Ellen yari mu busitani bwari hafi y'iwabo, umunyeshuri biganaga yararakaye cyane amutera ibuye  mu maso, rimusiga ameze nk'uwatakaje ubwenge ku kigero runaka mu gihe cy'ibyumweru  byinshi. Iyi mpanuka yatumye ahagarika amashuri ye mu buryo butunguranye, imusigira  n'ibibazo by'uburwayi ubuzima bwe bwose. Nanone kandi, kuba Ellen yari yarakomeretse  mu maso byatumye agira ipfunwe ku birebana n'isura ye, bigabanya ubwisanzure mu  mibanire ye n'abandi.

Ni muri icyo cyerekezo cyo kutamenya iby'ahazaza h'ubuzima bwe, Ellen Harmon wari ukiri muto yatangiye gutekereza ku ahazaza he mu by'umwuka. Yatinyaga Imana kandi yumvaga ko adatunganiye kujya mu ijuru. Ibyo byiyumvo by'ubwoba byarushijeho gukomera igihe umuryango wa Harmon wemeraga inyigisho z'Abamilerite ivuga ko Yesu agiye kugaruka bidatinze. Ndetse na nyuma y'aho Ellen abatijwe yibijwe mu mazi menshi mu Itorero ry'Abametodisiti mu mwaka wa 1842, yumvaga adakwiriye gukundwa n'Imana, Yizeraga ko yari "yarazimiye iteka ryose" (_Life Sketches of Ellen G. White_, p. 29; soma na pp. 20-31).

Muri ibyo bitekerezo bibabaje, Ellen wari ukiri muto yarose inzozi inshuro ebyiri zatumye atangira guhindura imitekerereze ye y'ukuntu yabonaga Imana. Ariko kandi, igihe yahuraga na Levi Stockman, umuvugabutumwa w'Umumetodiste, ni bwo yasobanukiwe urukundo rw'Imana n'ubuntu bwayo kandi abyiyumvamo. Ni nk'aho Imana yamuvugishaga igihe yari afite agahinda kenshi. Nyuma y'aho yaranditse ati: "Ubu noneho kwizera kwigaruriye umutima wanjye. Numvise nkunze Imana mu buryo budasubirwaho, kandi Mwuka wayo yampamirije ko ibyaha byanjye byababariwe. Uko nabonaga Data byarahindutse. Icyo gihe noneho natangiye kubona ko ari umubyeyi urangwa n'ineza n'impuhwe, aho kuba umunyagitugu uhatira abantu kumwumvira buhumyi. Umutima wanjye wamwirundumuriyemo ndamukunda cyane." - _Life Sketches of Ellen G. White_, p.39. Muri icyo gihe, Ellen White kandi yasobanukiwe inyigisho ivuga "uko abapfuye bamera kandi buhoro buhoro yaje gusobanukirwa ko nta muriro w'iteka ubaho. Ibyo yari amaze gusobanukirwa ku byerekeye Imana n'urukundo rwayo, byahinduye mu buryo bwuzuye imyifatire ye. Nk'uko tuzajya tubyiga buri wa Gatandatu mu byumweru bizakurikiraho, Ellen White yakunze Yesu cyane, maze aramukorera ubuzima bwe bwose.
