---
title:  Guhoza Yesu n'Ijambo rye ku Isonga
date:   23/10/2026
---

Nk'uko twabyize, Imana yahamagaraga abahanuzi mu gihe runaka ibahamagariye gukora inshingano ikomeye. Inshingano yabo y'ibanze yari iyo gutuma abantu bamenya Imana no kugirana umushyikirano ushyitse nayo. Icyo nicyo Ellen G. White nawe abayoboraho Umurimo we wari ufite inshingano ebyiri z'ingenzi: kuyobora abantu kuri Yesu no kubereka Ijambo Rye, ariryo Bibiliya.

Ellen G. White yari afitiye Yesu urukundo ruhoraho, akagira umuhati n'ubushake bwo kugeza urukundo rw'Imana ku bandi. Ibitabo bye bitanu bikurikirana byakunzwe cyane, byitwa 'Intambara y'Ibihe Byose,' bitangirana kandi bigasozanya n'aya magambo agira ati, "Imana ni urukundo." Mu gitabo cye cyitwa: Kugana Yesu, kikaba ahari ari nacyo gitabo cye cyamamaye cyane, yanditsemo aya magambo: "Igituma dukurira mu buntu, ni ukubana na Yesu iminsi yose. Ibyishimo byacu, umumaro wacu - byose bishingira ku mushyikirano tugirana na Kristo. Gusabana na We buri munsi, buri saha no komatana na We, nibyo bituma dukurira mu buntu bwe. Ntabwo ariwe banze ryo kwizera kwacu gusa, ahubwo ni na We ugusohoza." - _Kugana Yesu_, p. 70 [Steps to Christ p. 69].

Mu mwaka wa 1890, igihe yandikaga igitabo cy' _Uwifuzwa Ibihe Byose_, mu ikaye ye aho yabanzaga kwandikamo yandikisha ikaramu, yanditsemo aya magambo: "Igihe nandikaga ku mibereho ya Kristo nashenjaguwe nabyo. Nibagiwe guhumeka nk'uko bigomba. Ntabwo nabashije kwihanganira kwiyumvisha ibyo nandikaga igihe natekerezaga uburyo Kristo yababariye muri iyi isi yacu." -_Manuscript_ 70, 1897. Ellen G. White yakundaga kuvuga ibya Yesu. Bibiliya ni igitabo cya kabiri Ellen G. White yibanzeho mu murimo w'ivugabutumwa binyuze mu bwanditsi. Yabonye inyandiko "nk'umucyo muto" uyobora abantu ku "mucyo munini" - ariwo Bibiliya (_Selected Messages_, Book 3, p. 29, -_Ubutumwa Bwatoranyijwe_ 3, p. 29). Yahoraga ashishikariza abantu kujya mu Byanditswe Byera, aho Imana yahishuriye imico yayo. Yanditse aya magambo agira ati: "Ncuti musomyi, nkuragije Ijambo ry'Imana ngo abe ariryo rigenga ukwizera kwawe n'ibyo ukora." - _Selected Messages_, book 3, p. 30 (_Ubutumwa Bwatoranijwe_ p. 28).

Imyaka yakurikiyeho yanditse aya magambo: "Uwiteka Imana yifuza ko wiga Bibiliya. Nta wundi mucyo w'inyongera yigeze atanga ngo usimbure Ijambo Rye .... Igihe ririwe rikunzwe [Ijambo Rye] rihinduka amaraso atunga ubugingo. Igikurikiraho, ni imirimo myiza izagaragara nk'umucyo uvira mu mwijima."-_Letter_ 130, 1901.

Ellen G. White na none yizera ko abavuga ko ubuhanuzi bwa Kristo bugomba kwigisha "bagomba kwigisha ibyo Kristo yabigishije. Si ibyo yari yarivugiye we gusa, ahubwo n'ibyo yanyujije mu bahanuzi n'abigisha bo mu Isezerano rya Kera. Inyigisho z'abantu zikwiriye gushyirwa ku ruhande." _Uwifuzwa Ibihe Byose_, p. 649. Ellen G. White yakomeje gushyira Yesu n'Ibyanditswe byera ku ruhembe rw'imbere rw'imibereho ye ya Gikristo. Ku wa Gatandatu utaha, tuzareba icyo yavuze ku ihishurwa no guhumekerwa n'uburyo abibona.
