---
title:  Abahanuzi bo mu Isezerano Rishya
date:   17/10/2026
---

### Aho icyigisho cy'iki cyumweru gishingiye:
Matayo 16:13-16; Abefeso 2:20; Ibyahishuwe 22:6; Luka 2:36-38; Ibyakozwe n'Intumwa 21:9; Abefeso 6:10-18.

> <p>Icyo kwibukwa:</p>
> "Icyakora, hariho impano z'uburyo bwinshi, ariko Umwuka ni umwe. Kandi hariho uburyo bwinshi bwo kugabura iby'Imana, ariko Umwami ni umwe. Hariho n'uburyo bwinshi bwo gukora, ariko Imana ikorera byose muri bose ni imwe" (1 Abakorinto 12:4-6).

Mu gihe umwana na se bari mu modoka bagenda, uwo mwana w'umuhungu yagendaga afotora buri modoka yose ibanyuzeho kugira ngo aze gutoranya mo imodoka isa n'iya se. Se yamweretse imodoka imwe yari ibanyuzeho atangaye cyane maze aramubwira ati: "Muhungu wanjye, iriya modoka irasa neza neza n'imodoka yanjye ya mbere!" Bageze mu rugo, se yeretse agatabo k'amabwiriza y'imikoreshereze y'imodoka ye ya mbere, abigereranya n'ibiri mu gatabo k'iyo afite. Yagiye asobanurira uwo muhungu we ibigize izo modoka zombi, ibimeze kimwe, n'ibitandukanye, n'ibyo bagiye bahindura. Nyamara, igihe se w'uwo mwana yagereranyaga moteri zombi z'izo modoka, yasanze ari mahwi, ntacyo bigeze bahinduraho na gito. Yabwiye umuhungu we ko hari byinshi bahinduye kuri iyo modoka ya mbere, keretse gusa moteri, ko ariyo itarahindutse.

Imana yagiye ivugira mu bahanuzi baba abo mu Isezerano rya Kera n'abo mu Isezerano Rishya, ariko ubutumwa bwakomeje kuba bumwe kubera ko bose bahumekerwaga na Mwuka Wera akanabayobora, cyangwa tukaba twavuga ko ari we "moteri' wabwo. Buri gihe Mwuka Wera ni we wakoreshaga abahanuzi b'ukuri binyuze mu mpano ya gihanuzi. Ni na we ushyira Kristo hagati muri ubwo butumwa akaba izingiro ryabwo, nk'uko mu Abaheburayo 1:1-3 habivuga.

Muri iki cyumweru tuzasuzuma uburyo impano y'ubuhanuzi yakoreshejwe mu Isezerano Rishya, tureba ibyo abahanuzi bakoze, abakoraga nk'abahanuzi, uburyo abahanuzi bagereranwa n'intumwa, n'akaga gaterwa n'abahanuzi b'ibinyoma.
