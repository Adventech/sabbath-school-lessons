---
title:  Inshingano z'Abahanuzi bo mu Isezerano Rishya
date:   20/10/2026
---

`Ibyanditswe mu 1 Abakorinto 14:3, Abefeso 4:11-13, n'Ibyahishuwe 22:6 bitubwira iki ku ruhare rw'abahanuzi bo mu Isezerano Rishya?`

Avo masomo tubonye haruguru, nubwo ari ingezi bwose, ntabwo atanga uburyo bwumvikana bw'ibyo abahanuzi bo mu Izerano Rishya bakoze byose. Nyamara kandi, ibyinshi mubyo bakoze bigaragaza ibyo abahanuzi bo mu Isezerano rya Kera bari barakoze, ibyo bikaba bitanga ugukomeza kw'impano y'ubuhanuzi. Mu rwa 1 Abakorinto 12:10, ubuhanuzi buvugwa mu rutonde rw'izindi mpano za Mwuka. Mu mvugoshusho y'umubiri wa Kristo (1 Abakorinto 12:27, 28), abahanuzi bavugwa nyuma y'intumwa, nabo bakaba barahawe ihishurwa rivuye ku Mana. Dukurikije ibivugwa mu rwa 1 Abakorinto 14:3, haravuga hati: "Ariko uhanura we, abwira abantu ibyo kubungura n'ibyo kubahugura, n'ibyo kubahumuriza."

Mu Abefeso 4:11-13 tuhabona urundi rutonde rw'impano za Mwuka, nk'uko tuzisanga mu 1 Abakorinto 12. Abahanuzi bavugwa hamwe n'intumwa, ababwirizabutumwa bwiza, abagabura b'Ijambo ry'Imana, n'abigisha (Abefeso 4:11). Abo bantu bariho "kugira ngo abera batunganirizwe rwose gukora umurimo wo kugabura iby'Imana no gukomeza umubiri wa Kristo; kugeza ubwo twese tuzashobora kugira ubumwe bwo kwizera no kumenya Umwana w'Imana, kandi kugeza n'ubwo tuzashobora kuba abantu bashyitse, bageze ku rugero rushyitse rw'igihagararo cya Kristo" (Abefeso 4:12, 13). Nk'uko tubibona hano n'ahandi hose, Yesu ni We shingiro n'icyerekezo cy'ubutumwa bwabo.

Igitabo cy'Ibyakozwe n'Intumwa kivuga iby'abahanuzi babiri: Yuda na Silasi (nta n'umwe muri bo wigeze agira igitabo yandika cyo muri Bibiliya), ariko "_bahugūje_ bene Data amagambo menshi kandi bakabakomeza" (Ibyakozwe n'Intumwa 15:32). Nubwo yego rimwe na rimwe abahanuzi baba bagomba no gutanga imiburo, ndetse rimwe na rimwe no gutanga kwihanangiriza gukomeye, hari n'ibyo bakora bashingiye ku bihe abo babwira barimo, bakavuga amagambo abakomeza, cyane cyane mu bihe by'ibigeragezo, nta gushidikanya bene ayo magambo ashimwa n'abakeneye kuyumva, bakanyurwa n'ibyiringiro n'isezerano biyarimo.Umurimo wo gukomeza wanavuzwe mu cyerekezo cyo guhanura (1 Abakorinto 14:31). Mu bihe by'umubabaro n'ibigeragezo, impano y'ubuhanuzi yagiye ikoreshwa mu buryo bwo gukomeza abantu no kubagarurira ibyiringiro.

Mu gitabo cy' Ibyahishuwe 22:6, Yesu avugwa "nk'Umwami Imana utegeka imyuka y'abahanuzi," kandi amagambo ye akaba ari "ayo kwizerwa n'ay'ukuri". Birumvikana ko, buri kintu cyose kivuzwe n'abagaragu b'Imana, igihe bavugira Imana, kiba ari icyo "kwizerwa n'icy'ukuri." Mu isi aho abo dushobora kwiringira ari bake, tugomba kwizera ibyo abahanuzi b'Umwami Imana bavuga, cyane cyane iyo biri mu Ijambo ry'Imana, ariryo rifite ububasha ntagereranwa.
