---
title:  Inzira yo Guhumekerwa
date:   26/10/2026
---

Ibitabo byinshi n'inyandiko, bitari Bibiliya gusa, byageze kuri twe uko imyaka yagiye ihita indi igataha. Nyamara ntibitubuza gukomeza gufata Bibiliya nk'Ijambo ry'Imana. Ni iki kiyitandukanya n'izindi nyandiko?

`Soma 2 Timoteyo 3:15-17 na Yakobo 1:17. Ni ubuhe butumwa bw'ingenzi tugomba gukura muri iyo mirongo?`

Hari impaka zigeze kuba zerekeye uburyo bwiza bwasobanurwamo amagambo yo 2 Timoteyo 3:16, ariko abasobanuzi hafi ya bose ba Bibiliya bafata uwo murongo mu buryo bumwe, bashimangira ko Bibiliya yose uko yakabaye yavuye ku Mana (2 Petero 1:20, 21). Umwe mu basobanuzi ba Bibiliya asobanura ko "bisa cyane nk'aho Pawulo atekereza ibyanditswe byose uko byakabaye, akaba avuga ko ibyanditswe bigira umumaro wo 'kwigisha, kwemeza umuntu ibyaha, kumukosora, no kumumenyesha gutunganira Imana,' kurenza ko buri murongo wose w'ibyanditswe waba ufite akamaro mur'ubwo buryo."-G. W. Knight III, _The Pastoral Epistles: A Commentary on the Greek Text_ (_Grand Rapids_, MI: Eerdmans, 1992), p.445.

Ariko se ni mu buhe buryo dushobora gusobanukirwa uburyo bwo guhumekerwa bwakoreshejwe Bibiliya ikaboneka? Tugomba kumenya ko ubuhanuzi bwahumetswe bwayobowe na Mwuka Wera wafashije abahanuzi b'ukuri kugira ngo bashobore gutanga ubutumwa buvuye ku Mana. Ubwo bufasha bwa Mwuka Wera ntibutangwa mu gihe cyo kwakira ubutumwa gusa ahubwo bunatangwa no mugufasha uwahawe ubwo butumwa kubugeza kubo bugenewe.

Ubutumwa bw'Imana bwanyuzwaga mu mayerekwa no mu nzozi (Kubara 12:6; Yesaya 6:1,8). Imana yanakoreshaga abahamya biboneye n'amaso yabo ibintu biba (1 Yohana 1:1- 3), cyangwa binyuze mu gushakashaka mu mateka ibyahumetswe na mwuka (Luka 1:1-3), n'iby'Imana yazanaga mu ntekerezo z'abantu bayobowe na Mwuka Wera (2 Petero 1:21). Uko uburyo bwose bwaba bwarakoreshejwe n'Umwuka Wera bwaba bumeze kose, abahanuzi n'abanditsi ba Bibiliya bahawe ubutumwa bw'ukuri bufite intego, aho kuba ubw'umuntu ku giti cye, bagombaga gutanga. Ni ukuvuga ko ubutumwa bw'Imana bwaziraga kugira ikindi kibuvagwamo cyangwa guhindurwa n'ababuhawe, nubwo bakoreshaga imvugo n'amagambo byabo kugira ngo bumvikanishe ubwo butumwa.

Umurimo wo kubwira ubutumwa bwa gihanuzi abo bwabaga bugenewe ku ikubitiro washoboraga gukorwa mu magambo (2 Petero 1:19-21), cyangwa mu nyandiko (Yohana 20:30, 31), cyangwa mu buryo bw'ibigereranyo (Ezeliyeli 4:1-8).

`Nubwo hakibazwa ibibazo byinshi ku buryo Bibiliya yahumetswe, cyangwa uburyo ihumekwa rikorwa muri rusange, ni ukubera iki ari ingenzi kwiringira ko Bibiliya ari Ijambo ry'Imana ryahumetswe, nubwo hari ibyo dushobora kutumva neza ku buryo yanditswe?`
