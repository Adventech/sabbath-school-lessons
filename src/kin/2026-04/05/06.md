---
title:  Ikinankuru, Ibimenyetso, Imigani
date:   29/10/2026
---

Bibiliya ikungahaye ku magambo atandukanye akoreshwa mu buryo bw'amashusho n'igereranyo. NK urugero, muri gahunda y'ibitambo yo mu Isezerano rya Kera yose abakurambere bakoresnaga mu gihe cyo gutamba ibitambo, ihema ry'ibonaniro ryubatswe na Mose, n'urusengero rw'i Yerusalemu ahakoreshwaga amashusho mu gusobanura kwigitambo cya Kristo ku musaraba wari kuzaba impongano y'ibyaha byacu, n'umurimo we w'ubutambyi mu buturo bwo mu ijuru (Abaheburayo 8:1-6).

Mu buhanuzi bwa Daniyeli, ubwami n'ubutegetsi byo mu isi byagereranyijwe n'imigabane itandukanye y'igishushanyo kinini (Daniyeli 2:31-45), inyamaswa zitandukanye n'amahembe (Daniyeli 7 n'igice cya 8), n'abami babiri (Daniyeli 11). Ibishushanyo bimeze nk'ibyo kandi hubisanga no mu gitabo cy'Ibyahishuwe, mu buryo bwo gusobanura inyamaswa ebyiri zivugwa mu gice cya 13.

Mu nyigisho ze, Yesu yivuzeho, nk'urugero yiyita: umutsima w'ubugingo (Yohana 6:35, 41-58), Umucyo w'isi (Yohana 8:12, Yohana 9:5), n'Umuzabibu w'Ukuri (Yohana 15:1-8). Ahandi ni mu mugani w'umubibyi (Matayo 13:1-9, 18-23) n'izindi ngero nyinshi (Matayo 13:24-52, n'ahandi). Yesu yakoreshaga imvugoshusho avuga iby'imiterere y'ubwami bwe no kwaguka kwabwo.

`Soma muri Ezeliyeli 4:1-8. Ni ukubera iki utekereza ko Imana yavuze mu magambo ubutumwa bwayo ariko ikanatanga bumwe mu butumwa bwayo ikoresheje ikinankuru?`

Ikinankuru igaragaza ibyo tuyisanga muri Ezekiyeli 4:1-8, aho Imana yasabye umuhanuzi gukora ishusho nto ya Yerusalemu, ikanamusaba gukoresha uburyo bwo kuryama, bwa mbere akaryamisha urubavu rw'ibumoso iminsi 390, bishushanya inzu ya Isiraheli, maze kandi agahindukira akaryamisha urubavu rw'iburyo iminsi 40, bishushanya inzu ya Yuda. Irindi yerekwa yabonye ni irya Hoseya wategetswe n'Imana gushaka Gomeri, umumaraya, byashushanyaga uguhenebera mu bya mwuka kwa Isiraheli itarabaye indahemuka ku Mana (Hoseya 1:2-9).

Ibyabaye kuri Ezekiyeli na Hoseya bisa n'ibiteye ubwoba mu gihe nk'ikingiki. Ariko ni uko ibintu byari bimeze: byagomba kuba biteye ubwoba, bitari kuri twe ahubwo no ku bantu bariho muri icyo gihe cya kera. Rimwe na rimwe igihe imiburo y'abahanuzi ititabwagaho igasuzugurwa ku mugaragaro (reba 2 Ngoma 36:15-21), igikorwa cya nyuma cy'imbabazi z'Imana cyabaga icyo kwereka abantu ugushayisha kwabo kugira ngo babe bakwisubiraho bakihana by'ukuri. Ishusho nk'iyo yagombaga kuba yabereka ko bari gukina mu bikomeye, ko bakagombye kuba baritaye ku muburo bahawe n'Imana.

`Ni ubuhe bwoko bw'ikinankuru umuntu ashobora gutanga muri iki gihe natwe atuburira, atubwira, twebwe abo mu itorero rya Lawodokiya? (Soma mu lbyahishuwe 3:14-18).`
