---
title:  Ihishurwa Rusange n'Ihishurwa Ryihariye
date:   25/10/2026
---

`Soma mu Abaheburayo 1:1,2. Kuki Imana ishishikazwa no kuvugana natwe? Ni iki Itubwira binyuze muri Yesu no mu bahanuzi?`

Ni iki Umuremyi w'isi n'ijuru yaba ashaka kutubwira? Ni ukubera iki avugana natwe? Ni ukubera ko turi ibiremwa muntu byacumuye, tukaba turi mu isi y'icyaha kandi yugarijwe n'urupfu, umubabaro, n'ibigeragezo, bityo rero, Imana ikaba ishaka kugira icyo itubwira kuko Ishaka ko tumenya impamvu turi hano ku isi, impamvu ibintu ari bibi, kandi n'ubwo bimeze bityo, dushobora kugira ibyiringiro kubera urukundo Idukunda.

Ihishurwa rusange ry'Imana, nk'uko turibonera mu byaremwe, rivuga riranguruye iby'ubushobozi ntagereranwa bwayo n'urukundo rwita kubyo yaremye. Umwami Dawidi yaravuze ati: "Ijuru rivuga icyubahiro cy'Imana, isanzure ryerekana imirimo y'intoki zayo" (Zaburi 19:1). Intumwa Pawulo yasongeye ayo magambo agira ati: "Kuko ibitaboneka byayo, ni byo bubasha bwayo buhoraho n'ubumana bwayo, bigaragara neza, uhereye ku kuremwa kw'isi, bigaragazwa n'ibyo yaremye, kugira ngo batagira icyo bireguza" (Abaroma 1:20). Biranatangaje gutekereza ku mbaraga y'Imana irema bundi bushya igaragarira mu buryo bwo gukiza butangaje tubona mu isi yacu. Mu kuri, "Ububasha bw'Imana bukiza buboneka ahantu hose mu byaremwe. Iyo igiti gitemwe, iyo hagize umuntu ukomereka cyangwa akavunika, uwo mwanya ibyaremwe bitangira kwisuganya kugira ngo byomore uruguma. N'iyo nta mpuruza irabaho, imbaraga zikiza ziba ziteguye; kandi iyo hagize urugingo rw'umubiri rukomeretse, imbaraga zose zihutira kujya ku murimo wo gukiza urwo rugingo"- Ellen G. White, _Uburezi_ p. 116.

Uko byaba ari byiza kandi bitanga inama nziza nk'uko isi y'ibyaremwe ishobora kuba iri, ntabwo igaragaza ubushobozi bw'Imana n'urukundo rwayo gusa, inagaragaza ko n'ikibi gihari (soma mu Itangiriro 3:14-19, Matayo 13:24-30). Ubu butumwa buteye urujijo bushobora kumvikana neza gusa mu mucyo w'ihishurwa ryihariye ry'Imana - muri Yesu Kristo no mu Ijambo ry'Imana rivuga ku kwigira umuntu kwa Kristo, no muri Bibiliya, Ijambo Ryanditswe ry'Imana kandi rikaba rimuhamya (Yohana 5:39).

Ibyanditse byera ni ubutumwa bw'Imana bwahumetswe, bwandikiwe ibiremwamuntu byose by'ibihe byose. Bitubwira iby'Iremwa ry'isi n'akaga kayigwiriye ikagwa mu cyaha, bikatubwira n'iby'umugambi utangaje w'Imana wo gucungura isi, n'uburyo umubumbe w'Isi uzongera ukagirwa mushya ku iherezo ry'ibihe. N'ubwo inkuru zimwe na zimwe zo muri Bibiliya zishobora kuba ari ngufi kandi zitoranyijwe, buri gihe ziba ari izo kwiringirwa, kubera ko ari Imana iba ivugana natwe (soma 2 Timoteyo. 3:16, 2 Petero 1:19-21).

`Nubwo mu kuri ibyaremwe bitwereka ukuri kw'imbaraga y'Umuremyi n'urukundo rwe, ni ukubera iki tunakeneye cyane n'Ijambo Rye Ryanditswe? Ni iki iryo Jambo ryanditswe ritubwira ibyaremwe ubwabyo bitatubwira?`
