---
title:  Ubutumwa Bwanditswe
date:   28/10/2026
---

Zimwe mu mbwirwaruhame zamamaye zikora ku mitima n'imibereho y'abantu ndetse zigahindura ibyari kuzabageraho mu mateka yabo. Iyo izo mbwirwaruhame zitaza kwandikwa, amagambo yari kuba yaribagiranye cyangwa atakiri ku murongo umwe uko ibihe byagiye bihita. Niyo mpamvu, kwandika ari uburyo bwiza bwo kubika amagambo yavuzwe, bikanafasha ko ubwo butumwa bukwirakwizwa uko ibihe bihaye ibindi. Nyuma gato Abisiraheli bamaze kuva muri Egiputa batsinze Abameleki, Imana yabwiye Mose "kwandika ibyo bintu mu gitabo cy'urwibutso" (Kuva 17:14). Gutangirana na Mose wanditse ibitabo bitanu bibanza bya Bibiliya na Zaburi 90, n'igitabo cya Yobu, Mwuka Wera yayoboye abahanuzi bafite ibitabo banditse muri Bibiliya kwandika inyandiko ubu zigize ibitabo 66 bya Bibiliya.

`Soma mu gitabo cyo Kuva 17:14; Yohana 20:30, 31; 1 Abakorinto 10:11, n' Ibyahishuwe 1:19. Ni izihe mpamvu zatumye Imana isaba abahanuzi bayo kwandika ubutumwa bwabaga buri mu magambo yababwiye?`

Ibitabo bitandukanye byo muri Bibiliya bigaragaza ubuhanga n'ubushobozi abanditsi babyo bari bafite. Nk'uko tuzabiganiraho mu cyigisho tuziga mu cyumweru gitaha, ubumenyi n'imyandikire bya buri mwanditsi ntacyo bihindura ku butumwa bwabo kubera ko nta "rugero" runaka rubaho rwo guhumekerwa. Birumvikana ko imigabane imwe ya Bibiliya isobanuye mu buryo burambuye, ikanaba yoroshye gushyirwa mu bikorwa mu mibereho yacu y'iki gihe kurenza indi. Ariko uko kuri ntikugira umugabane umwe wa Bibiliya kuba warahumekewe kurusha undi.

Rimwe na rimwe abanditsi ba Bibiliya bandikaga ubutumwa bahumekewe nyuma yo kubanza kubuvuga mu magambo ubwabo, cyangwa babuhaye undi mufasha bizeye ngo abubavugire. Mu gitabo cyo Gutegeka kwa Kabiri, Mose yanditse imbwirwaruhame za nyuma yabwiye Abisiraheli mbere y'uko apfa. Ibitabo bigize ubutumwa bwiza bune birimo ibibwirizwa byinshi n'inyigisho bya Yesu yigishije mu gihe cy'umurimo we hano ku isi.

Ariko hari n'ibihe abahanuzi, mu myandikire yabo bifashishaga andi magambo atari ayo mu butumwa bwahumetswe (Ibyakozwe n'Intumwa 17:28, 1 Abakorinto 15:33, Tito 1:12) ndetse hari n'aho bandikaga n'amagambo yavuzwe na Satani (Itangiriro 3:1-5, Matayo 4:1- 11). Dushobora no kwibuka igihe abahanuzi bandikaga ibitekerezo n'ibyifuzo by'abandi, ndetse hari n'ubwo bagaragazaga n'imyitwarire yabo ku kintu runaka nk'abantu (soma muri Daniyeli 8:27, Ibyahishuwe 22:8, 8). Ibirenze ibyo. Bibiliya isobanura ingero nyinshi nziza zikwiriye gukurikizwa ariko n'izindi nyinshi zikwiriye kwirindwa (1 Abakorinto 10:1-11).

Mu gihe dusoma Bibiliya, tugomba kuzirikana ko, nubwo itanditse mu rurimi rutunganye, ikubiyemo ubutumwa butibeshya kandi budahinyuka butazigera buhindurwa n'igihe icyo aricyo cyose.
