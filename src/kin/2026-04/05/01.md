---
title:  Guhishurirwa no Guhumekerwa
date:   24/10/2026
---

### Aho icyigisho cy'iki cyumweru gishingiye:
Abaheburayo 1:1, 2; 2 Timoteyo 3:15-17; 2 Petero 1:18-21; Kuva 17:14; Ibyahishuwe 1:19; Ezekiyeli 4:1-8.

> <p>Icyo kwibukwa:</p>
> "Ibyahishuwe na Yesu Kristo, iby'Imana yamuhereye kugira ngo yereke imbata ze ibikwiriye kuzabaho vuba, agatuma marayika we, na we akabimenyesha imbata ye Yohana, uhamya ibyo yabonye byose, ari ijambo ry'Imana no guhamya kwa Yesu Kristo" (Ibyahishuwe 1:1, 2).

Intumwa Yohana yari umwe mu bagize intumwa Cumi n'ebyiri za Yesu (Luka 8:51; Matayo 17:1; Matayo 26:36, 37). Nyuma yo gusubira mu ijuru kwa Kristo, Yohana yakomeje umurimo we muri Yerusalemu. Nyamara, mbere yo gusenyuka kwa Yerusalemu (mu mwaka wa 70 nyuma ya Kristo), yimukiye muri Efeso ajyanywe no gusura amatorero yo mu ntara ya Roma muri Aziya Ntoya. Mu gihe cyo gutotezwa kw'Abakristo mu karengane katejwe n'umwami w'abami Domisiyani w'Abaroma, intumwa Yohana yaciriwe ku kirwa cya Patimo, ikirwa cyari mu Nyanja ya Ejeyani [Aegean].

Igihe Yohana yari i Patimo, yahawe ubutumwa bw'ubuhanuzi bwihariye butari bugenewe amatorero arindwi yo muri Aziya Ntoya gusa, ahubwo bwari bugenewe n'Abakristo bose bo mu bihe byose. (Ibyahishuwe 1:1-3, 9-11). Ubutumwa bw'ubuhanuzi bwagombaga kugera ku bantu bose, kandi Yohana na we yari umwe muri bo, bwari buteye nk'uku: bwavaga ku Mana Data, bukagezwa kuri Yesu Kristo, akabuha marayika We, na we akabumenyesha intumwa Yohana, intumwa ikabugeza ku basomyi n'abazabwumva. Uru ni urugero rwiza rw'uburyo bwo guhishurirwa no guhumekerwa Imana ikoreshamo abahanuzi kugira ngo bageze ubutumwa bwayo ku bantu.

Mu cyigisho cy'iki cyumweru tuzasuzuma ku buryo bw'umwihariko uburyo bw'ihishurwa- n'ihumekerwa byakorwagamo, bwibanda cyane ku buryo bw'imvugo, bw'inyandiko, n'ubw'ibimenyetso ubwo butumwa bw'Imana bwagiye bugezwa ku biremwamuntu.
