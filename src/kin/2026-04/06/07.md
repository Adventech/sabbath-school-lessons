---
title:  Inyandiko za Ellen G. White
date:   06/11/2026
---

Ibyabaye mu buzima bwa Ellen G. White ndetse n'inyandiko ze bihura neza neza n'ibyo twize muri iki cyumweru birebana n'intumwa z'Imana tubona muri Bibiliya. Na we Imana yamuhaye umurimo wo kwandika ibyo yari yahishuriwe. Igihe yongeraga gutekereza ku byabaye mu mwaka wa 1909, Ellen G. White yaranditse ati: "Kubera impanuka yari yarangejeje hafi yo gupfa, nari nagize intege nke z'umubiri kandi sinari nshoboye kwandika. .. Ariko mu ijoro rimwe marayika w'Uwiteka yaje iruhande rw'uburiri bwanjye arambwira ati: "Ugomba kwandika ibintu nguha." Naravuze nti: "Sinshobora kwandika." Na none nongeye gutegekwa ngo: "Andika ibintu nguha." Natekereje ko nshobora kwandika, maze nkurura ikintu nagombaga kwandikiraho nkivana ku meza, ntangira kwandika, maze mbona ko nashoboraga kwandika amagambo mu buryo bunyoroheye." _The General Conference Bulletin_, May 31, 1909.

Kimwe n'abanditsi b'Ibyanditswe Byera bahumekewe n'Imana, Ellen G. White na we yakoresheje umufasha mu bijyanye no kwandika. Abanyamabanga be bakoze umurimo abakosora inyandiko bari bitezweho gukora-umurimo wo kunganira abanditsi mu kuvuga neza ubutumwa badashyizemo ibitekerezo byabo bwite. Ntibari abanditsi bakoporora ibyo abandi banditse cyangwa abafatanya n'abandi kwandika. Umurimo wabo wari ushingiye ku byo Ellen G. White ubwe yanditse, byaba ibyanditswe n'ukuboko kwe bwite cyangwa ibyo yavugishije ururimi rwe.

Ellen G. White na we yasubiye mu magambo yavuzwe n'abandi ndetse akoresha inyandiko zanditswe n'abandi bantu igihe yandikaga kugira ngo bimufashe igihe yabaga atanga ubutumwa bwe. Igihe yabaga asabye abasomyi b'inyandiko ze kuzasoma bimwe muri ibyo bitabo yakoresheje, biragaragara ko atabaga agambiriye kuyobya abantu cyangwa kuriganya abanditsi b'ibyo bitabo, ahubwo yabaga yabonye mu mvugo yabo amagambo yashobora kugeza ku bantu ukuri yifuzaga kuvuga.

None se ni iki twavuga ku kutuzuzanya kw'amagambo cyangwa kuba habonekamo amakosa?

Yaranditse ati: "Ku byerekeranye no kwibwira ko umuntu atakwibeshya, nta na rimwe nigeze mvuga ko ntakwibeshya; Imana yonyine ni Yo itibeshya."-_Ubutumwa Bwatoranyijwe_, Igitabo cya 1, p. 42.

Umuntu ashobora guhura n'amagambo yo mu nyandiko ze aruhije gusobanukirwa cyangwa bikaba bigoye kuyahuza n'andi. Ubumuntu bwa Ellen G. White ntibwari buhishwe no kuba yarahamagawe n'Imana. Nk'uko bimeze kuri twebwe twese, Ellen White yashoboraga gukura maze akagera ku kigero cyo hejuru, gukura no kugera ku kigero cyo hejuru kugaragara mu nyandiko ze. Nk'uko byavuzwe mbere, nyamara bikaba ari byiza kubisubiramo, yanakosoye abizeraga ko buri jambo ryose yanditse ryabaga rivuye mu ijuru mu buryo butaziguye. "Hari ibihe bimwe ibintu bisanzwe bigomba kuvugwa, ibitekerezo bisanzwe bikaba mu bwenge, amabaruwa asanzwe akandikwa, n'amakuru yahererekanyijwe ava ku mukozi umwe ajya ku wundi agatangwa. Ntabwo amagambo nk'ayo n'amakuru nk'ayo atangwa ku bwo guhumeka kwihariye kwa Mwuka w'Imana."-_Ubutumwa Bwatoranyijwe_, igitabo cya 3, p.52
