---
title:  Abafasha mu Bijyanye no Kwandika
date:   03/11/2026
---

`Soma Yeremiya 36:17, 18 n'Abaroma 16:22. Iyi mirongo itubwira iki ku bijyanye n'uko banditsi ba Bibiliya bakoreshaga abafasha mu bijyanye no kwandika?`

Birashoboka ko Yeremiya yashoboraga kwandika ubutumwa bwe, nyamara aho kugenza atyo, yishingikirije ku mwanditsi w'umwiringirwa ngo amwandikire ubuhanuzi bwe atagize icyo ahinduraho kandi abugeza ku bayobozi b'ishyanga. Yeremiya si we rugero rwonyine rri muri Bibiliya, aho abahanuzi bakoresheje abafasha mu bijyanye no kwandika kugira ngo bageze ku bantu ubutumwa bw'Imana. Mu Isezerano Rishya, nubwo uwitwa Terutiyo ayuga ko "yanditse uru rwandiko" (Abaroma 16:22), biragaragara uhereye mu ntangiriro z'igitabo cy'Abaroma ko yari arimo kwandika gusa ibyo Pawulo yifuzaga kuvuga. Terutiyo vari umwanditsi we-umufasha mu bijyanye no kwandika washyiraga mu nyandiko amagambo ya Pawulo.

Iyi migenzereze ntabwo yari ikintu kidasanzwe kuri Pawulo. Ku musozo w'urwandiko yandikiye Abakolosayi n'urwo yandikiye Abatesalonike, Pawulo avuga ingingo yo kongeraho intashyo akoresheje "ukwe kuboko" (Abakolosayi 4:18; 2 Abatesalonike 3:17) nk'uburyo bwo guhamya umwimerere w'urwandiko rwose uko rwakabaye. Ibi bishatse kuvuga yuko yishingikirizaga ku wundi muntu kugira ngo amwandikire inzandiko ze. Kubera ko uku kuri gushobora kubyutsa gushidikanya niba koko urwandiko rwaramuhagarariraga mu buryo nyabwo-cyangwa byaba bibi kurushaho, bakavuga ko atari we warwanditse - Pawulo yanandikaga amagambo ku musozo warwo akoresheje ukuboko kwe bwite, kugira ngo hatabaho gushidikanya ku mwimerere w'urwandiko rwe. Abasobanuzi benshi bafata uguhindagurika k'uburyo bw'imyandikire kuboneka mu nzandiko za Pawulo bakaguhuza n'uko yakoreshaga abafasha mu bijyanye no kwandika. Ikintu nk'icyo na none bagikeka ku nzandiko ebyiri zanditswe na Petero bavuga bati: "Nubwo ari ukuri ko uburyo bw'imvugo bukoreshwa mu rwandiko rwa 2 rwa Petero butandukanye n'ubukoreshwa mu rwandiko rwe rwa mbere, ibi bishobora kuba byaratewe n'uko Petero ... yifashishaga abanditsi mu kubaka interuro yabaga yandika mu rurimi rw'Ikigiriki. Niba Pawulo wamaze igihe kirekire atuye mu Bugiriki yarakoresheje abanditsi, nk'uko bigaragara rwose ko yabikoze, birushaho kuba byumvikana gutekereza ko Petero wavugaga ururimi rw'Ikinyaramaya nk'ururimi rwa kavukire, yaba yarabikoze; kandi ko abo banditsi bashobora kuba baragize impinduka bazana mu myubakirwe y'interuro mu nzandiko ze yandikaga mu Kigiriki."-_The SDA Bible Commentary_, vol. 5, p. 186.

Ibi bishatse kuvuga ko nubwo umuntu ashobora kutagira ibyo ahindura ku bitekerezo n'ubusobanuro by'intumwa z'Imana, imyubakirwe y'amagambo n'iy'interuro ishobora kugaragaza ubushobozi mu by'imyandikire by'abafasha bakoreshaga mu bijyanye no kwandika. Ibitekerezo ni byo bihumekerwa, si ngombwa ko amagambo runaka yakoreshejwe mu kugaragaza ibitekerezo byahumetswe na yo ahumekerwa.
