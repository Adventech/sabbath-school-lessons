---
title:  Inkomoko z'Inyandiko
date:   04/11/2026
---


"Maze kandi, kuko Umubwiriza yari umunyabwenge, yakomeje kwigisha abantu ubwenge; ni ukuri, yaratekereje, agenzura ibintu, aringaniza imigani myinshi. Umubwiriza yashatse kumenya amagambo akwiriye n'ibyanditswe bitunganye, iby'amagambo y'ukuri" (Umubwiriza 12:9, 10).

Iyi mirongo ishobora gusomwa kugira ngo herekanwe ko imigani Salomo yanditse atari ko yose yahimbwe na we. Ahumekewe na Mwuka w'Imana, "yaringanije" imigani myinshi, kandi aragenzura kugira ngo abone "amagambo akwiriye." Mu by'ukuri, umugore w'imico myiza wavuzwe kenshi mu Imigani 31 bivugwa ko ari nyina w'umwami utazwi witwaga Lemuweli, ko atari Salomo (Imigani 31:1). Ibihamya byerekana ko ubuvanganzo bw'Abanyegiputa na bwo bwagize ibyo buzana mu gitabo cy'Imigani. Kimwe n'ibyo, abanyamateka bahumekewe bo mu Isezerano rya Kera batubwira ko bifashishije inyandiko za kera ndetse n'inyandiko zabaga zivuga igikorwa kimwe runaka cyabaga cyarabayeho kugira ngo babwire abantu inkuru ntacyo bagabanyijeho.

`Soma 1 Abami 11:41; Luka 1:1-4; na Yuda 14, 15. Ni iki iyi mirongo itubwira ku bijyanye n'ukuntu abanditsi rimwe na rimwe bakoreshaga isoko z'amakuru zitari Bibiliya?`

Ezira na Nehemiya ni izindi ngero z'abanditsi ba Bibiliya bifashishije inyandiko z'amateka kugira ngo bandike inkuru zabo. Ezira ashyiramo na kopi z'inzandiko n'amategeko- teka y'abami yarebanaga na Yerusalemu n'Ubuperesi (soma Ezira 4-7), kandi Nehemiya atubwira uruhererekane rw'imiryango yabonye maze akarushyira mu nkuru ye (soma Nehemiya 7:5-72). Iri hame rinaboneka no mu Isezerano Rishya. "Bigaragara neza uhereye ku byabaye kuri Luka ko Guhumekerwa gukora mu buryo butabusanya n'imikorere kamere y'imbaraga z'ubwenge kandi ntikubishyira ku ruhande. Aha tuhafite umwanditsi wahumekewe wari uyobowe na Mwuka Wera ngo yige abyitondeye inyandiko n'ibyavuzwe n'abantu yashoboraga kubona byerekeye ku mibereho ya Kristo, hanyuma akabyegeranya ngo akore inkuru ihurije hamwe amakuru yari yegeranyije mu buryo nk'ubwo."-_The SDA Bible Commentary_, vol.5, p. 669.

Yuda asa n'ukoresha amagambo aboneka mu gitabo cya 1 Henoki 1:9-igitabo kitashyizwe muri Bibiliya, umurimo bakoze mu gihe kiri hagati y'Amasezerano yombi. Pawulo na we yakoresheje amagambo y'abacurabwenge b'Abagiriki atagombye kuvuga amazina yabo (soma Ibyakozwe n'Intumwa 17:28 na Tito 1:12).

`Nubwo abanditsi ba Bibiliya inshuro nyinshi babaga bayobowe n'ihishurirwa ritaziguye binyuze mu mayerekwa n'inzozi, ibi ntibyakuragaho ko Mwuka Wera na We yahumekeraga muri izi ntumwa ngo zihitemo inyandiko zizikuye mu yandi masoko y'inkuru. Kubera iki iyi ari ingingo y'ingenzi tugomba kwibuka?`
