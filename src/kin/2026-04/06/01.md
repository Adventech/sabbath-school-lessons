---
title:  Inyandiko za Gihanuzi
date:   31/10/2026
---

### Aho icyigisho cy'iki cyumweru gishingiye:
Kuva 17:14; Yesaya 13:1; Yeremiya 36:17, 18; Abaroma 16:22; Luka 1:1-4; Yuda 14, 15; 2 Abakorinto 4:7.

> <p>Icyo kwibukwa:</p>
> "Ibyanditswe kera byose byandikiwe kutwigisha, kugira ngo kwihangana no guhumurizwa bitangwa na byo, biduheshe ibyiringiro" (Abaroma 15:4).

Ni mu buhe buryo wahitamo kwigishwa indirimbo yahimbwe n'Imana ubwayo? Mu Gutegeka kwa Kabiri 32, indirimbo yanditswe na Mose abibwirijwe n'Imana (Gutegeka 31:19-22). Si kenshi dutekereza ku ndirimbo igihe dutekereza ku nyandiko za gihanuzi, nubwo mu by'ukuri inyinshi muri izi Zaburi ari indirimbo zahumetswe, nubwo amagambo ubwayo azigize atari Uwiteka ubwe wategetse mu buryo butaziguye ko bayandika.

Bibiliya isobanura uburyo butatu abahanuzi bakoreshaga batanga ubutumwa bw'Imana: (1) uburyo bwo kuvuga; (2) binyuze mu nyandiko; n' (3) ibikorwa bishushanya ibindi bintu cyangwa gukina inkuru. Muri iki cyumweru tuzibanda ku nyandiko zivugwa. mu Byanditswe Byera. Bibiliya yanditswemo Ijambo ry'Imana, nk'uko rivugwa binyuze mu nagambo y'intumwa zayo, habariwemo n'ibibwirizwa n'imbwirwaruhame by'abahanuzi.

Tekereza ku runyurane rw'imyandikire ndetse n'amoko y'inyandiko mu nyandiko za gihanuzi: inkuru, amateka, ibisigo, amaganya n'inzandiko, ndetse n'ibindi. Abagabo n'abagore batoranyijwe bashyiraga mu mvugo ya kimuntu ibyo Mwuka Wera yabaga babwirije kuvuga. Muri iki cyumweru tuzareba ku nyandiko zabo, akamaro k'ababafashaga kwandika (abanditsi), turebe no ku bintu tugomba kuzirikana igihe twiga imirongo ikomeye.
