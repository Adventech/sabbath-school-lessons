title:  Kugerageza Abahanuzi

### Aho icyigisho cy'iki cyumweru gishingiye:
Daniyeli 10:7-19; Gutegeka kwa Kabiri 13:1-5; Yesaya 8:20; Yohana 11:25; Ibyakozwe n'Intumwa 15:36-39; Yeremiya 28:9, 15-17.

> <p>Icyo kwibukwa:</p>
> "Bakundwa, ntimwizere imyuka yose: ahubwo mugerageze imyuka ko yavuye ku Mana, kuko abahanuzi benshi b'ibinyoma badutse bakaza mu isi" (1 Yohana 4: 1).

Diyama y'umwimerere ni rimwe mu mabuye y'agaciro ku isi. Ni byo koko, ibindi bintu byinshi bishobora kugira icyo bikorwaho bikenda gusa neza neza na diyama nyamara bikaba nta gaciro bifite. Nonese umuntu yamenya ate niba diyama ari umwimerere cyangwa niba ari icyiganano? Uburyo buruta ubundi bwose bwo kuyimenya, ni ukuyisuzuma. Amasuzuma amwe ashobora gukorerwa mu rugo wifashishije ibikoresho bike byoroheje. Andi masuzuma akenera abahanga babigize umwuga. Amenshi muri ayo masuzuma si yo atanga umwanzuro wa nyuma, ariko iyo diyama inyuze mu masuzuma menshi ikemerwa, umuntu aba ashobora kugira icyizere ko diyama ye ari umwimerere.

Diyama ni kimwe mu bikoresho bikomeye cyane ku isi. Umugabane wo gukomera kwa diyama ushingiye mu bushobozi bwayo bwo guhangana n'ubushyuhe bwinshi ndetse no guhindura ubushyuhe mu buryo bwihuse. Kugira ngo usuzume diyama, ushobora kuyishyushya ukoresheje ikibiriti cyangwa umuriro wa buji, nyuma yaho ukayirekurira mu mazi akonje. Nubwo diyama y'umwimerere itazangirika, diyama y'icyiganano ishobora kumenagurika!

Impano y'ubuhanuzi irushijeho kuba iy'agaciro kurusha diyama, ariko umuntu uvuga ko afite iyi mpano ntabwo yafatwa gutyo gusa. Ibyo avuga bigomba gusuzumwa. Muri iki cyumweru tuzasuzuma abahanuzi bo muri Bibiliya kugira ngo dutwikurure amahame ya Bibiliya yakoreshwaga kugira ngo basuzume niba ari abahanuzi b'ukuri
