---
title:  "Nimusongongere, Mumenye"
date:   27/11/2026
---


Kuba Abadventisti bemera impano ya gihanuzi ya Ellen G. White, uhereye igihe yatangiriv Kugeza ubu, byagiye bihura n'ingorane zisa n'iz'abahanuzi benshi bo muri Bibiliya bahuva na zo. Nyuma y'iyerekwa rya mbere rya Ellen G. White ryabaye mu Kuboza 1844, bamy bakemanze ibyo yivugagaho ko ari umuhanuzi. Ibyo byatumye Abadventisti bakomezas Isabato (Abaje kwitwa nyuma Abadventisti b'Umunsi wa Karindwi) kubaka ingingo zishyigikira impano y'ubuhanuzi ya Ellen G. white.

Icya mbere: batanze ubusobanuro bushingiye ku iyobokamana bwerekana ko impano y'ubuhanuzi izakomeza kugeza ku mperuka y'ibihe. Bavuze yuko Bibiliya ishyigikira kwigaragaza kw'impano y'ubuhanuzi muri iki gihe. Bashingiye ku byanditswe muri Yoweli 2, Ibyahishuwe 12:17, no ku bindi byanditswe, impano y'ubuhanuzi yajyaga kwigaragaza mu "minsi iheruka" kandi igahinduka "ikimenyetso kiranga" ubwoko bw'Imana bwo mu minsi iheruka. (soma igitabo cyanditswe na Gerhard Pfandl cyitwa "The Gift of Prophecy and God's Remnant Church," The Gift of Prophecy, Chapter 4.)

Icya kabiri: Abadventisti ba mbere babonye ko impano ya Ellen G. White yari ifite imbaraga nziza ihindura abizera. Nk'urugero: ibitekerezo bye by'ubuhanuzi byafashije guhuriza hamwe abizera bakomezaga Isabato bahuriraga mu biterane bikomeye byakurikiye umwaka wa 1840, igihe habaga habayeho kudahuza ku nyigisho zimwe na zimwe za Bibiliya. Impano ye yakomeje "kwera imbuto" mu myaka mirongo yakurikiyeho uhereye icyo gihe, kandi inyandiko ze ziracyafite imbaraga mu muryango mugari w'abafite kwizera kw'Abadventisti b'Umunsi wa Karindwi.

Ingingo ya gatatu ishyigikira Ellen G. White irebana n'ibintu ndengakamere byamubagaho igihe yabaga ari mu iyerekwa. Igihe yabaga ari mu iyerekwa, nk'uko twabibonye ahabanza, Ellen G. White yagaragarwagaho n'ibintu ndengakamere-ibihamya bigaragara n'ibyamubagaho atari asanzwe azi, guhishura ibyo mu gihe kizaza, kugaragaza imbaraga nyinshi zirenze iz'umuntu usanzwe, no kuba atarashoboraga gutegeka cyangwa guhindura igihe ibyamubagaho mu iyerekwa byashoboraga kubera, ndetse n'ibindi.

Abadventisti b'Umunsi wa Karindwi bakomeza kugaragaza ibi bihamya mu gushyigikira ko impano ya Ellen G. White ifite agaciro. Nubwo izi ngingo ari nziza, "igihamya" gifite imbaraga gishyigikira impano ya Ellen G. White ni imibereho umuntu ku giti cye agirana n'inyandiko ze. Nk'uko Umunyazaburi Dawidi abivuga ati: "Nimusogongere, mumenye yuko Uwiteka agira neza" (Zaburi 34:9), igihe dusoma inyandiko za Ellen G. White dusenga, ni bwo dushobora kumenya agaciro k'ibyo yivugaho nk'umuhanuzi.

Ahantu ho gutangirira ni ku gatabo ke gato ariko gakundwa na benshi kitwa Kugana Yesu. Ukurikizeho uruhererekane rw'ibitabo bye bitanu ruvuga iby'Intambara z'Ibihe byose, bitangirira ku nsanganyamatsiko y'Intambara Ikomeye ihera mu Itangiriro ikageza mu Byahishuwe. Inyandiko ze ziboneka ari nyinshi mu mpapuro no kuri murandasi (rebera kuri uru rubuga: EGWWritings.org) kandi zahinduwe mu ndimi nyinshi.
