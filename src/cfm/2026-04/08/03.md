---
title:  Nunphung thuanthu
date:   16/11/2026
---

`Siar ding: Isaiah 65:17-20. Leithar le vanthar ah nauhak cu “kum 100 upa in a thi ding a timi sullam ziangsaw a si? (Isa. 65:20).`

Isaiah 65:20 hi a nunphung thuanthu thei mumal ih kan vun zoh ahcun leithar le vanthar ah misual le thihnak tivek a um lai ding tiih rel pang a ol te. Asinan cui ruahnak cu Isaiah cabu sungih midingfel a thimi pawl an thosal ding (Isa. 26:19), cule thihnak le riahsiatnak cu a netnak ah an um nawn lo ding (Isa. 25:8, Isa. 65:19) a timi thawn an kalh aw thluh.

Isaiah 65:20 cu duhsannei hminsinnak tongkam a si. Ziang nauhak saw kum 100 kim dah? Isaiah 65:17-20 ih nunphung thuanthu kan siar tikah himi thu (bungcang) cu Israel miphun ih an dong dingmi thlawsuahnak khihhmuhtu biazai a si timi kan theithiam ding. Asinan annih khal Thukham daan ih rinum zetih an um lawngih an ngah dingmi a si (Daanpeksalnak 28 thawn zoh aw). Jesuh in Matthai 24 ah thil a thleng cing dingmi cu lehhnu lam ih thil a thleng dingmi thawn a pehzom awkter. Cuvek ciah in Isaiah cun hitawk ahhin a tuah ve, a tu ih thil a thleng dingmi cu leithar le vanthar a um dingmi ih hmaihruaitu a si timi a khih hmuh.

Isaiah ih sersiam tharnak cu hmailam caan hrang lawng a si lo. Cui thil cu sualngaidamnak le tlennak ih Pathian sunlawih hnatuannak hmangin a tu khalah zamrang zetin a lut zo a si. Himi cang cun a tu rori hmanah, leitlun ih Israel pawl an um rero lai hmanah Pathian cun thihnak a neh zo. Himi ruahnak cu kum reipi nunnak le naute thihnak hlonhlo dingih thutiamkamnak hmangin a langter. Himi pawl hmuahhmuah cu a netnak ih thil a ra thleng dingmi phenthlam te men fang a si.

`Siar ding: 1 Korin 16:20. Himi thu hi a tui kan nunphung le tikcu caan ahhin ziangtin kan hmang thei ding?`

Thukham Thar san ih Grik nunphung ah laicin le rualpi naivai lakih cibai bukawknak cu hnamawknak in a thlun. Paul cun Khristian lakah hivek ih cibai bukawknak um ve dingin Rom 16:16, 1 Korin 16:20, 2 Korin 13:12, le 1 Thess 5:26 ah a sim. Himi thuhla ihsin Bible thuzirh mi ‘zumtu rak hmuakdan le Pathian innsang ah tel ve ih an theihawknak dingih cibai bukdan pawl’ kan zir ngah thei. Nunphung hrekkhat ahcun hnamawk tivek cu thil mawi lo zet a si men thei, cumi ai hnakin kut kaih awk (cibai awk), pawm awk, tihzah pek ih lukun tivek pawl cu nunphung ih pawm nuam deuh an si men thei. Nunphung lamih tarlang awknak pawl cu ziang a si hmanah Paul cun kawhhran pumkhawm awknak ah cohlang mi le duhdawtmi ih an theih awknak dingih va tuah dingah Paul cun forhfialnak in nei a si.
