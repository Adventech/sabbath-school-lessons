---
title:  Bible, amah ten a simfiang aw
date:   15/11/2026
---

Bible kan simfiangnak ahhin kan khawruahnak in nasa zet in a luat khat. Bible hi hlansan ih ca funkhawm menmen mi a si tiih a ruattu pawl cun cui cabu sungih cauk tam zetih pehzom awknak zianghman an hmu thei lo ding. Asinan kaihhruai awknak bulhram a si mi “Ca Thianghlim tinkim hi Pathian thawtkhummi a si ih hlawknak a nei” (2 Tim. 3:16, NASB) timi kan ruah ahcun an pumkhat awk thluh zia kan theifiang dingih, Bible hi amahten simfiang aw dingin rem kan ti ding a si.

`Siar ding: Mathai 5:17-48. Himi thu sung ahhin Jesuh cun Thukham Hlun ih thuzirhnak ziangtin a simfiang?`

Matthai 5:17-48 sung hi a tlunlam ihsin vun siar tikah Jesuh cun Thukham Hlun ih thuzirhnak cu Amai thuzirhnak thar thawn a thleng (aiawh) vekin tla a lang a si. Asinan ralrin zet in thaten kan vun zir tikah Jesuh cun Thukam hlun ih thlarau lam thuhla thuk zet a pholang ti a fiangter. Himi ahhin Jeremiah 31:31-33 ih um Thukham Thar hnuai ih Pathian daan cu an ruahnak sungah a ret ding ih, A mi pawl ih thinlung ah a ngan ding a timi thutiam a famkimter a si (zoh kop aw, Heb. 8:8-10, Heb. 10:16).

Khuimaw caan ahcun thungan mi pakhat simfiangnak cu cui thu ngannak cabu sungah ret in a um theu. Thimnak ah Daniel 2 ahkhan Siangpahrang Nebuchadnezzar ih zanmang thuthup relnak kan hmu (Dan. 2:31-35) cule tui mang ih simfiangnak khal a um hngal a si (Dan. 2:36-45). Cuvek ciah in Mathai 13 ahkhan thlaici vorh tahthimnak kan hmu ih (Matt. 13:1-9), cule a simfiannak khal kan hmu (Matt. 13:18-23).

Khuimaw caan ahcun thucah simfiannak cu neta ih profet pawl in an run relfiang. Thimnak ah 2 Sansiarnak 36:22,23 ahhin Jeremiah 25:11, 12 le Jeremiah 29:10 sungih um simcianak kum 70 sung simcianak thuhla kha a nemhnget a si. Ziangkhal sehla, Thukham Hlun ih thu tamzet cu Khrih ah a famkim thluh ih, Thukham Hlun ih thucah tamzet khal Thukham Thar ah simfiangin a um thluh.

“Thukham Hlun cu simduhsan nei hminsinnak sungih um thuthangtha a si. Thukham Thar cu a ngaingai ih ummi a si. Pakhat cu a dang vek thotho in a thupi zet mi a si”.—Ellen G. White, Selected Messages, book 2, p. 104. A ngaingai te cun Thukham Thar cu Thukham Hlun ih thawtkhum mi simfiangtu a si.

`Ziang ruangah saw Bible kan zir ahhin kan ruahnak pawl theihthiam hi a thupit? Kanmah in Pathian kamsuak thu kan then ding a silo ih, kanmah hi thuthen in kan um sawn ding a si cun ziang ruangah saw kan ruahnak thupi zet cu Pathian kamsuak thu vekih kan rinsan?`
