---
title:  Bible simfiangnak
date:   19/11/2026
---

Bible kan zirnak ahhin tumtah mi thil thupi zet pahnih kan hmuitin ding a si: bulpak ih thlarau lam thansoh le midang pawl Jesuh thawn laicin awknak rundamnak sungih hruailuh ding hi a si. Khrih cun Khristian dik takcu Thiang Thlarau hmangin “thutak hmuahhmuah sungih” (Johan 16:13) kaihhruai mi, “Pathian ih ka ihsin a suak mi thu kip thawn” (Matt. 4:4, NKJV) a nungtu, kan hnen ih A thupek “mi pawl hmuahhmuah” midang a zirhtu a si ding tiah a ti (Matt. 28:20).

`Siar ding: Luka 4:16-30 le Luka 24:25-27, 44-49. Hitawk ih Khrih in Cathianghlim a simfiang dan ihsin ziang kan zir suak thei ding?`

Jesuh Khrih cu “lamzin, thutak, le nunnak” a si ih, a si ringring ding (Johan 14:6, NKJV). Curuangah a thuzirh mi cu Cathianghlim sungah hnget zetin a phum aw a si. Thimnak ah Marka 1:14,15 ahkhan, “Tu ah Johan cu thawng thlak a si hnuah, Jesuh cu Pathian uknak ram thuthangtha sim in Galilee ah a ra ih, Caan a kim zo ih Pathian uknak ram cu a nai zo a si: siraw uhla thuthangtha hi zum uh, tiah a ti”. Hitawk ih caan a timi cun fiang zetin Daniel 9:24-27 a khihhmuh a si. Tlangpar thuthangtha simnak ahkhan (Matthai 5-7) Jesuh cun Suahlannak 20:13 le 14 (Matthai 5:21,27) sungta a run la suak. Luka 4 ahkhan Isaiah 61:1,2 sungta a la suak a si. Jesuh cu Bible simfiangtu famkim zet a si.

Tahthimnak dang pakhat cu Tirthhlah 8 ahkhan tilper mi Ethopia pakhat cu “Pathian bia dingin Jerusalem ah a ra” tiah in sim (Tirh 8:27, NKJV), cumi sullam cu anih hi Judah biaknak thawn a rak pehtlai aw tu le asilole bikin Judah pawl ih tuah mi puai ih feh theutu a si ko ding. Isaiah cabu hman a nei ih, inn lam pan ih a feh lai hmanah a siar rero. Asinan simcianak sungih sual a phurtu siahhlawh timi thuhla zianghman a theithiam lo. Curuangah amah simfiang tu ding mi pakhat a tul zet.

Himi tontehnak cun zirlai thupi zet malte in zirh duhmi a um. A hmaisabik ahcun Cathianghlim siar menmen hin a sungih thu um pawl thuk zetih theihthiamnak ah mi a hruai ringring lo. A pahnihnak ah, Cathianghlim zirnak ihsin pakhat le pakhat kan theithiam vivo thei. Pathumnak, Bible thucah hi midang hnen ih sim ve dingin bulpak tin in tuanvo kan nei thluh a si.

Filip in Isaiah cabu le a dang Thukham Hlun sungih thuhla um mi pawl a simfiang tikah tilper pa cun tipilnak a neih phah (Tirth 8:35-39; zoh kop Saam. 51:12). Cathianghlim kan simfiang dan khal ahhin tumtahnak bangaw mi kan nei ding a si (zoh aw, Johan 17:17).

`Khuimaw caan ahcun Bible thuhla kan theihfiang theinak dingah simfiang tu kan tul caan tla a um ding, asinan ziangahsaw kan rak zum zo mi thudik hi kan thinlung ih a um hnget ringring a tul?`
