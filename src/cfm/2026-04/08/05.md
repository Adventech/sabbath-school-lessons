---
title:  Cangan sullam
date:   18/11/2026
---

Khristian hmuahhmuah in anmai bulpak ih Bible an theihthiam a mul. Rundamnak lamzin theihthiam theinak dingah Bible cu a fiang tawk tuk. Asinan ruahnak fim a nei pawl hrangah lungbuai thlak zet thil khal a si thotho. Cathianghlim cu ziangtik hmanah a cem thei lo mi rundamnak thuhla a si ih, ziangtik hmanah a cem thei lo mi theihnak hlawnthil a si.

`Siar ding: Luka 10:25-28. Mi hrekkhat pawl cun Bible hi simfiang hran ciamco khal a tul lo tiah an ti theu. Hivek rak si ngaingai bangta sehla, ziangah saw Jesuh cun himi daan thiampai hnenah, “ ‘Pathian daan ah ziangtin a rak ngan aw? Ziangtin saw na simfiang’ ” (Luka 10:26 Thucah) tin thu a sut?`

Cangan mi sullam theithiam dingin a hmaisabik ah tongfang hmanmi kan theih thiam a tul, cule cumi thawn pehpar aw mi cangan thuanthu le Bible pumhlum ih a hmandan, a thuanthu nunphung fehdan, a netnak ah kan theih tawk ih kan hmandan pawl kan theihthiam a tul a si. A netabik ahcun kan thunet kharnak cu Cathianghlim ih thuzirhmi kauzet thawn an remawk thluh a tul a si. Cathianghlim ih thil bangaw a rel mi – Thuthangtha pali sungih a umdan vekin – kan theihnak a kau sinsin ding a si.

Matthai 5:43-48 cu Luka 6:27-36 thawn zohthim aw. Ziangtin saw Matthai 5:43-48 le cumi thawn a milaw mi Luka 6:36 ih ummi thu cun Mathai 5:48 ih um “famkim” timi sullam theithiam deuh dingih in bawm?

A dikmi Cathianghlim simfiangtu ih mizia thupi zet pakhat cu tangdornak le taimaknak komaw mi nun hi a si. Kanmah lakah zohman in thutak kan nei lo timi thei ringring aw; thlalang ah thim zetin kan hmu aw thluh a si.

Thurin dik zet neih kan tum ringring ding a si (2 Tim. 4:1-5). Thlun dingmi thil thupi zet cu kan theihfiang mi, thil fiang mi pawl hmuitin ih, cule cui thuhla ih thansoh ding hi a si. Cuticun caan a hungliam vivo khalah thuhla tamzet ahhin a tu ih kan theihthiam vak lomi thil pawl khal theithiam dingin in bawm ding a si. Kumkhua ih na let thei thluh lo dingmi thusuhnak a um ringring ding.

`Na thinlung a lo buaiter zettu, na theihtiam thei riai lomi, a sinan leh hnu ih simfiangnak na ngah sal mi thuhla kha vun ruat aw. Cui na hmuhton mi cun na theihthiam hrih lo mi A Kamsuak thu ah Pathian rinsannak nei thotho dingin ziangtin a lo bawm ding?`
