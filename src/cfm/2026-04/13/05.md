---
title:  Simcianak cun Pathian minung a hnem
date:   23/12/2026
---

`Siar ding: Matthai 11:28, 29. Jesuh khan ziang tuah dingin saw in sim? A hnen ih a fehtu pawl hnenah ziangmi thu a tiam?`

Pathian cun minung ih thatnak ding hi a ruat ringring theu. Evi le Adam a sersiam hlan ah Pathian cun an umnak ding tha famkim a timlam sak ih, cumi cun an lung a awi ter dingih, an aipuang ter ding a si (Semtirnak 1). Pathian cun an hnangam theinak dingah an tulmi ziangkim a pe. Sual ih an tluk hnu khalah anmah hawl in Pathian cu a ra kirsal (Semtirnak 3:9), Rundamtu a rat ding thu a simcia ih(Semtirnak 3:15), an taklawng khuhnak angki a tuahsak (Semtirnak 3:21). Pathian cun a sersiammi pawl ih an that theinak dingah hna a tuan a si.

Simcianak laksawng ih thlawsuahnak cu Pathian minung pawl hnangamnak le tharthawhnak a si theinak dingih hnatuan a si mi hnakin theihhngilh theih a si lo men thei.

`Siar ding: Isaiah 40:1-5. Pathian in natnak tuar minung hrangah A profet pawl hnenah ziangmi thucah a sim? Ziangtin saw cui thu bulhram cu tuini ah kan hman thei ding?`

Pathian cun Judah pawl hnenah A hrem hai khalah A tlansan dah cuang lo timi an theih ding a duh. Pathian cun lungkuai zet a minung pawl hrangih forhfialnak nei dingin Isaiah hnenah thucah a pe menmen lo. Isaiah hnenah thucah a phuangdan ding pawl a simfiang thluh. Thucah cu ngilneihnak, duhdawtnak le ngaihsaknak thawn phuang a tul a si. Pathian cun zianghman tanta loin a mipi pawl thinlung ih a thleng mi, hi hnangamnak thucah hi a ruat ve zet. Cuvek hnangamnak thucah cu Isaiah cabu sungah ngan kirsal rero mi a si (Isa. 12:1, Isa. 49:13, Isa. 51:3, zoh awla).

Tirhthlah Paul cun Khrih Jesuh ah Pathian duhdan vekih a nungtu pawl cun hremnak an tong ding tin ralrinnak a rak pe zo (2 Tim. 3:12). Hmailam caan ih harsatnak um ding mi ahhin hngamnak le ruahsannak tongkam pawl kan tul zet a si. Simcianak laksawng cu Pathian in a mi pawl inn lam ih hruai tlung dingih Jesuh a rat hlan ah A mi pawl hnemnak ding le thazang peknak dingih ah a hmang ding.

`Na nun ih khuitawk ahsaw hnangamnak le ruahsannak tongkam pawl theih na duh? Pathian in in hnem hnuah ziang ti dingin saw huham in pek? (2 Kor. 1:3, 4).`
