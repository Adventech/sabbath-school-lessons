---
title:  Simcianak hi Pathian Thutak tarlangtu a si
date:   22/12/2026
---

Mifiam thiam hrekkhat pawl cun tuisan ahhin leitlun ah khristian kawhhran 45000 hnakih tam an um tiah an ti. Ziangtin saw Jesuh Khrih ihsin a thokmi zumnak cu zumnak dangdang ah a thenawk thluh thiam? Thuletnak cu caan herliam vivo ah Cathianghlim simfiangnak dangdang dangdang a um a si. An zaten an dik thluh lo ding timi a fiang, ziangahtile thu tamzet ahhin kawhhran pakhat ih a zirh mi cu a dang pawl in an dokalh a si.

A hramthok ihsin ( zoh awla, Sem. 2:16, 17; Sem. 3:1-5), thinlung hnokbuaiternak hi Satan cun Pathian thutak ihsin minung a lakhran theinak dingih a hman mi lamzin ropi zet a si. Evi le Adam hnen ih a tuah vek ciah in Satan cun nunau le mipa pawl hi a thununnak hnuai ih a ret theinak dingah thudik tam zet cu thudik lo thawn a rawi cih thluh. Satan cun Pathian minung pawl cu caan liamzo ihsin a rak hruaisual rero zo (2 San. 36:14-16). Jesuh hman hruaisual dingin a bei (matt 4:1-11), asinan Jesuh cun Satan ih ngennak pawl cu “Tiin ngan a si” (Matt. 4:4, NKJV) timi hmangin fiang zetin a rak let thluh.

`Siar ding: 2 Timote 4:1-5. Pathian thu thawn pehpar aw in Paul in Timote hnenah ziangmi ruahnak thupi zet saw a pek? Ziangruangah saw Timote cu thil a ra thleng ding mi ah thlarau lam timlamnak nei ringring dingih a ngen?`

Paul cun profet mitin tikcu caan hung um vivo ding ahhin nunau le mipa pawl cun Pathian Kamsuak thu an duh nawn lo ding ih, an thlun duh lo ding timi a rak hmu a si. Cuhnak in an duhzawng tak thuanthu tuktak a sim theitu thlarau lam zirhtu pawl hawl hmu dingin an feh sawn ding. Cuvek pawl hrang ahcun Pathian cun an hawl rero mi thuphan pawl hmuahhmuah an zum theinak dingah zumsualnak a thlah ding: “Cun hi thu ruangah Pathian in an hnenah thuphan zum dingin, nasa zet mi zum sualnak a thlah ding a si: Cuticun thutak a zum lotu, dingfel lonak ih nuamawtu pawl an zatei’ thiam lo coter an si theinak dingah.” (2 Thess. 2:11, 12).

Asinan a ongaw tu pawl hnenah Jesuh cun “thutak an thei” ding tiah a rak tiamkam a si (Johan 8:32). Profet thuphuannak laksawng cu pathian in diklonak ihsin thutak, thimnak ihsin eng ih a thenhrannak bik a si.

Lamzin dang cun hi kawhhran bangaw lo tamzet ahhin thudik cu olsam zetin kan theithei a si. Nisarih ni Sabbath (Bible in cawlhni tiih a nemhnget mi) hi na bansan duh lo a si ahcun na ruahnak zateek 99.5 cu na hlonhlo thluh tinak a si ding. Ziangahtile kawhhran tamzet cun thupek palinak hi an ul duh lo a si.

Mithi pawl cu Jesuh thawn um tlang dingin vanram ah an kai hngal timi lole tu ah hell ram ah an kang rero, kumkhua in an kang ding timi na zum lo ahcun, ziangtin saw a si ding? Na zumnak 99.5 cu na mitter thluh zo ih, zateek 5 lawng a tang thlang tinak a si.

`Seventh-day Adventist kawhhran lo ah khuitawk ah na feh cuang ding?`
