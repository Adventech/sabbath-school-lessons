---
title:  Tuah tha sal aw
date:   25/12/2026
---

Kum ziangmaw zat liam ahkhan Kellogg kompani cun zinglam ih an thawhhlam thu ah tlangthannak thar an nei sal. Anmah rak dawr theutu hmundang dang ih rak thawnaw zo pawl hnenah sawmnak an nei sal ih, “ a voikhatnak ra tep sal hnik uh” tiah an ti. Hi thurel mi hi Bible le Ellen G. White thuhla zirnak ah caan tampi hmang ih, a cangngan mi “tep”nak ih caan hmangmal tu Seventh-day Adventist pawl hrangah hmantlak thu a si ve pei maw? Ellen G. White thuhla zirnak ih na tel cun a nun le a hnatuan pawl ah ruahnak a lo pe zet ding, asinan nangmah ten na “tep” lo ahcun a profet thucah ih thlarau lam thlawsuahnak tonnun na nei ngaingai thei lo dingih, na zoh hliah thei ngaingai lo ding. A canganmi siar ih a theifiang tu (a hleice in a thuanthu) lawngin Pathian ih in pekmi ah lungawinak famkim an nei ding a si.

Tui kuata sungih kan hminsin zo vekin Elle G. White cun Bible thudik ah in hngetter ih, Pathian thawn in pehzom. A simcianak thucah siarnak ahhin thlawsuahnak hleice a um.

Roger Dudley le Des Cummings, Jr. ih an hliakhlainak ahcun lunglutza thudik thenkhat an tarlang. Ellen G. White ih ca a siar ringringtu pawl cu a siar dah lotu pawl ai hnakin Jesuh thawn laicin awknak thuk sawn le hnget sawn an nei a si; rundamnak thu ah an hnget sawn ih, innsang khawmnak an nei ringring ih, midang hnen ih theihpitu si duhnak an nei deuhdeuh a si. “Hliakhlainak zirnak ahhin thutluknak pakhat tuah dingin nemhngetnak mumal zet a tul” tiah hliakhlainak neitu pahnih cun an rel. “Kawhhran thansohnak hliakhlainak ahhin bulpak lunglut mi thil asilole thiltuah mi ziang simaw thuhla pawl hmuahhmuah ahhin Ellen G. White ih ca a zir ringringtu member pawl cu voikhat voihnih hrawng lawng a siar tu pawl ai hnakin a sinak a sang deuh sawn theu a si…. Ellen White ih cabupawl a siar ringring tu in Khristian nun le thutheihpitu sinak ahhin danglamnak tha zet an nei a si”—Dudley and Cummings, “Who Reads Ellen White?” in Ministry, October 1982, p. 12.

Tui kuata sung ahhin Pathian cun A profet pawl hmangin pawlkomnak in neih pi ti kan hmu thei. An thucah pawl cun Pathian thawn in pehzom aw ter ih, minung nun lamzin ziangkim ahhin thianghlimnak a rung kengih, thianghlimnak in pe a si. A hleice in Jesuh sungih rundamnak thutiam: “Oh, Bawipai’thatnak hi tep uhla zoh uh” (Saam 34:8). Thianghlim zetih cangvai dingin, nun thleng danglamnak nei dingin, bulpak thuthangtha simnak nei ding, minung hnen ih riantuan dingah in ko a si. Himi cu Jesuh ratsal ding kan hngak lai ahhin leitlun ih netabik thuthangtha suahnak ih kan tuanvo famkimter dingah timlamnak thupi zet a si.
