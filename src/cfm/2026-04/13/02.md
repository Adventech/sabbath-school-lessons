---
title:  Profet thuphuan cun Khrih a khaisang
date:   20/12/2026
---

Profet thuphuan cu Pathian in A minung pawl thawn an biakawknak le Pathian thawn an pehzom awknak hi a si. Cumi a si ruangah Satan (rulhreng) cu nunau (Pathian kawhhran) tlunah a thinheng ih, a cithlah a tangsung pawl thawn doawknak nei ding ih a feh khal hi mangbang ding a si lem lo (zoh aw, Thup. 12:17).

Himi nunau cithlah a tangsun pawl ih danglamnak hminsin cu ziangsaw an si? Annih cun thupek pawl an thlun ih, Jesuh ih thutheihternak an dong a si. Thuphuan 19:10 ah himi thutheihternak cu “profet thuphuannak ih (simcianak) thlarau” a si tiah in sim.

`Siar ding: 1 Korin 2:2 le Galati 6:14. Paul in a nun ih cangvaihnak neitertu bik – Jesuh Khrih ih nunnak le hnatuannak ih tumtahmi hi ziangtin a hmu?`

Anmai tonteh mi lenglam ah tirhthlah pawl cun profet pawl le midingfel pawl in kum zabi tampi sungih an rak phuan mi Jesuh kha an pawm a si. Khristian leitlun ahhin Thukham Hlun le Thar thawngin nemhngetnak tamzet le famkim zet a nei a si: hlan ahcun Rundamtu a rat ding thu an rak khihhmuh, cule lehhnu ahcun Jesuh cu Rundamtu ih dinhmun famkimter tu vekin an tarlang lala. Himi cu a zum duhtu pawl hrang ahcun zumnak an dinhnget theinak dingih a tawk zet mi a si zo: “Pathian khawkhannak cu Pathian le A Fapa ih huham le Thiang Thlarau ih hnatuannak sungah zumnak thanso ter theinak dingih a dikzet mi tanta hi a si..”—Ellen G. White, The Spirit of Prophecy, vol. 3, p. 182.

Lamzin tamzet ahcun thubuai hi fimnak lam siloin, thatnak lam a si sawn. Lu lam siloin thinlung lam a si sawn. Mipi in an thinlung an khar tikah vansiatthlak zetin an ruahnak cun a thlun cih mai ding a si.

`Siar ding: Johan 1:1-5. Himi bungcang in Jesuh hi zo a si timi thu ah ziangsaw in si?`

Jesuh cu Pathian Thu, rak umcia, tisak ah rungcang ih, kan lakih um a si ahcun Jesuh cu Pathian in A minung pawl hnenih thucah a peknak ah asilole tongkaa ih thu a simnak pawl ah mi thupi zet a si ding tinak a si. Zianglam khal le tlang zetih Jesuh a khihhmuh lem mi thucah a kengtu profet pawl khal hi Thiang Thlarau ih thawtkhum mi an si thotho.

`Vanlam boruak hi ziangmi tluk ih kau saw a si timi ruat aw, cumi pawl a tuahtu Jesuh cu kan hrangih a thitu hi a si timi tla hi vun ruat aw. Cumi cun ziangmi ruahsannak ropi zet saw a lo pek?`
