---
title:  Profet thuphuan cun Bible a nemhnet
date:   21/12/2026
---

Bible hi profet thuphuannak laksawng hmangin Thiang Thlarau ih a hrinsuah mi a si. 2 Piter 1:21 ah Pathian in a hmandan fiangzet in a tarlang: “Ziangahtile profet thuphuannak cu hlanlai khal ah minung ih duhnak ihsin a ra suak dah lo: Thlarau Thianghlim ih a hruai dan vekin Pathian ih mithianghlim pawlin an rak sim sawn a si”.

`Siar ding: 2 Timote 3:16,17. Ziangtin saw himi thu cun Cathianghlim thuneihnak le thawtkhum a sidan thu uar zetih a rel?`

Bible tel lo cun minung cu leitlun sualnak ih tlaknak lamzin ihsin pialdan ding thei loin kan um ding ih, cumi cu siatralnak ding thil a si. Tuisan ahcun hmuntin ah Bible a um thlang ih, phun dangdang khal a suak thlang, asinan cuvek thil cu a rak um ringring dah lo. Thimnak ah Jesuh san ahkhan Thukham Hlun cazual siar ding a um ko, asinan puithiam le rabbis pawl lawngih siar theih mi a si. Mi menmen pawl cun Cathianghlim hi an duhduh in an siar thei lo. Culai san ahcun Pathian kamsuak thu pawl hi an mankhung tuk lawmmam.

`Siar ding: Luka 24:27. A thawhsal hnuah Jesuh cun dungthluntu beidong zet pahnih hnen ahcun A tumtah mi an theihthiam theinak dingah ziangmi bomnak aw a hman?`

Jesuh cu profet ropi bik a si ringring – Amah lawng hi Thu cu a si (Johan 1:1-3) —cule Cathianghlim a khaisang zet (Johan 7:38). Simcianak laksawng ih thlawsuahnak man nei bik cu Bible nemhngetnak hi a si.

“Pathian kamsuak Aw a si vekin Thiang Thlarau hmangih kaihhruai mi, Pathian thu thlunduhnak a neitu pawl cun nitin zir ding an si ih, nitin in thlarau lam theihnak le huham an dong, cumi cu Thiang Thlarau laksawng sungih um zumtu dik pawl hmuahhmuah hrang ih pek mi thil a si.”—Ellen G. White, in Signs of the Times, March 8, 1910. Himi simcianak laksawng cun Cathianghlim a khaisang ringring. Nunau le mipa pawl hnenah an zumnak le takram ih an tuah dingmi thil umsun a si vekin Pathian kamsuak thu lamah a hruai ringring theu.

`Ziangruangah saw Jesuh khan zo a si timi a langter hlanah a hmaisabik ah Cathianghlim a khihhmuh? Cumi cun THU cu kan hrangih thuam hnget zet a sinak thuhla ziangsaw in sim?`
