---
title:  Hngak rero caan ih Profet pakhat
date:   10/12/2026
---

`Siar ding: Thuphuan 1:1-3. Ziangtuah dingin Johan kawh a si?`

Khristian hmaisa pawl cun Jesuh a ratsalnak ding thutiam an rak hngakhlap zet. Tirthlah Johan cun meiro parih Jesuh a kaisoh lai a hmu ih, hivek ciah in A rungtum leh ding tii vancungmi ih simfiangnak khal a thei (Tirh 1:10, 11). Tu ahcun Khristian hmaisa pawl cun an theih lomi an hmaiton thlang. Si e, Jesuh cun A rungtum sal ding thu a rel. Asinan ziangtik ah a si ding?

Johan cu Patmos tikulh ah saltang ih a um lai ah Pathian cun Jesuh veihnih ratsalnak thutiam thawn pehpar aw in a run bia: “Ngai hnik, Anih cu meiro thawn a ra ding, mit hmuahhmuah in an hmu ding ih, Amah rak suntlang tu khal in an hmu ding” (Thup 1:7NKJV). Johan cun sancemnak ding hminsinnak hrangih thil a thleng dingmi thuphuan tamzet a dong a si. Kawhhran pasarih hrangih nunsimnak le forhfialnak a neih ih tlun ah(Thuphuan 2, 3), thuphuan cabu ahhin thuthennak(Thup. 14:6-12), leitlun sancemnak(Thuphuan 20), Khrih ratsalnak(Thup. 14:14-16), le leilung sersiam thar salnak tivek pawl langnak khal a tel a si (Thuphuan 21, 22).

Johan cun amah le mah cu “Kei Johan, nan unau khal a simi, le Jesuh Khrih ih harsatnak, le lalram le tuarnak tawmpitu” (Thup. 1:9) tin a ti aw. Tuarnak cu ziangvek a si ti a theifiang zet, rak hngak tento ih tuarnak lawng asi lo ih, Pathian kamsuak thu phuangsuaknak le Jesuh Khrih ih thutheihternak rak neih ruangih a ra dingmi tuarnak pawl khal an si.

Thuphuan cabu pumpi ahhin Johan cu zoh hliah dingih sawm a si ih, a hmuhmi pawl ngan dingih thupek a si. Bung netabik ahkhan “Cun kei Johan in hi thil pawl hi ka hmu ih, ka thei” tiah a ti (Thup. 22:8, NKJV). Johan cu caan neta ih thil thleng pawl theihpi tu profet a si ih, langnak ih a hmuhmi pawl cu amai san lai ih kawhhran pawl hnenah sim dingih fial mi a si (Thup. 22:16). Anih cu leilung thuanthu ih tikcu caan thupi zet a si mi Jesuh Khrih a rat hmaisabiknak thu a phuang rero tu Tipil Petu Johan vek ciah a si (Johan 1:6-8).

Johan vek ciah in kannih khal theihpitu dingih kawh kan si. Jesuh ih thutheihternak (Thup 19:10) – Profet pawl hmangih amai thuhla theihternak in pek mi pawl cu kan kilhim ding a si. Johan vekin langnak kan dong lo men thei, asinan kan nunnak ahhin A hnatuan theihpitu le in tlennak ding hminsinnak kan hngak phah ah Khrih ratsalnak ding hi thinsau zet kan hngak ding a si.

`Kan hnen ih pek mi eng pawl hmuahhmuah kha vun ruat aw. Tamzet pek kan si zo ruangah kan parah ziang tuanvo saw a ret?`
