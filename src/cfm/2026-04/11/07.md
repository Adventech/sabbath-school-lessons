---
title:  Profet simcianak anka
date:   11/12/2026
---

Pathian cun A profet pawl hmangin minung pawl thawn pehtlaihnak a nei theu. Himi cu santhuanthu ih thil thupi zet ahhin a dik mi a si. Pathian in caan neta ih a minung pawl hnenah thu a rel ve ding a si timi cu ruahsannak um zet a si ko lo sawm?

Millerite Adventist pawl in October 22, 1844 ih “beidonnak ropi” an ton tikah Pathian cun zumtu beidong pawl hrangih thucah petu dingah le profet hnatuan dingin Ellen Hermon (White) a ko. Langnak a hmuh hmaisabik mi cu an riahsiatnak hnemnak hi a si.

A thupi zet mi a dang pakhat cu Adventist casuahnak a um thoknak le Sabbath hmangtu Adventist pawlkom din hngetnak hi a si. An casuahnak le hnatuannak hmangin himi Sabbath hmangtu pawl cu kum 1860 so hrawng ahkhan member thawngtel in an pung man. Vancungmi pathum ih thucah (Thuphuan 14: 6-12) thaten phuangsuak theinak dingah an komkhawm awk a tul a si. Pawlkom awknak ruahnak hi a hrekkhat pawl in an dokalh lai ah Pathian cun Ellen G. White hmangin hmainor dingin a run fial. Cutin May 21, 1863 ahkhan Seventh-day Adventist kawhhran cu zalen zetin a ding suak.

A cui kum thotho ah Pathian in simcianak laksawng cu zumtu pawl ih harhdamnak thazang peknak ah a hmang. Seventh-day Adventist tam zet cu an hriselnak a tha lo ih, nun hrisel zet khal an hmang lo. Kum 1863 le 1865 ah Ellen G. White ih harhdamnak langnak pahnih hmangin himi lamzin cu thleng danglam in a um a si. Seventh-day Adventist pawl cu harhdamnak le hrahdamnak nun thehlartu ah an hung cang.

Kum 1870 ihsin 1890 tiang Ellen G. White cu Adventist fimzirnak lam ih thansohnak lam ah nasa zetih a tuantu a si. A fimthiam zirnak (ca zirnak, a cangthei mi, le thlarau lam) ih a khawruahnak pawl cu nuamte’n an cohlang ih an hmang. A tarlang vekin: “Fimzirnak diktak timi sullam cu zirnak lamzin pakhat ih a rel mi hnakin a nasa sawn a si. Cumi sullam cu a tui hringnun hrangih timlamnak ai hnakin a thupi sawn tinak a si. Cumi cu taksa, thinlung le thlarau lam huham thawn rem aw zetih thansohnak a si.”—Education, p. 13.

Kum 1880 ahkhan rundamnak thuhla ah Seventh-day Adventist Pathian thuzirnak ahhin tuanvo thupi zet a rak nei. Adventist pawl in daan an ngai thupi zetnak hnuai ahhin zumnak thawngin Pathian dingfelnak le Zangfahnak ih a thupi hi uar zetin a rel. Kum 1901 ihsin 1903 tiang Kawhhran in leitlun ih a hnatuannak tha zet ih a thehsuak theinak dingah kawhhran pawlkom ih remthat awknak pawlah a bawm a si.

A tawi zawng cun kan theih cio vekin Ellen G. White ih hnatuannak tel lo cun tuini ah Seventh-day Adventist kawhhran timi hi a um lo ding. Anih cu harsat caan ih Pathian thucah kengtu a si.
