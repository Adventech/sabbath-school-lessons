---
title:  Pathian thuphuangtu pawl
date:   01/10/2026
---

Sual a luh hnu ih Pathian in minung a biakdan thuhla thawn pehpar aw in a hnuai ih Bible cang pawl in ziangsaw an tarlang? (Siar ding: Heb. 1:1, 2; Rom. 1:19, 20; Mipum. 12:6; 2 Tim. 3:16, 17; Johan 14:7-9; Johan 10:27-30)

Sual um cing khal in Pathian cun a tlusia mi minung thawn pehtlaih awknak ding lamzin tamzet a hmu. Kan hnenah Pathian amah rori a tarlang awknak lamzin pakhat cu a sersiammi pawl ihsin a si. Lei le van hi A cangvaihnak mangbangza phuanglang awknak huham nasa zet a si. Mifim pawl cun van boruak ih nunnak “olsam zetih” a umdan pawl an zir tikah an mangbang zet. Asinan van boruak khal in tluksiatnak ih rah suak mi cu a tarlang ih, thil nung pawl hmuahhmuah lakah thihnak a um hi zohthimnak huham cak bik a si.

Pathian in minung a biaknak lamzin dang cu a profet pawl ihsin a si – thuphuangtu dingih a hril ngah mi pawl an si. Thimnak ah Pathian cun Noah, Abraham, Moses, le Daniel pawl hnenah thu a sim, A minung pawl hrangih a duhmi pawl a tarlang a si. Pathian profet thucah pawl cun Pathian in a fale pawl a theihngilh lo ih, an hnenah a um ringring lai a si timi theihternak dingah a si. A hrekkhat profet pawl ih cangan mi pawl hi Bible ah um khal sehla, midang pawl cun Bible bu zianghman an ngan ve lo.

Ziangkhal sehla, profet tamzet ih tongkam pawl cu Bible thu hmangih pehtlaih awknak nei mi an si (2 Tim. 3:16, 17). Bible cu Amah Pathian a phuanawknak a si. Amai tongkam ihsin zo a si timi kan zir thei ih, cem net neilo a duhdawtnak kan hmu thiam ih, kan nuntu khawsakdan ding pawl in kaihruaidan khal ka hmusuak thei a si.

Cui hlei ah Bible ihsin zosaw kan si, khuitawk ihsin kan ra, khuitawk ah kan feh ding timi pawl kan theithiam a si. Cutawk ahhin kan nun ih Pathian tumtah mi pawl kan hmu thei a si.

Asinan Pathian ih in biaknak thinlung nehtu bik lamzin cu Jesuh Khrih ihsin a si, anih cu Bible sungih kan hnen ih a phuang lang awtu a si. Pathian in Eden hmuan ih Adam le Evi a hawl vek ciah in Khrih cu “a hlo mi pawl hawl ih rundam dingin” (Luka 19:10) a ra a si. Jesuh cun, “Keimah I hmutu cun Pa a hmu zo a si” tiah a ti (Johan 14:9, NKJV). A nunnak, thihnak le thawhsalnak hmangin Khrih cun Eden hmuan ih Adam le Evi hnen ih thutiamkam cu a famkimter a si. Amah ihsin kumkhaw nunnak kan neih theinak dingah A ra ih, kan ai-awh in a ra thi a si.

Varam ih Jesuh a kai hnuah Thiang Thlarau cun “thutak hmuahhmuah sungih” in kaihruai dingin a pehzom ih, kan hnenah, “thil a ra thleng dingmi” (Johan 16:13, NIV)in sim a si. (John 16:13, NIV).

`Jesuh, A duhdawtnak, a nuncan ziaza, a pumpek awknak pawl cun Pa Pathian cu zovek a si timi kan hnenah a tarlang timi na theihnak ihsin ziangmi thlangamnak saw na ngah?`
