### Tuizarh zirlai hrang siar ding
Sem. 1:27, 28; Sem. 3:1-8; Luka 15:11-32; Sem. 3:14-24; Suah. 19:16-22; Johan 10:27-30.

> <p>Bible cangken</p>
> “A liam zomi caan ah voi tampi profet pawl hmangin pupa pawl hnenih a phunphun thu a rak simtu Pathian in, Hi ni netabik pawl ah a Fapa hmangin kan hnenah thu in sim zo, amah cu thil hmuahhmuah rocotu ah a ruat ih, amah ihsi thothoin leilung pawl a sersiam (Hebru 1:1, 2, NKJV).

Digital technology cun hmun hla zetih um rualpi le laicin pawl thawn kan hmuh awk theinak dingin thil a tuah. Himi hi thil tha zet a si ko. Asinan a ngaingai ih ton awk tluk hi tha hi a um cuang lo! Kan hmuhawk khawm tikah hleicetnak thil a um cuang, kan kuah aw ton ih, hmaiton rori in thu kan rel.

Tuizarh ahcun minung cu anmah sersiamtu thawn hmaiton rori in laicin awknak nei thei dingih sersiam mi an si timi kan zir ding. Ziangkhal sehla, Sual cun cui pehtlaih awknak cu hlanlai ihsin a siatsuah thluh. A duhdawtmi a sersiam thilnung pawl thawn pehtlaih awknak tuah thasal dingin, Pathian cun kanmah in biak tlang theinak dingah lamzin lamzet in tuahsak. Minung thawn pehtlaih awk theinak dingah Pathian cun mi hleice pawl a hril theu. Cumi cu profet tiih theih mi, kan hnen ih A thucah tarlang theutu pawl an si. Hi profet pawl in Bible sung ih cangan mi an nei theu, asinan cuvek cun a si thluh ringring cuang lo.

Pathian ih tumtahmi netabik cu kan sungih a hmuihmel, sualnak ih a siatsuahmi hmuihmel cu kan sungih tuahtha sal ding kha a si. Himi ciah hi rundam khawkhannak timi sullam ngaingai cu a si: Tuahthat salnak, hi tuahthatsalnak cu Pathian in, “a sungih dingfelnak a umnak hmun leithar le vanthar” (2 Pet. 3:13, NKJV) a tuah tikah a famkim thluh ding a si.

Cumi hlan cu Pathian cun in bia ringring, cui thil cu semrem zetih a feh theinak dingah a profet pawl a hmang rero lai.
