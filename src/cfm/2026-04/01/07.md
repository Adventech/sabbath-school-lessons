Pathian ih mi a pehtlaih pawlkomnak cu Bible tikcu caan lawng ahhin a net lo. Hmaizarh ih zirlai kan hmuh ding mi ahhin Pathian in minung a biakdan le Khrih a ra tum hlan tiang a tongkam pawl cu tuisan ih lamzin tamzet in phuanglang a si ding timi pawl kan zir ding.

Ellen Gould Harmon White le a phirpi Elizabeth cu Maine ram Gorham ah November 26, 1827 ahkhan an suak. Annih cu Eunice le Robert Harmon ih a hrinmi fanau 8 lakih nauhak bik an si. Kum reilote hnu ah an sungkua in Main khawpi Porland ah an vai. Cutawk ahcun Robert Harmon cu a sumdawnnak lukhuh a tuahmi cu kauh ter vivo a tum. Kum 9 a kim ah Ellen cun natnak a nei ih, a taksa le ngaihtuahnak pawl in a tuar phah zet. A inn kiangih park ih a um lai ah a tlawngkai pi pakhat in thinheng zetin a hmaiah lungto in a deng, cutin Ellen cu zianghman thei loin zarh ziang maw zat a um. Himi cangsualnak ruangah a zirnak a cawlhsan phah ih, hriselnak lamah harsatnak a tuar phah. A hmai ih tuarnak a um laiah Ellen cun a langdan pawl khalah theithiamnak a nei mal zet ih, harsatnak nasa zet a tuar.

Hivek ih a um lai ah Ellen Harmon cun a thlarau lam nun ding hrang khua a ruat thok. Pathian tihzettu a si ih, vanram co ban lo dingin a ruat aw. Cuvek tihnak thinlung a neih rero lai ah Millerite thurin a si mi Jesuh voihnihnak a rungtum cing ding timi cu Harmon sungkua pawl cun an pawm. Kum 1842 ah hnimphum rori in Methodist kawhhran ah tipilnak a neih hnu cun Ellen cun Pathian duhdawtnak dongtlak in a thei aw lo. “Kumkhua hloral” zo in a rak zum aw zet (Life Sketches of Ellen G. White, p. 29; le pp. 20-31).

Hivek khawruahnak a neih laiah tleirawlte Ellen cun sunmang pakhat a nei, cumi cu Pathian thuhla ih a ruahnak danglamter tu an si. Asinan Methodist thuthangtha simtu Levi Stockman a ton hlan tiang Pathian duhdawtnak le ngaihsaknak ngaingai hi a theifiang thei lo. Beidong zetih a um lai caan ah Pathian in a run bia vekin a thei. “Atu ahcun ka thinlung ah zumnak a rung lut” tiah a ngan. “Pathian duhdawtnak cu simfiang theih rual loin ka theifiang ngah, Ka sualnak cu ngaidam a si zo timi khal A Thlarau cun in theihpi a si. Pa Pathian ka hmuhdan cu a dang tuk. Tu ahcun a minung pawl hranhram ih a thu thlun dingih a nortu ai hnakin zangfahnak le lainatnak thawn a khatmi nu le pa vekin ka zoh thlang. Ka thinlung ah Amah ka duhdawtnak a thang so vivo.”—Life Sketches of Ellen G. White, p. 39. Cumi ahcun Ellen G White cun “thih hnu umdan” thurin a theithiam ih catbang loin kumkhua in hell meisa kang rero mi a um lo ding timi khal nuamnai teten a theifiang. Pathian a theihthiam tharnak le a duhdawtnak cun a nuntu khawsakdan pawl a thleng thluh.
