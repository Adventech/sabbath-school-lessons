---
title:  Profet dik theih dan
date:   12/10/2026
---

Israel ih thuanthu hmaisa lam ahkhan profet dik lo pawl an um timi nemhnget a si (Daan. 18:20). Curuangah Pathian cun a miphun pawl cu zo pawl hi profet dik an si timi theihnak pe lo in a tanta dah lo.

Pathian ih phuangsuak awknak le thuthangtha dongtu an si vekin profet dik pawl cun Pathian thucah cu rinum zet, dik zet le pumpe aw zetin an sim theu, khuimaw caan ahcun huham nasa zet nei pawl lakah zianghman lo men vek ah tla an tuah aw.

`Siar ding: 1 Siang 22:1-14. A rinum zetmi profet pawl ih hnatuandan ziangtin saw in sim?`

Siangpahrang pakhat cun profet pakhat thuhla ah, “Anih cu ka hua; ziangah tile anih in ka thuhla ah thu tha phuang loin thilthalo hlir a phuang theu, a ti” tiah a ti’ ” (1 Siang 22:8, NKJV). Hitawk ahhin siangpahrang thuhla ai hnakin profet thuhla a rel tam sawn a si. Anih (profet) cu thudik phuang ding a tih lo zettu a si, siangpahrang hmai hmanah mithmai tha co duh men in thil a tuah lo, a sim dingmi cu tlang zetin a rel.

Mikaiah cun siangpahrang hnen ih thudik a phuang tikah profet dik lo 400 pawl kha a dokalh a si. Siangpahrang sualral zet Ahab le a nupi Jezebel hmaiah Bawipa ih thucah a don mi cu danglam ter dingin an nor rero lai hmanah Mikaiah cun ziangtin an rak cohlang ding timi ruat buai loin Pathian kamsuak thudik cu a phuang thotho.

`Siar ding: 1 Samuel 9:3-14. Himi thu in midang pawl in Pathian ih profet rinum zet pawl an hmuhdan thuhla ziangsaw in sim?`

Bible sungah profet pawl ih nuntu ziaza tha tahthimnak tampi a um. Himi thuhla ahcun Saul cu a pa Kish in a laak hlo pawl hawl dingin a thlah. Saul thawn khual a tlawngtu siahhlawh cun a hawl lai mi bansan lo dingin ruahnak a pe, ziangahtile, “Hi khawpi sungah Pathian mi pakhat a um ih upat tlak zet mi a si,” (1 Sam. 9:6 NKJV) tiah a ti. Hitawk ih Samuel ih simfiangnak cun Pathian profet si dingih tlinnak a tarlang. Bible sungih profet dik pawl cu “ Pathian mi” tiih kawh an si, Amai ziaza zohthim dingih hril mi an si. Cuti rualrual in “Pathian mi” lakih hrekkhat pawl cu profet an si men ruangah sualnak zianghman nei thei lo minung cu an si hran cuang lo.

`“Pathian mi” tiih ngaisan a hlawh zet mi pawl lak ahhin zo saw sualnak zianghman nei lo ih na theih mi?`
