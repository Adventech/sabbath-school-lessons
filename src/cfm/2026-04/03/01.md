### Tuizarh zirlai hrang siar ding
James 5:17, 18; 1 Siang 19:1-16; 1 Siang 22:1-14; Suah. 15:20; 2 Siang 22:11-14; Jer. 14:1-15.

Bible Cangken: “Jehoshafat cu a ding ih, Maw Judah le Jerusalem luahtu pawl tla, i rak ngai uh; Bawipa nan Pathian cu zum uhla din hnget nan si ding; a profet pawl hi zum uhla nan lam a tluang ding, a ti’ “ (2 Sansiarnak 20:20, NKJV).

The Global Positioning System (GPS) cun khualtlawng pawl cu an umnak, an fehnak ding thuhla pawl a theihter ih tlun ah tihnung um thil pawl khal rinrinnak a pek. Thlarau lam khuatlawng pawl khal in tu ah le hmailam caan hrang khalah vanram kan thlen theinak dingah “vanram lamzin” kawh hmuhtu kan tul a si. A liamcia tikcu caan ih Pathian ih a tuahdan lamzin pakhat cu Thukham Hlun Profet pawl hmangin a si. An thuanthu ah, an thuron peknak ah, simcianak ah, biazai le hlaphuahmi tivek pawl ah mipi pawl hrangih Pathian thucah kengtu profet pawl thuhla tamzet kan hmu.

Tuizarh ahcun Thukham Hlun ih profet a simfiangnak pawl kan zoh ding, himi Pathian siahhlawh hrekkhat pawl ih an phuangsuak mi ihsin simcianak laksawng theithiam dingah in bawm ding a si.

Kan hmuh vekin hi minung pawl hi kanmah vekih kekkuai thei, tihphangnak nei thei le thinheng thei, beidong thei zet pawl an si. An hrangah hringnun hi thil ol-ai lo zet a si, kanmah vekin harsatnak le tuarnak ih khat thluh mi an si ve.

`Cun Pathian ih siahhlawh dingah a hleice ih kawhmi minung malte kan zohnak ihsin ziangsaw kan zir thei ding?`
