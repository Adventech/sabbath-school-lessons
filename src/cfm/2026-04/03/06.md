---
title:  Profet deu
date:   15/10/2026
---

Pathian in ziang a tuah khal le Satan cun a lem (lamcannak) a rak nei ringring theu. Cuvek ciah cu profet pawl par khalah a thleng. Israel pawl cun profet dik le profet deu (diklo) an hmaiton theu. A thupi bik mi cu an danglamnak theihngah hi a si.

`Siar ding: Jeremiah 14:1-15. Hitawk ah ziangthil a cang, cule Bawipa in Amai hmin in “profet pawl” tiih thudik lo simtu pawl ziangtin a ruat?`

Jeremiah 14 ahkhan Pathian cun Jeremiah hnenah Judah pawl hrangih thuthennak thucah a pe suak, thuthennak cu ralnam (raldonak), rilrawng le natnak pawl vekin a ra thleng ding. Cui tikcu caan ih profet deu pawl cun Jeremiah ih thucah cu an dokalh zet. Jeremiah cun fiah zetin simfiangnak a nei: “Bawipa in ka hnenah, Profet pawl in ka hmin in thuphan an rak sim: annih cu ka thlah hai lo, ka fial hai lo ih, ka be hai fawn lo: annih in a diklo mi langnak le hmailam thu phuannak, le thulolak, le an thinlung ih bumnak pawl an lo simmi a si, tiah i ti’ ” (Jer. 14:14).

Profet deu pawl hi an dik lo thei zet, ziangahtile an thusimmi pawl cu an biakmi a dik lomi “pathian pawl” hnen ihsin an ngah theu a si (Jer. 23:13, 14). Pathian cun ralrinnak a pe ih, “ ‘annih cu ka thlah hai lo, ka fial hai lo ih, ka be hai fawn lo’ “ tiah a ti(Jer. 14:14, NKJV). Annih pawl cu “ ‘anmai thinlung lala ih bummi profet an si ” (Jer. 23:26, NKJV). Ezekiel cu “ ‘anmai’ palung suak thu a phuangtu profet pawl” (Ezek. 13:2, NKJV) dokalh in thuphuan dingih fial a si.

Profet dik lo pawl cun mi tamsawn ih hmuhdan pawl an thlun. Himi cu 1 Siang 22 ih thil thleng mi a si. Siangpahrang Ahab ih thupeknak ruangah “profet”mipum 400 an pungkhawm aw (1 Siang 22:6). Tongkam khat lawngin Ramoth-Gilead do dingah siangpahrang hnenah ruahnak an pek. Ahab cun Asherah profet pawl hnen ihsin profet simcianak pawl a don hnuah Judah Siangpahrang Jehoshafat hnenah thu a rawn. Jehoshafat cun Ahab hnenah profet Mikaiah hnenah raldonak ih feh ding le ding lo thuah ruahnak la dingin a ngen: “Cun Mikaiah ko dingih feh palai in a hnenah, Tu ah ngaihnik, profet pawlih tongkam cun bangreptein siangpahrang hnenah thu tha an phuang: zangfahte’n, na thu cu an lakih mi pakhat ih thu vek si hram sehla, thutha sim hram aw, a ti.’ ” (1 Siang 22:13).

Mikaiah in thuphuanper dingih an nor ciamconak tonnun cu Asherah profet deu hmangih nornak ummi vek khi a bang zet, asinan Mikaiah cun mi tamsawn ih hmuhdan hi a thlun duh lo; cumi ai hnakin a rel dingmi Pathian ih a sim ding a rak hngak a si (1 Siang 22:14).

`Profet na si in, si lo khal awla, mipi tamsawn ih thlunmi rak thlun hi a ol ringring. Nunphung siatsuah thei a si tiih kan ruat tikah, ziangah hi thil cu ruahnak tha a si dah lo?`
