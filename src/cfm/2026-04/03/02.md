---
title:  Profet pawl ih minung sinak
date:   11/10/2026
---

Kan dung zarh ih kan hmuh vekin, dokalhnak um cing khal in Bawipa cun Israel sungah hnatuan thupi zet tuan dingin Elijah a hmang. Amah hmangin Bawipa ih a tuahsuak mi cu mangbangza a si. Asinan cui thil cu ziangsaw a si?

`James 5:17,18 ih Profet Elijah a simfiangdan kha vun ruat aw. Elijah cu “kanmah vek sinak nei mi a si” (NKJV) tiih a relmi sullam ziangsaw a si ding?`

Karmel tlang ahkhan Elijah cun hlawhtlinnak a nei (1 Siang 18); Pathian cun amah hmangin nasa zetin hna a tuan, cule Thukham Hlun thuanthu ih mangbangza a simi lakih pakhat a thleng ngah riai: Eilijah ih tongkam aw khawk ruangah van ihsin meisa a rung tla (1 Siang 18:38)! Hivek thil a thleng hnuah hi Pathian minung(profet) hi a lungawi zet ding tin na ruat ko ding.

`Siar ding: 1 Siang 19:1-16. Himi cun Pathian profet dinhmun thuhla ziangsaw in sim?`

“Maw Bawipa, tu ah ka nunnak hi la mai aw; ziangahtile kei hi ka pale pawl hnakin ka tha sawn cuang lo a si, a ti’ ” (1 Siang 19:4 NKJV). Himi cu thinlung natnak nei mi pakhat ih tuarnak vek tla khi a bang zet. Ziangah? Karmel tlang ih thil a thleng mi pawl hi olsam zetih thlun theih thil a si lo. Asinan hitawk ahhin thil ziangmaw a um.

Cui thil olsam lo zet cu amai tongkam ahhin hmuh a theih ko ding: “ ‘Ralkap pawl ih Bawipa Pathian hrangah thikthusia zetmi ka si: ziangahtile Israel faate pawl in na thukham cu an tlansan, na biaktheng pawl cu an ti cim thluh ih, na profet pawl cu ralnam in an that; cun keimah, keimah lawng hi ka tang ih, ka nunnak la dingin in hawl a si, a ti’ ” (1 Siang 19:10, NKJV). Hivek tongkam cu Pathian hnatuan hlawhsam zetih a theih awknak awkam vek tla khi a bang zet. Ziangtluk in zuam khal sehla, fehsualnak cu a um ringring lai. A langdan ahcun mi pakhat in amah lawng in thil thupi zet tuah dingih zuam vek khi a bang. Hitawk ahhin a netnak cu ziangvek a si khal le hi Pathian minung ih a kekkuai olnak le minung a sinak kan hmu thei. Phundang in kan sim ding asile Elijah cu “kanmah vekih sinak nei” mi a si.

`Na nunnak ah lole na zuamnak ahhin “hlawhsam” nei vekih theih awk hi ziangah a olsam zet? Beidon caan ih in bawmtu ding ziangmi Bible thutiam a um?`
