---
title:  Nunau profet
date:   14/10/2026
---

`Siar ding: Suah 15:20, Thuthentu 4:4, 2 Siang 22:14, le Nehemiah 6:14. Himi pawl hin profet daan le dun pawl ziangsaw in sim?`

Pathian in profet dingah nunau a ko timi cu ca thianghlim sungah nemhngetnak hngetkoh zet a um. (Hebru tongfang nevi’ah[Suah 15:20] le Grik tongfang prophetis [Luka 2:36] a hmandan hi zoh aw). Mikah 6:4 ah Miriam cu mipi pawl hnen ih thlah mi Moses le Aaron pawl thawn hmunkhat ih ret tlang mi a si. Miriam cu Israel pawl in Tipi Sen an kan tikih nehnak hla a saktu profet nunau vekih tarlang a si(Suah 15:20,21). Asinan hi profet nunau cu mipa profet pawl vekin sualnak nei lo cu a si riai lo, zahthlak zetin Moses le a nupi Ethopia nu parah thil a tuah ih, curuangah hi profet nunau cu dikzet in hremnak pek a si (Mipum 12:1-10).

Israel ah thuthentu hnatuan ih um Deborah thuanthu ahkhan a dang profet hnatuantu kan hmu thei (Thut. 4:1-4). A ngaingai te cun Deborah (Thut 4:4) cun Pathian hnen ihsin langnak a ngah timi nemhngetnak fiang zet a um (Thut. 4:4-9, 14-22). A nehnak hla (Thut 5:2-31) cu Pathian telve-nak theihpitnak a si ih, cumi caan ih san thuanthu ah miphun hrangah Pathian thuhla ah thupitnak thuk zet a nei.

`Siar ding: 2 Siang 33:11-14. Ziangah Siangpahrang Josiah ih milai pawl Huldah ah an feh? Himi in miphun sungih a tuanvo thupi zet thuhla ziangsaw in sim?`

Huldah “profet nunau” cu Siangpahrang Josiah ih thu kungron mipa pawl lakih mi thupi bik pakhat a si ih (2 Siang 22:14, 2 San. 34:22), siangpahrang hnen ih “Daan bu” (2 Siang 22:8 NKJV)(Daanpeksalnak a si ding tiih zummi a si) ih tongkam ummi pawl an siar thluh hnuah, cui tongkam pawl letin siangpahrang cun, “a angki a thlek (2 Siang 22:11, NKJV). Himi cu a thupi zetmi thil a si, cutin siangpahrang cun Huldah hnenah a mi pawl a vun thlah. Huldah cun Judah parih thuthennak a ra thleng dingmi thuhla ah Pathian hnen ihsin thucah a dong zo. Huldah ih profet thucah mi ihsin Josiah cun remthatnak a fehpi ih, cutin a minung pawl parih Pathian thuthennak cu a tlai ter a si.

`Mipa profet, nunau profet pawl hi Pathian ih kawh mi si khal sehla, annih pawl khal zangfahnak tulzettu misual an si thotho. An thuanthu ihsin ziangmi ruahsannak saw na lak thei ding?`
