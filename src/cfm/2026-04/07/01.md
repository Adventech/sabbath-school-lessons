### Tuizarh zirlai hrang siar ding
Mipum. 13:26-30; Jer. 36:20-26; 2 San. 36:15, 16; Matt. 23:30-35; 1 Kor. 10:6-12.

> <p>Bible cangken</p>
> “Tu ah hi thil hmuahhmuah hi zohthim dingah an parah a rak thleng: san netnak a ra thleng cuahco mi kanmah pawl nunsim-nak ah ngan in an um” (1 Korin 10:11).

Tuisan ah mi hrekkhat pawl cun “anmai thudik” – an hmuhton mi, an ruahdan, an tuldan le an ramri kham dan pawl, an zumnak pawl ziangvek a sikhal ah rel suak dingin an forh aw theu. “Thudik” ziangkim hi a dik mi an si ih (ruahnak tiangin a feh thei) daiten umpi men ding a si lo. Asinan hivek “thudik” pawl hmuahhmuah hi Thutak, Jesuh Khrih le a kamsuak thu thawn an kalhawk ahcun ziangthil a cang?

Thil a cang mi cu mi hrekkhat cun Bible ih thutak cu an pawm thiam ih, an thil tuahsualnak ihsin an kirsal; asinan mi hrekkhat pawl cun an thuphan relmi ah an um ringring lai. Mi hrekkhat cun thutak lam an hoi theinak dingah an ziaza an thleng ih, midang cun an rak dokalh. Hivek pawl cu minung san thuanthu ah a rak um rero zo.

Bible ih profet pawl cu mipi pawl ih theih duh lomi thucah phuangtu pawl tiih kawh an si theu. Cumi ahcun mi tamzet cun an relsia, an hmuhsuam ih, thihnak tiang le thah ding tiangin an hringhro (Heb. 11:32-38). Hivek pawl hmuahhmuah um cing khal in Pathian profet pawl cun rinum zetin an thucah ken mi pawl an phuang rero, ziangahtile Pathian profet simcia kaihhruai awknak cu an hrang duhhril ding a si lo. Cumi cu Pathian minung pawl hrang nun himnak zirhawknak a si. Curuangah thucah cu phuang ih a um a tul, bulpak in ziangtluk in cemral hman sehla phuangsuah ding a si.
