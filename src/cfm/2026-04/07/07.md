---
title:  A donmi thucah cohlang in a phuangsuak
date:   13/11/2026
---

Ellen G. White Estate ih director hna rak tuandahtu Ellen G. White ih tupa Arthur White cun a pi khan a damsung ah langnak a zatel a dong ih, profet zanmang khal tamzet a dong tin a rel. Langnak a hmuh hi minute reilote sung tla a si ih, nazi tamzet khal a si, culai caan ahcun mi tamzet khalin an thei. Khuimaw caan ahcun amah a zumlotu pawl khal in mipi vantlang ih a phuanglangawk lai ahhin amah an zoh hliah theinak ding caan tha an nei ve theu.

Bible ih profet vek ciah in Ellen G. White cun langnak a hmuh laiah thil danglam zet a tong ve theu. Kum 1868 ahkhan James White cun Ellen G. White ih langnak a hmuh lai cu famkim zetin a simfiang. Anih cun “a kiangih thil ummi pawl zianghman a thei nawn lo”, a thaw a thaw lo ih, a tit khal(tha) pawl cu “an khot zet” ih “an semrem thluh”; a cangvaihnak le kutke pawl cu, “zalen le mawi zet an si” cule a mit a meng ringring. Langnak a hmuh theh veten zianghman a hmu thei lo lawk, asinan a mit a siat cuang lo. Langnak cu ziangtluk a rei ding ti zohman in an thei lo, amah Ellen G White rori hman in a thei lo. Langnak cu ziangtikah a um ding, ziangtluk a rei ding ti hi zohman ih thunun theih mi a si lo, langnak cu Amah Ellen White rori hmanih beisei loin a um theu a si (zoh aw, James White, Life Incidents, pp. 271-274.)

Bible san vek ciah in mipi ih thucah rak dondan cu an bangaw lo ruri. Hrekkhat cun Pathian thuron cu an pawm ih, a hrek cun an hnong. Amah Ellen G. White khal in, “Pathian in A minung pawl nunsim tu dingah in ret a si…ka parih phurrit a ret vek ciah in himi thucah pek mi pawl cu ziangtin an zohkhen ding ti ah tuanvo neitu ah a tuah ding. Himi hnatuan nuam lo cu kei hrangah ka hril mi a si lo. Himi hnatuan hi milai ih duhdawtnak le thangthatnak i petu hna a si lo… I tlentu ih hmin le cahnak ahhin ka tuah theitawk cu ka tuan ding. Pathian Thlarau ih in fial vekin ralrin peknak, thuron le nunsimnak, forhfialnak tivek ka nei ding. Minung ih an theih ding lole an rak hnong a si hmanah ka tuan thotho ding. Ka hnatuan cu keimah le keimah hnangamnak pekawk hi a si lo, asinan hnatuan in petu van ih um Pa Pathian ih duhdan in ka tuan ding a si.”—Testimonies for the Church, vol. 4, pp. 231, 232.

Lunglutza thil ummi cu a thucah a rak hnongtu pawl in Pathian thulam zirnak ruangah siloin anmai bulpak ih Ellen G. White duh lo ruangah le an zum thei lo ruangah a si tlangpi. Ellen G. White cun profet thusimnak ih a thilpek a zum lo tu pawl parah zangfahnak a nei zet. A ngaingai te cun cuvek minung pawl cu “an dinhmun an hmusuak ih, an lungkimnak le an dokalhnak an hmusuak hlan sung cu, “thinsaunak le unau duhdawtnak thawn” zohkhen ding a si ti a fiangter..”—Testimonies for the Church, vol. 1, p. 328.

Ellen G. White cun a profet thilpek a donmi cu Pathian in a hnenih a pek mi thucah ngaingai a si timi a zum hnget zet. Mipi in thinlung tlaitluang zetih thudik an hawl ahcun a thilpek donmi cu thudik hlir an si timi an ra hmu suak ding tihi a zum zet. Theihthiamnak thasawn an hawlnak ah Pathian in a kaihruai ding tiah a zum, asinan Pathian in “thudik lam ih kan zumnak a kilkhawi theinak dingah” hmuhsaknak tawkfang in pe a si tiah a ngan.”—Testimonies for the Church, vol. 4, p. 231.
