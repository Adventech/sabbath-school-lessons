---
title:  Pathian thucah kengtu parih thinhengnak
date:   08/11/2026
---

Pathian mihril hruaitu Moses cun tiamcia ram hawl dingih nelrawn fehtlang ih mi burpi a kaihhruainak ahkhan lungawinak le natnak a theifiang zet.

`Siar ding: Mipum 14:4-11. Hitawk ahhin ziangthil a cang? Ziangah mipi pawl cu Moses le Aaron parah an thinheng?`

Himi bungcang hi fiangdeuh ih kan vun siar asile Israel fate pawl cun Pathian rinsannak an neih lo ruangah Pathian parah a thinheng zet ti a tarlang. Pathian thuneihnak le ukawknak netabik aiawhtu minung pahnih Moses le Aaron cu miphun thinhengnak ih hmuitin mi bik ih an can hi thil olsam zet a si. Taksa le ruahnak (thinlung) himnak hi minung zate ih kan neih duh ciomi le kan tul zetmi a si. Hringhronak simaw, asilole kan thinlung le taksa harhdamnak lam ih tihnung um zet ziang simaw kan theih ahcun kan thinheng zet thei ih, kan thinlung a buai zet thei fawn.

`Siar ding: Mipum 13:26-30. Israel fate pawl ih an tih zet mi ziangsaw a si? Kaleb in an hnenah ziangvek forhfialnak tongkam saw a sim?`

Israel fale pawl cun Tiamcia Ram an luah dingmi ih harsatnak thuhla ahhin tihphannak an neih zet, “asinan mipi pawl cun an thinlung ah zumlonak hmunram an ret tikah Satan ih kut hnuai ah an um ih, khuitawk tiang a hruai hai ding ti cu zohman in an thei lo.”—Ellen G. White, Patriarchs and Prophets, p. 389. Himi ahhin Moses cun hruaitu sinak ih harsatnak fehtlang pi thei lo ding khawp a tong a si. Mipi thinheng pawl cu Moses le Aaron parah an tuan ih, cule langngan zetin Pathian dokalhnak an nei a si. Mipi pawl in Pathian ih hrilmi profet/hruaitu aiawh ih hruaitu thar um dingin an ngen (Daan. 34:10-12, mipum. 14:4), Joshua le Kaleb cun an hnipuan an thlek ih, mipi pawl hnenah tihphannak nei cing khal in Pathian rinsan thotho dingin an ngen: “ ‘Bawipa kan parah a lung a awi a si ahcun, hi ram sungah in hruai lut ding ih, cawhnawi le khuaitizu luannak ram cu in pe ding a si. Bawipa hi do lo in um uhla, cui ram mi pawl khal tih fawn hlah uh’ ” (Mipum. 14:8, 9, NKJV).

`Thil ziangkim a fehtluan caan ahcun Pathian rinsan cu thil olsam zet a si, si ko lo maw? Vansiatnak pawl a thlen caan ah teh ziangtin? Pathian rinsan ringring timi sullam cu lungawi lonak le harsatnak a rak si caan ah teh ziangtin saw a si ding? Cuvek a si ahcun ziangsaw na tuah ding?`
