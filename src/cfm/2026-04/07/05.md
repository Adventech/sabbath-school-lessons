---
title:  Puarthaunak
date:   11/11/2026
---

Caan liamcia ih midang thil tuah mi thlir phah in kei sehla, an tuah mi cu ka tuah hrimhrim lo ding! tin nangmahten na ruat theu maw, ziangkhalsehla, kanmah le kanmah dik zetih kan kilkhawi awk cun midang pawl ih an tuahdan vek cekci in zianghman kan tuah lo men thei, asinan thil thuhmun in kan khawsa theu, cuvek sual cu kan tuah kirsal rero, kan tuah ngaingai mi pawl hmu duh loin kanmah ten kan hum aw rero men. Hivek ciah hi Jesuh amah rori ih a rel mi a si: “Nang midepde, na mit sungih thingtum kha la suak hmaisa ta awla, culawngah na unau mit sungih mit hnawm coih ding cu fiangtein na hmu thei ding a si’ ” (Matt. 7:5).

`Siar ding: Matthai 23:30-35. Hitawk ahhin ziang thilthupi zet, tuar nat thlak zet saw Jesuh ih a tuah mi? A tongkam ihsin ziang ralrinnak saw kan lak tul zet mi?`

Hitawk ahhin Jesuh cun Farasi pawl ih nuntu zia ah huatnak a nei timi a langfiang zet, ziangahtile an an depdepnak cu thleng danglam duh loih an luarkai deuhdeuh ruangah a si. Jesuh cun Farasi pawl ih sakhuanak hi a khawp tuk zo. Annih pawl cu hnipuan fual zet hruk in, thlarau lam milian vek in an um ko nan, mithi ruh hlirih khat sirang hnihmi thlan khur thawn an bangawk ruangah a beidong zet (Matt. 23:27, 28). An sualnak pawl theihter an si ih tlunah, Jesuh in sir-aw in rundam an si theinak ding caan tha a pek sal rero hmanah, an sualnak cu an phuang aw duh lo ih an fehsan duh cuang lo a si.

Hivek minung pawl thawn an hmaiton awk caan ah Jesuh in “depde” pawl tiih a kawh khal hi mangbang thlak ding a si tuk lem lo. Himi catluan cu hitin a feh theu : “Daanthiam le Farasi midepde pawl, nan hmakhua a pit!’ “ tiin ( Matt. 23:14, 15, 23, 25, 27).

A ngaingai te cun an nunnak cu thil pakhat sim rero cingin, a dang thil va tuah san deih khi a si. Profet pawl parih an thiltuah dan khal cuvek thotho a si. Profet pawl ih thlaan ceimawinak thawngin an zumnak an khaisang tin an ruat aw, asinan thil thupi sawn, profet pawl ih tongkam ih sualsirnak tivek pawl neih ding cu an rak hnong. Thiltitheinak le profet pawl ih huham vek neih ve an duh, asinan an thucah pawl ahcun an lungawi thei fawn si lo. Pathian siahhlawh pawl ih thlan cu mawi zetih an tuah tikah Pathian thucah kengtu pawl ih thisen suahnak ah kan tel lo a si tin an ruat aw. Cutin an ruat lai rori hmanah profet ai hnakih nasa sawn mi pakhat Messiah rori cu thahdan ding an timlam rero a si.

`Cuvek sualnak thotho: lenglam ahcun Pathian tihzahmi vekin nung rero cingin, kan sungril ah helnak, duhamnak le huat-awknak in a khatmi kan si lonak dingin ziangtin kan ruat thei ding?`
