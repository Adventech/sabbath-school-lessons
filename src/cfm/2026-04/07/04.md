---
title:  "Thucah siatsuahnak: Then 2"
date:   10/11/2026
---

Minung in simcianak thilpek an letdan cu phun dangdang in a si. Moses thuhla ahcun Israel fate pawl cun Tiamcia ram ih an lut tikih an va ton dingmi pawl an tih tuk ruangah Moses le Aaron cu nasa zetin an dokalh. Cui hnu kum ziangmaw zat ah simaw Siangpahrang Jehoiakim cun Pathian simcianak tongkam pawl siatsuah theh dingih ruahsannak thawn Pathian thucah a urkang thluh.

Ziangkhal sehla, hivek ih thilcang mi pawl hi anmah ten cang mi an si lo. Hlansan ih Israel le Judah pawl ih thuanthu ahcun annih cun profet pawl an hnong ringring a si.

`Siar ding: 2 Sansiarnak 36:15, 16. Ziangtin saw Judah miphun pawl khan Pathian ih A profet pawl hmangin an hrangih thucah a kuat mi an ret let? A netnak ahcun Judah pawl ih ziaza thu ah Pathian in ziangtin a let?`

Jeremiah ih profet hna a tuan sung cu Josiah, Jehoahaz, Jehoiakim, Jehoiachin le Zedekiah ih ramuk san ah a si. Nebuchadnezzar ih kaihhruai Babilon lalram cu tu ahcun Israel kawtka sangka ah a um zo. Jeremiah ih simcianak pawl hmuahhmuah cu kum 21 mi Zedekiah bawi tohkham ih a to ahkhan a famkim thluh a si (2 San. 36:11). Bible cun, “Cun Bawipa a Pathian ih mithmuh ah thilthalo a tuah ih Bawipai’ kaa ihsin thu simtu profet Jeremiah hmaiah hnuai a dor lo” tiah a ti (2 San. 36:12).

Jeremiah ih simcianak profet thilpek simfiangdan hi hminsin aw. Anih cu Pathian Kaa ihsin a tong mi vekih hmuh a si. Cuvek thil um cing khal in Zedekiah cu profet hmaiah a tangdor duh lo. Minung menmen, kanmah vekih misual a si mi Jeremiah cu Bawipa aiawh ih dingtu le a aiawh ih thuphuangtu a si.

Jeremiah cu cuvek hnatuantu tampi lakih pakhat a si. Kum zatel in Pathian cun a minung pawl hnenah profet pawl a thlah ringring (Suah. 3:15, Saam. 105:26, Mal. 3:1, Isa. 6:8, Jer. 25:4, Matt. 11:13, Luka 11:49), “zokhal an hloral ding” a duh lo ih, “sualsirnak thawn an rat ding a duh sawn a si” (2 Pit. 3:9, NKJV). Himi hlansan Israel le Judah thuanthu hi sualsirnak nei in, rundamnak ih A thutiam thawn a mipi pawl A hnen ih an rat theinak dingin cawl loih a zuam rero tu Pathian ih thuanthu a si.

Asinan Judah thuanthu ih caan neta lam ahcun Jeremiah profet simcianak au-aw ih thla-ngamnak le kaihhruainak thawngin Judah pawl hnen ih Pathian zangfahnak cu tangdornak, zumnak le sualsirnak thawn siloin hnihsuak sainak, hmuhsuamnak le nautatnak thawn an rak let a si.

`Pathian tongkam zianghman ah siar loin Pathian thucah kengtu an hmuhsuam ih, A profet an simsia. Tuisan ah cuvek cun zianghman kan tuah lo ding ti a fiang – asilole kan rak tuah pang maw?`
