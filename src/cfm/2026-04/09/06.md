---
title:  Simcianak famkimternak
date:   26/11/2026
---

Mi hrekkhat cun profet cu hmailam caan a rel theitu mi pakhat a si tin an ruat men theu, asinan cui thil cu profet ih a hnatuan peng pakhat fang lawng a si. Profet hnatuan cu thil tamzet funkhawm mi a si. Profet pawl cun mipi pawl cu Pathian kamsuak thu lamah an hruai kirsal, nun an sim, ralrinnak an pe ih, forhfialnak khal an nei.

`Hmailam caan ih thil a thleng ding mipi in an theihnak dingah le an timlam cia theinak dingih an bom tlun ah ziangruangah saw Profet pawl in hmailam thu an simcia? Zoh aw, Jer. 28:9, 15-17.`

Simciami famkimnak hi profet fiahnak lamzin dang pakhat a si. Profet dik ih a simcianak cu a simcia vekin a thleng ding a si: “Profet in Bawipai’ hmin in thu a sim tikah cui a simmi thil in a thlun lo ih a ra cang lo asile cucu Bawipai’ a sim lomi thil a si, profet cun hngal zetin a sim men a si: anih cu na tih lo ding, a ti’ ” (Daan. 18:22). Himi fiahnak (hniksaknak) hman ding cu thil harsa zet a si ding, ziangahtile simcianak hrekkhat pawl cu a boruak ih zirin a um: Cuti a si ruangah boruak ih zirin profet ih simcianak cu a ra thleng lo men thei.

Hivek thu hi Jonah le ralrinnak a pekmi Nineveh khua thuhla ahkhan kan hmu thei. A langdan ahcun Jonah ih thusimmi cu a tawi zet a bang, a thupi pawl a fiang zet: “Ni 40 a kim hlan ah Nineveh cu siatsuah a si ding’ ” (Jonah. 3:4, NKJV). Asinan a simciavek in zianghman a thleng lo. Ziangruangah aw?

`Siar ding: Jeremiah 18:7-10. Himi cun a boruak zirih simcianak thuhla ziangaw in sim?`

A boruak zirih simcianak cu mipi in Pathian thucah an rak letdan ah thum aw ih simcianak a si. Mipi pawl in an lungput le ziaza an thleng a si ahcun simcianak kha a thleng famkim lo men thei. Nineveh mipi pawl cun Jonah ih simcianak cu an thinlung ah an ret ih, an lamzin an thleng ruangah Pathian cun a siatsuah sal lo (Jon. 3:10). Hi profet ralrin peknak hi lehhnu ih rungum pawl in an hngilh thluh ih, Nineveh khawpi cu misual le rapthlak zet an si leh sal ih, Pathian cun a siatsuah hai a si. Pathian cun hmailam caan a thei, asinan kannih cun kan thei ve lo. Kan duh hrilnak cun kan hmailam caan hi nasa zetin a rak sim a si.

`Ziangruangah saw a boruak zirih simcianak thuhla theihthiam hi a thupit?Himi theihthiamnak cun Bible thutak theithiam deuh dingin ziangtin in bawm?`
