---
title:  “Tep awla, hmu aw”
date:   27/11/2026
---

Seventh-day Adventist cun Ellen G. White ih don mi profet thil pek hi an pawm ih, a thok ihsin tuisan tiang Bible sungih um profet tamzet ih an tonmi thawn a bangaw mi harsatnak tamzet a tong ve a si. Ellen G. White in langnak a hmuh hmaisabik kum 1844 ahkhan hrekkhat cun a profet thildon mi ah thusuhnak an nei. Himi cun Sabbath hmang Adventist (lehhnu ahcun Seventh-day Adventist) pawl cu Ellen G. White ih profet thilpek pawmpi ih thansoh pi dingin lam a hruai a si.

A hmaisabik ah caan net tiangin simcianak thilpek cu a um kop ringring ding timi hi Pathian thu ihsin an zir. Tuisan ih simciannak thilpek a um hi Bible cun a pawmpi a si. Joel 2 le Thuphuan 12:17 le Bible hmundang ah thum aw in, profet thilpek cu “ni netalam” ah hmuh theih in a um ding, cule caan neta ih Pathian minung pawl ih “theihter hminsinnak ah” a cang ding (zoh aw, Gerhard Pfandl, “The Gift of Prophecy and God’s Remnant Church,” The Gift of Prophecy, chapter 4.)

Pahnihnak ah Adventist mi hmaisa pawl khan Ellen G. White ih thilpek donmi cun zumlo pawl nun a luahkhat zet tiah an hminsin. Thimnak ah a profet thusimcianak cun kum 1840 so hrawng ih Sabbath Conference ih tel zumtu pawl in Bible thurin parah lungkim tlan lonak an neih mi ah nasa zetin a bawm a si. A thilpek cun cumi ihsin kum hra sung kha “tha a suah” pek vivo. A nganca pawl cu Seventh-day Adventist pawlkom zumnak sungah a um ringring lai.

Ellen G. White thuhla ih a pathumnak ahcun langnak a hmuhmi cu thiltitheinak thawn an pehzom aw timi hi a si. Langnak a hmuh lai ah, a hlanih kan rak theih thluh zo vekin Ellen G. White cun thil danglam zet a langter – a theihdah lomi thildik le dinhmun a sim, hmailam ih thil thleng cingding a langter, cahnak neihmi ai hnakih cak sawn, le langnak a hmuhnak tonnun tikcu caan pawl a thunun thei lo a si.

Tuisan ih Seventh-day Adventist pawl cun Ellen G. White ih thilpek an pawmpi zia an tarlang rero. Himi thuel buaiawknak a um lai ah Ellen G. White ih thilpek “hminsinnak” tha zet cu a cangan mi bulpak tonnun hi a si. Saam hlaphuahtu David cun “Bawipa hi a tha tihi tep uhla, thei aw” tiah a ti. Ellen G. White ih cangan mi cu thlacamnak thawn siar ding a si, cuticun a profet cangan mi kan theithiam ding a si.

Casiar thoknak dingah a cabu fate, asinan lar zetmi “Khrih hnen thlennak karbak” timi ihsin thok ding a si. Himi cu kumtluan doawknak timi cauk volume 5 in a run thlun lan ding, cumi cu Semtirnak in Thuphuan tiang ih doawknak ropi thupi sung ahkhan a tel ve a si. A cangan mi pawl cu kau zetin papar ah a lang ih, tong tamzet ih letlin mi a si.
