---
title:  Bible thawn milaw in
date:   23/11/2026
---

Kannih minung pawl cu thuel awk le bumawk hmang theutu kan si ko nan, Pathian cun thuphan a rel dah lo (Mipum 23:19). Profet simciannak thucah cu Pathian in profet pawl hmangin a tarlang a si. A thleng aw dah lo dingmi rinsantlak zet cu A pek mi profet simcianak thucah ahhin hmuh a theih a si.

`Siar ding: Isaiah 8:20. Ziangmi thil thupi zet saw a tuah?`

Thlarau thawtkhum tiih relawknak ziangvek khal ahhin Bible hi tahfung netabik a si ringring. Profet pawl cu an thurel mi le Bible ih relmi zohthim in fiahnak neih ding a si hrimhrim. Profet dik pakhat cun Bible thudik cu a dokalh dah lo ding, a el dah lo ding ih, a nepter dah fawn lo ding. Bawipa thlun duhtu, zirhtu diklo le profet diklo pawl ih bum a duhtu pawl hrangah Cathianghlim theih ngah hi a thupi tuk. Curuangah zirhtu dik lo pawl le profet dik lo pawl ih bumnak a tong duh lotu le Bawipa a thlunduhtu pawl hrangah Cathianghlim theihfiangnak hi a thupi tuk a si.

Thukham Hlun le Thar ahhin profet pawl cun anmai profet hnatuannak hrang tahfung ah a hlanih profet pawl ih simcianak cangan mi pawl an tarlang ih, an hmang theu. Lehhnu ih profet ra um pawl cun Pathian ih khawkhannak le rundamnak lamzin pawl an run tarlang, asinan Pathian ih a rak pek zo mi le thu bulhram pawl cu an dokalh dah lo.

`2 Piter 1:19-21, Rom 15:4, le James 1:17 pawl cun Pathian le kum zabi tamzet ih a rak pek zo mi profet simcianak thucah thuhla ziangsaw in sim?`

Rundam khawkhannak theihthiamnak dingih Thukham Hlun ih thumawknak bulhram bik cu Semtirnak 3:15 ahkhan Amah Pathian rori in simcianak hmaisabik a rak pe. Himi khawkhannak cu raithawinak fehdan hmangih tarlang a si. Asinan Pathian ih Tuufano, Jesuh Khrih cu minung hrangih a ratdan, a nundan le a thihdan famkim zet cu lehhnu ih simcianak pawlah karbak pakhat hnu pakhat tarlang vivo a si. Bible ih a ngandan ahcun Pathian cun A minung pawl in an theihthiam tikah thutak cu a tarlang timi a langter. Himi cun thutak theih theinak cu santhar ah thuk sin in an theithei ding tinak a si. Himi ciah hi “thutak phuangsuah” asilole “thutak a thangso” timi ruahnak ih sullam cu a si. Curuangah a hlan ai hnakin lehhnu ih santhar pawl in Pathian thudik cu thuksin in an thei ding tinak a si.

`Ziangkhal sehla, rel uar lutuk theih lo dingmi thil thupi zet pakhat a um: Thutak ih phuangawknak thar cun a hlan ih Pathian ih a rak pek zo mi thudik cu a dokalh dah lo ding a si.`
