### Tuizarh zirlai hrang siar ding
Dan. 10:7-19; Daan. 13:1-5; Isa. 8:20; Johan 11:25; Tirh 15:36-39; Jer. 28:9, 15-17.

> <p>Bible cangken</p>
> “Duhdawt mi tla, thlarau kip in va zum hlah uh, thlarau pawl cu Pathian hnen ihsi ra an si le si lo fiah sawn uh: ziangahtile profet deu tampi leitlun ah an ra suak” (1 Johan 4:1).

Diamond ngaingai hi cu leitlun ih thil man nei bik a si. Thildang tampi khal diamond thawn bangaw deuh nawn ih tuah mi an um ih, cumi pawl cun man neihnak an nei tuk lo. Diamond dik le diklo hi ziangtin mi pakhat in a theithiam thei ding? Lamzin thabik cu fiah(hniksaknak) hi a si. Thilri hrekkhat malte hmangin himi fiahnak hi inn lamah an tuah thei ko ding. A dang pawl cun fiahnak thildang tha sawn an tul ding. Fiahnak pawl hmuahhmuah hi anmah ten fiah theih thil a si lo, asinan diamond cun fiahnak pawl hmuahhmuah a pahtlang thluh a tul ih, cumi hnu lawngah diamond diktak theih a theihfang ding a si.

Diamond cu leitlun ih a hngetkhoh (cak zet) mi thilri lakih pakhat a si. Diamond ih cahnak hi boruak hlum le sa zet a thlengawk khalah ziangtin hman danglam loin, a um kelin a um ringring theu. Diamond fiah ding cun meisa sazet ah na ret ding ih, cule tidai dai zetah na thlak ding. Diamond ngaingai cu ziangtin hman a um lo ding, asinan diamond lem (diklo) cu a kuai theh ding.

Simcianak thilpek cu diamond ai hnakin man a nei, asinan himi thilpek nei vekin relaw tu mi pakhat cu manmual nei zetih ruat lawk men ding a si lo. Cui thu cu fiah(hniksak) a tul a si. Tuizarh ahcun Bible profet pawl kan zoh hliah ding ih, an diknak tahnak ih hman mi Bible thu bulhram pawl kan hmu ding.
