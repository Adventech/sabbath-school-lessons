---
title:  Profet hnatuan dingih kawhnak
date:   09/10/2026
---

Pathian cun a hleice ih tumtahmi a neih lai caan ahhin profet pawl a ko theu. Annih cu a tikcu caan te ih tulsam mi thucah kengtu an si. Himi thu ahcun Seventh-day Adventist cun Ellen G. White cu tuisan ih profet simcianak tarlangtu ah an pawm ih, profet vek ciah ih hna a tuantu a si (Himi cu a ra lai ding mi zirlai pawl ah a tam sinsin ding). Ellen Harmon(White) cun December 1844 ahkhan a hmaisabik kawhnak a dong, cumi cu Millerite Adventist pawl in Daniel 8:14: “ni 2300 tiang; cumi hnu ah hmun thianghlim thenfai a si ding” timi ah thum aw in Jesuh ratsalnak ding an rak beisei hnu zarh reilote ih thil thleng mi a si.

William Miller khan a ni le thla ciahciah a rel lo nan, amah thluntu pawl cun Bible sungih Remnak thawinak tikcu caan pawl an zoh ih, cumi ah thum aw in October 22,1844 cu leitlun ih Khrih a rungtumnak ding ni ah an rak pawm. Himi zumnak cun saklam America ram le leitlun hmun dangdang ah mi thawngtel ih hngakhlapnak nasa zet a run kengtel. A rei hlanah vanram ah anmah Rundamtu thawn an um khawm tlang mai ding a si. Jesuh a rungtum cing ding timi thu ruangah Miller khal lungtho zetin a um.

Asinan Khrih a rung lang lo, Miller zumtu pawl cun santhuanthu ih thilthleng rapthlak zet pakhat, riahsiatnak ropi zet an tong.

Himi cu Pathian in Ellen Harmon (White) hnen ih langnak a pek hmaisabiknak a si. Cumi ih thucah thupibik cu amah le a beidong mi zumtu pawlin an thu simfiangnak dik lo khal sehla, Pathian in a um pi lai ringring a si timi thazang peknak a si. Langnak cun lei ihsin van Jerusalem lam pannak lamzin a khihhmuh a si. Himi lamzin ahhin Jesuh rungtumsalnak zumtu pawl cu Khawpi Thianghlim lam pan in khualtlawng in an feh. “An hmai ih a fehtu Jesuh an zoh ringring ih, khawpi lam ah anmah hruai dingih rem an ti ahcun an him tengteng ding a si”—Ellen G. White, Spiritual Gifts, vol. 2, p. 31.

Himi langnak cu thuthangtha a si. Pathian cun lungbuai riahsiatnak ihsin cangvaihnak thar a sersiam thok a si. An hnenah Jesuh a um ringring lai. Anih cu a kirsal a si. Anih cun rundamnak lamzin ah a hruai rero lai. Amah lawng an “zoh” a tul. Annih cun harsatnak le beidonnak karlak khalah ruahsannak an hloh duh lonak ding san ropi zet an nei.

Ellen G. Harmon (White) hi kum 17 lawng si khal sehla, profet hnatuan dingih kawh mi a si. A hleice in a hramthok lam ahkhan a hrang ahcun lamzin nuam zet a si lem lo.

Himi thilpek cu nuamnai teten rak pawm vivo khal sehla, Ellen G White cun Pathian thucah kengtu si ding cu a ngam tuk lem lo. Pathian profet si cu thil olsam a si dah lo. Curuangah Ziangtin saw Ellen White khal Pathian kawhnak ih a pumpek awk thei?

Hmaizarh ah Thukham hlun profet pawl ih harsatnak an ton mi le a hnatuandan pawl kan zoh ding, an lakih hrekkhat pawl cun hmaizarh niruk ni ih kan zoh dingmi Ellen G. White ih nun le a ton mi vek an rak tong ve thluh.
