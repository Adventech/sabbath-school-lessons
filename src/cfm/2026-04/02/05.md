---
title:  Daniel, mi rinum
date:   07/10/2026
---

Khristian tamzet cun Daniel 1 ih um thuanthu hi an thei thluh, a nauhak tet lai ihsin a kaihngetmi hriselnak ih a dinhngetnak thuhla a si. Daniel cu rinumnak kengtu zohthim tlak zet a si(Dan 1:8). Siangpahrang inn ih ei in ding thawzet a umnak hmun ahcun anih cun siangpahrang cabuai ih hun mi pawl hnakin tidai thiangte le thinghnah ei a duh sawn a si kha (Dan. 1:12-16). Himi cu a ra lai dingmi thil pawl hrangih hniksaknak tenau zet a si. Daniel 6 ahkhan a rinumnak ruangah thihnak ding tiang a ton phah a si.

`Siar ding: Daniel 2:46-48; Daniel 4:18; Daniel 5:13, 14; le Daniel 6:25-27. Hitawk ahhin Daniel in a kiangkap ih pathian dang biatu pawl hnenih Theihpitu a sinak thuhla ziangsaw in sim?`

Hi pathian dang biatu pawl hnen ihsin Daniel in Pathian huham a theihterdan pawl khi vun zoh aw. Himi thuanthu cu danglam hleice mi a si ih, kaihhruai awknak thupi zet an tarlang thluh: kanmah hmangin kan sungih Pathian huham hnatuannak thawngin kan rinumnak, kan nun ihsin midang pawl in van ih um Pathian thuhla an zir thei ih, Anih cu “pathian pawl ih Pathian, siangpahrang pawl ih Bawipa” a ti timi an theih phah thei a si.

Cule Daniel simcianak ih thupi ummi pawl cu “thuthennak” thuhla deuh hlir a si ruangah Khrih ratsalnak thawn(Pathian ih nehnak netabik) khal a milaw zet ih, Daniel ih hmin sullam “Pathian cu ka thuthentu a si” ti thawn khal an rem aw zet. A cangan mi pawl cu tuisan ih thil thupi zet a si ruangah ralring zetih zir tul mi a si. Daniel ih simcianak cun kan nun ih “thil fate” khal ahhin rinum zetih um dingin le Pathian thupek thlun dingah in sim ringring a si.

Profet a si vekin Daniel cun miphun (rampi) tundin le tluksiat ding thu a phuang ih, cumi cu Pathian thu an ngai lo ruangih a rahsuak mi a si. Langnak a hmuhnak ihsin Daniel cun Pathian ih khawkhannak le a netnak ih nehnak thawn a Lalram a din ding thu a tarlang (Dan. 7:13, 14).

Daniel 2 hi vun ruat hman awla, Khrih a um hlan kum tamzet ih ngan cia mi a si. Daniel in leitlun uktu ropi zet pali ih a dinsohdan le tlukdan pawl a rel mi pawl hi an thleng dik tuk, kan san tiang in a rel (milem ih ke le kezung), himi cu tuisan ih Europe rampi relnak a si ih, simcianak cun hitin in zirh “annih cu minung cithlah pawl thawn an cawkrawiaw ding: asinain thir cu tlaklei thawi an kopawk thei lo bangin, annih cu pakhat le pakhat an kopaw thei lo ding” (Dan. 2:43). Europe thuhla ih simcianak cu mangbangza a si. Tuini tiang ahhin pumkhatnak um thei dingin an zuam ih, an nei aw rawi ruiri lai, an ramkulh ih um pawl cu daan le dun dangdang nei, nunphung dangdang le nupa daan dangdang nei in an um ringring lai. Ram pumhuap a tluksiat thluh hlante hman ahkhan mifim thiam pawl in thil a ra thleng dingmi an theih lo lai caan hmanah Daniel ih simcianak cun Pathian profet pawl rinsan theinak ding huham nasa zet in rak pe zo a si.

Daniel 2 ih lalram pawl cu simcia a si vekin an ra um ih, an hlosal thluh zo. Himi cun simcianak rinsan ding thuhla, kumkhua ih Pathian lalram ra um ding thuhla ziangsaw in rak sim (Dan. 2:44)?
