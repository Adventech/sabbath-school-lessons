---
title:  Isaiah, thuthangtha profet
date:   06/10/2026
---

Isaiah, thukham hlun ih profet ropi zet pakhat cu Pathian hnen ihsin profet hnatuan ding kawhnak a dong(Isaiah 6:1-8). Pathian ih thianghlim zia le sunlawizia a hmuh tikah Isaiah cun tling lo zetin a thei aw. Cumi ah seraf vancungmi pakhat cu vanram biaktheng ihsin meiling kengin Isaiah hnenah a ra zam ih, a sualnak a “thianghlim” ter. Cule Pathian in, “ Zosaw ka thlah ding, kan hrangah zosaw a feh ding?” tiah a sut. Isaiah ih letnak awkam cu kan letnak awkam si ve hram seh. “Hitawk ah ka um; keimah I thlah aw” (Isa. 6:8).

Isaiah kawh a sinak hi Israel thuanthu ih thil thupi zet ah a um. Amai tlinlonak pawl hmuahhmuah tan in Israel le an hruaitu pawl ih sualnak ahhin ralrinnak pek dingmi tuanvo thupi zet a nei. Isaiah ih simcianak pawl cu Bible sungih thuhla ummi thupi zetzet pawl an si. Pathian sinak, minung sinak le rundam khawkhannak lamih theihthiamnak khal nasa zetin a pe suak a si. A ngaingai te cun rinhlelnak ding zianghman um loin a thupi zetmi simcianak cun Jesuh ih thihnak lawng hi a tarlang lo ih, thihnak in leitlun ih a thiltuah mi pawl hi Isaiah cabu sungah hmuh a theih, cumi lakih hminthang zet cu Isaiah 53 hi a si.

`Siar ding: Isaiah 53. Ziangtin saw himi simcianak danglam zet, thinglamtah a um hlan kum tamzet ih ngan mi cun kan hrangah Khrish ih thihnak ih thil titheinak a tarlang? Himi simcianak cun simcianak thilpek thuhla ziangsaw in zirh?`

Isaiah profet thuphuan cun Messiah tumram le hnatuannak a simcia thluh (Isa. 53:3-5). Khrih cu “thah dingih hruai mi tuu fano vek” a sidan pawl an simfiang (Isa. 53:7, NIV) cule a pumpek awk raithawinak in A minung pawl cu an sualnak ihsin a rundamdan pawl khal an simfiang. Cutawk ahcun Khrih ih thihnak cu fiangzetin tarlang a si ih, minung ai-awh ih remnak lungphum a langter.

“Kan zatein tuurual bangin kan vakvai; mikip mai’ lamzin cio ah kan pial theh; kan zatei’ diklonak cu Bawipa in amai’ parah a thum” (Isa. 53:6). Leitlun pumhuap sualnak cu hitawk ahhin tarlang in a um: thinglamtah ih sinak le sual relfeldan khal hitawk ah a um. Himi bung vek lawmmam ih rundam khawkhannak fiah le fiang hi Thukham Hlun le Thar khalah hmuh a si lem zik lo.

Isaiah profet tuan dingih kawhnak hi thil olsam zet a si lem lo. Siatralnak ding simcianak thu a phuang tikah mipi pawl cun atthlak za in an hmu, tuisan khalah sualnak ih a rahsuah mi thuhla ih ralrinnak pek a si tikih mi tamzet cun atthlak za ah an ngai theu (Rom. 6:23). Isaiah ih simcianak cu amai san le kan san khalih thil thupi zet a si ti a fiang (Isai 2:4, Isa 5:20). Cumi cu miphun (rampi in) in diknak le remthatsalnak tuah ding an duh lo ih an hnong ruangah Jerusalem a siat phah a si. Cuvek sualnak cu Khrih a ratsal tikah leitlun ih misual pawl siatsuahnak hminsinnak a si ding.

`Isaiah 53 kha zoh sal awla, cuih bungcang ih a tarlang vekin Jesuh in kan hrangih a tuahmi hmuahhmuah ngan aw. Khrih thihnak in kan hrangih a tuahsak mi thuhla profet ih a phuangsuak mi ihsin ziangvek ruahsannak ropi saw na neih thei ding?`
