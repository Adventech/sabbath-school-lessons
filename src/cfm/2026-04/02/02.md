---
title:  Abraham, Thukham kilhimtu
date:   04/10/2026
---

Semtirnak ihsin thuphuan tiang ahhin Pathian cun A zangfahnak let dingah thuawih dingih kawhnak rak cohlangtu mi rinum zet a tangsun pawl a nei ringring (zoh aw, Jer 31:7, 31-34). Thimnak ah Babel lungdawl thekdarh a si hnu kum tampi hnuah Pathian cun Abram (lehhnu ah Abraham) cu a innsang hrang lawngah si loin, a ra lai dingmi san hmuahhmuah hrang khalah thukham zumnak zohthim dingih a hril mi a si. Fehsualnak le nuncan siava pawl ih kulh thluh mi a si. Asinan Abraham cun Pathian kawhnak ah rinumnak a langter (zoh, Sem. 12:1-9.)

Pathian cun Abraham hnenah pawlkom awknak thukham a simfiang ih, Khrih ih raithawinak hmangin ziangtin minung rundamnak a cangsuak ding timi a hmuh a si (Sem. 15:6-17). Abraham ih zumnak hniksak dingah le hmailam caan ih Pathian fapa raithawinak langternak ah Abraham cu a fapa Isak, Pathian hnen ih raithawinak ih hlan dingih kawh a si. (Sem. 22:1-17). Mi rinum zet pai’ hrang ahcun ziangtluk ih hniksaknak harsa zet saw a si ding. Pathian thupek thlun duh lo dingin Abraham ih a hman thei mi phuahhlam pawl hmuahhmuah kha vun ruat hnik aw, cuvek um cing khal in anih cun rinum zetin Pathian thu a thlun. Pathian cun Isak thahnak ihsin Abraham kut a kham ih, a ai ah tuu a pek.

Profet pawl hmuahhmuah hi siatnak ra thleng dingmi ah ralrinnak petu dingih Pathian ih kawh mi an si thluh lo. Asinan an zaten rinum dingin mipi tuahtha sal dingih kawh an si thluh, a caan ahcun nunsimnak, mi rethei le tulsam pawl neekrawk tu pawl ralrin peknak le thukham le daan pawl zianghman siarlotu pawl ralrinnak petu dingih kawh an si.

Ziang khal lole a thupibik mi cu profet pawl in Messiah sung ih rundamnak thuthiam an phuangsuak ding a si. Bible profet pawl le kan san ih A Thiang thlarau cun leitlun ih siatnak a ra thleng dingmi theithiam dingin le midang hnen ih sim dingah in ko a si. Asinan cui ralrin peknak cu Jesuh ih mi rinum zet, Amah thawn laicin awknak thukham ih a um ringring tu hrangih tlennak thuthangtha tha a si (Thup. 12:17).

`Siar ding: Semtirnak 15:6, Galati 3:6-9, le Hebru 11:8. Abraham kha mi famkim si lo khal sehla, Pathian rinsan dingih tlinnak a petu cu ziangsaw a si?`

Abraham san le kan san ahhin bangawknak tamzet a um. Abraham san ih milem biaknak le Pathian nung bia lomi an tam vek ciah in, tuisan khalah theihfimnak zuamawknak lam hin kan Khristian nun a eiral rero a si. Cumi thawn kalh aw ciah in tuisan ih Pathian minung pawl cu rinum zetin nitin an nunnak ahhin midang hnen ih A hmuihmel langter ding ih kawh mi an si, himi ahcun vancungmi pathum thucah an phuang mi khal a tel ding a si (Thup. 14:6-12).

`Cathianghlim cun Abraham “in Bawipa cu a zum ih, cucu a dingfelnak ah a cosak” (Sem. 15:6) tiah a ti. Himi thu ahhin thilti theizet thuthangtha ih thutiam tarlang mi ziangtin na hmu? Himi ihsin nangmai hrangah ziangmi ruahsannak na la thei ding?`
