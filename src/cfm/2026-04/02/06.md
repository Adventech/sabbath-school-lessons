---
title:  Tipil petu Johan, mipi timlamtu
date:   08/10/2026
---

`Siar ding: Marka 1:2-5. Tipil petu Johan ih hnatuannak ih tumtah mi kha ziang a si?`

Thuthangtha Marka cun Tipil petu Johan ih a ratnak cu Isaiah ih simcianak famkimternak a si tiah in rak sim (Isa. 40:3). Johan hi a nautet lai ihsin a pitling tiang Pathian ih rinum zettu tiah hminsin mi a si. Johan in nelrawn ramcar ih thuthangtha a sim mi Satan ih a theih hnu cun a thuhnuai ih minung a retmi pawl a hloh thluh pang kei ti a rak phang zet (zoh aw, Ellen G. White, The Desire of Ages, p. 224). Johan ih thusimmi ngaitu misualral pawl cun satan lung an phangter zet. Satan cun a thuhnuai ih Israel a ret cu a tihnung um tuk thlang. Tipil petu Johan cu a nundan rinum zet le Messiah a zumnak pawl siatsuah sak dingin a theitawk in a zuam rero nan, a hlawhsam thotho.

`Siar aw: Matthai 11:1-6. Thuthangtha phuangtu cu thawnginn ih a um laiah Jesuh in Johan ih zumnak kha ziangtin saw a rel?`

Satan cun Jesuh hi sualnak tuah dingin a thlem thei lo, nelrawn ih nehnak a co lo ruangah a thinheng tuk. Cutin a thiltuahdan a thleng ih, Jesuh ih unau pa Johan cu thawnginn sungah a thlak. Jesuh hi Satan in sual ah thlem tlu thei lo khal sehla, thawnginn sungih Johan a umnak cun Jesuh a na ter zet. Himi cu Satan in Johan a siatsuahdan cu a si, cumi rual in Rundamtu ih lungthin khal a nater zet. Asinan Johan cun Jesuh hnen ihsin thazang peknak tongkam a dong. A tuarnatnak dinhmun pawl cu tanta in, Jesuh cu thiltitheinak thawn nasa zetih a um laiah ziangah anih cu thawnginn sungah a um timi theifiang lo khal sehla, Johan cun thihnak tiang tuar in rinum zetin a um.

Hlan profet vek ciah in kannih khal sualsirnak le zumnak le Khrih ih rinumnak thucah pawl phuang dingih kawh kan si (Thup. 14:6-12). Tipil petu Johan nun ih a tuahvek ciah in Satan cun kan nun ih Pathian hnatuan ringhlel dingah in tuah thei a si. Nunau sualral le mipa derdai zet ih cangvaihnak ruangih thawngthlak le thah a tuartu Johan parih thil thlengmi vek ciah in kan hrang thil ziangkim a feh tluang lo caan hmanah Kan nun hi Pathian in a kaihruai a si timi kan cing ringring ahcun riahsiatnak ding zianghman a um lo. Minung in kan parah thil ziangvek an tuah khalah zumnak in Pathian kan ko thei ringring, Anih cun in let ding a si. A duhzawng tuah thei dingin huham pek in tiam a si (Tirh 1:8). Khristian a si zo mi sungah le a si hrih lomi pawl hnen khalah ziangvek dinhmun ih kan um hmanah Khrih hrangih theihpitu rinum zet kan si ding hi a thupi zet a si.

`Thawnginn sungih Johan a um vek ciah in thil tha lo zetzet na parih a thlennak san thei lo ih na um caan a um ve dah maw? Pathian na zumnak le na rinsannak cu ziangtin na kai hnget ringring lai?`
