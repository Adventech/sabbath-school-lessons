---
title:  Lemcangnak, Hminsinnak, Tahthimnak
date:   29/10/2026
---

Bible cun tongzuk le tahthimnak tamzet a nei. Thimnak ah, Thukham Hlun ih raithawinak biaktheng pawl hmuahhmuah, Moses ih biakbuk le Jerusalem ih um biakinn pawl cu thinglamtah ih Khirh ih raithawi pekawknak rak zohtertu le van ih puithiam sang a tuannak rak khihhmuhtu tongzuk pawl an si (Heb. 8:1-6).

Daniel ih simcianak ahhin leitlun ih uknak le huham pawl cu milem ropi zet ih peng dangdang (Dan. 2:31-45), ramsa le ramsa ki pawl (Daniel 7 le 8), le siangpahrang pahnih (Daniel 11) tivek pawl hmangih tarlang a si. Cuvek simfiangnak cu Thuphuan cabu sung ahhin a um ve, bung 13 ih sahrang pahnih thu ahkhan a fiang zet.

Jesuh ih thuzirhnak ahkhan Amah cu nunnak sang (Johan 6:35, 41-58), leitlun ih eng (Johan 8:12, Johan 9:5), le Sabit kung dik (Johan 15:1-8) a si thu a rel aw. Thlaici vorh (Matt. 13:1-9, 18-23) le a dang pawl (Matt. 13: 24-52, etc) hmangin Jesuh cun a Uknak ih sinak le thansohnak a simfiang.

`Siar ding: Ezekiel 4:1-8. Pathian in tongkam lawng siloin thucah hrekkhat pawl cu tongzuk hmangih a simfiang hi ziangah a si ding tin na ruat?`

Ezekiel 4:1-8 ahhin fiangzetin tongzuk tarlang a si. Cutawk ahcun Pathian cun Profet hnenah Jerusalem khawpi zuklem tuah ih, a kiangih ret dingin a fial. A hmaisabik ahcun Israel innsang pawl aiawh in a kehlam sir ah ni 390 sung a ret ding ih, Judah innsang aiawh in a vorhlam sir ah ni 40 sung ret ih, it dingin a ngen. A dang pakhat cu Pathian in Hosea cu hlawhhlang nu Gomer thawn nei aw dingih a fial kha a si. Cumi cun Pathian parih Israel ih thlarau lam rinum lonak a langter (Hos. 1:2-9).

Ezekiel le Hosea ih an tonteh mi cu tuisan ahcun mangbangza thil a si. Cui thil cu a thupi zet mi a si: kanmah lawng siloin anmai hrang khalah mangbangza a si ko ding, Culawng siloin khalai san ih minung pawl hrang khalah a si thotho ding. Culai caan ah profet pawl ih ralrin peknak cu mipi pawl in langngan zetih an hnong tikah (2 San 36:15-21) Pathian ih zangfah cangvaihnak netabik cu mipi pawl an sual an sir theinak dingah an dinhmun diktak cu lemcannak hmangin theihternak a neih hi a si. Cuvek ih lemcannak cun Pathian ih ralrin peknak an ngaihsak a tulzia a tarlang a si.

`Tuini ahhin Laodicea mi kan si timi ralrin peknak ah mi pakhatkhat in ziangvek lemcangnak saw a tuah thei ding? (See Rev. 3:14-18.)`
