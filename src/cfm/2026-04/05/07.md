---
title:  Ellen. G. White parih Thuphuan le thawtkhum
date:   30/10/2026
---

Seventh-day Adventist pawl cun Ellen G. White cu Pathian thawtkhum mi a si tiah an zum. A cangan mi, a nunnak, a hnatuannak pawl cu profet dik tahfung a tling a si. Bible sungih profet pawl vek ih thawtkhum mi a si, asinan amah cun a cangan mi pawl cu Bible tluk in a ruat dah lo. Ellen G. White cun Bible hi a khaisang ringring theu. Asinan ziangtin saw amah rori in Pathian in a hnen ih profet thuphuan le a thawtkhum a si timi hi a theihthiam?

A hmaisabik ah Ellen G. White cun Bible cu Pathian ih thuneihnak le a palh thei lomi phuansuahnak a si tiah a ruat ringring. Bible cun Pathian thudik le a tlusia mi minung hrangih rundam khawkhannak a neihmi pawl a tarlang. Cumi ahcun kan zir rero lai vekin profet hmangin a thucah a sim suak.

Pahnihnak ah Ellen G. White cun thawtkhum fehdan ahhin Pathian le minung karlak ih pehtlaihawknak a tel ti a theifiang. Pathian cun a thucah a sim thei, asinan profet cun amai tongkam hmangin Pathian ih thuzirh mi cu a simfiang ding a si. Ellen G. White cun, “Bible sungih tongfang pawl hi a thawtkhum a si lo ih, a minung pawl kha a thawtkhum sawn a si. Thawtkhum cangvaihnak hi minung tongkam le a thusimnak ah siloin amah minung ahkhan a si sawn, annih cu Thiang Thlarau kaihhruainak hnuai ih um le ruahnak hmang pawl an si … Pathian ih lungput le duhdan pawl cu minung lungput le duhdan thawn an kop aw; curuangah milai ih thurel mi cu Pathian tongkam an si”—Selected Messages, book 1, p. 21.

Profet ih mang le langnak thawn pehpar aw in Ellen. G. White cun “ruahnak thawtkhumnak” timi a theifiang zet, cumi cu Pathian ih thuphuan le minung ih tonteh mi thawn a kop aw mi vekin a theithiam. Anih cun, “ ‘Bible cun Pathian ih thudik pekmi pawl cu minung tong in a tarlang, Pathian le minung komkhawmnak a langter’ “—Selected Messages, book 1, p. 25. Pathian cun A thucah kengtu pawl ih thinlung ah A thucah an theihthiam theidan ding lamzin tamzet a hmang. Himi ahcun tongzuk zohthimnak, tongkam, ruahnak tivek pawl a tel. Ellen G. White ih tonteh mi pawl cu Bible ngantu pawl thawn an bangaw zet.

Pathumnak ah Ellen G. White cun a minung sinak ih a tonteh mi le pehtlaih awknak pawl hmuahhmuah hi thawtkhum mi an si lo tiah a rel. Anih cun “a tlangpi thu ih thil bangrep pawl relsuah a tul caan a um theu, ruahnak a bangrep aw mi pawl cun thinlung a luahkhat caan a um, a tlangpi ih ngan mi cakuat khal ngan tengteng a tul, theihternak pek mi pawl cu hnatuantu pakhat hnu pakhat hnenih thlen a tul. Cuvek tongkam, theihter awknak tivek pawl cu a hleice in Pathian Thlaru ih thawtkhumnak hnuai ih pek an si thluh lo” tiah a ti—Selected Messages, book 1, p. 39. A cangan mi le a tonteh mi, a hleice in a nitin nun ih ca nganmi pawl kan siar tikah himi thil danglamnak te hi kan theih thiam ding a thupi zet.

Ellen G. White ih Thuphuan le thawtkhumnak thuhla va theihthiamnak cun thil ti sual lutuk dingmi hrial in, a ruahnak pekmi kha fimkhur zetin le theihthiamnak thawn siar in in bawm a si.
