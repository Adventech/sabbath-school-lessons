### Tuizarh zirlai hrang siar ding
Heb. 1:1, 2; 2 Tim. 3:15-17; 2 Pet. 1:18-21; Suah. 17:14; Thup. 1:19; Ezek. 4:1-8.

> <p>Bible cangken</p>
> “Jesuh Khrih ih Thuphuan, Pathian in a siahhlawh pawl theihter dingih a hnenih a pekmi, a ra thleng cing dingmi thil pawl; cun anih in cucu a vancungmi hmangin a siahhlawh Johan hnenah a kuat ih a theihter: Anih cun Pathian thu le Jesuh Khrih ih thu le a theihtermi thil hmuahhmuah ih thu a theihpi” (Thuphuan 1:1, 2).

Tirhthlah Johan hi dungthluntu 12 sungih mi thupi zet lakih tel tu a si (Luka 8:51; Matt. 17:1; Matt. 26:36, 37). Vanram ih Khrih a kai hnuah Jerusalam ah a hnatuan a pehzom sal. Asinan khawpi tluksiatnak (A.D. 70) a um hlanah Rom uknak Asia monor lam ah kawhhran kilkhawi dingin Efesa ah a feh. Rom uktu Domitian in Khristian pawl a hremthat rero lai caan ahkhan tirthlah Johan cu Aegean tipi ih um tikulh Patmos ah sal dinhmun in a tang.

Patmos ah Johan cun Asia Minor ih kawhhran pasarih hrang lawng siloin santiluan ih um vivo Khristian pawl hrang ah a hleice in simcianak thucah a dong a si (Thup. 1:1-3, 9-11). Johan hnen ih thleng mi profet simcianak thu cu hivek deuh in a rung um: Pa Pathian cun Jesuh Khrih hnenah, Jesuh Khrih cun vancungmi pawl hnenah, vancungmi cun Johan hnenah, Johan cun casiartu le a ngaitu pawl hnenah a sim sawng a si. Himi cu Pathian in profet pawl hmangin minung pawl hnenih a thucah a phuangdan – Thuphuan le Thawtkhumdan zohthim ding thazet a si.

Tuizarh zirlai cun thuphuan-thawtkhum umdan pawl kan zoh ding ih, cumi ah tongkaa in simaw, nganca in simaw minung hnen ih Pathian thucah a thlen theudan pawl kan zoh ding a si.
