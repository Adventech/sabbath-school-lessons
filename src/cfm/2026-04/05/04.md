---
title:  Kaa ih simmi thucah
date:   27/10/2026
---

`Siar ding: 2 Piter 1:18-21. Pathian profet pawl ih simmi tongkam pawl cu nganmi pawl vek thotho in thawtkhum mi an si ih rinsan a tlak ti hi ziangtin saw kan theihfiang thei ding?`

Pathian cun Adam le Evi kha sual ih an tluk hlan (Sem. 2:16, 17) le an tluk hnuah (Sem. 3:9-19) a bia hai. Cumi thu ahcun A tongkam pawl hi famkim zetin an theithiam. A tongkam cu Sinai tlang ah fiangzet ih theih a si ih (Suah. 20:1-20), Jesuh ih tipilnak ah le danglamnak tlang khalah theih a si (Matt. 3:17, Matt. 17:5). Midang ih in biak tikah kan theisual men thei, asinan Pathian in zo simaw a biak ahcun theihsualnak le bumawknak zianghman a um lo ding.

Bible cun a relfiang zet ih, “ Pathian in Moses a bia ih a hnen ah: “Keimah cu Bawipa ka si’ “ tiah a ti (Suah. 6:2, NKJV). Bible ngantu tamzet pawl cun Pathian in a run biak hai tikah Pathian ih tongkam cu an rak theithiam. Himi thu ahcun tthimnak ah Isaiah (Isa. 8:1, 11), Jeremiah (Jer. 1:4, 9, 11), le Ezekiel (Ezek. 3:16, Ezek. 6:1) pawl ah hmuh theih a si. Himi rak hnongtu le hivek deuh nawn thildang rak dokalhtu pawl cun profet pawl an rinsan thei lo ih, Bible khal thumawknak tlak ah an ngai lo a si.

Pathian in minung fimnak tongkam in a profet pawl a biak vek ciah in profet pawl khal in an thungaitu pawl hnenah an sim ve a si. Thimnak ah Noah cu Pathian profet dik le rinum zet “dingfelnak thuthangtha simtu” a si (2 Pet. 2:5). Bible sung ahhin cangan mi zianghman a nei lo, asinan Pathian thucah cu a thu ngaitu pawl hnenah tongkam rori in a sim hai, a thucah cu Bible sungih cangantu profet pawl ai hnakin thawtkhum mi a si lo deuh tivek zianghman a um cuang lo.

Profet dang pawl Nathan, Elijah, Elisha, le Tipil Petu Johan tivek pawl cun Bible sungah zianghman cangan mi an nei lo, asinan an thucah ken mi cu tongkam rori in an phuang.

Jesuh Khrih teh ziangtin saw a si? Thuthangtha cabu pawl cun A leitlun hnatuannak ahkhan “leilung ah a kutzung in ca a ngan” thu an rak rel (Johan 8:6, NKJV). Cumi vial a si. Jesuh in ca a ngan thu khuitawk hmanah ngan khum mi kan nei lo. Asinan a thuzirh mi le tongkam ih thu a simnak ahcun “thuneihnak nei mi pakhat vek” a si (Matt. 7:29, NKJV). A thusim ngaitu hrekkhat pawl cun, “Amah vekin zohman in thu an sim dah lo” tiah an phuang (Johan 7:40, 46, NKJV). Jesuh cu tongkam rori ih Pathian thucah keng hmaisabik tu a si. Amah rori in zianghman Bible a ngan lo ruangah A thucah pawl cu thuneihnak nei lo le thil lolam men a si riai lo.

`Pathian in Bible sungih ca rak ngan ve lo profet pawl a neih hi ziangtluk lunglutza saw a si. Ziangruangah saw Bible sungih cangan mi hman nei ve lo profet pawl in thuneihnak an kentel thotho?`
