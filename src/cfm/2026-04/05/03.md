---
title:  Thawtkhum umdan pawl
date:   26/10/2026
---

Bible lawng hman siloin cabu tamzet le nganca tamzet pawl cu a santiluan in kan hnenah an ra thleng rero. Cumi cing khal in Bible cu Pathian Kamsuak thu ah kan ngai cio theu. A dang nganca pawl thawn ziangsaw danglamnak um?

`Siar ding: 2 Timote 3:15-17 le James 1:17. Himi thu ihsin ziang thucah thupi zet saw kan lak thei ding?`

2 Timote 3:16 thu ahhin letlin thabik thuhla ah relkhawmnak tamzet a um theu, asinan Bible let mi pawl hmuahhmuah cun hi thu ahhin an hmuhdan a bangaw deuh thluh, Bible pum hlum hi Pathian ihsin a ra suak mi a si thu an rel uar zet hai (2 Pet. 1:20, 21).

Bible simfiangtu pakhat cun, “Hitawk ahhin Paul cun cathianghlim pawl hmuahhmuah a vun ruat ih, ca thianghlim pawl hmuahhmuah cu “zirhawknak ah siseh, kawkawknak ah siseh, rem thatawknak ah siseh, dingfelnak hrang zirhawknak ah siseh, hlawknak a si”, ti ai hnakin cathianghlim pawl hmuahhmuah cu himi lamzin ahcun hlawknak hlir a si” tin a simfiang”—G. W. Knight III, The Pastoral Epistles: A Commentary on the Greek Text (Grand Rapids, MI: Eerdmans, 1992), p. 445.

Asinan Bible hramtohnak thawtkhummi cu ziangtin saw kan theihthiam thei ding? Profet thawtkhumnak cu Thiang Thlarau in Pathian thucah ih pawlkom awknak rintlak zet a um theinak dingah Profet dik pawl a bawm a si timi hi kan theih ding a tha. Cui bawmtu cun thucah dong ding lawngin a bawm lo ih, cui thucah cu a rak dongtu pawl in fiang zetih an theithhiam theinak dingah a bawm a si.

Pathian thucah cu langnak le mang ihsin a ra mi a si (Mipum. 12:6; Isa. 6:1, 8). Pathian cun mit ih hmutu pawl khal a hmang (1 Johan 1:1-3), Thlarau cun san thuanthu hliakhlaitu pawl a thawtkhum (Luka 1:1-3), cule Pathian cun an ruahnak ah a um pi a si. Thiang Thlarau in ziangtluk in hmang khal sehla, profet le Bible ngantu pawl hnen ah thudik thucah an phuangsuah theinak dingah a thucah ai hmanin a tumtah mi sawn kha pek in an um theu. Cumi sullam cu Pathian thucah pawl cu A thucah kengtu in a simfiangnak dingah anmai tongkam pawl an hman khal le ziangtivek lam khal in an danglamter ding a si lo, an thleng danglam ding khal a si lo.

Profet pawl ih thucah cu a rak dong hmaisatu pawl hnen ahcun tongkam rori in simaw (2 Pet. 1:19-21), asilole nganca in simaw(Johan 20:30, 31), asilole lemcannak in simaw a rak si theu (Ezek. 4:1-8).

`Bibel hi ziangtin thawtkhum in a um timi le a tlangpi thuin thawtkhummi in ziangtin hna a tuan timi ahhin thusutnak tamzet a um hrih lai, ziangtin Bible an rak ngan timi thei famkim thluh lo khal sehla Bible cu Pathian thawtkhummi kamsuak a si timi zumhnget hi ziang ruangah a thupi?`
