---
title:  Thukham Thar ih Profet deu pawl
date:   22/10/2026
---

`Siar ding: Matthai 24:23,24. Hitawk ahhin Jesuh in ziangmi ralrinnak saw in pek? Himi ralrin peknak hi ziangah kan ngai thutak ding?`

Jesuh amah rori khal in hi hnakih fiang deuhin a sim thei lo: “Ziangahtile Khrih deu le profet deu pawl an ra suak ding ih, hmuhsaknak le mangbangza nasa zetzet an tuah ding; cang thei sehla cu hrilmi pawl rori hman an bum ding’ ” (Matt. 24:24, NKJV).

Bumawknak thuhla ih ralrinnak a peknak ahcun “hmuhsaknak le mangbangza” pawl an tel ve ding ruangah thinlung neh thei zet cu a si tengteng ding. Anmah ten a cangsuak thei mi mangbangza pawl cu Pathian hnatuan mi nemhngetnak an si lo, mi an bum theinak dingih an langdan men a si. Thilti thei zet doral kan nei a si. Paul cun hitin ralrinnak in rak pe, “Ziangahtile kan buan mi hi tisa le thisen an si lo, lalnak pawl, thuneihnak pawl, hi leitlun thim uktu pawl, hmun sangih thlarau sual pawl tla an si sawn” (Efesa. 6:12, NKJV). Cule hi thilti theinak hnatuan pawl cu profet dik lo pawl hnen ihsin an si.

A ngaingai te cun profet dik lo tivek cu thil thar khal a si lem lo. Thukham Thar cun Thukham Hlun ih profet dik lo pawl thuhla a rel (Luka 6:26). Thukham Thar ahcun profet deu pawl cu “Tuu vun sinih, an sunglam cu cinghnia koham vek an si’ ” (Matt. 7:15, NKJV), cule “an rah ihsin kan thei ding” tiah in sim (Matt. 7:16, NKJV).

An hnatuan bik cu mibum hi a si (Matt. 7:17–20). Jesuh rori hmanin “ ‘profet deu tamzet an ra suak dingih mi tampi an bum ding’ ” (Matt. 24:11, NKJV), cule “ ‘cang thei hram sehla, hrilcia pawl rori hman a bum ding’ ” (Matt. 24:24, NKJV; zoh awla, Marka 13:22, 1 Johan 4:1) tiah a ti.

`Efesa 6:10-18 vek asile ziangtin saw bumnak ihsin kanmah le kanmah kan humhim awk thei ding?`

Zumnak thawn nungin “rinumnak tangphaw” bun in “thutak” sungih thuam awk, thlacamnak thawng in Thiang Thlarau ih ap awk le a Kamsuakthu ih mhumawknak pawl - hi pawl hi thlarau lam le Pathian thu lam ih in hruai sualnak hmuahhmuah ihsin kilhim awknak lamzin pawl an si.

`Kan leitlun ih bumnak hmuahhmuah dokalh dingin ziangtin na ralthuam tha deuhdeuh na tuah thei ding?`
