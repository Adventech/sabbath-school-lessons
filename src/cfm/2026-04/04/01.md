### Tuizarh zirlai hrang siar ding
Matt. 16:13-16, Ef. 2:20, Thup. 22:6, Luka 2:36-38, Tirh 21:9, Efe. 6:10-18.

> <p>Bible cangken</p>
> “Tu ah thilpek a phunphun a um, asinain Thlarau cu hmun khat a si. Cun rongbawlnak khal adangdang a um na’n Bawipa cu pakhat lawng a si. Cun hnatuan dan a phunphun a um na’n Pathian cu pakhat, mi zate hnenih ziangkim a tuahtu a si” (1 Korin 12:4-6, NKJV).

A pa in motor a mawng rero lai ah a fapa cun a pai’ motor thawn a bangaw mi motor pawl cu a zoh kual rero. A pa cun motor pakhat cu a khi ih, thotha zet in, “Ka fapa, khakha cu motor ka neih hmaisabik mi thawn an bangaw tuk” tiah a ti. Inn an thlen tikah a pa cun a motor hmaisabik neitu ih ca thupi zet pawl a phorh ih, a tu ih motor a neih mi ca thupi pawl thawn a fapa a hmuh. A fapa hnen ahcun ralring zetin motor pakhat hnu pakhat ih a langdan pawl a hmuh ih, a bangawknak le danglamnak khal a hmuh thluh. Asinan a pa in motor engine pakhat a tahthim tikah ziangkim an bangaw, thil dang cu bangaw lo ruiri khal sehla, engine cu a danglam lo.

Pathian cun Thukham Hlun le Thar ih um profet pawl hmangin thu a sim, an thucah pawl a fehtluang vivonak le semrem zetih an um theinak cu Thiang Thlarau – a hrampi bik, asilole Pathian thawkkhum “engine” cu a um ringring ruangah a si ding. Thiang Thlarau cun simcianak thilpek hmangin profet dik pawl a cakter a si. Anih cun profet thucah laimu bik ah Khrih a um ter, cumi cu Hebru cakuat khal in a fiangter zet (Heb. 1:1-3).

Tuizarh ah Thukham Thar sungih thlarau thilpek, profet vekih cangvaitu pawl in ziang an tuah, profet pawl cu dungthluntu pawl thawn an tahthim dan le profet deu ih tihnundan pawl kan zoh ding.
