---
title:  Pathian hnatuannak thuhla
date:   18/12/2026
---

Efesa 4 ahkhan Paul cun thlarau laksawng ih tumtah mi bik cu Pathian hnatuannak ih zumtu pawl bom ding hi a si timi a tarlang. Ellen G. White ih profet hnatuannak khal cuvek thotho cu a si. A thucah cun zumtu pawl cu Pathian thawn laicinawknak thuk zet an neih theinak dingah le midang hnen ih duhdawtnak an sim theinak dingah nasa zetin a bawm a si.

“Khristian tiih a relaw tu pawl hmuahhmuah lakah Seventh-day Adventist pawl cu leitlun hmai ih Jesuh khaisannak ahhin hmaihruaitu ding bik an si. Vancungmi a pathumnak ih thucah phuangsuah mi cun Sabbath thudik a tarlang a si. Himi thudik hi, thucah sungih a tel mi a dang pawl thawn phuangsuak ding an si; Asinan mi hipnak laimu bik Jesuh khrih cu hlonhlo ding a si lo. Khrih ih thinglamtah ahhin zangfahnak le thutak an um khawm ih, dingfelnak le daihnak cu pakhat le pakhat an hnam aw a si. Misual cu Kalvari lam hoi dingih hruai a si ding; nauhakte ih neihmi zumnak thawngin Rundamtu ih thatnak cu a rinsan ding a si ih, A dingfelnak pawm in, A zangfahnak a zum ding a si”—Gospel Workers, pp. 156, 157.

Cui hlei ah zumtu mikip hi Pathian hnatuannak ih tel ve dingih kawh kan si thluh. “Kan kawhhran member pawl in bulpak hnatuan taimaknak nasa zet an neih ding hi Bawipa in a duh a si”—Evangelism, p. 113.

“Bawipai’ hnatuannak ahhin zangzel tivek an um ding a si lo. Mi dangdang pawl cu minung kaitu vekin hnatuannak ah pumkhat aw hai seh. Leitlun siatralnak ihsin Khrih duhdawtnak ih rundamnak thianghlim ah thlarau hruaikhawm tum hram seh.”—Evangelism, p. 115. Amah Ellen G. White rori hman hi kum 1880 ahkhan Europe ah missionary hnatuan in a um ih, cule kum 1890 ahkhan Australia ah missionary hnatuan in a um sal.

Pathian ih simcianak in bulpak pumpehawknak le hnatuan dingih kawhnak ruangah Satan cu caan netabik ah Pathian minung pawl parah “a thin a heng” (Thup. 12:17, NKJV) a si. Annih pawl cu profet ih kaihruai mi, caan neta ih thucah a neitu, Jesuh ih dungthluntu pawl an si: “ “Bawipa cun kan kiangkap le kan hmailam ih a um mi tihnungza thil pawl a tarlang. Profet thuphuannak ih thlarau hmangin, leitlun sal ih hruai luh theinak bumnak pawl cu a tarlang ih, a mi pawl hnenah, ‘Himi cu lamzin a si; cuih lamzin ah nan feh pei,’ tiah a sim. (Isaiah 30:21) ... Doawknak ropi cun Satan ih bumnak pawl a tarlang a si”—Ellen G. White, manuscript 31, 1890.

Mipi in Jesuh hnen ih an nunnak a hlan tikah Satan pa cu a thinheng zet ih, Pathian profet au-aw pawl cu a cang thei tawk in a hmuhsuam zawk rero. Ellen G. White ih profet laksawng cu “a thupi lo tuk” tiih hmuhsuam ding hi a tumtah mi hrekkhat an si. Ziangkhal sehla a cangan lai le tui kan san cu a thluai aw tuk zo. Midang pawl khal rinhlelnak thawn thusuhnak nei in profet deu a si tiah an ti. Asinan Ellen G. White ih cangan mi pawl a thupi nawn lo tiih an rel theinak dingih lamzin thabik pakhat cu tuisan minung pawl in a cangan mi anmah ten an siar lonak hi a si.
