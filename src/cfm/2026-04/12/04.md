---
title:  Thlarau laksawng
date:   15/12/2026
---

Khrih ih kawhhran a fehdik theinak dingah Pathian cun an hnatuan a olsam deuh theinak dingah laksawng phunphun a pe hai. Thukham Thar ahhin thlarau laksawng phunphun ngan mi kan hmu, an zaten an bangaw deuh thluh, asinan famkim zet cun an bangaw lo ruiri. Hi laksawng pawl hi an pehzom aw tha zet hai a si.

`Siar ding: Efesa 4:11-14. Ziang ruangah Pathian in hivek laksawng pawl hi kawhhran ah a pek? Ziangtin saw simcianak laksawng cun kawhhran ah Pathian khawkhan thehsuaknak ih a bawm?`

Thlarau laksawng pawl hmuahhmuah ih tumtah mi cu thuthangtha phuangsuahnak ding a si vek ciah in kawhhran kilkhawi le dinpinak ding khal a si. Paul cun phun dangdang pali a rel mi ah simciannak laksawng a telh (“profet” timi tongkam ihsin ra suah mi a si). Laksawng dang pawl cu voikhat lawng rellang a si. Himi netkharnak hi thaten vun zoh sehla, simcianak laksawng cu thlarau laksawng pawl ih a thupi zet mi pakhat a si tin net kan khar thei ding.

Profet pawl thuhla ahhin thil umtudan tlangpi hrekkhat pawl kan zoh ding:

1. Profet pawl cu Pathian ih kawh mi an si (zoh aw, 1 Siang 19:16, 19-21 le Isaiah 6). Khuimaw caan ahcun tling lo zetin an thei aw, an tuanvo lak ding an huam lo, asinan thu an thlun.
2. Pathian cun profet pawl cu langnak, zan-mang, asilole theihthei mi tongkam hmangin a bia. Khuimaw caan ahcun Pathian cun hmailam can ih thilthleng dingmi a phuang suak. A cancan ahcun miphun pawl asilole mipi pawl ih tulai thuhla a sim hai (Daniel 2).
3. Cathianghlim cun taksa cangvaihnak a simfiang: profet pawl cu taksa cahnak an hloh men thei, asilole an thaw a cawl men thei, asinan huham thar thawn an khat thei (Dan. 10: 8-10, 18, 19).
4. Profet pawl cun an thucah cu lamzin dangdang in an sim - tongkam (Tirh 21:10-12), cangvaihnak hmangin (Ezekiel 4), asilole cakuat hmangin (Thuphuan 2, 3). Profet hrekkhat pawl cun calai lam bawmtu an hmang, cuvek ciah in Jeremiah bawmtu cu Barack a si (Jer. 36:1-4).
5. Profet ih thucah cu a hmaisa profet ih thucah thawn an bangaw ding a si, ziangahtile Pathian cu amah le mah a kalhaw dah lo a si (Isa. 8:20).
6. Profet thucah ih thupi pawl, profet bulpak nun ihsin thil bul hramthoknak cu Pathian a si timi a tarlang ding a si (Matt. 7:15-20).
7. Satan cun Pathian ih thil pawl hi a lem ih rak tuah ve a tum ringring. Himi hi profet laksawng hrang ah a dik mi a si. Caan neta ahcun profet deu pawl an um ding (Matt. 24:24, 2 Pet. 2:1).
