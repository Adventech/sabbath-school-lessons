---
title:  Santhar phuangsuak awknak
date:   17/12/2026
---

Tuisan ih zumnak nei tamzet hrekkhat pawl cun hlansan Bible profet pawl ih an diknak cu an pawm men thei, asinan kan san khalah khavek thotho in profet an um ding an zum pei maw? Joel 2:28-31 ih profet thilpek, caan neta ah sunglawi zetin tarlang a si leh ding timi kan zum ahcun, ziangvek saw a si ding?

`Siar ding: Tirh 2:36-42. Pentikos ni ih Piter ih thuthangtha simnak ih a thucah thupi zet kha ziangsaw a si? Piter ih thungaitu pawl in ziangtin an let?`

A.D 31 ih Pentikos ih Thiang Thlarau burh ih a um kha nasa zet khal sehla, caan neta ih Thiang Thlarau bur ih a um lai caan ih thil a thleng dingmi rak khihhmuh tu a si. Pentikos cu Jesuh khaisannak, sualsirnak karhzaiternak le Pathian hnatuan dingah mipi pawl ralring cia dingih sersuahnak a si. Piter ih thucah mi Khrih thuhla cu Thiang Thlarau in huham nasa zet a pe ih, cumi cun mipum 3,000 cu sualsir in tipilnak nei dingin a hruai a si. Thiang Thlarau ih thlawsuah mi “ruah hmaisa cun a mankhung zetmi thlaici, kawhhran – zumtu taksa pumkhat, Jesuh khrih ih duhdawtnak zohthim tlak zet le a ratsalnak ding thu phuangtu cu a phun ngah a si”.

Ruah hmaisa cu “a man khung zet mi thlaici that soh theinak dingih pek mi a si vek ciah in, ‘ruah neta’ cu rawlkhawmnak ding hrang a netnak ih pek a si ding.”—Ellen G. White, The Great Controversy, p. 611. Phundang in kan sim ding asile, tuisan ih profet laksawng phuangsuaknak – ruah neta – cun Pentikos ni ih ruah hmaisa ih phuang awknak vek thawn an bangaw zet ding.

Joel 2:28-31 ih kan hmuh zo vekin caan neta ih profet laksawng cu tleirawl, kum upa, nunau le mipa pawl hrangah on a si ding. Thiang Thlarau ih hrilmi pawl hmangin Pathian in thu a sim ding a si. Thiang Thlarau in caan neta lam ih Pathian thucah kengtu dingih a hrilmi pawl ih thucah cun Jesuh an khaisang ding ih, sualsirnak nei dingin mipi an ko ding, cule Pathian hnatuan thehsuak ih a um theinak dingah mipi a timlam ding a si. Himi ziaza pathum hi Ellen G. White profet hnatuannak ih hminsin mi a si. A nunsung ahkhan a thusim le cangan ahhin Jesuh le Cathianghlim a khaisang ringring; Seventh-day Adventist le midang pawl cu thinlung takten sualsirnak nei ding a ko hai ih, cule a hlo mi pawl hrang Pathian hnatuannak ih tel ve dingah mi hmuahhmuah a forh hai

`Ellen G. White ih hnatuannak thawngin mangbangza laksawng in pekmi hi ziangtlukin so na hmang tha?`
