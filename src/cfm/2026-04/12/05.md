---
title:  Caan neta ih a tangsun
date:   16/12/2026
---

Thuphuan 12 cun doawknak ropi thuanthu kau zet, vanram ihsin a thok ih, caan netabik tiang a um dingmi thuhla rel.

`Siar ding: Thuphuan 12:17. “Jesuh ih thutheihternak” hi ziangsaw a si? Thuphuan 19:10 cu Thuphuan 22:8,9 thawn zohtlang aw.`

Thuphuan 12:17 ahkhan caan neta lam ih kawhhran ziaza theihtheinak pahnih a tarlang. Kawhhran cun Thupek pahra a kilkhawi tha ding a si (Sabbath thupek khal a tel a si), cule “Jesuh ih thutheihternak” khal a nei ding a si. Himi thutheihternak hi ziang saw a si?

Bible letlin hrekkhat pawl cun “Jesuh ih thutheihternak” timi sullam cu zumtu kan si vekin Jesuh thu kan phuang ding ih kan hnatuannak le thuthangtha simnak thawngin Amah cu kan thehlar ding a si tin an simfiang (Zoh aw, Thup 19:10 NIV: “Jesuh ih thutheihternak cu kai hnget aw” Jesuh cu Khristian pawl ih theihpi mi a si). Bible dangdang cun sullam dangdang in pe, Jesuh amah rori in A kawhhran ah thutheihternak a pe vek khi a bang (Thup 19:20, GNT “Jesuh ih phuang mi thudik” – Jesuh cu thutheihternak kengtu a si). A ngaingai te cun Grik ih nganmi tong bulhram cu khuivek khal in lehlin theih veve mi a si. A sicun Johan ih rel duhsan cu ziangsaw a si ding?

Simdan pahnih hi kan vun zohtlang cun tongkam remdan cu an bangaw nasa a si timi kan hmu thei ding (Kan Bible ai hnakin tong bulhram Grik ahcun an bangaw sinsin). “Pathian thupek pawl” cu Pathian hnen ihsin a ra ti a fiang vek ciah ih, “Jesuh ih thutheihternak” khal hi Jesuh hnen ihsin a ra mi a si. Cuti a sicun thutheihpitu cu Jesuh a si. Anih cun kawhhran dik a theihter a si. Ziangtin a tuah? Simcianak laksawng hmangin a tuah a si.

Thuletnak cu Thuphuan 19:10 le Thuphuan 22:9 sungah hmuh a theih. Himi ahcun “Jesuh ih thutheihternak” timi cu “Profet thuphuannak ih thlarau” thawn an bangrep ti a fiang. Johan amah rori hi profet a si ih, a ziaza ahhin “Jesuh ih thutheihternak” a langter a si. Profet cu A mipawl hnenih Pathian thucah kengtu dingah a hleice ih kawh mi an si, sual sir dingih kawh le forh tu ding ah, thazang petu dingah le hmailam caan tarlang tu dingih kawh mi an si.

Thuphuan 12:17 in Jesuh cun himi simcianak laksawng thawngin A kawhhran theihthiam a theih a si timi a langter. Himi sullam cu Pathian in Thukham Hlun ih Israel le Judah pawl hnenah a profet pawl a thlah vek ciah in asilole tirthlah Johan cu Khristian kawhhran hmaisa pawl hrangih a thlah vek ciah in profet pawl cu leitlun sancemnak ni netabik ah an hung suak ve ding a si.

`Ellen G. White ih hnatuan cu “siamcianak thlarau” hnatuannak ih zohthim ding a dikmi a si tiih a tarlang mi ziangsaw na hmuh zo? (Thup. 19: 10).`
