---
title:  Joel ih thutiam
date:   13/12/2026
---

`Siar aw: Joel 2:28-32. Ni neta ah Pathian in ziang tuah ding a tiam?`

B.C kum zabi pasarihnak ih profet a si mi Joel cun hmailam caan ih thil a thleng dingmi thuhla ah theihtheinak thupi zet a pe. Himi thu kan siar tikah amai san ih um ding thuhla maw a rel, asilole hmailam ih um ding thuhla saw a rel tin kan mangbang men thei.

Pathian thu zirmi pawl cun himi simcianak pahnih karlak ahcun ruatdan pahnih an nei – A si theudan le Thuphuandan timi hi a si. A si theudan simcianak cun miphun, nambat le hmin tivek pawl hi a ngaingai in a rel thluh (zoh aw, thimnak ah, Jer 25:1-11). Cule khat lam ah Thuphuandan simcianak cun duhsannei hminsinnak tongkam, tikcu caan hrangih hmuhsaknak nambat pawl a hmang ih, vanlam hminsinnak pawl a rel theu (zoh aw, Daniel 7). Khuimaw caan ahcun himi simcianak pahnih hi an pehzom aw in kan hmu theu (zoh aw, thimnak ah Mathai 24).

Joel 2:28-29 teh ziangsaw a si ding? Himi thu cu a netatu sawn ahkhin a um in a lang. Cumi sullam cu profet san ih a thleng famkim ding hi cun kan zum lo, asilole a hnatuan a theh hnu reilote ih thleng ding khal in ruahsannak kan nei lo. Cumi thawn kalh aw zetin himi simcianak hi hmailam caan ih a famkim dan ding kan thlir ding a si. Bawipa in simcianak laksawng a peknak ding thupi le tikcu caan pawl hminsin aw, “ Bawipai’ ni maksak le tihnung za a ra thlen hlanah” (Joel 2:31 NKJV) ti a si kha!. Ziangtik ni saw si ding? Jesuh voikhat a rungtumnak maw, asilole voihnih a rungtum saw?lole a pahnih ih a rel tum mi teh a si kem?

Joel simcianak sungih ni le thla hminsinnak pawl kan vun ruat tikah himi ruahnak pawl cu Bible hmundang ah hmuhding tamzet a um tihi zamrang zetin kan thei thei a si. Jesuh cun a dungthluntu pawl hnenah ni le thla le arsi ih hminsinnak thuhla hi A caan netnak lam ih a thurel hminthang zet ahkhan a rel lang a si(Mathai 24:29). Cuvek ciah in Thuphuan ngantu Johan khal in hminsinnak bangaw a tarlang ve (Thup 6:12,13). Jesuh le Johan cun kan Bawipa veihnihnak a rat sal hlan ih vanlam hminsinnak netabik an hmuitin a si.

Curuangah cuvek hmuhsaknak thotho khihhmuh tu Joel simciannak hi caan neta lam ahhin ziangmaw cin cu a tel ve ding tin kan ruat: “ ‘Cun van pawl ah le leilung ah mangbangza ka langter ding, thisen, meisa, le meikhu zual pawl thawn. Bawipai’ ni maksak le tihnung-za a ra thlen hlanah ni cu thim ah a cang ding ih, thlapi cu thisen ah a cang ding’ ” (Joel 2:30, 31).

`Simcia vekih a thleng dik thluh mi simciannak pawl khi vun ruat aw. Himi cun hmailam caan ih thil thleng ding – Jesuh veihnih ratsalnak tivek pawl ahhin Pathian rinsan thei dingin ziangtin in bawm ding?`
