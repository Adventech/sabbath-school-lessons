### Simcianak Thilpek

Neuroscientists pawl cun minung naute pawl ih an tongkam cangvaihdan mangbangza hi an theithiam. Thla 6 an kim ah nauhakte pakhat cun laitong fang a awndan a theithiam asilole tongdang 7,000 tlun kai ih awthluk dan pawl a theithiam. Tongdang hman a si le an kai ol ve hngal. Himi mangbangza tikcu caan a um lai ahhin an thinlung cu kau zetin a ongaw ih, an suah tik ih thil awn mi 800 tlun lai ihsin mai suahpi tong dangdang 40 tlun hrawng cu an kai cih ve mai. Asinan thil pakhat a um mi cu: Nauhak pawl cu an tong thei tengteng ding timi hi a si. Cui thil cu nausuak pek hrangih an zirmi zirlai tin na rel thei ding.

Pathian in kan pehtliah ih in biak ding hi in duh lo bangta sehla ziangah hivek ih tongkam kaih olsam theinak nei dingin mangbangza ih in tuah? Thusuhnak cu tongmawi hmandan hi a si. Si e, Kan Bible cun fiangzetin in rak sim, “A liam zomi caan ah voi tampi profet pawl hmangin pupa pawl hnenih a phunphun thu a rak simtu Pathian in, Hi ni netabik pawl ah a Fapa hmangin kan hnenah thu in sim zo, amah cu thil hmuahhmuah rocotu ah a ruat ih, amah ihsi thothoin leilung pawl a sersiam” (Hebru 1:1-2)

Adam le Eve kha sual ah tlu in Pathian thawn pawlkom awknak an neih thein awn lo hnu ah Pathian cun a tanta men lo ih ih, sual ih tlu zo Adam le Eve khal a fehsan men lo. A va hawl hai ih Adam hnenah, “Khuitawk ahsaw na um”? (Sem 3:9)tiah a sut. Pathian cun leitlun sualnak ihsin rundamtu ding mi pakhat a suah ding thuhla simcianak a va rel (Sem 3:15). Sual ih tluknak hmun Eden rori ah Jesuh a suak dingih, misual pawl a lam ah a hip ding timi simcianak a um ih, cumi cu an ruahsannak umsun a si.

Tui kuata ahcun simcianak laksawng timi kan zir ding, Pathian cun sual ih a tlu zo minung, Amah thawn hmaiton ih pawlkom awknak nei thein awn lo pawl hrangih lamzin a hawldan le pehtlaih awksal a duhdan pawl kan zoh ding. Hi Thiang Thlarau laksawng hleice pawl hi 1 Korin 12, 1 Korin 14, Efesa 4, le Rom 12 pawl ah an lang. Thiang Thlarau cu himi le a dang thlarau laksawng zemdarh tu bik a si ih, “Amai duhdan vekin mikip bukpak cio hnenah a zem darh” (1 Kor. 12:11, NKJV). Tikcu caan tinkim ahhin sual ih tluk veten Pathian in Eden hmuan ih a thil tuah mi vek ciah in Pathian thawn laicin awknak thar kan neih theisalnak ding le tuahthat awk theisalnak ding Pathian thucah cu thlarau thilpek dawngngah mi pakhat hnen ihsin a um ringring.

Pathian in profet pawl a kawhdan le a ngaingai an si mi timi kan fiah theidan ding pawl kan zoh ding. Thukham hlun le Thar ih um profet pawl ih bangawknak le danglamnak khal kan zoh ding ih, cumi phah ah thuphuangsuahnak le kamsuak thu pawl in kan nun ah ziangvek in hmual a nei timi kan theihthiamdan khal kan zir ding. Hrekkhat profet pawl cu thu an phuang, hrekkhat in ca an ngan ih, an hrekkhat cun thuphuang in ca khal an ngan kop. Himi pawl hi kan thlir dingih, Pathian ih a thuhla (nganca) a remkhawmdan, an relduh san pawl le hi profet pawl ih thucah a thluntu pawl ih thlawsuahnak pawl khal kan thlir ding.

Pathian cun simcianak laksawng hi thil thupi panga ah a hmang: 1. Amah le A thudik phuanglang awknak le a tlusia mi minung pawl hrangih a duhdan phuang lang awknak dingah, (2) Thil hi a sinak vek ih hmuh le thil thupi a thleng rero lai mi le a thleng dingmi thawn pehpar aw ih thurawn peknak ding hrangah, (3) Khrih ih taksa ngaingai- kawhhran – Pathian hnatuanak hrang ah, (4) A dungthluntu pawl thlarau lam ih thazang peknak hrang (5) Misual thangharh ter ih, sualsir dingih kawhnak ding hrang pawl ah an si. Cui hlei ah “Simcianak laksawng” cu caan neta ih a tangsun kawhhran hrang Pathian ih hminsinnak pahnih lakih pakhat a si (Thup. 12:17, Thup. 19:10).

Curuangah Pathian cun mi hlo pawl cu runinn ah a hruai ding ih, caan neta ih ummi pawl hrangah simcianak laksawng a pe a si (Joel 2:28-31). Seventh-day Adventist pawl cun – san le vang thatzet in – Ellen G White cu kawhhran hmusuaktu pakhat, tuisan ih profet thilpek dongtu a si timi an zum.

Curuangah Cathianghlim sungih simcianak laksawng thuhla kan zir ahhin niruk ni (Friday) tin in Ellen G. White ih Profet hnatuannak nei dingih kawh a sinak kan zir ding. Kawhhran kan si vekin hi laksawng mangbangza hi in pe a si. Ziangtin saw tha zetih kan zir hman thei ding?

Si e, naute kan si lai ihsin tong thei dingih duan kan si. Pathian ih Profet in kan hnen ih a rel mi ziang a si timi ngai hi ziangtluk in a thupi?
