---
title:  Pathian thuneihnak
date:   29/11/2026
---

Na aiawh ih thurel dingin mi pakhat hnenah remtinak na pek hlanah na mi rinsan mi hril lo in na um kem? Anih cun na dinhmun a lo theihfiang ih, rinum zetin thazetih a simfiang thei ding hi thil thupi zet a si.

`Siar ding: Suahhlannak 2:11-15 le Suahhlannak 3:1-3, 10-13. Ziangtin saw Moses kha tuu khaltu a si lai ahkhan Pathian hrang ih thusimtu dingih a timlam awk thiam?`

Moses cu a kum hmaisa kum 40 sung kha Izipt ram ih an peksuak mi fimthiamnak thabik ngah theinak dingah tikcu caan a hmang. Asinan himi cun Pathian thucah kengtu si dingin a timlam ter lo a si. A dang kum 40 sung cu leitlun ih hnaihnoknak zianghman um loin nelrawn ah tuu khal in tikcu caan a hmang ding. Lamzin tamzet ahcun siangpahrang faduat a si vekih a rak zirmi tamzet pawl cu a hlonhloh thluh a tul. Israel pawl suah zalen dingih hna a tuan hlanah, Moses cun a ai-awh ih thu a simsak dingmi Pathian a theihngah ta a tul a si.

`Siar ding: Suahlannak 4:10-16. Profet kawhnak le hnatuandan thuhla ahhin Pathian le Moses karlak biakawknak ihsin ziang kan zir thei ding?`

Moses cu Pathian dinhmun ih um dingih fial a si ih, Aaron cu Moses aiawh ih tongtu a si (Suah. 4:16; zoh awla, Suah. 7:1). Moses le Aaron tlun ih a tuah vek ciah in Pathian cun profet pawl cu a thucah a pek mi pawl hnen ih an thlen theinak ding lamzin thabik hawl dingin a bawm a si.

Moses cun a hlan ahcun a rak hlawhsam zo ti a thei. Israel mi pawl thawn an hmu awk netabik kha a vun mang suak, cumi ahcun thuneihnak a neih mi ahkhan thusutnak a rak tong zo a si kha (Suah. 2:14). Pathian cun Moses ih a tih thupnak pawl a theithiam zet. A tih thupnak cu thinsau zetin a zawi vivo. Phuahhlam a neih ding pawl hmuahhmuah cu hlonhlo sak thluh khal sehla, Moses cun kawhnak cu a el thotho. A tih thupnak cun zum lonak ah a hruai lut a si. A duh lonak ruangah Pathian in a hman theihdan ding thu a sut. Cumi ciah ah Pathian cun profet in duh lo khal sehla, a pawm tengteng dingmi thupek a pe suak a si (Suah. 3:14-17).

`Profet na si lo men thei, asinan na nun sung ahhin Pathian in a hrangih hnatuan dingih a lo kawh mi hi ziangsaw a si? Himi kawhnak hrangah nangmahten na timlam awknak thabik hi ziangsaw a si ding?`
