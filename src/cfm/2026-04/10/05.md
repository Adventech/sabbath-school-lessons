---
title:  Pawlkom thuneihnak
date:   02/12/2026
---

Hosesa 12:13 cun “profet hmangin Israel cu Izipt ihsin Bawipa in a hruai suak ih, profet hmangin anih cu a humhim” tiah fiangzet in a rel. Pathian minung kaihhruai le kilhim cu Bible profet pawl ih hnatuannak ah a thupi zet mi le a tul zet mi a si.

Thuthentu 4:4-8, 2 Sansiarnak 20:14-17, Suahlannak 12:1-3, le Daanpeksalnak 1:9-16 tivek pawl kan siar tikah Profet pawl cu Pathian in a minung pawl hrangah Amai kaihhruainak tarlangnak ahhin an thupi zet timi kan hmu thei. Doawknak le harsatnak a um caan ah Pathian in Jehaziel le Deborah hmangih thil a rak ruah vek ciah in a minung pawl le an hruaitu pawl hnenah ruahsannak, forhfialnak le kaihhruai awknak a peknak hmanrua cu profet pawl hi an si. Profet pawl cun puai le biaknak tivek thawn pehpar aw khal in kaihhruainak an pe a si (Suah. 12:1-3). Caan dang ahcun Pathian cun miphun ukawknak thawn pehpar aw in a profet pawl a bia theu. A tawizawng cun Israel nunram ih khuitawk hmun khal ahhin Pathian in A thucah kengtu dingah a hril mi hmangih A kaihhruai lonak a um lo.

`Simcianak laksawng in Khristian kawhhran hmaisa pawlkomnak le kaihhruaiawknak ih a bomnak hrekkhat pawl kha ziang an si? Tirh 6:1-7, 1 Kor. 5:1-5, 1 Kor. 7:10-16, Titas 1:5.`

Hnatuannak lam ih kaihhruai awknak le hmuhtheih hruaitu um riai loin pawlkom thar a um thok ding timi vun ruat aw. Cuvek pawlkom cu harsatnak tonnun an nei tengteng ding. Hivek ciah hi Khristian kawhhran hmaisa ih an ton mi dinhmun cu a si. Jesuh cu vanah a kai zo ih, A dungthluntu pawl hnenah a um nawn lo. A hnen ihsin thupek fiang zet an nei: “ ‘ leitlun pumpi ah va feh uhla, thu va sim uh” timi hi a si (Marka 16:15, NKJV). Dungthluntu 12 cu hrilsuah mi si khal sehla, an hnatuannak ih anmah bawmtu ding pawlkom kaihhruai awknak mumal zianghman a um lo.

Kawhhran hmaisa cun harsatnak tamzet an tong, cumi pawl cu kawhhran pawlkom awkdan, Pathian hnatuan dingih timlam awkdan le an lak ih nun thianghlim lo, le fehsualnak theihfiang le mawhthluk a tuldan pawl hi a si. A ngaingai te cun kawhhran hmaisa ih simcianak laksawng hnatuannak cun kilhimnak le humhimnak lamah a thupi zetmi hnatuan an nei a si (Tirh 13:1, 2). A ngaingai te cun Pathian hnatuannak ih kekarnak le pawlkom awknak ih bawmtu tha zet profet simcianak thilpek um lo bangta sehla, kawhhran hmaisa pawl khan ziangtin an rak thok thei ding tihi ruat ding harsa zet a si.

Mi hrekkhat cun “Sakhuanak pawlkom” ahhin rinhlelnak an nei in a lang. Ziangvek harsatnak a um khal ah, Kawhhran kaihhruai awknak ih rak telvenak in ziang mi hlawknak a nei? Nangmah hnakin a tum sawn mi thil pakhatkhat ih sungtel na sinak ihsin ziang na pek thei ih, ziang na ngah thei?
