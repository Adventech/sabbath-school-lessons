---
title:  Jesuh Thuneihnak
date:   30/11/2026
---

Jesuh ih thuneihnak bulhram kha ziang a si? Himi thuneihnak nemhngetnak ziangsaw a si? zoh aw, Matt. 21:23-27 le Johan 17:2.

Jesuh in thu a sim rero lai ah puithiam pawl a hnenah an ra ih, hi hnatuannak ahhin zo in thuneihnak a pe timi theihduh ah simfiang dingin an ngen. Ziangahtile Jesuh cu ziangvek tualsung tlawng hman ih thehsuak mi a si lo ih, Rabbi pawl ih nunphung tlawng khal ah zirnak a rak nei lo. An thusuhnak pawl let lem cuang loin Jesuh cun thulet dan ding lamzin a khihhmuh ih, thusuhnak a neitu pawl hnenah ziangti lam hman ih el theih lomi Tipil petu Johan ih thuneihnak a langter mi thuhla a sut hai. Thusuhnak let duh loin Johan ih hnatuannak Lal lukhuh khumtu Pathian ih huham cu hnongin, Johan vekih Pathian ih a thlah mi Jesuh ih thuneihnak cu an hnong a si.

`A hnuai ih Bible cang pawl in Jesuh ih thuneihnak thuhla ziangsaw in sim? Matt. 7:28, 29; Marka 1:21-27; Luka 8:22-25; Luka 9:1; Johan 5:25-27; le Johan 7:38.`

Minung sersiamtu (Johan 1:3) le rundamtu (Rom 3:24) a si vekin thuneihnak pawl hmuahhmuah cu Jesuh ta an si thluh. Anih cu Pathian kamsuak Thu a si. Jesuh in lei ih a nun le a hnatuan lai ih Pathian thuneihnak a neih mi cu zohman in an zohthim thei lo ih, mahte ih tuahcop theih mi thil khal a si lo. Jesuh ih thuzirhnak cun a ngaitu pawl mangbang zetin a tuah, ziangahtile thuneihnak neitu mi pakhat vek ciah in a zirh hai ruangah a si (Matt. 7:29).

Thuthangtha cabu hmuahhmuah cun Jesuh ih profet thuneihnak a neihmi pawl an ngan khum. Jesuh cun sual a ngaidam (Marka 2:10, 11), khawsia a dawisuak (Marka 3:15) cule mi ih thuruah mi a theithei (Johan 2:24, 25). Mithi a kai tho ih, kumkhaw nunnak a pe suak thei (Johan 10:28). Kanmah in Tlentu vekin leitlun ih a um lai ahkhan Jesuh cun Pa Pathian ih a pek mi thuneihnak pawl a hmang (Johan 17:2), cule ziangtik lai caan khalah Pa thawn hna an tluan tlang ih, Pa Pathian ah a thum aw a si (Johan 5:19), Cule Amai thuhla theihpitu tiih a rel mi Cathianghlim a khihhmuh theu. Jesuh cun thuneihnak pumhlum a nei thei ih, cui thuneihnak cu A dungthluntu pawl hnenah a pe thei a si (Marka 6:7). Pathian Kamsuak Thu a si vekin, A theihternak pawl cu A pekmi profet pawl hnen ihsin an ra a si (Thup. 19:10).

`Jesuh cun nemhngetnak tamzet a pek hmanah mi hrekkhat pawl cun an hnong lai thotho. Kan zum theinak ding ih a san le vang ummi pawl hmuahhmuah pek thluh kan si hnuah zum lonak thang sungih kan tluk lonak dingah ziangtin kan ral ring ding?`
