### Tuizarh zirlai hrang siar ding
Suah 4:10-16, Luka 8:22-25, Johan 3:1-10, Tirh 16:25-34, 1 Kor. 5:1-5, Luka 7:28.

> <p>Bible cangken</p>
> “Ziangahtile Pathian thu cu a nungmi, le huham a neimi, le ziangvek ralnam kap hnih hriam hnak khalih hriam sawn, nunnak le thlarau, le hlikhlok le thlik thenawknak tiang khal suntlangtu, le thinlung ih ruatmi, le tumtahmi pawl theitu a si” (Hebru 4:12).

November 9, 1989 ahkhan Günter Schabowski cun Germany nisuaknak lam ah TV ih thuthang a phuangnakah tisual mi a nei ih, Berlin kulhpi in a tuar phah rori a si. Germany nisuaknak lam ih thuthang latu a si vek in Schabowski cun a thuphuan mi ahkhan dik lonak a rak nei. Nisuaknak lam Germany pawl cun harsa zetin visas tha zet an neih thei hnuah nitlaknak lam Europe ah an tlawng thei tiih thuthan ding kha a simsual ih, nisuahnak lam German pawl cun nitlaknak lam Europe ahhin zalen zetin an tlawng thei tiah a sim. Thuthang pesuak tu in cui thil cu ziangtik in a thok ding tin a sut tikah, Schabowski cun, “ka theih ban tawk ahcun, a thok cing ko ding, a rei lo ding” tiah a ti. Minute reilote sungah mipi pawl cu ramri zohfelnak hmunah an ra thleng thluh ih, kumpi in a than thluh zo ruangih an feh tlang duhthu an au suahpi a si.

Mi cu kompani asilole rampi aiawh ih thurel dingin thuneihnak an pe suak theu. Asinan Pathian hrangih thurel tu pawl thuhla teh ziangtin a si ding? Tuizarh ahcun Bible profet pawl in ziang thuneihnak an nei timi kan zir ding. Cule Bible ngan ve lotu profet pawl – profet Nathan, Tipil petu Johan, si e, Ellen G. White khal tel in ziang thuneihnak an nei ve timi pawl kan zir ding.
