---
title:  Athen ihsin Korin ah
date:   29/06/2026
---

`Siar ding: Tirhthlah 17:16–34. Paul kha Korin khua ih a feh hlanah khuitawk ah a feh? Cutawk ahcun ziangsaw a tuah?`

Tirthlah 17:16-34 ahkhan Paul kha Korin khua ih a feh hlanah Athen mi pawl hnenah thuthangttha a simthu kan hmu. Ngaingai te cun Athen ih feh kha a tum lo lawk, asinan Berea khua ih dokalhnak a ton ruangah a rualpi hrekkhat pawl ih bomnak thawngin Athen ah a feh(Tirh 17:13–15).

Paul thawn Athen khua ih a fehtu pawl cu Berea khua ah an kirsal ih, Timote le Silas cu a rang thei bikin Paul va bawm dingin thupek an si (Tirh 17:15). Paul in Timote le Silas an rat ding a hngak lai ah ziangthil a rak tuah timi cu Tirh 17:16–34 sungah kan hmu. Sinakok, bazar le Areopagus ah Jesuh thuhla a rak sim rero. Jesuh thuhla rel hi a cawlhsan thei lo, tikcu remcang a cuh ih a sim ringring ttheu.

`Siar ding: Tirh 18:1-11. Paul in Korin khua a thlen ih, cutawk khawpi sungih a um sung ahkhan ziangsaw a tuah?`

Paul cu voihnih Missionary khual a tlawnnak ah Korin khua a pal. Cutawk ah Paul hi kumkhat le hrek sung a um thu Luka cun in rak sim.

A tidan ttheu vekin Paul cu Sinakok khawmnak ah Pathian hnattuan a thok. (Tirh 18:4–6). Tirh 17:1, 2 ahkhan cui thil cu a tuahdan ttheu a si timi in rak sim. “Jews miphun cu hmaisabik ah ret ding” timi fehdan a thlun (Rom. 1:16, Tirh 13:46), cumi cu Jesuh in a dungthluntu pawl hnenih thu a pekvek ciah kha a si (zoh awla, Tirh 1:8).

A netnak ahcun Silas le Timote cun Korin khua ih an um pi tikah, Paul cu “Thlarau in a turhnawh ih, Judah pawl hnenah Jesuh cu Khrih a si ti thu a sim fiang” (Tirth 18:5). Korin khua ih a um lai ah, “Pathian kamsuak thu zirh in” a buai thei zet (Tirh 18:11, NKJV). Himi thu khalah cuvek ttongkam hminthang zet pawl a hmang ih, “Jesuh Khrih le khenbet mi pa ih thu siarlo” Korin mi pawl hnenah “zianghman theih a tum lo” (1 Kor. 2:2, NKJV).

Athen le Korin khua ah Paul ih missionary hnattuannak ihsin thuthangttha phuang dingah caan remcang hmuahhmuah hmang thluh dingin ziangsaw kan zir thei ding? Jesuh thu midang hnenih sim theinak caan remcang na neihmi le ziangtin caan na hmang thei ding ti ruat aw.
