---
title:  Paul, Pathian kawh mi Jesuh ih Tirhhlah
date:   28/06/2026
---

Paul cun Korin khaw mi pawl hnen ih a cakuat hramthoknak ahkhan “Pathian duhdan vek in” (1 Korin 1:1; 2 Kor 1:1 thawn zoh kop awla, )Jesuh ih Tirhthlah a si thu a rel aw. Jesuh thawn an pehtlaih awknak ahhin ziangvek mi a si timi a nem hnget ih, hivek ttongkam pawl hi a cakuat mi pawl hmuahhmuah ongnak ahhin a tuah ttheu.

`Siar ding: 1 Korin 1:1 le Rome 1:1. Himi bungcang pahnih ahhin Paul ih a hnattuannak ih thil thupi zet kha ziang saw a si? (Gal. 1:1 khalah zoh aw.)`

Paul cun Pathian duhzawng famkimter tu si dingih kawh a si thu le tirhhlah a si thu a rel. Kawh a sinak cu minung ihsin a ra mi siloin Pathian hnen ihsin a ra mi a si ti a zum hnget (Gal 1:1). Paul hi Pathian in a nui pumsung ihsin Jeremiah a kawh vek ciah in (Jer. 1:5), Pathian zangfahnak ih cangvaihnak nei dingin kawh a si(Gal. 1:15), Cumi cu Pathian in Zentel pawl hnenah Khrih thuthangttha phuangsuah theinak dingah a hung cang a si.

1 Korin 15:8 ahkhan Paul cun Khrih thawhsal hnu ih mi tampi lakih a langnak ahkhan a rak tel ve thu a rel (1 Kor. 15:5–7). Cumi hnu reilote ah tirhthlah si ding ih kawh a sinak cu Jesuh thawn an tonawknak ruangih ra ummi a si tin a rel aw (1 Kor. 15:9–11).

“Jesuh ih tirhtlah” timi thulu cun ruahdan phunphun a huap thluh thei. A pakhatnak ahcun, Jesuh ih kawh mi rori a si timi a langter. Ziangkhal sehla, Paul cun himi ttongkam hi Khrih ih siahhlawh a sinak(Rom. 1:1, Titus 1:1, Gal. 1:10) le zirhtu le thusimtu a si thu a rel awknak ah a hmang(1 Tim. 2:7, 2 Tim. 1:11). Thusimtu a si khalah, zirhtu a si khalah Khrih cu a tarlang ringring. A tawi zawngin Paul cu Jesuh ih Tirhthlah a si.

Paul hi Jesuh ih tirhthlah lawnghman siloin, a nunnak ih a laimu bik cu Jesuh a si. Paul ih ruahnak le thinlung cu Jesuh hnen ah a khat liam thluh. Himi a langfiangnak cu 1 Korin ahkhan thu hmaisa lam le lungawi thusimnak ahkhan Jesuh thu a sim leuhleuh (cang 9 ah voi 9 a um). Paul cun Jesuh a duhdawttuk ruangah A thuhla rel lole ruat loin a um thei lo. A ngaihsak mi pawl hnenah Jesuh thuhla sim a duh, cuticun an hringnun ih laimu bik ah Khrih a um thei ding a si. Tirhthlah si dingih kawh a si lai ah ziangvek dinhmun khalah Jesuh thluntu rinum ding si dingih kawh an si.

`Paul hi Tirhthlah si dingih kawh mi a si. Nangteh ziangmi si dingih kawh saw na si, cule cui thil cu nangmah lo kawhnak a si timi hi ziangtin na thei? Zianghman rel suah ding na neih lo ahcun, Pathian thawn nan lentlannak ahhin ziang thildik lo saw a um ding?`
