Ellen G. White, “Corinth,” pp. 243–254, in The Acts of the Apostles.

“Korin khua ih thuthangttha a sim tikah tirhthlah cun Athen khawpi ih a hnattuan dan vek ciah in a ttuan nawn lo….elawknak le thuruah relnak pawl a hnong thluh ih, “Jesuh Khrih le khenbetmi pa ih rundamnak siar lo” Korin khawmi pawl lakah “zianghman theih a tum lo.’”—Ellen G. White, The Acts of the Apostles, p. 244.

“Pawl cun hlawhtlinnak ih tahnak a nei” asinan “cutawk ih a hmuh mi thilri pawl ihsin biakinn saknak ding fimnak ahhin rinlelnak a nei. Korin cu thusuhnak tamzet a suahnak hmun a si tin a ruat ih, suahsan men dingin thutluknak a tuah…

“Khawpi suahsan men dingih a timlam awk rero lai ah…. Zanah Bawipa cu langnak hmangin a hnenah a rung lang ih, “ ttih aw hlah, thu phuang aw… ziangahtile hi khawpi ahhin minung tampi ka nei a si” tiah a run ti. Paul cun himi cu Korin ih um hrih dingih thupek a si timi le Bawipa in thlaici a karhzai ter ding timi a theithiam….Jesuh Khrih ih thlazar hnuai ah kawhhran tumzet a suak thei” —Ellen G. White, Sketches from the Life of Paul, pp. 106, 107

“Paul cu Korin khua ah kum 1 le thlaruk sung a um. A hnattuannak cu ziangkhal sehla, cui khawpi lawngah a si lo… Korin khua hi a hmunpi bik ah a tuah… kawhhran tampi a run suahphah… Paul cun a ngaihsakmi kawhhran pawl ah a um thei lo, asinan a thupi zetmi thucah pawl an hnen ih a thlen tikah Pathian kamsuak thu vekin an rak dong thiam… Hivek cakuat pawl hi biakinn ah an rak siar ttheu”—Ellen G. White, Sketches from the Life of Paul, p. 109.

**Relkhawm ding thusuhnak pawl:**

`Paul cun Jesuh ih tirhthlah a si zia le himi kawhnak cu Pathian hnen ihsin a si zia a theifiang zet. Zo kan si timi le in kawhnak hi ziang a si timi theihthiam hi ziangruangah saw a thupit?`

`Caan reilote sung cu Paul cun Korin khua ih missionary a ttuannak cu cawlhsan ih, khawpi suahsan a tum. A thinlung thleng danglam dingih a tuahtu kha ziangsaw a si? Missionary hnattuan bansan kan tum tikah himi cun ziangtin in rak bawm?`

`Korin kawhhran member pawl cu an kiangkap ih um nunphung pawl cun an ciahneh zet. Himi cu tui kan san ah kan lakih thil ummi khal a si thotho. Ziangtin saw Leitlun ih kan um lai ahhin (Johan 17:11, 15) “leitlun ih thil um… tisa cakhiarnak le mit cakhiarnak le nun puarthaunak” ” (1 Johan 2:16, ESV) tivek pawl ih in ciahneh thei lo dan ding? Kan kiangkap nunphung in a sia zawngin Kawhhran a ciahneh theidan hi ziangvek saw an si?`
