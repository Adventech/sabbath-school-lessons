---
title:  Korin khawpi
date:   30/06/2026
---

`Siar ding: Tirh 18:1–3, 1 Korin 5:9–11, le 1 Korin 8:4. Korin khua ih pursum leilawnnak, nuncan ziaza le an biaknak pawl hi ziangsaw kan hliakhlai thei ding?`

Korin khua hi hlansan leitlun ih hmun thupi zet a si, sumdawnnak lam ah nasa zetih a len ruangah a hminthang zet. Korin khawpi cu Rome in 146 BC ahkhan a rak siatbal thluh ih, 44 BC ahkhan Julius Caesar ih Rom uknak hnuaiah tuahthar sal in a um. Himi Rome Korin hi Thukham thar ih a langmi a si. Pual a um lai ahkhan Korin khua cu Athen khawpi ih a rak zuam zetmi khua lakih pakhat a si ih, lamzin tamzet ah a tluk thei lo. Korin khua cun lawng cawlhnak hmun thupi zet pahnih a nei ih, cumi cun pursumleilawnnak thlengawknak le pursumleilawnnak tthansohternak ding ih thil olsam zet a si.

A ngaingai te cun Paul in Korin khua a hrilnak san cu leiram lam ih a thupitnak hmunram luahtu a si ruangah a si: “Thuthangttha thehdarhnak ding hrangih remcang zet a si. Korin ih dinhnget a si hnu ahcun Leitlun hmun dangdang ah olsam zetih thehlar a theih ding”—Ellen G. White, Sketches From the Life of Paul, p. 99.

Cui hlei ah Korin khua hi sumdawnnak lam ih hminthang zet a si ruangah Paul hrangah cui khawpi ih thuthangttha a sim phah ah puanthlam tuah le zuar phah ih a todelh awk theinak ding hrang thil olsam zet a si (Tirh 18:2, 3). Khawpi tum zet le lian zetih missionary hnattuan ahhin harsatnak a um thotho timi a fiangter. Korin cu biaknak tamzet um khawm ruirinak hmun a si(1 Kor. 8:5), cui thil fiangtertu bik cu Apollo, Athena, le Aphrodite pathian pawl upatnak ih sak mi biakinn tamzet a um ih, Izipt pawl ih pathian Sarapis le Isis tivek tiang in an bia.

Hi sakhua biaknak dangdang a um ih tlun ah Korin khua hi mipat nunau-nak (nupa sualnak) lam khalah theih hlawh zetmi a si. Leilung lam zirmi le san thuanthu ngantu Grik mi Strabo cun Korin khua ih Aphrodite sakhua biakinn ahhin hlawhlang thianghlim 1,000 tlun an um thu a rel. Himi thuhla hi mi thiam tamzet cun Athen khawmi pawl ih rel mi men a si ding tiah rinhlelnak an nei, asinan hlansan leitlun ahkhan hlawhtlang hi an rak tam hrimhrim timi cu a fiang zet. Nupa sualnak hi Korin khua ih buainak a si ih, hmundang khalah buainak a rak si. Milem biaknak le nupa sualnak cu an nitin nun ih peng pakhat a si. Hi thil riahsiatza cu Korin cakuat pakhat le pahnihnak ahkhan simfiang in a um.

`Korin khua ih Missionary a ttuannak ah Paul cun milem biaknak le khawtlang nun ttha lo ruangah harsatnak pawl a tong. Tuisan ahhin thuthangttha phuang ding ih harsatnak a thlentu nunphung hi ziangsaw a si? Ziangtin saw kan va neh thei ding? Tuisan ih khawpi tamzet le Korin khawpi karlak ahin ziangmi danglamnak a um?`
