---
title:  “Hi khawpi ah mi tampi”
date:   01/07/2026
---

`Siar ding: Tirh 18:4-8. Paul ih thuthangttha a phuangnak rah kha ziang pawl an si?`

Korin khua ih Judah pawl hnen ih Paul ih hnattuan cu a duh vekin rahttha a suah thei lo. Donak le huatnak a tong. Bible cun “ annih in an rak dokalh ih, an thangsiat” tiah a ti (Tirh 18:6, NKJV). Grik ttong ih _blasphēmeō_ (“thangsiat”) timi hi milai relnak ih hman a si ahcun, “hmuhsuak, nautat” tinak a si. Phundang ih kan sim ding asile, Paul ih hmin tthatnak siatsuah le missionary hna a ttuannak hlawhtling dingmi khap an tum tinak a si.

Vantthat thlak zetin Paul in Korin mi pawl khawmnak sinakok ih a thusimmi cu a lak a si lo. Ziangkim tlun ah Pathian cu Amai hnattuannak ih ttuanvo neitu bik a si. Hitin tiamkamnak a nei, “(Ka ttongkam) cu zianghman lo men in Ka hnenah an kir lo ding” (Isa. 55:11, ESV). Judah pawl cun Sinakok ih hruaitu Krispas le a innsang pawl in Jesuh kha Messiah ah pawm in tipilnak an neih ding hi an rak ruat ban lo (Tirh 18:8). Anmah lawng siloin, “Korin mi tampi khal in an ngai ih, an zum ih, tipilnak an nei” (Tirth 18:8, NKJV), himi cu Krispas ih a rak ciahneh tuk ruang khalah a si men thei.

`Siar ding: Tirh 18:9, 10. Korin khua ah Paul in harsatnak a ton lai ih a lungput kha ziangtin kan simfiang thei ding? Pathian in a siahhlawh kha ziangtin thazang a pe?`

Paul in Sinakok a suahsan veten, thazang caktertu tonteh mi a nei. Khrih Amah rori in zan ih langnak ah a rung lang ih, Isaiah 41:10 “ Tih aw hlah, ka lo umpi” timi a run rel. A ngaingai te cun Paul in Korin khua ih a um kha, “cak lo le ttih in nasa zetih khur phah in”(1 Korin 2:3) a um thu a rel. Dokalhnak nasa zet ruangah Berea khua ihsin Athen ih a feh a ttul. Hivek ih dokalhnak ruangah Korin khua khal a suahsan a ttul ding tin a ruat aw men thei. Asinan Jesuh cun a hnenah, “Hitawk khawpi ah minung tampi ka nei” tiah a sim (Tirh 18:10, NKJV). Cule Paul cu Rundamnak thuthangttha an hnen ih sim dingah A hman mi a si.

`Siar ding: Isaiah 41:10. Himi thu ahhin ziang mangbangza thutiam in pek? Cumi pawl cun ziangtin na nitin nun a run tham?`
