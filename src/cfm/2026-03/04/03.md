---
title:  Thubuai hmaitondan
date:   20/07/2026
---

Nupa sualnak thawn pehpar aw mi thubuai hmaiton hi thil harsa zet a si ringring. Paul hrang ah a harsa ih, kan hrang khalah a harsa zet. Hivek dinhmun ahcun Ca thianghlim ah kan rinum tengteng a tul, cule thlacamnak le duhdawtnak thawn thubuai cu kan hmaiton ding a si. Kan hmuitin mi cu remthatsal a si timi hi kan hngilh ding a si lo.

`Siar ding: 1 Korin 5:1-13 siar sal aw. Hivek dinhmun hmaiton dan ding hi Paul in ziangtin saw a rel?`

Paul cun 1 Korin 5 ahhin nunau mipatnak lam ih sualnak ahcun kawhhran ih nunsimnak a thupi tiah a tel. Cuvek mipa cu hlawn ding (1 Kor. 5:2), Thuthen ding (1 Kor. 5:3), Setan hnen ih pek ding (1 Kor. 5:5), cule “ ‘dawisuah ding’ ” (1 Kor. 5:13, NKJV) tiah a sim. Kawhhran member pawl hnenah “Amah thawn pawlkom aw tlang lo ding” (1 Kor. 5:9, 11, ESV), le “cuvek mi thawn ei tlang” (1 Kor. 5:11, ESV) lo dingin a sim. Paul ih tongkam hi tuisan ahcun a rapthlak tuk deuh men thei, asinan a tongkam pawl cu anmai dinhmun zirih simmi a si timi theihthiam a tha. Cule sualnak langsar zet tuahngam ih, sual nun hmang tu thawn an hmaiton aw timi theih a tha. A ngaingai te cun, hivek dinhmun ahhin tongkam khauhzet hman a tul. Ziangvek buainak khalah tongfang hrekkhat tawi deuh ih simfiang hi a tangkai zet.

“Nan lak ihsin lak hloh si seh” (1 Kor. 5:2, ESV; 1 Kor. 5:13). Himi cu kawhhran nunsimnak (kaihhruai awknak) a relnak a si.

“Hipa cu Setan hnenah pek si ko seh” (1 Kor. 5:5, ESV). Ziangahtile himi pa cun Pathian thu awih ih nun hmangin, Pathian kilhimnak hnuai ih um lo dingin duhhrilnak a tuah ruangah Satan ih siatsuah dingah a olsam zet ding. Curuangah himi tongkam ih sullam cu, “a duhhrilnak rah cu atter uh” tinak men khi a si.

“Pawlkom lo dingin” (1 Kor. 5:9, 11, NASB) “cuvek mi thawn ei tlang lo dingin” (1 Kor. 5:11, RSV). Nupa sualnak a tuahtu pawl thawn pawlkom tlan awknak cu tihnung umzet tiih ruah mi a si. Ziangahtile an thil tha lo tuah mi cun midang a suan thei a si. Hlansan ahkhan rawlei tlang timi cu ziangkim hlawm aw thlang thluh tinak khi a rak si. Kan zaten kan kiangkap ih thil ummi pawl cun in luahneh tuk, a cang thei tawk in kanmah ten kan kilhim awk a tul, a hleice in hivek thil hmaitonnak ahhin kan kilhim awk a tul.

“A thlarau rundam a si theinak dingah” (1 Kor. 5:5, NASB). Kawhhran nunsimnak cu remthatsalnak a si. Himi cun misual cu an theihnak nei tha sal in, an nuncan sualnak tlansan dingih tumtahnak a si. Himi ciah cu Paul in “Taksa siatsuah” a timi sullam tla a si men thei (1 Kor. 5:5). Cuvek thotho in 1 Korin 5 ih sual a tuahtu pa hi netalam ih a rel mi sualsir tupa kha a si men thei (zoh awla, 2 Cor. 2:5–10). A dik lo zetmi kawhhran member pakhat cu kawhhran ih a tel thei sal tikah Kawhhran ih nunsimnak cun a tum a hlensuak a si.
