---
title:  Zumnak le tuahnak karlak rualrem awk lonak
date:   19/07/2026
---

Khristian san thuanthu, Pathian thu zirmi pawl, Pastor pawl le kawhhran member pawl khal in Kawhhran hi ziangvek saw a si ding timi theih duh ah Thukham Thar nasa zetin an zir cio. Thimnak ah Tirhthlah cabu sungih Kawhhran pawl khi mangbangza an si. Asinan thil thupi zet cu kan hmuh thelh theu: cumi cu mikip in buainak an nei thluh timi hi a si. Kawhhran hi ziangvek a si lo ding timi Bible ih a simmi hmu dingin Thukham Thar kan siar thotho tla khi a bang zet. Hi thuhla theifiang dingih hramthoknak ding ahcun Paul in Korin mi pawl hnen ih a cakuat mi hi a tha zet.

`Siar ding: 1 Korin 5:1-13. Himi bungcang ahhin ziangmi dinhmun zahthlak zet saw Pual in a rel, cule ziangah a buaithlak?`

“A pa ih nupi” (1 Korin 5:1) tiin Paul ih a rel mi cu mipa pakhat le a nu-ei karlak laicinawknak a rel tum mi a si. Hivek thuhla (dinhmun) hi “Khloe tei’ innsang” in an rak sim a si ko ding (1 Kor. 1:11, NKJV). Nupa sualnak cu a rapthlak zetmi sual a si ih, cui thil cu “ pathian nung bia lo pawl lak khalah theih a si tuk lo” (1 Kor. 5:1, ESV). Asinan tu ahcun Khristian hmaisa kawhhran sungah a thleng rero. 1 Korin 5:1,2 ih um Paul ih tongkam cun kawhhran member in hivek thil a tuah ti a theih ruangih mangbang thu a tarlang.

`Ziangkhal sehla, hi a tha lo zet mi dinhmun cu a luarkai sinsin. Cui dinhmun tiang an thlen ruangah riahsiatnak nei lo in, Korin mi pawl cun khavek sual an tuahnak ah anmah le anmah an uangaw zet timi Paul in a theih tikah a mangbang sinsin (1 Kor. 5:1, 2, NIV). Curuangah nuncan ziaza lawng siloin zumnak le tuahnak karlak ih rualrem awk lonak khal kawhhran ah tuahthat a tum. A ngaingai te cun, Paul cun kawhhran in hivek nupa sualnak pawl hi a tuahthat a tul rori a si tiah nasa zetin a rel fiang. Asinan cuvek ih nunau mipatnak sual an tuah mi ahcun an uangaw sawn riangri hai (1 Kor. 5:2, 6). Himi cu Paul hrangih tuar a har zet mi a si. Hi minung pawl ahhin ziangsaw dik lo um?`

Korin kawhhran sung ahhin ziangah cuvek ih sual raptlak zet a um timi simfiangnak tamzet kan nei lo. Anih cu kawhhran ihsin hlawknak nasazet ngahtu milian zet a si men thei? Asilole “ziangkim a thiang thluh” tiih an zum ruangah (1 Kor. 6:12), an thil tuah mi cu a dik lonak an thei lo tla a si men thei? A ngaihnak kan thei lo.

`A san le vang cu ziang a si khalah Cathianghlim daan pahbalnak ahcun mitcaw ah an cang (Pui. 18:7, 8). Cule an porh aw lailai fawn?`

`Kawhhran kan si vekin, “duhdawtnak” le “ngaihsaknak” timi hmin in ziangvek nuncan ziaza saw Bible in fiangfaiten a mawhthluk?`
