---
title:  Khrih bangih riantu sinak
date:   15/07/2026
---

`Siar ding: 1 Korin 4:1,2. Himi ah milai hruaitu in thlirdan dik an neih ding thuhla ziangsaw in zirh?`

1 Korin 3:1-4 ah Paul cun pawlkom fatete a umnak cu thlarau lam pitlinnak tlaksamnak ih rahsuah mi a si tiah a rel. Ziangkhal sehla, himi thuhla a rel suah hlanah, “Khrih ih lungput kan nei” tiah a run nemhnget ta (1 Kor. 2:16, NKJV). Himi thu cun Khrih ih ruahdan le cangvaihnak pawl a khihhmuh deuh in a lang. Phundang in kan sim ding asile, zumtu cun Khrih vekin ruahnak le cangvaihnak a neih tikah “Khrih ih lunput” a nei a si. Asinan ziang dinhmun khalah hringnun ih hivek lungput hman ding cu thil olsam zet cu a si zik lo, a si ko lo maw? Grik – Rome san lai ahkhan ramkhelhnak pawl, khawruah fimnak pawl, thuruat thiam pawl, le sakhaw hruaitu pawl karlak ah nasa zet in zuamawknak a um. Nunphung khaisan duhnak cun Korin kawhhran cu leitlun daan thlun dingin a hruai. Hivek cu a tuisan kawhhran hrang khalah tihnung um a si men thei.

`Siar ding: Filipi 2:5–8. Himi cang cun “Khrih lungput” (1 Cor. 2:16) timi thu theithiam dingin ziangtin saw in bawm?`

Korin khua vekin thenthek awknak hi Filipi kawhhran khalah a um (Filipi. 2:1–4), Korin khua vek lawmman cun a si deuh lem lo tla a si men thei. Filipi 2:1-8 ah Khrih bangih riantu si dingah mai tanghmai sialnak lamih thih a tul ih, Jesuh vekin kanmah hnakin midang hrangih thlawsuahnak hawl a tul timi in zirh a si.

Khirh bangih riantu si ding cu Paul in “Khrih ih siahhlah” a timi sullam ciah kha a si. (1 Kor. 4:1, NKJV). Himi tongkam cun Khrih ih hnatuan bawmtu, lole a hnuai ih um vek ciah in Khrih ih hna an tuan tinak a si. Milai hruaitu pawl dikzet ih thlirnak cu Khrih ih kaihhruai awknak ah a thum aw a si timi cu a fiang zet. Siahhlawh pawl cu “kilkhawitu” tiih tarlang vivo a si (1 Kor. 4:1, 2). Kilkhawitu timi cu rinsannak thawn midang thilri kilkhawi dingih pek mi a si. Ziangkim kan neih mi hi Khrih ih ta an si thluh.

`Thlacamnak nei phah in Filipi 2:5-8 ih um thucah hi vun ruat aw. Pathian in in duhdawt tuk ruangih Amah ten a hnongawknak thhla ziangsaw in sim? Ziangtin saw kan nun ah himi duhdawtnak hi kan lakluh ve theidan ding?`
