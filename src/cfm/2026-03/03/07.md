Ellen G. White, “The Training of the Twelve,” pp. 17–24, in The Acts of the Apostles.

“Pathian thutak zumtu a mipi tangsun pawl ih pumkhat awknak le hmunkhatawknak cun leitlunpi hnenah thutak an neih thu le Pathian ih mi hleice, a hril miphun an sinak thucah ropi zet an keng suak ding. Hi pumkhat awknak le hmunkhatawknak cun doral a um lo ding ih, anih cu um ding a si lo timi a tarlang. Tulai thutak, thinlung ih zum le nun ih zohthim tlak Pathian mi pawl cu pakhat ah a tuah ih, huham nasa zet pek an si”—Ellen G. White, Testimonies for the Church, vol. 1, p. 327.

“ Pathian in kumkhaw thutak kaihhruai awknak ah mipi pawl cu famkim zetin pumkhat ih um dingih a hruai. “Thiltha tuahnak ih thahnem a ngaimi amai’ hrangih miphun hleice si dingih in thianghlimter dingah” Khrih cu leitlun ah a pe aw a si. Himi thianghlimternak cu dingfellonak le thurel buainak thlarau pawl hmuahhmuah hlon in kawhhran hi thianghlim ih tuah dingih duan mi a si, cuticun siatbal loin an dinhnget thei ding ih, an hmai ih hnatuan ropi zet ah thazang silhkhat in an hmuitin thei ding a si. Pathian cun a minung pawl cu zumnak ih pumkhat awknak ah ra thluh dingin khawkhannak a nei. Khenbet a si hlan te ih Khrih ih thlacamnak ahkhan Pa Pathian thawn pumkhat an si vek ciah in a dungthluntu pawl thawn pumkhat an si theinak ding hrangah a si, cuticun Pa in a thlah a si ti leitlun mi in an thei ding a si. Himi cu tui kan san tiangin, san tinkim ahhin thinlung neh zet le thil ti thei zet thlacamnak a si ringring lai. Ziangahtile A tongkam pawl cu: “Hi pawl hrang lawngah thla ka cam lo, an thu ruangih keimah i zumtu ding pawl hrang khalah a si’”—Ellen G. White, Testimonies for the Church, vol. 4, p. 17.

**Relkhawm ding thusuhnak pawl:**

`A lei hnatuanak a cem zawng ah, Jesuh cun pumkhat awknak ding hrang thlacamnak a nei: “Pumkhat an si theinak ding in… cuticun leitlun in I thlah a si timi an thei ding a si” (Johan 17:21–23, NASB). Ziangah pumkhat awknak hi Pathian in leitlun rundam dingin a Fapa a thlah a si timi thudik relnak ih a thilti theizet a si? Himi thawn pehzom aw in, ziangahsaw pumkhat awk lonak hi kan kawhhran hnatuannak rak donkham tu a si?`

`1 Korin 4:9-13 kha siar awla, cule tirhthlah in himi thucah hi ziangtin a tarlang timi theih tum aw. Ziangtin saw tirhthlah ih a tarlang mi cu kan leitlun in kaihhruai awknak a ngai thupi zetmi thawn an kalh aw thiam? Himi bungcang ahhin Pathianin a tahnak le leitlun ih tahnak an danglamzia thu ah ziangsaw in zirh duh mi a neih?`

`1 Korin 4:16 ahkhan Paul cun Korin khawmi pawl hnenah amah zohthim dingin a forh. Milai hruaitu pawl ih zohthim ding ih um na duh maw? Ziangtin saw zohthim tlak hruaitu cu hruaitu mawi lo zet, le tihnung um zet khaisang mi hruaitu thawn an danglam awknak?`
