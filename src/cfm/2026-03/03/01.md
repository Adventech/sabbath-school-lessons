### Tuizarh zirlai hrang siar ding
1 Kor. 1:12–17, Rom. 1:29, 1 Kor. 1:10, 1 Kor. 3:1–4, Filipi. 2:5–8, 2 Kor. 11:23–28, Kol. 1:24.

> <p>Bible cangken</p>
> “Tu ah u le nau tla, nan zatein thu hmunkhat sim in, le nan lakah ṭhenawknak um lo in, thinlung hmun khat le thuṭhennak hmun khat ah famkim zetih nan pehzomawk sawn theinak dingah, kan Bawipa Jesuh Khrih hmin in ka lo dil” (1 Korin 1:10).

Ramsa hliakhlai tu pawl in ramsa hrekkhat pawl cu a bur in, a tum le a fate pawl khal an um khawm thluh ti an thei. Cinghnia ihsin dolphin nga, sihte bur tivek tiangin a bur in an um thluh. Zawng rual pawl khal pa 5 ihsin 150 tiang hmunkhat ah a rual in an um khawm ruiri theu ruangah an hmin a thang zet. Ziangkhal sehla, hivek laicin awknak pawl ahhin an rem aw lo ve tuk theu, khui maw caan ahcun zawng rual pawl khal anmah le anmah an sual aw theu.

Khuimaw caan ahcun minung khal cuvek thotho cu kan si. Pawlkom dangdang ih um an duh men lo ih, an umnak pawlkom ah anmah le anmah an sual aw theu. Cuvek cu kan kawhhran sung khalah a um! Mi burpi lakah hruaitu thazet an um. Cule a besia mi thil cu pawlkom pakhat le pakhat an remawk thei lo hi a si.

Cuvek cu nan kawhhran ah na hmu dah maw?

Na hmu dah a sile, Paul in Korin khua ih buainak a tonmi pawlah ruahdan na nei thei ding. Tui zarh ahcun 1 Korin 1-4 kan zir ding. Paul in Khrih ih pumkhatnak hmangin kawhhran sung ih thu el buai awknak pawl a relfel dan pawl kan thlir ding.
