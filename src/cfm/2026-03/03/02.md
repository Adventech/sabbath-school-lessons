---
title:  Kawhhran sungih harsatnak
date:   12/07/2026
---

Paul cun “Nan lakah thenthek awknak um loin, thinlung hmunkhat le thuthennak hmunkhat ah pumkhat aw sawn uh” (1 Kor. 1:10, ESV) tiih a dilnak hi Korin cakuat pakhatnak ih bung hmaisabik ah a um. A ngaingai te cun zirmi pawl cun pumkhat awknak cu a cakuat pawl hmuahhmuah kaihkhawm thluhnak thulu a si tiah an lungkim tlang thluh.

`Siar ding: 1 Korin 1:12-17. Ziangtin saw himi bungcang in tualsung kawhhran ih pawlkom dinmi buainak pawl theithiam dingih in bawm? Paul ih a relfelnak kha ziangsaw a si?`

Paul cun Korin khua ih kawhhran member pawl lakih pumkhat awknak an tlaksam zia tarlangnak ah tongkam khauh zet a hmang. Grik tong ih _schisma_ (“Thenthek awknak,” 1 Kor. 1:10, NKJV) le _eris_ (“elbuai awknak,” 1 Kor. 1:11, NKJV) timi a hmang. _Schisma_ (_schizo_ ti khal ih an ti mi cu “ thendarh” tinak khal a si) timi cu Thukham thar ahhin ruahdan a bangaw lo, pawlkom fatete (midang umter duh loih, mahte ding pawlkom) pawl thekdarh awknak relnak ih hman a si. Khat lamah _eris_ (elbuai awknak) timi cu Khristian pawl ih an tuah lo dingmi sualnak cazin ah a lang theu.

`Siar ding: Rom 1:29, Rom 13:13, 1 Korin 3:3, 2 Korin 12:20, le Galati 5:20. Ziangvek sualnak dang saw eris thawn tarlang a si?Himi hi thilsual rapthlak zia a si thu ziangsaw in sim?`

Korin kawhhran sungih thu lungkim tlan lonak buainak cu a rung langfiang – pakhat le pakhat thubuai tuahawknak tiang in an thleng (1 Kor. 6:1–3). Kawhhran sungtel pakhat le pakhat karlak ih thubuai an tuahsak awknak thu ah Paul cun, “ Nan ningzak dingah ka rel a si” tiah a ti (1 Kor. 6:5, NKJV). A ngaingai te cun, Bawipai’ zanriah puai an tuah lai khalah an remawk lonak cu an tuahtha duh hrih cuang lo (1 Kor. 11:17–22).

Kawhhran member lakih pumkhat awknak an tlaksam hi tihnung um zet thil a si. Paul cu a thinhar zet, himi thu cu hi Korin cakuat ih buainak a rel hmaisabik mi a si.

`Siar sal aw: 1 Korin 1:12–27. Himi bungcang in kawhhran pumkhat awknak dingah pawkom fate te pawl ih an tihnung umzia theithiam dingin in bawm a si. Himi buainak hi hrial thei dingin na umnak tualsung kawhhran in ziangtin saw a tuah?`
