---
title:  Thinglamtah zohtertu nuncan
date:   16/07/2026
---

Milai hruaitu pawl umnak kiangkap ah pawlkom fatete kan tuah ding a si lo kan ti tikah kan hruaitu pawl kan somdawl lo ding tinak lam a si lo. Kawhhran a thansoh theinak ding ih hmai hruaitu pawl hi kan bawm ding ih, kan sunlawih thiam ding a si. Pathian in leitlun ahhin A hnatuan dingah mipi pawl thu a pe. Thinglamtah rak khihhmuh theitu nuncan nei kawhhran hruaitu pawl hi thlun tlak zet hruaitu pawl an si.

Thinglamtah cun Pathian kamsuak thu ih tulut awk duhtu ziangvek nuncan khal thleng danglam theinak huham a nei a si. Khrih vekih hruaitu pawl cun an hnatuannak ih hlawhtlinnak cu Pathian ruangah a si tiah an ti. Leitlun ih hna A tuan lai ahkhan, Jesuh khal in minung a si vekin Pathian hnenah sunlawihnak a pe a si (Johan 17:4).

Paul ih ruahdan ahcun Khristian rinum zet ih hnatuannak cu Thinglamtah ih Pathian thu zirnak tiih kawh mi ahhin a thum aw tengteng ding a si. Thinglamtah cu Pathian ih fimnak le in rundam dingih a huham phuangsuahnak a si. Cuti a si laiah minung fimnak cu atthlak za a si zia khal tarlang in a um. 1 Korin 4:1-13 ahkhan Paul cun Thinglamtah ih Pathian thu zirnak cu ziangvek a si timi a rel fiang zet. A hmaisabik ah Khristian kaihhruai awknak tahfung tuahtu cu Pathian a si timi a tarlang (1 Kor. 4:1–5). Pahnihnak ahcun, harsat tuarnak hi Khristian hnatuan tu diktak hminsinnak ngaingai a si timi a tarlang (1 Kor. 4:9, 11–13). Himi a pahnihnak tu hi rel hlawh vivo dingin a tha zet.

`Siar ding: 2 Korin 11:23–28 le Kolose 1:24. Himi cun Khrih ruangih tuarnak timi sullam ziangsaw in zirh?`

Khristian hruaitu pawl cun Jesuh ih keneh an thlun ih, an hnatuannak ruangah thihnak tiang tuar tul khal sehla, an unau pawl hrangah harsatnak tuar khal an huam a si. Paul cun amah le Apollos cu “thah dingih ruat mi” (1 Kor. 4:9, NKJV) vekin a tarlang aw. Annih cu tidai in ding le rawl ei ding tlasam zet vekih tarlang an si ih, cuvek ciah in, “ thuamhnaw se zet, vuak an tuar ih, inn umnak ding khal an nei lo” (1 Kor. 4:11, NKJV). Cui hlei ah relsiat, hrem, le hminsiatnak an hlawh ih, “leitlun ih thil tenum vekah, le thil hmuahhmuah hlonkhawm mi hnawmhne vekah tui ni tiang” an si (1 Kor. 4:12, 13, NKJV). Cui hlei ah Korin khawmi pawl cu milian, siangpahrang pawl, mifim pawl le mi cungcuang pawl tiah rel tahat in (1 Kor. 4:8, 10), Paul cun puarthaunak cu Khristian kaihhruai awknak ah hmunram a um lo timi a tarlang, ziangahtile cui thil cu kawhhran ih thenthek awknak hrampi bik a si (1 Kor. 4:6).

`Kawhhran ah ziangvek tuanvo nei in, Khrih ruangah ziangtluk na tuar zo? Na thuletnak ah ziangmi zirlai saw hmuh a si thei ding?`
