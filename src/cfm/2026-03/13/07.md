Ellen G. White, “ ‘Let Not Your Heart Be Troubled,’ ” pp. 662–680, in The Desire of Ages.

“Jesuh Khrih ih duhdawtnak lawng in lungto vekih hak thinlung cu tisa vekih nem thinlung ah a thleng thei, cule Pathian hnenah nung dingin a tuah thei a si … Minung cu thlarau thiam a coter thei lo ih, thinlung a thianfai thei lo. Nuncan lam ih natnak cu Sibawi ropi bik ih huham thawn lo cun damter a theih lo. Van ih thilpek ropi bik, zangfahnak le thutak ih khat, Pa ih neihsun lawng in a hlo mi pawl a tlen thei a si.”—Ellen G. White, in The Signs of the Times, May 2, 1892.

“ ‘Pathian cu duhdawtnak a si’ 1 Johan 4:16. A sinak le A daan cu duhdawtnak a si. A rak si dah zo ih, hmailam caan khalah a si vivo lai ding. ‘kumkhua ih um cungnung le sang’ ih lamzin cu kumkhua a si’ a thleng aw dah lo. Amah thawn cun “danglamnak, le a thawn theihmi hlimthlam a um lo’ Isaiah 57:15; Habakkuk 3:6; James 1:17. Sersiammi thil ziangkim hi cemni neilo duhdawtnak tarlangnak a si. Pathian ropi zet cun thil sersiam thar mi pawl hmuahhmuah hrangah thlawsuahnak a kengtel … Sia le that karlak ih buainak tumzet thuanthu hi a hmaisabik ah van ihsin a thok ih, a netabik ah dokalhnak hlonhlo in, sual a nawtreh thluhnak ahhin a thleng aw dah lo mi Pathian duhdawtnak a tarlang a si.”—Ellen G. White, Patriarchs and Prophets, p. 33.

“Thiang Thlarau hi Pathian mi pakhat a si vek ciahin Anih khal mi pakhat a si timi hi kan theih a tul. Thiang Thlarau cun sinak ziaza a nei, cutin nei lo sehla, kan thlarau ah theihpitu a si thei lo ding ih, Pathian fale kan sinak khal ka nunah in sim thei lo ding a si. Anih cu Pathian a si tengteng a tul, asi lo ahcun Pathian thinlung sung ih thuhmi thuthup a hawl hmu thei lo ding.”—Ellen G. White, The Faith I Live By, p. 52.

“Van lam mi pathum lakah a nungmi pathum an um. Hi huham ropi zet pathum – Pa, Fapa, le Thiang Thlarau hmingin zumnak nung thawngin Khrih pomtu pawl cu tipilnak an nei, himi huham pawl cun Khrih ah nungthar ih nung dingin vanlam ih thuawih tu pawl thawn hna an tuan tlang ding a si”—Ellen G. White, Evangelism, p. 615.

**Relkhawm ding thusuhnak pawl:**

`Khristian hla ih theihlawh zet thulu cu “A zangfahnak mangbangza” timi a si. Jesuh zangfahnak thuhla ah ziangsaw mangbangza um?`

`Pathian duhdawtnak tarlangnak mawi zet cu fapa tlanhlo tahthimnak ahkhan kan um thei. Ziangtin saw himi tahthimnak ih pa a si mi hi duhdawttu pa a si timi kan theih?`

`Ziangtin saw tualsung kawhhran pawl in “Thlarau pawlkomnak” cu a um ngaingai a si timi an phuangsuah thei?`
