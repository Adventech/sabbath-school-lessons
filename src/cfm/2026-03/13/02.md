---
title:  Jesuh ih zangfahnak
date:   20/09/2026
---

2 Korin a cem zawng ahhin a thoknak lam vek ciah in Jesuh ih zangfahnak a run rel a si timi kan hmu (2 Kor. 1:2, 2 Kor. 13:14). Paul cun hi cakuat a thoknak le a cemnak ah Jesuh zangfahnak thu a run hmang. Tui kuata a thok lam ih kan hmuh zo vek ciah in, Jesuh thuhla rel le ruat hi a cawlhsan thei lo.

“Ziangahtile kan Bawipa Jesuh Khrih ih zangfahnak cu nan thei, anih cu milian a si na’n, nanmai’ ruangah misiaṭha ah a cang, a siatṭhatnak zarih nan len theinak dingah” (2 Kor. 8:9, NKJV).

Jesuh zangfahnak hi ziangtluk mangbangza so a si. Mi rethei ih rung cang dingin kumkhaw a umnak vanram lennak pawl a suahsan. Hlansan Galilee lamzin bal zetah a teivak. “Thihnak tiang thuawih zetin a tangdor, thinglamtah thihnak tiangin” (Filipi. 2:8, ESV). Cumi cu kan “len” theinak ding hrangih a tuah mi a si ih, cumi cu vanram ah Amah thawn um theinak caan tha kan neih ve theinak dingah kan hrangih a tuah mi a si. Leitlun ahhin sualnak, thihnak, tuarnak tivek lawng a theitu kan hrang ahcun hitawk ah ra dingin le kan hrangih a nunnak a hlan theinak dingah Jesuh in van ropitnak a suahsannak sullam vun theihthiam ding cu thil harsa zet a si.

`Siar ding: Rom 16:20, Galati 6:18, Filipi 4:23, le 1 Thessalonika 5:28. Himi thu ahhin ziangmi zirhawknak thupi na hmu?`

Paul cun a cakuat ahhin Jesuh ih zangfahnak hi a rel leuhleuh theu. A fiang zet mi thil pakhat cu, “Jesuh Khrih ih zangfahnak thawngin a lakih thilpek mi cu … mi tampi parah a tam sinsin a si” (Rom. 5:15, ESV). Himi zangfahnak tamzet dongtu pawl cu “Jesuh … ruangah nunnak ahcun an uk” ding a si (Rom. 5:17, ESV). 2 Korin ih thil a thleng mi vek ciah in Paul cun a cakuat dang pawl ih a thoknak le netnak ah Jesuh ih zangfahnak a telh thluh (Rom. 1:7, Rom. 16:20, 1 Kor. 1:3, 1 Kor. 16:23, Gal. 1:3, Gal. 6:18, Filipi. 1:2, Filipi. 4:23). Himi thu cun a ruahnak a luahkhat ih, Korin khawmi pawl ih thinlung khalah um sehla a duh zet.

Himi cu kawhhran hmuahhmuah hrangih a duh thusam a si. Efesa mi pawl hnenih a rel mi kha hminsin aw: “Kan Bawipa Jesuh Khrih cemni nei lo ih a duhdawt ngaingai tu pawl hnenah zangfahnak um hram seh” (Efesa. 6:24, NIV). Cemni nei lo ih duhdawtnak ai hnakih nep deuh in Jesuh kan duhdawt ding hi a duhdan a si pei maw? Si lamlam hlah. A ngaingai te cun a duhdan cu Jesuh zangfahnak hi “midang tampi hnen ih” (2 Kor. 4:15, NIV) thlen le a hrangih a tawk vek ciah in an hrang khal ih a tawk theinak ding hi a duh mi a si (2 Kor. 12:9).

`Na hnenih Pathian zangfahnak hi vun ruat aw. Na tongmi le na tuah mi thil ih na phutawk cu ziangsaw a si? Ziangkhal sehla, Pathian zangfahnak in ziangsaw a lo pek?`
