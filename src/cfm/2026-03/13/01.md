### Tuizarh zirlai hrang siar ding
2 Kor. 8:9; Rom. 16:20; 1 Johan 4:8–11; 2 Kor. 13:11; Filipi. 2:1, 2; Gal. 4:4–6.

> <p>Bible cangken</p>
> “Bawipa Jesuh Khrih ih zangfahnak, le Pathian ih duhdawtnak, le Thlarau Thianghlim ih pawlkomnak cu nan zate hnenah um hram seh” (2 Korin 13:14).

Paul cun a cakuat ih thil thupi zetzet panga rel kirsal in 2 Korin netkharnak a tuah. Cumi cu thil a tul zet mi panga an si (2 Kor. 13:11).

Pakhatnak, “Lungawi uh” (ESV) timi cun a hmaisa lam ih cakuat mi pawl a run sim thei kirsal a si.

Pahnihnak ah, “tuahthatnak famkim um dingin bei uh” (NIV), Grik tongfang pakhat (_katartizō_) timi ihsin laksuah mi a si ih, hitawk ah le 1 Korin 1:10 ah a lang a si.

Pathumnak ah, “pakhat le pakhat thazang pe aw ton uh” (NIV) timi cu 2 Korin 1:2-7 ta a run rel kirsal. Paul cun a cakuat pakhatnak le pahnihnak cu thazang peknak in a thok ih net a khar a si. Midang thazang kan pek theinak dingah Pathian hnen ihsin thazang peknak kan dong a si (2 Kor. 1:4, 6).

Palinak le panganak ah, “thinlung hmunkhat pu uh, remawte in um uh” (2 Kor. 13:11, NKJV) timi cu pumkhat aw dingih kawhnak a si. Himi lungawinak, tuahthatsalnak, thazang peknak, pumkhatnak, le remawknak timi pawl cu, “Pathian duhdawtnak le daihnak” (2 Kor. 13:11, ESV) a umnak hmun a si. Himi cu minung thinlung sungih thumkom Pathian hnatuannak ihsin a rahsuak mi a si (2 Cor. 13:14).

Zangfahnak, duhdawtnak le pawlkomnak pawl cu kan hrang thumkom Pathian ih hnatuannak ihsin a ra suak mi an si. Himi Khristian ih a neih dingmi thil pathum cu Pathian umpitnak ziaza tarlangnak a si.
