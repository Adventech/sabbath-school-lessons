---
title:  Kan thumkom Pathian
date:   24/09/2026
---

2 Korin 13:14 siar ahcun Khrih cu zangfahnak bulhram umsun a si ih, Pathian cu duhdawtnak bulhram umsun le Thlarau Thianghlim cu pawlkomnak bulhram umsun a si tin ruahnak a um men thei, asinan himi cu thudik a si lo.

`Siar ding: 1 Korin 1:3, 4, 9; 1 Korin 10:16; 2 Korin 1:2, 12; Rom 8:35; Rom 15:30; Galati 2:20; le Efesa 3:19. Himi bungcang pawl in Thukom member pawl thawn pehtlainak sungih zangfahnak, duhdawtnak le pawlkomnak thuhla ziangsaw an rel?`

Kan rundamnak ding hrangah Fa, Fapa, le Thiang Thlarau cun hna an tuan tlang thluh. Zangfahnak, duhdawtnak, le pawlkomnak cu pakhat lawng ihsin a ra mi a si lo ih, an pathum ihsin a ra mi a si. Cumi cingin rundamnak thuanthu feh vivo ahhin pakhat cio in hnatuan hleice an nei thluh. Paul cun cui thil cu a theifiang ih, a cakuat ih thuzirhnak khalah a rel uar zet. Tahhtimnak ah rundam khawkhannak cu Galati 4:4-6 ahhin tongkam mawi zetin a run sim, Pathian sinak ih mi pathum an tel a si. Pa cun Jesuh a thlah, cumi cun Pa cu himi khawkhannak ih bulhrambik a si timi a langter (Gal. 4:4). Fapa cu nunau ihsin a suak (Gal. 4:4), cumi cu minung ih a hungcannak relnak a si ih, hlanlai pi ih thutiam famkimnak a si timi a khihhmuh (Seem. 3:15). Fapa cun in tlen ih, Pa thawn laicin awknak dikah in hruai kirsal, Satan ih bumnak kha a remtha a si (Seem. 3:5). Cule Thiang Thlarau cun Pathian fale kan si timi in nemhnget a si (Gal. 4:6).

Paul ih cakuat pawl ah thumkom Pathian rellangnak a dang tamzet a um lai. An pathum in an cangvai tlang, Pathian hnatuannak hrangah kawhhran a cakter (1 Kor. 12:4–6), thlarau lam cahnak in pe (Efesa. 3:14–19), Kawhhran member pawl lakah pumkhatnak a run tuah ih, Pathian sinak member ih laicin awknak hminsinnak a run tarlang (Efesa. 4:4–6). Paul ih theithiam dan ahcun Pathian pathumnak lawng kha siloin, Pathian sinak ih mi pathum cun kan rundamnak ding hrang hna an tuantlang a si (Efesa. 1:3, 13, 14). Efesa ahkhan, Paul cun Fa (Efesa. 3:19), Fapa (Efesa. 4:13), le Thiang Thlarau (Efesa. 5:18) ih famkimnak ahhin kan khat thluh ding a si timi a sim vivo.

Korin mi pawl hnen ih thurel mi kharnak ah (2 Kor. 13:14), Paul cun ruahsannak thutiam thawn netkharnak a nei – leitlun vandap ih mi thupi pathum, vanlam ih thumkom cun a tu ah le a ra lai dingmi san khal ahhin kan hnenah um hram seh.

`Ziangtin saw kawhhran member lak ih pawlkomnak cun Pathian sinak ih laicin awknak duhum zet a va tarlang thei ding?`
