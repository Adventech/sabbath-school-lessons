---
title:  Pathian duhdawtnak
date:   21/09/2026
---

“Bawipa Jesuh Khrih ih zangfahnak, le Pathian ih duhdawtnak, le Thlarau Thianghlim ih pawlkomnak cu nan zate hnenah um hram seh” (2 Kor. 13:14, NKJV). Himi thuthawn Paul hin a cakuat pahnihnak net a khar. Himi ahhin thumkom Pathian ih mi pathum : Fapa, Pa, le Thlarau Thianghlim a rel a si timi hminsin aw. An pathum ih hnatuannak thawngin Pathian cu ziangvek a si ih, kan hrang ziang a tuah timi kan theifiang sinsin thei a si.

`Siar ding: Johan 3:16, 17; Rom 8:37–39; le 1 Johan 4:8–11. Himi thu pawl in Pathian duhdawtnak ziangsaw in sim?`

1 Johan 4:8 ahcun “Pathian cu duhdawtnak a si” tiah a ti. Duhdawtnak cu Pathian ziaza thupi zet a si. Johan cun Pathian in kan hrangah thi dingih a Fapa neihsun in pek ruangah duhdawtnak kan thei a si tiah uar zetin a rel (Johan 3:16). Pathian cun runsuahnak hnatuan dingin Jesuh a run thlah (Johan 3:17), cumi cu rundamnak ih hnatuan peng pakhat a si (Tirh 3:20, 21; 1 Johan 4:10, 14). Jesuh cun thuthangtha cabu ah Pa Pathian cu Amah thlahtu a si tin voi tamzet a rel (Matt. 10:40, Mark 9:37).

Hminsintlak zet in Paul cun, “ Pathian cun hitin in duhdawtnak a langter: Misual kan si lai hmanah Khrih cu kan hrangah a thi a si” (Rom. 5:8, NIV)tiah a ti. Pathian duhdawtnak cu a thlum zetmi nupi le pasal karlak ah, cuvek ciah in nu le pa le fale karlak ah, rualpi thatawknak le hitivek pawl karlak ahhin kan hmu thei a si. Pathian duhdawtnak cu boruak tinkim khalah kan hmu thei. Himi thuthawn pehpar aw in Ellen G White cun, “ Pathian cu duhdawtnak a si” timi cu a par suak dingmi pangpar mum pawl ah le thlaihring tinkim parah ngan a si. Duhnungza van vate cun boruak ah lungawinak zaithlah an sak ih, pangpar pawl khal in famzet zetin boruak ah an rimthawtnak an suah ve, hramlak ih thingkung pawl khal in an thinghnah hring dendi pawl an suah ih, himi pawl hmuahhmuah cun Kan Pathian ih ngaihsak zia le A fale pawl lungawi aipuang zetih an um ding a duhzia an tarlang thluh a si”tiah a ti—Steps to Christ, p. 10.

Ziangkhal sehla, kan sualnak hrangih raithawinak ah Pathian in Jesuh in peknak hi zianghman in an tluk thei cuang lo. Pathian ih in duhdawt zia cu kan hrang a nunnak pe dingin Jesuh a run thlah timi kan theihthiam tikah, kan letnak ding cu lungtho zetin “unau pawl hrangah kan nunnak liam ve ding” (1 Johan 3:16, ESV) hi a si.

Paul cun Korin mi pawl pumkhat ih an um ding a duh. Ziangkhal sehla, duhdawtnak tel lo cun pumkhat awknak a um thei lo. Curuangah Paul cun “duhdawtnak cun mi a tuahtha” (1 Kor. 8:1, ESV) cule cui duhdawtnak tel lo cun ziangkim hi zianghman lo men an si ih, an kawlawng thluh tiah a rak zirh (1 Kor. 13:1–3). Curuangah kan tuahmi ziangkim hi duhdawtnak ih tuah ding a si (1 Kor. 16:14), cui duhdawtnak cu Pathian duhdawtnak kauhter sinsinnak a si.

`Jesuh Amah rori hi Pathian famkim le Kumkhaw mi rak si lo bangta sehla, thuthangtha ahhin ziangsaw kan hloh ding?`
