---
title:  Duhdawttu Pathian
date:   22/09/2026
---

Hlansan ih zumlotu leitlun ahhin mipi pawl cun pathian pawl in minung an duhdawt hi an zum lo. Cuhnakin pathian pawl cu thilsual tuahduhtu le thinheng hmang an si, an thinhengnak daihter tul an si. Bible ih kan hmuh vekin duhdawttu Pathian timi ruahnak cu thil tharhlam zet a si. Himi thu hi Paul ih san ah mangbangza thil a si. Paul cun kan Pathian cu, “duhdawtnak le remnak Pathian” tin a rel (2 Kor. 13:11).

`Siar ding: 2 Korin 13:11. Hitawk ih a ummi ihsin ziangtin saw ruahsannak na neih thei ding? Himi thu ih a zirmi hi ziangtin saw na theihthiam?`

“Duhdawtnak le remnak Pathian” timi cu sullam pahnih ih simfiang a theih. Pakhat cu, Pathian cu duhdawtnak le remnak ih bulhram vekih hmuh a si. Khat lam ahcun Pathian cu duhdawtnak le remnak hmangih simfiang a si. Ziangkhal sehla, himi pahnih lakah hril deuh mi neih hi thil tul lutuk a si lem cuang lo. Duhdawtnak le remnak cu Pathian sinak ziaza a si ruangah Anih cun duhdawtnak le remnak in pe a si.

Hmundang ah Paul cun Pathian cu “Tuarfainak le thazang petu Pathian” (Rom. 15:5, ESV); “ruahsannak” (Rom. 15:13, ESV); “remnak” (Rom. 15:33, Rom. 16:20, 1 Kor. 14:33, Filipi. 4:9, 1 Thess. 5:23), “lainatnak ih Pa” (2 Kor. 1:3), le “hnangamnak hmuahhmuah ih Pathian” (2 Kor. 1:3) tiah a ti. Pathian cu thlawsuahnak pawl hmuahhmuah ra suahnak bulhram a si. A duhdawtnak cem thei lo ihsin thlawsuahnak pawl hmuahhmuah in pe suak a si.

“Remnak Pathian” timi hi Bible ah ziangtin simaw langtam zet khal sehla, “Duhdawtnak Pathian” timi cu hitawk lawng ahhin a lang (2 Kor. 13:11), curuangah fimkhur zetih kan ngaihtuah ding a phu zet a si.

Simfiangtu tam zet cun Paul in 2 Korin 13:14 ih thlawsuah peknak ahhin thumkom Pathian a rel hlanah duhdawtnak Pathian thuhla malte a run relmi hmangin Pathian cu mi pathum vekin a ruat timi an theithiam: “Hitawk ahhin “Pathian” timi tongkam hi pathum lakih pakhat relnak ah a hmang ko nan, a cakuat hmun dang ih Jesuh le Thlarau a theihthiamnak cun… A thu pumhlum in Pathian pakhat a rel ih, cumi cu kawhhran hmaisa pawl in mi pathum um ih an rak hmuh mi kha a si timi hmu ve thiam dingah in rak bawm a si. Pathian thu lam ih mifim thiam zetzet pawl an um hlan kum zabi tamzet ihsin….Paul ih a rak hman zo mi a tawi zawngih hmannak ah “thumkom Pathian” timi tongkam an run hmang thok”—Tom Wright, Paul for Everyone: 2 Corinthians (London: Society for Promoting Christian Knowledge, 2004), p. 148.

Kannih cu Pathian pakhat, duhdawtnak ih laicin awknak sungah kumkhua ih a nung mi pathum pumkhatawknak hi kan zum a si. Himi thumkom Pathian cun in duhdawt ih, An lakih duhdawtnak a um vek ciah in pakhat le pakhat duhdawt aw dingah in sawm a si.
