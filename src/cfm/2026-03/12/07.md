Ellen G. White, “The Laodicean Church,” p. 125, in The Advent Review and Sabbath Herald, September 30, 1873.

“Bawipa cun a minung pawl cu a liamcia nun ih an tuahsual mi tuah kirsal lo dingin a kilhim ringring. Zirhtu dik lo, thurin dik hnongih thil thanghlim lo tuahtu, thungai bangzet nan a sualmi, bumnak nun, le a theih hram cun hrilcia mi hman bum dingih taimaknak diklo kaihhruainak cu a um ringring ding”—Ellen G. White, in The Advent Review and Sabbath Herald, January 7, 1904.

“Bawipa cun kan ruahnak hi fiahnak um dingin a tuah. Zumnak ah kan um ngaingai maw timi kan hmuh awk theinak dingah kan nun hi tha zet ih kan zoh hliah awk a tul a si. Thutak zumtu tiih a relaw tu tamzet cun a netnak ah, “ Milian ka si, thiltha tamzet thawn kan thangso, zianghman neih tul bet mi ka nei lo” tiih an ti ding”—Ellen G. White, Counsels to Writers and Editors, p. 36.

“Thutak cu fiangter a si tikah minung tampi cun tuahsual mi an nei theu: an thurin cu Pathian kamsuak thu ah hruai lut lo sehla, an thurin mil zelin Pathian kamsuak thu an siar lo ding ih, an ruahdan a dik zia tarlangnak ah thimnak le mitcawtnak ah an feh lo ding, asilole diklonak an ngaisang lo ding. Mi tampi cun Cathianghlim ih um kamsuak thu pawl hi anmai ruahdan thawn mil aw in an simfiang, cule Pathian kamsuak thu simfiangnak dik lo hmangih bum an si ih, anmah ten an hruaisual aw thluh a si. Pathian kamsuak thu kan zir tikah thinlung tangdornak thawn kan tuah ding a si. Mai tanghmai sialnak, duhdawtnak hmaisa pawl hmuahhmuah cu sir ih hnong ding a si. Ruahdan ngaisannak pawl cu dik lo thei lo vekih ruat ding a si lo.”—Ellen G. White, Counsels to Writers and Editors, pp. 36, 37.

**Relkhawm ding thusuhnak pawl:**

`Siar ding: 2 Korin 10:1-6. Pathian thudik hrang ah thlarau lam “raldo awknak” a hmaitonnak ih Paul ih khawkhannak kha ziangsaw a si, ziangtin saw kan thlarau raldo awknak ahhin kan hman ve thei ding?`

`Bible cun netnak a ra thlen hlanah zirhtu diklo pawl cun thudik ihsin mi tamzet hruai peng an tum ding tiah a ti. Nan tualsung kawhhran ih a um mi tla a si men thei mi zirhtu dik lo pakhat in thlemnak ihsin kawhhran member kilhim dingin nan tualsung kawhhran in ziangsaw nan tuah thei mi? Ziangruangah saw himi hi kawhhran hnatuan famkimternak ding hrangih a thupit?`

`Ziangruangah saw Paul khan harsat tuarnak thu ih uangawk kha a thupi tiih a hmuh (2 Kor. 11:16–33)? Cule, “Bawipa ah uang aw ding” timi sullam ziangsaw a si?`

`Zumnak ih an um le um lo theihfiangnak dingah ziangah saw kawhhran member tin in an zohfiah awk hi a thupit (2 Kor. 13:5)? Cumi in ziangmi danglamnak a tuah?`
