---
title:  Zirhtu dik lo pawl theihnak
date:   15/09/2026
---

Thukham Thar ahhin Khristian nunphung ah zirhtu deu pawl lakih ralrin peknak hmun tamzet a um. Amah Jesuh rori hman in dungthluntu pawl hnenah hivek thu ah ralrinnak a pe (Matt. 7:15–20). Tirthlah pawl khal in ralrinnak tlang an rak au pi ve nasa (Gal. 1:6–9, 1 Tim. 6:3–5, 2 Pit. 2:1–3).

`Siar ding: 2 Korin 11:1–15. Ziangtin saw Paul in himi zirhtu diklo pawl thawn an hmaiton awknak ih harsatnak a tonmi pawl a rel?`

Paul cun zirhtu diklo pawl ih hnatuannak a tarlang. Cumi rual in a hnatuan cu Khrih – thupibik ih ret hi a si timi a tarlang. Korin khua ih kawhhran cu mo vekin a tahthim ih, amah le mah cu a pa vekin a rel aw, Khrih hnen ih ap dingin tuanvo a nei a si (2 Kor. 11:2). Kawhhran a duhdawt tuk ruangah hivek in thil a tuahnak san a si (2 Kor. 11:11). Curuangah, kawhhran ih a bom dingmi sikhal sehla, sumpai lamah phurrit ih kawhhran a ret ding a duh lo (2 Kor. 11:7–12).

Khatlam ahcun “tirhtlah langsar zet pawl” (himi cu zirhtu dik lo pawl a rel duhnak a si men thei) cu Evi a rak bumtu rul vekih tahthim an si (2 Cor. 11:3). Eden hmuandum ih rulpi vek ciah in Korin khua ih zirhtu dik lo pawl cu bumnak le diklonak ah an ziaza a lang (2 Kor. 11:3, 4, NKJV). Paul ih a ruat mi bik cu Korin khawmi pawl hi Khrih ih pumpe aw zet le rinum zet an sinak ihsin an hruaisual pang ding timi hi a si.

Sawm lomi thusimtu cun Paul ih sim lo mi a phuang – Jesuh dang a phuang ih, thuthangtha danglam zet a sim (2 Kor. 11:4). Himi hin Jesuh thuhla phuangtu pawl hmuahhmuah hi Pathian ih a hmanmi an si thluh lo timi a tarlang a si. Himi thawn pehpar aw in Jesuh Amah rori in, “Bawipa, Bawipa, tiih I kotu pawl hmuahhmuah hi vancung uknak an thleng thluh lo ding, asinan van ih um Ka Pai’ duhnak a tuahtu lawng an thleng ding a si” tiah a ti’ ” (Matt. 7:21, NKJV). Galati 1:6–9 ah Paul cun zokhal thuthangtha danglam zet phuangtu cun amai parah camsiatnak a thlen a si, asinan Korin khua ih mi hrekkhat pawl cun hivek thil diklo hi an rak tuar tiah a ti.

Paul cun tirhthlah deu pawl cu “bum hmang hnatuantu pawl” tiah a ko hai, annih cu anmah ten Khrih ih tirhthlah tiih a tuahaw tu pawl an si” (2 Kor. 11:13, NKJV). Annih cu Khrih ih tirhthlah vek in an tuah aw, “Satan amah rori hman eng ih vancungmi ah a tuah awk” vek ciah in “ a (Satan ih)siahhlawh pawl khal dingfelnak siahhlawh vekin anmah ten an tuah aw” (2 Kor. 11:14, 15, ESV). Thil um tudan cu riahsiat thlakza a si: Khrih ih hnatuantu siahhlawh vekih rel aw, Satan ih palai an si. Paul cun himi thu ih a net kharnak ah an “netnak cu an hnatuanmi vekin a si ding” (2 Kor. 11:15, NKJV) tiah a ti.

`Paul hi Kawhhran sungih diklonak a um cun ziangtluk in a cangvai timi vun zoh hnik aw. Himi cun kan hnenah ziangsaw in sim ve?`
