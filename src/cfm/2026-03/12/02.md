---
title:  Thlarau lam raldonak
date:   13/09/2026
---

`Siar ding: 2 Korin 10:1-11. Paul cu Korin khawmi pawl thawn an dawr awknak ah a nunnemnak hi, hrekkhat pawl cun a caklonak a si tiah an ruat. Himi thu ahhin ziangmi tongkam in saw Korin khua ih zirhternak dik lo buainak hmaiton dingih Paul ih ralthatnak a tarlang?`

Paul cun 2 Korin 10 hi bulpak nun relnak in hram a thok: “A tu ahcun Keimah Paul rori in ka lo ngen a si” (2 Kor. 10:1, HCSB). Himi cun Paul in kawhhran ih a luanglut mi zirhternak dik lo a ngaihven zia a tarlang a si. 2 Korin 10:1 ih a tongkam ahhin amah dokalhtu cun anih cu hlaral ih cakuat a ngan tikah tihnung umza a si ih, asinan hmaiton awk tik ahcun a tihhrut tuk tiih an mawhthluknak pawl a run sim (2 Kor. 10:10, 11). Thazang cak lo vekih a langmi cu tangdornak thilti thei zet le Khrih bangih nunnemnak vek ih hmuh a si tiin a run let.

Zirhtu diklo pawl cu ngamhuai le raltha zetih hmaiton ding a si (2 Kor. 10:2), asinan Khrih ih nunnemnak thawn a rawi aw ding a si (2 Kor. 10:1). Jesuh cun hitin a rak rel, “Keimah cu mi nunnem le thinlung tangdor mi ka si” (Matt. 11:29, NIV). Ziangkhal sehla, Jesuh khal in paisa thlengtu pawl hmaiah ngamhuai zet in an cabuai pawl linglet sak thluh in, rukru pawl tiah a ko hai (Matt. 21:12, 13). Farasi pawl khal detde pawl le thlan sirang hnihmi pawl tiah an hmai rori ah a ko hai (Matt. 23:23–27). Jesuh vekin Paul cun thlarau lam raldonak ah kan um ih, Pathian ralthuam famkim hman a tul timi a thei (Efesa. 6:12–17).

Paul in 2 Korin 10 ih a hmanmi tongkam hi ralkap thawn a pehpar aw mi a si, ziangahtile nunnak hi a tihnung um tuk (2 Kor. 10:3–6). Himi cu minung doawknak menmen a si lo ih, Khrih hnen ih minung thlentheinak ding hrang Pathian doawknak lam a si. Cui pehtlaih awknak ahhin Pathian kamsuak thu ah thum aw in thuel buai awknak diklo, le ruahdan dik lo pawl hmuahhmuah cu hmaiton ih siatsuah thluh ding a si, cuticun khawruahnak pawl hmuahhmuah cu “Khrih thuawih dingin” (2 Kor. 10:5, NIV) sal ih hruailuh theih an si ding.

Himi thlarau lam doawknak ahhin Paul cu Khrih ih thuneihnak ah a cangvai a si. ziangkhal sehla, himi thuneihnak cu siatsuahnak siloin thansohnak ding hrangih tumtah mi a si (2 Kor. 10:8). Thlarau lam hruaitu pawl hrangah annih cu Pathian ih thuneihnak ah an cangvai a si timi an theih a tha. Asinan an thuneihnak cu Khrih ih pek mi a si timi an thei ringring ding a si. Amah vekin an nunnem in an thinlung a tangdor tengteng ding a si. Paul cun Khrih ih pek mi thuneihnak a relnak sanbik cu Korin khawmi pawl in mi dik lo ih thu simmi rak ngai in, Khrih ah an rak rinum lo pang kei timi a ngaihven ruangah a si.

`Zirhtu dik lo thawn kan dawrawk tikah ziangtin saw ngamhuai zet le raltha zetin kan um thei ding? Ziangruangah a pahnih in kan neih tengteng a tul?`
