---
title:  Bawipa ah uangawknak
date:   14/09/2026
---

Mani sun ah Paul le a kaihhruai hnatuantu pawl cun an hnatuannak cu thlarau lam raldonak a si ih, Pathian ralthuam an hmang timi pawl kan hmu thluh zo. Tuini ahcun Zirhtu diklo pawl in minung ih tahfang hmangin cangvaihnak an nei timi kan hmu ding. An uangawknak cu uangawknak tlak lo zet a si. Khatlam ah Paul ih uangawnak cu Bawipa lawngah a si. Hitin a ngan, “Sihmansehla a uangawtu cun Bawipa ah uangaw seh’ ” (2 Kor. 10:17, NKJV).

`Siar ding: 2 Korin 10:13-17. Ziangtin saw zuamawknak boruak cun thuthangtha phuangnak ah harsatnak a pek thei?`

Paul in mah le mah uangawknak tongkam a hman mi hi kum zabi tamzet sung simfiangtu pawl buaitertu a si. Ziangkhal sehla, mah le mah uangawknak cu hlansan leitlun ahcun an ti theudan a rak si, a rak ngaitu mipi pawl ih thinhengternak hrial dingah pumkhawm awknak hmangih thununnak a si. Paul cun cuvek ih pumkhawm awknak a thei, cule amah khal in a thlun cih ve. Cui hleiah Paul cun a uangawkdan cu zirhtu deu pawl thawn an bangaw lo tuk tiah a rel fiang. Anih cu Bawipa ah a uangaw a si (2 Kor. 10:17). Himi cu Thukham Hlun ihsin lak mi a si: “Kei Bawipa cu leitlun ah duhdawt zangfahnak, thuthennak, le dingfelnak a hmangtu ka si timi in theithiam ih in theihnak ahhin a uangawtu cun uangaw sawn seh’ ” (Jer. 9:24). Himi thu Jeremiah sungih ta la tahrat in Paul cun a hmuitin mi bik cu Khrih – Khrih ih duhdawtnak, diknak le dingfelnak a si timi a tarlang.

Phundang in kan sim cun Paul ih uangawknak cu Khrih sungah Pathian in a hlensuak mi hnatuan kha a si.Curuangah a uangawknak cu Bible thu lam a si ih, mi nater theitu khal a si lo. Khatlam ahcun amah dotu pawl cu pakhat le pakhat eltai aw in, zuam awknak boruak ah an um ruri. Himi cu atnak a si (2 Kor. 10:12).

2 Korin 10:14-16 ahkhan Paul cun Korin khua le a lenglam hmun dang khalih thuthangtha phuang hi a hnatuan ih a hmuitin bik mi a si tiah a sim. Paul in Jesuh a duhdawtnak tuk ruangah Khrih ih thihnak le thawhsalnak ih a hmuh ngah mi rundamnak thuthangtha rel rero a duh a s.

Korin khua ih anmahten a sunlawih aw tu Zirhtu dik lo pawl vek siloin, Paul cu Pathian sunlawih mi le nemhnget mi a si (2 Kor. 10:12, 18). Anih cu “Pathian duhdan vekin Jesuh Khrih ih tirhthlah si dingih kawh mi” a si (1 Kor. 1:1, NKJV). Himi kawhnak ahhin a nun cem tiang a rinum a si (2 Tim. 4:7).

`Siar aw: 2 Korin 10:12–18. Ziangtin saw kawhhran hruaitu pawl, asilole kawhhran member pawl khal in zuamawknak boruk kan hrial thei ding? Ziangruangah saw thil thupi tuk lem lo ahhin hnuaihnih awk a olsam emem?`
