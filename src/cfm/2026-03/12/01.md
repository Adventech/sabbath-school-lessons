### Tuizarh zirlai hrang siar ding
2 Kor. 10:1–17; Jer. 9:24; 2 Kor. 11:1–15, 22–28; 2 Kor. 12:20, 21; 2 Kor. 13:5.

> <p>Bible cangken</p>
> “Ziangahtile kan raldonak hriamhrei pawl hi tisa hriamhrei an si lo, Pathian ih huham in hmun hngetkhoh thiatbalnak ding sawn a si (2 Korin 10:4, ESV).

Paul in thubuai neih a khawp thei lo si awm takin thubuai dang relfel ding pakhat a um sal lala: kawhhran sungih zirhawknak sual a si. Hi minung pawl cun amah le a hnatuannak pawl an dokalh. Cumi ai hnakih rapthlak sawn cu Korin kawhhran member hrekkhat pawl khal an rak thlem. Paul cun himi buainak a donak cu thlarau lam doawknak a si tiah a ti.

Cui thu cu a luan tuk deuh maw? Lamlam. Paul cun annih pawl cun amah dokalh loin Khrih an dokalh a si timi a theifiang. Anih cu hminthatnak le huham le thuneihnak pawl kilkhawi a duh zettu hruaitu lakah a tel lo. A phuangmi thucah cu thihnak le nunnak thuhla a si ih, kumkhaw daih thil a si timi a theifiang zet. Himi phuang dingin Amah Pathian rori ih thlah mi a si ti khal a thei aw. “Pual, Pathian duhdan vekin Jesuh Khrih ih tirhhlah si dingih kawh mi” (1 Kor. 1:1)ti a si kha!

Zirhnak sual a ra lut tikah kawhhran cun Ca thianghlim ih thuneihnak ah thum aw in, fekzetin duhdawtnak thawn a rak cangvai ding a si. Kumkhaw ruahsannak hi thlarau hlo pawl hnen ih pek a si theinak dingah thuthangtha cu humhim, siatter lo le thianhlimter a tul a si.
