---
title:  Thinglamtah ih thuthangttha
date:   05/07/2026
---

Paul cun thinglamtah thucah cu kan hrangah Pathian ih huham a si tiah a ti. Curuangah “Jesuh Khrih le Khenbetmi pa” hi a thusimnak ih a laimu bik ih a rak ret khal hi mangbangza a si lem lo (1 Kor. 2:2, NKJV).

`Siar ding: 1 Korin 1:17–31. Hitawk ahhin Paul in thil thupi zet ziangsaw a tuah?`

1 Korin 1:18-31 ahhin Paul cun minung atnak le Pathian fimnak karlak ih an bangawk lo dan a rel. Thinglamtah cun Pathian ih tthatnak le minung ih sualzia tarlang theinak huham a nei. 1 Korin ih thuhlawm ummi cu 1 Korin 1:17 ahhin hmeltheihternak a run nei. Khrih ih thinglamtah huham cu zianghman lo men ih um ding a si lo (1 Kor. 1:17), Thinglamtah thucah cu kan thusimnak ih laimu bik ah a um tengteng ding a si (zoh awla, 1 Kor. 2:2).

Paul cun tipilnak pe dingih thlah mi si loin, Thinglamtah thuthangttha phuang dingih thlahmi a si thu a rel. Himi thu cun thil thupi zet pahnih a rel tel. Pakhatnak “thlah” timi hi Grik ttong apostello timi ihsin letlin mi a si ih, cumi cu tirhthlah timi ttong bulhram ihsin lak mi a si. Curuangah Paul ih tirhthlah hnattuan ding thupi bik cu thuthangttha phuang hi a si. A pahnihnak ahcun Paul in tipilnak thuhla a rel mi ahhin tipilnak hi a thupi lo, asilole thuthangttha phuang ai hnakih a nep deuh tiih a relnak lam a si lo. Tipilnak nei zo tu pawl in an tipil san Jesuh hmuitin lo in, tipilnak lam lawnglawng ruat rero ruangih nun a simnak a si.

“Fimnak tttongkam” (1 Kor. 1:17) timi ahhin Paul cun thurel thiam zet pawl hi a ngaingai ahcun an ttha lo tiih a relnak lam a si lo. A reltummi bik cu minung fimnak cun Thinglamtah thucah hi a khuh ding a si lo timi a rel duh mi a si. Himi cun a tikcu lai ih Grik le Rom mi ih ttongkam hleifuan hmandan a rel duhnak a si. Athen khawpi ahcun Paul cun thuruah thiamnak (logic), khawthlir thiamnak (Science), khawruah thiamnak (Philosophy) tivek pawl a hmang. Asinan himi cun ruah a suah mal zet. Curuangah “Ngaihsak tu le zianghman ih a siar lotu pawl ih ngaihsak a hlawh theinak dingah Korin khua ah hnattuan dang nei dingin thutluknak a tuah. Thuelawknak le thubuai reltlangnak pawl hnong thluh in, “Rundamtu Jesuh Khrih le Khenbetmi pa” thu siar lo Korin khawmi pawl hnen ihsin “zianghman thei lo” dingin thutluknak a tuah.’ ”¬—Ellen G. White, The Acts of the Apostles, p. 244.

`Ziang lamzin in saw Thinglamtah thucah cu famkim zetih phuang a si thei ding? Ziangruangahsaw Jesuh Khrih le khengbetmi pa ih thu a simnak ahhin Athen ih thuruah thiamnak, khawthlir thiamnak, khawruah thiamnak pawl a hman ai hnakin Korin khua ah rah ttha a suah tam deuh? Thuthanttha phuangsuahnak ahhin thuruah thiamnak, khawthlir thiamnak, khawruah thiamnak pawl hi a hmannak ding a um thei pei maw?`
