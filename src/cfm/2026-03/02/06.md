---
title:  Khrih, Pathian ih fimnak le huham
date:   09/07/2026
---

1 Korin 1:19, 20, 30, le 31 ah Paul cun Pathian fimnak le minung fimnak ih an dang awk verver dan pawl a rel, curuangah pakhat le pakhat cu bangawlo tak an si. Paul cun fimnak hrimhrim hi a hnong lo ih, Pathian eltai tumnak minung fimnak kha a hnonmi a si timi hmin sin aw. Minung fimnak cun sual ihsin minung a suah zalen thei lo. Pathian fimnak Khrih lawngin hi hna hi a ttuan thei a si. A hnuai ih um mi hi zoh aw…

| Rundam mi kan hrangah | (Thinglamtah ih thucah) cu Pathian ih huham a si | 1 Kor. 1:18 (NKJV) |
| Kawh mi pawl hrangah | Khrih ih Pathian huham a si | 1 Kor. 1:24 (NKJV) |

1 Korin 1:18 le 1 Korin 1:24 cun Khrih cu Pathian ih huham a si timi an tarlang veve. Himi thu ah Anih cun minung pawl an sualnak ihsin rundam theinak huham a nei tinak a si. A ngaingai te cun, “thusim aatthlak hmang in a zumtu pawl rundam ding cu Pathian ih lungawi zawng a si” (1 Kor. 1:21). “Kannih rundam mi”(1 Kor. 1:18, NKJV), “a zumtu pawl” (1 Kor. 1:21), timi le “kawh mi pawl” (1 Kor. 1:24) timi ttongkam pawl cun thil thuhmun an rel, zumnak thawng in rundamnak tonnun neitu minung pawl an rel thluh a si. “Khrih ih thuthangttha cu… a zumtu pawl hmuahhmuah hrangah rundamnak hrang Pathian ih huham a si” (Rom. 1:16).

Khrih cu huham lawng siloin Pathian ih fimnak a si. Cumi sullam cu Amah hmangin Pathian cun, sual ih buainak a hmaiton ih a relfel, cumi cu minung fimnak ih a relfel thei riai lomi buainak a si. Hi leitlun ih fimnak cun mipi hnenah Pathian a theihter thei lo (1 Kor. 1:21). Cumi thawn kalh aw ciah in Khrih thawngin rundamnak hrangah mifim ah kan cang (2 Tim. 3:15).

`Siar aw: 1 Korin 1: 24–29. Khitawk ih ttongfang um mi khi hminsin aw, “atthlak”, “cak lo”, “huham”, le “fimnak”. Ziang saw a rel tum mi?`

1 Korin 1:24-29 vun siar tikah, mi pakhat cun atnak (lole atthlak) le “cak lo” (lole cahlonak) timi a hmu ding. A sullam cu minung fimnak cun Thinglamtah thucah cu atthlak lole cahlohnak vekin a ruah men thei tinak a si. Ziangkhal sehla, “Pathian ih atnak cu minung hnakin a fim sawn, Pathian ih cahlonak cu minung hnakin a cak sawn” (1 Kor. 1:25, ESV). Himi sullam cu Pathian hi cahlonak nei, asilole atthlak za a si tinak lam a si lo. Pathian huham le fimnak cu milai hnakin ziangtluk in a tum sawn timi langternak men a si

`Himi ttongkam pawl hi hminsin aw, “taksa lamih mifim tampi siseh, micak tampi siseh, mi upa tampi siseh, kawh an si lo zia nan thei” (1 Kor. 1:26). Khitawk ahhin kan hrang ziang thucah a um?`
