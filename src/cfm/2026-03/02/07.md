Ellen G. White, “Calvary,” pp. 741–757, in The Desire of Ages.

“ Tuisan ih minung tamzet ih thinlung ahcun Kalvari thinglamtah cu ruahnak thianghlim in a kulh thluh. Thianghlim pawlkom awknak cu thinglamtah khenbetnak thawn an pehtlaih aw thluh. Asinan Paul san ahcun thinglamtah cu huatawknak le tihnung um vekih ruah mi a si. Mi pakhat hi milai Rundamtu vekin thinglamtah parah thihnak tong bangta sehla, netalam ahcun hnihsuaknak le hminsiatnak lawng a suah pi men thei.

“Paul cun a thucah ken mi cu Korin ih um Judah le Grik pawl in ziangtin an rak pawm ding timi cu a theifiang zet… A thu rak ngaitu Judah mi pawl lakah hrekkhat cu a thucah kengmi ruangah thinheng in an um ding timi khal a thei. Grik mi pawl cun a thusimmi ttongkam cu atthlak za tiah an ruat. Thinglamtah cu minung rundamnak asilole miphun rundamnak thawn an pehtlaih awk zia a tarlang tum ruangah a thluak kim lo ah an ruat.

“Asinan Paul hrang ahcun thinglamtah cu a ngaihven bik mi a si. Thinglamtah ih khenbet mi Nazareth thluntu pawl hremnak a ton tum ih, kaih a ton ihsin thinglamtah sunlawihnak pek hi a cawlhsan nawn lo. Cumi caan ah Khrih thihnak ih a langmi Pathian duhdawtnak cu a hnenah phuang a si; cule mangbangza thlengawknak cun a nun a run danglam ter thluh, a tumtah mi le a khawkhan mi pawl hmuahhmuah cu vanram thawn a rem aw thlepthli thluh…Amai bulpak tontehnak ihsin Pathian Fapa ih raithawinak ih a hmuh vekin Pathian duhdawtnak hi misual in a kaihhnget tikah Pathian umpitnak co in, thinlung thlengdangmnak a um ding ih, ziangkim ah Khrih a um thluh timi a thei.”—Ellen G. White, The Acts of the Apostles, pp. 245, 246.

**Relkhawm ding thusuhnak pawl:**

`Gesthesmane hmuan ahkhan Jesuh cun, “Aw, Ka Pa, a cang thei a si ahcun himi khuaihai hi ka hnen ihsin i lan hram seh” tiah a ti (Matt. 26:39, NKJV). Himi thlacamnak in Jesuh thinglamtah parih a pekmi mankhung zet thuhla ziangsaw a rel?`

`Paul cun, “Pathian ih atnak cu minung hnakin a fim sawn (1 Kor. 1:25, NKJV). Ziang lamzin in saw Pathian ih fimnak cu minung fimnak thawn an danglam awk?`

`Khrih ih raithawinak thucah cu Judah pawl hrangah dokalh tu a si ih, Grik mi pawl hrangah atnak a si. A tuisan mi pawl hnenah rahtha a suah theinak dingah ziangmi Bible thulu pawl saw kan sim ding, ziangruangah?`

`Paul cun, “tisa mi cun Pathian Thlarau ih thil pawl cu a cosang lo” (1 Kor. 2:14, NKJV). Curuangah ziangtin saw hivek minung pawl hnenah Jesuh thuhla kan sim thei ding? Asilole kan cangvaihnak lawng in an hnen ah thuthangttha kan sim thei ding ha?`
