---
title:  Hloral rero mi pawl hrangah atnak
date:   06/07/2026
---

Minung atnak le Pathian ih fimnak kalh aw zetin Paul cun, “Thinglamtah thucah cu a hloral rero mi pawl hrang ahcun atnak a si” tiah a rak ngan (1 Kor. 1:18, NKJV). Himi cu 1 Korin 1:18-31 sung ih atnak thawn pehpar aw ih a relmi paruk lakih a hmaisabik a si.

`Siar ding: 1 Korin 1:20, 21, 23, 25, le 27. Hitawk ih atnak timi a um mi pawl hin thinglamtah cu hloral rero mi pawl hrangah cun atnak a si tiih Paul ih a rak rel mi theithiam dingin ziangtin in rak bawm?`

1 Korin 1:18 sungih atnak timi hi Grik ttong ih moria timi a si. Himi ttongfang hi thukham thar ah voi 3 lawng a rel, cu hmanah a zaten 1 Korin hlir ah an um (1 Kor. 1:18, 21, 23; 1 Kor. 2:14; 1 Kor. 3:19). Hivek deuh nawn thawn a pehpar aw mi pawl cu thukham thar ah tamzet a um, cumi pawl lakih a hrek cu Paul ih cakuat ah an um a si. Thukham thar sungih hivek ttongkam thawn hnauihnih aw deuh pawl hi 1 Korin ahhin an um thluh deuh thaw.

Paul in 1 Korin 1:18,23 ih atnak tiih a rel mi cu fimnak tlaksamnak, nuntu khawsakdan le ruahnak borhbahnak pawl thawn an pehpar aw lem lo, asinan thleidan thiamnak ih tlaksamnak le Pathian dokalhnak lam sawn a si. Curuangah Korin cakuat pakhatnak sung ahhin himi thuhla a rel tam zet.

Hi khawpi ih Paul’ dinhmun vun ruat aw. Anih cu anmah te ih fimthiamnak le theihnak le nunphung khaisang awk ciamconak hmun ah a ra thleng. Cule himi thu ahhin anih cun Galili ram ih Judah mi Nazareth Jesuh, Rom pawl in an khenbet hnuah thihnak ihsin a thosal tu ih thuhla a ra sim – cumi pawl hmuahhmuah cu an sual man lawng hman siloin leitlun sualman rulh a si theinak dingah a si. Hi pa hi a thungai pei maw? Zosaw a capoh rero le? Himi cu khawruah fimthiamnak thar thukzet ih ummi a si lo, khawruah fimthiamnak hmangih theihthiam le theihfiang theih rual a si lo: Korin khua ih fimnak le theihnak hmangih ruahban ding rual a si lo ih, atthlakza thil men a si.

Paul ih thucah hi zumlotu pawl hrangih atthlak za a si vek ciah in Thinglamtah thucah hi Judah mi tamzet hrangah a bese sawn tla a bang zet. Ziangvek Judah mi in saw Messiah hi Rom pawl ih thah theih ding ih rak beisei dah? Messiah cu Rom pawl ih khengbet ding mi siloin, Rom pawl a neh thluh tu a si ding a si.

Curuangah a hramthok ihsin Korin khua ahhin Pual cun dokalhtu tamzet a nei. Asinan hivek pawl hmuahhmuah dungtun in thlarau lam ih Judah le Zentel pawl cu Thuthangttha ahhin nehnak an co a si.

Ziangvek dokalhtu kan ton khalah Pathian in thudik ngai dingih ongaw zet minung a nei a si. Hivek minung pawl an umnak hmun kipah, Korin khaw mi pawl vekih nunsia hmang pawl hnenah, asilole annih ai hnakih a luarkai pawl umnak hmun khalah Thuthangttha sim dingih Pathian ih hman theih dingin kan ralring ringring ding a si.
