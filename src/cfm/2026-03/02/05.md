---
title:  Khenbet mi Messiah
date:   08/07/2026
---

Paul cun “Judah pawl in hmuhsaknak an dil, Grik pawl cun fimnak an hawl” (1 Kor. 1:22, ESV) tiah a ti. Thinglamtah – Messiah khenbet dingih Pathian ih ruahnak cu Judah pawl ih an ruahsanmi hmuhsaknak a si lo. Grik pawl ih an duh ih an hawl mi fimnak khal a si lo. Cui thil cu mikip ih an beisei lodan in thil a feh thluh.

A ngaingai te cun mi pakhat ih a tuah dingmi cu Jesuh khenbetnak thuhla ah a hleice in Judah pawl hrangah ziangtluk mikemza a si timi a hmuh thiam thok theinak dingah ahcun dungthluntu pawl ih an rak sawnlet pawl a siar a ttul (Zoh awla, Marka 8:31, 32; Marka 9:30–32; le Marka 10:32–34). A hmaisa lam ih simrel zo vekin Judah pawl cun Messiah in Rom uknak ram a neh ding tiah an rak beisei, cui thil cu a thleng lo, leitlun rakap ih “ralneh” timi lam khi a si ve lo.

Kum zabi tamzet sung cu Khristian pawl hrang ahcun Thinglamtah cu zumnak hminsinnak a rak si. Kum zabi 21 ih um Khristian pawl hrangah Thinglamtah parih khenbetmi Pathian ih ruahnak hi kum zabi 1 ih thinlung khawruahnak ah ziangtluk in atthlak a si ti theihthiam a har zet.

Ziangkhal sehla, himi hi thucah mangbangza a si ruangah thinlung thuk zetih kan ngaihtuah a ttul. Thinglamtah parih khenbetmi Messiah cun Pathian in tlennak khawkhannak tuahfel ding hi ziangtluk in a duh timi leitlun pumhuap ah a tarlang a si. Thinglamtah le thinglamtah parih Bawipai thihnak cu kannih leitlun misual pawl hrangah mangbangza ngaingai a si. (Van ih Bawipa Jesuh a theitu, cibai a buktu, sualnak zianghman nei lo pawl hrang ah ziangmi sullam a nei ding tin vun ruat hman aw)

`Siar ding: Tirhthlah 13:16–47 (a hleice in cang 26, 38, le 47). Himi bungcang cun Thinglamtah sullam ziangsaw in sim?`

Paul cun Khrih in thuthanttha phuang dingih a thlah thu a rel. Curuangah Paul cun khenbetmi Messiah ih thucah pawl a phuang (1 Kor. 1:23). Himi ruahnak hi 1 Korin 2:1-5 ah a phorhsal. Tirhthlah cu Khrih ih thupek mi ah a rinum zet. Thuthangttha a phuangnak ah “a nasa zetmi ttongkam le fimnak” a hmang lo (1 Kor. 2:1, ESV); cumi ai hnakih “Jesuh Khrih le Khenbetmi pa” lawng a hmuitin sawn (1 Kor. 2:2, ESV). A ttongkam le thucah cu “Fimnak ttongkam in a si lo ih, Thlarau le huham ih langternak thawn a si sawn” (1 Kor. 2:4, ESV) ziangahtile a ngaingai te cun, “Minung fimnak cu Pathian huham thawn” a kalh aw zet a si. (2 Kor. 2:5, ESV).

`Thinglamtah ih khenbet mi Messiah cu Judah le Grik pawl ih an beisei riai lomi a rak si. Himi cun kan beisei vekin Pathian in thil a tuah ringring lo timi thuhla ziangsaw in sim? A hleice in kan beisei vekih thil a feh lo caan ah ziangah himi hi kan theihthiam a ttul?`
