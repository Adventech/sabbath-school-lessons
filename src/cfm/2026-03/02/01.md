### Tuizarh zirlai hrang siar ding
1 Kor. 1:17–31, Kol. 1:20, 1 Pet. 2:24, Tirh 13:16–47, 1 Kor. 2:1–5.

> <p>Bible cangken</p>
> “Ziangahtile Thinglamtah thucah cu a hloral rero mi pawl hrangah cun atnak a si, asinan kannih rundam mi pawl hrang ahcun Pathian ih huham a si (1 Korin 1:18, NIV).

Rom mi pathian dang biatu cangan le thurel thiam zet Cicero cun Rom mipi pawl hnenah thinglamtah cu hremnak rak zohtertu a si tiih an ruahnak ihsin thiar fihlim aw dingin a rak sim. Asinan Cicero hi Jesuh a suah hlan kum zabi hrek hrawng ah rak thi zo khalsehla, Rom mipi pawl in Thinglamtah an rak hmuh ttheudan pawl hi a tarlang fuh zet. Cui thuron a pek mi zianghman ih an rat ruahsak duh lo cu a poi zet.

Cuvek ih khawruahnak thawn kalh aw ciah in Paul cun hitin a rak ngan: “Thinglamtah thucah cu…. Pathian ih huham a si” (1 Kor. 1:18, NIV). Paul hrang ahcun thinglamtah cu Pathian le minung karlak rualrem awknak (Efesa. 2:16, Kol. 1:20), Jesuh ih tangdorzia zohternak (Fili. 2:8), le a nasa zetmi kan leiba rulh ih a umnak hmun a si(Kol. 2:14).

Thinglamtah cu Korin khawmi pawl ih thubuai relfelnak a si. Thubuai tum zet pakhat: ‘Kawhhran ih tthendarh awknak’ a ngaihven zet timi theifiang dingin 1 Korin hman hi hla pi tiang na siar a ttul lo ding. Paul cun cibai buknak a nei ih(1 Kor. 1:1–3), lungawi thusimnak a neih ttheh veten (1 Kor. 1:4–9), hi thu hi a rel hmaisabik mi thulu a si(1 Kor. 1:10–17). Tuizarh ahcun himi buainak le Korin khua ih buainak dang pawl letnak ah thinglamtah thucah ih huham a neihdan kan thlir ding.
