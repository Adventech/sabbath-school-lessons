---
title:  Milem biaknak donehnak
date:   30/07/2026
---

1 Korin 8:1-3 ah Paul cun duhdawtnak in milem biaknak ihsin mi a huhim thu a rel. Himi thubuai hi 1 Korin 10:23-11:1 ah a run rel sal ih, kau zetih a run sim. 1 Korin 8:3 ah Pathian kan duhdawtnak thuhla a rel. “zo khal in mai bulpak hrang hawl hlah seh, zozo khal in midang thatnak ding hrang hawl sawn seh” (1 Kor. 10:24, NKJV). Himi cu midang duhdawtnak a si.

`Siar ding: Marka 10:17–22 le Marka 12:28–31. Himi pahnih in ziang bangawknak saw an neih? Ziangtin saw 1 Korin 10 ih dinhmun ah ziangtin saw hman a theih dan ding?`

Paul in 1 Korin 10 ih a tuah mi cu Jesuh in Marka 12:28-31 ih a tuah vek ciah kha a si; Daan ih thupek ropi zet pahnih cu a ret kop: thil ziangkim tlun ih Pathian duhdawt le midang duhdawtnak hi a si. Milian tlangvalpa ih thuanthu ahkhan (Marka 10:17–22), Jesuh cun hi duhdawtnak phun 2 hi a pehzom awter, cumi cu Daanpeksalnak 6:4 (zohaw Marka 10:18) sungta le thupek hlawm pahnih ih(zoh aw, Mark a10:19) a netatu ta lak mi kha a si. Milian tlangval pa ih buainak cu Pathian le a innhnen pawl a duhdawt ai hnakih nasa in a thilneih mi a duhdawtnak khi a si (Marka 10:22). Vanlam hlawnthil ai hnakin a lei lam hlawnthil a ngai na deuh. Mi farah pawl ai hnakin a paisa a duh sawn a si (Marka 10:21). Anih cu milem betu rori a si.

Jesuh ih thuzirhnak thlun in Paul cun ziangkim tlun ih Pathian duhdawt le mah le mah duhdawtawk vekin inn hnen duhdawt ding timi thupi cu 1 Korin 10:27, 28 ih dinhmun ah hman ding a si ti thu a rel. Himi sullam cu thil tuah a thiangmi khal hi midang ih khawruahnak a siatsuah thei ruangah a tha hnem lo dingih, a thangso thei lo ding tinak a si (1 Kor. 10:23). Himi kaihhruai awknak cu “Ziangkim Pathian sunlawinak ah tuah uh” (1 Kor. 10:31, NKJV )timi tongkam ahhin mawi zetin an pehzom aw khawm thluh a si. Pathian sunlawinak ah ziangkim tuah thluh ding a si timi ahhin Paul cun Milem biaknak cu a phunphun in a langter, ziangahtile Pathian sunlawinak siatsuahter theitu cu milem biaknak lawng hi a si (Isa. 42:8).

Paul in 1 Korin 10:31-11:1 ih a thurel mi hi bung 8-10 ih net kharnak a si. Anih cun amai hlawknak hawl loin, “mi tampi rundam an si theinak ding, mi tampi ih hlawknak a hawl” (1 Kor. 10:33, ESV) sawn timi a fiangter. Cumi cu Khrih a zohthimdan a si (1 Kor. 11:1).

`Nangmah na duhdawt awk vekin na innhnen na duhdawt deuhdeuh theidan ding hi ziangtin na zir thei ding?`
