Ellen G. White, “Idolatry at Sinai,” pp. 315–330, in Patriarchs and Prophets.

“Pakhat le pakhat pawlkom awknak hi dik zetin hmang sehla ziangtluk thiltha a si ding! vanlam hlawknak a dong ngahzo tu pawl cun midang lamzin ih eng porhsuak ding tuanvo an nei thluh a si….Pathian a duhdawt ngaingai tu pawl cun anmah le anmah milem biaknak an bansan ding a si.”—Ellen G. White, in The Advent Review and Sabbath Herald, November 18, 1884, p. 730.

“Paul cun a unau pawl hnenah an tongkam le an thiltuahmi in midang pawlah ziangtluk in a poi thei timi anmah ten sut aw dingan ngennak a nei, ziangkhal sehla, milem biaknak remtisaknak simaw, zumnak lam ih cak lo pawl thinna tertu tivek a si ahcun zianghman tuah lo dingin a forhfial hai. “Nan ei ah siseh, nan in ah siseh, ziangkim nan tuah ah Pathian sunlawinak ah tuah uh…”

“Tirhthlah in Korin khawmi kawhhran pawl hnenih ralrin peknak tongkam cu tikcu caan tinkim le a hleice in a tui kan san khalah hman a theih thluh. Milem biaknak a timi ahhin milem ngaingai va biaknak men a rel tum mi a si lo, mah le mah ngaisan awknak, duhdawt awk le, ei duhnak le duham tuknak pawl a relnak khal a si.”—Ellen G. White, The Acts of the Apostles, pp. 316, 317.

“Tuah dingih diknak famkim na neih mi thil ziang simaw na tuah ahhin Pathian hnatuanak rak dokalh tu a si ahcun cui thil tuah mi pawl hmuahhmuah cu bansan aw. Midang in thudik an dokalh theinak thinlung thawn a nai aw mi thil ziangvek khal tuah hlah… thil ziangkim hi tuah a thiang thluh men thei, asinan ziangkim hi an thahnem thluh lo”—Ellen G. White, Testimonies for the Church, vol. 9, p. 215.

**Relkhawm ding thusuhnak pawl:**

`Paul ih reldan vek a sile a pitling mi Khristian nuncan cun khuimaw caan ahcun a pitling lomi Khristian nuncan ih thansohnak a kham thei. Hivek thil a hung um theidan dinhmun pawl kha ruat aw. Ziang ruangah saw ziangkim tlun ih Pathian duhdawtnak timi le mah duhdawt awk vekin midang duhdawt ding timi kaihhruai awknak hi himi harsatnak relfelnak ding lamzin umsun a si?`

`An ralrin lo ahcun Khristian pawl hman in an biak thei mi milem kha ziangsaw a si? Milem ih kan canter thei mi thil tha zetzet pawl kha ziangsaw an si? Thil ziangmaw na rak ngaihsak zet mi cu milem ah a cang timi hi ziangtin saw na theih ding?`

`Paul cun thuthangtha a simnak ah santlai lo ih a suah lonak dingah a taksa a thunun thu a rel (1 Kor. 9:27). Tuizarh zirlai ah thum aw in, thuthangtha phuangtu mi pakhat hi santlai lo ih a tuah theitu hi ziang a si ding tin na ruat?`

`1 Korin 10 ahkhan Paul cun milem biaknak ih tihnung a umzia a rel ih, “Milem biaknak tlansan uh” tiah a ti (1 Kor. 10:14). Ziangah milem biaknak hi a that lo ciamco?`
