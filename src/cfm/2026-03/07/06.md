---
title:  Zumnak, ruahsannak, le duhdawtnak
date:   13/08/2026
---

A tutiang ahhin duhdawtnak cu thinsaunak, ngilneihnak, lungawinak, thinlung zangnak, zumnak, ruahsannak, le tuarfainak (lole zuamnak) a si timi kan zir ngah zo, ziangahtile Jesuh cu hivek thil pawl hmuahhmuah hi a si. Himi pawl hmuahhmuah ih tlinnak hi Jesuh ih kan hmuh ngah tik ahcun a lehpek um ding mi cu a Amah zohthim ding hi a si. Cumi ciah cu Paul in Korin mi pawl hrangih a duh mi a si. Ziangkhal sehla, duhdawtnak tarlangnak ih a tha lomi pariat pawl lak ihsin “lo” timi kan hlawn ding ahcun, “ An kawhhran sungih Korin khawmi pawl ih nuncan simfiangnak thazet kan nei thei ding : Iksik, puarthau, hngal, misual, mai tanghmai sial, thinheng ol, le midang ih thiltuah dik lomi hawl hmuh tum ih zoh kual rero tivek nuncan ziaza pawl an si. Paul cun hitawk ih a hmanmi tongfang pawl cu Korin khawmi pawl dinhmun thawn rem aw zetin a hmang.”—Verlyn D. Verbrugge, “1 Corinthians,” in The Expositor’s Bible Commentary: Romans–Galatians, revised edition (Grand Rapids, MI: Zondervan, 2008), p. 372.

Korin mi pawl cun zir ding tamzet an nei. Kannih khal in kan nei ve. Duhdawtnak ih a tuah mi le a tuah lomi pawl a simfiang thluh hnuah Paul cun duhdawtnak a cak theinak dingah kumkhaw duhdawtnak thuhla relin net kharnak a nei.

Nikhat ni ahcun simcianak tivek pawl hi an tul nawn lo ding; tong pakhat lawng kan hmang ding ih, minung theihnak tlaksamnak ahcun Pathian theihnak thar um theinak ding hmunram a pe ding (1 Kor. 13:12). Thlarau thilpek pawl cu a tumtah mi hrangih a famkim thluh hnu ahcun an hloral thluh ding (1 Kor. 13:10). “Asinan duhdawtnak vu kumkhua in a um ding!” (1 Kor. 13:8, NLT).

Cuvek ciah in Khrih a ratsal tikah zumnak cun hmuhtheinak hmun a tuah ding (2 Kor. 5:7), cule caan reizet kan rak ruahsanmi cu a ngaingai an si thlang ding tinak a si (Rom. 8:24). Cule a thupi bik cu Duhdawtnak hi thumkom Pathian ziaza hminsinnak vekin a um kumkhua ding a si. Zumnak le ruahsannak khal kumkhua ih an um ve-nak ding san a um. Zumnak cu rundamnak tonnun vekin a um ding ih(Rom. 4:3), ruahsannak cu leithar ih lungawinak le theihnak thar neih ding duhnak le beiseinak vekin, tlenmi pawl ih tonun cu kumkhua hrangih hminsinnak a si ding. Asinan duhdawtnak, Pathian duhdawtnak cun kumkhua in a neh ding.

A rei hlanah hmaiton rori in kan Bawipa kan hmu ding (1 Kor. 13:12). Cuini a thlen hlan sung kan nun cu himi thil pathum : zumnak, ruahsannak le duhdawtnak hmangin kan relfiang ding a si. Himi cu Thlarau hmangin Khristian nun famkim ai-awh tu a si. Curuangah Khristian biaknak ahhin rel kirsal rero mi a si (Rom. 5:1–5; Gal. 5:5, 6; Efesa. 1:15, 18; 4:1–5). Ziangkhal sehla, Duhdawtnak cu a tumbik mi a si; A ngaingai te cun Pathian sinak relnak ih hman theih mi a si (1 Johan 4:8).

`“Pathian cu duhdawtnak a si” timi thu kha ruat lawk aw. Cui thil ih sullam ngaingai ziangtin saw kan theihfiang thei ding? Cui ruahnak cu a hrek hrawng lawng kan thei a si hmanah ziangah cui thil cu kan hrangah thuthangtha a si thiam?`
