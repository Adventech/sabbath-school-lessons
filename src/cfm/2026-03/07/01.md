### Tuizarh zirlai hrang siar ding
1 Korin 13; Matt. 24:12; Gal. 5:22, 23; 1 Tim. 1:14; 1 Johan 4:8.

> <p>Bible cangken</p>
> “Cun tu ah zumnak, ruahsannak, duhdawtnak, thil pathum an um; asinain hi pawl lakih tumbik cu duhdawtnak a si” (1 Korin 13:13, NIV).

Duhdawtnak cun ziangkim a neh thluh. Paul in nasa zetih a rak relnak san khal a si. _Agapaō_ timi tongkam – Thukham thar sungih Grik tongfang hman lar zet mi cu duhdawtnak relnak ih hman a si ih, a cakuat ahcun voi 135 tlun lai a um. Himi cun thukham thar sungih thuhla ummi pawl hmuahhmuah lak ihsin a hrek zik hrawng a huap thluh. Himi in Korin kawhhran hnen ih Paul ih cakuat mi a laimu bik ziangsimaw in sim a si.

Thukham thar ahhin duhdawtnak thuhla nganmi hminsin tlak zet tampi a um – cubang pawl cu Rom 8:35–39, 1 Korin 2:9, 1 Korin 8:3, Galati 2:20, Kolossians 1:13, 1 Thessalonika 3:12 pawl tla an si.

Asinan ziangdang hman in 1 Korin 13 hi an tahthim thei lo.

Kan dungzarh ahkhan duhdawtnak tel lo cun thlarau thilpek khal tel in, thil ziangkim hi zianghman lo men an si ti kan hmu. Tuizarh ahcun 1Korin 13 hi thuk sin in kan zir dingih, duhdawtnak phuangsuahdan mangbangza kan zoh ding.

Kan hmuh ding vekin Duhdawtnak hi thinlung khawruahnak lam a si tuk lem lo ih, tongkam le tuahnak hmangih hringnun tarlangnak lungput lam sawn hi a si; cuvek asilo cun sullam zianghman a nei zik lo.

Duhdawtnak cu ziang saw a si, ziangmi ciah saw a si timi cu Jesuh nun ahkhan famkim zetin tarlang a si.
