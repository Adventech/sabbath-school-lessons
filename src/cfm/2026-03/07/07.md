Ellen G. White, “The Need of Love,” pp. 545, 546, Review and Herald, August 28, 1888.

“Ziangvekin dinhmun sang khal sehla, Pathian le a minung pi duhdawtnak in a thinlung a luahkhat lomi cu Khrih ih dungthluntu diktak a si thei lo. Zumnak nasa zet nei in, mangbangza tuah theinak huham khal nei khal sehla, duhdawtnak tel lo cun a zumnak cu santlai lo men a si. Thilpek nasa zet a langter thei ding, asinan duhdawtnak ngaingai tel lo in cangvaihnak nei in a thil neihmi pawl hmuahhmuah zuarin mifarah cawm khal sehla, a cangvaihnak cu Pathian lung a awi ter thei cuang lo. Biaknak lam ih a pumpek awk tuk ruangah sarthih tiang a tong men thei, asinan duhdawtnak hmangin cangvaihnak a si lo ahcun Pathian cun mibum hmang, asilole tumsang nei miti awter vekah a ruat men ding a si”—Ellen G. White, The Acts of the Apostles, pp. 318, 319.

“Sermon ding tamzet kan nei. Thil a tul bik mi cu… a hloral cuahco mi hrangih duhdawtnak neih hi a si. Cui duhdawtnak cu Pathian Bawi tohkham ihsin a ra mi a si. Khristiannak diktak cun duhdawtnak cu hmunkip ah a thehdarh a si. Peng tin a fangkual, tluak, thinlung, bomnak kut, kee pawl a run tham thluh, cutin minung cu Pathian in ding hnget dingih a tinak hmunah an ding thei a si. Cuticun an ke hrangah lamzin a kelkawi lo ding ih, cuti lo cun lamzin ah tuahmawh in a um pang ding. Hloral dingmi pawl hrang Khrih ih duhdawtnak meisa alh cu Khristian biaknak ih umtu pawl hmuahhmuah ih hringnun a si.”—Ellen G. White, Lift Him Up, p. 134.

“Khrih thinlung ihsin a luangsuak mi duhdawtnak lawngin mi a damter thei. Cui duhdawtnak cu thinlung sungih hnai le taksa sungih thisen luangsuak vekih a luangsuak tu lawngin hliam ih um thlarau a tuah tha theisal a si”—Ellen G. White, Education, p. 114.

**Relkhawm ding thusuhnak pawl:**

`Paul in duhdawtnak lam ih thiltha a run porhsuak mi pawl hi a kim thluh in na thei maw? na thei lo asi ahcun ziangvek saw bet ding na neih?`

`Paul in “duhdawtnak duh uh” (1 Kor. 14:1, NKJV) tiih a thupek mi ih a relduhsan hi ziang a si ding tin na ruat?Himi cu 1 Korin 13:4-7 ih a rel mi thawn ziangsaw pehzom awknak an neih?`

`Nitin na nun lamzin ahhin duhdawtnak ziaza pawl lakah ziangsaw na tul bik mi? Khuimi duhdawtnak biksaw tualsung kawhhran ih a tul bik mi?Ziangruangah saw Paul in duhdawtnak cu thuphuan theinak, tong dang ih tong theinak le theihnak vekih thilpek thawn a tahthim (1 Kor. 13:8)?`

`Paul cun Korin khua ih member pawl lakah pumkhat awk lonak relfeldan cu duhdawtnak hi a si thu ziang ruangah a rel? Himi hi tuisan kan kawhhran ah ziangtin kan hmang ding?`
