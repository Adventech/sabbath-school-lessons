---
title:  Jesuh tarlangnak
date:   12/08/2026
---

1 Korin 13:4-7 kan siar tikah duhvek in duhdawtnak ih sullam ngaingai kan phorhsuak thei lo ti kan thei ngah ih kan riah a sia zet. Paul cun 1 Korin 13 a ngan ahhin a ruahnak ah Jesuh ziaza hi a ruat a si ko ding. A ngaingai te cun, cuvek duhdawtnak ziaza cu Khrih lawngin famkim zetin a tarlang thei ding. Curuangah a netabik ah Paul cun Duhdawtnak cu Jesuh ziaza ah a tarlang.

`Siar ding: Johan 13:1, 34; Johan 15:9, 12; 1 Timote 1:14; 2 Timote 1:7, 13; 1 Johan 3:16; le 1 Johan 4:7–12, 19–21. Himi thu ummi pawl ihsin duhdawtnak thuhla ziang kan zir thei ding?`

Pathian cu duhdawtnak a si (1 Johan 4:8). Pathian cun in duhdawt tuk ruangah A fapa neihsun in pe (Johan 3:16). Jesuh cu himi duhdawtnak phuangsuahnak famkim a si (Heb. 1:3). Duhdawtnak hi amah le mah ziangtin a tarlang aw timi theih kan duh ahcun Jesuh hi reizet kan zoh a tul. Thukham Thar ih Jesuh a tarlangdan hi fiangzet in kan zoh asile, 1 Korin 13 ih um duhdawtnak ih a thatnak pawl hmuahhmuah hi Amah ah hmuh a theih timi kan theifiang ding.

Jesuh cu thinsau zet a si. “Sikhalsehla Khrih Jesuh in a thinsaunak [_makrothymia_] ka parah a langter theinak dingah a zaangfahnak cu ka parah a langter. Keimah hi misual sungih misual bik ka si” (1 Tim. 1:16, NIV).

Jesuh cu a hngil nei. Bible cun “Bawipa cu a ngilnei” tiah a ti (1 Pet. 2:3, LEB). Himi thuhla ih “Bawipa” timi tongfang cu Jesuh khihhmuhnak a si. “Ngilnei” timi cu Grik tong _chrēstos_ timi ihsin a ra mi a si ih, cumi cu 1 Korin 13:4 ih um _chrēsteuomai_ (ngilhneihnak langternak)timi ihsin a ratnak bulhram an bangaw a si.

Jesuh cu thudik ah a lungawi. Jesuh ih lungawinak tonnun cu A pa ih duhnak thlun le Amah duhdawtnak neih hi a si (Johan 15:9–11, Johan 17:12–14).

Jesuh cun ziangkim a tuar thei. Hebru 12:2,3 ahcun Jesuh cu “thinglamtah ah a tuar … misual pawl ih cutituk dokalhnak a tuar tento” (NKJV). Jesuh vekih tuar zohman an um lo (Filipi. 2:8). A hmai ih retmi lungawinak ruangah a tuah mi a si!

Jesuh in ziangkim a zum. Annias in Pual ih nun thlengawknak thuhla ih thusuhnak a tuah tikah(Tirh 9:13,14), Jesuh cun, “Anih cu ka hrilmi kan hmanrua a si” tiah a let (Tirh 9:15, ESV). Jesuh cun mipi pawl hi an sinak vek siloin, Amai huham hmangin an hung si dingmi a rak zoh a si.

`Duhdawtnak diktak ziang a si timi kan hnen ih Jesuh ih a tarlangmi a dang lamzin ziangsaw um?`
