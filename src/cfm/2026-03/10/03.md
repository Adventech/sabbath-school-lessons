---
title:  Tuarnak le sunlawinak
date:   31/08/2026
---

`Siar ding: 2 Korin 4:7–18. Paul ih a tuarmi pawl kha a sangsang ten vun ngan aw. Ziangtin saw a harsatnak pawl hi a tuarsuak thei?`

John Huss, Bohemia ih remthatu ropi zet cun Jesuh thuhla a rak sim dah, “Anih cu leitlun ih Bawi a si, kannih cu thih theinak neitu men kan si – cumi cingin nasa zetin harsatnak a tuar! Ziangruangah saw kannih in tuar loin, kan thianghlim theinak dingah anih ih a rak tuar?”—Ellen G. White, The Great Controversy, p. 105.

Paul cun Khrih hrangih tuar a huamnak thuhla hi kum zabi hmaisa lam ahkhan a tarlang zo. Anih cu lei ih tuah mi, a kek thei leibeel vek fang a si ti a thei thiam aw (2 Kor. 4:7). Anih cun harsatnak, mangbangnak, hremnak, hlonthlaknak a tuar, asinan siatsuah cun a um lo, beidong in a um lo, tansan in a um lo, siatbal in a um lo (2 Kor. 4:8, 9). “Jesuh nun a neih theinak dingah Jesuh thihnak taksa” (2 Kor. 4:10, 11, ESV) phur vivo in, a nun ih tarlang ding a duh a si.

“Jesuh ih thihnak” timi a rel tikah Paul cun cang hmaisa lam ih harsat tuarnak kha a sim duhnak san a si ko ding. Khat lam ah “Jesuh nunnak” timi cun thihnak ihsin runsuahnak le a tui nunnak hrangih thlarau lam huham a khihhmuh in a lang. Asinan, a netnak ahcun himi cu thawhsalnak relnak a si (2 Kor. 4:12).

Lunglutza thil cu “ thihnak le nunnak” timi hi 2 Korin 4:10-12 ah voithum rori a lang. Himi cun a tuisan ahhin nunnak cu thihnak thawn an ngerhaw kual a si timi in theihter. Asinan hmailam ropitnak ahcun thihnak um lonak ah nunnak tonnun kan nei ding a si (Thup. 20:14, Thup. 21:4).

A thupi bik cu 2 Korin 4:7-18 cun sunlawinak cu Pathian hnen lawngih a feh theinak dingah minung cak lo zet hmangin thuthangtha phuang a si timi a tarlang (2 Kor. 4:15). Missionary hnatuantu pawl cun an hnatuan sungih an tuarmi harsatnak pawl hi a mal lo zet. Asinan hitawk ih kan harsatnak pawl cu in rak hngaktu kumkhaw ropitnak thawn tahthim cun zianghman lo men a si (2 Kor. 4:17). Zumtu pawl cu hmuhtheih thil ah siloin zumnak thawngin an nung a si (2 Kor. 4:18, 2 Kor. 5:7).

Himi hmailam tikcu caan hrangih ruahsannak cun Paul ih thinlung nasa zetin a la ih, thucah a rel mi pawl khalah a rel rero (2 Kor. 5:1–10). A taksa thithei cu lei ih inn tahthimnak hmangin a rel. Cumi thawn kalh aw ciah in, “Pathian ihsin sak mi” cu thosal taksa tahthimnak hmangin a rel (2 Kor. 5:1), Cui thil cu zumtu pawl hmuahmuah hrangih ruahsannak tum zet a si.

`Ziangruangah saw ziangvek dinhmun ih kan um khalah thawhsalnak beiseinak, kan thawhsalnak, kan hmai ih a ummi kan theih ringring ding a thupit?`
