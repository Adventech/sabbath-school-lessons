Ellen G. White, “The Message Heeded,” pp. 323–334, in The Acts of the Apostles.

Kan dung zarh ahkhan a tlun ih cabu tirhthlah pawl ih cangvaihnak timi sungta kan siar zo. Siar kirsal dingkhawp ih manhla a si. A tu ahcun a fek zetmi Paul ih cakuat thuhla kha malten kan vun zohsal ih, cule ca a ngan tikih a thinlung put le sualsir ngaingainak thuthangtha lungawi ten a rak dondan pawl kha vun ruat sal aw. Himi in Paul ih hnatuannak a dikzia thuhla ziangsaw in zirh, cule Khrih hrangih kan hnatuannak ah ziangmi zirlai saw kan lak thei ve ding?

“Leitlun pumhuap ah, sual ih a tlu mi le sual ih a tlu lomi leitlun khalah Pathian thawn sual ngaidamnak a um, cui Pathian ih duhdawtnak cun Pathian thawn rualrem aw sal dingin in tuah a si timi hi kan tarlang thluh ding. Minung cu a sual a sir, a thinlungah sirawknak a nei ih, raithawinak ih a pekawknak ah Khrih a zum a si, cule Pathian cu amah thawn rualrem awknak a rak si timi a theifiang”—Ellen G. White, Special Testimonies On Education, p. 223.

“Kawhhran kan si vekin, eng ropizet kan dong. Himi eng cu leitlun ah thlawsuahnak le hlawknak a um theinak dingah Pathian in rinsannak thawn kan hnen ih a pek mi a si. Kan hnenah rualrem awknak hnatuannak hrangih in pek zo mi a si. Tlun lam ih huham ummi thawn Pathian thawn rualrem aw dingin minung pawl hnenah thu kan sim thei ding a si”—Ellen G. White, Letter 32, 1903.

Pathian thawn voikhat remawknak a um cun minung pawl cun thianghlimnak an hawl ding a si. 2 Korin 7:1 ih thuhla ummi simfiang in Ellen G. White cun Paul in, “Pathian tihzahnak ah a famkim mi thianghlimnak” (2 Kor. 7:1, NKJV) tiih a rel duhmi cu a run simfiang. Paul cun piangthar pekte pawl cu, “mahte thum awk theinak nei, Khristian thangso, zumnak ih cak, taima zet, thinlung hmuahhmuah in Pathian lam ih pumpek awknak le A Lalram thansoh theinak dingih hnatuan” dingin a forhfial tin a rel.”—Ellen G. White, The Acts of the Apostles, p. 201.

**Relkhawm ding thusuhnak pawl:**

`Paul cun kan hnenah “leibeel” cu thuthangtha hlawhthil umnak tin a rel (2 Kor. 4:7, ESV). Minung dinhmun cu a tawntai, kekdarh thei ih a khatmi a si ruangah ziangtin thuthangtha phuangsuaknak siatsuah loin a thangso thei ding?`

`“Sersiam thar” (2 Kor. 5:17, NKJV) timi sullam hi ziangsaw a si? Himi cun kan nitin nun ah ziangtin a tel? Ziangtin saw Pathian in sersiamthar ih a lo tuah?`

`2 Korin 6:4,5 ah Paul cun thuthangtha ruangih harsatnak an tonmi pawl a run ngan. A harsatnak tonmi pawl kha ziangtin a rak cohlang (zoh awla, 2 Kor. 6:6, 7)? Himi cun harsatnak na tuar caan ih na rak letdan ding ziangtin bomnak a lo pek?`

`Paul cun leitlun riahsiatnak le pathian lam ih riahsiatnak an bangawk lonak a vun khaikhin (2 Kor. 7:10). Ziangvek zawngin saw riahsiatnak hi sualsirnak thawn an pehtlaih awk? Leitlun riahsiatnak thawn kalh aw zetin Pathian lam ih riahsiatnak thuhla hi ziangtin na rel ding?`
