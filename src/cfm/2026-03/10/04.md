---
title:  Khrih ih hmuitin rualrem awk salnak hnatuan
date:   01/09/2026
---

`Siar ding: 2 Korin 5:11–15. Ziangtin saw himi thu cun Paul ih hnatuannak ah Khrih cu a laimu bik ih a tarlang?`

Paul cun Khrih hnenah a hnatuannak thuhla pawl a rel ding timi a theifiang (2 Kor. 5:10). Anih cun “Bawipa tih” timi a theithiam ih, Khrih ih thuthangtha khal mipi ih pawm dingin a zuam rero (2 Kor. 5:11, ESV). Himi “tih” timi cu Khrih tihzah upat le sunlawinak pe tinak a si ih, curuangah Paul in Khrih a duhdawtnak le Khrih in amah a duhdawtnak ve timi a rinsannak thawn an pehzom aw a si. Thukham Hlun sungah Bawipa tih timi sullam cu A lamzin ih feh, Amah duhdawt, le na nunnak le thinlung hmuahhmuah thawn amah riantuan tinak a si (Daan. 10:12).

Paul ih Pathian hnatuannak cu mah le mah thupi ih ret awknak siloin Khrih sawn kha thupibik ih retnak a si. Amah ten a porh aw lo. A porh awknak san bik cu Khrih a si (2 Kor. 12:9). Hitin a ti, “asinan kan Bawipa Jesuh Khrih ih thinglamtah siar lo keimai lam ihsin zianghman suan ding ka nei lo” tiah a ti (Gal. 6:14, ESV). Curuangah Korin khawmi pawl in a thuhla an suan theinak ding caan remcang (2 Kor. 5:12) timi sullam cu a hnatuannak ah Khrih cu a thupi bik ih a retnak cu suan tlak rori a si timi hi a si. Amah dokalh tu pawl cun anmah le mah thupi ah ret aw in hna an tuan a si.

`Siar ding: 2 Korin 5:16–21, Kolosa 1:19–23, le Efesa 2:13–16. Paul in “rualrem awksalnak ih hnatuannak” a timi sullam hi ziangsaw a si?`

Khrih cu rualrem awknak hnatuantu thabik a si. Cuvekin Anih cun, “remawk salnak tuan ding hna in pe” (2 Kor. 5:18, NKJV). Rualrem awk salnak timi hi 2 Korin 5:16-21 hmuahhmuah ahhin a rung langsal rero. Himi cu Paul hrangah a thupi tuk, kan hrang khalah a thupi tuk thotho.

Pathian cun remawknak ih A Fapa thihnak hmangin minung pawl cu Amah thawn an rualrem aw zo. Pathian thawn rualrem awknak a neizo tu pawl cu sersiamthar an si (2 Kor. 5:17). Tu ahcun Khrih ih thuthangtha phuangnak thawn in “rualremnak thucah” timi hi an fehpi ding mi a si (2 Kor. 5:19, ESV). Himi ahhin, “Pathian in kanmah hmangih a lo dil hai bangin, kannih hi Khrih hrangih palai kan si (2 Kor. 5:20, NKJV).

`Khrih in na hrang a tuahzo mi pawl vun ruat aw. Na diklonak, na sualnak, mawhnak pawl cu nangmai ta ding si khal sehla, na hrang thinglamtah ih a lo tuahsak mi ziang a si tin vun ruat aw. Himi thil um ngaingai mi pawl cun midang pawl thawn, a hleice in Bawipa thei hrih lo pawl pehtlaih awknak ah ziangtin hmual a nei thei ding?`
