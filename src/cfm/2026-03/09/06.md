---
title:  Khrih ah nehnak
date:   27/08/2026
---

`Siar ding: 2 Korin 2:12, 13. Paul in an hnen ih “a fek zetmi cakuat” a ngan hnuah khuitawk ahsaw a feh? Cutawk ahcun ziangsaw a tuah?`

Titas a hngah lai ah Paul ih thinlung cu thinharnak in a khat (2 Kor. 7:5, 6). Cuvek in lungnuam lo zetih a um lai khalah Jesuh thuhla rel a cawlhsan thei cuang lo (2 Kor. 2:12). Jesuh a duhdawt tuk lawmmam. Titas thawn hmuh awk ih, Korin khawmi pawl hnen ih a cakuat mi thuhla theih a cak zet.

Troas khua ah Paul ih hnatuannak cu a hlawhtling zet, asinan “cutawk ah reipi a cambang thei lo. ‘A thinlung cu kawhhran pawl ngaihsaknak, a hleice in Korin kawhhran ngaihsaknak in a khat thluh. Troas khua ah Titas thawn tongaw in, Korin khawmi pawl hnen ih nunsimnak le thurawn peknak ca a kuat mi an rak dondan pawl a hnen ihsin zir ngah ding a beisei zet, asinan himi ahcun a riahsia. ‘Ka unau Titas ka hmuh lo ruangah ka thlarau lamah colhhahdamnak ka nei lo’ tin a hmuhton mi a ngan. Curuangah Troas a suah san ih, Mesidonia lam pan in a feh ih, Filipi ah Timote thawn an tong aw ngah”—Ellen G. White, The Acts of the Apostles, p. 323.

`Siar ding: 2 Korin 2:14–17. Mesidonia ram ih Titas a hmuh le Korin khawmi pawl hnen ihsin thu lungawi um a theih tikah Paul in ziangtin a let?`

Lungawi zetin Paul cun Pathian hin “Khrih ah nehnak in pe ringring” (2 Kor. 2:14, NKJV) timi a nemhnget. Mangbangza thu ngan mi a si! Khrih umnak ih a khat mi lungthin cun “hmunkip ah Amah theihnak rimthaw” cu a thehdarh theu (2 Kor. 2:14, NKJV).

Paul cu Khrih ah a lungawi zet, ziangahtile a fekzet mi cakuat cun a tumtah mi rah a khawm ngah thei a si (2 Kor. 7:5–9). Himi cu nehnak ropi zet a si. Himi ahhin Paul cun 2 Korin 2:17 ah Khrih ih tirhthlah a sinak diktak a nemhnget a si (2 Kor. 2:17, 2 Kor. 1:12). Himi ih thu umdan vek asile Khrih ih siahhlawh rinum zet le Pathian hnatuantu deu ih bangawk lonak cu a neta sawn hi mai hlawknak hawl ih thuthangtha phuangtu a si ih, a hmaisa tu hi thinlung hmuahhmuah ih Jesuh duhdawtnak thawn Pathian thu phuangtu a si.

`Na tuahmi pawl hmuahhmuah ah, a hleice in Jesuh hmin ih na tuah mi ah ziangmi in saw phurnak lo pe?`
