Ellen G. White, “The Message Heeded,” pp. 323–334, in The Acts of the Apostles.

“Riahsiatnak nasa zet a tuartu pawl hi midang pawl hnenah nasa zetih hnemnak run kengtu pawl an si theu, an fehnak hmunkip ah tleunak an keng. Cuvek mi pawl cu an harsat tuarnak in nasa zetin a nen ih, buainak nasa zet an parih a thlen hman ah Pathian ah rinsannak an neih mi cu an hloh cuang lo, asinan Duhdawtnak ih a kilhimnak ah an bet hnget deuhdeuh. Cuvek mi pawl cu Pathian in ngilnei zetih a ngaihsak zia tarlangnak nung an si.”—Ellen G. White, God’s Amazing Grace, p. 122.

“Khristian nun thianghlim cun ziangtik lai khalah daihnak, hnemnak le eng a pe suak ringring a si. Cumi cu thianghlimnak, thudik, tluangtlam le hmantlaknak ziaza ihsin a suakmi a si. Cumi cu a thianghlim mi tanghmai sial lo duhdawtnak cun a thunun a si. Cumi cu Khrih ah a khat thluh ih, a neitu in a fehnak piangah eng cu a tanta ringring a si.”—God’s Amazing Grace, p. 122.

“Paul cun kawhhran ih diklonak nunsim a tulzia cu a hmu ngah, asinan cumi a remthat ruangah a thunun awk theinak a hloh lo. A cangvaihnak sullam pawl a simfiang. Thildik lo a tuahtu pawl hnenah rualpi vekih lang dingin nasa zetin hna a tuan. Anmah vek ciah in an lunglut thil pawl ah a lunglut ve tuk timi an theih dingin a tan ta hai”—Ellen G. White Comments, in The SDA Bible Commentary, vol. 6, p. 1094.

**Relkhawm ding thusuhnak pawl:**

`2 Korin 2:1-14 ah Paul cun Pathian hnatuannak ih a diknak a nemhnget. Ziangruangah saw Pathian hnatuannak ahhin diknak hi a thupit?`

`Paul in khualtlawnnak ding khawkhannak a thlengnak ahhin Khristian hnatuannak ih tuahrem awkdan thiam a tulzia ziangsaw in sim? Ziangruangah saw a tul tik caan ih thleng danglamnak a um hi a thupit?`

`Paul cun a hnatuannak ah thinbangnak le thinharnak a tong. Himi cun khawhhran upa pawl cu midang vek thotho in thinbangnak a tong thei minung an si timi a langfiangter. An hnatuan a olsam thei deuhnak dingah kawhhran member in ziangsaw an tuah thei ding?`

`Paul cun Khrih ah a nehnak (2 Kor. 2:14)a rel hlan te ah a thinlung a cawlh hahdam thei lonak san a rel (2 Kor. 2:13). Ziangtin saw a cah lonak le cahnak hi a rel kop thei? Kannih teh kan rel kop thei pei maw?`
