---
title:  Tluangtlamnak le diknak
date:   24/08/2026
---

Mani ahkhan Paul in Korin khawmi pawl a duhdawt zia cu an harsatnak ah Pathian hnen ihsin hnangamnak a don vek ciah in, a hnemnak ah a langsuak a si timi kan zir ngah zo (1 Kor. 1:1–11). Tuini ahcun anmah a duhdawtnak cu amah le a hnatuanpi pawl in Korin khua ih kawhhran member pawl hnen ih rinumnak an langternak ihsin tarlang in a um timi kan hmu ding.

`Siar ding: 2 Korin 1:12–14 hi 2 Korin 2:17 le 2 Korin 4:2 thlirnak hnuai ah siar aw. Ziangtin saw Paul in thinlung takten Korin khawmi pawl a duhdawt zia a tarlang?`

2 Korin 1:12-14 ahhin Paul cun a cakuat pawl hmuahhmuah ih a thansohpi dingmi simfiangnak a run rellang. A diknak le tirhthlah a sinak ahhin Korin khawmi hrekkhat pawl cun thusuhnak an nei. Paul in zianghman thuhla theihfelnak a nei lo tin an ruat, cumi cu tirhthlah hnatuan dingih a tlinlonak ding a si. Cumi letin Paul in amah le a hnatuanpi pawl cun an parah dikzetin (rinum zet) an um a si timi a rel uar zet.

Tangkam pahnih cun Paul le a hnatuanpi pawl ih nuncan ziaza a run tarlang: tluantlamnak le diknak (2 Kor. 1:12). “Diknak” timi cu Grik tong _haplotēs_ timi ihsin a ra suak mi a si. Cumi cu thusim mi asilole nuncan ziaza lam ih bulpak dikzia tarlangnak ih hman a si theu; a tawizawng cun cangvaihnak thianghlim zet a tarlang a si (Efesa. 6:5, Kol. 3:22). Khatlam ah “diknak”( Grik tong ih, _eilikrineia_ a si) timi cu cangvaihnak ih thianghlim le rinumnak timi khal a simduhnak a si thotho.

Paul ih a tumtah mi fiangzet hi Korin khawmi pawl hin rak ringhlel ding an si lo. A tluangtlamnak le diknak ih bulhram cu Pathian hnen ihsin a suak timi fiangzetin a rel. Himi ruahdan hi New English Translation Bible cun fiangzetin a rak rel, “diknak le cangvaihnak thianghlim cu Pathian hnen ihsin a ra mi an si” (2 Korin 1:12). Cumi bungcang thotho ah Paul cun himi hnatuannak ziaza pawl hi “ Pathian zangfahnak hmang” ih in pek thu a rel kirsal.

A hlan ih biangbiak awknak cangan mi ahkhan Paul dokalhtu pawl in a thurel mi cu dik lo zetin an simfiang timi a fiang zet (2 Kor. 1:13, 14). Paul cun a tumtah mi thil cu a fiangtuk ih, theihtiam olzet mi an si ding timi a fiang zet. A tumtahmi, a tongkam le a cangvaihnak pawl cu “Bawipa Jesuh a rungtum ni” ah theifiang ter thluh a si ding ti a theifiang. (2 Korin. 1:14, NKJV).

`Tumtah mi, lole cangvaihnak, lole sullam nei zet le dikzet an va si hmanah nangmah rori in thusuhnak lole harsatnak na tonmi pawl hi ziangsaw a si? Himi thu in midang ih cangvaihnak ih thusuhnak a um tikah ziangtluk kan ralrin a tul timi hi ziangsaw in sim?`
