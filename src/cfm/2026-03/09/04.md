---
title:  Duhdawtnak hrang khawkhannak thleng
date:   25/08/2026
---

Korin ih um mi hrekkhat pawl cun Paul ih tumtah mi le duhdawtnak hi an ringhlel deuh nawn timi kan hmu thei. Tuini ahcun a san le vang ih a langmi thil pakhat kan zoh ding: Cumi cu a khualtlawndan ding khawkhannak a thleng hi a si (2 Kor. 1:15–2:4).

`Siar ding: 1 Korin 16:5-7. Paul ih a khualtlawn tumdan kha ziangsaw a si?`

Paul hi Korin Khua ah a rak um dah zo. 1 Korin 16:5,6 ih a langdan vek asile Korin khua a feh phah ah Mesidonia ram a pal phah tum a si. Cutin thlatang ah Korin khua ah a caambang men thei. Cutin Korin khua ihsin Jerusalem ih um mi rethei pawl hrangah Mesidonia ram ah thawhhlawm khawng khawm in Judea ram tiang a feh ding. Asinan Korin khua ihsin Timote ih thuthan mi a that tuk lo ruangah a tumtah dan pawl a thleng a si (1 Kor. 4:17, 1 Kor. 16:10, 2 Kor. 1:1).

Paul cun Efesa ihsin Korin ih feh lan zurzo dingin tumtahnak a nei, cutawk ahcun Timote ih a run thlenmi thubuai cu relfel a tum a si. A khualtlawn tumdan khawkhannak thar cu Efesa – Korin – Masidonia – Korin – Judea tin a si thlang (2 Kor. 1:15, 16). Efesa ihsin Korin ah feh dingin a pok suak, asinan Efesa ah a kir leh sal. A khawkhandan pawl a thleng. A khawkhan vekin Korin ah a feh thei lo, a silobik ah thil a feh dik thei lo a si bik. Cuticun Efesa ah a kirsal ih, an hrangah ca a ngan sawn a si. Hmundang va tlawn ih thiltha lo zet um ding hnakin cakuat men hi a duh sawn a si (2 Kor. 2:1, 3).

Paul in a va tlawn netabik tum ih a tumtah mi ah theihsual awknak a um. Korin khua ih mi hrekkhat pawl cun anmah duhdawt tawk lo, le thumawknak ngaingai tlak lo vekin an rel (2 Kor. 1:17). An puhnak pawl letin Paul cun Korin mi pawl cu Khrih ih thuthangtha lamah an a hoipi hai. Pathian in Khrih hmangin an hnen ih a thutiam cu rinum zetih a famkimter vek ciah in caantha a um veten Korin khua tlawng dingih a tumtah mi ahcun a rinum zet a si (2 Kor. 1:18–22).

“Ziangahtile amah ih um Pathian thutiam hmuahhmuah cu, a si, an si ih, amah ahcun, Amen, a um, kanmah ihsin Pathian a sunlawinak dingah” (2 Kor. 1:20, NKJV).

Cuticun a thuletnak cu an relvek ciah in, a dinhmun ih zirin “a si” asilole “asilo” tivek lam ih thu buaithlak zet siloin, “Khrih sungih Pathian hnatuan cu “a si ringring” a si vek ciah in “a si ringring” a si (2 Kor. 1:19, ESV).

Curuangah Paul in Korin khawmi pawl va tlawng loih, ca a kuat sawnnak cu a duhdawt ngaingai ruang sawn ah a si (2 Kor. 2:4). Natnak tuar hnu ih a va tlawnsal ahcun lungawinak a thlentum mi cu natnak nasa deuhdeuh ah a cang thei a si (2 Kor. 1:24, 2 Kor. 2:3). A tumtah mi thazet cu simfiang sual a olsam zet a si.
