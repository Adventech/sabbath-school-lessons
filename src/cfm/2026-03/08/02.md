---
title:  Khrih thawhsalnak phuangin
date:   16/08/2026
---

Paul cun 1 Korin 15 cu thuthangtha hmuitin in hram a run thok. Thuthangtha thuhla a rel: (1) Korin khawmi pawl hnen ih a rak sim zo mi; (2) an rak don zo mi; (3) an rak dinnak le (4) rundam an si zonak pawl an si (1 Kor. 15:1, 2, NKJV). Hi thuhla ih onnak a runtuah tikah ca siartu pawl hnenah bung neta ih a ra dingmi thuhla hrangah timlamnak a tuah sak ih, kan rundamnak ding hrangih Khrih thawhsalnak a thupit zia khal a run tarlang a si (zoh awla, Rom. 10:9, 10). A thawhsalnak hi thuthangtha thucah ih a thupi zet mi peng pakhat a si ih, cumi rak dokalh cu dunglam Khrih ih zumnak dokalhnak a si.

`Siar ding: 1 Korin 15:1-4; Luka 24:44–47; le Rom 1:1–4. Himi thu pawl ih an bangaw deuhnak pawl kha ziangsaw a si?`

1 Korin 15:1-4 ah Paul ih thucah khaikhawmnak hmuh a theih ko ding. “Cathianghlim vekin” timi tongkam cun Thukham Hlun ih thungan mi a khihhmuh maw, asilole Thukham Hlun pumpi a khihhmuh ti hi a thupi lem lo. Jesuh ih thihnak le thawhsalnak cun Thukham Hlun sungih hmuhtheih mi Pathian thutiam a famkim ter a si.

`Siar ding: 1 Korin 15:2,11. Ziangahsaw himi bungcang ahhin thusim le zumnak hi an rel kop? Himi pahnih karlak laicin awknak cu ziangsaw a si?`

Khrih a thosal tiih a phuangtu pawl hmuahhmuah cun a hmaisabik ah A thawhsalnak cu san thuanthu ngaingai ih thil thlengmi a si timi a zum ta a tul. Himi thu ahhin 1 Korin 15:5-8 cun Thukham Thar ahhin a thupi zetmi dinhmun a luahkhat a si. Himi thu ahcun Khrih a thawhsal hnuah mi tampi in an hmuh thu cathianghlim cun fiangzetin a nemhnget ih, Paul in himi ca a ngan lai hmanah mi tampi cu nungdam in an um lai a si (1 Kor. 15:6).

Tluangtlam zetin Paul cun feh ula, an thil hmuhmi pawl cu nanmah rori in sut uh tiah a ti. Cuvek rori in a ngaingai ih Khrih ih thawhsalnak hi a zum hnget a si.

Hi mipi pawl cu mit ih hmutu pawl an si. Jesuh cun an sidan ding vek ciah in a rak rel zo, “hi thu theihpitu pawl” an si (Luka 24:48).

`Khrih thawhsalnak hi ziangmi san le vang ruangah saw kan zum ding? Ziangmi thildang saw kanmah rori in hmu riai lo khal sehla, kan zum hnget emem mi?`
