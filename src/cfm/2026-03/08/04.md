---
title:  Khrih, rahhmaisabik
date:   18/08/2026
---

Jesuh hi a nung lo a si ahcun hmailam thuhla ih ruahsannak ziangvek khal hi ruahnak sisual an si thluh ding (1 Kor. 15:12–19). “Asinan a ngaingai in Khrih cu thihnak ihsin a thosal zo” (1 Kor. 15:20, ESV). A thawhsalnak thu hi a ngaingai ih thil a thleng mi a si. Curuangah Khrih ih a thi mi zokhal A ratsal tikah an thosal ding a si timi kan fiang a si (1 Kor. 15:20–23).

`Siar ding: 1 Korin 15:20–23. Jesuh cu “rah hmaisabik” a si timi sullam hi ziangsaw a si?`

Sual cemnak cu Khrih ah a thi mi pawl ih taksa thawhsalnak in hminsinnak a si ding (1 Kor. 15:24; Thup. 20:5, 6). Adam neta a si vekin Khrih cun hi leitlun ih ukawknak pawl cu a la kirsal ih, lalram cu Pa hnenah a pe kirsal (1 Kor. 15:25–28). Khrih cu Pathian hnenah a pe aw thluh (1 Kor. 15:28), himi ihsin Adam le Khrih ih laicin awknak cu theihthiam a theih ko ding. Tlennak khawkhannak ahcun Adam netabik a si vekin (1 Cor. 15:45), Jesuh cun Pa ih duhnak ah a pe pumhlum aw thluh, cui thil cu Adam hmaisa ih a rak tuah lo mi thil a si.

1 Korin 15:29-34 ah Paul cun Khrih thawhsalnak dokalh ih atthlak zia thuhla a run ruat sal. Tipilnak cu tahthimnak ah a run hmang, ziangahtile cumi cu zumtu pakhat relnak a si(Rom. 6:3, 4; Kol. 2:12); Thawhsalnak ngaingai rak dokalh hin sullam zianghman a nei lo. Ziangkhal sehla, theih ngah thiam ding harsa bik cu Paul in “mithi hrangah tipilnak” tiih a run rel mi hi a si (1 Kor. 15:29).

“Simfiangdan phunphun a um ko, asinan thawhsalnak ni ah an duhdawtzet mi mual liam cia pawl thawn pumkhat te ih an um tlan theinak dingah mi hrekkhat cun tipilnak nei dingin thutluknak an rak tuah tiih simfiangnak hi a tha bik ko ding. Himi thutluknak cu Khrih ih a thi zo mi pawl ih nun zohthim ih an rak tuah mi tla a si men thei. Himi buainak ahcun mipi pawl in mithi hrangah tipilnak an nei a si loih, mithi ruangah tipilnak an nei sawn a si”—Carl P. Cosaert, “1 Corinthians,” Andrews Bible Commentary: New Testament (Berrien Springs, MI: Andrews University Press, 2022), p. 1652.

Pahnihnak ah, thawhsalnak a um lo ahcun thihnak khamtlang ih din hin sullam a nei lem zik lo (1 Kor. 15:30–32). Thawhsalnak a um lo ahcun hi leitlun ih nomnak ahhin lungawinak a um sawn ding (1 Kor. 15:32).

`2 Timote 1:12 ih Paul ih tongkam kha vun ruat sal aw. Ziangtin saw hmailam caan a feng thei? Ziangtin kannih teh kan fiang thei ding?`
