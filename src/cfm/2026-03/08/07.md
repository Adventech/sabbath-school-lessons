Ellen G. White, “God’s People Delivered,” pp. 635–652, in The Great Controversy.

“Khrih ih Pathian sinak cu zumtu pawl hmuahhmuah ih kumkhaw nunnak ih amkhannak a si. Jesuh cun “Keimah i zumtu cu thi hmansehla a nung ding: Cun zokhal nung cung ih i zumtu cu a thi dah lo ding. Hihi na zum maw?” a ti. Hitawk ahcun Khrih cun a ratsalnak ding caan a rak thlir. Miding fel mithi pawl cu siatral thei lo dingin kaihthawh an si ding ih, nungdam ih um mi dingfel pawl cu thihnak zianghman tong lo in vanram ih lak an si ding. Khrih ih a tuah ding mangbangza, thihnak ihsin Lazarus kaithawhnak kha mi dingfel thi pawl hmuahhmuah ih thawhsalnak rak zohtertu a si.”—Ellen G. White, The Desire of Ages, p. 530.

“Pathian Fapa ih aw in mi thianghlim itthat zo pawl a kaihthoh lai ahcun leilung pi cu nasa zetin a hnin. Annih cun kawhnak cu an cohlang ih, thithei lo ropitnak sin phah in an rung suak ih, lungawi zetin, “Nehnak, nehnak, thihnak le thlaan thlun ah! Maw thihnak, na tur cu khuiah saw a um? Maw thlan, na nehnak cu khuiah saw a um’ (1 Kor. 15:55) tian an au ding. Cule Mi thianghlim nungdam pawl le kaihthawh mi pawl cun fehsuak in nehnak au thawm an suah. Thlaan sungih phummi taksa cun natnak hminsinnak a keng tel, cule thihnak cu thazet le hriselzet thithei nawn lo in a thosal ding. Mi thianghlim nungdam pawl cu reilote ah thleng danglam an si ih, mitthep karah kaihthawh mi thawn vanlam ah laksoh an si ding ih, boruak ah an Bawipa an hmu khawm ding. Oh, ziangtluk a sunglawi mi hmuhkhawm awknak a si ding! Thihnak ih a rak thenhran mi rualpi pawl thawn an tong khawm aw ding, ziangtik hmanah an then aw sal nawn lo ding”—Ellen G. White, The Story of Redemption, pp. 411, 412.

**Relkhawm ding thusuhnak pawl:**

`Khrih thawhsalnak a hmu ngahtu pawl ih thuhla kha vun ruat aw (Tirh 1:22, Tirh 2:32, Tirh 3:15, Tirh 4:33, Tirh 5:30–32). Ziangtin saw cui thil a thlen ihsin kum 2000 lai liamzo kannih hin A thawhsalnak theihpitu kan si ve thei ding?`

`Khrih ih thawhsalnak cu thuthangtha thucah ih a thupi zetmi peng pakhat a si (1 Kor. 15:1–4). Thawhsalnak um lo sehla, Khrih thihnak thu phuang rero mi cun sullam a nei zik lo (1 Kor. 15:14). Khrih ih thihnak khal hi a thupi zik lo. Ziang ruangah? Himi na letnak ahhin Khrih thawhsalnak ih thilti theinak thuhla ziangsaw a rel?`

`Paul ih thurel mi ngai nuam zet : “Mithi pawl an thawhsal lo ahcun…Ei uhsila in uhsi; ziangahtile thaisun ah kan thi ding a si” (1 Kor. 15:32, NKJV) timi hi vun ruat aw. Ziangsi a rel duhsan?`

`Nan zirnak ahkhan, thih hnu umdan rel uh. Thih veten rundammi pawl cu vanram ih an kai hngal cun ziangah 1 Korin 15 in sullam a nei lo?`
