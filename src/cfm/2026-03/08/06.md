---
title:  Thihnak tlunnih netabik nehnak
date:   20/08/2026
---

`Siar ding: 1 Korin 15:54-57. Himi bungcang cun thihnak kan nehnak ding netabik thuhla ziangsaw in sim?`

Paul in 1 Korin 15 ih catluan netabik cu lunglutza thu thawn a thok: “Tisa le thisen in Pathian uknak a luah thei lo ding” (1 Kor. 15:50, NKJV). Bible siartu tamzet pawl cun Paul in vanram ih a um lo ding thil a rel a si tiah an phuang. Asinan a thu ummi cun thildang in a run simfiang. 1 Korin 15:50 ih um “tisa le thisen” timi cu “siatral thei mi” timi thawn an bangaw ih, “Pathian uknak” timi cu siatral thei lo timi thawn an bangaw. 1 Korin 15:42-49 ih thil a thlengmi vek ciah in hitawk ah Paul cun a tui taksa (asilole mithi ruak)le thosal taksa ih danglamnak thu a rel. Phum mi taksa cu siatralnak le thih theinak ih hminsin a si ih, cuti rualin thosal taksa cu siatthei lo le thi thei lo ih hminsin a si (1 Kor. 15:50, 53, 54). Theihthiam olsam bik in, Paul cun kan taksa hin vanram a luah theinak dingah thleng danglamnak nasa zet a ton a tul a si tiah a sim.

Paul cun siatral thei le thithei timi ruahnak cu misual kan sinak relnak ah a hmang. Judah ih cangan mi ah “tisa le thisen” timi cu sual ih tlu zo minung relnak ih hman a si. Curuangah taksa hi A rat kirsal tikah thleng danglam le famkim lonak pawl hi thianghlimter a tul a si.

Misual kan sinak in hlonhlo sak lawngah (1 Kor. 15:54) le sunlawinak ton nun(1 Kor. 15:51–53, 1 Thess. 4:13–17) kan pahtlang hnuah “thihnak cu nehnak in a dolh thluh zo” (1 Kor. 15:54, NKJV) timi a famkim ding a si. Cule himi ralthatnak hla hi sak a si ding: “ ‘Maw thihnak, na tur cu khuiah saw a um? Maw thlan, na nehnak cu khuiah saw a um? (1 Kor. 15:55, NKJV). Himi cu veihnihnak Khirh a ratsal tikah a cangsuak ding a si (1 Kor. 15:51, 52).

Ruat awla: Thihnak ah kan mit kan sing, neta ih kan ton ding mi veihnih Jesuh ratsalnak, thihnak ihsin in kaihthawhnak tikcu caan a si hngal ding. Zumtu cu a thihnak kum thawng tampi liamzo khal sehla, “cuicaan ah mit thep kar ah” nunter an si ding. “ziangahtile tawtawrawt a awn ding ih, mithi pawl cu tawih thei lo taksa in thawhtersal an si ding, cule kannih cu danglamter in kan um ding (1 Kor. 15:52, NKJV).

`Nunnak ih in feh liampi cakzia thu ahhin zosaw riahsia lo ih um thei ding? Kanmai tonnun ah, Jesuh ih veihnih ratsalnak cu cuvek ih rang cu a si ko ding. A ratsalnak ih kan ruahnak ih um hmaisabikmi cu, “Aw, Bawipa, na ratsalnak ni a nai ngaingai a si hi!” timi a si men thei. Ziangtinsaw himi thuhla cun “a tlai” tiih kan hmuh mi theithiam deuh dingih in bawm?`
