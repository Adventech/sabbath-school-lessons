---
title:  Pumkhat awknak
date:   10/09/2026
---

Paul cun Korin kawhhran member pawl hnenah Judea ram ih um kawhhran rethei pawl hrangih thilpek khonkhawmnak ih tel ve dingih a forhfial thu kan hmu zo. A tumtah mi lakih pakhat cu pumkhat awknak a um ding hi a si. Anih cun Pathian hnatuannak ih an tel ve ding a duh hai. Zentel kawhhhran pawl khal Jerusalem ih um Judah zumtu pawl vekin Pathian innsang an si ve thotho timi a khihhmuh duh a si. Cumi pawl cu a hlan ahcun dokalhtu an rak si, a tu ahcun Pathian Thukham Thar ih a tangsung member an si ve thlang. Paul cun Khristian innsang pawl hmuahhmuah, Judah le Zentel pawl khalin thiltitheinak ah pumkhatten um in, san a ra um lai dingmi ah kawhhran ih theihpitu hna an tuantlang thei ding hi a hmu duh a si.

Titas le rinsan mi pahnih cun sumpai lamah tuanvo an nei. Pathian cun hi kawhhran ngaihsaknak hi Titas ih thinlung ah a ret (2 Kor. 8:16). Kawhhran hmangin Pathian cun unau midang pahnih khal a hril (2 Kor. 8:18–23). Annih cu “Kawhhran ih palai le Khrih ih sunlawinak” an si (2 Kor. 8:23, NKJV). “Khrih ih sunlawinak” timi hi unau rinum zet pahnih a relnak maw, asilole kawhhran a rel duhnak a si ding timi thu hi ruat buai tuk ding thil a si lem lo. Thilpek hi a netnak ahcun kawhhran Lu a si mi Khrih parih rinumnak hminsinnak a si (Efesa. 4:15).

2 Korin 8-9 ahhin thilpek cu Pathian in kawhhran hmangih a ruatmi pawl hnenah pek ding a si timi a tarlang. Himi cu “Kawhhran hmuahhmuah” (2 Kor. 8:18, NKJV), “Kawhhran hril mi” (2 Kor. 8:19, NKJV), le “kawhhran palai” (2 Kor. 8:23, NKJV) timi ih a khihhmuh mi a si. Curuangah a hnuai ih um forhfialnak, “Na duhdawtnak cu kawhhran pawl hmai ah langter aw” (2 Kor. 8:24, ESV) timi khal hi mangbang ding a um lo.

Pathian in leitlun ih a ret mi – Biakinn ih thilpek run kengnak hin – pumkhat awknak a umter thei ih, cutirual in pumkhat awknak sullam khal a suahter thei (2 Kor. 8:13, 14). Paisa cun pumkhat awknak ropi zet a suakter thei. Khatlam zawng ahcun Pathian sunlawinak ding hrang a si lo ahcun paisa cun thendarh awknak khal a tuah thei a si.

`Rom 15:26,27 ahhin ziangtin saw Paul in pumkhat awknak a duh zia a tarlang?`

A netnak ahcun Paul cun thawhkhawm mi cu hnatuannak asilole Pathian hnatuannak, zangfah cangvaihnak, thlawsuahnak, Pathian cibai buknak le pawlkom awknak tivek zawngin a simfiang. Hivek pawl hmuahhmuah hi thilpek ihsin an ra suak thluh thei pei maw? Vun ruat aw.

`Ziangtin saw kawhhran le hmunhla zet ih Pathian hnatuannak ding hrangih kan pek mi pawl cun kan leitlun kawhhran ah pumkhat awknak um dingin a rak bom thei?`
