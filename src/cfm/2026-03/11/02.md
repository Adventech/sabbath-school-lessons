---
title:  Jesuh zohthim ding
date:   06/09/2026
---

2 Korin 8 le 9 ahhin Paul cun Korin khua ih member pawl hnenah Judea ram ih um kawhhran harsa pawl hrangih paisa thawhkhawm dingin forhfialnak a nei. Cutih ih thil tuah dingin an pumpe aw dah zo vek tla a bang zet(2 Kor. 8:10, 11; 2 Kor. 9:5; cule 1 Kor. 16:1–4), asinan anmah le Paul ih karlak ah thubuai a um cuangah zianghman lo men ah a suak kirsal a si. Himi thuhla a relfel hnu ah (2 Korin 1–7), tu ahcun Paul in cui thu ih a netkharnak lam a hoipi thlang (2 Korin 8–9).

A thok ahcun Paul cun Mesidonia mi pawl zohthim dingin a fial (2 Kor. 8:1–7), an siatha tuknak (an retheihnak) cun “tirhsiannak ih lennak” (2 Kor. 8:2, ESV) ah luangliam thluhthlo lo dingin a kham thei cuang lo(an rethei zetinan tirhsiannak ah an luangliam thotho). A dik zet, tirhsiannak le retheihnak cu an um khawm tlang theu. Ziangkhal sehla, himi Mesidonia mi pawl ih tirhsiannak ropi zet hi Jesuh in kan hrangah tirhsian zetih a pekawknak zohthimnak a rak si (2 Kor. 8:8–15).

`Siar ding: 2 Korin 8:9. Himi thu cun Jesuh zohthim ding ziangsaw in sim?`

Paul in 2 Korin 8:9 ih a thurel mi hi Bible pumpi ih mangbangza, huham nei zet le thu thuk zet lak ih pakhat a si. Paul cun Jesuh ih hnatuannak thuhla a rel, asinan tongkam mangbangza a telh. Himi ahhin Pathian thu zir dingmi tamzet a um. Himi cu tlennak thuanthu a si, asinan cangkhat te lawngah a um kim thluh thei.

Cuhnak ih mangbangza um mi cu himi thuanthu hi sumpai lam tongkam hmangih rel mi a si. Jesuh cu milian zet a si. A lennak cu vanram ih rak um zo a sinak in a fiangter (Johan 17:5). Vanlam sunlawinak fehsan in, riahsiatnak ih khat leitlun ah um in, mi rethei ih cang dingah thutluknak a tuah. Anih cu mi rethei ngaingai mi a si (Luka 9:58). Pathian thawn bangaw khal sehla, “Minung pianzia phur phah in siahhlawh pianzia la in Amah le mah a kawlawng awter thluh” (Filipi. 2:7, NASB). “Cun minung vek pianzia nei in hmuh a si, hnuai a dor ih, thihnak tiang zawnlung in a um, thinglamtah ih thihnak cu” (Filipi. 2:8, ESV).

A hnenah kumkhua ih kan um theinak dingah Jesuh cun Amai nunnak rori a hlan a si. Himi cun tumtahmi a nei: kan rundamnak a si. Kilkhawitu hnatuan le Pathian hnatuan hi an fehtlang a si. 2 Korin 8-9 cun paisa thawhkhawm thuhla in sim, asinan himi thuanthu hi Jesuh parih a thum aw mi a si. Tuizarh sungah Khrih in amah le mah a pumpek awknak ih thum aw mi thilpek thawhhlawm thawn pehpar Pathian thu bulhram pawl kan hmu ding.

`Jesuh ih suahnak thu, a nunnak, a thihnak le a thawhsalnak pawl ruat aw. Hitawk ih harsatnak leng lamah thil ziangmaw ruahsannak na neih theinak dingah himi pawl hmuahhmuah hi na hrangih a tuah mi a si timi na theihfiang tikah ziangsi na thuletnak cu a si ding?`
