---
title:  Lungput
date:   09/09/2026
---

`Siar ding: 2 Korin 8:1-5. Mesidonia mi pawl cun cuvek in siangzet ih thilpek an pek duhnak lenglam ah ziang mi thil a um ding?`

Mesidonia mi pawl ih lungput thazet cu lamzin ziangmaw zat in hmuh a theih:

A hmaisabik ah, lungawinak nasa zet thawn an pe (2 Kor. 8:2). Paul cun Mesidonia mi pawl cu, “nasa zetin an lungawi ih, rethei zet khalah tirhsiannak lamah lian zetin an luangliam” tiah a ti (2 Kor. 8:2, ESV). Cumi hnu ah hitin a run rel sal, “Pathian cun lungawi zetih a petu a duhdaw” tin (2 Kor. 9:7, NKJV). Grik tong ih “lungawi zet” timi hi Thukham Thar ah himi lawngah a lang. Cumi thawn pehpar aw deuh nawn in hmundang ahcun a um: “Zangfah cangvaihnak a tuahtu cun lungawi zetin mi zangfah seh” (Rom. 12:8, ESV) tiah a ti. Himi tongkam thawn thu hmunkhat pawl hi Bible leng cangan mi tivek ahhin lungawinak le aipuannak tivek pawl ah an hmang. 2 Korin 9:7 ah lungawizet ih petu timi sullam cu zianghman thukthumnak um lo ih peknak tinak a si.

Pahnihnak ah, tirhsiannak thawn an pe (2 Kor. 8:2). Mesidonia mi pawl ih tirhsian zia a rel hlanah Paul cun “an retheih zia” (ESV) a rel hmaisa! “Tirhsiang” (Grik cun _haplotētos_) timi cu 2 Korin 8 le 9 ah voi 3 a lang. Paul cun, “siangzet ih nan pek suak theinak dingah ziangkim ah lenter nan si” (2 Kor. 9:11, NIV) tiah a ti. A sullam cu kan pek theinak dingah pek in kan um tinak a si. Cumi hnuah hitin a run ti, “siangzet ih nan thawhkhawm mi” tiah a ti (2 Kor. 9:13, ESV). Himi thu ah, siangzet ih thawhkhawmnak hi Khrih thuthangtha phuangsuahnak lamzin pakhat a si.

A pathumnak ah, “anmai palung suak rori” in an pe (2 Cor. 8:3, NLT). Himi sullam cu mai lungtho tein an pe tinak a si. Himi cun an neih hleifuan an pe lo timi a fiangter, ziangahtile neihmi neimal zet an si. Hivek thu hi Paul cun Korin khua ih mai palung te ih feh duh zet Titas ih ziaza relnak ah a hmang. Titas cu amai lungtho teiin Korin khua ah a feh a si (2 Kor. 8:17).

Palinak ah, peknak hi tuanvo a si timi theihnak thawn an pe (2 Kor. 8:4). Himi lungput cu Mesidonia mi pawl in thilpek khawngkhawmnak ih tel ve dingih an ngennak ahhin a fiang zet. “Himi hnatuannak ih tuanvo nei ve dingin nasa zetin in ngen” (2 Kor. 8:4, NIV).

Netabik ah, pumpek awknak cangvaihnak ih thilpek khawnkhawmnak ah an tel ve (2 Kor. 8:5). Paul cun, “ A hmaisa in Bawipa hnenah an pe aw ih, cule Pathian duhdan vekin kan hnenah an pe aw” tiah a ti (2 Kor. 8:5, ESV).

Bawipa hnen ih pek awknak cun midang hnen ih pek duhnak a suah pi a si. Mesidonia mi pawl cun sumpai lam lawng siloin Pathian hnatuannak ih an tuanvo an kauh vivo. Himi cun peknak le tirhsiannak hi paisa lam lawng a si lo timi a tarlang.
