Ellen G. White, “A Liberal Church,” pp. 335–345, in The Acts of the Apostles.

“Khrih duhdawtnak ih a khat thluhmi thinlung cun milian kan si theinak dingah retheihnak hmangin kan hrang mi rethei ih cangtu cu a thlun ding a si. Paisa, tikcu caan, khawruahnak – laksawng pawl hmuahhmuah hi Pathian kut ihsin an don mi a si thluh, Thuthangtha hnatuannak ih thansohnak ding hrangah man nei zet in an hmang ding. Cumi cu Kawhhran hmaisa khalah a rak um dah zo, tuisan kawhhran khalah Thlarau ih thiltitheinak hmangin kawhhran member pawl cun leitlun thilri ihsin an thiar fihlim aw ih, an minungpi pawl in thuthangtha an theih ve theinak dingih an pumpek awk a si ahcun thutak phuangsuah mi pawl cu a rak ngaitu pawl hrangah thiltitheinak nasa zet a si ding”—Ellen G. White, The Acts of the Apostles, p. 71.

“Kan Bawipa cun kan thilpek hi a mamawh lo. Kan thilpek hmangin kan lianter thei cuang lo. Saam cangantu cun, ‘Ziangkim na hnen ihsin an ra, kan lo pek mi pawl khal nangmai ta an si’ tiah a ti. Cumi cingin Pathian cun mah le mah hnongaw in midang kan va bomnak hmangin A duhdawtnak kan ngai thupit zia langternak ding caan in onsak a si. Himi lawng hi Pathian hnen ih lungawithu sim le kan duhdawtnak lungput kan langter theinak umsun a si. Lamzin dang zianghman in onsak lo a si.”—Ellen G. White, Counsels on Stewardship, pp. 18, 19.

“Minung hnen ih Pathian thilpek hi a va ropi ve, kan Pathian ih a tuah mi hi an va ropi ve! Minung fale ih dokalhnak pawl a rundam theinak dingah le a duhdawtnak le a tumtah mi an hmuh theinak dingah thilpek man nei zet a pe suak. Na laksawng le thilpek pawl hmangin “A fapa neihsun a pe ih a zumtu pawl hmuahhmuah cu thi loin kumkhaw nunnak a nei ding” tiih a fapa a petu sunlawihnak na tarlang thei kem?”—Ellen G. White, in The Advent Review and Sabbath Herald, May 15, 1900.

**Relkhawm ding thusuhnak pawl:**

`2 Korin 8:9 kha zoh aw. Ziangruangah saw kilkhawitu hnatuannak thawn pehpar aw in Jesuh zohthim hi a thupit?`

`Johan 3:16 ahkhan van tong cu peknak hi a si timi a langfiang. Johan 15:13, Efesa 5:2, 25; Galati 2:19, 20; le 1 Johan 3:16 kha siar aw. Himi bungcang pawl le Johan 3:16 in ziangsaw bangawknak an neih, cule cumi ihsin ziangmi thucah saw kan lak thei ding?`

`2 Korin 8-9 ah thum aw in, peknak ih bulpak hlawknak cu ziangsaw a si?`

`Thilpek fehdan ahkhan Jesuh ih pekawknak zohthimnak ah ziang thildang saw na tuah thei mi um?`
