---
title:  Lung thawhnak
date:   07/09/2026
---

`Siar ding: 2 Korin 8:1, 5 le 2 Korin 9:7, 9, 13, 15. Himi thu ih thucah laimu bik cu ziangsaw a si?`

2 Korin 8-9 ahhin peknak tongkam nasa zetin a run hmang: “Pathian zangfahnak … cu pek in a um” (2 Kor. 8:1, ESV); “An pe aw” (2 Kor. 8:5, ESV); “zokhal a thinlung sungih a tumtah vekin pe seh…Ziangahtile Pathian cun lungawi zetih a petu cu a duhdaw a si” (2 Kor. 9:7, NIV); “Anih cun mi siatha pawl a pe” (2 Kor. 9:9, ESV); “Siangzetih nan pek ruangah ….annih cun Pathian an sunloih a si” (2 Kor. 9:13, ESV); “Simfiang theih lo A thilpek ruangah Pathian hnenah lungawi thu um hram seh” (2 Kor. 9:15, ESV). 2 Korin 8 le 9 cu peknak tongkam in a thok ih netkhar a si (2 Kor. 8:1 le 2 Kor. 9:15). Thinlung sungih peknak umter phah in himi bung 2 hi kan siar ding a si. Thil kan peknak san tumzet pali hrawng a run tarlang:

Pathian zangfahnak ruangih lungawithu simnak (2 Kor. 8:1; 2 Kor. 9:14, 15). 2 Korin 8–9 cu “Pathian zangfahnak” tin hramthok a si (2 Kor. 8:1). Cumi deuh hnuah Paul cun “kan Bawipa Jesuh Khrih ih zangfahnak cu nan thei” (2 Kor. 8:9 ESV) tiah a ti. Himi ahhin Pathian le Khrih ih zangfahnak cu thilpek an peknak ding san bik pakhat a si timi a run tarlang. Khrih in peknak thawngin Pathian cun thil tamzet kan hrang in tuah sak. Thilpek kan pek kirsal tikah kan nun ah Pathian ih in zangfahnak kan theifiang a si.

Thilpek timi ruahnak thawngin “zangfahnak” (Grik cun _charis_) timi tongkam hi 2 Korin 8-9 ah tarlang in a um kirsal. Cuvek ciah in _charis_ timi tongkam hi, hi thuhla a thoknak le a cemnak ahhin a lang a si (2 Kor. 8:1; 2 Kor. 9:14, 15). Paul cun himi tongkam hi kan nun ih Khrih ih zangfahnak cun midang zangfahnak le lungawi thusim duhnak a suahpi a si timi relnak ah a sullam dangdang in uar zetin a hmang a si.

Jesuh ih tuahdan thlun duhnak (2 Kor. 8:9). Jesuh cu milian a si ih, mi farah ah a hung cang (Himi hi kumkhua ih a rak um zonak, le minung ih a rung cannak a si timi hi thei ringring aw). Cui thil a hungcang suah theinak ding lamzin pakhat lawng a um: A pek awk thluhnak hi a si. Kan hrang ahcun kan thilpek kan zemdarhnak thawngin midang pawl in Khrih an theihnak ding lamzin kan sialsak a si.

Pathian thlawsuah zemdarh duhnak (2 Kor. 9:10, 11). Pathian hnen ihsin kan don hmaisa ruangah midang kan pe theifang a si. Anih cun tirhsian dingah in lianter a si.

Duhdawtnak dik (2 Kor. 8:8, 24). Peknak cu duhdawtnak diktak a si. Thinlung ah duhdawtnak a um timi nemhngetnak tha zet a si. Laizo tongih kan hman ding ahcun, “Na kaa umnak ah na paisa ret aw” ti a si.

`Ziangtluk tirhsiang mi saw na si? Thinglamtah eng hnuai ahhin na pek ding zat thawn zohthim in ziangmi tluk tam in na pe?`
