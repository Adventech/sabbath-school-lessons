---
title:  Khawkhannak
date:   08/09/2026
---

`Siar ding: 2 Korin 9:7. Himi thu cun peknak cangvaihnak thuhla ziangsaw a rel?`

Pathian in leitlun rundam ding ih thutluknak a tuah mi hi sual ih leitlun a tluk hlan pi ihsin a rak si daih zo. Kan hrangah thih ding ih Khrih a ratnak hi hlansan ihsin rak khawkhan cia mi a si (Thup 13:8). Pathian hrangih thil a thleng pang mi a rak si lo. Jesuh hmangin amah le mah pumpe aw dingin khawkhannak a nei. 2 Korin 8-9 ah khawkhannak cu thilpek cangvaihnak thawn pehpar aw in Pathian thu zirnak ah bulhram thupi zet a luah a si. Himi cu lamzin pahnih in kan hmu thei ko ding:-

Pakhatnak, khawkhannak ahhin thutluknak man nei zet a tel. Paul cun, “zo cio khal nan thinlung ih thutluknak vek in pe uh” (2 Kor. 9:7, NIV) tiah a ti. Grik tong ih let mi “thutluknak” timi hi _proaireō_ timi a si. Himi cun sullam thuk zet a nei. _Pro_ timi cu “hlanah” asilole “hlan khan in” tinak a si, cule _aireō_ timi cu himi thu ahcun “thutluknak tuah” tinak a si. Curuangah _proaireō_ timi cun hlankhan ihsin thutluknak tuah timi a khihhmuh a si. Curuangah a thurel hramthoknak ah, “zo cio khal” a ti ruangah Paul cun zokhal an pek dingzat a bangrep aw thluh lo ding timi a tarlang. A reltum mi bik cu zo khal in pe suak dingih thutluknak an tuah tikah, ralring zetin tuah haiseh a tinak men khi a si. An pek ding zat a dik tiih an zummi cu an pe ding a si.

Pahnihnak ah, peknak ahcun neihmi thawn milaw zetih peknak timi thuzirhnak a tel. Paul cun Mesidonia mi pawl cun “an ti thei tawk in an pe a si” (2 Kor. 8:3, ESV) tiah a ti. Himi kaihhruai awknak hi Korin khawmi pawl par khalah a hmang a si. An rak tumtah cia mi cu hlensuak ngah dingin a forhfial hai, cule an neihsiah hmangin cui hnatuan cu thehsuak lawlaw ding khal in a ngen hai a si (2 Kor. 8:11). Himi thu ahcun thilpek cu “a neih lo dan vek siloin, a neihdan ih zir sawn in” (2 Kor. 8:12, ESV) pe dingin thunet a khar. Bible cun thenhra thenkhat hi zateek 10 tiih a rel vek ciah in thilpek ahcun zianghman bikhiah a um ve lo. “zo cio khal nan thinlung ih thutluknak vek in pe uh” (2 Kor. 9:7, NIV) tiah a ti. Himi cu neihmi thawn milaw ih peknak thu zirh awknak ah a run hmang a si. Phundang in kan sim ding asile, zo cio khal in an hlawhsuah mi ihsin ziangzat an pe ding timi cu anmai thutluknak a si. Mikip cun an neihzat milin an pe ding a si. Hi thil cu khawkhannak tel lo cun zianghman a cang suak thei lo ding.

`Na lian simaw, na farah a si hmanah thenhra thenkhat le thilpek (thawhhlawm) ah ziangtluk na rin um? Tamdeuh in na pe thei timi na theih caan ah, cuvek ih pe suak lo dingin ziang phuahhlam saw na hmang theu?`
