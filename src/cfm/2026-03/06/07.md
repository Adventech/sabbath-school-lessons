Roswell F. Cottrell, “Spiritual Gifts,” pp. 5–16, in Ellen G. White, Spiritual Gifts, vol. 1.

“Minung cu Bawipa rinsannak thawn hnatuan dingah feh seh, Anih cu an hnenah a feh ding ih, mi thinlung ah thu sim in, thlarau piantharnak a tuah ding. Hnatuantu pakhat cu thuthangtha simtu dingih ralring zetmi a si men thei, a dang pakhat cu cangan tu si dingih mancia a si men thei, a dang pakhat cu thinlung ngaingai in, taima zetin thlacamnak thilpek neitu a si men thei, a dang pakhat cu hlasak thilpek dongtu a si men thei. Midang pakhat cu fiah felfai zetin Pathian kamsuak thu simfiang theinak huham hleice neitu a si men thei. Thilpek tinkim hi Pathian hrangah huham nei zetah an cang ding, ziangahtile Anih cun hnatuantu pawl thawn hna a tuan a si. Mi pakhat hnenah Pathian cun fimnak tongkam a pe, a dang pakhat hnenah theihnak a pe ih, a dang pakhat hnenah zumnak a pe. Asinan an zaten lubik ih hnuai ah hna an tuan thluh. Thilpek phun dangdang cun hnatuandan phun dangdang ah mi a hruai, “asinan Pathian cu pakhat, mi zate hnenih ziangkim a tuahtu a si” (1 Kor. 12:6).

“Zohman in thilpek mal deuh dong ih ruat mi cu hmuhsuam hlah seh. Mi hmuahhmuah cu hnatuan dingin feh suak sawn seh. Zo khal in thil ropi zet tuan thei lo ih an ruah awk ruangah zumlonak in an kut beng men hlah seh. Mah le mah thlir awknak cu cawlhsan uh. Nan hruaitu kha zoh uh. Tangdornak, thinlung taknak le duhdawtnak thawngin na tuah thei mi cu tuah aw”—Ellen G. White, in The Advent Review and Sabbath Herald, April 12, 1906.

“Midang ruahnak ihsin kan don theih mi bawmnak hi kan tul thluh. Kan ruahnak ai hnakin midang ruahnak ah Pathian in hna a tuan sawn ding. Mi dangdang hnen ih thilpek dangdang pek hi “mithianghlim pawl tifamkimnak dingah, rongbawl hnatuannak dingah, Khrih ih taksa sawhngetnak dingah a si” (Efesa. 4:12).

“Kanmai ah harsatnak cu a um ringring ding, asinan in hruaitu kan thlun a tul, cule kan harsatnak pawl cu pumkhat in, kut kai awin, kan hmaiton ding a si”—Ellen G. White, The Upward Look, p. 141.

**Relkhawm ding thusuhnak pawl:**

`Thuphuan theinak thilpek thuhla vun ruat bet aw. Ziangruangah saw thuphuan theinak thilpek hi theihlo tong ih tong theinak thilpek(simfiang a si lo ahcun) ai hnakih a thupit sawn?A tul cun Paul ih thusimmi na sar theisalnak dingah 1 Korin 14 kha siar sal aw.`

`Sabbath sikul zirnak ah Ellen G White hringnun le a hnatuannak pawl rel khawm uh, kawhhran kan si vekin ziangruangah saw anih cu thuphuan theinak thilpek tarlangtu ih kan zum? Himi thilpek ihsin kawhhran ah ziangmi thlawsuahnak ropi saw runglut? Himi thilpek hmandan ding thabik lamzin va theihnak ahhin ziangmi harsatnak a um?`

`A lo duhdawt ngaingai tu mi pathum ihsin panga hrawng kha vun ruat aw. An lo duhdawtnak kha duhdawtnak ngaingai a si timi ziangtin na theithei? Himi cun Paul in thlarau thilpek a rel mi ah duhdawtnak thuhla nasa zetih a relnak san ziangsaw a lo sim?`

`Duhdawtnak hi ziangtluk ih a thupit khalah ziangahsaw mi pakhat in thudik a rel le rel lo asilole ngaithlak tlak a si le silo tahfung ih hman theih mi umsun a si lo?`
