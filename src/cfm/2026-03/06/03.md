---
title:  Thilphun dangdang hmangih pumkhatnak
date:   03/08/2026
---

Pumkhatnak tongkam 1 Korin 12:4-6 ih a hman mi cu (“Thlarau hmunkhat,” “Bawipa hmunkhat,” le “Pathian hmunkhat”) 1 Korin 12 ih thudang ummi pawl ahhin a fiang vivo. Himi pawl hi Paul in “ thlarau hmunkhat le pakhat” (1 Kor. 12:11, NKJV), “taksa ruangpi cu pakhat” (1 Kor. 12:12), “taksa pumkhat” (1 Kor. 12:12, 13, 20), “Thlarau pakhat” (1 Cor. 12:13 [voi hnih]), “khat le khat bangrep ih ngaihsak aw ton” (1 Kor. 12:25, NKJV) timi thu a rel mi ahhin a fiang tuk.

Pumkhat awknak ahhin Paul cun Khrih ih taksa then dangdang pawl cu“ taksa then tampi” (1 Kor. 12:12, 20), “kan zaten tipilnak pek kan si” (1 Kor. 12:13, NKJV), “thenkhat lawng siloin then tampi” (1 Kor. 12:14, NKJV), “taksa pumpi” (1 Kor. 12:17), “anmah pakhat cio in” (1 Cor. 12:18, NKJV), “taksa ruangpi ih taksa then pawl” (1 Kor. 12:22, 23), “taksa then hmuahhmuah” (1 Kor. 12:26) tivek pawl hmangin a tarlang a si. Hivek ih pumkhat awknak le thil phun dangdang thupi zetih retnak hin thlarau thilpek hmuitin mi cu thil phun dangdang hmangih pumkhat awknak hi a si.

Himi thil phun dangdang ih pumkhat awknak cun Pathian ziaza a langter tengteng ding a si. Pa cu mi pakhat, Fapa cu a dang pakhat, cule Thiang Thlarau cu a dang leh a si. An Pathum in kawhhran ih hnatuannak le thansoh theinak dingah pumkhat in hnatuan tlang khal sehla, an bulpak sinak an kai hnget a si (1 Kor. 12:4–6, Efesa. 4:11–13).

`Siar ding: 1 Korin 12:12-31. Ziang ruangah saw taksa pakhat ih then dangdang ummi pawl cu kawhhran le cumi ih then dangdang ummi pawl khihhmuhnak ih a milawk thiam?`

1 Korin 12 ih thu ruahnak bulhram bik cu taksa then dangdang pawl hi pakhat le pakhat bangaw lo zet khal sehla (1 Korin 12:15-20), pakhat le pakhat ah an thum aw thluh a si timi hi a si (1 Kor. 12:21–26). Kee cu a fehnak ding a hmuh theinak dingah mit parah a thum aw, cule khat lam ah mit cun zianghman a tham thei lo, kut lawngin a tham thei. Taksa then hrekhat cu a dang ai hnakin a cak lo deuh, asilole a zahum lo deuh (1 Kor. 12:23) tivek ih ruahnak cu hmundan men a si, an zaten an tul aw thluh (1 Kor. 12:22).

Korin khawmi pawl lak ahhin thilpek dang hnong ih, a dang cu duh hleice cuang nei mi hrekkhat an um. Korin khawmi pawl in hi thil diklo an tuah lonak dingah Paul cun duhdawtnak lam ngaihven dingin a sawm hai, cumi cu “lamzin thabik” a si (1 Kor. 12:31, NKJV). Phundang in kan sim ding asile, thilpek cu ziangvek a va si hmanah duhdawtnak le fimkhur zetih hman a si ahcun Pathian hrangah a sunglawi a si.

`1 Korin 12:8–10, 28; Rom 12:6–8; le Efesa 4:11 ih thlarau thilpek pawl hi zoh aw. Na don mi thlarau thilpek cu ziangsaw a si? Khrih ih taksa thansohnak dingah ziangtin na hmang?`
