---
title:  “Lamzin thabik”
date:   04/08/2026
---

“Duhdawtnak cu thilpek tamzet lakih pakhat lawng a si lo. Thilpek pawl hmuahhmuah in an tumtah mi netabik an thlentheinak ding lamzin a si—Carl P. Cosaert, “1 Corinthians,” Andrews Bible Commentary: New Testament (Berrien Springs, MI: Andrews University Press, 2022), p. 1643.

`1 Korin 13:1-7 le 1 Piter 4:8-11. Thlarau thilpek thawn pehpar aw in duhdawtnak ih tuanvo cu ziangsaw a si?`

1 Korin 13 cun duhdawtnak thawng lawngin thlarau thilpek cu thazet in hman a theih ding thu in zirh. Paul cun 1 Korin 13 cu 1 Korin 12 sungih thilpek hmangin hram a thok, duhdawtnak hmangih kaihhruai lo ahcun thupitternak cang an thei lo ding ruangah a si. Cuvek thotho in theihnak(1 Kor. 12:8) le zumnak (1 Kor. 12:9) khal a si, Zumnak in “ tlang thawn thei” (1 Kor. 13:2, ESV) khal sehla, duhdawtnak a tel lo cun zianghman lo men a si (1 Kor. 13:2). Duhdawtnak tel loin tong phun dangdang ih tong theinak (1 Kor. 12:10, 28, 30) cu “dar awn ruiri asilole darsumsel awn ruri” (1 Kor. 13:1, NIV) vek men fang a si. Cuvek ciah in thuphuan theinak thilpek khal hi duhdawtnak tel lo cun zianghman lo men a si (1 Kor. 13:2).

1 Korin 13:4-7 sungah Paul cun duhdawtnak ih a sinak le a silonak hmuitin in a rel; a hleice in duhdawtnak ih a tuah mi le, a tuah lo mi a run tarlang. Duhdawtnak relnak ih tongkam a hman mi cun duhdawtnak cu thil ziangmaw kan theihnak lam siloin thil ziangmaw a ngaingai ih kan tuahsuaknak lam a si tiah a tarlang. Cuvekin Paul cun duhdawtnak cun (1) tuarfainak a langter; (2) ngilhneihnak a langter; (3) thudik ah a lungawi; (4) ziangkim a tuar thei; (5) ziangkim a zum; (6) ziangkim a ruahsan; (7) ziangkim a tuarsuak tiah a ti. Cumi kalh ciah in duhdawtnak cu (1) a duh am lo; (2) a porh aw lo; (3) a puarthau fawn lo; (4) hngal zetin a um lo; (5) amai diknak a hawl lo; (6) olsam ten a thinai thok lo; (7) thil dik lo a cing ringring lo; (8) thildik lo ah a lungawi lo.

Himi thu 15 cun thilpek hmannak ih ziaza tha langternak ding hrang kaihhruai awknak a pe a si. Himi duhdawtnak relkhawm mi hi 1 Korin 12 le 1 Korin 14 karlak ih ummi a si, cutawk ahcun Paul cun thlarau thilpek pawl thawn pehpar aw ih buainak pawl a hmaiton a si. A ngaingai te cun duhdawtnak cu thlarau thil pek pawl fimzet ih hmannak ding tawhfung a si. Duhdawtnak khal hi zumnak le ruahsannak thawn umkhawm tlang mi a si. Asinan “himi pawl lakih a tumbik mi cu duhdawtnak a si” (1 kor. 13:13, NKJV).

`Ziangahsaw duhdawtnak cu kan zumnak ih laimu bik a si. Thlacamnak hmangin midang duhdawtnak kan va langternak ai hnakih Pathian duhdawtnak ngaingai tonun thadeuh lamzin dang ziangsaw um?`
