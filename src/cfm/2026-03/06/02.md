---
title:  Thilpek phunphun
date:   02/08/2026
---

Paul cun 1 Korin 12:1 ahhin thupi thar a run hmel theihter, “ tu ahcun kan ruat thlang” timi a run hmang. Mithiam pawl cun “thlarau thilpek” thuhla maw, asilole “Thlarau mi pawl” thuhla a rel timi an ruat buai zet, ziangahtile Grik tong ih, _tōn pneumatikōn_ timi cun a pahnih ih simnak ah hman a theih veve a si. 1 Korin 12:4 thu ahcun “Thlarau thilpek” timi hi a mil aw deuh in a lang, cutawk ahcu Paul cun thlarau lam thilpek thuhla a rel tam zet a rel. 1 Korin 12:2,3 ah Paul cun Thlarau thilpek hmaisabik cu Jesuh hi Bawi a si timi raltha zetih puangsuahnak hi a si tiah a ti (1 Kor. 12:2, 3). Thukham Thar san ahcun Jesuh cu Bawi a si tiih rel cu Sizar hi bawi a si lo tiih relnak a si (Tirh 17:7; Johan 19:12, 15). Himi cu ram uktu thuneihnak dokalhnak ih ruahmi a si ruangah, thihnak daantat theih mi a si.

Jesuh le Paul cun hremnak le thihnak a um khalah Pathian ah zumnak neih hi – Thlarau thilpek a si tiah uar zetin an sim. A ngaingai te cun zumnak cu thilpek bulhram bik a si. 1 Korin 13:13 ih a ummi thu ahhin zumnak hi a hmaisabik ih a um khal hi mangbangza a si tuk lem lo. Cui zumnak cu thlarau thilpek a si timi cu Paul in 1 Korin 12:9 ih a rel mi ahhin a fiang zet. Ziangkhalsehla thilpek dang khal tamzet a um. Thiang Thlarau in thilpek dangdang hi “bulpak tin cio ah Amai duhdan vekin” (1 Kor. 12:11, NKJV) a zem hai a ti tikah an zaten an thupi thluh timi a langter a si.

`Siar ding: 1 Korin 12:1-6. Himi thu ih a thupitter mi kha ziangsaw a si?`

“Phun dangdang” timi rel kirsal rero mi cun thilpek tamzet a tarlang a si. Paul in “thlarau thilpek” tiin 1 Korin 12:1 ih a rel mi hi cang 4-6 ahcun phun dangdang pathum in a ra suak: “thilpek” (_charisma_), “riantuannak” lole “hnatuannak” (_diakonia_), le “Cangvaihnak” (_energēma_) timi pawl tla an si. Himi tongkam pawl hi sullam bangaw lo zet hlir an si, asinan an karlak thu ih bangawknak a um ruangah an tumram dang verver thluh vekih sim ding hi thil tul ciamco mi a si lo. Thlarau thilpek cu thumkom Pathian ziaza ah thum aw in, pumkhat awknak tuahnak dingah a si timi theih khal hi a tha zet (zoh aw, Efesa. 4:8–11). Thlarau thilpek hi zumtu pawl hnenih pek a si ih, zumtu pawlkom awknak sungah Khrih riantuan dingih thilti theinak petu ngaingai cu Pathian a si (1 Kor. 12:5, 6). Zumtu mikip hnenah bulpak zetin thilpek pek an si (1 Kor. 12:11), asinan an zaten zumtu pawl komkhawmnak ih hlawknak hrangah an si.

`Paul in pumkhat awknak a ngai thupitzia kha vun hminsin sal aw. Himi hi ziangah kawhhran ah a thupi?`
