### Tuizarh zirlai hrang siar ding
1 Korin 12, Efesa. 4:11–13, 1 Korin 13, 1 Pet. 4:8–11, 1 Kor. 14:27, Amos 3:7.

> <p>Bible cangken</p>
> “ Duhdawtnak cu thluh uhla, thlarau thilpek cu nasa zetin duh uh, a hleice in thu nan phuan theinak dingah a si” (1 Korin 14:1, ESV).

Taksa vek ciah in kawhhran hi pakhat a si ih, cumi ahcun member tamzet a nei, cumi pawl ih tuanvo, hnatuan le thilpek pawl hi an bangaw thluh lo. Duhdawtnak thawn cun hi thlarau thilpek pawl cun thumkom Pathian ih ziaza pumkhat awknak kha an tarlang a si.

Tuizarh ahcun 1 Korin 12-14 le cumi ih a zirhmi thlarau thilpek thuhla kan zirfiang ding. Himi thuhlawm then hi a kau zet ih, Paul in biaknak lam ih Khristian ziaza a ruahsan mi pawl khal a sim (1 Korinthians 11–14). Paul ih a thinlung ih um zet mi cu pumkhawm awk thei lonak buainak hi a si. Hi buainak a letnak cu kawhhran cu taksa peng dangdang nei mi a si ih, “Khrih ih taksa sawhngetnak dingah ih” (Efesa4:12) a tha zet mi a si timi hi a si. A tawizawng in, Pathian cun thil phun dangdang hi pumkhat ih an um theinak dingah kawhhran hnenah thlarau thilpek a pe a si.

1 Korin ih a hmaisa lam bung 4 ih buainak ummi pawl kha Paul ih thinlung ah a um ringring lai ti a fiang zet, cumi ahcun kawhhran member pawl karlak ih remawk lonak thubuai ih a letnak cu Khrih ih pumkhatnak hi a si. Paul ih ruahdan ahcun Khrih le Thlarau ih pumkhatnak cu thendarh awknak hrial theinak umsun a si.
