---
title:  Theih lo tong thilpek
date:   05/08/2026
---

Theih lo tong thilpek thuhla teh ziangtin? Bible hmun dang ih thilpek tarlang mi thawn (Mark 16:17, Acts 2:1–13, Acts 10:44–48, Acts 19:6) milaw zetin, 1 Korin sungih theih lo tong thilpek hi Thlarau in tongdang (ramdang tong) ih tong theinak thilpek a si ding a zum um bik.

Paul cun theih lo tong thilpek hi 1 Korin 12:8-10 sung ih thilpek pawl ngan mi ahkhan a rak sim zo(himi khalah zoh aw, 1 Kor. 12:28, 30; 1 Kor. 13:1, 8). Ziangkhalsehla, 1 Korin 14 ah a run rel sal. A ngaingai te cun Grik tongfang glossa (“tong”) timi cu 1 Korin 12-14 sung ahhin vei 20 tlun lai a um, cumi lak ih vei 15 cu 1 Korin 14 lawng ahhin a um. Cui hlei ah Grik tongfang _heteroglōssos_ (“Tong dang”) timi khal 1 Korin 14:21 ah a um. Theih lo tong ih tong theinak thilpek hi a tam tuk ruangah Paul cun himi hi a thupi zetmi buainak a si tiah a ruat. Himi thilpek hi Korin khua ih kawhhran in a hmang dik lo ih, a hman sual ahcun vantlang khawmnak ah lungbuainak le thinlung sisualnak a suahter thei a si (1 Kor. 14:23, 27, 33, 40).

`Siar ding: 1 Korin 14:5, 13, 26, 27 le 1 Korin 12:10, 30. Theih lo tong thawn pehpar aw in Pual in ziangmi zirlai saw a pek?`

Theih lo tong ih tong theinak thilpek le simfiang theinak thilpek hi a kop ih an um a tulnak san hi cui tongkam theihthiam a tul ruangah a si (1 Kor. 14:9); cutilo cun cui thilpek hmannak ah hlawknak zianghman a um lo ding a si (1 Kor. 14:6). Himi cun Pathian in simfiangnak le theihthiamnak a ngai thupitnak san a relfiang a si. Theih lo tong thilpek hrimhrim hi a simsia lo ih, asinan thaisun zirlai ih kan hmuh ding vek ciah in, Korin khawmi pawl in thupi zetih an ret lutuk ruangah thuphuan theinak thilpek zianghman an ngaihsak lo.

Himi ah Paul cun Korin khawmi pawl in tongdang (ramdang tong) an thiam thluh ding duh zet khal sehla (1 Kor. 14:5), cui thil a thlen ding hi a beisei ngam lo (1 Kor. 12:10). Curuangah “mi hmuahhmuah in Thiang Thlarau ih tipilnak nei dingih an rel awk hlanah theih lo tongin a tong ding a si” timi ruahnak cu 1 Korin 12 le 14 sung ih Paul ih zirhmi rak danglamternak a si”—Raoul Dederen, Handbook of Seventh-day Adventist Theology, electronic ed., vol. 12 of Commentary Reference Series (Hagerstown, MD: Review and Herald Publishing Association, 2001), p. 620.

`Nan kawhhran ah tongdang hmang an um maw? Himi an thiamnak hi Khrih hnen ih midang hruai thlennak ah ziangtin an rak hmang? Himi cun Paul in tongdang thuhla a rel mi ngaingai theithiam dingin ziangtin saw in bawm?`
