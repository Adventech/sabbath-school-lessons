---
title:  Thuphuan theinak thilpek
date:   06/08/2026
---

Thlarau thilpek thuhla ih Paul ih relkhawmnak a neihnak ahkhan thuphuan theinak thilpek cun hmunram thupi zet a rak luah a si. Thuphuan theinak thilpek hi theih lo tong ih tong theinak thilpek hlanah rel tlangpi mi a si (1 Kor. 12:10, 28; 1 Kor. 13:8). Theih lo tong ih tong theinak thilpek hi rel hmaisa mi rak si bangta sehla, thuphuan theinak thilpek hi a thupi vak lo tin rel a theih ko ding (1 Kor. 14:4, 5, 6, 22).

`Siar ding: Efesa 4:11-13 le 1 Korin 14:3, 4. Thlarau lam thilpek tlangpi le a hleice in thuphuan theinak thilpek thuhla ahhin himi bungcang pawl in ziangsaw an rel?`

Thuphuan theinak thilpek cu tuahthatnak, forhfialnak, le thlangamnak pe dingih khawkhan mi a si (1 Kor. 14:3; himi thawn zoh kop aw Tirh 15:32). Himi cun Thuphuannak hi hmailam caan relcianak lam siloin a tuisan ih nundan ding relnak lam sawn a si timi a langfiang ter. Grik tong _prophēteuō_ timi sullam cu “hlan khan ih thil ziangmaw relnak” asilole “mi pakhat ai-awh ih thil ziangmaw relnak” timi khal a si thei thotho. Tahthimnak ah, a hmaisa ta kha Tirh 2:29-31 ahkhan kan hmu thei (zohkop aw, Amos 3:7), cutawk ahcun David cu “hmailam hmucia” vekih rel a si ruangah profet a si ding timi ruatdan a um. A pahnihnak tu cu Tirh 15:32 ahkhan hmuh a theih, cutawk ahcun Judas le Silas cu profet vekih rel an si. Ziangkhalsehla, an “thuphuan” mi cun “unau pawl cu tongkam tamzet” (ESV) hmang in thazang an pe ih, an hnget khohter a si.

Efesa 4:11-13 ihsin thlarau thilpek cu tirhthlah pawl san lawng ahkhan a tawp lo a si timi kan zir suak thei. Sancem tiang an um ding a si (Tirh 2:39). Ziangkhalsehla, mi pakhat in profet ah a sal aw a si ahcun ca thianghlim ah thum aw in zohhliahnak neih ding a si. A kau zawngih rel ding cun thlun ding daan pali a um. Pakhatnak ah thuphuan mi an thleng dik ding a si (Daan. 18:22; Jer. 28:8, 9). Pahnihnak, thucah cu a hlan ih profet pawl ih thucah thawn an mil aw ding (Daan. 13:1–3, Isa. 8:20). Pathumnak ah, nitin nun ah Khrih ih a pumpek awknak a langter ding (1 Johan 4:1–3). Palinak ah, Jesuh cun profet deu pawl cu an rah ihsin theih a si ding a ti (Matt. 7:15–20). Himi hi Profet dik thawn pehpar aw ih a dikmi pawl an si.

Thuphuan cabu ahhin thuphuan theinak thilpek hi a tangsun kawhhran ih danglam hleicenak a si tiah a tarlang (Thup. 12:17, Thup. 19:10). Seventh-day Adventists kan si vekin thuphuan theinak thilpek cu Ellen G. White hnen ih pek a si timi kan zum ih, a thungan mi pawl khalah an lang fiang zet.

`Ellen G. White ih profet thuphuan theinak thilpek kan zumnak ding san kan neih mi pawl cu ziangsaw an si? A tuanvo le thuneihnak thuhla ah ziangsaw thusuhnak um hrih thei lai?`
