---
title:  Ukucashela UNkulunkulu
date:   28/09/2026
---

Ngenxa yokuthi uNkulunkulu uluthando (1 Johane 4:8), wayengeke adale abantu “abangamarobothi.” Uthando lwamukela Ukukhetha. Ukuze bakwazi ukuthanda, u-Adamu no-Eva kwafuneka badalwe nelungelo lokuzikhethela; kungenjalo, uthando, uthando lweqiniso, babengeke bakwazi ukulunikeza.

Ngalokho, u-Adamu no-Eva babenenkululeko yokulalela noma yokungalaleli uMdali wabo. Iqiniso lalelilungelo lokuzikhethela labonakaliswa emyalelweni wokuthi bangadli emthini wokwazi okuhle nokubi (buka uGen. 2:15-17). Baxwayiselwani kwasekuqaleni ukuba bangadli ngaphandle kokuba benelungelo lokukwenza lokho na?

“abazali bethu bokuqala, nakuba bedalwe benswulwa futhi bengcwele, babesenokukwazi ukwenza okungalungile. UNkulunkulu wabenza izidalwa ezikwazi Ukukhetha, ezikwazi ukuthakasela ubuhlakani nobumnene besimilo Sakhe kanye Nobulungiswa bezimfuno Zakhe, futhi benelungelo elingcwele lokulalela noma lokungaleli. Kwakumele bajabulele ukuxhumana noNkulunkulu nezingelosi ezingcwele. . . . Ngaphandle kwelungelo lokukhetha, ukulalela kwakhe kwakungeke kube ngokokuzikhethela, kepha kwakuyoba ngokwempoqo.”—Ellen G. White, Patriarchs and Prophets, pp. 48, 49.

`Kungani u-Adamu no-Eva bazama ukucashela uMdali wabo emuva kokungamlaleli Yena na? Buka uGenesisi 3:1-8 (qhathanisa no-Jer. 23:24 no-Heb. 4:13).`

UGenesisi 3 uveza umbono omubi kakhulu—emuva kokuwa kuka-Adamu no-Eva. Emuva kwengxoxo enkulu phakathi kwenyoka (Sathane) no-Eva, abazali bethu bokuqala balingeka ukuba badle “emthini wokwazi okuhle nokubi,” ababengavunyelwe ukukwenza. Usathane wasebenzisa amandla amazwi, emizwa, kanye nokukhangisa okuthokozisayo ukuletha ukuvumisa u-Eva, owabe esevumisa u-Adamu, ukuba benze okuphambene nomyalelo kaNkulunkulu. U-Eva waqaphela ukuthi umuthi “ulungele ukudliwa,” “uyabukeka emehlweni,” futhi “unxanelekile ekuhlakaniphiseni” (Gen. 3:6, NKJV). Isithembiso sikaSathane sokuthi babezoba “ ‘njengoNkulunkulu, bazi okuhle nokubi’ ” (Gen. 3:5, NKJV) sezwakala singenakuhlulwa.

Empeleni, inkulumo kaSathane yayimanaphanapha ngamanga. Usathane wabafihlela imiphumela yokungalaleli kwabo. Emuva kokungamlaleli uNkulunkulu, amehlo abo “avuleka” ngokuphazima, futhi babona ukuthi “babenqunu.” “Ingubo” kaNkulunkulu yokukhanya nenkazimulo (Hub. 104:1, 2), eyeyibonakalisa “umfanekiso” wezulu, yayisisukile. Babengasambathisiwe ngalokho uNkulunkulu ayebahlinzeke ngakho. Akumangalisi ukuthi u-Adamu no-Eva bacashela uMdali wabo (sengathi kuyenzeka ngempela ukucashela uNkulunkulu). Bazama ukucasha ngoba isono sisenza sizizwe sinecala futhi, ecaleni lethu, sifuna ukubalekela ubukhona bukaNkulunkulu.
