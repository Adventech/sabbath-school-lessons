### Funda Lokhu Kuleliviki
Gen. 1:27, 28; Gen. 3:1-8; Luka 15:11-32; Gen. 3:14-24; Eksod. 19:16-22; Johane 10:27-30.

> <p>Indimana Yenkumbulo</p>
> “UNkulunkulu esekhulumile endulo kokhokho ngabaprofethi ngamathuba amaningi nangezindlela eziningi, 2 ekugcineni kwale mihla ukhulume kithina ngeNdodana ayimise ibe yindlalifa yakho konke, owadala ngayo futhi izwe” (Heberu 1:1, 2, NKJV).

I Theknoloji yenza iningi lethu sibone Abangani nezihlobo ezindaweni ezikude. Lokhu kuhle. Kepha, Kusenjalo, bekuyoba kuhle kakhulu ukuba sibonane buqu! Kukhona okuthile okukhethekile okwenzekayo uma sihlangana, sangane, futhi sikhulume ubuso nobuso.

Kuleliviki sizokhunjuzwa ngokuthi abantu babedalelwe ubudlelwane bobuso nobuso noMdali wabo. Isono, nokho, sabhubhisa lokhu kuxhumana okusondelene emandulo. Ukubuyisela lokho kuxhumana nezidalwa Zakhe ezithandekayo, uNkulunkulu unhlinzeka ngezindlela zokukhuluma kithina. Okuyiyona ndlela ebonakala kakhulu yokuxhumana Kwakhe Nesintu ngeyabantu abakhethwe ngokucophelela, abaziwa ngabaprofethi, abambula Imibiko Yakhe kithina. Lababaprofethi bavama ukuba nemibhalo eBhayibhelini, kepha hhayi ngasosonke isikhathi.

Icebo elikhulu likaNkulunkulu ngelokubuyisela umfanekiso Wakhe kithina, umfanenkiso owahlaphazwa yisono. Yilokhu okumayelana necebo losindiso: ukubuyiswa, ukubuyiswa okuyopheleliswa ngenkathi uNkulunkulu edala “amazulu amasha nomhlaba Omusha okuhlala kuyo ukulunga” (2 Pet. 3:13, NKJV).

Kuze kube yilesosikhathi, uNkulunkulu usakhuluma kithina, futhi usasebenzisa abaprofethi Bakhe ukwenza nje lokho.

_Funda isifundo saleliviki ukulungiselela iSabatha, uMfumfu 3._
