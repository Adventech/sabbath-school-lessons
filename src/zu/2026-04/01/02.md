---
title:  Ukuxhumana E-Eden
date:   27/09/2026
---

UGenesisi 1 no 2 ubika ngendaba yokuDala. UNkulunkulu wadala izwe elihle, futhi kulo wabeka u-Adamu no-Eva, isicoco sendalo. Abantu behluka kuzo zonke izidalwa emhlabeni ngoba babenziwe “ngomfanekiso kaNkulunkulu” (Gen. 1:27, NKJV), into eshiwo ngabo kuphela hhayi, ngokwesibonelo, mayelana nezinyoni noma ezinye izilwane. Ngenxa yalokho, kwakumele baphile izimpilo eziphelele nezichichimayo, babenamava othando ngezindlela zonke, futhi bahambe ngokuxhumana noNkulunkulu uqobo.

Futhi, siyafunda kuGenesisi 2:18-23 ukuthi uNkulunkulu wadala u-Eva emdalela u-Adamu ngoba, uNkulunkulu wathi “ ‘akukuhle umuntu abeyedwa’ ” (Gen. 2:18, NKJV). Kusukela ekuqaleni, ke, abantu babedalelwe ukuxhumana. Abesayensi yokuhlalisana kwabantu (Sociologists) namuhla bafundisa ukuthi ukuxhumana kwabantu kunikeza inzuzo eningi enyameni kanye nasemqondweni, ngenkathi umzwangedwa ungumbulali.

Akumangalisi ke, ukuthi emuva kokudala u-Eva, u-Adamu wababaza, “ ‘Lo useyithambo lamathambo ami nenyama yenyama yami; uyakubizwa ngokuthi indodakazi, ngokuba uthathwe endodeni’ ”(Gen. 2:23, NKJV). Lesisakhiwo somndeni esasungulwa ngowesilisa oyedwa kanye nowesifazane oyedwa behlanganiswe ngokugana singukumiswa kokuqala kokuhlalisana kwabantu uNkulunkulu asihlinzekela isintu, ukuze sona sibe namava aphelele obudlelwane ngokuxhumana. Akumangalisi futhi ukuthi omunye umgomo omkhulu kaSathane ngowokubulala isimo esiyisisekelo somndeni wokuqala.

`Funda uGenesisi 1:27, 28; Genesisi 2:15-17; noGenesisi 3:9. Iyiphi inhlobo yokuxhumana eyayikhona phakathi kukaNkulunkulu nabantu Ensimini yase-Edeni na?`

U-Adamu no-Eva babethakasela ukuxhumana kobuso nobuso noNkulunkulu. Njengezidalwa eziphusile, babekwazi ukuhlangana noNkulunkulu futhi balalele imiyalelo Yakhe. Njengabalondi bensimu emangalisayo kaNkulunkulu, bakhula olwazini nasemakhonweni njengalokhu babenakekela izitshalo nezilwane. Usuku ngalunye babeba nesikhathi esikhethekile noNkulunkulu “ekupheleni kosuku” (Gen. 3:8). ISabatha ngokukhethekile, ingxenye esemqoka yesenzo sikaNkulunkulu sokudala, kwakumele libe yisikhathi esiqokwe ngokukhethekile sokuxhumana phakathi kukaNkulunkulu nabantwana Bakhe (Gen. 2:2, 3; Eksod. 20:8-11). Ngalolosuku, abazali bethu bokuqala babebanamava othando lukaNkulunkulu kanye nokubanakelela Kwakhe ngendlela ekhethekileyo.

`Kulukhuni kithina namuhla, esiqhele ngezinkulungwane zeminyaka e-Eden, ukucabanga ngokuthi kwakunjani ngaphambi kwesono. Kepha, namanje, imvelo isibhekisa isibuyisela kanjani endalweni yokuqala kaNkulunkulu na?`
