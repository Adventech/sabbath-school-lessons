---
title:  Ukudingiswa Ensimini
date:   30/09/2026
---

Omunye wemiphumela emibi kakhulu yesono sika-Adamu no-Eve kwakungukwehlukana noNkulunkulu. Badingiswa Ensimini futhi bangakwazi Ukukhuluma noNkulunkulu ubuso nobuso. Umprofethi u-Isaya wabhala mayelana nalenselelo yesimo somuntu: “Ububi benu bunahlukanisile noNkulunkulu, nezono zenu zibusithile ubuso bakhe kini ukuba angezwa” (Isa. 59:2, NKJV).

Ngeqiniso, u-Adamu no-Eva babesengabantwana bakaNkulunkulu. Kepha, umphumela wesono wawungeke ugwemeke, futhi kwafuneka bephumile e-Eden. Kwakungeke kusafana emuva kokungamlaleli kwabo.

`Isono siguqule isintu ngezindlela eziningi (buka uGen. 3:14-24). Kodwa isono sikuguqule kanjani ukuxhumana kwethu noNkulunkulu na? (Buka futhi u-Eksod. 19:16-22.)`

Isono saguqula impilo yabantu ngezindlela esingeke sazicabanga, ngoba konke esikwaziyo ngumhlaba wesono. Ngenxa yesono, umhlaba “waqalekiswa” futhi wakhiqiza “ameva namakhakhasi.”Abantu babezoba nobuhlungu, futhi kwakuzomele “bajuluke” ukuze bakwazi ukuzihlinzekela ngokudla, ukuba ekugcineni bafe babuyele emhlabathini. Kuzo zonke izikhumbuzo zesono, ukufa kuseyikona okunamandla. Ngesikhathi esifanayo, futhi, ngenxa yesono salahlekelwa ngukuxhumana ubuso nobuso noNkulunkulu.

Nakuba kunjalo, u-Adamu no-Eva bathenjiswa isisombululo sokumelana nolinyazwa yisono. Nakuba abazali bethu bokuqala babengasayikumbona uNkulunkulu futhi bakhulume Naye ngokuqondile, baphuma e-Eden nethemba lamanje nelakusasa. UNkulunkulu wakususela kuYena futhi wachaza icebo Lakhe lokuhlenga.

UGenesisi 3:15, obizwa “ngesithembiso sokuqala sevangeli,” wawugcwele ithemba ngabo (nangathi), nakuba kwenzeke okwenzekile. Isithembiso soMhlengi ozayo sasizohlinzeka ubutha bamanje nesono kanye nethemba losindiso lwesintu oluzayo. Empeleni, ukuhlinzeka kukaNkulunkulu ngezembatho ku-Adamu no-Eva ngaphambi kokuba bashiye Insimu kwakuyisenzo esiphawula ukuhlengwa kwabo okuzayo. Ngokufa kanye nokuvuka kukaJesu, ukuxhumana noNkulunkulu kwakuzobuyiselwa (Johane 14:1-3).

Okungukuthi, khona lapho e-Eden, ngemuva nje kokungena kwesono, isintu sanikezwa ithemba ngesithembiso sokuthi inkinga yesono yayizoxazululwa ekugcineni. Ekungeneni nje kwesono, kwaba nesithembiso soMsindisi.

`Uzizwe unjani emuva kokwenza okuthile okwakungalungile na? Ukuba necala na? Amahloni na? Ukuzinyanya na? Kwaba yini imiphumela na? Ukholo lwakho kuNkulunkulu, kanye nesithembiso sevangeli, kwakugcina kanjani ukuba ungaphelelwa wukholo ngokuphelele na?`
