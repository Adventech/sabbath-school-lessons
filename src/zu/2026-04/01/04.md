---
title:  UNkulunkulu Ucinga Isintu
date:   29/09/2026
---

Siyabona kuGenesisi 3:9, 10 ukuthi uNkulunkulu wakususela kuYena ukucinga abantwana Bakhe abawileyo. Umbuzo “Ukuphi na?” wawungadinge mpendulo, ngoba uNkulunkulu wayekwazi okwakwenziwe ngu-Adamu no-Eva nokuthi babekuphi. Isono, nokho, sanqamula ukuxhumana okujwayelekile okwakukhona phakathi Kwakhe nesibili sokuqala sesintu. Ukukhathazeka okuphuthumayo kukaNkulunkulu kwakungokokubuyisela ukuxhumana nabantwana Bakhe, ukuhlangana nabo, kanye nokubanikeza ithemba ekudikibaleni kwabo. Empeleni, isenzo sesiHeberu uqara’, “ukubiza,” kuGenesisi 3:9, sivama Ukusetshenziswa ekuchazeni ubizo lukaNkulunkulu lokusungula ukuxhumana nabantu Bakhe.

`Funda uLuka 15:11-32. Lomfanekiso ukubonakalisa kanjani lokho okwenzeka kuGenesisi 3:9, 10 na?`

Ukucinga kukaNkulunkulu u-Adamu no-Eva kwembula uthando Lwakhe olungenamibandela ngabantwana Bakhe. UNkulunkulu akasideli (buka uLuka 15). Wenza konke anako ukubuyisela ukuxhumana Kwakhe kanye nathi, noma singaya kocasha. Ngaphambi kokubethelwa kukaJesu, ngenkathi uPetro emphika izikhathi ezintathu, uKristu “waphenduka wabuka” (Luka 22:61, NKJV) yena. Nangaphandle kwamazwi, Yena “wakhuluma” kuPetro futhi wafuna ukufinyelela kumngani Wakhe osephutheni.

Qaphela, kuGenesisi 3:11-13, indlela isono, ngokuphazima, esaguqula ngayo ubudlelwane buka-Adamu no-Eva. Kunokuba bavume isono sabo ngamunye baqala ukusola abanye. U-Adamu wasola u-Eva futhi, ngokugudlile, uNkulunkulu, ngoba Yena wayemnikeze u-Eva. “ ‘Owesifazane Wena owanginikeza yena, unginikezile okomuthi, futhi mina ngadla,’ ” kusho u-Adamu (Gen. 3:12, NKJV). U-Eva, kusenjalo, wasola inyoka ngokungalaleli kwakhe. “ ‘Inyoka ingikhohlisile, futhi ngadla’ ” (Gen. 3:13) kwaba yisilandulo sakhe. Akekho kubo, okungenani uma bebuzwa okokuqala ngesono sabo, owavuma icala lakhe. Akukaguquki lutho eminyakeni eyizinkulungwane eziyisithupha.

Iqiniso ngukuthi isono sithikameze sonke isintu. Umpostoli uPawule usichaza kahle isimo sethu kwabaseRoma 7:15-24, lapho ebalula ngokusobala ubuhlungu bokulangazelela ukwenza okuhle, kepha uzithole wenza okuphambene: “Ngokuba lokho engikwenzayo, angikuqondi. Ngokuba lokho engifuna ukukwenza, kona angikwenzi; kodwa lokho engikuzondayo, yikona engikwenzayo” (Rom. 7:15, NKJV).

Ngubani okwazi lokhu na?

Okushiwo nguPawule kukhulu. Singabantu abalimele abaqhubeka nokwenza izinto okungamele bazenze. Ngubani ongenawo amava alokhu na?

Okuyithemba lethu kuphela, kusho uPawule, ngukuhlengwa “ngoJesu Kristu iNkosi yethu” (Rom. 7:25). Yingakho uNkulunkulu eqhubeka nokusicinga ukusiholela kuJesu, okungukuphela Kwakhe ongasibumbela emfanekisweni Wakhe futhi asisindise ekuchithakaleni okudalwe yisono.
