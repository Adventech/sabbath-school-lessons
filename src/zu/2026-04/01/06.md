---
title:  Abakhulumela UNkulunkulu
date:   01/10/2026
---

`Yini eyembulwa yizindimana ezilandelayo mayelana nendlela uNkulunkulu akhulume ngayo kubantu emuva kokungena kwesono na? (Funda uHeb. 1:1, 2; Rom. 1:19, 20; Num. 12:6; 2 Tim. 3:16, 17; Johane 14:7-9; Johane 10:27-30.)`

Ngaphandle kwesono, uNkulunkulu wathola izindlela eziningi zokuxhumana Nesintu esiwileyo. Enye yezindlela uNkulunkulu azembula ngayo kithina yingendalo. Indalo iyisambulo esinamandla sezenzo Zakhe ezimangalisayo. Ososayensi bamangaliswa yizimanga zomkhathi kanye nobunkimbinkimbi bezinto “ezilula” zempilo. Kepha indalo ibonkalisa futhi imiphumela Yokuwa, ngokufa kwakho konke okuphilayo njengesibonelo esinamandla.

Enye indlela uNkulunkulu akhulume ngayo nesintu ngeyabaprofethi—abathile Yena abakhethile njengabakhulumeli Bakhe. Ngokwesibonelo, uNkulunkulu wakhuluma kuNowa, Abrahama, Mose, noDaniyeli, embula intando Yakhe ngabantu Bakhe. Kumongo wayo, Imibiko kaNkulunkulu Yesiprofetho iyisikhumbuzo sokuthi Yena akabakhohliwe abantwana Bakhe futhi usenabo. Futhi nakuba abanye abaprofethi babhala lokho okufike kithina njengeZibhalo, abanye abazange babhale izincwadi zeBhayibheli.

Kepha, amazwi amaningi abaprofethi ayedluliswa ngeZibhalo (2. Tim. 3:16, 17). IBhayibheli liyisambulo ngoNkulunkulu ngoKwakhe. Kungamazwi Akhe lapho sifunda ukuthi Yena ungubani, lapho sibona uthando Lwakhe olungadikibali, futhi sithole ukuhola Kwakhe ngendlela okumele siphile ngayo.

Ngale kwalokhu, kungeBhayibheli lapho siqonde ukuthi thina singobani, siqhamuka kuphi, nokuthi siyakuphi. Kulo sithola inhloso kaNkulunkulu ngezimpilo zethu.

Kodwa okuyiyona ndlela esebenza kunazo zonke uNkulunkulu akhulume ngayo kithina ingoJesu Kristu, nembala, owembulwe eBhayibhelini. Njengalokhu uNkulunkulu acinga u-Adamu no-Eva Ensimini yase-Edene, uKristu weza “ukuzocinga nokusindisa lokho okwakulahlekile” (Luka 19:10). “ ‘Lowo obone Mina usembonile uBaba,’ ” kumemezela uJesu (Johane 14:9, NKJV). Ngokuphila, nokufa, kanye nokuvuka, uKristu wagcwalisa isithembiso esasinikezwe u-Adamu no-Eva e-Edene. Yena weza futhi wafa esikhundleni sethu ukuze thina sibe nokuphila Okuphakade ngaYe.

Umuva kokwenyukela ezulwini kukaJesu, uMoya Ongcwele uqhubekile nokusiholela “ ‘kulo lonke iqiniso’ ” kanye nokutshela thina “ ‘ngalokho okuzayo’ ” (Johane 16:13, NIV).

`Ingakanani induduzo oyithola ekwazini ukuthi uJesu, uthando Lwakhe, isimilo Sakhe, kanye nokuzinikela Kwakhe, kwembula kithina lokho uNkulunkulu uBaba ayikona ngempela na?`
