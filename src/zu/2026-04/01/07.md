---
title:  U-Ellen G. White
date:   02/10/2026
---

Ukuxhumana kukaNkulunkulu akupheli ezikhathini zeBhayibheli. Njengoba sizobona emavikini ezayo, uNkulunkulu ukhuluma kubantu ingunaphakade, futhi amazwi Akhe ayobonakaliswa ngezindlela zamanje aze abuye uKristu.

U-Ellen Gould Harmon White kanye nodadewabo owayeyiwele naye u-Elizabeth bazalwa ngoULwezi 26, 1827, e-Gorham, Maine. Babengothumbu ezingane ezingu-8 zika-Eunice no Robert Harmon. Eminyakeni embalwa, umndeni wahamba wayohlala edolobheni i-Portland, Maine, kulo u-Robert Harmon wayenethemba lokukhulisa imboni yakhe yokwakha izigqoko. Esemeninyaka engu-9, u-Ellen walimala ngendlela eyamthinta enyameni, emizweni, kanye nasemphakathini. Ngenkathi esepaki eduze kwakubo, uzakwabo waseklasini wajikijela itshe elamshaya ebusweni, lishiya u-Ellen esimweni sokungathi uqulekile amaviki amaningana. Lengozi yamisa ngesaquphe ukufunda kwakhe futhi yamshiya nezinselele zempilo yakhe yonke. Ukulimala ebusweni kwenza u-Ellen angakhululeki ngokubonakala kwakhe, wangafuna Ukubonakala kakhulu emphakathini.

Kukulengqikithi yokungaqiniseki lapho u-Ellen Harmon omncane aqala ukuzindla ngesiphelo sakhe kwezikamoya. Wayesaba uNkulunkulu futhi ezizwa engalifanele izulu. Lemizwa yokwesaba yaqina ngenkathi umndeni wakwa-Harmon wamukela imfundiso ka-Miller yokuza kwesibili kukaJesu maduzane. Nasemuva kokubhabhathizwa kwakhe ngokucwiliswa Ebandleni lama-Methodist ngo-1842, u-Ellen akazizwanga elufanele uthando lukaNkulunkulu. Wayekholwa ukuthi yena “ungolahlwe ingunaphakade” (Life Sketches of Ellen G. White, p. 29; also see pp. 20-31).

Phakathi kwemicabango ebuhlungu, u-Ellen omncane waba namaphupho amabili aqala ukuguqula umqondo wakhe ngoNkulunkulu. Kodwa kwaba ngukuhlangana kwakhe no-Levi Stockman, umshumayeli wase-Methodist, lapho aqonda khona futhi waluzwa uthando lukaNkulunkulu nomusa. Kwakusengathi uNkulunkulu wayekhulumisana nokudikibala kwakhe. “Ukholo lwabe selulawula inhliziyo yami,” kubhala yena kamuva. “Ngezwa ngiba nothando engingakwazi ukuluchaza ngoNkulunkulu futhi ngaba nobufakazi boMoya Wakhe wokuthi izono zami zazithethelelwe. Indlela engangibuka ngayo uBaba yaguquka. Ngasengibuka kuYena njengomzali omnene nothambileyo, hhayi umbusi onesihluku ophoqa abantu ekulaleleni obungaboni. Inhliziyo yami yamthanda ngothando olujulile noluqinisekile.”—Life Sketches of Ellen G. White, p. 39. Kusenjalo, u-Ellen White waqonda futhi imfundiso “yesimo sabafileyo” futhi kancane, kancane wabamba umqondo wokuthi asikho isihogo esivutha ingunaphakade. Lokhu kuqonda kwakhe okusha ngoNkulunkulu kanye nothando Lwakhe kwaguqula Ukubuka kwakhe ngokuphelele. Njengalokhu sizofunda ngoLwesihlanu ngamunye emavikini alandelayo, u-Ellen White wathanda uJesu ngendlela ejulile futhi wasebenzela Yena impilo yakhe yonke.
