### Funda Lokhu Kuleliviki
Eksod. 4:10-16, Luka 8:22-25, Johane 3:1-10, Izenzo 16:25-34, 1 Kor. 5:1-5, Luka 7:28.

> <p>Indimana Yenkumbulo</p>
> “Ngokuba izwi likaNkulunkulu liphilile, linamandla, libukhali kunezinkemba zonke ezisika nhlangothi zombili, lihlaba kuze kwahlukaniswe umphefumulo nomoya, amalungu nomnkantsha, lahlulela imicabango nezizindlo zenhliziyo” (Heberu 4:12, NKJV).

Ngo ULwezi 9, 1989, inkulumo ka-Günter Schabowski eyayisakazwa bukhoma kumabonakude wase-East Germany yamaKomanisi yaqala umnyakazo wokuwa kwe-Berlin Wall. Ekhuluma njengokhulumela abezindaba zase-East Germany, Schabowski wabheda inkulumo ngesikhathi ekhuluma kwi-tv. Kunokuba amemezela ukuthi abase-East Germany bangavunyelwa ukuvakashela i-Western Europe emuva kohlelo olude nolunzima lokuthola imvume efanele, wamane wathi abase-East Germeny bavumelekile ukuvakashela i-Western Europe. Lapho intatheli imbuza ukuthi ushintsho lwepholisi luyoba nini, u-Schabowski wathi, “Ngokwazi kwami, lokhu kuqala ukusebenza . . . manje, ngokungalibazisi.” Ngemizuzu embalwa abantu baqala ukutheleka emingceleni, befuna ukuvunyelwa badlule, njengalokhu uhulumeni ememezelile.

Kuvamile ukuthi abantu banikezwe Igunya lokukhulumela inkampani noma izwe. Kodwa kwenzekani ngalabo Abakhulumela uNkulunkulu na? Kuleliviki sizohlola Igunya abaprofethi basebhayibhelini abanalo, nokuthi yiliphi, uma kukhona, Igunya abaprofethi abangakaze babhale incwadi yeBhayibheli abanalo, njengo-Nathan umprofethi, uJohane Umbhabhathizi, kanye naye u-Ellen G. White.

_Funda isifundo saleliviki ukulungisela iSabatha lika uZibandlela 5._
