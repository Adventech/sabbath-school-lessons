---
title:  Igunya LeBhayibheli
date:   01/12/2026
---

Ibhayibheli lingumphumela weSambulo sesiprofethi nesiphefumulelwe sikaNkulunkulu. Liphendula imibuzo emikhulu yempilo futhi liyindlela eyodwa kaNkulunkulu yokuzembula kubantu. NgeZibhalo, sifunda isizathu sokuba khona kwabantu, isimilo nentando kaNkulunkulu, kanye nenhloso kaNkulunkulu ngezimpilo zethu. Ibhayibheli liyisilinganiso esiphakeme seqiniso, Igunya ngalo okumele senze izinqumo zempilo. Liyisisekelo sobuKrsitu sokubuka izwe kanye nesisekelo salo lonke iqiniso elembuliwe. Liyinkomba yokholo nokwenza komKristu.

Singalamukela noma silichithe iZwi likaNkulunkulu, futhi akudingekile ukuba size sifinyele ekushiseni amaBhayibheli ethu, njengalokhu kwenza Inkosi u-Jehoiakim (buka uJer. 36:22-27) ekuchitheni lona. Ukuliziba, nakuba sithi sikholwa kulo, kungaba yinkinga nako. Ngenkathi sithola futhi samukela iZwi likaNkulunkulu, singakwazi, njengeNkosi u-Josiah, ukuguqulwa futhi siholele nabanye kuNkulunkulu (buka u-2 Amakhosi 22:10-13).

Namuhla abantu bavama ukuya Ezibhalweni beyofuna amaphutha azo kanye nokuzijivaza. Abanye babona iBhayibheli lingahambisani nezinkambiso zamanje (livamile ukubanjalo, futhi kumele libenjalo). Abaningi balihleka usulu ngoba liphikisana nezinkambiso “zesayensi.”

EbuKristwini, iBhayibheli livama Ukusetshenziswa kuphela ekuphaseni isimo ezithile semfundiso kunokuba lisetshenziswe njengento yokukhula kwezikamoya kanye nokuguqulwa. Abanye bahumusha iBhayibheli ngendlela yokuthi lingabi negunya lomlando noma lokwenza ezimpilweni zabo zansuku zonke.

Enye yezindlela elula, mhlawumbe futhi nejwayelekile, yikuchitha Igunya LeBhayibheli ngeyokuliziba futhi ungathathi isikhathi sokufunda futhi usebenzise Izibhalo ezimpilweni zethu.

`Waba yini umphumela weZwi likaNkulunkulu elikhulunyiwe ezimweni ezilandelayo na? Buka uJer. 38:1-4, Johane 3:1-10, neZenzo 16:25-34.`

Ezikhathini zasendulo, izinto zokubhala zaziyimvela kancane futhi ziyinani elikhulu ngentengo. Ngaphezu kwalokhu, abantu abaningi babengakwazi ukufunda, ngalokho iZwi likaNkulunkulu elikhulunyiwe ngabaprofethi lalibalulekile.

Ngaphansi kwamandla kaMoya oNgcwele, iZwi likaNkulunkulu, likhulunyiwe noma libhaliwe, lingangena enhliziyweni ukwembula isono kanye nokuletha inguquko kanye nokubuyiselwa. Izwi lisibizela ekuphendukeni nasekulaleleni (Zenzo 5:39; buka futhi Heb. 4:12). Futhi lokho kuvama ukuba yisizathu abantu abaningi okumele ngabe bazi kangcono belichitha. Abayithandi imibango yalo ezimpilweni zabo.
