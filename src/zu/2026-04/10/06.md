---
title:  AbaProfethi Ababhale Ibhayibheli Nabangalibhalanga
date:   03/12/2026
---

`Funda u-2 Kronike 29:29 no 2 Kronike 9:29. Yini oyiqaphela ngabanye balaba baprofethi ababalulwe kulezizahluko ezimbili na?`

Kwakunabaprofethi abaningi abanjengo-Nathan, Gad, Ahijah, no-Iddo ababevunywa njengabaprofethi kepha izincwadi zabo azibanga yingxenye yeBhayibheli.

`Funda u-1 Amakhosi 11:29-39 no Luka 7:28. Lemibhalo ifundisani mayelana negunya labaprofethi ozincwadi zabo zingekho eBhayibhelini na?`

Nakuba singazi ukuthi kungani uNkulunkulu avumela imibiko yabanye abaprofethi ifakwe eBhayibhelini ngenkathi eminye ishiywa ngaphandle, siyazi ukuthi abaprofethi abangazange babeyingxenye yeBhayibheli nabo babephefumulelwe nguNkulunkulu. Ibhayibheli lifundisa ukuthi isipho zikamoya, kufaka nesipho sokuprofetha, siyoqhubeka kuze kube sekupheleni kwesikhathi. Ibhayibheli libikezela ukuthi kuyobakhona abaprofethi ngale kwebhayibheli, omibhalo yabo ingekho eBhayibhelini “ukuhlomisa abangcwele ngomsebenzi wokukhonza, nokufundisa umzimba kaKristu, size sonke sifinyelele ebunyeni bokholo” (Efes. 4:12, 13, NKJV), futhi bazobakhona “ ‘ngaphambi kokuza kosuku olukhulu nolwesabekayo lukaJehovah’ ” (Joweli 2:31, NKJV).

Emuva kokuba uDavide ephingile futhi wabulala, uNkulunkulu wathumela umprofethi u-Nathan Ukubhekana naye (2 Sam. 12:7-14). Nakuba u-Nathan engazange abhale ncwadi yebhayibheli, uDavide akazange alingabaze Igunya lika-Nathan, ngenxa yokuthi Igunya lemibhalo yabaprofethi lisekelwe ekuphefumulelweni kwabo, hhayi ekutheni bayingxenye noma cha yemibhalo yebhayibheli. Nakuba kunjalo, bonke abaprofethi beqiniso omibhalo yabo ingekho ebhayibhelini, ikhakulu abaprofethi abangemuva kweBhyibheli—futhi ngokukhethekile umprofethi wanamuhla njengo-Ellen G. White—bazibona bengaphansi kwegunya lebhayibheli. Imibiko yabo kwafuneka ivivinyiwe yiBhayibheli futhi ayilichithi iBhayibheli njengomthombo wokukholwa kanye nemfundiso.

`U-Ellen G. White wabhala, “UJehovah ulangazelela ukuba nifunde amaBhayibheli enu. Yena akanikezile okunye ukukhanya kokuthatha indawo yeZwi Lakhe. Lokhu kukhanya ngokokubuyisela imiqondo edidekile eZwini Lakhe, okungathi, uma lidliwe futhi lacolisiswa, lifana nokuphila komphefumulo.”—Selected Messages, book 3, p. 29. Siwalandela kangakani amazwi akhe njengebandla?`
