---
title:  Ukulamulela Igunya LikaNkulunkulu
date:   29/11/2026
---

Ngaphambi kokuba unikeza umuntu imvume yokukhulumela wena, ubungeke yini uqinisekise Ukukhetha othile omethembayo na? Bekuyobaluleka ukuthi lowo azi ukuthi umi kuphi futhi akwethule lokho ngokwethembeka.

`Funda u-Eksodusi 2:11-15 no Eksodusi 3:1-3, 10-13. Isikhathi sikaMose engumelusi samlungiselela kanjani ukuba abengokhulumela uNkulunkulu na?`

UMose wayechithe iminyaka engu-40 yokuqala yempilo yakhe ethola imfundo ephakeme yaseGibhithe, kodwa lokhu Kwakungeke kumlungiselele ukuba yisithunywa sikaNkulunkulu. Kwafuneka echithe iminyaka engu-40 elandelayo ehlane njengomalusi, kungekho zithikamezo zomhlaba. Ngezindlela eziningi kwakufuneka kukhishwe konke ayekufundile njengenkosana. Ngaphambi kokuba ahambisane ngokukhulula uIsrayeli, uMose kwafuneka azane naloNkulunkulu ayezomkhulumela.

`Funda u-Eksodusi 4:10-16. Yini esingayifunda kulengxoxo Ephakathi kukaNkulunkulu noMose ngendlela abaprofethi ababizwa ngayo nangendlela abasebenza ngayo na?`

UMose wacelwa ukuba adlale indima kaNkulunkulu, no-Aaron ethathe indawo yokuba ngumlomo kaMose (Eksod. 4:16; buka futhi u-Eksod. 7:1). Njengalokhu enza ngoMose no-Aaron, uNkulunkulu ulekelela abaprofethi Bakhe ukuthola indlela engcono yokwethula umbiko Yena abanikeza wona.

UMose wayazi ukuthi wayehlulekile ngaphambilini. Wakhumbula ukunqwamana kwakhe kokugcina nomIsrayeli wakubo, ngenkathi Igunya lakhe lingatshaziwe (Eksod. 2:14). UNkulunkulu wakuqonda Ukwesaba kukaMose. Wabhekana nokwesaba kwakhe ngesineke. Zonke isizathu zezaba zasezisusiwe, kodwa, nakuba kunjalo, uMose wamelana nobizo. Ukwesaba kwakhe kwaphenduka ukungakholwa. Ngokunqikaza kwakhe, wayengabaza ikhono likaNkulunkulu lokumsebenzisa yena. Yilaphoke uNkulunkulu anikeza umyalelo oqinile wokwenza umprofethi onqikazayo ukuba ahambe (Eksod. 3:14-17).

`Usenokungabi ngumprofethi, kodwa yini uNkulunkulu akubizele yona empilweni yakho na? Iyiphi indlela engcono yokuzilungiselela lolubizo na?`
