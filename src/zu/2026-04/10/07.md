---
title:  Ukukhuluma NgeGunya
date:   04/12/2026
---

Abaprofethi bakhuluma kithina ngegunya nangokufanele isikhathi ngoba uNkulunkulu ubakhethile bona njengezithunywa Zakhe. Kukhona abaprofethi omibhalo yabo isebhayibhelini, abaprofethi omibhalo yabo ingekho ebhayibhelini, kanye nabaprofethi abangale kwebhayibheli (labo ababonakala ngemuva kwezikhathi zeTestamente Elisha). Bone babephefumulwe ngokulinganayo, futhi imibiko yabo yayinophawu negunya lasezulwini.

U-Ellen G. White ukuleliqembu labaprofethi elingemuva kwebhayibheli, futhi omibhalo yakhe ingafakwanga eBhayibhelini. Kepha ama-Seventh-day Adventist awayiboni imibhalo yakhe injengethayisela eBhayibhelini. Noma ilingana nalo. Imibhalo yakhe, nokho, ingeyesikhathi esifanele futhi ikhuluma ngegunya, iphuzu elashiwo ngu-Ellen G. White mathupha: “ ‘Kulezincwadi engizibhalayo, kubufakazi engibuthweleyo, mina ngethula kinina lokho uJehovah akwethule kimina. Angibhali lutho olungumcabango wami nje. Ziyilokho uNkulunkulu akubonse kimi ngombono—imisebe eyigugu yokukhanya ephuma esihlalweni sobukhosi.’ ”— Selected Messages, book 1, p. 29.

Ngokuhambisana NeBhayibheli, u-Ellen G. White ukhomba ubunjalo besono nokudinga kwethu uJesu, okunguye kuphela ithemba lokuphila Okuphakade, okunikezwa ngomusa kubo bonke abathi ngokholo bazibangele kona, enye indikimba enamandla emibikweni yakhe.

Kuyo yonke imibiko yakhe (okukanye imibiko kaNkulunkulu ngaye) sibona impi phakathi kokulunga kanye nobubi, kuqala ngokuwa kukaLusifa ezulwini, futhi iqhubeke njalo kuze kube sekupheleni kwesikhathi, lapho isono nobubi,kanye noSathane nezingelosi zakhe eziwileyo, kuyobhujiswa ingunaphakade. Imibhalo ka-Ellen G. White inikeza ukubona izimiso zebhayibheli ngokwengqikithi yesikhathi sokugcina. Ukhuluma, ngokwesibonelo, ngengozi yokuhlanganisa ibandla nombuso kanye nokubaluleka kwenkululeko yezenkolo; axwayise mayelana nokonakala kokuziphatha; aphakamise umthetho kaNkulunkulu kanye nokubaluleka kwawo; futhi nakuba ukuqonda kwethu, ngokwesibonelo, “Uphawu Lwesilo” nembala kungasekeliwe emibhalweni yakhe, amazwi akhe ayasinikeza ukubona okumangalisayo kwezigameko zokugcina.

Uphinde futhi agcizelele ukusebenza kokuphila njengomKristu. Imibiko yakhe mayelana nezikamoya, ezempilo, imfundo, ubudlelwane bomndeni, ukuba ngumzali, impilo yokuziphatha kahle, kanye nokuningi isize abaningi baguqule indlela yabo yokuphila futhi ngalokho baphile kangcono emazingeni amaningi.

Imibhalo ka-Ellen G. White ayikho eBhayibhelini, kodwa uNkulunkulu umsebenzisile yena ukuhola amakholwa aphila ezinsukwini zokugcina zomlando wezwe. Imibhalo yakhe isakhuluma ngegunya Lesiprofetho kanye nokufaneleka kwesikhathi.
