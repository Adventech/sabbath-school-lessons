---
title:  Igunya LikaJesu
date:   30/11/2026
---

`Kwakuyini isisekelo segunya likaJesu na? Kwakuyini ubufakazi baleligunya na? buka u-Mat. 21:23-27 no Johane 17:2.`

Esafundisa uJesu, abapristi beza kuYe futhi bafuna ukwazi ukuthi ngubani owayemnikeze Igunya lokwenza lomsebenzi. Nembala, uJesu wayengafundanga ezikoleni zendawo, futhi engatholanga ukuqeqeshwa ezikoleni ezivamili zorabi. Kunokuba aphendule umibuzo yabo, uJesu wakhomba indlela eya empendulweni, ngokubuza abafuna ukwazi ngegunya elisobala elalibonakaliswa nguJohane Umbhabhathizi. Ngokungavumi ukuphendula, banqaba amandla kaNkulunkulu ayegcobe umsebenzi kaJohane futhi bachitha negunya likaJesu yena, njengoJohane, owayethunywe nguNkulunkulu.

`Izindimana ezilandelayo zisitshelani ngegunya likaJesu na? Mat. 7:28, 29; Marko 1:21-27; Luka 8:22-25; Luka 9:1; Johane 5:25-27; no Johane 7:38.`

Lonke Igunya lingelikaJesu njengoMdali wabantu (Johane 1:3) kanye noMsindisi (Rom. 3:24). Yena uyiZwi likaNkulunkulu. Igunya lezulu ayenalo uJesu ngesikhathi sokuphila nokusebenza Kwakhe emhlabeni lalingakwazi ukulingiswa noma othile azakhele lona. Imfundiso kaJesu yamangaza abalaleli ngoba Yena wayefundisa njengaLowo onegunya (Mat. 7;29).

Onke amaVangeli abhala ngegunya lobuprofethi bukaJesu. UJesu wayethethelela izono (Marko 2:10, 11), exosha amadimoni (Marko 3:15) futhi azi ukuthi abantu bacabangani (Johane 2:24, 25). Yena wavusa abafileyo futhi wanikeza ukuphila Okuphakade (Johane 10:28). Eselapha emhlabeni njengoMhlengi wethu, uJesu wasebenzisa Igunya ayelinikezwe nguNkulunkulu uBaba (Johane 17:2), futhi wayencike ku, futhi ebambisene no Baba (Johane 5:19) ngazozonke izikhathi, imvamisa ebhekisa Ezibhalweni, ayethi, zifakaza ngaYe. UJesu futhi wayenegunya elingaphikiswa ekwazi ukunikeza lelogunya kubafundi Bakhe (Marko 6:7). NjengeZwi likaNkulunkulu, ubufakazi Bakhe futhi babuqhamuka kubaprofethi Yena ayekhulume ngabo (Samb. 19:10).

`Nabo bonke ubufakazi uJesu abunikeza, abanye abantu baqhubeka nokumlahla. Singaqikelela kanjani ukuba singaweli kunoxhaka ofanayo wokungakholwa, sinabo bonke ubufakazi bokukholwa na?`
