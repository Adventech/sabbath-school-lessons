---
title:  Igunya Lebandla
date:   02/12/2026
---

UHoseya 12:13 ukubeka obala ukuthi “ngomprofethi uJehovah wakhipha uIsrayeli eGibhithe, futhi nangomprofethi wagcinwa” (NKJV). Ukuvikela kanye nokuhola abantu bakaNkulunkulu kwakuyingxenye eyakhayo nesemqoka yomsebenzi wabaprofethi basebhayibhelini.

Uma sifunda imibhalo efana naBahluleli 4:4-8, 2 IziKronike 20:14-17, Eksodusi 12:1-3, no Duteronomi 1:9-16, siyabona ukuthi abaprofethi babebalulekile ekwembuleni icebo likaNkulunkulu lokuhola abantu Bakhe. Ngezikhathi zempi nezinkinga, abaprofethi babeyindlela ngayo uNkulunkulu ayinikeza umkhombandlela, inkuthazo, kanye nethemba kubantu Bakhe kanye nabaholi babo, njengalokhu enza ngo-Jehaziel no-Deborah. Abaprofethi babenikeza futhi ukuqondisa mayelana nemikhosi kanye nokukhonza (Eksod. 12:1-3). Kwezinye izikhathi, uNkulunkulu wayekhuluma kubaprofethi mayelana nokubuswa kwezizwe. Ngokufushane, ayikho ingxenye yempilo kaIsrayeli eyayingalawulwa futhi ingaqondiswa nguNkulunkulu ngezithunywa Zakhe.

`Yiziphi ezinye izingxenye lapho isipho sokuprofetha esalekelela khona ibandla nobuholi bebandla lokuqala na? Zenzo 6:1-7, 1 Kor. 5:1-5, 1 Kor. 7:10-16, Titu 1:5.`

Cabanga nisungula Inhlangano entsha ngaphandle komqulu oyisicacisi noma umholi obonakalayo. Lenhlangano nembala izoba nezinselelo ezinkulu. Ngomqondo othile sasinjalo isimo ebandleni lokuqala lobuKristu. UJesu wayesenyukele ezulwini futhi engasekho phakathi kwabafundi Bakhe. Babenesiqondiso esisobala Yena ayebanike sona: “ ‘Hambani emhlabeni wonke futhi nishumayele’ ” (Marko 16:15, NKJV). Nakuba abaPostoli Abayishumi Nambili babekhethiwe, lwalungekho uhlelo lwenhlangano lokubasiza baqale.

Ibandla lokuqala labhekana nezinselele eziningi, njengokuhleleka kwebandla, isimo somsebenzi, kanye nesidingo sokukhomba futhi kukhuzwe imfundiso yamanga nokungaziphathi kahle phakathi kwabo. Ngendlela eqondile, isipho sesiprofetho phakathi kwebandla lokuqala sadlala indima esemqoka ekuligcineni nasekulivikeleni (Zenzo 13:1, 2). Kulukhuni, empeleni Ukucabanga ukuthi lalingaqala kanjani ibandla lokuqala ngaphandle kosizo lwesipho sesiprofetho ekulekeleleni ukuhlela nokuqhubela phambili nomsebenzi.

`Abanye abantu babukeka bengayingabaza “inkolo ehlelekile.” Noma yiziphi izinselelo, iyiphi inzuzo yokuba yingxenye yebandla elihlekile na? Yini ongayinikela, futhi uyithole, ngokuba yingxenye yalokho okukhulu kunawe na?`
