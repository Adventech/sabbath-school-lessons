---
title:  Abaprofethi BeSifazane
date:   14/10/2026
---

`Funda u-Eksodusi 15:20, Abahluleli 4:4, 2 Amakhosi 22:14, no Nehemiya 6:14. Lezizindimana zisifundisani ngendima yobuprofethi na?`

Kunobufakazi obuningi Ezibhalweni bokuthi uNkulunkulu wabiza nabesifazane ekubeni ngabaprofethi. (Buka Ukusetshenziswa kwisiHeberu kweGama-nevi’ah [Eksod. 15:20] nengama lesiGriki prophtis [Luka 2:36].) KuMika 6:4, u-Miriam ubalwa noMose no-Aroni njengalabo abathunyelwe kubantu. U-Miriam wethulwa njengomprofethikazi owacula Ingoma yokunqoba ngenkathi abakwaIsrayeli bewele Ulwandle Olubomvu (Eksod. 15:20, 21). Kepha, lomprofethikazi, njengozakwabo, wayenazo izici, njengoba umoya wakhe oyihlazo ngakuMose nonkosikazi wakhe woMtopiya uveza, izenzo lomprofethikazi ajeziselwa zona (buka uNum. 12:1-10).

Siyawubona umsebenzi womunye Umprofethi endabeni ka-Deborah, owayengumahluleli kwaIsrayeli (Abahl. 4:1-4). Empeleni, kunobufakazi obucacile bokuthi u-Deborah (Abahl. 4:4) wathola isambulo kuNkulunkulu (Abahl. 4:49, 14-22). Ingoma yakhe yokunqoba (Abahl. 5:2-31) ingukuvuma ukungenelela kukaNkulunkulu futhi yayinemfundiso ejulile esizweni ngalesosikhathi somlando.

`Funda u-2 Amakhosi 22:11-14. Kungani amadoda Enkosi u-Josiah aya ku-Huldah na? Kumele lokhu kusitsheleni ngendima yakhe ebalulekile esizweni na?`

U-Huldah “umprofethikazi,” wabuzwa ngamadoda asesikhundleni esiphakeme esigungu seNkosi u-Josiah (2 Amakhosi 22:14, 2 Kron. 34:22) emuva kokuba efunde amazwi “Encwadi yoMthetho” (2 Amakhosi 22:8, NKJV) (okukholakala ukuthi kwakunguDuteronomi) enkosini, eyathi, ngokuphendula, “yadabula izingubo zayo” (2 Amakhosi 22:11, NKJV). Lokhu ngokusobala kwakuwudaba olubalulekile, futhi nenkosi yaluthatha kanjalo, okuyingakho amadoda aqonda ngqo ku-Huldah. Wamukela umbiko ovela kuNkulunkulu mayelana nokwahlulelwa kukaJuda. Izinguquko zika-Josiah zaba ngumphumela wemibiko Yesiprofetho sika-Huldah futhi zagwema ukwahlulela kukaNkulunkulu phezu kwabantu Bakhe.

`Abaprofethi besilisa, abaprofethi besifazane, nakuba bebizwe nguNkulunkulu, nabo babeyizoni ezidinga umusa. Yiliphi ithemba ongalithatha ezindabeni zabo na?`
