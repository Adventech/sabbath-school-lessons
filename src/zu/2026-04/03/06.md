---
title:  Abaprofethi Bamanga
date:   15/10/2026
---

Noma yini eyenziwa nguNkulunkulu, uSathane unezakhe izintombumbulu. Kuyafana nangabaprofethi. UIsrayeli wabhekana nabaprofethi beqiniso nabamanga. Ukusemqoka ngukwazi umehluko.

`Funda uJeremiya 14:1-15. Kwenzekani lapha, futhi uJehovah wabathatha kanjani labo ababekhuluma amanga njenga-“baprofethi” egameni Lakhe na?`

KuJeremiya 14, uNkulunkulu wanikeza uJeremiya umbiko wokwahlulela uJuda, ukwahlulela okwakuzoza ngesimo senkemba (impi), indlala, nobhubhane. Abaprofethi Bamanga ngalesosikhathi baphikisa umbiko kaJeremiya. UJeremiya ubachaza bona ngamazwi asobala kakhulu: “Futhi uJehovah wathi kimi, ‘Abaprofethi baprofetha amanga egameni Lami. Angibathumanga, angibayalezanga, angikhulumanga kubo; baprofetha kuwe umbono wamanga, ukubhula, into Eyize, kanye nenkohliso yezinhliziyo zabo’ ” (Jer. 14:14, NKJV).

Abaprofethi bamanaga bakhohlakele ngoba umthombo wabo weziprofetho uvama ukuba “ngonkulunkulu” Bamanga ababakhonzayo (Jer. 23:13, 14). UNkulunkulu uyaxwayisa, “ ‘Angibathumanga, angibayalezanga, angikhulumanga kubo’ ” (Jer. 14:14, NKJV). Bona bangaba- “ ‘profethi benkohliso yezinhliziyo zabo’ ” (Jer. 23:26, NKJV). U-Ezekiel wayalezwa ukuba aprofethe ngokumelene “ ‘nabaprofethi . . . abaprofetha ngokusuka ezinhliziyweni zabo’ ” (Ezek. 13:2, NKJV).

Abaprofethi bamanga bavama ukulandela imibono edumile. Kwakunjalo ku-1 Amakhosi 22, ngenkathi iqembu “labaprofethi” abangu-400 libuthana ngokuyaleza kweNkosi u-Ahab (1 Amakhosi 22:6). Ngezwi elilodwa baluleka inkosi ukuba ilwe nabase-Ramoth Gilead. U-Ahab waxoxisana no-Jehoshaphat, inkosi wakwaJuda, emuva kokuzwa iseluleko sesiprofetho sabaprofethi baka-Ashera. U-Jehoshephat wakhuthaza u-Ahab ukuba athole iseluleko kumprofethi u-Micaiah sokuthi baye yini empini noma cha. “Inceku eyayithunywe ukuyobiza u-Micaiah yathi kuye, ‘lalela manje, amazwi abaprofethi ngokuvumelana akhuthaza inkosi. Ngiyacela, izwi lakho alifane nelabo, khuluma inkuthazo’ ” (1 Amakhosi 22:13, NKJV).

Incindezi abhekana nayo u-Micaiah yokuqamba amanga kungenzeka yayifana nencindeza eyayizwakala kubaprofethi bamanga baka-Ashera, kodwa u-Micaiah akahambisananga nombono weningi; kunalokho, walinda ukuba uNkulunkulu asho ukuthi yena athini (1 Amakhosi 22:14).

`Noma unguye noma awuyena Umprofethi, kulula ngasosonke isikhathi ukulandela into edumile. Ngokubona ukungcola kwesikompilo lethu, kungani lokho kungewona umoqondo omuhle na?`
