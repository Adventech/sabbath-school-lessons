### Funda Lokhu Kuleliviki
Jakobe 5:17, 18; 1 Amakhosi 19:1-16; 1 Amakhosi 22:1-14; Eksod. 15:20; 2 Amakhosi 22:11-14; Jer. 14:1-15.

> <p>Indimana Yenkumbulo</p>
> “UJehoshafati wema, wathi ‘Ngizweni, Juda nani bakhileyo eJerusalema: Kholwani nguJehova uNkulunkulu wenu, niyakuqiniswa; kholwani ngabaprofethi bakhe, niyakuphumelela’” (2 Kronike 20:20, NKJV).

I -Global Positioning System (GPS) isiza abahambi ukuba bazi ukuthi bakuphi, nokuthi bayaphi, futhi ize inikeze nezixwayiso zezingozi. Abahambi bezikamoya abaya ezulwini nabo badinga ukuholwa manje nakusasa ngohlelo lwasezulwini lokuthi bakuphi. Enye yezindlela uNkulunkulu akwenza ngayo lokhu ezikhathini ezidlule kwakungokusebenza kwabaprofethi beTestamente Elidala. Ezinkulumweni zabo, izeluleko, iziprofetho, izinkondlo, namaculo, sithola okuningi mayelana neningi labaprofethi abethula Imibiko kaNkulunkulu kubantu.

Isifundo saleliviki sihlola izincazelo zeTestamente Elidala zabaprofethi, izincazelo esisiza thina siqonde Isipho sesiprofetho njengalokhu sibonakaliswa ngezinye zalezizinceku zikaNkulunkulu.

Njengalokhu sizobona, lababantu babefana nathi, ngokuthi babebuthaka besaba, bethukuthela, futhi bedikibala. Impilo kubona yayingelula, kodwa yayigcwele izinselele nobunzima, njengoba injalo kithina sonke.

Yini, ke, esingayifunda ngokubuka idlanzana lalezizinceku zikaNkulunkulu ezibizwe ngokukhethekile na?

_Funda isifundo saleliviki ukulungiselela iSabatha, uMfumfu 17._
