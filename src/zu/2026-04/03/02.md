---
title:  Ubuntu BabaProfethi
date:   11/10/2026
---

Njengalokhu sibonile ngeviki elidlulile, uJehovah wasebenzisa u-Eliya umprofethi ukwenza umsebenzi omkhulu kwaIsrayeli, nakuba kunokumelana okuqinile. Lokho uJehovah ayekufezile ngaye kwakumangalisa. Kepha . . . yini na?

`Zindla ngendlela uJakobe ethula ngayo u-Eliya, kuJakobe 5:17, 18. Kungabe uchaza ukuthini ngokuthi u-Eliya waye “nesimo esifana nesethu” (NKJV) na?`

U-Eliya waba nempumelelo emangalisayo Entabeni Ikameli (1 Amakhosi 18); uNkulunkulu wayesebenze ngendlela enamandla ngaye, futhi esinye sezimangaliso esithathekisayo emlandweni weTestamente Elidala senzeka: ngomsindo wamazwi ka-Eliya, umlilo wehla uvela ezulwini (1 Amakhosi 18:38)! Wawungacabanga ukuthi lendoda kaNkulunkulu yajabula kakhulu emuva kwento efana nalena.

`Funda u-1 Amakhosi 19:1-16. Kusitshelani lokhu ngesimo semizwa somprofethi kaNkulunkulu na?`

“ ‘Kwanele! Manje, Jehovah, thatha umphefumulo wami, ngokuba angingcono kunawobaba bami!’ ” (1 Amakhosi 19:4, NKJV). Lokhu kuzwakala njengomuntu ophethwe yingcindezi. Kungani na? Okwenzeka Entabeni Ikameli kwakungesona isenzo esingalinganiseka, nembala; kodwa, kusenjalo, kukhona okunye okwenzeka lapha.

Mhlawumbe umongo utholakala kulawa mazwi akhe, “ ‘Ngishisekele nokushisekela uJehova uNkulunkulu Sebawoti, ngokuba abantwana bakwa-Israyeli balahlile isivumelwano sakho, badilizile ama-altare akho, babulele abaprofethi bakho ngenkemba, kusele mina ngedwa; sebefuna umphefumulo wami ukuba bawususe’ ” (1 Amakhosi 19:10, NKJV). Lokhu kwazwakala sengathi ulilela lokho akholwa ukuthi kungukwehluleka kwakhe Emsebenzini. Ukuhlubuka kuyaqhubeka, ngale kwemizamo yakhe, sengathi nguye kuphela owayengaguqula, noma yini ekugcineni eyenzeka lapha, siyabona ubuntu nobuthakathaka balendoda kaNkulunkulu. Ngamanye amazwi, u-Eliya wayengumuntu owaye “nesimo esifana nesethu.”

`Kungani kungaba lula ngezinye izikhathi ukuba nomuzwa “wokuhluleka” empilweni yakho noma emizamweni yakho na? Yiziphi izithembiso zeBhayibheli ongazithatha ukukusiza ubhekane nokudikibala na?`
