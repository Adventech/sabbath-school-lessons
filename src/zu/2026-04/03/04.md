---
title:  Imisebenzi YoMprofethi
date:   13/10/2026
---

`Funda u-1 Samuweli 8:4-22 no Levitikus 8:10-13. Babesebenza kanjani abaprofethi ngokuhlobene namakhosi nabapristi na?`

Abaprofethi babexhumana namakhosi ezimweni ezinhle nakwezimbi. Ngenkathi uIsrayeli efuna inkosi (1 Sam. 8:4-22), wathi ufuna “inkosi ezosahlulela njengazo zonke izizwe” (1 Sam. 8:5). Ewu isibonelo esidabukisayo nesinamandla sengozi abantu bakaNkulunkulu ababhekane nayo kuzo zonke izikhathi: isifiso sokufana nababazungezile. Kulokhu, babengazi ukuthi lesisicelo sizoza nani, nakuba babexwayisiwe (1 Sam. 8:11-18) nguNkulunkulu ngomprofethi Wakhe. Ngokwesifiso sabo, futhi nangaphandle kwesixwayiso kubo, kungekudala uSamuweli wagcoba inkosi yokuqala yakwaIsrayeli, eyaziwa emlandweni weBhayibheli njengeNkosi uSawule.

Izincwadi zeTestamente Elidala u-1 no 2 Amakhosi no-1 no 2 Izikronike zibika ngohlobo kanye nezinga lokubusa elabonakaliswa ngamakhosi akwaIsrayeli noJuda. Umlando ongcwele ubonisa ukuthi lawo amakhosi enza ububi ayemaningi ukwedlula lawo enza okulungileyo phambi kukaNkulunkulu. Omunye wemisebenzi engumongo wabaprofethi kwakungukusiza amakhosi angathembekile abuyele ekulaleleni umthetho kaNkulunkulu. Ngalokho, lababaprofethi babebhekana nokwesatshiswa, abanye balinyazwe futhi balulawe ngamakhosi ayeholele abantu bakaNkulunkulu kude Naye (1 Amakhosi 18-19, 2 Kron. 24:20-22).

Abaprofethi babexhumana futhi nabapristi. UMose, Umprofethi kaNkulunkulu, wagcoba u-Aroni namadodana akhe njengabapristi bokuqala kwaIsrayeli (Lev. 8:10-13). Kwakubakhona izikhathi lapho abapristi bamukela amazwi okukhuzwa nguNkulunkulu ngabaprofethi (Hos. 6:9, Mal. 2:1-3). Esinye isibonelo esithathekisayo yileso sawo wonke umndeni ka-Eli, owakhuzwa ngezenzo zawo ezikhohlakeleyo (1 Sam. 2:27-36). Lokhu kwakungokunye ukukhuzwa okuqinile ngenkohlakalo eyandile phakathi kwabantu ababenikwe amalungelo akhethekileyo.

Abaprofethi babengothisha babantu (2 Izikronike 34). Babethula Imibiko esuka kuNkulunkulu kubapristi, amakhosi, kanye nakwisizwe (Jer. 5:31, Ezek. 22:26). Abaprofethi babekhuluma nezizwe ezibakakile banikeze icebo likaNkulunkulu ngazo, indikimba ephindaphindwa izikhathi eziningana emibikweni yabaprofethi beTestamente Elidala (Isa. 60:1-3; Isa. 62:1, 2). Abaprofethi babelindeleke ukuba bangadlulisi nje Imibiko ngokwethembeka uNkulunkulu ayebanike yona kodwa ngezinye izikhathi babetshelwa ukuba bayibhale phansi futhi bayilondoloze (kanye nabo bonke ubuthakathaka obuhambisana nayo), babengamadoda kanye nabesifazane bomkhuleko, iZwi, kanye nokusebenzela uNkulunkulu nabantu Bakhe (Jer. 42:4, 2 Amakhosi 22:15-17).
