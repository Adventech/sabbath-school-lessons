title:  Engaphelele Kepha Evuma
date:   16/10/2026
---

Njengoba sesibonile, izimpilo zabaprofethi beTestamente Elidala zikwenza kube sobala ukuthi akukaze kubelula ukuba yisithunywa sikaNkulunkulu. U-Ellen G. White waba namava okwesaba afana nawabaprofethi basebhayibhelini. Njengoba sifudile ngoLwesihlanu oludlule, wayemusha, enamahloni, futhi engaphilile kahle ngenkathi ethola ubizo lukaNkulunkulu. Lapho umbono wesibili uqinisa ubizo lwakhe, u-Ellen Harmon [White] waqhubeka novalo ngobizo lukaNkulunkulu, ebhala: “Emuva nje kweviki kwenzekile lokho uJehovah wanginikeza omunye umbono, futhi wangibonisa izinhlupheko okuzomele ngidlule kuzo; ukuthi kuzofuneka ngihambe futhi ngixoxela abanye ngalokho ayengembulele kona; ukuthi ngizobhekana nempikiswano enkulu, futhi ngihlupheke kakhulu emoyeni. . . .

Lombono wangihlupha kakhulu. Impilo yami yayingeyinhle kakhulu, futhi ngangineminyaka nje engu-17 ubudala. . . . Ngakhuleka ngokuqiniseka ukuba lomthwalo ubekwe komunye umuntu. Kodwa konke ukukhanya engangingakuthola kwakuyilokhu, ‘Yazisa abanye ngalokho engikwembule kuwena.’ Ngangingavumelananga nokuya ezweni. Ngokwemvelo ngangingazithembi. . . . ngalibuka ngalifisa ithuna. Ukufa kwabonakala kungcono kunemithwalo okumele ngiyithwale.”—Spiritual Gifts, vol. 2, pp. 35, 36.

Ngabe uqaphelile ukuthi u-Ellen Harmon [White] waqoka ukufa kunokwamukela ubizo lwakhe na? Emuva kokukhuleka okuningi nokukhuthaza kwabakholwa naye, wavuma futhi wanikela impilo yakhe ekulandeleni “ukuthuma Kwakhe.”—Ellen G. White, Spiritual Gifts, vol. 2, p. 38.

Wayezoba yilona zwi elabanethonya ukwedlula onke Ebandleni lama-Seventh-day Adventist. Nakuba kunjalo, u-Ellen Harmon [White] wayengumuntu futhi enezici zakhe nokuhlupheka enyameni, izinselele zomndeni, nezinkinga zobudlelwane. Kepha phakathi kubuntu bakhe, sibona isifundo esikhulu: uNkulunkulu ukhetha abantu abangaphelele abavumayo ukusebenzela Yena.

Ngaphansi kokululeka kukaMoya Ongcwele, umsebenzi ka-Ellen G. White wokuprofetha wakhuthaza amakholwa futhi wawaxhumanisa namaqiniso Ebhayibheli. Ngo-1860, wadlala indima yokuhola ekuthuthukiseni ibandla elalisafufusa lama-Seventh-day Adventist kanye nomsebenzi walo wokumemezela imibiko yezingelosi ezintathu emhlabeni. UNkulunkulu wayezohola lamakholwa ukuhlela Ibandla lama-Seventh-day Adventist ngo-1863. Eminyakeni elandelayo, izwi lokuprofetha lika-Ellen G. White laba nethonya ekusunguleni Izinhlelo zama-Adventist zempilo nemfundo emhlabeni wonke. Kodwa, okuyilona galelo elibaluleke kunawo onke omsebenzi wakhe yimibhalo yakhe, efinyele cishe kumakhasi angu-100,000. Imibhalo ka-Ellen G. White Ephefumulelwe iyaqhubeka nokukhuluma emhlabeni wonke namuhla.
