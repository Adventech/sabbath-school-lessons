---
title:  Ukukhomba Abaprofethi Beqiniso
date:   12/10/2026
---

Emlandweni wokuqala wakwaIsrayeli kukhona ubufakazi babaprofethi bamanga (Dut. 18:20). Ngakhoke, uNkulunkulu akazange abashiye abantu Bakhe bengenaso iseluleko ngabaprofethi Bakhe beqiniso.

Njengabamukeli besambulo sikaNkulunkulu nokuphefumulelwa, abaprofethi beqiniso ngokwethemba, ngokweqiniso, nangokuqiniseka badlulisa Imibiko kaNkulunkulu, nakuba ngezinye izikhathi bengathandeki kulabo ababusayo.

`Funda u-1 Amakhosi 22:1-14. Kusitshelani lokhu ngendlela yokusebenza kwabaprofethi abathembekileyo na?`

Ukuba kube nenkosi ethi ngomprofethi “ ‘Ngiyamzonda, ngoba akaprofethi okuhle ngami kepha okubi’ ” (1 Amakhosi 22:8, NKJV) kusho okuningi ngenkosi, kepha okungaphezulu ngomprofethi. Nembala, wayeyindoda eyayingesabi Ukukhuluma iqiniso, nakuyo inkosi uqobo—hhayi indlela yokuthandwa yinkosi, okungenani.

U-Micaiah wema yedwa emelene nabaprofethi abangu-400 bamanga lapho ethula iqiniso ayelambulelwe. Ebhekene nokuphoqwa ukuba aguqule Imibiko emibi evela kuJehovah ukuze ajabulise inkosi ekhohlakele u-Ahab nomkahe, u-Jezebel (1 Amakhosi 22:13, 14), u-Micaiah wethula iqiniso likaNkulunkulu, ngale kokuthi lingamukelwa kanjani.

`Funda u-1 Samuweli 9:3-14. Lezizindimana zisitshelani ngendlela abanye ababebuka ngayo abaprofethi bakaNkulunkulu abathembekileyo na?`

Kunezibonelo eziningi ngesimo sabaprofethi Ebhayibhelini. Kulokhu, uSawule wayethunywe nguyise, uKish, ukuyocinga izimbongolo ezilahlekile. Inceku ehamba noSawule yancoma ukuthi angayeki Ukucinga, ngoba kukhona “ ‘kulelidolobha indoda kaNkulunkulu, futhi ingumuntu ohloniphekile’ ” (1 Sam. 9:6, NKJV). Incazelo enikezwa lapha ngoSamuweli Umprofethi iveza izimpawu ezazilindelekile kubaprofethi bakaNkulunkulu. Abaprofethi beqiniso Emibhalweni bavezwa “njengabantu bakaNkulunkulu,” abakhethelwe kokunye ukuveza isimilo Sakhe. Ngesikhathi esifanayo, ukuthi abanye phakathi “kwabantu bakaNkulunkulu” bangaba ngabaprofethi, akubenzi bangabi nezici.

`Ngubani, naphakathi kwabahlonipheke kunabo bonke “abantu bakaNkulunkulu,” obazi bengenaso isici na?`
