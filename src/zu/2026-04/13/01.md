### Funda Lokhu Kuleliviki
1 Kor. 2:2; Johane 1:1-5; 2 Tim. 3:16, 17; 2 Tim. 4:1-5; Isa. 40:1-5; Johane 17:20-23; 1 Kor. 1:10-13.

> <p>Indimana Yenkumbulo</p>
> “Bavuka ekuseni, baphuma baya ehlane laseThekhowa; ekuphumeni kwabo uJehoshafati wema, wathi, ‘Ngizweni, Juda nani bakhileyo eJerusalema: Kholwani nguJehova uNkulunkulu wenu, niyakuqiniswa; kholwani ngabaprofethi bakhe, niyakuphumelela’ ” (2 IziKronike 20:20, NKJV).

U Nkulunkulu akasiceli ukuba sikholwe ngokungaboni. Kunalokho, ubufakazi bobukhona Bakhe bunamandla. Indalo iyodwa, nasemuva kweminyaka engu-6,000 yesono, ifakaza ngeqiniso hhayi nje likaNkulunkulu onamandla amangalisayo okudala (buka izithombe ezikwi-James Webb Space Telescope) kepha futhi ngoNkulunkulu wothando.

Kepha kusekuningi. Nasemuva kokuba “izwi elaqhamuka ezulwini salizwa, ngenkathi sinaye entabeni engcwele” uPetro wayesakwazi ukubhala ukuthi thina “sinezwi eliqinisekile lesiprofetho; okuthi nenze kahle ukuba nililalele, njengokukhanya okukhanya endaweni emnyama, kuze kube ngukusa, futhi inkanyezi yokusa intwele ezinhliziyweni zenu” 92 Pet. 1:18, 19). Kungenzeka singakwazi, njengoPetro, ukuzwa izwi likaNkulunkulu, kodwa nathi futhi sinalo “izwi eliqinisekileyo lesiprofetho.

UNkulunkulu ukhulumile kithina ngabaprofethi Bakhe, omibhalo yabo iseBhayibhelini nabamibhalo yabo ingekho eBhayibhelini. Ubufakazi esibunikeziwe buningi kakhulu. Umbuzo osalayo yilo: Siyavuma ukulamukela na?

Umlando ongcwele uyabonisa ukuthi iningi lalingavumi. Mhlawumbe thina, ngomusa kaNkulunkulu, ngeke senze iphutha elifanayo.

_Funda isifundo saleliviki ukulungisela iSabatha lika uZibandlela 26._
