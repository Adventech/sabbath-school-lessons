---
title:  Isiprofetho Siduduza Abantu BakaNkulunkulu
date:   23/12/2026
---

`Funda uMathewu 11:28, 29. UJesu usitshela ukuba senzeni na? Yini ayithembisa boke abeza kuYe na?`

UNkulunkulu ulokhu ekhathazekile ngokuphila kwabantu. Ngaphambi kokudala u-Adamu no-Eva, uNkulunkulu wabalungiselela indawo yokuhlala epheleleyo eyayizobathathekisa futhi ibagculise (Genesisi 1). UNkulunkulu wahlinzekela ngakho konke okudingekela ukunethezeka kwabo. Emuva kokuba isibili esingcwele siwile, uNkulunkulu weza ukuzobacinga (Gen. 3:9), waprofetha ngokuza koMsindisi (Gen. 3:15). UNkulunkulu uzinikele ekuphileni kwezidalwa Zakhe.

Mhlawumbe asikho isibusiso sesipho sesiprofetho esinganakiwe njengesomsebenzi waso wokuba ngumthombo wenduduzo kanye nokuvuselelwa kwabantu bakaNkulunkulu esikhathini sonke

`Funda uIsaya 40:1-5. Yimuphi umbiko uNkulunkulu awunikeza umprofethi Wakhe ngabantu Bakhe abalimeleyo na? Singasisebenzisa kanjani lesosimo kithina namuhla na?`

UNkulunkulu wayefuna uJuda azi ukuthi nakuba Yena kwakufanele abajezise, weyengeke abalahle ngokuphelele. Akanikezi nje uIsaya umbiko wenkuthazo wabantu Bakhe abalimele. Wayaleza uIsaya ngendlela yokwethula umbiko. Umbiko kwakufuneka wethulwe ngokuthamba okukhulu, ngothando, nangokunakelela. UNkulunkulu wayekhathazeke kakhulu ngalombiko wenduduzo ukuba ufike ezinhliziyweni zabantu Bakhe Yena akazange abe budlabha ngawo. Imibiko enjalo yenduduzo yaphinda phindwa kuyo yonke incwadi kaIsaya (Isa. 12:1, Isa. 49:13, Isa. 51:3, etc.).

Umpostoli uPawule waxwayisa ngokuthi labo abaphila impilo yobunkulunkulu kuKristu Jesu bayobhekana nokushushiswa okwesabekayo (2 Tim. 3:12). Ezikhathini ezinzima ezizayo, sizodinga amazwi enduduzo nethemba. Isipho sesiprofetho siyindlela uNkulunkulu azoyisebenzisa ukupholisa nokuqinisa abantu Bakhe ngaphambi kokuba uJesu abuye ukuzosithatha siye ekhaya.

`Yisiphi isigaba sempilo yakho la udinga kuso ukuzwa amazwi enduduzo nethemba na? Yini uNkulunkulu asinikeza amandla okukwenza emuva kokuba esiduduzile na? (2 Kor. 1:3, 4).`
