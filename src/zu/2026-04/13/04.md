---
title:  Isiprofetho Siyinkomba kaNkulunkulu Yeqiniso
date:   22/12/2026
---

Ezinye izazi zilinganisa ukuthi kukhona izinkonzo zamaKristu ezingaphezu kuka45,000 emhlabeni namuhla. Ukholo olwaqala ngoJesu Kristu lwakhula kanjani lwaba yizinhlelo eziningi ezahlukene zokholo na? Impendulo ilele ezihumushweni eziningi ezingafani zeZibhalo ezibe khona ngekuhamba kwesikhathi. Nembala, azikwazi zonke ukuba ngezilungile, ngoba ezikhathini eziningi okufundiswa elinye ibandla kuphikisana nokwenziwa ngabanye.

Ukusukela ekuqaleni (buka uGen. 2:16, 17; Gen 3:1-5) ukudideka kwakungenye yezindlela ezinkulu zikaSathane zokuhola abantu basuke eqinisweni likaNkulunkulu. Njengoba enza ku-Adamu no-Eva, uSathane uxuba iqiniso elanele kanye namanga ukuze adonsele abantu ngaphansi kolawulo lwakhe. Usathane uholela ophathe abantu bakaNkulunkulu esikhathini sonke 2 Kron. 36:14-16). Waze wazama nokuholela ophathe uJesu (Mat. 4:1-11), kepha uJesu wahlangabenza nokunxusa kukaSathane ngezwi elisobala “ ‘Kulotshiwe’ ” (Mat. 4:4, NKJV).

`Funda u-2 Thimothewu 4:1-5. Yisiphi iseluleko esibalulekile uPawule asinikeza uThimothewu mayelana neZwi likaNkulunkulu na? Kungani eqinisa ngokuthi uThimothewu abe ngolindele kwezikamoya lokho okuzayo na?`

Ngeso lesiprofetho, uPawule wabona phambili isikhathi lapho abantu babengeyukulithanda noma balilalele iZwi likaNkulunkulu. Kunalokho, babeyohamba befuna othisha bezikamoya abakitaza izinkanuko zabo ngezinganekwane zobuqili. Kwabanjalo, uNkulunkulu uthumela izinkohliso ezinamandla ukuze bakholwe lawo manga abawafunayo. “Ngalokho-ke uNkulunkulu ubathumela amandla okuduka, baze bakholwe ngamanga, ukuba bonke abangakholwanga yiqiniso benamela ukungalungi balahlwe” (2 Thes. 2:11, 12, NKJV).

Kepha kulabo abavulelekile, uJesu wathembisa ukuthi bona “bayolazi iqiniso” (Johane 8:32). Isipho sesiprofetho siyindlela kaNkulunkulu yokwehlukanisa iqiniso emangeni, ukukhanya ebumnyameni.

Ngomqondo othile, singalazi kalula iqiniso phakathi kwamabandla amaningi ahlukeneyo. Uma ungezukuliyeka iSabatha losuku lwesikhombisa (elisebhayibhelini ngokuqina), ususe okulinganiselwa ku-99,5 percent kongakhetha kukho, njengoba emaningi amabandla angawugcini umthetho wesine.

Uma ungezukukholwa ukuthi abafileyo baya ezulwini ngaso lesosikhathi ukuyoba noJesu noma bayasha manje Esihogweni futhi bayoba lapho ingunaphakade, bese kwenzekani na?

Usususe u-99,5 percent kulo0.5 percent oseleyo.
