title:  Kuzame Futhi
date:   25/12/2026
---

Eminyakeni ethile edlulile, inkampani yaKwa-Kellogg yaqhamisa isiqubulo esikhangisayo sezidlo zayo zasekuseni. Ngkuzama Ukuzuza abathengi ababeshiyile bathenga ezinye izinhlobo, yabakhuthaza ngokuthi “wazame futhi okokuqala.” Ngabe singasebenza kuma-Seventh-day Adventist asechithe isikhathi efunda ngeBhayibheli nango-Ellen G. White achitha isikhathi esincane “enambitha” imibhalo yakhe na? Ukuya ezifundweni ngo-Ellen G. White kuzokunikeza umqondo ngempilo yakhe nomsebenzi, kodwa ngaphandle kokuba “unambithele” ngokwakho, ngeke ukwazi Ukuhlola futhi ube namava esibusiso sikamoya semibiko yakhe Yesiprofetho. Yilabo kuphela abafuna imibhalo yakhe, ikakhulu ngokwegqikithi, abangathakasela ngokuphelele lokho uNkulunkulu asinikeza kona.

Njengoba siqaphele kuyo yonke lekota, u-Ellen G. White uzinzisa thina eqinisweni LeBhayibheli futhi asixhumanise noNkulunkulu. Kunezibusiso ezithile zokufunda imibiko yakhe Yesiprofetho.

Ukuhlola okwenziwe ngu-Roger Dudley no Des Cummings Jr., kwembula amaqiniso athathekisayo. Labo abafunda njalo imibhalo ka-Ellen G. White banobudlelwane obujulile nobusondelene noJesu kunalabo abangamfundi; banesiqiniseko esiqinile sosindiso, nokukhuleka okuvamile nomndeni, kanye nesifiso sokuvangela abanye. “Akuvamile ukuba isifundo sophando sithole ubufakazi obincike kakhulu esiphethweni esisodwa,” kubhala lababahloli ababili. “Ekuhlolweni kokukhula kwebandla, esigabeni ngasinye esimayelana nemimoya noma imikhuba, ilungu elivama ukufunda izincwadi zika-Ellen G. White livama ukuba sezingeni eliphezulu kunaleli elimfunda ngokungavamile noma elingamfundi nhlobo. . . . Nembala umthelela ngukuthi ukuvama ukufunda imibhalo ka-Ellen G White kwenza umehluko omuhle empilweni yomKristu nasekufakazeni.”—Dudley and Cummings, “Who Reads Ellen White?” in Ministry, October 1982, p. 12.

Kulekota, sifundile ukuthi uNkulunkulu ukhuluma ngabaprofethi Bakhe. Imibiko yabo ixhuma thina kuNkulunkulu futhi ilethe ithonya elingcwele neliphefumulelwe kuso sonke isigaba sempilo yomuntu, ikakhulu esithembisweni sosindiso olutholakala kuJesu: “Yizwani nibone ukuthi uJehova muhle” . Basibizela ekwenzeni okungcwele, ekuguqukeni kwempilo, ekuvangeleni mathupha, nakwimpilo esebenzela banye. Lokhu ngamalungiselelo asemqoka ngakithina ekumemezeleni kokugcina ezweni lapho sibheka ukuza kukaJesu.
