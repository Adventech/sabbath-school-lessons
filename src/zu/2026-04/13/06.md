---
title:  Isiprofetho Senza Umzimba KaKristu Ube Munye
date:   24/12/2026
---

`Funda uJohane 17:20-23 no 1 Korinte 1:10-13. Yiluphi ubizo olungumomngo olukulezizindimana ezimbili zeZibhalo na?`

Ngenkathi uJesu esezoya esiphambanweni, inhliziyo Yakhe yathinteka ngokulangazelela okwengameleyo. Wayefuna bonke abalandeli Bakhe babemunye, njengoba Yena noBaba bemunye othandweni, ngenhloso, nangokwenza. Ubunye uJesu abukhulekela kwakungekona nje ukufaniselana. UJesu wakhuleka isiprofetho esinamandla esizogcwaliseka ngaphambi kokuba abuye—umfanekiso onothile wabantu abehlukene ngemvelephi, ubuzwe, nemibono babeyoba munye kuYena nakuBaba, ngamandla kaMoya oNgcwele. Umpostoli uPawule wakhela kulendikimba ngenkathi encenga amakholwa aseKorisnte ukuba abenomqondo ofanayo, akhulume iqiniso elifanayo, futhi angabi nakwehlukana (1 Kor. 1:10). Kodwa kungani lobubunye besiprofetho bubaluleke kangaka na?

UJesu wakhuleka “ ‘ukuba bonke babe munye, njengalokhu wena Baba ukimi, nami ngikuwe, ukuba nabo babe kithina, ukuze izwe likholwe ukuthi wena ungithumile’ ” (Johane 17:21, NKJV).

UNkulunkulu ulangazelela ukuthi bonke abantu basindiswe, kodwa ngaphambi kokuba basindiswe, kumele bakholwe kuJesu futhi bakholwe ukuthi uNkulunkulu wathumela Yena ukuzosindisa izwe. Lapho abantu abangafani bebanga uJesu njengeNkosi noMsindisi wabo, bethandana futhi besebenzelena ngokuzidela, uyothi umhlaba ukholwe ukuthi uJesu wayeyilokho ayethe uyikona. Ubunye uJesu ayekulangazelela bunenhloso futhi bunamandla. Ngeshwa, ukusukela ekuqaleni, iyanhlukwano yaphazamisa lobobunye. Buka, ngokwesibonelo, Izenzo 6:1. Futhi ukusukela lapho ubuKristu  behlukene kakhulu!

Lobubunye bungumsebenzi kaMoya oNgcwele ngezipho abelana ngazo. Abase-Efesu 4:11-15 basitshela ukuthi izipho zikamoya oNgcwele zinikelwa ukuphelelisa amakholwa kanye nokondla umzimba kaKristu, “size sifinyelele sonke ebunyeni bokukholwa nasekwazini iNdodana kaNkulunkulu, ebudodeni obupheleleyo, esilinganisweni sobukhulu sokugcwala kukaKristu” (Efes. 4:13, NKJV). Isipho sesiprofetho, esinye sezipho eziphambili zikamoya oNgcwele, sisemqoka ekwenzeni munye abantu bakaNkulunkulu, ikakhulu lapho besondela ekuzeni kwesibili kukaJesu, ukuze bakholwe eNkosini uJesu Kristu basindiswe (Zenzo 16:31).

`Ngesikhathi lapho ibandla lama-Seventh-day Adventist lingumzimba wenkolo yamaProtestanti ehlukene kakhulu, singalubonisa kanjani loluhlobo lobunye olusiza abantu bakholwe kuJesu na? Yiziphi izinto ezibeka lobubunye engozini na?`
