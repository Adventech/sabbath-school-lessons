---
title:  Isiprofetho Siphakamisa UKristu
date:   20/12/2026
---

Isiprofetho siyindlela kaNkulunkulu yokukhuluma nabantu Bakhe kanye neyokubaxhumanisa Naye. Akumangalisi, ke, ukuthi uSathane (udrago) wayethukuthelele Owesifazane (ibandla likaNkulunkulu) futhi wahamba wayolwa nabaseleyo benzalo yakhe (Samb. 12:17).

Yiziphi izimpawu ezahlukanisayo zalensali Yembewu yowesifazane na? Bagcina imithetho futhi banobufakazi bukaJesu. Isambulo 19:10 sisitshela ukuthi lobubufakazi ‘”ngumoya wesiprofetho.”

`Funda u-1 Korinte 2:2 no Galathiya 6:14. UPawule uyibona Iyini inhloso yempilo Yakhe kanye nomsebenzi—isikhuthazo esikhulu ayesiphilela na?`

Ngaphandle kwamava abo, abapostoli bamukela uJesu phezu kobufakazi babaprofethi kanye namadoda alungileyo, omazwi abo alulekela esikhathini samakhulu-khulu eminyaka. Umhlaba wamaKristu unobufakazi obugcwele obusukuka kwiTestamente Elidala buye kwiTestamente Elisha: kokungaphambilini, okukhomba kuMsindisi ozayo, futhi kokwakamuva, okwembula uJesu engogcwalisa imibandela yoMsindisi. Konke lokhu kwanele ukusukela ukholo lwalabo abavumayo ukukholwa. “Inhloso kaNkulunkulu kwakungeyokushiyela izizwe ithuba elingcono lokukhulisa ukholo emandleni kaNkulunkulu naweNdodana yakhe kanye nakumsebenzi kaMoya oNgcwele.”—Ellen G. White, The Spirit of Prophecy, vol. 3, p. 182.

Ngezindlela eziningi, udaba akulona olwengqondo; ngolwesimilo. Yinto yenhliziyo, hhayi eyekhanda. Lapho abantu bevala izinhliziyo zabo, imiqondo yabo, Ngeshwa, iyalandela.

`Funda uJohane 1:1-5. Lezizindimana zisitshelani ngokuthi uJesu ungubani na?`

Uma uJesu, uliZwi likaNkulunkulu, wayekhona ngaphambilini, wenziwa inyama, futhi Wahlala phakathi kwethu, kuyalandela ukuthi uJesu wayemunye namazwi noma imibiko eyanikezwa nguNkulunkulu kubaprofethi Bakhe. Nabo abaprofethi abathwala lemibiko engazange ikhombe kuJesu obala nabo babephefumulelwa nguMoya oNgcwele.

`Cabanga ngobukhulu bezulu nomhlaba, bese Ucabanga ngaLowo owadala kona, uJesu, futhi nguYena owafela thina. Yiliphi ithemba elikhulu lokho okusinikeza lona na?`
