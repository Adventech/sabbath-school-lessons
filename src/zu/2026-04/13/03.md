---
title:  Isiprofetho Siqinisa Ibhayibheli
date:   21/12/2026
---

Ekugcineni iBhayibheli lingumphumela kaMoya oNgcwele, ngesipho sesiprofetho. UPetro wesibili 1:21 wambula uhlelo uNkulunkulu alusebenzisa: “Ngokuba akuzange kuvezwe isiprofetho ngentando yomuntu, kepha abantu beqhutshwa nguMoya oNgcwele bakhuluma okukaNkulunkulu” (NKJV).

`Funda u-2 Thimothewu 3:16, 18. Lesisahluko sigcizelela kanjani kokubili igunya nokuphefumulelwa kweZibhalo na?`

Ngaphandle kweBhayibheli, abantu bebeyosala bengenayo indlela yokucusha phakathi kwezihibe zomhlaba wesono, futhi lokho bekuyoba yindlela yokubhubha. Namuhla iBhayibheli likuyo yonke indawo ngezimo ziningi, kodwa lokho kwakungenjalo sonke isikhathi. Ngokwesibonelo, ngesikhathi sikaJesu, yimiqulu yeTestamente Elidala kuphela eyayikhona ukuba ifundwe, futhi ngabapristi noma orabi kuphela ababengakwenza lokho. Abantukazana babevama ukungafinyeleli ngokuqondile Ezibhalweni. Izwi eliphuma kuNkulunkulu laliyigugu ngalezozinsuku.

`Funda uLuka 24:27. Emuva kokuvuka Kwakhe, yini ayisebenzisa uJesu ukusiza abafundi ababili abadangele ukuba baqonde inhloso Yakhe eyodwa na?`

UJesu, uMprofethi omkhulu kunabo bonke—Yena kuphela onguliZwi (Johane 1:1-3)—waphakamisa Izibhalo (Johane 7:38). Phakathi kwezibusiso eziyigugu kunazo zonke zesipho sesiprofetho ngesokuthi siqinisa iBhayibheli.

“Labo abathintwe yiZibhalo eziNgcwele njengezwi likaNkulunkulu, futhi belangazelela ukulandela izimfundiso zalo, kumele bafunde nsuku zonke, bamukele nsuku zonke intshisekelo yezikamoya kanye namandla, okuhlinzekelwe lonke Ikholwa langempela kwisipho soMoya oNgcwele.”—Ellen G. White, in Signs of the Times, March 8, 1910. Isipho sokuprofetha seqiniso siphakamisa Izibhalo njalo. Njalo sikhomba abesilisa nabesifazane eZwini likaNkulunkulu njengomgomo kuphela wokholo nokwenza.

`Kungani uJesu aqala ngokubabhekisa Ezibhalweni ngaphambi kokwembula kubona ukuthi Yena wayengubani na? Lokhu kumele kusitsheleni ngokuba yisisekelo kweZwi kithina na?`
