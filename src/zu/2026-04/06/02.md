---
title:  Ubizo Lokubhala
date:   01/11/2026
---

Indlela yokuqala uNkulunkulu ayisebenzisa Ukudlulisa imibiko kubantu kwakungukukhuluma okuqondile, njengamazwi azwakalayo, noma amaphupho, noma ngezingelosi. Kwaze kwabe ngesikhathi sikaMose lapho Izibhalo zibonisa umyalelo wokuqala mayelana nalamava, wokuthi kubhalwe “encwadini” lokho okushiwo nguNkulunkulu.

`Yikhuphi ukubalulwa kokuqala kokubhala Ebhayibhelini na? Funda u-Eksodusi 17:14.`

KwiTestamente Elidala sithola izibonelo eziningana zikaNkulunkulu eyaleza izithunywa Zakhe ukuba zilonde amazwi Akhe ngabantu. NjengoMose, uJeremiya watshelwa, “ ‘Zithathele umqulu wencwadi, ulobe kuwo onke amazwi engiwakhulume kuwe ngo-Israyeli nangoJuda, nangazo zonke izizwe, kusukela osukwini engakhuluma ngalo kuwe, kusukela ezinsukwini zikaJosiya kuze kube namuhla’ ” (Jer. 36:2, NKJV).

Wanikezwa futhi nesizathu sokuthi abhale iziprofetho: “ ‘Mhlawumbe indlu yakwaJuda iyakuzwa konke okubi engicabanga ukukwenza kuyo ukuba ibuye, kube yilowo nalowo endleleni yakhe embi, ngiyithethelele amacala ayo nezono zayo’ ”(Jer. 36:3, NKJV). Izibikezelo zazinikelwe ukuholela abantu osindisweni, kunokuba nje nosikisiki lokwazi ngekusasa.

Izithunywa zikaNkulunkulu zabhala ngaphansi kwezimo eziningi ezahlukahlukene. UMose wabhala izincwadi ezinhlanu zokuqala zeBhayibheli ngenkathi esehlane. UDavide waqamba iningi lamahubo akhe ngenkathi ezingelwa nguSawule (buka AmaHubo 52, 54, 58, 59). UDaniyeli wabhali imibono yakhe ngenkathi esebenza ezinkundleni zaseBhabhiloni ne-Medo-Persia. Ngokwehlukile, uPawule wabhala izincwadi zakhe kwabase-Efesu, Filipi, Kolose noFilemoni ngenkathi esejele, futhi uJohane wabhala iSambulo ngenkathi edingiselwe e-Patmos. Kusobala ukuthi ngenkathi izithunywa zikaNkulunkulu zibanomuzwa wokubhala, zazinikezwa amandla nguMoya Ongcwele nakuba izimo zabo zinjani.

Ngokuthathekisayo, zonke izimfundiso ezilondiwe zikaJesu ziza kithina ngesimo sokulotshwa ngabantu uNkulunkulu abakhetha Ukugcina lamaqiniso ayigugu. Lokhu kubonisa thina iqholo uNkulunkulu analo lokuthi imibiko Yakhe ingaqoshwa ngokuthembeka futhi ilondwe yizithunywa Zakhe.

`Ungamphendula ngokuthini othile othi asikwazi ukulithemba Ibhayibheli ngoba asinayo imiqulu yokuqala na? Kungani lokho, ngempela, kuyimpiki swano engenasisindo na?`
