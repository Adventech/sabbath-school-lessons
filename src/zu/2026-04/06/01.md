### Funda Lokhu Kuleliviki
Eksod. 17:14; Isa. 13:1; Jer. 36:17, 18; Rom. 16:22; Luka 1:1-4; Juda 14, 15; 2 Kor. 4:7.

> <p>Indimana Yenkumbulo</p>
> “Ngokuba konke okwalotshwa ngaphambili kwalotshelwa ukufundiswa kwethu, ukuze ngokubekezela nangenduduzo yemibhalo sibe nethemba” (Roma 15:4, NKJV).

U Yafisa ukufundiswa iculo elaqanjwa nguNkulunkulu Mathupha na? KuDuteronomi 32, amagama eculo abhalwa nguMose ngokuyalezwa nguNkulunkulu (Dut. 31:19-22). Asivamile Ukucabanga ngezingoma lapho sicabanga ngemibhalo Yesiprofetho, nakuba iningi lamahubo, nembala, kungamaculo aphefumulelwe, nakuba amazwi ngokwawo engazange ayalezwe ngokuqondile nguJehovah.

Ibhayibheli lichaza izindlela ezintathu eziphambili abaprofethi ababedlulisa ngayo imibiko kaNkulunkulu: (1) ngokukhuluma; (2) ngokubhala; kanye (3) ngezimpawu noma ukulingisa. Kuleliviki sizogxila emibhalweni eseBhayibhelini. Ibhayibheli liqopha iZwi likaNkulunkulu, njengalokhu lethulwe ngamazwi ezithunywa Zakhe, kufaka izintshumayelo nezinkulumo zabaprofethi.

Cabanga ngezitayela ezahlukene zokubhala kanye nezinhlobo zemibhalo Yesiprofetho: izingxoxo, umlando, izinkondlo, izililo, kanye nezincwadi, ukubalula ezimbalwa. Amadoda nabesifazane Abakhethiweyo bafaka ulimi lwesintu kulokho ababeholwe nguMoya Ongcwele ukukudlulisa. Kuleliviki sizobuka emibhalweni yabo, Ukusetshenziswa konobhala, kanye nezinto okumele sizigcine emqondweni lapho sifunda Izahluko ezilukhuni.

_Funda isifundo saleliviki ukulungiselela iSabatha, uLwezi 7._
