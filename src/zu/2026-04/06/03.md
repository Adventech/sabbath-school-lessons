---
title:  Uhlobo NeNdlela Yokubhala
date:   02/11/2026
---

Ibhayibheli liqukethe izindlela ezahlukahlukene zokubhala ezazisetshenziswa yizithunywa eziphefumulelwe zikaNkulunkulu. Ezinye zifana nokubhala kwi-diary (buka uNum. 33:1-49). Ngakwesinye isandla, izicwadi zikaDaniyeli, Zakariya, noJohane ziqukhethe izibonelo zemibhalo Yokuphela kwesikhathi, ephawulwa yizimpawu ezinkulu, izindikimba zezwe lonke, kanye nezincazelo zezidalwa zezulu, kokunye.

`Funda uIsaya 13:1. Ibhayibheli lapho libhekisa kanjani embikweni u-Isaya awunikezayo na?`

Imibono etholwa ngabaprofethi ngezinye izikhathi ibizwa “ngemibiko yezikamoya”—okuchaza ukuthi ngukuxhumana noma izambulo ezivela kuNkulunkulu (ezinye Izihumusho zithi leligama “imithwalo”). Izihumusho ezintsha zeBhayibheli zibonisa ukuthi okuningi kwabhalwa ngendlela yenkondlo, evama ukusebenzisa ukuhambisana. Ukuhambisana yilokhu kokuthi imisho iyaphindana noma yehluke Kweyokuqala. ngokwesibonelo: “Ngalolo suku abantu bayakumbheka uMenzi wabo, namehlo abo ayakumbuka oNgcwele ka-Israyeli” (Isa. 17:7, NKJV). “uMenzi” no “oNgcwele” bayahambisana, umfanekiso ngamunye unikeza ulwazi ngomunye.

Abaprofethi babesebenzisa futhi izinsiza zokubhala ezivamile njengezingathekiso (izimpawu) kanye nehaba. Kithina ukuba siqonde kangcono futhi sisebenzise imibiko yabo, kubalulekile ukubona uhlobo lokubhala abalusebenzisayo.

Enye indlela Yokubhala evamile Ezibhalweni ngeye-“cala lwesivumelwano.” Ngokwesibonelo, kuJeremiya 2:4-9, uJeremiya wethula ukukhononda kukaNkulunkulu ngakubantu Bakhe ngokuphula izibopho zabo zesivumelwano. Ngokusebenzisa umfanekiso wasenkantolo, umbhalo ubonisa uNkulunkulu njengomshushisi obeka izizathu Zokwahlulela Kwakhe.

Izibhalo ziqukethe futhi imibhalo Yesiprofetho, umlando, amaculo, kanye nemfanekiso. Akukho ukwehlukanisa kwigunya lohlobo lokuxhumana ngaphezu kolunye. Izincwadi zikaPawule zazithathwa njengezinesisindo esilingana nesabaprofethi basendulo, ngoba uPawule unikeza ubufakazi obusobala ezincwadini zakhe bokuthi zaziphefumulelwe nguMoya oNgcwele (buka u-1 Kor. 7:10, 12, 40).

`Okunye ukufunda kugcizelela uhlangothi lwesintu Ezibhalweni ngokunciphisa ubunkulunkulu. Kungani lokhu kuyindlela eya ekushabalaleni kwezikamoya na?`
