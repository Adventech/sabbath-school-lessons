---
title:  Imibiko Engcwele Nezici Zabantu
date:   05/11/2026
---

`Yini eshiwo nguPawule ngengcebo kaNkulunkulu ku-1 Korinte 4:7 na?`

UPawule ubhala ngengcebo kaNkulunkulu njengegciniswe “kwizitsha zobumba” (abantu), ukuze amandla kaNkulunkulu abonakaliswe ngokuqhathaniswa nobuthakathaka bethu. Izibhalo zembula ukuthi njengalokhu izithunywa zikaNkulunkulu zazigciniwe ngamandla ezulu ekuhlulekeni ezimpilweni zazo (Mose, Eliya, Davide, Jona, noPetro, ngokwesibonelo), kanjalo nokubhala kwabo kwakungephunyuke ezicini zokuqonda kwabantu ngokuxhumana.

“Ukubumbana kobunkulunkulu kanye nesintu, okubonakala kuKristu, kukhona futhi naseBhayibhelini. Amaqiniso ambulwe onke ‘anikwe ngokuphefumulela kukaNkulunkulu;’ kepha ethulwe ngamazwi abantu futhi alungiselwa izidingo zabantu.”—Ellen G. White, Testimonies for the Church, vol. 5, p. 747.

Ukuba ngabantu kwezithunywa zikaNkulunkulu akwenganyelwa ubizo lwabo lobunkulunkulu lokukhuluma izingcebo zeqiniso, njengalokhu uPawule abhala ku-2 Korinte 4:7. Kube futhi kungabonakali sengathi uMoya Ongcwele wabona kufanele ukuvimbela izithunywa Zakhe ekwenzeni izinkulumo nasekubhaleni amazwi namuhla esiwathola elukhuni ukuwahlanganisa noma ukuwachaza. Nakuba zimbalwa lezizindimana, kuthatha umqondo omfishane ukukhubeka kuzo. NjengamaKristu akholelwa Ebhayibhelini, kumele sizinikele eZwini likaNkulunkulu ezingeni esiliqonda ngalo futhi sithembe uJehovah, nakuba kukhona esingakuqondi.

Okunye Ukungezwani kungabalelwa emaphutheni amancane enzeka kulabo ababebhala besusela kokunye, noma emithombeni emibili ephikisanayo Ababhali abaphefumulelwe ababethathela kuyo. Lapho sibhekana nezahlukho eziyinkinga, kumele sizibuze ukuthi ngabe siyayiqonda incazelo ephelele yamazwi ombhali, nokuthi ukufunda kabanzi kungayixazulula yini inkinga.

Noma singakwazanga ukuxazulula konke okubukeka kungabayiphutha noma ukungezwani, sithola ukuthi akubalulekile ngokomongo wombiko owethulwayo. Singaba neqholo lokuthi amaqiniso angcwele Ezibhalo embulwe ngokwethembeka nakuba izithunywa zikaNkulunkulu zinezici.

`Ucabanga ukuthi kungani uNkulunkulu akhetha abantu abanamaphutha kunokuba akhethe izingelosi ezingenasono ukuba babe yizithunywa Zakhe eziphambili na?`
