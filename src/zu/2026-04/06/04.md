---
title:  Onobhala
date:   03/11/2026
---

`Funda uJeremiya 36:17, 18 no Roma 16:22. Lezizindimana zisitshelani ngababhali bebhayibheli besebenzisa onobhala na?`

UJereminya wayesenokuzibhalela imibiko yakhe, kepha kunalokho wathembela kunobhala ukuqopha iziprofetho zakhe ngokwethembeka kanye nokwabelanangazo nabaholi besizwe. UJeremiya akuyena yedwa isibonelo Ebhayibhelini la abaprofethi besebenzisa onobhala Ukudlulisa imibiko kaNkulunkulu.

KwiTestamente Elisha, nakuba u-Tertius ethi “wabhala lencwadi” (Rom. 16:22), kusobala ukusukela ekuqaleni kwencwadi yabaseRoma ukuthi wayebhala kuphela lokho uPawule ayefisa ukukusho. U-Tertius wayengunobhala owaguqula amazwi kaPawule abe ngabhaliweyo.

Lomkhuba wawuvamile kuPawule. Ekuphetheni izincwadi zakhe kwabaseKolose nabaseThesalonika, uPawule uyaqiniseka ukuba afake isibingelelo “ngesandla sami” (Kol. 4:18, 2 Thes. 3:17, NKJV) njengendlela yokuqinisa yonke incwadi. Lokhu kuncoma ukuthi wayencike komunye ukubhala izincwadi zakhe. Ngenxa yokuthi Leliqiniso lingavusa ukungabaza ngokuthi izincwadi zimele yena ngempela—noma cha, ukuthi kwakungeyena kwaphela—uPawule wahlinzeka ngombhalo wakhe obhalwe nguyena ekugcineni ukuze kungabikho kungabaza ngobuqiniso bencwadi Iningi labaphawuli lithi lokhu kwehluka endleleni Yokubhala okutholakala ezincwadini zikaPawule kungenxa yokusebenzisa kwakhe onobhala. Okufanayo kuyasolakala mayelana nezincwadi ezimbili zikaPetro: “Nakuba kuyiqiniso ukuthi uhlelo lolimi ku-2 Petro lwehlukile kunalolo lwencwadi yokuqala, lokhu kungase kube kwabangwa ngukuthi uPetro . . . wasebenzisa umbhali ekubhaleni imisho yena ayeyibhala ngesiGriki. Uma uPawule, owayesazi kakhulu isiGriki, asebenzisa onobhala, njengalokhu kubonakala wakwenza, kufanele kakhulu Ukucabanga ukuthi uPetro, olimi lwakhe luyisi-Aramaic, wayengenza kunjalo, nokuthi laba onobhala babengafaka ithonya endleleni Yokubhala izincwadi zakhe ngesiGriki.”—The SDA Bible Commentary, vol. 5, p. 186.

Lokhu kuncoma ukuthi bethembekile emicabangweni nasencazelweni yezithunywa zikaNkulunkulu, amagama kanye nokwakhiwa kwemisho kungabonisa onobhala ababasebenzisa. Yimicabango Ephefumulelwe, hhayi Izinhlamvu zamagama asetshenziswe ukwethula leyomicabango ephefumulelwe.
