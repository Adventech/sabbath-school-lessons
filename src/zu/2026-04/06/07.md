---
title:  Imibhalo ka-Ellen G. White
date:   06/11/2026
---

Amava ka-Ellen G. White kanye nemibhalo ihambisana kakhulu nokuningi esikufundile kuleliviki mayelana nezithunywa zikaNkulunkulu Ebhayibhelini. Yena, futhi, wamukela ukuthunywa kwasezulwini kokubhala lokho okwakwembulwe kuyena.

Ecabanga ngo-1909, u-Elle G. White wabhala, “Ngenxa yengozi eyacishe yangibulala, ngangibuthakathaka empilweni futhi ngingakwazi ukubhala. . . . kodwa ngobunye ubusuku ingelosi kaJehovah yeza ngasembhedeni wami yathi kimi, ‘Kumele ubhale lezizinto engikunikeza zona.” Mina ngathi, ‘Angikwazi ukubhala.’ Waphinda wanikezwa umyalelo, ‘Bhala lezizinto engikunikeza zona.’ Ngacabanga ukuthi ngizame, futhi ngathatha ibhodi etafuleni, ngaqala ukubhala, futhi ngathola ukuthi ngiyakwazi ukuloba amagama kalula.”—In General Conference Bulletin, May 31, 1909.

Njengababhali abaphefumulelwe Ezibhalweni, u-Ellen G. White naye wasebenzisa onobhala. Onobhala bakhe benza umsebenzi abahleli abalindelwe ukuwenza— ukulekelela Ababhali ekwethuleni imiyalezo yabo bengayifakanga imicabango yabo. Babengebona ababhala kanye naye. Umsebenzi wabo wawuncike kulokho u-Ellen G. White ayekukhiqiza, kubhaliwe ngesandla noma kwethulwe ngenkulumo.

U-Ellen G. White naye wacaphuna futhi wasebenzisa imibhalo ephuma kweminye Imithombo ekubhaleni kwakhe ukulekelela ukwethulwa kwemibiko yakhe. Ngokuncoma kwabafunda okwakhe ezinye zezincwadi ayezisebenzisa, kusobala ukuthi wayengaqondile ukukhohlisa noma ukungenzi ubulungiswa kulabo babhali, kodwa wayethola olimini lwabo izisho ezifanele Ukudlulisa amaqiniso ayefisa ukuwethula.

Kwenzekani ngokungahambisani noma ngokungacopheleli na?

“Mayelana nokungabi naphutha,” wabhala, “Angikaze ngikubange ukungabinaphutha; nguNkulunkulu kuphela ongenalo iphutha.”—Selected Messages, book 1, p. 37.

Othile anganqwamana nezinye izisho emibhalweni yakhe ekunzima ukuyiqonda noma ukuhlanganisa nezinye. Ukuba ngumuntu kuka-Ellen G. White akuzange kwenganyelwe wubizo lwakhe lobunkulunkulu. Njengathi sonke, u-Ellen White wayekhula futhi ephusa, ukukhula nokuphusa okwembuleka emibhalweni yakhe.

Njengalokhu sekushiwo, kodwa kukuhle kuphindwe, wabalungisa nalabo ababekholwa ukuthi lonke izwi ayelibhalile lalisuka ngokuqondile ezulwini. “Kukhona izikhathi lapho izinto esivamile kumele zishiwo, imicabango evamile kumele ibe semqondweni, izinhlamvu zamagama ezivamile kumele zibhaliwe futhi nolwazi olunikeziwe oludluliselwa komunye nomunye wezisebenzi. Lawamazwi, lolulwazi, alunikeziwe ngaphansi kokuphefumulelwa okukhethekile koMoya kaNkulunkulu.”—Selected Messages, book 3, p. 58.
