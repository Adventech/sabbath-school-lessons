---
title:  Imithombo Yokokubhala
date:   04/11/2026
---

“Futhi ngokuba uMshumayeli wayehlakaniphile, wayefundisa abantu ukwazi, wazindla, waphenya walungisa izaga eziningi. UMshumayeli wafuna ukuthola amazwi anamisayo afanele ukulotshwa, amazwi eqiniso” (Mshum. 12:9, 10, NKJV).

Lesisahluko singafundwa ukubonisa ukuthi uSolomoni akazange asungule zonke izaga aziqopha. Ngaphansi kokuphefumulelwa nguNkulunkulu, yena “wafuna” izisho eziningi futhi wasesha ukuthola “amazwi afanelekile.” Empeleni, incazelo evame ukuphawulwa yowesifazane ofanelekile kwiZaga 31 ibalelwa kumama weNkosi u-Lemuel engaziwa, hhayi uSolomoni (Zag. 31:1). Ubufakazi bukhomba kwithonya lemibhalo yaseGibhithe kwiZaga.

Ngokufanayo, abezomlando abaphefumulelwe beTestamente Elidala basitshela ukuthi batomula kwimibhalo yomlando ukubika ngokwethembeka indaba yabantu bakaNkulunkulu.

`Funda u-1 Amakhosi 11:41; Luka 1:1-4; no Juda 14, 15. Lezizindimana zisitshelani ngendlela Ababhali abaphefumulelwe abasebenzisa ngayo Imithombo engeyona eyebhayibheli ngezinye izikhathi na?`

U-Ezra no Nehemiya ngabanye bezibonelo zababhali bebhayibheli abancika emibhalweni yomlando ukuqopha izincwadi zabo. U-Ezra ufaka namakhophi ezincwadi kanye nezimiso zamakhosi mayelana neJerusalema ne-Persia (buka u-Ezra 4-7), futhi u-Nehemiya usitshela ngohla lozalo aluthola futhi walufaka ekubhaleni kwakhe (buka Neh. 7:5-72) Lesisimiso sibonakala nakwiTestamente Elisha. “Kusobala kumava kaLuka ukuthi Ukuphefumulelwa kusebenza ngendlela efanayo nokusebenza kwengqondo futhi ayikubeki eceleni. Lapha umbhali ophefumulelwe owayeholwa nguMoya oNgcwele ukufundisisa lokho Okukhona okukhulunyiwe nokubhaliweyo ngempilo kaKristu, bese futhi ekuhlanganisa kube ngumbiko oxhumene wolwazi oluqoqiweyo.”—The SDA Bible Commentary, vol. 5, p. 669.

UJuda naye ubukeka ecaphuna ku-1 Enoch 1:9—umsebenzi wamaJuda ongekho Ezibhalweni. Engabizanga magama abo, uPawule naye wacaphuna kosolwazi bamaGriki (buka iZenzo 17:28 no Titus 1:12).

`Nakuba Ababhali bezibhalo babevama ukulawulwa yisambulo ngemibono namaphupho, lokhu akuzange kubekele eceleni uMoya Ongcwele ekuphefumuleleni lezizithunywa Ukukhetha imibhalo kweminye Imithombo. Kungani lokhu kungumqondo obalulekile ukuba siwukhumbule na?`
