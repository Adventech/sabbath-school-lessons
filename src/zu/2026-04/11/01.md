### Funda Lokhu Kuleliviki
2 Pet. 2:5; Abahl. 4:4-16; 1 Amakhosi 18:21, 38, 39; Isa. 59:1-4; Neh. 9:30; Samb. 1:1-3.

> <p>Indimana Yenkumbulo</p>
> “Ngeqiniso iNkosi uJehovah ayenzi lutho, ingayambulanga imfihlakalo yayo ezincekwini zayo abaprofethi” (Amose 3:7, NKJV).

Selokhu kwangena isono, abantu balengele ekukhetheni izintokozo zesono okwesikhashana, ngokumelene nokuphila Okuphakade kuJesu. UNkulunkulu uyalazi Leliqiniso elidabukisayo, futhi, ngaphandle kwalo, Yena uyasithanda futhi usebenzela usindiso lwethu. Yingakho, emizuzwini ebucayi emlandweni, uNkulunkulu wenze ngezindlela ezimangalisayo ngokusebenza kwabaprofethi ngabantu Bakhe. Kepha, lapho imizamo Yakhe ingamukelwanga, Yena uxwayise ngokwahlulela. Ngabaprofethi Bakhe, uJehovah uqhubeka nokubika ngezenzo Zakhe ngaphambi kokuba enze (Amose 3:7). Uma abantu bephenduka, Yena uyashesha ukuthethelela. Uqhubeka nokuxhumana nathi ngendlela efanayo namuhla. Kuyaqondeka, nokho, ukuthi uNkulunkulu kumele akhulume nabantu Bakhe ezinsukwini zokugcina zomhlaba, njengalokhu akhuluma nabantu Bakhe ngaphambilini.

Kuleliviki sizobuka emuva ezikhathini ezibucayi emlandweni wosindiso ngenkathi uNkulunkulu asebenzisa abaprofethi ukwenza intando Yakhe ibesobala kubantu Bakhe nasezweni ngokubanzi. Sizohlola futhi indima yomsebenzi ophefumulelwe ka-Ellen G. White ezikhathini ezibalulekile zokukhula kwenhlangano yama-Adventist.

_Funda isifundo saleliviki ukulungisela iSabatha lika uZibandlela 12._
