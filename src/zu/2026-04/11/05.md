---
title:  Ithemba Ekudingisweni
date:   09/12/2026
---

“UJehova uNkulunkulu wawoyise wathuma kubo ngezandla zezithunywa zakhe, ethuma njalonjalo, ngokuba wahawukela abantu bakhe nendawo yakhe yokuhlala” (2 Kron. 36:15, NKJV).

UNkulunkulu wathumela abaprofethi abaningi emuva kuka-Elijah ukududuza, ukuxwayisa, nokufundisa abantu Bakhe, kodwa imibuso yakwaIsrayeli noJuda yawela ebubini uNkulunkulu wayiqhathanisa neSodoma neGomora (Isa. 1:9, 10). Abantu bakaNkulunkulu basebezigqaja, benomhobholo, futhi bekhonza izithombe. Babecindezela abasebenzi babo, abafelokazi, izintandane, kanye nezihambi ezazizofuna usizo kubo. (Buka uIsaya 58 ukuthola impendulo kaNkulunkulu ngokuhluleka KukaIsrayeli.) Ekugcineni, ukwahlulela kukaNkulunkulu kwaletha kuIsrayeli ukuthunjelwa e-Assyria (2 Amakhosi 17:1-6) kanye nokudingiswa kukaJuda ezweni laseBhabhiloni (2 Amakhosi 24, 25).

Ejezisa abantu Bakhe uNkulunkulu, akazange abashiye bodwa ekudingisweni. Waqhubeka nokubathumela abaprofethi, njengo-Ezekiel noDaniyeli, ukubanikeza ithemba ngekusasa.

`Funda uIsaya 59:1-4 no Nehemiya 9:30. Yini eshiwo lapha esebenza kusosonke isikhathi na?`

Kuwo wonke umlando, imibuso yakwaIsrayeli noJuda yabhekana nemiphumela yesono. Ngokusuka kuNkulunkulu, babeshiye ukuphepha kokunaka Kwakhe futhi bazibizela ukwahlulela Kwakhe. UNkulunkulu akamelani nokucindezela nokukhonza izithombe, kodwa akazange abathumele ekudingisweni ekoneni kwabo kokuqala. UNkulunkulu wasebenzisana nabantu Bakhe, ebancenga ngabaprofethi Bakhe ukuba baphenduke futhi babuyele kuYena. Abantu banqaba ukulalela. Empeleni, siyamangalisa, isikhathi uJehovah afuna ngaso ukuphendula bantu Bakhe ezonweni zabo. Iminyaka engamakhulu-khulu, ngabaprofethi, ekhipha izixwayiso, uNkulunkulu wayefuna ukufinyelela kubo.

UJesu mathupha wathi: “ ‘Jerusalema, Jerusalema, wena obulala abaprofethi nokhanda ngamatshe abathunyelwe kuwe, kukangaki ngithanda ukubuthela ndawonye abantwana bakho njengesikhukukazi sibuthela amazinyane aso phansi kwamaphiko aso, kepha anivumanga!’ ” (Mat. 23:37, NKJV).

Kepha nakuba besesekudingisweni, uNkulunkulu akazange abashiye abantu Bakhe bengenalo ithemba. Ngaphandle kokuhluleka kwabo okungapheli, uNkulunkulu wathembisa, “ ‘Kepha uyakufika eSiyoni engumhlengi, nakulabo abakwaJakobe ababuya eziphambekweni’ ” (Isa. 59:20, NKJV).

`Cabanga ngokuthembeka kukaNkulunkulu nakuba uIsrayei ephambukile kuYena. Ulibone kanjani iqiniso lalesisigaba lwesimiso sothando sikaNkulunkulu empilweni yakho na? Ngabe ukuphi ukuba Yena ubengabekezeli futhi enothando na?`
