`Funda iSambulo 1:1-3. Wabizelwa ukwenzani uJohane na?`

AmaKristu okuqala aphila esimweni sokulindela ukubuya kukaJesu ngokwesithembiso. Umpostoli uJohane wayebonile uKristu ekhuphukela emafwini futhi wayizwa ingelosi ichaza ukuthi Yena uzobuya ngendlela efanayo (Zenzo 1:10, 11). Manje ibandla lokuqala lalibhekene nokungaziwa. Yebo, uJesu wayeshilo ukuthi uzobuya. Kodwa, nini na?

Ngenkathi uJohane esekudingisweni esiqhingini sase-Patmos, uNkulunkulu wakhuluma kuye mayelana nesithembiso sokuza kwesibili kukaJesu: “Bhekani, uyeza ngamafu, namehlo onke ayakumbona, nabo abamgwazayo, nezizwe zonke zomhlaba ziyakulila ngaye. Yebo, Amen” (Samb. 1:7, NKJV). Johane wathola isambulo esibanzi sezigameko ezaziphawula ukuphela kwesikhathi. Ngaphandle kokukhuthaza nokukhuza amabandla ayisikhombisa (Sambulo 2, 3), izambulo zafaka imibono yokwahlulela (Samb. 14:6-12), ukuphela komhlaba (Sambulo 20), ukuza kwesibili kukaKristu (Sam. 14:14-16), kanye nokudalwa kabusha komhlaba (Sambulo 21, 22).

UJohane waziveza yena njengo-“mfowenu nomhlanganyeli kanye nani ekuhluphekeni nasembusweni nasekubekezeleni kukaJesu” (Samb. 1:9, NKJV). Wayekwazi Ukuhlupheka, hhayi nje iminjunju yokulinda, kodwa kokushushiswa okwakubangwa ngukushumayela iZwi likaNkulunkulu nokuba nobufakazi bukaJesu Kristu.

Kuyo yonke incwadi yeSambulo, uJohane wamenyelwa ekuboneni futhi wayalwa ukuba abhale lokho ayekubona. Esahlukweni sokugcina wabhala, “Manje mina, Johane, ngabona futhi ngazizwa lezizinto” (Samb. 22:8, NKJV). UJohane wayengufakazi wesiprofetho wezigameko zezinsuku zokugcina, ethunywe ukuba abelane namabandla esikhathi sakhe lokho ayekubonisiwe embonweni (Samb. 22:16). Usikhumbuza ngoJohane Umbhabhathizi, owamemezela ukuza kokuqala kukaJesu Kristu (Johane 1:6-8) ngomzuzu obucayi womlando wezwe.

Nathi futhi sibizelwe Ukufakaza njengoba kwenza uJohane. Nathi kumele sigcine ubufakazi bukaJesu (Samb. 19:10)—ubufakazi Yena abunikeza mayelana ngaYe ngabaprofethi. Singethole imibono njengoJohane, kodwa singalindela ngokubekezela ukubuya kukaKristu sibe sifakaza ngomsebenzi Wakhe ezimpilweni zethu futhi sibuka izimpawu zokukhululwa kwethu.
