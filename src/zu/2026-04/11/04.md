`Funda Abahluleli 2:16-19. Yimuphi umfanekiso obonakala lapha na?`

Ngokuguqukela okubucayi ebukhosini, ngesicelo sikaIsrayeli senkosi (1 Samuweli 8), isikhathi sabahluleli saphela. Kodwa lenguquko yaletha isidingo esikhulu sabaprofethi ukweluleka, Ukulungisa, ukukhuza inkosi, abaholi besizwe, kanye nabantu.

Ngesikhathi sokubusa kuka-Ahab, uNkulunkulu wathumela u-Elijah nombiko wesigwebo: ngeke kubekhona imvula ngoba uIsrayeli waye “ ‘lahle imithetho kaJehovah futhi balandele oBhali’ ” (1 Amakhosi 18:18, NKJV). Kunokuba aphenduke, u-Ahab wazama ukuzingela u-Elijah nokumbulala, kodwa uNkulunkulu wamvikela (1 Amakhosi 18:10-12). Ngenkathi, ngonyaka wesithathu wendlala, u-Elijah ebonakala enkosini, u-Ahab wasola yena ngokungabikhona kwemvula (1 Amakhosi 18:17)!

U-Elijah wabizela wonke uIsrayeli embukisweni wokugcina phakathi kukaNkulunkulu nabaprofethi bakaBhali (1 Amakhosi 18:19).

`Funda isimemezelo sika-Elijah kuIsrayeli kanye nempendulo ku-1 Amakhosi 18:21, 38, 39. Yini eyenzeka na?`

Isenzo sikaNkulunkulu somlilo owawuvela ezulwini, sihlangene nombiko ka-Elijah wesiprofetho, sathinta kakhulu abantu bakaNkulunkulu sabaholela ekuphendukeni. njengoIsrayeli owayenqikaza ekuzinikeleni ngokuphelele kuNkulunkulu, siphila kumasiko-mpilo angamvumi uNkulunkulu njengoMdali noMhlengi. Kunokuba sibengofakazi bakaNkulunkulu sinxuse abanye bathembeke kuYena, sivama ukubalekela ubizo lwethu Ngokulandela izinkanuko zethu, njengoba noIsrayeli enza.

UNkulunkulu uxwayisa ngokuthi ukulandela izinkanuko zethu kuzoholela ekulimaleni, ebuhlungwini, futhi kugcine ngokufa. Kodwa uma silandela izwi Lakhe, ngalokho “ ‘UJehova uyakukuhola njalo, asuthise umphefumulo wakho ezindaweni ezomileyo, aqinise amathambo akho; uyakuba njengensimu eniselweyo nanjengomthombo wamanzi, omanzi awo angaphuthi’ ” (Isa. 58:11, NKJV).

`Indalo yethu yenyama isenza sihlubuke, futhi singanakile, kuNkulunkulu. Yikuphi Ukukhetha okudinga sikwenze ukuze singaweli kuleyomvelo na?`
