---
title:  Izwi Lesiprofetho
date:   11/12/2026
---

UNkulunkulu wayelokhu njalo exhumana nabantu ngabaprofethi Bakhe. Lokhu kwakuyiqiniso ngezikhathi ezibucayi emlandweni. Akufanele yini ukulindela ukuthi uNkulunkulu akhulume kubantu Bakhe ekupheleni kwesikhathi na?

Ngenkathi ama-Millerite Adventist eba namava “Okudumala Okukhulu” ngo-UMfumfu 22, 1844, uNkulunkulu wabizela u-Ellen Harmon (White) emsebenzini wobuprofethi futhi wanikeza yena umbiko wamakholwa adumeleyo. Umbono wakhe wokuqala kwakungowokuwaduduza.

Esinye isikhathi esibucayi saba sekuqaleni kokushicilelwa kwemibhalo yama-Adventist nasekuhleleni ama-Adventist agcina iSabatha ekubeni yibandla. Ngemibhalo yawo kanye nangovangelo, laba bagcini beSabatha, ngama-1860, basebande babayizinkulungwane zamalungu. Kwadingeka behlelekile ukumemezela imibiko yezingelosi ezintathu (Samb. 14:6-12) ngokufanele. Ngenkathi abanye bephikisana nomqondo wokuhleleka, uNkulunkulu wasebenzisa u-Ellen G. White Ukukhuthaza lesisinyathelo. Ngo-May 21, 1863, ibandla i-Seventh-day Adventist lasekwa ngokusemthethweni.

Ngawo lowonyaka, uNkulunkulu wasebenzisa isipho sesiprofetho Ukukhuthaza ezempilo phakathi kwamakholwa. Iningi lama-Seventh-day Adventist lalingenayo impilo enhle noma lingaphili ngokwezempilo. Ngemibono emibili ebanzi yezimpilo ngo-1863 no-1865, lendlela yaguqulwa. Ama-Seventh-day Adventist aba ngabathuthukisi bezempilo nokuphila ngezempilo.

Ukusukela kuma-1870 kuye kuma 1890, u-Ellen G. White wayengumsizi ekusunguleni uhlelo lwama-Adventist lwezemfundo. Umqondo wakhe wemfundo eyengamela konke (ukufunda, ukwenza, nezikamoya) yamukelwa kancane, kancane futhi yasetshenziwa. Njengalokhu ayezokhomba: “Imfundo yangempela ichaza okungaphezulu kokulungiselela lempilo ekhona. Imayelana nobuntu bonke, kanye nobukhona bonke obungase bube ngobomuntu. Ingukuthuthukisa ngokuhambisana amandla omzimba, engqondo, kanye namandla ezikamoya.”—Education, p. 13.

Ngama-1880, wadlala indima eqinile ekuletheni ukulinganisa kwimfundiso yama-Seventh-day Adventist yosindiso. Wagcizelela ukuba ngumongo komusa kaNkulunkulu ngokulunga ngokukholwa ekugcizeleleni okukodwa Komthetho kwama-Adventist. Ukusukela ngo-1901 kuye ku-1903, wasiza ekuhleleni kabusha isakhiwo sebandla ukuze umsebenzi webandla emhlabeni ufezeke kangcono.

Kafushane, ngabe alikho ibandla lama-Seventh-day Adventist njengoba silazi namuhla ngaphandle komsebenzi ka-Ellen G. White. Wayeyisithunywa sikaNkulunkulu ngezikhathi ezibucayi.
