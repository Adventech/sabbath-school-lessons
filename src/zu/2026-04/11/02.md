---
title:  Isixwayiso Ngengozi Enkulu
date:   06/12/2026
---

UNkulunkulu ufuna ukuhlenga isintu. Nangemuva koKuwa, uNkulunkulu wafuna ubudlelwane no-Adamu no-Eva, futhi esikhathini eside izakhamuzi zasemhlabeni zazibiza “igama likaJehovah” (Gen. 4:26), ngokudlula kwezizukulwane, inani lalabo ababehamba noNkulunkulu lancipha. Labo ababeqhubeka nokulandela uNkulunkulu bafakaza ngokulunga Kwakhe, bagxeka izono zalabo ababebazungezile, futhi baxwayisa ngemiphumela yezenzo zabo.

Ngesikhathi sikaNowa,nokho, izakhamuzi zasemhlabeni zasezikhohlakele kangangokuba uNkulunkulu wanquma ukubabhubhisa (Gen. 6:5-7). UNowa, umshumayeli wokulunga (2 Pet. 2:5), ngokungangabazeki waxwayisa bonke ababelalela mayelana nokubhubha okuzayo. UNkulunkulu wanikeza uNowa imiyalelo yokwakha umkhumbi wokuphila wokusindisa bonke ababezolalela izixwayiso zakhe futhi baphenduke.

`Funda uHeberu 11:7, 2 Petro 2:5 no Genesisi 6:9. Kungaziphi izindlela uNowa “engumshumayeli wokulunga” na?`

Uzamcolo wawungumzuzu obalulekile emlandweni wesintu, futhi umbiko uqhamuka ngokuphindaphinda kwiTestamente Elidala neLisha njengesibonelo sokujula kokungcola kwesintu kanye nokuphakama komusa omangalisayo kaNkulunkulu. Ukulunga kukaNkulunkulu kubonakaliswa ekwahluleleni Kwakhe indalo enesono kanye nokukhulula Kwakhe bonke abamukela usindiso.

“Phakathi kokungcola, u-Methuselah, uNowa, kanye nabanye abaningi basebenzela Ukugcina ulwazi lukaNkulunkulu weqiniso luphila kanye nokukhuza igagasi lobubi. Eminyakeni eyikhulu namashumi amabili ngaphambi kukaZamcolo, uJehovah ngengelosi engcwele wamemezela kuNowa ngenhloso Yakhe, futhi wamkhomba ekwakheni umkhumbi. Esawakha umkhumbi kwakumele ashumayele ngokuthi uNkulunkulu wayezoletha Uzamcolo wamanzi phezu komhlaba ukubhubhisa ababi. Labo ababezowukholwa umbiko, futhi balungiselele lesosigameko ngokuphenduka nangokuguquka, babezothola intethelelo futhi basindiswe.”—Ellen G. White, Patriarchs and Prophets, p. 92.

UNkulunkulu akatshelanga uNowa ngoZamcolo nje kuphela futhi wasindisa ababekuwo umkhumbi, Yena futhi wasungula isivumelwano noNowa nendalo yonke, ethembisa ukungaphinde abhubhise umhlaba ngamanzi futhi (Gen. 9:8-17). Lonke uthingo luqukethe isithembiso, kanye nethemba, losindiso.

`Uma uphinda ubona uthingo, cabanga ngesithembiso esitholaka kulo. Kungani lokhu kumele kukududuze, wazi ukuthi uNkulunkulu uyazigcina izithembiso Zakhe na?`
