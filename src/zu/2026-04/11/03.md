---
title:  Umama KwaIsrayeli
date:   07/12/2026
---

Emuva kokuba ekhulule abakwaIsrayeli ebugqilini eGibhithe, uNkulunkulu wabanikeza izwe laseKanana. Wathembisa ukubabusisa kakhulu uma belandela imithetho Nemiyalelo Yakhe. Wabaxwayisa ngokuthi ukuphula kwabo isivumelwano sabo naYe kuyoba nomphumela wokuhlupheka nokubhubha (Duteronomi 27-30). Emuva kokufa kukaJoshuwa, abakwaIsrayeli baphambuka ekugcineni isivumelwano. Base behlukunyenzwa yizizwe ezingomakhelwane futhi bakhala kuNkulunkulu. Ekuphenduleni, “uJehovah wavusa Abahluleli ababakhulula ezandleni zalabo ababebaphanga” (Abahl. 2:16, NKJV). Waphinda futhi wathumela abaprofethi Ukukhuthaza abakwaIsrayeli bavuselele isivumelwano sabo noNkulunkulu kanye nokubanikeza amandla okumelana nababacindezelayo.

`Funda Abahluleli 4:4-16,Abahluleli 5:1-5.Yini u-Doborah ayenzela uIsrayeli na?`

U-Deborah wadlala indima ebalulekile yobuprofethi ekudluliseni umbiko kaNkulunkulu kuIsrayeli no-Barak, umlawuli wesizwe, ngenkathi ebiza amabutho. Kwaba ngu-Deborah owanikeza u-Barak Uphawu lokuqala impi, ethi, “ ‘Phakama! Ngokuba lolu wusuku uJehovah anikele ngalo u-Sisera ezandleni sakho. UJehovah akakuhambelanga phambili na?’ ” (Abahl. 4:14, NKJV).

Njengomahluleli kwaIsrayeli, u-Deborah wayengumongo. Ubuholi bakhe kanye nokholo lwakhe ezithembisweni zikaNkulunkulu zokunqoba baqinisa abakwaIsrayeli ekulweni kwabo nezitha zabo. Ikhono lika-Barak njengomkhuzi wempi kanye nokuvuma kwakhe, nakuba emanqika, ukuhola umkhosi wakwaIsrayeli kwakubalulekile, kodwa akukona okwanqoba impi.

Lokhu mhlawumbe yiwona mbiko obaluleke kunayo yonke endabeni yomprofethi u-Deborah no-Barak isosha. Phakathi kokubungaza ukunqoba okumangalisayo, okuyibona bantu abanamandla kwaIsrayeli—umahluleli kanye nomholi onqobile— bahole umphakathi ekudumiseni uNkulunkulu. Lokhu wuhlobo lwangempela lobuholi bezikamoya.

Umsebenzi ka-Deborah wobuprofethi ufakaza ngokuthembeka kukaNkulunkulu ngezikhathi zokuxakaniseka nokucindezelwa. Ngenkathi uNkulunkulu emnikeza umbiko ka-Barak, akazange angabaze ukuthi kungenzeka kutholakale amabutho anele, noma aphikisane ngokuthi abakwaIsrayeli babengenamandla okunqoba. Kunalokho, wadlulisa umbiko ngokuqiniseka futhi wakhuthaza u-Barak ukuba angangabazi futhi athembe ukuhlinzeka kukaNkulunkulu.
