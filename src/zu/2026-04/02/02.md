---
title:  U-Abrahama, Umvikeli WeSivumelwano
date:   04/10/2026
---

Ukusukela kuGenesisi kuye kwiSambulo, uNkulunkulu ubenayo insali ethembekileyo eyamukele ubizo lokulalela ngokuphendula umusa Wakhe (buka uJer. 31:7, 31-34). Ngokwesibonelo, eminyakeni eminingi emuva kokuhlakazeka kwaseBhabheli, uNkulunkulu wakhetha u-Abram (kamuva Abrahama) ukuba abe yisibonelo sesivumelwano sokholo, hhayi kuphela emndenini wakhe kodwa kuzozonke izizukulwane ezilandelayo. Ehanqwe ngukuhlubuka kanye nokungaziphathi kahle ngokwesimilo, u-Abrahama wabonakalisa Ukuthembeka obizweni lukaNkulunkulu.

UNkulunkulu wakhuluma imibandela yesivumelweno ku-Abrahama futhi wambonisa indlela usindiso lwesintu olwaluzofezeka ngayo ngomhlatshelo kaKristu (Gen. 15:6-17). Ukuvivinya ukholo luka-Abrahama kanye ngokulinganisa umhlatshelo weNdodana kaNkulunkulu, u-Abrahama wabizelwa ukuba anikele ngendodana yakhe, u-Isaka, njengomhlatshelo kuNkulunkulu (Gen. 22:1-17). Kwakuyisivivinyo esibuhlungu kubaba wabakholwayo. Cabanga ngazo zonke izizathu u-Abrahama ayengazisebenzisa ukuziba umyalelo osobala kaNkulunkulu, kepha ngokwethembeka walalela. UNkulunkulu wasimisa isandla sika-Abrahama ekubulaleni u-Isaka futhi wahlinzeka ngenqama.

Akubona bonke abaprofethi uNkulunkulu ayebabizela ukuxwayisa ngokubhubha okuzayo. Bonke, nokho, babebizelwa ukubuyisela abantu ekuthembekeni, futhi babaxwayise ngokuhlubuka, ngokucindezela labo abampofu nabasweleyo, kanye nokuphambuka esivumelwaneni nasemibandeleni yaso.

Okusemqola kunakho konke, nokho, abaprofethi kwakumele bambule isithembiso sosindiso esitholakala kuMesiya. Ubufakazi obumi njalo babaprofethi beBhayibheli kanye noMoya esikhathini sethu esilandela esabapostoli busibizela ukuba sibone futhi sixwayise abanye ngamanqamu ezayo azowela phezu komhlaba. Kepha kumongo walesosixwayiso yizindaba ezilungileyo zokuhlengwa kwabantu bakaJesu abathembekileyo, labo abahlezi esivumelwaneni sobudlelwane kanye Naye (Samb. 12:17).

`Funda uGenesisi 15:6, Galatiya 3:6-9, no Heberu 11:8. Yini mayelana nempilo ka-Abrahama kanye nesimilo eyamenza wafaneleka ukusebenzela uNkulunkulu, nakuba wayengumuntu onezici na?`

Kuningi okuhambisanayo phakathi kwesikhathi sika-Abrahama kanye nesethu. Njengalokhu ubuhedeni nokukhonza izithixo kwakudlangile ngesikhathi sika-Abrahama, kunjalo iningi lemiqondo encintisanayo namuhla ihlose ukususa noma ukuchitha ubuKristu. Ngokumelene nesiko elandile, abantu bakaNkulunkulu namuhla babizelwe ekubonakaliseni umfanekiso Wakhe ngokwethembeka ekuxhumaneni kwansuku zonke nabanye, kuhlangene nokumemezela umbiko wezingelosi ezintathu (Samb. 14:6-12).

`Izibhalo zithi u-Abrahama “wakholwa kuJehovah; futhi lokho kwabalelwa kuyena njengokulunga” (Gen. 15:6). Usibona kanjani isithembiso sevangeli esembulwe ngamandla kulendimana na? Yiliphi ithemba ongazithathela lona kulokhu na?`
