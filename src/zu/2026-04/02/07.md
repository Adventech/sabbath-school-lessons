---
title:  Ukubizelwa Emsebenzi Wobuprofethi
date:   09/10/2026
---

UNkulunkulu ubiza abaprofethi ngezikhathi eziqokiwe ngezinhloso ezikhethekileyo. Babelapho ngesikhathi Imibiko idingeka ngalesosikhathi. Kulengqikithi, ama-Seventh-day Adventist akholwa ukuthi u-Ellen G. White ngomunye walabo bantu owabonakalisa isibonakaliso sanamuhla sesipho sesiprofetho, esebenza njengomprofethi ongabaluliwe Ezibhalweni (sokhuluma kabanzi ngalokho ezifundweni ezizayo). U-Ellen Harmon [White] wamukela ubizo lwakhe lokuqala ngoUZibandlela 1844, emavikini ambalwa emuva kokuba ama-Adventist ka-Miller ayelindele uJesu abuye ngokukaDaniyeli 8:14: “Koze kube yizinsuku eziyizinkulungwane ezimbili namakhulu amathathu; bese iyahlanjululwa indlu engcwele.”

Nakuba u-Miller engazange abeke usuku okuyilona, abalandeli bakhe bagcina ngokubeka u-UMfumfu 22, 1844, ngokwesikhathi seBhayibheli soSuku Lokubuyisana njengesikhathi sokubuya kukaKristu emhlabeni. Lenkolelo yaletha ukulindela okumangalisayo kwizinkulungwane ngezinkulungwane e-North America nakwezinye izifunda zomhlaba. Ngokushesha babezohlangana noMsindisi wabo ezulwini. U-Miller, naye, wagcwaliswa yinjabulo nentshisekelo lapho kusondela lesisikhathi Sokuza kukaJesu.

Kodwa uKristu akazange aqhamuke, amakholwa ka-Miller abanamava abuhlungu kunawo onke emlandweni, i-Great Disappointment.

Lokhu kuyisisekelo kuso uNkulunkulu anikeza u-Ellen Harmon [White] umbono wakhe wokuqala. Umbiko wawo ophambili kwakungowokukhuthaza amakholwa ajabhileyo ngokuthi uNkulunkulu wayesenabo nakuba behumushe ngokungesiko. Umbono wakhomba endleleni esuka emhlabeni iya eJerusalema elisezulwini. Kulendlela kwakunamakholwa Abalindi ehamba eya Edolobheni Elingcwele. Kuthi “uma egcina amehlo awo kuJesu, owayephambi kwawo, ebaholela edolobheni, babephepile.”—Ellen G. White, Spiritual Gifts, vol. 2, p. 31.

Lombono wawuyizindaba ezinhle. UNkulunkulu wayeqala ukudala iqembu elisha kulokhu kudideka kokujabha. UJesu wayesenabo. Wayesabuya. Wayesabaholela endleleni yosindiso. Kwakumele nje bagcine amehlo abo “enamathele” kuYena. Babenezizathu ezinkulu zokungaphelelwa Yithemba, nakuyo inxushunxushu yokujabha.

Ngalokho, u-Ellen G. Harmon [White], intombazanyana ena-17 iminyaka ubudala, yabizelwa Emsebenzini wayo wobuprofethi. Okwakhe kwakungeyona indlela elula, ikakhulu ekuqaleni.

Nakuba kancane, kancane lesisipho samukelwa njengesiyiqiniso, u-Ellen G. White wayesaba ukuba yisithunywa sikaNkulunkulu. Ukuba ngumprofethi kaNkulunkulu kwakungakaze kubelula noma kuhlonipheke. Ngalokho, u-Ellen White wayezozinikela kanjani obizweni lukaNkulunkulu na?

Ngeviki elizayo sizobuka lomsebenzi okhethekileyo kanye nezinselele abaprofethi BeTestamente Elidala ababhekana nazo, ezinye zazo ezabonakala empilweni nasemsebenzini ka-Ellen G. White, njengalokhu sizofunda ngoLwesihlanu oluzayo.
