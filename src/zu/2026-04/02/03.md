---
title:  Umbiko Ka Eliya
date:   05/10/2026
---

`Funda u-1 Amakhosi 18:19-39. Yini ongayibona kulendaba mayelana nendlela ubuprofethi obungasebenza ngayo na?`

Ubizo luka-Eliya kanye nendlela yokukhuluma yayibonisa indima yakhe njengesithunywa sikaNkulunkulu. Ukuqeda ukuhlubuka KukaIsrayeli, uNkulunkulu wathumela u-Eliya Enkosini u-Ahabi ukuze amemezele ngeminyaka yesomiso. UNkulunkulu wasebenzisa lobubuhlungu ukuguqula abantu Bakhe basuke ekukhonzeni izithixo futhi babuyelel ekulaleleni. Inkosi nezwe basola Umprofethi u-Eliya ngosizi lwabo futhi bafuna ukuthi afe.

Entabeni Ikameli, u-Eliya wanqwamana nabaprofethi bakaBhali no-Ashera. Isithunywa sikaNkulunkulu senza ubizo olugcwele uMoya kuIsrayeli ukuba ahlukane nonkulunkulu bamanga futhi abuyele kuJehovah. Abantu babazi ukuthi izinqumo zabo zaziholele ekuhlubukeni kwezizwe. Babekulungele manje ukwamukela uNkulunkulu ka-Eliya futhi, owayezibonakalise njengophakeme kunonkulunkulu babo bamanga, empeleni, ababengekho.

Ngaphambili nje kokuza kwesibili kukaKristu, uNkulunkulu uyaleza ibandla Lakhe ukuba lmemezele umbiko ka-Eliya futhi, nokuthi abantu baguquke “ezithixweni” zabo futhi balungele ukuma kulolosuku Olukhulu lweNkosi. UNkulunkulu usakhuluma nabantu Bakhe ngemigudu eqokwe nguYena. Izwi Lakhe kanye nesipho sesiprofetho linikeza umyalo, isixwayiso, iseluleko, kanye nesikhuthazo.

“Imvuselelo yobunkulunkulu beqiniso phakathi kwethu yisidingo esikhulu kunazo zonke futhi nesiphuthuma kunazo zonke. Ukuthola lokhu kumele kube ngumsebenzi wethu wokuqala. Kumele kube nomzamo okhuthazelayo wokuthola isibusiso SikaJehovah, hhayi ngoba uNkulunkulu engazimisele ukuthula isibusiso Sakhe phezu kwethu, kepha ngoba asikulungele ukusamukela.”—Ellen G. White, Selected Messages, book 1, p. 121.

`Funda uMalakhi 4:5, 6. Umbiko ka-Eliya uwathinta kanjani amakhaya alabo abalandela uNkulunkulu na?`

Imvuselelo yeqiniso iqala enhliziyweni kanye nasekhaya. Umndeni uyitshe legumbi lebandla kanye nebhulokhi elidingekayo lomphakathi. Imvuselelo ekhaya isabalalisa uthando lwezulu ebandleni, emphakathini, nasesizweni. Lena yinhloso kaNkulunkulu ngekhaya lobuKristu. Imvuselelo igqugquzela abantu bakaNkulunkulu ukuba basebenze.

`Hlobo luni lwezithixo, uma zikhona, ezisemakhaya ethu, ezinhliziyweni, nasebandleni namuhla na?`
