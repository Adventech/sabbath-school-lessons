---
title:  UDaniyeli, Umprofethi Othembekileyo
date:   07/10/2026
---

AmaKristu amaningi ayayazi indaba kaDaniyeli 1, kuyo wama waqina Emele izimiso zempilo ayezifunde esemncane. UDaniyeli wayengumfanekiso wokuthembeka (Dan. 1:8). Lapho enikezwa izibiliboco zasebukhosini, waphikelela ngokudla Isidlo sezitshalo kanye nokuphuza amanzi kunokuba athabathe ukudla ayekunikezwa etafuleni lenkosi (Dan. 1:12-16). Lokhu Kwakuyisivivinyo esincane ngokumelene nalokho okwakuzoza kamuva, njengakuDaniyeli 6, lapho Ukuthembeka kwakhe kwakungaba ngokufa kuyena nje kalula.

`Funda uDaniyeli 2:46-48; Daniyeli 5:13, 14; no Daniyeli 6:25-27. Lezizindimana zisitshelani ngohlobo lofakazi uDaniyeli ayeyilona kubahedeni ababemkakile na?`

Buka ubufakazi balaba bahedeni ngamandla kaNkulunkulu kaDaniyeli. Nakuba lezizindaba zizodwa, zonke zembula isimiso esisemqoka: ngezimpilo zethu, nokuthembeka kwethu, kanye nangamandla kaNkulunkulu esebenza kithina nangathi, abanye bangakwazi futhi kumele bafunde ngoNkulunkulu wasezulwini, nokuthi Yena “unguNkulunkulu wonkulunkulu, neNkosi yamakhosi.”

Futhi, ngenxa yendikimba ephindaphindayo yeziprofetho zikaDaniyeli engokwahlulela, efinyelela ekuzeni kwesibili kukaKristu (ukunqoba kokugcina kukaNkulunkulu), incazelo yegama likaDaniyeli, “uNkulunkulu ngumahluleli wami,” ifaneleke kakhulu. Izibhalo zakhe zifanele ukufundisiswa futhi zinakwe njengalokhu zifanelekile namuhla. Iziprofetho zikaDaniyeli zisebenza njengesikhumbuzo njalo sokuhlala siqwashile futhi sigcine imithetho kaNkulunkulu, nakuzo izinto “ezincane” zokuphila.

Futhi, njengomprofethi, uDaniyeli wabikezela ngokuphakama kanye nokuwa kwezizwe kanye Nemiphumela yokungalaleli kwazo uNkulunkulu. Ngemibono yakhe Yesiprofetho, uDaniyeli wembula icebo likaNkulunkulu kanye nokunqoba kombuso kaNkulunkulu (Dan. 7:13, 14).

Cabanga, ngokwesibonelo, ngoDaniyeli , owabhalwa eminyakeni engamakhulu amahlanu ngaphambi kukaKristu. Ngokungenaphutha uDaniyeli wabonisa ukuvuka nokuwa kwalemibuso emikhulu emine yomhlaba, kuze kufinike esikhathini sethu (leso sezinyawo nezinzwane zesithombe), ngokusobala ebhekise Ezizweni zase-Europe yanamuhla, njengalokhu isiprofetho sifundisa ngokusobala ukuthi, “bazozihlanganisa nenzalo yabantu: kodwa ngeke banamathelana omunye komunye, njengalokhu insimbi inganamathelani nobumba” (Dan. 2:43). Isibikezelo esimangalisayo nge-Europe, kuze kube namuhla, nakuba beshadiselene futhi nakuyo yonke imizamo yokuba munye, izwekazi lalizoqhubeka njengezizwe ezahlukene, ezinemithetho ehlukene, amasiko, nezinkambiso. Naseminyakeni embalwa ngaphambi kokuwa kwe-Soviet Union, Akukho nazinhloli ezakubona lokho kuwa kuza, isibikezelo sikaDaniyeli sinikeza izizathu ezinamandla zokuthemba abaprofethi bakaNkulunkulu.

`Yonke imibuso kaDaniyeli 2 yafika yadlula njengesibikezelo. Lokhu kumele kusitsheleni ngokuthemba isiprofetho, esikhuluma ngombuso kaNkulunkulu waphakade ozayo (Dan. 2:44) na?`
