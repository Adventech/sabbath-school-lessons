### Funda Lokhu Kuleliviki
Gen. 15:6, Gal. 3:6-9, 1 Amakhosi 18:1939, Isaya 53, Dan. 2:46-48, Marko 1:2-5, Mat. 11:1-6.

> <p>Indimana Yenkumbulo</p>
> “Ngasengizwa futhi izwi likaJehovah, lithi:‘Ngubani engingamthuma, futhi ngubani ongasihambela na?’ ngasengithi, ‘Ngilapha! Thuma mina’” (Isaya 6:8, NKJV).

Ezikhathini zeBhayibheli, abaprofethi babevame ukudlala indima esemqoka emlandweni wabantu bakaNkulunkulu. Ngezinye izikhathi babegqama, bethatha iqhaza elinamandla, futhi elibucayi empilweni yomphakathi.

Kepha, ukuba ngumprofethi kwakulubizo olunzima. Izimpilo zabo zazivama ukungabi lula, kepha zigcwele usizi, Ukulahlwa, ukungaqondisiswa, kanye nokukhungatheka.

Imvamisa abaprofethi bakaNkulunkulu babengalwamukeli kalula ubizo lwabo Lokuba ngabaprofethi. Akumangalisi. Iningi labaprofethi lalibhekana nokumelana okuqinile kulabo okwakumele bamukele Imibiko yabo. Abaprofethi bakaNkulunkulu babezitshwa, begconwa, beshaywa, noma bafakwe ejele. Abanye babekhandwa ngamatshe noma bazingelwe njengezilwane zasendle. Iningi lafela inkolo.

Namuhla esizukulwaneni esigcwele udlame, umhobholo, ukungaziphathi kahle, ukuxhaphaza, kanye nawo onke amathonya kaSathane, izwi lomprofethi livama Ukulahlwa noma linganakwa. Sibizelwe ekusabeleni ezwini labaprofethi bakaNkulunkulu kanye nokuba yizithunywa zethemba elinikezwe thina ngabaprofethi.

Ngeviki elidlule sifunde ukuthi, emuva koKuwa e-Eden, uNkulunkulu wasebenzela ukusungula kabusha ukuxhumana nesintu. Kuleliviki sizohlola ezinye zezikhathi ezinkulu emlandweni ngenkathi uNkulunkulu ebiza abaprofethi ukuba bahlele kabusha abantu Bakhe ngemibiko Yakhe yezwe eliwileyo.

_Funda isifundo saleliviki ukulungiselela iSabatha, uMfumfu 10._
