---
title:  UJohane Umbhabhathizi, Ukulungisa Abantu
date:   08/10/2026
---

`Funda uMarko 1:2-5. Kwakuyini inhloso yomsebenzi kaJohane Umbhabhathizi na?`

Ivangeli likaMarko lisitshela ukuthi ukufika kukaJohane Umbhabhathizi kwakungukugcwaliseka kwesiprofetho sika-Isaya (Isa. 40:3). Ukukhula kukaJohane kusukela ebunganeni kuye ebudodeni kwaphawulwa ngukuthembeka kuNkulunkulu. Usathane wayesaba ukuphunyukwa ngukubusa kwakhe phezu kwesintu emuva kokuzwa izwi likaJohane ehlane (buka u-Ellen G. White, The Desire of Ages, p. 224). Inkohlakalo yabalaleli bakaJohane yambulwa ngendlela eyenza uSathane atatazele. Ukuxhakathisa kukaSathane phezu KukaIsrayeli kwakusengcupheni. Wazama ngokuphindaphinda futhi wahluleka ukususa Umbhabhathizi empilweni yakhe yokuthembeka kanye nokuthembela kuMesiya.

`Funda uMathewu 11:1-6. UJesu waluqinisa kanjani ukholo lukaJohane ngenkathi umshumayeli esetilongweni na?`

Usathane akakwazanga ukulinga uJesu ukuba one, futhi ukunqotshwa kwakhe ehlane kwamthukuthelisa kakhulu. Wayeseshintsha ukwenza ngokuthi umzala kaJesu, uJohane, afakwe ejele. Ukuboshwa kukaJohane kwamlimaza uJesu, nakuba uSathane engakwazanga ukumluthela esonweni. Lena kwakuyindlela kaSathane yokunqoba uJohane, ebe futhi ethwesa inhliziyo yoMsindisi ngosizi. Kodwa uJohane wathola izwi lenkuthazo kuJesu. Futhi ngaphandle Kwesimo sakhe esibi, nembala engaqondi ukuthi wayesahleleleni ejele uJesu enamandla, noma kunjalo uJohane waqhubeka nokuthembeka, kuze kube sekufeni.

Njengabaprofethi basendulo, nathi sibizelwe ukumemezela umbiko Wokuphenduka nokukholowa nowokulunga okuphelele kukaKristu (Samb. 14:6-12). Njengalokhu enza ngoJohane Umbhabhathizi, uSathane angasebenzisa ukungaqiniseki ukuba singabaze inhloso kaNkulunkulu ngezimpilo zethu. Akudingekile ukuba sidikibale uma sikhumbula ukuhola kukaNkulunkulu ezimpilweni zethu, ikakhulu lapho izinto zingasihambeli kahle, njengalokhu kwenzeka kuJohane, owaboshwa futhi wabulawa ngezenzo zowesifazane okhohlakeleyo kanye nendoda engenawo umgogodla. Noma yini abantu abangayenza kithina, singafinyelela kuNkulunkulu ngokholo, futhi Yena uyakusiphendula. Yena usithembisile amandla okwenza intando Yakhe (Zenzo 1:8). Emhlabeni ongemuva kwesikhathi sobuKristu nobuhedeni, kuyaphoqa ukuthi sibe ngofakazi abathembekile bakaKristu, noma zinjani izimo.
