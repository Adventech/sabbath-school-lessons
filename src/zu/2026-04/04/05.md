---
title:  AbaProfethi BeSifazane Nabafundisi Abasebasha
date:   21/10/2026
---

`Funda uLuka 2:36-38 neZenzo 21:9. Zisitshelani mayelana nabaprofethi besifazane na?`

Ngezikhathi zeTestamente Elidala, uNkulunkulu wabiza abesilisa nabesifazane ukuba yizithunywa Zakhe. Ngezikhathi zeTestamente Elisha, kunobufakazi obusobala babaprofethi besifazane. U-Anna waba ngowokuqala ukubizwa “ngomprofethikazi” kwiTestamente Elisha. Ungothile owathi, ekusebenzeni kwakhe ngokugcwele, “wakhonza uNkulunkulu ngokuzila nangokukhuleka ubusuku nemini. Futhi . . . wabonga uJehovah, futhi wakhuluma Ngaye” (Luka 2:37, 38, NKJV).

Izenzo 21:9 zithi uFilipi “wayenamadodakazi amane, izintombi eziprofethayo” (NKJV). Abomlando weTestamente Elisha, njengo-Eusebius, bathola kamuva imibhalo esho ukuthi ayehlala kuphi, nokuthi angcwatshwa kuphi. Noma kunjalo, esikubonayo ngabesifazane, Okungenzeka Abasebasha, abanesipho sokuprofetha. Asazi ngokuqondile ukuthi babeprofetha ngani noma izinga lomsebenzi wabo. Okukodwa, nokho, esikwaziyo ngalaba besifazane abasebasha abanesipho sokuprofetha: Akukho mazwi abo esiprofetho angena Ebhayibhelini. Okungukuthi, njengabaningi abanye, kwakukhona abaprofethi abazange bangene emqulwini, abaprofethi beqiniso omazwi abo, imibhalo, noma yini, engazange ibe yizincwadi zebhayibheli.

Ngaphezu kwabaprofethi besifazane, kunabafundisi abasha abahlobene kakhulu nabapostoli. Abapostoli bakhulisa abaholi abasha Emsebenzini. UPawule, ngokwesibonelo, ubhekisa kuThimothewu njenge-“ndodana yeqiniso Enkolweni” (1 Tim. 1:2, NKJV; buka futhi 1 Tim. 4:12). UPawule wafundisa uThimothewu izisekelo zomsebenzi wobufundisi. Uthi mayelana nobizo lwakhe “Lo myalo ngiwuthwesa wena mntanami Thimothewu njengeziprofetho ezandulelayo ngawe, ukuze ulwe ukulwa okuhle ngazo” (1 Tim. 1:18, NKJV).

Ku-Titus 1:4, uPawule wethula u-Titus njenge-“ndodana yeqiniso enkolweni yethu evamile” (NKJV). UPawule wanikeza u-Titus umsebenzi wokugcoba abaholi bamakholwa e-Crete, ibandla elaliyinselelo. Wanikeza u-Titus izeluleko kwezikamoya ngendlela yokubhekana nalomthwalo osemqoka, waphetha ngelithi: “Khuluma lokho, uyale, ukhuze ngamandla onke. Ungadelelwa muntu” (Titus 2:15, NKJV).

`Abasha, abadala, abesilisa, abesifazane—bonke bangaba nendima emsebenzini kaNkulunkulu. Iyiphi eyakho indima, futhi ungenza kanjani okwandile na?`
