---
title:  AbaProfethi Bamanga KwiTestamente Elisha
date:   22/10/2026
---

`Funda uMathewu 24:23, 24. Yini uJesu asixwayisa ngayo lapho na? Kungani lesisixwayiso kumele sisithathele phezulu na?`

UJesu ngoKwakhe wayengeke abesobala kunalokhu: “ ‘Ngokuba kuyakuvela okristu bamanga nabaprofethi bamanga, baveze izibonakaliso ezinkulu nezimangaliso, ukuze kudukiswe nabakhethiweyo, uma kungenzeka’ ” (Mat. 24:24, NKJV).

Izinkohliso Yena asixwayisa ngazo kungathi zinamandla kakhulu, uma, nembala, “izimpawu nezimangaliso ezinkulu” zizoba yingxenye yazo. Izigameko okungezezulu, zona ngokwazo, azibona ubufakazi bokusebenza kukaNkulunkulu, noma zingakholisa kangakanani. Sinezitha zemimoya yasemkathini. Njengalokhu uPawule asixwayisa: “Ngokuba asibambene negazi nenyama, kodwa sibambene nemibuso, namandla, nababusi bezwe balobu bumnyama, nabawomoya bobubi emkhathini” (Efes. 6:12, NKJV). Futhi lamandla asebenza ngabaprofethi bamanga.

Empeleni, abaprofethi bamanga akuyona into entsha. ITestamente Elisha libhekisa kubaprofethi bamanga bezikhathi zeTestamente Elidala (Luka 6:26). KwiTestamente Elisha abaprofethi bamanga bafaniswa “ ‘nezimpisi eziphangayo’ ” aba-“ ‘mbathise okwezimvu’ ” (Mat. 7:15, NKJV), futhi siyatshelwa ukuthi “ ‘siyobazi ngezithelo zabo’ ” (Mat. 7:16, NKJV).

Umsebenzi wabo omkhulu ngukukhohlisa (Mat. 7:17–20). UJesu ngoKwakhe wamemezela ukuthi “ ‘abaprofethi bamanga abaningi bayakuvela futhi badukise abaningi’ ” (Mat. 24:11, NKJV), futhi “ ‘uma kungenzeka, nabakhethiweyo’ ” (Mat. 24:24, NKJV; buka futhi no-Marko 13:22, 1 Johane 4:1).

`Ngokuka Efesu 6:10-18, singazivikela kanjani kunoma yiluphi uhlobo lwenkohliso na?`

Ukuphila ngokukholwa, ukugqoka “isivikelo sesifuba sokulunga,” sibophe izinkalo zethu “ngeqiniso,” kuhambisane nokuzinikela kuMoya Ongcwele ngokukhuleka, futhi sizinze Ezwini Lakhe—lezi yizindlela esingavikeleka ngazo kukho konke okungase kusiholele ophathe kwezikamoya nangemfundiso.

`Yini ongayenza ukuzihlomisa kangcono ukumelana nayo yoke inkohliso engamele izwe lethu na?`
