### Funda Lokhu Kuleliviki
Mat. 16:13-16, Efes. 2:20, Samb. 22:6, Luka 2:36-38, Zenzo 21:9, Efes. 6:10-18.

> <p>Indimana Yenkumbulo</p>
> “Kepha kukhona iziphiwo zomusa ezahlukeneyo, kepha nguye lowo Moya. Kukhona izinkonzo ezahlukeneyo, kepha yiyo leyo Nkosi. Kukhona imisebenzi yamandla eyahlukeneyo, kepha nguye lowo Nkulunkulu osebenza konke kubo bonke” (1 Korinte 12:4-6, NKJV).

Njengoba ubaba wayeshayela, indodana yakhe yayibuka izimoto ezidlulayo ifuna ukubona ezifana nekababa wakhe. Ubaba wayesekhomba imoto wababaza, “Ndodana, leyana ifana nemoto yami yokuqala!” Lapho befika ekhaya, ubaba watomula incwadi yokuchasisa yemoto yakhe yokuqala, kanye naleyo yalemoto yakhe yamanje. Ngokuqikelela wabonisa indodana yakhe izinto zemoto ngayinye, egqamisa okufanayo nokwehlukile. Kodwa, ngenkathi ubaba eqhathanisa izinjini, konke kwakufana. Kuningi okwakushintshile, kodwa hhayi injini.

UNkulunkulu wakhuluma ngabaprofethi kwiTestamente Elidala nakwElisha, futhi ukuqhubeka nokuzwana kwemibiko yabo kungenxa yokuthi uMoya Ongcwele— Umthombo—noma “injini,” yokuphefumulela kwezulu—akazange ashintshe. UMoya Ongcwele njalo unikeza amandla abaprofethi beqiniso ngesipho sesiprofetho. Yena futhi ubeka uKristu kumongo wemibiko Yesiprofetho, njengoba amaHeberu ekubeka obala (Heb. 1:1-3).

Kuleliviki sizohlola Isipho sokuprofetha kwiTestamente Elisha, sibuke okwakwenziwa ngabaprofethi, ukuthi ngobani ababesebenza njengabaprofethi, ukuthi abaprofethi babeqhathaniswa kanjani nabapostoli, kanye nengozi yabaprofethi bamanga, futhi.

_Funda isifundo saleliviki ukulungiselela iSabatha, uMfumfu 24._
