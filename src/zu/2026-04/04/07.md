---
title:  Ukugcina UJesu kanye neZwi Lakhe kumongo
date:   23/10/2026
---

Njengalokhu sesifundile, uNkulunkulu wayebiza abaprofethi ngezikhathi ezithile futhi abanikeze Imisebenzi ebalulekileyo. Umsebenzi wabo omkhulu kwakungowokwenza abantu bazi ngoNkulunkulu futhi babenobudlelwane obuthembekileyo kanye Naye. Yilokho okwakuhola u-Ellen G. White. Ukusebenza kwakhe kwakunezinto ezimbili ezisemqoka: ukuholela abantu kuJesu kanye nokubakhomba eZwini Lakhe—Ibhayibheli.

U-Ellen G. White wayenentshisekelo ngoJesu impilo yakhe yonke futhi elangazelela Ukwabelana nabanye ngothando lukaNkulunkulu. Izincwadi zakhe ezinhlanu ezaziwayo, i-The Conflict of the Ages, ziqala futhi ziphothule ngamazwi “uNkulunkulu uluthando.” Kwi-Steps to Christ (Izinyathelo Eziya kuKristu), mhlawumbe okuyiyona ncwadi yakhe eyaziwa kunazo zonke, wabhala: “Ukukhula kwethu emuseni, Injabulo yethu, ukusebenziseka kwethu—konke kuncike phezu kobunye bethu noKristu. Kungokuxhumana naYe, nsuku zonke, ihora ngalinye— ngokuhlala kuYena—lapho sikhula emuseni. Yena akayena nje uMqambi, kepha unguMphelelisi wokholo lwethu.”—Steps to Christ, p. 69.

Ngesikhathi sama-1890, ngenkathi esebenza ukubhala i-Desire of Ages, wabhala kwi-diary yakhe: “Ekulobeni ngempilo kaKristu ngizizwa nginomthwalo ojulile. Ngiyakhohlwa ukuphefumula ngokufanele. Angikwazi ukumelana nokuqina kwemizwa engehlulayo lapho ngicabanga ngokuhlupheka kukaKristu emhlabeni wethu.”—Manuscript 70, 1897. U-Ellen G. White wayethanda Ukukhuluma ngoJesu.

Okwesibili ayegxila kukho u-Elle G. White kwakuyiBhayibheli. Wayebona imibhalo yakhe njenge-“sibani esincane” esiholela abantu “ekukhanyeni okukhulu”— Ibhayibheli (Selected Messages, book 3, p. 29). Njalo wayekhuthaza abantu ukuba baye Ezibhalweni, kuzo uNkulunkulu ambule khona isimilo Sakhe sothando kanye necebo losindiso. Njengalokhu asho ekuqaleni komsebenzi wakhe: “Ngitusa, kuwe mfundi othandekayo, iZwi likaNkulunkulu njengomlawuli wokholo lwakho kanye nokwenza.”—Selected Messages, book 3, p. 30.

Eminyakeni ekamuva wabhala: “UJehovah ulangazelela ukuba nifunde amaBhayibheli enu. Yena akanikezanga ukukhanya okwengeziwe bokuthatha indawo yeZwi Lakhe. . . . Uma lidliwe lacolisiswa, [iZwi Lakhe] liba ngukuphila komphefumulo. Bese Imisebenzi Yokulunga ibonakala njengokukhanya ebumnyameni.”—Letter 130, 1901.

U-Ellen G. White wayekholwa futhi ukuthi bonke ababanga isiphuku sikaKristu sobuprofethi kumele bafundise “lokho uKristu ayekufundisa. Lokho ayekukhulumile, hhayi kuphela ngenkathi elapha mathupha, kodwa ngabo bonke abaprofethi nothisha beTestamente Elidala, kufakwa lapha. Imfundiso yabantu ivalelwa ngaphandle.”— The Desire of Ages, p. 826.

U-Ellen G. White wagcina uJesu kanye neBhayibheli kungumongo wamava akhe obuKristu. NgoLwesihlanu oluzayo, sizobuka imicabanga yakhe ngesambulo kanye nokuphefumulelwa.
