---
title:  Imisebenzi YabaProfethi BeTestamente Elisha
date:   20/10/2026
---

`Yini esiyitshelwa ngu-1 Korinte 14:3, Efesu 4:11-13, ne Sambulo 22:6 mayelana nendima yabaprofethi beTestamente Elisha na?`

Lezizindimana ezingenhla, nakuba zilusizo olungakanani, azethuli umbono ophelele ngakho konke okwenziwa ngabaprofethi beTestamente Elisha. Nakuba kunjalo, okuningi abakwenza kubonakalisa lokho okwenziwa ngabaprofethi beTestamente Elidala, ngalokho kunikeza ukuqhubeka kwesipho sesiprofetho.

Ku-1 Korinte 12:10, ukuprofetha kubalulwa phakathi kwezinye izipho zikamoya. Emfanekiweni womzimba kaKristu (1 Kor. 12:27, 28), abaprofethi babalwa belandela abapostoli, nabo abathola izambulo kuNkulunkulu. Ngokuka-1 Korinte 14:3: “Kepha oprofethayo ukhuluma kube ngukwakha nokuyala, nokududuza kubantu” (NKJV).

Abase-Efesu 4:11-13 benza uhla olufanayo lwezipho zikamoya, njengaku-1 Korinte 12. Abaprofethi babalwa phakathi kwabapostoli, abavangeli, abafundisi, kanye nothisha (Efes. 4:11). Lababantu bakhona “ukuze abangcwele bapheleliselwe umsebenzi wokukhonza, kwakhiwe umzimba kaKristu, size sifinyelele sonke ebunyeni bokukholwa nasekwazini iNdodana kaNkulunkulu, ebudodeni obupheleleyo, esilinganisweni sobukhulu sokugcwala kukaKristu” (Efes. 4:12, 13, NKJV). Njengalokhu kubonakala kwenye indawo, uJesu ungumongo wombiko wabo.

Incwadi yeZenzo yakhuluma ngabaprofethi ababili, uJudasi noSila (akekho kubo owabhala incwadi Ebhayibhelini), “bayala futhi baqinisa abazalwane ngamazwi amaningi (Zenzo 15:32, NKJV). Nakuba, yebo, ngezinye izikhathi abaprofethi kumele baxwayise, baze baqinise izwi ngezinye izikhathi, baphinde—ngokwezimo— bakhulume amazwi enkuthazo, ikakhulu ngezikhathi ezinzima, amazwi nembala amukelwa ngenjabulo yilabo abadinga ukuwezwa kanye nethemba ngesithembiso esiqukethwe yiwo.

Umsebenzi wokukhuthaza uyabalwa ngokwengqikithi yokuprofetha (1 Kor. 14:31, NKJV). Ngezikhathi zokuhlupheka nokushushiswa, isipho sokuprofetha sibe ngumthombo wenkuthazo kanye nethemba.

KwiSambulo 22:6, uJesu wethulwa njenge-“Nkosi uNkulunkulu wabaprofethi abangcwele” futhi “ ‘omazwi abo ethembekile futhi eyiqiniso’ ” (NKJV). Nembala, noma yini eqhamuke ezincekwini zikaNkulunkulu, ngenkathi bekhulumela uNkulunkulu, izo-“thembeka futhi ibe yiqiniso,” futhi. Emhlabeni kuwo esingathemba okuncane, nembala singakwethemba lokho okushiwo ngabaprofethi bakaJehovah, ikakhulu eZwini Lakhe, okuyigunya lethu lokugcina nelikhulu.
