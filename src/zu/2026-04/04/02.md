---
title:  UJesu EnguMprofethi
date:   18/10/2026
---

UMose wabikezela ukuthi “ ‘uJehovah uNkulunkulu wenu uyakunivusela uMprofethi ofana nami phakathi kwenu, kubazalwane benu. Yena niyomlalela’ ” (Dut. 18:15, NKJV). Kwakungubani lomprofethi na? Izibhalo zikubeka obala: kwakukhomba kuJesu ngoKwakhe. “ ‘Ngokuba uMose wathi kokhokho, “INkosi uNkulunkulu iyakunivusela umprofethi kubazalwane benu njengami, niyakumlalela kukho konke azakukukhuluma kini” ’ ” (Zenzo 3:22, NKJV; buka futhi uJohane 6:14).

`Iyini indima yobuprofethi bukaJesu na? Waphenduleka kanjani lombuzo ngesikhathi sikaJesu na? (Mat. 16:13-16, Marko 8:27-29, Luka 9:18-20).`

UJesu, njengoKristu, uMesiya, nguYena bonke abaprofethi beTestamente Elidala ababekhomba kuYe. KwiTestamente Elisha, uJesu ubonwa njengesigcwalisekiso seziprofetho zabo zikaMesiya. KuLuka 24:13-35, Yena uzethula njengaLowo iziprofetho ngoMesiya ezazibhekise kuYe.

Empeleni, kuJohane 1:1-5, incazelo enikezwayo kaliZwi lapha ayihambisani nanoma yimuphi umuntu noma umprofethi nje ongumuntu. UliZwi ukhonjwa njengoMdali, ngokuba “zonke izinto zenziwa ngaYe, futhi ngaphandle Kwakhe Akukho okwenziwe okwadalwa” (Johane 1:3, NKJV). Okungukuthi, Yena wadala konke okwadalwa, okuyikho konke ezulwini nasemhlabeni, manje okulinganiselwa ebubanzini obungu-93 billion light-years.

Kukho konke “uNkulunkulu wayesethi” kukaGenesisi 1, sizwa uJesu ekhuluma. Yena owathi, “Makube khona ukukhanya” (Gen. 1:3) nguYena futhi “ongukukhanya kwabantu” (Johane 1:4), futhi ngecebo losindiso (Johane 1:4, 5), ususa ubumnyama bezwe.

Amazwi kaNkulunkulu esiprofetho aqinisekile futhi agcizelelwe. Uma elalelwa, aba “ngukukhanya okukhanya endaweni emnyama, kuze kuse futhi inkayezi yokusa iphakame ezinhliziyweni zenu” (2 Pet. 1:19, NKJV). Isiprofetho siyoqhubeka nokuletha ukukhanya emhlabeni wethu Onesono aze uJesu, “Inkanyezi yoKusa,” kanye noMthombo wokukhanya kwesiprofetho, angenise umbuso Wakhe waphakade.

Kuze kube yilesosikhathi, ngokuphila Kwakhe, nokufa, kanye nokuvuka, uKristu unikeza thina sonke ukuphila Okuphakade. Umbuzo okuyiwona kuphela ngowokuthi sizokwamukela noma cha.

`Gxila eqinisweni lokuthi uJesu, uMdali (Johane 1:1-3), nguYena futhi owafa esiphambanweni ngenxa yezono zakho. Leliqiniso elimangalisayo lisifundisani ngeqiniso lothando lukaNkulunkulu ngathi na?`
