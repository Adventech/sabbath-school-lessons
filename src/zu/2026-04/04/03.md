---
title:  AbaPostoli NabaProfethi
date:   19/10/2026
---

ITestamente Elisha libalula umsebenzi wabaprofethi kanye nowabapostoli. Abapostoli babe-“yizithunywa,” noma “ngabathunyiwe” bevela kuNkulunkulu, njengalokhu kuchaza igama lesiGriki apostolos. Kuwo onke amaVangeli, igama “umpostoli” libhekisa kubafundi abangu-12. Lonke ke iTestamente Elisha liphasa lomqondo futhi lifake nabathile ababengekho phakathi kwabafundi bokuqala bakaKristu.

UPawule uzibiza ngompostoli izikhathi ezingu-12 ezincwadini zakhe. KwabaseRoma 1:1, 2, uxhuma ubizo lwakhe lobupostoli, kanye nokuqukethwe ekushumayeleni kwakhe, nombiko wabaprofethi beTestamente Elidala. UPawule uzethula njengompostoli wabeZizwe (Rom. 11:13). Wagcotshwa “ngokwentando kaNkulunkulu” (1 Kor. 1:1).

Ngokungafani nabapristi ababelandela uzalo ukusukela kobaba kuye kumadodana, uNkulunkulu wayeqoka abaprofethi nabapostoli ngokwentando Yakhe. Lapho ubupostoli bukaPawule buphikiswa, wavikela ubizo lwakhe (1 Kor. 9:1, 2). UPawule wabona ubupostoli bakhe njegobakhela phezu komsebenzi kaKristu, Isisekelo sebandla (Efes. 2:20). Njengabamukeli besambulo sikaNkulunkulu, abapostoli babanokuphefumulelwa okusezingeni elilingana nokwabaprofethi (Efes. 3:1-5).

KuPetro 3:1, 2, uPetro ubhekisa kubaprofethi njengalabo abeza ngaphambilini futhi omazwi abo ayebalulekile, kepha manje abapostoli bethulwa njengabathwele Igunya elifanayo. Kulezizindimana, kubhekisa ezikhathini zeTestamente Elidala nakwElisha. Lapho abaprofethi bebonakala beyizithunywa zikaNkulunkulu ngesikhathi seTestamente Elidala, abapostoli beyizithunywa zikaNkulunkulu ngesikhathi seTestamente Elisha, kwakukhona futhi abantu ababeprofetha noma besebenzisa isipho sokuprofetha ababengebona abapostoli. Isipho sokuprofetha sasingagcini kubapostoli ngezikhathi zeTestamente Elisha. Futhi akubona bonke abaprofethi ababengabapostoli (Zenzo 15:32).

`Abapostoli nabaprofethi basebenza kanjani ngokuhlobene noKristu nebandla, ngokuka-Efesu 2:20 no-Efesu 3:5 na?`

Futhi siyazi ngokufunda lokho abapostoli abakubhala kwiTestamente Elisha, uJesu wayengumongo cishe wakho konke ababekubhala. Nganoma iyiphi indlela, impilo Yakhe, ukufa, ukuvuka, umsebenzi wobupristi obukhulu, kanye nokubuya babegxile kukho.

`Okuyikona kuphela okubhaliwe okusemthethweni ngamazwi abapostoli nabaprofethi mayelana noJesu, impilo Yakhe, nemisebenzi Yakhe, yiBhayibheli. Lokhu kumele kusitshele ini ngesizathu sokuthi iBhayibheli kumele libe negunya lokugcina ngalokho esikukholwa ngoJesu nangokusifela Kwakhe na?`
