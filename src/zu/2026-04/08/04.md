---
title:  Ingqikithi Yokubhala
date:   17/11/2026
---

Indaba exoxwa ngumshumayeli owaziwao ikhuluma ngendoda eyayibhekene nezinkinga eziqinile futhi eyayifuna ukwazi intando kaNkulunkulu ngayo. Yavula Ibhayibheli layo yakhomba nje, umnwe wayo Wahlala KuMathewu 27:5, ofundeka kanje, “UJudasi wahamba futhi wazilengisa” (NKJV). Yethuswe yilendimana, waphinda wenza okufanayo, ezama ukuthola umbiko okhuthazayo, ukuba nje umunwe wayo uhlale KuLuka 10:37: “Wayesthi uJesu kuye, ‘Hamba wenzenjalo” (NKJV). Zombili yizindimana eziyiqiniso zeZibhalo, kodwa kumele ziqondwe ngokwengqikithi yazo.

Nembala, inhlanekezela eningi yemibiko yeBhayibheli isuka emkhubeni ovamile wokufunda Izindimana ngazodwa ngaphandle kwengqikithi yazo. Lendlela ivulela Izibhalo kulo lonke uhlobo lokuhumusha okungeyilo.

`Funda uGenesisi 3:1-5 no Mathewu 4:1-11. Usathane wawahlanekezela kanjani amazwi kaNkulunkulu ngokuwasebenzisa ngaphandle kwengqikithi yawo na?`

Abanye abantu bahlanekezela umbiko weBhayibheli ngokuqiniseka ngenxa yokungazi lutho. Kepha abanye bekwenza lokho ngamabomu, njengalokhu uSathane enza ezikhathini ezimbili ezibalulwe Ezibhalweni ezingenhla. Nakuba kuyini okukhuthazayo, umphumela uyingozi sonke isikhathi.

Ensimini yase-Eden (Gen. 3:1-5; qhathanisa neSamb. 12:9), uSathane wabonakala ku-Eva njengenyoka ehehayo nefuna ukwazi ekwazi Ukukhuluma ulimi lwabantu. Ehlwitha amazwi kaNkulunkulu kwingqikithi yawo yokuqala, uSathane waqala wawalulaza wayesewanikeza isihumusho sakhe senkohliso.

Ngenkathi uJesu ethathwa nguMoya oNgcwele umusa ehlane (Mat. 4:1-11, Luka 4:1-13), “Usathane weza njengengelosi yokukhanya ehlane lokulingwa ukuzokhohlisa uKristu.”—Ellen G. White, in The Advent Review and Sabbath Herald, December 18, 1888. Ekukhulumisaneni kwakhe noKristu, uSathane wacaphuna ngokungekona iHubo 91:11, 12, ukuze agudle ukuthi uNkulunkulu wayezovikela nalabo abazifaka ngamabomu ezimweni eziyingozi.

Amava asEnsimini yase-Eden kanye nasehlane lokulingwa aqinisa ubukhona bempikiswano enkulu, efaka ukuhumshwa kweZwi likaNkulunkulu, elikhulunyiwe nelibhaliwe. Kumele asisize ukubona ukubaluleka kokugcina ukuqonda kwethu kwemibiko yeBhayibheli Iqinisekile kwingqikithi yayo.

`Iqiniso alidinge manga ukwembulwa. Kungani, ke, kumele sigweme isilingo sokukhipha imibhalo kwingqikithi ukuzama ukuqinisa amaphuzu ethu, nakuba eyiqiniso kangakanani lawomaphuzu na?`
