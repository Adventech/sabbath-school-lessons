Ukuhumusha kwethu Izibhalo kuthintwa yithonya lokuhlawumbisela kwethu. Labo ababona Ibhayibheli njengeqoqo lemiqulu yasendulo Abakwazi ukubona ubunye phakathi kwezincwadi zalo ezahlukene. Kodwa ngokusebenzisa isimiso esiyisisekelo sokuthi “zonke Izibhalo ziphefumulelwe nguNkulunkulu futhi ziyinzuzo” (2 Tim. 3:16, NASB), singakuthakasela ubunye balo futhi sivumele Ibhayibheli lizihumushe ngokwalo.

`Funda uMathewu 5:17-48. UJesu wazichaza kanjani izimfundiso zeTestamente Elidala ezibalulwe kulesisahluko na?`

Ukufunda ngokuthi hwaphu uMathewu 5:17-48 kunganikeza umqondo wokuthi uJesu uchitha izimfundiso zeTestamente Elidala ngokufaka ezintsha Zakhe. Kodwa ukufunda ngokuqikelela kuveza obala ukuthi uJesu wayembula izigaba ezijulile zikamoya zeTestamente Elidala. Kulomqondo, wayegcwalisa isithembiso sikaJeremiya 31:31-33 sokuthi ngaphansi kwesivumelwano esisha umthetho kaNkulunkulu wawuzofakwa ezingqondweni futhi ubhalwe ezinhliziyweni zabantu Bakhe (qhathanisa no Heb. 8:8-10, Heb. 10:16).

Ngezinye izikhathi isihumusho sesahluko sinikeziwe kuyo incwadi isahluko esitholakala kuyo. Ngokwesibonelo, kuDaniyeli 2, sithola incazelo yephupho eliyimfihlo LeNkosi u-Nebuchadnezzar Idan. 2:31-35) elandelwa yisihumusho sephupho (Dan. 2:36-45). Ngokufanayo, KuMathewu 13 sinomfanekiso womhlanyeli (Mat. 13:1-9), bese kuba yisihumusho sawo umfanekiso (Mat. 13:18-23).

Ngezinye izikhathi isihumusho sombiko sinikezwa ngumprofethi wakamuva. Ngokwesibonelo, u-2 IziKronike 36:22, 23 ziqinisa ukugcwaliseka kwesiprofetho seminyaka engu70, njengalokhu sibikezelwe kuJeremiya 25:11, 12 nakuJeremiya 29:10. Kepha, iningi lezimpawu zeTestamente Elidala zagcwaliseka kuKristu, futhi iningi lemibhalo yeTestamente Elidala ihumushwa kwiTestamente Elisha.

“ITestamente Elidala liyivangeli ngemifanekiso ngezimpawu. ITestamente Elisha lingokuphathekayo. Elinye libalulekile njengelinye.”—Ellen G. White, Selected Messages, book 2, p. 104. Nembala, iTestamente Elisha liyisihumusho esiphefumulelwe seTestamente Elidala.
