---
title:  Incazelo Yombhalo
date:   18/11/2026
---

Onke amaKristu kumele aziqondele ngokwawo iBhayibheli. Ibhayibheli lisobala ngokwanele ukuba bonke abantu baqonde indlela yosindiso, kepha liphica ngokwanele ukuphonsa inselelo kwabahlakaniphe kakhulu. Izibhalo zingumnotho ongapheli wolwazi olusindisayo ongeke waqedwa ngokuphelele.

`Funda uLuka 10:25-28. Abanye abantu babanga ukuthi iBhayibheli alidinge kuhumushwa. Uma kunjalo, kungani uJesu abuza ummeli lokhu, “ ‘Yini ebhaliwe Emthethweni kaNkulunkulu na? Ukuhumusha kanjani na?’ ” (Luka 10:26, Message)?`

Ukubamba incazelo Yombhalo, kumele siqonde kuqala amagama asetshenzisiwe, bese kuba yindlela asetshenziswe ngayo ngokuhambisana nengqikithi Yombhalo kanye nayo yonke Ingqikithi yebhayibheli, futhi nesimo sawo somlando wesikompilo, kangangokuba singakwazi ukukwenza lokho. Ekugcineni, iziphetho zethu kumele zihambisane ngokugcwele nezimfundiso ezibanzi zeZibhalo. Ukuqhathanisa Izibhalo ezihambisanayo—njengalokhu kuvamile kumaVangeli amane—kunganothisa ukuqonda kwethu.

`Qhathanisa uMathewu 5:43-48 noLuka 6:27-36. Ingqikithi kaMathewu 5:43-48 nombhalo ohambisana nawo, Luka 6:36, isisiza kanjani ekuqondeni kangcono incazelo yoku-“phelela” KuMathewu 5:48 na?`

Obunye ubunjalo obubalulekileyo bomhumushi Othembekileyo weZibhalo yinhlanganisela yokwenzisisa nokuzithoba. Khumbulani, akekho kithina ongumnikazi weqiniso, sonke sibuka kalufifi.

Kumele njalo siphokophele imfundiso ephusile (2 Tim. 4:1-5). Isimiso esisemqoka ngesokugxila kulokho esikuqondayo, kokusobala, bese sakhela kukho, okuthi ezikhathini eziningi, ngokuhamba kwesikhathi, kusisize ekuqondeni izinto njengamanje ezingekho obala. Nganeno kwephakade, kuyohlala njalo kunemibuzo engaphenduleki.

`Cabanga ngesikhathi owawungaqondi okuthile okwakukuhlupha kakhulu, bese uthola incazelo yako kamuva. Lawomava kumele akulekelele kanjani ekuthembeni uNkulunkulu ezintweni eZwini Lakhe, okwamanje, ongakaziqondi na?`
