---
title:  Umhlahlelo WeBhayibheli
date:   19/11/2026
---

Ukufunda kwethu kweBhayibheli kumele kugxile ezinhlosweni ezimbili ezinkulu: ukukhula kwethu mathupha kwezikamoya kanye nokuholela abanye ebudlelwaneni obusindisayo noJesu. UKristu wachaza umKristu weqiniso njengaLowo oholwa nguMoya oNgcwele “kulo lonke iqiniso” (Johane 16:13), ophila “ ‘ngalo lonke izwi eliphuma emlonyeni kaNkulunkulu’ ” (Mat. 4:4, NKJV), kanye nofundisa abanye “zonke izinto”Yena asiyaleze ngazo (Mat. 28:20).

`Funda uLuka 4:16-30 no Luka 24:25-27, 44-49. Singafundani ngendlela uKristu achaza ngayo Izibhalo lapha na?`

UJesu Kristu waye, futhi useyiyo, futhi uyohlala eyiyo “indlela, neqiniso, nokuphila” (Johane 14:6, NKJV). Nakuba kunjalo, ukushumayela Kwakhe kwakugxile kakhulu Ezibhalweni. Ngokwesibonelo, uMarko 1:14, 15 uthi, ,”Manje emuva kokuba uJohane efakwe etilongweni, uJesu weza eGalile, eshumayela ivangeli lombuso kaNkulunkulu, ethi, ‘Isikhathi sesigcwalisekile, futhi umbuso kaNkulunkulu usufikile’ ” (NKJV). Isikhathi ngokusobala sasikhomba kuDaniyeli 9:24-27. Entshumayelweni yasEntabeni (Mathewu 5-7), wacaphuna ku-Eksodusi 20:13 no 14 (Mat. 5:21, 27). KuLuka 4, wacaphuna kuIsaya 61:1, 2. Ngokuphele uJesu wayengumshumayeli weBhayibheli.

Kwesinye isibonelo, iZenzo 8 zisitshela ukuthi umthenwa woMtopiya “wayeze eJerusalema ukuzokhonza” (Zenzo 8:27, NKJV), Okuchaza ukuthi wayejwayelene nenkolo yamaJuda okungenani eminye yemikhosi yayo. Wayeze abe neyakhe incwadi kaIsaya, ayeyifunda ngokuqiniseka endleleni ebuyela ekhaya. Kepha wayengasiqondi isiprofetho seNceku ethwele izono, ngalokho wacinga othile ukuba amchazele ngaso.

Lamava asifundisa izifundo ezimbalwa ezibalulekile. Sokuqala, ukufunda nje umbhalo weBhayibheli akuholeli njalo ekuqondeni okujulileyo kumbhalo. Okwesibili, ekufundeni Izibhalo singakwazi, futhi simele, ukufunda komunye nomunye. Okwesithathu, sinomthwalo thina mathupha wokwabelana ngemfundiso ephusile nabanye.

Ukuhlahlela kukaFilip kwencwadi kaIsaya neminye imibhalo yeTestamente Elidala kwaholela umthenwa ekubhabhathizweni (Zenzo 8:35-39; qhathanisa neHub. 51:12). Ukucacisa kwethu iZibhalo kumele kube nenhloso efanayo (buka uJohane 17:17).

`Nakuba ngezinye izikhathi singadinga usizo ekuqondeni imibhalo, kungani kumele siqiniseke ngalokho esikukholwayo na?`
