### Funda Lokhu Kuleliviki
Mathewu 5:17-48; 1 Korinte. 16:20; Mat. 4:1-11; Luka 10:25-28; Luka 24:25-27, 44-49.

> <p>Indimana Yenkumbulo</p>
> “Wayesethathela kuMose nakubaprofethi bonke, Yena wabachasisela emibhalweni yonke izinto eziqondene naye” (Luka 24:27, NASB).

Kwimvelamuva yokubethelwa kanye nokuvuka kukaJesu, uKristu wabonakala kubafundi ababili ababedangele, bobabili bavuma, “ ‘Besithembile ukuthi nguYena obezohlenga uIsrayeli’ ” (Luka 24:21, NKJV). Abafundi babelindele uMesiya owayezokhulula amaJuda ekuphathweni ngamaRoma. Lokhu kulindela okungamanga kanye nomphumela wakho wokudangala kwakusuka ekuhumusheni okungeyikho nokucalanye kweziprofetho zikaMesiya zeTestamente Elidala.

UKristu waxazulula ukungaqondi kwalababafundi ababili ngokuchaza Izibhalo kubona ngokubanzi (Luka 24:25-27). Ukuhumusha kukaKristu kweZibhalo kwavula amehlo abo futhi kwavutha izinhliziyo zabo phakathi kwabo (Luka 24:31, 32). Kamuva uJesu wabonakala eqenjini elikhulu labafundi eJerusalema, futhi “wayesevula ingqondo yabo, ukuze baqonde imibhalo” (Luka 24:45, NKJV; buka futhi no Luka 24:33, 36, 44-49).

Lamava agqamisa imiphumela emihle yokuqonda ngokufanele imibhalo Yesiprofetho. Ukuqonda kwamanga kwesiprofetho kungaholela engozini kwezikamoya.

_Funda isifundo saleliviki ukulungisela iSabatha lika uLwezi 21._
