---
title:  Ukufunda U-Ellen G. White
date:   20/11/2026
---

Simfunda kanjani u-Ellen G. White na? Nazi iziqondiso ezimbalwa zokuqonda ngokufanele zokusetshenziswa kwimibhalo ka-Ellen G. White.

Esokuqala, qala ngomoya wokukhuleka, omuhle kanye nomqondo ovulekile, kunokuba nomoya wokungabaza. Umqondo wethu uyisibuko esipenda kone esikuzwayo, esikufundayo, nesikubonayo. Sidinga uMoya oNgcwele ukuqondisa Ukucabanga kwethu. Njengoba u-Ellen G. White mathupha aqaphela: “Uma uhlola Izibhalo Ukuvikela imibono yakho, ngeke wafinyelela eqinisweni. Cinga ngenhloso Yokufunda lokho uJehovah akushoyo.”—Christ’s Object Lessons, p. 112. Kunjalo nangemibhalo yakhe.

Okwesibili, cabanga ngengqikithi yalokho okufundayo. Abantu bavama ukusekela ukuqonda kwabo imibhalo ka-Ellen G. White emushweni ozimele wodwa noma ocezwini lwesigaba olususiwe kwingqikithi yalo yomlamndo noma umbhalo. U-Ellen G. White wabhala ukuthi “isikhathi nendawo kumele iqikelelwe.”—Selected Messages, book 1, p. 57. Lomkhuba oneshwa wokuziba Ingqikithi idale, futhi isaqhubeka nokudala, ukuqhubeka kwenkinga kwamanye amalungu ebandla.

Okwesithathu, Gxila kwizimiso. Yiliphi iphuzu eliphambili (amaphuzu) u-Ellen G. White awagcizelela kulombhalo othile na? ngokwesibonelo, ngo-1894 u-Ellen G. White wabhalela amalungu ebandla e-Battle Creek, Michigan, esola ukuthengwa kwamabhayisikili (buka kwi-Testimonies for the Church, vol. 8, pp. 50-53). Isimiso u-Ellen G. White ayefuna ukusikhuthaza ngesobunceku obufaneleyo bengcebo enikezwe nguNkulunkulu, njengemali (ngalesosikhathi amabhayisikili ayebiza kakhulu).

Okwesine, funda konke u-Ellen G. White akubhala ngezihloko ezithile. Hlola imisho efihlakele ngokwengqikithi yemfundiso yakhe ngesihloko. Empilweni yakhe u-Ellen G. White walila ngokusetshensizwa budedengu kwemibhalo yakhe: “Okuningi okuthiwa ngumbiko ovela kuDade White, kwenza ukuthi angamelwa kahle uDade White, kumenze afakaze ngokuhambisa nezinto ezingavumelani nomqondo wakhe noma ukwahlulela.”—Ellen G. White, Selected Messages, book 1, p. 44.

Ekugcineni, qinisekisa ukuthi u-Ellen G. White wakusho lokho. Ziningi izisho okuthiwa ngezika-Ellen G. White kube kungenjalo. Okuyiyona ndlela ephephile yokuqiniseka ngokuthi esikufundayo ngokuka-Ellen G. White ngeyokubheka inkomba yomothombo wako. Lapho sesiqinisekile ukuthi kutholakala kuphi, singakuhlola ngokusebenzisa izimiso ezifaneleyo.

Sinikeziwe isipho esimangalisayo emibhalweni ka-Ellen G. White Yesiprofetho. Kusemqoka kakhulu ukuthi siyifunde kahle leyomibhalo, engaba—futhi—eyiso isibusiso kwizigidi. Kuyinhlekelele uma ingasetshenziswa ngokufaneleyo.
