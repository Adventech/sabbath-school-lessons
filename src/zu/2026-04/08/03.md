title:  Ingqikithi Yomlando-Sikompilo
date:   16/11/2026

`Funda uIsaya 65:17-20. Kuchaza ukuthini ukuthi ezulwini elisha nasemhlabeni Omusha ingane “iyokufa ineminyaka eyikhulu” na? (Isa. 65:20, NKJV).`

Ukuthatha uIsaya 65:20 umkhipha kwisimo sawo emlandweni, kungabalula kothile ukugabadela ukubakhona kokufa nezoni ezulwini elisha nasemhlabeni Omusha. Kodwa umqondo uphikisana nezithembiso kuIsaya zokuthi Abalungileyo abafileyo bayovuswa (Isa. 26:19) nokuthi ukufa nokudabuka akusayikubakhona ekugcineni (Isa. 25:8, Isa. 65:19).

UIsaya 65:20, nembala, wulimi lomfanekiso. Iyiphi ingane eneminyaka engu100 ubudala na? Kunalokho, lapho sifunda uIsaya 65:17-20 ngokwesimo somlando okuso, singabona ukuthi Lendimana yayikhomba esibusisweni esasizokwamukelwa yisizwe sakwaIsrayeli, kodwa kuphela uma beqhubeke nokuthembeka ezimisweni zesivumelwano (qhathanisa no Duteronomi 28). Njengalokhu uJesu enza KuMathewu 24, exhuma izigameko ezizokwenzeka nalezo zakamuva, uIsaya wenza okufanayo lapha, ebonisa ukuthi lokho okuzokwenzeka manje kuyoba yisandulelo samazulu amasha kanye nomhlaba Omusha.

KuIsaya indalo entsha ayikho kwikusasa ngokuphelele. Kungenwa kuyo ngokuphazima ngomsebenzi kaNkulunkulu wesihle wokuhlenga kanye nokuthethelela. Indimana imemezela ukuthi namanje, kuIsrayeli wamanje, uNkulunkulu useyakunqoba ukufa. Lomqondo wethulwa ngesithembiso sokuphila isikhathi eside kanye nokususwa kokufa kwezingane (Isa. 65:20). Kodwa lokhu kwakukhomba kokuzayo ekugcineni.

`Funda u-1 Korinte 16:20. Siyisebenzisa kanjani Lendimana esikhathini sethu nakwisikompilo manje na?`

Kwisikompilo lesiGriki ezikhathini zeTestamente Elisha, isibingelelo phakathi kwezihlobo nabangani abasondelene sasihambisana nokwanga. UPawule unikeza umyalelo ngalokho kubingelela phakathi kwamakholwa angamaKristu kuRoma 16:16, 1 Korinte 16:20, 2 Korinte 13:12 naku-1 Thesalonika 5:26. Kulengqikithi, singatomula isimiso sebhayibheli sokubingelela amakholwa ngemfudumalo nokuwenza azizwe eyingxenye yomndeni kaNkulunkulu. Kwamanye amasikompilo, ukwanga akufanelekile, futhi ukuxhawula, ukusingatha, noma ukukhothama kungamukeleka. Noma ngabe isikompilo lamukela kuphi, nokho, uPawule uyasikhuthaza ukuba senze abantu bazizwe bamukelekile futhi bethandwa emihlanganweni yebandla.
