---
title:  I-Pentecost
date:   14/12/2026
---

`Funda iZenzo 2:1-21. Kungani uPetro aphinda amazwi kaJoweli ngokwengqikithi yokuza kokuqala kukaKristu na? Iyiphi indima edlalwa yisiprofetho kulesisahluko na?`

Kulezizindimana zeTestamente Elisha, sithola esinye sezicaphuno ezinde kunazozonke zeTestamente Elidala. Lapho sifunda ukuhlaziya kukaPetro, singabuza, “Petro, Uthini ngezimpawu na? Asiziboni esimweni sakho.” UPetro angase aphendule, Kwabakhona ukugcwaliseka kwezimpawu. Ngenkathi uJesu elenga esiphambanweni ezinsukwini ezimbalwa ezidlulile, ilanga labamnyana amahora amathathu (Marko 15:33).

Okuyiyona nkulumo eyayivumisa kuPetro, mhlawumbe, ayigqugquzeli kakhulu namuhla. Sisalindele ukugcwaliseka okuphelele kwesiprofetho SikaJoweli. Ukhiye wesisombululo ulele embhalweni kaJoweli ngokwawo. KuJoweli 2:23, uJoweli ubalula imvula yokuqala neyakamuva. KwiZenzo 2 yisigcwaliseko sokuqala salesisiprofetho—imvula yokuqala. Ukugcwaliseka kokugcina kuyoba ngukuthelwa kukaMoya ngaphmbili nje kwesikhathi sokugcina ngemvula yakamuva.

Abantu bavama ukuchaza isiprofetho njenge-“sibikezela ngekusasa.” Lokhu akulona iphutha, nembala, kepha abaprofethi bebhayibheli babebizela abantu ekuphendukeni. Nembala, njengoba uPetro ayekhuluma, abanye esixukwini bavuma.

“Sebekuzwa bahlabeka enhliziyweni, bathi kuPetru nakwabanye abaphostoli: “Madoda, bazalwane, siyakwenze njani na?’ ” (Zenzo 2:37, NKJV). Futhi yathini impendulo kaPetro na? “ ‘Phendukani, yilowo nalowo abhapathizwe egameni likaJesu Kristu kukho ukuthethelelwa kwezono zenu; khona niyakwamukeliswa isiphiwo sikaMoya oNgcwele. Ngokuba isithembiso ngesenu nesabantwana benu, esabo bonke abakude, bonke iNkosi uNkulunkulu wethu eyakubabiza’ ” (Zenzo 2:38, 39, NKJV).

UJohane Umbhabhathizi ungesinye isibonelo esihle. Nakuba ememezela ngokuza kokuqala kukaJesu, wayebuye futhi ashumayele ngokuphenduka ezonweni zabo. “ ‘Phendukani, ngokuba umbuso wezulu ususondele!’ ” (Mat. 3:2, NKJV).

Ngalokho, uJoweli 2 uyothola ukugcwaliseka kokugcina kwikusasa. Njengoba uJoweli noPetro benza, abaprofethi besikhathi sokugcina bazobizela ekuphendukeni, bakhombe kuJesu, futhi bahlinzeke ngokuhlola ibandla likaNkulunkulu lesikhathi sokugcina.

`Ekuboneni izibonakaliso okungezona zasemhlabeni, isixuku ekuqaleni sacabanga ukuthi yisigameko semvelo. Kulokhu sacabanga ukuthi Lamadoda ayedakiwe! (Amadoda adakiwe ngokuphazima ekhuluma izilimi angazazi na?) Nakuba kungenzi mqondo ukuphendula kwabo, yini  eyenza abantu, nakuba bebona Okungekona Kwasemhlabeni, bazama ukuthola izincazelo zemvelo na?`
