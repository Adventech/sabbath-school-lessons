---
title:  Konke NgoMsebenzi
date:   18/12/2026
---

KwabasEfesu 4, uPawule uveza ukuthi inhloso enkulu yezipho zikamoya ngeyokusiza amakholwa aqhube umsebenzi kaNkulunkulu. Umsebenzi ka-Ellen G. White wokuprofetha wawunenhloso efanayo. Imibiko yakhe Ephefumulelwe isize amakholwa amaningi ekubeni nobudlelwane obujulile noNkulunkulu futhi wabafunzela ekwabelaneni nabanye ngalolothando.

“Kubo bonke abazibiza ngamaKristu,” ebhala, “ama-Seventh-day Adventist kumele abephambili ekuphakamiseni uKristu phambi kwezwe. Ukumenyezelwa kombiko wengelosi yesithathu kubizela ekwethulweni kweqiniso leSabatha. Leliqiniso, namanye afakiwe, kumele limenyezelwe; kodwa okuyiwona mongo wokuheha, uKristu Jesu, akumele asale ngaphandle. Kusesiphambanweni sikaKristu lapho umhawu neqiniso kuhlangana khona, futhi ukulunga nokuthula kwangane. Isoni kumele siholelwe ekubukeni eKhalivari, ngokholo olulula lwengane encane kumele athembe ukufaneleka koMsindisi, amukele ukulunga Kwakhe, ekholelwa emuseni Wakhe.”—Gospel Workers, pp. 156, 157.

Ngale kwalokhu, Ikholwa ngalinye, ephikelela, libizelwe ukuba yingxenye yomsebenzi kaNkulunkulu. “UJehovah ufuna ukuthi umzamo omkhulu womuntu ufakwe ngamalungu emabandleni ethu .”—Evangelism, p. 113.

“Emsebenzini kaJehovah akumele kubekhona abangenzi lutho. Akuthi abangafani babemunye ekusebenzeni njengabadobi babantu. Mabafune ukubutha Imiphefumulo ekungcoleni kwezwe babalethe ebumsulweni bothando lukaKristu.”—Evangelism, p. 115. U-Ellen G. White uqobo wayengumvangeli e-Europe ngama-1880, nase-Australia ngama-1890.

Kungenxa yobizo lukaNkulunkulu lobuprofethi ekuzinikeleni komuntu okwenza uSathane “athukuthelele” (Samb. 12:17, NKJV) abantu bakaNkulunkulu esikhathini sokugcina. Laba ngabalandeli bakaJesu abanombiko wesikhathi sokugcina, benobuholi besiprofetho, kanye nokubona okungabonwayo: “UJehovah uzambulile izingozi ezisikakile neziphambi kwethu. Ngesithunywa soMoya wesiprofetho, uyembulile inkohliso ezokwengamela umhlaba, futhi wakhuluma kubantu Bakhe, ethi, ‘Nansi indlela; hambani ngayo.’ [Isaya 30:21.] . . . . Impikiswano Enkulu yengula Izinkohliso zikaSathane.”—Ellen G. White, manuscript 31, 1890.

Usathane unobudlangudlangu ngenkathi abantu benikela izimpilo zabo kuJesu, futhi wenza konke angakwenza ukunciphaza izwi likaNkulunkulu Lesiprofetho. Ezinye zezindlela zakhe zibheke ekunciphazeni izipho sika-Ellen G. White zesiprofetho njenge-“zimbangcazayo.” Nembala, wabhala ngesikhathi esehluke kakhulu kunesethu. Omunye umzamo ngowokufaka ukungabaza emibangweni yakhe yokuprofetha nokumkhomba njengomprofethi wamanga. Kodwa okuyiyona ndlela esebenza kunazo zonke zokwenza u-Ellen G. White angafaneleki esikhathini sethu ngeyokwehlukanisa isizukulwane sanamuhla ekuzifundeleni ngokwaso imibhalo yakhe.
