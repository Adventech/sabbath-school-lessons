`Funda abasEfesu 4:11-14. Kungani uNkulunkulu enikeza lezizipho ebandleni Lakhe na? Isipho sesiprofetho sisiza kanjani ekufezeni amacebo kaNkulunkulu ngebandla na?`

Inhloso yazo zonke izipho zikamoya ngeyokondla kanye nokwakha ibandla, kanye nokumemezela ivangeli. UPawule wafaka isipho sokuprofetha (okugudlwa yigama “abaprofethi”) kwizinhla zone. Ezinye izipho ziqhamuka kube kanye. Ngokubuka ukuphinda-phinda kwalokhu kufakwa, singaphetha ngokuthi ukuprofetha kungesinye sezipho ezibaluleke kunazo zonke zikamoya.

Ake sibuke ezinye izimpawu ezivamile ngabaprofethi:

1. Abaprofethi babizwa nguNkulunkulu (buka u-1 Amakhosi 19:16, 19-21 no Isaya 6). Imvamisa, bazizwa bengafanelekile noma benqikaza Ukuthatha umsebenzi wabo, kodwa bayalalela.
2. UNkulunkulu ukhuluma kubaprofethi ngemibono, ngamaphupho, noma ngokukhuluma okuzwakalayo. Ngezinye izikhathi uNkulunkulu wembula izigameko zekusasa. Kwezinye izikhathi abazise ngezinto ezenzeka ngalesosikhathi abakuso ngezizwe noma ngabantu (Daniyele 2).
3. Izibhalo zichaza izehlo zenyama abaprofethi bangaphelelwa ngamandla noma bangaphefumuli, kodwa futhi bangagcwaliswa ngamandla amasha (Dan. 10:8–10, 18, 19).
4. Abaprofethi badlulisa imibiko yabo ngezindlela ezahlukene—ngomlomo (Zenzo 21:10-12), ngokwenza (Ezekiel 4), noma ngezincwadi (Sambulo 2, 3). Abanye abaprofethi besebenzisa Ababhali, njengalokhu kwakunjalo ngo-Baruch, owalekelela uJeremiya (Jer. 36:1-4).
5. Umbiko womprofethi kumele uhambisane nowabaprofethi abandulelayo, ngoba uNkulunkulu akaziphikisi ngoKwakhe (Isa. 8:20).
6. Okuqukhethwe ngumbiko womprofethi, kanye nomthelela empilweni yomprofethi, kuyofakaza ngokuthi usuka kuNkulunkulu (Mat. 7:15-20).
7. Usathane ngasosonke isikhathi uzama ukulingisa okwasezulwini. Lokhu kuyiqiniso ngesipho sokuprofetha. Esikhathini sokugcina, kuyobakhona abaprofethi bamanga (Mat. 24:24, 2 Pet. 2:1).
