---
title:  Insali Yesikhathi Sokugcina
date:   16/12/2026
---

Isambulo 12 sigqamisa ububanzi bomlando wempikiswano enkulu, ukusukela ekuqaleni kwayo ezulwini kuze kufike ezinsukwini zokugcina.

`Funda iSambulo 12:17. Yini “ubufakazi bukaJesu” na? Qhathanisa iSambulo 19:10 neSambulo 22:8, 9.`

KwiSambulo 12:17, izimilo ezimbili ezikhomba ibandla lesikhathi sokugcina ziyabalwa. Ibandla libambelele futhi ligcina Imithetho Elishumi (efaka nomthetho weSabatha) futhi lino-“bufakazi bukaJesu.”Yini lobubufakazi na?

Ezinye izihumsho zeBhayibheli zihumsha “ubufakazi bukaJesu” ngokuchaza ukuthi thina makholwa sivuma uJesu futhi simazise ngomsebenzi wethu kanye novangelo (buka iSamb. 19:10, NIV: “bambelelani kubufakazi bukaJesu,” uJesu ebe engumgomo wokuvangela komKristu). Ezinye Izihumusho zeBhayibheli zinikeza incazelo ehlukile, ethi uJesu uQobo unikeza ubufakazi ebandleni Lakhe (Samb. 19:10, GNT: “iqiniso elambulwa nguJesu”—uJesu ungumenzi othwele ubufakazi). Empeleni, umbhalo wokuqala wesiGriki ungashiwo nangayiphi kulezizihumusho. Ngakhoke, wayechaza ukuthini uJohane na?

Uma siqhathanisa izimpawu ezimbili ezikhombayo, sibona ukufana okukhulu endleleni ezishiwo ngazo (kubonakala kakhulu esiGrikini sokuqala ngaphezu kwamaBhayibheli ethu). Njengoba “imithetho kaNkulunkulu” nembala iqhamuka kuNkulunkulu, kanjalo “ubufakazi bukaJesu” busuka kuJesu. Ngalokho, nguJesu othwala ubufakazi. Yena ukhomba ibandla leqiniso. Ukwenza kanjani lokhu na? Yena ukwenza ngesipho sesiprofetho.

Izimpendulo zitholakala kwiSambulo 19:10 nakwiSambulo 22:9. Lapha kusobala ukuthi “ubufakazi bukaJesu” buvezwa ngo-“moya wesiprofetho.” UJohane uqobo ungumprofethi futhi umele kuye “ubufakazi bukaJesu.” Umprofethi ngumuntu onobizo olukhethekileyo njengesithunywa sikaNkulunkulu kubantu Bakhe, noma ezoyala futhi abizele ekuphendukeni, ezokhuthaza, noma ukuzokwembula ikusasa.

Isambulo 12:17 sibonisa ukuthi uJesu wenza ibandla Lakhe libonakale ngesipho sesiprofetho. Lokhu kuchaza ukuthi nanjengoba uNkulunkulu athumela abaprofethi kubantu beTestamente Elidala bakwaIsrayeli noJuda, noma umpostoli uJohane ebandleni lokuqala lamaKristu, abaprofethi bazobakhona ezinsukwini zokugcina zomlando womhlaba.

`Yibuphi ubufakazi obubonile obuveza ukuthi umsebenzi ka-Ellen G. White ungakhonjwa ngokufanele njengesibonakaliso sokusebenza “koMoya wesiprofetho” na? (Samb. 19:10).`
