---
title:  Isithembiso SikaJoweli
date:   13/12/2026
---

`Funda uJoweli 2:28-32. Yini ethenjiswa nguNkulunkulu ukuyenza ngezinsuku zokugcina na?`

UJoweli, umprofethi wakwi-seventh-century B.C., usinikeza ukubona okungabonwa kwizigameko ezizayo. Lapho sifunda lesisahluko, sizibuza ukuthi ukhuluma ngesikhathi sakhe sasendulo noma ngezinto ezizokwenzeka kwikusasa elikude.

Abezesayensi yeBhayibheli banomkhuba wokwehlukanisa phakathi kwezinhlobo ezimbili zeziprofetho—eziqhamile nezokuphela kwezwe. Isiprofetho esiqhamile sikhuluma ngezizwe eziphathekayo futhi sisebenzise amanani angampela namangama (buka, ngokwesibonelo, Jer. 25:1-11). Isiprofetho sokuphela komhlaba, sisebenzisa ulimi lwezimpawu, amanani ezimpawu, futhi imvamisa izimpawu zezulu (buka uDaniyeli 7). Ngezinye izikhathi sithola ukuhlangana kwalezizinhlobo ezimbili zesiprofetho (buka, ngokwesibonelo, Mathewu 24).

Kuthini mayelana noJoweli 2:28-31 na? Kubukeka sengathi kumele singalindeli ukugcwaliseka ngesikhathi sokuphila komprofethi, noma eminyakeni elandela ukusebenza kwakhe. Kunalokho, kumele sibuke ukugcwaliseka kwalesisiprofetho kwikusasa. Qaphela Ingqikithi kanye nesikhathi uNkulunkulu ayonikeza ngaso lesisipho sokuprofetha—“ ‘ngaphambi kosuku olukhulu nolwesabekayo lukaJehovah’ ” (Joweli 2:31, NKJV). Yiluphi lolosuku na? Ukuza kokuqala kukaJesu na? Noma okwesibili na? Noma kokubili na?

Lapho sicabanga ngokubalulwa kwezimpawu elangeni nasenyangeni kwesiprofetho SikaJoweli, sishaywa ngokushesha yiqiniso lokuthi sithola lomqondo ezahlukweni eziningana zebhayibheli. UJesu watshela abafundi Bakhe ngezimpawu elangeni, enyangeni, nasezinkanyezini enkulumeni Yakhe edumile yesikhathi sokugcina (Mat. 24:29). Ngokufanayo, uJohane umambuli usho ngezibonakaliso ezifanayo (Samb. 6:12, 13). Bobabili uJesu noJohane bakhuluma ngezimpawu zezulu ngaphambi kokuza kwesibili kweNkosi yethu.

Ngalokho, singangathekisa ukuthi isiprofetho SikaJoweli, esikhomba ezimpawini ezifanayo, kumele sibe nomthelela ezikhathini zokugcina: “ ‘Ngiyakuveza izimangaliso ezulwini nasemhlabeni, igazi, nomlilo, nezinsika zomusi. Ilanga liyakuphenduka ubumnyama, inyanga ibe yigazi, lungakafiki usuku olukhulu nolwesabekayo lukaJehova’ ” (Joweli 2:30, 31, NKJV).

`Cabanga ngazo zonke iziprofetho esezifezekile. Kungani lokhu kumele kusisize ekuthembeni uNkulunkulu ngalezo eziseza—njengaleso sokuza kwesibili kukaJesu na?`
