---
title:  Izibonakaliso Zanamuhla
date:   17/12/2026
---

Abantu abaningi benkolo namuhla bangavuma ngokusemthethweni abaprofethi basendulo bebhayibheli, kodwa ngabe bayakholwa lokho kungenzeka esikhathini sethu na? Uma isithembiso sikaJoweli 2:28-31 sesibonakaliso sesikhathi sokugcina sesipho sesiprofetho kumele sikholakale, singabukeka kanjani na?

`Funda iZenzo 2:36-42. Yimuphi umyalezo ongumongo wentshumayelo kaPetro kwi-Pentecost na? Baphendula kanjani abalaleli bakaPetro na?`

Ukuthelwa kukaMoya oNgcwele nge-Pentecost ngo A.D. 31 ngumfanekiso omuhle kakhulu walokho okuzokwenzeka ngesikhathi sokuthelwa kukaMoya oNgcwele esikhathini sokugcina, nakuba kuzoba banzi kakhulu. I-Pentecost yayinokuphakamisa uJesu, ukuphenduka okusabalele, kanye nokudalwa kwabantu abalungele futhi behlomele umsebenzi kaNkulunkulu. Umbiko kaPetro ogxile kuKristu wawunikezwe amandla nguMoya oNgcwele, futhi lokho kwaholela Imiphefumulo engu-3,000 ukuba iphenduke futhi ibhabhathizwe. “Lemvula yokuqala” eyisibusiso sikaMoya oNgcwele satshala futhi Imbewu eyigugu, iBandla—umzimba omunye wamakholwa ayisibonelo futhi abelana ngothando lukaJesu Kristu ngenkathi limemezela ngokubuya Kwakhe.

Njengalokhu imvula yokuqala yanikezwa “ukudala ukhuluma kwimbewu eyigugu, kunjalo ‘nemvula yakamuva’ izonikezwa ekupheleni ukuvuthiswa isivuno.”— Ellen G. White, The Great Controversy, p. 611. Ngamanye amazwi, izibonakaliso Zanamuhla zesipho sokuprofetha—imvula yakamuva—zizofana nezibonakaliso zangaphambilini zemvula yokuqala nge-Pentecost.

Njengoba sesibonile kuJoweli 2:28-31, isipho sesiprofetho ekugcineni kwesikhathi siyonikezwa kwabasha nabadala, abesilisa nabesifazane. UNkulunkulu uzokhuluma ngalabo abakhethwe nguMoya oNgcwele. Noma yinini uMoya oNgcwele ukhetha isithunywa sesikhathi sokugcina, umbiko waso uyophakamisa uJesu, ubizele abantu ekuphendukeni, futhi ulungiselele abantu umsebenzi kaNkulunkulu. Lezizimpawu ezintathu zibonaka emsebenzini ka-Ellen G. White wokuprofetha. Empilweni yakhe yonke, waphakamisa uJesu neZibhalo ekhuluma noma ebhala; wabizela ama-Seventh-day Adventist nabanye ekuphendukeni; futhi wancenga bonke ukuba bangene emsebenzi kaNkulunkulu wabalahlekileyo.

`Uyithathe kangakanani inzuzo yalesisipho esimangalisayo esisinikiwe ngokusebenza kuka-Ellen G. White na?`
