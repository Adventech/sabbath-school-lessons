### Funda Lokhu Kuleliviki
Joweli 2:28-32, Zenzo 2:1-21, Efes. 4:11-14, Samb. 12:17, Samb. 19:10, Zenzo 2:36-42.

> <p>Indimana Yenkumbulo</p>
> “ ‘Kuyakuthi ngasemuva ngithulule uMoya wami phezu kwayo yonke inyama; amadodana enu namadodakazi enu ayakuprofetha, abadala benu baphuphe amaphupho, izinsizwa zenu zibone imibono; naphezu kwezinceku nezincekukazi ngalezo zinsuku ngiyakuthulula uMoya wami’ ” (Joel 2:28, 29 NKJV).

Emthethweni othile kahulumeni kukhona “umhlinzeko wokuqeda.” Lelitemu lezomthetho alinalutho nokwenza nokushona kwelanga. Kunalokho, ngumhusho onikeza ukuphela Komthetho ngosuku oluthile. Ohulumeni bafaka imisho ewuloluhlobo emthethweni ngezizathu ezahlukene. Ngokwesibonelo, bangenza imithetho ethile enciphisa amalungu ezakhamuzi ngezikhathi zengozi esizweni. Ohulumeni ngezinye izikhathi bafaka lemisho yokuphelisa lemithetho ngosuku oluthile ukuze kubuye kwizakhamuzi amalungelo abekade esuswe isikhashana.

Ngabe uNkulunkulu wafaka Imihlinzeko yokuphelisa ngenkathi uMoya oNgcwele unikeza isipho seziprofetho ezikhathini zasendulo na? Ngabe sikhona isikhathi ngaphambi kokubuya kukaJesu lapho lesisipho sizoyeka ukusebenza na? Ngeviki elidlule sifundile ukuthi uNkulunkulu wakhuluma ngabaprofethi kubantu Bakhe ngezikhathi ezibucayi. Kuleliviki, sizohlola isipho sokuprofetha esikhathini sokugcina—umsebenzi waso okhethekile nokuqhubeka kwaso—kanye nendima yomsebenzi ka-Ellen G. White wokuprofetha lapho kusondela ukuza kwesibili kukaJesu.

_Funda isifundo saleliviki ukulungisela iSabatha lika uZibandlela 19._
