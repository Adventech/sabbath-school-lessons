---
title:  Ukuthukuthelela Isithunywa SikaNkulunkulu
date:   08/11/2026
---

UMose, umholi okhethwe nguNkulunkulu, wayeyazi Injabulo nobuhlungu bokuhola isixuku esixubene ehlane becinga Izwe leSithembiso.

`Funda uNumeri 14:4-11. Yini eyenzeka kulezizindimana na? Kungani abantu bethukuthelele uMose noAaron na?`

Ukufundisisa lezizindimana kwembula ukuthi abantwana bakwaIsrayeli babethukuthelele uNkulunkulu ngoba babengamethembi. UMose no Aaron, abantu abamele ubuKhosi obuphakeme bukaNkulunkulu, kwakulula ukuba bathukuthelelwe yisizwe. Isidingo sokuphepha enyameni nasemqondweni yisikhuthazo esingumongo esikumuntu wonke. Uma okunye kwako kusengozini, noma sibona okuthile okuyingozi enyameni noma emqondweni, singaba nolaka futhi silahlekelwe ngukucabanga.

`Funda uNumeri 13:26-30. Kungani abakwaIsrayeli besaba kangaka na? Yiliphi izwi lesikhuthazo u-Caleb abelana nabo ngalo na?`

AbakwaIsrayeli babenokwesaba okufanele mayelana nezinselelo ezaziphambi kwabo ekuthatheni Izwe Lesithembiso, “kodwa lapho abantu bevumele izinhliziyo zabo zingakholwa bazibeka ngaphansi kokulawulwa nguSathane, futhi akekho ongasho umgamo angabaholela kuwo.”—Ellen G. White, Patriarchs and Prophets, p. 389. UMose wabhekana nenselelo yobuholi ecishe inganqobeki kulomzuzu. Ukwesaba kwabantu kwaphenduka ngokushesha kwaba ngukuthukuthelela u-Aaron noMose kanye nokuvukela uNkulunkulu okusobala. Ngenkathi abantu sebefuna abaholi abasha endaweni yomprofethi/umholi okhethwe nguNkulunkulu (Dut. 34:10-12, Num. 14:4), uJoshuwa no-Caleb badabula izingubo zabo futhi bancenga abantu ukuba bathembe uNkulunkulu nakuba besaba: “ ‘Uma uJehova esithanda, uyakusingenisa kulelo zwe, asinike lona, izwe elivame ubisi nezinyosi.” Kodwa ningahlubuki kuJehova, futhi ningesabi abantu balelo zwe’ ” (Num. 14:8, 9, NKJV).

`Kulula ukuthemba uJehovah ngenkathi izinto zihamba kahle, akunjalo na? Kwenzekani uma kwehla ingozi na? Kwenzekani uma ukuthemba uNkulunkulu kusho ukungabi nokunethezeka noma ukungahlalisiseki na? Wenza kahle kanganani ngalesosikhathi na?`
