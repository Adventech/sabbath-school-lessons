---
title:  Ubuzenzisi
date:   11/11/2026
---

Usuke wabuka izinto ezenziwe ngabanye ngaphambilini wase uzicabangela, ukube bekuyimina, bengingeke ngenze lokho okwenziwa yilabo bantu! Kepha, uma singazikhohlisi, singekwenze ngempela lokho abakwenzile, kodwa sivama ukuziphatha ngezindlela ezifanayo sibe, mhlawumbe, sizenza abangakuboni okuyikona ngempela esikwenzayo. Lesi yisimiso uJesu Mathupha akhuluma ngaso ngenkathi ethi: “ ‘Mzenzisi! Qala ngokususa ugongolo esweni lakho, khona uzokwazi ukubonisisa ucezwana esweni lomfowenu’ ” (Mat. 7:5, NKJV).

`Funda uMathewu 23:30-35.Yiliphi iphuzu elibalulekile kepha elibuhlungu uJesu alenza lapha na? Yisiphi isixwayiso okungadingeka sisithathe kumazwi Akhe na?`

Kunomuzwa kulezizindimana wokuthi uJesu wayeyizonda indlela yokuziphatha yabaFarisi ngoba ukuzenzisa kwabo kwakuba kubi kakhulu ngenxa yokuzimisela kwabo ukungafuni Ukuguquka. UJesu wayesecikekile yinkolo yabaFarisi. Wayesekhathele yilaba ababezibona bengcono kwezikamoya nemijiva ababefana namathuna acakiweyo kanti agcwele amathambo abafileyo (Mat. 23:27, 28). Babengavumi futhi bengashiyi izono, nakuba izono bezibonisiwe, nakuba uJesu ngokuphindaphinda ebanikeza Ithuba lokuphenduka futhi basindiswe.

Ekubhekaneni Kwakhe nalamadoda, akumangalisi ukuthi uJesu awabize “ngabaZenzisi”! inkulumo yayivama ukuthi: “ ‘Maye kinina, babhali nani baFarisi, baezenzisi!’ ” (buka uMat. 23:14, 15, 23, 25, 27).

Izimpilo zabo, nembala, zaziphawulwa ngukusho okunye bebebenza okunye. Kwakunjalo Ukubhekana kwabo nabaprofethi. Babecabanga, ngokwesibonelo, ukuthi ngokuhlobisa amathuna ababproefthi, babehlonipha inkolo; kodwa kokubalulekile, njengokuphenduka emazwini abaprofethi, babenqafuni. Kunalokho, babefuna ukuzimatanisa namandla kanye nomunyu wabaprofethi, kodwa hhayi ingqikithi yemibiko yabo. Ekuhlobiseni amathuna ezinceku zikaNkulunkulu, babecabanga ukuthi babeyobe bengachithanga igazi lezithunywa zikaNkulunkulu. Kepha, ngaso lesosikhathi babehlela ukubulala othile owayengaphezu komprofethi—uMesiya uqobo Lwakhe.

`Singaqinisekisa kanjani ukuthi ngokwesimiso, asenzi okufanayo: sibonakala ngaphandle sikholwa kodwa ngaphakathi sigcwele ukuhlubuka, umhobholo, nenzondo na?`
