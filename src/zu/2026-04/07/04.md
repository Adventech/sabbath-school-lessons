---
title:  "Ukuchitha Umbiko: Isigaba 2"
date:   10/11/2026
---

Ukuphendula kwabantu kwisipho sesiprofetho kungathatha izimo eziningi. Odabeni lukaMose, abantwana bakwaIsrayeli bawela ekuvukeleni uMose no-Aaron, beqhutshwa ngukwesaba izitha ababezobhekana nazo lapho bezama Ukungena Ezweni Lesithembiso. Eminyakeni elandelayo, Inkosi u-Johoiakim washisa umbiko kaNkulunkulu ngethemba lokuchitha izwi likaNkulunkulu Lesiprofetho.

Lokhu, nokho, kwakungezona izinto ezingazodwa. Umlando kaIsrayeli noJuda basendulo, ngaphandle kokungavamile, wawungowokuchitha abaprofethi.

`Fundau-2 IziKronike 36:15, 16. Isizwe sakwaJuda Saphendula kanjani kwimibiko eyayithunyelwe kubo nguNkulunkulu ngabaprofethi Bakhe na? UNkulunkulu wagcina ngokwenzani ngokuziphatha kukaJuda na?`

Umsebenzi kaJeremiya wobuprofethi wengamela ukubusa kukaJosiya, Jehoahaz, Jehoiakim, Jehoiachin, no-Zedekiah. Umbuso waseBhabhiloni, uholwa ngu-Nebuchadnezzar, wawusemnyango kaIsrayeli. Iziprofetho zikaJeremiya zasezizogcwaliseka ngenkathi oneminyaka engu-21 ubudala ogama lingu-Zedekiah wakhuphukela esihlalweni sobukhosi (2 Kron. 36:11). Ibhayibheli lithi, “Wenza okubi emehlweni kaJehova uNkulunkulu wakhe; akazithobanga phambi kukaJeremiya umprofethi womlomo kaJehova” (2 Kron. 36:12, NKJV).

Qaphela indlela okuchazwa ngayo isipho sikaJeremiya sokuprofetha. Ubonakala enjengokhuluma Ngomlomo kaNkulunkulu. Nakuba kunjalo, u-Zedekiah akazithobanga phambi komprofethi. Kepha uJeremiya, owayengumuntu nje, isoni njengathi sonke, wayemele uJehovah, futhi ekhulumela Yena.

UJeremya wayephakathi kwabaningi kulendima. Kumakhulu eminyaka, uNkulunkulu wayelokhu ethumela abaprofethi kubantu Bakhe (Eksod. 3:15, Hub. 105:26, Mal. 3:1, Isa. 6:8, Jer. 25:4, Mat. 11:13, Luka 11:49, njalo, njalo.), engafuni “kube khona ababhubhayo kepha bonke baphenduke” (2 Pet. 3:9, NKJV). Lomlando kaIsrayeli wasendulo kanye noJuda yindaba yemizamo engapheli kaNkulunkulu yokufinyelela kubantu Bakhe abahlubukileyo ngezithembiso zosindiso, uma nje bengaphenduka.

Kodwa kulelihora lokugcina lomlando kaJuda, umusa owelulwe nguNkulunkulu kuJuda ngokuhlola kanye nokududuza ngezwi likaJeremiya Lesiprofetho wahlangatshezwa, hhayi ngokuzithoba, ngokukholwa, nangokuphenduka, kepha ngokuhleka, ukujivaza, nangesiyeye.

`Bagcona izithunywa zikaNkulunkulu, badelela amazwi kaNkulunkulu, baklolodela abaprofethi Bakhe. Ngeqiniso, thina namuhla besingeke sakwenza lokho—noma kunjalo na?`
