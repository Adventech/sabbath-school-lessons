---
title:  Ukwamukela Nokudlulisa Imibiko Yakhe
date:   13/11/2026
---

U-Arthur White, umzukulu ka-Ellen G. White nowayengumqondisi we-Ellen G. White Estate, walinganisela ukuthi ngesikhathi esaphila ugogo wamukela amakhulu emibono kanye namaphupho esiprofetho. Lemibono yayithatha imizuzu kuye kumahora amaningana kuthi ngezinye izikhathi kube nabantu abaningi abafakazayo. Kwezinye izikhathi nabangamkholwa babebanethuba lokumhlola ngezikhathi zokwenzeka kwalokhu emphakathini.

Njengabaprofethi basebhayibhelini, u-Ellen G. White wayebanamava angewona asemhlabeni ngenkathi esembonweni. Ngo-1868, u-James White wanikeza iminingwane ngesimo sika-Ellen White ngenkathi esembonweni. Wayeba “ngongaqwashile ngalokho okwakwenzeka eduze kwakhe”; wayengaphefumuli; imisipha yakhe “yayiqina” futhi “ukuhlangana kwamalungu kume”; ukunyakaza kwakhe “kukhululeke futhi kube nokuphiqilika”; kuthi amehlo abe ngavulekile. Emuva kombono wayeba ngongaboni isikhashana, kepha kungekho okumthikamezile ekuboneni. Akekho owayekwazi ukulawula isikhathi kanye nobude bokuba sembonweni kwakhe, njengalokhu imibono yayingalindelekanga naku-Ellen White uqobo. (Buka ku-James White, Life Incidents, pp. 271-274.)

Njengasezikhathini zeBhayibheli, ukusabela kwabantu emibikweni kwakwehlukana. Abanye babamukela izeluleko zikaNkulunkulu; abanye bazichithe. Njengoba u-Ellen G. White aphawula: “UNkulunkulu ungibeke njengomkhuzi wabantu Bakhe; futhi . . . njengoba ebeke phezu kwami umthwalo onzima, Yena uzokwenza labo abanikezwa umbiko babenomthwalo ngendlela abawuphatha ngayo. . . . Mina angizange ngizikhethele lomsebenzi ongathandeki. Akuwona umsebenzi ozongenza ngithandeke noma ngituswe ngabantu. . . . egameni nasemandleni oMsindisi wami ngizokwenza engingakwenza. Ngizoxwayisa futhi ngiluleke futhi ngikhuze futhi ngikhuthaze njengalokhu uMoya kaNkulunkulu ulawula, noma ngabe abantu bayezwa noma bayakhuzeka. Owami umsebenzi akuwona owokuthokozisa mina, kepha ngowokwenza intando kaBaba wami osezulwini, onginike umsebenzi wami.”—Testimonies for the Church, vol. 4, pp. 231, 232.

Ngokuthathekisayo, iningi lalabo elalichitha imibiko yakhe Yesiprofetho lalikwenza lokhu ngokuncike ekungakholweni kwabo noma ekungavumelanini kwabo no-Ellen G. White, kunoba kube ngokwemfundiso. U-Ellen G. White wayebamnene kakhulu ngakulabo ababebonisa ukungasikholwa isipho sakhe sokuprofetha. Empeleni, wakubeka obala ukuthi labobantu kumele baphathwe “ngokubekezela nangothando lobuzalwane . . . baze babe sesimweni sokuvuma noma bamelane naso.”— Testimonies for the Church, vol. 1, p. 328.

U-Ellen G. White wayeqinisekile ngokuthi ubufakazi bangempela besipho sakhe sokuprofetha kwakuyimibiko uNkulunkulu ayemnikeza yona. Uma abantu babefuna iqiniso ngokwethembeka, wayekholelwa ukuthi babesibona isipho sakhe njengesiyiqiniso. Wayethemba uNkulunkulu ukuba abahole ekuzameni kwabo ukuqonda kangcono, kepha wabhala futhi ukuthi uNkulunkulu usinikeze ubufakazi obanele “bokulinganisa ukholo lwethu ngasohlangothini lweqiniso.”—Testimonies for the Church, vol. 4, p. 231.
