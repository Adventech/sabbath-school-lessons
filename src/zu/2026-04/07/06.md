---
title:  Ukuyala Thina
date:   12/11/2026
---

Zonke lezizindaba ezikwiTestamente Elidala, nakuba zingcolile futhi zidabukisa, zinikwa ngesizathu esihle: inhloso enkulu, ukuba sifunde izifundo kuzo ngalokho okwenzeka uma sihlubuka kuNkulunkulu.

`Funda u-1 Korinte 10:6-12. Ngokukampostoli uPawule, kungani uNkulunkulu aphefumulela abaprofethi ukuba babhale lokho abakubhala Ebhayibhelini na?`

Kulezizindimana uPawule utomula ukuxhumana okuqondile phakathi kokudlulile kanye nokwamanje, exhumanisa uIsrayeli wasendulo kanye nebandla, thina esamukele uJesu njengeNkosi noMsindisi esesiyingxenye yalo (Gal. 6:14-16).

UPawule ukuveza obala ukuthi umlando odlulile wamava esintu noNkulunkulu— amahle noma amabi—akumele azitshwe noma enziwe ize. Empeleni, uNkulunkulu wakubona kusemqoka kakhulu kangangokuba wakuphawula njengezibonelo “kithina size singafisi okubi, njengalokho bona babekufisa” (1 Kor. 10:6, NKJV). Ngamanye amazwi, ingaphambilini liba yisingeniso sekusasa. Ingaphambilini livama ukubeka ingqikithi yalokho okwenzeka manje nokuzokwenzeka kusasa.

Ngokuqondile, uNkulunkulu uthi asigwetshelwe ekuphindeni amaphutha ezizukulwane ezidlulile, uma nje kuphela silalela iziyalo zeZibhalo kanye nemibiko Yesiprofetho ethunyelwe nguYena kithina ngabaprofethi Bakhe. Uma sizwa, sifunda, futhi silalela. Izimpilo zethu zizosekelwa, futhi sizophumelela (2 Kron. 20:20). Kwabanye, nakho ukuphumelela kwezikamoya kuchazwe ngokuba nezindlu ezinhle, izimoto, okokugqoka, kanye nokunya okuyizimpahla. Ngenkathi uNkulunkulu ethembisa ukunaka izidingo zethu, Yena ufuna futhi Imiphefumulo yethu iphumelele, njengalokhu siphumelela ngezinye izindlela (3 Johane 2). Kepha ukugcwaliseka kweqiniso kwezikamoya nokungapheli empilweni yobuKristu kudinga ukulalela imibiko kaNkulunkulu.

UNkulunkulu akamoshi amagama. Lapho ekhuluma, amazwi Akhe angunaphakade. Amandla nokusebenza kwawo akupheli (Isa. 40:8). Anikelwa Ukuyala thina “osekufikile kithi ukugcina kwezikhathi” (1 Kor. 10:11, NKJV). Uma lokhu kwakuyiqiniso ngaphambilini, kumele asebenze kangakani lamazwi kithina namuhla na?

`Ngenkathi ufuna izindaba Ebhayibhelini zokuhlubuka nokungakholwa, yiziphi izifundo ongazithatha kuzo ezingakusiza ugweme okufanayo na?`
