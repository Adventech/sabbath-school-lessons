`Funda uJeremiya 36:20-26. Kwenzekani Enkundleni, futhi abaholi baphendule kanjani kwesiprofetho sesixwayiso na?`

UJeremiya wayeyizwi likaNkulunkulu Lesiprofetho esizweni sakwaJuda ngesikhathi sokubhubha okwakusondele. Iminyaka eminingi uNkulunkulu ngoJeremiya wayexwayisa uJuda ngokungathokozi Kwakhe ngabo, nokuthi iBhabhiloni lalizobanqoba libathumbele ekudingisweni (Jer. 6:8-19, Jeremiya 26). UJehovah wayefuna ukubasindisa, kodwa kwakumele balalele.

Izono zikaJuda zaziningi, zisobala, futhi zingaguquki. Ukukhonza izithombe, imihlatshelo ngabantwana, ukuphinga, nokuphatha ngesihluku kwakungumkhuba ngesikhathi sikaJeremiya, kepha izixwayiso zomprofethi kanye nobizo akunakwanga. Lapho ukusebenza kobuprofethi bukaJeremiya sekuya ngasekupheleni, ukunxusa kukaNkulunkulu kwabantwana Bakhe kwaqina, kepha imizamo Yakhe yayilokhu ingeyokuhlenga. KuJeremiya 32, ngokwesibonelo, uNkulunkulu utshela uJeremiya ukuthi athenge isiqephu somhlaba futhi agcine itayitela, “ ‘Ngokuba uthi uJehovah sebawoti . . .: “Izindlu namasimu nemivini niyakuba nayo kulomhlaba futhi” ’ ” (Jer. 32:15, NKJV).

Kwakuluthando olwaqhuba uNkulunkulu ukuba abhale loluhlobo lokuncenga kokugcina lwenguquko Nokuphenduka. Impendulo ka-Jehoiakim kulombiko yaba ngeyokudelela uNkulunkulu, umbiko Wakhe, kanye nesithunywa Sakhe.

`Funda uJeremiya 36:27-31. UNkulunkulu wakuphendula kanjani ukudelela kuka-Jehoiakim na?`

Ngezinye izikhathi kuba nomkhuba wokukholwa ukuthi uma sichitha noma sibulala imibiko kaJehovah noma izithunywa Zakhe, umbiko nawo uzonyamalala. Ukungavumi kwethu ukunaka iZwi likaNkulunkulu Lesiprofetho akucimi umbiko uNkulunkulu anawo ngathi. Ukudelela kwethu kuqinisa nje ukulimala okuzesehlela thina nemindeni yethu ngenxa yokubanenkani yokungazi noma ukuhlubuka. Isiprofetho sikaJeremiya mayelana nesiphetho seNkosi u-Johoiakim siqukethe isixwayiso esesabekayo kumuntu wonke (Jer. 36:30, 31): siziba izixwayiso zikaNkulunkulu kithina ngokuyingozi kithina, ngokuba sizovuna imiphumela yesono sethu kanye nobubi.

`Lokhu kusitsheleni ngesimilo sikaNkulunkulu sothando ngokuthi aphikelele ekuzameni ukufinyelela kubantu Bakhe nakuba benenkani na?`
