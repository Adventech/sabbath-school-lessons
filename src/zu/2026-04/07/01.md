### Funda Lokhu Kuleliviki
Numeri 13:26-30; Jeremiya. 36:20-26; 2 Kron. 36:15, 16; Mathewu. 23:30-35; 1 Kor. 10:6-12.

> <p>Indimana Yenkumbulo</p>
> “Kepha lezo zinto zabehlela bona kube yisibonelo; kulotshelwe ukusiyala thina, osekufikile kithi ukugcina kwezikhathi.” (1 Korinte 10:11, NASB).

Abanye abantu namuhla bakhuthazwa ukuba bakhulume iqiniso labo”—amava abo, imiqondo yabo, izidingo zabo, imingcele yabo, izinkolelo zabo—noma lokho kuyini. Lonke “iqiniso” liyilona (kusho imicabango) futhi akumele lithuliswe. Yini eyenzekayo, nokho, uma amanye “alamaqiniso” akhona ephikisana neqiniso, uJesu Kristu neZwi Lakhe Elibhaliweyo na?

Okwenzekayo ngukuthi abanye abantu bazokwamukela amaqiniso Ebhayibheli futhi basuke emangeni abo; abanye, nokho, bazobambelela emangeni abo. Abanye baguqula ukwenza kwabo bevumelana neqiniso; abanye balwa nalo. Kube njalo emlandweni wesintu.

Abaprofethi bebhayibheli babevama Ukubizelwa ukwethula imibiko abantu ababengafuni ukuyizwa. Ngenxa yalokho, iningi laligxekwa, likhishwa inyumbazane, banye basatshiswe ngokufa noma nembala babulawe (Heb. 11:32-38). Ngaphandle kwakho konke lokhu, abaprofethi bakaNkulunkulu bamemezela ngokwethembeka imibiko yabo ngoba ukuholwa yisiprofetho sikaNkulunkulu kwakungeyona into yokuyikhetha noma uyinqabe kubona. Kwakungumyalezo osindisayo kubantu bakaNkulunkulu. Yingakho imibiko kwakumele yethulwe, noma ngabe kwakumbizani empilweni umprofethi.

_Funda isifundo saleliviki ukulungisela iSabatha lika uLwezi 14._
