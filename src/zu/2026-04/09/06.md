---
title:  Iziprofetho Ezigcwalisekile
date:   26/11/2026
---

Abantu bavama Ukucabanga ngomprofethi engumuntu obikezela ngekusasa, kodwa lokhu kuyisigaba esisodwa somsebenzi womprofethi. Umsebenzi womprofethi wengamela okuningi. Abaprofethi baholela abantu ekubuyeleni Ezwini likaNkulunkulu futhi baluleke, baxwayise, bakhuthaze.

`Ngaphandle kokusiza abantu bazi futhi balungele izigameko ezizayo, kungani abaprofethi bebikezela ngekusasa na? Buka uJer. 28:9, 15-17.`

Izibikezelo ezigcwalisekile zingenye yezindlela zokuvivinya umprofethi. Iziprofetho zomprofethi weqiniso kumele zenzeke njengalokhu ziprofethiwe: “ ‘Ngenkathi umprofethi ekhuluma egameni likaJehova, bese ingenzeki leyo nto, ingaveli iyiyo leyo nto, akusiye uJehova oyikhulumile; umprofethi uyikhulumile ngokuziqhenya; ungamesabi’ ” (Dut. 18:22, NKJV). Lesisivivinyo singaba yinselele ngoba ezinye iziprofetho zinemibandela; okungukuthi, zingenzeka njengalokhu ebikezele umprofethi uma imibandela ingagcwalisekanga.

Sibona lesisimiso ngoJona kanye nesixwayiso sakhe ngeNiniva. Intshumayelo kaJona ibukeka imfushane futhi iqondile: “ ‘Ezinsukwini ezingamashumi amane, iNiniva iyakubhujiswa!’ ” (Jon. 3:4, NKJV). Kepha akwenzekanga lokho njengokubikezela kwakhe. Ngani na?

`Funda uJeremiya 18:7-10. Lokhu kusifundisani, futhi, mayelana nesiprofetho esinemibandela na?`

Iziprofetho ezinemibandela yilezo ziprofetho ezincike endleleni abantu abasabela ngayo emibikweni kaNkulunkulu. Uma abantu beguquka emoyeni nasekuziphatheni, okubikezelwe kusenokungenzeki. Abantu baseNiniva basamukela isibikezelo sikaJona futhi baguqula izindlela zabo, ngalokho uNkulunkulu akazange ababhubhise (Jon. 3:10). Lapho izizukulwane zakamuva zikhohlwa lesisixwayiso sesiprofetho, futhi iNiniva ibuyela ekubeni yindawo ekhohlakele nenesihluku, uNkulunkulu wayibhubhisa. UNkulunkulu uyalazi ikusasa, thina asilazi. Ukukhetha kwethu kukhomba iqiniso lekusasa lethu.

`Kungani kusemqoka ukuqonda umqondo wesiprofetho esinemibandela na? Lokhu kuqonda kungasisiza kanjani ekubambeni iqiniso leBhayibheli na?`
