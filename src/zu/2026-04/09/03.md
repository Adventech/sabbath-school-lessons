---
title:  Ukuvumelana NeBhayibheli
date:   23/11/2026
---

Nakuba thina bantu sivama ukuziphikisa futhi sinamanga, uNkulunkulu akaqambi manga (buka uNum. 23:19). Imibiko Yesiprofetho Yena ayinikezayo ngabaprofethi yembula ukungaguquki Kwakhe. Iqiniso Lakhe elingashintshi lizobonakala emibikweni Yesiprofetho ayinikezayo.

`Funda uIsaya 8:20. Yiliphi iphuzu elisemqoka alenza lapha na?`

Njalo iBhayibheli liyisilinganiso sokugcina sakho konke okubanga ukuphefumulelwa. Abaprofethi kumele bahlolwe ngokuqhathanisa lokho abakushoyo kanye nalokho okushiwo yiBhayibheli. Umprofethi weqiniso ngeke aphikisana, anciphise, noma asuse ukunakwa kweqiniso lebhayibheli. Yingakho ulwazi lweZibhalo lusemqoka kakhulu kulabo abafuna ukulandela uJehovah futhi bangakhohliswa ngabafundisi noma abaprofethi bamanga.

KwiTestamente Elidala nakweLisha, abaprofethi ngokulandelana kwabo bebevama ukugqamisa futhi babhekisa kwimibhalo yomprofethi owandulelayo njengesilinganiso somsebenzi wabo wobuprofethi. Kamuva abaprofethi bebhayibheli bambula ukubona okwandile kumacebo kaNkulunkulu nakwindlela yosindiso, kodwa abaphikisani nezimiso eziyisisekelo kanye nemiqondo esinikeziwe nguNkulunkulu.

`Yini u-2 Petro 1:19-21, Roma 15:4, no Jakobe 1:17 abasifundisa ngayo mayelana noNkulunkulu nemibiko Yesiprofetho ayinikezile kumakhulu eminyaka na?`

Isisekelo seTestamente Elidala sokuqonda icebo losisindiso sabekwa esiprofethweni sokuqala uNkulunkulu asinika Mathupha, kuGenesisi 3:15. Uhlelo lwemihlatshelo lwalubonakalisa lelicebo, kodwa imininingwane yendlela Iwundlu likaNkulunkulu, uJesu Kristu, ayezoza ngayo, aphile, futhi afele isintu yachasiswa ngesinyathelo ngasinye eziprofethweni zakamuva. Umbhalo weBhayibheli uveza ukuthi uNkulunkulu usombulula iqiniso ngenkathi abantu Bakhe bekwazi ukuliqonda. Lokhu kuvumela ukuthi ukuqondwa kweqiniso kujule ngesizukwane esisha. Yilokhu okuchazwa ngomqondo “weqiniso elisombulukayo” noma “iqiniso eliqhubekela phambili.” Okungukuthi, isizukulwane sakamuva sivama ukuba nokuqonda okujulileyo kweqiniso likaNkulunkulu kunesangaphambili.

Kukhona, nokho, iphuzu elisemqoka elingegcizelelwe ngokweqile: izambulo ezintsha zeqiniso ngeke zaphikisana neqiniso uNkulunkulu ayelinikeze ngaphambili.
