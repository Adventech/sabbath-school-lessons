---
title:  Ukuthatha Umzimba Wobuntu NguKristu
date:   24/11/2026
---

Ngokuka-1 Johane 4:1-3, Isivivinyo sokukholelwa ukuthi uJesu wathatha umzimba Wobuntu kwakuyoshesha ukwehlukanisa abantu ababethi bangabaprofethi nabakwezinye izinkolo ezinkulu zomhlaba, noma kulabo ababanga ukuthi bakwazi ukuxhumanisa ezikamoya ngaphandle kokuhambisana nenkolo ethile. Uma umuntu “engavumi ukuthi uJesu Kristu weza ngokwenyama,” lowo muntu “akayena okaNkulunkulu” (1 Johane 4:3, NKJV).

Ngenkathi uJohane ebhala lezizindimana, wayecabanga ngothisha bamanga ababefundisa ukuthi uJesu wayengeyena umuntu, kodwa wayebonakala engumuntu nje. Lesisivivinyo, nokho, siqhubekela phambili. Asisho ukuthi ngumprofethi kuphela okumele avume ukuthi uJesu wayengumuntu ngempela owayephila emhlabeni. Iningi lamaKristu liyakukholwa lokhu ngoJesu. UJohane wayebhekene nodaba oluqondile ngalesosikhathi; kepha, amazwi kaJohane esiprofetho, nakuba edingeka ezimweni ezithile, asaqukethe amaqiniso abalulekile kwizizukulwane zakamuva, ngokunjalo.

`Izindimana ezilandelayo zisitshelani ngoJesu na? Johane 3:16; 2 Kor. 5:21; Johane 11:25; Heb. 4:14, 15; Johane 14:1-3.`

Abaprofethi beqiniso kumele bakholwe ukuthi uJesu wayenguNkulunkulu othathele kuye umzimba Wobuntu kepha engumuntu ngempela. Kumele futhi bamukele lonke iqiniso ngoJesu—umsebenzi Wakhe, impilo Yakhe engenasono, ukufa okubuyisanayo, Uvuko, umsebenzi wobupristi obukhulu ezulwini, kanye nokuza Kwakhe okwesibili. Lokhu Kungaphezu kokuvuma nje ngengqondo ukuthi uJesu waphila emhlabeni. UJesu kumele abengumongo wezimfundiso zomprofethi hhayi nje into eyengeziwe. Imibiko yakhe kumele iholele abantu ebudlelwaneni obusindisayo noJesu.

Izwi eliphefumulelwe nguMoya lingukugqama kukaNkulunkulu, lixosha ubumnyama kithina aze uJesu, Inkanyezi Yokusa, antwele ezinhliziyweni zethu, futhi isipho sesiprofetho sihlanganisa thina Naye futhi siqhubeke nokusibhekisa kuYena.

Abaprofethi abaphakamisa uJesu ngeke bazama ukudala abalandeli babo noma ukuzikhangisa (Zenzo 4:12). Bazolungela ukusebenza hhayi ukuzizuzela labo abathunyelwe kubo. Ngesikhathi esisodwa, njengalokhu sizobona kusasa, abaprofethi bakaNkulunkulu babengabantu futhi, njengathi sonke, izoni ezidinga ivangeli.

`Kungani ukuqonda ukuthi uJesu, onguNkulunkulu ngokuphelele, wafa esikhundleni sethu, ethwala kuye inhlawulo yezono zethu, kungumongo wokukholwa kwethu na? Silahlekelwa yini uma silahla leyomfundiso na?`
