### Funda Lokhu Kuleliviki
Dan. 10:7-19; Duteronomi 13:1-5; Isaya 8:20; Johane 11:25; Zenzo 15:36-39; Jer. 28:9, 15-17.

> <p>Indimana Yenkumbulo</p>
> “Bathandekayo, maningakholwa yibo bonke omoya, kepha hlolani omoya ukuthi bangabakaNkulunkulu yini; ngokuba baningi abaprofethi bamanga abaphumele ezweni” (1 Johane 4:1, NKJV).

I Dayimani langempela lingelinye lamagugu amakhulu emhlabeni. Nembala, ziningi ezinye izinto ezingenziwa zibukeke zifana nedayimani kodwa zibe ziyize. Ngalokho singalazi kanjani idayimani langempela nedayimane mbumbulu na? Okuyiyona ndlela engcono kakhulu ngeyokulivivinya. Ezinye izivivinyo zingenziwa ekhaya ngamathuluzi ambalwa. Ezinye zidinge izingcweti. Izivivinyo eziningi aziphelelisi ngolwazi, kodwa uma idayimani liphumelela izivivinyo ezihlukahlukane, othile angaba neqholo lokuthi idayimani lingelangempela.

Idayimani lingelinye lezinto eziqine kunazozonke emhlabeni. Ingxenye yokuqina kwedayimani kungukwazi kwalo ukumelana namazinga aphakeme okushisa kanye nenguquko esheshayo yokushisa. Ukuvivinya idayimane, ungalishisa kuthi lisashisa bese uliphonsa emanzini abandayo. Nakuba elangempela lingeke lalimala, elenkohliso lingase liqhephuke!

Isipho sesiprofetho sibaluleke ngaphezu kwedayimani, kepha othile osibangayo lesisipho akakwazi ukumane avunywe nje. Lokho kubanga kumele kuvivinyiwe. Kuleliviki sizohlola abaprofethi bebhayibheli ukwembula izimiso zemibhalo ezisetshenziswa Ukuvivinya ubuqiniso babo.

_Funda isifundo saleliviki ukulungisela iSabatha lika uLwezi 28._
