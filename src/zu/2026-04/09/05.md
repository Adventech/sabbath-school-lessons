---
title:  Izithelo Ezinhle
date:   25/11/2026
---

Entshumayelweni yaseNtabeni (Mathewu 5-7), uJesu waveza izisekelo zebandla lobuKristu. Kubonakala sengathi umsebenzi wobuprofethi wawuzoba yingxenye yalesosisekelo. Nembala, uJesu waxwayisa ngabaprofethi bamanga, ethi, “ ‘Ngezithelo zabo niyobazi’ ” (Mat. 7:20, NKJV).

Ubunjalo besithelo bungabonakala kuphela ngokuhamba kwesikhathi. Ngokubona isimilo somprofethi kanye nokubuka imiphumela yemibiko yakhe, singakwazi ukubona uma umprofethi edonsela abanye kuNkulunkulu noma ebaqhelisa kuYena.

`Funda uJona 1:1-3, Zenzo 15:36-39, no Galathiya 2:11-14. Lezizindimana zisifundisani mayelana nabaprofethi na?`

Ukuba ngumprofethi, nokho, akumenzi umuntu angabi nasono noma angabi naphutha. Sibona eBhayibhelini izibonelo zamaphutha kanye nezici ezimilweni zikaJona, uPawule, kanye noPetro. NguNkulunkulu kuphela ophelele; izithunywa Zakhe azinjalo. Abaprofethi, njengathi sonke, banobuthakathaka, kodwa inkambiso yempilo kumele iye kuNkulunkulu ngokusobala, futhi abaprofethi akumele baphikise umsebenzi ngezimpilo ezimelene nemibiko ababelana ngayo.

Akekho umprofethi oyisibonelo esiphelele sikaJesu, kodwa umprofethi weqiniso uzinikele ezimisweni kanye nasempilweni yombuso kaNkulunkulu (Mat. 7:16-20). Noma ngubani obanga ukuba yisithunywa sikaNkulunkulu kodwa impilo yakhe iphikisana nombiko awuthwele kumele abukwe ngeso elinenzinsolo.

Nakuba ubizo lobuprofethi lunzima, iningi labaprofethi labonisa “Izithelo ezilungileyo” ngesikhathi sokusebenza kwabo. Ngokwesibonelo, uMose waphumelela ekuholeni abantwana bakwaIsrayeli ehlane (Dut. 34:10-12). Ukuthembeka kuka-Haggai kwakhuthaza izakhamuzi zakwaJuda ukuba ziqale ukwakha kabusha ithempeli Lesibili, okwakumiswe iminyaka engu-19 (Hag. 1:12). Ukusebenza kukaIsaya okunesibindi kwaba kumakhosi amane akwaJuda, ebayala ngesikhathi esinenxushunxushu futhi ekhomba kuMesiya (Isa. 39:5, 6; Isa. 53:46). Singemlibale uDaniyeli evuma ngenhliziyo yakhe (Dan. 9:15), noma uJeremiya ezinikela ngezinyembezi (Jer. 9:1). Ngaphandle kwendlela ababevama Ukushushiswa nokuhlushwa ngayo, abaprofethi bakaNkulunkulu babenonisa uthando lweqiniso ngaYe nangabantu Bakhe, Uphawu lomsebenzi oqhamile.

`Kuvame kangakani ukuba abantu bakaNkulunkulu bakudumaze ngezenzo zabo noma ngezimilo ezingezinhle na? Yisiphi isifundo esibalulekile ongasifunda kulawomava na?`
