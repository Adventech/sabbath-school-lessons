---
title:  Indima Yokungesikho Kwasemhlabeni
date:   22/11/2026
---

Ngesikhathi Sofuduko, uJehovah wakhuluma kubantu Bakhe mayelana nesipho sokuprofetha phakathi kwabo, wathi, “ ‘Yizwani manje amazwi Ami: Uma kukhona umprofethi phakathi kwenu, Mina, Jehovah, ngizazisa Mina kuyena ngombono; Mina ngikhuluma kuyena ngephupho’ ” (Num. 12:6, NKJV).

Umbiko wesiprofetho kanye nobizo uvama Ukufaka Okungekona Kwasemhlabeni; kwedlulela ngale komuzwa noma umqondo umuntu angaba nawo. Umprofethi Uxhumana bukhoma noNkulunkulu, imvamisa ngamaphupho esiprofetho noma imibono (Dan. 8:1, Isa. 1:1, Zenzo 9:10, njalo, njalo) angahambisana nezigameko eziphathekayo.

`Yenza uhla lwezigameko zenyama umprofethi uDaniyeli aba nazo ngenkathi esembonweni. Buka uDan. 10:7-19.`

UDaniyeli waphelelwa ngamandla lapha ebasembonweni. Wayencike kuNkulunkulu esikhathini sonke salawomava. Nakuba labo ababenaye bengabanga sembonweni, ubukhona bukaNkulunkulu babunamandla kangangokuba babebaleka bacashe ngokwesaba. Nakuba kunjalo, uNkulunkulu usebenzisa izigameko zenyama ukubhekisa kumprofethi ukuze umbiko wesiprofetho uqapheleke.

`UDuteronomi 13:1-5 usifundisani mayelana nesipho sesiprofetho na?`

Nakuba izigameko ezibonakalayo zibonisa ukusebenza kwamandla okungewona asemhlabeni, lokhu akumane kuchaza ukuthi umuntu onalamava alezizigameko uphefumulelwe nguNkulunkulu. Usathane naye uyakwazi ukubonakalisa Okungekona kwalomhlaba. Akubona ubufakazi obubodwa obuqinisekisa isipho sokuprofetha. Ngaphandle kwezigameko zenyama, abaprofethi kumele bavivinywe. Izibonakaliso zokungekona Kwasemhlabeni akuzona izivivinyo zesipho sokuprofetha sangempela. Zonke izivivinyo zabhayibheli zomprofethi kumele zibonakale ukuze siqinisekise ukuthi othile uyisithunywa sikaNkulunkulu. Umlando, Ngeshwa, ugcwele abaprofethi bamanga (buka uMat. 7:15).

`Kwamanye amasikompilo, izehlo ezingezona zasemhlabeni zivamile kunezinye. Kungani, nokho, kumele siqaphele lapho sifuna ukwazi uma okuthile okungekona okwasemhlabeni kungokukaNkulunkulu ngempela na?`
