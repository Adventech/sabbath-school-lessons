---
title:  “Nambithani Nibone”
date:   27/11/2026
---

Ukwamukela kwama-Seventh-day Adventist isipho sika-Ellen G. White sokuprofetha, ukusukela ekuqaleni kwaso kuze kube sesikhathini samanje, kubhekane nezinselele eziningi ezifanayo nalezo zabaprofethi abaningi baseBahyibhelini. Emuva kombono wokuqala ka-Ellen G. White ngoDesemba 1844, abanye bangabaza imibango yakhe yokuprofetha. Lokhu kwaholela kuma-Adventist agcina iSabatha (kamuva ama-Seventh-day Adventist) ukuba asungele Izimpikiswano zokuphasa isipho sika-Ellen G. White sokuprofetha.

Okokuqala, banikeza ukuvuna ngokwemfundiso yebhayibheli yokuqhubeka kwesipho sokuprofetha kuze kube sekupheleni kwesikhathi. Ibhayibheli liphasa ukubonakaliswa kwanamuhla kwesipho sokuprofetha, bebanga. NgokukaJoweli 2, Sambulo 12:17, kanye neminye imibhalo, isipho sokuprofetha sizobonakala “ezinsukwini zokugcina” futhi sibe “wuphawu olukhomba” abantu bakaNkulunkulu besikhathi sokugcina. (Buka u-Gerhard Pfandl, “The Gift of Prophecy and God’s Remnant Church,” The Gift of Prophecy, chapter 4.)

Okwesibili, ama-Adventist okuqala aqaphela ukuthi isipho sokuprofetha sika-Ellen G. White sasinethonya elihle kumakholwa. Ngokwesibonelo, ukubona kwakhe okusithekile kwasiza ukuhlanganisa amakholwa ayekwizinkomfa zeSabatha ngasekupheleni kwama-1840 ngenkathi engavumelani ngezimfundiso zebhayibheli. Isipho sakhe saqhubeka ukuthula “Izithelo ezinhle” eminyakeni elandelayo, futhi imibhalo yakhe iqhubeka nokuba nethonya phakathi komphakathi wokukholwa wama-Seventh-day Adventist.

Okwesithathu ukugomela ngokuvikela u-Ellen G. White kuhlobene nezinto ezingezona zalomhlaba kumava akhe emibono. Ngenkathi esembonweni, njengoba siphawulile ngaphambilini, u-Ellen G. White wayebonakalisa Okungekona kwalapha emhlabeni—ebika okuyikona nezimo angazazi yena, embula izigameko zekusasa, ebonakalisa amandla amakhulu kunawemvelo, futhi engakwazi ukulawula noma abenethonya kwizikhathi zamava akhe emibono, kokunye.

Ama-Seventh-day Adventist namuhla ayaqhubeka nokugqamisa lezizinkulumo ukuphasa ukufaneleka kwesipho sika-Ellen G. White. Nakuba lemigomo iphusile, “ubufakazi” obunamandla besipho sika-Ellen G. White sokuprofetha ngobamava omuntu mathupha ngemibhalo yakhe. Njengalokhu umhlabeleli uDavide asho: “Yizwani Nibone ukuthi uJehovah ulungile” (Hub. 34:8). Kungesikhathi kuphela sokufunda ngokukhuleka imibhalo ka-Ellen G. White lapho sikwazi Ukuhlola imibango yakhe yobuprofethi.

Enye indawo esingaqala kuyo kusencwadini yakhe encane nedumile Izinyathelo Eziya KuKristu (Steps to Christ). Lamanisa lencwadi ngezincwadi ezinhlanu ze-Conflict of the Ages, elandela umkhondo wempikiswano enkulu ukusukela kuGenesisi kuye kwiSambulo. Imibhalo yakhe iyatholakala ngokubanzi (bheka ku-EGWWritings.org) futhi ihumushelwe ezilimini eziningi.
