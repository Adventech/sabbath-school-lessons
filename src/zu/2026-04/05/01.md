### Funda Lokhu Kuleliviki
Heb. 1:1, 2; 2 Tim. 3:15-17; 2 Pet. 1:1821; Eksod. 17:14; Sambulo. 1:19; Hezek. 4:1-8.

> <p>Indimana Yenkumbulo</p>
> “Isambulo sikaJesu Kristu amnika sona uNkulunkulu ukuba abonise izinceku zakhe izinto ezimelwe ukwenzeka masinyane, ethuma ngengelosi yakhe, esibonakalisa encekwini yakhe uJohane, owafakaza ngezwi likaNkulunkulu nangobufakazi bukaJesu Kristu, konke akubonayo” (Sambulo 1:1, 2, NKJV).

Umpostoli uJohane wayeyingxenye yesigungu esiphakathi sabaPostoli Abayishumi Nambili (Luka 8:51, Mat. 17:1; Mat. 26:36, 37). Emuva kokunyuka kukaKristu, waqhubeka nomsebenzi wakhe, eJerusalema. Kepha, ngaphambi kokuwa kwedolobha (a.d. 70), wathuthela e-Efesu ukuze ayokwengamela amabandla Esifundeni saseRoma sase-Asia Minor. Ngesikhathi sokushushiswa kwamaKristu ngaphansi kombusi wamaRoma u-Domitian, umpostoli wadingiselwa e-Patmos, isiqhingi esikwi-Aegean Sea.

E-Patmos, uJohane wathola imibiko ekhethekile Yesiprofetho eyayibhekiswe hhayi kuphela kumabandla ayisikhombisa ase-Asia Minor kodwa kumaKristu esikhathini sonke (Samb. 1:1-3, 9-11). Loluhlelo lokudlulisa imibiko, ayeyingxenye yalo uJohane, lwalubukeka kanje: uNkulunkulu uBaba, kuye kuJesu Kristu, kuye kwingelosi, kuye kumpostoli uJohane, kuye kubafundi nakubalaleli. Lokhu yisibonelo esiqhamile sohlelo lwesambulo nokuphefumulelwa kwaso uNkulunkulu asebenzisa abaprofethi Ukudlulisa imibiko Yakhe kubantu.

Isifundo saleliviki sihlola ngokuqondile uhlelo olunezimo eziningana lwesambulo nokuphefumulelwa, ngokugxila ngokukhethekile kokukhulunywayo, okubhaliwe, nokukhangisiwe kokudlulisa umbiko kubantu.

_Funda isifundo saleliviki ukulungiselela iSabatha, uMfumfu 31._
