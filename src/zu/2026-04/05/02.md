---
title:  Isambulo Esivamile NesiKhethekileyo
date:   25/10/2026
---

`Funda amaHeberu 1:1, 2. Kungani uNkulunkulu ethathekile ngokukhuluma kithi na? Yini asitshela yona ngoJesu nangabaprofethi?`

Kungayini uMdali wezulu nomhlaba angafuna ukuyisho kithina na? Kungani exhumana nathi na? Kungenxa yokuthi siyisintu esiwileyo emhlabeni wesono futhi sibhekene nokufa, ubuhlungu, kanye nezinhlupheko; uNkulunkulu, nokho, ukhuluma kithina ngoba Yena ufuna sazi isizathu sokuba lapha, ukuthi kungani izinto zizimbi kangaka, nokuthi kungani, ngaphandle kwakho konke, singathembela othandweni Lwakhe ngathi.

Isambulo sikaNkulunkulu esivamile, njengalokhu sibonakala endalweni, sikhuluma ngobuciko ngamandla Akhe aphakade kanye nothando Lwakhe olunakayo. Inkosi uDavide yababaza, “Amazulu amemezela inkazimulo kaJehovah, nomkhathi ubonisa umsebenzi wezandla zakhe” (Hub. 19:1, NKJV). Umpostoli uPawule wathayisela, “Ngokuba selokhu kwadalwa izwe okungabonwayo kwakhe, okungukuthi, amandla akhe aphakade nobuNkulunkulu bakhe, kubonakala kahle, kwaqondakala ngalokho okwenziwe, ukuze bangabi nezaba” (Rom. 1:20, NASB).

Kuyamangalisa futhi ukuzindla ngamandla kaNkulunkulu okubuyisela abonakaliswe ohlelweni olufihlakele lokuphulukisa umhlaba wethu. Nembala, “Amandla kaNkulunklu okwelapha akuyo indalo. Uma umuthi usikiwe, uma umuntu elimele noma ephuka ithambo, indalo iqala ngalesosikhathi ukulungisa ukulimala. Nangaphambi kokuba isidingo sibekhona, izididiyeli zokwelapha zihlala zilindile; kuthi nje uma kubakhona ukulimala, onke amandla abhekane nomsebenzi wokubuyisela.”—Ellen G. White, Education, p. 113.

Iyinhle futhi ifundisa kanjalo indalo, ayembuli nje kuphela amandla kaNkulunkulu nothando kodwa nobukhona bobubi (buka uGen. 3:14-19, Mat. 13:24-30). Lombiko ongacacile ungaqondakala kangcono ngokubuka isambulo esikhethekile sikaNkulunkulu—kuJesu Kristu, nakuliZwi owaba ngumuntu, nakwiBhayibheli, iZwi Elilotshiwe likaNkulunkulu elifakaza ngaYe (Johane 5:39).

Izibhalo zingumbhalo ophefumulelwe wombiko kaNkulunkulu kusosonke isintu ezikhathini zonke. Zisitshela ngoKudalwa komhlaba kanye nokuwa wawo, nangecebo elimangalisayo likaNkulunkulu losindiso kanye nokubuyiselwa komhlaba ekugcineni. Nakuba zimfushane futhi zikhethile ezinye izingxoxo zebhayibheli, zithembekile ngasosonke isikhathi, ngoba nguNkulunkulu ekhuluma kithina (buka u-2 Tim. 3:16, 2 Pet. 1:19-21).

`Nakuba indalo ikhomba eqinisweni lamandla nothando loMdali, kungani sisalidinga kakhulu neZwi Elilotshiweyo na? Yini esiyitshelwa yiZwi indalo, ngokwayo, engakwenzi na?`
