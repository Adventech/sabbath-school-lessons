---
title:  U-Ellen G. White kwiSambulo noKuphefumulelwa
date:   30/10/2026
---

Ama-Seventh-day Adventist akholwa ukuthi u-Ellen G. White wayephefumulelwe nguNkulunkulu. Imibhalo yakhe, impilo, kanye nokusebenza kufeza izimiso zomprofethi weqinso. Wayenezimpawu ezifanayo zokuphefumulelwa kwabaprofethi bebhayibheli, nakuba engazange abone imibhalo yakhe njengefana neZibhalo. Kunalokho, u-Ellen G. White wayephakamisa Ibhayibheli ngasosonke isikhathi. Kodwa yena ngokwakhe wayeziqonda kanjani izambulo zeziprofetho zikaNkulunkulu kuyena kanye nenkambiso yokuphefumulelwa kwakhe na?

Okokuqala, njalo u-Ellen G. White wayebona Ibhayibheli liyisambulo sikaNkulunkulu esinegunya nesingenaphutha. Ibhayibheli lembula iqiniso likaNkulunkulu kanye necebo Lakhe lokusindisa isintu esiwileyo. Kulolohlelo, njengalokhu sifunda, Yena usebenzisa abaprofethi Ukukhuluma imibiko Yakhe.

Okwesibili, u-Ellen G. White wayebona ukuthi inkambiso yokuphefumulelwa yayifaka ukuxhumana kukaNkulunkulu nomuntu. UNkulunkulu wakhuluma imibiko Yakhe, kodwa Umprofethi wayedlulisa umyalezo kaNkulunkulu ngokusebenzisa amazwi akhe. Njengoba u-Ellen G. White asho: “Akuwona amagama eBhayibhelini aphefumulelwe, kepha ngabantu ababephefumulelwe. Ukuphefumulelwa akusebenzi kumazwi omuntu noma ekuzibonakaliseni kwakhe kepha kumuntu uqobo, okuthi, ngaphansi kwethonya likaMoya Ongcwele, agcwaliswe ngemicabango. . . . umcabango kaNkulunkulu kanye nentando kuhlangana nomqondo nentando yomuntu; ngalokho Ukukhuluma komuntu kuba yizwi likaNkulunkulu”—Selected Messages, book 1, p. 21.

Ngokuphathelene nemibono kanye namaphupho esiprofetho, u-Ellen G. White wakuqonda “ukuphefumulelwa komcabango” njengokuhlangana kweSambulo sikaNkulunkulu kanye namava omuntu. wabhala, “ ‘Ibhayibheli, kanye namqiniso alo anikezwa nguNkulunkulu lethulwa ngolimi lwabantu, liletha ubunye bobuNkulunkulu kanye nesintu’ ”—Selected Messages, book 1, p. 25. UNkulunkulu wasebenzisa izindlela eziningi zokuletha ukuqonda umbiko Wakhe emqondweni wesithunywa Sakhe. Lokhu kwakufaka imbiko ngemifanekiso, amazwi, nemicabango eyayifaka yonke imizwa yomprofethi. Amava ka-Ellen G. White ayefana nalawo Ababhali bebhayibheli.

Okwesithathu, u-Ellen G. White waqaphela ukuthi kwakungewona onke amava akhe obuntu kanye nokuxhumana kwakhe okwakuphefumulelwe. “Kukhona izikhathi lapho izinto esivamile kumele zishiwo,” kululeka yena, “imicabango evamile kumele ibe semqondweni, izinhlamvu zamagama ezivamile kumele zibhaliwe futhi nolwazi lunikezwe oludluliselwa komunye nomunye wezisebenzi. Lawamazwi, lolulwazi, alunikeziwe ngaphansi kokuphefumulelwa okukhethekile koMoya kaNkulunkulu.”—Selected Messages, book 1, p. 39. Kubalulekile ukubona lomehluko lapho sifunda sihambisana nemibhalo kanye namava akhe, ngokukhethekile ukuxhumana kwakhe kanye nama-diary.

Ukwazi ukuqonda kuka-Ellen G. White kweSambulo noKuphefumulelwa kusiza thina sigweme ihaba futhi sifunde izeluleko zakhe ngokuhlakanipha nangokuqonda.
