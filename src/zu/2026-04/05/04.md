---
title:  Imibiko Ekhulunywe Ngomlomo
date:   27/10/2026
---

`Funda u-2 Petro 1:18-21. Yiziphi iziqiniseko esinazo zokuthi imibiko eyethulwa ngenkulumo ngabaprofethi bakaNkulunkulu yayiphefumulelwe futhi ithembeke njengebhaliweyo na?`

UNkulunkulu wakhuluma ku-Adamu no-Eva ngaphambi koKuwa (Gen. 2:16, 17) nangemuva koKuwa (Gen 3:9-19). Kuzo zombili izikhathi, bawaqondisisa amazwe Akhe. Izwi Lakhe lezwakala ngokucacileyo futhi eSinayi (Eksod. 20:120), nasekubhabhathizweni nasekuguqulweni kukaJesu (Mat. 3:17, Mat. 17:5). Singekuhumushe ngokufanele uma abanye abantu bekhuluma kithi. Kepha, ngokuqinisekile, lapho uNkulunkulu ekhuluma kunoba ngubani, ngeke kwabakhona ukungaxhumani okusophathe noma inkohliso.

Ibhayibheli liyakuqinisa ukuthi “uNkulunkulu wakhuluma kuMose wathi kuye: ‘Mina nginguJehovah’ ” (Eksod. 6:2, NKJV). Iningi lababhali bebhayibheli liyakuvuma ukuzwa izwi likaNkulunkulu ngenkathi ekhuluma kubo. Kwakunjalo, ngokwesibonelo, ngoIsaya (Isa. 8:1, 11), Jeremiya (Jer. 1:4, 9, 11), no-Ezekiel (Ezek. 3:16, Ezek. 6:1). Noma yimuphi umzamo wokukuphika lokhu kanye neminye imibango kugcina kungabaza Ukuthembeka kwabaprofethi kanye nokuqiniseka kweBhayibheli uqobo.

Njengoba uNkulunkulu akhuluma kubaprofethi ngolimi lwesintu oluzwakalayo, kunjalo nabaprofethi bakhuluma nabantu. Ngokwesibonelo, uNowa wayengumprofethi weqiniso kaNkulunkulu kanye “nomshumayeli wokulunga” Othembekileyo (2 Pet. 2:5). Akazange abhale lutho Ebhayibhelini, kodwa wethula umbiko kaNkulunkulu ngokukhuluma nalabo ayephila nabo, futhi umbiko wakhe wawuphefumulelwe njengayo imibhalo yabaprofethi ababebhala phansi.

Iningi labaprofethi, kufaka u-Nathan, Eliya, Elisha, noJohane Umbhabhathizi, abazange babhale lutho Ebhayibhelini; kunalokho, bethula imibiko yabo ngenkulumo.

Kangakanani ngoJesu Kristu ngoKwakhe na? Umbiko weVangeli ubalula ukuthi ngesikhathi sokusebenza Kwakhe emhlabeni, “wabhala emhlabathini ngomunwe Wakhe” (Johane 8:6, NKJV). Yilokho nje. Asinako okubika ukuthi kukhona okunye uJesu akubhala. Kodwa wafundisa futhi washumayela ngenkulumo “njengothile onegunya”(Mat. 7:29, NKJV). Abanye ababemlalele bavuma, “akekho umuntu owake wakhuluma njengaleNdoda” (Johane 7:40, 46, NKJV). UJesu wayegxile kakhulu ekudluliseni imibiko kaNkulunkulu ngokukhuluma. Imibiko Yakhe yayinegunya futhi inezibopho ezinganciphile ngoba Yena engabhalanga zincwadi zeBhayibheli.

`Kuyathathekisa ukuthi uNkulunkulu unabaprofethi abangazange babhale lutho Ebhayibhelini. Kungani lababaprofethi abangabhale lutho nabo benegunya na?`
