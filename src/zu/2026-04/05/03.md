---
title:  Inkambiso Yokuphefumulelwa
date:   26/10/2026
---

Izincwadi eziningi, hhayi nje Ibhayibheli kuphela, zidluliswe kithina ezikhathini zonke. Kepha sibona Ibhayibheli njengeZwi likaNkulunkulu. Yini elihlukanisa kweminye imibhalo na?

`Funda u-2 Thimothewu 3:15-17 no Jakobe 1:17. Yimuphi umyalezo obalulekile okumele siwuthathe kulezizindimana na?`

Kunezingxoxo mayelana nesihumusho esingcono kunazozonke sika-2 Thimothewu 3:16, kodwa abahumushi abaningi beBhayibheli bayethula Lendimana ngendlela efanayo, begcizela ukuthi Ibhayibheli lonke liqhamuka kuNkulunkulu (2 Pet. 1:20, 21).

Omunye ophawulayo uchaza ukuthi “kubukeka kungenzeka kakhulu ukuthi uPawule ubuka Izibhalo ngokuphelela kwazo lapha nokuthi athi zonke Izibhalo ‘zilungele ukufundisa, nokusola, nokuqondisa, nokuyala ekulungeni,’ kunokuthi zonke Izindimana zikulungele lokhu.”—G. W. Knight III, The Pastoral Epistles: A Commentary on the Greek Text (Grand Rapids, MI: Eerdmans, 1992), p. 445.

Kepha kumele siyiqonde kanjani inkambiso yokuphefumulelwa eyasungula Ibhayibheli na? Kumele sibone ukuthi ukuphefumulelwa kobuprofethi nguMoya Ongcwele ulekelela abaprofethi beqiniso ngokuxhumana okuthembekeile kwemibiko kaNkulunkulu. Lokhu kulekelela kuhlinzekwa hhayi kuphela ngenkambiso yokwamukela imibiko kodwa nasekudluliseni imibiko ngokusobala kubalaleli.

Imibiko kaNkulunkulu yeza ngemibono (Num. 12:6; Isa. 6:1, 8). Futhi wayesebenzisa ofakazi ababone ngamehlo (1 Johane 1:1-3), ukuhlola umlando okuqhutshwe nguMoya (Luka 1:1-3), kanye nemicabango efakwe nguNkulunkulu (2 Pet. 1:21). Noma iyiphi indlela esetshenziswe nguMoya Ongcwele, abaprofethi kanye nababhali bebhayibheli babenikezwe imibiko yeqiniso engancikile emizweni yabo, nakuba babesebenzisa amazwi abo ukwethula leyomibiko.

Umsebenzi wokukhuluma imibiko Yesiprofetho kubalaleli babo bokuqala kwakungenziwa ngokukhuluma (2 Pet. 1:19-21), noma ngokubhalwa (Johane 20:30, 31), noma nangakho ukukhangisa (Ezek. 4:1-8).

`Nakuba iminingi imibuzo esele ngendlela yokuphefumulelwa kweBhayibheli, noma ukuthi ukuphefumulelwa kusebenza kanjani ngokuvamile, kungani kusemqoka kakhulu ukuthemba Ibhayibheli njengeZwi likaNkulunkulu eliphefumulelwe, ngale kokuthi singekuqonde ngokuphelele ukuthi labhalwa kanjani na?`
