---
title:  Imbiko Elotshiweyo
date:   28/10/2026
---

Ezinye zezinkulumo ezaziwayo zithinte izimpilo zabantu ngokudephile futhi zazezaguqula nomlando. Uma lezizinkulumo zazingaqoshiwe, amazwi ngabe afiphala noma ahlanekezelwa ngokuhamba kwesikhathi. Ngalokho, ukubhala kunikeza ukungaphezi kwezwi elikhulunyiweyo, futhi lokho kungaphezi kuvumela umbiko ukuba udluliswe esikhathini sonke.

Kungekudala emuva kokuba uIsrayeli eshiye eGibhithe futhi wanqoba ama-Amaleki, uNkulunkulu wayaleza uMose ukuba “abhale lokhu encwadini yesikhumbuzo” (Eksod. 17:14, NKJV). Ukusukela kuMose, owabhala izincwadi ezinhlanu zokuqala zeBhayibheli kanye neHubo 90, uMoya Ongcwele wahola abaprofethi bezibhalo ukukhiqiza imibhalo okuyiyona manje eyizincwadi ezingu-66 zeBhayibheli.

`Funda u-Eksodusi 17:14; Johane 20:30, 31; 1 Korinte 10:11; ne Sambulo 1:19. Yiziphi ezinye zezizathu uNkulunkulu atshela ngazo abaprofethi Bakhe ukuba babhale phansi imibiko ayebanika yona na?`

Izincwadi ezahlukahlukene Ebhayibhelini zibonisa uhlobo lokubhala kanye namakhono Ababhali ngokwehlukana kwabo. Njengalokhu sizoxoxisana esifundweni seviki elilandelayo, ukwehluka komuntu ngamunye kanye nezindlela zombhali ngamunye akususi emibikweni yabo, ngoba awekho “amazinga” okuphefumulelwa. Ezinye izingxenye zeBhayibheli ziqondene ngokusobala futhi zingena ezimpilweni zethu nasokholweni lwethu namuhla kunezinye izingxenye. Kodwa Leliqiniso alenzi enye ingxenye yeBhayibheli iphefumulelwe kakhulu kunenye ingxenye.

Ngezinye izikhathi Ababhali bebhayibheli baqopha imibiko ephefumelelwe ababeyithole ekuqaleni ngenkulumo, ngokwabo, noma ngomsizi Othembekileyo. Encwadini kaDuteronomi, uMose waqopha inkulumo yokugcina ayeyethule kwabakwaIsrayeli ngaphambi kokufa kwakhe. AmaVangeli amane abhala ngezintshumayelo nezimfundiso eziningi zikaJesu ngesikhathi sokusebenza Kwakhe emhlabeni.

Kodwa kunezikhathi lapho abaprofethi becaphune imithombo engaphefumulelwe Izenzo 17:28, 1 Kor. 15:33, Titu 1:12) ngisho futhi namazwi kaSathane (Gen. 3:1-5, Mat. 4:1-11).

Kumele sikhumbule ukuthi njengoba abaprofethi bebhala imizwa nezifiso zabanye abantu, babeveza nendlela bona abaphatheke ngayo njengabantu (buka uDan. 8:27; Samb. 22:8, 9). Ngaphezu kwalokhu, Ibhayibheli lichaza izibonelo eziningi ezinhle okumele zilingiswe kodwa neziningi ezimbi okumele zigwenywe (1 Kor. 10:1-11).

Ngenkathi sifunda Ibhayibheli, kumele sikhumbule ukuthi, nakuba lingabhaliwe ngolimi oluphelele, liqukethe umbiko ongenaphutha oyoma ingunaphakade.
