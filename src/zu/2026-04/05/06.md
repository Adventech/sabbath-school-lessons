title:  I-Drama, Izimpawu, Imifanekiso
date:   29/10/2026
---

Ibhayibheli linothile ngemifanekiso yamazwi ahlukahlukene kanye nezingathekiso. Ngokwesibonelo, lonke uhlelo lwemihlatshelo yezinzalamizi zeTestamente Elidala, itabernakeli likaMose, kanye nethempeli eJerusalema kwakungumfanekiso obonakalayo womhlatshelo kaKristu wokubuyisana esiphambanweni kanye nobupristi Bakhe basezulwini (Heb. 8:1-6).

Eziprofethweni zikaDaniyeli, imibuso namandla ezweni zathulwa ngezingxenye ezahlukene zesithombe esikhulu (Dan. 2:31-45), izilwane ezahlukene kanye nezimpondo (Dan. 7 no 8), kanye namakhosi amabili (Dan. 11). Ukwethula okufanayo kuyabonakala futhi encwadini yeSambulo, njengasodabeni lwezilo ezimbili zesahluko 13.

Ekufundiseni Kwakhe, uJesu wakhuluma Ngaye, ngokwesibonelo, njengeSinkwa Sokuphila (Johane 6:35, 41-58), Ukukhanya kwezwe (Johane 8:12, Johane 9:5), kanye noMvini weQiniso (Johane 15:1-8). Futhi ngomfanekiso Womhlwanyeli (Mat. 13:1-9, 18-23) kanye nangeminye ehlukahlukene (Mat. 13:24-52, njalo, njalo), uJesu wafanekisa uhlobo kanye nokukhula kombuso Wakhe.

`Fundau-Ezekiel 4:1-8. Ucabanga ukuthi kungani uNkulunkulu engakhulumanga nje kodwa wabonakalisa imibiko Yakhe na?`

Isibonakaliso esisobala kakhulu siqhamuka ku-Ezekiel 4:1-8, lapho uNkulunkulu ethuma umprofethi ukuthi enze umfanekiso omncane weJerusalema nokuthi alale phansi, okokuqala kwesokunxele sakhe izinsuku ezingu-390, Emele Indlu yakwaIsrayeli, bese elala kwesokudla sakhe izinsuku ezingu-40, okumele Indlu yakwaJuda. Esinye isibonkaliso ngesomshado ogcotshwe nguNkulunkulu phakathi kukaHoseya noGoma, Isifebe, omele ukungathembeki kwezikamoya kukaIsrayeli kuNkulunkulu (Hos. 1:2-9).

Lamava abonakalisiwe ka-Ezekiel noHoseya abukeka ethusa namuhla. Kodwa yilokho kanye okwakufuneka: kwakumele ethuse, hhayi kithina kuphela kepha nakubantu ngalesosikhathi. Ngesikhathi lapho izixwayiso zabaprofethi zazichithwa bukhoma (buka u-2 Kron. 36:15-210, isenzo sokugcina somusa kaNkulunkulu kwakungesokubonisa ngokomdlalo kubantu isimo sabo sangempela ukuze baholeleke ekuphendukeni kweqiniso. Lokhu kwethula nembala kwakumele kubabonise ukuqina okwakumele bathathe ngakho ukuxwayisa kukaNkulunkulu kubona.

`Yikuphi ukulingisa okungase kwenziwe ngothile namuhla ukusixwayisa, asithi, ngokuba ngabaseLawodekiya na? (Buka iSamb. 3:14-18.)`
