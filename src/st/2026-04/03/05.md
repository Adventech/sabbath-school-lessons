---
title:  "Baprofeta ba Basali"
date:  14/10/2026
---

`Bala Exoda 15:20, Baahloli 4:4, 2 Marena 22:14, le Nehemiea 6:14. Litemana tsee li ruta eng hape ka karolo ea baprofeta?`

Ho na le bopaki bo bongata Bibeleng bo bontšang hore Molimo o ile a bitsa basali ho ba baprofeta. (Sheba tšebeliso ea lentsoe la Seheberu nevi’ah [Ex. 15:20] le lentsoe la Segerike prophetis [Luka 2:36].) Ho Mekia 6:4, Miriama o ngotsoe hammoho le Moshe le Aarone e le ba rometsoeng ho batho. Miriama o hlahisoa e le moprofeta oa mosali ea ileng a phokola pina ea tlholo ha Ba-Isiraele ba feta tšela Leoatle le Lefubelu (Ex. 15:20, 21). Leha ho le joalo, moprofeta enoa, joalo ka balekane ba hae ba banna, ha a hlaha a se na molato; joalo ka boitšoaro ba hae bo makatsang khahlanong le Moshe le mosali oa hae oa Mo-Cushe bo bontša, liketso tseo moprofeta enoa a ileng a otloa ka lebaka la tsona ka toka (sheba Num. 12:1-10).

Re ka bona mosebetsi oa moprofeta e mong oa mosali ka pale ea Deborah, ea ileng a sebeletsa e le moahloli ho Israele (Baahl. 4:1-4). Hantle, ho na le bopaki bo hlakileng ba hore Deborah (Baahl. 4:4) o ile a fumana tšenolo ho tsoa ho Molimo (Baahl. 4:4-9, 14-22). Pina ea hae ea tlholo (Baahl. 5:2-31) ke kananelo ea tšebetso ea Molimo ‘me e ne e na le bohlokoa bo tebileng ba thuto ea tumelo bakeng sa naha nakong eo ea nalane.

`Bala 2 Marena 22:11-14. Hobaneng banna ba Morena Josiase ba ile ho Huda? See se lokela ho re bolella eng ka karolo ea hae ea bohlokoa sechabeng?`

Huda, “moprofeta oa mosali,” o ile a atameloa ke banna ba holimo ba lekhotla la Morena Josiase (2 Marena 22:14, 2 Likronike 34:22) kamora hore ba bale mantsoe a “Buka ea Molao” (2 Marena 22:8) ho Morena, eo, e le karabelo, a “ileng a hahola liaparo tsa hae” (2 Marena 22:11). Ena e ne e le taba e tebileng haholo, ’me Morena a e nka joalo, ke ka lebaka leo banna ba hae ba ileng ba ea hang-hang ho Huda. O ile a amohela molaetsa ho tsoa ho Molimo mabapi le kahlolo e tlelang Juda. Liphetoho tsa Josiase li ile tsa tlisoa ke melaetsa ea boprofeta ea Huda ’me tsa thibela kahlolo ea Molimo ho batho ba Hae.

`Baprofeta ba banna, baprofeta ba basali, leha ba bitsitsoe ke Molimo, e ne e le baetsalibe ba hlokang mohau. Ke tšepo efe eo u ka e nkang ho tsoa lipaleng tsa bona?`
