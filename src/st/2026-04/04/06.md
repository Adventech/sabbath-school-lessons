---
title:  "Baprofeta ba Bohata Testamenteng e Ncha"
date:  22/10/2026
---

`Bala Matheu 24:23, 24. Jesu o re hlokomelisa ka eng moo? Hobaneng re lokela ho nkela hloohong tlhokomeliso ee?`

Jesu ka Sebele o ne a ke ke a hlaka ho feta mona: “Hobane bo-Kreste ba bohata le baprofeta ba bohata ba tla hlaha, ba etse lipontšo tse kholo le mehlolo, ho thetsa, haeba ho ka khoneha, le bona bakhethoa” (Mattheu 24:24).

Lithetso tseo Jesu a lemosang ka tsona li tlameha e be ke tse kholisang haholo, kaha “lipontšo tse kholo le mehlolo” e tla ba karolo ea tsona. Liketsahalo tsa bomolimo ha se bopaki ba hore Molimo oa sebetsa, leha li ka bonahala li kholisa hakae. Re na le lira tsa moea. Joalokaha Paulosi a re lemositse: “Hobane re loantšana eseng le nama le mali, empa le marena, le matla, le babusi ba lefifi la lefatše lena, le meea e mebe e sebakeng sa leholimo” (Baefese 6:12). ’Me matla ana a sebetsa ka baprofeta ba bohata.

Ha e le hantle, baprofeta ba bohata ha se ntho e ncha. Testamente e Ncha e bua ka baprofeta ba bohata ba mehleng ea Testamente ea Khale (Luka 6:26). Testamente e Ncha e bapisa baprofeta ba bohata le “liphokojoe tse hlaha” tse “apereng koko la linku” (Mattheu 7:15), ’me re bolelloa hore re tla “ba tseba ka litholoana tsa bona” (Mattheu 7:16).

Mosebetsi oa bona o moholo ke thetso (Mattheu 7:17–20). Jesu ka Sebele o ile a bolela hore “baprofeta ba bohata ba bangata ba tla hlaha, ba thetse batho ba bangata” (Mattheu 24:11), le hore “haeba ho ka khoneha, ba tla thetsa le bakhethoa” (Mattheu 24:24; bona hape Mareka 13:22, 1 Johanne 4:1).

`Ho latela Baefese 6:10-18, re ka itšireletsa joang litekong tsohle tsa thetso?`

Ho phela ka tumelo, ho apara “thebe ea poloko,” ho itlama ka “’nete,” hammoho le ho inehela ho Moea o Halalelang ka thapelo, le ho tiisoa ka Lentsoeng la Hae — tsena ke litsela tseo re ka sireletsoang ka tsona khahlanong le tsohle tse ka re khelosang moeeng le lithutong tsa tumelo.

`U ka etsa joang ho itlhomella betere khahlanong le lithetso tsohle tse fielang lefatše la rōna?`
