`What was happening at the court of King Jehoiakim? What did the leaders do when they heard Jeremiah’s warning? Read Jeremiah 36:20–26 for the answers.`

Jeremiah was God’s messenger to the kingdom of Judah. At that time, disaster was coming. For many years, Jeremiah warned Judah that God was not happy with them. Jeremiah also warned that the Babylonians would beat Judah in a war. Then the Babylonians would carry many of God’s people to Babylon (Jeremiah 6:8–19; Jeremiah 26). The Lord wanted to save His people. But they needed to obey Him first.

God’s people were guilty of many public sins. They worshiped false gods. They killed their children and offered them to the gods. They cheated on their wives. They were not fair. They hurt people. Jeremiah warned God’s people to stop these sins. But they paid no attention. As Jeremiah’s work neared its end, God tried to warn His people even more. In Jeremiah 32, God tells Jeremiah to buy some land. God also tells Jeremiah to keep the piece of paper that shows that Jeremiah is the landowner: “The Lord Almighty, Israel’s God, says, ‘A time will come when people in this land [will] again buy houses, fields and vineyards [grape fields]’ ” (Jeremiah 32:15, EASY). God’s message is meant to give the people hope after they are carried away to Babylon.

God loved His people so much. That’s why He asked Jeremiah to write a final message of warning to them. King Jehoiakim’s bad behavior showed that He didn’t respect God, His message, or His messenger.

`How did God answer the king in Jeremiah 36:27–31?`

Sometimes we think that if we pay no attention to God’s messengers, their messages will go away. But we cannot destroy God’s message for us. Jeremiah’s message about King Jehoiakim’s end is an awful warning for every one of us (Jeremiah 36:30, 31). When we pay no attention to God’s warnings, we will be in terrible danger. For sure, we will suffer for our sins and evil choices.

`God kept trying to save His people. He continued to warn them, even after they refused to pay attention to Him. What does this Bible truth tell us about His love?`
