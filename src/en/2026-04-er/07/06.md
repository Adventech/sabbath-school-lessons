---
title:  Warnings For Us
date:   12/11/2026
---

Many Old Testament stories are sad. Terrible things often happened in them. God gave us these stories for an impor­tant reason. God wants us to learn lessons from these stories about what happens when we fight against Him.

`Why did God tell His Bible messengers to write what they wrote? Read 1 Corinthians 10:6–12 for the answer.`

In these verses, Paul connects the past with the present. He connects ancient Israel with the modern church. Today, we are part of this church because we accept Jesus as our Lord and Savior (Galatians 6:14–10).

Paul says we must learn from ancient Israel’s history. We must learn from their experiences, both good and bad. God thought that these experiences were so important to us that He told His messengers to write about them. As Paul says, “We must learn from those things that happened to our ancestors. We must not want to do very bad things, like [the same as] they did” (1 Corinthians 10:6, EASY). The past often shows us what will happen in the future.

God wants us to understand that we don’t need to make the same mistakes that our ancestors made. If we obey His messengers, we will have success (2 Chronicles 20:20). Many people believe that success is having a nice home, cars, clothes, and other stuff. For sure, God will take care of our needs. But God also wants us to have success in other ways (3 John 2). He wants us to have health and spiritual success. He wants us to win the fight against sin. We will have these things when we obey God’s messages.

God always means what He says. When He speaks, His words are everlasting and powerful (Isaiah 40:8). His words do what God wants them to do. God gave us His messages to help us to meet Jesus. Paul says, “The things that happened to those people are examples. They were written to be warnings for us. We live in the time that all those past histories were pointing to [showing to us]” (1 Corinthians 10:11, ERV). If these words were important to follow back then, how much more should we follow them today?

`Think about people in the Bible who fought against God or didn’t believe His messages. What lessons do their stories teach you? How can their stories help you not to make their same mistakes?`
