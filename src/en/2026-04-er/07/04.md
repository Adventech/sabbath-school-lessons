---
title:  "Destroying The Message: Part 2"
date:   10/11/2026
---

For the most part, the history of Israel and Judah in Bible times shows that they rejected God’s messengers. In the story of Moses, the Israelites were afraid to enter the Promised Land. So, they fought against Moses and Aaron. Years later, King Jehoiakim burned God’s message. The king hoped that the fire would destroy God’s warning forever.

`Read 2 Chronicles 36:15, 16. What did the people of Judah do when they heard God’s messages? How did God finally answer Judah?`

Jeremiah was God’s messenger during the times of King Josiah, King Jehoahaz, King Jehoiakim, King Jehoiachin, and King Zedekiah. Babylon was about to attack God’s people. Everything that Jeremiah warned about was soon to happen. Then 21-year-old Zedekiah became king of Judah (2 Chronicles 36:11). The Bible says that Zedekiah “did things that the Lord said were evil. The prophet [God’s messenger] Jeremiah spoke the Lord’s message to Zedekiah. But Zedekiah was too proud [filled with pride] to listen to him” (2 Chronicles 36:12, EASY).

The Bible says that Jeremiah “spoke for the Lord” (2 Chronicles 36:12, NLV). Even so, Zedekiah refused to empty his heart of all pride. Jeremiah was only a man. He was a sinner, the same as the rest of us. But God used him as His messenger. Jeremiah announced the message God gave him.

Jeremiah is only one of God’s many messengers. For hundreds of years, God sent His messengers to warn His people (Exodus 3:15; Psalm 105:26; Malachi 3:1; Isaiah 6:8; Jeremiah 25:4; Matthew 11:13; Luke 11:49, and so on). God warned His people because He wanted to save them from sin (2 Peter 3:9). The history of Israel and Judah in Bible times shows us God’s love for His people. His love never ends. God continued to try to save His people. He promised to save them if they gave their hearts back to Him.

Sad to say, the people of Judah rejected God’s promise to save them. The king and the leaders laughed at Jeremiah’s warnings. They didn’t respect God’s message.

`The king and the leaders laughed at God’s words. For sure, we wouldn’t do the same thing today, right? Or would we?`
