---
title:  Pretending To Be Religious
date:   11/11/2026
---

Do you sometimes look at the mistakes people made in the past and say, “I would never do that!” Maybe we don’t make the exact same mistakes these people made. But let’s be honest. We often behave the same way they did, right? Jesus warned us against this behavior in Matthew 7:5. He tells us to correct our bad behavior first. Then we can tell our friends about their mistakes.

`Read Matthew 23:30–35. What is Jesus warning us against in these verses? Why is this warning so important?`

Jesus didn’t like the behavior of the Jewish leaders. Worse, they refused to change. The leaders were the same as graves filled with dead men’s bones (Matthew 23:27, 28). The Jewish leaders refused to confess their sins or to stop sinning, even when they knew they were wrong. Again and again, Jesus tried to save them.

When Jesus talked with the leaders, He often said they pretended to be religious. That’s why Jesus named them hypocrites (read Matthew 23:14, 15, 23, 25, 27). Hypocrites are people who pretend to be holy in public. But in private, they are living a lie.

For sure, the leaders’ behavior didn’t match their teachings. (Just look at what the leaders in the past did to God’s messengers.) The leaders in Jesus’ day decorated the graves of these Old Testament messengers. The leaders made their graves look beautiful. The leaders thought they were honoring God’s messengers for their faith. But the leaders didn’t follow the teachings of God’s messengers in their hearts. When the leaders made the graves beautiful, they thought they showed everyone that they would never kill God’s messengers, the same as the leaders did in the past. But at that very time what were the leaders in Jesus’ day planning to do? They were planning to kill Jesus! Jesus was not just any special messenger. He was the Savior.

`The Jewish leaders in Jesus’ day behaved the same as very religious people in public. But their hearts were selfish and filled with hate. How can we make sure that we don’t behave the same as these Jewish leaders?`
