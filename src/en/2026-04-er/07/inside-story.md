### “God Is #1”: Part 2

_by Gina Wahlen_

Naum remembers well the day he learned that there would be a Seventh-day Adventist high school and college in his own country. Naum lived in what was Yugoslavia at the time.

Naum says, “A church leader came to visit my church. The leader told us about the school that was going to be opening in Maruševec. The news made us so happy!”

The school in Maruševec opened in 1969 with 45 students.

Naum says, “This school was a miracle! I was one of its first students. The teachers studied the book Education, by Ellen G. White. Our teachers gave us the best education!”

The government didn’t accept the school at the time. So, the students needed to pass all 17 government tests on different study topics at the end of every school year.

Naum says, “The tests were hard. But at the end of the fourth year, we scored better and higher grades than the students from the State schools! Everyone said we were the best school in what used to be the country of Yugoslavia.”

When Naum finished high school, he went to the university in the city of Zagreb. There, Naum studied French and Latin. When he finished his studies, Naum had two job offers: one from the government and one from the Seventh-day Adventist high school in Maruševec.

Naum met with the government leaders who offered him the job. Naum said, “You know I am a Seventh-day Adventist. I’ll go to church every Sabbath. So, why are you offering me a job?”

The leaders’ answer surprised Naum. They said, “We are offering you a job because you are honest. We need honest workers. There aren’t many honest workers in the government.”

Naum said, “Thank you. But I accepted a job at the school in Maruševec.”

For many years, Naum taught the students at Maruševec. He taught them French and Latin. He also taught them important Bible rules.

Naum says, “The first things I wanted my students to learn about were God and His plan to save them. After that, I taught them knowledge and how to live. We are living in hard times.”

In 2017, your 13th Sabbath Offering helped build a much-­needed building at Maruševec Adventist School in Croatia. This quarter, your 13th Sabbath Offering will help build a new church in the capital city of Zagreb. This church will replace a very old, large church that earthquakes destroyed in 2020. The new church building will be a place of worship for members and a teaching center for the people in Zagreb. The building will include a classroom, a kitchen, and a large dining room. The church will serve the needs of the people in the community.
