---
title:  Spoken Messages
date:   27/10/2026
---

`The Holy Spirit helped men write the Bible. So, we can trust that the Bible is God’s Written Word. God also gave His messengers words to speak. Why can we trust that their spoken messages also came from God? Read 2 Peter 1:18-21 for the answer.`

God spoke to Adam and Eve before they sinned (Genesis 2:16, 17). God also spoke to them after they sinned (Genesis 3:9–19). When God spoke to them, they understood His words completely. People heard God speak at Sinai (Exodus 20:1–20). People also heard God speak when Jesus was baptized (Matthew 3:17) and when Jesus’ clothes became bright (Matthew 17:5). We don’t always understand what people say when they talk to us. But when God talks to us, He always talks clearly. He doesn’t trick us or lie.

The Bible says, “God said to Moses, ‘I am the Lord’ ” (Exodus 6:2, ERV). Many Bible writers tell us that they heard the voice of God. Isaiah heard God speak to him (Isaiah 8:1, 11). So did Jeremiah (Jeremiah 1:4, 9, 11) and Ezekiel (Ezekiel 3:16; Ezekiel 6:1). We must believe the words of these messengers about hearing God’s voice. If we doubt their honesty, we will doubt the Bible itself.

God spoke to His messengers with words they understood. In the same way, God’s messengers spoke His words to His people. Noah was a true messenger from God. Peter tells us that “Noah preached about the right way to live” (2 Peter 2:5, NIrV). Noah didn’t write any part of the Bible. But he preached God’s message to the people living before the Flood. Noah’s message came from God as much as the Bible did. Many other messengers weren’t Bible writers. These messengers include Nathan, Elijah, Elisha, and John the Baptist. They didn’t write God’s messages down. They spoke their messages to God’s people.

What about Jesus? The Bible says that He “wrote on the ground with His finger” (John 8:6, NKJV). That’s the only time we see the Bible talk about Jesus writing something. But He taught and preached with power (Matthew 7:29). Some people said, “ ‘We have never heard anyone say such [these types of] amazing things!’ ” (John 7:46, ERV). So, Jesus didn’t write any books. But His messages were powerful.

`God has messengers who didn’t write any part of the Bible. Why can we trust that their messages are from God?`
