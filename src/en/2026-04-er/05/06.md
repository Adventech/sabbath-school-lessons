---
title:  Actions, Symbols, And Picture Stories
date:   29/10/2026
---

The Bible is filled with symbols. Some of these symbols show Jesus’ death and His work for us in heaven (Hebrews 8:1–6).

In the book of Daniel, we read about a mighty statue. The different parts of the statue show us the kingdoms and powers that will control the earth, one after another. Later, Daniel uses animals and horns to show us these same kingdoms and powers (Daniel 7 and 8). In chapter 11, Daniel uses two kings to talk about what will happen in the future. The book of Revelation uses some of these same symbols. In Revelation 13, we read about two wild animals. These animals are symbols for two powers on earth.

In His teachings, Jesus also used symbols. He compared Himself to many things. He named Himself the Bread of Life (John 6:35, 41–58), the Light of the World (John 8:12; John 9:5), and the True Vine (John 15:1–8). In the story about the farmer (Matthew 13:1–9, 18–23) and many other stories (Matthew 13:24–52 is one example), Jesus really was talking about His kingdom and its growth.

`Why do you think God spoke to Ezekiel and, at the same time, used a special experience to help Ezekiel understand His messages (read Ezekiel 4:1–8)?`

In Ezekiel 4:1–8, God told Ezekiel to lie down on his left side for 390 days. Then Ezekiel lay on his right side for 40 days. Ezekiel’s left side was a symbol for how long God would punish Israel. Ezekiel’s right side was a symbol for how long God would punish Judah. God asked Hosea to marry Gomer, a prostitute. A prostitute is someone who sells sex for pay. Hosea’s marriage showed Israel’s broken relationship with God. The Israelites were the same as a prostitute because they ‘cheated’ on God with other gods.

The experiences of Ezekiel and Hosea surprise us. They were meant to, and not just us, but God’s people back then. At that time, much the same as today, the people refused to pay attention to God’s warnings (read 2 Chronicles 36:15–21). So, God asked Ezekiel and Hosea to do surprising things to get the people’s attention.
