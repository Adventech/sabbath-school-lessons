---
title:  The Bible And Nature
date:   25/10/2026
---

`Read Hebrews 1:1, 2. Why is God so interested in talking to us? What does He tell us with the words of Jesus and in the writings of His messengers?`

What does God want to say to us? Why does He want to communicate with us? God wants us to know why we are here. We live on an earth filled with sin. Sometimes awful things happen. God wants us to know why things are so bad. He wants us to know why we can hope in His love for us, too.

God uses nature to communicate His messages and Bible truth. Nature shows us God’s power and caring love. King David announced, “The heavens tell about the glory [power] of God. The skies announce what his hands have made” (Psalm 19:1, ERV). Paul says, “Ever since God made the world [earth and skies], he has been showing people clearly about himself. We cannot see God. But the things that he has made show us clearly what he is like [who He is]. We can understand his great [wonderful] power that continues for ever. We can know that he is the true God. So there is no reason for anyone to say, ‘We could not know about God’ ” (Romans 1:20, EASY).

For sure, God communicates with us in nature. He also uses nature to show us His love. A powerful example of God’s amazing love for us is the way that He made our bodies to heal. “God’s healing power is in all of nature. What if a tree is cut? Or what if a person breaks a bone? Right away, nature starts to repair the hurt. God made us and other living things ready to heal our own cuts and injuries. The moment a part is injured, the things that God made start to fix their own broken parts.”—Ellen G. White, Education, page 113, adapted.

Yes, nature shows us God’s power and love. But nature also can show us evil at work (read Genesis 3:14–19; Matthew 13:24–30). How can something good and beautiful also show us evil at work? God gave us the Bible and sent us Jesus to help us understand the answer. Jesus is the Living Word of God. The Bible is the Written Word of God. Both Jesus and the Bible tell us about God (John 5:39).

The Bible is a record of God’s messages to us, too. The Bible tells us why we are here and God’s answer to the sin problem. We can trust the Bible because its verses are God’s words to us (read 2 Timothy 3:16; 2 Peter 1:19–21).
