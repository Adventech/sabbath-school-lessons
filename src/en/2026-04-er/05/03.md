---
title:  How God Sends Us His Messages
date:   26/10/2026
---

We believe that the Bible is the Word of God. But what makes the Bible different from other books?

`Read 2 Timothy 3:15–17 and James 1:17. What impor­tant message do these verses give us?`

2 Timothy 3:16 helps us to understand that the whole Bible comes from God. (Also read 2 Peter 1:20, 21.)

But how did God make the Bible? The Holy Spirit helped God’s messengers write the Bible. How did He do that? The Holy Spirit communicated God’s messages to these men. So, the Holy Spirit helped them to accept and understand God’s messages.

God sent His messages in dreams (Numbers 12:6; Isaiah 6:1, 8). God also communicated His messages to people who saw and heard God’s truth (1 John 1:1–3). The Holy Spirit helped men to research history (Luke 1:1–3) and to choose the correct information to include in their messages. The Holy Spirit also communicated ideas and thoughts to people’s minds. The messengers used their own words to communicate God’s messages. But they were not allowed to change God’s thoughts in any way.

How did God’s messengers share His messages with people? The messengers often told them to the people (2 Peter 1:19–21). Or the messengers wrote them down for people to read (John 20:30, 31). Sometimes the messengers did special things to show the people what God wanted them to do (Ezekiel 4:1–8).

`We may have many questions about how God wrote the Bible or how He communicated His words to His messengers. Why is it so important to trust that the Bible is from God, even if we don’t understand fully how it was written?`
