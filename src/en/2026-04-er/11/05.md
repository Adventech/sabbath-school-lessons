> <callout></callout>
> “The Lord sent his servants many times to warn his people. He wanted to be kind to them. He wanted to keep his temple safe” (2 Chronicles 36:15, EASY).

God sent many messengers to His people after Elijah. The messengers warned, taught, and brought hope to God’s people. But the kingdoms of Israel and Judah sinned against God. God compared these kingdoms to Sodom and Gomorrah (Isaiah 1:9, 10). God’s people were selfish, filled with pride, and worshiped false gods. They hurt their workers, widows, orphans, and the strangers who asked them for help. (Read Isaiah 58 to learn what God thought about Israel’s sins.) Finally, God punished Israel. The Assyrians carried the Israelites away from their land. Later, the Babylonians carried the people of Judah to Babylon (2 Kings 24, 25).

God punished His people but gave them encouragement, too. He sent them messengers, including Ezekiel and Daniel. These men gave God’s people hope for the future.

`Read Isaiah 59:1–4 and Nehemiah 9:30. What helpful advice in these verses does God give to His people? How is this advice helpful to us today?`

When the kingdoms of Judah and Israel sinned, God didn’t judge them right away. He sent His messengers to warn them. The messengers told the people to confess their sins and come back to God. But the people and their leaders refused to obey. God kept trying to save them. Again and again, He sent His messengers to them with warnings.

Jesus said these words about the Jewish leaders: “ ‘Jerusalem, Jerusalem! You kill the prophets [God’s messengers] and kill with stones those men God sent to you. Many times I wanted to help your people! . . .But you did not let me’ ” (Matthew 23:37, ICB).

God never left His people without hope. Even when they were carried far away from home, God promised: “A Redeemer [Savior] will come to Zion. He will come to save Jacob’s people who turn away from [stop doing] their sins” (Isaiah 59:20, EASY).

`Where would you be in life if God were not so patient and loving with you?`
