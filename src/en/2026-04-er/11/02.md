---
title:  A Warning About Coming Disaster
date:   06/12/2026
---

God wants to save us. Even after Adam and Eve sinned, God wanted to heal His relationship with them. After the Fall, the people on the earth followed God for a time (Genesis 4:26). But as time passed, very few people followed God. God’s loyal followers praised Him for His love. They also warned the people around them about God’s punishment for sin.

By Noah’s time, the people on the earth were very evil. So, God chose to destroy them (Genesis 6:5–7). “Noah preached about the right way to live” (2 Peter 2:5, NIrV). Noah warned everyone who would pay attention to him about the coming Flood. God told Noah to build a lifeboat (the ark) to save everyone who believed the warnings.

`Read Hebrews 11:7; 2 Peter 2:5; and Genesis 6:9. In what ways did Noah preach “about the right way to live” (2 Peter 2:5, NIrV)?`

The Flood was an important happening in human history. The Flood shows us what happens when evil controls the human heart. The Flood also shows us God’s love and forgiveness. God punished sinners with the Flood. He saved everyone who accepted His invitation to enter the ark.

“The people on the earth were very evil. Methuselah, Noah, and many other people believed in God. They stood against the evil of their time. 120 years before the Flood, the Lord sent a holy angel to Noah. The angel told Noah to build a boat. The angel told Noah to preach, too. Noah must warn everyone that God would destroy evil people with a Flood. The people who believed the message must get ready for what was coming. They must stop sinning. They must change their lives for the better. They must ask God for forgiveness. Then God would save them.”—Ellen G. White, Patriarchs and Prophets, page 92, adapted.

God told Noah about the Flood and saved him and his family. God also made a promise with Noah and all living things on the earth. God promised never to destroy the earth again with water (Genesis 9:8–17). Every rainbow helps us remember this promise. The rainbow also gives us hope that God will save us.

`Next time you see a rainbow, think about the promise God gave to Noah. Why should this promise give you hope?`
