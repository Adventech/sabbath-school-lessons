---
title:  A Mother In Israel
date:   07/12/2026
---

After God led the Israelites out of slavery in Egypt, He gave them the land of Canaan. God promised to bless His people very much. But they must promise to obey His law. God warned the Israelites about breaking their promise. If they did, they would suffer and be destroyed (Deuteronomy 27–30). After Joshua died, the Israelites stopped following God. Soon the people from nearby countries started to fight against the Israelites and hurt them. The Israelites cried to God for help. God sent judges to His people to save them from their enemies (Judges 2:16). God also sent His messengers to encourage the Israelites to be loyal to God.

`Read Judges 4:4–16 and Judges 5:1–5. What did Deborah do for Israel?`

Deborah had an important part in Israel’s history. Deborah shared God’s message with Israel and Barak. Barak was the leader of Israel’s army. Deborah gave Barak the signal to start the battle: “Then Deborah said to Barak, ‘Get ready! This [today] is the day that the Lord will put Sisera under your power [control]! The Lord will lead you into battle!’ ” (Judges 4:14, EASY).

Deborah was an important leader in Israel. Her leader­ship and faith in God’s promise to fight for Israel gave the people hope and the courage to fight. Barak was a skilled war commander. But his skill wasn’t the thing that helped Israel win. At first, he was afraid to lead Israel’s army. But then Deborah promised to go with him.

The story of Deborah and Barak teaches us an impor­tant lesson about success. Together, Deborah and Barak led Israel into battle. After they won, Deborah and Barak ­invited everyone to join them in praising God. That’s what real leaders do when they win. They give God all the praise and honor for their success.

Deborah’s work as God’s messenger shows His love for us in times of suffering. When God gave Deborah the message for Barak, she didn’t question how they would win. She didn’t argue that the Israelites were too weak to win. Deborah believed God’s promise and encouraged Barak to trust in Him, too.

`How can we learn to trust God when things don’t always look encouraging or fill us with hope?`
