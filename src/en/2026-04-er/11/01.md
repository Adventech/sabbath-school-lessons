### Read for This Week’s Lesson
Hebrews 11:7; Judges 4:4–16; Judges 2:16–19; Isaiah 59:1–4; Revelation 1:1–3.

> <p>Memory Verse</p>
> “Before the Lord God decides to send trouble, he first tells his prophets [messengers] about it” (Amos 3:7, EASY).

Since Adam and Eve sinned, our hearts want to do evil. Without Jesus’ help, we would choose sin and pleasure over everlasting life. God knows this sad truth about us. But He loves us anyway. He works to save us from ourselves.

That’s why the Lord sends His messengers to help His people at important times in history. Sad to say, God’s people rejected His offer of help again and again. So, God sent them warnings that He would punish them.

God always communicates His plans to His messengers before He acts or does anything (Amos 3:7). If people confess their sins, God is ready to forgive.

God continues to communicate with us in the same way today. Just as God had messages for His people in the past, He has messages for us today.

In this week’s lesson, we will look at important times in Bible history when God used His messengers to share His plans with His church and with other people worldwide. We also will talk about the work of Ellen G. White. We will look at how she served God at important times in the growth of the Seventh-day Adventist Church.
