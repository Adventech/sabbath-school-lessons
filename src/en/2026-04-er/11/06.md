---
title:  Messengers For The Waiting Time
date:   10/12/2026
---

`What task did God give to John? Read Revelation 1:1–3 for the answer.`

The first Christians hoped to see Jesus come back in their lifetime. John saw Jesus go up in the clouds. The angel explained that Jesus would come back the same way (Acts 1:10, 11). Jesus didn’t come back right away. Then many Christians died. Now the church faced an important question. Yes, they believed Jesus would come back. But when?

While John was on Patmos Island, God talked with him about the Second Coming. John wrote, “Look, Jesus is coming with the clouds! Everyone will see him, even those [people] who stabbed him. And all peoples of [on] the earth will cry loudly because of him. Yes, this [promise] will happen! Amen” (Revelation 1:7, ICB). Jesus showed John the things that would happen in the end-times. God asked John to encourage and warn the seven churches in Asia Minor, too (Revelation 2, 3). John also warned that God would punish evil (Revelation 14:6–12). John wrote about the end of the earth (Revelation 20), the Second Coming (Revelation 14:14–16), and the new earth (Revelation 21, 22).

John wrote, “I am John. I am a believer [follower of Jesus], as you are. I am like [the same as] your brother and your friend. Because we belong to Jesus’ kingdom, we are having trouble and pain. So we all need to be strong and patient, as Jesus helps us. The Roman leaders punished me because I taught God’s message. I told people the true message about Jesus. That is why they sent me to Patmos island” (Revelation 1:9, EASY). For sure, John understood what it meant to suffer for his faith.

Jesus told John many times to write down what he saw. Jesus told John to share these things with the seven churches (Revelation 22:16). John is much the same as John the Baptist, who announced Jesus’ first coming (John 1:6–8). Jesus wants us to tell other people about Him, too. We also must keep God’s Words about Himself in our hearts. God gave these words to His messengers to give to us (Revelation 19:10). Maybe we aren’t messengers. But we may work for God while we wait.

`Think about all the Bible truth God gave us. What does God ask us to do with all this light?`
