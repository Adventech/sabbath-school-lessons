`Read Judges 2:16–19. What is happening in these verses? What do the Israelites keep doing again and again?`

Soon the time of the judges ended. Then Israel wanted a king (1 Samuel 8). God’s people needed His messengers more than ever at this time. The messengers gave advice to the king, the leaders, and the people.

During King Ahab’s time, Israel stopped following God and started worshiping the Baals (1 King 18:18). So, God sent Elijah with a special message: there would be no rain. But Ahab didn’t stop worshiping false gods. He hunted for Elijah to kill him. But God kept Elijah safe (1 Kings 18:10–12). During the third year of the food shortage, Elijah went to talk to the king. Ahab blamed him because there was no rain (1 Kings 18:17).

`Read Elijah’s announcement to Israel. How did Israel answer? What happened? Read 1 Kings 18:21, 38, 39 for the answers.`

Fire came down from heaven. Elijah’s powerful message touched the hearts of the people. Both the fire and the message helped the people to come back to God. Today, we live in communities that don’t honor God as their Savior. The people in these communities are the same as the Israelites. They don’t want to give their lives fully to the Lord. Many of us often want to make ourselves happy and not God.

God warns us that living to make ourselves happy will cause pain and suffering to us and other people. This selfish wish to live for ourselves will end in death. So, we must obey God and follow His voice. Then God will keep this promise to us in Isaiah 58:11:

“The Lord will always lead you.\
He will satisfy your needs in dry lands.\
He will give strength to your bones.\
You will be like [the same as] a garden that has much water.\
You will be like [the same as] a spring that never runs dry” (ICB).

`As sinners, we have a natural wish to do things that are wrong and evil. What choices do we need to make to help us not give in to the evil in our hearts?`
