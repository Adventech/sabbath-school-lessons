God always sent His messengers to communicate with His people at important times in history. We also should expect that God will talk with His people in the end-times, too.

When Jesus didn’t come back on October 22, 1844, the Millerites were disappointed. God chose Ellen Harmon (White) to be His messenger. God gave her a message for the disappointed group of Millerites. Ellen White’s first vision gave them hope.

Soon the Adventists started printing books. In the 1860s, the Adventists grew to several thousand members. Now they needed to become a worldwide church so that they could announce the three angels’ messages with power. Some people were against the idea of becoming a worldwide church. So, God used Ellen White to encourage the Adventists to start the Seventh-day Adventist Church. On May 21, 1863, the Seventh-day Adventist Church was born.

That same year, God used the gift of special messages to encourage Seventh-day Adventists to be healthy. Most church members weren’t healthy at all. They also didn’t live healthy lives. So, God gave Ellen White two special messages. The first message was in 1863. The second message was in 1865. These messages changed the lives of many church members. Then Seventh-day Adventists started to support good health and healthful living.

From the 1870s to 1890s, Ellen White developed an education plan for the Seventh-day Adventist Church. She wrote: “Real education means more than studying a topic or just getting ready for this life now. Real education is about the whole person and what he can become. Real education develops the body, mind, and spirit.”—Education, page 13, adapted.

In the 1880s, Ellen White helped the church to understand better God’s plan to save sinners. She explained that God’s love and forgiveness were important to this plan. Ellen White helped the church to better understand the connection between God’s love and His law. From 1901 to 1903, Ellen White suggested changes that helped the church to do God’s work better.

In short, there would be no Seventh-day Adventist Church as we know it today without Ellen White. She was God’s messenger at an important time in history.
