### Demons and Death: Part 1

_by Mission Editor_

Leena and her friends, Anneli and Timo, were studying the Bible in her apartment. Leena lived in a Finnish town a few hundred miles north of the capital, Helsinki.

Leena said, “I have a feeling that something bad is about to happen. Let’s pray.”

The three university students knelt in prayer. Just then, a tall dark figure ran into the living room. The figure came near Anneli and tried to grab her.

Timo and Leena started to pray harder.

Then a bright figure entered the room. The bright figure chased out the dark figure. The dark figure stood at the door and tried to reenter. But the bright figure blocked every move that the dark figure made. After about 10 minutes, the dark figure gave up and left.

After that, the students talked about what happened. Leena said she saw a fight between two figures. One was bright and filled with light. The other figure was dark. Timo hadn’t seen figures. He saw only what looked the same as light and dark shadows that ran past him across the floor. Anneli didn’t want to talk about what she saw. Later on, the students learned that the strange attack in Leena’s apartment happened at the same time that someone killed himself in a nearby home.

Leena said, “That’s why I had the bad feeling.”

Then Anneli told her friends something they didn’t know about her. She did witchcraft in the past. Evil spirits still attacked her. But Anneli said that God was way more powerful than the evil spirits. After the attack, Anneli went home and lay down on her bed. The bright figure came into her room, sat down on the end of her bed, and stayed with her until morning.

The demon attacks stopped after Anneli was baptized into the Seventh-day Adventist Church. That was the first experience Timo had up close with the war between Jesus and Satan. But that was not Timo’s last. Timo was a deep sleeper. He hardly ever woke up at night after he fell asleep. But soon after the attack in the apartment, Timo woke up when he felt someone was staring at him in the dark. Then he heard a voice.

The voice said, “Don’t get baptized.”

Timo was a computer engineering student. He was about to be baptized into the Seventh-day Adventist Church. Timo looked into the dark room. He couldn’t see anything. But he could tell someone was there. Timo prayed, and the figure left.

The next day, Leena told Timo that someone killed herself near his home last night.

Timo asked, “Do you know what time that happened?”

Leena did. The person died the exact time Timo woke up.
