---
title:  Hiding From God
date:   28/09/2026
---

God is love (1 John 4:8). Because God is love, He gives us free choice. He made us free to love Him and other people. Without free choice, we have no brains, the same as robots.

We see God’s gift of free choice in His command not to eat the forbidden fruit (read Genesis 2:15–17). Why warn Adam and Eve not to eat the fruit if they can’t choose?

“God made our first parents innocent and holy. God also made them free to choose between good and evil. Adam and Eve respected God’s wisdom. They knew that God was love and that His law was fair. God made them free to obey Him. . . . But what if God didn’t make Adam and Eve free? Then they are forced to obey Him.”—Ellen G. White, Patriarchs and Prophets, pages 48, 49, adapted.

`Why did Adam and Eve hide from God after they sinned? Read Genesis 3:1–8 for the answer. Compare with Jeremiah 23:24 and Hebrews 4:13.`

Genesis 3 tells an awful story: the sin of Adam and Eve. After Eve’s talk with the snake, she ate the forbidden fruit. Satan used powerful words and feelings in a sneaky way to trick Eve. Then Eve convinced Adam to join her in sinning against God. Eve saw “that the tree was beautiful and the fruit looked so good to eat. She also liked the idea that it [eating the fruit] would make her wise” (Genesis 3:6, ERV). Satan promised Eve, “ ‘When you eat the fruit from this tree, you will understand things. You will become like [the same as] God himself. You will know about good things and evil things’ ” (Genesis 3:5, EASY).

Satan’s sermon was filled with lies. He lied to Adam and Eve about what would happen if they chose to sin. After they sinned, they understood right away that they broke God’s law. They saw that they weren’t wearing any clothes. Before they sinned, God covered them with clothing made from light (Psalm 104:1, 2). Adam and Eve lost this clothing when they sinned. No wonder they hid from God! (Of course, we can’t really hide from God, right?!) Why did Adam and Eve try to hide? They hid because they felt guilty. When we feel guilty, we want to escape from God.

`Think of a time when you sinned against God. Did you try to hide, too? What made you want to hide? Did you feel shame or guilt? What did your experience teach you?`
