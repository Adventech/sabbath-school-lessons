---
title:  God’s Special Messengers
date:   01/10/2026
---

`How did God talk with people after sin separated them from Him? Read Hebrews 1:1, 2; Romans 1:19, 20; Numbers 12:6; 2 Timothy 3:16, 17; John 14:7–9; and John 10:27–30 for the answer.`

As we already saw, people no longer communicated with God face-to-face after sin. But God continued to communicate with sinners in other ways. One of those ways is with nature. Nature helps us to understand God’s wonderful acts. Nature shows us the things we lost because of sin. As we saw yesterday, the death of every living thing is the most powerful example of what sin cost us.

Another way that God talks to us is with His messengers. God chooses men and women to be His messengers and to talk for Him to His people. Another name for messenger is prophet. Messengers share God’s words with His people. Some examples of messengers are Noah, Abraham, Moses, and Daniel. God’s messengers help us understand that He remembers us. God continues to be with us, too. Some messengers wrote down the messages God gave them. Many of these messages are now part of the Bible. Other messengers didn’t write any books of the Bible. But many things that these messengers said were included in the Bible (2 Timothy 3:16, 17). The Bible also shows us God. We see God’s love in the Bible. The Bible gives us advice about how to live. The Bible helps us understand who we are, where we came from, and where we are going. In the Bible, we learn about God’s plan for our lives.

For sure, nature and the Bible are powerful ways that God communicates with us. But the most powerful way that God communicates with us is in Jesus. Just as God searched for Adam and Eve in the Garden of Eden, so Jesus came looking for sinners (Luke 19:10). Jesus announced: “ ‘Anyone who has seen me has seen the Father too’ ” (John 14:9, ERV). When Jesus died on the cross, He kept the promise God made to Adam and Eve in Eden. Jesus died as our Substitute so that He could give us everlasting life.

After Jesus went to heaven, God poured His Holy Spirit on the church. The Spirit continues to lead us into all truth. The Spirit helps us understand what will happen in the future (John 16:13).
