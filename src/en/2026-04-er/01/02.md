---
title:  Communication In Eden
date:   27/09/2026
---

Genesis 1 and 2 tell us that God made the skies and the earth. God made the earth very beautiful for Adam and Eve. Humans were different from everything else God made. That’s because God “made them to be like [much the same as] himself” (Genesis 1:27, EASY). The Bible doesn’t say that about anything else God made. Because we are like God in many ways, He made us to be happy. He made us to love and be loved, too. He made us to communicate with Him.

Also, we read in Genesis 2:18–23 that God made Eve for Adam. Why? Because God said that man should not “ ‘live alone’ ” (Genesis 2:18, EASY). From the start, God made us to live with other people. Today, scientists teach that people need relationships. Relationships help us grow. They keep us healthy. On the other hand, when we feel lonely, we may not think that our lives are really worthwhile.

Adam understood this feeling. That’s why he made this announcement after God makes Eve for him: “ ‘Finally, this [person] is someone who is right for me. She has bones that are taken from my bones. Her body comes from my body. I will call [name] her “woman” because God used me, a man, to make her’ ” (Genesis 2:23, EASY). God made marriage and families in the Garden of Eden. The Bible teaches us that marriage is a relationship between a male and a female. God gave us marriage as a gift. In this relationship, we learn how to love and communicate. Satan wants to destroy God’s plan for marriage and the family!

`How did God communicate with humans in the Garden of Eden? Read Genesis 1:27, 28; Genesis 2:15–17; and Genesis 3:9 for the answer.`

Adam and Eve communicated with God face-to-face. They listened to His teachings. God made Adam and Eve the managers of the Garden of Eden. As managers, Adam and Eve learned how to care for the plants and animals. Each day they had special time with God. The Sabbath day was a very special time between God and His children, too (Genesis 2:2, 3; Exodus 20:8–11). On that day, God showed our first parents His love and care for them in a special way.
