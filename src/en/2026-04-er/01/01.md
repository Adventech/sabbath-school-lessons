### Read for This Week’s Lesson
Genesis 1:27, 28; Genesis 3:1–8; Luke 15:11–32; Genesis 3:14–24; Hebrews 1:1, 2.

> <p>Memory Verse</p>
> “Many years ago, God spoke to our ancestors by his prophets [messengers]. He spoke many times and in many different ways. But in these last days, he has spoken to us in his Son. In the beginning, God worked through [with] his Son to make the whole universe [everything that God made on earth, in heaven, and in outer space]. He [God] decided that everything should belong to his Son” (Hebrews 1:1, 2, EASY).

We use computers and other scientific inventions to communicate with friends in faraway places. Of course, that’s wonderful. But don’t you like meeting in person much better? For sure, something special happens when we get together, hug, and talk in person.

As humans, we were made for a face-to-face relationship with Jesus. Sin destroyed that face-to-face connection long ago. Because God loves us so much, He made a way to communicate with us again. He chose messengers to share His words with us. Another name for messenger is prophet. Many of these messengers wrote parts of the Bible.

God wants to make us new. God will finish this work when He makes “a new sky and a new earth. . . . That will be a home for everything that is good and right” (2 Peter 3:13, EASY).

Until that day, God continues to talk to us. He uses His messengers to do just that.
