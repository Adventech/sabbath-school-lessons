---
title:  Forced To Leave The Garden
date:   30/09/2026
---

Adam and Eve’s sin separated them from God. God forced them to leave the Garden of Eden. They no longer could talk to God face-to-face. Isaiah talks about how sin separates us from God: “But your sins have separated you from your God. They have caused him to turn his face away from you. So he won’t listen to you” (Isaiah 59:2, NIrV).

Of course, Adam and Eve were God’s children, even after they sinned. But sin caused their separation from God. So, Adam and Eve needed to leave Eden. Nothing was the same after they ate the fruit.

`Sin changed the human family in many ways (read Genesis 3:14–24). How did sin change our communication with God? (Also read Exodus 19:16–22 for the answer.)`

Sin completely changed human life in ways we can’t even start to imagine in our minds. Because of sin, the land was under a curse. The land was filled with thorns and weeds. Because of sin, humans felt pain. Now they must sweat and work hard to grow their food or earn a living. In the end, we all die and became dirt. Death is the most powerful example of sin. Because of sin, we lost our face-to-face communication with God, too.

But God promised to solve the sin problem. Yes, our first parents, Adam and Eve, no longer could see God or talk to Him face-to-face. But they left Eden with hope for the future. God explained to them His plan to save the human family from sin.

Genesis 3:15 is the first Bible promise that Jesus saves us from sin. This first promise filled Adam and Eve (and us) with hope. God made clothes for Adam and Eve before they left Eden. The clothes were a symbol of what Jesus would do for sinners. Jesus would cover them with His holy life, the same as clothes. Because of Jesus, we can hope to communicate with God face-to-face again.

So, right there in Eden after Adam and Eve sinned, God gave them hope. God promised that He would solve the sin problem. As soon as humans sinned, God promised a Savior.

`How do you feel after doing something bad? Do you feel guilty? Do you feel shame? Do you hate yourself? How does sin cause you to suffer? How does your faith in God and the Good News help you not to give up?`
