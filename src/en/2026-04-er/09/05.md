---
title:  How We Live Tells People Who We Are
date:   25/11/2026
---

> <callout></callout>
> “ ‘In the same way, look at the way people live. Then you will know what they are really like [who they really are]’ ” (Matthew 7:20, EASY).

Jesus said the words above in His sermon on the mountain (Matthew 5–7). In this sermon Jesus talked about the basic rules and teachings for the Christian church. Part of the work for the church included the work that God’s special messengers would do. That’s why Jesus warned against false messengers in the church.

We must have enough time to know who someone really is. When we see how a messenger lives his life, we can see if he is really from God or not. Is he bringing people close to Jesus? Or is he leading people away from Him?

`Read Jonah 1:1–3; Acts 15:36–39; and Galatians 2:11–14. What do these verses teach us about messengers?`

Just because someone is a messenger doesn’t mean he is without sin. Just look at Jonah, Paul, or even Peter. They made mistakes. Only God is perfect. But His messengers never are. Messengers at times behave badly. They are sometimes weak in faith. But for the most part, their lives must show that they are walking with God.

A real messenger lives Bible truth. He builds up God’s kingdom (Matthew 7:16–20). What if someone says he is God’s messenger, but his life doesn’t match his message? Then we should not trust this person.

Many messengers had hard lives. Moses led the children of Israel through the desert (Deuteronomy 34:10–12). Haggai helped the people in Judah to start building the second temple again after 19 years (Haggai 1:12). Isaiah served four kings during a difficult time (Isaiah 39:5, 6; Isaiah 53:4–6). We also must remember Daniel (Daniel 9:15) and Jeremiah (Jeremiah 9:1). God’s messengers always showed love for God and His people, even when the people were mean to them. Their love was proof that God was with them.

`How often do people disappoint you with their behavior? What lesson can you learn from that experience?`
