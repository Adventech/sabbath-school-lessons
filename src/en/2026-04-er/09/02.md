title:  Miracles And Signs
date:   22/11/2026
---

When God’s people left Egypt, the Lord talked to His people about messengers. The Lord said, “ ‘You will have prophets [messengers]. I, the Lord, will let them learn about me through [in] visions. I will speak to them in dreams’ ” (Numbers 12:6, ERV).

God often uses miracles and special signs to show that someone is His messenger. Messengers get their ­messages straight from God. Often God sends them His messages in visions or dreams (Daniel 8:1; Isaiah 1:1; Acts 9:10; and so on). God may send miracles with these dreams as proof that they come from Him.

`Read Daniel 10:7–19. Make a list of some of the miracles that happened when God appeared to Daniel?`

Daniel lost his strength when God appeared to him. Then he needed to depend on God during the rest of the experience. People were with Daniel who didn’t see God appear or hear the message that God gave Daniel. But the experience with God was so powerful that they were afraid. So, they ran away and hid. God uses miracles to show people that He chose someone to be His messenger. The miracles help bring God’s messages to people’s attention.

`What does Deuteronomy 13:1–5 teach us about the gift of special messages?`

Miracles, signs, and wonders show that someone is at work who is more powerful than any human. That doesn’t mean that the miracles are always from God. Satan also can work miracles. So, miracles alone are not proof that someone is a messenger from God. Also, more than one miracle or sign is needed. Messengers must be tested, too. Miracles are not a test of the real gift of special messages. A person must pass all tests in the Bible before we can accept him as a real messenger from God. Sad to say, history is filled with many false messengers (read Matthew 7:15).
