Some Seventh-day Adventists accept Ellen G. White’s gift. Other Seventh-day Adventists question it. God’s people often felt the same way about His messengers in Bible times. God gave Ellen White her first vision in December 1844. At that time, some people doubted if she was a real messenger from God. Their questions helped Adventists to come up with reasons for their support of Ellen White’s special gift.

First, the Adventists showed from the Bible that the gift of special messages would be in the church until the end-times. The Bible supports this idea. (Read Joel 2 and Revelation 12:17.) These and other verses promise that the gift of special messages will be a sign of God’s end-time people. (Read Gerhard Pfandl, “The Gift of Prophecy and God’s Remnant Church,” The Gift of Prophecy, chapter 4.)

Second, the first Adventists saw that Ellen White’s gift changed people’s lives for the better. Her ideas helped them to work together in peace and love during the Sabbath meetings in the late 1840s. At this time, many of the Adventists didn’t agree on Bible teachings. Ellen White’s gift helped them to agree on Bible truth. Today, her writings continue to help the Seventh-day Adventist community of faith to work together in peace and love.

Third, God did many miracles when He sent His ­messages to Ellen White. During these public experiences, Ellen White shared information about things she had no way of knowing anything about. She told people what would happen in the future. During more than one experience, she showed she had strength that was more than human.

Today, Seventh-day Adventists continue to use these reasons to support their belief in Ellen White’s gift. A powerful proof of Ellen White’s gift is our personal experience with her writings. As a writer of the Psalms said, “Give the Lord a chance to show you how good he is” (Psalm 34:8, ERV). We must prayerfully read Ellen White’s writings. Then we can test her messages to see if she really is God’s messenger.

Start with her small but most popular book, Steps to Christ. Then read the Conflict of the Ages books. This set of books shows the war between good and evil, from the books of Genesis to Revelation.
