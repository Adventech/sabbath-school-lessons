### Finally, I Became a Bible Worker!

_by Grace Nkeshimana_

When I was 16, I wanted to volunteer as a Bible ­worker. I was excited at the idea of serving overseas. But I was too young at the time. So, I needed to wait for a few years. I had fun looking at the volunteer jobs on the Adventist Volunteers Services (AVS) website anyway. I used to imagine myself teaching overseas as a Bible worker.

When I started university, I forgot all about being a Bible worker. I thought about what I would do when I finished school. Being a Bible worker wasn’t on my list of things to do any longer. But in my final year of school, I took a break. I went as a Bible worker to India for a month. I had a wonderful experience. But I wasn’t convinced that being a full-time Bible worker was part of God’s plan for my life.

When I was 22, I started to look for work. The perfect job for me would give me enough free time to do Bible work. I wanted to serve God in my community. As I thought about this idea more, I knew I really wanted to be a Bible worker. So, I started looking for a teaching job on the AVS website. I got accepted to be a teacher at Malang Adventist Academy.

I left Manchester, England, for Indonesia just one day after my 23rd birthday. No question, this teaching job is the best ‘gift’ I ever gave myself!

My time in Indonesia was hard but in the best of ways. Being at Malang Adventist Academy was the best year of my life.

The school welcomes students from all faiths and churches. The school is really making the lives of their students better. One of my students was a Sunday keeper. He is now a baptized member of the Seventh-day Adventist Church because of his experience at school. I was happy when another student signed up for a Pathfinder camping trip. This student was Muslim. He knew that we would worship Jesus during our trip. He came to our meetings anyway and enjoyed them all! I enjoyed helping my students grow strong in faith. I shared my story with them about Jesus. I led Bible meetings. I encouraged my students to pray.

During my time at Malang, I learned so much about myself and God. I am grateful for my fellow teachers and the church community. Everyone was loving and caring from the start. Malang always will feel the same as a second home to me.

Are you, too, interested in Bible work? If yes, please check out the volunteer jobs on AVS. The class “Preparation [getting ready] for Mission [Bible work]” is an excellent class to take. This class will help you get ready to be a Bible worker.
