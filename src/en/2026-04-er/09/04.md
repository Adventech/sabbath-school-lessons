---
title:  When Jesus Was Born As A Baby
date:   24/11/2026
---

What if someone says he is a messenger but doesn’t believe in Jesus? Then this person is a false messenger. That includes messengers who are part of other religions. As John tells us, “How do you know whether a person has God’s Spirit in him? He must agree that Jesus is God’s Messiah [Savior] who was born as a human baby. If someone says that, then he has God’s Spirit. But if someone does not agree to say that about Jesus, he does not have God’s Spirit” (1 John 4:2, 3, EASY).

When John wrote these verses, he was talking about false teachers. These teachers said Jesus wasn’t human. He only looked human. Yes, John is saying a messenger should agree that Jesus was a real person who lived on earth. (Most Christians agree.) But John’s test does more than that. John’s test helps solve a very real problem. This test is as important for our time as it was for John’s.

`What do the following verses tell us about Jesus? John 3:16; 2 Corinthians 5:21; John 11:25; Hebrews 4:14, 15; John 14:1–3.`

Real messengers must believe that Jesus was God. They must also believe that He was a real human being. They must believe that Jesus came to this earth and lived a life without sin. He died for our sins on the cross. He woke up from the dead. He now works for us in heaven. Real messengers also must believe that Jesus is coming again. At the same time, real messengers must do more than agree that Jesus lived on earth. Jesus must be the most impor­tant part of their teachings. A real messenger will help people to have a saving relationship with Jesus.

The Bible helps us to see the sin in our hearts and get rid of it. Then Jesus can live in us. God’s gift of special messages always brings us close to Him.

A real messenger won’t try to get a group of people to follow him and his teachings. A real messenger won’t brag about himself (Acts 4:12). God’s true messengers will be ready to serve Him. They won’t do anything to hurt the people that God sent them to help.

`Jesus is fully God and fully human. He died for our sins. Why is this belief so important to our faith? What do we lose if we throw out this teaching?`
