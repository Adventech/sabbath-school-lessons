---
title:  When The Message Happens
date:   26/11/2026
---

People often think that a messenger is someone who tells about what will happen in the future. For sure, God’s messengers do that. But that’s only one part of a messenger’s work. A messenger’s work includes so much more. Messengers help people to come back to the Bible. Messengers give advice, warnings, and encouragement.

`Messengers help people know and get ready for the future. Why else do messengers tell us what will happen in the future? Read Jeremiah 28:9, 15–17 for the answer.`

We can test a messenger with this simple test: Do the things happen that he says will happen? When a messenger says that something will happen, then it should happen. As the Lord told Moses: “ ‘A prophet may say that he speaks with my authority [in My name], and he may say what will happen. But if it does not happen, you will know that his message has not come from me. He has not spoken my message. That prophet has spoken his own ideas. So do not be afraid of him!’ ” (Deuteronomy 18:22, EASY). This test can be hard to do because some messages may not happen if the people do what God asks.

We see this important rule in the story of Jonah. Jonah warned the people of Ninevah, “ ‘After 40 more days, God will destroy Nineveh’ ” (Jonah 3:4, EASY). But Ninevah was not destroyed. Why?

`Read Jeremiah 18:7–10. What do these verses teach us about special messages?`

What happens often depends on the choice that God’s people make when they hear His warnings. If the people change their behavior, the punishment may not happen. The people of Nineveh obeyed Jonah’s warning. They changed their lives. So, God did not destroy them (Jonah 3:10). Later, the future children of these Ninevites forgot about Jonah’s warning. Nineveh again became an evil city. So, God destroyed it. God knows the future. But we don’t. Our future happiness depends on the choices we make now.

`Why is it important to understand that God’s punishments depend on what we do when we hear His warnings? How can this rule help us better understand Bible truth?`
