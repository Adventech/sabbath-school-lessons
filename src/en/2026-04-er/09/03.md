---
title:  Agreement With The Bible
date:   23/11/2026
---

People may lie. But God does not lie (read Numbers 23:19). The messages God gives His messengers also will show that God always tells the truth. God’s truth will be seen in the messages He gives.

`Read Isaiah 8:20. What important rule is God giving us in this verse?`

The Bible is always the final test for anyone who says he is a messenger. How do we test a messenger? We must compare what he says with what the Bible says. A real messenger from God will never go against Bible truth. That is why we must know the Bible if we want to follow the Lord. Then false teachers and false messengers cannot trick us.

In the Old and New Testaments, many messengers honored the writings of messengers who lived before they did. These new messengers give us more light about the writings from the past. But the new messengers don’t disagree with the basic rules or ideas that God already gave in the Bible.

`What do 2 Peter 1:19–21; Romans 15:4; and James 1:17 teach us about God and the messages He gives us in the Bible?`

The Old Testament helps us understand God’s plan to save sinners. The first special message that God gave us shows us this Bible truth (Genesis 3:15). The Old Testament offerings also show us God’s plan to save us. Later, God gave His messengers more Bible truth about how Jesus was God’s offering for sin. God gave His messengers more information about how Jesus would come to this earth as a baby. Jesus would live as a man. He would die for sinners. The Bible shows us that God gives His people more truth when they are ready to understand it. Their understanding of Bible truth grows with every age. A later group of God’s people often will have more understanding about God’s truth than His people who lived in the past.

But we must remember one important rule. We cannot say this rule enough: new Bible truths will **always** agree with the truth that God already gave to us.
