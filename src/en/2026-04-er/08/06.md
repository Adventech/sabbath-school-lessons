title:  Explaining The Bible
date:   19/11/2026
---

When we study the Bible, we should pray for two things: (1) personal spiritual growth and (2) the Holy Spirit’s help to lead other people to Jesus. Jesus said that a real Christian lives under the control of the Holy Spirit. Jesus promises us that the Holy Spirit “ ‘will help you to understand everything that is true’ ” (John 16:13, EASY). Jesus also said, “ ‘Man must not live only on bread. He must also live on every word that comes from the mouth of God’ ” (Matthew 4:4, NIrV). Jesus’ followers also honor His command to teach other people “ ‘to obey everything that I have taught you’ ” (Matthew 28:20, EASY).

`Read Luke 4:16–30 and Luke 24:25–27, 44–49. What do these verses teach about how Jesus explained the Bible?`

Jesus was, is, and always will be “the way, the truth, and the life” (John 14:6, ERV). Jesus’ teachings came from the Bible. Mark 1:14, 15 is an example. Mark writes, “After John was put in prison, Jesus went into Galilee. He preached the good news of [about] God. ‘The time has come,’ he said. ‘The kingdom of God has come near’ ” (NIrV). What time is Jesus talking about? Jesus is talking about the time in Daniel 9:24–27. In the sermon on the mountain (Matthew 5–7), Jesus uses quotes from Exodus 20:13, 14 (Matthew 5:21, 27). In Luke 4, Jesus uses quotes from Isaiah 61:1, 2. So, we see that Jesus preaches Bible truth.

In another example, Acts 8 says that a man from Ethiopia traveled “home from Jerusalem. He had been there to worship God” (Acts 8:27, EASY). This information tells us the man knew about the Jewish religion. He even owned a copy of Isaiah’s book. The man read it on his way home. But he didn’t understand the message about the suffering Savior. The man needed Philip to explain it to him.

This experience teaches us important lessons. First, just because we read a chapter doesn’t mean we will always understand it. Second, we can learn from each other. Third, we must share what we know with others. Philip’s knowledge about the Bible helped the Ethiopian man get baptized (Acts 8:35–39; compare with Psalm 51:12). Our understanding about the Bible can help people accept Jesus, too (John 17:17).
