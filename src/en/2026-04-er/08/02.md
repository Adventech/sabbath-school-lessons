---
title:  The Bible Explains Itself
date:   15/11/2026
---

How we explain the Bible depends on what we believe about it. Some people think the Bible is a collection of old writings. They don’t think these writings agree. Other people believe that “everything that is written in the Bible comes from God’s Spirit. It helps us in many ways. The Bible teaches us what is true. It warns us when we are doing wrong things. It shows us what is right. It teaches us how to live good lives” (2 Timothy 3:16, EASY).

`Read Matthew 5:17–48. How did Jesus explain the Old Testament teachings in these verses?`

Some people may think that Jesus was replacing the Old Testament teachings with new ones. But if we read the verses carefully, we see that Jesus helps us understand the Old Testament teachings more fully. Jesus does what Jeremiah promised the Savior would do (Jeremiah 31:31–33). Jesus puts God’s law in the minds and hearts of His people (compare with Hebrews 8:8–10; Hebrews 10:16).

Sometimes the Bible explains a dream or message right away. In Daniel 2, we read about the dream that King Nebuchadnezzar had (Daniel 2:31–35). Then we read Daniel’s explanation of the dream (Daniel 2:36–45). Also, in Matthew 13, we read the picture story about the farmer (Matthew 13:1–9). Then Jesus explains that picture-story in Matthew 13:18–23).

Sometimes God gave a message about something many years before it happened. Later, another messenger explained the message. We see that happen in 2 Chronicles 36:22, 23. In these verses, we learn that God kept His promise about the 70 years, just as Jeremiah said He would (Jeremiah 25:11, 12; Jeremiah 29:10). In the same way, a lot of Old Testament symbols show us the things Jesus came to do. The messengers in the New Testament explain these symbols.

“The Old Testament uses symbols to show us God’s promises about Jesus. The New Testament shows us how God kept these promises. Both testaments are important.”—Ellen G. White, Selected Messages, book 2, page 104, adapted.
