---
title:  Reading The Writings Of Ellen G. White
date:   20/11/2026
---

How should we read and understand the writings of Ellen G. White? Here are a few rules to help us:

First, start with prayer. Don’t read her work with a spirit of doubt. How we think or feel has the power to change what we read, see, or hear. So, the Holy Spirit must control our thinking. As Ellen White said, “If you study the Bible to find proof for your own ideas, you will never learn Bible truth. Study the Bible to learn what the Lord says.”—Christ’s Object Lessons, page 112, adapted.

Second, make sure you understand fully what you read. Don’t take a sentence or part of a paragraph by itself and try to understand it without studying the paragraph or whole chapter it came from. Ellen White wrote, “We must understand when and where something was written. Time and place are important.”—Selected Messages, book 1, page 57, adapted. Sad to say, some people don’t take the time to understand this rule when they read the writings of Ellen White. This problem has caused no end of trouble for some church members.

Third, ask yourself: “What important Bible truth or rule does she talk about in the work I am reading?” Here’s an example: in 1894, Ellen White wrote to church members in Battle Creek, Michigan. She told them not to buy bicycles (read Testimonies for the Church, volume 8, pages 50–53). Ellen White wanted church members to follow an important Bible rule. She wanted them to be good managers of their money. (Back then, bicycles were very expensive.)

Fourth, study everything Ellen White wrote on a topic. Some things she wrote are hard to understand. Her teachings will help you understand her more difficult ideas.

Finally, many people say that Ellen White said things she never said. So, make sure that she really said it. What is the best way to do that? Look for the name of the book that an Ellen White quote comes from. Make sure the quote is really from there. When you see that it is, then you may use the rules above to study and understand her words.

God gave us a wonderful gift in the writings of Ellen G. White. We must follow the above rules to read her writings wisely. Her writings are a blessing to millions of people worldwide. So, we must not use them in the wrong way.
