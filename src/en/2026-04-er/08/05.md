---
title:  The Meaning Of The Verse
date:   18/11/2026
---

Every Christian should understand the Bible. The Bible is plain and simple enough so that each person can understand God’s plan to save us. At the same time, the Bible is deep enough to interest the minds of the most intelligent people. The Bible is the same as an endless treasure. It always has something new to teach us.

`Read the story about Jesus and the lawyer in Luke 10:25–28. Some people say that the Bible doesn’t need to be explained. If that is so, why does Jesus ask the lawyer, “ ‘What is written in the law? What do you understand from it?’ ” (Luke 10:26, ERV).`

If we want to understand a verse, we should first try to understand what the words in the verse mean. Then we should try to understand how the words are used. What can we learn from ancient history to help us understand the verse? What symbols does the writer use, if any? The understanding we come to should agree with accepted Bible truth. When we compare different verses about the same idea or story, we will better understand the Bible. An easy way to learn how to do that is to compare the Gospels. The Gospels are the first four books of the New Testament.

`Compare Matthew 5:43–48 with Luke 6:27–36. How do these verses help us better understand the meaning of the word “perfect” (ERV) in Matthew 5:48?`

If we want to understand the Bible, we must empty our hearts of all pride. We also must study the Bible very carefully. Remember, no one owns truth. We all need the Holy Spirit’s help to understand it. We always should try to come to a correct understanding of Bible truth. To do that, follow this important rule: start your study with what you understand. Then build from that. Over time, this study plan will help us understand things that might not be so easy right now. Until we get to heaven, we will always have questions about the Bible that we can’t answer.

`Think of a time when you were bothered by something you didn’t understand. Later, you got an explanation that helped. How can that experience help you trust God with the things in the Bible that, for now, you don’t understand?`
