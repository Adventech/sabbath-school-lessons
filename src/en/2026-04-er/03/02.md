date:   11/10/2026

As we saw last week, the Lord used Elijah to do a power­ful work.

`What does James say about Elijah? Read James 5:17, 18 for the answer. What do you think James means when he says that “Elijah was a human being [person], just as we are” (James 5:17, NIrV)?`

Elijah’s success on Carmel was amazing (1 Kings 18)! God used Elijah to do wonderful work. One of the most powerful miracles in Old Testament history happens in this story: Elijah made fire come down from heaven (1 Kings 18:38)! You would think that Elijah would be very happy and excited after doing that miracle.

`Read 1 Kings 19:1–16. What does this story tell us about how Elijah felt after the miracle on Carmel?`

“Then he [Elijah] traveled for one day into the desert. He came to a small bush. He sat down under it. He prayed that he would die. ‘Lord, I’ve had enough,’ he said. ‘Take my life. I’m no better than my people of [from] long ago’ ” (1 Kings 19:4, NIrV). For sure, Elijah is very sad. The question is, “Why is Elijah sad?”

Maybe the answer is found in Elijah’s words: “ ‘Lord, God of heaven’s armies, I have always served [worked for] you the best I could. But the people of Israel have broken their agreement [promise] with you. They have destroyed your altars. They have killed your prophets [messengers] with swords. I am the only prophet left. And now they are trying to kill me, too!’ ” (1 Kings 19:10, ICB). Does Elijah believe that he didn’t do a good job? Maybe he feels that he failed to serve God as well as he should. Whatever is going on here, we can see that Elijah suffers the same as we do.

`Are there times in your life when you feel you didn’t do a good job? What Bible promises can you believe in that can help you during these times?`
