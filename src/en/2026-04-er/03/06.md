---
title:  False Messengers
date:   15/10/2026
---

When God does something, Satan always tries to copy it. God chose men and women to be His messengers. So, Satan also chose people to be his messengers. Satan used these messengers to try to destroy God’s work and His people. How can we know God’s real messengers from the false messengers that Satan sends?

`Read Jeremiah 14:1–15. What is happening in these verses? What does the Lord think about false messengers?`

In Jeremiah 14, God gave Jeremiah a message. The message was a warning for the kingdom of Judah. God would punish Judah with war, food shortage, and disease. False messengers at the time said that Jeremiah’s message wouldn’t happen. Jeremiah talks about the false messengers: “Then the Lord said to me, ‘Jeremiah, those prophets [messengers] are telling lies in my name. I did not send them or command them or speak to them. Their prophecies [messages] came from false visions [dreams], worthless [useless] magic, and their own wishful [foolish] thinking’ ” (Jeremiah 14:14, ERV).

False messengers are evil. Often their messages come from the false gods they worship (Jeremiah 23:13, 14). But God did not send these messengers to His people (read Jeremiah 23:26; Ezekiel 13:2).

False messengers often preach popular views or ideas. That’s what happened in 1 Kings 22. In this story, Ahab commanded 400 ‘messengers’ to give him advice. All of the false messengers told the king to fight against Ramoth-Gilead. Then Ahab asked Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, what to do. Jehoshaphat told Ahab to talk to Micaiah, God’s messenger. One of the false messengers told Micaiah to tell Ahab what he wanted to hear: “ ‘Look. The other prophets [messengers] agree. All of them are saying the king will have success. So agree with them. Say the same thing they do’ ” (1 Kings 22:13, NIrV).

Micaiah felt the same terrible pressure to lie that the false messengers felt. But Micaiah refused to lie. He waited for God to tell him what to say (1 Kings 22:14).
