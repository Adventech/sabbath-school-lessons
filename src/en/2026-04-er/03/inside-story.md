### From Criminal to Church Leader: Part 2

_by Mission Editor_

Marek’s life started to change as he read The Great Controversy in Poland. Marek decided to keep the 7th-day Sabbath. He also wanted to stop smoking but couldn’t.

On the Sabbath, Marek passed a street poster. The poster advertised a five-day stop smoking class at a Seventh-day Adventist church. Marek never heard of that church before.

Marek went straight to the church. He arrived at 2 o’clock that Sabbath afternoon. The church would have been empty at that time. But a group of Bible booksellers was there. They invited Marek to come back next Sabbath morning.

Marek went back the next Sabbath and enjoyed the sermon. Marek was amazed at the love the church members showed him. He learned that a big group of young people was meeting the next Sabbath. The members invited Marek to go.

The next Sabbath, Marek waited for some church members to come to take him to the meeting. He heard two voices in his heart. One voice said, “Don’t go. Enjoy the nice weather.” The other voice said, “You really need to go to this meeting.” So, Marek decided to continue waiting.

The church members arrived and took Marek to the meeting. The meeting amazed Marek. Every word touched his heart. The preacher was a British pastor from London. The pastor talked until noon. Then he said, “I know I need to end now. But I know there is somebody here who needs Jesus.”

Marek thought, “Who told the pastor about me?”

Then the pastor shared his personal story. He was born into a religious family. But he left the church. He used drugs and drank. Both his church and his mother stopped praying for him.

The pastor said, “Then I met Jesus. He lifted me up from the bottom where I was. Now I am here to tell you about His power. Jesus can change your life, too. If you want Jesus to change your life, just come here to the front.”

Marek looked at the crowd of 1,000 people and sat back in his seat with fear. He didn’t want to go forward in front of that big crowd of people.

The pastor said, “Don’t think about people looking at you. Just come forward. Your coming forward is between you and God.”

Marek stood up. He couldn’t sit anymore. His heart pumped fast as he walked to the front. As the pastor prayed, Marek understood for the first time God’s plan to save sinners. Marek started to cry. Nothing could stop his tears. But Marek also was excited and happy.

Today, Marek is happy he decided to give his life to Jesus. He now serves as a church leader in Poland. He continues to share his faith with other people.
