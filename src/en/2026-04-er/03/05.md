title:  Special Messengers Who Were Women
date:   14/10/2026
---

`Read Exodus 15:20; Judges 4:4; 2 Kings 22:14; and Nehemiah 6:14. What do these verses teach us about some of the people that God chose to be His messengers?`

The Bible shows us that God, at times, chose women to be His messengers. (Read Exodus 15:20 and Luke 2:36.) In Micah 6:4, Miriam is included with Moses and Aaron as a messenger that God sent to His people. Miriam sang a special song to honor God after the Israelites crossed the Red Sea (Exodus 15:20, 21). But Miriam was also a sinner, the same as any human. She had evil feelings in her heart against Moses and his Ethiopian wife. So, God punished her (read Numbers 12:1–10).

Deborah is another woman who was God’s messenger. Deborah also helped lead Israel in a terrible time (Judges 4:1–4). Her song (Judges 5:2–31) showed what God did to save Israel.

`Read 2 Kings 22:11–14. Why did King Josiah’s men go to Huldah? What does this story tell us about the important work that Huldah did in Israel?`

Huldah was a messenger from God. The top leaders in King Josiah’s government met with her (2 Kings 22:14; 2 Chronicles 34:22) after they read the “Book of [about] the Law” (2 Kings 22:8, ERV) to the king. (We believe this book was the book of Deuteronomy.) After the king heard the words in this book, he “tore his clothes” (2 Kings 22:11, ERV) to show that he was very upset. The king understood that Israel wasn’t following the teachings in the Book of the Law. The book warned that bad things would happen to Israel if they didn’t follow its teachings. The king believed these warnings. So, he sent his men right away to Huldah. God gave Huldah a message. Soon He would punish the kingdom of Judah. King Josiah made many changes for the better because of Huldah’s messages. The king’s changes helped God not to punish His people during King Josiah’s time.

`God’s messengers were people who needed His love and forgiveness, the same as you and me. What hope do their stories give you?`
