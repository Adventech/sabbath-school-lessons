---
title:  Female Messengers And Young Assistant Pastors
date:   21/10/2026
---

`Read Luke 2:36–38 and Acts 21:9. What do these verses tell us about female messengers?`

In Old Testament times, God chose both men and women to be His messengers. In New Testament times, God continued to choose women to be His messengers. Anna is the first woman who is named a special messenger in the New Testament. Anna “never left the temple. She stayed there day and night to worship God. She fasted [ate no food for a short time] and she prayed” (Luke 2:37, 38, EASY).

Acts 21:19 says that Philip “had four daughters who were not married. They spoke messages from God” (EASY). We don’t know what messages Philip’s four daughters communicated to the church. But we do know that none of their messages were included in the Bible.

We also know about young assistant pastors who helped church leaders do their work. Church leaders helped these young pastors become church leaders. Paul helped Timothy become a church leader and pastor. Paul wrote these words to Timothy: “I told you the message about Jesus and you believed in him. So you are like [the same as] my own child” (1 Timothy 1:2, EASY). Paul taught Timothy how to be a pastor. Paul wrote, “Timothy, you are like my own child. So now I am telling you to serve God well. Remember the words from God that people spoke about you, when you became a servant of God. Those words will help you to be strong, like a soldier who fights well” (1 Timothy 1:18, EASY).

In Titus 1:4, Paul writes these words to Titus: “I told you the message about Jesus and now we both believe in him. So you are like my own child” (EASY). Paul gave Titus the job of choosing leaders for the church in Crete. This job was not easy. Paul offered young Titus spiritual advice on how to do this task. Paul ended with these words: “You must teach these things to the believers [church members]. Help them to live in a good way. Warn them when they do wrong things. Use all the authority [power] that you have as a leader in the church. Make sure that everyone respects you” (Titus 2:15, EASY).

`Young or old, male or female, we all have a part in God’s work. What is your part? What else can you do for God?`
