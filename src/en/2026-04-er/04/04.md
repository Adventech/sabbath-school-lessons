`What do 1 Corinthians 14:3; Ephesians 4:11–13; and Revelation 22:6 tell us about the work of God’s messengers in New Testament times?`

The verses above about God’s messengers are helpful. But these verses don’t tell us everything that the New Testament messengers did. New Testament messengers did many of the things that Old Testament messengers did. So, we can look at the lives of Old Testament messengers to better understand the work of messengers in the New Testament.

In 1 Corinthians 12:10, special messages are included in the list with other spiritual gifts. In 1 Corinthians 12:27, 28, special messengers are included in the list after apostles. As we saw yesterday, apostles were church leaders who also got ­messages from God. As we read in 1 Corinthians 14:3, “But if someone speaks a clear message from God, he helps the people who hear him. He helps them to become stronger [powerful; strong] as believers [Christians]. He [the Holy Spirit] helps them to be happy and strong in their troubles” (EASY).

Ephesians 4:11–13 also gives us a list of spiritual gifts. Special messengers are included in this list, too. Apostles, preachers, pastors, and teachers are also in the list (Ephesians 4:11). These persons help grow the church until Jesus comes back (read Ephesians 4:12, 13).

The book of Acts talks about two of God’s messengers: Judas and Silas. These men didn’t write any books in the Bible. The words of Judas and Silas made the faith of church members strong (Acts 15:32). Yes, at times, God’s messengers must warn His people. But at other times, God’s messengers also encourage church members, the same as Judas and Silas did. The church members needed to hear their messages. For sure, the words of Judas and Silas filled the church members with hope.

So, we can see God’s messages include encouraging promises (1 Corinthians 14:31). In hard times, the gift of special messages encourages people and gives hope to their hearts.

In Revelation 22:6, Jesus is named the Lord God of the special messengers. This verse also tells us that Jesus’ words are holy and true. So, we can trust that the words of His messengers will be holy and true, too. We can trust so little in this life. But we can trust God’s messengers in the Bible. For sure, Revelation 22:6 is good news!
