---
title:  "Jesus: More Than A Messenger"
date:   18/10/2026
---

Moses said, “The Lord your God will give to you a prophet [messenger] like [much the same as] me. He will be one of your own people. Listen to [obey] him” (Deuteronomy 18:15, ICB). Who was this messenger? The Bible tells us the answer: Jesus (read Acts 3:22; also read John 6:14).

`Who did the Bible say that Jesus was? How did Jesus’ followers answer the question? Read Matthew 16:13–16; Mark 8:27–29; and Luke 9:18–20 for the answers.`

All the Old Testament promises about the coming Savior showed us Jesus. New Testament writers understood that Jesus kept all of these promises. In Luke 24:13–35, Jesus showed that He was the Savior that the Old Testament promised would come.

In John 1:1–5, John talks about someone named the Word. The Word is not just any person or messenger. The Word “made all things. Nothing was made without Him making it” (John 1:3, NLV). So, the Word made everything that was made. He made all the planets and everything in heaven and on earth. That Word is Jesus.

So, every time Genesis 1 says, “and God said” we know these words mean Jesus is talking. Jesus announced, “ ‘There will be light!’ ” (Genesis 1:3, EASY). This same Jesus “brought light to all people” (John 1:4, EASY). Jesus was the most important part of God’s plan to save us. Jesus came to this earth to shine the same as a light in the dark (John 1:4, 5).

God’s promises will always happen. We can trust Him. God always does what He says He will do. God’s messages are the same as light that shines in a dark place (2 Peter 1:19). God’s messages will continue to encourage people until Jesus comes back and starts His kingdom. His kingdom will continue forever.

Until that time, Jesus offers us everlasting life. The question we must ask ourselves is: “Will we accept His gift?”

`Think about how Jesus made the earth and the skies and everything in them that is good (John 1:1–3). This same Jesus died on the cross for your sins. What does this amazing Bible truth show us about God’s love for us?`
