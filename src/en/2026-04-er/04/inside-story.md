### A School for Berta

_by Mission Editor_

Berta was excited when she heard that the first Seventh-day Adventist church school opened in Poland. Berta was a teacher who was looking for work. But there was a problem. The Seventh-day Adventist school was outside Warsaw, far away from her home in Krakow.

Berta thought sadly, “This job is not for me.”

Then a friend started to talk to Berta about the Seventh-day Adventist school. Again, Berta thought the school was too far away. So, she decided to look for work in Krakow. Then another friend told her about the Seventh-day Adventist school.

The friend said, “I heard about this new Seventh-day Adventist school. Maybe you would like to be a teacher there.”

After this conversation, Berta wondered if God was trying to tell her something about becoming a teacher there.

Berta prayed, “God, what should I do?”

There was also a teaching job at the public school in Krakow. Berta decided that she would try to get a job with both schools. Then she would accept the job from the first school that offered her a job.

The Seventh-day Adventist school was the first school to write back to Berta. The school manager asked Berta to come to the school to discuss the job.

Then Berta got worried. She had a 16-year-old son named Jacob. Jacob’s school and friends were in Krakow. Would he be willing to move to another city?

Berta prayed again. She decided to give Jacob the choice. He could move with her to Warsaw or stay with a family member in Krakow.

Jacob said, “Mother, let’s move together. I want to be with you.”

Berta was surprised and pleased. Jacob’s words were more proof that God was leading Berta to Warsaw.

Berta accepted the job. Today, Berta is the school manager for the Seventh-day Adventist elementary school near Warsaw. Berta knows that God led her to the school. Her son says that his mom made the best decision. Jacob didn’t have any Seventh-day Adventist friends in Krakow and wasn’t interested in church. But now he has many Seventh-day Adventist friends and is interested in church.

Jacob said, “Mom, you made a wonderful decision to come here. I am so glad we did.”

Berta said that she is happy. She knows that God brought her to the school. She says, “That’s why I am here.” Thank you for your Sabbath School offerings that support Seventh-day Adventist education in Poland and worldwide.
