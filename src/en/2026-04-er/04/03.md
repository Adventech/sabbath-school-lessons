---
title:  Church Leaders And God’s Messengers
date:   19/10/2026
---

The New Testament talks about the work of messengers and church leaders. The church leaders were named apostles. The apostles also were God’s messengers. God sent the apostles to teach and lead the church. In the Bible, “apostle” is another name for each of Jesus’ 12 followers. The rest of the New Testament supports this idea. In the New Testament, we see that other people are named apostles who weren’t part of Jesus’ 12 followers.

Paul says 12 times in his letters that he is an apostle. In Romans 1:1, 2, Paul connects his preaching and being an apostle with the words of the Old Testament messengers. Paul says that God chose him to preach (Romans 11:13).

Some people refused to accept that God chose Paul to be an apostle. Paul gave them reasons and proof to help them accept that God chose him (1 Corinthians 9:1, 2). Paul understood that He was continuing to do the work that Jesus started. Jesus is the leader of the church (Ephesians 2:20). The Holy Spirit shows the apostles who God is. So, the Holy Spirit gives the apostles the same understanding that He gives to God’s messengers (Ephesians 3:1–5).

In 2 Peter 3:1, 2, Peter talks about God’s messengers from Old Testament times. God gave the apostles the same power to speak and work for Him. Peter also compares God’s messengers in the Old Testament with God’s messengers in the New Testament. All during Bible history, the Holy Spirit gave certain people the gift of understanding God’s messages. In the New Testament, both apostles and God’s messengers communicated His words to other people. But not all messengers were apostles (Acts 15:32).

`What work do apostles and messengers do for Jesus? Read Ephesians 2:20 and Ephesians 3:5 for the answer.`

The apostles tell us that Jesus was the most important idea they wrote about. These New Testament writers wrote about the life and death of Jesus. They also wrote about His work in heaven and His Second Coming.
