---
title:  False Messengers In The New Testament
date:   22/10/2026
---

`Read Matthew 24:23, 24. What does Jesus warn us about in these verses? Why should we follow this advice?`

Jesus said, “ ‘People will come and say they are the Christ [the Savior]. People will come and say they are prophets [messengers from God]. But they are not true [they are lying]. They will show big signs [miracles] and do wonderful things to fool [trick] people. They will fool God’s chosen people if they can’ ” (Matthew 24:24, WE).

As we can see, Jesus warns us about end-time lies. These lies will be really powerful. There will be “big signs” and “wonderful things [miracles]” that “fool [trick] people.” So, we must be careful. Miracles do not prove that God did them. We have very powerful enemies. Satan and his evil angels also can make miracles happen. As Paul warns us: “Our fight is not with people. It [our fight] is against the leaders and the powers [demons] and the spirits [the devil and his demons] of darkness [evil] in [on] this world [earth]. It [our fight] is against the demon world [Satan’s army of evil angels] that works in the heavens” (Ephesians 6:12, NLV). These evil powers work with false messengers to trick people.

Sad to say, false messengers are not new. The New Testament talks about the false messengers in the Old Testament times (Luke 6:26). In the New Testament, false messengers are compared to hungry wolves who pretend to be the same as ‘sheep’ (Matthew 7:15). We can tell who they really are because of the way they behave (Matthew 7:16).

False messengers lie and trick people (Matthew 7:17–20). Jesus announced, “ ‘Many people will come and say they are prophets [messengers] of [from] God. But they will not be true [cannot be trusted]. They will fool [trick] many people’ ” (Matthew 24:11, WE). Jesus also warns, “ ‘They will fool God’s chosen people if they can’ ” (Matthew 24:24, WE; also read Mark 13:22 and 1 John 4:1).

`How can we protect ourselves from Satan’s lies? Read Ephesians 6:10–18 for the answer.`

We must put on God’s battle suit. His battle suit will protect us from Satan’s lies. Also, we must ask the Holy Spirit to control us. We must study the Bible, too. These things will protect us and keep us close to Jesus.
