God chose His messengers to do important work at special times. The most important job of a messenger was to help people know God better and to help them have a loving relationship with Him. Ellen G. White did this work. Her work had two important parts: (1) to lead people to Jesus and (2) to help them trust His Word, the Bible.

Ellen White loved Jesus her whole life. She wanted to share her love for God with other people. She wrote a famous set of five books named The Conf l ict of the Ages. This set of books starts and ends with the words “God is love.” Maybe her most popular book is Steps to Christ. In it, she wrote: “Our growth in God’s love, our joy, our skills all depend on our relationship with Jesus. When we communicate with Jesus every day, we will live in Him. We will grow in love and forgiveness. We will grow strong in the fight against sin. Jesus started the work to save us. He will finish this work, too.”—Steps to Christ, page 69, adapted. Ellen White loved to talk about Jesus.

Respect for the Bible was an important part of Ellen White’s work for God. Ellen White compared her writing to a small light and the Bible to a big light. Ellen White believed her writing helped people better understand the Bible. The Bible showed God’s love and His plan to save sinners. Early in Ellen White’s work for God, she wrote: “Dear Reader, make the Bible the reason for your faith and behavior.”—Selected Messages, book 3, page 29, adapted.

Years later, she wrote: “The Lord wants you to study your Bibles. He didn’t give us any new truth to replace the Bible. . . . If we study the Bible, God will give life to our spirits. Then we will do good works. These works will shine the same as light in the dark.”—Letter 130, 1901, adapted. Ellen White also believed that people who said they were God’s messengers needed to teach “what Jesus taught. Jesus’ teachings include more than just His own words. His teachings include the words of the messengers and teachers in the Old Testament. Jesus’ teachings do not include human teachings.”—The Desire of Ages, page 826, adapted.

Ellen White made Jesus and the Bible the most important part of her Christian experience. Next Friday, we will look at her beliefs about how God communicated His words to His chosen messengers.
