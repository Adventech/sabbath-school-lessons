### Demons and Death: Part 2

_by Mission Editor_

Timo and his friends, Leena and Anneli, had a frightening experience during a Bible study in Leena’s apartment. Anneli later explained that she used to do witchcraft. Even now, evil spirits tried to hurt her. But after Anneli was baptized into the Seventh-day Adventist Church, the demons stopped hurting her. Timo was a computer engineering student. He also was getting ready for baptism. He woke up at night when he heard a scary voice tell him, “Don’t get baptized.”

The scary warning didn’t stop Timo from getting baptized. He later became a Seventh-day Adventist pastor. But his experiences with demons and people who tried to end their own lives didn’t stop.

After Timo became a pastor, he and some other pastors got on a boat. They were on their way to a meeting in Sweden.

That night, Timo couldn’t sleep. He felt a strong need to pray. As soon as he started to pray, Timo heard an evil laugh. Timo knew something bad was happening. But he didn’t know what it was. He prayed hard for the next two hours.

At breakfast, an older pastor came to Timo. Something woke the pastor up in the middle of the night.

The pastor said, “What was happening with you last night? The Holy Spirit told me to pray for you.”

Then another pastor came to the breakfast table.

The pastor said, “Last night, I woke up and felt the need to go outside for some fresh air. When I got to the boat deck, I saw a man about to jump into the sea.”

The pastor talked with the man for an hour. The pastor told the man not to kill himself. The man came back to the deck.

Then Timo and the other two pastors compared their experiences. They discovered that everything happened to them last night at the same time! Timo knew God woke him up to pray, even if he didn’t know who or what he was praying for. God woke up the older pastor to pray for Timo. The older pastor’s prayers gave Timo the strength to keep praying. Timo and the old pastor were the same as Aaron and Hur, who held up Moses’ arms when the Israelites went to fight (Exodus 17:8–12). As long as Moses’ arms stayed up, the Israelites won (verse 11). In the same way, God used the prayers of Timo and the older pastor to help their friend fight against evil and save a man’s life.

Timo is now 45. He is the communication director for the Seventh-day Adventist Church in Finland. Timo’s experience showed him that the war between good and evil is very real.

Timo said, “This war is happening all around us. Jesus already won the fight. So, we don’t need to be afraid. Jesus will protect us. Satan can’t hurt us when we trust in Jesus.”
