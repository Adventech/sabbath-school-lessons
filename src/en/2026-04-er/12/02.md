---
title:  A Promise In The Book Of Joel
date:   13/12/2026
---

`Read Joel 2:28–32. What does God promise to do in the end-times?`

Joel was a messenger for God during Old Testament times. Joel lived many hundred years before Jesus was born. When we read Joel’s writings, we can learn important information about the future. When we read Joel 2:28–32, we may ask, “Is Joel talking about his own time? Or is he talking about things far in the future?”

Bible thinkers teach us that there are two types of special messages: (1) classical messages and (2) apocalyptic messages. Classical messages are messages for people, countries, and leaders. They use real numbers and real names. (Read Jeremiah 25:1–11 for an example.) Apocalyptic messages use symbols and signs. They use numbers as symbols, too, to explain when something will happen in the future. (Read Daniel 7 for an example.) Sometimes God’s messengers will mix the two types of messages together. (Read Matthew 24 for an example.)

What about Joel 2:28–31? Is this a message about things happening in Joel’s time only? Or is this a special message about the future? Joel 2:28–31 is a message about the future. So, we should not expect this promise to happen in Joel’s lifetime. We also shouldn’t expect this promise to happen in the years right after Joel dies. We should look for this promise to happen in the end-times. Notice the time when Joel says that God will keep this promise. The Lord tells Joel: “The sun will become dark and the moon will become red. Then the great [powerful] and frightening day of the Lord will come” (Joel 2:31, EASY).

Elsewhere, the Bible talks about the sun becoming dark and the moon becoming red. In His sermon about the end-times, Jesus talked about signs in the sun, moon, and stars (Matthew 24:29). Also, John talks about the same signs (Revelation 6:12, 13). Both Jesus and John talk about the final signs that will happen before the Second Coming. So, Jesus and John help us to understand that Joel’s message also must be about the end-times, too.

`Think about the messages that happened just as the Bible promised. Why should they help us to trust God about His future promises, including the Second Coming?`
