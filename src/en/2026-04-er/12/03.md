title:  God Pours His Spirit On His Church
date:   14/12/2026
---

`Read Peter’s sermon in Acts 2:1–21. Why did Peter use a quote from Joel when he talked about Jesus’ first coming? How does Peter use special messages from God in his sermon?`

In Acts 2:1–21, we read a quote from the book of Joel. This quote is one of the longest quotes from the Old Testament. Peter uses this quote when he preaches to the people in Jerusalem. When we read what Peter says, we may wonder, “Peter, what about the signs of the dark sun and the red moon? We can’t see them happening in your time.” Peter might answer, “The signs happened in my time. When Jesus hung on the cross a few days ago, the sun became dark for three hours” (see Mark 15:33).

What was proof to Peter back then may not convince us today. In these end-times, we expect God to more fully keep the promise He made to Joel. What proof do we have that God will keep this promise more fully in our time? The answer is in Joel 2:23. In this verse, Joel talks about the early and the late rains. Acts 2 shows us when the first part of this promise from Joel happens. The final promise will happen when God pours His Spirit on people just before the end of human history. This time is named the Latter Rain.

Often people think special messengers from God preached only about the future. But Bible messengers did more than that. Often, Bible messengers asked people to stop sinning and come back to God. We see the people feeling guilty for their sins while Peter is preaching to them (Acts 2:37–39).

John the Baptist warned the people to stop sinning, too. As God’s messenger, John announced the first coming of Jesus. While John preached this message, he also warned, “ ‘Stop doing wrong things and turn [come] back to God! The kingdom of heaven is almost here’ ” (Matthew 3:2, WE).

When God pours His Spirit on all people in the end-times, He fully will keep the promise He made in Joel 2. God’s messengers in the end-times will also warn people, as John the Baptist and Peter did. God’s end-time messengers will warn people to stop sinning and come back to God. God’s end-time messengers also will tell people to look to Jesus to save them. These messengers will help God’s church to understand and follow Bible truth.
