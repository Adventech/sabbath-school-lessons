In Ephesians 4, Paul says that God gave the church spiritual gifts to help us do His work. That’s the reason God gave Ellen G. White the gift of prophecy, or special messages. Her messages help people to have a strong relationship with God and to share His love with other people.

Ellen White wrote, “More than any other Christian group, Seventh-day Adventists should honor and praise Jesus. We must announce the Sabbath truth along with the third angel’s message. The most important part of this message is Jesus. We must not leave Him out. At the cross, Jesus joined mercy with truth, and obedience with peace. So, the sinner must look to Calvary. He must have the simple faith of a child. He must trust in Jesus’ love and forgiveness to save him. The sinner must accept Jesus’ perfect life.”—Gospel Workers, pages 156, 157, adapted.

Ellen White taught that God invites each Christian to join Him in His work to save people. “The Lord wants His people to do far more than they are doing now to save sinners.”—Evangelism, page 113, adapted.

“We must not be lazy. Let us work together to bring sinners to Jesus and His love.”—Evangelism, page 115, adapted. Ellen G. White was a Bible worker in Europe in the 1880s. During the 1890s, she worked for God in Australia.

Satan is angry in the end-times because God commands His people to serve Him (Revelation 12:17). God’s end-time followers have a message for their time. God leads them and gives them understanding. “The Lord shows us the dangers that are around us and ahead. With the gift of prophecy, or special messages, God shows us the lies that will trick people in the end-times and make them slaves. God tells His people, ‘This is the right way. Walk in it.’ ” [Isaiah 30:21, EASY]. . . . The Great Controversy shows us Satan’s lies.”—Ellen G. White, Manuscript 31, 1890, adapted.

Satan is angry when people give their lives to Jesus. So, Satan does everything to cause people to doubt the gift of prophecy and Ellen G. White’s writings. Satan causes people to believe that her writings are not important for our time. Satan also causes people to believe she is a false messenger. But Satan succeeds the most when he causes young people today not to read her writings.
