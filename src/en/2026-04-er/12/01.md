### Read for This Week’s Lesson
Joel 2:28–32; Acts 2:1–21; Ephesians 4:11–14; Revelation 12:17; Acts 2:36–42.

> <p>Memory Verse</p>
> “ ‘In the last days [end-times] I will send [pour] My Spirit on all men [people]. Then your sons and daughters will speak [communicate] God’s Word. Your old men will dream dreams. Your young men will see special dreams. Yes, on My servants, both men and women, I will pour out My Spirit in those days’ ” (Joel 2:28, 29, NLV).

Some goverments make special laws named “sunset rules.” (No, sunset rules are not about the time when the sun sets!) Sunset rules help protect citizens. Sometimes a government will make strict laws during a time of war, a natural disaster, or an emergency. These strict laws remove certain freedoms from citizens during dark and difficult times. Some governments will include sunset rules to end these strict laws after a certain time. The sunset rules give the people back their freedoms.

Did God include a sunset rule with the gift of special messages? Is there a time before Jesus comes back when God will stop pouring this gift on His people? Last week, we learned that God communicated to His people with His messengers. This week, we will study the topic of the gift of special messages in the end-times. What part does the gift of special messages have in the end- times? What part does Ellen G. White have in earth’s final history?
