---
title:  Gifts From The Holy Spirit
date:   15/12/2026
---

God gave the church many gifts. These gifts help the church to serve Him better. In the New Testament, we read many lists of spiritual gifts (Romans 12:6–8; 1 Corinthians 12:8–10; 1 Corinthians 12:28; Ephesians 4:11).

`Read about spiritual gifts in Ephesians 4:11–14. Why does God give these gifts to His church? How does the gift of prophecy, or special messages, help the church do God’s work?`

Spiritual gifts help the church to grow and to preach Bible truth. Paul included the gift of prophecy, or special messages, in all four lists. That tells us that the gift of prophecy, or special messages, is one of the most important gifts.

Let’s look at some Bible truth about God’s messengers:

1. God chooses His messengers and gives them their work (read 1 Kings 19:16, 19–21 and Isaiah 6). Often, God’s messengers don’t feel worthy to serve Him. But in the end, they obey.

2\. God talks to His messengers in dreams. Or He speaks to them so that they can hear His voice and words. Sometimes God tells His messengers what will happen in the future (Daniel 2). At other times, God tells His messengers about what is happening in the countries or with the leaders of that time.

3\. The Bible talks about things that happen to God’s messengers when He talks with them or gives them a message. Some messengers lose strength. Or they may stop breathing. Other times, the experience with God may fill the messenger with new strength or power (Daniel 10:8–10, 18, 19).

4\. Messengers communicate their messages in different ways: (1) with words or sermons (Acts 21:10–12); with their behavior or actions (Ezekiel 4), or with writing letters (Revelation 2, 3). Some messengers used secretaries or assistants. Baruch helped Jeremiah (Jeremiah 36:1–4).

5\. A messenger’s words must agree with Bible truth (Isaiah 8:20).

6\. A messenger’s behavior and message will show that God chose him to serve Him (Matthew 7:15–20).

7\. Satan always tries to copy God’s work. The same is true for the gift of prophecy. In the end-time, there will be false messengers (Matthew 24:24; 2 Peter 2:1).
