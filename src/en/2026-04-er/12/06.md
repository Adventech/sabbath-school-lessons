---
title:  Modern Messengers
date:   17/12/2026
---

Most Christians today believe that God gave men and women His messages in Bible times. But do they really believe that the same thing can happen today? If the promise in Joel 2:28–31 is real, then how will God give us the gift of special messages in the end-times? What will this look the same as?

`Read Acts 2:36–42. What was the message of Peter’s sermon in these verses? How did Peter’s audience answer?`

God first poured His Spirit on His church in a.d. 31. This experience is an excellent picture of what will happen during the end-times. God will pour His Spirit on His end-time church, too. But He will pour out much more of His Spirit at that time. When God first poured His Spirit on His church, the church preached about Jesus. They also told people to stop sinning and to come back to God. God’s Spirit helped the church to do this work. The Holy Spirit gave Peter the power to preach about Jesus. Peter’s sermon caused 3,000 people to give their hearts to Jesus and to get baptized. This time was named Pentecost and the “early rain.”

God gave the early rain or the Holy Spirit to help the church to grow. In the same way, God will pour “ ‘the latter rain’ on the church in the end-times to finish His work on earth.”—Ellen G. White, The Great Controversy, page 611, adapted. So, we can see, the gift of special messages in the end-times will look much the same as it did in Acts 2.

As we already saw in Joel 2:28–31, God will pour His gift of special messages on His church in the end-times. He will pour this gift on young and old people, and on men and women. God will talk with them. When the Holy Spirit chooses an end-time messenger, the messenger will praise and honor Jesus. The messenger will ask people to come back to God and help them to finish God’s work on earth. Ellen G. White did all these things. During her life, she honored Jesus and the Bible above everything else in her work. She told people to come back to God. She invited everyone to help God save lost people.

`How grateful are you for the gift of Ellen G. White’s writings in our church?`
