Revelation 12 talks about the history of the war between good and evil. This chapter shows us how the war started in heaven and what will happen in the end-times.

`What is “the truth that Jesus taught” (Revelation 12:17, ERV)? Compare Revelation 19:10 with Revelation 22:8, 9.`

In Revelation 12:17, we read about God’s end-time church. We will know the end-time church because of two things: (1) this church obeys God’s Ten Commandments and (2) it has “the truth that Jesus taught” (ERV). What is this truth?

Some Bible thinkers say that “the truth that Jesus taught” means our belief in Jesus. Other Bible thinkers give this verse a different meaning. They say that Revelation 12:17 means that Jesus Himself gives a special message to His end-time church (also read Revelation 19:10). The Greek language of the Bible verse allows for both explanations. So, which one did John really mean?

If we compare the two things about the end-time church, we see that both things are said or written in much the same way. So, just as the commandments of God come from God, the truth that Jesus taught comes from Jesus, too. Jesus gives the message. So, He shows us who is His real church. How does Jesus do that? He shares this important information with John to give to us.

The Bible gives us proof in Revelation 19:10 and Revelation 22:9. These verses show us that “the truth that Jesus taught” (Revelation 12:17, ERV) is the same as the gift of special messages. As we already saw, God’s messengers tell God’s people to come back to Him. Or they show us what will happen in the future.

In Revelation 12:17, Jesus shows us who is His real church in the end-times. His end-time church will keep His commandments and have the gift of special messages. God sent messengers to the people in Old and New Testament times. In the same way, God will send His messengers to His people in the end-times.
