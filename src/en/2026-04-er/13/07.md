Many years ago, the Kellogg company wanted to win back customers who switched to other brands. They made an advertisement to encourage people to “taste our cereals again for the very first time.” This saying may be helpful for Seventh-day Adventists, too. Many of us have spent time learning about Ellen G. White without really ‘tasting’ or reading her writings. Learning about her life and work is helpful and important. We must read her work for ourselves. If we don’t ‘taste’ her writings for ourselves, we won’t be able to understand how much God blessed our church with her messages. When we read her writings, we will understand the gift that God gave to us. As we already saw, Ellen White connects us to Bible truth and to God. God blesses us deeply when we read her writings.

Roger Dudley and Des Cummings Jr., did a study about Ellen White’s writings. They discovered that people who often read Ellen White’s writings have a more powerful relationship with Jesus than people who don’t read her writings. Their faith is strong compared to people who don’t read her work. Dudley and Cummings also discovered that people who read Ellen White’s writings will worship together more often with their families. These same readers want to talk more about Jesus to other people, too. Dudley and Cummings wrote, “Our study shows that the church members who often study Ellen G. White’s writings have better spiritual growth than members who don’t read her writings often or at all.”—Dudley and Cummings, “Who Reads Ellen White?,” Ministry, October 1982, page 12, adapted.

During this quarter, we learned that God communicates with His messengers. Their messages connect us to God. God’s special messages help us to be holy in every part of our lives. God’s messengers invite us to trust that God will save us in Jesus. God’s special messages will change our lives. That’s why the Bible invites us to “taste [give God a chance to show you; learn and study] and see that the Lord is good!” (Psalm 34:8, EASY). God’s messages help us to preach Bible truth, share Jesus, and serve people. We must do these things if we want Jesus to come back. We must do our part in announcing the Second Coming as we wait for Jesus to come back. God’s special messages will give us the courage and strength to do exactly that.
