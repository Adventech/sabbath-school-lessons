### Where Are You, God?

_by Hannele Amberla_

I opened my cousin’s letter and started to read. She wrote, “I gave my life to Jesus.” I wondered, “What does that mean?” I wanted a close relationship with God. But I wasn’t sure how to do that. Sure, I went to different churches. But I didn’t find that special connection with God that my cousin talked about.

After I finished university, I moved back to Helsinki, Finland, where I met my future husband. I invited him to go to church with me. I hoped he would come to know the Lord and show me how to have a relationship with Him, too. But it didn’t work.

Then our children were born. My life became busy. I had everything I wanted except for God. I continued visiting ­churches and prayed, “Lord, I can’t find You. Please find me.”

Our teenage daughter decided to study in Australia for a year. I was worried and prayed for her to find a good family to live with. She did! My daughter and I used text messaging to stay in touch. The family she was staying with started to text me, too. I got to know them on the group chat we set up. The father of the family was named Greg. I told Greg and his family that I was searching for God. I learned that Greg no longer went to church. But he told me to read his favorite book, The Desire of Ages. I found a copy of the book online and started to read it. The book changed my life for the better! I found answers to my questions and had a closer ‘walk’ with God.

Then Greg told me to visit a Seventh-day Adventist church near me in Helsinki. When I arrived at the church, I felt so much love from the church members. Those people really knew so much about the Bible! I loved the sermon, too. I went to church the following week.

I fell in love with Jesus, just as my cousin did do many years earlier. That summer, I visited the church’s Bible camp. There, I studied the Bible deeply.

I came home so happy from Bible camp. I shared my love for God with my husband. I asked him to give his life to Jesus. I didn’t know then, but that was our last conversation about God. Two days later, he died in a car accident.

I couldn’t understand why God took my husband so soon after I gave my life to Him. Now I understand that God gave me a church family to support me and pray for me during those hard times. The Bible verses I read gave me peace. The Holy Spirit filled my heart with hope.

Soon I started to work for the church at its Bible school. I now help other people who are suffering the same things I did. I met a good man at church. We got married. God gave us work to do for Him together. As for Greg in Australia, he came back to the Lord. For sure, God works in amazing ways!
