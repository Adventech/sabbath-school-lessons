---
title:  Working Together In Peace And Love
date:   24/12/2026
---

`Read John 17:20–23 and 1 Corinthians 1:10–13. What important idea do we learn from those verses?`

Before the time came for Jesus to die, His heart was filled with a deep wish for His people. Jesus and His Father were one in heart, mind, and spirit. Jesus wanted all His followers to be one in heart, spirit, and mind with each other, too. Jesus prayed a powerful prayer for His people to work together in love. This prayer also was a special message about His church in the future. This promise will happen before Jesus comes back. The Holy Spirit will help God’s people everywhere on earth from every country to work together in peace and love. Paul also talks about this idea. He asks church members in Corinth to agree with each other about Bible truth. They must teach the same things. They must not allow anything to separate them.

Jesus prayed, “ ‘Father, I pray that all who believe in me can be one. You are in me and I am in you. I pray that they can also be one in us. Then the world [people everywhere] will believe that you sent me’ ” (John 17:21, ERV).

God wants everyone to be saved. But before people can be saved, they must believe in Jesus. They must believe that God sent Jesus to save us. When different groups of people accept Jesus as their Lord and Savior, they will show His love. Their lives will be powerful proof that Jesus is who He says He is. Jesus wanted this peace more than anything else for His church. Satan worked hard from the start to destroy this peace. (Read Acts 6:1 for an example.) Sad to say, there has been separation in the church since that time.

But God sends us the Holy Spirit to give us hope. The gifts of the Holy Spirit help the church to work together in peace and love. Ephesians 4:11–15 tells us that these gifts also help the church do God’s work. Paul explains: “All of us will become united [joined together], like [the same as] one person. We will all believe in Jesus as the Son of God. We will all know him. We will become like [the same as] a man who has grown up well. We will be completely as God wants us to be, just like Christ himself” (Ephesians 4:13, EASY). The gift of prophecy, or special messages, is an important part of helping the church work together in peace and love.
