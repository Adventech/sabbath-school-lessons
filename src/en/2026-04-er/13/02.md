---
title:  Special Messages Praise And Honor Jesus
date:   20/12/2026
---

God uses special messages to communicate with His people. Special messages connect people to God. No wonder, then, that Satan (the dragon) is angry at the woman (God’s church). Because Satan is angry, he goes to war against God’s people (read Revelation 12:17).

Last week, we learned who God’s end-time people are. They keep the commandments of God. They have the truth that Jesus taught. Revelation 19:10 tells us that this truth is the gift of special messages.

`What does Paul say is the reason for his life and his work for God? What did he live for? Read 1 Corinthians 2:2 and Galatians 6:14 for the answers.`

Jesus’ followers accepted what God’s messengers in the Old Testament said about Jesus. As Christians, we have promises about Jesus from both the Old and New Testaments. These promises show us a coming Savior. Jesus kept all the promises. All this proof helps make our faith grow strong. “God gave people a fair chance to grow their faith in God, His Son, and the Holy Spirit.”—Ellen G. White, The Spirit of Prophecy, volume 3, page 182, adapted.

In some ways, faith may be more of a ‘heart thing,’ than a ‘head thing.’ What does that mean? It means that when people shut their hearts, their minds shut, too.

`Read John 1:1–5. What do these verses tell us about who Jesus is?`

Jesus lived before there was anything that was made. Jesus came to this earth as a baby. He was born with a human body. He lived with us. Jesus is the living Word of God. As the Living Word, Jesus was an important part of the words and messages that God gave to His messengers.

`Think about how big outer space is with all its planets and stars. Jesus made all of it. This same Jesus died for us. What wonderful hope does this thought give you?`
