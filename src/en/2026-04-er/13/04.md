---
title:  The Bible Is God’s ‘lie Detector’
date:   22/12/2026
---

Did you know that there are more than 45,000 different types of Christian faith groups today? How did a religion that started with Jesus separate into so many different groups? The answer is that people started to believe different things about the Bible. Today, what one church teaches goes against what other churches teach. For sure, not all their teachings or explanations can be correct.

From the start, Satan confused people and caused them to reject Bible truth (read Genesis 2:16, 17; Genesis 3:1–5). Satan mixed just enough Bible truth with lies when he tricked Adam and Eve. Satan does the same thing with us today to get people under his control. Satan lied to God’s people from the beginning of time (2 Chronicles 36:14–16). Satan even tried to trick Jesus (Matthew 4:1–11). But Jesus met Satan’s lies with Bible truth (Matthew 4:4).

`Read 2 Timothy 4:1–5. What important advice does Paul give Timothy about the Bible? Why did Paul want Timothy to be ready? What danger was coming?`

Paul saw a time when people would not love or obey Bible truth. They would choose spiritual teachers who preached lies. God will allow these people to believe the lies they are looking for because they rejected His truth. “For [because of] this reason, God will allow them to follow false teaching so they will believe a lie. They will all be guilty as they stand before God because they wanted to do what was wrong” (2 Thessalonians 2:11, 12, NLV).

But for people who love Bible truth, Jesus promised: “ ‘You will know the truth and the truth will make you free’ ” (John 8:32, NLV). God gave us the gift of special messages to help us to separate Bible truth from lies. That’s why God’s special messages are the same as a lie detector. They show us what is Bible truth and what’s is false.

This idea helps us understand why there are so many Christian faith groups and teachings today. If we believe Bible truth, we will honor the Sabbath. If we believe Bible truth, we will reject the false teaching that the dead are in heaven or burning in hell. That limits about 99.5% of all the churches we can belong to, right? So, where else can we go except to the Seventh-day Adventist Church?
