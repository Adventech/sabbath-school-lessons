---
title:  God’s Messages Give Hope To His People
date:   23/12/2026
---

`Read Matthew 11:28, 29. What does Jesus tell us to do? What does He promise everyone who comes to Him?`

God always cares about us and how we live. Before God made Adam and Eve, He made a perfect home for them to make them happy (Genesis 1). God gave Adam and Eve everything they needed. After they sinned, God went looking for them (Genesis 3:9). God told them about the coming Savior (Genesis 3:15). God made them clothes (Genesis 3:21). For sure, God cares about His children.

God shows us He cares for us in other ways, too. He sent us His messengers in the past because He loves us. God also gives His church the gift of prophecy, or special messages, today. This gift gives us hope and helps make our lives new.

`What message does God give to Isaiah to share with His hurting people? Read Isaiah 40:1–5 for the answer. How can we follow this important rule in our lives today?`

God punished His people in Judah. But God wanted the people from Judah to know that He continued to love them. So, He gave Isaiah a message of hope to share with the people. Isaiah must convince the people that God loves them very much. God cared so much about touching the hearts of His people with the message He gave Isaiah. So, God tells Isaiah exactly what to say and how to say it. God encourages His people with many caring messages in the book of Isaiah (Isaiah 12:1; Isaiah 49:13; and Isaiah 51:3).

Paul warned that people who live for Jesus will suffer for their faith (2 Timothy 3:12). In the difficult times ahead, we will need words of comfort and hope. God uses the gift of prophecy to help encourage His people. This gift gives us strength while we wait for Jesus to come back to take us home.
