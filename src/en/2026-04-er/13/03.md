---
title:  Special Messages Support Bible Truth
date:   21/12/2026
---

The Bible is the work of the Holy Spirit. The Holy Spirit worked with special messengers to make the Bible. 2 Peter 1:21 helps us understand how God works with His messengers: “None of the prophets’ [messengers’] messages came because any person wanted to say something. Instead [but], God’s Holy Spirit caused people to speak words that came from God” (EASY).

`Who made the Bible? How does the Bible help us? Read 2 Timothy 3:16, 17 for the answers.`

Without the Bible, we wouldn’t know how to live. The Bible teaches us how to escape sin. Today, the Bible is everywhere. But that was not so in the past. In Jesus’ day, only the Jewish leaders were allowed to read the Bible. So, God’s messages were precious in those days.

`After Jesus woke up from the dead, what did He use to help His two sad followers better understand His work? Read Luke 24:27 for the answer.`

Jesus is the most important Messenger who lived. He didn’t just teach God’s Word. Jesus is God’s Word. As God’s Word, Jesus honored the Bible (John 7:38). That’s one of the most important things that special messages do.

“Do you believe that the Bible is the Word of God? Do you wish to follow its teachings? Then you must learn daily from the Bible. Every day you must ask God to fill your heart with the wish to serve Him. You must ask Him for strength. God will give us these gifts. He will send the Holy Spirit to give them to us.”—Ellen G. White, in Signs of the Times, March 8, 1910, adapted. The gift of prophecy, or special messages, always honors the Bible. The gift of prophecy always causes us to read the Bible. The Bible gives us the reasons for our faith.

`When Jesus walked on the road to Emmaus with His two followers, He started the conversation with an explanation about Bible truth. After that, He told His followers who He was. Why did Jesus explain the Bible first? What should that tell us about how important the Bible is to our faith?`
