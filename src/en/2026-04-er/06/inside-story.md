### “God Is #1”: Part 1

_by Gina Wahlen_

Naum stood firm for his faith when he was only seven years old. In Yugoslavia, the law said that children must attend school every Saturday. But Naum, a first-grader, decided not to attend school on Sabbath. After Naum missed class for two Saturdays, he was ordered to explain why he was absent.

When Naum got to class, a group of five people waited for him. The group told all the students to leave except for Naum.

The seven-year-old boy bravely stood in front of the group. The group included the school manager, two teachers, and two policemen. One of the policemen was the manager for the secret police in that area.

The group leader asked, “Why aren’t you in class on Saturdays?”

Naum explained, “Because I believe in God. The fourth commandment of His law teaches us to rest on the Sabbath. That’s why I’ll be in church every Sabbath, and not in school.”

The man frowned at Naum. “We will kick you out of school. Then you won’t be allowed to get an education.”

Naum said, “I’ll be in church because God is #1 in my life.”

The man asked, “What do you do at your church?”

“We read the Bible, sing songs, and pray.”

The group said, “Sing us a song!” So, Naum sang a hymn. Then he prayed a simple prayer. Naum thanked God for blessing him. He thanked God for the government. He prayed for God to be with the group that was questioning him. Naum asked God to give them wisdom to make the right decision.

When Naum finished praying, he was asked if his father told him to not go to school.

Naum said honestly, “No.”

If Naum answered “Yes,” his father would be thrown in jail right away. That’s why Naum’s father never told his son not to attend school.

The group was silent for a few moments. Then they told Naum, “You will be told soon if you may stay in school.”

Naum went home to tell his parents what happened. His parents weren’t surprised. They knew their son’s faith in Jesus was strong. So, they knew he would stand firm for God.

The school leaders never announced their decision to Naum. So, Naum continued to attend school, except on Sabbaths.

When Naum completed elementary school, he wasn’t allowed to continue his education because of his decision to keep the Sabbath. So, at age 15, Naum started working full-time with his father on their farm.
