---
title:  God’s Message And Weak Human Messengers
date:   05/11/2026
---

`What does Paul say about God’s treasure in 2 Corinthians 4:7?`

Paul compares God’s people to clay pots: “Treasure is kept in clay jars. In the same way, we have the treasure of the good news in these earthly [weak] bodies of ours. That shows that the mighty power of the good news comes from God. It [the power] doesn’t come from us” (2 Corinthians 4:7, NIrV). The Bible shows that God’s messengers weren’t perfect. They made mistakes in their personal lives. (Just look at Moses, Elijah, David, Jonah, and Peter.) So, their writings also showed the limits of human understanding and communication.

“Jesus showed us how God and men can work together as one. We see this same work between God and humans in the Bible. God gave humans Bible truth. His messengers wrote these Bible truths in human language. The messengers also wrote these truths to meet our needs.”—Ellen G. White, Testimonies for the Church, volume 5, page 747, adapted.

For sure, God’s messengers made mistakes at times. They were human, as Paul wrote in 2 Corinthians 4:7. The Holy Spirit didn’t stop God’s messengers from writing some things that we have trouble explaining or understanding today. As Bible-believing Christians, we must accept the Bible as God’s Word. We must trust the Lord, even with the parts we don’t understand.

Some mistakes could happen when people copied the Bible later on after it was written. When we study some of these verses or other parts that are hard to understand, we need to ask ourselves some helpful questions. Do we fully understand the meaning of the writer’s words? How might more prayerful study help us solve the problem?

Maybe we can’t solve every problem we have with understanding the Bible. But the little things that trouble us when we read are not important compared to the message of Bible truth. We can trust that the Bible truths are perfect, even if God’s messengers are not.
