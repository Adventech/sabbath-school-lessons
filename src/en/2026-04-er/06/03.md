---
title:  Different Types Of Writing
date:   02/11/2026
---

The Bible contains different types of writing. Some Bible books are much the same as diaries. The writers wrote about what happened in everyday life (read Numbers 33:1–49). The books of Daniel, Zechariah, and John are examples of special messages about the future. Their writings are filled with symbols, angels, and end-time happenings.

`Read Isaiah 13:1. How does the Bible talk about the message that Isaiah is giving in this verse?`

Sometimes the dreams that God gives His messengers are named “messages” or “the word of the Lord.” These words help us understand that messages are special words or communications from God. Many messengers wrote God’s messages as poems. In Bible times, writers often used an idea in one line of a poem and then said the idea again later in another line. Here is an example:

“At that time people will look to God, their Maker\
[the Lord who made them].\
Their eyes will see the Holy One of Israel”\
(Isaiah 17:7, ICB).

Do you see how the words “the Holy One of Israel” are another way of saying “God, their Maker [the Lord who made them]”? Each line gives us more information about the same idea that helps us understand it better.

God’s messengers also used symbols and powerful language. These things help us to better understand their messages and obey. When we understand the type of writing God’s messengers used, we can know what they wanted to say. Then we will know better how to follow their advice.

Going to court is another idea that some Bible writers used. In Jeremiah 2:4–9, Jeremiah tells Israel about God’s legal complaint against them. Israel broke God’s law. So, God was taking Israel to court. Jeremiah uses the symbol of a court of law to show us God’s work as Judge. This symbol helps us better understand why God was judging His people.

The Bible also contains messages about the future, history, songs, and picture stories. God told Jeremiah to write a book with all his messages (Jeremiah 36:2). Much of the New Testament is filled with letters. Paul’s letters are just as important as the writings of the Old Testament messengers. Paul plainly tells us that the Holy Spirit gave him his messages (read 1 Corinthians 7:10, 12, 40).
