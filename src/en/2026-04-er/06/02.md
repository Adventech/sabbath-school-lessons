---
title:  Chosen To Write
date:   01/11/2026
---

The first way that God chose to communicate His messages to people was with voices, dreams, or angels. Later, God told Moses to write about the experiences of God’s people. Moses wrote them in a book, as God told him.

`What is the first time that writing is talked about in the Bible? Read Exodus 17:14 for the answer.`

In the Old Testament, we read many examples about how God told His messengers to write His words for His people. God told Jeremiah, “ ‘Take a book and write in it all the words which I have said to you about Israel, Judah, and all the nations. Write all I have said since the day I first spoke to you, from the days of Josiah until today’ ” (Jeremiah 36:2, NLV).

God told Jeremiah the reason he must write His ­messages. Jeremiah must “ ‘warn the people of Judah about the very bad things that I have decided to do to them. When they hear about it, perhaps [maybe] they will stop doing the evil things that they have been doing. Then I will forgive their sins and everything wrong that they have done’ ” (Jeremiah 36:3, EASY). So, God warned His people about the future because He wanted to save them.

God’s messengers wrote for many different reasons and in different places. Moses wrote the first five books of the Bible while he was in the desert. David wrote many of his poems while King Saul hunted him (read Psalms 52, 54, 57, 59). Daniel wrote his dreams while he served in the courts of Babylon and Media-Persia. Paul wrote his letters to the Ephesians, Philippians, Colossians, and Philemon while he was in jail. John wrote the book of Revelation after he was sent in punishment to Patmos Island. When God told His messengers to write, the Holy Spirit gave them power to do it, wherever they were.

God also chose people to write Jesus’ teachings. God trusted His messengers to write down the holy truths that Jesus said.
