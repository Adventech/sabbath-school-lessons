---
title:  The Power Of God’s Word, The Bible
date:   01/12/2026
---

God helped His messengers write the Bible. The Bible answers life’s big questions. God shows us who He is in the Bible. The Bible teaches us the meaning of life. We learn from the Bible why we are here. The Bible also teaches us God’s plan for our lives. The Bible shows us truth. Bible truth helps us to make the best choices in life. The Bible is the rule for our faith and behavior.

We can accept the Bible, or we can reject it. We don’t need to burn our Bibles to reject Bible truth, as King Jehoiakim did (read Jeremiah 36:22–27). We may say we believe the Bible. But what if we don’t follow it? Then we reject it. When we discover Bible truth and accept it, we can be changed, the same as King Josiah (read 2 Kings 22:10–13). Then we can help other people accept Jesus, too.

Today, people often make fun of the Bible. Or they look for mistakes when they read it. Some people don’t think the Bible is important for their lives today. They think that Bible truth is old-fashioned and not up-to-date with accepted ideas and behavior. (For sure, the Bible often goes against these things. And it should!) Many people also laugh at Bible truth because it goes against science or popular teachings.

Sad to say, some Christians use the Bible to support personal beliefs that go against the Bible. Other people explain the Bible in a way that gives it no control or power over our lives. That is the same as rejecting it.

One of the easiest ways to reject the Bible is not to read the Bible or live its truth.

`How did God’s spoken message change people’s lives? Read Jeremiah 38:1–4; John 3:1–10; and Acts 16:25–34 for the answer.`

In Bible times, writing material was expensive. Many people couldn’t read. So, the spoken message from God’s messengers was very important.

Bible truth can touch the heart. With the Holy Spirit’s help, God’s truth can show us our sin. Bible truth can change our lives for the better and make us new. The Bible tells us to stop sinning and obey God (Acts 5:29; also read Hebrews 4:12). That’s why many people reject the Bible. They don’t want to give it any control over their lives.
