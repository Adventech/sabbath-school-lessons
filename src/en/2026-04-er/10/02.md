---
title:  Permission To Talk For God
date:   29/11/2026
---

Before you give a person permission to talk for you, you must choose someone you trust. This person must know what you believe and then communicate it.

`How did Moses’ time as a shepherd help him to be God’s spokesperson? Read Exodus 2:11–15 and Exodus 3:1–3, 10–13 for the answer.`

Moses spent the first 40 years of his life getting the best education that Egypt offered. But this learning didn’t teach him to become God’s messenger. Moses needed to spend the next 40 years of his life as a shepherd in the desert. In many ways, Moses had to undo much of the education he got as a prince in Egypt. Moses would be talking for God. So, before Moses could help God lead Israel out of Egypt, Moses needed to get to know God better.

`Read the conversation between God and Moses in Exodus 4:10–16. What can this conversation teach us about how God chooses His messengers? How do His messengers work for Him?`

Moses was God’s spokesperson. Aaron spoke for Moses (Exodus 4:16; also read Exodus 7:1). God helped Moses and Aaron to decide the best way to share the message He gave them. God does the same thing with all His messengers.

Moses knew that he made mistakes in the past. Moses remembered his last meeting with his fellow Israelites. At that time, the Israelites did not believe Moses could help them. So, God understood Moses’ worries and fears. God was patient with Moses. Moses made many excuses about why he wasn’t the best person for the job. God removed all reasons for Moses’ excuses. Finally, Moses’ fears showed his doubt. He questioned God’s choice in using him. So, God commanded Moses to stop making excuses and get to work (Exodus 3:14–17).

`You may not be a special messenger. But what did God ask you to do for Him with your life? What is the best way to get ready for this work? Or, if you are already doing this work, what is the best way to continue doing it?`
