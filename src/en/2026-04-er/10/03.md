---
title:  Jesus Had Permission To Talk For God
date:   30/11/2026
---

`Did God give Jesus the power and permission to talk for Him? If yes, what was the proof? Read Matthew 21:23–27 and John 17:2 for the answers.`

While Jesus was teaching, the Jewish leaders asked Him a question. They wanted to know who gave Jesus the permission to speak for God. Jesus didn’t go to any of the local schools for religion. None of the teachers trained Jesus. Jesus answered with a question about John the Baptist: Who gave John permission to speak for God? The Jewish leaders refused to answer. In doing so, they rejected God’s power in the lives of both John and Jesus.

`Read Matthew 7:28, 29; Mark 1:21–27; Luke 8:22–25; Luke 9:1; John 5:25–27; and John 7:38. What do these verses tell us about Jesus’ power?`

All power belongs to Jesus. He made humans (John 1:3). He is our Savior (Romans 3:24). Jesus also is the Word of God in a human body. Jesus had power from God to do His work on earth. Jesus’ teaching surprised His audiences because He taught with power from God (Matthew 7:29).

The first four books of the New Testament talk about Jesus’ power as God’s messenger. Jesus forgave sins (Mark 2:10, 11). Jesus commanded demons to come out of people (Mark 3:15). Jesus knew what people were thinking (John 2:24, 25). Jesus woke up people from the dead. He offered the gift of everlasting life (John 10:28). While Jesus lived on earth, He used the power that God gave Him (John 17:22). Jesus depended on the Father and always worked together with Him (John 5:19). Jesus often used quotes from the Old Testament. Jesus said the Old Testament explained who He was. Jesus had all power in heaven and on earth. He could give power to His followers (Mark 6:7). Jesus gave power to His messengers to communicate His messages and talk for Him (Revelation 19:10).

`Jesus gave so much proof that He was sent from God. But some people rejected Him anyway. How can we be careful not to make this same terrible mistake?`
