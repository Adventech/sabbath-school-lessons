title:  The Power Of God’s Messengers
date:   04/12/2026
---

God’s messengers speak for Him. Their messages have the power to change lives for the better. Some of these messengers wrote part of the Bible. Other messengers didn’t write any part at all. Some messengers lived after the Bible was written. God gave all these men and women their messages. Their messages showed that God chose them.

Ellen G. White belongs to this last group of messengers who lived after Bible times. As Seventh-day Adventists, we do not include Ellen White’s writings as part of the Bible. We do not say that her writings are the same as the Bible. At the same time, her writings help people. They tell us what God wants us to know. Ellen White wrote, “In the messages I give, I share with you what the Lord showed me. I don’t write one article about my own ideas. I write only what God showed me in a vision. His messages are the same as precious light that shines from His throne.”—Selected Messages, book 1, page 29, adapted.

Ellen White’s writings always agree with Bible truth. She warns against sin. She tells us about our need for Jesus. Jesus is the only hope of everlasting life.

In God’s messages to Ellen White, we see the fight between good and evil. This fight started with Lucifer in heaven. It will continue to the end-times. Then sin and evil, along with Satan and his angels, will be destroyed forever. Ellen White’s writings help us understand what the Bible teaches about the end-times. She talks about the danger of joining the church with the government. She teaches us that religious freedom is important. She warns against sin and evil. She honors God’s law. She helps us understand Bible truth about the mark of the beast.

Ellen White also talks about Christian living. Her messages have helped to change many lives for the better. These messages include topics about health, education, family relationships, being parents, and being holy.

Ellen White’s writings are not in the Bible. But God uses her to help people who are living in the final days of earth’s history. Her writings speak with power and meaning to our lives.
