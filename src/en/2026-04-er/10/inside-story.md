### To Finland With Love: The Work of Elsa Luukkanen

Elsa Luukkanen was born on May 20, 1916, in Sortavala, Finland. Elsa grew up very poor. At 16, Elsa went to Seventh-day Adventist Bible study meetings. Then she was baptized. Because Elsa kept the Sabbath, she lost her home and her job for a time. But she kept her faith.

When Elsa was 17, she led her first person to Jesus. When she was 19, she started preaching.

A church member helped pay for Elsa to study at the Toivonlinna Adventist school. She worked as a Bible bookseller before she became a Bible worker. When World War 2 started, Elsa and another Bible worker named Aino held Bible meetings across Finland.

Many people came to Elsa’s meetings. Her preaching touched hearts. Often, she sang and played the guitar. In Joensuu, the Seventh-day Adventist membership grew from five to 150 people in just two years. A new church started. Elsa helped to start more churches in other cities. One Bible worker said that Elsa was “filled with the Holy Spirit. Her sermons were powerful. Her sermons touched hearts and minds and brought people to God.”

After the war, Elsa’s work for God continued. In Helsinki in 1957, Elsa preached up to three times a day to big crowds. 80 people were baptized. Elsa worked so hard. So, church leaders asked her to take a break and rest. Elsa, along with Aino, went to North America for a vacation. They went to Finnish communities. There they preached and sang. They supported their Bible work with money they made from dressmaking. Elsa named her work “vacation preaching”!

Elsa went back to Finland. In the late 1960s, she started the Adventist Welfare Society (AWS). This center gave tens of thousands of blankets and clothes to poor people all across Eastern Europe. Elsa and Aino even collected offerings to sew clothes and build a new church and a new building for AWS in Kajaani. Elsa said, “I couldn’t just preach. I had to help the children. I loved all the poor children in Finland as if I were their mother. They were the same as my children. I had to find homes for them.” Elsa Luukkanen found and built homes for these children across Finland.

Elsa died in 1996. Over her lifetime, she led 700 people to Jesus. She helped start many churches. In 1975, Elsa was honored during the worldwide Women’s Year celebration. She spent her whole life serving Jesus. She did not let war, hard times, or being poor stop her from her work.
