---
title:  God’s Messengers And Church Leadership
date:   02/12/2026
---

Hosea 12:13 says, “The Lord used a prophet [messenger] to bring the Israelites out of Egypt. He used a prophet to keep them safe in the wilderness [desert]” (EASY). As we see in this verse, part of the job of God’s messengers was to protect and lead God’s people.

If we read Judges 4:4–8; 2 Chronicles 20:14–17; Exodus 12:1–3; and Deuteronomy 1:9–16, we can see that God’s messengers showed His people His plan for leadership in their lives. In times of war or trouble, the messengers shared God’s advice with His people. Messengers also gave hope and encouragement to the people and their leaders. (Read the stories of Jehaziel and Deborah.) Messengers also gave advice to the leaders about worship and religious celebrations. At other times, the messenger helped the leaders make good decisions. God sent His messengers to help His people with advice for every part of their lives.

`How did God’s messengers help the church in New Testament times? Read Acts 6:1–7; 1 Corinthians 5:1–5; 1 Corinthians 7:10–16; and Titus 1:5 for the answer.`

When Jesus went back to heaven, He left His followers without a visible leader on earth. But Jesus told His followers what He wanted them to do: “ ‘Go everywhere in the world [earth]. Tell the Good News to everyone’ ” (Mark 16:15, ERV). Yes, Jesus chose the Twelve Apostles to lead His church before He went to heaven. Apostles were the leaders and teachers in the church. But there was nothing already in place to help the apostles get started. They needed help. So, God sent His messengers to them.

The New Testament church had many difficult ­experiences at first. How would they lead and guide the church? How would they share God’s message? How would they punish false teachings and sins in the church? God’s messengers helped answer these questions. The messengers helped keep the church safe (Acts 13:1, 2). The church couldn’t get started without God’s messengers. They helped the church move forward with its work for God.
