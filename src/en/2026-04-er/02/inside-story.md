### From Criminal to Church Leader: Part 1

_by Mission Editor_

Matthew and Martin were Marek’s best friends. If a fight happened at school, the same three teens were always to blame: Matthew, Martin, and Marek. Today, Matthew is dead. Martin spent seven years in jail. Marek used to sell bad drugs for a living. Now he is one of the leaders for the Seventh-day Adventist Church in Poland. What happened?

Marek grew up in a Christian family in southern Poland. When Marek was 18 years old, his girlfriend left him. He prayed: “God, I want her back.” Nothing happened. Then he prayed, “God, I don’t want to wake up anymore.” Again, nothing happened.

Marek stopped praying. He stopped believing in God. He started taking bad drugs. Then his friend Martin decided to sell them. Martin invited Marek to sell them, too. Marek joined him.

Two years later, Marek wanted to go to a big New Year’s Eve party. But he couldn’t find anyone to join him. So, he decided not to go. He went to visit his grandmother. His grandmother had a picture of Jesus in her home. Marek stared at the picture. He thought, “Even if I don’t believe in God, Jesus was real.” Marek remembered reading the Bible as a boy.

A few days later, Marek visited a fortuneteller. The fortuneteller warned Marek that life on Planet Earth would end soon. Marek was surprised. He asked the woman when that would happen. She said in one year.

Marek started to read the book of Revelation. He hoped to learn how life on earth would end. Then he started to read about Jesus in the New Testament. Marek was amazed to learn that Jesus had a kingdom where people would be happy and live forever.

Later, after a party, Marek sat in a car with some friends. Out of the window he saw a bookstore named “Signs of the Times.”

The next day, Marek went to the bookstore and asked, “Do you have some books about a man named Nostradamus?”

The woman behind the counter said, “No. But if you want to know about the future, we have The Great Controversy.”

Marek bought the book. He was amazed at what he read. He checked what he read with the Bible. Everything matched.

At night, Marek read The Great Controversy in bed. He asked himself, “Am I getting ‘light’ from God when I read this book?”

Just then, the lightbulb above his head started to blink. That was strange! Marek saw a reflection of himself in a window. All he could see was his head and the light bulb above it. The bulb stopped blinking and shone brightly again.

Marek believed God used the lightbulb to answer his question. For the first time in his life, Marek felt God’s love. Marek knelt and said, “God, if You are this loving, I want to serve You.”
