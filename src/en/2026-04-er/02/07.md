date:   09/10/2026
---

God chooses His messengers to do special work at a special time. These men and women share the messages needed most for their time. Seventh-day Adventists believe that Ellen G. White was God’s special messenger. God chose Ellen in December 1844. A few weeks earlier, the Millerite Adventists expected Jesus to come back and life on earth to end. They believed that Daniel 8:14 taught that Jesus was coming in 1844: “It [the things in Daniel’s dream] will continue for 2,300 evenings and mornings. After that, God’s temple will be clean again” (Daniel 8:14, EASY).

William Miller’s followers expected Jesus to come back to this earth on October 22, 1844. Thousands of people across North America and worldwide waited for Jesus to come back. Soon they would be with their Savior. He would take them to heaven.

But Jesus didn’t come. The Millerite Adventists were so sad. That time when Jesus didn’t come back is named the Great Disappointment.

During that time, God gave Ellen Harmon [White] her first dream. The dream filled her and the disappointed followers with hope. God was with them. They saw that they misunderstood Daniel 8:14. They got the date right. But they were wrong about what happened at that time. Ellen Harmon [White’s] dream showed a path leading from earth to the Holy City, the new Jerusalem. On this path, the Advent people traveled to the Holy City. “Jesus was in front of them. Jesus led them to the city. If the people kept looking at Jesus, they were safe.”—Ellen G. White, Spiritual Gifts, volume 2, page 31, adapted. This dream was good news. God was leading a new group of followers out of the Great Disappointment. Jesus was with His people. He was coming back. They just needed to keep looking at Him. God gave His people many reasons not to give up hope, even after Jesus didn’t come back when they expected.

Ellen G. Harmon [White] was a 17-year-old teenager when God chose her to be His messenger. Her job wasn’t easy. Slowly, people accepted that Ellen’s dreams were from God. But Ellen was afraid to be God’s messenger. How would she answer God? Next week, we will look at Old Testament messengers and their work. Ellen had some of the same problems they did, as we will see next Friday.
