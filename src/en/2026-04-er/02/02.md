---
title:  Abraham, Defender Of God’s Promise
date:   04/10/2026
---

From Genesis to Revelation, we see that God always has a loyal group of followers (read Jeremiah 31:7, 31–34). Abram is an example of one of these loyal followers.

God communicated the rules of His Promise to Abraham. God showed Abraham how He would save sinners (Genesis 15:6–17). Later, God gave Abraham a test of faith. This test showed how God would give His Son Jesus to die for our sins. God asked Abraham to give his son Isaac as an offering to Him (Genesis 22:1–17). This test was very hard for Abraham. Think about all the reasons why Abraham could have refused to obey God. But Abraham obeyed. God stopped Abraham from killing Isaac. God sent a ram as a substitute.

God sent His messengers to help His people to come back to Him. Many of God’s messengers also warned His people about terrible happenings or punishments in the future. But not every messenger warned about the future. Often God’s messengers warned God’s people against sin and about not hurting the poor and needy. God’s messengers also warned His people not to break the rules of His Promise.

Most important, God’s messengers helped His people to understand the promise about the coming Savior. Today, God’s messengers help us understand the work God gives us to do. We must warn other people about what the Bible says will happen in the future. Part of this message is the Good News that Jesus saves us from our sins. As His loyal people, we will keep His commandments (Revelation 12:17).

`For sure, Abraham wasn’t perfect. But what ­experiences helped Abraham to serve God? Read Genesis 15:6; Galatians 3:6–9; and Hebrews 11:8 for the answer.`

Abraham’s story helps us understand our own time. Today, in the same way as in Abraham’s day, people try to replace Bible truth with false teachings. As Abraham did, we must show God’s love to people daily. Part of our life’s work includes announcing the three angels’ messages (Revelation 14:6–12).

`The Bible says that “Abram believed the Lord. The Lord was pleased with Abram because he believed. So Abram’s faith made him right [accepted] with the Lord” (Genesis 15:6, NIrV). Where is the promise of the Good News in this verse? What hope does this promise give us?`
