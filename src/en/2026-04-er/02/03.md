`Read the story in 1 Kings 18:19–39. What does this story teach us about the type of work that God asks His messengers to do?`

Elijah’s work and his message show us that God chose him to be His messenger. God sent Elijah to lead Israel back to God. Elijah went to King Ahab. Elijah announced that there would be no more rain for a long time. God used this disaster to help His people stop worshiping the false gods Baal and Asherah. King Ahab and his countrymen blamed Elijah for their misery. They tried to kill Elijah.

On Carmel, Elijah challenged the messengers of the false gods to a test. Elijah asked the Israelites to give up their false gods and come back to the Lord. The people finally understood that their choice caused them to reject God. They were now ready to come back to God again. God was more powerful than their false gods, which weren’t even real.

Just before Jesus comes back, God commands His church to announce the Elijah message again to everyone on earth. But first, God commands His people to turn away from their own ‘false gods’ and get ready for His coming. God continues to use His messengers in the Bible to communicate with us. The Bible and the writings of Ellen G. White continue to give us warnings, advice, and encouragement.

“We need a new life in Jesus more than we need anything else. The first thing we must do is ask God for this gift. God wants very much to give it to us. So, why don’t we have it? We aren’t ready. So, we must do everything we can to get ready for it.”—Ellen G. White, Selected Messages, book 1, page 121, adapted.

`How does the Elijah message change the lives and homes of people? Read Malachi 4:5, 6 for the answer.`

A new life in Jesus starts in the heart and home. A new life in the home causes new life in the church, the community, and the country. That’s God’s plan for the home.
