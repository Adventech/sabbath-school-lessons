date:   07/10/2026
---

Most Christians know the story in Daniel 1. In this story, Daniel honors the health laws that he learned as a child. Daniel is a powerful example of faith (Daniel 1:8). He refused to eat the food or drink the wine from the king’s table (Daniel 1:12–16). Daniel chose to eat a plant-food diet. He ate vegetables, fruits, nuts, seeds, and grains. Daniel also chose to drink only water. This diet test was small compared to the tests that God gave to Daniel later. But this test helped Daniel to stay loyal to God later on when his faith could get him killed.

`How did Daniel share God’s love and truth with the people around him? Read Daniel 2:46–48; Daniel 4:18; Daniel 5:13, 14; and Daniel 6:25–27 for the answer.`

Look what these people say about Daniel’s God! The stories about Daniel’s life show us important Bible truth. When we obey God, we can teach other people about the God of heaven. Our God is more powerful than any false god or king.

Daniel’s name means ‘God is my judge.’ God’s work as our Judge is an important topic in the book of Daniel. So, Daniel’s name helps us remember to get ready and stay ready for the Second Coming. We must keep God’s commandments, even with the ‘little’ things in life.

Think about the statue in chapter 2. Daniel wrote this chapter 500 years before Jesus was born. The statue shows us the beginning and end of four powerful kingdoms. The feet and toes of the statue show us the countries of Europe in our time. Many people tried to join these countries with marriages, wars, or promises. But the countries continue to stay separate, just as Daniel said. Daniel’s dream gives us powerful reasons to trust God’s messengers. The Soviet Union is proof of that. No one thought the Soviet Union would break apart. But it broke into separate countries. Why? Because Daniel 2 shows us that the countries of Europe will stay separate until Jesus comes back.

`All the kingdoms in Daniel 2 came and went, just as Daniel said. God’s everlasting kingdom is the next and last kingdom. Daniel has been right so far. Why is the statue in Daniel 2 a good reason to trust God about what will happen next?`
