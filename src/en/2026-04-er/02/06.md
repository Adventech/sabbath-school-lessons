---
title:  "John The Baptist: Getting People Ready"
date:   08/10/2026
---

`What special work did God give to John the Baptist? Read Mark 1:2–5 for the answer.`

The book of Mark tells us that John the Baptist is the man that God promised to send (Isaiah 40:3). John’s job was to announce Jesus as the Lamb of God. John was loyal to God his whole life. When Satan heard John preach, he got scared. Satan feared he would lose his control over Israel and the whole human family. (Read Ellen G. White, The Desire of Ages, page 224.) John showed the people their sins. John’s powerful message made Satan afraid that he would lose his hold on people’s hearts. So, Satan tried again and again to get John to sin and give up his work.

`Read Matthew 11:1–6. While John was in prison, what did Jesus say to make John’s faith strong?`

Satan couldn’t get Jesus to sin. Jesus won the fight against sin and Satan in the desert. Jesus’ win made Satan so angry. So, Satan changed his plan. He got John thrown in jail. John was Jesus’ cousin. The news about John hurt Jesus. Satan wanted to break John and to fill Jesus’ heart with pain. But Jesus sent John a message of encouragement. John didn’t understand why he was in jail when Jesus was so powerful. But Jesus’ words helped John. John stayed loyal to Jesus, even when he died.

Today, Jesus gives our church a message to share (Revelation 14:6–12) with everyone on earth. Just as John did, we must warn people to stop sinning and accept Jesus. Satan will try to make us doubt the work God gives us. We do not need to lose hope if we remember how God led us in the past. Sometimes things will not go well for us. Then we must remember John the Baptist. An evil woman threw him in jail. A weak king killed him. When John was in jail, he did not lose faith. Whatever people do to us, we can pray to God in faith. He will answer us. God promises us power to do His work (Acts 1:8). Today, we live in a time when many people reject Jesus and His teachings. So, we must be loyal followers for Jesus, wherever we are.
