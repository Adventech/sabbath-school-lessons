---
title:  Isaiah, Messenger Of The Good News
date:   06/10/2026
---

Isaiah is one of God’s most important messengers in the Old Testament. God personally asked Isaiah to serve Him (read Isaiah 6:1–8). Isaiah didn’t feel worthy. Then an angel flew to Isaiah with a burning coal. The coal came from the altar in heaven. The angel touched the coal to Isaiah’s lips. The touch of the hot coal ‘removed’ Isaiah’s sins.

God sent Isaiah to warn Israel and its leaders of their sins. This task was very important. Isaiah’s messages help us understand God and His plan to save sinners. One of Isaiah’s most famous messages is chapter 53. This chapter shows us Jesus’ death and explains what it did for us.

`Read Isaiah 53. God gave this message to Israel more than 500 years before Jesus died on the cross. How does this message show us Jesus’ death for us? What does this message teach us about why we can trust the Bible?`

Isaiah’s messages tell us about the Savior’s plan and work (Isaiah 53:3–5). Isaiah explained why Jesus “was like [the same as] a lamb that people were leading away to kill” (Isaiah 53:7, EASY). Isaiah also talked about how Jesus died for the sins of all people in every age of history.

“We have all turned away, like [the same as] sheep that take the wrong path. Each of us has turned to follow our own way. But the Lord has taken all our sins and put the punishment on him” (Isaiah 53:6, EASY). In this verse, Isaiah shows us the sin problem. This verse also shows us God’s answer to it. More than any other chapter in the Bible, this chapter plainly shows God’s plan to save sinners.

Isaiah’s job wasn’t easy. People laughed at his warnings. Today, people may do the same thing when we warn them about sin (Romans 6:23). Isaiah’s warning for Israel is for us, too (Isaiah 2:4; Isaiah 5:20). The Israelites weren’t fair. They refused to change their lives. So, God destroyed Jerusalem. The same sin will cause the people on this earth to be destroyed when Jesus comes back.

`Read Isaiah 53 again. Write down everything Jesus does for us. What hope does Isaiah’s message give you?`
