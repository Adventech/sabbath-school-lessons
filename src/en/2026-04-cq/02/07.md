As we journey through this study, it’s important to engage deeply with the material and reflect on its implications for our faith and daily lives. In your Sabbath School class (or Bible study group), use the outline of questions below to lead a thoughtful discussion based on this week’s main passage and supporting verses.

**Isaiah’s calling:**

1. What kind of vision made Isaiah feel fully unworthy and unprepared to be God’s messenger? What made him willing to embrace God’s call to prophetic ministry? Isa. 6:1–8
2. How would Isaiah’s audiences respond to his messages? Isa. 6:9–11

**The gospel prophet:**

1. How did Isaiah’s prophecies reveal with astounding clarity the suffering, trials, death, and burial of Christ more than five hundred years ahead of time? Isa. 53
2. How did Isaiah also predict the ministry of John the Baptist as the forerunner of Christ? Isa. 40:3; Matt. 3:1–3

`Personal reflection: While he certainly warned of coming disasters, Isaiah’s prophecies are filled with beautiful assurances of the promised Messiah. What might this teach us about the kind of prophetic messages God wants us to share with the world today?`

**Profiles of other prophets:**

1. What kind of hardships did John the Baptist endure during his prophetic ministry? What does this teach us about the types of difficulties often faced by God’s prophets? Matt. 11:1–6
2. What parallels do you see between the story of God calling Isaiah and the story of God calling Abraham? Gen. 12:1–6
3. How was the gospel revealed to Abraham through the covenant God made with him? Gen. 15:6–17; 22:1–17
4. How did Daniel prove himself faithful when tested both as a young man and as an old man?
Dan. 1:8–16; Dan. 6

`Personal reflection: The prophets of the Bible faced incredible challenges and made tremendous sacrifices. Who is your favorite Bible prophet? How does his/her story inspire you?`

**Key takeaways to remember:**

- Prophets received their call to prophetic ministry directly from God.
- Prophets were to reveal the promise of salvation found in the Messiah.
- Being a prophet was a difficult calling. Their lives were often not easy.
