### Daniel, the Faithful Seer

Daniel is another key Old Testament prophet whose writings reveal the future of God’s people. When Jesus was answering questions about Jerusalem’s future, He encouraged His followers to pay attention to Daniel’s writings (Matt. 24:15).

Most Christians know the story of Daniel 1, in which he stood firm for the health principles he had learned as a child. Daniel was a model of faithfulness (Dan. 1:8). When presented with royal delicacies, he insisted on eating a plant-based diet and drinking only water instead of partaking in the decadent foods and wines offered from the king’s table (Dan. 1:12–16). This was a small test compared to what would come later, such as in Daniel 6, when his faithfulness should have meant death.

The pagan leaders who worked with him noticed Daniel’s faithfulness. They acknowledged the power of Daniel’s God. However unique Daniel’s situation, his story reminds us of a crucial principle: by our lives, our faithfulness, and the power of God working in and through us, others can and should learn about the God of heaven, that He is “the God of gods, and the Lord of kings” (Dan. 2:47).

Because the recurring theme of Daniel’s prophecies is judgment, culminating in Christ’s second coming (God’s ultimate victory), the meaning of Daniel’s name, “God is my judge,” is quite appropriate. His writings deserve careful study and attention, as they are relevant today. Daniel’s prophecies serve as a reminder to always remain vigilant and keep God’s commandments, even in the little things of life.

As a prophet, Daniel foretold the rise and fall of nations and the consequences of their disobedience to God. Through his prophetic visions, Daniel revealed God’s plan and the ultimate victory of God’s kingdom (Dan. 7:13, 14). Think, for example, about Daniel 2, written half a millennium before Christ was born. Daniel accurately depicted the rise and fall of the four great world empires: Babylon, Medo-Persia, Greece, and Rome. His prophecy continued right up to our time, describing the feet and toes of the statue that clearly refer to the nations of modern Europe. As the prophecy states, “they shall mingle themselves with the seed of men: but they shall not cleave one to another, even as iron is not mixed with clay” (Dan. 2:43, KJV). What an amazing prediction! Even to this day, despite intermarriage and attempts at unity, the continent remains as separate nations with separate laws, customs, and traditions. Although several attempts to unite Europe seemed unstoppable and destined to succeed, such as those of Charlemagne, Napoleon, and Hitler, they all failed. Daniel’s accurate and specific predictions give us powerful reasons to trust God’s prophets.
