As we journey through this study, it’s important to engage deeply with the material and reflect on its implications for our faith and daily lives. In your Sabbath School class (or Bible study group), use the outline of questions below to lead a thoughtful discussion based on this week’s main passage and supporting verses.

**Help to understand:**

1. Why did the visitor from Ethiopia need someone to help him understand the meaning of Isaiah’s prophecy? Acts 8:26–35
2. What resulted from this Bible study? Acts 8:36–40
3. How did Jesus help His disciples understand the meaning of the scriptures? Luke 24:25–27, 44, 45

`Personal reflection: How have others helped you to understand the Bible? How are you helping others to better understand the Bible?`

**The importance of proper interpretation:**

1. How did Jesus acknowledge the need to rightly interpret the meaning of Scripture? Luke 10:26
2. In the grand scheme of the great controversy, how does Satan constantly try to lead people to misinterpret Scripture? Matt. 4:5–7
3. What are the dangers of private interpretation and twisting the meaning of Scripture? 2 Pet. 1:20; 3:16
4. Why should we pray that God gives us a good understanding of truth? Ps. 119:125; Dan. 9:13

`Personal reflection: How can you be safe from the deceptions of people who misuse Scripture?`

**Principles for interpretation:**

1. Why is it important to first look at the immediate context to understand the proper interpretation of a passage? Consider the example of Dan. 2:31–45.
2. How do some books of the Bible help interpret other books of the Bible? Consider the example of 2 Chron. 36:20–23.

`Personal reflection: What other principles can help us properly interpret the Bible?`

**Key takeaways to remember:**

- God needs us to explain the Bible to people who could never understand without help.
- Satan tries to get people to misinterpret Scripture so he can lead them astray.
- The Bible is a unified whole, and it interprets itself.
