### Reading Ellen G. White

It’s important to correctly understand and apply Ellen G. White’s writings. Here are a few guidelines:

First, begin with a prayerful, positive attitude and an open mind, rather than a spirit of doubt. Our mindset is a lens that colors all we hear, read, and see. We need the Holy Spirit to guide our thinking. As Ellen White herself noted: “If you search the Scriptures to vindicate your own opinions, you will never reach the truth. Search in order to learn what the Lord says.”<sup>1</sup> The same is true regarding her writings.

Second, consider the context of what you read. People have often based their understanding of Ellen White’s writings on an isolated statement or paragraph removed from its original historical and literary context. Ellen White wrote that “time and place must be considered.”<sup>2</sup> This unfortunate practice of ignoring context has caused, and continues to cause, no end of trouble for some church members.

Third, focus on principles. What’s the main point (or points) that Ellen was emphasizing in a particular passage? For example, in 1894, Ellen wrote to church members in Battle Creek, Michigan, condemning the purchase of bicycles.<sup>3</sup> The principle that Ellen was seeking to encourage was proper stewardship of God-given resources, such as money, because bicycles were very expensive back then.

Fourth, study everything Ellen White wrote on a particular topic. Examine obscure statements in the context of her general teachings on a subject. During her lifetime, Ellen White lamented the misuse of her writings: “Much that purports to be a message from Sister White, serves the purpose of misrepresenting Sister White, making her testify in favor of things that are not in accordance with her mind or judgment.”<sup>4</sup>

Finally, make sure Ellen White truly said it. Numerous statements are falsely attributed to Ellen G. White. The safest way to be sure that what we read is from Ellen White is to look for a reference to its source. Once we have verified where it’s found, we can examine it using the correct principles.

We’ve been given a wonderful gift in Ellen G. White’s prophetic writings. It’s so important that we properly read those writings, which can be—and are—such a blessing to millions. It’s tragic when they’re misused and misapplied.
