As we journey through this study, it’s important to engage deeply with the material and reflect on its implications for our faith and daily lives. In your Sabbath School class (or Bible study group), use the outline of questions below to lead a thoughtful discussion based on this week’s main passage and supporting verses.

**Resisting the message:**

1. How did King Jehoiakim respond to the prophetic warning? Jer. 36:20–26
2. How did God respond to Jehoiakim’s act of defiance? Jer. 36:27–31
3. How was Jeremiah’s message received some years later by King Zedekiah? 2 Chron. 36:11, 12
4. How did the nation of Judah respond to the messages God sent them through His prophets? 2 Chron. 36:15, 16
5. Why do you think this history of rejecting the prophets grieved Jesus so deeply? Matt. 23:37

`Personal reflection: How does Jesus respond when we ignore the messengers He sends?`

**A history for us:**

1. How did the Israelites resist God’s leaders and prophets during their wilderness wanderings? Num. 14:1–4
2. According to the apostle Paul, why did God inspire prophets to record the stories we have in the Bible? 1 Cor. 10:6–11
3. How does the Bible give us hope that we are not doomed to repeat the mistakes of the past? 1 Cor. 10:12, 13

`Personal reflection: The Israelites mocked God’s messengers, despised His words, and scoffed at His prophets. Surely, we would never do anything like that today. . . or would we? Do you think there are subtler ways of undermining or dismissing the prophets without openly opposing them?`

**Key takeaways to remember:**

- God’s people often resisted the prophets’ messages.
- Jesus is grieved when His people reject the messengers He sends them.
- The stories in the Bible were written to help us avoid repeating the history of those who rejected God’s messages.
