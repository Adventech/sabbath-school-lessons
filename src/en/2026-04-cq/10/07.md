As we journey through this study, it’s important to engage deeply with the material and reflect on its implications for our faith and daily lives. In your Sabbath School class (or Bible study group), use the outline of questions below to lead a thoughtful discussion based on this week’s main passage and supporting verses.

**A spokesperson for God:**

1. How did Moses’ time as a shepherd prepare him to be a spokesperson for God? Exod. 2:11–15; 3:1–3, 10–13
2. What can we learn from the conversation between God and Moses about how prophets are called and the role they are given? Exod. 4:10–16
3. What can we learn from the relationship between Moses and Aaron about the role of prophets? Exod. 7:1, 2
4. Who was the ultimate spokesperson for God, and how was His authority questioned and challenged? Heb. 1:1–3; Matt. 21:23–27

`Personal reflection: How well do your words, actions, or influence represent God to others?`

**Organizational authority:**

1. How was Moses’ prophetic role instrumental in establishing and organizing the nation of Israel? Hosea 12:13
2. After Moses, how did God continue to use prophets as part of His leadership strategy for Israel? Judg. 4:4–8; 2 Chron. 20:14–20
3. How did God use the prophetic role to structure the early church for mission, respond to challenges in the church, and guide the church’s expansion? Acts 6:1–7; 1 Cor. 5:1–5; Titus 1:5

`Personal reflection: Where in your life do you most need God’s direction, correction, or encouragement right now? How open are you to receiving that guidance through whatever means God chooses? Do you tend to listen, resist, delay, or act quickly?`

**Authority of non-canonical prophets:**

1. Did some prophets write books that were not included in the Bible? 1 Chron. 29:29; 2 Chron. 9:29
2. Did prophets who did not write books of the Bible have less authority? Luke 7:28

`Personal reflection: In what ways should prophetic writings continue to provide relevant guidance for the church’s work and mission today?`

**Key takeaways to remember:**

- God calls prophets to be His spokespersons. Jesus came to this world as God’s ultimate spokesperson.
- God used prophets to guide the formation and organization of His people in both the Old and New Testaments.
- Scripture affirms the authority of non-canonical prophets—those who did not write books of the Bible.
