### Speaking with Authority

The prophets speak to us with authority and relevance because God has chosen them as His messengers. There are canonical prophets (those whose writings became part of the Bible), non-canonical prophets (those who did not write a book in the Bible), and post-biblical, non-canonical prophets (those who appear after New Testament times). All were equally inspired, and their messages bore the stamp of divine authority.

Ellen G. White belongs to the post-biblical, non-canonical group of prophets. Seventh-day Adventists do not consider her writings an addition to the Bible nor equal to it. Her writings are, however, relevant and they speak with authority, a point stated by Ellen White herself:

In these letters which I write, in the testimonies I bear, I am presenting to you that which the Lord has presented to me. I do not write one article in the paper expressing merely my own ideas. They are what God has opened before me in vision—the precious rays of light shining from the throne.<sup>1</sup>

Consistent with the Bible, Ellen White’s writings highlight the reality of sin. Another powerful theme in her messages is our need for Jesus, the only hope of eternal life, which is given by grace to all who, by faith, claim it for themselves.

Through her messages (or rather God’s messages through her), we see the battle between good and evil, starting with the fall of Lucifer in heaven, and continuing through to the end of time, when sin and evil, along with Satan and his fallen angels, will be forever destroyed. Ellen White’s writings provide insight into biblical principles within the end-time context. To give a few examples, she spoke of the danger of merging church and state and the importance of religious liberty; she warned about the decay of morality; and she uplifted God’s law and its significance. Additionally, though our beliefs about some end-time topics, such as the mark of the beast, are not based on her writings, her words often provide a fuller picture and impart wisdom we would not otherwise have.

She also emphasized the practical application of Christian living. Her messages about spirituality, health, education, family relations, parenting, moral life, and much more have helped many to change their lifestyles and thus experience better, more fulfilling lives in a number of aspects. Ellen White’s writings are not part of the Bible, but God has used her to guide the believers who are living in the final days of earth’s history. Her writings speak with prophetic authority and relevance.
