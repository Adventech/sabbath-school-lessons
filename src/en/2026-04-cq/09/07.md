As we journey through this study, it’s important to engage deeply with the material and reflect on its implications for our faith and daily lives. In your Sabbath School class (or Bible study group), use the outline of questions below to lead a thoughtful discussion based on this week’s main passage and supporting verses.

**Examining the fruit:**

1. Why is it important to examine the life and ministry of anyone claiming to be a prophet? Matt. 7:15–20
2. Though true prophets have good fruits, do they ever make mistakes and have character flaws? Jon. 1:1–3; Acts 15:36–39; Gal. 2:11–14

**Testing the prophets:**

1. How should we respond to someone claiming to receive prophetic messages? 1 Thess. 5:19–21
2. What types of supernatural manifestations can be expected with prophets? Num. 12:6; Dan. 10:7–17
3. Are supernatural manifestations and fulfilled prophecies necessarily proof that someone is a true prophet? Deut. 13:1–5
4. How should every prophet be tested? Isa. 8:20; 1 John 4:1–3
5. How must a true prophet relate to the truth of Jesus? 1 John 4:1–3

`Personal reflection: Supernatural occurrences are more common in some cultures than in others. Why must we be very careful when exploring whether something supernatural really is of God? What are some ways we can tell?`

**Predicting the future:**

1. How do fulfilled predictions help verify whether a prophet is true or false? Jer. 28:9, 15–17
2. In seeing whether a prophecy has come true, why is it important to understand and remember the nature of conditional prophecies? Jer. 18:7–10; Jon. 3:4–10
3. How do conditional prophecies influence your perspective of God’s character?

`Personal reflection: If someone came to you claiming to be a prophet with a message for you, how would you respond? What do you believe are the most important tests of a prophet?`

**Key takeaways to remember:**

- Prophets should not be automatically rejected but should be carefully tested.
- True prophets have good fruits in their lives and ministries. Their messages uplift Jesus and agree with the teachings of the Bible.
- Prophets accurately predict the future, but some prophecies are conditional on people’s responses.
