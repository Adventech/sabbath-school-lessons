**Read This Week’s Passage:** Matthew 7:13–29

### Like a Diamond

A real diamond is one of the most valuable gems on earth. Of course, many other things can be made to look nearly identical to diamonds, but these are ultimately worthless. The best way to tell whether a diamond is real or fake is, of course, to test it. Most tests are not conclusive on their own, but if a diamond passes several tests, one can be confident that the diamond is real.

A diamond is one of the strongest materials on earth. A genuine diamond can withstand very high temperatures and dramatic temperature changes. To test a diamond, you can heat it with a lighter or a torch and then drop it into cold water. A real diamond will not be damaged, but a fake diamond will likely shatter.

The gift of prophecy is more valuable than any diamond, but someone who claims to have this gift cannot simply be taken at face value. That claim must be tested (1 Thess. 5:19–21). No single piece of evidence confirms a prophetic ministry. Anyone who claims to be a prophet should be evaluated according to a variety of biblical tests to determine whether the prophetic manifestation is genuine. The Bible offers certain non-negotiable qualifications for true prophets that must be relied on to discern the difference between true and false claims.

One important test is to assess the role of the supernatural. During the Exodus, the Lord spoke to His people about the prophetic gift among them, saying, “Hear now My words: If there is a prophet among you, I, the Lord, make Myself known to him in a vision; I speak to him in a dream” (Num. 12:6). A prophetic message and calling involves the supernatural; it undeniably goes beyond a mere impression or idea that a person may have.

A prophet receives direct communication from God, often through prophetic dreams or visions (Dan. 8:1; Isa. 1:1; Acts 9:10, etc.), that may be accompanied by marked physical phenomena. When Daniel received visions from God, certain unmistakably supernatural phenomena occurred (Dan. 10:7–19). Daniel stopped breathing and lost his strength as he went into vision. He was then dependent on God for the duration of the experience. Although those who were with him did not go into vision, the presence of the Divine was so strong that they ran away and hid in terror. God sometimes uses physical phenomena to draw attention to a prophet so that the prophetic message will be noted.

Though visible phenomena show that a supernatural power is at work, this does not automatically mean the person experiencing the phenomena is inspired by God (Deut. 13:1–3). Satan can demonstrate the supernatural too. Apart from physical phenomena, a prophet’s message must also be tested. For us to validate someone as God’s messenger, they must pass all the biblical tests of a prophet.

The Bible is always the final standard of any claim to inspiration (Isa. 8:20). Prophets must be tested by comparing what they say with what the Bible says. A true prophet will never contradict, minimize, or draw attention away from biblical truth. That’s why knowledge of Scripture is so crucial for those who want to follow the Lord and not be deceived by false teachers or false prophets.

In the Old and New Testaments, successive prophets often highlighted and pointed back to previous prophetic writings as the standard for their own prophetic ministry. Later biblical prophets revealed additional insights into God’s plans and the way of salvation, but they never contradicted basic principles and concepts already given by God.

The Old Testament foundation for understanding the plan of salvation was established in the first prophecy given by God Himself, in Genesis 3:15. The sacrificial system exemplified this plan, but the details of how God’s Lamb, Jesus Christ, would come, live, and die for humanity were slowly clarified in later prophecies. The biblical record shows that God unfolds truth when His people are able to comprehend it. This allows the understanding of truth to deepen with each new generation.

There is, however, a crucial point that cannot be overemphasized: new revelations of truth will never contradict the truth God has already given.
