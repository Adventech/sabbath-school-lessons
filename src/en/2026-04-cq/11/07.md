As we journey through this study, it’s important to engage deeply with the material and reflect on its implications for our faith and daily lives. In your Sabbath School class (or Bible study group), use the outline of questions below to lead a thoughtful discussion based on this week’s main passage and supporting verses.

**Crucial phases in history:**

1. How did God use the prophetic role to warn and save His people during earth’s early history? Heb. 11:7
2. Why was the prophetic role important during the time of the judges? Judg. 4:14
3. How did prophets such as Elijah call leaders and people to faithfulness during the time of the kings? 1 Kings 18:18–21
4. How does God use prophetic revelation to guide and sustain His church during their time of waiting? Rev. 1–3
5. How important is the prophetic role in the last days? Joel 2:28–31

`Personal reflection: Why do you think God would want to use the prophetic role again for His people in the last days?`

**God’s close relationship with His people:**

1. Who does God reveal His plans to before major events? Gen. 18:17–19; Amos 3:2, 7
2. How did the New Testament church learn the mysteries of God? Eph. 3:3–5
3. Why does Jesus want to make all things known to His people? John 15:15
4. Why did God continue sending prophets to His people despite their unwillingness to listen?
Neh. 9:30; Matt. 23:37

`Personal reflection: Think about God’s faithfulness even when Israel turned away from Him. How have you seen this aspect of God’s loving character in your own life? Where would you be if He weren’t so patient and loving?`

**Key takeaways to remember:**

- God sends prophets at crucial moments in history to reveal His plans.
- God uses the prophetic role again in the last days to guide His people.
- God wants to share His thoughts with His people because of His close relationship with them.
