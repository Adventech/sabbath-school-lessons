As we journey through this study, it’s important to engage deeply with the material and reflect on its implications for our faith and daily lives. In your Sabbath School class (or Bible study group), use the outline of questions below to lead a thoughtful discussion based on this week’s main passage and supporting verses.

**Believe the prophets:**

1. What serious crisis did King Jehoshaphat and his people face? 2 Chron. 20:1–4, 12
2. What counsel did God’s prophet give King Jehoshaphat? 2 Chron. 20:14–17
3. How does God bless those who heed the prophetic word? 2 Chron. 20:20
4. What’s the result of turning away from the prophets? Jer. 26:4–6

`Personal reflection: Considering modern manifestations of the gift of prophecy, what blessings have you gained from Ellen G. White’s writings?`

**Jesus, the best gift:**

1. How does the gift of prophecy exalt Jesus? 1 Cor. 2:2; Gal. 6:14
2. What’s the gift of prophecy called in the book of Revelation, and why will it be important in the last days? Rev. 12:17; 19:10
3. What happens in our hearts when we heed the prophetic word? 2 Pet. 1:19

`Personal reflection: If Jesus chooses to speak through prophets (see Rev. 1:1–3), why might it be critical for us to carefully consider the gift of prophecy and how we relate to it?`

**Strengthening the foundations:**

1. How does the genuine prophetic gift affirm the Bible? 2 Tim. 3:14–17
2. How will the truths of the Bible be subtly undermined in the last days? 2 Tim. 3:13; 4:1–4

`Personal reflection: What main ideas do you want to remember and apply from studying the gift of prophecy this quarter?`

**Key takeaways to remember:**

- The Bible promises special blessings to those who heed the prophets and receive their counsel.
- The genuine gift of prophecy always exalts Jesus and draws people closer to Him.
- True prophets affirm the Bible and strengthen people’s faith in it.
