### An Established and Prosperous People

The blessings God longs to give His people depend on their willingness to cooperate with Him and their openness to receive His gifts. God often cannot bless His people to the degree He wants to because of their reluctance and unbelief. Many times in Israel’s history, God could not bless the nation because of their determined resistance against the prophets’ messages. However, Jehoshaphat was one king of Judah who welcomed the prophets’ message and helped the people of his nation follow God’s counsel.

King Jehoshaphat was tested when large armies of Moabites, Ammonites, and their allies marched against Judah. Understanding the severity of the threat, the king proclaimed a set time for the nation to come together and seek the Lord through fasting and prayer (2 Chron. 20:1–12). Jehoshaphat humbled his heart and publicly acknowledged that they didn’t have the wisdom or power to resist their enemies. He confessed that their only hope was in God. He claimed God’s promises and pled for deliverance.

God responded through the prophet Jahaziel, who told them not to fear because this was the Lord’s battle, and God would be with them (2 Chron. 20:14–17). It would’ve been easy for the people to dwell on the strength of the enemy armies and allow doubt and fear to take over. However, Jehoshaphat placed full confidence in the word of God given through the prophets. He rose early the next morning to encourage the people and strengthen their resolve.

As they marched toward battle, Jehoshaphat assured the people, “Believe in the Lord your God, and you shall be established; believe His prophets, and you shall prosper” (2 Chron. 20:20). He inspired the people with confidence in God and reminded them that God would give a special blessing to those who followed the prophets’ counsel.

Following Jehoshaphat’s encouragement, those who believed the prophets were filled with boldness and deep faith in God. They set aside their fears and marched into battle, trusting that God would deliver them from their enemies and bring them victory.

The prophets helped the Israelites know which battles to fight and what the outcome of those battles would be. Today, the prophetic word is just as needed to help us understand the issues we face and how to prepare for what’s coming next. Those who reject the prophetic word will undoubtedly be led astray, but those who heed it will be established.
