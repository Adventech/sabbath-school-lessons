### Modern Manifestations

In other words, modern manifestations of the prophetic gift—the latter rain—will look a lot like previous manifestations of the former rain at Pentecost.

Most people of faith today accept the legitimacy of ancient biblical prophets, but do they believe the same is possible in our day? Many Christians today are quite skeptical of any modern manifestation of the gift of prophecy. However, the Bible promises that the moving of God’s Spirit in the last days will include God guiding people through prophetic visions and dreams. This promise should make us wonder what believers might expect from a modern prophet. If the promise in Joel 2:28–31 of a glorious end-time manifestation of the prophetic gift is to be believed, what might it look like?

The outpouring of the Holy Spirit at Pentecost in a.d. 31 is an excellent picture of what will happen during the end-time outpouring of the Holy Spirit. Pentecost was characterized by uplifting Jesus, widespread repentance, and the creation of a people ready and equipped for God’s mission. The Holy Spirit empowered Peter’s Christ-centered message, leading three thousand souls to repent and be baptized. This “former rain” blessing of the Holy Spirit also planted a precious seed, the Church—a unified body of believers who exemplify and share the love of Jesus Christ while heralding His return.

As the former rain was given “to cause the upspringing of the precious seed, so the ‘latter rain’ will be given at its close for the ripening of the harvest.”<sup>1</sup> In other words, modern manifestations of the prophetic gift—the latter rain—will look a lot like previous manifestations of the former rain at Pentecost. The book of Acts foreshadows things that will happen again in the last days.

As we have already seen in Joel 2:28–31, the prophetic gift at the end of time will be available to young and old, man and woman. God will speak through those whom the Holy Spirit selects. Whenever the Holy Spirit chooses an end-time messenger, his or her message will uplift Jesus, call people to repentance, and prepare people to fulfill God’s mission. These three characteristics are hallmarks of Ellen G. White’s prophetic ministry. Throughout her life, she uplifted Jesus and Scripture in her speaking and writing, she called Seventh-day Adventists and others to heartfelt repentance, and she urged all to contribute to God’s mission for the lost. Today, her writings continue to draw people to Jesus and help them prepare for His soon return.
