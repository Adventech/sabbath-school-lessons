### Promises for the Last Days

In Acts 2:17–21, we find one of the longest New Testament quotations from the Old Testament. In his sermon to the people gathered in Jerusalem, Peter quoted extensively from Joel 2, claiming that Joel 2:28–31 was being fulfilled in front of their eyes through the events of Pentecost. When reading Peter’s reasoning, we might ask, “What about the signs? We can’t see them in your situation.” Peter would probably answer, “There was a fulfillment of the signs. When Jesus was hanging on the cross a few days ago, the sun was darkened for three hours” (Mark 15:33).

What was a convincing argument for Peter is perhaps less persuasive for us today. We’re still expecting a more complete fulfillment of Joel’s prophecy. The key lies in the text itself. Joel 2:23 mentions the early and latter rains. Acts 2 is the first fulfillment of this prophecy—the early rain. The final fulfillment will be the outpouring of the Spirit just before the end of time through the latter rain.

When we consider the mention of signs in the sun and moon in Joel’s prophecy, we’re immediately struck by the fact that we can find this idea in several other biblical passages as well. Jesus told His disciples about signs in the sun, moon, and stars in His famous end-time discourse (Matt. 24:29). Similarly, John the revelator mentioned the same signs (Rev. 6:12, 13). Both Jesus and John targeted the very last cosmic signs before the second coming of our Lord.

We can assume that Joel’s prophecy, which points to similar signs, should have some impact on the end times: “And I will show wonders in the heavens and in the earth: blood and fire and pillars of smoke. The sun shall be turned into darkness, and the moon into blood, before the coming of the great and awesome day of the Lord” (Joel 2:30, 31). Joel 2 will find its final fulfillment in the last days, not long before Christ’s second coming.

At the heart of Joel’s prophecy is a promise that God’s Spirit will be poured out on His people. This special endowment of the Holy Spirit will include a manifestation of the gift of prophecy through men and women who receive prophetic visions and dreams. God used prophets when His Spirit was poured out on the early church, and we can expect something very similar in God’s end-time church. Those who believe in the Bible can expect to see prophets in the last days.
