As we journey through this study, it’s important to engage deeply with the material and reflect on its implications for our faith and daily lives. In your Sabbath School class (or Bible study group), use the outline of questions below to lead a thoughtful discussion based on this week’s main passage and supporting verses.

**Last-day prophets:**

1. What kind of outpouring of the Holy Spirit does God promise to give in the last days? Acts 2:17–21
2. Who will receive visions, dreams, and prophecies in the last days? Joel 2:28, 29
3. The signs in the heavens mentioned in Acts 2:19, 20 are associated with what time period and event? Matt. 24:29, 30

`Personal reflection: Why is it difficult for many Christians to accept the possibility of God sending modern prophets?`

**The spirit of prophecy:**

1. What are the identifying marks of God’s end-time church? Rev. 12:17
2. What is the testimony of Jesus? Rev. 19:10
3. Who are the “fellow servants” or “brethren” (Rev. 22:9) who have the spirit of prophecy?

`Personal reflection: How would you respond to someone who says they can’t believe in modern prophets because they believe in the Bible alone?`

**The gifts of the Spirit:**

1. What’s the significance of prophets being mentioned in all four passages where spiritual gifts are mentioned? Rom. 12:6–8; 1 Cor. 12:8–10; 1 Cor. 12:28; Eph. 4:11–13
2. Why is prophecy one of the core spiritual gifts given for the health and success of the church?

`Personal reflection: What books by Ellen G. White have you read? How have they impacted you personally?`

**Key takeaways to remember:**

- God promises in the Bible to send true prophets in the last days.
- In Revelation, the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy, meaning Jesus makes Himself known through the prophets.
- The gift of prophecy is one of the core spiritual gifts given to help the church prosper.
