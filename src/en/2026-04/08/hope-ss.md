### Prophets of God – Part 8

Welcome to Hope Sabbath School—an in-depth interactive study of the Word of God. We are continuing our series on Prophets of God. Today’s topic: INTERPRETING PROPHETIC WRITINGS.

**Scripture Song:** Listen to counsel – Proverbs 19:20

- Learning from the Bible studies of Jesus
	- Dr. Luke records the story of an amazing Bible study Jesus gave on the Emmaus Road. Luke 24:13-27
	- How did these two disciples respond to the Bible study Jesus presented to them? Luke 24:31-32
	- Later that same day, Jesus met with the disciples in Jerusalem. Luke 24:44-45
	- What important lessons can we learn from these two Bible studies given by Jesus?
- Principles for interpreting prophetic writings
	- View the Bible as a whole. 2 Timothy 3:14-17
	- Remember that Christ is the central figure in all the Scriptures. John 5:39-40 (Luke 24:27)
	- Look at the context.
		- Daniel 2:31-35 is followed by the interpretation in Daniel 2:36-45.
		- The parable of Jesus recorded in Matthew 13:1-9 is followed by the interpretation in Matthew 13:18-23
	- Allow other prophets to interpret an earlier prophecy.
		- Isaiah 7:14, Matthew 1:18-23
		- Isaiah 9:1-2, Matthew 4:12-16
		- Jeremiah 25:11-13, 2 Chronicles 36:22-23
		- Other examples?
	- Consider the historical-cultural context
		- 1 Corinthians 16:20 What was the cultural context for this appeal of the Apostle Paul to Christians in Corinth? How should we apply this saying to our lives today?
		- 1 Corinthians 14:34 What was the cultural context for this appeal? How should we apply this saying to our lives today?
	- Compare Scripture with Scripture
		- How does Genesis 2:16-17 expose the lies of Satan in Genesis 3:1-5?
		- How did Jesus respond to Satan’s misuse of Scripture when tempting Him to jump off the pinnacle of the Temple? Matthew 4:5-7 (Deuteronomy 6:16)
		- What truth is revealed as we compare Matthew 5:48 with Luke 6:36?
		- How does Jesus answer the question asked by Job in Job 14:14? John 5:28-19, 6:40, 11:25-26
- Asking for divine guidance
	- Who can help us to rightly interpret the prophetic writings? John 16:13-15, Luke 11:13
	- Share a time you sensed the Holy Spirit helping you to more clearly understand the Word of God.
