### Prophets of God – Part 4

Welcome to Hope Sabbath School—an in-depth interactive study of the Word of God. We are continuing our series on Prophets of God. Today’s topic: NEW TESTAMENT PROPHETS.

**Scripture Song:** Listen to counsel – Proverbs 19:20

- Jesus, the greatest prophet
	- What inspired testimony did the author of the book of Hebrews give about Jesus? Hebrews 1:1-3
	- Read the prophecy of Moses recorded in Deuteronomy 18:15. In what ways was Jesus like the prophet Moses, and in what ways was He different? (1 Peter 2:21-24, John 6:38)
	- How did Jesus describe His mission as a fulfillment of prophecy? Luke 4:18-19 (quoting Isaiah 61:1-2)
	- How complete was the prophetic ministry of Jesus? John 17:4
	- What prophecies of Jesus are very relevant for our lives today? John 14:1-3, John 5:28-29, Acts 1:8, etc.
- Apostles and New Testament Prophets
	- Are the titles apostle and prophet synonyms? Were all apostles prophets, and were all New Testament prophets apostles?
	- Which apostles were also prophets?
	- Which apostles wrote books of the New Testament? (Matthew, John, Peter, Paul)
	- Give some examples of New Testament prophets who were not one of the twelve apostles. (John the Baptist, Anna, Dr. Luke, Mark, Judas, Silas, 4 daughters of Philip the deacon, etc.)
	- What was the function of apostles and New Testament prophets? Acts 1:8,21-26, 15:32, Ephesians 2:19-20, 3:1-5, 4:11-16
- False prophets in the early Christian church
	- What warning about false prophets did Jesus give in His Sermon on the Mount? Matthew 7:15
	- What false prophet opposed Paul and Barnabas on the island of Cyprus? Acts 13:4-8
	- How did the Apostle Paul respond to this false prophet? Acts 13:9-12
	- Who was the power at work behind this false prophet? Acts13:9 (son of the devil), John 8:44
	- What warning did the Apostle Peter give about false prophets? 2 Peter 2:1-3
	- Why did the Apostle Peter offer such a strong condemnation of false prophets in 2 Peter 2:17? (see Luke 17:1-2)
- False prophets in the last days
	- What prophecy did Jesus give about false prophets in the last days of earth’s history? Matthew 24:24
	- What great signs and wonders are false prophets performing today that are out of harmony with the inspired word of God?
	- How can we avoid being deceived by false prophets?
