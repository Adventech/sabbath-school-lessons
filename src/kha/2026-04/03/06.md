---
title:  Ki Nongïathuhlypa Ba Bakla
date:   15/10/2026
---

Kampher kaei kaba U Blei U la leh, U Soitan bad ki jingpynthame jong u. Ka long kumjuh bad ki nongiathuhlypa. Ki Israel ki ïa kynduh ïa ki nongiathuhlypa kiba Shisha bad kiba bakla. Ki mat ba kongsan ka long kumno ba ngin pyniapher ïa ki.

`Pule Jeremiah 14:1-15. Kaei ka ba la jia hangne, bad kumno U Trai u leh ïa kito kiba kren lamler kum ki “nongiathuhlypa” ha ka kyrteng jong U?`

Ha u Jeremaiah 14, U Blei u la ai ha u Jeremaiah ïa ka khubor jong ka jingbishar ïa ka Judah, ka jingbishar ka ban wan ha ka dur jong ka waitlam (ka thma), ka jingthngan bad ka jingiap them. Ki nongiathuhlypa lamler ha kata ka por ki la pyrshah ïa ka khubor jong u Jeremaiah. U Jeremaiah u batai ïa ki da ki kyntien kiba shai bha: “Bad U Trai u la ong ha nga, ‘Ki nongiathuhlypa ki iathuhlypa lamler ha ka kyrteng jong Nga, Ngam shym la phah ïa ki, ngam shym la hukum ïa ki, lymne ngam shym la kren ha ki, ki iathuhlypa ha phi ïa ka jingithuhpaw kaba lamler, ïa ka jingiaphiah, ïa kaei kaei kaba khlem jingmyntoi, bad ïa ka jingshukor jong ka dohnud jong ki” (Jeremiah14:14).

Ki nongiathuhlypa lamler ki long kiba sniew namar ka tynrai jong ba ki nongiathuhlypa ki kam bunsien ki long ki “blei” lamler kiba ki mane (Jeremiah. 23:13, 14). U Blei u maham, “Ngam shym la phah ïa ki, ngam shym la hukum ïa ki, lymne ngam shym la kren ha ki’” (Jeremiah. 14:14,). Ki long “ki nongiathuhlypa jong ka jingshukor jong ka dohnud jong ki hi” (Jeremiah. 23:26). La bthah ïa u Esekiel ban iathuhlypa pyrshah ïa “ki nongiathuhlypa ... kiba iathuhlypa na la ka jong ka dohnud” (Esekiel. 13:2).

Ki nongiathuhlypa lamler bunsien ki bud ïa ki jingmut ne jingiohi kiba pawnam. Kane ka la long kumta ha ka kitab 1 Syiem 22, haba ka kynhun jong ki 400 ngut ki “nongïathuhlypa” ki la ïalum lang katkum ka hukum u Syiem Ahab (1 Syiem 22:6). Da kawei ka sur ki la ai jingmut ïa u syiem ban ialeh pyrshah ïa ka Ramoth-Gilead. U Ahab u la iamir jingmut bad u Jehoshaphat, u syiem Judah, hadien ba ki la iohsngew ïa ki jingiathuhlypa na ki nongiathuhlypa jong ka Asherah. U Jehoshaphat u la kyntu ïa u Ahab ban iasyllok bad u nongiathuhlypa Mikaiah la ki dei ban leit sha ka thma ne em. “Te uta u nonglam khubor uba la leit ban khot ïa u Mikaiah u la kren ha u, u da ong, ‘Mynta to sngap, ki ktien jong ki nongiathuhlypa da kawei ka jingmut ki pynshlur ïa u syiem. Sngewbha, to ai ba ka ktien jong me kan long kum ka ktien jong uwei na ki, bad to kren pynshlur” (1 Syiem 22:13).

Ka jingpynbor kaba u Mikaiah u la mad ban thok ka long kumba kajuh ka jingpynbor kaba ki nongiathuhlypa lamler jong ka Asherah ki sngew, hynrei u Mikaiah um shym la bud ïa kane ka jingpyrkhat kaba pawnam; ha ka jaka kata, u la ap ïa U Blei ban iathuh ïa u ïa kaei kaba un ong (1 Syiem 22:14).

`La phi dei u nongiathuhlypa ne em, ka long kaba suk ban bud ïa kaei kaei kaba pawnam. Haba pyrkhat haduh katno ka kolshor ka lah ban long kaba bamsap, balei ba kane kam ju long ka jingmut kaba bha?`
