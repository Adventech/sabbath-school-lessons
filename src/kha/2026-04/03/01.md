### Ki Jingpule Na Ka Bynta Kane Ka Taïew
Jakob 5:17, 18; 1 Ki Syiem 19:1-16; 1 Ki Syiem 22:1-14; Eksodos. 15:20; 2 Ki Syiem 22:11-14; Jeremiah. 14:1-15.

> <p>Dkhot Spel-lyndet</p>
> “U Jehoshaphat u la ïeng bad u la ong, To sngew ïa nga, ko Judah, bad phi ki nong Jerusalem; to ngeit ha U Trai U Blei jong phi, kumta yn pynskhem ïa phi; to ngeit ïa ki nongiathuhlypa jong U, kumta phin man bha.” (2 Khronikl. 20:20).

Ka Global Positioning System (GPS) ka iarap ïa ki nongleit jingleit ban tip shano ki don, shano ki leit, bad wat ka ai jingmaham ïa ki jingma. Ki nongleit jingleit ba kynja mynsiem kiba iaid lynti sha bneng ruh ki donkam ïa ka jingialam mynta bad ha ka lawei na ka “positioning system” kaba kynja bneng. Kawei ka lad ba U Blei U la leh ïa kane ha ki por ba la leit noh ka long lyngba ka jingshakri jong ki nongiathuhlypa jong ka Testament Barim. Ha ki jingiathuhkhana, ki jingai jingmut, ki jingiathuhlypa, ki poitri bad ki jingrwai jong ki, ngi lap shibun shaphang ki phew ngut ki nongiathuhlypa kiba la pynpaw ïa ki khubor U Blei ha ki briew.

Ka jinghikai jong kane ka taiew ka wad bniah ïa ki jingbatai jong ka Testament Barim shaphang ki nongiathuhlypa, ki jingbatai kiba iarap ïa ngi ban sngewthuh ïa ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa kumba la pynpaw da ki katto katne na kine ki shakri U Blei.

Kumba ngin sa iohi, kine ki long ki briew kum ma ngi, ha kaba ki long ki briew kiba tlot kiba don ha ka jingtieng, jingbitar bad jingduh mynsiem. Ka jingim na ka bynta jong ki kam shym la long kaba suk hynrei ka dap da ki jingtynjuh bad ki jingeh, kumba ka long na ka bynta jong ngi baroh.

Te, ngi lah ban ioh jinghikai aiu haba ngi peit ïa ki katto katne na kine ki shakri U Blei kiba la khot kyrpang?

_Pynkhreh ïa kane ka Lynnong na ka bynta ka Sabbaton, October 17_
