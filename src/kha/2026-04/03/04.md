---
title:  Ki Jingtrei Jong Ki Nongiathuhlypa
date:   13/10/2026
---

`Pule 1 Samuel 8: 4-22, Lebitikos 8: 10-13. . Kumno ki nongiathuhlypa ki trei kam ha kaba iadei sha ki syiem bad ki lyngdoh?`

Ki nongiathuhlypa ki iakynduh bad ki syiem ha ki jinglong kiba sngewtynnad bad ki bym sngewtynnad. Haba ki Israel ki dawa ïa u syiem (1 Samuel. 8:4-22), ki la ong ba ngi kwah ïa “u syiem ban bishar ïa ngi kum kiwei pat ki jaitbynriew baroh” (1 Samuel. 8:5). Ka la long kaba sngewsih hynrei ka long ka nuksa kaba donbor jong ka jingma kaba ki briew U Blei ki la iakynduh ha man la ka arta: ka jingkwah ban long kum kito kiba don sawdong jong ki. Ha kane ka khep, kim shym la don jingmut ei ei ïa kaei kaba kane ka jingkyrpad kan wanrah, wat la la maham lypa ïa ki (1 Samuel. 8:11-18) da U Blei lyngba u nongiathuhlypa jong U. Da kaba pynshong nongrim ha ka jingkwah jong ki, bad wat hapdeng ka jingmaham jong u ïa ki, u Samuel u la pynsleh kloi ïa u syiem ba nyngkong eh jong ki Israel, uba la tip ha ka histori jong ka baibl kum u Syiem Saul.

Ki kitab jong ka Testament Barim jong ka 1 bad 2 Syiem bad 1 bad 2 Khronikl ki thoh ïa ka jait bad ka jingbha jong ka jingsynshar ba la pyni da ki syiem Israel bad Judah. Ka histori bakhuid ka pyni ba ki syiem kiba leh sniew ki kham bun shibun ban ïa kito kiba leh bha ha khmat U Blei. Kawei na ki kam ba kongsan jong ki nongiathuhlypa ka long ban iarap ban ialam biang ïa ki syiem ki bym iAiñeh sha ka jingkohnguh ïa ka hukum U Blei. Kum ka jingmih, kine ki nongiathuhlypa ki la iakynduh ïa ki jingma, ha kaba don wat kiba la shah pynmynsaw bad shah pyniap ha ki syiem kiba la ialam ïa ki briew U Blei ban jngai na U (1 Syiem 18:4-19, 2 Khron. 24:20-22).

Ki nongiathuhlypa ruh ki iakynduh bad ki lyngdoh. U Moses, u nongiathuhlypa jong U Blei, u la pynsleh ïa u Aaron bad ki khun shynrang jong u kum ki lyngdoh Israel kiba nyngkong (Lebitikos. 8:10– 13). Don ki por ba ki lyngdoh ki ioh ïa ki ktien jingsneng na U Blei lyngba ki nongiathuhlypa (Hosia. 6:9, Malakhi. 2:1-3). Kawei ka nuksa kaba ktah bha, kane ka long ka iing jong u Eli baroh kawei, kaba la shah pynrem namar ki kam bamsap jong ki (1 Samuel. 2:27-36). Kine ki long katto katne ki jingsneng kiba khlAiñ bha pyrshah ïa ka jingbamsap kaba la pur sted hapdeng ki briew kiba la ai ïa ki kabu kiba kyrpang.

Ki nongiathuhlypa ki long ki nonghikai ïa ki briew (2 Khronikl 34). Ki la ai ïa ki khubor na U Blei ha ki lyngdoh, ki syiem bad ka jaitbynriew (Jeremiah. 5:31, Esekiel. 22:26). Ki nongiathuhlypa ki la kren ruh sha ki jaitbynriew kiba don sawdong jong ki da kaba batai ïa ka jingthmu jong U Blei na ka bynta jong ki, ka phang kaba la pynkylla bunsien ha ki khubor jong ki nongiathuhlypa jong ka Jutang Barim (Isaiah. 60:1-3; Isaiah. 62:1, 2). La khmih lynti ba ki nongiathuhlypa ym tang ba kin pynphriang da ka jingshaniah ïa ki khubor kiba U Blei U la ai ha ki hynrei teng teng la bthah ïa ki ban thoh ïa ki bad ban pynneh pynsah ïa ki (2 Khronikl. 13:22). Sa shisien, wat la ki nongiathuhlypa ki long kiba kynja briew katno katno ruh (bad ryngkat bad ki jingtlot baroh kiba iadon ki ïa iaid ryngkat bad kata), ki long ki shynrang bad kynthei jong ka jingduwai, ka Ktien, bad ka jingshakri ïa U Blei bad ïa ki briew jong U (Jeremiah. 42:4; 2 Syiem 22:15-17).
