---
title:  Ki Nongïathuhlypa Kynthei
date:   14/10/2026
---

`Pule Eksodos 15:20; Ki Nongbishar 4:4; 2 Ki Syiem 22;14 bad Nehemiah 6:14. Kaei ba kine ki dkhot ki hikai ïa ki aiñ jong ka jinglong nongïathuhlypa?`

Ki don shibun ki sakhi ha ka Jingthoh Bakhuid ba U Blei U la khot ïa ki kynthei ban long ki nongiathuhlypa. (Peit ïa ka jingpyndonkam ïa ka kyntien Hebru nevi’ah [Eksodos. 15:20] bad ka ktien Grik prophētis [Loukas. 2:36].) Ha ka kitab Mikah 6:4, la buh kyrteng ïa ka Miriam ryngkat bad u Moses bad u Aaron kum kito kiba la phah sha u paidbah. La pyni ïa ka Miriam kum ka nongiathuhlypa kaba la rwai ïa ka jingrwai jong ka jingjop haba ki Israel ki la iaid lyngba ïa ka Duriaw Basaw (Eksodos. 15:20, 21). Hynrei kane ka nongiathuhlypa kynthei, kum ki shynrang, kam shym la long kaba khlem jingbakla, kumba ka jinglong ba ialeh pyrshah jong ka ïa u Moses bad ïa ka tnga jong u kaba na Ithiopia ka pynpaw, ki kam ki jongleh kiba la pynshitom ïa ka nongiathuhlypa kynthei (peit Jingkhein 12:1-10).

Ngi lah ban iohi ïa ka jingtrei jong uwei pat u nongiathuhlypa ha ka jingiathuhkhana shaphang ka Deborah, kaba la shakri kum ka nongbishar ha Israel (Nongbishar 4:1-4).

Ha ka jingshisha, don ki sakhi kiba shai ba ka Deborah (Nongbishar 4:4) ka la ioh ïa ka jingpynpaw na U Blei (Nongbishar 4:4-9, 14-22). Ka jingrwai jong ka jingjop (Nongbishar 5:2-31) ka long ka jingithuh ïa ka jingwan rung U Blei bad ka don ka jingmut kaba jylliew ha ka liang ka theology kaba don kam na ka bynta ka jaitbynriew ha kata ka por ha ka histori.

`Pule 2 Ki Syiem 22:11-14. Balei ki briew jong u Syiem Josaiah ki la leit sha Huldah? Kane ka dei ban iathuh aiu ïa ngi shaphang ka bynta kaba kongsan jong ka ha ka jaitbynriew?`

Ia ka Huldah “ka nongiathuhlypa” la kylli jingmut na ki rangbah ba ha khlieh duh jong ka dorbar jong u Syiem Josaiah (2 Syiem 22:14, 2 Khronikl. 34:22) hadien ba ki la pule ïa ki kyntien jong ka “Kitab ka Hukum” (2 Syiem 22:8) (la ngeit ba ka dei ka jingkynthoh jong u, “uba la ong) Ki Syiem 22:11). Kane ka paw shai ba ka long ka kam kaba khia shibun, bad u syiem u la shim ïa ka kumta, dei na kata ka daw ba ki briew jong u ki la leit beit sha Huldah. Ka la ioh ïa ka khubor na U Blei shaphang ka jingbishar kaba la jan ïa ka Judah. Ki jingpynkylla jong u Josaiah ki la mih na ki khubor jong ka jingiathuhlypa jong ka Huldah bad ki la pynkylla ïa ka jingbishar U Blei ïa ki briew jong U.

`Ki nongiathuhlypa shynrang bad ki nongiathuhlypa kynthei, wat la la khot ïa ki da U Blei, ki long ruh ki nongpop kiba donkam ïa ka jingaiei. Kaei ka jingkyrmen kaba phi lah ban shim na ki jingiathuhkhana jong ki?`
