---
title:  Ka Jinglong Briew Jong Ki Nongiathuhlypa
date:   11/10/2026
---

Kumba ngi la iohi mynshem taiew, U Trai u la pyndonkam ïa u Elijah u nongiathuhlypa ban leh ïa ka kam kaba khraw ha Israel, wat hapdeng ka jingpyrshah kaba jur. Kaei kaba U Trai u la pyndep lyngba jong u ka long kaba ym lah ban ngeit. Bad pynban . . . kaei?

`Pynleit jingmut ba kumno U Jakob u iathuh ïa U Elijah u nongiathuhlypa ha u Jakob 5:17-18. Kaei ka ba u mut da kaba ong?`

U Elijah u la ioh ïa ka jingjop kaba phylla ha u Lum Karmel (1 Syiem 18); U Blei u la trei ha ka rukom kaba khraw lyngba jong u, bad kawei na ki kambah kaba phylla tam kaba la pyni ha ka histori jong ka Jutang Barim ne Testament Barim ka la jia: ha ka jingsawa jong ki ktien jong u Elijah hi, ka ding ka la hiar shisha na bneng (1 Syiem 18:38)! U la pyrkhat ba une u briew jong U Blei u la dei ban long uba kmen tam ha dien ka jingjia kaba kum kata.

`Pule jingmut 1 Ki Syiem 19:1-16. Kaei ba kane ka iathuh ïa ngi sha phang ka jingktah ha ka jingmut jingpyrkhat jong ki nongiathuhlypa jong U Blei?`

“Ka la biang, mynta, ko Trai, to shim noh ïa ka jingim jong nga, namar ngam kham bha ban ïa ki kpa jong nga!” (1 Syiem 19:4). Kane ka sngew kum u briew uba shah shitom na kano kano ka jait jingsngewsih. Balei? Kaei kaba la jia ha u lum Karmel kan ym long ka jingleh kaba suk ban bud, kaba thikna, hynrei, wat la katta ruh, ka don kaei re kaei kaba jia hangne.

Lehse lah ban shem ïa ka mat ha ki kyntien jong u, “Nga la long uba sngewrit shibun na ka bynta U Trai U Blei jong ki paid, namar ki khun Israel ki la iehnoh ïa ka jutang jong Me, ki la pynpra ïa ki duwan jong Me, bad ki la pyniap ïa ki nongiathuhlypa jong Me da ka waitlam. Tang ma nga hi nga la sah, bad ki wad ban shim noh ïa ka jingim jong nga’” (1 Syiem, 19:10). Kane ka sawa kum ba u sngewsih ïa kaei kaba u ngeit ba ka long ki jingbymlah jong u ha ka jingshakri. Ka jingphaidien ka dang sah, wat la u pyrshang katba lah, kumba tang ma u hi u la lah ban pynsangeh ïa ka jingphaloh. Kaei kaba kut kaba la jia hangne, ngi lah ban iohi ïa ka jinglong ba sngewrit bad jinglong kaba jem jong une u briew jong U Blei. Ha kiwei pat ki kyntien, u Elijah u long u briew uba don “ka jinglong kaba kum ka jong ngi.”

Balei ka lah ban long kaba suk teng teng ban don ka jingsngew “ba la shah rem” ha la ka jong ka jingim ne ha ki jingpyrshang? Kiei ki jingkular jong ka Baibl kiba phi lah ban kam ba kin iarap ïa phi ban leh lyngba ka jingduh mynsiem?
