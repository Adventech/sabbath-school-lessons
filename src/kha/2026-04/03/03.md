---
title:  Ka Jingithuh Ïa Ki Nongïathuhlypa Bashisha
date:   12/10/2026
---

Ha ka history kaba nyngkong jong ki Israel, ka don ki sakhi jong ki nongiathuhlypa ba bakla (Dueteronomi 18:20). U Blei, namar kata, um shym la iehnoh ïa ki briew jong U khlem ka jingialam ha kaba tip kiei ki nongiathuhlypa bashisha ki long.

Kum ki nongpdiang ïa ka jingpynpaw bad jingpyrsad mynsiem jong U Blei, ki nongiathuhlypa bashisha ki pynphriang da ka jingshaniah, da kaba thikna bad da kaba hok ïa ki khubor U Blei, wat la, ha ki por, kane ka pynlong ïa ki ban ym sngewtynnad ïa ki briew kiba don ha ka bor.

`Pule 1 Ki Syiem 22:1-14. Ka iathuh aiu ïa ngi shaphang kumno ki nongiathuhlypa kiba iAiñeh ki treikam?`

Ban don ïa u syiem ong u nongiathuhlypa ba “Nga shun ïa u, namar um iathuhlypa ïa kaba bha shaphang jong nga, hynrei ïa kaba sniew’” (1 Syiem 22:8,) ka kren shibun shaphang u syiem, hynrei kham bun shaphang u nongiathuhlypa. Ka la long, kaba shai, u briew u bym tieng ban kren ïa ka jingshisha, wat ha u syiem hi — ym ka lad ban ioh ïa ka jingsngewbha jong u syiem, ban ong ïa kaei kaei kaba rit tam.

U Mikaiah u la ieng marwei pyrshah ïa ki 400 ngut ki nongiathuhlypa lamler katba u dang pyni ïa ka jingshisha ba la pynpaw ha u. Ha khmat ka jingpynbor ban pynkylla ïa ki khubor kiba sniew na U Trai ban pynsngewbha ïa u Syiem Ahab uba sniew bad ïa ka tnga jong u, ka Jesebel (1 Syiem 22:13, 14), u Mikaiah u la pyni ïa ka jingshisha jong U Blei, khlem da khein kumno yn pdiang ïa ka.

`Pule 1 Samuel 9:3-14. Kine ki dkhot ki iathuh aiu ïa ngi shaphang kumno kiwei ki peit ïa ki nongiathuhlypa ba iAiñeh jong U Blei?`

Ki don shibun ki nuksa jong ka jinglong jingman jong ka jingim ba la im da ki nongiathuhlypa ha ka Baibl. Ha kane ka jingjia, la phah ïa u Saul da u kpa jong u, u Kish, ban leit wad ïa ki kada jong u kiba la jah. U shakri uba leit jingleit bad u Saul u la ai jingmut ba un ym iehnoh ïa ka jingwad, namar don “ha kane ka nongbah u briew jong U Blei, bad u long u briew uba donburom’” (1 Sam. 9:6). Ka jingbatai ba la ai hangne shaphang u Samuel u nongiathuhlypa ka pyni ïa ki jinglong ba la khmih lynti na ki nongiathuhlypa U Blei. Ki nongiathuhlypa bashisha ha ka Jingthoh Bakhuid la ithuh kum ki “briew jong U Blei,” ba la jied ha kawei ka bynta ban pyni nuksa ïa ka jinglong jong U. Ha kajuh ka por, tang namar ba don napdeng ki “briew U Blei” kiba lah ban long ki nongiathuhlypa, lane kane kam pynlong ïa ki ki briew kiba khlem jingduna.
