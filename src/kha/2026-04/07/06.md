---
title:  Na Ka Bynta Ka Jingsneng ïa Ngi
date:   12/11/2026
---

Kine ki jingiathuhkhana baroh ha ka Testament Barim, wat la ki long kiba jakhlia bad kiba sngewsih katno katno ruh, la ai na ka daw kaba bha: kaba bha eh, khnang ba ngin ioh jinghikai na ki shaphang kaei kaba jia haba ngi leh pyrshah ïa U Blei.

`Pule 1 Korinth 10:6-12. Katkum ka jingong u apostol Paul, balei U Blei u la pyrsad mynsiem ïa ki nongïathuhlypa ban thoh ïa kaei kaba ki la thoh ha ka Baibl?`

Ha kine ki dkhot, U Paul u buh ïa u laiñ uba beit uba pyniasnoh ïa kaba la leit noh bad ka mynta, ka pyniasnoh ïa ki Israel ba mynshuwa bad ka balang, ha kaba ïa ngi kiba la pdiang ïa U Jisu kum U Trai bad U Nongpynim bad mynta ki la long shibynta.

U Paul u la pynpaw shai bha ba ka jingbuh jingkynmaw ïa ka por ba la leit noh jong ka jingiashem jong ki briew bad U Blei – la da ki long kiba bha ne kiba sniew, ngim dei ban khlem da peit bniah ïa ki bad ban leh lyndet ïa ki. Ha ka jingshisha, ba U Blei U pyrkhat ba ka long kaba donkam bad u la pynlong ïa ki kum ka nuksa “Te kine kiei kiei ruh ki la ialong jingmaham ïa ngi, khnang ba ngin ym ikwah ïa kiei kiei ruh kiba sniew, kumba kita ruh ki la ikwah.” 1 Korinth 10:6. Ha kiwei pat ki kyntien ka mynnor bunsien ki pynpaw ïa ka lawei. Ka por ba la leit noh bunsien ka buh ïa ka jinglong jong kaei kaba jia mynta bad kaei kaban sa jia ha ka lawei.

Ha ka jingshisha, U Blei U ong ba ngim shym la shah pynrem ban leh biang ïa ki jingbakla jong ki pateng kiba la leit noh, lada tang lada ngi sngap ïa ki jingsneng jong ka Jingthoh Bakhuid bad ki khubor jingiathuhlypa kiba U la phah sha ngi lyngba ki nongiathuhlypa jong U. Lada ngi sngap, ngi nang bad ngi kohnguh, ka jingim jong ngi kan skhem, bad ngin roi (2 Khronikl. 20:20). Na ka bynta ki katto katne, wat ïa ka jingroi jingpar ba kynja mynsiem ruh la batai da kaba don ki ïing kiba itynnad, ki kali, ki jaiñ bad kiwei kiwei ki jingdon jingem. Katba U Blei U kular ban pyndap ïa ki jingdonkam jong ngi, U kwah ruh ba ki mynsiem jong ngi kin nangsan, wat kumba ngi roi ngi san ha kiwei pat ki liang (3 Ioannis 2). Hynrei ka jingdap ba kynja mynsiem kaba neh kaba shisha ha ka jingim Khristan ka donkam ïa ka jingkohnguh ïa ki khubor U Blei.

U Blei um ju pynsyrwa ei ïa ki ktien. Haba U kren, Ki Ktien jong U ki long kiba neh junom. Ka bor bad ka jingtreikam jong ki kam ju kut (Isaiah 40:8). La ai ïa ki na ka bynta ka jingsneng jong ngi, “ha kiba la jia ki jingkut ki rta” (1 Korinth. 10:11). Lada kata ka long kaba shisha ha kito ki por, katno kita ki kyntien ki dei ban iadei bad ngi mynta?

`Haba phi pule ïa ki jingïathuhkhana ha ka Baibl shaphang ka jingleh pyrshah bad ka jingbymngeit, kiei ki jinghikai kiba phi lah ban ïoh na ki kiba lah ban ïarap ïa phi ban kiar na kaba leh ïa kaei kaei kaba kum kata?`
