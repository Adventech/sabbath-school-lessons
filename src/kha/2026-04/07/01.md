### Ki Jingpule Na Ka Bynta Kane Ka Taïew
Jingkheiñ. 13:26-30; Jeremiah. 36:20-26; 2 Khronikl. 36:15, 16; Mathaios. 23:30-35; 1 Korinth. 10:6-12.

> <p>Dkhot Spel-lyndet</p>
> “Te kine kiei kiei baroh ki la jia ha ki kum ki dak: la thoh te ïa ki ban long ka jingsneng ïa ngi, ha kiba la jia ki jingkut jong ki rta.” (1 Korinth 10:11).

Katto katne ki briew mynta ki sngi la pynshlur ban kren ïa ka “jingshisha jong ki” — ïa ki jingshem jong ki, ïa ki jingmut jong ki, ïa ki jingdonkam jong ki, ïa ki sawdong sawkun jong ki, ïa ki jingngeit jong ki — la kine ki long kumno kumno ruh. Kawei pa kawei ka “jingshisha” ka long kaba dei (ka jingpyrkhat ka don) bad ym dei ban pynsngap jar. Hynrei kaei kaba jia haba katto katne na kine ki “jingshisha” kiba la bat tynrai bha ki ialeh pyrshah bad ka Jingshisha, U Jisu Khrist bad ka Ktien ba la thoh jong U?

Kaei kaba jia ka long ba don ki briew ki ban pdiang ïa ki jingshisha jong ka baibl bad kin phai noh na ki jingbakla jong ki; kiwei pat kin bat skhem ïa ki jinglamler jong ki. Don kiba pynkylla ïa ka jinglong jong ki kum ka jingkynthoh ïa ka jingshisha; kiwei pat ki ialeh pyrshah ïa ka. Kum kata ka la long ka histori jong ka longbriew manbriew.

La ju khot ïa ki nongiathuhlypa jong ka Baibl ban pynpoi ïa ki khubor kiba ki briew kim kwah ban iohsngew. Kum ka jingmih, bun ki la shah kynthoh, shah beh, wat shah byrngem da ka jingpyniap ne da shisha ki la shah pyniap (Hebru. 11:32-38). Wat hapdeng kine baroh, ki nongiathuhlypa jong U Blei ki la pynbna da ka jingshaniah ïa ki khubor jong ki namar ka jingialam jong ka jingiathuhlypa ba kynja Blei kam long kaba lah ban jied na ka bynta jong ki. Ka long ka jinghikai kaba pynim ïa ki briew U Blei. Kumta la dei ban pynpoi ïa ki khubor, wat la ka long kumno kumno ïa ka jingim ba shimet ïa u nongiathuhlypa.

_Pynkhreh ïa kane ka Lynnong na ka bynta ka Sabbaton, November 14._
