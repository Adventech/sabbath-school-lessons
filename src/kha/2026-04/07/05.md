---
title:  Ka Jingarsap
date:   11/11/2026
---

Hato phi la ju peit ïa kiei kiei kiba kiwei ki la leh ha ki por ba la leit noh bad hato phi la ju pyrkhat ha lade, Lada kata ka dei ïa nga, ngam shym la leh satia ïa kaei kaba kita ki briew ki la leh! Hynrei lada ngi long kiba lehhok da lade, ngin leh ïa kiei kiei kiba kiwei ki leh, hynrei bunsien ngi leh ha kaba ki wei ruh ki leh kum kita, ngi iai leh biang ïa kijuh ki jingbakla, lehse, ngi pynmatlah ïa lade da la ka mon sngewbha ïa kaei kaba ngi leh da shisha. Kane ka dei ka nongrim kaba U Jisu hi U la kren haba U la ong: “Me uba arsap! khlaw noh shuwa ïa ka khung na ka khmat jong me hi; kumta te men iohi bha ban khlaw noh ïa u thngiat na ka khmat u paralok jong me.” (Mathaios. 7:5).

`Pule Matthaios 23:30–35. Ka la long ka mat kaba kongsan hynrei kaba sngewsih kaba U Jisu u kren hangne? Kaei ka jingmaham kaba don kam kaba ngi lah ban shim na ki ktien jong U?`

Ka don ka jingsngew kaba na shapoh kaba don ha kine ki dkhot ba U Jisu u shun ïa ka jinglong jong ki Pharisi namar ba ïa ka jingleh arsap jong ki la pynsniew shuh shuh da ka jingkut jingmut ban kyntait ban ym kylla. U Jisu u la ngiah ïa ka niam jong ki Pharisi. La pynkut bad ki riew pawkhmat ba kynja mynsiem ha ki jAiñkup ba jrong kiba noh kiba long tang kum ki maw jingtep ba la pushara lieh ba la pyndap da ki shyieng jong kiba iap (Mathaios 23:27, 28). Kin ym phla ne iehnoh ïa ki pop jong ki, wat haba la kdew ïa ki pop ha ki, wat haba U Jisu u ailad na ka por sha ka por ïa ki ban kylla bamut bad ban ioh jingpynim.

Ha dien ba U la trei bad ki kine ki briew, kam long kaba phylla ba U Jisu u la khot ïa ki “ki ba arsap”! Ka lAiñ bunsien ka iaid kumne: “Kordit ïa phi, ki nongthoh bad ki Pharisi, ki nongarsap!” (peit Mathaios. 23:14, 15, 23, 25, 27).

Ka jingim jong ki ka la long, da shisha, kaba la pynpaw da kaba kren ïa kawei ka kam katba ki leh ïa kawei pat. Kum kata ka juh ka rukom ki la ïa kynduh ïa ki nongiathuhlypa. Ki la pyrkhat, kum ka nuksa, ba da kaba pynitynnad ïa ki jingtep jong ki nongiathuhlypa, ki pyndonburom ïa ka jingngeit; hynrei ha kiei kiei kiba kongsan, kum ka jingkylla kaba mut na ki ktien ki nongiathuhlypa, ki la kyntait ban leh kumta. Ha ka jaka kata, ki kwah ban don ïa ka jingiadei bad ka bor bad ka jingiashem ba sngewsih jong ki nongiathuhlypa hynrei ym bad ka jingdon jingmut jong ki khubor jong ki. Ha kaba pynphyrnai ïa ki jingtep bad ki jingtep jong ki shakri U Blei, ki la pyrkhat ha lade ba kin ym shym la pynmih satia ïa ka snam jong ki nongrah khubor U Blei. Hynrei ha kata hi ka por ki dang thmu ban pyniap ïa Uwei uba kham palat ban ïa u nongiathuhlypa — ïa U Messaiah hi.

`Kumno ngin pynthikna ba ha ka nongrim, ngim leh ïa kajuh ka kam: shabar ngi leh kum kiba hok katba hapoh ngi dap da ka jingleh pyrshah bad ka jingkwah brai bad jingshun?`
