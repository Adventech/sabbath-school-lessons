---
title:  Ka Jingbitar ïa U Nongrah Khubor Jong U Blei
date:   08/11/2026
---

U Moses, U Nongialam ba la jied jong U Blei, u la tip ïa ki jingkmen bad ki jingpang ha kaba ialam ïa ka kynhun bah ba la ïa khlehlang lyngba ka ri khlaw ha kaba wad ïa ka Ri Jingkular.

`Pule Jingkheiñ 14: 4-11. Kaei kaba la jia ha kine ki dkhot? Balei ba ki briew ki la sngewsih ïa U Moses bad u Aaron?`

Haba pule bniah ïa kine ki dkhot ka pynpaw ba ki khun Israel ki la bitar ïa U Blei namar ba kim shaniah ha U. U Moses bad u Aaron, ki nongmihkhmat ba kynja briew jong U Blei u ba long u kynrad, ki long ki nongshah thombor kiba suk na ka bynta ka jingbitar jong ka jaitbynriew. Ka jingdonkam ïa ka jingshngAiñ ha ka met bad ka jingmut jingpyrkhat ka long ka pdeng jong ka jingpynshlur ba kongsan kaba baroh ki briew ki don. Haba uwei na ki u shah byrngem, ne ngi shim ïa kaei kaei kum ka jingma ïa ka jingkoit jingkhiah jong ka met ne ka jingmut jingpyrkhat jong ngi, ngi lah ban long kiba bitar ne wat kiba khlem jingmut.

`Pule Jingkheiñ 13: 26-30. Balei ba ki Israel ki la sheptieng? Kiei ki kyntien jong ka jingpynshlur jong u Kaleb kiba u la iasam bad ki?`

Ki Israel ki la buhrieh ïa ki jingtieng kiba dei hok shaphang ki jingeh kiba ap ha khmat ban ioh ïa ka Ri Jingkular, “hynrei haba ki briew ki aiti ïa la ki dohnud ha ka jingbymngeit ki buh ïa lade hapoh ka jingsynshar U Soitan, bad ym don uba lah ban iathuh haduh katno un ialam ïa ki.” —Ellen G. White, Patriarchs and Prophets, p. 389. U Moses u la iakynduh ïa ka jingeh bym lah ban jop ne ban ialeh pyrshah ban long nongialam ha kane ka khyllipmat. Ki jingtieng jong ki briew ki la kylla long shen sha ka jingbitar pyrshah ïa u Aaron bad ïa u Moses bad sha ka jingleh pyrshah kaba paw pyrshah ïa U Blei. Haba ki briew ki la wad ïa ki nongialam kiba thymmai ban bujli ïa u nongiathuhlypa/nongialam ba la jied da U Blei (Deuteronomi 34:10–12, Jingkheiñ 14:4), u Joshua bad u Kaleb ki la kad ïa ki jaiñ jong ki bad ki la kyrpad ïa ki briew ban shaniah ha U Blei wat hapdeng ka jingtieng jong ki: “Lada U Trai u sngewbha ha ngi, te un ai ha ngi ïa kane ka ri “ka tuid da ka dud bad ka ngap.” Tangba wat leh pyrshah ïa U Trai, lymne wat sheptieng ïa ki briew jong ka ri’” (Jingkheiñ 14:8, 9).

`Ka long kaba suk ban shaniah ha U Blei haba kiei kiei ki iaid bha? Kumno haba jia ka jingjia ba sngewsih? Kumno haba ka jingshaniah ha U Blei ka mut ka jingbymsuk ne jingbym shngaiñ? Katno phi leh haba kumta?`
