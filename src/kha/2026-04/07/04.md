---
title:  "Ka Jingpynjot ïa Ka Khubor: Ka Bynta II"
date:   10/11/2026
---

Ki jingkynthoh jong ki briew ïa ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa ki lah ban long ha kiba bun ki rukom. Ha ka jingjia ïa u Moses, ki khun Israel ki la hap ha ka jingïaleh pyrshah kaba paw pyrshah ïa u Moses bad u Aaron, ba la wan rah da ka jingtieng ïa ki nongshun kiba kin iakynduh haba ki pyrshang ban rung sha ka Ri Jingkular. Ha ki snem kiba hadien, u Syiem Jehoiakim u la thang ïa ka khubor U Blei da ka jingkyrmen ban pynduh ïa ka Ktien jong ka jingiathuhlypa jong U Blei.

Kine, la kumno kumno, kine kim dei ki jingjia ba kyrpang. Ha ka histori jong ki Israel bad Judah hyndai ka la long, ba tang khyndiat eh, kiba kyntait ïa ki nongiathuhlypa.

`Pule 1 Khronikl 36:15–16. Kumno ka jait bynriew Judah ka jubab ïa ka khubor ba la phah da U Blei, sha ki lyngba ki nongiathuhlypa jong U? Kumno U Blei u la leh ha ka ba kut ïa ka jinglong jong ka Judah?`

Ka kam jong ka jingiathuhlypa jong u Jeremaiah ka la neh ha ki por synshar jong u Josaiah, u Jehoahas, u Jehoiakim, u Jehoiakhin bad u Sedekaiah. Ka hima Babilon ba la ialam da u Nebukhadnessar ka la don mynta ha ka jingkhang jong ka Judah. Ki jingiathuhlypa jong u Jeremaiah ki la urlong lut haba uwei u samla uba dang 21 snem ka rta uba kyrteng u Sedekaiah u la wan ha ka khet (2 Khronikl. 36:11). Ka Baibl ka ong, “U la leh ïa kaba sniew ha khmat U Trai U Blei jong u, bad um shym la pynrit ïa lade ha khmat U Jeremaiah u nongiathuhlypa, uba la kren na ka shyntur U Trai” (2 Khronikl. 36:12,).

Peit kumno la batai ïa ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa jong u Jeremaiah. La iohi ïa u kum uba kren na ka shyntur U Blei hi. Wat la katta ruh, u Sedekaiah um shym la pynrit ïa lade ha khmat u nongiathuhlypa. Bad wat la katta ruh u Jeremaiah uba long tang u briew, u nongpop kum ma ngi baroh, u la long u nongmihkhmat jong U Trai, na ka bynta jong no ba u la kren.

U Jeremaiah u la long uwei napdeng kiba bun ha kane ka bynta kum u nongiathuhlypa. Da ki spah snem, U Blei u la ju phah ïa ki nongiathuhlypa sha ki briew jong U (Eksodos 3:15, Salm 105:26, Malakhi 3:1, Isaiah 6:8, Jeremiah 25:4, Mathaios 11:13, Loukas 11:49, bad kiwei kiwei” um da mon “ba uno uno un jot ei hynrei ba baroh kinwan sha ka jingkylla kaba mut” 2 Petros 3:9. Kane ka histori jong ka Israel bad ka Judah hyndai ka long ka jingiathuhkhana shaphang ka jingpyrshang khlem sangeh jong U Blei ban poi sha ki briew jong U kiba la phaidien, da ki jingkular jong U ïa ka jingpynim, tang lada kin kylla bamut.

Hynrei ha kane ka por kaba khatduh ha ka histori jong ka Judah, ïa ka jingaiei kaba la pynjlan da U Blei sha ka Judah lyngba ka jingialam bad jingpyntngen da ka sur jong ka jingiathuhlypa jong u Jeremaiah, ym da ka jingsngewrit, jingngeit, bad jingkylla kaba mut hynrei da ka jingrkhie, jingkren beiñ bad jingrkhie beiñ.

`Ki la kren beiñ ïa ki nongrah khubor jong U Blei, ki la niewbeiñ ïa ki ktien U Blei, bad ki la kren beiñ ïa ki nongiathuhlypa jong U. Hooid, ngi mynta ngin ym leh satia ïa kaei kaei kaba kum kata — ne ngin leh kumta?`
