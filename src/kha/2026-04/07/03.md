---
title:  "Ka Jingpynjot ïa Ka Khubor: Ka Bynta I"
date:   09/11/2026
---

`Pule Jeremiah 36: 20-26. Kaei kaba la jia ha ka ïing bishar, bad kumno ki nongialam ki la jubab sha ka jingmaham jong ka jingiathuhlypa?`

U Jeremaiah u la long ka sur jong ka jingiathuhlypa jong U Blei ïa ka jaitbynriew Judah ha ka por jong ka jingpynjot kaba la jan. La bun bun snem, U Blei lyngba u Jeremaiah u la ju maham ïa ki Judah shaphang ka jingbymsngewbha jong U ïa ki bad ba ka Babilon kan jop ïa ki bad kan ring mraw ïa ki sha ka jinglong mraw (Jeremaiah. 6:8-19, Jeremaiah 26). U Trai u la wad ban pynim ïa ki, ki la dei ban kohnguh.

Ki pop jong u Judah ki long kiba bun, kiba paw bad ki bym don ka jingkylla. Ka jingmane bleithaw, ka jingkñia ïa ki khynnah, ka jingklim bad ka jingsynshar donbor ki long ki rukom ha ka por u Jeremaiah, hynrei ym shym la sngap ïa ki jingmaham bad ki jingkyrpad jong u nongiathuhlypa. Katba ka jingshakri jong ka jingiathuhlypa jong u Jeremaiah ka nang jan sha kaba kut, ki jingkyrpad U Blei ïa ki khun kiba la lyndet jong U ki la nang jur, hynrei ki jingpyrshang jong U ki dang iai long ban pynim. Ha ka Jeremaiah 32, kum ka nuksa, U Blei u ong ïa u Jeremaiah ban thied khyndiat ka khyndew bad ban bat ïa ka kata ka kot, “Namar kumne u ong U Trai ki paid ... : ïa ki iing bad ïa ki brisoh waiñ yn dang iathies pat ha kane ka ri” (Jeremiah. 32:15, 16).

Dei ka jingieit kaba la pynlong ïa U Blei ban ai ïa u Jeremaiah ban thoh ïa ka jait jingkyrpad kaba khatduh na ka bynta ka jingkylla kaba mut bad ka jingpynthymmai. Ka jingkynthoh jong u Jehoiakim ïa kane ka khubor ka long kaba sngewsih ïa U Blei, ïa ka khubor jong U bad ïa u nonglam khubor jong U.

`Pule Jeremaiah 36:27-31. Kumno U Blei u la jubab ïa ka jingleh pyrshah jong u Jehoiakim?`

Teng teng ka don ka jinglong ban ngeit ba lada ngi pynduh ne pynjot ïa ki khubor U Blei ne ki nonglam khubor jong U, ka khubor hi kan jah noh. Ka jingbym mon jong ngi ban sngap ïa ka Ktien ka jingiathuhlypa jong U Blei kam pyndam noh ïa ka khubor kaba U Blei U don na ka bynta jong ngi. Ka jingbymsuidniew jong ngi ka pynjur ïa ka jingma kaban sa jia ïa ngi bad ïa ki bahaiing hasem jong ngi namar ka jingbymtip ne jingleh pyrshah kaba thmu jong ngi. Ka jingiathuhlypa jong u Jeremaiah shaphang ka jingkut jong u Syiem Jehoiakim ka bat ïa ka jingmaham kaba jur ïa uwei pa uwei u briew (Jeremiah. 36:30, 31): ngi iehnoh ïa ki jingmaham U Blei ïa ngi ha ka jingma jong ngi hi, namar ngin kheit ïa u soh jong ka pop bad jingsniew jong ngi.

`Ka iathuh aiu ïa ngi shaphang ka jinglong ba ieit jong U Blei ba U la iaieh ban pyrshang ban poi sha ki briew jong U wat hapdeng ki jingpyrshah jong ki?`
