---
title:  Ka Jingkyrmen Ha Ka Jinglong Mraw
date:   09/12/2026
---

“Bad U Trai, U Blei ki kpa jong ki, U la phah sha ki da ki nonglamktien jong U, U da khie dang step bad U phah; namar U la sngewsynei ïa la ki briew, bad ïa la ka jaka shong:” (2 Khronikl. 36:15).

U Blei u la phah shibun ki nongiathuhlypa hadien u Elijah ban pyntngen, ban maham bad ban hikai ïa ki briew jong U, hynrei ki hima Israel bad Judah ki la hap ha ka jingsniew kaba khraw haduh ba U Blei u la ianujor ïa ki sha ka Sodom bad Gomorrah (Isaiah. 1:9, 10). Ki briew U Blei ki la kylla long kiba sngew sarong, kiba kwah brai bad kiba mane bleithaw. Ki la ban beiñ ïa ki nongtrei jong ki, ki riewkynthei, ki khunswet bad ïa ki nongwei kiba wan pan jingiarap na ki. (Peit Isaiah 58 na ka bynta ka jingkynthoh U Blei ïa ki jingbakla jong ki Israel.) Khatduh eh, ka jingbishar U Blei ka la wan ialam ïa ki Israel sha ki nong Assyria ban long ki mraw (2 Syiem 17:1-6) bad la pynlong mraw ïa ki Judah sha ka ri Babilon (2 Syiem 24, 25).

Katba U Blei U dang pynshitom ïa ki briew jong U, Um shym la iehnoh marwei ïa ki ha ka jinglong mraw. U dang phah ïa ki ïa ki nongïathuhlypa, kum u Esekiel bad u Daniel ban ai jingkyrmen ïa ki na ka bynta ka lawei.

`Pule Isaiah 59:1-4. Kaei kaba la kren hangne ka thew ïa baroh lyngba ki yrta?`

Lyngba ka histori jong ki, ki hima Israel bad Judah ki la mad ïa ki jingeh kiba mih na ka jingleh jong ka pop. Ha kaba phai noh na U Blei, ki la iehnoh ïa ka jingshngaiñ jong ka jingsumar jong U bad ki la pynmih ïa ka jingbishar jong U. U Blei um shah ïa ka jingshah lehbeiñ bad jingmane bleithaw, hynrei Um shym la phah ïa ki sha ka jinglong mraw ha ka sien kaba nyngkong ba ki la leh pop. U Blei u la trei shitom bad ki briew jong U, U da kyrpad lyngba ki nongiathuhlypa jong U ba kin kylla bamut bad kin wan phai biang sha U. Ki briew ki kyntait ban sngap. Ha ka jingshisha, ka long kaba phylla, ha ka jingshisha, haduh katno U Trai u la wad ban pynphai noh ïa ki briew jong U na ki pop jong ki. Ha ki spah snem kiba jrong, lyngba u nongiathuhlypa uwei hadien uwei pat u nongiathuhlypa, da kaba pynmih ïa ka jingmaham kawei hadien kawei ka jingmaham, U Blei u la wad ban poi sha ki.

U Jisu hi u la ong kaba bha tam: “Ah! Jerusalem, Jerusalem! pha kaba pyniap noh ïa ki nongiathuhlypa, bad kaba lynthem maw ïa kita ïa kiba la phah ha pha! katno sien ba nga la kwah ban kdup ïa ki khun jong pha, kumba ka syiar ka kdup ïa ki khun jong ka hapoh ki thapbniang jong ka, phi te phim mon!” (Mathaios. 23:37).

Hynrei, wat haba ki dang don ha ka jingshah ring mraw, U Blei um ju iehnoh ïa ki briew jong U khlem jingkyrmen. Wat hapdeng ki jingbymlah jong ki, U Blei U la kular, “Bad U Nongsiewspah un wan ha Seion, bad ha kita kiba phai noh na ka jingryngkang ha U Jakob, ong U Trai.” (Isaiah. 59:20,).

`Pyrkhat ïa ka jinglong baiAiñeh jong U Blei wat haba ki Israel ki la phai noh na U. Kumno phi la iohi ïa ka jingshisha jong kane ka bynta jong ka jinglong ba ieit jong U Blei ha la ki jinglong haka jingim jong phi? Hangno phin don lada Um shym la long uba iaishah bad uba ieit?`
