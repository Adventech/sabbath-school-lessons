---
title:  U Nongiathuhlypa Na Ka Bynta Ka Por Jong Ka Jingïaiap
date:   10/12/2026
---

`Pule Jingpynpaw 1:1-3. La Khot ïa U Ioannis ban leh aiu?`

Ki Khristan banyngkong ki la im ha ka jinglong kaba khmih lynti ïa ka jingkular jong ka jingwan pat U Jisu. U apostol loannis u la iohi ïa U Khrist ba u la kiew sha ki lyoh bad u la iohsngew ïa u angel ba u la batai ba un wan pat ha kajuh ka rukom (Ki Kam 1:10, 11). Mynta ka balang kaba nyngkong ka la iakynduh ïa kaba ym lah ban tip. Hooid, U Jisu u la ong ba Un wan phai pat. Hynrei lano?

Katba u dang shah pynryngkang par ha ka dewlynnong Patmos, u Ioannis u la thoh shaphang ka jingkular jong ka jingwan arsien U Jisu: “Ha khmih, U wan ha ki lyoh, bad ki khmat baroh kin iohi ïa U, wat kita kiba la sum ïa U” (Jingpynpaw 1:7). U Ioannis u la ioh ruh ïa ka jingpynpaw kaba jylliew shaphang ki jingjia ki ban pyni ïa ka jingkut jong ka por. Nalor ka jingpynshlur bad jingsneng na ka bynta ki hynñiew tylli ki balang (Jingpynpaw 2, 3), ki jingpynpaw ki kynthup ïa ki jingithuhpaw jong ka jingbishar (Jingpynpaw 14:6–12), shaphang ka jingkut jong ka pyrthei (Jingpynpaw 20), shaphang ka jingwan arsien U Khrist (Jingpynpaw 14:14–16), bad shaphang ka jingthaw thymmai ïa ka pyrthei (Jingpynpaw-2).

U loannis u la pynithuh ïa lade kum “uba u para bangeit jong phi, bad u nongiaioh bynta lem ha ka jingjynjar, bad ha ka hima bad ka jingiaishah U Jisu Khrist” (Jingpynpaw 1:9). U tip kaei kata ïa ka ban shah shitom, ym tang na ka jingjynjar jong ka jingïaiap, hynrei na ka jingshah pynshitom beiñ kaba wan ryngkat bad ka jingpynbna ïa ka Ktien U Blei bad ka jingdon ïa ka jingphla ïa U Jisu Khrist.

Ha baroh kawei ka kitab Jingpynpaw, la khot ïa u Ioannis ban iohi bad la hukum ïa u ban thoh ïa kaei kaba u la iohi. Ha ka lynnong kaba khadduh, u la thoh, “Mynta nga, u Ioannis, nga la iohi bad nga la iohsngew ïa kine kiei kiei” (Jingpynpaw 22:8). U loannis u la long u nongsakhi ba la iathuhlypa ïa ki jingjia ha ki sngi kiba khatduh, la bthah ïa u ban iasam bad ki balang ha ka por jong u ïa kaei kaba la pyni ha u ha ka jingithuhpaw (Jingpynpaw 22:16). U pynkynmaw ïa u Ioannis Nongpynbaptis, uba la pynbna ïa ka jingwan nyngkong U Jisu Khrist (Ioannis 1:6–8) ha ka por kaba don kam tam ha ka histori jong ka pyrthei.

Ïa ngi ruh la khot ban sakhi kumba u Ioannis u la leh. Ngi dei ruh ban sumar ïa ka jingphla U Jisu (Jingpynpaw 19:10) — ka jingphla kaba U la ai shaphang ïa lade lyngba ki nongiathuhlypa. Ngin lah ban nym ioh pdiang ïa ki jingithuhpaw kumba u Ioannis u la ioh, hynrei ngi lah ban ap da ka jingsbun ïa ka jingwan pat U Khrist katba ngi dang ïai sakhi ïa ka jingtrei jong U ha ka jingim jong ngi bad ngi dang khmih ïa ki dak jong ka jingpyllait im jong ngi.

`Pyrkhat ïa ka jingshai baroh kaba la ai ha ngi. Kiei ki jingkitkhlieh kiba shong halor jong ngi tang namar ba la ai ha ngi shibun kiei kiei?`
