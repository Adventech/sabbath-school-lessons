---
title:  Ka Jingkhot Sha Ka Jingkylla Bamut
date:   08/12/2026
---

`Pule Ki Nongbishar 2: 16-19. Kaei kaba ngi dei ban bud nuksa, ne ka bynta ba kongsan, kaba la iohi hangne?`

Da ka jingkylla kaba kongsan tam sha ka jinglong syiem, bad da ka jingkyrpad jong ki Israel ban ioh da u syiem (1 Samuel 8), ka por jong ki nongbishar ka la kut noh. Hynrei kane ka jingkylla ka la wanrah ïa ka jingdonkam kaba kham khraw shuh shuh ïa ki nongiathuhlypa ban ai jingmut, ban pynbeit bad ban sneng ïa u syiem, ïa ki nongialam jong ka jaitbynriew bad ïa ki paidbah.

Ha ka por ka jingsynshar u Ahab, U Blei u la phah ïa u Elijah da ka khubor jong ka jingbishar: yn ym don slap namar ki Israel ki la “‘iehnoh ïa ki hukum U Trai bad ki la bud ïa ki Baal’” (1 Syiem 18:18). Ha ka jaka ban kylla bamut, u Ahab u la pyrshang ban beh ïa u Elijah bad ban pyniap ïa u, hynrei U Blei u la iada ïa u (1 Syiem 18:10-12). Haba, ha ka snem kaba lai jong ka nemsniew, u Elijah khatduh khatwai u la leit sha u syiem, u Ahab u la kynnoh ïa u namar ka jingbym don slap (1 Syiem 18:17)!

Nangta u Elijah u la khot ïa ki Israel baroh na ka bynta ka jingialeh kaba khatduh hapdeng U Blei bad ki nongiathuhlypa jong u Baal (1 Syiem 18:19).

`Pule ïa ka jingpynbna jong U Elijah sha ki Israel bad ka jingjubab ha ki 1 Ki Syiem 18: 21, 38, 39, kaei kaba la jia?`

Ka jingleh ba kynja blei da ka ding kaba hap na bneng, ryngkat bad ka khubor ba la iathuhlypa jong u Elijah, ka la ktah jur ïa ki briew U Blei bad ka la ialam ïa ki ban kylla bamut. Kumba ki Israel ki la artatien ban aiti lut sha U Blei, ngi ruh ngi im ha ki dustur ki bym pdiang ïa U Blei kum U Nongthaw bad U Nongsiewspah. Ha ka jaka ban shakri kum ki sakhi U Blei ban kyntu ïa kiwei ban long kiba iAiñeh ha U, bunsien ngi phet lyndet na ka jingkhot ïa ngi da kaba bud ïa la ki jong ki jingkwah, kumba la leh ki Israel.

U Blei u maham ba ka jingbud ïa la ki jong ki jingkwah kan ialam sha ka jingpait dohnud, jingsngewsih, bad, khatduh, sha ka jingiap. Hynrei lada ngi bud ïa ka sur jong U, Kumta “U Trai un ialam ruh ïa me ha la ka sngi, bad un pyndap ïa ka mynsiem jong me ha ki jaka ryngkhiang, bad un pynkhlaiñ ïa ki shyieng jong me; men long ruh kum ka kper ba la ai um, bad kum ka umpohliew, kiba ki um jong ka kim duh noh.” (Isaiah. 58:11).

`Ki jinglong doh jong ngi ki pynbor ïa ngi ban leh pyrshah, wat da kaba sngewthuh, ban ïa leh pyrshah ïa U Blei. Kiei ki jingjied kiba ngi donkam ban leh khnang ban ym ngop ha kata ka jinglong?`
