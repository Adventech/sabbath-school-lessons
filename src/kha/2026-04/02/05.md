---
title:  U Daniel, U Nongithuhpaw Ba ÏAiñeh
date:   07/10/2026
---

Kiba bun ki Khristan ki tip ïa ka jingiathuhkhana jong u Daniel 1, ha kaba u la ieng na ka bynta ki nongrim jong ka koit ka khiah kiba u la nang bad tip naduh ba u dang rit. U Daniel u la long u nongmuna jong ka jinglong ba iAiñeh (Daniel 1:8). Haba la ai ha u ïa ki jingbam ba bang jong u syiem, u la iengskhem ban bam ïa ka jingbam ki ba long tang da ki jhur ba ki jingthung bad ban dih tang ïa ka um ha ka jaka ban iashim bynta ha ka jingbam ba la tyrwa ïa u na ka miej jong u syiem (Dan 1:12-16). Kane ka la long ka jingtynjuh kaba rit haba ianujor bad kaei kaban sa wan hadien, kum ha Daniel 6, ha kaba ka jinglong baiAiñeh jong u ka lah da kaba suk ban ïa kynduh ïa ka jingiap na ka bynta jong u.

`Pule Daniel 2:46-48; Daniel 4:18; Daniel 5:13, 14; bad Daniel 6:25-27. Kaei ba kine ki dkhot ki iathuh ïa ngi shaphang ka rukom sakhi kaba u Daniel u la long sha ki nongmane blei thaw kiba don sawdong jong u?`

Peit ïa ka jingphla na kine ki nongmane bleithaw ïa ka bor U Blei jong u Daniel. La kine ki jingiathuhkhana ki long kiba kyrpang katno katno ruh, baroh ki pynpaw ïa ka nongrim kaba kongsan: da ka jingim jong ngi, da ka jinglong ba iAiñeh jong ngi, bad da ka bor U Blei kaba trei ha ngi bad lyngba jong ngi, kiwei pat ki lah bad ki dei ban tip shaphang U Blei jong ka bneng, bad tip ba U long “U Blei jong ki blei, bad U Trai jong ki syiem.”

Kumjuh ruh, namar ba ka phang kaba ju wan biang ha ki jingiathuhlypa jong u Daniel ka long ka jingbishar, kaba kut ha ka jingwan arsien U Khrist (ka jingjop kaba kut jong U Blei), ka jingmut jong ka kyrteng jong u Daniel, “U Blei u long u nongbishar jong nga,” ka long kaba iadei bha. Ïa ki jingthoh jong u dei ban da pule bniah bad ban pyrkhat bha namar ki long kiba iadei bad kine ki sngi. Ki jingiathuhlypa jong u Daniel ki long kum ka jingpynkynmaw man ka por ban long kiba husiar bad ban sumar ïa ki hukum U Blei, wat ha kiei kiei kiba “rit” jong ka jingim.

Kumjuh ruh, kum u nongiathuhlypa, u Daniel u la iathuhlypa ïa ka jingkiew bad jinghiar jong ki jaitbynriew bad ïa ka jingmih na ka jingbym kohnguh jong ki ïa U Blei. Lyngba ki jingithuhlypa jong u, u Daniel u la pynpaw ïa ka jingthmu U Blei bad ïa ka jingjop kaba kut jong ka hima U Blei (Daniel. 7:13, 14).

Pyrkhat, kum ka nuksa, shaphang u Daniel 2, uba la thoh shiteng hajar snem shuwa U Khrist. U Daniel u la pyni thikna bha ïa ka jingkiew bad jinghiar jong kine ki saw tylli ki hima ba khraw jong ka pyrthei, wat haduh ka por jong ngi (kata jong ki kjat bad ki shympriah kjat jong ka dur blei), da kaba kdew shai ïa ki jaitbynriew jong ka Europe kaba mynta, kiba, kumba ka jingiathuhlypa ka hikai shai bha, “kin ïa khleh lang bad u symbai jong ki briew: hynrei kawei kan ym iasoh bad kawei pat.” (Daniel 2:43). Ka jingiathuhlypa kaba phylla shaphang ka Europe, wat haduh kine ki sngi, ha kaba, wat hapdeng ka jingshongkha para briew bad ki jingpyrshang baroh ban iatylli, ka dewbah kan sah kum ki jaitbynriew ba la ïa pra bad ki Aiñ kiba ïa pher, ki riti dustur bad ki jinglehniam ba kyrpang. Haba wat katto katne snem shuwa ka jinghap jong ka Soviet Union, ym don kano kano ka kynhun wad bniah kaba la iohi ba ka kyllon kan sa wan, ka jingiathuhlypa jong u Daniel ka ai ïa ngi ki daw kiba khlAiñ ban shaniah ha ki nongiathuhlypa jong U Blei.

`Baroh ki hima ha u Daniel 2 ki la wan bad ki la leit kumba la iathuhlypa. Kane ka dei ban iathuh aiu ïa ngi shaphang ka jingshaniah ha ka jingiathuhlypa, kaba kren shaphang ka hima bymjukut jong U Blei kaban sa wan hadien kane (Daniel. 2:44)?`
