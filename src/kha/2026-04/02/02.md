---
title:  U Abraham U Nongiada Jong Ka Jutang
date:   04/10/2026
---

Naduh Jenesis haduh Jingpynpaw, U Blei U la don ka kynhun kaba sah kaba iAiñeh kaba la pdiang ïa ka jingkhot ban kohnguh ha ka ban jubab ïa ka jingaiei jong U (peit Jeremiah. 31:7, 31-34). Kum ka nuksa, bun snem hadien ka jingïapra na Babel, U Blei u la jied ïa u Abram (hadien u Abraham) ban long ka nuksa jong ka jingngeit jutang, ym tang ïa ka iing ka sem jong u hynrei ïa baroh ki pateng ban wan. La ker sawdong da ka jingphaidien bad ka jingartatien ïa ka jinglong babha, u Abram u la pynshisha ba u long uba iAiñeh ha ka jingkhot U Blei. (Peit Jenesis. 12:1-9).

U Blei u la pyntip ïa ki kyndon jong ka jutang ha u Abraham bad u la pyni ïa u kumno ba yn pyndep ïa ka jingpynim jong u briew lyngba ka jingkñia U Khrist (Jenesis. 15:6-17). Ban tynjuh ïa ka jingngeit u Abraham bad ban pyni nuksa ïa ka jingkñia jong u Khun U Blei ha ka lawei, la khot ïa u Abraham ban aiti ïa u Isaak, u khun jong u, kum ka jingkñia ha U Blei (Jenesis. 22:1-17). Haduh katno ka la long ka jingtynjuh kaba shyrkhei ïa u kpa jong kiba ngeit. Pyrkhat ïa ki daw baroh kiba u Abraham u lah ban pyndonkam ban kyntait ïa ka hukum ba shai jong U Blei, hynrei u la kohnguh da ka jingngeit. U Blei u la pynsangeh ïa ka kti jong u Abraham ban pyniap ïa u Isaak bad u la pynbiang ïa u langbrot ha ka jaka jong u.

Ym baroh ki nongiathuhlypa la khot da U Blei ban maham ïa ka jingjot kaba la jan. La khot ïa baroh, pynban, ban pynphai biang ïa ki briew sha ka jinglong bashisha bad bunsien ban maham pyrshah ïa ka jingphaidien, pyrshah ïa ka jingpynshitom beiñ ïa kito kiba duk bad kiba kyrduh, bad pyrshah ïa ka jingpynkheiñ ïa ka jutang bad ki kyndon jong ka.

Hynrei kaba kham kongsan eh ka long ba ki nongiathuhlypa ki dei ban pynpaw ïa ka jingkular jong ka jingpynim kaba la shem ha U Messaiah. Ka jingphla kaba neh jong ki nongiathuhlypa jong ka Baibl bad jong U Mynsiem jong U ha ka arta jong ngi hadien ki apostol ka khot ïa ngi ban ithuh bad ban maham ïa kiwei pat ïa ka jingeh kaba la jan kaban sa wan ha ka pyrthei. Hynrei kaba kongsan eh ha kata ka jingmaham ka long ka khubor babha jong ka jingpynim na ka bynta ki briew kiba iAiñeh jong U Jisu, kito kiba la sah ha ka jingiadei jutang bad U (Jingpynpaw 12:17).

`Pule Jenesis 15:6, Galatia 3:6-9, bad Hebru 11:8. Kumno shaphang ka jingim bad ka jinglong jong u Abraham kaba la pynlong ïa u ban shakri ïa U Blei, wat la u long, da shisha, u briew uba duna?`

Ki don shibun ki jingiasyriem hapdeng ki sngi jong u Abraham bad ki jong ngi. Kumba ka jingmane bleithaw bad ka jingleh awria ka la nang jyllei ha ka por u Abraham, kumta ruh mynta bun na ki jingmut jingpyrkhat kiba kynja pyrthei ki thmu ban pynduh ne buh sha rud ïa ka niam Khristan. Ha ka kolshor kaba la khanglad, ïa ki briew U Blei mynta la khot ban pynpaw ïa ka dur jong U da kaba ïAiñeh ha ki jingïadei jong ki man la ka sngi bad kiwei pat, kynthup ïa ka jingpynbna jong ki ïa ki khubor jong ki lai angel (Jingpynpaw 14:6–12).

`Ka Jingthoh Bakhuid ka ong ba u Abram “u la ngeit ha U Trai, bad U la niew ïa kata ka hok ha u” (Jenesis. 15:6). Kumno phi iohi ïa ka jingkular jong ka gospel ba la pynpaw da ka bor ha kane ka jingthoh? Kaei ka jingkyrmen kaba phi lah ban shim na kane na ka bynta lade?`
