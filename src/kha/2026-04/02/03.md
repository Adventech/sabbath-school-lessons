---
title:  Ka Khubor U Elijah
date:   05/10/2026
---

`Pule 1 Ki Syiem 18: 19-39. Kaei kaba ngi lah ban iohi na kane ka jingiathuhkhana shaphang ba kumno ka jingiarhuhlypa ka lah ban trei kam?`

Ka jingkhot bad ka rukom kren jong u Elijah ka pynpaw ïa ka bynta jong u kum u nonglam khubor jong U Blei. Ban pynsangeh ïa ka jingphaidien jong ki Israel, U Blei u la phah ïa u Elijah sha u Syiem Ahab khnang ban pynbna ïa ka jingtyrkhong kaba bun snem. U Blei u la pyndonkam ïa kane ka jingjynjar ban pynphai ïa ki briew jong U na ka jingmane bleithaw bad ban phai biang sha ka jingkohnguh. U Syiem bad ka ri ki la kynnoh ïa u nongiathuhlypa Elijah na ka bynta ka jingjynjar jong ki bad ki la wad ïa ka ban pyniap ïa u.

Ha u lum Karmel, u Elijah u la ialeh pyrshah ïa ki nongiathuhlypa jong u Baal bad u Asherah. U nonglam khubor jong U Blei u la pynlong ka jingkyrpad kaba dap da U Mynsiem ïa ki Israel kiba mane bleithaw ban pynrem ïa ki bleithaw jong ki bad ban wan phai biang sha U Trai. Ki briew ki tip ba ki jingjied jong ki ka la ialam ïa ki sha ka jingphaidien kum ka jaitbynriew. Ki la pynkhreh mynta ban pdiang biang ïa U Blei jong u Elijah, uba la pynshisha ba u kham halor ban ïa ki blei ba bakla jong ki, kiba, ha ka jingshisha, kim shym l amih ruh.

Ha shuwa ka jingwan arsien U Khrist, U Blei U hukum ïa ka balang jong U ban pynbna biang ïa ka khubor jong u Elijah, bad ba ki briew kin phai noh na ki “bleithaw” jong ki bad kin long kiba la pynkhreh ban ieng ha ka sngi bakhraw jong U Trai. U Blei u dang iakynduh bad ki briew jong U lyngba ki lad kiba U la jied. Ka Ktien jong u bad ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa ki ai ka jingsneng, ka jingmaham, ka jinghikai bad jingpynshlur.

“Ka jingpynkhih im pat ïa ka jinglong riewblei bashisha hapdeng jong ngi ka long kaba khraw tam bad kaba kyrkieh tam na ki jingdonkam jong ngi baroh. Ban wad ïa kane ka dei ban long ka kam kaba nyngkong eh jong ngi. Dei ban don ka jingpyrshang kaba jur ban ioh ïa ka jingkyrkhu U Trai, ym namar ba U Blei Um mon ban theh ïa ka jingkyrkhu jong U halor jong ngi, hynrei namar ba ngim long kiba la pynkhreh ban pdiang ïa ka.”— Ellen G. White, Selected Messages, book 1, p. 121.

`Pule Malakhi 4;5,6. Kumno ba ka khubor jong u Elijah ka ktah ïa ki ïing jong kito kiba bud ïa U Blei?`

Ka jingpynthymmai kaba shisha ki sdang na ka dohnud bad ha iing. Ka longiing longsem ka long u maw hakhlieh ka dong jong ka balang bad u maw tei uba donkam eh ban tei ïa ka imlang sahlang. Ka jingpynthymmai ha iing ka pynphriang ïa ka jingieit bneng sha ka balang, ka imlang sahlang bad ka jaitbynriew. Kane ka long ka jingthmu jong U Blei na ka bynta ka iing Khristan. Ka jingpynthymmai ka pynshlur ïa ki briew U Blei na ka bynta ka mishon.

`Kiei ki jait bleithaw, lada don, kiba don ha ki iing ki sem, ha ki dohnud bad ki iingmane jong ngi mynta?`
