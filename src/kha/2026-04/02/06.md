---
title:  U Ïoannis Nongpynbaptis, U Pynkhreh ïa Ki Briew
date:   08/10/2026
---

`Pule Markos 1: 2-5, Kaei ka jingthmu jong ka jingshakri jong U Ioannis Nongpynbaptist?`

Ka Gospel jong u Markos ka iathuh ïa ngi ba ka jingwan poi ka jong U Ioannis nongpynbaptis ka long ka jingpyndep ïa ka jingiathuhlypa jong u Isaiah (Isaiah 40:3). Ka jingsan jong u Ioannis na duh ba u dang rit sha ka jinglong rangbah la pynpaw da ka jinglong ba iAiñeh sha U Blei. U Soitan u la sheptieng ban duh noh ïa ka jingsynshar jong u ha lor ki jait bynriew hadein ka jingiohsngew ïa ka ktien jong u Ioannis ha ka ri khlaw. Ka jingsniew ki nong sngap jong u Ioannis ka la paw ha ka rukom kaba la pynlong ïa u Soitan ba khynñiuh. Ka jingbat u soitan ha lor ki Israel la pynkhynñiuh. U la iai pyrshang bunsien bad um shym la lah ban pynduh jingkyrmen ïa U Ioannis Nongpynbaptist na ka rukom im kaba iAiñeh bad ka jingshaniah jong u ha U Messiah.

`Pule Mathaios11: 1-6, Kumno U Jisu U pynskhem biang ïa ka jingngeit jong u Ioannis katba u nongialap u don ha phatok?`

U Soitan um lah ban pynshoi ïa U Jisu sha ka pop, bad ka jingshah rem jong u ha ka ri khlaw ka la pynbitar ïa u. Nangta u la pynkylla buit da kaba pynlong ïa u Ioannis, u para jong U Jisu ban shah set phatok. Ka jingshah set phatok jong u Ioannis ka la pynsngewsih ïa U Jisu, wat la u Soitan um lah ban pynngop ïa U ban leh pop. Kane ka la long ka rukom jong u Soitan ban pyniakhlad ïa u Ioannis, katba u dang ktah ïa ka dohnud jong U Nongpynim ha ka jingsngew. Hynrei u Ioannis u la ioh ïa ka ktien ai mynsiem na U Jisu. Bad wat hapdeng ki jinglong kiba shyrkhei jong u, bad thikna ba um sngewthuh balei ba u dang sah ha phatok haba U Jisu u donbor haduh katta katta, u Ioannis wat la katta ruh u la iaisah uba iAiñeh, wat haduh ka jingiap.

Kum ki nongiathuhlypa hyndai, ïa ngi ruh, la khot ban pynbna ïa ka khubor jong ka jingkylla kaba mut bad ka jingngeit bad ïa ka jinglong ba pura jong ka hok U Khrist (Jingpynpaw 14:6-12). Kumba u la leh bad u Ioannis Nongpynbaptis, u Soitan u lah ban pyndonkam ïa ka jingbym lah ban shaniah ban pynlong ïa ngi ban artatien ïa ka mishon U Blei na ka bynta ka jingim jong ngi. Ngim donkam ban duh jingkyrmen lada ngi kynmaw ïa ka jingialam U Blei ha ka jingim jong ngi, khamtam haba kiei kiei kim iaid bha na ka bynta jong ngi, kumba la jia ïa u Ioannis, uba la shah set phatok bad nangta la pyniap ïa u da ki kam jong ka kynthei kaba sniew bad u briew uba tlot kabor mynsiem. La ki briew ki leh kaei kaei ïa ngi, ngi lah ban kner sha U Blei da ka jingngeit, bad Un jubab ïa ngi. U la kular ïa ngi ïa ka bor ban leh ïa ka mon jong U (Ki Kam 1:8). Ha ka pyrthei hadien ka jinglong Khristan bad ka pyrthei bym dei Khristan, ka long kaba donkam eh ba ngin long ki sakhi kiba iAiñeh na ka bynta U Khrist, la ka long kumno kumno ruh ka jinglong jingim jong ngi.

`Hato phi la ju don ha ka kyrdan, kumba u John u la don katba u dang don ha phatok, ha kaba phi shu lah ban sngewthuh balei ba kum kine kiei kiei kiba sniew ki jia ha phi? Kumno phi la pynneh ïa ka jingngeit bad jingshaniah jong phi ha U Blei?`
