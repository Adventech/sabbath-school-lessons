---
title:  U Isaiah, U Nongiathuhlypa Gospel
date:   06/10/2026
---

U Isaiah, u nongiathuhlypa uba khraw jong ka Testament Barim, u la ioh ïa ka jingkhot jong U sha ka kam nongiathuhlypa beit beit na U Blei (peit Isaiah 6:1-8). Da kaba shah pynkhih ha ka jingkhuid bad jingkhraw jong U Blei, u Isaiah u la sngewthuh ïa ka jingbym bitdor jong u. Ha kata ka khyllipmat, uwei u seraph u la her sha u Isaiah ryngkat bad u rnga ding uba im na ka duwan bneng bad u la “pynkhuid” ïa ki pop jong u. Nangta U Blei u la kylli, “Iano ngan phah, bad mano ban leit na ka bynta jong ngi?” Ka jubab jong u Isaiah ka dei ban long ka jong ngi: “Nga hangne, phah ïa nga” (Isaiah 6:8).

Ka jingkhot jong u Isaiah ka la wan ha ka por kaba kongsan ha ka histori jong ki Israel. Wat hapdeng ki jingbakla jong u hi, ka kam ban maham ïa ki Israel bad ki nongialam jong ki shaphang ki pop jong ki ka long kaba kongsan bha. Ki jingiathuhlypa jong u Isaiah ki long ki bynta ba kongsan jong ka Jingthoh Bakhuid, kiba ai ka jingshemphang kaba jylliew shaphang ka jinglong U Blei, ka jinglong briew, bad ka jingthmu jong ka jingpynim kaba la pynpaw. Ha ka jingshisha, kaei kaba khlem artatien ka long ka jingiathuhlypa kaba khlAiñ tam kaba pyni ym tang ïa ka jingiap U Jisu hynrei ïa kaei kaba ka jingiap jong U ka la leh na ka bynta ka pyrthei la shem ha Isaiah 53.

`Pule Isaiah 53. Kumno kane ka jingiathuhlypa kaba phylla, kaba la thoh palat shiteng hajar snem shuwa ka Diengphna, ka la pynpaw da ka bor ïa ka jingiap U Khrist na ka bynta jong ngi? Kane ka jingiathuhlypa ka dei ban hikai aiu ïa ngi shaphang ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa?`

Ki jingiathuhlypa jong u Isaiah ki la iathuhlypa ïa ka jingthmu bad ka kam jong u Messaiah (Isaiah. 53:3-5). Ki la batai balei ba la “ialam ïa U Khrist kum u khun langbrot sha ka jingpyniap” (Isaiah. 53:7,) bad kumno ka jingkñia pyniasuk jong U ka la pynim ïa ki briew jong U na ki pop jong ki. Ka bynta jong ka jingiap U Khrist la pyni shai bad pynpaw bha hangta, kaba pyni ïa ka nongrim jong ka jingpyniasuk kaba la pynlong na ka bynta ka jaitbynriew baroh kawei.

“Ngi baroh kum ki langbrot ngi la sakma, ngi la phai uwei pa uwei sha la ka jong ka lynti, bad U Trai u la buh halor jong u ïa ka jingbymhok jong ngi baroh” (Isaiah. 53:6). ïa ka jinglong ba kyllum jong ka pop la pyni hangne; ka jinglong ba kyllum jong ka Diengphna, ka jingpynduh ïa ka pop, la pyni ruh hangne . Kane ka lynnong, lehse ka kham bun ban ïa kiwei pat ha baroh ar ki Testament Barim bad Bathymmai, ka pyni ïa ka jingthmu jong ka jingpynim ha kum kine ki kyntien kiba shai bad ki bym don jingartatien.

Hynrei ka jingkhot jong ka jingiathuhlypa jong u Isaiah kam shym la long kaba suk. Ki briew ki shem ba ki jingiathuhlypa ba shyrkhei jong u shaphang ka jingpynjot ki long kiba bakla, kumba bun ki leh mynta haba la maham ïa ki shaphang ki jingma kiba mih na ka pop (Rom 6:23). Ka sur iathuhlypa jong u Isaiah na ka bynta ka por jong u bad jong ngi ka long kaba shai (peit Isaiah 2:4, Isaiah 5:20). Dei ka jingbym kwah jong ka jaitbynriew ban leh ïa ka hok bad ban pynthymmai kaba la pynlong ïa ka jingpynjot ïa ka Jerusalem. Kajuh ka pop kan pyni ïa ka jingpynjot ïa ki nongshong shnong bymman jong ka pyrthei ynda U Khrist un wan phai pat.

`Leit biang lyngba ka Isaiah 53 bad thoh lut ïa kiei kiei baroh kiba U Jisu U leh na ka bynta jong ngi, kumba la pyni ha kita ki dkhot. Kaei ka jingkyrmen kaba khraw kaba phi lah ban shim na ka bynta ïa lade na kaei kaba u nongiathuhlypa u la pynpaw shaphang ka jingiap U Khrist ba ka la leh na ka bynta jong ngi?`
