---
title:  Ka Jingiathuhlypa Ka Long Ka Jingpynpaw Ba Shisha U Blei
date:   22/12/2026
---

Katto katne ki riewstad ki khein ba ki don palat sawphew san hajar tylli ki kynhun niam Khristan ha ka pyrthei mynta. Kumno ka jingngeit kaba la sdang na U Jisu Khrist ka la lah ban san sha kiba bun ki rukom ngeit bapher bapher? Ka jubab ka shong ha ki jingbatai bapher bapher jong ka Jingthoh Bakhuid kiba la nangbun katba nang mih ki sngi. Ka paw shai ba kim lah ban long kiba dei baroh namar, nalor baroh, ha kiba bun ki khep, kaei kaba kawei ka balang ka hikai ka iapyrshah ïa kaei kaba kiwei pat ki leh.

Naduh kaba sdang (peit Jenesis. 2:16, 17; Jenesis. 3:1-5), ka jingkulmar ka long kawei na ki lad ba khraw tam jong u Soitan ban ialam ïa ki briew sha jngai na ka jingshisha U Blei. Kumba u la leh ïa u Adam bad ka Im, u Soitan u khleh-lang tang kat ban biang ka jingshisha bad ka jingbakla khnang ban khring ïa ki shynrang bad kynthei hapoh ka jingsynshar jong u. U Soitan u la ialam bakla ïa ki briew U Blei lyngba ka por (2 Khronikl. 36:14-16). U la pyrshang wat ban ialam bakla ïa U Jisu (Mathaios. 4:1-11), hynrei U Jisu u la iakynduh ïa ki jingkyrpad jong u Soitan da ka jingkren kaba shai “La thoh” (Mathaios. 4:4,).

`Pule 2 Timothy 4:1-5. Kaei ka jingai jingmut kaba kongsan kaba u Paul u ai ha u Timothi shaphang ka Ktien U Blei? Balei u Paul u da kwah haduh katta katta ba u Timothi un long uba la pynkhreh bad u bym khih ha ka jinglong mynsiem na ka bynta kaei kaban sa wan?`

Da ka khmat jong ka jingiathuhlypa, u Paul u la iohi lypa ïa ka por ba ki shynrang bad ki kynthei kin ym ieit ïa ka Ktien U Blei bad kin ym kohnguh ïa ka. Ha ka jaka kata, kin leit wad ïa ki nonghikai ba kynja mynsiem ban pynkhih ïa ki jingkwah brai jong ki da ki pharshi ba la shna da ka buit stad. Ha kiba kum kita, U Blei U phah ïa ki jingpynthame kiba khlaiñ khnang ba kin ngeit ïa ki jinglamler ïa kaba ki hi ki ïa wad. “Te namar kane U Blei un phah ha ki ïa ka jingtrei ka jingbakla, ba kin ngeit ïa ka lamler: khnang ba yn ioh pynrem ïa kita baroh kibym shym la ngeit ïa ka jingshisha, hynrei kiba la sngewbha ha ka jingbymhok.” (2 Thessoloni. 2:11, 12).

Hynrei ha kito kiba plie, U Jisu U la kular ba kin “tip ïa ka jingshisha” (loannis. 8:32). Ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa ka long ka lad jong U Blei ban pyniakhlad ïa ka jingshisha na ka jingbakla, ïa ka jingshai na ka jingdum.

Ha kawei ka jingmut, ngi lah ban tip ïa ka jingshisha da kaba suk bha hapdeng kine ki kynhun niam bapher bapher baroh. Lada phim thmu ban iehnoh ïa ka sngi ba Hynniew ka Sabbaton jong ka Baibl, phi la pynduh pyndam noh 99.5 percent na ki lad ki lynti jong phi, namar kiba bun ki balang kim sumar ïa ka hukum kaba saw.

Lada phim ym ngeit ba kiba iap ki leit mardor sha bneng ban don ryngkat bad U Jisu ne ki dang shah thang mynta ha dujok bad sha ki bymjukut? haduh bymjukut, kumjuh— te kumno?

U la pynduh noh ïa kaba bun, lada ym baroh, na ka .5 percent kaba sah.

Shano pat phin leit lait na ka Balang Seventh Day Adventist?
