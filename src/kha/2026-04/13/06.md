---
title:  Ka Jingiathuhlypa Ki Pyniatylli ïa Kamet Ha U Khrist
date:   24/12/2026
---

`Pule Ioannis 17:20-23 bad 1 Korinth 1:10-13; Kaei ka jingkyrpad ba kongsan kaba son ha ka pdeng jong kine ki ar tylli ki bynta jong ka Jingthoh Bakhuid?`

Katba U Jisu u nang wan shajan ka diengphna, la pynkhih ïa ka dohnud jong U da kawei ka jingkwah kaba khraw. U kwah ba ki nongbud jong U baroh kin long kawei, kumba Ma U bad U Kpa ki long kawei ha ka jingieit, ka jingthmu bad ka jingleh. Ka jingiatylli kaba U Jisu u la duwai kam shym la long kano kano ka jinglong kawei kaba suk. U Jisu u la duwai ïa ka jingiathuhlypa kaba donbor kaba yn pyndep shuwa ka jingwan phai jong U — ka jingtap jong ka jingriewspah jong ki briew kiba don ki jinglong bapher bapher, ki jaitbynriew bad ki jingpyrkhat bapher bapher baroh kin iatylli ha U bad ha U Kpa, lyngba ka bor U Mynsiem Bakhuid. U apostol Paul u la tei halor kane ka phang haba u la kyrpad ïa ki bangeit ha Korinth ban don kajuh ka jingmut, ban kren ïa kajuh ka jingshisha, bad ban ym don ïa ki jingiapait iapra (1 Korinth. 1:10). Hynrei balei kane ka jingiathuhlypa jong ka jingïatylli ka long kaba kongsan?

U Jisu u la duwai “ba baroh kin ialong shitylli; kum ma me, ko Kpa, ha nga, nga ruh ha me, ba ki ruh kin ialong shitylli ha ngi; ba kata ka pyrthei kan ioh ngeit ba la phah ma me ïa nga.” (Ioannis 17:21).

U Blei u kwah ba baroh ki shynrang bad kynthei kin ioh jingpynim, hynrei shuwa ba kin ioh jingpynim, ki dei ban ngeit ha U Jisu bad ngeit ba U Blei U la phah ïa U ban pynim ïa ka pyrthei. Haba ka kynhun jong ki briew ba pher bapher ki kam ïa U Jisu kum U Trai bad U Nongpynim jong ki, kiba ieit bad shakri iwei ïa iwei pat khlem da pyrkhat ialade, te ka pyrthei kan ngeit ba U Jisu U long uba U la kam ba U long. Ka jingiatylli kaba U Jisu u kwah eh ka long kaba don jingthmu bad kaba donbor. Kaba sngewsih ka long ba wat naduh kaba sdang, ki bor ki la pynma ïa kata ka jingiatylli. Peit, kum ka nuksa, ïa ki Kam 6:1. Bad ka niam Khristan ka la nang iai ïa pait ïa pra naduh kata ka por!

Kane ka jingiatylli ka long ka jingtrei jong U Mynsiem Bakhuid lyngba ki jingai kiba U ai. Ka Ephesos 4:11–15 ka iathuh ïa ngi ba la ai ïa ki jingai jong U Mynsiem Bakhuid ban pynkhreh ïa kiba ngeit bad ban tei ïa ka met U Khrist, “tadynda ngi ioh poi baroh sha kata ka jinglong kawei ka jong ka jingngeit, bad ka jong ka Jingtip ïa U Khun U Blei, haduh ka jinglong rangbah bha. haduh ka jingthew ka rynieng ka jingdap U Khrist” (Ephesos 4:13). Ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa, kawei na ki jingai ba kongsan jong U Mynsiem, ka long kaba donkam eh ban pyniatylli ïa ki briew jong U Blei, khamtam haba ki la jan sha ka jingwan arsien U Jisu, khnang ba kin ngeit ha U Trai Jisu Khrist bad ban ioh jingpynim (Ki Kam 16:31).

`Ka la don ka por ba ka Balang Seventh-day Adventist ka long lehse ka kumba ka paw kynhun niam Protestant kaba bun jait bynriew tam ha ka pyrthei, kumno ngin lah ban pyni ïa ka jinglong shitylli kaba iarap ïa ki briew ban ngeit ha U Jisu? Kiei ki bor kiba pynthut ïa kata ka jingiatylli?`
