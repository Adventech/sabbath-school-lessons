Katto katne snem mynshuwa, ka kompeni Kellogg ka la pynmih ïa ka jingkren slokan kaba sngewtynnad na ka bynta ki jingbam mynstep ba la shna jong ka. Ha ka jingpyrshang ban ïoh biang ïa ki nongthied kiba la leit sha kiwei pat ki brand, ka la pynshlur ïa ki ban “mad biang ïa ki kum ha ka sien nyngkong eh.” Hato kane ka jingong ka lah ban treikam ruh na ka bynta ki Seventh Day Adventist kiba la pynlut bun ka por ban pule shaphang ka Baibl bad ka Ellen G. White bad kham duna ka por ban “mad” ïa ki jingthoh jong ka? Ka jingleit sha ki seminar shaphang ka Ellen G. White kan ai jingmut ïa phi shaphang ka jingim bad ka jingshakri jong ka, hynrei lymda phi “mad” hi, phin ym lah shisha ban bishar bad mad ïa ka jingkyrkhu ba kynja mynsiem jong ki khubor jingiathuhlypa jong ka. Tang kito kiba pule ïa ka, khamtam ha ka jingiadei, kiba lah ban sngewthuh pura ïa kaei kaba U Blei U la ai ha ngi.

Kumba ngi la iohi ha kane ka kwartar baroh kawei, ka Ellen G. White ka pynskhem ïa ngi ha ka jingshisha jong ka Baibl bad ka pyniasoh ïa ngi bad U Blei. Ki don katto katne ki jingkyrkhu jong ka jingpule ïa ki khubor jingiathuhlypa jong ka.

Ka jingwad bniah ba la leh da u Roger Dudley bad u Des Cummings Jr. ka pynpaw ïa ki katto katne ki jingshisha kiba sngewtynnat. Kito kiba ju pule man ka por ïa ki jingthoh jong ka Ellen G. White ki don ka jingiadei kaba kham jylliew bad kaba jan bad U Jisu ban ïa kito ki bym ju pule ïa ka; ki don ka jingsngewskhem kaba kham khlain ïa ka jingpynim, ka jingiaseng step miet ha ka longiing kaba kham man ka por, bad ka jingkwah ban sakhi ïa kiwei pat. “Ka jingwad bniah ka lap ïa ki sakhi kiba la noh haduh katta katta sha kawei ka jingpynkut,” la thoh ki arngut ki nongwad bniah. “Ha ka jingwad bniah ïa ka jingsan jong ka balang, ha kawei pa kawei ka mat kaba iadei bad ki jinglong ba shimet ne ki jingleh, u dkhot uba ju pule man ka por ïa ki kot jong ka Ellen G. White u kham don ha jrong ban ban ïa u dkhot uba pule ïa ki tang teng teng ne bym ju pule satia. ... Hooid ka jingmut ka long ba ka jingpule man ka por ïa ki jingthoh jong ka Ellen White ka pynlong shisha ïa ka jingkylla kaba bha ha ka jingim bad jingsakhi Khristan.”—U Dudley bad u Cummings, “Who Reads Ellen White?” Ministry, October 1982, p. 12.

Ha kane ka quarter, ngi la ïoh jinghikai ba U Blei U ïakren lyngba ki nongïathuhlypa jong U. Ki khubor jong ki ki pyniasoh ïa ngi bad U Blei bad ki wanrah ïa ka jingktah kaba khuid bad kaba pynkyntang ha ki bynta jong ka jingim u briew baroh, khamtam kaba iadei bad ka jingkular jong ka jingpynim kaba la shem ha U Jisu: “Ah to mad bad to khmih ba U Trai u long uba bha” (Salm 34:8). Ki khot ïa ngi sha ka jingleh kiba bakhuid, ka jingkylla ha ka jingim, ka jingialap shimet, bad ka jingim ban shakri ïa kiwei pat. Kane ka long ka jingpynkhreh kaba donkam eh ban pyndep ïa ka bynta jong ngi ha ka jingpynbna kaba khatduh sha ka pyrthei katba ngi dang khmih lynti ïa ka jingwan U Jisu.
