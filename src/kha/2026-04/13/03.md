---
title:  Ki Jingiathuhlypa Ki Pynskhem ïa Ka Baibyl
date:   21/12/2026
---

“Ka Baibl ka long kaba kut kaba long ka jingmih jong U Mynsiem Bakhuid, lyngba ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa. 2 Petros 1:21 ka pynpaw ïa ka rukom treikam kaba U Blei u la pyndonkam: “Namar ym da ka mon u briew la wan ka jingiathuhlypa mynno mynno ruh: hynrei ki briew ki la kren na U Blei keiñ, haba la pynkhih ïa ki da U Mynsiem Bakhuid.”

`Pule 2 Timothy 3:16, 17. Kumno kine ki dkhot kiban jur ha baroh ar ha ka bor bad ka jingpyrsad mynsiem jog ka jingthoh?`

Khlem ka Baibl, ki briew kin sah khlem da shem ïa ka lad ban iaid lyngba ïa ki jingeh jong ka pyrthei kaba pop, bad kata kan long ka rukom ïa ka jingpynjot. Mynta ka Baibyl ka don ha man la ki jaka bad ka don ha ki rukom bapher bapher, hynrei kane kam shym la long kumta man ka por. Kum ka nuksa, ha ka por U Jisu, ki don tang ki kot ba kli jong ka Testament Barim kiba lah ban pule, bad tang ki lyngdoh ne ki Nonghikai kiba lah ban leh kumta. Ki briew ki riew paid bah bunsien kim ju ioh lad beit ban ioh ïa ka Jingthoh Bakhuid. Ka ktien na U Blei ka long kaba kordor ha kito ki sngi.

`Pule Loukas 24:27. Hadien jong ka jingmihpat jong U, kaei kaba u Jisu U Pyndonkam ban iarap ïa ki ar ngut ki synran kiba la duh jingkyrmen ban sngewthuh ïa ka jingthmu kaba kyrpang jong U?`

U Jisu, u Nongiathuhlypa uba khraw tam haduh mynta — Uta Uba long tang marwei U Ktien (Ioannis 1:1-3) — u la pynkhraw ïa ka Jingthoh Bakhuid (Ioannis 7:38). Napdeng ki jingkyrkhu kiba kordor tam jong ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa ka long kaba pynskhem ïa ka Baibl.

“Kito kiba la shah pynkhih da ki Jingthoh Bakhuid kum ka sur U Blei, bad kiba kwah ban bud ïa ki jinghikai jong ka, ki dei ban long kiba pule man ka sngi, kiba pdiang man ka sngi ïa ka jingkmen bad ka bor ba kynja mynsiem, ïa kiba la pynbiang na ka bynta uwei pa uwei u bangeit bashisha ha ka jingai U Mynsiem Bakhuid.”—Ellen G. White, ha ka Signs of the Times, March 8, 1910. Ka pynkhraw ïa ka Jingthoh Bakhuid. Ka ju kdew biang ïa ki shynrang bad kynthei sha ka Ktien U Blei kum ka kyndon jong ka jingngeit bad jingleh jong ki kaba tang ma ka.

`Balei U Jisu U la kdew nyngkong ïa ki arngut ki synran ha ka lynti sha Emmaus sha ki Jingthoh Bakhuid shuwa ban pynpaw ha ki Uei U long? Aiu kane ka iathuh ïa ngi shaphang ka nongrim ka Ktien ka dei ban long ïa ngi?`
