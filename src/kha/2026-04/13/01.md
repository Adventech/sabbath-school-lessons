### Ki Jingpule Na Ka Bynta Kane Ka Taïew
1 Korinth. 2:2; Ioannis 1:1–5; 2 Timothi. 3:16, 17; 2 Timothi. 4:1–5; Isaiah. 40:1–5; Ioannis 17:20–23; 1 Korinth. 1:10–13.

> <p>Dkhot Spel Lyndet</p>
> “Ka jingthoh baroh ka long kaba la pyrsad mynsiem da U Blei, bad kaba myntoi ïa ka jinghikai, ïa ka jingsneng, ïa ka jingpynbeit, ïa ka jingpynmlien kaba ha ka hok: khnang ba u briew jong U Blei un ioh long janai, haba la pyndap biang ïa u sha ka jingleh babha baroh.” (2 Timothi 3:16, 17).

U Blei um ju ong ïa ngi ba ngin shu ngeit matlah. Ha kaba ïa pyrsha ïa kata, ka sakhi ïa ka jingiashem jong U ka long kaba don bor. Ka mariang marwei hi, wat hadien 6000 ki snem jong ka pop, ki jingsakhi ïa ka jingshisha ym dei tang ba U Blei uba don ka bor thaw kaba phylla (peit ïa ki dur na ka James Webb Space Telescope) hynrei ïa U Blei jong ka jingieit.

Bad la katta ruh ki dang don shuh shuh. Wat hadien kine ki kyntien “Te ïa kane ka ktien ngi la iohsngew ma ngi ba ka da wan na bneng, mynba ngi iadon lem bad u ha Uta u lum bakyntang.” U Petros un dang thoh ba ngi “Bad ngi don kata ka ktien ka jingiathuhlypa ba la kham pynskhem: ha kaba phi leh bha haba phi pynshah shkor ïa ka, kumba ha ka sharak batyngshain ha ka jaka badum, tadynda kan mih ka sngi, bad u lur shai un da mih ha ki dohnud jong phi:” (2 Petros 1: 18,19). Ngi lah ban ym iohsngew, kumba u Petros u la iohsngew, ïa ka sur U Blei, hynrei ngi ruh, ngi don ïa ka “ktien jingiathuhlypa kaba kham skhem kham tikna.”

U Blei u la kren ha ngi lyngba ki nongiathuhlypa jong U, kiba long canonical (ki jingthoh ha ka Baibyl) bad ki non-canonical (ki jingthoh ki bym don ha ka Baibyl). Ki sakhi ba la ai ha ngi ki long kiba kham bun ban ïa kaba bun. Ka jingkylli kaba sah ka long: Hato ngi plie bad kloi ban pdiang ïa ka?

Ka histori ka pyni ba bun kim shym la plie. Lehse ngi, da ka jingaiei U Blei, to ngin ym leh ïa kajuh ka jingbakla,

_Pynkhreh ïa kane ka lynnong na ka bynta ka Sabbaton December 26._
