---
title:  Ki Jingiathuhlypa Ki Kyntiew ïa U Khrist
date:   20/12/2026
---

Ka jingiathuhlypa ka long ka lad jong ka jingiakren jong U Blei bad ki briew jong U bad ban pyniasoh ïa ki bad U Blei. Kam long kaba phylla, namarkata, ba u Soitan (u bsein) u la bitar ïa kata ka kynthei (ka balang U Blei) bad u la leit ban ialeh thma bad kiba sah na u symbai jong ka (peit Jingpynpaw 12:17).

Kiei ki jingkynthoh kiba pyniapher ïa kane ka bynta kaba sah jong u symbai jong ka kynthei? Kita kiba sumar ïa ki hukum bad ki don ïa ka jingphla ïa U Jisu. Ka Jingpynpaw 19:10 ka iathuh ïa ngi ba kane ka jingphla ka long “U mynsiem ka jingiathuhlypa.”

`Pule 1 Korinth 2:2 bad Galatia 6:14. Kumno u Paul u iohi ïa ka jingthmu jong ka jingim bad ka jingshakri jong u — ka jingpynshlur kaba khraw na ka bynta kaba u la im?`

Nalor ki jingshem jong ki hi, ki apostol ki la pdiang ïa U Jisu halor ka jingphla jong ki nongiathuhlypa bad ki briew kiba hok, kiba ki ktien jong ki ki la jrong ha ka por kaba bun spah snem. Ka pyrthei Khristan ki la dap pura bad ki la don ki kynjri kiba dap pura da ki sakhi kiba iaid lyngba ïa ka Testament Barim bad Bathymmai: ha kaba nyngkong, kaba kdew sha U Nongpynim u ban wan, bad ha kaba ar, kaba pynpaw ïa U Jisu kum uba pyndep ïa ki kyndon jong U Nongpynim. Kine baroh ki la biang ban pynskhem ïa ka jingngeit jong kito kiba mon ban ngeit. “Ka jingthmu U Blei ka long ban iehnoh ïa ka jingiakop kum ka lad kaba biang ban pynsan ïa ka jingngeit ha ka bor U Blei bad U Khun jong u bad ha ka jingtrei U Mynsiem Bakhuid.”—Ellen G. White, U Mynsiem jong ka jingiathuhlypa, vol. 3, ka p. 182.

Ha kiba bun ki liang, ka mat jong ka jingngeit kaba pynim kam dei kaba iadei bad ka jingmut jingpyrkhat; ka jinglong ba kynja mynsiem. Ka dei ka jinglong jong ka dohnud, ym ka jinglong jong ka khlieh. Haba ki briew ki khang ïa la ki dohnud, ïa ki jingmut jingpyrkhat jong ki, kaba sngewsih, ka long ba kin kan bud.

`Pule Ioannis 1:1-5. Kaei ba kine ki dkhot ki iathuh ïa ngi shaphang U Jisu U dei U ei?`

Lada U Jisu, U Ktien jong U Blei, u la don lypa, u ba la Pynlong doh, bad u la shongsah hapdeng jong ngi, te kaba bud ka long ba U Jisu u la long uba kongsan eh ha ki kyntien ne ki khubor ba la ai da U Blei ha ki nongiathuhlypa jong U. Wat ki nongiathuhlypa kiba rah ïa ki khubor ki bym shym la kdew shai shaphang U Jisu wat la katta ruh la pyrsad mynsiem da U Mynsiem Bakhuid.

Pyrkhat katno ka pyrthei bneng ka long kaba heh, bad ban pyrkhat ba Uta Uba la thaw ïa ka, U Jisu, U long ruh Uta Uba la iap na ka bynta jong ngi! Kaei ka jingkyrmen kaba khraw kaba kata ka tyrwa ha phi?
