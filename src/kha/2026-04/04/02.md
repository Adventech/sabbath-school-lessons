---
title:  U Jisu Kum U Nongiathuhlypa
date:   18/10/2026
---

U Moses u la iathuhlypa ba “U Trai uba U Blei jong me un pynmih ha me ïa u nongiathuhlypa napdeng jong me, na ki para jong me, uba kum ma nga: ïa u phin shahshkor;” (Deut. 18:15). Uei u nongiathuhlypa? Ka Jingthoh Bakhuid hi ka pynshai: ka la kdew sha U Jisu hi. “‘Namar u Moses u la ong shisha ha ki kpa tymmen jong ki, “U Trai U Blei jong phi un pynmih na ka bynta jong phi ïa u nongïathuhlypa uba kum ma nga na ki para jong phi. ïa U phin iohsngew ha kiei kiei baroh, ïa kaei kaei kaba U ong ha phi” (Ki Kam 3:22; peit ruh Ioannis 6:14).

`Kaei ka kam Jong ka jingiathuhlypa jong U Jisu? Kumno la jubab ïa kne ka jingkylli ha ka por jong U Jisu? (Mathaios. 16:13-16, Markos 8:27-29, Loukas. 9:18-20).`

U Jisu, kum U Khrist, U Messaiah, U long Uta sha Uba baroh ki nongiathuhlypa jong ka Testament Barim ki la kdew. Ha ka Testament Bathymmai, la ithuh ïa U Jisu kum ka jingpyndep ïa ki jingiathuhlypa jong ki shaphang U Messaiah. Ha U Loukas 24:13-35, U pyni ïa lade kum Uta Uba ki jingiathuhlypa shaphang U Messaiah ki kdew.

Ha ka jingshisha, ha Ioannis 1:1–5, ka jingbatai ba la ai shaphang ka Ktien hangne kam iahap bad uno uno u briew ne tang u nongiathuhlypa briew. La ithuh ïa U Ktien kum U Nongthaw, namar “la thaw ïa kiei kiei baroh da U, bad khlem ma U ym shym la thaw ei ei kaba la thaw” (loannis. 1:3). Kata ka mut, U la thaw ïa kiei kiei baroh kiba la thaw, kata ka pyrthei bneng baroh kawei, kaba mynta la antad ba ka heh 93 billion snem ka jingshai (light-years wide).

Ha man la ka “bad U Blei u la ong” ha Jenesis 1, ngi iohsngew ïa U Jisu ba u kren. Uta uba la ong, “To long ka jingshai” (Jenesis. 1:3) u long ruh ka “jingshai jong ki briew” (Ioannis. 1:4), bad lyngba ka jingthmu jong ka jingpynim (Ioannis. 1:4, 5), U la pynduh noh ïa ka jingdum jong ka pyrthei.

Ki ktien iathuhlypa jong U Blei ki long kiba thikna bad kiba la pynskhem. Haba shah shkor, ki long “ka jingshai kaba tyngshAiñ ha ka jaka kaba dum, haduh ba ka sngi kan mih bad u khlur mynstep un mih ha ki dohnud jong phi” (2 Pet. 1:19, NKJV). Ka jingiathuhlypa kan iai bteng ban wanrah jingshai ïa ka pyrthei pop jong ngi haduh ba U Jisu, “U Khlur Mynstep,” bad U Tynrai jong kata ka jingshai jong ka jingiathuhlypa, un wanrah ïa ka hima bymjukut jong U.

Haduh kata ka por, lyngba ka jingim, ka jingiap bad ka jingmihpat jong U, U Khrist U tyrwa ha ngi baroh ïa ka jingim bymjukut. Ka jingkylli ka long tang la ngin pdiang ne em ïa ka.

`To shong ha ka jingshisha ba U Jisu, U Nongthaw (Ioannis 1:1–3), U long ruh Uta Uba la iap halor ka diengphna na ka bynta ki pop jong phi. Kane ka jingshisha kaba phylla ka hikai aiu shaphang ka jingshisha jong ka jingieit U Blei ïa ngi?`
