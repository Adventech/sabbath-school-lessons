---
title:  Ki Kam Jong Ki Nongiathuhlypa Jong Ka Jutang Bathymmmai (New Testament)
date:   20/10/2026
---

`Pule 1 Korinth 14: 3; Ephesos 4: 11-13 bad Jingpynpaw 22:6: ka iathuh ïa ngi ka jingtrei kam jong ki nongiathuhlypa ka Jutang Bathymmai?`

Kine ki dkhot haneng, wat la ki long kiba iarap, kim ai ïa ka jingpeit kaba bniah ïa kiei kiei baroh kiba ki nongiathuhlypa jong ka Jutang Bathymmai ki la leh. Wat la katta ruh, bun na kiei kiei kiba ki la leh ki pynpaw ïa kaei kaba ki nongiathuhlypa ha ka Jutang Barim ruh ki la leh, kumta ki ïai bteng bad ka jingai jingiathuhlypa.

Ha 1 Korinth 12:10, la kdew ïa ka jingiathuhlypa hapdeng kiwei pat ki jingai ba kynja mynsiem. Ha ka met U Khrist (1 Korinth. 12:27, 28), la kdew ïa ki nongiathuhlypa hadien ki apostol, kiba ki ruh ki la ioh ïa ki jingpynpaw na U Blei. Kat kum ka 1 Korinth 14:3: “Uta uba iathuhlypa u kren ïa ka jingtei bad ka jingpynshlur bad ka jingpyntngen ïa ki briew”.

Ka Ephesos 4:11–13 ka jer ïa ka kynhun kaba kum kata jong ki jingai ba kynja mynsiem, kumba 1 Korinth 12. La kdew ïa ki nongiathuhlypa hapdeng ki apostol, ki nongialap, ki pastor bad ki nonghikai (Ephesos 4:11). Kine ki briew ki don na ka bynta “ia kaban pynjanai ïa ki riewkhuid, ïa ka jingtrei ka jingshakri tien Blei, ïa kaban tei ïa ka met U Khrist: tadynda ngi ioh poi baroh sha kata ka jinglong kawei ka jong ka jingngeit, bad ka jong ka Jingtip ïa U Khun U Blei, haduh ka jinglong rangbah bha. haduh ka jingthew ka rynieng ka jingdap U Khrist:” (Ephesos. 4:12,13). Kumba lah ban iohi hangne bad shawei pat, U Jisu u long u pdeng jong ka khubor jong ki.

Ka kitab ki Kam ki Apostol ka la kren shaphang arngut ki nongiathuhlypa, u Judas bad u Silas (ym don uwei ruh uba la thoh ïa ka kitab ha ka Baibl), kiba “la kyntu bad pynskhem ïa ki para bangeit da ki ktien kiba bun” (Ki Kam 15:32). Wat la, hooid, teng teng ki nongiathuhlypa ki hap ban pynmih ïa ki jingmaham, wat ki jingmaham kiba tyngeh teng teng, ki ruh — kat kum ka jinglong jingman — ki ai ïa ki kyntien ai mynsiem, khamtam ha ki por ba eh, ki kyntien kiba khlem artatien la sngewnguh shibun eh da kito kiba donkam ban iohsngew ïa ki bad ïa ka jingkyrmen bad jingkyrmen kaba ki don.

Ia ka kam pynshlur ruh la kdew ha ka jinglong jong ka jingiathuhlypa (1 Kor. 14:31, NKJV). Ha ki por jong ka jingjynjar bad jingshah pynshitom bein, ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa ka la long ka tynrai jong ka jingpynshlur bad jingkyrmen.

Ha ka Jingpynpaw 22:6, la pyni ïa U Jisu kum “U Trai U Blei jong ki nongiathuhlypa bakhuid” bad uba “ki ktien ki long kiba lah ban shaniah bad kiba shisha”. (Hooid, kano kano kaba wan na ki shakri U Blei, haba ki kren na ka bynta U Blei, kan sa long “kaba iAiñeh bad kaba shisha,” ruh. Ha ka pyrthei ha kaba ngi lah ban shaniah tang khyndiat eh, ngi lah shisha ban shaniah ïa kaei kaba ki nongiathuhlypa jong U Trai ki ong, khamtam eh ha ka Ktien jong U, ka bor kaba khatduh bad kaba kut jong ngi.
