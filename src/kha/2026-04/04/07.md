---
title:  Ka Jingbuh ïa U Jisu Bad ïa Ka Ktien Jong U Ha Pdeng
date:   23/10/2026
---

Kumba ngi la ïoh jinghikai, U Blei u la khot ïa ki nongïathuhlypa ha ki por ba la buh kyrpang bad u la ai ha ki ïa ki kam kiba kongsan. Ka kamram ba kongsan jong ki ka long ban wanrah ïa ki briew sha ka jingtip ïa U Blei bad ka jingiadei kaba iAiñeh bad U. Kane ka la long ka jingialam ban hikai jong ka Ellen G. White. Ka jingshakri jong ka ka don ar tylli ki kam ba kongsan: ban ialam ïa ki briew sha U Jisu bad ban pyni ïa ki sha ka Ktien jong U — ka Baibl.

Ka Ellen G. White ka la don ka jingieit ïa U Jisu baroh shi jingim bad ka jingthrang ban iasam ïa ka jingieit U Blei bad kiwei pat. Ka kot ba pawnam jong ka kaba don phra tylli ki kot, Ka Jingialeh jong ki Yrta, ka sdang bad kut da ki kyntien “U Blei U long ka jingieit.” Ha ka Mawjam Sha U Khrist Steps to Christ), lehse ka kot kaba pawnam tam jong ka, ka la thoh kumne: “Ka jingsan jong ngi ha ka jingaiei, ka jingkmen jong ngi, ka jinglongba larkam jong ngi — baroh ki shong ha ka jingiasyllok jong ngi bad U Khrist. Dei da ka jingiasyllok bad U, man ka sngi, man ka kynta — da kaba iaisah ha U — ba ngi dei ban san ha ka jingaiei. Um long tang U Nongpynlong jong ka jingngeit jong ngi, hynrei U Khrist u nongpynjanai sha ka jingngeit.” Steps to Christ, p. 69.

Ha ki snem 1890, haba ka dang thoh ïa ka kot The Desire of Ages, ka la thoh kumne ha ka diary jong ka: “Ha kaba thoh shaphang ka jingim U Khrist nga shah ktah ha ka jingpyrkhat ban shah shitom. Nga klet ban ring mynsiem kumba nga dei ban ring mynsiem Ngam lah ban shah ïa ka jingjur jong ka jingsngew kaba wan halor jong nga katba nga pyrkhat ïa kaei kaba U Khrist U la shah ha ka pyrthei jong ngi.”— Manuscript 70, 1897. Ellen G. White loved to speak about Jesus.

Ka Baibl ka la long ka mat kaba ar jong ka jingpynleit jingmut jong ka Ellen White. Ka la iohi ïa ki jingthoh jong ka kum ka “jingshai kaba kham rit” kaba ialam ïa ki briew sha ka “jingshai kaba kham khraw” — ka Baibl (Selected Messages, kot 3, sla 29). Ka ju pynshlur ïa ki briew ban leit sha ka Jingthoh Bakhuid, ha kaba U Blei U la pynpaw ïa ka jinglong ba ieit bad ka jingthmu jong ka jingpynim jong U. Kumba ka la ong ha kaba sdang jong ka jingshakri jong ka: “Nga ai jingmut ha phi, ko nongpule baieit, ïa ka Ktien U Blei kum ka hukum jong ka jingngeit bad jingleh jong phi.”— Selected Messages, book 3, p. 30.

Hadien bun snem kan sa thoh kumne: “U Trai u kwah ïa phi ban pule ïa ki Baibl jong phi. Um shym la ai kano kano ka jingshai kaba kham heh ban shim ïa ka jaka jong ka Ktien jong U... Lada bam bad nguid ïa ka, [Ka Ktien jong U] ka long kum ka snam-kaba im jong ka mynsiem. Te ïa ki kam babha yn iohi kum ka jingshai kaba tyngshAiñ ha ka jingdum.”— Letter 130, 1901.

Ka Ellen G. White ruh ka ngeit ba baroh kiba kam ïa ka jAiñkup nongiathuhlypa jong U Khrist ki dei ban hikai “ia kaei kaba U Khrist U la hikai. ïa kata kaba U la kren, ym tang ha lade, hynrei lyngba ki nongiathuhlypa bad ki nonghikai baroh jong ka Testament Barim, la kynthup lang hangne. The Desire of Ages, p. 826.

Ka Ellen G. White ka la buh ïa U Jisu bad ïa ka Jingthoh Bakhuid ha pdeng jong ka jingshem Khristan jong ka. Ha ka sngi pynkhreh ban wan, ngin sa wad bniah ïa ki jingmut jong ka shaphang ka jingpynpaw bad jingpyrsad mynsiem.
