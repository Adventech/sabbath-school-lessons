---
title:  Ki Nongïathuhlypa Ha Ka Jutang Bathymmai
date:   22/10/2026
---

`Pule Mathaios 24:23-24. Kaei kaba U Jisu U maham ïa ngi hangne? Balei ba ngi dei ban shim khia ïa kane ka jingmaham?`

U Jisu hi un jin da la lah ban kham shai: “Namar ki Khrist lamler bad ki nongiathuhlypa lamler kin mih bad kin pyni ïa ki dak bad ki jingphylla kiba khraw ban shukor, lada lah, wat ïa kiba la jied’” (Mathaios. 24:24).

Ki jingpynthame kiba U maham ki dei ban long kiba la khring shibun, lada, shisha, “da ki dak bad ki jingphylla kiba khraw” kin long shibynta ha kane. Ki jingjia kiba phylla, ha lade hi, kim long ki sakhi jong ka jingtrei U Blei, wat la ki lah ban paw kiba pynthame katno katno ruh. Ngi don ki nongshun kiba kham khraw. Kumba u Paul u la maham ïa ngi: “Naba ka jingialeh jong ngi kam long ïa ka snam bad ka doh, hynrei ïa ki hima, ïa ki bor, ïa ki nong-synshar pyrthei jong kane ka jingdum, ïa ki jingbymman bakynja mynsiem ha ki jaka bneng.” (Eph 6:12). Bad kine ki bor ki trei lyngba ki nongiathuhlypa lamler.

Ha ka jingshisha, ki nongiathuhlypa lamler kim dei kiei kiei kiba thymmai. Ka Jutang Bathymmai ka thew ïa ki nongiathuhlypa lamler ha ki por jong ka Jutang Barim (Loukas. 6:26). Ha ka Jutang Bathymmai, la pynianujor ïa ki nongiathuhlypa lamler bad “ki suri kiba shyrkhei’” kiba don “ha ka shñiuh langbrot’” (Mathaios. 7:15), bad la iathuh ïa ngi ba ngin “‘ithuh ïa ki da ki soh jong ki’” (Mathaios. 7:16).

Ka kam ba kongsan jong ki ka long ka jingpynshoi (Mathaios. 7:17-20). U Jisu hi u la pynbna ba “bun ki nongiathuhlypa lamler kin mih bad kin shukor ïa kiba bun” (Mathaios. 24:11), bad “lada lah, wat ïa kiba la jied” (Mathaios. 24:24; peit ruh Markos 13:22, 1 Ioannis 4:1).

`Kat kum Ephesos 6:10-18, kumno ngin iada ïa lade na kine ki jait jingpynthame?`

Ka jingim da ka jingngeit, ka jingdeng ïa ka “jingda shadem jong ka hok,” ka jingpanpoh ïa la ka syngkai da ka “jingshisha,” ryngkat bad ka jingaiti ha U Mynsiem Bakhuid lyngba ka jingduwai, bad ba la pynshong nongrim ha ka Ktien jong U — kine ki long ki lad kiba lah ban iada ïa ngi na kiei kiei baroh kiba lym kumta kin ialam bakla ïa ngi ha ka jinglong mynsiem bad ha ka phAiñoh na ka jingtip ïa U Blei.

`Kaei kaba phi lah ban leh ban pynkhreh kham bha ïa lade pyrshah ïa ki jingpynshoi baroh kiba la jyllei ha ka pyrthei jong ngi?`
