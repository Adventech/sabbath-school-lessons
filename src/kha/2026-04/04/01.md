### Ki Jingpule Na Ka Bynta Kane Ka Taïew
Mathaios. 16:13-16, Ephesos. 2:20, Jingpynpaw 22:6, Loukas 2:36-38, Ki Kam 21:9, Ephesos. 6:10-18.

> <p>Dkhot Kynmaw</p>
> “Te don kiba pher ki jingai, hynrei ujuh U Mynsiem. Bad don kiba pher ki jingshakri hynrei ujuh U Trai. Bad don kiba pher ki jingtrei, hynrei u long ujuh U Blei uba pyntrei ïa kiei kiei baroh ha baroh.” (1 Korinth 12:4-6).

Katba u kpa u dang ñiah kali, u khun jong u u la peit ïa ki kali kiba iaid lyngba khnang ban lap ïa kito kiba long kumjuh bad ka jong u kpa jong u. Nangta u kpa u la kdew sha kawei ka kali bad u la pyrta, “Ko khun, kata ka long kumjuh bad ka kali kaba nyngkong jong nga!” Ynda ki la poi ha iing, u kpa u la sei ïa ka kot jinghikai ka kali kaba nyngkong jong u, ryngkat bad ka kot jinghikai ïa ka kali kaba mynta. U la pyni bha ïa la u khun ïa ki jinglong jong kawei pa kawei ka kali, da kaba pynpaw ïa kiei kiei kiba iadei dur bad ïa kaei kaba la pynkylla. Hynrei haba u kpa u la ianujor ïa ki ar tylli ki engine, baroh ki long kiba syriem ka juh ka dur. Ka la kylla shibun ha kiwei, hynrei ym ka enjin.

U Blei U la kren lyngba ki nongiathuhlypa ha ka Testament Barim bad Bathymmai, bad ka jingiaid beit bad ka jingiasyriem ha ki khubor jong ki ka long namar ba U Mynsiem Bakhuid — Ka Tynrai, ne “Engine” jong ka jingpyrsad Mynsiem U Blei — ka ïai sah kumjuh. U Mynsiem Bakhuid U ai bor ïa ki nongiathuhlypa bashisha lyngba ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa. U buh ruh ïa U Khrist ha pdeng jong ki khubor jong ki nongiathuhlypa, kumba ki Hebru ki pynshai (Heb. 1:1-3).

Ha kane ka taiew ngin bishar ïa ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa ha ka jutang Bathymmai, da kaba peit mano ba trei kum ki nongiathuhlypa, kaei kaba ki nongiathuhlypa ki leh, kumno ki nongiathuhlypa ki ianujor bad ki apostol, bad ka jingma jong ki nongiathuhlypa lamler, kumjuh.

_Pynkhreh ïa kane ka Lynnong na ka bynta ka Sabbaton, October 24._
