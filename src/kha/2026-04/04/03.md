---
title:  Ki Apostol Bad Ki Nongïathuhlypa
date:   19/10/2026
---

Ka Testament Bathymmai ka kdew ïa ka kam jong ki nongiathuhlypa bad ki apostol. Ki apostol ki long “ki nongrah Khubor,” lane “kiba la phah” na U Blei, kumba la batai da ka ktien Grik apostolos. Ha ki Gospel baroh kawei, ka kyntien “apostol” ka thew ïa ki 12 ngut ki synran. Kiwei pat ki bynta jong ka Testament Bathymmai ki kyrshan ïa kane ka jingmut bad ki kynthup ïa kiwei pat ki briew shimet ki bym shym la don hapdeng ki synran ba lajied jong U Khrist.

U Paul u khot ïa lade u apostol 12 sien ha ki shithi jong u. Ha ki Rom 1:1, 2, u pyniadei ïa ka jingkhot apostol jong u, bad ïa ka jingdon jong ka jingialap jong u, bad ka khubor jong ki nongiathuhlypa jong ka Testament Barim. U Paul u pyni ïa lade kum u apostol ha ki Jentil (Rom 11:13). La thung ïa u “da ka mon U Blei” (1 Korinth. 1:1).

Ym kum ki lyngdoh kiba bud ïa ka jinghiar pateng na ka iing naduh ki kpa haduh ki khun, U Blei U la thung ïa ki nongiathuhlypa bad ki apostol kat kum ka mon jong U. Haba ka jinglong apostol jong u Paul ka la don ha ka jingiakajia, u la iada ïa ka jingkhot jong u (1 Korinth. 9:1, 2). U Paul u iohi ïa ka jinglong apostol jong u kum kaba tei halor ka kam U Khrist, ka Nongrim jong ka balang (Ephesos. 2:20). Kum ki nongpdiang ïa ka jingpynpaw U Blei, ki apostol namarkata ki don ïa kajuh ka kyrdan jong ka jingpynshlur kum ki nongiathuhlypa (Ephesos. 3:1-5).

Ha 2 Petros 3:1, 2, u Petros u kdew ïa ki nongiathuhlypa kum kito kiba la wan shuwa bad kiba ki ktien jong ki ki long kiba kongsan, hynrei mynta la pyni ïa ki apostol kum kiba kit ïa kajuh ka bor. Ha kine ki dkhot, la kdew sha ki por jong ka Testament Barim bad Bathymmai. Katba ki nongiathuhlypa ki lah ban long kumba ki long ki nonglam khubor jong U Blei ha ka juk jong ka Testament Barim bad ki apostol ki long ki nonglam khubor jong U Blei ha ka juk jong ka Testament Bathymmai, ki la don ruh ki briew kiba la iathuhlypa ne pyndonkam ïa ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa hynrei kim dei ki apostol. Ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa kam shym la long tang ha ki apostol ha ki por jong ka Testament Bathymmai. Bad ym baroh ki nongiathuhlypa ki la long ki apostol (Ki Kam 15:32).

`Pule Ephesos 2:20 bad Ephesos 3:5. Kumno ki Apostol bad ki nongïathuhlypa ki trei kam ha ka jingiadei U Khrist bad ka balang?`

Bad kumba ngi tip ruh na kaba pule ïa kaei kaba ki apostol ha ka Testament Bathymmai ki la thoh, U Jisu u la long ka pdeng jong jan baroh kaba ki la thoh. Ha kawei ka rukom ne kawei pat, ka jingim, ka jingiap, ka jingmihpat, ka jingshakri rangbah lyngdoh bad ka jingwan phai pat jong U ki long ki jingthmu ba kongsan jong ki.

`Ka Baibl ka long tang ma ka ka jingthoh kaba thikna kaba ngi don shaphang ki ktien jong ki apostol bad ki nongiathuhlypa shaphang U Jisu, ka jingim bad ki kam jong U. Kane ka dei ban iathuh aiu ïa ngi shaphang balei ba ka Baibl ka dei ban don ka bor kaba khatduh ïa kaei kaba ngi ngeit shaphang U Jisu bad ka jingiap jong U na ka bynta jong ngi?`
