---
title:  Ki Nongiathuhlypa Kynthei bad ki Nongsharai Samla
date:   21/10/2026
---

`Pule Loukas 2:36-38; bad Ki Kam 21:9. Kaei ba kine ki dkhot ki iathuh ïa ngi sha phang ki nongiathuhlypa Kynthei?`

Ha ki por jong ka Jutang Barim, U Blei U la khot ïa ki shynrang bad kynthei ban long ki nongrah khubor jong U. Ha ka por jong ka Jutang Bathymmai, ka don ka jingpynshisha kaba shai shaphang ki nongiathuhlypa kynthei. Ka Anna ka long kaba nyngkong eh kaba la khot “ka nongiathuhlypa” ha ka Jutang Bathymmai. Ka long kawei kaba, ha ka jingshakri ba pura jong ka, “ka la shakri ïa U Blei da ka jingshah jingit bad ki jingduwai mynmiet bad sngi bad ... ka la Aiñguh ïa U Trai, bad ka la kren shaphang jong U” (Loukas. 2:37, 38).

Ki Kam ki Apostol 21:9 ka ong ba u Philip u don “saw ngut ki khun kynthei kiba dang sotti kiba la iathuhlypa”. Ki nongthoh histori jong ka Jutang Bathymmai, kum u Eusebius, ki la shem ïa ki jingthoh kiba hadien kiba kdew ïa ka jaka ba ki shong, bad nangta la tep ïa ki. La kumno kumno, kaei kaba ngi iohi ki long ki kynthei, kiba kham bunsien ki long ki samla kynthei, kiba don ïa ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa. Ngim tip thikna ïa kaei kaba ki la iathuhlypa ne ka jingïar jong ka jingshakri jong ki. Hynrei kawei kaba ngi tip shaphang kine ki samla kiba don ïa ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa: ym don kawei ruh na ki ktien jingiathuhlypa jong ki kaba la pynrung ïa ka ha ka canon, ka Baibl. Kata ka long, kum kiwei pat, ki long ki nongiathuhlypa ki bym dei ki kyndon ki nongiathuhlypa, ki nongiathuhlypa bashisha kiba ïa ki ktien, ki jingthoh, kaei kaei, ym shym la pynlong ïa ki kum ki kitab jong ka baibl.

Nalor ki nongiathuhlypa kynthei, ki pastor samla ki trei ha ka jingiasyllok kaba jan bad ki apostol. Ki Apostol ki la hikai ïa ki nongialam samla na ka bynta ka jingshakri. U Paul, kum ka nuksa, u la khot ïa u Timothi kum “u khun bashisha ha ka jingngeit” (1 Timothi. 1:2; peit ruh 1 Timothi. 4:12). U Paul u la hikai ïa u Timothi ïa ki jingdonkam jong ka jingshakri u pastor. U ong shaphang ka jingkhot jong u: “Nga aiti ïa kane ha me, ko Timothi u khun jong nga, katba kum kita ki jingiathuhlypa ba la long mynshuwa shaphang jong me, ba da ki men ioh ialeh thma kata ka jingialeh thma babha” (1 Timothi. 1:18).

Ha u Titos 1:4, u Paul u la pynithuh ïa u Titos kum “u khun bashisha ha ka jingngeit ba iadei jong ngi”. U Paul u la ai ha u Titos ïa ka kam ban thung ïa ki nongialam na ka bynta ki bangeit ha Krit, ka balang kaba don jingeh. U la ai jingmut ba kynja mynsiem ïa u samla Titos kumno ban iakynduh ïa kane ka jingkitkhlieh kaba kongsan, nangta u la pynkut kumne: “To kren ïa kine kiei kiei, to kyntu bad to sneng da ka bor baroh. Wat ai ba uno uno un niewbeiñ ïa me” (Titos 2:15).

`Samla, tymmen, shynrang, kynthei—baroh ngi lah ban don ka bynta ban leh ha ka kam U Blei. Kaei ka bynta jong phi, bad kumno phi lah ban leh kham bun?`
