### Ki Jingpule Na Ka Bynta Kane Ka Taïew
Hebru. 1:1, 2; 2 Timothi. 3:15-17; 2 Petros. 1:18-21; Eksodos. 17:14; Jingpynpaw 1:19; U Esekiel. 4:1-8.

> <p>Dkhot Spel-lyndet</p>
> “Ka jingpynpaw U Jisu Khrist, ïa kaba U Blei u la ai ha u, ban pyni ha ki shakri jong u ïa kiei kiei ruh kiba dei ban jia long shen: bad haba u la phah da u angel jong u, u la pyntip ïa ka ha U Ioannis u shakri jong u. uba la phla ïa ka ktien U Blei, bad ïa ka jingphla U Jisu Khrist, ïa kita kiei kiei baroh kein kiba u la iohi” (Jingpynpaw 1:1, 2).

U apostol Ioannis u la long shi bynta na ka jylli kaba shapoh jong ki Khadar ngut ki Apostol (Loukas. 8:51; Mathaios. 17:1; Mathaios. 26:36, 37). Hadien ka jingkiew jong U Khrist sha bneng, u Ioannis u la bteng ïa ka jingshakri jong u, ha Jerusalem. Hynrei shuwa ban kyllon ka nongbah ha ka (a.d. 70), u la leit sha Ephesos khnang ban peit ïa ki Balang ha Asia kiba rit jong ka Rom. Ha ka por jong ka jingpynshitom beiñ ïa ki Khristan hapoh ka jingsynshar jong u Patsha Roman u Domitian, ïa u apostol la pynkynriah noh sha Patmos, ka dewlynnong ha ka duriaw Aegean.

Ha Patmos, u Ioannis u la ioh ïa ki khubor iathuhlypa kiba kyrpang kiba la kren ym tang sha ki hynñiew tylli ki Balang jong ka Asia Minor hynrei sha ki Khristan ruh ha ki yrta baroh (Jingpynpaw 1:1-3, 9-11). Ka jingpynpoi ïa ka khubor jong ka jingiathuhlypa, kaba u Ioannis u la long shi bynta jong ka, ka la long kumne: U Blei U Kpa, ha U Jisu Khrist, ha u angel, ha u apostol Ioannis, bad ha ki nongpule bad ki nongsngap. Kane ka long ka nuksa kaba donkam jong ka jingpynpaw-ba la pyrsad mynsiem ha kaba U Blei U pyndonkam ïa ki nongiathuhlypa ban pynsngew ïa ki khubor jong U sha ki briew.

Ka jinghikai jong kane lynnong ha kane ka taiew ka bishar bniah kyrpang ïa ka rukom jingpynpaw-ba la pyrsad mynsiem ba bun rukom, da kaba pynleit jingmut kyrpang ha ki rukom kren, jingthoh bad jingpynlong drama ba la pynsngew ïa ka khubor ba kynja Blei sha ki briew.

_Pynkhreh ïa kane ka Lynnong na ka bynta ka Sabbaton, October 31._
