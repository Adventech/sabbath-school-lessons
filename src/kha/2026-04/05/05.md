---
title:  Ki Khubor Ba La Thoh
date:   28/10/2026
---

Katto katne ki jingkren kiba pawnam ki la ktah jur ïa ka jingim jong ki briew bad wat ki la pynkylla ïa ka jinglong jong ka histori. Lada ym shym la buh jingthoh ïa kine ki jingkren, ki kyntien kin jin da la lip ne kylla blad katba nang iaid ka por. Kumta ka jingthoh ka ai ïa ka jinglong ba neh ïa ka ktien ba la kren, bad kata ka jinglong ba neh ka ailad ïa ka khubor ban iaid lyngba ka por.

Kham hadien ba ki Israel ki la mih na Ijipt bad ki la jop ïa ki Amalek, U Blei u la bthah ïa u Moses ban “thoh ïa kane kum ka jingkynmaw ha ka kitab” (Eksodos. 17:14). Sdang na u Moses, uba la thoh ïa ki san tylli ki kitab ba nyngkong jong ka Baibl, ka Salm 90 bad u Job, U Mynsiem Bakhuid u la ialam ïa ki nongiathuhlypa ba la pynshong nongrim ha ka Baibl ban pynmih ïa ki jingthoh kiba pynlong mynta ïa ki 66 tylli ki kitab jong ka Baibl.

`Pule Eksodos 17: 14; Ioannis 20:30; 31:1; 1 Korinth 10:11 bad jingpynpaw 1:19. Kiei ki katto katne ki daw kiba U Blei U la Kylli ïa ki nongiathuhlypa ban thoh ïa ka Khubor kaba u la ai ha ki?`

Ki kitab bapher bapher jong ka Baibl ki pynpaw ïa ka rukom thoh bad ka jingtbit jong ki nongthoh. Kumba ngin sa ïa kren ha ka jingpule jong ka taiew ban wan, ki jinglong ba kyrpang bad ki rukom thoh bapher bapher jong uwei pa uwei u nongthoh kim shym la shim ne weng noh ïa ki khubor jong ki, namar ym don ki “Kyndon” jong ka jingpyrsad mynsiem. Katto katne ki bynta jong ka Baibl ki paw shai ba ki kham iadei bad thew bad ka jingim jong ngi bad ka jingngeit jong ngi mynta ban ïa kiwei pat ki bynta. Hynrei kane ka jingshisha kam pynlong ïa kawei ka bynta jong ka Baibl kaba la kham pyrsad mynsiem ban ïa kawei pat ka bynta.

Teng teng ki nongthoh Baibl ki ju thoh ïa ki khubor ba la pyrsad mynsiem kiba ki la kren nyngkong eh da ka ktien, da lade, ne da u nongtrei ryngkat ba ki lah ban shaniah. Ha ka kitab Deuteronomi, u Moses u la thoh ïa ki jingkren ba khatduh kiba u la kren ha ki Israel shuwa ba un iap. Ki saw tylli ki Gospel ki buh jingkynmaw ïa kiba bun ki jingialap bad ki jinghikai jong U Jisu ha ka por ba U shakri ha kane ka pyrthei.

Hynrei ki don ki khep ha kaba ki nongiathuhlypa ki shim na ki tyllong ki bym shym la pyrsad mynsiem (Ki Kam Ki Apostol 17:28, 1 Korinth. 15:33, Titos 1:12) bad wat na ki ktien u Soitan (Jenesis. 3:1-5, Mathaios. 4:1-11).

Ngi dei ban kynmaw ba katba ki nongiathuhlypa ki dang thoh ïa ki jingsngew bad ki jingkwah jong kiwei pat ki briew, ki pynpaw ruh ïa la ki jong ki jingsngew briew (peit Daniel. 8:27; Jingpynpaw 22:8, 9). Shuh shuh, ka Baibl ka batai shibun ki nuksa kiba bha ban bud hynrei ka batai ruh shibun ki nuksa kiba sniew kiba dei ban kiar (1 Korinth. 10:1-11).

Katba ngi pule ïa ka Baibl, ngi dei ban buh ha ka jingmut ba, wat la ym shym la thoh ha ka ktien kaba janai, hynrei ka kit ïa ka khubor bym lah bakla kaba lah ban ieng ha ka jingtynjuh jong ka por.
