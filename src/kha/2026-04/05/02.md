---
title:  Ka Jingpynpaw Kyllum Bad Kyrpang
date:   25/10/2026
---

`Pule Hebru 1:1,2. Balei U Blei U sngewtynnad ban kren sha ngi? Kaei kaba U iathuh ïa ngi lyngba U Jisu bad ki nongiathuhlypa?`

U Nongthaw jong ka pyrthei bneng un don aiu ban ong ïa ngi? Balei U ïa kren bad ngi? Ka long namar ba ngi long ki briew kiba la hap ha ka pyrthei kaba pop bad ngi iakynduh ïa ka jingiap, ka jingjynjar bad ki jingtynjuh; U Blei, namarkata, U kren ha ngi namar U kwah ba ngin tip balei ngi don hangne, balei kiei kiei ki long kiba sniew haduh katne, bad balei, wat hapdeng kiei kiei baroh, ngi lah ban kyrmen ha ka jingieit jong U ïa ngi.

Ka jingpynpaw kyllum jong U Blei, kumba ngi iohi ha kane ka pyrthei, ka kren shai shaphang ka bor bymjukut bad ka jingieit kaba sumar sukher jong U. U Syiem Dabid u la pyrta, “Ki bneng ki pynbna ïa ka burom U Blei, bad ka sahit bneng ka pyni ïa ka jingleh ki kti jong U” (Salm 19:1,). U apostol Paul u la bynrap, “namar ïa kita kiei kiei ruh kibym lah iohi ki jong u naduh ka jingpynlong ïa ka pyrthei la iohi shynna, ba la shemphang ïa ki na kita kiei kiei ruh ba la thaw, kiba long, ka bor bymjukut ruh bad ka jinglong Blei jong u, khnang ba kin long khlem jingkrendong shuh.” (Rom 1:20,).

Ka long ruh kaba phylla ban pyrkhat ïa ka bor pynthymmai jong U Blei kaba paw ha ki rukom ki jingpynkoit ba phylla ha ka pyrthei jong ngi. Hooid, “Ka bor pynkhiah U Blei ki iaid lyngba ka mariang baroh kawei. Lada la ot ïa u dieng, lada u briew u la mynsaw ne u la kum pynkheiñ ïa ka shyieng, ka mariang ka sdang mardor ban pynbha ïa ka jingmynsaw. Wat shuwa ba ka jingdonkam kan don, ki bor pynkhiah ki long kiba la pynkhreh; bad tang shu pynmong, ïa kawei pa kawei ka bor la pynkhih ïa ka kam jong ka jingpynbha. Ellen G. White, Education, p. 113.

Katba ka pyrthei mariang ka lah ban long kaba itynnad bad kaba ai jinghikai, ka pynpaw ym tang ïa ka bor bad jingieit U Blei hynrei ka pynpaw ruh ïa ka jingdon jong ka jingsniew (peit Jenesis. 3:14-19, Mathaios. 13:24-30). ïa kane ka khubor kaba don ai ki jingmut lah ban sngewthuh bha tang ha ka jingshai jong ka jingpynpaw ba kyrpang jong U Blei — ha baroh ar U Jisu Khrist, ka Ktien U Blei kaba la wan long briew, bad ha ka Baibl, ka Ktien ba la thoh jong U Blei kaba sakhi shaphang jong U (Ioannis 5:39).

Ki Jingthoh Bakhuid ki long ka jingthoh ba la pyrsat mynsiem jong ki khubor U Blei sha ki briew baroh ha ki juk baroh. Ki iathuh ïa ngi shaphang ka Jingthaw ïa ka pyrthei bad ka jinghap kaba sngewsih jong ka, bad shaphang ka jingthmu ba phylla jong U Blei ban pynim bad ka jingpynkylla kaba khatduh ïa ka pyrthei Pyrthei. Katba ki katto katne ki jingiathuhkhana jong ka Baibl ki lah ban long kiba lyngkot bad kiba la jied, ki long kiba lah ban shaniah, namar dei U Blei uba kren ha ngi (peit 2 Timothi 3:16; 2 Petros 1:19-21).

`Wat la ka mariang ka kdew thikna ïa ka jingshisha jong ka bor bad jingieit jong u Nongthaw, balei ngi dang donkam eh ïa ka Ktien Ba la Thoh, ruh? Kaei kaba ka Ktien ka iathuh ïa ngi ba ka mariang, ha lade hi, kam iathuh?`
