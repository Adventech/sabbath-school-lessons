---
title:  Ka Drama, Ki Nuksa, Ki Pharshi
date:   29/10/2026
---

Ka Baibl ka don shibun ki kyntien ha ki dur bad ki jingpyni nuksa. Kum ka nuksa, ka rukom kñia jong ka Jutang Barim baroh kawei jong ki duwan jong ki kpa tymmen, ka ïingjaiñ jong u Moses, bad ka templ ha Jerusalem ka long ka jingpyni dur ïa ka jingkñia shahïap U Khrist halor ka diengphna bad ka jinglong lyngdoh jong U ha bneng (Hebru. 8:1-6).

Ha ki jingiathuhlypa u Daniel, ïa ki hima bad ki bor ha ka pyrthei la pyni da ki bynta bapher bapher jong kawei ka dur blei kaba khraw (Daniel. 2:31-45), ki mrad bad ki reng bapher bapher (Daniel 7 bad 8), bad arngut ki syiem (Daniel 11). Ki jingpyni kiba kum kita ki paw ruh ha ka kitab Jingpynpaw, kumba long ha ki atylli ki mrad jong ka lynnong 13.

Ha ki jinghikai jong U, U Jisu U la kren shaphang jong U, kum ka nuksa, kum U Kpu jong ka Jingim (Ioannis 6:35, 41-58), U Jingshai jong ka pyrthei (Ioannis 8:12, Ioannis 9:5), bad U Diengsohwaiñ Bashisha (Ioannis 15:1-8). Bad lyngba ka pharshi jong u nongbet (Mathaios. 13:1-9, 18-23) bad katto katne kiwei pat (Mathaios. 13:24-52, bad kiwei kiwei), U Jisu u la pyni ïa ka jinglong bad ka jingsan jong ka hima jong U.

`Pule Ezekiel 4:1–8. Balei phi pyrkhat ba U Blei um shym la kren tang da ka ktien hynrei u la pynlong kum ki Jingpynwandur ruh ïa ki katto katne ki khubor jong U?`

Ka jingpynwandur kaba shai bha ka paw ha Esekiel 4:1–8, ha kaba U Blei u la kyrpad ïa u nongiathuhlypa ban shna ïa ka dur kaba rit jong ka Jerusalem bad ban thiah ïa lade, nyngkong ha ka liang kadiang jong u haduh 390 sngi, kaba pyni ïa ka iing Israel, bad nangta ha ka liang kamon jong u haduh 40 sngi, kaba pyni ïa ka iing Judah. Kawei pat ka jingjia kaba lah ban iohi ka long ka jingshongkurim jong u Hosia kaba la buh da U Blei bad ka Gomer, ka nongkhaii met, kaba pyni ïa ka jingbymhok ba kynja mynsiem jong ki Israel ha U Blei (Hosia. 1:2-9).

Ki jingiashem jong ka jingpynidur jong u Esekiel bad u Hosia ki i kumba ki long kiba pynkyndit bad pynlyngngoh shibun ha kine ki sngi. Hynrei kata ka long ka jingmut: ki la dei ban long kiba la pynkyndit, ym tang ïa ngi hynrei ïa ki briew ruh ha kata ka por. Ha ka por ba la kyntait paidbah ha ka ba paw ïa ki jingmaham jong ki nongiathuhlypa (peit 2 Khronikl. 36:15-21), ka kam kaba khatduh jong ka jingisynei blei ka long ban pyntip da kaba phylla ïa ki briew shaphang ka jinglong bashisha jong ki khnang ban ialam ïa ki sha ka jingkylla kaba mut kaba shisha. Kum kane ka jingpyni drama shisha ka la dei ban pyni ïa ki haduh katno ki dei ban shim khia ïa ka jingmaham U Blei ïa ki.

`Kaei ka jait jingleh ban pynidur ha kaba mano re mano u lah ban leh mynta ban maham ïa ngi shaphang, ong, ka jinglong kum ki nong Laodikaia? (Peit Jingpynpaw 3:14-18).`
