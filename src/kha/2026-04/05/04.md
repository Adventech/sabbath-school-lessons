---
title:  Ki Khubor Da Ka Ktien
date:   27/10/2026
---

`Pule 2 Petros 1:18-21. Kaei ka jingpynthikna kaba ngi don ba ki khubor ba la kren da ki nongiathuhlypa jong U Blei ki long kiba la pyrsad mynsiem bad kiba lah ban shaniah kum ki khubor ba la thoh?`

U Blei u la kren ha u Adam bad ka Im shuwa ka jinghap (Jenesis. 2:16, 17) bad hadien (Jenesis. 3:9-19). Ha baroh ar ki khep, ki la sngewthuh janai ïa ki ktien jong U. La iohsngew ïa ka sur jong u ha ki ktien kiba shai kum ha Sinai (Eksodos. 20:1-20), kumjuh ruh ha ka jingpynbaptis bad ka jingpynkylla dur jong U Jisu (Mathaios. 3:17, Mathaios. 17:5). Ngi lah ban sngewthuh bakla haba kiwei pat ki briew ki ïa kren bad ngi. Hynrei, shisha, haba U Blei U iakren ha uno uno baroh, kan ym don kano kano ka jingiakren bym sngewthuh ne jingpynthame.

Ka Baibl ka pynskhem ba “U Blei u la kren ha u Moses bad u la ong ha u: ‘Nga long U Trai’” (Eksodos. 6:2). Bun ki nongthoh Baibl ki la kam ba ki la iohsngew ïa ka sur U Blei katba U dang kren ha ki. Kane ka la long jingjia, kum ka nuksa, ha u Isaiah (Isaiah. 8:1, 11), u Jeremaiah (Jeremiah. 1:4, 9, 11) bad u Esekiel (Esekiel. 3:16, Esekiel 6:1). Kano kano ka jingpyrshang ban len ïa kine bad kiwei kiwei ki jingkam kiba iasyriem kiba kum kita ka pynkut noh da kaba buh jingkylli ïa ka jinglong hok jong ki nongiathuhlypa bad ka jingshaniah jong ki ha ka Baibl.

Kumba U Blei U la kren ha ki nongiathuhlypa ha ka ktien briew kaba lah ban sngewthuh, kumta ruh ki nongiathuhlypa ki la kren ha la ki jong ki jong ki nongsngap. Kum ka nuksa, u Noah u la long u nongiathuhlypa bashisha jong U Blei bad u “nongialap ïa ka hok” uba iAiñeh (2 Petros. 2:5). Um shym la thoh ïa kano kano ka bynta jong ka Baibl, hynrei u la pynpoi ïa ka khubor U Blei da ka ktien sha ki paralok jong u, bad ka khubor jong u kam duna ïa ka jingpyrsad mynsiem ban ïa ki jingthoh jong ki nongiathuhlypa ba la pynshong nongrim ha ka tynrai jong ka jingngeit.

Katto katne ngut kiwei pat ki nongiathuhlypa, kynthup ïa u Nathan, u Elijah, u Elisha bad u loannis Nongpynbaptis, kim shym la thoh satia ïa kano kano ka bynta jong ka Baibl; ha ka jaka kata, ki la pynpoi ïa ka khubor jong ki da ka ktien.

Bad kumno shaphang U Jisu Khrist hi da lade? Ka jingthoh jong ka Gospel ka kdew ba, ha ka por ba U shakri ha ka pyrthei, U la “thoh ha ka khyndew da ka shynriah kti jong U” (Ioannis 8:6). Kata ka long. Ngim don jingthoh shaphang ka jingthoh jong U Jisu. Hynrei U la hikai bad ialap da ka ktien “kum uba don ka bor” (Mathaios. 7:29). Katto katne na ki nongsngap jong U ki la phla wat, “Ym don mano mano ruh uba la kren kum une u Briew” (loannis. 7:40, 46). U Jisu u la long nyngkong eh u nongpynsngew ktien ïa ki khubor U Blei. Ki khubor jong U kim shym la duna ka bor ne ka jingteh tang namar ba U hi Um shym la thoh ïa kino kino ki kot jong ka Baibl.

`Katno ka long kaba sngewtynnad ba U Blei u don ki nongiathuhlypa ki bym don jingthoh ha ka Baibl. Balei kine ki nongiathuhlypa ki bym dei ki nongiathuhlypa ruh ki kit ïa ka bor?`
