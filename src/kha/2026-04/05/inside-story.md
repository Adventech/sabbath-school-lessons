### Na Ka Jingduh Jingkyrmen Sha Ka Thong

Ha ka jingkhriat kaba jur jong u bnai January 1906, uwei u samla Slovenia uba kyrteng u Albin Močnik u la ieng halor kawei ka jingkieng ha Bremerhaven, Germany, u da pyrkhat ban pyniap ialade. Hynrei katba u dang don ha ka tduh jong ka jingduh jingkyrmen, uwei u briew phylla uba um ithuh u la rung shapoh, da kaba pyni ïa u sha kawei ka kynhun ki longiing Adventist kiba shna juti kaba la pynkylla ïa ka jinglong jong ka histori na ka bynta ki ri Balkan khlem da tip.

U Močnik u la leit sah noh bad ki longiing Schiefer bad Banas. Ka jingsakhi kaba jar jar jong ki ka la pynkhih ïa ka dohnud jong u, bad, ha u bnai Risaw 1906, la pynbaptis ïa u. Kane ka la pyni ïa ka jingsdang jong ka mission kaba neh shi jingim ka ban sAiñ dur ïa ka Balang Adventist ha ka ri Yugoslavia barim.

Shen hadien ka jingpynbaptis jong u, u Močnik u la leit sha ka jingialang jong ka German Union kaba la long ha Friedensau, khlem da tip ba u dei u Slovenia Adventist uba nyngkong eh. Da ka jingpynshlur jong u John F. Huenergardt ban shakri ïa la ki jong ki briew, u la pynrung kyrteng ha ka skul Friedensau, ha kaba ka jingsngewtynnat jong u ïa ka kam mishon ka la nangsan. U la sdang ban pynkylla sha ka ktien Serbo-Croatia bad sam ïa ki kot Sabbath Skul.

Ha ka snem 1908, ka jingangnud jong u Albin ka la sdang ban wan dur. Ynda u la pyndep ïa ka jingpule jong u, u la leit sha Croatia bad u Pastor Robert Schillinger. Wat la u la shah khanglad ha ki jingkhanglad jong ka ktien bad ki jingkhanglad jong ka Aiñ, hynrei u Močnik u la iasam ïa ka gospel ha Zagreb bad Split. U la leit sah sha Srem, kaba long ka bynta jong ka Croatia ha kata ka por, bad u la thmu ban leit sha Serbia, da kaba ialap bad pynbaptis ïa kiba ngeit thymmai, bunsien u la shah set phatok.

Wat hapdeng ka jingpang kaba u don bad ka jingpyrshah jong ka sAiñ hima sima, u Močnik um shym la iehnoh ïa ka mishon jong u. Ha ka thma bah ba nyngkong, u la shakri kum u shipai, ki jingpang kiba jur bha bad ka jingsngewsih namar ka jingkhlad noh na ka iing ka sem jong u kiba la iaheh iasan. Hynrei wat ha ka bym shait bha, u la pynmih ïa ka kot jingrwai Adventist kaba nyngkong eh ha ka ktien Serbo-Kroatia, da kaba thaw hi 120 tylli ki jingrwai.

Hadien ka thma, u Močnik u la ialam ïa ki jingpyrshang ban ialeh pyrshah ïa ka Reform Movement kaba pyniakhlad bad ban tei pat ïa ka Balang Adventist. Kane ka jingkhih ka la sdang shuwa ka thma bah ba nyngkong, haba ki jingmut kiba iapher shaphang ka kam shipai ki la mih hapdeng ki German Adventist. Khatduh khatwai, ka jingshakri jong u Albin ka la sei soh: u la pynim biang ïa ka balang, u la pynmih ïa ki nongialam kiba thymmai, bad, ha ka snem 1925, la jied ïa u kum u president ba nyngkong eh jong ka Yugoslavia Union Conference.

Kum u president, u Močnik u la ai ïa ka jingkren ïa ka jingngeit Adventist wat ha ka jinglong jingman jong ka sAiñ pyrthei kaba pyrshah. Ka kot Adventism jong u ha ka snem 1925 ka la pynshai ïa ki jingngeit jong ka balang sha ka sorkar bad ka la iarap ban ioh ïa ka jingithuh aiñ ïa ka kynhun niam. U la ialam ïa ka kam pynkylla ktien bad u la pynbeit ïa ka kot jong ka balang. U la seng ruh ïa ka skul na ka bynta ki Pastor ha ka snem 1931.
