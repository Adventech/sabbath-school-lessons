---
title:  Ka Rukom Jong Ka Jingpyrsad Mynsiem
date:   26/10/2026
---

Shibun ki kot bad ki jingthoh, ym dei tang ka Baibl, kaba la wan sha ngi lyngba ki yrta. Hooid ngi shim ïa ka Baibl kum ka ktien jong U Blei. Kaei kaba la pyniakhlad ïa ka na kiwei pat ki jingthoh?

`Pule 1 Timothi 3:15-17, bad u Jakob 1:17. Kaei ka khubor kaba donkam kaba ngi dei ban shim na kine ki dkhot?`

Ka don katto katne ka jingiakren shaphang ka jingpynkylla kaba bha tam ïa ki 2 Timothi 3:16, hynrei kiba bun ki jingpynkylla ktien ïa ka Baibl ki pynkylla ïa kane ka bynta ha kajuh ka rukom, da kaba banjur ba ka Baibl baroh kawei ka long kaba mih na ka tynrai jong ka jinglong Blei (2 Pet. 1:20, 21).

Uwei u nongbatai shaphang ka Baibl u batai ba “ka long kumba kham lah ban long ba u Paul u pyrkhat ïa ka jingthoh bakhuid baroh kawei hangne bad ba un ong ba ka jingthoh bakhuid baroh kawei ka long kaba ‘myntoi ban hikai, ban sneng, ban pynbeit bad ban hikai ha ka hok,’ ban ïa kaba ong ba kawei pa kawei ka bynta jong ka jingthoh bakhuid ka long kaba myntoi ha kine ki rukom.”—G. U W. Knight III, Ki Shithi Pastoral: Ka Jingbatai shaphang ka Jingthoh Grik (Grand Rapids, MI: Eerdmans, 1992), p. 445.

Hynrei kumno ngi dei ban sngewthuh ïa ka rukom jong ka jingpyrsad mynsiem kaba la sdang na ka Baibl? Ngi dei ban ithuh ba ka jingpyrsad mynsiem jong ki nongiathuhlypa ka dei da U Mynsiem Bakhuid uba iarap ïa ki nongiathuhlypa bashisha ki ba lah ban shaniah ban iathuh ïa ki khubor ba kynja Blei. Kata ka jingiarap ka long kaba la ai ym tang ha ka rukom ban ioh pdiang ïa ki khubor hynrei ruh ha kaba pynsngew ïa ki khubor da kaba shai sha ki nongpdiang jong ki.

Ki khubor U Blei ki la wan lyngba ki jingiohipaw bad ki jingphohsniew (Jingkheiñ 12:6; Isaiah 6:1, 8). U Blei u la pyndonkam ruh ïa ki sakhi kiba la iohi da la ki khmat (1 Ioannis 1:1-3), ki jingwad bniah kat kum ka histori ïa ka jingpyrsad mynsiem da U Mynsiem (Loukas. 1:1-3), bad ki jingpynsngew ba kynja Blei ha ka jingmut jingpyrkhat (2 Petros. 1:21). Wat la ka long da kino kino ki lad ba la pyndonkam da U Mynsiem Bakhuid, ba ki nongiathuhlypa bad ki nongthoh jong ka Baibl la ai ïa ki khubor jong ka jingshisha kiba long kiba shisha, ba ïa ki khubor jong ka jingshisha ban iasam, ban iasam. Kata, ïa ki khubor jong U Blei ba kim dei ban shah pynwit ne pynkylla ha kano kano ka rukom da ki nongrah khubor jong U, wat lada ki pyndonkam da la ki jong ki kyntien ban pynpaw ïa kita ki khubor.

Ia ka kam ban pynsngew ïa ki khubor jong ka jingiathuhlypa sha ki nongsngap ki ba dei ban ioh sngew, ki lah ban leh ne kren da ka ktien (2 Petros. 1:19-21), da ka jingthoh (loannis. 20:30, 31), lane wat lyngba ka jingpynlong drama (Esekiel. 4:1-8).

`Wat la ki dang don bun ki jingkylli shaphang kumno la pyrsad mynsiem ïa ka Baibl, lane kumno ka jingpyrsad mynsiem ka trei kam ha ka jinglong kyllum, balei ka long kaba kongsan eh ban shaniah ha ka Baibl kum ka Ktien ba la pyrsad mynsiem jong U Blei, khlem da kheiñ ïa kaei kaba ngi lah ban nym sngewthuh pura shaphang kumno la thoh ïa ka?`
