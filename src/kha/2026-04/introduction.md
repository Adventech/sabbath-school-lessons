### Ka Jingai Jong Ka Jingïathuhlypa

Ki stad saian jong ka khlieh ki la sdang ban sngewthuh ïa ka jinglah kaba phylla jong ki khyllung briew ban nang ïa ka ktien. Ha ka rta kaba hynriew bnai, i khunlung i lah ban sngewthuh ïa ki sur kiba kynthup ïa ki kyntien phareng, lane ki kyntien na kano kano na kiwei pat ki ktien ba kren ïa palat 7,000 tylli. Lada ki iohsngew sa ïa kawei pat ka jait ktien kaba ar, ki lah ban nang ïa kata ka ktien, kumjuh. Ha kane ka por, kaba sngewphylla ka long ba ka jingkhang jong ka jabieng ka plie, kaba ailad ïa ki khyllung ban pyniapher ïa ki 40 tylli ki sur kiba ha ka jingmut kaba kyllum lang kiba pynlong ïa ka ktien tynrai na ki 800 tylli ki sur bapher bapher kiba ki lah ban sngewthuh ha ka por ba kha ïa ki. Hynrei ka don kawei ki ioh kem: ka dei ba ki khyllung ki dei ban kren. Phi lah ban khot ïa ka ki jinghikai sur na ka bynta ki khyllung ba dang shu kha.

Balei U Blei Un thaw ïa ngi da kane ka jinglah kaba phylla ban sngewthuh ïa ka jingkren bad ban ioh ïa ka ktien lada Um shym la thmu satia ban kren bad ngi? Ka jingkylli ka long kaba sngewtynnat, hooid — namar ka Baibl ka pynbna, “U Blei, uba ha ki por bapher bad ha ki rukom bapher bapher u la kren ha ki kpa tymmen da ki nongiathuhlypa, ha kine ki sngi kiba khatduh u la kren ha ngi da U Khun jong U, ïa Uba U la thung ban long u nongioh pateng ïa kiei kiei baroh, da Uba ruh U la thaw ïa ki pyrthei” (Hebru 1: 1, 2 ).

Haba ka pop ka la pynpra ïa ka jingiadei U Blei bad u Adam bad ka Im, Um shym la sait ïa ki kti jong U na ki bad iehnoh ïa ki arngut kiba la hap ba kin jot hi da lade. U la wan wad ïa ki baroh arngut, da kaba kylli ïa u Adam, “Hangno me don?” (Jenesis. 3:9). U la iathuhlypa shaphang ka jingkha ïa Uwei Uban pynim ïa ka pyrthei na ka pop (Jenesis. 3:15). Naduh ba la sdang ïa ka, hangta hi ha Eden hadien ka jinghap, ka jingiathuhlypa ka la kyntiew ïa U Jisu bad ka la kdew ïa ki nongpop sha U, uba long tang ma ka ka jingkyrmen jong ki.

Ha kane ka quarter ngin pule ïa ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa, ka jingpynwandur ba stad jong U Blei ban pynsngew ïa ka mon bad ka lynti jong U sha ki briew kiba la hap, ki bym lah shuh ban iakynduh markhmat bad U. ïa kane ka jingai ba kyrpang jong U Mynsiem Bakhuid la kdew shai ha 1 Korinth 12, 1 Korinth 14, Ephesos 4, bad Rom 12. U Mynsiem Bakhuid U long U Nongsam Bakhraw ïa kane bad ïa kiwei pat ki jingai ba kynja mynsiem baroh, “u da sam ha uwei pa uwei katba U mon” (1 Korinth 12:11). Ha ka por baroh, ki nongkren kiba don ka sap ba kynja mynsiem ki la pynpoi ïa ki khubor ba kynja Blei ban pyniohpat bad pynthymmai ïa ka jingiadei jong ngi bad U Blei, kumba U Blei u la leh ha Eden hadien ka jinghap.

Ngin sa tip kumno U Blei U khot ïa ki nongiathuhlypa bad kumno ngi lah ban tynjuh ïa ka jingshisha jong ki. Ngin sa iohi ïa ki jingiasyriem bad jingiapher hapdeng ki nongiathuhlypa jong ka Testament Barim bad Bathymmai, kumjuh ruh ngin ioh ïa ka jingsngewthuh kaba trei kam kumno ka jingpynpaw bad ka jingpynshlur ka treikam ha ka jingim jong ki. Katba don ki nongiathuhlypa kiba kren, kiwei pat ki thoh; don kiba leh ïa baroh ar. Ngin sa bishar ïa kine bad kiwei kiwei ki rukom kren ba kynja blei, ïa ki jingmut jong ki, bad ki jingkyrkhu jong ka jingkohnguh ïa ki khubor kiba kine ki nongiathuhlypa ki la pynsngew.

U Blei U pyndonkam ïa ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa na ka bynta san tylli ki jingthmu kiba kongsan: (1) ban pynpaw ïa lade, ïa ka jingshisha jong U, bad ïa ka mon jong U na ka bynta ka jinglong briew kaba la hap; (2) ban ai ïa ka jingiohi bad ka jingkyntu ha kaba iadei bad ki jingjia bad ki ba jia kiba kongsan; (3) ban pynkhreh ïa ka met U Khrist — ka balang — na ka bynta ka mishon; (4) ban ai jingkyrshan ba kynja mynsiem ïa ki nongbud jong U; (5) ban pynkyndit ïa ki nongpop bad ban khot ïa ki sha ka jingkylla kaba mut. Nalor kata, “U mynsiem jong ka jingiathuhlypa” ka long kawei na ki ar tylli ki dak kiba pyniapher ïa ka balang ba sah jong U Blei ha ka por ba khatduh (Jingpynpaw 12:17, Jingpynpaw 19:10).

Kumta u Blei u la pynlut ha kaba ialam ïa ka iing kaba don ka jingiada bad kaba la jah ba ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa la ai na ka bynta ki sngi kiba khatduh ruh (Joel 2:28-31). Ki Seventh day Adventist ki ngeit — da ki daw kiba biang bha— ba ka Ellen G. White, ka nongiaseng lang ïa ka balang, ka la pyndonkam ïa ka jingpynpaw ïa ka juk mynta jong ka jingai jingiathuhlypa.

Dei na kane ka daw, ryngkat bad ka jingpule jong ngi ïa ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa ha ka Jingthoh Bakhuid, man la ka sngi pynkhreh ngin wad bniah ïa ka jinglong jingman jong ka jingkhot jong ka Ellen G. White sha ka jingshakri jong ka jingiathuhlypa. Ngi, kum ka balang, la ai ïa kane ka jingai kaba phylla. Kumno ngi pyndonkam bha ïa ka?

Hooid, naduh dang rit, ngi la shah pasiaw ban kren. Katno ka long kaba donkam, namarkata, ba ngin sngap ïa kaei kaba ki nongiathuhlypa U Blei ki iathuh ha ngi!
