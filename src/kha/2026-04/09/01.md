### Ki Jingpule Na Ka Bynta Kane Ka Taïew
Daniel. 10:7-19; Deuteronomi. 13:1-5; Isaiah. 8:20; Ioannis 11:25; Ki Kam 15:36-39; Jeremiah. 28:9, 15-17.

> <p>Dkhot Spel-lyndet</p>
> “Ko hep baieit, wat ngeit ïa ki mynsiem baroh, hynrei to phin tynjuh ïit ïa kita ki mynsiem, la ki long ne em na U Blei: naba shibun ki nongiathuhlypa balamler ki la leit mih noh sha ka pyrthei.” (1 Ioannis 4:1)

U Mawkordor diamond bashisha u long uwei na ki mawkordor ba kordor tam ha ka pyrthei. Hooid, bun kiwei pat kiei kiei kiba lah ban pynlong kiba dei dur bad u mawkordor diamond hynrei ki long ki bym don dor. Kumta, kumno u briew un tip la u mawkordor u dei uba shisha ne uba thok? Ka lad kaba bha tam ka long ban tynjuh ïa ka. Katto katne ki jingtynjuh lah ban leh ha iing da ki katto katne ki tiar kiba suk. Kiwei pat ki donkam ïa ka jingstad ba tbit. Kiba bun ki jingpynbna kim long kiba pynkut hi dalade, hynrei lada u mawkordor diamond u iaid lyngba katto katne ki jingtynjuh, lah ban ngeit skhem ba u mawkordor diamond u long uba shisha.

U mawkordor diamond u long uwei na ki mar kiba eh tam tam ha ka pyrthei. Ka bynta jong ka jingkhlaiñ jingeh jong u mawkordor diamond ka long ka jinglah jong u ban shah ïa ka jingshit kaba jur bha bad ki jingkylla jong ka jingshit kiba shyrkhei. Ban tynjuh ïa u mawkordor diamond, phi lah ban pynkhluit ïa u da ka sharak ne ka ding bad nangta sa pynhap ïa u ha ka um kaba khriat. Wat la u mawkordor diamond bashisha un ym sniew, hynrei u mawkordor diamond thok u lah ban pait!

Ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa ka kham kordor ban ïa uno uno u mawkordor diamond, hynrei lada uno uno uba kam ïa kane ka jingai ym lah ban shu shim ba ka long kaba dei. Kata ka jingdawa ka dei ban shah tynjuh. Ha kane ka taiew ngin bishar bniah ïa ki nongiathuhlypa jong ka Baibl ban sei madan ïa ki nongrim jong ka Baibl ba la pyndonkam ban tynjuh ïa ka jingshisha jong ki.

_Pynkhreh ïa kane ka Lynnong na ka bynta ka Sabbaton, November 28_
