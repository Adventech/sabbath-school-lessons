---
title:  Ka Jingwan Long Briew U Khrist
date:   24/11/2026
---

Kat kum ka 1 Ioannis 4:1–3, ka jingtynjuh jong ka jingngeit ha ka jingwan long briew U Jisu kan pynduh noh kloi ïa ki briew kiba kam ba ki long ki nongiathuhlypa na kiwei pat ki niam ba kongsan jong ka pyrthei, lane na kito kiba kam ba ki don ka jingiadei bad ka pyrthei ba kynja mynsiem khlem da iadon bynta ha kano kano ka niam. Lada u briew “um phla ba U Jisu Khrist u la wan ha ka doh,” uta u briew “um long na U Blei” (1 Ioannis 4:3).

Haba u Ioannis u thoh ïa kine ki dkhot, u pyrkhat ïa ki nonghikai lamler kiba hikai ba U Jisu um long u briew, hynrei u shu paw kum u briew. Kane ka jingtynjuh, pynban, ka iaid kham sha jngai. Kam dei kaba ong tang ba u nongiathuhlypa u dei ban mynjur ba U Jisu u long u briew uba shisha uba la im ha ka pyrthei. Kiba bun ki Khristan ki ngeit ïa kane shaphang U Jisu. U Ioannis u dang ïa leh bad kawei ka mat kaba kyrpang bha ha kata ka por; hynrei, ki ktien jong ka jingiathuhlypa jong u Ioannis, wat la ki donkam na ka bynta kane, ki dang don ki jingshisha kiba kongsan na ka bynta ki pateng kiba hadien, ruh kumjuh.

`Kaei ba ki kine ki dkhot kiba bud ki ïa thuh ïa ngi shaphang U Jisu? Ioannis 3:16; 2 Korinth. 5:21; Ioannis 11:25; Hebru. 4:14, 15; Ioannis 14:1-3.`

Ki nongiathuhlypa bashisha ki dei ban ngeit ba U Jisu u long U Blei uba la wan long briew hynrei u long shisha u briew. Ki dei ruh ban pdiang ïa ka jingshisha kaba pura shaphang U Jisu — ka mishon jong U, ka jingim khlem pop, ka jingiap kaba pyniasuk, ka jingmihpat, bad ka jingshakri kum u rangbah lyngdoh ha bneng, bad kumjuh ruh ka jingwan arsien jong U. Kane ka long kham palat ban ïa ka jingiateh kaba shu pyrkhat ha ka jingmut ba U Jisu U la im ha ka pyrthei. U Jisu u dei ban long ka pdeng jong ki jinghikai jong u nongiathuhlypa ym tang kaei kaei kaba la shu pyndap. Ki khubor jong u ne ka ki dei ban ialam ïa ki briew sha ka jingiadei kaba pynim ha U Jisu.

U Mynsiem Bakhuid U la pyrsad mynsiem ïa ki ktien jong ka jingiathuhlypa ka ba long ka jingshai jong U Blei, kaba pynduh ïa ka jingdum kaba ha ngi haduh ba U Jisu, U Khlur Mynstep, un don ha ki dohnud jong ngi, bad ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa kan pyniasoh ïa ngi bad U bad kan iai kdew ïa ngi sha U.

Ki nongiathuhlypa kiba kyntiew ïa U Jisu kin ym pyrshang ban thaw ïa la ka jong ka jingbud ne ban kyntiew ïa lade (Ki Kam 4:12). Kin long kiba kloi ban shakri bad ban ym shim kabu ïa kiba la phah ïa ki. Ha kajuh ka por, kumba ngin sa iohi lashai, ki nongiathuhlypa U Blei ki long ki briew bad, kiba long kum ma ngi, ki nongpop kiba donkam ïa ka gospel.

`U Jisu, U Blei uba pura, u la iap kum u Nongmih bujli jong ngi, da kaba kit ïa ka jingpynshitom na ka bynta ki pop jong ngi. Balei ka jingsngewthuh ïa kane ka long kaba kongsan ha ka jingngeit jong ngi? Ngi duh aiu lada ngi duh noh ïa kata ka jinghikai?`
