---
title:  Ki Soh Kiba Bha
date:   25/11/2026
---

Ha ka Jingiathuhkhana halor u Lum (Mathaios 5–7), U Jisu u la batai ïa ki mat kiba kongsan ïa ki nongrim jong ka balang Khristan. Ka lah ban i kumba ka jingshakri jong ka jingiathuhlypa ka long ka bynta jong kata ka nongrim. Nalor baroh, U Jisu u la maham pyrshah ïa ki nongiathuhlypa lamler, da kaba ong, “Na ki soh jong ki phin ithuh ïa ki” (Mathaios. 7:20).

Ia ka jingbha jong u soh lah ban bishar tang ha ka por kaba la buh. Da kaba iohi ïa ka jinglong jong u nongiathuhlypa bad peit ïa ki jingmih kiba mih na ki khubor jong u, ngi lah ban iohi la uta u nongiathuhlypa u khring ïa kiwei sha U Blei ne u ialam ïa ki sha jngai na U.

`Pule Jonah 1: 1-3, Ki Kam 15: 36-39, bad Kalatia 2: 11-14? Kaei ba kine ki dkhot ki hikai iangi sha phang ki nongiathuhlypa?`

Ban long u nongiathuhlypa kam pynlong ïa u briew uba khlem pop ne uba khlem jingbakla ha ka jinglong shimet. Ngi iohi ha ki nuksa jong ka Baibl ïa u Jonah, u Paul bad wat u Petros ruh ïa ki jingbakla ne jingduna ha ka jinglong. U Blei marwei u long uba khlem jingduna; Ki nongrah khubor jong u kim ju long kumta. Ki nongiathuhlypa, kum ma ngi baroh, ki don ki jingduna, hynrei ka jinglong jong ka jingim jong ki ka dei ban long kaba shai sha U Blei, bad ki nongiathuhlypa kim dei ban len ïa ka jingshakri jong ki da ka rukom im kaba pyrshah ïa ka khubor kaba ki iasam.

Ym don nongiathuhlypa uba pyni nuksa pura ïa ka nuksa jong U Jisu, hynrei u nongiathuhlypa uba shisha u long uba aiti ha ki nongrim bad ka rukom im jong ka hima U Blei (Math 7:16-20). Uno uno uba kam ba u long u nonglam khubor jong U Blei bad pynban uba ka rukom im jong u ka ïa pyrshah jur ïa ka khubor kaba u rah, dei ban peit ïa u da ka jingartatien.

Wat la ka jingkhot sha ka jingiathuhlypa ka long kaba eh, hynrei bun ki nongiathuhlypa ki la pyni ïa ki “soh kiba bha” ha ka por ba ki dang ialap. Kum ka nuksa, u Moses u la jop ban ialam ïa ki khun Israel lyngba ka ri khlaw (Deuterenomi 34:10-12). Ka jinglong bashisha jong u Haggai ka la pynshlur ïa ki nongshong shnong jong ka ri Judah ban sdang ban tei pat ïa ka templ kaba ar, kaba la sangeh la 19 snem (Haggai. 1:12). Ka jingshakri ba khlem artatien jong u Isaiah u la shakri ïa ki saw ngut ki syiem Judia, u la ialam ïa ki ha ka por kaba jynjar bad kaba la kdew sha U Messaiah (Isaiah. 39:5, 6; Isaiah. 53:4-6). Ngim lah ban klet ïa ka jingphla pop jong u Daniel naduh ka dohnud (Daniel. 9:15) lane ïa ka jingkular kaba dap da ki ummat jong u Jeremaiah (Jeremiah. 9:1). Wat la ka rukom ba bunsien ki shah pynshitom beiñ bad shah lehbeiñ, ki nongiathuhlypa U Blei ki ju pyni ïa ka jingieit bashisha ïa U bad ïa ki briew jong U, ka dak jong ka jingshakri kaba seisoh.

`Katno sien ki shynrang ne kynthei jong U Blei ki la pynduh jingkyrmen ïa phi da ki kam ne ki jingduna ha ka jinglong jong ki? Kaei ka jinghikai kaba kongsan kaba phi lah ban ïoh na kata ka jingiashem?`
