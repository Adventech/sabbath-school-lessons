---
title:  Ki Jingiathuhlypa Ba La Pyndep
date:   26/11/2026
---

Ki briew bunsien ki pyrkhat ïa u nongiathuhlypa kum u briew uba iathuhlypa ïa ka lawei, hynrei kane ka long tang kawei ka bynta jong ka kam jong u nongiathuhlypa. Ka kam jong u nongiathuhlypa ka kynthup ïa shibun kiei kiei. Ki nongiathuhlypa ki ialam biang ïa ki briew sha ka Ktien U Blei bad ki ai jingmut, ka jingmaham bad ka jingpynshlur.

`U Nalor ba ki iarap ïa ki briew ban tip shaphang bad pynkhreh ïa ki jingjia ha ka lawei, balei ki nongiathuhlypa ki iathuhlypa ïa ka lawei? Peit Jeremiah. 28:9, 15-17`

Ki jingpyndep ïa ka jingiathuhlypa ki long kawei pat ka lad ban tynjuh ïa u nongiathuhlypa. Ki jingiathuhlypa jong u nongiathuhlypa bashisha ki dei ban jia kumba la iathuhlypa: “Haba u nongiathuhlypa u kren ha ka kyrteng jong U Trai, lada kata ka ktien kam urlong kumta, lymne kam wan jia, ïa kata ka ktien keiñ U Trai um shym la kren: hynrei uta u nongiathuhlypa u la kren ïa ka da kaba kop sop, men ym sheptieng ïa u.” (Deauterenomi 18:22). Kane ka jingtynjuh ka lah ban long kaba eh ban pyntreikam namar don ki jingiathuhlypa kiba don ki kyndon; kata, ki lah ban ym jia kumba la iathuhlypa da u nongiathuhlypa lada ki kyndon ki long kiba la pyndep.

Ngi iohi ïa kane ka nongrim bad u Jonah bad ka jingmaham jong u pyrshah ïa ka Ninebeh. Ka jingialap jong u Jonah ka i kumba ka long kaba lyngkot bad kaba shai bha: “Sa sawphew sngi, yn pynjot noh ïa ka Ninebeh!” (Jonah. 3:4). Hynrei kata kam shym la jia kumba u la iathuhlypa. Balei?

`Pule Jeremiah 18: 7-10 Kane ka hikai aiu ïa ngi, ruh, shaphang ka jingiathuhlypa ba don kyndon?`

Ki jingiathuhlypa kiba don kyndon ki long ki jingiathuhlypa kiba shong ha ka rukom ba ki briew ki jubab ïa ki khubor U Blei. Lada ki briew ki kylla ïa ka jinglong bad jingleh jong ki, ka jingjia ba la iathuhlypa ka lah ban ym jia. Ki briew jong ka Ninebeh ki la shim ïa ka jingiathuhlypa jong u Jonah ha la ka dohnud bad ki la kylla ïa ka lynti jong ki, kumta U Blei um shym la pynjot ïa ki (Jonah. 3:10). Haba ki pateng kiba hadien ki la klet ïa kane ka jingmaham ba la iathuhlypa, bad ka Ninebeh ka la kylla long biang ka jaka kaba sniew, kaba donbor, U Blei u la pynjot ïa ka. U Blei u tip ïa ka lawei, hynrei ngim tip. Ki jingjied jong ngi ki ka rai ïa ka jinglong ba shisha jong ka lawei jong ngi.

`Balei ka long kaba donkam ban sngewthuh ïa ka jingmut jong ka jingiathuhlypa kaba don ïa ki kyndon? Kumno kane ka jingsngewthuh ka lah ban iarap shibun ïa ngi ban sngewthuh kham bha ïa ka jingshisha jong ka Baibl?`
