---
title:  Ka Bynta Jong Ka Bor Ba Kynja Mynsiem
date:   22/11/2026
---

Ha ka por jong ka jingmih noh, U Trai u la kren ha ki briew jong U shaphang ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa hapdeng jong ki, da kaba ong, “To sngap mynta ïa ki ktien jong Nga: Lada u nongiathuhlypa jong U Trai u don hapdeng jong phi, Ngan pynpaw ïa lade ha u ha ka jingithuhpaw, Ngan kren ha u ha ka jingphohsniew” (Jingkheiñ 12:6).

Ka khubor bad jingkhot jong ka jingiathuhlypa bunsien ka kynthup ïa ka bor ba kynja mynsiem; kaba batai shai da kaba thew sha lyndet jong ka jingsngew ne jingmut kaba shu sngew kaba u briew u lah ban don. U nongiathuhlypa u ioh ïa ka jingiakren beit beit na U Blei, bunsien lyngba ki jingphohsniew ne ki jingithuhpaw kiba iathuhlypa (Daniel 8:1, Isaiah 1:1, Ki Kam 9:10, bad kiwei kiwei) kiba lah ban don ryngkat bad da ki dak kiba paw shabar.

`Thoh katto katne ki dak kiba paw shabar kiba u nongiathuhlypa Daniel u la mad haba u don ha ka Jingithuhpaw. Peit ïa u Daniel. 10:7-19.`

U Daniel u la duh noh ïa bor katba u dang don ha ka jingithuhpaw. Nangta u la shaniah ha U Blei ïa kata ka por jong ka jingiashem jong u. Wat la kito kiba don ryngkat bad u kim shym la ioh ïa ka jingithuhpaw, hynrei ka jingdon ryngkat jong ka bor Blei ka long kaba khlaiñ haduh katta katta ba ki la phet noh bad ki la rieh noh da ka jingtieng. Wat la katta ruh, U Blei U pyndonkam ïa ki dak kiba paw ban khring ïa ka jingpyrkhat sha u nongiathuhlypa khnang ba yn lah ban buh jingkynmaw ïa ka khubor jong u nongiathuhlypa.

`Kaei kaba ka Deuteronomi 13:1–5 ka hikai ïa ngi shaphang ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa?`

Lyngba ki kiei kiei kiba lah ban iohi ki dak kiba paw kiba lah ban pyni ba ka bor ba kynja mynsiem ka trei kam, hynrei kane kam mut ba ka shu long hi ba u briew uba mad ïa kaei kaei kaba lah ban sngewthuh u dei uba la pyrsad mynsiem da U Blei. U Soitan u lah ruh ban pyni ïa ka jingjia ba phylla. Ym don kawei ruh ka sakhi kaba pynskhem ïa ka jingshakri jong u nongiathuhlypa. Lait na kaei kaei ka dak ka ba paw shabar, ïa ki nongiathuhlypa dei ban shah tynjuh. Ki jingpynpaw ba kynja mynsiem kim dei ka jingtynjuh ïa ka jingai bashisha jong ka jingiathuhlypa. Baroh ki jingtynjuh da ka Baibl ïa u nongiathuhlypa ki dei ban don khnang ba ngin lah ban pynshisha ïa u kum u wei na ki nongrah khubor jong U Blei. Ha ka histori, kaba sngewsih, ka long ba la pyndap da ki nongiathuhlypa lamler (peit Math. 7:15).

`Ha ki katto katne ki dustur, ki bor ba kynja mynsiem kiba jujia ban ïa kiwei pat. Hynrei balei ngi dei ban husiar bha haba ngi wad ban tip la kaei kaei ka bor ba kynja mynsiem la ka long shisha na U Blei?`
