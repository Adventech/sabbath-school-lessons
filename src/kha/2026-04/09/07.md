---
title:  “To Mad Bad To Khmih”
date:   27/11/2026
---

Ka jingpdiang jong ki Adventist ïa ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa jong ka Ellen G. White, naduh ba la sdang ïa ka haduh kine ki por mynta, ka la iakynduh ïa ki jingeh kiba kum kita kiba bun ki nongiathuhlypa jong ka Baibl. Hadien ka jingïohipaw kaba nyngkong jong ka Ellen G. White ha u bnai December 1844, don kiba buh jingkylli ïa ki jingkam jong ka jingïathuhlypa jong ka. Kane ka la pynlong ïa ki Adventist kiba sumar ïa ka Sabbaton (hadien la khot ki Seventh Day Adventist) ban pynmih ïa ki jingkynthoh ban kyrshan ïa ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa jong ka Ellen G. White.

Nyngkong eh, ki la tyrwa ïa ka jingpynshisha na ka Baibl na ka bynta ka jinglong junom jong ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa haduh kaba kut jong ka por. Ka Baibl ka kyrshan ïa ka jingpyni jong ka por ba mynta ïa ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa, ki la kynthoh. Da kaba pynshong nongrim ha ka Joel 2, Jingpynpaw 12:17, bad kiwei kiwei ki jingthoh, ka jingai jong ka jingïathuhlypa kan sa paw ha ki “sngi ba khatduh” bad kan sa long ka “dak ba pynithuh” ïa ki briew jong U Blei ha ka por ba khatduh. (Peit Gerhard Pfandl, “Ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa bad ka Balang ba sah jong U Blei,” ha ka Jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa, lynnong 4).

Kaba ar, ki Adventist banyngkong ki la kdew ba ka jingai jong ka Ellen G. White ka don ka jingktah kaba bha ïa ki bangeit. Kum ka nuksa, ki jingshemphang jong ka shaphang ka jingiathuhlypa ki la iarap ban pyniatylli ïa ki bangeit kiba leit sha ki jingialang Sabbaton conference ha ki snem 1840 ba la leitnoh haba kim ïa mynjur lang ha ki jinghikai doctrines ha ka baibl. Ka jingai jong ka ka la iai bteng ban sei “u soh babha” ha ki phew snem naduh kata ka por, bad ki jingthoh jong ka ki dang don ka jingktah hapoh ka imlang sahlang jong ka jingngeit jong ki Adventist.

Ka jingkynthoh kaba lai ban iada ïa ka Ellen G. White ka iadei bad ki jingiashem jong ki bor ba phylla ha ka jingithuhpaw jong ka. Katba ka dang don ha ka jingithuhpaw, kumba ngi la kdew mynshuwa, ka Ellen G. White ka la pyni ïa ki jingpynpaw ba phylla — ka la iathuh ïa ki jingshisha bad ki jinglong kiba ka kam ju tip, ka la pynpaw ïa ki jingjia ha ka lawei, ka la pyni ïa ka bor kaba kham khraw ban ïa ka jinglong ba shu long hi, bad ka jingbym lah ban tehlakam ne ïa ka bor ktah ïa ka por jong ki jingshem jong ka ha ka jingithuhpaw.

Ki Adventist mynta ki dang iai bteng ban pynpaw ïa kine ki jingkyrshan na ka bynta ka jinglong ba dei biang jong ka jingai jong ka Ellen G. White. Katba kine ki jingkynthoh ki long kiba skhem, ka “jingpynshisha” kaba khlAiñ ïa ka jingai jong ka Ellen G. White ka long ka jingshem shimet jong ka bad ki jingthoh jong ka. Kumba u nongrwai Salm Dabid u kyntu: “Ah To mad bad to khmih ba U Trai u long uba bha” (Salm 34:8). Dei tang haba ngi pule da ka jingduwai ïa ki jingthoh jong ka Ellen G. White ba ngi lah ban bishar bniah ïa ki jinglong nongiathuhlypa jong ka.

Kawei ka bynta ban sdang ka long ha ka kot rit hynrei kaba pawnam tam jong ka, Ki Mawjam sha U Khrist. Pynbud ïa kane da ki kot kiba don san tylli ki kot ba la buh lang jong ka Jingialeh jong ki Arta, kaba wad ïa ka phang pdeng jong ka jingialeh bakhraw naduh ka Jenesis haduh ka Jingpynpaw. Ki jingthoh jong ka ki long kiba lah ban ioh ha ki kot bad ki bor computor (peit EGWWritings.org) bad la pynkylla ïa ki sha kiba bun ki ktien.
