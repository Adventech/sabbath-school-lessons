---
title:  Ka Jingmut Jong Ka Jingthoh
date:   18/11/2026
---

Baroh ki Khistan ki dei ban sngewthuh ïa ka Baibl na ka bynta ïa lade. Ka Baibl ka la shai biang bha na ka bynta ki briew baroh ban sngewthuh ïa ka lynti jong ka jingpynim, Pynban ka long kaba eh bha ban iai tynjuh ïa ki jingmut kaba shai. Ka jingthoh Bakhuid ka long ka spah bym kut jong ka jingtip kaba pynim kaba ym lah ban pynduh.

`Pule Luakas 10:25–28. Kato katne ki briew ki kam ba ka Baibl kam donkam ban batai ïa ka. Lada kane ka long ka jingjia, balei ba U Jisu U la kylli ïa une u riewsaid, “Kaei kaba la thoh ha ka Hukum U Blei? Kumno phin batai ïa ka” Luakas 10:16?`

Ban sngewthuh ïa ka jingmut jong kano kano ka jingthoh, ngi dei ban sngewthuh nyngkong ïa ki kyntien ba la pyndonkam. Nangta ngi dei ban pyrkhat haduh katno ngi lah ban sngewthuh ba kumno la pyndonkam ïa ki hapoh ka jinglong jong ka thoh ka tar ba iadei bad ka jinglong ba pura jong ka Baibl, bad kumjuh ruh ïa ka jinglong jong ka Histori ha ki rukom ki dustur. Khatduh eh, ki jingpynkut jong ngi ki dei ban iadei pura bad ki jinghikai kiba kham jylliew jong ka Jingthoh Bakhuid. Ka jingïanujor ïa ki jingthoh ba ïadei dur — kumba la ju leh ha ki saw tylli ki Gospel — ka lah ban pynriewspah ïa ka jingsngewthuh jong ngi.

`Ianujor ïa u Mathaios 5:43-48 bad u Loukas 6:27-36. Kumno ka jingmut jong u Mathaios 5:43–48 bad ka jingthoh kaba iadei bad kane ka dkhot, Loukas 6:36, ka iarap ïa ngi ban sngewthuh kham bha ïa ka jingmut jong ka “ba janai” ha ka Mathaios 5:48?`

Kawei na ki jinglong ba kongsan tam jong u u nongbatai ktien ba iAiñeh jong ka Jingthoh Bakhuid ka long ka jingpyniasoh lang ïa ka jinglong kaba janai bad ka jinglong ba sngewrit. Kynmaw, ym don uwei ruh na ngi uba don ïa ka jingshisha na ngi hi; baroh ngi iohi lyngba byrngut byrnget.

Ngi dei ban ialeh barabor ïa ka jinghikai kaba bha (2 Tim 4:1-5). Ka nongrim kaba kongsan ban bud ka long ban pynleit jingmut ha kaei kaba ngi sngewthuh, ha kaei kaba shai, bad ban tei na kata, kaba ha kiba bun ki khep, katba nang mih ki sngi, kan iarap ïa ngi ban sngewthuh ïa kiei kiei kiba mynta ki lah ban ym shai. Ban shu batai lyngkot ïa ka bymjukut, kin don beit ki jingkylli ki bym don jubab.

Pyrkhat ïa ka por ba phim sngewthuh ïa kaei kaei kaba la pynkhuslai shibun ïa phi, bad ban ioh ïa ka jingbatai hadien. Kumno kata ka jingjia ka dei ban iarap ïa phi ban shaniah ha U Blei halor kiei kiei kiba don ha ka Ktien jong U kiba, ïa mynta, phim sngewthuh?
