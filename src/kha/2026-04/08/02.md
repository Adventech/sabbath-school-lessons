---
title:  Ka Baibl, Ka Batai Da Lade Ïa Lade
date:   15/11/2026
---

Ka jingbatai jong ngi ïa ka Jingthoh Bakhuid ka long kaba la ktah shibun eh da ki jingmutdur jong ngi. Kito kiba kheiñ ïa ka Baibl kum kaba shu lum lang ïa ki kot ki sla kiba rim kim lah ban iohi ïa kano kano ka jingiatylli hapdeng ki kot bapher bapher jong ka. Hynrei da kaba shim khia ïa ka nongrim ba kongsan ba “ka Jingthoh Bakhuid baroh ka long kaba la pyrsad mynsiem da U Blei bad kaba myntoi” (2 Timothi. 3:16,), ngi lah ban sngewthuh ïa ka jinglong kawei kaba don hapoh jong ka bad ka shah ïa ka Baibl ban long ka nongbatai da lade ïa lade.

`Pule Mathaios 5:17–48. Kumno U Jisu U batai ïa ka jinghikai jong ka Jutang Barim kaba la kdew ha kine ki dkhot?`

Ka jingpule kaba tang na sla ïa ka Mathaios 5:17–48 ka lah ban ai jingmut ba U Jisu u la bujli ïa ki jinghikai jong ka Testament Barim da ki jinghikai ba thymmai jong U hi. Hynrei ka jingpule kaba kham bniah ka pynshai ba U Jisu u dang pynpaw ïa ki jinglong ba jylliew bad ba kynja mynsiem jong ka Testament Barim. Ha kane ka jingmut, U dang pyndep ïa ka jingkular jong u Jeremaiah 31:31-33 ba, hapoh ka jutang bathymmai, ïa ka hukum U Blei yn buh ha ki jingmut bad thoh ha ki dohnud ki briew jong U (ianujor bad Hebru 8:8-10, Hebru 10:16).

Teng teng la pynbiang ïa ka jingbatai ïa kano kano ka bynta ba la ai hapoh kajuh ka kot ha kaba kata ka bynta ka paw. Kum ka nuksa, ha u Daniel 2, ngi shem ïa ka jingbatai shaphang ka jingphohsniew ba phylla jong u Syiem Nebukhadnessar (Daniel. 2:31-35), bud sa ka jingbatai ïa ka jingphohsniew (Daniel. 2:36-45). Kumjuh ruh ha Mathaios 13, ngi don ïa ka pharshi u nongbet (Mathaios. 13:1-9) bad nangta sa ka jingbatai jong ka (Mathaios. 13:18-23).

Teng teng ïa ka jingbatai ïa ka khubor la pynbiang da u nongiathuhlypa uba hadien. Kum ka nuksa, 2 Khronikl 36:22, 23 ka pynskhem ïa ka jingpyndep ïa ka jingiathuhlypa shaphang ki 70 snem, kumba la iathuhlypa ha u Jeremaiah 25:11, 12 bad u Jeremaiah 29:10. Nalor kata, kiba bun bynta ha ka Testament Barim la pyndep ha U Khrist, bad bun ki bynta jong ka Testament Barim la pynshai ne batai ha ka Testament Bathymmai.

“Ka Testament Barim ka long ka gospel ha ki dur bad ki dak. Ka Testament Bathymmai ka long ka tynrai ba Kawei ka donkam kum kawei pat.”—Ellen G. White, Selected Messages, kitab 2, p. 104. Shisha, ka Testament Bathymmai ka long ka nongpynshai ba la pyrsad mynsiem jong ka Testament Barim.

`Balei ka long kaba kongsan ban tip ïa ki jingmut jingpyrkhat jong ngi katba ngi pule ïa ka Baibl? Balei ka jingmut kaba kongsan tam jong ngi ka dei ban long ba ngi shaniah ha ka kum ka Ktien U Blei bad ba ka dei ban bishar ïa ngi, ym ïa ngi ïa ka?`
