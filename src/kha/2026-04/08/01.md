### Ki Jingpule Na Ka Bynta Kane Ka Taïew
Mathaios. 5:17-48; 1 Korinth. 16:20; Mathaios. 4:1-11; Loukas 10:25-28; Loukas 24:25-27, 44-49.

> <p>Dkhot Spel-lyndet</p>
> “Te haba u la sdang na U Moses, bad na ki nongiathuhlypa baroh, u la pynpaw ha ki jingthoh baroh ïa kiei kiei ruh shaphang ïa lade.” (Loukas 24:27).

Hadien ka jingshah sahnarphna bad ka jingmihpat U Jisu, U Khrist u la paw ha arngut ki synran kiba la sngewsih, kiba baroh arngut ki la phla, “Ngi hynrei ngi la iakyrmen ba long ma u u ban sa siewspah ïa U Israel:” (Loukas 24:21). Ki synran ki la khmih lynti ïa u Messaiah u ban pyllait ïa ki Jiw na ka jingsynshar jong ki Roman. Kane ka jingkhmih lynti kaba bakla bad bAiñong jong ka jingduh jingkyrmen kaba mih na kata ka jingbatai kaba la pynphai tang shiliang, kaba ka jingbatai kaba tang shibynta jong ka jingiathuhlypa shaphang u Messaiah jong ka Testament Barim.

U Khrist u la pynbeit ïa ka jingbymsngewthuh jingmut jong kine ki arngut ki synran da kaba batai ïa ki Jingthoh Bakhuid ha ki na ka jingpeit kaba kham ïar (Loukas 24:25–27). Ka jingbatai U Khrist ïa ka Jingthoh Bakhuid ka la plie ïa ki khmat jong ki bad ka la pynlong ïa ki dohnud jong ki ban thang hapoh jong ki (Loukas. 24:31, 32). Hadien, U Jisu u la paw ruh ha ka kynhun ki synran kiba kham bun ha Jerusalem, bad “U la plie ïa ka jingshemphang jong ki, ba kin sngewthuh ïa ki Jingthoh Bakhuid” (Loukas. 24:45; peit ruh Loukas. 24:33, 36, 44-49).

Kine ki jingjia ki pynpaw ïa ki jingmih kiba bha jong ka jingsngewthuh da kaba dei ïa ki jingthoh jong ki nongiathuhlypa. Ka jingsngewthuh bakla ïa ka jingiathuhlypa ka lah ban ialam sha ka jingjot ba kynja mynsiem.

_Pynkhreh ïa kane ka Lynnong na ka bynta ka Sabbaton, November 21_
