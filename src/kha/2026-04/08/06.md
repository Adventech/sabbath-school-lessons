---
title:  Ka Jingpynshai Shaphang Ka Baibl
date:   19/11/2026
---

Ka jingpule jong ngi ïa ka Baibl ngi dei ban pynleit jingmut ha ki ar tylli ki jingthmu ba kongsan: Ka Jingsan ba kynja mynsiem ba shimet bad ban ialam ïa kiwei sha jingiadei ba janai bad U Jisu. U Khrist u batai ïa ka jingong Khristan ka ba shisha kum kita kiba la ialam da U Mynsiem Bakhuid “Sha ka jingshisha baroh” Ioannis 16:13, u ba im “da kawei pa kawei ka ktien kiba la mihnoh na ka shyntur U Blei” Mathaios 4:4, bad uba hikai sha kiwei “ia baroh ïa kiei kiei” kiba U la hokum ïa ngi. Mathaios 28: 20.

`Pule Luakas 4:16–30 bad Luakas 24:25–27, 44–49. Kaei kaba ngi lah ban nang na ka lynti kaba U Khrist u la batai ïa ka Jingthoh hangne?`

U Jisu Khrist u la long, u dang long, bad un iai long “Ka lynti, ka jingshisha, bad ka jingim” (loannis. 14:6). Wat la katta ruh, ka jingialap jong U ka la long kaba shong skhem ha ki Jingthoh Bakhuid. Kum ka nuksa, Markos 1:14, 15 ka ong, “Te hadien ba la set byndi ïa u Ioannis, U Jisu u la wan sha Galilaia, u da ialap ïa ka gospel jong ka hima U Blei, bad u da ong, ‘La pyndep ïa ka ia, bad ka hima U Blei ka la jan”. Ka por ka la kdew shai sha u Daniel 9:24-27. Ha ka Jingiathuhkhana halor u Lum (Mathaios 5-7), U la sot na Eksodos 20:13, 14 (Mathaios. 5:21, 27). Ha u Loukas 4, U la sot na u Isaiah 61:1, 2. U Jisu u long u nongialap uba janai bha jong ka Baibl.

Ha kawei pat ka nuksa, Ki Kam 8 ka iathuh ïa ngi ba u Eunoch u nong Ethiopia “u ba la wan ban mane sha Jerusalem” (Ki Kam 8:27), kaba mut ba u la tip bha ïa ka niam Jiw bad la kumno kumno ïa ki katto katne ki jinglehniam jong ka. U la don wat ka kopi jong ka kitab u Isaiah, kaba u la pule sngewtynnad bha ha ka por ba u leit phai sha la iing. Hynrei um lah ban sngewthuh ïa ka jingiathuhlypa jong u Shakri uba kit noh ïa ki pop, kumta u la wad ïa u briew ban batai ïa kata sha u.

Kane ka jingjia ka hikai ïa ngi katto katne ki jinghikai kiba kongsan. Nyngkong, ka jingpule kaba shu pule ïa kawei ka bynta jong ka Jingthoh Bakhuid kam ju ialam sha ka jingsngewthuh kaba jylliew ïa kata ka bynta. Kaba ar, ha ka jingpule ïa ka Jingthoh Bakhuid ngi lah, bad ngi dei ban ioh jinghikai na iwei ïa iwei pat. Kaba lai, ngi don ka jingkitkhlieh shimet ban ïasam ïa ki khubor kiba bha jong ka Baibl bad kiwei pat.

Ka jingbatai jong u Philip ïa ka kitab u Isaiah bad kiwei pat ki bynta jong ka Testament Barim ka la ialam ïa u eunuch sha ka jingpynbaptis (Kam 8:35-39; ianujor bad Salm 51:12). Ki jingbatai jong ngi ïa ka Jingthoh Bakhuid ki dei ban don kajuh ka jingthmu (peit Ioannis 17:17).

`Wat la teng teng ngi lah ban donkam jingiarap ban sngewthuh ïa ki jingthoh, balei ngi dei ban sngewskhem ha la ka jong ka jingmut jingpyrkhat ïa kaei kaba ngi ngeit?`
