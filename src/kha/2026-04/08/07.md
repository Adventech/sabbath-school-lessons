---
title:  Ka Jingpule ïa Ka Ellen G. White
date:   20/11/2026
---

Kumno ngi pule ïa ka Ellen G. White? Hangne ki don katto katne ki jingbthah ban sngewthuh bad pyndonkam da kaba beit ïa ki jingthoh jong ka Ellen G. White.

Nyngkong eh, sdang da ka jingduwai, ka jingmut kaba bha bad ka jingmut kaba plie, ym da ka mynsiem kaba artatien. Ka jingmut jingpyrkhat jong ngi ka long ka iit kaba pyni ïa ki bynta ïa baroh kaba ngi iohsngew, pule bad iohi. Ngi donkam ïa U Mynsiem Bakhuid ban ialam ïa ka jingpyrkhat jong ngi. Kumba ka Ellen G. White hi ka la kdew: “Lada phi wad ïa ki Jingthoh Bakhuid ban pynshisha ïa la ki jong ki jingmut, phin ym poi satia sha ka jingshisha To wad khnang ban tip ïa kaei kaba U Trai U ong.”—Christ’s Object Lessons, p. 112. Ka long kumjuh ruh shaphang ki jingthoh jong ka.

Kaba ar, pyrkhat ïa ka jinglong jong kaei kaba phi pule. Ki briew bunsien ki la pynshong nongrim ïa ka jingsngewthuh jong ki ïa ki jingthoh jong ka Ellen G. White ha ka jingkren ba kyrpang ne ka bynta jong ka jingthoh ka ba la weng noh na ka jinglong tynrai jong ka histori ne thoh ka tar. Ka Ellen G. White ka la thoh ba “dei ban pyrkhat ïa ka por bad ka jaka.”—Selected Messages, kot 1, p. 57. Kane ka jingleh ka long kaba sngewsih ban iehnoh ïa ka jinglong jingman ka la jia, bad ka dang jia, ïa ka jingeh kaba khlem kut na ka bynta ki katto katne ki dkhot balang.

Kaba lai, pynleit jingmut ha ki nongrim. Kaei ka mat (ne ki mat) ba kongsan ba ka Ellen G. White ka pynksan ha kawei ka bynta? Kum ka nuksa, ha ka snem 1894, ka Ellen White ka la thoh sha ki dkhot balang ha Battle Creek, Michigan, da kaba pynrem ïa ka jingthied baicycle (peit Testimonies for the Church, vol. 8, pp. 50-53). Ka nongrim kaba ka Ellen White ka wad ban pynshlur ka long ka jingsumar hok ïa ki jingmih kiba don kam ki ba la ai da U Blei, kum ka pisa (ha kata ka por, ki baicycle ki long kiba rem dor bha).

Kaba saw, pule ïa baroh kaba ka Ellen G. White ka la thoh halor kawei ka Sobjek. Peitbniah ïa ki jingkren kiba jylliew ha ka jinglong jong ka jinghikai kyllum jong ka halor kawei ka sobjek. Ha ka por ba ka dang im, ka Ellen G. White ka la sngewsih ïa ka jingpyndonkam bakla ïa ki jingthoh jong ka: “Kaba bun kaba pynpaw ba ka long ka khubor na ka Mrs White, hynrei ka la trei kum ka jingthmu ban pynpaw bakla ïa ka Mrs. White, kaba pynlong ïa ka ban sakhi ha ka liang jong kiei kiei kiba ym iadei bad ka jingmut ne jingbishar jong ka.”— Selected Messages, book 1, p. 44.

Khatduh eh, pynthikna ba ka Ellen G. White ka la ong ïa kata. Ki don shibun ki jingkren kiba la pynshong nongrim bakla ïa ka Ellen G. White. Ka lad kaba shngAiñ tam ban pynthikna ba kaei kaba ngi pule ka dei na ka Ellen White ka long ban wad ïa ka jingpynkynmaw ïa ka tynrai jong ka. Shisien ba ngi la pynshisha hangno la shem ïa ka, ngi lah ban bishar bniah ïa ka da kaba pyndonkam ïa ki nongrim kiba dei.

La ai ha ngi ïa ka jingai kaba phylla ha ki jingthoh jingiathuhlypa jong ka Ellen G. White. Ka long kaba kongsan haduh katta katta ba ngin pule bha ïa kita ki jingthoh, kiba lah ban long—bad ki long—kum kata ka jingkyrkhu ïa ki million ngut. Ka long kaba sngewsih haba ki shah pyndonkam bakla bad shah pyniahap bakla.
