---
title:  Ki Tynrai Ka Toh Ka Tar
date:   04/11/2026
---

“Bad nalor kane, namar ba u nongiathuhkhana u la long uba stad, u la iai hikai ïa ka jingstad ha u paidbah; shisha, u la puson, bad u la wad noh, bad u la buh ryntih bun ki proberb. U nongiathuhkhana u la wad ban shem ïa ki ktien basngewtynnad, bad kata kaba la thoh ha ka hok, ki ktien ka jingjingshisha kein.” (Eklessiatis. 12:9, 10).

Lah ban pule ïa kane ka bynta ban pyni ba u Solomon um shym la sdang ïa ki pharshi baroh kiba u la thoh. Hapoh ka Jingpyrsad mynsiem U Blei, u la “wad” shibun ki ktien bad u la wad ban shem ïa ki “ktien kiba pdiang sngewbha.” Ha ka jingshisha, ïa ka jingbatai kaba la ju shim bunsien shaphang ka kynthei kaba bhabriew ha ka Proberb 31 la ai burom sha ka kmie jong u Syiem Lemuel uba ym lah ban tip u dei u ei, ym ha u Solomon (Proberb 31:1). Ki sakhi ki kdew ïa ka jingktah jong ka thoh ka tar jong ki nong Egypt ïa ki Proberb ruh kumjuh.

Kumjuh ruh, ki nongthoh histori ba la pyrsad mynsiem jong ka Testament Barim ki iathuh ïa ngi ba ki la pynshong nongrim na ki kot lyngkdop bad kiwei kiwei ki kot histori ban iathuh da ka jingshaniah ïa ka jingiathuhkhana jong ki briew U Blei.

`Pule 1 Syiem 11:41; Loukas 1:1-4; bad Judah 14, 15. Kine ki dkhot ki ïathuh aiu ïa ngi shaphang kumno ki nongthoh ba la pyrsad mynsiem teng teng ki pyndonkam ïa ki tyllong kiba ym dei na ka Baibl?`

U Esra bad u Nehemaiah ki long kiwei pat ki nuksa jong ki nongthoh Baibl kiba shaniah ha ki jingthoh histori ban buh jingthoh ïa ki jingthoh jong ki. U Esra u kynthup ïa ki kopi jong ki shithi bad ki hukum syiem shaphang ka Jerusalem bad ka Persia (peit Esra 4-7), bad u Nehemaiah u iathuh ïa ngi shaphang ki jingthoh jingtar kiba u la shem bad pynrung ha ka jingiathuhkhana jong u (peit Nehemiah. 7:5-72).

Kane ka nongrim ka paw ha ka Testament Bathymmai ruh kumjuh. “Ka paw shai na ka jingshem jong u Loukas ba ka jingpyrsad mynsiem ka treikam ha ka rukom kaba iadei bad ka jingtreikam ba kynja met jong ki bor pyrkhat bad kam buh ïa ki sharud. Hangne u don u nongthoh ba la pyrsad mynsiem uba la ialam da U Mynsiem Bakhuid ban ai ka jingpule kaba minot ïa ki jingthoh kiba don shaphang ka jingim U Khrist, bad nangta ban pyniasoh lang ha ka jingiathuhkhana kaba iadei ïa ki jingtip kiba la lum kumta.”— The SDA Bible Commentary, vol. 5, p. 669.

U Judah u i kumba u shim na ka 1 Enokh 1:9 —ka kot jong ki Jiw kaba ym dei ka hukum, kaba don hapdeng ki testament. Khlem da kdew ïa ki kyrteng jong ki, u Paul u la sot ruh na ki riewstad ki Greek (peit Kam 17:28 bad Titos 1:12).

`Wat la ki nongthoh baibl ki la shah ialam da ka jingpynpaw ba beit lyngba ki jingithuhpaw bad ki jingphohsniew, hynrei kane kam shym la kyntait ïa U Mynsiem Bakhuid na ka ban pyrsad mynseim ruh ïa kine ki nongrah khubor ban jied ïa ki jingthoh na kiwei pat ki tyllong. Balei kane ka long ka jingmut kaba kongsan ïa ngi ban kynmaw?`
