---
title:  Ka Khubor Ba Kynja Blei Bad Ki Jingbym Janai Jong U Briew
date:   05/11/2026
---

`Kaei kaba u Paul u ong shaphang ka jingkynshew U Blei ha 2 Korinth 4:7?`

U Paul u thoh shaphang ka spah U Blei kum kaba la aiti ha ki “khiew khyndew” (ki briew), khnang ba yn pyni ïa ka bor U Blei ha ka jingiapher na ki jingtlot jong ngi hi. Ka Jingthoh Bakhuid ka pynpaw ba kumba ki nongrah khubor jong U Blei kim shym la shah pynsah da ka bor ka ba phylla ba kynja blei na ki jingbakla ha ka jingim shimet jong ki (u Moses, u Elijah, u Dabid, u Jonah, bad u Petros, kum ka nuksa), kumta ruh ki jingthoh jong ki kim shym la lait na ki jingbym janai jong ka jingsngewthuh bad ka jingiakren jong u briew.

“Ka jingiasoh jong ka jinglong blei bad ka jinglong briew, kaba paw ha U Khrist, ki mih ki paw ruh ha ka Baibl. Ki jingshisha ba la pynpaw ki long baroh kiba la ai da ka jingpyrsad mynsiem jong U Blei; pynban la pynpaw ïa ki ha ki ktien jong ki briew bad la pynbeit katkum ki jingdonkam jong u briew.”—Ellen G. White, Testimonies for the Church, vol. 5, ka p. 747.

Ka jinglong briew jong ki nongrah khubor U Blei kam shym la shah pynrem ha ka jingkhot ba kynja Blei jong ki ban pynsngew ïa ki jingkynshew jong ka jingshisha, kumba u Paul u la thoh ha 2 Korinth 4:7. Bad kam shym la paw ruh ba U Mynsiem Bakhuid U la iohi ba ka long kaba dei ban khanglad ïa ki nongrah khubor jong U ban kren bad thoh ïa ki kyntien kiba ngi mynta ngi shem jingeh ban pynbeit ne ban batai. Katto kine na kine ki dkhot.ka long ïa kiba rit ka jingmut ban jynthut ha ki. Kum ki Khristan kiba ngeit ïa ka Baibl, ngi dei ban pyndem ha ka Ktien U Blei haduh katta katta ba ngi sngewthuh ïa ka bad shaniah ha U Trai, wat la ngim sngewthuh.

Katto katne ki jingbym iahap lah ban pynshong nongrim ha ki jingbakla bym don jingmut da ki nongthoh hadien ne ha ki jingbuh jingthoh (ki jingthoh) kiba ïa pyrshah kiba ki nongthoh ba la pyrsad mynsiem ki la shaniah. Haba ngi pyrkhat ïa ki dkhot kiba don jingeh, ngi donkam ban kylli ïa lade lada ngi sngewthuh pura ïa ka jingmut jong ki kyntien jong u nongthoh bad hato ka jingpule bniah shuh shuh ka lah ban pynbeit ïa ka jingeh.

Wat la ngim lah ban pynbeit ïa kawei pa kawei ka jingbakla ne jingbym iahap kaba paw, ngi shem ba ki long ki bym don ka jingiadei ha ka jinglong tynrai jong ka khubor ba la pynpaw. Ngi lah ban don ka jingshaniah ba ïa ki jingshisha ba kynja Blei jong ka Jingthoh Bakhuid la pynpaw da ka jingshaniah wat hapdeng ki jingbym janai jong ki nongrah khubor jong U Blei.

`Balei phi pyrkhat ba U Blei U la jied ban pyndonkam ïa ki briew kiba lah ban bakla ban ïa ki angel ki bym don pop kum ki nongrah khubor ba kongsan jong U?`
