---
title:  Ki Nongiarap Ha Ka Thoh Ka Tar
date:   03/11/2026
---

`Pule Jeremiah 36: 17, 18; bad Rom 16:12. Kumno kine ki dkhot ki iathuh ïa ngi sha phang ki nongthoh ïa ka Baibl ki pyndon kam ïa ki nongiarap ban pynthoh?`

La shim ba ka long kaba shisha, ba u Jeremaiah u la lah ban thoh ïa ki khubor jong u, hynrei ha ka jaka kata u la shaniah ha u nongthoh uba shaniah ban buh jingthoh ïa ki jingiathuhlypa jong u da ka jingshaniah bad ban iasam ïa ki bad ki nongialam jong ka jaitbynriew. U Jeremaiah um dei tang ma u ka nuksa ha ka Baibl ha kaba ki nongiathuhlypa ki pyndonkam ïa ka jingshakri jong u nongiarap thoh kot ban pynsngew ïa ka khubor ba kynja Blei.

Ha ka Testament Bathymmai, wat lada u Tertius u ong ba u la “thoh ïa kane ka shithi” (Rom 16:22), hynrei ka paw shai naduh kaba sdang jong ka kitab Rom ba u thoh tang ïa kaei kaba u Paul u kwah ban ong. U Tertius u la long u nangthoh kat kaba u ong, ne u nongthoh jong u — u nongiarap thoh kot uba la buh ïa ki ktien jong u Paul ha ka dur jingthoh.

Kane ka jingleh kam shym la long kaba phylla ïa u Paul. Ha kaba pynkut ïa ki shithi jong u sha ki Nong Kolosai bad Thessaloni, u Paul u buh jingmut ban pynrung ïa ka jingkhublei jong u da “ka kti jong nga hi” (Kolosai. 4:18, 2 Thessaloni. 3:17) kum ka lad ban pynshisha ïa ka shithi baroh kawei. Kane ka pyni ba u la shaniah ha uwei pat ban thoh ïa ki shithi na ka bynta jong u. Namar ba kane ka jingshisha ka lah ban pynmih jingartatien la ka shithi ka pyni hok ne em ïa u — lane kaba kham sniew, ba kam dei na u satia — u Paul u la pynbiang ïa la ka jong ka jingthoh da ka kti ha kaba kut khnang ba kan ym don jingartatien shaphang ka jinglong ba shisha jong ka shithi jong u.

Bun ki nongthoh ki pynshong nongrim ïa ka jingpher ha ki rukom thoh kot ba la shem hapdeng ki shithi jong u Paul namar ka jingpyndonkam jong u ïa ki nongiarap thoh kot. La suba ba kajuh ka long shaphang ki ar tylli ki shithi jong u Petros: “Watla ka long ka jingshisha ba ka rukom thoh jong ka 2 Petros ka pher na ka jong ka shithi kaba nyngkong, hynrei lah ban pynshong nongrim ïa kane da ka jinglong ba u Petros […] u la pyndonkam ïa ka jingïarap jong u nongthoh ha kaba pynwandur ïa ka shithi kaba u la thoh ha ka ktien Grik. ka paw shai ba u la leh kumta, ka kham long kaba shongnia shuh shuh ban pyrkhat ba u Petros, uba ka ktien tynrai jong u ka long ka ktien Aramaic, un jin da la leh kumta, bad ba kine ki nongthoh kin sa don ka jingktah kaba thikna ha ka rukom thoh ïa ki kyntien jong ki shithi jong u ha ka ktien Grik.”—Ka SDA Bible Commentary, vol. 5, ka p. 186.

Kane ka pyni ba, wat la ki dang iAiñeh ha ki jingpyrkhat bad jingmut jong ki nongrah khubor U Blei, ka rukom shna kyntien bad sentens ka lah ban pynpaw ïa ka jinglah thoh kot bapher bapher jong ki nongiarap kiba ki la pyntreikam. Dei ki jingpyrkhat kiba la pyrsad mynsiem, ym da ki kyntien ba kyrpang ba la pyndonkam ban pynpaw ïa kita ki jingpyrkhat ba la pyrsad mynsiem.
