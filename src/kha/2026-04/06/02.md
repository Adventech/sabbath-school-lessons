---
title:  La Khot Banthoh
date:   01/11/2026
---

Ka rukom kaba mynshuwa eh kaba U Blei U pyndonkam ban pynsngew ïa ki khubor sha ki briew ka long lyngba ka jingiakren beit beit da ka ktien, kum ki sur ba lah ban iohsngew, ne ki jingphohsniew, ne ki angel. Dei haduh ka por u Moses ba ka Jingthoh Bakhuid ka la thoh ïa ka jingbthah Blei kaba nyngkong eh ha kaba iadei bad kine ki jingjia, kaba long ban thoh ha ka “kot” ïa kaei kaba U Blei U la kren.

`Kaei ka jingkdew kaba nyngkong eh shaphang ka jingthoh ha ka Baibl? Pule Eksodos 17:14.`

Ha ka Testament Barim ngi shem bun ki nuksa jong ka jingai jingbthah jong U Blei ïa ki nonglam khubor jong U ban pynneh pynsah ïa ki ktien jong U na ka bynta ki briew jong U. Kum u Moses, la ong ïa u Jeremaiah, “To shim ha me kawei ka kot bakli, bad to thoh ha ka la ki ktien baroh kiba nga la kren ha me pyrshah ïa ki Israel, pyrshah ïa ki Judah, bad pyrshah ai ki jaid bynriew baroh, naduh kata ka sngi ba nga la kren ba me pyrshah ïa ki Israel, bad pyrshah ïa ki Judah, bad pyrshah ïa ki jaid bynriew baroh, naduh kata ka sngi ba nga la kren ha me, na ki sngi jong U Josaiah, haduh kane ka sngi keiñ.” (Jeremiah. 36:2,).

La ai ruh ïa u ïa ka daw ba u dei ban thoh ïa ki jingiathuhlypa: “Lehse ka ïing Judah kan shahshkor ïa kata ka jingsniew baroh ïa kaba nga la thmu ban leh ha ki; ba kin phai noh uwei uwei na ka lynti basniew jong u; ba ngan ioh map ïa ki jingleh bymhok bad ki pop jong ki.” (Jeremiah. 36:3). ïa ki jingiathuhlypa la ai ban ialam ïa ki briew sha ka jingpynim, ym na ka bynta ka jingkwah tip shaphang ka lawei.

Ki nongrah khubor jong U Blei ki la thoh hapoh ki jinglong kiba khraw kiba mih na ki jingjia kiba pher bapher. U Moses u la thoh ïa ki san tylli ki kitab ba nyngkong jong ka Baibl katba u dang don ha ri khlaw. U Dabid u la thaw katto katne ki Salm jong u katba u dang shah beh ha u Saul (peit Salm 52, 54, 57, 59). U Daniel u la thoh ïa ki jingphohsniew jong u mynba u dang shakri ha ki iingbishar jong ka Babilon bad ka Medo-Persia. Ka jingïapher, u Paul u la thoh ïa ki shithi jong u sha ki Nong Ephesos, Philipi, Kolossai bad Philemon mynba u dang don hapoh phatok, bad u Ioannis u la thoh ïa ka Jingpynpaw mynba u dang shah pynryngkang par ha Patmos. Ka paw shai ba haba ki nongrah khubor jong U Blei ki la shah pynkhih ban thoh, la pynkup bor ïa ki da U Mynsiem Bakhuid khlem da kheiñ ïa ka jinglong jingman jong ki.

Kaba sngewtynnad ka long ba baroh ki jinghikai ba la pynsah jong U Jisu ki wan ha ngi ha ka dur ba la thoh lyngba ki briew kiba U Blei U la jied ban shaniah ha kita ki jingshisha ba la buh kyrpang. Kane ka pyni ïa ngi ïa ka jingshaniah kaba U Blei U don ba ïa ki khubor ba kynja Blei yn thoh bad pynneh pynsah da ki nongrah khubor jong U.

`Kumno phin jubab ïa u briew uba ong ba ngim lah ban shaniah ïa ka Baibl namar ba ngim don ïa ki kot tynrai? Balei kane, da shisha, ka long ka jingkynthoh kaba tlot?`
