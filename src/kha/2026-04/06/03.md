---
title:  Ki Jait Bad Ki Rukom Thoh Kot
date:   02/11/2026
---

Ka Baibl ka don ki rukom thoh bapher bapher ba la pyndonkam da ki Nongrah khubor ba la pyrsad mynsiem da U Blei. Don kiba iasyriem bad ka jingthoh ha ka kot buh jingkynmaw (peit Jingkhein 33:1-49). Ha kawei pat ka liang, ki kitab u Daniel, u Sekharaiah bad u Ioannis ki don ki nuksa jong ka jingthoh jong ka jingpynpaw, kaba la pynpaw da ki dak kiba khraw, ki phang pdeng jong ka pyrthei, bad ki jingbatai shaphang ki jingthaw ba kynja bneng, nalor kiwei pat ki jinglong tynrai.

`Pule Isaiah. 13:1. Kumno ka Baibl hangne ka kdew sha ka khubor kaba u Isaiah u ai?`

Ki jingiohipaw ba la ioh da ki nongiathuhlypa la ju khot teng teng “ki jingkren”— kaba mut ba ki long ki jingpyntip ne ki jingpynpaw na U Blei (don ki jingpynkylla kiba pynkylla ïa ka kyntien kum “ka jingkit”). Ki jingpynkylla thymmai jong ka Baibl ki pyni ba bun la thoh ha ka dur jong ka poitri, bunsien da kaba pyndonkam da ki kyntien ba ïasyriem. Ka ba ïa syriiem ha ki jingbatai ka long haba ki lAiñ kiba hadien ki pynbud ne teng teng ki iapher bad ki lAiñ kiba kham mynshuwa. Kum ka nuksa: “Ha kata ka sngi u briew un khmih sha la U Nongthaw, bad ki khmat jong u kin ñiewkor ïa Uta U Bakhuid jong U Israel” (Isa. 17:7, NKJV). “U Nongthaw” bad “U Bakhuid” ki ïa ryngkat iwei ïa iwei pat, kawei pa kawei ka dur ka ai ïa ngi ïa ka jingtip kaba kham bniah shaphang kawei pat.

Ki nongiathuhlypa ki pyndonkam ruh ïa ki tiar thoh kot kiba ju pyndonkam kum ki jingpyni nuksa (ki dak) bad ki jingpynheh (pynheh palat). Khnang ba ngin sngewthuh kham bha bad pyntreikam ïa ki khubor jong ki, ka long kaba donkam ban ithuh ïa ka jait jingthoh kaba ki pyndonkam.

Kawei pat ka rukom thoh kot kaba ju shem ha ka Jingthoh Bakhuid ka long ka “jingmudui jutang.” Kum ka nuksa, ha ka Jeremaiah 2:4–9, u Jeremaiah u pyni ïa ka jingujor aiñ jong U Blei pyrshah ïa ki briew jong U namar ba ki la pynkheiñ ïa ki jingkitkhlieh jong ki ha ka jutang. Da kaba pyndonkam ïa ki dur ha ka kamra bishar, u nongthoh u pyni ïa U Blei kum u nongmudui uba buh ïa ki daw jong ka jingbishar jong U.

Ka jingthoh bakhuid ka kynthup ruh ïa ki jingthoh jong ka jingiathuhlypa, ka histori, ka jingrwai bad ki pharshi. La ong ïa u Jeremaiah ban thaw ïa ka kitab jong ki khubor jong u baroh (Jeremiah. 36:2), katba bun na ki jingthoh jong ka Testament Bathymmai ki kynthup ïa ki shithi kiba la thoh sha ki briew shimet ne sha ki shnong ki thaw. Ym ju pyniapher ïa ka bor jong kawei ka rukom kren halor kawei pat. Ki shithi jong u Paul ki dei ban ñiew ba ki don bad kit ïa kajuh ka jingshongkhia kum ki nongiathuhlypa hyndai, namar u Paul u ai sakhi shai ha ki shithi jong u ba la pyrsad mynsiem ïa u da U Mynsiem Bakhuid (peit 1 Korinth. 7:10, 12, 40).

`Katto katne ki riewstad ki banjur ïa ka jingmyntoi ba kynja briew ha ka Jingthoh Bakhuid ha ka jingshimkhia jong ka jinglong blei. Balei ba kata ka long ka lynti kaba thikna sha ka jingjot ba kynja mynsiem?`
