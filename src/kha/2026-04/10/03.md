---
title:  Ka Bor Ka Iktiar Jong U Jisu
date:   30/11/2026
---

`Kaei ka nongrim jong ka bor ka iktiar jong U Jisu? Kaei ka sakhi jong kane ka bor ka iktiar? Peit ïa u Mathauios. 21:23-27 bad Ioannis 17:2.`

Katba U Jisu U dang hikai, ki lyngdoh ki la wan sha U bad ki la dawa ban tip mano ba la ai ha U ïa ka bor ban leh ïa kane ka jingshakri. Nalor baroh, U Jisu um shym la pyndep ïa kano kano ka jingpule ha ka skul jong ka shnong, bad um shym la ioh jinghikai ruh ha ki skul tynrai jong ki rabbi. Ha ka jaka ban jubab ïa ki jingkylli jong ki, U Jisu u la kdew ïa ka lynti sha ka jubab, da kaba kylli ïa ki nongkylli jong U shaphang ka bor bym lah ban bakla kaba la pyni da u Ioannis Nongpynbaptis. Da kaba ki kyntait ban jubab, kim shym la kyntait tang ïa ka bor U Blei kaba la pynkup ïa ka jingshakri u Ioannis hynrei ki la kyntait ruh ïa ka bor U Jisu uba kum u Ioannis, la phah ruh da U Blei.

`Ki dkhot kiba bud ruh ki iathuh aiu shaphang ka bor ka iktiar U Jisu? Mathaios. 7:28, 29; Markos 1:21-27; Loukas 8:22-25; Loukas 9:1; Ioannis 5:25-27; bad Ioannis 7:38.!`

Ka bor baroh ka long ka jong U Jisu kum u Nongthaw jong ki briew (Ioannis 1:3) bad u Nongpynim (Rom 3:24). U long U Ktien U Blei. Ka bor blei kaba U Jisu u la don ha ka jingim bad jingshakri jong U ha ka pyrthei ym lah ban bud ne pynmih hi dalade da uno uno ruh. Ka jinghikai U Jisu ka la pynlyngngoh ïa ki nongsngap namar U la hikai kum Uba don ka bor (Mathaios. 7:29).

Baroh ki Gospel ki thoh ïa ka bor jong ka jingiathuhlypa ba kynja mynsiem jong U Jisu kaba sha lyndet kaba kham bha. U Jisu u la map ïa ki pop (Markos. 2:10, 11), u la beh noh ïa ki ksuid (Markos. 3:15), bad u la tip ïa kaei kaba ki briew ki pyrkhat (loannis. 2:24, 25). U la pynmihpat ïa kiba iap bad u la tyrwa ïa ka jingim bymjukut (loannis. 10:28). Katba u dang don ha ka pyrthei kum u Nongsiewspah jong ngi, U Jisu U la pyndonkam ïa ka bor ba la ai ha U da U Blei U Kpa (Ioannis 17:2), bad U la shaniah bad iatreilang bad U Kpa (Ioannis 5:19) ha ki por baroh, bunsien U da kdew biang sha ki Jingthoh Bakhuid, ha kaba, U la kam, u la phla shaphang jong U. U Jisu ruh u don ïa ka bor kaba pura bad u lah ban aiti ïa kata ka bor ha ki synran jong U (Markos 6:7). Kum ka Ktien U Blei, ka jingphla jong U ka la wan ruh sha ki nongiathuhlypa lyngba kiba U la kren (Jingpynpaw 19:10).

`Wat la ki sakhi baroh kiba U Jisu u la ai, don ki briew kiba dang kyntait ïa U. Kumno ngin husiar ba ngin ym ngat ha kajuh ka jingma jong ka jingbymngeit, haba la ai ïa ka jingpynshai baroh kiba ngi dei ban ngeit?`
