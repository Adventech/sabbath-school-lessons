### Ki Jingpule Na Ka Bynta Kane Ka Taïew
Eksodos. 4:10-16, Loukas 8:22-25, Ioannis 3:1-10, Ki Kam 16:25-34, 1 Korinth. 5:1-5, Loukas 7:28.

> <p>Dkhot Spel-lyndet</p>
> “Namar ka ktien U Blei ka long kaba im, bad kaba don bor, bad kaba kham nep ban ïa kano kano ka waitlam arliang syrti, bad kaba pei haduh kaba pynkhlad ïa ka jingim bad ïa ka mynsiem, ïa ki mat bad ïa ka khlein shyieng ruh, bad kaba bishar ïa ki jingmut bad ïa ki jingthmu ka dohnud.” Hebru 4:12.

Ha ka 9 tarik u Nohprah, 1989, ka jingkren jong u Günter Schabowski ha ka TV ha Germany Mihngi jong ka ri Komunist ka la pynlong ïa ka jinghap jong ka kynroh Berlin. Haba kren kum u nongaiktien jong ki nongthoh khubor jong ka East Germany, u Schabowski u la pynbakla ïa ka jingkren ha ka jingïalang jong ki nongthoh khubor ha ka telebishon. Ha ka jaka ban pynbna ba ki East Germany ki lah ban shah ban leit jngoh ïa ka ri Europe sepngi hadien ka jingtrei kaba jrong bad kaba eh ban ioh ïa ki visa kiba biang, u la shu ong ba yn shah ïa ki nong Germany mihngi ban leit jngoh ïa ka ri Europe sepngi. Haba u nongthoh khubor u la kylli lano ka jingkylla ha ka polisi kan treikam, u Schabowski u la ong, “Katba nga tip, kane ka treikam ... mardor, khlem da pynslem.” Tang hapoh katto katne minit ki briew ki la sdang ban tuid sha ki jaka talasi ha khappud, da kaba dawa ban shah ïa ki ban ïaid lyngba, kumba la pynbna da ka sorkar.

Bunsien la ai bor ïa ki briew ban kren na ka bynta kawei ka kompeni ne wat na ka bynta ka ri. Hynrei kumno shaphang kito kiba kren na ka bynta U Blei? Ha kane ka taiew ngin bishar ïa ka bor aiu ki nongiathuhlypa jong ka Baibl ki don, bad kaei, lada don, ka bor ba la bat da ki nongiathuhlypa ki bym pat ju thoh ïa ka kitab jong ka Baibl, kum u Nathan u nongiathuhlypa, u loannis Nongpynbaptis, bad, hooid, wat ka Ellen G. White ruh.

_Pynkhreh ïa kane ka Lynnong na ka bynta ka Sabbaton, December 5._
