---
title:  Ki Nongiathuhlypa Kiba Don Thoh Bad Bym Don Jingthoh
date:   03/12/2026
---

`Pule 1 Khronikl 29:29 bad 2 Khronikl 9:29. Kaei kaba phi iohi shaphang ki katto katne ki nongiathuhlypa kiba la kdew ha kine ki ar tylli ki dkhot?`

Don shibun ki nongiathuhlypa kiba kum u Nathan, u Gad, u Ahijah, bad u Iddo kiba long ki nongiathuhlypa kiba la ithuh, bad ba ïa ki kitab jong ki kim shym la long shi bynta jong ka Baibl.

`Pule 1 Ki Syiem 29:39 bad Laukas 7:28. Kaei kaba kine ki dkhot ki hikai ïa shaphang ka bor jong ki nongiathuhlypa ki bym don jingthoh?`

Wat la ngim tip balei ba U Blei U la shah ban pynrung ïa ki katto katne ki khubor jong ki nongiathuhlypa ha ka Baibl katba ïa kiwei pat la iehnoh khlem pynrung, hynrei ngi tip ba ki nongiathuhlypa ki bym dei ki nongiathuhlypa kiba ym shym la pynrung ha ka Jingthoh Bakhuid ki long kiba la pyrsad mynsiem kumjuh da U Blei. Ka Baibl ka hikai ba ki jingai ba kynja mynsiem, kynthup ïa ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa, kin iai bteng haduh kaba kut jong ka por. Ka Baibl ka iathuhlypa ba kin don ki nongiathuhlypa kiba hadien ka Baibl, ki bym dei ki nongiathuhlypa kiba don jingthoh “na ka bynta ban pynkhreh ïa ki riewkhuid na ka bynta ka kam shakri, na ka bynta ban tei ïa ka met U Khrist, haduh ba ngin da poi baroh sha ka jingiatylli jong ka jingngeit” (Ephesos. 4:12, 13), bad kin don “ha shuwa jong ka sngi kaba khraw bad kaba itieng jong U Trai (Joel . 2:31).

Hadien ba u Dabid u la klim bad pyniap briew, U Blei u la phah ïa u nongiathuhlypa u Nathan ban ialeh pyrshah ïa u (2 Samuel. 12:7-14). Wat la u Nathan um shym la thoh ïa ka kitab ba iadei bad ka Baibl, hynrei u Dabid um shym la buh jingkylli ïa ka bor jong u Nathan, bad kata ka long namar ba ka bor jong ki jingthoh jong ki nongiathuhlypa ka shong ha ka jingpyrsad mynsiem jong ki, bad wat la pynrung ne em ïa ki ha ka jingthoh jong ka Baibl. Wat la katta ruh, baroh ki nongiathuhlypa bashisha ki bym don jingthoh, khamtam eh ki nongiathuhlypa hadien ka por jong ka Baibl — bad khamtam eh u nongiathuhlypa jong kane ka por ba mynta kum ka Ellen G. White — ki la iohi ïa lade kum kiba hapoh ka bor jong ka Baibl. Ki khubor jong ki ki la hap ban shah tynjuh da ka Baibl bad kim bujli ïa ka Baibl kum ka tyllong jong ka jingngeit bad jinghikai.

Ka Ellen G. White ka la thoh, “U Trai u kwah ïa phi ban pule ïa ki Baibl jong phi. Um shym la ai kano kano ka jingshai ba kham bun ban shim ïa ka jaka jong ka Ktien jong U. Kane ka jingshai ka long ban wanrah ïa ki jingmut kiba klumar sha ka Ktien jong U, kaba, lada bam bad longdoh long snam, ka long kum ka snam ba pynim ïa ka mynsiem.”— Selected Messages, kot 3, p. 29.

`Haduh katno ngi kum ka balang ngi kohnguh ïa kine ki ktien jong ka?`
