---
title:  Ka Basah Jong Ka Por Kaba Kut
date:   16/12/2026
---

Ka Jingpynpaw 12 ka pyni ïa ka histori ba iar jong ka jingialeh bakhraw, naduh ba ka la sdang ha bneng haduh ki sngi ba khatduh.

`Pule Jingpynpaw 12:17. Kaei ka “jingphla U Jisu”? ïa nujor Jingpynpaw 19:10 bad Jingpynpaw 22: 8,9.`

Ha ka Jingpynpaw 12:17, la kdew ïa ar tylli ki jinglong kiba pynithuh ïa ka balang jong ka por ba khatduh. Ka balang ka bat bad sumar ïa ki Shiphew Hukum (kiba kynthup ïa ka hukum Sabbaton) bad ka don ïa ka “jingphla U Jisu.” Kaei kane ka jingphla?

Katto katne ki jingpynkylla Baibl ki batai ïa ka “jingphla U Jisu” ba ngi kum kiba ngeit ngi phla ïa U Jisu bad ngi pynpaw ïa U lyngba ka mishon bad ka jingialap jong ngi (peit Jingpynpaw 19:10, “bat ïa ka jingphla U Jisu,” U Jisu u long ka jingthmu jong ka jingphla jong ki Khristan). Kiwei pat ki jingpynkylla Baibl ki ai ka jingmut kaba pher, da kaba ong ba U Jisu hi U ai ka jingphla ïa ka balang jong U (Jingpynpaw 19:10, “ka jingshisha kaba U Jisu u la pynpaw”— U Jisu u long u sobjek uba kit ïa ka jingphla). Ha ka jingshisha, ïa ka jingthoh tynrai jong ka Grik lah ban pynkylla ha kano kano ka rukom. Kumta, kaei ka jingmut jong u Ioannis?

Lada ngi ianujor ïa ki ar tylli ki dak jong ka jingithuh, ngi iohi ïa ka jingiasyriem kaba khraw ha ka rukom thoh ïa ki (wat kham paw ha ka ktien Grik tynrai ban ïa ki Baibl jong ngi). Kumba ki “hukum U Blei” ki wan shai na U Blei, kumta ruh ka “jingphla U Jisu” ka mih na U Jisu. Kumta, dei U Jisu uba sakhi. U pynithuh ïa ka balang kaba shisha. Kumno U leh ïa kane? U leh ïa kata lyngba ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa.

Ia ki jubab la shem ha ka Jingpynpaw 19:10 bad Jingpynpaw 22:9. Hangne ka paw shai ba ïa ka “jingphla U Jisu” la pyniadei bad ka “mynsiem jong ka jingiathuhlypa.” U Ioannis hi u long u nongiathuhlypa bad u pyni ha ka jinglong jong u ïa ka “jingphla U Jisu.” U nongiathuhlypa u dei u briew uba don ka jingkhot kaba kyrpang kum u nonglam khubor jong U Blei sha ki briew jong U, la ka long ban kyntu bad khot ban kylla bamut, ban pynshlur, ne ban pynpaw ïa ka lawei.

Ka Jingpynpaw 12:17 ka pyni ba U Jisu U pynlong ïa ka balang jong U kaba lah ban ithuh lyngba kane ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa. Kane ka mut ba kumba U Blei U la phah ïa ki nongiathuhlypa sha ki briew jong ka Testament Barim jong ka Israel bad Judah, lane ïa u apostol Ioannis sha ka balang Khristan kaba nyngkong, ki nongiathuhlypa ruh kin mih ha ki sngi ba khatduh jong ka histori jong ka pyrthei.

`Kiei ki sakhi kiba phi la iohi ban pyni ba ïa ka jingshakri jong ka Ellen G. White lah ban ithuh ban pynksan kum ka nuksa jong ka jingtrei jong “u mynsiem ka jingiathuhlypa” (Jingpynpaw 19:10)?`
