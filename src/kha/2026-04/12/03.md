---
title:  Ka Pentekost
date:   14/12/2026
---

`Pule Ki Kam 2:1-21. Balei ba u Petros u la shim na u Joel ha kaba iadei bad ka jingwan kaba nyngkong jong U Khrist? Kaei ka bynta ba ka jingiathuhlypa ka don ha kine ki dkhot?`

Ha kine ki dkhot jong ka Testament Bathymmai, ngi shem ïa kawei na ki jingshim kylliang ïa ka jingkren ka ba jrong tam na ka Testament Barim. Haba ngi pule ïa ka jingmut jong u Petros, ngi lah ban kylli, “Ko Petros kumno shaphang ki dak? Ngim lah ban iohi ïa ki ha ka jinglong jong phi.” U Petros un jubab lehse, Ka la don ka jingpyndep ïa ki dak. Ha ka por ba U Jisu u la sahnarphna ha ki khyndiat sngi mynshuwa, ka sngi ka la dum haduh lai kynta (Mark 15:33).

Kaei kaba la long ka jingpynshisha kaba pynngeit ïa u Petros, lehse, ka kham duna ka jingpynngeit ïa ngi mynta. Ngi dang khmih lynti ïa ka jingpyndep kaba pura jong ka jingiathuhlypa jong u Joel. Ka mat ka shong ha ka jingthoh jong u Joel hi. Ha u Joel 2:23, u Joel u kdew shaphang u slap uba nyngkong bad uba khadduh. Ki Kam 2 ka long ka jingpyndep kaba nyngkong eh jong kane ka jingiathuhlypa — u slap uba nyngkong. Ka jingpyndep kaba khatduh kan long ka jingtheh noh ïa U Mynsiem tang shu kut ka por lyngba u slap uba khatduh.

Bunsien ki briew ki batai ïa ka jingiathuhlypa kum “ka jingiathuhlypa ïa ka lawei.” Kane kam long kaba bakla, hynrei ki nongiathuhlypa jong ka Baibl ruh ki khot ïa ki briew ban kylla kaba mut. Nalor baroh, hadien katba u Petros u dang kren, don napdeng u paidbah kiba la shah ktah mynsiem.

“Te haba ki la iohsngew ïa kane, ki la sngewsih, bad ki la ong ha u Petros bad ha kiwei pat ki apostol, ‘Ah ki rangbah bad ki para bangeit, kumno ngin leh?’” (Ki Kam 2:37).

Bad u Petros u la jubab aiu?

“To phin kylla kaba mut, bad yn pynbaptis ïa phi uwei pa uwei ha ka kyrteng U Jisu Khrist, ïa kaban ioh map ki pop: bad phin ioh diang ïa ka jingai U Mynsiem Bakhuid? Namar ka jingkular ka long ïa phi, bad ïa ki khun jong phi, bad ïa baroh kiba sha jngai, kat kiba U Trai uba U Blei jong ngi un khot ha u.” (Ki Kam 2:38, 39).

U Ioannis Nongpynbaptis, kum u nongiathuhlypa, u la khot ruh ïa ki briew sha ka jingkylla kaba mut. Wat la u pynbna ïa ka jingwan nyngkong U Jisu, hynrei u ialap ruh ïa ka jingkylla kaba mut na ka bynta ki pop jong ki. “To kylla kaba mut, namar ka hima ki bneng ka la jan!” (Mathaios. 3:2).

Kumta, ha u Joel 2 un shem ïa ka jingpyndep kaba khatduh ha ki por ban wan. Kumba u Joel bad u Petros ki la leh, ki nongiathuhlypa jong ki por ba khatduh kin khot ïa ka jingkylla kaba mut, kin kdew sha U Jisu, bad kin ai ban ialam ïa ka balang jong U Blei ha ka por ba khatduh.

`Mar ïa iohi ïa kine ki jingpynpaw ba phylla, ki paidbah ha kaba sdang ki la pyrkhat ba ka dei kaei kaei ka jait jingjia jong ka mariang kiba shulong hi. Ha kane ka khep, ki la pyrkhat ba ki shynrang rangbah ki la buaid! (Ki briew kiba la buaid kiba kren kynsan da ki ktien nongwei?) Wat la ka jubab jong ki ka long kaba khlem jingmut, kaei kata shaphang ki briew kaba pynlong ïa ki, wat ha khmat ki bor ba kynja bneng, ban pyrshang ban wad ïa ka jingbatai kaba tynrai?`
