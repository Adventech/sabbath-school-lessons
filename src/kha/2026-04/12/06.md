---
title:  Ki Jingpynpaw ïa Ka Juk Ba Mynta
date:   17/12/2026
---

Kiba bun ki briew kiba don ka jingngeit mynta ki lah ban ai kat kumba shong ka jinglong jong ki nongiathuhlypa hyndai jong ka Baibl, hynrei hato ki ngeit ba kajuh ka jingjia ka lah ban long ha kine ki sngi jong ngi? Lada dei ban ngeit ïa ka jingkular jong u Joel 2:28–31 shaphang ka jingpynpaw ba donburom ha ka por ba khatduh jong ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa, ka lah ban long kumno?

`Pule Ki Kam 2:36-42. Kaei ka pdeng jong ka khubor jong ka jingialap U Petros ha ka Pentekost? Kumno ki nong sngap jong u Petro ski leh?`

Ka jingtheh noh ïa U Mynsiem Bakhuid ha ka Pentekost ha ka snem. 31 A.D ka long ka jingpyni kaba bha eh ïa kaei kaban sa jia ha ka por ban thehnoh ïa U Mynsiem Bakhuid ha ka por ba khatduh, wat la ka long ha ka jingtheh kaba kham khraw shibun. Ka Pentekost ka la pawnam da kaba kyntiew ïa U Jisu, ka jingkylla kaba mut kaba la saphriang, bad ka jingpynthemmai ïa ka jaitbynriew kaba la pynkhreh bad kaba la pynbiang na ka bynta ka kam U Blei. Ka khubor jong u Petros kaba pynshong nongrim ha U Khrist la pynkup bor da U Mynsiem Bakhuid, bad kane ka la ialam ïa ki 3,000 ngut ki mynsiem ban kylla kaba mut bad ban shah pynbaptis. Kane ka jingkyrkhu “u slap ba ha shuwa” jong U Mynsiem Bakhuid u la bet ruh ïa u symbai uba kordor, ka balang — ka met ba iatylli jong ki bangeit kiba pyni nuksa bad iasam ïa ka jingieit U Jisu Khrist katba ki dang pynbna ïa ka jingwan pat jong U.

Kumba la ai ïa u slap uba mynshuwa “ban pynlong ïa ka jingmih jong u symbai ba kordor, kumta ruh ïa u ‘slap uba khatduh yn ai ha kaba kut jong ka jingih jong ka jingot.”—Ka Ellen G. White, Ka Jingialeh Bakhraw, p. 611. Ha kiwei pat ki kyntien, ki jingpynpaw jong ka juk mynta jong ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa — u slap uba hadien — kin long shibun kum ki jingpynpaw kiba mynshuwa jong u slap uba ha shuwa ha ka Pentekost.

Kumba ngi la dep ban iohi ha u Joel 2:28-31, ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa ha kaba kut jong ka por kan plie ïa ki samla bad ki tymmen, shynrang bad kynthei. U Blei un kren lyngba kito kiba U Mynsiem Bakhuid U jied. Lano lano ruh haba U Mynsiem Bakhuid U jied ïa u nonglam khubor jong ka por ba khatduh, ka khubor jong u kan kyntiew ïa U Jisu, kan khot ïa ki briew ban kylla kaba mut, bad kan pynkhreh ïa ki briew ban pyndep ïa ka kam jong U Blei. Kine ki lai tylli ki jinglong ki long ki dak jong ka jingshakri nongiathuhlypa jong ka Ellen G. White. Ha ka jingim jong ka baroh kawei, ka la kyntiew ïa U Jisu bad ïa ka Jingthoh Bakhuid ha ka jingkren bad jingthoh jong ka. Ka la khot ïa ki Adventist bad kiwei pat ban kylla bamut naduh ka dohnud, bad ka la kyntu ïa baroh ban pynrung kyrteng ha ka mishon U Blei na ka bynta kiba la jah.

`Katno phi la shim kabu ïa ka jingai kaba phylla kaba la ai ha ngi lyngba ka jingshakri jong ka Ellen G. White.`
