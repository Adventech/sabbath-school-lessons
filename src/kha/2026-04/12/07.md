---
title:  Baroh Ka Dei Tang Shaphang Ka Mission
date:   18/12/2026
---

Ha ki Ephesos 4, u Paul u pyni ba ka jingthmu ba kongsan jong ki jingai ba kynja mynsiem ka long ban iarap ïa kiba ngeit ban pyniaid shaphrang ïa ka mishon U Blei. Ka jingshakri nongiathuhlypa jong ka Ellen G. White ka don kajuh ka jingthmu. Ki khubor ba la pyrsad mynsiem jong ka ki la iarap ïa kiba ngeit ban nangsan ha ka jingiadei kaba kham jylliew bad U Blei bad ki la pynlong ïa ki ban iasam ïa kata ka jingieit bad kiwei pat.

“Na baroh kiba kam Khristan,” ka la thoh, “Ki Seventh Day Adventist ki dei ban long kiba hakhmat eh ha kaba kyntiew ïa U Khrist ha khmat ka pyrthei. Ka jingpynbna ïa ka khubor jong u angel uba lai ka khot ïa ka jingpyni ïa ka jingshisha jong ka Sabbaton. ïa kane ka jingshisha, ryngkat bad kiwei pat kiba la kynthup ha ka khubor, ka long ban pynbna; hynrei ïa ka pdeng kaba khraw jong ka jingkhring ka dei, U Khrist Jisu, ym dei ban iehnoh shabar. Dei ha kadiengphna jong U Khrist ba ka jingisynei bad ka jingshisha ki iakynduh lang, bad ka jinglong hok bad ka jingsuk ki iakynduh iwei ïa iwei pat.Dei ban ialam ïa u nongpop ban peit sha Kalbari da ka jingngeit jong ki khynnah rit u dei ban shaniah ha ki jingbha jong U Nongpynim, da kaba pdiang ïa ka hok jong U, da kaba ngeit ha ka jingisnei jong U.”— Gospel Workers, pp. 156, 157.

Nalor kata, ïa uwei pa uwei u bangeit, ka la ban jur, la khot ïa u ban long shibynta jong ka mishon U Blei. “U Trai u dawa ba ka jingpyrshang shimet kaba kham khraw shibun dei ba yn buh da ki dkhot jong ki balang jong ngi.”—Evangelism, p. 113.

“Ha ka kam U Trai ym dei ban don kiba alhia. To ai ba kiba don ka jingiapher kin iatylli ha ka jingtrei kum ki nongtong briew. To ai ba kin wad ban lum ïa ki mynsiem na ka jingsniew jong ka pyrthei sha ka jingkhuid kaba pynim jong ka jingieit U Khrist.” —Evangelism, p. 115. Ka Ellen G. White hi ka la long ka mishoneri sha Europe ha ki snem 1880, bad ha Australia hapdeng ki snem 1890.

Dei namar ka jingkhot kum u nongiathuhlypa jong U Blei ïa ka jingaiti bad ka mishon shimet ba u Soitan u “bitar” (Jingpynpaw 12:17) ïa ki briew jong U Blei ha kaba kut jong ka por. Kine ki long ki nongbud jong U Jisu kiba don ka khubor jong kaba kut ka por, Kine ki long ki nongbud jong ka jingialam jong ka jingiathuhlypa bad ka jingshemphang: “U Trai u la pynpaw ïa ki jingma kiba don sawdong bad ha khmat jong ngi. Lyngba ka bor jong U Mynsiem jong ka jingiathuhlypa, U la pynpaw ïa ki jingpynshoi kiban shim mraw ïa ka pyrthei, bad U la kren ha ki briew jong U, U da ong, ‘Kane ka long ka lynti.’ iaid phi ha ka’ (Isaiah 30:21) . The Great Controversy unmasks the deceptions of Satan.”—Ellen G. White, Manuscript 31, 1890.

U Soitan u bitar lep haba ki briew ki aiti ïa la ka jong ka jingim ha U Jisu, bad u leh ïa kiei kiei baroh ban shukor ïa ka sur jong ka jingiathuhlypa jong U Blei. Katto katne na ki rukom jong u, ki thmu ban shukor ïa ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa jong ka Ellen G. White kum ka “bym don jingmut.” Nalor baroh, ka la thoh ha ka por kaba pher bha na ka por jong ngi. Kawei pat ka jingpyrshang ka long ban wanrah ïa ka jingartatien da kaba buh jingkylli ïa ki jingong jong ka kum ka nongiathuhlypa bad ban kdew ïa ka kum ka nongiathuhlypa ba lamler. Hynrei ka lad kaba kham tbit ban pynlong ïa ka Ellen G. White kaba khlem jingmut ka long ban pynduh ïa ka jingiadei jong ka pateng kaba mynta na kaba pule ïa ki jingthoh jong ka na ka bynta jong ngi.
