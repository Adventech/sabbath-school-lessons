title:  Ki Jingai Jong U Mynsiem
date:   15/12/2026

`Pule Ephesos 4:11-14. Balei U Blei U ai ïa kine ki jingai sha ka balang jong U? kumno ki jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa ka iarap ban pyndep ïa ka jingthmu jong U Blei na ka bynta ka balang?`

Ka jingthmu jong ki jingai ba kynja mynsiem baroh ka long ban tei ïa ka balang, kumjuh ruh ban pynbna ïa ka gospel. U Paul u la kynthup ïa ka jingai jong ka jingiathuhlypa (kaba la pynpaw na ka kyntien “ki nongiathuhlypa”) ha baroh saw tylli ki thup. Kiwei pat ki jingai ki jia tang shisien. Kumta ngi lah ban pynkut nia ba ka jingiathuhlypa ka long kawei na ki jingai ba kynja mynsiem kaba kongsan tam. To ngin ïa pyrkhat katto katne ki jinglong ba kongsan shaphang ki nongiathuhlypa:

1. ïa ki nongiathuhlypa la khot da U Blei (peit 1 Syiem 19:16, 19-21 bad Isaiah 6). Bunsien ki sngew ba kim long kiba bitdor bad ki sngew artatien ban shim ïa ka jingkitkhlieh jong ki, hynrei ki kohnguh.
2. U Blei U kren ha ki nongiathuhlypa da ki jingithuhpaw, ki jingphohsniew, ne ki jingkren ba lah ban iohsngew. Teng teng U Blei U pynpaw ïa ki jingjia ki ban sa wan. Ha kiwei pat ki por, U pyntip ïa ki shaphang ki jingjia kiba mynta jong ki jaitbynriew ne ki briew (Daniel 2).
3. Ki Jingthoh Bakhuid ki batai ïa ki jingjia ha ka met: ki nongiathuhlypa ki lah ban duh bor met ne wat ban sangeh ban ring mynsiem, hynrei ki lah ruh ban dap da ka bor kaba thymmai (Dan. 10:8-10, 18, 19).
4. Ki nongiathuhlypa ki pynsngew ïa ki khubor jong ki ha ki rukom bapher bapher — da ka ktien (Ki Kam 21:10-12), lyngba ka jingleh ba la pynpaw (Esekiel 4), lane da ki shithi (Jingpynpaw 2, 3). Katto katne ki nongiathuhlypa ki pyndonkam ïa ki nongiarap thoh kot (peit Jeremiah. 36:1-4).
5. Ka khubor jong u nongiathuhlypa ka dei ban long kat kum ki nongiathuhlypa barim, namar U Blei um ialeh pyrshah ïa lade (Isaiah. 8:20).
6. Ka jingdon jong ka khubor jong u nongiathuhlypa, bad kumjuh ruh ka jingktah jong ka jingim shimet jong u nongiathuhlypa, kan sakhi ïa ka tynrai ba kynja blei (Mathaios 7:15-20).
7. U Soitan u ju pyrshang ban shukor ïa ka jinglong blei. Kane ka long ruh kumjuh na ka bynta ka jingai jingiathuhlypa. Ha ka por kaba khatduh, kin don ki nongiathuhlypa lamler (Mathaios. 24:24, 2 Pet. 2:1).
