---
title:  Hunhu hweVanhu muVaporofita
date:  11/10/2026
---

Sezvatakaona svondo rapfuura, Ishe akashandisa muporofita Eria kuita basa guru muIsraeri, kunyange paiva nekupikiswa kukuru. Zvakaitwa naIshe kubudikidza naye zvaishamisa zvikuru. Asi zvakadaro . . . chii chakazoitika?

`Fungisisa kuti Jakobho anotsanangura sei muporofita Eria muna Jakobho 5:17, 18. Anorevei paanoti Eria aiva “nomuviri wakafanana nowedu” (NKJV)?`

Eria akawana kubudirira kunoshamisa paGomo reKarimeri (1 Madzimambo 18); Mwari akanga ashanda nesimba guru kubudikidza naye, uye chimwe chezvishamiso zvikuru zvinotaurwa muTestamende Yekare chakaitika: pamashoko aEria pachake, moto wakaburuka kubva kudenga (1 Madzimambo 18:38)! Ungadai wakafunga kuti murume uyu waMwari aizofara zvikuru mushure mechiitiko chakadaro.

`Verenga 1 Madzimambo 19:1–16. Izvi zvinotiudzei pamusoro pemamiriro epfungwa nemanzwiro emuporofita waMwari?`

“Zvaringana zvino! Zvino, Jehovha, torai upenyu hwangu, nokuti handisi nani kupfuura madzibaba angu!” (1 Madzimambo 19:4, NKJV). Izvi zvinonzwika semashoko emunhu ari kutambura neimwe mhando yekuora mwoyo. Sei? Chokwadi, zvakaitika paGomo reKarimeri zvaive zvakaoma kuti zviteverwe nechimwe chinhu chikuru kudaro; asi, zvakadaro, pane chimwe chinhu chiri kuitika pano.

Zvichida kiyi yacho inogona kuwanikwa mumashoko ake ekuti, “Ndakashinga zvikuru nokuda kwaJehovha Mwari wehondo; nokuti vana vaIsraeri vakasiya sungano Yenyu, vakaputsa aritari Dzenyu, uye vakauraya vaporofita Venyu nomunondo. Ini ndoga ndasara; uye vanotsvaka kundiuraya” (1 Madzimambo 19:10, NKJV). Izvi zvinonzwika sekunge ari kuchemera zvaaitenda kuti zvakanga zviri kukundikana kwake mubasa. Kutsauka pakutenda kwakanga kuchiripo, kunyange mushure mekuedza kwake kukuru, sokunge iye ega aigona kumisa mafungu acho. Chero chii chaiva chiri kuitika pano, tinogona kuona hunhu hwevanhu uye kusasimba kwemurume uyu waMwari. Nemamwe mashoko, Eria aiva munhu aiva “nomuviri wakafanana nowedu.”

`Sei dzimwe nguva zvichiva nyore kunzwa sekuti wakundikana muupenyu hwako kana mumabasa ako? Ndezvipi zvivimbiso zveBhaibheri zvaungabata kuti zvikubatsire kukunda kuora mwoyo?`
