---
title:  Kuziva Vaporofita Vechokwadi
date:  12/10/2026
---

Munhoroondo yekutanga yaIsraeri pane humbowo hwekuti kwaiva nevaporofita venhema (Deut. 18:20). Naizvozvo, Mwari haana kusiya vanhu Vake vasina gwara rekuziva kuti vaporofita Vake vechokwadi ndivanaani.

Sevanogamuchira kuratidzwa nekufemerwa kwaMwari, vaporofita vechokwadi vaisvitsa mashoko aMwari nokutendeka, nemazvo, uye nechokwadi, kunyange dzimwe nguva izvi zvaiita kuti vasafarirwa nevanhu vaiva nesimba.

`Verenga 1 Madzimambo 22:1–14. Izvi zvinotiudzei pamusoro pekuti vaporofita vakatendeka vanoshanda sei?`

Kuti mambo ataure pamusoro pemuporofita achiti, “Ndinomuvenga, nokuti haandiporofitiri zvakanaka, asi zvakaipa” (1 Madzimambo 22:8, NKJV), zvinotaura zvakawanda pamusoro pamambo, asi zvakawanda zvikuru pamusoro pemuporofita. Zviri pachena kuti aiva munhu asingatyi kutaura chokwadi, kunyange kuna mambo pachake—uye iyi haisi nzira yekuwana kufarirwa neumambo.

Mikaya akamira ega achipikisana nevaporofita venhema mazana mana apo aitaura chokwadi chaakanga aziviswa naMwari. Pakamanikidzwa kuti achinje mashoko akaipa aibva kuna Ishe kuti afadze mambo akaipa Ahabhi nemukadzi wake Jezebheri (1 Madzimambo 22:13, 14), Mikaya akataura chokwadi chaMwari, zvisinei nekuti chaizogamuchirwa sei.

`Verenga 1 Samueri 9:3–14. Ndima idzi dzinotiudzei pamusoro pekuti vamwe vanhu vaiona sei vaporofita vaMwari vakatendeka?`

Mubhaibheri mune mienzaniso mizhinji yehunhu hweupenyu hwairarama vaporofita. Panyaya iyi, Sauro akatumwa nababa vake, Kishi, kunotsvaga mbongoro dzavo dzakanga dzarasika. Muranda aifamba naSauro akakurudzira kuti vasarega kutsvaga, nokuti muguta iroro maiva “nomunhu waMwari, uye munhu anokudzwa” (1 Sam. 9:6, NKJV). Tsananguro iyi yaSamueri muporofita inoratidza hunhu hwaitarisirwa kuvaporofita vaMwari. Vaporofita vechokwadi muMagwaro vanozivikanwa se“vanhu vaMwari,” vakasarudzwa muchikamu kuti varatidze hunhu Hwake. Panguva imwe cheteyo, kungoti vamwe ve“vanhu vaMwari” vangava vaporofita hazvirevi kuti havana zvavanokanganisa sevanhu.

`Ndiani, kunyange pakati pe“vanhu vaMwari” vanoremekedzwa zvikuru, wawakamboziva asina kana kukanganisa?`
