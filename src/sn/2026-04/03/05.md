---
title:  Vaporofita Vakadzi
date:  14/10/2026
---

`Verenga Ekisodho 15:20, Vatongi 4:4, 2 Madzimambo 22:14, na Nehemia 6:14. Ndima idzi dzinotidzidzisawo chii pamusoro pebasa reuporofita?`

MuMagwaro mune humbowo hwakawanda hwekuti Mwari akadana vakadzi kuti vave vaporofita. Ona kushandiswa kwezwi rechiHebheru nevi’ah (Eks. 15:20) uye rechiGiriki prophētis (Ruka 2:36). Muna Mika 6:4, Miriami anonyorwa pamwe chete naMosesi naAroni sevaya vakatumwa kuvanhu. Miriami anoratidzwa semuporofitakadzi akaimba rwiyo rwekukunda apo vaIsraeri vakayambuka Gungwa Dzvuku (Eks. 15:20, 21). Zvisinei, muporofitakadzi uyu, sevamwe vake varume, akanga asina kukwana, sezvinoratidzwa nemafungiro ake asina kunaka kuna Mosesi nemukadzi wake wekuEtiopia; izvi zvakaita kuti arangwe zvakakodzera (ona Num. 12:1–10).

Tinogonawo kuona basa remumwe muporofita munyaya yaDhebhora, uyo akashanda semutongi muIsraeri (Vatongi 4:1–4).

Muchokwadi, pane humbowo hwakajeka hwekuti Dhebhora (Vatongi 4:4) akagamuchira kuratidzwa kubva kuna Mwari (Vatongi 4:4–9, 14–22). Rwiyo rwake rwekukunda (Vatongi 5:2–31) runobvuma kupindira kwaMwari uye rwaiva nechirevo chakadzama chechidzidzo pamusoro paMwari kurudzi panguva iyoyo munhoroondo.

`Verenga 2 Madzimambo 22:11–14. Sei varume vaMambo Josia vakaenda kuna Hurida? Izvi zvinofanira kutiudzei pamusoro pebasa rake rakakosha murudzi?`

Hurida “muporofitakadzi” akabvunzwa nevarume vakuru vedare raMambo Josia (2 Madzimambo 22:14; 2 Makoronike 34:22) mushure mekunge vaverenga mashoko e“Bhuku reMurayiro” (2 Madzimambo 22:8, NKJV), iro rinofungidzirwa kuva Duteronomio, kuna mambo, uyo akapindura nokubvarura nguo dzake (2 Madzimambo 22:11, NKJV). Zviri pachena kuti iyi yaiva nyaya yakakomba zvikuru, uye mambo akaiona saizvozvo, ndosaka varume vake vakaenda zvakananga kuna Hurida. Akanga agamuchira shoko kubva kuna Mwari pamusoro pekutongwa kwaJudha kwaiva pedyo. Kuvandudzwa kwakaitwa naJosia kwakabva pamashoko euporofita aHurida uye kwakadzivirira kutongwa kwaMwari pamusoro pevanhu Vake.

`Vaporofita varume nevaporofita vakadzi, kunyange vakadanwa naMwari, vaivawo vatadzi vaida nyasha. Tariro ipi yaunogona kutora kubva munyaya dzavo?`
