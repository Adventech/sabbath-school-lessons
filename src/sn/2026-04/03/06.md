---
title:  Vaporofita Venhema
date:  15/10/2026
---

Chero zvipi zvinoitwa naMwari, Satani anogara aine zvake zvekunyepedzera. Zvakafanana nevaporofita. Israeri yakatarisana nevaporofita vechokwadi nevaporofita venhema. Chinhu chikuru chaiva kuziva musiyano.

`Verenga Jeremia 14:1–15. Chii chaiva chichiitika pano, uye Ishe vakaona sei vaya vaitaura zvenhema se“vaporofita” muzita Rake?`

Muna Jeremia 14, Mwari akapa Jeremiya shoko rekutongwa kwaJudha, kutongwa kwaizouya nenzira yemunondo, kureva hondo, nzara, uye denda. Vaporofita venhema panguva iyoyo vakapikisa shoko raJeremiya. Jeremiya anovatsanangura nemashoko akajeka zvikuru: “Jehovha akati kwandiri, ‘Vaporofita vanoporofita nhema muzita Rangu. Handina kuvatuma, handina kuvaraira, uye handina kutaura navo; vanokuporofitirai chiratidzo chenhema, kuvuka, chinhu chisina maturo, uye kunyengera kwemwoyo yavo’ ” (Jer. 14:14, NKJV).

Vaporofita venhema vakaora nokuti sosi yezvavanoti chiporofita kazhinji yaiva “vamwari” venhema vavainamata (Jer. 23:13, 14). Mwari anoyambira achiti, “Handina kuvatuma, handina kuvaraira, uye handina kutaura navo” (Jer. 14:14, NKJV). Ivo “vaporofita vekunyengera kwemwoyo yavo” (Jer. 23:26, NKJV). Ezekieri akarairwa kuporofita achipikisa “vaporofita . . . vanoporofita zvinobva mumwoyo yavo” (Ezek. 13:2, NKJV).

Vaporofita venhema vanowanzotevera zvinofarirwa nevanhu vakawanda. Izvi ndizvo zvakaitika muna 1 Madzimambo 22, apo boka revaporofita mazana mana rakaungana parayiro yaMambo Ahabhi (1 Madzimambo 22:6). Neinzwi rimwe chete vakakurudzira mambo kuti aende kuhondo achirwisa Ramoti-Gireadhi. Ahabhi akabvunzana naJehoshafati, mambo weJudha, mushure mekunge vanzwa zano revaporofita vaAshera. Jehoshafati akakurudzira Ahabhi kuti abvunze muporofita Mikaya kuti vazive kana vaifanira kuenda kuhondo kana kwete. “Ipapo mutumwa akanga aenda kunodana Mikaya akataura naye achiti, ‘Zvino inzwa, mashoko evaporofita ose anokurudzira mambo. Ndapota, shoko rako ngarive seshoko remumwe wavo, utaure zvinokurudzira’ ” (1 Madzimambo 22:13, NKJV).

Kumanikidzwa kwakaitwa Mikaya kuti anyepe kunogona kunge kwakafanana nekumanikidzwa kwakaitwa vaporofita venhema vaAshera. Asi Mikaya haana kutevera maonero aifarirwa nevanhu vakawanda; panzvimbo pezvo, akamirira kuti Mwari amuudze zvaaifanira kutaura (1 Madzimambo 22:14).

`Kunyangwe uri muporofita kana kwete, zvinogara zviri nyore kutevera zvinofarirwa nevakawanda. Tichifunga kuti tsika dzemunyika dzinogona kuva dzakaora zvakadii, sei izvi zvisiri pfungwa yakanaka?`
