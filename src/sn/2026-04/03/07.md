---
title:  Vasina Kukwana Asi Vakazvipira
date:  16/10/2026
---

Sezvataona, upenyu hwevaporofita veTestamende Yekare hunoratidza pachena kuti hazvina kumbova nyore kuva mutumwa waMwari. Ellen G. White akasangana nekushushikana kwakafanana nekwaiva nevaporofita veBhaibheri. Sezvatakadzidza Chishanu chapfuura, aiva mudiki, ainyara, uye aiva neutano husina kusimba paakagamuchira kudanwa kwaMwari. Kunyange chiratidzo chechipiri chakatsigira kudanwa kwake, Ellen Harmon [White] akaramba achitya kudanwa kwaMwari, achinyora kuti:

“Anenge vhiki imwe chete mushure meizvi Ishe vakandipa chimwe chiratidzo, vakandiratidza miedzo yandaifanira kupfuura nayo; kuti ndaifanira kuenda ndoudza vamwe zvavakanga vandiratidza; kuti ndaizosangana nekupikiswa kukuru, uye kutambura kukuru kwemweya. . . .

Chiratidzo ichi chakandinetesa zvikuru. Utano hwangu hwaiva husina kusimba, uye ndaingova nemakore gumi nemanomwe chete. . . . Ndakanyengetera nemwoyo wose kuti mutoro uyu uiswe pane mumwe munhu. Asi chiedza chose chandakawana chaiva chekuti, ‘Zivisa vamwe zvandakuratidza.’ Ndakanga ndisingadi kubuda munyika. Pakuberekwa ndakanga ndisina kuzvivimba zvikuru. . . . Ndakatarisa muguva nechishuwo. Rufu rwakaratidzika kwandiri serwuri nani kupfuura mabasa andaifanira kutakura.”—Spiritual Gifts, vol. 2, pp. 35, 36.

Waona here kuti Ellen Harmon [White] aitoda kufa pane kugamuchira kudanwa kwake? Mushure memunamato wakawanda nekukurudzirwa nevamwe vatendi, akabvuma uye akatsaurira upenyu hwake hwose kutevera “kuraira Kwake.”—Ellen G. White, Spiritual Gifts, vol. 2, p. 38.

Aizova inzwi rine simba zvikuru kuSeventh-day Adventist Church yaizouya—uye kunyange richimiririra kereke iyoyo. Zvisinei, Ellen Harmon [White] aiva munhu uye aiva nezvikanganiso zvake, miedzo yemuviri, matambudziko emhuri, uye kusawirirana nevamwe. Asi pakati pehunhu hwake hwevanhu, tinoona chidzidzo chikuru: Mwari anosarudza vanhu vasina kukwana asi vakazvipira kumushumira.

Pasi pekutungamirirwa neMweya Mutsvene, basa reuporofita raEllen G. White raizokurudzira vatendi uye kuvabatanidza nechokwadi cheBhaibheri. Pakazosvika 1860, akanga atotora chikamu chakakosha mukukura kweSeventh-day Adventist denomination yakanga ichangotanga uye mubasa rayo rekuzivisa mashoko engirozi nhatu kunyika. Mwari aizotungamirira vatendi ava kuti varonge Seventh-day Adventist Church muna 1863. Mumakore akatevera, inzwi reuporofita raEllen G. White rakava nesimba mukuvakwa kwehurongwa hweAdventist hweutano nedzidzo pasi rose. Zvisinei, mupiro mukuru kupfuura zvose webasa rake ndiwo mabhuku nezvinyorwa zvake, zvinokwana anenge mapeji 100,000. Zvinyorwa zvakafemerwa zvaEllen G. White zvinoramba zvichitaura pasi rose nanhasi.
