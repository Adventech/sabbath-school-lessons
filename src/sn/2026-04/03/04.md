---
title:  Mabasa eVaporofita
date:  13/10/2026
---

`Verenga 1 Samueri 8:4–22 na Revhitiko 8:10–13. Vaporofita vaishanda sei maererano nemadzimambo nevapirisiti?`

Vaporofita vaidyidzana nemadzimambo muzviitiko zvakanaka nezvisina kunaka. Apo Israeri yakakumbira mambo (1 Sam. 8:4–22), vakati vaida “mambo kuti atitongere semamwe marudzi ose” (1 Sam. 8:5). Uyu muenzaniso unosuwisa asi une simba wenjodzi yakatarisana nevanhu vaMwari munguva dzose: kuda kufanana nevanhu vakavapoteredza. Panyaya iyi, vakanga vasingazivi kuti chikumbiro ichi chaizounza chii, kunyange vakanga vayambirwa (1 Sam. 8:11–18) naMwari kubudikidza nemuporofita Wake. Zvichienderana nezvido zvavo, uye kunyange mushure meyambiro yake, Samueri akazodza mambo wekutanga waIsraeri, uyo anozivikanwa munhoroondo yeBhaibheri saMambo Sauro.

Mabhuku eTestamende Yekare, 1 na2 Madzimambo uye 1 na2 Makoronike, anonyora mhando nehunhu hwehutongi hwaive nemadzimambo aIsraeri naJudha. Nhoroondo tsvene inoratidza kuti madzimambo akaita zvakaipa akanga akawanda kupfuura avo vakaita zvakanaka pamberi paMwari. Rimwe remabasa makuru evaporofita raiva kubatsira kudzosa madzimambo asina kutendeka mukuteerera murayiro waMwari. Nekuda kweizvozvo, vaporofita ava vakatarisana nekutyisidzirwa, uye vamwe vakatombokuvadzwa kana kuurayiwa nemadzimambo akatungamirira vanhu vaMwari kure Naye (1 Madzimambo 18:4–19; 2 Makoronike 24:20–22).

Vaporofita vaidyidzanawo nevapristi. Mosesi, muporofita waMwari, akazodza Aroni nevanakomana vake sevapristi vekutanga veIsraeri (Revhitiko 8:10–13). Pane nguva dzaiitika apo vapristi vaigamuchira mashoko ekutsiurwa kubva kuna Mwari kubudikidza nevaporofita (Hos. 6:9; Maraki 2:1–3). Muenzaniso unoshamisa ndewemhuri yose yaEri, yakatarisana nekutsiurwa nokuda kwemabasa avo akasviba(1 Sam. 2:27–36). Uku kwaiva kutsiurwa kwakasimba zvikuru pamusoro pehuori hwakapararira pakati pevanhu vakapiwa mikana yakakosha.

Vaporofita vaiva vadzidzisi vevanhu (2 Makoronike 34). Vaisvitsa mashoko kubva kuna Mwari kuvapristi, kumadzimambo, uye kurudzi (Jer. 5:31; Ezek. 22:26). Vaporofita vakataurawo kumarudzi akapoteredza vachitsanangura chirongwa chaMwari kwavari, dingindira rinodzokororwa kakawanda mumashoko evaporofita veTestamende Yekare (Isa. 60:1–3; Isa. 62:1, 2). Vaporofita vaitarisirwa kwete kungosvitsa nokutendeka mashoko avakapiwa naMwari chete, asi dzimwe nguva vairairwawo kuanyora pasi nekuachengetedza (2 Makoronike 13:22). Zvakare, kunyange vaporofita vaiva vanhu, nezvose zvinouya nekusasimba kwevanhu, vaiva varume nevakadzi vemunamato, veShoko, uye vebasa kuna Mwari nevanhu Vake (Jer. 42:4; 2 Madzimambo 22:15–17).
