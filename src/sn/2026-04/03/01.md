### Verenga zveChidzidzo cheVhiki Ino
Jakobho 5:17, 18; 1 Madzimambo 19:1–16; 1 Madzimambo 22:1–14; Eks. 15:20; 2 Madzimambo 22:11–14; Jer. 14:1–15.

> <p>Ndima Yekuyeuka:</p>
> “Jehoshafati akamira akati, ‘Nditeererei, imi vaJudha navagari veJerusarema: Tendai kuna Jehovha Mwari wenyu, mugosimbiswa; tendai kuvaporofita vake, mugobudirira’ ” (2 Makoronike 20:20, NKJV).

Global Positioning System, kana kuti GPS, inobatsira vafambi kuziva pavari, kwavari kuenda, uye inotovapa yambiro pamusoro penjodzi dziri munzira. Vafambi vemweya vari parwendo rwekuenda kudenga vanodawo kutungamirirwa zvino uye mune ramangwana ne“positioning system” yekudenga. Imwe nzira yakashandiswa naMwari munguva dzakapfuura yaiva kubudikidza nebasa revaporofita veTestamende Yekare. Munyaya dzavo, mazano avo, zviporofita zvavo, nhetembo dzavo, nenziyo dzavo, tinowana zvakawanda pamusoro pevaporofita vakawanda vakasvitsa mashoko aMwari kuvanhu.

Chidzidzo chevhiki rino chinoongorora tsananguro dzeTestamende Yekare pamusoro pevaporofita, tsananguro dzinotibatsira kunzwisisa chipo chechiporofita sezvachakaratidzwa kuburikidza nevaranda vaMwari.

Sezvatichaona, ava vaiva vanhu vakaita sesu, nokuti vaiva vanhu vasina kusimba, vaingogonawo kutya, kutsamwa, uye kuora mwoyo. Upenyu hwavo hwaisava nyore, asi hwaizara nematambudziko nemiedzo, sezvinoitawo hwedu tose.

Saka, tingadzidzei kubva pakuongorora vamwe vevaranda vaMwari vakadanwa nenzira yakakosha?

_*Dzidza chidzidzo chevhiki rino kugadzirira Sabata, Gumiguru 17._
