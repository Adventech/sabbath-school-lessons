---
title:  Zvibereko Zvakanaka
date:  25/11/2026
---

MuMharidzo yePagomo (Mateo 5–7), Jesu akatsanangura hwaro hwekereke yechiKristu. Zvinoita sekuti basa reuporofita raizova chikamu chehwaro ihwohwo. Mushure mezvose, Jesu akayambira pamusoro pevaporofita venhema achiti, “Muchavaziva nezvibereko zvavo” (Mat. 7:20, NKJV).

Hunhu hwechibereko hunogona kutongwa chete nekufamba kwenguva. Nokutarisa hunhu hwemuporofita uye kuona migumisiro inobva kumashoko ake, tinogona kuona kana muporofita iyeye ari kukwevera vamwe kuna Mwari kana kuvatsausa kubva kwaari.

`Verenga Jona 1:1–3, Mabasa 15:36–39, na VaGaratia 2:11–14. Ndima idzi dzinotidzidzisei pamusoro pevaporofita?`

Kuva muporofita hakuiti kuti munhu asave nechivi kana kuti asakanganise somunhu. Tinoona mumienzaniso yeBhaibheri yaJona, Pauro, uye kunyange Petro zvikanganiso kana kusakwana kwehunhu. Mwari oga ndiye asina kukanganisa; vatumwa Vake havambodaro. Vaporofita, sesu tose, vane kusasimba, asi mafambiro eupenyu hwavo anofanira kuratidza kutungamira kuna Mwari, uye vaporofita havafaniri kuramba kana kushora basa ravo neupenyu hunopesana neshoko ravanoparidza.

Hapana muporofita anoratidza zvizere muenzaniso waJesu, asi muporofita wechokwadi anozvipira kumisimboti nemararamiro eumambo hwaMwari (Mat. 7:16–20). Chero munhu anoti mutumwa waMwari asi mararamiro ake achipikisana pachena neshoko raanotakura anofanira kufungidzirwa nokungwarira.

Kunyange kudanwa kweuporofita kwakaoma, vaporofita vazhinji vakaratidza “zvibereko zvakanaka” panguva yebasa ravo. Somuenzaniso, Mosesi akatungamirira vana vaIsraeri kubudikidza murenje zvinobudirira (Deut. 34:10–12). Kutendeka kwaHagai kwakakurudzira vagari veJudha kutangazve kuvaka temberi yechipiri, basa rakanga ramira kwemakore 19 (Hag. 1:12). Basa raIsaya risina kutya rakashandira madzimambo mana eJudha, richivatungamirira panguva yakaoma uye richinongedzera kuna Mesia (Isa. 39:5, 6; Isa. 53:4–6). Hatigoni kukanganwa kureurura kwaDanieri kwemwoyo wose (Dan. 9:15), kana kuzvipira kwaJeremiya kwakazara nemisodzi (Jer. 9:1). Zvisinei nekushushwa nekubatwa zvisina kunaka kwavaiwanzosangana nako, vaporofita vaMwari vaigara vachiratidza rudo rwechokwadi kwaari nekuvanhu Vake, chiratidzo chikuru chebasa rine zvibereko.

`Kangani varume kana vakadzi vaMwari vakakuodza mwoyo nemaitiro avo kana kusakwana kwehunhu hwavo? Chidzidzo chipi chakakosha chaungadzidza kubva pachiitiko ichocho?`
