---
title:  Kuberekwa kwaKristu MuNyama
date:  24/11/2026
---

Maererano na 1 Johani 4:1–3, muyedzo wekutenda mukuzvarwa kwaJesu muviri womunhu waibva wabvisa vanhu vanoti vaporofita vanobva kune mamwe marudzi makuru ezvitendero zvenyika, kana vaya vanoti vanobatana nenyika yemweya vasina kubatana nechitendero chakasarudzika. Kana munhu “asingapupuri kuti Jesu Kristu akauya munyama,” munhu iyeye “haasi waMwari” (1 Johani 4:3, NKJV).

Johani paakanyora ndima idzi, aifunga nezvevadzidzisi venhema vaidzidzisa kuti Jesu akanga asiri munhu chaiye, asi aingoratidzika semunhu. Zvisinei, muyedzo uyu unoenda kure kupfuura ipapo. Hausi kungoti muporofita anofanira kubvuma kuti Jesu aiva munhu chaiye akararama panyika. VaKristu vazhinji vanotenda izvi pamusoro paJesu. Johani aibata nyaya yakanga yakakosha panguva yake; asi mashoko ake euporofita, kunyange aidiwa pachiitiko chakati, achiri nemashoko akakosha kune zvizvarwa zvakazotevera.

`Ndima dzinotevera dzinotiudzei pamusoro paJesu? Johani 3:16; 2 VaKorinde 5:21; Johani 11:25; VaHebheru 4:14, 15; Johani 14:1–3.`

Vaporofita vechokwadi vanofanira kutenda kuti Jesu aiva Mwari akazova munhu, asi achiri munhu chaiye. Vanofanirawo kugamuchira chokwadi chakazara pamusoro paJesu—basa Rake, upenyu Hwake husina chivi, rufu Rwake rwekuyananisira, kumuka Kwake, basa Rake rehupristi mukuru kudenga, pamwe nekudzoka Kwake kwechipiri. Izvi zvinopfuura kungobvumirana mupfungwa kuti Jesu akararama panyika. Jesu anofanira kuva pakati pedzidziso dzemuporofita, kwete kungova chinhu chakawedzerwa. Mashoko ake anofanira kutungamirira vanhu kuupenyu hunoponesa naJesu.

Shoko reuporofita rakafemerwa noMweya Mutsvene ndiro chiedza chaMwari, chinodzinga rima riri matiri kusvikira Jesu, Nyeredzi yeMangwanani, abuda mumwoyo yedu; uye chipo chechiporofita chinotibatanidza Naye uye chinoramba chichitinongedzera kwaari.

Vaporofita vanosimudzira Jesu havazotsvaki kugadzira vateveri vavo kana kuzvisimudzira ivo pachavo (Mabasa 4:12). Vachava vakagadzirira kushumira, kwete kushandisa zvisina kunaka vaya vavatumirwa. Panguva imwe cheteyo, sezvatichaona mangwana, vaporofita vaMwari vaiva vanhu, uye sevamwe vedu vose, vatadzi vaida evhangeri.

`Sei kunzwisisa kuti Jesu, Mwari wakazara, akafa somutsivi wedu, achitakura murango wezvivi zvedu, kuri pakati pekutenda kwedu? Tinorasikirwa nei kana tikarasikirwa nedzidziso iyi?`
