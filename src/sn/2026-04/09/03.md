---
title:  Kuwirirana neBhaibheri
date:  23/11/2026
---

Kunyange isu vanhu tichiwanzopikisana nesu pachedu uye tichinyengera, Mwari haarevi nhema (ona Num. 23:19). Mashoko euporofita aanopa kubudikidza nevaporofita acharatidzawo kuwirirana uku. Kutendeka Kwake kusingachinji kuchaonekwa mumashoko euporofita aanopa.

`Verenga Isaya 8:20. Pfungwa ipi yakakosha iri kutaurwa pano?`

Bhaibheri nguva dzose ndiro chiyero chekupedzisira chechero munhu anoti akafemerwa. Vaporofita vanofanira kuedzwa nokufananidza zvavanotaura nezvinotaurwa neBhaibheri. Muporofita wechokwadi haazombopikisi, kuderedza, kana kubvisa pfungwa dzevanhu pachokwadi cheBhaibheri. Ndicho chikonzero kuziva Magwaro kwakakosha zvikuru kune avo vanoda kutevera Ishe uye kusanyengerwa nevadzidzisi venhema kana vaporofita venhema.

MuTestamende Yekare neItsva, vaporofita vakateverana vaiwanzosimbisa uye kunongedzera kuzvinyorwa zveuporofita zvakapfuura sechiyero chebasa ravo reuporofita. Vaporofita veBhaibheri vakazotevera vanoratidza kunzwisisa kutsva pamusoro pezvirongwa zvaMwari nenzira yeruponeso, asi havambopikisi misimboti nemafungiro makuru akatopiwa naMwari.

`2 Petro 1:19–21, VaRoma 15:4, na Jakobho 1:17 zvinotidzidzisei pamusoro paMwari nemashoko euporofita aakapa kwemazana emakore?`

Hwaro hweTestamende Yekare hwekunzwisisa chirongwa cheruponeso hwakaiswa muchiporofita chekutanga chakapiwa naMwari pachake, muna Genesisi 3:15. Hurongwa hwezvibayiro hwakaratidza chirongwa ichi, asi mashoko akadzama ekuti Gwayana raMwari, Jesu Kristu, raizouya sei, rararama sei, uye raizofira vanhu sei akajekeswa zvishoma nezvishoma muzviporofita zvakazotevera. Nhoroondo yeBhaibheri inoratidza kuti Mwari anoburitsa chokwadi apo vanhu Vake vanenge vava kukwanisa kuchinzwisisa. Izvi zvinoita kuti kunzwisisa kwechokwadi kudzame nechizvarwa chimwe nechimwe chitsva. Izvi ndizvo zvinorehwa nepfungwa yekuti “chokwadi chinoramba chichivhurika” kana kuti “chokwadi chinofambira mberi.” Kureva kuti, chizvarwa chakazotevera chinowanzova nekunzwisisa kwakadzama kwechokwadi chaMwari kupfuura chizvarwa chakapfuura.

Zvisinei, pane pfungwa yakakosha isingafaniri kumbokanganwikwa: kuratidzwa kutsva kwechokwadi hakuzombopikisi chokwadi chakambopiwa naMwari kare.
