title:  “Ravirai Mugoona”
date:  27/11/2026
---

Kubvuma kweSeventh-day Adventist chipo cheuporofita chaEllen G. White, kubva pakutanga kwacho kusvika panguva ino, kwakatarisana nematambudziko akafanana neaya akasangana nevaporofita vakawanda veBhaibheri. Mushure mechiratidzo chekutanga chaEllen G. White muna December 1844, vamwe vakabvunza chokwadi chezvaaiti nezvechipo chake cheuporofita. Izvi zvakaita kuti maAdventist aichengeta Sabata, avo vakazova maSeventh-day Adventist, vagadzire nharo dzaitsigira chipo cheuporofita chaEllen G. White.

Chekutanga, vakapa tsananguro yedzidziso inotsigira kuenderera mberi kwechipo chechiporofita kusvikira pakuguma kwenguva. Vakati Bhaibheri rinotsigira kuratidzwa kwechipo chechiporofita munguva yanhasi. Zvichienderana na Joere 2, Zvakazarurwa 12:17, nemamwe magwaro, chipo chechiporofita chaizoonekwa “mumazuva okupedzisira” uye chaizova “chiratidzo chinozivisa” vanhu vaMwari venguva yekupedzisira. Ona Gerhard Pfandl, “The Gift of Prophecy and God’s Remnant Church,” The Gift of Prophecy, chapter 4.

Chechipiri, maAdventist ekutanga akaona kuti chipo chaEllen G. White chaiva nesimba rakanaka pavatendi. Somuenzaniso, kunzwisisa kwake kweuporofita kwakabatsira kubatanidza vatendi vaipinda maSabbath Conferences ekuma1840 apo vaisawirirana pamusoro pedzidziso dzeBhaibheri. Chipo chake chakaramba chichibereka “zvibereko zvakanaka” mumakumi emakore akatevera, uye zvinyorwa zvake zvinoramba zvine simba mukati memhuri yekutenda yeSeventh-day Adventist.

Pungwa yechitatu yekutsigira Ellen G. White ine chekuita nezvinhu zvisinganzwisisiki zvaiitika muzviratidzo zvake. Paakanga ari muchiratidzo, sezvatakamboona, Ellen G. White airatidza zviratidzo zvisinganzwisisiki—achitaura zvinhu nemamiriro ezvinhu zvaainge asingazivi, achiratidza zviitiko zveramangwana, achiratidza simba rinopfuura remunhu, uye asina kugona kudzora kana kukanganisa nguva yaipinda kana kupera zviratidzo zvake, pakati pezvimwe.

Nhasi maSeventh-day Adventist anoramba achisimbisa zvinhu izvi mukutsigira chokwadi chechipo chaEllen G. White. Kunyange nharo idzi dzakasimba, “humbowo” hune simba hwechipo chaEllen G. White hunowanikwa muchiitiko chemunhu pachake nezvinyorwa zvake. Sezvakakurudzirwa nomunyori wePisarema Dhavhidhi: “Ravirai muone kuti Jehovha akanaka” (Mapisarema 34:8). Tinogona kuongorora zvirevo zvake zveuporofita chete kana tikaverenga zvinyorwa zvaEllen G. White nemunamato.

Imwe nzvimbo yekutanga ibhuku rake diki asi rinonyanya kuzivikanwa, Steps to Christ. Mushure maizvozvo unogona kutevera nemabhuku mashanu eConflict of the Ages series, ayo anotevera dingindira rehondo huru kubva kuna Genesisi kusvika kuna Zvakazarurwa. Zvinyorwa zvake zvinowanikwa zvakanyanya mumabhuku akadhindwa uye muformat yemagetsi (ona EGW Writings.org) uye zvakaturikirwa mumitauro yakawanda.
