---
title:  Mashoko Akanyorwa
date:  28/10/2026
---

Dzimwe hurukuro dzine mukurumbira dzakakanganisa zvikuru upenyu hwevanhu uye dzakatombochinja mafambiro enhoroondo. Dai hurukuro idzi dzisina kunyorwa pasi, mashoko acho angadai akapera kana kukanganiswa nekufamba kwenguva. Saka kunyora kunopa kugara kwenguva refu kushoko rakataurwa, uye kugara uku kunobvumira shoko kuti ripfuudzwe kubva kunguva kuenda kune imwe.

Nguva pfupi mushure mekunge vaIsraeri vabuda muIjipiti uye vakunda vaAmareki, Mwari akaraira Mosesi kuti “nyora izvi mubhuku kuti zvive chiyeuchidzo” (Eks. 17:14, NKJV). Kutanga naMosesi, uyo akanyora mabhuku mashanu ekutanga eBhaibheri na Mapisarema 90, Mweya Mutsvene akatungamirira vaporofita veMagwaro kuti vagadzire zvinyorwa zvinozova mabhuku 66 eBhaibheri.

`Verenga Ekisodho 17:14; Johani 20:30, 31; 1 VaKorinde 10:11; na Zvakazarurwa 1:19. Ndezvipi zvimwe zvikonzero zvakaita kuti Mwari akumbire vaporofita Vake kunyora pasi mashoko aakavapa?`

Mabhuku akasiyana eBhaibheri anoratidza manyorero neunyanzvi hwevanyori vawo. Sezvatichakurukura muchidzidzo chevhiki rinouya, kusiyana kwehunhu nemaitiro emunyori mumwe nomumwe hakubvisi simba remashoko avo, nokuti hapana “matanho” ekufemerwa. Zvimwe zvikamu zveBhaibheri zviri pachena kuti zvine chekuita zvakanyanya uye zvinoshanda zvakanyanya muupenyu hwedu nekutenda kwedu nhasi kupfuura zvimwe zvikamu. Asi chokwadi ichi hachiiti kuti chikamu chimwe cheBhaibheri chive chakafemerwa kupfuura chimwe.

Dzimwe nguva vanyori veBhaibheri vakanyora mashoko akafemerwa avakatanga kusvitsa nemuromo, ivo pachavo kana kubudikidza nemunhu wavaivimba naye. Mubhuku raDuteronomio, Mosesi akanyora hurukuro dzake dzekupedzisira dzaakapa kuvaIsraeri asati afa. Mabhuku mana eEvhangeri anochengeta mharidzo zhinji nedzidziso dzaJesu panguva yebasa Rake repanyika.

Asi pane zviitiko apo vaporofita vakatora mashoko kubva kuzvinyorwa zvisina kufemerwa (Mabasa 17:28; 1 VaKorinde 15:33; Tito 1:12) uye kunyange mashoko aSatani (Gen. 3:1–5; Mat. 4:1–11).

Tinofanira kurangarira kuti apo vaporofita vakanyora manzwiro nezvido zvevamwe vanhu, vakaratidzawo maitiro avo pachavo evanhu (ona Dan. 8:27; Zvakazarurwa 22:8, 9). Uyezve, Bhaibheri rinotsanangura mienzaniso yakawanda yakanaka yekutevedzera asiwo yakawanda yakaipa yekudzivisa (1 VaKorinde 10:1–11).

Patinenge tichiverenga Bhaibheri, tinofanira kurangarira kuti, kunyange risina kunyorwa mumutauro wakakwana, rinotakura shoko risingakanganisi richamira muedzo wenguva.
