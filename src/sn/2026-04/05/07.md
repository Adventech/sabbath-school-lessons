---
title:  Ellen G. White Pamusoro peKuratidzwa neKufemerwa
date:  30/10/2026
---

MaSeventh-day Adventist anotenda kuti Ellen G. White akafemerwa naMwari. Zvinyorwa zvake, upenyu hwake, nebasa rake zvinoenderana nezviratidzo zvemuporofita wechokwadi. Aiva nechiyero chimwe chete chekufemerwa sevaporofita veBhaibheri, kunyange hazvo asina kumbofunga kuti zvinyorwa zvake zvakaenzana neMagwaro. Kusiyana naizvozvo, Ellen G. White aigara achisimudzira Bhaibheri. Asi iye pachake ainzwisisa sei kuratidzwa kweuporofita kwaMwari kwaari uye hurongwa hwekufemerwa kwake?

Chekutanga, Ellen G. White aigara achiona Bhaibheri sekuratidzwa kwaMwari kune simba uye kusingakanganisi. Bhaibheri rinoratidza chokwadi chaMwari nechirongwa Chake cheruponeso kuvanhu vakawira muchivi. Muhurongwa ihwohwo, sezvatiri kudzidza, anoshandisa vaporofita kuti asvitse mashoko Ake.

Chechipiri, Ellen G. White akaziva kuti hurongwa hwekufemerwa hwaisanganisira kushanda pamwe chete kwaMwari nemunhu. Mwari aitaura mashoko Ake, asi muporofita aibva asvitsa kuraira kwaMwari achishandisa mashoko ake pachake. Sezvakataurwa naEllen G. White: “Hazvisi mazwi eBhaibheri akafemerwa, asi vanhu ndivo vakafemerwa. Kufemerwa hakushandi pamashoko emunhu kana matauriro ake, asi pamunhu pachake, uyo, pasi pesimba reMweya Mutsve ne, anozadzwa nemifungo. . . . Pfungwa nekuda kwaMwari zvinobatanidzwa nepfungwa nekuda kwemunhu; nokudaro mashoko emunhu anova shoko raMwari.”—Selected Messages, book 1, p. 21.

Maererano nezviratidzo nehope dzeuporofita, Ellen G. White ainzwisisa “kufemerwa kwemifungo” sekubatana kwekuratidzwa kwaMwari neruzivo rweupenyu hwemunhu. Akanyora kuti, “Bhaibheri, rine chokwadi charo chakapiwa naMwari chakataurwa mumutauro wevanhu, rinoratidza kubatana kwezvaMwari nezvevanhu.”—Selected Messages, book 1, p. 25. Mwari akashandisa nzira dzakawanda kuti agadzire kunzwisisa kweshoko Rake mupfungwa dzemutumwa Wake. Idzi dzaisanganisira mifananidzo, mazwi, nemifungo yaibata manzwiro ose emuporofita. Chiitiko chaEllen G. White chaive chakafanana nechevanyori veBhaibheri.

Chechitatu, Ellen G. White akacherechedza kuti hazvisi zvose zvaiitika muupenyu hwake semunhu kana kutaurirana kwake zvaiva zvakafemerwa. Akapa zano achiti, “Pane nguva apo zvinhu zvakajairika zvinofanira kutaurwa, mifungo yakajairika inofanira kugara mupfungwa, tsamba dzakajairika dzinofanira kunyorwa uye ruzivo runofanira kupiwa rwakabva kune mumwe mushandi kuenda kune mumwe. Mashoko akadaro, ruzivo rwakadaro, hazvipiwi pasi pekufemerwa kwakakosha kweMweya waMwari.”—Selected Messages, book 1, p. 39. Zvakakosha kuziva musiyano uyu patinoverenga uye patinonzwisisa zvinyorwa nezviitiko zvake, kunyanya tsamba nemadhayari ake.

Kuziva manzwisisiro aEllen G. White pamusoro pekuratidzwa nekufemerwa kunotibatsira kudzivisa maonero akanyanyisa uye kuverenga mazano ake nokuchenjera uye nokunzwisisa.
