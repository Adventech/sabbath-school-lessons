---
title:  Kuratidzwa Kwakajairika neKuratidzwa Kwakakosha
date:  25/10/2026
---

`Verenga VaHebheru 1:1, 2. Sei Mwari achifarira zvikuru kutaura nesu? Ari kutitaurirei kubudikidza naJesu nevaporofita?`

Musiki wezvose angada kutiudza chii? Sei achitaurirana nesu? Imhaka yekuti tiri vanhu vakawira muchivi munyika ine chivi uye tinosangana nerufu, kutambura, nemiedzo; naizvozvo, Mwari anotaura nesu nokuti anoda kuti tizive kuti sei tiri pano, kuti sei zvinhu zvakashata kudai, uye kuti sei, zvisinei nezvose izvi, tichigona kuva netariro murudo Rwake kwatiri.

Kuratidzwa kwakajairika kwaMwari, sekunoonekwa muzvisikwa, kunotaura nesimba pamusoro pesimba Rake risingaperi nerudo Rwake runotichengeta. Mambo Dhavhidhi akadanidzira achiti, “Denga rinoparidza kubwinya kwaMwari; uye nzvimbo yokudenga inoratidza basa ramaoko Ake” (Mapisarema 19:1, NKJV). Muapostora Pauro akawedzerawo achiti, “Nokuti kubva pakusikwa kwenyika, zvinhu zvake zvisingaoneki, iro simba rake risingaperi nehuMwari hwake, zvakaonekwa pachena, zvichinzwisiswa nezvinhu zvakaitwa, kuti varege kuva nepembedzo” (VaRoma 1:20, NASB).

Zvinoshamisawo kufungisisa pamusoro pesimba raMwari rekudzorera, rinoratidzwa munzira dzakavanzika dzekuporesa dziri munyika yedu. Chokwadi, “Simba raMwari rekuporesa rinomhanya muzvisikwa zvose. Kana muti ukatemwa, kana munhu akakuvara kana kutyoka pfupa, zvisikwa zvinotanga pakarepo kugadzirisa kukuvara ikoko. Kunyange dambudziko risati ravapo, nzira dzekuporesa dzinenge dzakagadzirira; uye pakangokuvara chikamu chimwe, simba rose rinotungamirirwa kubasa rekudzorera.”—Ellen G. White, Education, p. 113.

Kunyange nyika yezvisikwa yakanaka uye ichidzidzisa, hairatidzi chete simba nerudo rwaMwari, asiwo kuvapo kwehuipi (ona Gen. 3:14–19; Mat. 13:24–30). Shoko iri rine zviviri zvinoratidzika sezvinopesana rinogona kunzwisiswa zvakarurama chete muchiedza chekuratidzwa kwakakosha kwaMwari—muna Jesu Kristu, Shoko raMwari rakazova munhu, uye muBhaibheri, Shoko raMwari rakanyorwa rinopupura nezvake (Johani 5:39).

Magwa ro ndiwo chinyorwa chakafemerwa chemashoko aMwari kuvanhu vose mumazera ose. Anotiudza pamusoro peKusika kwenyika nekudonha kwayo kunosuruvarisa, uye pamusoro pechirongwa chinoshamisa chaMwari cherudzikinuro nekudzorerwa kwekupedzisira kwepasi rino. Kunyange dzimwe nyaya dzeBhaibheri dziri pfupi uye dzakasarudzwa, dzinogara dzakavimbika, nokuti Mwari ndiye ari kutaura nesu (ona 2 Timoti 3:16; 2 Petro 1:19–21).

`Kunyange zvisikwa zvichiratidza chokwadi chesimba nerudo rweMusiki, sei tichiri kuda zvakanyanya Shoko rakanyorwa? Shoko rinotiudzei izvo zvisikwa, pazvazvo, zvisingatiudzi?`
