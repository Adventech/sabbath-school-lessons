---
title:  Mashoko Akataurwa Nemuromo
date:  27/10/2026
---

`Verenga 2 Petro 1:18–21. Chivimbo chipi chatinacho chekuti mashoko akataurwa nemuromo nevaporofita vaMwari akanga akafemerwa uye akavimbika sezvakaita akanyorwa?`

Mwari akataura naAdhamu naEvha vasati vawa muchivi (Gen. 2:16, 17) uye mushure meKuwa (Gen. 3:9–19). Muzviitiko zvose izvi, vakanzwisisa zvakakwana mashoko Ake. Inzwi Rake rakanzwikawo zvakajeka paSinai (Eks. 20:1–20), pamwe chete pakubhabhatidzwa nekushandurwa kwaJesu pamberi pavo (Mat. 3:17; Mat. 17:5). Tinogona kusanzwisisa kana vamwe vanhu vachitaura nesu. Asi chokwadi ndechekuti kana Mwari achitaura nomunhu, hapazovi nekusanzwisisana kana kunyengera.

Bhaibheri rinotsigira kuti “Mwari akataura naMosesi akati kwaari: ‘Ndini Jehovha’ ” (Eks. 6:2, NKJV). Vanyori vakawanda veBhaibheri vakabvuma kuti vakanzwa inzwi raMwari paaitaura navo. Izvi zvakaitika, somuenzaniso, kuna Isaya (Isa. 8:1, 11), Jeremiya (Jer. 1:4, 9, 11), naEzekieri (Ezek. 3:16; Ezek. 6:1). Chero kuedza kuramba zvirevo izvi nezvimwe zvakafanana kunopedzisira kwava kubvunza kuvimbika kwevaporofita uye kuvimbika kweBhaibheri pacharo.

Sezvo Mwari akataura kuvaporofita mumutauro wevanhu unonzwisisika, saizvozvowo vaporofita vakataura kuvateereri vavo. Somuenzaniso, Noa aiva muporofita wechokwadi waMwari uye “muparidzi wokururama” akatendeka (2 Petro 2:5). Haana kunyora chikamu chipi nechipi cheBhaibheri, asi akasvitsa shoko raMwari nemuromo kuvanhu venguva yake, uye shoko rake rakanga risina kuderera mukufemerwa kupfuura zvinyorwa zvevaporofita vari muBhaibheri.

Vamwe vaporofita vakawanda, kusanganisira Natani, Eria, Erisha, naJohani Mubhabhatidzi, havana kunyora chikamu chipi nechipi cheBhaibheri; panzvimbo pezvo, vakasvitsa mashoko avo nemuromo.

Ko Jesu Kristu pachake? Nhoroondo yeEvhangeri inotaura kuti panguva yebasa Rake repanyika, “akanyora pasi nomunwe Wake” (Johani 8:6, NKJV). Ndizvozvo chete. Hatina chinyorwa chekuti Jesu akanyora chimwe chinhu. Asi akadzidzisa uye akaparidza nemuromo “semumwe ane simba” (Mat. 7:29, NKJV). Vamwe vevateereri Vake vakatobvuma vachiti, “Hakuna munhu akambotaura sezvinoita Munhu uyu” (Johani 7:40, 46, NKJV). Jesu ainyanya kuva mutauriri wemashoko aMwari nemuromo. Mashoko Ake akanga asina kuderera pasimba kana pakusungira nokuti Iye pachake haana kunyora mabhuku eBhaibheri.

`Zvinoshamisa sei kuti Mwari ane vaporofita vasina zvinyorwa muBhaibheri. Sei vaporofita ava vasiri veMagwaro matsvene vachitakurawo simba?`
