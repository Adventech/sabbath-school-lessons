---
title:  Mutambo, Zviratidzo, neMifananidzo
date:  29/10/2026
---

Bhaibheri rine mifananidzo yemashoko nemadimikira zvakawanda. Somuenzaniso, hurongwa hwose hwezvibayiro muTestamende Yekare—maaritari emadzibaba ekare, tende raMosesi, neTemberi yeJerusarema—hwaiva mufananidzo wairatidza chibayiro chaKristu chekuyananisira pamuchinjikwa uye basa Rake rehupristi mudenga (VaHebheru 8:1–6).

Muzviporofita zvaDanieri, humambo nemasimba enyika zvakaratidzwa nezvikamu zvakasiyana-siyana zvemufananidzo mukuru (Dan. 2:31–45), mhuka dzakasiyana-siyana nenyanga (Danieri 7 na8), uye madzimambo maviri (Danieri 11). Mifananidzo yakafanana inoonekwawo mubhuku raZvakazarurwa, semhuka mbiri dziri muchitsauko 13.

Mudzidziso Dzake, Jesu akazvitaura, somuenzaniso, seChingwa cheUpenyu (Johani 6:35, 41–58), Chiedza chenyika (Johani 8:12; Johani 9:5), uye Muzambiringa weChokwadi (Johani 15:1–8). Uye kubudikidza nemufananidzo wemudzvari (Mat. 13:1–9, 18–23) nemimwe mifananidzo yakawanda (Mat. 13:24–52, nezvimwe), Jesu akatsanangura chimiro nekukura kweumambo Hwake.

`Verenga Ezekieri 4:1–8. Sei uchifunga kuti Mwari haana kungotaura nemashoko chete, asi akaratidzawo mamwe mashoko Ake nenzira yezviitiko?.`

Mufananidzo une simba unoonekwa muna Ezekieri 4:1–8, apo Mwari akakumbira muporofita kuti agadzire mufananidzo mudiki weJerusarema uye avate pasi, kutanga nerutivi rwake rweruboshwe kwemazuva 390, achimiririra imba yaIsraeri, uyezve nerutivi rwake rwerudyi kwemazuva 40, achimiririra imba yaJudha. Chimwe chiitiko chinoonekwa ndechewanano yaHosea naGomeri, mukadzi aiva pfambi, yakarairwa naMwari, ichimiririra kusatendeka kwemweya kwaIsraeri kuna Mwari (Hos. 1:2–9).

Zviitiko zvinoratidzwa naEzekieri naHosea zvinoita sezvinotyisa nhasi. Asi ndizvo zvaiva chinangwa chazvo: zvaifanira kushamisa vanhu, kwete isu chete asiwo vanhu venguva iyoyo. Panguva iyo yambiro dzevaporofita dzairambwa pachena (ona 2 Makoronike 36:15–21), chiito chekupedzisira chengoni dzaMwari chaiva chekuyambira vanhu zvine simba pamusoro pemamiriro avo chaiwo kuitira kuti vavatungamire kukutendeuka kwechokwadi. Kuratidzwa kwakadaro nenzira ine simba kwaifanira kuvabatsira kuona kukomba kwavaifanira kuita yambiro yaMwari kwavari.

`Munhu angashandisa rudzii rwekuratidza nhasi kuti atiyambire pamusoro pekuva sevaRaodikia, semuenzaniso? Ona Zvakazarurwa 3:14–18.`
