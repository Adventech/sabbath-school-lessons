---
title:  Hurongwa hweKufemerwa
date:  26/10/2026
---

Mabhuku mazhinji nezvinyorwa zvakawanda, kwete Bhaibheri chete, zvakasvika kwatiri kubudikidza nemazera. Zvisinei, isu tinoona Bhaibheri seShoko raMwari. Chii chinoita kuti risiyane nezvimwe zvinyorwa?

`Verenga 2 Timoti 3:15–17 na Jakobho 1:17. Shoko ripi rakakosha ratinofanira kutora kubva mundima idzi?`

Pane kukurukurirana pamusoro pekududzirwa kwakanakisisa kwa 2 Timoti 3:16, asi shanduro zhinji dzeBhaibheri dzinopa ndima iyi nenzira yakafanana, dzichisimbisa kuti Bhaibheri rose rakabva kuna Mwari (2 Petro 1:20, 21).

Mumwe mududziri weBhaibheri anotsanangura kuti “zvinoita sokuti Pauro ari kufunga nezveRugwaro rwose pano, uye kuti angadai achiti Rugwaro rwose ‘runobatsira pakudzidzisa, pakutsiura, pakururamisa, nekurayira mukururama,’ pane kuti ndima imwe neimwe yeRugwaro inobatsira munzira idzi dzose.”—G. W. Knight III, The Pastoral Epistles: A Commentary on the Greek Text (Grand Rapids, MI: Eerdmans, 1992), p. 445.

Asi tinofanira kunzwisisa sei hurongwa hwekufemerwa hwakaburitsa Bhaibheri? Tinofanira kuziva kuti kufemerwa kweuporofita ndiko kubatsirwa kwevaporofita vechokwadi noMweya Mutsvene kuti vasvitse mashoko aMwari nenzira yakavimbika. Rubatsiro irworwo harupiwi chete pakugamuchira mashoko, asiwo pakusvitsa mashoko acho zvakajeka kune avo vanofanira kuagamuchira.

Mashoko aMwari akauya kubudikidza nezviratidzo nehope (Num. 12:6; Isa. 6:1, 8). Mwari akashandisawo zvapupu zvakaona nezvazvo (1 Johani 1:1–3), tsvakurudzo yenhoroondo yakafemerwa noMweya (Ruka 1:1–3), uye pfungwa dzakaiswa naMwari mupfungwa dzemunhu (2 Petro 1:21). Chero nzira yaishandiswa noMweya Mutsvene, vaporofita nevanyori veBhaibheri vakapiwa mashoko echokwadi aibva kuna Mwari, kwete pfungwa dzavo pachavo. Kureva kuti, mashoko aMwari aisafanira kuchinjwa kana kukanganiswa nenzira ipi neipi nevatumwa Vake, kunyange kana vakashandisa mazwi avo pachavo kutsanangura mashoko iwayo.

Basa rekusvitsa mashoko euporofita kuvateereri vekutanga raigona kuitwa nemuromo (2 Petro 1:19–21), kana nokunyora (Johani 20:30, 31), kana kunyange nokuratidza nenzira yezviitiko kana mutambo (Ezek. 4:1–8).

`Kunyange paine mibvunzo yakawanda yasara pamusoro pekuti Bhaibheri rakafemerwa sei, kana kuti kufemerwa kunoshanda sei kazhinji, sei zvakakosha zvikuru kuvimba neBhaibheri seShoko raMwari rakafemerwa, zvisinei nezvatisinganyatsonzwisisi pamusoro pekuti rakanyorwa sei?`
