---
title:  Tariro muUtapwa
date:  09/12/2026
---

“Uye Jehovha Mwari wamadzibaba avo akavatumira yambiro kubudikidza nevatumwa Vake, achimuka mangwanani-ngwanani achivatuma, nokuti aiva netsitsi kuvanhu Vake uye kunzvimbo Yake yokugara” (2 Makoronike 36:15, NKJV).

Mwari akatuma vaporofita vazhinji mushure maEria kuti vanyaradze, vayambire, uye vadzidzise vanhu Vake, asi humambo hwaIsraeri nehwaJudha hwakawira muuipi hwakadaro zvekuti Mwari akavienzanisa neSodhoma neGomora (Isa. 1:9, 10). Vanhu vaMwari vakanga vava kuzvikudza, kuva nemakaro, uye kunamata zvifananidzo. Vaidzvinyirira vashandi vavo, chirikadzi, nherera, nevatorwa vaiuya kwavari kuzotsvaga rubatsiro. Ona Isaya 58 kuti uone mhinduro yaMwari kukukundikana kwaIsraeri. Pakupedzisira, kutongwa kwaMwari kwakaita kuti Israeri itapwe neAsiriya (2 Madzimambo 17:1–6) uye Judha iendeswe kuutapwa kunyika yeBhabhironi (2 Madzimambo 24, 25).

Kunyange Mwari akaranga vanhu Vake, haana kuvasiya vari voga muutapwa. Akaramba achivatumira vaporofita, vakadai saEzekieri naDanieri, kuti avape tariro yeramangwana.

`Verenga Isaya 59:1–4 na Nehemia 9:30. Chii chiri kutaurwa pano chinoshanda mumazera ose?`

Munhoroondo yavo yose, humambo hwaIsraeri nehwaJudha hwakatambura migumisiro yechivi. Pakutsauka kubva kuna Mwari, vakasiya kuchengetedzwa kwavaiva nako pasi pekutarisira Kwake uye vakazvikonzera kutongwa Kwake. Mwari haashiviriri kudzvinyirira uye kunamata zvifananidzo, asi haana kuvatuma kuutapwa kekutanga pavakatadza. Mwari akashanda nevanhu Vake, achivakumbira kubudikidza nevaporofita Vake kuti vatendeuke uye vadzokere kwaari. Vanhu vakaramba kuteerera. Chokwadi, zvinoshamisa kufunga kuti Ishe akatsvaga kwenguva yakareba sei kudzosa vanhu Vake kubva muzvivi zvavo. Kwemazana emakore, kubudikidza nemuporofita mushure memuporofita, achipa yambiro mushure meyambiro, Mwari akatsvaga kusvika kwavari.

Jesu pachake akazvitaura zvakanakisisa achiti, “Jerusarema, Jerusarema, iwe unouraya vaporofita uye unotema nematombo avo vakatumwa kwauri! Kangani ndakada kuunganidza vana vako pamwe chete, sehuku inounganidza nhiyo dzayo pasi pemapapiro ayo, asi hamuna kuda!” (Mat. 23:37, NKJV).

Asi kunyange vaiva muutapwa, Mwari haana kumbosiya vanhu Vake vasina tariro. Zvisinei nekukundikana kwavo kwakadzokororwa, Mwari akavimbisa kuti, “Mudzikinuri achauya kuZioni, uye kune avo vanotendeuka kubva pakudarika munaJakobho” (Isa. 59:20, NKJV).

`Funga pamusoro pekutendeka kwaMwari kunyange Israeri payakatsauka kubva kwaari. Waona sei chokwadi chechikamu ichi chehunhu hwaMwari hwerudo muupenyu hwako? Ungadai uri kupi dai asina kuva nemwoyo murefu uye rudo rwakadai?`
