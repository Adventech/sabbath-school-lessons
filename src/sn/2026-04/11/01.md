### Verenga zveChidzidzo cheVhiki Ino
2 Petro 2:5; Vatongi 4:4–16; 1 Madzimambo 18:21, 38, 39; Isa. 59:1–4; Neh. 9:30; Zvakazarurwa 1:1–3.

> <p>Ndima Yekuyeuka:</p>
> “Zvirokwazvo Ishe Jehovha haaiti chinhu, asina kutanga azivisa chakavanzika Chake kuvaranda Vake vaporofita” (Amosi 3:7, NKJV).

Kubvira paKuwa, vanhu vakagara vaine chido chekuzvisarudzira mafaro echivi kwechinguvana, panzvimbo peupenyu husingaperi munaJesu. Mwari anoziva chokwadi ichi chinosuruvarisa, asi kunyange zvakadaro anotida uye anoshanda kuti tiponeswe.

Ndicho chikonzero, panguva dzakakosha munhoroondo, Mwari akashanda nenzira dzinoshamisa kubudikidza nebasa reuporofita achiitira vanhu Vake. Zvisinei, kana kuedza Kwake kwarambwa kakawanda, akayambira nezvekutongwa.

Kubudikidza nevaporofita Vake, Ishe vanogara vachizivisa zvavachaita vasati vazviita (Amosi 3:7). Kana vanhu vakatendeuka, anokurumidza kuregerera.

Anoramba achitaurirana nesu nenzira imwe cheteyo nanhasi. Saka zvine musoro kuti Mwari ataure nevanhu Vake mumazuva okupedzisira epasi, sezvaakataura nevanhu Vake mumazana emakore apfuura.

Chidzidzo chevhiki rino chichatarisa kumashure panguva dzakakosha munhoroondo yeruponeso apo Mwari akashandisa vaporofita kuti ajekese kuda Kwake kuvanhu Vake uye kunyika yose. Tichaongororawo basa reushumiri hwakafemerwa hwaEllen G. White panguva dzakakosha mukukura kwesangano reAdventist.

_*Dzidza chidzidzo chevhiki rino kugadzirira Sabata, Zvita 12._
