---
title:  Kudanwa Kukutendeuka
date:  08/12/2026
---

`Verenga Vatongi 2:16–19. Muenzaniso upi, kana kuti maitiro api, anoonekwa pano?`

Pakuchinja kukuru kuenda kuhutongi hwemadzimambo, uye chikumbiro chaIsraeri chekuda mambo (1 Samueri 8), nguva yevatongi yakapera. Asi shanduko iyi yakauyisa kudiwa kukuru kwevaporofita kuti vape zano, varuramise, uye vatsiure mambo, vatungamiri verudzi, nevanhu.

Munguva yekutonga kwaAhabhi, Mwari akatuma Eria neshoko rekutongwa: hakwaizova nemvura nokuti Israeri yakanga “yasiya mirayiro yaJehovha uye yakatevera vaBhaari” (1 Madzimambo 18:18, NKJV). Panzvimbo pekutendeuka, Ahabhi akaedza kuvhima Eria kuti amuuraye, asi Mwari akamudzivirira (1 Madzimambo 18:10–12). Pakazosvika gore rechitatu renzara, Eria paakazouya kuna mambo, Ahabhi akamupomera mhosva yekushaikwa kwemvura (1 Madzimambo 18:17)!

Ipapo Eria akaunganidza Israeri yose kuitira kukakavadzana kwekupedzisira pakati paMwari nevaporofita vaBhaari (1 Madzimambo 18:19).

`Verenga kuzivisa kwaEria kuna Israeri nemhinduro yavo muna 1 Madzimambo 18:21, 38, 39. Chii chakaitika?`

Chiito chaMwari chemoto wakaburuka kubva kudenga, pamwe neshoko reuporofita raEria, zvakabata vanhu vaMwari nenzira huru uye zvakavatungamirira kukutendeuka. Sezvo vaIsraeri vaizeza kuzvipira zvizere kuna Mwari, isuwo tinorarama mutsika dzisingagamuchiri Mwari seMusiki neMudzikinuri. Panzvimbo pekushanda sezvapupu zvaMwari zvinokurudzira vamwe kuva vakatendeka kwaari, tinowanzotiza kudanwa kwedu nokutevera zvido zvedu, sezvakaitwa naIsraeri.

Mwari anoyambira kuti kutevera zvido zvedu kuchatungamirira kukuputsika, kurwadziwa, uye pakupedzisira rufu. Asi kana tikateerera inzwi Rake, ipapo “Jehovha achakutungamirira nguva dzose, agogutsa mweya wako panguva yekuoma, agosimbisa mapfupa ako; uchava sebindu rinodiridzwa, uye setsime remvura, rine mvura isingaperi” (Isa. 58:11, NKJV).

`Hunhu hwedu hwenyama hunotisundira kupandukira Mwari, kunyange tisingazvioni. Ndezvipi zvisarudzo zvatinofanira kuita kuti tisakundwe nehunhu ihwohwo?`
