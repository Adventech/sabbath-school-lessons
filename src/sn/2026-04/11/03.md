---
title:  Mai muIsraeri
date:  07/12/2026
---

Mushure mekunge Mwari anunura vaIsraeri kubva muuranda muIjipiti, akavapa nyika yeKenani. Akavimbisa kuvakomborera zvikuru kana vakatevera mitemo nemirayiro Yake. Akayambirawo kuti kutyora sungano yavo Naye kwaizounza kutambura nekuparadzwa (Deuteronomio 27–30). Mushure mekufa kwaJoshua, vaIsraeri vakatsauka kubva pakuchengeta sungano. Vakabva vadzvinyirirwa nemarudzi aiva pedyo navo uye vakachema kuna Mwari. Mukupindura, “Jehovha akamutsa vatongi vakavanunura kubva mumaoko evaya vaivapamba” (Vatongi 2:16, NKJV). Akatumirawo vaporofita kuti vakurudzire vaIsraeri kuvandudza sungano yavo naMwari uye kuti avape simba pamusoro pevadzvinyiriri vavo.

`Verenga Vatongi 4:4–16 na Vatongi 5:1–5. Dhebhora akaitirei Israeri?`

Dhebhora akaita basa rakakosha reuporofita mukusvitsa shoko raMwari kuna Israeri uye kuna Bharaki, mutungamiri wemauto erudzi, paakanga achiunganidza varwi. NdiDhebhora akapa Bharaki chiratidzo chekuti atange hondo, achiti, “Simuka! Nokuti iri ndiro zuva rapiwa Sisera muruoko rwako naJehovha. Ko Jehovha haana kubuda pamberi pako here?” (Vatongi 4:14, NKJV).

Semutongi muIsraeri, Dhebhora aiva munhu akakosha. Hutungamiri hwake nekutenda kwake muvimbiso yaMwari yekukunda zvakasimbisa vaIsraeri muhondo yavo nevavengi vavo. Unyanzvi hwaBharaki semutungamiri wehondo uye kuda kwake, kunyange aizeza, kutungamirira uto raIsraeri kwaivawo kwakakosha, asi handizvo zvakakunda hondo.

Iri pamwe ndiro shoko rinonyanya kukosha munyaya yemuporofitakadzi Dhebhora nemurwi Bharaki. Pakati pekupemberera kukunda kunoshamisa, vanhu vane simba zvikuru muIsraeri—mutongi nemutungamiri wehondo akakunda—vakatungamirira vanhu kukudza Mwari. Uku ndiko kutungamirira kwemweya kwechokwadi.

Basa reuporofita raDhebhora rinopupura kutendeka kwaMwari munguva dzekutambudzika nekudzvinyirirwa. Mwari paakamupa shoko raBharaki, haana kubvunza kuti zvaibvira here kuunganidza varwi vakakwana, uye haana kupokana achiti vaIsraeri vaiva vasina simba rekukunda. Panzvimbo pezvo, akasvitsa shoko rake nechivimbo uye akakurudzira Bharaki kuti abvise kusahadzika kwake uye avimbe nekutarisira kwaMwari.

`Tingadzidza sei kuvimba naMwari kana, panguva yatiri mairi, zvinhu zvisingaoneki zvine tariro?`
