---
title:  Yambiro yeNjodzi
date:  06/12/2026
---

Mwari anoda kuponesa vanhu. Kunyange mushure meKuwa, akatsvaga hukama naAdhamu naEvha, uye kwenguva yakati vagari venyika vakadana “zita raJehovha” (Gen. 4:26). Sezvo mazera aipfuura, nhamba yevaya vaifamba naMwari yakaderera. Avo vakaramba vachitevera Mwari vakapupura pamusoro pekunaka Kwake, vakashora zvivi zvevaivapoteredza, uye vakayambira pamusoro pemigumisiro yezviito zvavo.

Pakazosvika nguva yaNoa, zvakadaro, vagari vepasi vakanga vava vakaipa zvekuti Mwari akasarudza kuvaparadza (Gen. 6:5–7). Noa, muparidzi wokururama (2 Petro 2:5), pasina mubvunzo akayambira vose vaizomuteerera pamusoro pekuparadzwa kwaizouya. Mwari akapawo Noa mirayiridzo yekuvaka chikepe cheruponeso, areka, kuitira kuponesa vose vaizoteerera yambiro dzake votendeuka.

`Verenga VaHebheru 11:7, 2 Petro 2:5, na Genesisi 6:9. Nenzira dzipi Noa aiva “muparidzi wokururama”?`

Mafashamo aiva nguva yakakosha zvikuru munhoroondo yevanhu, uye nyaya yacho inodzokororwa kakawanda muTestamende Yekare neTestamende Itsva semuenzaniso wekudzika kwekuipa kwevanhu uye kukwirira kwenyasha dzinoshamisa dzaMwari. Kururama kwaMwari kunoratidzwa mukutonga Kwake pamusoro pezvisikwa zvakazadzwa nechivi uye mukununura Kwake vose vaizogamuchira ruponeso.

“Pakati pehuori hwakanga hwazara, Metusera, Noa, nevamwe vazhinji vakashanda kuti vachen getedze ruzivo rwaMwari wechokwadi uye kuti vamise mafungu ehuipi hwetsika. Makore zana nemakumi maviri Mafashamo asati auya, Ishe vakazivisa kuna Noa kubudikidza nengirozi tsvene chinangwa Chavo, uye vakamuraira kuvaka areka. Paakavaka areka, aifanira kuparidza kuti Mwari aizounza mafashamo emvura panyika kuti aparadze vakaipa. Avo vaizotenda shoko iri, uye vaizogadzirira chiitiko ichocho kubudikidza nekutendeuka nekuvandudzwa, vaizowana ruregerero uye vaizoponeswa.”—Ellen G. White, Patriarchs and Prophets, p. 92.

Mwari haana kungoudza Noa pamusoro peMafashamo uye kuponesa vaiva muareka chete; akasimbisawo sungano naNoa nezvisikwa zvose, achivimbisa kuti haazomboparadza nyika zvakare nemvura (Gen. 9:8–17). Muraraungu wese une vimbiso iyoyo, netariro yerudzikinuro.

`Nguva inotevera paunoona muraraungu, funga pamusoro pevimbiso irimo. Sei uchifanira kukunyaradza, kuziva kuti Mwari anochengeta zvivimbiso Zvake?`
