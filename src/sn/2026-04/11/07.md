title:  Inzwi reChiporofita
date:  11/12/2026
---

Mwari agara achitaurirana nevanhu kubudikidza nevaporofita Vake. Izvi zvaiva zvechokwadi zvikuru panguva dzakakosha munhoroondo. Saka hazvina musoro here kutarisira kuti Mwari achataura nevanhu Vake pakuguma kwenguva?

Apo maMillerite Adventist vakasangana ne“Great Disappointment” musi waOctober 22, 1844, Mwari akadana Ellen Harmon, uyo akazova Ellen White, kubasa reuporofita uye akamupa shoko revatendi vakanga vaodzwa mwoyo. Chiratidzo chake chekutanga chaiva chekuvanyaradza mukusuwa kwavo.

Imwe nguva yakakosha yaiva kutanga kwezvinyorwa zveAdventist uye kurongwa zviri pamutemo kwevaAdventist vaichengeta Sabata kuva sangano. Kubudikidza nezvinyorwa zvavo nebasa reevhangeri, maSabbatarian aya, kusvika kuma1860, akanga akura kusvika kunhengo zviuru zvinoverengeka. Vaida kurongwa kuti vazivise mashoko engirozi nhatu (Zvakazarurwa 14:6–12) nenzira inoshanda zvakanyanya. Kunyange vamwe vaipokana nepfungwa yekurongwa, Mwari akashandisa Ellen G. White kukurudzira danho iri. Musi waMay 21, 1863, Seventh-day Adventist Church yakazvarwa zviri pamutemo.

Mugore iroro rimwe chete, Mwari akashandisa chipo chechiporofita kukurudzira kuvandudzwa kweutano pakati pevatendi. Vazhinji veSeventh-day Adventist vakanga vasina hutano hwakanaka kana mararamiro ane hutano. Kubudikidza nezviratidzo zviviri zvakazara zveutano zvaEllen G. White muna 1863 na1865, nzira iyi yakachinjwa. MaSeventh-day Adventist vakava vatsigiri veutano nemararamiro ane hutano.

Kubva kuma1870 kusvika kuma1890, Ellen G. White akava munhu akakosha mukuvakwa kwehurongwa hwedzidzo yeAdventist. Mafungiro ake edzidzo yakazara, kureva dzidzo yepfungwa, yemaoko, neyemweya, akagamuchirwa zvishoma nezvishoma uye akashandiswa. Sezvaakataura: “Dzidzo yechokwadi inoreva zvakawanda kupfuura kungotevera chirongwa chekudzidza. Inoreva zvakawanda kupfuura kugadzirira upenyu hwazvino. Ine chekuita nemunhu wose, uye nenguva yose yekurarama inobvira kumunhu. Iko kukura kwakabatana kwemasimba emuviri , epfungwa, neemweya.”—Education, p. 13.

Muma1880, akaita basa rakakosha mukuunza kuenzanisa mudzidziso dzeSeventh-day Adventist pamusoro peruponeso. Akasimbisa kukosha kwenyasha dzaMwari nekururama nokutenda mukati mekusimbisa kwakasiyana kweAdventist pamusoro pemurayiro. Kubva muna 1901 kusvika 1903, akabatsira kuronga patsva chimiro chesangano kuitira kuti basa rekereke kunyika riitwe zviri nani.

Muchidimbu, pangadai pasina sangano reSeventh-day Adventist sezvatinoziva nhasi pasina basa raEllen G. White. Aiva mutumwa waMwari panguva dzakakosha.
