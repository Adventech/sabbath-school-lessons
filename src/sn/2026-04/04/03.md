---
title:  Vaapostora neVaporofita
date:  19/10/2026
---

` Testamende Itsva inotaura nezvebasa revaporofita nevaapostora. Vaapostora vaiva “vatumwa,” kana kuti “vakatumwa kubva kuna Mwari,” sezvinotsanangurwa nezwi rechiGiriki apostolos. Mumabhuku eEvhangeri, shoko rokuti “muapostora” rinoreva vadzidzi gumi nevaviri. Mamwe mabhuku eTestamende Itsva anotsigirawo pfungwa iyi uye anosanganisira vamwe vanhu vasiri pakati pevadzidzi vekutanga vaKristu.

Pauro anozviti muapostora ka12 mutsamba dzake. Muna VaRoma 1:1, 2, anobatanidza kudanwa kwake kweuapostora, uye zvaiparidzwa naye, nemashoko evaporofita veTestamende Yekare. Pauro anozviratidza semuapostora kuvaHedheni (VaRoma 11:13). Akagadzwa “nokuda kwaMwari” (1 VaKorinde 1:1).

Kusiyana nevapristi vaitevera mutsara wemhuri kubva kuna baba kuenda kuvanakomana, Mwari ndiye aigadza vaporofita nevaapostora maererano nekuda Kwake. Apo uapostora hwaPauro hwakanga huchipikiswa, akadzivirira kudanwa kwake (1 VaKorinde 9:1, 2). Pauro akaona uapostora hwake sehwaivaka pamusoro pebasa raKristu, Hwaro hwekereke (VaEfeso 2:20). Sevanhu vakagamuchira kuratidzwa kwaMwari, vaapostora vaiva nechiyero chimwe chete chekufemerwa sevaporofita (VaEfeso 3:1–5).

Muna 2 Petro 3:1, 2, Petro anoreva vaporofita sevaya vakauya kare uye mashoko avo akanga akakosha, asi zvino vaapostora vanoratidzwa sevane simba rimwe chete. Mundima idzi, panotaurwa nezvenguva dzeTestamende Yekare neItsva. Kunyange zvazvo vaporofita vachionekwa sevatumwa vaMwari venguva yeTestamende Yekare uye vaapostora sevatumwa vaMwari venguva yeTestamende Itsva, kwaivawo nevanhu vaiporofita kana kushandisa chipo chechiporofita asi vasiri vaapostora. Chipo chechiporofita hachina kugumira kuvaapostora munguva yeTestamende Itsva. Uye havasi vaporofita vose vaiva vaapostora (Mabasa 15:32).

`Vaapostora nevaporofita vanoshanda sei maererano naKristu nekereke, maererano na VaEfeso 2:20 na VaEfeso 3:5?`

Sezvatinozivawo nekuverenga zvakanyorwa nevaapostora muTestamende Itsva, Jesu ndiye aiva pakati pezvinenge zvose zvavainyora. Nenzira imwe kana imwe, upenyu Hwake, rufu Rwake, kumuka Kwake, basa Rake rehupristi mukuru, uye kudzoka Kwake ndizvo zvaiva pakati pemashoko avo.

`Nhoroondo yepamutemo chete yatinayo yemashoko evaapostora nevaporofita pamusoro paJesu, upenyu Hwake, nemabasa Ake, iBhaibheri. Izvi zvinofanira kutidzidzisei pamusoro pekuti sei Bhaibheri richifanira kuva nesimba rekupedzisira pane zvatinotenda pamusoro paJesu nerufu Rwake nokuda kwedu?`
