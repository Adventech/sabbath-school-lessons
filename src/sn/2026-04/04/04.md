---
title:  Mabasa eVaporofita veTestamende Itsva
date:  20/10/2026
---

`1 VaKorinde 14:3, VaEfeso 4:11–13, na Zvakazarurwa 22:6 zvinotiudzei pamusoro pebasa revaporofita veTestamende Itsva?`

Ndima idzi dziri pamusoro, kunyange dzichibatsira, hadzipi mufananidzo wakazara wezvose zvaiitwa nevaporofita veTestamende Itsva. Zvisinei, zvakawanda zvavaiita zvinoratidza zvaiitwa nevaporofita veTestamende Yekare, nokudaro zvichiratidza kuenderera mberi kwechipo chechiporofita.

Muna 1 VaKorinde 12:10, chiporofita chinotaurwa pakati pezvimwe zvipo zvemweya. Mumufananidzo wemuviri waKristu (1 VaKorinde 12:27, 28), vaporofita vanotaurwa mushure mevaapostora, avo vakagamuchirawo kuratidzwa kubva kuna Mwari. Maererano na 1 VaKorinde 14:3: “Anoporofita anotaura kuvaka, kukurudzira, nokunyaradza vanhu” (NKJV).

VaEfeso 4:11–13 inonyorawo boka rakafanana rezvipo zvemweya, sezvinoita 1 VaKorinde 12. Vaporofita vanotaurwa pamwe nevaapostora, vaevhangeri, vafudzi, nevadzidzisi (VaEfeso 4:11). Vanhu ava varipo “kugadzirira vatsvene kubasa reushumiri, kuvaka muviri waKristu, kusvikira tose tasvika pakubatana kwekutenda nokuziva Mwanakomana waMwari, kuva munhu akakwana, pachiyero chekuzara kwaKristu” (VaEfeso 4:12, 13, NKJV). Sezvinoonekwa pano nekumwewo, Jesu ndiye ari pakati pemashoko avo.

Bhuku raMabasa rinotaura nezvevaporofita vaviri, Judhasi naSirasi, avo vasina kunyora bhuku muBhaibheri, asi “vakakurudzira nokusimbisa hama nemashoko mazhinji” (Mabasa 15:32, NKJV). Hongu, dzimwe nguva vaporofita vanofanira kutaura yambiro, kunyange yambiro dzakasimba. Asiwo, zvichienderana nemamiriro ezvinhu, vanopa mashoko ekukurudzira, kunyanya panguva dzakaoma, mashoko angangove akafarirwa zvikuru nevaya vaida kuanzwa pamwe netariro nevimbiso dzaaiva nadzo.

Basa rekukurudzira rinotaurwawo muchirevo chekuporofita (1 VaKorinde 14:31, NKJV). Munguva dzekutambudzika nekushushwa, chipo chechiporofita chave chiri sosi yekukurudzira netariro.

Muna Zvakazarurwa 22:6, Jesu anoratidzwa sa“Ishe Mwari wevaporofita vatsvene” uye ane “mashoko akatendeka uye echokwadi” (NKJV). Chokwadi, chero chinhu chinobva kuvaranda vaMwari, kana vachitaura vachimiririra Mwari, chichava “chakatendeka uye chechokwadi.” Munyika umo matisingagoni kuvimba nezvakawanda, tinogona zvechokwadi kuvimba nezvinotaurwa nevaporofita vaIshe, kunyanya muShoko Rake, simba redu rekupedzisira uye guru.
