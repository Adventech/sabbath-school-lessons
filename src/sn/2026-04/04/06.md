---
title:  Vaporofita Venhema muTestamende Itsva
date:  22/10/2026
---

`Verenga Mateo 24:23, 24. Jesu anotiyambira nezvei pano? Sei tichifanira kutora yambiro iyi zvakanyanya?`

Jesu pachake akataura zvakajeka zvikuru: “Nokuti vanaKristu venhema nevaporofita venhema vachamuka, vachiratidza zviratidzo zvikuru nezvishamiso kuti vanyengere, kana zvichibvira, kunyange vakasarudzwa” (Mat. 24:24, NKJV).

Kunyengera kwaanoyambira nezvako kunofanira kuva kune simba zvikuru, kana chokwadi “zviratidzo zvikuru nezvishamiso” zvichava chikamu chazvo. Zviitiko zvemweya zvisinganzwisisiki, pazvazvo, hazvisi humbowo hwekushanda kwaMwari, kunyange zvichigona kuoneka zvinogutsa. Tine vavengi vemweya vane simba risingaonekwi. Sezvatakanyeverwa naPauro: “Nokuti hatirwi nenyama neropa, asi nemasimba makuru, nemasimba, nevatongi verima renyika ino, nemweya yakaipa iri munzvimbo dzokudenga” (VaEfeso 6:12, NKJV). Uye masimba aya anoshanda kubudikidza nevaporofita venhema.

Muchokwadi, vaporofita venhema havasi chinhu chitsva. Testamende Itsva inotaura nezvevaporofita venhema vemunguva dzeTestamende Yekare (Ruka 6:26). MuTestamende Itsva, vaporofita venhema vanofananidzwa “nemapere anoparadza” ari “munguvo dzamakwai” (Mat. 7:15, NKJV), uye tinoudzwa kuti “muchavaziva nezvibereko zvavo” (Mat. 7:16, NKJV).

Basa ravo guru kunyengera (Mat. 7:17–20). Jesu pachake akazivisa kuti “vaporofita venhema vazhinji vachamuka vachinyengera vazhinji” (Mat. 24:11, NKJV), uye “kana zvichibvira, kunyange vakasarudzwa” (Mat. 24:24, NKJV; onawo Mako 13:22; 1 Johani 4:1).

`Maererano na VaEfeso 6:10–18, tingazvidzivirira sei kubva kumhando ipi neipi yekunyengera?`

Kurarama nokutenda, kupfeka “chidzitiro chechipfuva chekururama,” kusungwa “nechokwadi,” pamwe nekuzvipira kuMweya Mutsvene kubudikidza nemunamato, uye kusimbiswa muShoko Rake—idzi ndidzo nzira dzatingadzivirirwa nadzo kubva pane zvose zvaizotitungamirira mukutsauka pamweya nepachidzidzo.

`Chii chaungaita kuti uzvishongedzere zviri nani pakurwisana nekutsveyamisa kwose kuri kufukidza nyika yedu?`
