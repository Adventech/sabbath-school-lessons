---
title:  Kuchengeta Jesu neShoko Rake Vari Pakati
date:  23/10/2026
---

Sezvatadzidza, Mwari akadana vaporofita panguva dzakasarudzika uye akavapa mabasa akakosha. Basa ravo guru raiva rekutungamirira vanhu kuti vazive Mwari uye vave muukama hwekutendeka Naye. Izvi ndizvo zvaiva hwaro hwebasa raEllen G. White. Basa rake raiva nezvinangwa zvikuru zviviri: kutungamirira vanhu kuna Jesu uye kuvanongedzera kuShoko Rake—Bhaibheri.

Ellen G. White aiva nerudo rukuru kuna Jesu kweupenyu hwake hwose uye chido chekugovera rudo rwaMwari nevamwe. Mabhuku ake mashanu ane mukurumbira, The Conflict of the Ages, anotanga uye anopera nemashoko ekuti “Mwari rudo.” Mubhuku Steps to Christ, pamwe ndiro bhuku rake rinonyanya kuzivikanwa, akanyora kuti: “Kukura kwedu munyasha, mufaro wedu, kubatsira kwedu—zvose zvinoenderana nekubatana kwedu naKristu. Kubudikidza nekudyidzana Naye, zuva nezuva, awa neawa—nokugara maAri—ndiko kwatinofanira kukura munyasha. Haasi chete Muvambi, asiwo Mupedzisi wekutenda kwedu.”—Steps to Christ, p. 69.

Muna1890, paakanga achishanda paThe Desire of Ages, akanyora mudhayari rake kuti: “Pakunyora pamusoro peupenyu hwaKristu ndinobatwa zvakadzama. Ndinokanganwa kufema sezvandinofanira. Handigoni kutsungirira kusimba kwemanzwiro anondibata pandinofunga nezvezvakatambudzwa naKristu munyika yedu.”—Manuscript 70, 1897. Ellen G. White aida zvikuru kutaura nezvaJesu.

Bhaibheri ndiro raiva chinhu chechipiri chakanyanya kukosha mubasa raEllen White. Akaona zvinyorwa zvake se“chiedza chidiki” chinotungamirira vanhu ku“chiedza chikuru”—Bhaibheri (Selected Messages, book 3, p. 29). Aigara achikurudzira vanhu kuti vaende kuMagwaro, umo Mwari akazivisa hunhu Hwake hwerudo nechirongwa cheruponeso. Sezvaakataura pakutanga kwebasa rake: “Ndinokurudzirai, muverengi anodiwa, Shoko raMwari semutemo wekutenda kwenyu nemaitiro enyu.”—Selected Messages, book 3, p. 30.

Makore akazotevera akanyora kuti: “Ishe vanoda kuti mudzidze maBhaibheri enyu. Haana kupa chiedza chipi nechipi chekuwedzera kuti chitore nzvimbo yeShoko Rake. . . . Kana [Shoko Rake] rikadyiwa nekugayiwa, rinenge ropa roupenyu hwemweya. Ipapo mabasa akanaka achaonekwa sechiedza chinopenya murima.”—Letter 130, 1901.

Ellen G. White aitendawo kuti vose vanoti vane nguo yeuporofita yaKristu vanofanira kudzidzisa “zvakadzidziswa naKristu. Zvaakataura, kwete chete ari pano pachake, asi kubudikidza nevaporofita nevadzidzisi vose veTestamende Yekare, zvinosanganisirwa pano. Dzidziso yevanhu inorambidzwa.”—The Desire of Ages, p. 826.

Ellen G. White akachengeta Jesu neMagwaro zviri pakati pechiitiko chake chechiKristu. Chishanu chinotevera, tichaongorora maonero ake pamusoro pekuratidzwa nekufemerwa.
