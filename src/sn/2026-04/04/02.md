---
title:  Jesu seMuporofita
date:  18/10/2026
---

Mosesi akafanotaura kuti, “Jehovha Mwari wako achakumutsira Muporofita akafanana neni kubva pakati pako, kubva kuhama dzako. Iye ndiye wamuchateerera” (Deut. 18:15, NKJV). Muporofita uyu aiva ani? Rugwaro pacharwo runozvijekesa: rwaireva Jesu pachake. “Nokuti Mosesi akati kumadzibaba, ‘Jehovha Mwari wenyu achakumutsirai Muporofita akafanana neni kubva kuhama dzenyu. Iye ndiye wamuchateerera pazvinhu zvose zvaachataura kwamuri’ ” (Mabasa 3:22, NKJV; onawo Johani 6:14).

`Basa reuporofita raJesu nderei? Mubvunzo uyu wakapindurwa sei munguva yaJesu? Ona Mat. 16:13–16; Mako 8:27–29; Ruka 9:18–20.`

Jesu, saKristu, Mesia, ndiye Uyo akanongedzerwa kwaari nevaporofita vose veTestamende Yekare. MuTestamende Itsva, Jesu anozivikanwa sekuzadziswa kwezviporofita zvavo pamusoro paMesia. Muna Ruka 24:13–35, anozviratidza saIye akanongedzerwa nezviporofita zvaMesia.

Muchokwadi, muna Johani 1:1–5, tsananguro inopiwa pamusoro peShoko haikwani kumunhuwo zvake kana kumuporofitawo zvake. Shoko rinoratidzwa seMusiki, nokuti “zvinhu zvose zvakaitwa kubudikidza Naye, uye pasina Iye hapana chinhu chakaitwa pane zvakaitwa” (Johani 1:3, NKJV). Kureva kuti, Ndiye akasika zvose zvakasikwa, kureva nyika yose, iyo zvino inofungidzirwa kuva yakafara nemakore echiedza anenge mabhiriyoni 93.

Mukati me“uye Mwari akati” yose iri muna Genesisi 1, tinonzwa Jesu achitaura. Iye akati, “Chiedza ngachivepo” (Gen. 1:3), ndiye zvakare “chiedza chevanhu” (Johani 1:4), uye kubudikidza nechirongwa cheruponeso (Johani 1:4, 5), akanga achibvisa rima renyika.

Mashoko aMwari euporofita ane chokwadi uye akasimbiswa. Kana achiteererwa, ari “chiedza chinovhenekera munzvimbo ine rima, kusvikira mambakwedza asvika uye nyeredzi yemangwanani yabuda mumwoyo yenyu” (2 Petro 1:19, NKJV). Chiporofita chicharamba chichiunza chiedza munyika yedu ine chivi kusvikira Jesu, “Nyeredzi yeMangwanani,” uye Sosi yechiedza icho cheuporofita, aunza umambo Hwake husingaperi.

Kusvikira panguva iyoyo, kubudikidza neupenyu Hwake, rufu Rwake, uye kumuka Kwake, Kristu anotipa tose upenyu husingaperi. Mubvunzo chete ndewekuti tichahugamuchira here kana kuti kwete.

`Fungisisa chokwadi chekuti Jesu, Musiki (Johani 1:1–3), ndiye zvakare Uyo akafa pamuchinjikwa nokuda kwezvivi zvako. Chokwadi ichi chinoshamisa chinotidzidzisei pamusoro pechokwadi cherudo rwaMwari kwatiri?`
