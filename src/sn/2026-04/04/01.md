### Verenga zveChidzidzo cheVhiki Ino
Mat. 16:13–16; VaEfeso 2:20; Zvakazarurwa 22:6; Ruka 2:36–38; Mabasa 21:9; VaEfeso 6:10–18.

> <p>Ndima Yekuyeuka:</p>
> “Kune marudzi akasiyana-siyana ezvipo, asi Mweya mumwe chete. Kune marudzi akasiyana-siyana emabasa, asi Ishe mumwe chete. Uye kune marudzi akasiyana-siyana ekushanda, asi ndiMwari mumwe chete anoshanda zvose muzvose” (1 VaKorinde 12:4–6, NKJV).

Vamwe baba pavakanga vachityaira, mwanakomana wavo aitarisa mota dzaipfuura achitsvaga dzakanga dzakafanana neyababa vake. Ipapo baba vakamunongedzera imwe mota vakati, “Mwanangu, iyo yakanga yakafanana nemota yangu yekutanga!” Pavakasvika kumba, baba vakaburitsa bhuku remirayiridzo remota yavo yekutanga pamwe neremota yavo yazvino. Vakanyatsoratidza mwanakomana wavo zvinhu zvaive pamota imwe neimwe, vachiratidza zvakanga zvakafanana nezvakanga zvachinja. Asi pavakaenzanisa mainjini emota mbiri idzi, zvinhu zvose zvaive zvakangofanana. Zvakawanda zvakanga zvachinja, asi injini yakanga isina kuchinja.

Mwari akataura kubudikidza nevaporofita muTestamende Yekare neTestamende Itsva, uye kuenderera mberi nekubatana kwemashoko avo kunokonzerwa nekuti Mweya Mutsvene—, kana kuti “Injini,” yekufemerwa kutsvene—akaramba ari mumwe chete. Mweya Mutsvene nguva dzose anosimbisa vaporofita vechokwadi kubudikidza nechipo chechiporofita. Anoisa zvakare Kristu pakati pemashoko evaporofita, sezvinoratidzwa pachena mubhuku raVaHebheru (VaHebheru 1:1–3).

Vhiki rino tichaongorora chipo chechiporofita muTestamende Itsva, tichitarisa zvakaitwa nevaporofita, kuti ndivanaani vakashanda sevaporofita, kuti vaporofita vaienzaniswe sei nevaapostora, uye njodzi yevaporofita venhema.

_*Dzidza chidzidzo chevhiki rino kugadzirira Sabata, Gumiguru 24._
