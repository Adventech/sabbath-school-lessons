---
title:  Kuverenga Ellen G. White
date:  20/11/2026
---

Tinofanira kuverenga sei Ellen G. White? Heano mamwe mazano ekunzwisisa nokushandisa zvakarurama zvinyorwa zvaEllen G. White.

Chekutanga, tanga nemunamato, nepfungwa dzakanaka uye dzakavhurika, kwete nemweya wekusahadzika. Mafungiro edu igirazi rinopenda zvose zvatinonzwa, zvatinoverenga, nezvatinoona. Tinoda Mweya Mutsvene kuti atungamirire kufunga kwedu. Sezvakataurwa naEllen G. White pachake: “Kana mukatsvaga Magwaro kuti mutsigire mafungiro enyu, hamuzombosviki pachokwadi. Tsvagai kuti mudzidze zvinotaurwa naIshe.”—Christ’s Object Lessons, p. 112. Izvi zvinoshandawo pazvinyorwa zvake.

Chechipiri, funga mamiriro ezvauri kuverenga. Vanhu vakawanda vakavakira kunzwisisa kwavo zvinyorwa zvaEllen G. White pamutsara wakatorwa woga kana chikamu cheparagraph chakabviswa mumamiriro acho enhoroondo kana echinyorwa. Ellen G. White akanyora kuti “nguva nenzvimbo zvinofanira kufungidzirwa.”—Selected Messages, book 1, p. 57. Maitiro akaipa aya ekuregeredza mamiriro ezvinyorwa akakonzera, uye achiri kukonzera, matambudziko asingaperi kune vamwe nhengo dzekereke.

Chechitatu, tarisa pamisimboti. Pfungwa huru, kana pfungwa huru dziri kusimbiswa naEllen G. White muchikamu chauri kuverenga ndedzipi? Somuenzaniso, muna 1894 Ellen White akanyorera nhengo dzekereke muBattle Creek, Michigan, achishora kutengwa kwemabhasikoro (ona Testimonies for the Church, vol. 8, pp. 50–53). Musimboti waida kusimbiswa naEllen White waiva hutungamiri hwakanaka hwezviwanikwa zvakapiwa naMwari, zvakadai semari; panguva iyoyo mabhasikoro aidhura zvikuru.

Chechina, dzidza zvose zvakanyorwa naEllen G. White pamusoro penyaya imwe. Ongorora mashoko akaoma kunzwisisa muchirevo chedzidziso yake yakafara panyaya iyoyo. Panguva yeupenyu hwake, Ellen G. White akachema pamusoro pekushandiswa zvisizvo kwezvinyorwa zvake achiti: “Zvakawanda zvinonzi ishoko kubva kuna Sister White zvinongoshandira chinangwa chekureva zvisiri izvo pamusoro paSister White, zvichiita sokunge ari kutsigira zvinhu zvisingaenderani nepfungwa kana kutonga kwake.”—Selected Messages, book 1, p. 44.

Pakupedzisira, iva nechokwadi chekuti Ellen G. White ndiye akazvitaura. Pane mashoko mazhinji anonzi akataurwa naEllen G. White asi asiri ake. Nzira yakachengeteka yekuziva kuti zvatiri kuverenga zvinobva kuna Ellen White ndeyekutsvaga reference yesosi yacho. Kana tangosimbisa kuti zviri kupi, tinogona kuzviongorora tichishandisa misimboti yakarurama.

Takapiwa chipo chinoshamisa muzvinyorwa zvechiporofita zvaEllen G. White. Zvakakosha zvikuru kuti tiverenge zvinyorwa izvi nenzira yakarurama, nokuti zvinogona kuva—uye zvave zviri—ropafadzo kumamiriyoni. Zvinorwadza kana zvikashandiswa zvisizvo kana kududzirwa zvisizvo.
