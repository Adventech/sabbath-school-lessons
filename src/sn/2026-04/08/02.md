---
title:  Bhaibheri, Mududziri Waro Pacharo
date:  15/11/2026
---

Kududzira kwatinoita Magwaro kunonyanya kukanganiswa nezvatinotanga tafungidzira kana kutenda. Avo vanoona Bhaibheri sekungova muunganidzwa wezvinyorwa zvekare havakwanisi kuona kubatana kuri pakati pemabhuku aro akasiyana. Asi kana tikafunga musimboti mukuru wekuti “Rugwaro rwose rwakafemerwa naMwari uye runobatsira” (2 Timoti 3:16, NASB), tinogona kuona kubatana kwarwo kwemukati uye kubvumira Bhaibheri kuti rizvidudzire pacharo.

`Verenga Mateo 5:17–48. Jesu akatsanangura sei dzidziso dzeTestamende Yekare dzinotaurwa muchikamu ichi?`

Kuverenga Mateo 5:17–48 pamusoro-pamusoro kunogona kupa pfungwa yekuti Jesu akanga achitsiva dzidziso dzeTestamende Yekare nedzidziso Dzake itsva. Asi kuverenga nokungwarira kunoratidza kuti Jesu akanga achiburitsa zvakadzama, zvemweya, zvaive muTestamende Yekare. Muchirevo ichi, akanga achizadzisa vimbiso ya Jeremia 31:31–33 yekuti pasi pesungano itsva murayiro waMwari waizoiswa mupfungwa uye kunyorerwa pamwoyo yevanhu Vake (enzanisa na VaHebheru 8:8–10; VaHebheru 10:16).

Dzimwe nguva kududzirwa kwechikamu cheMagwaro kunopiwa mubhuku rimwe chete umo chikamu chacho chinowanikwa. Somuenzaniso, muna Danieri 2, tinowana tsananguro yehope dzinoshamisa dzaMambo Nebhukadhinezari (Dan. 2:31–35), yobva yateverwa nekududzirwa kwehope idzodzo (Dan. 2:36–45). Saizvozvowo, muna Mateo 13 tine mufananidzo wemudzvari (Mat. 13:1–9), wobva wateverwa nekududzirwa kwawo (Mat. 13:18–23).

Dzimwe nguva kududzirwa kweshoko kunopiwa nemuporofita akazouya gare gare. Somuenzaniso, 2 Makoronike 36:22, 23 inosimbisa kuzadziswa kwechiporofita chemakore 70, sezvakanga zvafanotaurwa muna Jeremia 25:11, 12 na Jeremia 29:10. Zvisinei, mifananidzo mizhinji yeTestamende Yekare yakazadziswa muna Kristu, uye zvikamu zvakawanda zveTestamende Yekare zvinodudzirwa muTestamende Itsva.

“Testamende Yekare ivhangeri mumifananidzo nezviratidzo. Testamende Itsva ndiyo chinhu chacho chaicho. Imwe neimwe yakakosha zvakafanana.”—Ellen G. White, Selected Messages, book 2, p. 104. Chokwadi, Testamende Itsva ndiyo mududziri akafemerwa weTestamende Yekare.

`Sei zvakakosha kuziva zvatinofungidzira patinodzidza Bhaibheri? Sei fungidziro yedu yakakosha zvikuru ichifanira kuva yekuti tinovimba naro seShoko raMwari uye kuti ndiro rinofanira kutitonga, kwete isu kuritonga?`
