---
title:  Mamiriro eChinyorwa
date:  17/11/2026
---

Imwe nyaya inowanzotaurwa nemuparidzi inoti pane murume akanga akatarisana nematambudziko makuru uye aida kuziva kuda kwaMwari kwaari. Akavhura Bhaibheri rake, akanongedzera munwe wake zvisina kurongwa kuna Mateo 27:5, inoti, “Uye [Judhasi] akaenda akazvisungirira” (NKJV). Ashamiswa nendima iyi, akadzokorora nzira imwe cheteyo, achiedza kuwana shoko rinomukurudzira, asi munwe wake ukazomira pana Ruka 10:37: “Ipapo Jesu akati kwaari, ‘Enda unoita saizvozvowo’ ” (NKJV). Ndima dzose idzi ndima dzechokwadi dzeMagwaro, asi dzinofanira kunzwisiswa mumamiriro adzo.

Chokwadi, kukanganiswa kwakawanda kwemashoko eBhaibheri kunobva patsika yakajairika yekubvisa ndima mumamiriro echinyorwa chadzo. Maitiro aya anovhura Magwaro kumadudziro akagadzirwa nevanhu asingabvi muchokwadi.

`Verenga Genesisi 3:1–5 na Mateo 4:1–11. Satani akatsveyamisa sei mashoko aMwari nokuaashandisa kunze kwemamiriro awo?`

Vamwe vanhu vanotsveyamisa shoko reBhaibheri vasina chinangwa chakaipa, nokuda kwekusaziva kwavo. Asi vamwe vanozviita nemaune, sezvakaitwa naSatani muzviitiko zviviri zvataurwa mumagwaro ari pamusoro. Chero chikonzero chacho chingava chipi, mugumisiro wacho unogara uchikuvadza.

MuBindu reEdeni (Gen. 3:1–5; enzanisa na Zvakazarurwa 12:9), Satani akaonekwa kuna Evha senyoka yakanaka uye inoda kuziva, inokwanisa kutaura mutauro wevanhu. Achitsveyamisa mashoko aMwari kubva mumamiriro awo ekutanga, Satani akatanga nokuaunganidza zvachose, obva apa kududzirwa kwake kwekunyengera.

Apo Jesu akatungamirirwa noMweya Mutsvene kuenda murenje (Mat. 4:1–11; Ruka 4:1–13), “Satani akauya sengirozi yechiedza murenje rekuyedzwa kuti anyengere Kristu.”—Ellen G. White, in The Advent Review and Sabbath Herald, December 18, 1888. Mukukurukurirana kwake naKristu, Satani akashandisa zvisizvo Mapisarema 91:11, 12 kuti areve sokunge Mwari aizodzivirira kunyange vaya vanozviisa nemaune mumamiriro ane ngozi.

Zviitiko zveBindu reEdeni nezvekuedzwa murenje zvinoratidza kuvapo kwehondo huru, iyo inosanganisirawo kududzirwa kweShoko raMwari, rakataurwa uye rakanyorwa. Zvinofanira kutibatsira kuona kukosha kwekuchengeta kunzwisisa kwedu ndima dzeBhaibheri kwakavimbika kumamiriro adzo.

`Saka, sei tichifanira kudzivisa muyedzo wekubvisa ndima mumamiriro adzo kuti tiedze kuratidza pfungwa dzedu, kunyange kana pfungwa dzedu dzakarurama?`
