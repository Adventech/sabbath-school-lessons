---
title:  Kutsanangura Bhaibheri
date:  19/11/2026
---

Kudzidza kwedu Bhaibheri kunofanira kutarisa pazvinangwa zvikuru zviviri: kukura kwemweya kwedu pachedu uye kutungamirira vamwe kuupenyu hunoponesa munaJesu. Kristu akatsanangura muKristu wechokwadi semunhu anotungamirirwa noMweya Mutsvene “muzvokwadi yose” (Johani 16:13), anorarama “neshoko rose rinobuda mumuromo maMwari” (Mat. 4:4, NKJV), uye anodzidzisa vamwe “zvinhu zvose” zvaakaraira (Mat. 28:20).

`Verenga Ruka 4:16–30 na Ruka 24:25–27, 44–49. Tingadzidzei kubva munzira yakatsanangura Kristu Magwaro pano?`

Jesu Kristu aiva, achiri, uye acharamba ari “nzira, chokwadi, noupenyu” (Johani 14:6, NKJV). Kunyange zvakadaro, kuparidza Kwake kwakanga kwakadzika midzi muMagwaro. Somuenzaniso, Mako 1:14, 15 inoti, “Zvino mushure mekunge Johani aiswa mujeri, Jesu akauya kuGarirea, achiparidza evhangeri youmambo hwaMwari, achiti, ‘Nguva yazadziswa, uye umambo hwaMwari hwaswedera’ ” (NKJV). Nguva iyi yaireva pachena Danieri 9:24–27. MuMharidzo yePagomo (Mateo 5–7), akatora mashoko kubva kuna Ekisodho 20:13 na14 (Mat. 5:21, 27). Muna Ruka 4, akatora mashoko kubva kuna Isaya 61:1, 2. Jesu aiva muparidzi akadzika midzi muBhaibheri zvakakwana.

Mumwe muenzaniso uri muna Mabasa 8, umo ngomwa yeEtiopia “yakanga yauya kuJerusarema kuzonamata” (Mabasa 8:27, NKJV), zvinoreva kuti aiva neruzivo nechitendero chevaJudha uye zvimwe zvemaitiro acho. Aitova nekopi yebhuku raIsaya, iyo yaiverenga nechido parwendo rwake kudzokera kumba. Asi aisanzwisisa chiporofita cheMuranda anotakura zvivi, saka akatsvaga munhu kuti amutsanangurire.

Chiitiko ichi chinotidzidzisa zvidzidzo zvishoma zvakakosha. Chekutanga, kuverenga chikamu cheMagwaro chete hakugari kuchitungamirira pakunzwisisa kwakadzama kwechikamu ichocho. Chechipiri, mukudzidza Magwaro tinogona, uye tinofanira, kudzidza kubva kune mumwe nomumwe. Chechitatu, tine basa remunhu mumwe nomumwe rekugovera mashoko eBhaibheri akarurama nevamwe.

Kutsanangura kwaFiripi bhuku raIsaya nezvimwe zvikamu zveTestamende Yekare kwakaita kuti ngomwa ibhabhatidzwe (Mabasa 8:35–39; enzanisa na Mapisarema 51:12). Kutsanangura kwedu Magwaro kunofanira kuva nechinangwa chimwe chetecho (ona Johani 17:17).

`Kunyange dzimwe nguva tingada rubatsiro pakunzwisisa ndima, sei tichifanira kugara tiine chokwadi mupfungwa dzedu pamusoro pezvatinotenda?`
