---

Chiitiko nezvinyorwa zvaEllen G. White zvinoenderana nezvakawanda zvatadzidza vhiki rino pamusoro pevatumwa vaMwari muBhaibheri. Iye,wo, akagamuchira basa rakapiwa naMwari rekuti anyore zvaakanga aratidzwa.

Achifungisisa muna 1909, Ellen G. White akanyora kuti, “Nokuda kwetsaona yakanga yoda kunditorera upenyu, ndakanga ndava neutano husina kusimba uye ndisingagoni kunyora. . . . Asi humwe husiku ngirozi yaIshe yakauya pamubhedha wangu ikati kwandiri, ‘Unofanira kunyora zvinhu zvandinokupa.’ Ndakati, ‘Handigoni kunyora.’ Murayiro wakapihwa zvakare, ‘Nyora zvinhu zvandinokupa.’ Ndakafunga kuti ndiedze, ndikatora bhodhi diki kubva patafura, ndikatanga kunyora, ndikawana kuti ndaigona kunyora mashoko zviri nyore.”—In General Conference Bulletin, May 31, 1909.

Sevanyori veMagwaro vakafemerwa, Ellen G. White akashandisawo vabatsiri pakunyora. Vanyori vake vaiita basa rinotarisirwa kuitwa nevapepeti—kubatsira munyori kutaura mashoko ake zvakajeka vasina kuisa mifungo yavo pachavo. Vakanga vasiri ghostwriters kana vanyori pamwe naye. Basa ravo raibva pazvinhu zvakagadzirwa naEllen G. White pachake, zvingava zvakanyorwa neruoko kana zvakataurwa nemuromo.

Ellen G. White akatorawo uye akagadzirisa zvinhu kubva kune zvimwe zvinyorwa mukunyora kwake kuti zvimubatsire pakupa mashoko ake. Nokukurudzira vaverengi vake kuverenga mamwe emabhuku aakashandisa, zviri pachena kuti aisava nechinangwa chekunyengera kana kutorera vanyori ivavo zvisina kururama, asi akawana mumutauro wavo mashoko akakodzera ekusvitsa chokwadi chaaida kutsanangura.

Ko pamusoro pezvinoratidzika sekusawirirana kana kusarurama?

“Pamusoro pekusakanganisa,” akanyora, “handina kumboti ndinako; Mwari oga ndiye asingakanganisi.”—Selected Messages, book 1, p. 37.

Munhu anogona kusangana nemamwe mashoko muzvinyorwa zvake akaoma kunzwisisa kana kuyananisa nemamwe. Hunhu hwevanhu hwaEllen G. White hahuna kubviswa nekudanwa kwake kutsvene. Saizvozvo nesu tose, Ellen White aikura uye aikura pakunzwisisa, kukura uku kuchiratidzwa muzvinyorwa zvake.

Sezvatotaurwa, asi zvakakosha kudzokorora, akatsiurawo avo vaitenda kuti shoko rimwe nerimwe raakanyora rakabva kudenga zvakananga. “Pane nguva apo zvinhu zvakajairika zvinofanira kutaurwa, mifungo yakajairika inofanira kugara mupfungwa, tsamba dzakajairika dzinofanira kunyorwa uye ruzivo runofanira kupiwa rwakabva kune mumwe mushandi kuenda kune mumwe. Mashoko akadaro, ruzivo rwakadaro, hazvipiwi pasi pekufemerwa kwakakosha kweMweya waMwari.”—Selected Messages, book 3, p. 58.
