### Verenga zveChidzidzo cheVhiki Ino
Eks. 17:14; Isa. 13:1; Jer. 36:17, 18; VaRoma 16:22; Ruka 1:1–4; Judha 14, 15; 2 VaKorinde 4:7.

> <p>Ndima Yekuyeuka:</p>
> “Nokuti zvinhu zvose zvakanyorwa kare zvakanyorerwa kudzidza kwedu, kuti kubudikidza nokutsungirira nokunyaradzwa kweMagwaro tive netariro” (VaRoma 15:4, NKJV).

Ungafarira kudzidziswa rwiyo rwakanyorwa naMwari pachake here? Muna Deuteronomio 32, mashoko erwiyo rwacho akanyorwa pasi naMosesi achitungamirirwa naMwari (Deut. 31:19–22). Kazhinji hatifungi nezvenziyo patinofunga nezvezvinyorwa zvechiporofita, kunyange hazvo mazhinji emaPisarema ari nziyo dzakafemerwa, kunyange kana mashoko acho asina kutaurwa zvakananga naIshe pachake.

Bhaibheri rinotsanangura nzira huru nhatu dzakashandiswa nevaporofita kusvitsa mashoko aMwari: nemuromo;nokunyora; kubudikidza nezviito zvezviratidzo kana kuratidza nenzira yemutambo. Vhiki rino tichatarisa zvakanyanya zvinyorwa zviri muMagwaro. Bhaibheri rinonyora Shoko raMwari, sezvarakataurwa kubudikidza nemashoko evatumwa Vake, kusanganisira mharidzo nematauriro evaporofita.

Funga nezvekusiyana-siyana kwemanyorero nemhando dzezvinyorwa zvechiporofita: nyaya, nhoroondo, nhetembo, kuchema, netsamba, kungotaura zvishoma chete. Varume nevakadzi vakasarudzwa vakaisa mumutauro wevanhu zvavakatungamirirwa noMweya Mutsvene kuti vataure. Vhiki rino tichatarisa manyorero avo, kushandiswa kwevabatsiri pakunyora, uye zvinhu zvinofanira kurangarirwa patinodzidza zvikamu zvakaoma kunzwisisa.

_*Dzidza chidzidzo chevhiki rino kugadzirira Sabata, Mbudzi 7._
