---
title:  Kudanwa Kwekunyora
date:  01/11/2026
---

Nzira yekutanga-tanga yakashandiswa naMwari pakusvitsa mashoko kuvanhu yaiva yekutaura zvakananga nemuromo, semanzwi ainzwika, kana hope, kana ngirozi. Hazvina kusvika panguva yaMosesi kuti Rugwaro runyore murayiro waMwari wekutanga pamusoro pezviitiko izvi, wokuti zvinotaurwa naMwari zvinyorwe mu“bhuku.”

`Kutaurwa kwekutanga kwekunyora muBhaibheri ndekupi? Verenga Ekisodho 17:14.`

MuTestamende Yekare tinowana mienzaniso yakawanda apo Mwari akapa mirayiridzo kuvatumwa Vake kuti vachengete mashoko Ake nokuda kwevanhu Vake. SaMosesi, Jeremiya akaudzwa kuti, “Tora mupumburu webhuku unyoremo mashoko ose andakataura kwauri pamusoro paIsraeri, napamusoro paJudha, napamusoro pendudzi dzose, kubvira pazuva randakataura newe, kubva pamazuva aJosia kusvikira nhasi” (Jer. 36:2, NKJV).

Akapihwawo chikonzero chekuti sei aifanira kunyora zviporofita izvi: “Zvichida imba yaJudha ichanzwa zvakaipa zvose zvandinofunga kuvauyisira, kuti mumwe nomumwe atendeuke panzira yake yakaipa, kuti ndivakanganwire kuipa kwavo nechivi chavo” (Jer. 36:3, NKJV). Zviporofita zvakapiwa kuti zvitungamirire vanhu kuruponeso, kwete kungogutsa chido chekuziva nezveramangwana.

Vatumwa vaMwari vakanyora vari mumamiriro akasiyana-siyana zvikuru. Mosesi akanyora mabhuku mashanu ekutanga eBhaibheri ari murenje. Dhavhidhi akanyora mamwe emaPisarema ake achivhimwa naSauro (ona Mapisarema 52, 54, 57, 59). Danieri akanyora zviratidzo zvake achishanda mumatare eBhabhironi neMedo-Persia. Kune rumwe rutivi, Pauro akanyora tsamba dzake kuvaEfeso, vaFiripi, vaKorose, naFiremoni ari mujeri, uye Johani akanyora Zvakazarurwa ari muutapwa kuPatimosi. Zviri pachena kuti kana vatumwa vaMwari vakasundwa kuti vanyore, vaisimbiswa noMweya Mutsvene zvisinei nemamiriro avo ezvinhu.

Zvinofadza kuti dzidziso dzose dzaJesu dzakachengetedzwa dzinosvika kwatiri dziri muchimiro chakanyorwa, kubudikidza nevanhu vakasarudzwa naMwari kuti vavimbiswe chokwadi ichocho chakakosha. Izvi zvinotiratidza kuvimba kwaMwari kuti mashoko matsvene aizonyorwa uye kuchengetedzwa nokutendeka nevatumwa Vake.

`Ungapindura sei munhu anoti hatigoni kuvimba neBhaibheri nokuti hatina zvinyorwa zvekutanga chaizvo? Sei pfungwa iyoyo iri isina simba?`
