---
title:  Shoko raMwari neKusakwana kweVanhu
date:  05/11/2026
---

`Pauro anoti chii pamusoro pepfuma yaMwari muna 2 VaKorinde 4:7?`

Pauro anonyora nezvepfuma yaMwari seyakaiswa “mumidziyo yevhu,” kureva vanhu, kuitira kuti simba raMwari rionekwe richipfuura kusasimba kwedu. Rugwaro runoratidza kuti, sezvo vatumwa vaMwari vasina kudzivirirwa nenzira inoshamisa kuti vasakundikana muupenyu hwavo pachavo—Mosesi, Eria, Dhavhidhi, Jona, naPetro, somuenzaniso—saizvozvowo zvinyorwa zvavo hazvina kubvisirwa kusakwana kunobva pakunzwisisa nekutaura kwevanhu.

“Kubatana kwezvaMwari nezvevanhu, kunoratidzwa munaKristu, kunowanikwawo muBhaibheri. Zvokwadi dzakaziviswa dzose ‘dzakapiwa nekufemerwa naMwari’; asi dzakataurwa mumashoko evanhu uye dzakagadzirirwa zvinodiwa nevanhu.”—Ellen G. White, Testimonies for the Church, vol. 5, p. 747.

Hunhu hwevanhu muvatumwa vaMwari hahubviswi nekudanwa kwavo kutsvene kwekusvitsa pfuma yechokwadi, sezvakanyorwa naPauro muna 2 VaKorinde 4:7. Zvinoitawo sokuti Mweya Mutsvene haana kuona zvakakodzera kudzivirira vatumwa Vake kubva mukutaura kana kunyora mashoko atinowana nhasi akaoma kuyananisa kana kutsanangura. Kunyange ndima dzakadaro dziri shoma, munhu ane pfungwa nhete ndiye anogumburwa nadzo. SevaKristu vanotenda Bhaibheri, tinofanira kuzviisa pasi peShoko raMwari kusvika pachiyero chatinorinzwisisa, uye kuvimba naIshe kunyange pane zvatisinganzwisisi.

Zvimwe zvingaonekwa sekusawirirana zvinogona kukonzerwa nezvikanganiso zvidiki zvisina kukosha zvakaitwa nevanyori vekopi vekare, kana zvinyorwa zvakasiyana-siyana zvakashandiswa nevanyori vakafemerwa. Kana tichifunga nezvezvikamu zvinonetsa, tinofanira kuzvibvunza kana tanyatsonzwisisa zvinorehwa nemashoko emunyori, uye kana kudzidza kwakadzama kungagadzirisa dambudziko racho.

Kunyange kana tisingakwanisi kugadzirisa chose chose kukanganisa kunoratidzika kana kusawirirana kwose, tinoona kuti hazvikanganisi musimboti weshoko riri kupiwa. Tinogona kuva nechivimbo chekuti chokwadi chaMwari cheMagwaro chakaratidzwa nokutendeka, zvisinei nekusakwana kwevatumwa vaMwari.

`Sei uchifunga kuti Mwari akasarudza kushandisa vanhu vanokanganisa panzvimbo pengirozi dzisina chivi sevashumiri Vake vakuru vekusvitsa mashoko?`
