---
title:  Mhando dzeManyorero neMaitiro
date:  02/11/2026
---

Bhaibheri rine mhando dzakasiyana-siyana dzekunyora dzakashandiswa nevatumwa vaMwari vakafemerwa. Dzimwe dzakafanana nekunyora mudhayari (ona Num. 33:1–49). Kune rumwe rutivi, mabhuku aDanieri, Zakariya, naJohani ane mienzaniso yekunyora kweapocalyptic, kunozivikanwa nezviratidzo zvikuru, nyaya dzine chekuita nedenga nepasi rose, uye tsananguro dzezvisikwa zvekudenga, pakati pezvimwe.

`Verenga Isaya 13:1. Bhaibheri pano rinoreva sei shoko riri kupiwa naIsaya?`

Zviratidzo zvakagamuchirwa nevaporofita dzimwe nguva zvinonzi “oracles”—kureva mashoko kana kuratidzwa kunobva kuna Mwari; dzimwe shanduro dzinodudzira izwi iri se“mutoro.” Shanduro itsva dzeBhaibheri dzinoratidza kuti mazhinji acho akanyorwa muchimiro chenhetembo, achiwanzoshandisa parallelism. Parallelism ndipo panodzokororwa pfungwa nemitsara inotevera kana dzimwe nguva ichipikisana nemitsara yapfuura. Somuenzaniso: “Nezuva iro munhu achatarira kuMusiki wake, uye meso ake achakudza Mutsvene waIsraeri” (Isa. 17:7, NKJV). “Musiki” na“Mutsvene” zviri kufambirana, mufananidzo mumwe nomumwe uchitipa rumwe ruzivo pamusoro peumwe.

Vaporofita vakashandisawo nzira dzemanyorero dzakajairika dzakadai semadimikira, kureva zviratidzo, uye hyperbole, kureva kuwedzeredza kwekutaura. Kuti tinzwisise zviri nani uye tishandise mashoko avo, zvakakosha kuziva mhando yekunyora yavakashandisa.

Imwe nzira yemanyorero inowanikwa kazhinji muMagwaro ndiyo “nyaya yemhosva yesungano.” Somuenzaniso, muna Jeremia 2:4–9, Jeremiya anopa nyaya yaMwari yepamutemo achipikisa vanhu Vake nokuda kwekutyora kwavo zvaidiwa nesungano. Achishandisa mufananidzo wedare, munyori anoratidza Mwari semuchuchisi ari kupa zvikonzero zvekutonga Kwake.

Rugwaro rune zvakare kunyora kwechiporofita, nhoroondo, nziyo, nemifananidzo. Jeremiya akaudzwa kuti anyore bhuku rine mashoko ake ose (Jer. 36:2), ukuwo zvinyorwa zvakawanda zveTestamende Itsva zviri mutsamba dzakanyorerwa vanhu kana mapoka evatendi. Hapana kusiyanisa kunoitwa pasimba remhando imwe yekutaura pamusoro peimwe. Tsamba dzaPauro dzaifanira kutorwa sedzine simba rakaenzana neramashoko evaporofita vekare, nokuti Pauro anopa humbowo hwakajeka mutsamba dzake kuti akafemerwa noMweya Mutsvene (ona 1 VaKorinde 7:10, 12, 40).

`Dzimwe dzidzo dzinonyanya kusimbisa chikamu chemunhu muMagwaro dzichideredza chikamu chaMwari. Sei iyi iri nzira yakajeka inoenda kukuparara kwemweya?`
