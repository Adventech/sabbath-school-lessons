---
title:  Zvinyorwa Zvakashandiswa
date:  04/11/2026
---

“Uyezve, nokuti Muparidzi akanga akachenjera, akaramba achidzidzisa vanhu ruzivo; akafungisisa, akatsvaga, uye akaronga zvirevo zvizhinji. Muparidzi akatsvaga kuwana mashoko anogamuchirika; uye zvakanyorwa zvakanga zvakarurama—mashoko echokwadi” (Muparidzi 12:9, 10, NKJV).

Ndima iyi inogona kuverengwa ichiratidza kuti Soromoni haana kutanga zvirevo zvose zvaakanyora. Pasi pekufemerwa naMwari, “akatsvaga” mashoko mazhinji uye akatsvaga “mashoko anogamuchirika.” Muchokwadi, tsananguro inowanzo dzokororwa yemukadzi ane unhu muna Zvirevo 31 inonzi yakabva kuna amai vaMambo Remueri, mambo asinganyatsozivikanwi, kwete kuna Soromoni (Zvirevo 31:1). Uchapupu hunoratidza zvakare pesvedzero yezvinyorwa zveIjipiti pazvirevo.

Saizvozvowo, vanyori venhoroondo veTestamende Yekare vakafemerwa vanotiudza kuti vakashandisa magwaro ekuchengetera nhoroondo nezvimwe zvinyorwa zvenhoroondo kuti vanyore nokutendeka nyaya yevanhu vaMwari.

`Verenga 1 Madzimambo 11:41; Ruka 1:1–4; na Judha 14, 15. Ndima idzi dzinotiudzei pamusoro pekuti vanyori vakafemerwa dzimwe nguva vaishandisa sei zvinyorwa zvisiri zveBhaibheri?`

Ezira naNehemiya mimwe mienzaniso yevanyori veBhaibheri vakavimba nezvinyorwa zvenhoroondo kuti vanyore nhoroondo dzavo. Ezira anosanganisira makopi etsamba nemirairo yamambo pamusoro peJerusarema nePezhiya (ona Ezira 4–7), uye Nehemiya anotiudza nezvemazita emadzinza aakawana ndokuaisa munyaya yake (ona Neh. 7:5–72).

Musimboti uyu unoonekwawo muTestamende Itsva. “Zviri pachena kubva pane zvakaitwa naRuka kuti kufemerwa kunoshanda nenzira inoenderana nekushanda kwepfungwa dzemunhu, uye hakuzvibvise. Pano pane munyori akafemerwa akatungamirirwa noMweya Mutsvene kuti anyatsodzidza zvinyorwa nemashoko aivapo pamusoro peupenyu hwaKristu, obva abatanidza ruzivo rwaakaunganidza kuita nyaya yakabatana.”—The SDA Bible Commentary, vol. 5, p. 669.

Judha anoita sokunge ari kutora mashoko kubva muna 1 Enoch 1:9—basa rechiJudha risingasi reMagwaro matsvene, rakanyorwa pakati peTestamende Yekare neItsva. Asingatauri mazita avo, Pauro akatorawo mashoko kubva kuvafilosofi vechiGiriki (ona Mabasa 17:28 na Tito 1:12).

`Kunyange vanyori veBhaibheri vaiwanzotungamirirwa nekuratidzwa kwakananga kubudikidza nezviratidzo nehope, izvi hazvina kureva kuti Mweya Mutsvene aisagona kuvafemera zvakare kuti vasarudze zvinhu kubva kune mamwe matsime. Sei pfungwa iyi yakakosha kuti tiirangarire?`
