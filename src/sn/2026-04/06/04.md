---
title:  Vabatsiri Pakunyora
date:  03/11/2026
---

`Verenga Jeremia 36:17, 18 na VaRoma 16:22. Ndima idzi dzinotiudzei pamusoro pekushandiswa kwevabatsiri pakunyora nevanyori veBhaibheri?`

Zvinofungidzirwa kuti Jeremiya aigona kunyora mashoko ake, asi panzvimbo pezvo akavimba nemunyori waaivimba naye kuti anyore zviporofita zvake nokutendeka uye agovere mashoko acho kuvatungamiri verudzi. Jeremiya haasi iye ega muenzaniso muBhaibheri wemuporofita akashandisa rubatsiro rwemunyori pakusvitsa shoko raMwari.

MuTestamende Itsva, kunyange Tertio achiti ndiye “akanyora tsamba iyi” (VaRoma 16:22), zviri pachena kubva pakutanga kwebhuku raVaRoma kuti aingonyora zvaida kutaurwa naPauro. Tertio aiva amanuensis wake, kana kuti munyori—mubatsiri pakunyora aiisa mashoko aPauro muchimiro chakanyorwa.

Maitiro aya aisava asingajairiki kuna Pauro. Pakupera kwetsamba dzake kuvaKorose navaTesaronika, Pauro anotaura nezvekukwazisa kwaanoita “neruoko rwangu pachangu” (VaKorose 4:18; 2 VaTesaronika 3:17, NKJV) senzira yekusimbisa kuti tsamba yose ndeyechokwadi. Izvi zvinoratidza kuti aivimba nemumwe munhu kuti anyore tsamba dzake. Nokuti chokwadi ichi chaigona kumutsa kusahadzika kuti tsamba yacho yaimumiririra zvakarurama here—kana kuti yakanga isiri yake zvachose—Pauro akawedzera chinyorwa chake chaakanyora neruoko pakupedzisira kuitira kuti pasava nekusahadzika pamusoro pechokwadi chetsamba yake.

Vatsananguri vazhinji vanoti kusiyana kwemaitiro ekunyora kunowanikwa mutsamba dzaPauro kunogona kutsanangurwa nekushandisa kwake vabatsiri pakunyora. Zvimwe chete izvi zvinofungidzirwawo pamusoro petsamba mbiri dzaPetro: “Kunyange chiri chokwadi kuti mutauro we2 Petro wakasiyana newekutanga, izvi zvinogona kutsanangurwa zvine musoro nekuti Petro . . . angangodaro akashandisa rubatsiro rwemunyori mukugadzira tsamba yaainyora muchiGiriki. Kana Pauro, uyo ainyatsoziva chiGiriki, akashandisa vanyori, sezviri pachena kuti akaita, zvinotonyanya kuva nemusoro kufunga kuti Petro, uyo mutauro wake wekutanga waive Aramaic, angadai akaita saizvozvo, uye kuti vanyori ava vangadai vakava nesimba pakuumbwa kwemashoko etsamba dzake muchiGiriki.”—The SDA Bible Commentary, vol. 5, p. 186.

Izvi zvinoratidza kuti, kunyange vachiramba vakatendeka kumifungo nerevo yevatumwa vaMwari, mazwi uye kuvakwa kwemitsara zvingaratidza kusiyana kweunyanzvi hwevabatsiri vakashandiswa. Mifungo ndiyo yakafemerwa, kwete hazvo mazwi chaiwo akashandiswa kutsanangura mifungo yakafemerwa.
