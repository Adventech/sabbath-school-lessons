---
title:  Vatauriri vaMwari
date:  01/10/2026
---

`Magwaro anotevera anoratidza chii pamusoro pekuti Mwari akataura sei nevanhu mushure mekupinda kwechivi? Verenga VaHebheru 1:1, 2; VaRoma 1:19, 20; Num. 12:6; 2 Timoti 3:16, 17; Johani 14:7–9; Johani 10:27–30.`

Zvisinei nechivi, Mwari akawana nzira dzakawanda dzekutaurirana nevanhu vakawira muchivi. Imwe nzira Mwari yaanozviratidza nayo kwatiri ndeyezvisikwa. Zvisikwa chiratidzo chakasimba chemabasa Ake anoshamisa. Vanamuzvinadzidzo vanoshamiswa nezvinoshamisa zviri mudenga nepasi uye nekugozha kunoshamisa kweupenyu kunyange hwezvinhu zvinonzi “zviri nyore.” Asi zvisikwa zvinoratidzawo migumisiro yeKuwa, nerufu rwezvinhu zvose zvipenyu rwuri muenzaniso wakasimba kupfuura zvose.

Imwe nzira Mwari yaakataura nayo nevanhu ndeye vaporofita—vanhu vaakasarudzwa kuti vave vatauriri Vake. Somuenzaniso, Mwari akataura naNoa, Abrahama, Mosesi, naDanieri, achiratidza kuda Kwake kuvanhu Vake. Pakadzika pemashoko avo, mashoko echiporofita chiyeuchidzo chekuti Mwari haana kukanganwa vana Vake uye achiri pamwe navo. Uye kunyange vamwe vaporofita vakanyora zvazova Magwaro kwatiri, vamwe havana kumbonyora mabhuku eBhaibheri.

Zvisinei, mashoko mazhinji evaporofita akasvitswa kubudikidza neMagwaro (2 Timoti 3:16, 17). Bhaibheri kuratidzwa kwaMwari pachake. Kubudikidza nemashoko Ake, tinodzidza kuti Ndiye ani, tinoona rudo Rwake rusinganeti, uye tinoziva kutungamira Kwake pamusoro pekuti tingararama sei.

Uyezve, kubudikidza neBhaibheri tinonzwisisa kuti tiri vanaani, takabva kupi, uye tiri kuenda kupi. Imomo tinowana chinangwa chaMwari paupenyu hwedu.

Asi nzira yakanyanya kushanda nayo Mwari pakutaura nesu ndeyekubudikidza naJesu Kristu, uyo, chokwadi, anoratidzwa kwatiri muBhaibheri. Sezvo Mwari akatsvaga Adhamu naEvha muBindu reEdeni, saizvozvowo Kristu akauya “kuzotsvaga nokuponesa chakarasika” (Ruka 19:10). Jesu akati, “Akaona Ini, aona Baba” (Johani 14:9, NKJV). Kubudikidza neupenyu Hwake, rufu Rwake, uye kumuka Kwake, Kristu akazadzisa vimbiso yakapiwa kuna Adhamu naEvha muEdeni. Akauya akafa panzvimbo yedu kuti tive neupenyu husingaperi kubudikidza Naye.

Mushure mekukwira kwaJesu kudenga, Mweya Mutsvene akaramba achititungamirira “muzvokwadi yose” uye achitaura nesu “zvinhu zvichauya” (Johani 16:13, NIV).

`Unonyaradzwa zvakadii nekuziva kuti Jesu, rudo Rwake, hunhu Hwake, uye kuzvipira Kwake, zvinotiratidza kuti Mwari Baba vakaita sei chaizvo?`
