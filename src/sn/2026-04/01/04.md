---
title:  Mwari Anotsvaga Vanhu
date:  29/09/2026
---

Tinogona kuona muna Genesisi 3:9, 10 kuti Mwari ndiye akatanga kutsvaga vana Vake vakanga vawira muchivi. Mubvunzo wekuti “Uri kupi?” waiva mubvunzo usingatsvagi ruzivo, nokuti Mwari aiziva zvaitwa naAdhamu naEvha uye kwavaive. Asi chivi chakanga chaparadzanisa kutaurirana kwakajairika kwaivapo pakati Pake nevanhu vekutanga. Chinhu chaiva pamoyo paMwari pakarepo chaiva chekudzorera kutaurirana nevana Vake, kusangana navo, uye kuvapa tariro mukushaya kwavo tariro. Muchokwadi, izwi rechiHebheru rokuti qara’, kureva “kudana,” muna Genesisi 3:9 rinowanzo shandiswa kutsanangura kudana kwaMwari pakutanga kutaurirana nevanhu Vake.

`Verenga Ruka 15:11–32. Mufananidzo uyu unoratidza sei zvakaitika muna Genesisi 3:9, 10?`

Kutsvaga kwakaita Mwari Adhamu naEvha kunoratidza rudo Rwake rusingaperi kuvana Vake. Mwari haambotiregi (ona Ruka 15). Anoedza zvikuru kuti adzorere kudyidzana Kwake nesu, kunyange patinenge tichivanda. Jesu asati arovererwa pamuchinjikwa, apo Petro akamuramba katatu, Kristu “akatendeuka akatarisa” (Ruka 22:61, NKJV) kwaari. Kunyange pasina mashoko, “akataura” kuna Petro uye akatsvaga kusvika kushamwari Yake yakanga yatsauka.

Cherechedza muna Genesisi 3:11–13 kuti chivi chakashandura sei hukama hwaAdhamu naEvha pakarepo. Panzvimbo pekureurura chivi chavo somunhu mumwe nomumwe, vakatanga kupomera vamwe mhosva. Adhamu akapomera Evha, uye zvisina kunaka , akapomera Mwari, nokuti Ndiye akamupa Evha. Akati, “Mukadzi wamakandipa , ndiye akandipa zvemuti, ndikadya” (Gen. 3:12, NKJV). Evha,wo, akapomera nyoka nokuda kwekusateerera kwake. Akati, “Nyoka yakandinyengera, ndikadya” (Gen. 3:13). Hapana mumwe wavo, pakutanga pavakatarisana nechivi chavo, akabvuma mhosva yake pachake. Zvinoratidza kuti zvinhu hazvina kunyanya kushanduka mumakore zviuru zvitanhatu.

Chokwadi ndechekuti chivi chakakanganisa vanhu vose. Muapostora Pauro anotsanangura mamiriro edu zvakanaka muna VaRoma 7:15–24, paanotsanangura kurwadziwa kwekuda kuita zvakanaka asi wozviwana uchiita zvakaipa. “Nokuti zvandinoita handizvinzwisisi. Nokuti zvandinoda kuita, handizviiti; asi zvandinovenga, ndizvo zvandinoita” (VaRoma 7:15, NKJV).

Ndiani asinganzwisisi izvi?

Zvinotaurwa naPauro zvakadzama. Tose tiri vanhu vakaputsika vanoramba vachiita zvinhu zvatinoziva kuti hatifaniri kuita. Ndiani asina kumbosangana nechokwadi ichi?

Pauro anoti tariro yedu yoga iri pakununurwa “kubudikidza naJesu Kristu Ishe wedu” (VaRoma 7:25). Ndicho chikonzero Mwari achiramba achititsvaga kuti atitungamirire kuna Jesu, uyo oga anogona kutiumba kuti tive nemufananidzo Wake uye kutiponesa kubva mukuparadzwa kunokonzerwa nechivi.
