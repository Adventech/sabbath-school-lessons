Kutaurirana kwaMwari hakugumiri munguva dzeBhaibheri chete. Sezvatichaona mumavhiki anouya, Mwari anotaura nevanhu nguva dzose, uye mashoko Ake acharatidzwa nenzira dzemazuva ano kusvikira Kristu adzoka.

Ellen Gould Harmon White nemukoma wake Elizabeth vakazvarwa musi waNovember 26, 1827, kuGorham, Maine. Vaiva vana vadiki pane vana vasere vakaberekwa naEunice naRobert Harmon. Mushure memakore mashoma, mhuri yakatamira kuguta rePortland, Maine, uko Robert Harmon aitarisira kuwedzera bhizimusi rake rekugadzira ngowani. Ava nemakore mapfumbamwe, Ellen akakuvara nenzira yakamukanganisa mumuviri, mupfungwa, uye mumagariro. Paakanga ari mupaki pedyo neimba yavo, mumwe waaidzidza naye akatsamwa akakanda dombo rakamurova kumeso, zvikamusiya ari mumamiriro ekusaziva zvakazara kwemavhiki akati wandei. Tsaona iyi yakangoerekana yamisa dzidzo yake yepamutemo uye yakamusiya aine matambudziko ehutano kweupenyu hwake hwose. Kukuvara kumeso kwake kwakaitawo kuti Ellen azvitarisire pasi nezvekutaridzika kwake, zvichideredza hukamahwake nevamwe vanhu.

Mukati memamiriro ezvinhu aya asina kugadzikana, Ellen Harmon mudiki akatanga kufunga zvakadzama nezveramangwana rake remweya. Aitya Mwari uye ainzwa asingakodzeri denga. Manzwiro iwayo ekutya akawedzera apo mhuri yeHarmon yakagamuchira dzidziso yeMillerite yekuuya kwaJesu kwechipiri kwaive pedyo. Kunyange mushure mekubhabhatidzwa kwake muMethodist Church muna 1842, Ellen haana kunzwa akakodzera rudo rwaMwari. Aitenda kuti akanga “arasika nokusingaperi” (Life Sketches of Ellen G. White, p. 29; onawo pp. 20–31).

Pakati pemafungiro aya airwadza, Ellen mudiki akarota hope mbiri dzakashanduramafungiro ake pamusoro paMwari. Asi akazonyatsonzwisisa uye kunzwa rudo nenyasha dzaMwari mushure mekunge asangana naLevi Stockman, muparidzi weMethodist. Zvakaita sokunge Mwari akataura naye mukushungurudzika kwake. Akazonyora achiti, “Kutenda zvino kwakatora mwoyo wangu. Ndakanzwa rudo rusingatsananguriki kuna Mwari uye ndakava neuchapupu hweMweya Wake kuti zvivi zvangu zvakanga zvaregererwa. Maonero angu pamusoro paBaba akashanduka. Ndakanga zvino ndava kuona Iye somubereki ane mutsa nemoyo munyoro, kwete somutongi ane hasha anomanikidza vanhu kuteerera vasingadi. Mwoyo wangu wakaenda kwaari murudo rwakadzama uye rwakasimba.”—Life Sketches of Ellen G. White, p. 39. Panguva imwe cheteyo, Ellen White akanzwisisawo dzidziso ye“mamiriro evakafa” uye zvishoma nezvishoma akanzwisisa pfungwa yekuti hakuna gehena rinopisa nokusingaperi. Kunzwisisa kwake kutsva pamusoro paMwari nerudo Rwake kwakachinja zvachose maitiro ake. Sezvatichadzidza Chishanu chega chega mumavhiki anouya, Ellen White akazadzwa nerudo rukuru kuna Jesu uye akamushumira kweupenyu hwake hwose.
