---
title:  Kuvanda Kuna Mwari
date:  28/09/2026
---

Nokuti Mwari rudo (1 Johani 4:8), haana kusika vanhu vakaita se“marobhoti.” Rudo runosanganisira sarudzo. Kuti vakwanise kuda, Adhamu naEvha vaifanira kusikwa vaine rusununguko rwekuzvisarudzira; dai zvisina kudaro, rudo rwechokwadi, rwaisazokwanisika kubva kwavari.

Saka Adhamu naEvha vaiva nerusununguko rwekuteerera kana kusateerera Musiki wavo. Chokwadi cherusununguko urwu chakaratidzwa mumurayiro wekuti vasadya kubva pamuti wekuziva zvakanaka nezvakaipa ( Gen. 2:15–17). Sei vaizoyambirwa kuti vasadya kubva pauri kana vasina rusununguko rwekuita sarudzo pakutanga?

“Vabereki vedu vekutanga, kunyange vakasikwa vasina mhosva uye vari vatsvene, havana kuiswa panzvimbo yavaisagona kuita zvakaipa. Mwari akavaita vanhu vane rusununguko rwekuzvisarudzira mutsika, vachikwanisa kunzwisisa uchenjeri nerunako rwehunhu Hwake uye kururama kwezvaanoda, uye vaine rusununguko rwakakwana rwekuteerera kana kusateerera. Vaifanira kufarira kudyidzana naMwari nengirozi tsvene. . . . Pasina rusununguko rwekuzvisarudzira, kuteerera kwake kwaisazova kwekuzvidira, asi kwekumanikidzwa.”—Ellen G. White, Patriarchs and Prophets, pp. 48, 49.

`Sei Adhamu naEvha vakaedza kuvanda Musiki wavo mushure mekusaterera? Ona Genesisi 3:1–8; enzanisa na Jer. 23:24 na VaHebheru 4:13.`

Genesisi 3 inoratidza chiitiko chinosuwisa zvikuru—kuputsika kwaAdhamu naEvha. Mushure mehurukuro huru pakati penyoka, Satani, naEvha, vabereki vedu vekutanga vakayedzwa kuti vadye kubva “pamuti wekuziva zvakanaka nezvakaipa,” wavakanga varambidzwa kudya. Satani akashandisa simba remashoko, manzwiro, nezvinhu zvinofadza kuti anyengetedze Evha, uyo akazonyengetedzawo Adhamu, kuti vapokane nemurayiro waMwari. Evha akaona kuti muti waiva “wakanaka kudyiwa,” “unofadza meso,” uye “unodikanwa kuti munhu ave nouchenjeri” (Gen. 3:6, NKJV). Vimbiso yaSatani yekuti vaizova “saMwari, mukuziva zvakanaka nezvakaipa” (Gen. 3:5, NKJV) yakanzwika seinokwezva zvikuru.

Muchokwadi, mashoko aSatani aiva azere nenhema. Satani akavavanzira magumo ekusateerera kwavo. Mushure mekusaterera Mwari, meso avo “akavhurika” pakarepo, uye vakaona kuti vakanga “vasina kupfeka.” “Nguo” yaMwari yechiedza nekubwinya (Mapisarema 104:1, 2), iyo yairatidza “mufananidzo” waMwari, yakanga yabva. Vakangavachisina kufukidzwazvavakanga vapiwa naMwari. Hazvishamisi kuti Adhamu naEvha vakazvivanza kuMusiki wavo, sokunge zvaigona kuitika kuvanda kuna Mwari. Vakaedza kuvanda nokuti chivi chinoita kuti tinzwe mhosva, uye patinenge tine mhosva, tinoda kutiza kubva pamberi paMwari.

`Chii chawakamboona muupenyu hwako pamusoro pekunyara uye kuda kuvanda kuna Mwari mushure mekunge waita chivi?`
