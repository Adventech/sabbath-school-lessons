---
title:  Kudzingwa muBindu
date:  30/09/2026
---

Mumwe wemigumisiro inorwadza zvikuru yechivi chaAdhamu naEvha kwaiva kuparadzaniswa naMwari. Vakadzingwa muBindu uye vakanga vasisakwanisi kutaura naMwari chiso nechiso. Muporofita Isaya akanyora pamusoro pechimiro chevanhu achiti: “Zvakaipa zvenyu zvakakuparadzanisai naMwari wenyu; uye zvivi zvenyu zvakaviga chiso Chake kwamuri” (Isa. 59:2, NKJV).

Muchokwadi, Adhamu naEvha vakanga vachiri vana vaMwari. Asi migumisiro yechivi yaisagona kudzivirirwa, uye vaifanira kubva muEdeni. Hapana chaizoramba chakafanana mushure mekusateerera kwavo.

`Chivi chakashandura vanhu munzira dzakawanda (ona Gen. 3:14–24). Asi chivi chakashandura sei kutaurirana naMwari? Ona zvakare Eks. 19:16–22.`

Chivi chakashandura upenyu hwevanhu zvachose nenzira dzatisingagoni kunyatsonzwisisa, nokuti chinhu chatinoziva chete inyika yechivi. Nokuda kwechivi, nyika “yakatukwa” uye yaizobereka “minzwa neminzwa yakapinza.” Vanhu vaizotambura marwadzo, uye vaizofanira “kudikitira” kuti vawane zvokudya, pakupedzisira vafa vodzokera muvhu. Pakati pezviyeuchidzo zvose zvechivi, rufu ndirwo rwakasimba kupfuura zvose. Panguva imwe cheteyo, nokuda kwechivi, takarasikirwa nekutaurirana naMwari chiso nechiso.

Zvisinei, Adhamu naEvha vakapiwa vimbiso yemhinduro yaizokunda kuparadza kwechivi. Kunyange vabereki vedu vekutanga vakanga vasisazooni kana kutaura naMwari zvakananga, vakabuda muEdeni vaine tariro pamusoro peramangwana . Mwari ndiye akatanga uye akavatsanangurira chirongwa Chake cheruponeso.

Genesisi 3:15, iyo inonzi “vimbiso yekutanga yevhangeri,” yakanga izere netariro kwavari, uye kwatiriwo, zvisinei nezvakanga zvaitika. Vimbiso yeMununuri aizouya yaizopa kuvengana nechivi panguva yazvino uye tariro yeruponeso rwevanhu mune ramangwana. Muchokwadi, kupfekedzwa kwaAdhamu naEvha nguo naMwari vasati vabuda muBindu kwaiva chiratidzo chairatidza rudzikinuro rwavo rwaizouya. Kubudikidza nerufu nekumuka kwaJesu mune ramangwana, kudyidzana naMwari kwaizodzorerwa (Johani 14:1–3).

Saka, ipapo muEdeni, pakarepo mushure mekupinda kwechivi, vanhu vakapiwa tariro nevimbiso yekuti dambudziko rechivi raizopedzisira ragadziriswa. Pakangovapo chivi, pakavapowo vimbiso yeMuponesi.

`Wakambonzwa sei mushure mekuita chinhu chisiri chakanaka? Mhosva? Kunyara? Kuzvivenga? Migumisiro yacho yakava yei? Kutenda kwako muna Mwari, uye vimbiso yevhangeri, zvakakubatsira sei kuti urege kurasa kutenda zvachose?`
