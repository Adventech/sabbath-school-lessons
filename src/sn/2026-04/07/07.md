---
title:  Kugamuchira neKusvitsa Mashoko Ake
date:  13/11/2026
---

Arthur White, muzukuru waEllen G. White uye aimbova director weEllen G. White Estate, akafungidzira kuti panguva yeupenyu hwake ambuya vake vakagamuchira mazana ezviratidzo nehope dzeuporofita. Zviratidzo izvi zvaitora maminitsi mashoma kusvika maawa akati wandei uye dzimwe nguva zvaionekwa nemapoka makuru evanhu. Pane dzimwe nguva, kunyange vanhu vaisamuvimba vakawana mukana wekumutarisa panguva yezviratidzo zvakadaro zvepachena.

Sezvakaitika munguva dzeBhaibheri, Ellen G. White akasangana nezviitiko zvisingatsananguriki zvemweya paakanga ari muchiratidzo. Muna 1868, James White akapa tsananguro yakadzama yemamiriro aEllen White panguva yechiratidzo. Aiva “asingazivi zvachose zvaiitika kumupoteredza”; aisafema; tsandanyama dzake dzaiita “dzakaoma” uye “majoini ake akasimba”; kufamba kwake nezviratidzo zvemaoko zvaiva “zvakasununguka uye zvine runako”; uye maziso ake aiva akavhurika. Mushure mekubuda muchiratidzo aimbotadza kuona kwechinguvana, asi kuona kwake kwaisakanganisika. Hapana munhu aigona kudzora kuti aizopinda muchiratidzo rini kana kuti chaizotora nguva yakareba sei, nokuti zviratidzo zvaiwanzouya zvisingatarisirwi naEllen White pachake. Ona James White, Life Incidents, pp. 271–274.

Sezvaiva munguva dzeBhaibheri, kupindura kwevanhu kumashoko kwakasiyana. Vamwe vakagamuchira mazano aMwari; vamwe vakaramba. Sezvakataurwa naEllen G. White: “Mwari akandiisa semutsiuri wevanhu Vake; uye . . . sezvaakaisa pamusoro pangu mutoro unorema, achaita kuti avo vanopiwa shoko iri vave nemhosva maererano nemabatiro avanoita shoko iroro. . . . Handina kuzvisarudzira basa iri risingafadzi. Harisi basa richandiunzira kufarirwa kana kurumbidzwa nevanhu. . . . Muzita nesimba reMudzikinuri wangu ndichaita zvandinogona. Ndichayambira, ndichapa zano, ndichatsiura, uye ndichakurudzira sezvinorairwa noMweya waMwari, kana vanhu vakateerera kana kuti vakaramba. Basa rangu harisi rekuzvifadza, asi rekuita kuda kwaBaba vangu vekudenga, avo vakandipa basa rangu.”—Testimonies for the Church, vol. 4, pp. 231, 232.

Zvinofadza kuti vazhinji vevakaramba mashoko ake euporofita vakadaro vachibva pakusatenda kwavo kana kusawirirana kwavo pachavo naEllen G. White, kwete pazvikonzero zvedzidziso. Ellen G. White aiva nemutsa mukuru kune avo vairatidza kusahadzika pamusoro pechipo chake cheuporofita. Muchokwadi, akajekesa kuti vanhu vakadaro vanofanira kubatwa “nekushivirira kukuru nerudo rwehama . . . kusvikira vawana nzvimbo yavo uye vagadzikana kuti vari kutsigira kana kupikisa.”—Testimonies for the Church, vol. 1, p. 328.

Ellen G. White aiva nechokwadi chekuti humbowo chaihwo hwechipo chake cheuporofita hwaiva mumashoko aMwari aakamupa. Kana vanhu vaiva vachitsvaga chokwadi nemwoyo wakatendeseka, aitenda kuti vaizopedzisira vaona kuti chipo chake chaiva chechokwadi uye chakavimbika. Aivimba kuti Mwari aizovatungamirira murwendo rwavo rwekunzwisisa zviri nani, asi akanyorawo kuti Mwari akatipa humbowo hwakakwana “kuti hutende hwedu hutsamire kudivi rechokwadi.”—Testimonies for the Church, vol. 4, p. 231.
