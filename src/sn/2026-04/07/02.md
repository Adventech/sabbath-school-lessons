---
title:  Kutsamwira Mutumwa waMwari
date:  08/11/2026
---

Mosesi, mutungamiri akasarudzwa naMwari, aiziva mufaro nemarwadzo ekutungamirira boka revanhu vakasanganiswa vachipfuura murenje vachitsvaga Nyika Yakapikirwa.

`Verenga Numeri 14:4–11. Chii chiri kuitika mundima idzi? Sei vanhu vakatsamwira Mosesi naAroni?`

Kuverenga nokungwarira ndima idzi kunoratidza kuti vana vaIsraeri vakanga vakatsamwira Mwari nokuti havana kumuvimba. Mosesi naAroni, vamiriri vaMwari vari vanhu, ndivo vakava vanhu vari nyore kupomerwa mhosva nekutsamwa kwerudzi. Kuda kuchengetedzeka kwemuviri nepfungwa chinhu chikuru chinokurudzira vanhu vose. Kana chimwe chezvinhu izvi chikatyisidzirwa, kana tikangoona sekunge pane chiri kutyisidzira kugara kwedu zvakanaka mumuviri kana mupfungwa, tinogona kutsamwa zvikuru kana kutotadza kufunga zvakanaka.

`Verenga Numeri 13:26–30. Sei vaIsraeri vaitya zvakadaro? Shoko ripi rekukurudzira rakagoverwa naKarebhi kwavari?`

VaIsraeri vaiva nekutya kwaive nemusoro pamusoro pematambudziko aiva mberi pavo pakutora Nyika Yakapikirwa, “asi kana vanhu vakapa mwoyo yavo pakusatenda, vanozviisa pasi pekutonga kwaSatani, uye hapana anogona kutaura kuti achavasvitsa papi.”—Ellen G. White, Patriarchs and Prophets, p. 389. Panguva iyi, Mosesi akatarisana nedambudziko rehutungamiri rainge rakaoma zvikuru, rinenge risingakundiki. Kutya kwevanhu kwakakurumidza kukura kukava kutsamwira Aroni naMosesi uye kupandukira Mwari pachena. Apo vanhu vakatsvaga vatungamiri vatsva kuti vatsive muporofita/mutungamiri akanga asarudzwa naMwari (Deut. 34:10–12; Num. 14:4), Joshua naKarebhi vakabvarura nguo dzavo uye vakakumbira vanhu kuti vavimbe naMwari zvisinei nekutya kwavo: “Kana Jehovha achitifarira, achatipinza munyika iyi agoiisa kwatiri, ‘nyika inoyerera mukaka nouchi.’ Asi musapandukira Jehovha, uye musatya vanhu venyika iyoyo” (Num. 14:8, 9, NKJV).

`Zviri nyore kuvimba naMwari kana zvinhu zviri kufamba zvakanaka, handizvo here? Ko kana dambudziko rarova? Ko kana kuvimba naMwari kuchireva kusagadzikana kana kusagutsikana? Unoita sei panguva yakadaro?`
