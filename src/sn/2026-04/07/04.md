---
title:  'Kuparadza Shoko: Chikamu 2'
date:  10/11/2026
---

Mapinduriro anoita vanhu kuchipo chechiporofita anogona kuva mazhinji. Panyaya yaMosesi, vana vaIsraeri vakapinda mukupandukira pachena vachipikisa Mosesi naAroni, vachitungamirirwa nekutya vavengi vavaizosangana navo pakupinda muNyika Yakapikirwa. Makore akazotevera, Mambo Jehoyakimi akapisa shoko raMwari achitarisira kuparadza shoko reuporofita raMwari.

Zvisinei, izvi hazvina kungova zviitiko zvakasiyana. Nhoroondo yeIsraeri yekare neJudha, kunze kwezviitiko zvishoma, yaiva nhoroondo yekuramba vaporofita.

`Verenga 2 Makoronike 36:15, 16. Rudzi rwaJudha rwakapindura sei mashoko akatumwa naMwari kwarwo kubudikidza nevaporofita Vake? Mwari akazopedzisira apindura sei maitiro aJudha?`

Basa reuporofita raJeremiya rakapfuura munguva dzehutongi hwaJosia, Jehoahazi, Jehoyakimi, Jehoyakini, naZedekia. Umambo hweBhabhironi, hwaitungamirirwa naNebhukadhinezari, hwakanga hwava pamusuwo waJudha. Zviporofita zvaJeremiya zvakanga zvava pedyo kuzadziswa apo mukomana ane makore 21, Zedekia, akagara pachigaro choumambo (2 Makoronike 36:11). Bhaibheri rinoti, “Akaita zvakaipa pamberi paJehovha Mwari wake, uye haana kuzvininipisa pamberi paJeremiya muporofita, aitaura kubva mumuromo maJehovha” (2 Makoronike 36:12, NKJV).

Cherechedza kuti chipo cheuporofita chaJeremiya chinotsanangurwa sei. Anoonekwa somunhu aitaura zvichibva mumuromo maMwari pachake. Zvisinei, Zedekia haana kuzvininipisa pamberi pemuporofita. Asi Jeremiya, kunyange aingova munhu, mutadzi semumwe nomumwe wedu, aiva mumiriri waIshe, uyo aitaura achimumiririra.

Jeremiya aiva mumwe wevakawanda vaiva mubasa iri. Kwemazana emakore, Mwari akanga achitumira vaporofita kuvanhu Vake (Eks. 3:15; Mapisarema 105:26; Maraki 3:1; Isa. 6:8; Jer. 25:4; Mat. 11:13; Ruka 11:49, nezvimwe), asingadi “kuti mumwe munhu aparare asi kuti vose vasvike pakutendeuka” (2 Petro 3:9, NKJV). Nhoroondo yeIsraeri yekare neJudha inyaya yekuedza kwaMwari kusingaperi kusvika kuvanhu Vake vakanga vatsauka, achivapa zvivimbiso zveruponeso, dai chete vatendeuka.

Asi panguva iyi yekupedzisira munhoroondo yaJudha, nyasha dzaMwari dzakapiwa kuJudha kubudikidza nekutungamirwa nekunyaradzwa kweinzwi reuporofita raJeremiya hadzina kugamuchirwa nekuzvininipisa, rutendo, kana kutendeuka; asi dzakasangana nekusekwa, kunyombwa, uye kuzvidzwa.

`Vakaseka vatumwa vaMwari, vakazvidza mashoko aMwari, uye vakaseka vaporofita Vake. Chokwadi, isu nhasi hatimbofi takaita chinhu chakadaro—kana kuti tingadaro here?`
