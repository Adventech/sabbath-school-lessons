---
title:  Unyengeri hweKuzviita Mutsvene
date:  11/11/2026
---

Wakambotarisa here zvinhu zvakaitwa nevamwe vanhu kare ukazviti, Dai ndaiva ini, handaimboita zvakaitwa nevanhu ivavo! Asi kana tikava vakatendeseka nesu pachedu, kunyange tisingaiti zvinhu zvacho zvakafanana chaizvo, tinowanzozvibata nenzira dzakafanana, tichidzokorora zvikanganiso zvimwe chete, ukuwo tichizvivhara nemaune kuti tisaone zvatiri kuita chaizvo. Uyu ndiwo musimboti wakataurwa naJesu pachake paakati: “Munhu anonyengera! Tanga wabvisa danda riri muziso rako, ipapo uchaona zvakanaka kubvisa kabanzu kari muziso rehama yako” (Mat. 7:5, NKJV).

`Verenga Mateo 23:30–35. Pfungwa ipi yakakosha asi inorwadza iri kutaurwa naJesu pano? Yambiro ipi yatingatora kubva pamashoko Ake?`

Pane pfungwa mundima idzi yekuti Jesu akavenga maitiro evaFarisi nokuti unyengeri hwavo hwakatowedzerwa nekusada kwavo zvachose kuchinja. Jesu akanga aguta nechitendero chevaFarisi. Akanga asisade zvevatungamiri vezvemweya vaipfeka nguo refu asi vaingova semakuva akapendwa chena akazara mapfupa evakafa (Mat. 23:27, 28). Vaisada kureurura kana kusiya zvivi zvavo, kunyange zvivi zvacho zvaratidzirwa kwavari, kunyange Jesu paakavapa mukana kakawanda wekuti vatendeuke voponeswa.

Mukubata Kwake nevarume ava, hazvishamisi kuti Jesu aivadaidza kuti “Vanyengeri!” Kazhinji mutsara wacho waienda seizvi: “Mune nhamo imi vanyori nevaFarisi, vanyengeri!” (ona Mat. 23:14, 15, 23, 25, 27).

Upenyu hwavo hwaizivikanwa nokutaura chimwe chinhu asi vachiita chimwe. Ndiwo maitiro avakanga vakaita kuvaporofita. Vaifunga, semuenzaniso, kuti nokushongedza makuva evaporofita vakanga vachikudza kutenda; asi pazvinhu zvakakosha, sekutendeuka pamashoko evaporofita, vakaramba kuzviita. Panzvimbo pezvo, vaida kubatanidzwa nesimba nemanzwiro akadzama evaporofita, asi kwete nezviri mumashoko avo. Pavaishongedza makuva nemakuva evashumiri vaMwari, vaizvifunga vachiti havangadai vakadeura ropa revatumwa vaMwari. Asi panguva iyoyo chaiyo vakanga vachironga kuuraya Mumwe mukuru zvikuru kupfuura muporofita—Mesia pachake.

`Tingava nechokwadi sei kuti, pamusimboti, hatiiti chinhu chimwe chetecho: kuzvibata sevanamati kunze asi mukati mezere nekupandukira, makaro, neruvengo?`
