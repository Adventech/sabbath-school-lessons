---
title:  'Kuparadza Shoko: Chikamu 1'
date:  09/11/2026
---

`Verenga Jeremia 36:20–26. Chii chaiva chichiitika mudare, uye vatungamiri vakapindura sei kuyambiro yeuporofita?`

Jeremiya aiva inzwi raMwari reuporofita kurudzi rwaJudha panguva yavaive vava pedyo nekuparadzwa. Kwemakore mazhinji Mwari, kubudikidza naJeremiya, akanga achiyambira Judha pamusoro pekusafadzwa Kwake navo, uye kuti Bhabhironi raizovakunda nekuvatapa kuutapwa (Jer. 6:8–19; Jeremia 26). Ishe aitsvaga kuvaponesa, asi vaifanira kuteerera.

Zvivi zvaJudha zvaiva zvakawanda, zviri pachena, uye zvisingachinji. Kunamata zvifananidzo, kubayira vana, upombwe, nekudzvinyirira vanhu ndizvo zvakanga zvakajairika munguva yaJeremiya, asi yambiro nekukumbira kwemuporofita hazvina kuteererwa. Sezvo basa reuporofita raJeremiya raiswedera kumagumo, kukumbira kwaMwari kuvana Vake vakanga vatsauka kwakawedzera kusimba, asi kuedza Kwake kwairamba kuine chinangwa cherudzikinuro. Muna Jeremia 32, somuenzaniso, Mwari anoudza Jeremiya kuti atenge chikamu chevhu uye achengete gwaro racho, “Nokuti zvanzi naJehovha wehondo . . .: ‘Dzimba neminda neminda yemizambiringa zvichatorwazve munyika ino’ ” (Jer. 32:15, NKJV).

Rudo ndirwo rwakaita kuti Mwari audze Jeremiya kunyora sokunge chikumbiro chekupedzisira chekuti vanhu vatendeuke uye vavandudzwe. Mhinduro yaJehoyakimi kushoko iri yaiva yekuzvidza Mwari, shoko Rake, nemutumwa Wake.

`Verenga Jeremia 36:27–31. Mwari akapindura sei chiito chaJehoyakimi chekuzvidza?`

Dzimwe nguva pane pfungwa yekuti kana tikaramba kana kuparadza mashoko aMwari kana vatumwa Vake, shoko racho pacharo rinonyangarika. Asi kusada kwedu kuteerera Shoko raMwari reuporofita hakubvisi shoko raMwari kwatiri. Kuzvidza kwedu kunongowedzera kukuvara kuchatiwira isu nemhuri dzedu nokuda kwekusaziva kwedu kwatinozvisarudzira kana kupandukira. Chiporofita chaJeremiya pamusoro pekuguma kwaMambo Jehoyakimi chine yambiro yakakomba kuvanhu vose (Jer. 36:30, 31): kana tikaregeredza yambiro dzaMwari kwatiri, tinozviisa panjodzi, nokuti tichakohwa migumisiro yechivi nehuipi hwedu.

`Zvinotiudzei pamusoro pehunhu hwaMwari hwerudo kuti akaramba achiedza kusvika kuvanhu Vake kunyange vakanga vachimumukira?`
