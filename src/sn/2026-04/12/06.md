---
title:  Kuratidzwa Kwemazuva Ano
date:  17/12/2026
---

Vanhu vazhinji vane kutenda nhasi vanogona kubvuma kuti vaporofita vekare veBhaibheri vaiva vechokwadi, asi vanotendawo here kuti chinhu chimwe chetecho chinogona kuitika munguva yedu? Kana vimbiso ya Joere 2:28–31 yekuratidzwa kunobwinya kwechipo chechiporofita munguva yokupedzisira ichifanira kutendwa, ingaratidzika sei?

`Verenga Mabasa 2:36–42. Shoko guru remharidzo yaPetro paPentekosta raiva rei? Vateereri vaPetro vakapindura sei?`

Kudururwa kweMweya Mutsvene paPentekosta muna A.D. 31 mufananidzo wakanaka wezvichaitika panguva yekudururwa kweMweya Mutsvene pakupedzisira, kunyange zvichizova nesimba rakakura zvikuru. Pentekosta yakaratidzwa nekusimudzira Jesu, kutendeuka kwakapararira, uye kugadzirwa kwevanhu vakagadzirira uye vakashongedzerwa basa raMwari. Shoko raPetro raiva pakati paro Kristu, rakasimbiswa noMweya Mutsvene, uye izvi zvakaita kuti vanhu 3,000 vatendeuke uye vabhabhatidzwe. Ropafadzo iyi ye“mvura yekutanga” yeMweya Mutsvene yakadyarawo mbeu inokosha, Kereke—muviri wakabatana wevatendi vanoratidza uye vanogovera rudo rwaJesu Kristu vachizivisa kudzoka Kwake.

Sezvo mvura yekutanga yakapiwa “kuti imerese mbeu inokosha, saizvozvowo ‘mvura yekupedzisira’ ichapiwa pakupera kwayo kuti goho riibve.”—Ellen G. White, The Great Controversy, p. 611. Nemamwe mashoko, kuratidzwa kwemazuva ano kwechipo chechiporofita—mvura yekupedzisira—kuchafanana zvakanyanya nekuratidzwa kwekutanga kwemvura yekutanga paPentekosta.

Sezvatatoona muna Joere 2:28–31, chipo chechiporofita panguva yokupedzisira chichavhurirwa vadiki nevakuru, varume nevakadzi. Mwari achataura kubudikidza nevaya vanosarudzwa noMweya Mutsvene. Pose panosarudzwa mutumwa wenguva yokupedzisira noMweya Mutsvene, shoko rake richasimudzira Jesu, richadaidzira vanhu kukutendeuka, uye richagadzirira vanhu kuzadzisa basa raMwari. Zvinhu zvitatu izvi ndizvo zviratidzo zvebasa reuporofita raEllen G. White. Muupenyu hwake hwose, akasimudzira Jesu neMagwaro mukutaura nekunyora kwake; akadaidzira maSeventh-day Adventist nevamwe kukutendeuka kwemwoyo; uye akakurudzira vose kupinda mubasa raMwari rekuponesa vakarasika.

`Wakashandisa zvakadii chipo chinoshamisa chatakapiwa kubudikidza nebasa raEllen G. White?`
