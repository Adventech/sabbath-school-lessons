---
title:  Zvose Zviri Pamusoro peMishoni
date:  18/12/2026
---

Muna VaEfeso 4, Pauro anoratidza kuti chinangwa chikuru chezvipo zvemweya ndechekubatsira vatendi kufambisira mberi mishoni yaMwari. Basa reuporofita raEllen G. White raiva nechinangwa chimwe chetecho. Mashoko ake akafemerwa akabatsira vatendi kukudziridza hukama hwakadzama naMwari uye akavakurudzira kugovera rudo irworwo nevamwe.

Akanyora kuti, “Pakati pevaKristu vose vanozviti vaKristu, maSeventh-day Adventist anofanira kuva pamberi pakusimudzira Kristu pamberi penyika. Kuziviswa kweshoko rengirozi yechitatu kunoda kuratidzwa kwechokwadi cheSabata. Chokwadi ichi, pamwe nezvimwe zviri mushoko, chinofanira kuziviswa; asi nzvimbo huru inokwezva, Kristu Jesu, haifaniri kusiiwa. Pamuchinjikwa waKristu ndipo panosangana ngoni nechokwadi, uye kururama nerugare zvinotsvodana. Mutadzi anofanira kutungamirirwa kuti atarise kuKarivhari; nerutendo rwakareruka rwemwana mudiki anofanira kuvimba nezvakafanira zveMuponesi, achigamuchira kururama Kwake, achitenda mutsitsi Dzake.”—Gospel Workers, pp. 156, 157.

Pamusoro pezvo, akasimbisa kuti mutendi mumwe nomumwe akadanwa kuva chikamu chemishoni yaMwari. “Ishe vanoda kuti kuedza kukuru kwemunhu pachake kuitwe nenhengo dzemakereke edu.”—Evangelism, p. 113.

“Mubasa raIshe hamufaniri kuva nevasina basa. Vakasiyana ngavabatane mukushanda sevabati vevanhu. Ngavatsvage kuunganidza mweya kubva muhuori hwenyika kuenda mukuchena kunoponesa kwerudo rwaKristu.”—Evangelism, p. 115. Ellen G. White pachake aiva mumishinari kuEurope muma1880, uye kuAustralia muma1890.

Nokuda kwekudanwa kwaMwari kweuporofita kukuzvipira kwemunhu nemishoni, Satani “anotsamwa” (Zvakazarurwa 12:17, NKJV) nevanhu vaMwari panguva yokupedzisira. Ava ndivo vateveri vaJesu vane shoko renguva yokupedzisira, kutungamirirwa kweuporofita, uye kunzwisisa: “Ishe vazivisa njodzi dzakatipoteredza nedziri pamberi pedu. Kubudikidza neMweya wechiporofita, akafumura kunyengera kuchabata nyika senhapwa, uye akataura kuvanhu Vake achiti, ‘Iyi ndiyo nzira; fambai mairi.’ [Isaya 30:21.] . . . The Great Controversy inofumura kunyengera kwaSatani.”—Ellen G. White, manuscript 31, 1890.

Satani anoshatirwa kana vanhu vachipa upenyu hwavo kuna Jesu, uye anoita zvose kuti asvibise inzwi raMwari reuporofita. Dzimwe nzira dzake dzinotsvaga kuratidza chipo cheuporofita chaEllen G. White sechisina “kukosha.” Mushure mezvose, akanyora munguva yakasiyana zvikuru neyedu. Imwe nzira ndeyekuunza kusahadzika nekubvunza chokwadi chezvaaiti nezvechipo chake cheuporofita uye kumupomera kuti muporofita wenhema. Asi nzira inonyanya kushanda yekuita kuti Ellen G. White aoneke seasina kukosha ndeyekubvisa chizvarwa chazvino pakuverenga zvinyorwa zvake pachacho.
