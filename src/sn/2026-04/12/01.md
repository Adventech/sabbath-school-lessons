### Verenga zveChidzidzo cheVhiki Ino
Joere 2:28–32; Mabasa 2:1–21; VaEfeso 4:11–14; Zvakazarurwa 12:17; Zvakazarurwa 19:10; Mabasa 2:36–42.

> <p>Ndima Yekuyeuka:</p>
> “Zvino shure kwaizvozvo, ndichadurura Mweya Wangu panyama yose; vanakomana venyu navanasikana venyu vachaporofita, vakuru venyu vacharota hope, majaya enyu achaona zviratidzo. Uye napamusoro pavaranda Vangu navarandakadzi Vangu ndichadurura Mweya Wangu mumazuva iwayo” (Joere 2:28, 29, NKJV).

Mune mimwe mitemo yehurumende mune chinonzi “sunset provision.” Izwi iri remutemo harina chekuita nekuvira kwezuva. Rinoreva chikamu chemutemo chinoita kuti mutemo wacho upere kushanda wega pazuva rakatarwa. Hurumende dzinoisa zvikamu zvakadai mumitemo nezvikonzero zvakasiyana-siyana. Somuenzaniso, dzinogona kugadzira mimwe mitemo inoderedza kodzero dzevagari panguva dzenjodzi yenyika. Dzimwe nguva hurumende dzinoisa “sunset provisions” kuti mitemo iyoyo ipere pazuva rakatarwa, kuitira kudzoreredza kodzero dzevagari dzakanga dzambotorwa kwechinguvana.

Ko Mwari akaisa “sunset provision” here paakapa chipo chechiporofita kubudikidza noMweya Mutsvene munguva dzekare? Pane nguva here Jesu asati adzoka apo chipo ichi chichamira kushanda? Vhiki rapfuura takadzidza kuti Mwari akataura kubudikidza nevaporofita kuvanhu Vake panguva dzakakosha. Vhiki rino tichaongorora chipo chechiporofita panguva yokupedzisira—basa racho rakakosha uye kuenderera kwacho—pamwe nebasa reushumiri hwaEllen G. White sezvo kuuya kwechipiri kwaJesu kuri kuswedera.

_*Dzidza chidzidzo chevhiki rino kugadzirira Sabata, Zvita 19._
