---
title:  Vakasara veNguva Yokupedzisira
date:  16/12/2026
---

Zvakazarurwa 12 inoratidza mufananidzo mukuru wenhoroondo yehondo huru, kubva pakutanga kwayo kudenga kusvika kumazuva okupedzisira.

`Verenga Zvakazarurwa 12:17. Chii chinonzi “uchapupu hwaJesu”? Enzanisa Zvakazarurwa 19:10 na Zvakazarurwa 22:8, 9.`

Muna Zvakazarurwa 12:17, zvinotaurwa zvinhu zviviri zvinozivisa kereke yenguva yokupedzisira. Kereke iyi inobata uye inochengeta Mirayiro Gumi, inosanganisira murayiro weSabata, uye ine “uchapupu hwaJesu.” Uchapupu uhwu chii?

Dzimwe shanduro dzeBhaibheri dzinodudzira “uchapupu hwaJesu” sekureva kuti isu sevatendi tinopupura Jesu uye tinomuzivisa kubudikidza nebasa redu nemishoni (ona Zvakazarurwa 19:10, NIV: “hold to the testimony of Jesus,” Jesu ari chinhu chinopupurirwa nevaKristu). Dzimwe shanduro dzeBhaibheri dzinopa imwe revo, dzichiti Jesu pachake ndiye anopa uchapupu kukereke Yake (Zvakazarurwa 19:10, GNT: “the truth that Jesus revealed”—Jesu ndiye anopa uchapupu). Muchokwadi, mutauro wechiGiriki chepakutanga unogona kududzirwa nenzira mbiri idzi. Saka Johani airevei?

Kana tikaenzanisa zviratidzo zviviri zvekuzivisa, tinoona kufanana kukuru kwemanyorerwo azvo, kunyanya muGiriki yepakutanga kupfuura mumashanduro edu. Sezvo “mirayiro yaMwari” zviri pachena kuti inobva kuna Mwari, saizvozvowo “uchapupu hwaJesu” hunobva kuna Jesu. Saka Jesu ndiye anopupura. Anozivisa kereke yechokwadi. Anozviita sei? Anozviita kubudikidza nechipo chechiporofita.

Mhinduro dzinowanikwa muna Zvakazarurwa 19:10 na Zvakazarurwa 22:9. Pano zviri pachena kuti “uchapupu hwaJesu” hunozivikanwa se“mweya wechiporofita.” Johani pachake muporofita uye anomiririra mumunhu wake “uchapupu hwaJesu.” Muporofita munhu ane kudanwa kwakakosha semutumwa waMwari kuvanhu Vake, kungava kukurudzira nekudanidzira kutendeuka, kupa kurudziro, kana kuratidza ramangwana.

Zvakazarurwa 12:17 inoratidza kuti Jesu anoita kuti kereke Yake izivikanwe kubudikidza nechipo ichi chechiporofita. Izvi zvinoreva kuti, sezvo Mwari akatuma vaporofita kuvanhu veTestamende Yekare veIsraeri neJudha, kana muapostora Johani kukereke yekutanga yechiKristu, vaporofita vachamukawo mumazuva okupedzisira enhoroondo yepasi.

`Uchapupu hupi hwawaona hunoratidza kuti basa raEllen G. White rinogona kuonekwa nenzira yakarurama semuenzaniso wekushanda kwe“mweya wechiporofita” (Zvakazarurwa 19:10)?`
