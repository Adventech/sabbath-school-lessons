`Verenga Mabasa 2:1–21. Sei Petro akatora mashoko aJoere muchirevo chekuuya kwekutanga kwaKristu? Chiporofita chine basa rei muchikamu ichi?`

Mundima idzi dzeTestamende Itsva, tinowana chimwe chezvinyorwa zvakarebesa zvakatorwa kubva muTestamende Yekare. Patinoverenga mafungiro aPetro, tinogona kubvunza kuti, “Petro, ko zviratidzo zvacho? Hatizvioni mumamiriro enyu.” Petro angangopindura achiti, Pakanga paine kuzadziswa kwezviratidzo. Jesu paakanga akarembera pamuchinjikwa mazuva mashoma apfuura, zuva rakasviba kwemaawa matatu (Mako 15:33).

Izvo zvaiva humbowo hunogutsa kuna Petro zvinogona kunge zvisingatinyanyi kugutsa isu nhasi. Tichiri kutarisira kuzadziswa kwakazara kwechiporofita chaJoere. Kiyi iri muchinyorwa chaJoere pachacho. Muna Joere 2:23, Joere anotaura pamusoro pemvura yekutanga nemvura yekupedzisira. Mabasa 2 ndiko kuzadziswa kwekutanga kwechiporofita ichi—mvura yekutanga. Kuzadziswa kwekupedzisira kuchava kudururwa kweMweya nguva yokupedzisira isati yasvika kubudikidza nemvura yekupedzisira.

Kazhinji vanhu vanotsanangura chiporofita sekuti “kufanotaura ramangwana.” Izvi hazvina kuipa, chokwadi, asi vaporofita veBhaibheri vaidananawo vanhu kukutendeuka. Mushure mezvo, Petro paakanga achitaura, vamwe muboka revanhu vakabayiwa pamwoyo.

“Zvino pavakanzwa izvi, vakabayiwa pamwoyo, vakati kuna Petro nekune vamwe vaapostora, ‘Varume hama, tichaitei?’ ” (Mabasa 2:37, NKJV).

Petro akavapindura sei?

“ ‘Tendeukai, uye mumwe nomumwe wenyu ngaabhabhatidzwe muzita raJesu Kristu kuti mukanganwirwe zvivi; uye muchagamuchira chipo cheMweya Mutsvene. Nokuti chivimbiso ichi ndechenyu nevana venyu, uye nevose vari kure, vose vachadanwa naIshe Mwari wedu’ ” (Mabasa 2:38, 39, NKJV).

Johani Mubhabhatidzi aiva muporofita aiparidza kutendeuka. Kunyange aidaidzira kuuya kwekutanga kwaJesu, aiparidzawo kuti vanhu vatendeuke pazvivi zvavo. “Tendeukai, nokuti umambo hwekudenga hwaswedera!” (Mat. 3:2, NKJV).

Saka Joere 2 ichazowana kuzadziswa kwayo kwekupedzisira mune ramangwana. Sezvakaita Joere naPetro, vaporofita venguva yokupedzisira vachadanidzira kutendeuka, vachanonongedzera kuna Jesu, uye vachapa kutungamirwa kukereke yaMwari yenguva yokupedzisira.

`Pavakaona kuratidzwa kwesimba risinganzwisisiki uku, boka revanhu pakutanga rakafunga kuti chaingova chiitiko chakasikwa. Panyaya iyi vakafunga kuti varume ava vakanga vakadhakwa! Varume vakadhakwa vachitaura mitauro yekune dzimwe nyika kamwe kamwe here? Kunyange mhinduro yavo yakanga isina musoro, chii chiri muvanhu chinoita kuti, kunyange pamberi pezvinhu zvemweya zvisinganzwisisiki, vaedze kutsvaga tsananguro yepanyika?`
