---
title:  Vimbiso yaJoere
date:  13/12/2026
---

`Verenga Joere 2:28–32. Mwari anovimbisa kuitei mumazuva okupedzisira?`

Joere, muporofita wemuzana remakore rechinomwe B.C., anopa kunzwisisa kwakakosha pamusoro pezviitiko zvenguva yemberi. Patinoverenga chikamu ichi, tinogona kushamisika kuti ari kutaura nezvenguva yake yekare here kana kuti nezvezvinhu zviri kure kwazvo mune ramangwana.

Vadzidzi vezveBhaibheri vanowanzosiyanisa pakati pemhando mbiri dzechiporofita—classical prophecy neapocalyptic prophecy. Classical prophecy inotaura nezvemarudzi chaiwo uye inoshandisa manhamba nemazita chaiwo (ona, somuenzaniso, Jer. 25:1–11). Asi apocalyptic prophecy inoshandisa mutauro wezviratidzo zvakawanda, inoshandisa manhamba ezviratidzo ekutaura nguva, uye inowanzotaura nezvezviratidzo mudenga nepasi rose (ona Danieri 7). Dzimwe nguva tinowana kusanganirana kwemhando mbiri idzi dzechiporofita (ona, somuenzaniso, Mateo 24).

Ko Joere 2:28–31 yakaita sei? Zvinoita sokuti chikamu ichi chinowira muboka repiri iri. Izvi zvinoreva kuti hatifaniri kutarisira kuzadziswa kwacho mukati meupenyu hwemuporofita, kana kunyange mumakore aitevera basa rake pakarepo. Kusiyana naizvozvo, tinofanira kutsvaga kuzadziswa kwechiporofita ichi mune ramangwana. Cherechedza mamiriro nenguva apo Mwari achapa chipo chechiporofita—“zuva guru uye rinotyisa raJehovha risati rasvika” (Joere 2:31, NKJV). Zuva ripi iroro? Kuuya kwekutanga kwaJesu here? Kana kwechipiri? Kana zvose?

Patinofunga nezvekutaurwa kwezviratidzo pazuva nemwedzi muchiporofita chaJoere, tinobva tangobatwa nechokwadi chekuti pfungwa iyi inowanikwawo mune dzimwe ndima dzeBhaibheri dzakawanda. Jesu akaudza vadzidzi Vake pamusoro pezviratidzo pazuva, pamwedzi, uye panyeredzi muhurukuro Yake inozivikanwa pamusoro penguva yokupedzisira (Mat. 24:29). Saizvozvowo, Johani muZvakazarurwa anotaura nezvezviratidzo zvakafanana (Zvakazarurwa 6:12, 13). Vose Jesu naJohani vanonongedzera kuzviratidzo zvikuru zvekupedzisira mudenga kuuya kwechipiri kwaIshe wedu kusati kwaitika.

Saka, tinogona kufunga kuti chiporofita chaJoere, chinonongedzerawo kuzviratidzo zvakafanana, chine chekuita nenguva yokupedzisira: “Ndicharatidza zvishamiso kudenga nepanyika: ropa, moto, neshongwe dzeutsi. Zuva richashandurwa kuva rima, uye mwedzi kuva ropa, zuva guru uye rinotyisa raJehovha risati rasvika” (Joere 2:30, 31, NKJV).

`Funga nezvezviporofita zvose zvakazadziswa sezvazvakafanotaurwa. Sei izvi zvichifanira kutibatsira kuvimba naMwari pamusoro pezvisati zvazadziswa—sekudzoka kwechipiri kwaJesu?`
