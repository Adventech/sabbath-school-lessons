### Verenga zveChidzidzo cheVhiki Ino
1 VaKorinde 2:2; Johani 1:1–5; 2 Timoti 3:16, 17; 2 Timoti 4:1–5; Isa. 40:1–5; Johani 17:20–23; 1 VaKorinde 1:10–13.

> <p>Ndima Yekuyeuka:</p>
> “Rugwaro rwose rwakafemerwa naMwari, uye runobatsira pakudzidzisa, pakutsiura, pakururamisa, pakurayira mukururama, kuti munhu waMwari ave akakwana, akagadzirirwa zvakakwana mabasa ose akanaka” (2 Timoti 3:16, 17, NKJV).

Mwari haambotikumbiri kuti titende tiri mapofu. Kusiyana naizvozvo, humbowo hwekuvapo Kwake hwakasimba. Zvisikwa zvoga, kunyange mushure memakore 6,000 echivi, zvinopupura chokwadi chekuti kune Mwari ane simba guru rekusika—tarisa mifananidzo kubva kuJames Webb Space Telescope—uye zvakare kuti ndiMwari werudo.

Asi pane zvimwe. Kunyange mushure mekunge Petro anzwa “inzwi rakabva kudenga, patakanga tinaye pagomo dzvene,” akazonyora kuti isu “tinewo shoko reuporofita rakasimba zvikuru, ramunoita zvakanaka kana muchirichenjerera, sechiedza chinopenya munzvimbo ine rima, kusvikira zuva ravira uye nyeredzi yemangwanani yabuda mumwoyo yenyu” (2 Petro 1:18, 19). Tingasanzwa inzwi raMwari sezvakaita Petro, asi isuwo tine “shoko reuporofita rakasimba zvikuru.”

Mwari akataura nesu kubudikidza nevaporofita Vake, vose vari muCanon, kureva avo vane zvinyorwa zviri muBhaibheri, uye vasiri muCanon, kureva vane zvinyorwa zvisiri muBhaibheri. Humbowo hwaakatipa hwakawanda kupfuura zvakakwana. Mubvunzo wasara ndewekuti: Takavhurika here kuti tihugamuchire?

Nhoroondo tsvene inoratidza kuti vazhinji havana kuvhurika. Dai isu, nenyasha dzaMwari, tisaita chikanganiso chimwe chetecho.

_*Dzidza chidzidzo chevhiki rino kugadzirira Sabata, Zvita 26._
