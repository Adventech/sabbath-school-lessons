---
title:  Chiporofita Chinosimudzira Kristu
date:  20/12/2026
---

Chiporofita inzira yaMwari yekutaurirana nevanhu Vake nekuvabatanidza naMwari. Hazvishamisi, saka, kuti Satani, dragoni, akatsamwira mukadzi, kureva kereke yaMwari, akaenda kunorwa nevakasara vembeu yake (ona Zvakazarurwa 12:17).

Ndezvipi zviratidzo zvinozivisa vakasara vembeu yemukadzi? Vanochengeta mirayiro uye vane uchapupu hwaJesu. Zvakazarurwa 19:10 inoti uchapupu uhwu ndi“mweya wechiporofita.”

`Verenga 1 VaKorinde 2:2 na VaGaratia 6:14. Pauro anoona sei chinangwa cheupenyu nebasa rake—chikonzero chikuru chaakararamira?`

Kunze kwezviitiko zvavo pachavo, vaapostora vakagamuchira Jesu pahuchapupu hwevaporofita nevarume vakarurama, avo mashoko avo akapararira mumazana emakore mazhinji. Nyika yechiKristu ine cheni yakazara uye yakakwana yehumbowo inomhanya muTestamende Yekare neTestamende Itsva: muTestamende Yekare, inonongedzera kuMuponesi aizouya; muTestamende Itsva, inoratidza Jesu semunhu akazadzisa zvinodiwa kuti ave Muponesi. Zvose izvi zvakakwana kusimbisa kutenda kweavo vanoda kutenda. “Chinangwa chaMwari chaiva chekusiya rudzi rwevanhu ruine mukana wakarurama wekukudziridza kutenda musimba raMwari neMwanakomana Wake nebasa reMweya Mutsvene.”—Ellen G. White, The Spirit of Prophecy, vol. 3, p. 182.

Munzira dzakawanda, nyaya yekutenda kunoponesa haisi yekungwara kwepfungwa; inyaya yetsika. Inyaya yemwoyo, kwete yemusoro chete. Kana vanhu vakavhara mwoyo yavo, pfungwa dzavo, zvinosuwisa, dzinozotevera.

`Verenga Johani 1:1–5. Ndima idzi dzinotiudzei pamusoro pekuti Jesu ndiAni?`

Kana Jesu, Shoko raMwari, aivapo kare, akazova nyama, uye akagara pakati pedu, zvinotevera kuti Jesu aiva chikamu chakakosha chemashoko akapiwa naMwari kuvaporofita Vake. Kunyange vaporofita vakatakura mashoko asina kutaura zvakananga nezvaJesu, vakaramba vakafemerwa noMweya Mutsvene.

`Funga pamusoro pekukura kwakaita nyika yose, wobva wafunga kuti Iye akasika zvose izvi, Jesu, ndiye zvakare akatifira. Tariro huru ipi yaunowana muchokwadi ichi?`
