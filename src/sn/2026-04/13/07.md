title:  Edzai Zvakare
date:  25/12/2026
---

Makore apfuura, kambani yeKellogg yakashandisa mutsara wekushambadza waibata pfungwa yezviyo zvayo zvemangwanani. Mukuedza kudzosa vatengi vakanga vatamira kune mamwe mabrand, yakavakurudzira kuti “varavire zvakare sokunge kekutanga.” Ko mutsara uyu ungashandawo here kuvaSeventh-day Adventist vakapedza nguva yakawanda vachidzidza pamusoro peBhaibheri naEllen G. White asi nguva shoma “vachiravira” zvinyorwa zvake? Kupinda masemina pamusoro paEllen G. White kunokupa pfungwa pamusoro peupenyu nebasa rake, asi kana usinga“raviri” wega, hauzokwanisi kunyatsoongorora uye kuona ropafadzo yemweya iri mumashoko ake euporofita. Vaya chete vanomuverenga, kunyanya vachifunga mamiriro ezvinyorwa, ndivo vanogona kunyatsonzwisisa zvatakapiwa naMwari.

Sezvatakaona muchikamu chino chegore, Ellen G. White anotisungirira pachokwadi cheBhaibheri uye anotibatanidza naMwari. Pane maropafadzo chaiwo ekuverenga mashoko ake euporofita.

Ongororo yakaitwa naRoger Dudley naDes Cummings, Jr., inoratidza zvinhu zvinofadza. Vaya vanoverenga zvinyorwa zvaEllen G. White nguva dzose vane hukama hwakadzama uye hwepedyo naJesu kupfuura avo vasingamuwanzoverenga; vane chivimbo chakasimba cheruponeso, kunamata kwemhuri kunoitwa nguva dzose, uye chido chekuti vapupurire vamwe. “Kashoma kuti ongororo itsvage humbowo hwakanyanya kutsigira mhedziso imwe chete,” vatsvakurudzi vaviri ava vakanyora. “Muongororo yekukura kwekereke, pachinhu chimwe nechimwe chine chekuita nemafungiro kana maitiro emunhu, nhengo inogara ichidzidza mabhuku aEllen G. White inowanzova pamusoro kupfuura nhengo inongomaverenga pano neapo kana isingamaverengi zvachose. . . . Chokwadi zvinoreva kuti kuverenga nguva dzose zvinyorwa zvaEllen White kunoita musiyano wakanaka muupenyu hwechiKristu nekupupura.”—Dudley and Cummings, “Who Reads Ellen White?” in Ministry, October 1982, p. 12.

Muchikamu chino chegore, tadzidza kuti Mwari anotaurirana kubudikidza nevaporofita Vake. Mashoko avo anotibatanidza naMwari uye anounza pesvedzero tsvene uye inotsvenesa mumativi ose eupenyu hwevanhu, kunyanya maererano nevimbiso yeruponeso iri munaJesu: “Ravirai muone kuti Jehovha akanaka” (Mapisarema 34:8). Anotidanira kumabasa matsvene, kushandurwa kweupenyu, evhangeri yemunhu pachake, uye upenyu hwekushumira vamwe. Izvi zvakakosha pakugadzirira kuzadzisa chikamu chedu mukuziviswa kwekupedzisira kunyika sezvatakamirira kuuya kwaJesu.
