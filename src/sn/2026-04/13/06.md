---
title:  Chiporofita Chinobatanidza Muviri munaKristu
date:  24/12/2026
---

`Verenga Johani 17:20–23 na 1 VaKorinde 1:10–13. Chikumbiro chikuru chiri pakati pezvikamu zviviri izvi zveMagwaro ndechei?`

Jesu paakanga ava pedyo nemuchinjikwa, mwoyo Wake wakabatwa nechido chimwe chikuru. Aida kuti vateveri Vake vose vave vamwe, sezvo Iye naBaba vari vamwe murudo, muchinangwa, uye mukuita. Kubatana kwakanyengetererwa naJesu kwaisava kufanana kusina upenyu. Jesu akanyengetera chiporofita chine simba chichazadziswa asati adzoka—muunganidzwa wakapfuma wevanhu vane mamiriro akasiyana, marudzi akasiyana, nemaonero akasiyana vachabatana maAri naBaba, kubudikidza nesimba reMweya Mutsvene. Muapostora Pauro akavakira padingindira iri paakakumbira vatendi veKorinde kuti vave nemafungiro mamwe, vataure chokwadi chimwe, uye vasava nekupatsanurana (1 VaKorinde 1:10). Asi sei kubatana kweuporofita uku kwakakosha?

Jesu akanyengetera achiti, “Kuti vose vave vamwe, semi Baba muri Mandiri, uye Ini Mamuri; kuti ivowo vave vamwe Matiri, kuti nyika itende kuti Makandituma” (Johani 17:21, NKJV).

Mwari anoda kuti varume nevakadzi vose vaponeswe, asi vasati vaponeswa, vanofanira kutenda munaJesu uye kutenda kuti Mwari akamutuma kuponesa nyika. Kana boka revanhu vakasiyana-siyana richiti Jesu ndiye Ishe neMuponesi wavo, vachidanana uye vachishumirana vasina kuzvitsvakira, ipapo nyika ichatenda kuti Jesu aiva Uyo waaiti Ndiye. Kubatana kwaidiwa naJesu kune chinangwa uye kune simba. Zvinosuwisa kuti, kunyange kubva pakutanga, masimba akatyisidzira kubatana ikoko. Ona, somuenzaniso, Mabasa 6:1. Uye chiKristu chakapatsanurwa zvikuru kubva ipapo!

Kubatana uku ibasa reMweya Mutsvene kubudikidza nezvipo zvaanogovera. VaEfeso 4:11–15 inotiudza kuti zvipo zveMweya Mutsvene zvinopiwa kuti vagadzirire vatendi uye kuvaka muviri waKristu, “kusvikira tose tasvika pakubatana kwekutenda nokuziva Mwanakomana waMwari, kuva munhu akakwana, pachiyero chekuzara kwaKristu” (VaEfeso 4:13, NKJV). Chipo chechiporofita, chimwe chezvipo zvikuru zveMweya, chakakosha pakubatanidza vanhu vaMwari, kunyanya sezvavanoswedera pakudzoka kwechipiri kwaJesu, kuitira kuti vatende kuna Ishe Jesu Kristu uye vaponeswe (Mabasa 16:31).

`Panguva iyo Seventh-day Adventist Church ingava rimwe remasangano echiProtestant ane vanhu vakasiyana-siyana zvikuru munyika, tingaratidza sei kubatana kunobatsira vanhu kutenda munaJesu? Masimba api anotyisidzira kubatana uku?`
