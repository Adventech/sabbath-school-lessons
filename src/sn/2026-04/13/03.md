---
title:  Chiporofita Chinosimbisa Bhaibheri
date:  21/12/2026
---

Pakupedzisira, Bhaibheri chibereko cheMweya Mutsvene kubudikidza nechipo chechiporofita. 2 Petro 1:21 inoratidza hurongwa hwakashandiswa naMwari: “Nokuti chiporofita hachina kumbouya nokuda kwemunhu, asi vanhu vatsvene vaMwari vakataura vachitungamirirwa noMweya Mutsvene” (NKJV).

`Verenga 2 Timoti 3:16, 17. Chikamu ichi chinosimbisa sei simba nekufemerwa kweMagwaro?`

Pasina Bhaibheri, vanhu vangadai vasina nzira yekufamba nayo vachinzvenga misungo yenyika ine chivi, uye izvi zvaizova nzira yekuparadzwa. Nhasi Bhaibheri riri kwose uye rinowanikwa mumhando dzakawanda, asi hazvina kugara zvakadaro. Somuenzaniso, munguva yaJesu, mipumburu yeTestamende Yekare chete ndiyo yaivepo yekuverengwa, uye kazhinji vapristi kana vaRabhi ndivo vaigona kuiverenga. Vanhuwo zvavo vaiwanzoshaya mukana wakananga wekuwana Magwaro. Shoko rinobva kuna Mwari raiva rinokosha zvikuru mumazuva iwayo.

`Verenga Ruka 24:27. Mushure mekumuka Kwake, Jesu akashandisei kubatsira vadzidzi vaviri vakanga vaora mwoyo kunzwisisa chinangwa Chake chakasiyana?`

Jesu, Muporofita mukuru kupfuura vose—iye oga ari Shoko (Johani 1:1–3)—akasimudzira Magwaro (Johani 7:38). Pakati pemaropafadzo anokosha echipo chechiporofita pane izvi: chinosimbisa Bhaibheri.

“Avo vakabayiwa mwoyo neMagwaro Matsvene senzwi raMwari, uye vanoda kutevera dzidziso dzaro, vanofanira kudzidza zuva nezuva, vachigamuchira kupisa kwemweya nesimba zuva nezuva, izvo zvakapihwa mutendi wese wechokwadi muchipo cheMweya Mutsvene.”—Ellen G. White, in Signs of the Times, March 8, 1910. Chipo chechiporofita chechokwadi chinogara chichisimudzira Magwaro. Chinogara chichinongedzera varume nevakadzi kuShoko raMwari semutemo wavo oga wekutenda nemaitiro.

`Sei Jesu akatanga avanongedzera kuMagwaro asati azviratidza kwavari kuti Ndiye ani? Izvi zvinofanira kutidzidzisei pamusoro pekuti Shoko rinofanira kuva hwaro hwakadii kwatiri?`
