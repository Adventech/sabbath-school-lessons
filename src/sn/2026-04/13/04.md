---
title:  Chiporofita iDetector yeChokwadi chaMwari
date:  22/12/2026
---

Vamwe vadzidzi vanofungidzira kuti kune mapoka echiKristu anodarika 45,000 munyika nhasi. Zvingaitika sei kuti kutenda kwakatanga naJesu Kristu kwakakura kukava masisitimu akawanda kudaro ekutenda akasiyana? Mhinduro iri mukududzirwa kwakasiyana-siyana kweMagwaro kwakagadzirwa nekufamba kwenguva. Zviri pachena kuti haangavi ose ari echokwadi, nokuti kazhinji zvinodzidziswa neimwe kereke zvinopikisana nezvinodzidziswa nedzimwe.

Kubva pakutanga (ona Gen. 2:16, 17; Gen. 3:1–5), kuvhiringidza kwaiva imwe yenzira huru dzaSatani dzekutungamirira vanhu kure nechokwadi chaMwari. Sezvaakaita kuna Adhamu naEvha, Satani anosanganisa chokwadi chakakwana nezvikanganiso kuti aise varume nevakadzi pasi pesimba rake. Satani akatsausa vanhu vaMwari kwemazera ose (2 Makoronike 36:14–16). Akatoedza kutsausa Jesu (Mat. 4:1–11), asi Jesu akapindura zvikumbiro zvaSatani ne “Zvakanyorwa” yakajeka (Mat. 4:4, NKJV).

`Verenga 2 Timoti 4:1–5. Zano ripi rakakosha rinopiwa naPauro kuna Timotio pamusoro peShoko raMwari? Sei aishingaira zvikuru kuti Timotio agadzirire pamweya zvinhu zvaizouya?`

Neziso reuporofita, Pauro akaona nguva iyo varume nevakadzi vaisazoda Shoko raMwari kana kuriteerera. Panzvimbo pezvo, vaizoenda kunotsvaga vadzidzisi vemweya vanovagadzirira zvavanoda kunzwa, nengano dzakagadzirwa nounyanzvi. Kune vakadaro, Mwari anotumira kunyengera kwakasimba kuti vatende nhema dzavaitsvaga. “Uye nokuda kweizvozvo Mwari achavatumira kunyengera kwakasimba, kuti vatende nhema, kuti vose vatongwe avo vasina kutenda chokwadi asi vakafadzwa nekusarurama” (2 VaTesaronika 2:11, 12, NKJV).

Asi kune avo vakavhurika, Jesu akavimbisa kuti vachaziva “chokwadi” (Johani 8:32). Chipo chechiporofita inzira yaMwari yekuparadzanisa chokwadi nekukanganisa, chiedza nerima.

Mune imwe pfungwa, tinogona kuziva chokwadi zviri nyore pakati pemasangano akasiyana aya. Kana usiri kuzorega Sabata rezuva rechinomwe, iro rakasimbiswa zvikuru neBhaibheri, unenge wabvisa zvikamu zvikuru-zvikuru zvezvaungasarudza, sezvo makereke mazhinji asingachengeti murayiro wechina.

Kana usingazotendi kuti vakafa vanoenda pakarepo kudenga kuna Jesu kana kuti vari kutsva mugehena zvino uye nokusingaperi—zvino chii?

Unenge wabvisa zvakare chikamu chikuru chezvasara.
