---
title:  Simba reHurongwa hweKereke
date:  02/12/2026
---

Hosea 12:13 inojekesa kuti “nemuporofita Jehovha akabudisa Israeri muIjipiti, uye nemuporofita akachengetedzwa” (NKJV). Kuchengetedza nekutungamirira vanhu vaMwari kwaiva chikamu chakakosha uye chekutanga chebasa revaporofita veBhaibheri.

Kana tikaverenga magwaro akadai sa Vatongi 4:4–8; 2 Makoronike 20:14–17; Ekisodho 12:1–3; na Deuteronomio 1:9–16, tinoona kuti vaporofita vaiva vakakosha pakuratidza nzira yaMwari yehutungamiri kuvanhu Vake. Munguva dzehondo nematambudziko, vaporofita ndivo vaishandiswa naMwari kupa kutungamirirwa, kurudziro, netariro kuvanhu Vake nevatungamiri vavo, sezvaakaita kubudikidza naJehazieri naDhebhora. Vaporofita vakapawo kutungamirirwa pamusoro pemitambo yekunamata nekushumira (Eks. 12:1–3). Pane dzimwe nguva, Mwari akataura kuvaporofita pamusoro pekutongwa nekurongwa kwerudzi. Muchidimbu, hapana chikamu cheupenyu hwaIsraeri chakanga chiri kunze kwekutungamirirwa naMwari kubudikidza nevatumwa Vake vaakasarudza.

`Ndezvipi zvimwe zvikamu zvakati zvakananga umo chipo chechiporofita chakabatsira kurongwa nehutungamiri hwekereke yekutanga yechiKristu? Mabasa 6:1–7; 1 VaKorinde 5:1–5; 1 VaKorinde 7:10–16; Tito 1:5.`

Fungidzira kutanga sangano idzva risina bhuku remashandiro uye risina mutungamiri anooneka. Sangano rakadaro chokwadi raizotarisana nematambudziko makuru. Munzira imwe, izvi ndizvo zvakanga zvakaita kereke yekutanga yechiKristu. Jesu akanga akwira kudenga uye akanga asisipo nevadzidzi Vake nenzira yaaive navo kare. Vaiva nemurayiro wakajeka kubva kwaari: “Endai munyika yose muparidze” (Mako 16:15, NKJV). Kunyange Vaapostora Gumi neVaviri vakanga vasarudzwa, pakanga pasina chimiro chehurongwa chakajeka chaizovabatsira kutanga.

Kereke yekutanga yakatarisana nematambudziko mazhinji, akadai sekurongwa kwekereke, chimiro chebasa remishoni, uye kudiwa kwekuziva nekutuka dzidziso dzenhema nekusazvibata pakati pavo. Muchokwadi, chipo chechiporofita chaishanda mukereke yekutanga chakaita basa rakakosha pakuchengetedzwa nekuvambwa kwayo (Mabasa 13:1, 2). Zvakaoma kufungidzira kuti kereke yekutanga yaizotanga sei pasina rubatsiro rwechipo chechiporofita pakuronga nekuifambisa mberi kubasa remishoni.

`Vamwe vanhu vanoita sekunge havavimbi ne“chitendero chakarongwa.” Zvisinei nematambudziko aripo, ndezvipi zvakanaka zvekuva chikamu chehurongwa hwekereke yakarongeka? Chii chaunogona kupa, uye chii chaunogona kuwana, nekuva chikamu chechinhu chikuru kupfuura iwe pachako?`
