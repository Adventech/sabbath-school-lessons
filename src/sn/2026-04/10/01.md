### Verenga zveChidzidzo cheVhiki Ino
Eks. 4:10–16; Ruka 8:22–25; Johani 3:1–10; Mabasa 16:25–34; 1 VaKorinde 5:1–5; Ruka 7:28.

> <p>Ndima Yekuyeuka:</p>
> “Nokuti Shoko raMwari ibenyu uye rine simba, rakapinza kupfuura munondo unocheka kumativi maviri, richibaya kusvikira parinoparadzanisa mweya nomweya, namajoini nomwongo, uye rinotonga mifungo nezvinangwa zvemwoyo” (VaHebheru 4:12, NKJV).

Musi waNovember 9, 1989, chirevo chaGünter Schabowski paTV muCommunist East Germany chakamutsa zviitiko zvakatungamirira kuwa kweBerlin Wall. Semutauriri wenhau weEast Germany, Schabowski akakanganisa mashoko ake pamusangano wenhau waiterevhizheni. Panzvimbo pekuzivisa kuti vanhu veEast Germany vangangobvumidzwa kushanyira Western Europe mushure mekuita nzira refu uye yakaoma yekuwana mavhiza akakodzera, akangoti vanhu veEast Germany vaizobvumidzwa kushanyira Western Europe. Mutori wenhau paakabvunza kuti mutemo mutsva uyu waizotanga kushanda riini, Schabowski akati, “Sekuziva kwangu, izvi zvinotanga kushanda . . . pakarepo, pasina kunonoka.” Mumaminitsi mashoma vanhu vakatanga kuwanda vachienda kumapoinzi emuganhu, vachida kupinzwa, nokuti hurumende yakanga yazvizivisa.

Vanhu vanowanzopihwa simba rekutaura vakamirira kambani, kana kunyange nyika. Asi ko vaya vanotaura vakamirira Mwari? Vhiki rino tichaongorora kuti vaporofita veBhaibheri vane simba ripi, uye kuti, kana riripo, simba ripi rine vaporofita vasina kunyora bhuku reBhaibheri, vakadai saNatani muporofita, Johani Mubhabhatidzi, uye hongu, kunyange Ellen G. White.

_*Dzidza chidzidzo chevhiki rino kugadzirira Sabata, Zvita 5._
