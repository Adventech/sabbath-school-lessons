---
title:  Simba reBhaibheri
date:  01/12/2026
---

Bhaibheri chibereko chekuratidzwa kweuporofita kwakafemerwa naMwari. Rinopindura mibvunzo mikuru yeupenyu uye ndiro nzira yakasarudzika yaMwari yekuzviratidza kuvanhu. Kubudikidza neMagwaro, tinodzidza zvinorehwa nekuvapo kwevanhu, hunhu nekuda kwaMwari, uye chinangwa chaMwari muupenyu hwedu. Bhaibheri ndiro chiyero chegoridhe chechokwadi, simba ratinofanira kushandisa pakuita sarudzo dzeupenyu. Ndiro hwaro hwemaonero emuKristu pamusoro penyika uye hwaro hwechokwadi chose chakaziviswa. Ndiro mutemo wekutenda nemaitiro omuKristu.

Tinogona kugamuchira kana kuramba Shoko raMwari, uye hatifaniri kusvika pakunyanyisa kwekupisa maBhaibheri edu, sezvakaitwa naMambo Jehoyakimi (ona Jer. 36:22–27), kuti tirirambe. Kuringoregeredza, kunyange tichiti tinotenda mariri, kunogonawo kuva dambudziko rakakura. Kana tikawana uye tikagamuchira Shoko raMwari, tinogona, saMambo Josia, kushandurwa uye kutungamirira vamwe kuna Mwari (ona 2 Madzimambo 22:10–13).

Nhasi vanhu vanowanzosvika kuMagwaro vachitsvaga zvikanganiso zvaro kana kurisekesa. Vamwe vanoona Bhaibheri serisingaenderani netsika dzemazuva ano—kazhinji harisi kuenderana nadzo, uye rinofanira kudaro. Vazhinji vanorishora nokuti rinopesana nezvinonzi “sainzi” kana mafambiro etsika.

MuchiKristu, Bhaibheri rinowanzoshandiswa chete kutsigira nzvimbo yakatobatwa pakudzidzisa, panzvimbo pekushandiswa sek nzira yekukura kwemweya uye kuvandudzwa kwemunhu. Vamwe vanodudzira Bhaibheri nenzira inoramba simba raro renhoroondo kana rekushanda muupenyu hwedu hwezuva nezuva.

Imwe yenzira dziri nyore, uye pamwe yakajairika zvikuru, dzekuramba simba reBhaibheri ndeyekuriregeredza uye kusatora nguva yekuverenga nekushandisa Magwaro muupenyu hwedu.

`Shoko raMwari rakataurwa rakava nemigumisiro ipi mumamiriro anotevera? Ona Jer. 38:1–4; Johani 3:1–10; na Mabasa 16:25–34.`

Munguva dzekare, zvinhu zvekunyorera zvaive zvishoma uye zvaidhura. Uyezve, vanhu vazhinji vaisagona kuverenga, saka Shoko raMwari rakataurwa kubudikidza nevaporofita Vake raive rakakosha.

Pasi pesimba reMweya Mutsvene, Shoko raMwari, ringava rakataurwa kana rakanyorwa, rinogona kubaya mwoyo kuti riratidze chivi uye riunze kuvandudzwa nekudzorerwa. Shoko rinotidanira kukutendeuka nekuteerera (Mabasa 5:29; onawo VaHebheru 4:12). Uye ndicho chikonzero chinoita kuti vanhu vazhinji vanofanira kuziva zviri nani varirambe. Havadi zvarinoti pamusoro peupenyu hwavo.
