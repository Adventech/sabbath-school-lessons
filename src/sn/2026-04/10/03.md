---
title:  Simba raJesu
date:  30/11/2026
---

`Hwaro hwesimba raJesu hwaiva chii? Humbowo hwesimba iri hwaiva hupi? Ona Mat. 21:23–27 na Johani 17:2.`

Jesu paaive achidzidzisa, vapristi vakauya kwaari vachida kuziva kuti ndiani akanga amupa simba rekuita basa iri. Mushure mezvose, Jesu akanga asina kudzidza kuzvikoro zvemunharaunda, uye akanga asina kudzidziswa muzvikoro zvechivanhu zvavaRabhi. Panzvimbo pekupindura mibvunzo yavo zvakananga, Jesu akavanongedzera kunzira yemhinduro, achivabvunza pamusoro pesimba risingarambiki rakaratidzwa naJohani Mubhabhatidzi. Nokuramba kupindura, vakaramba simba raMwari rakapfekedza basa raJohani, uye vakarambawo simba raJesu, uyo, saJohani, akanga atumwa naMwari.

`Ndima dzinotevera dzinotiudzeiwo pamusoro pesimba raJesu? Mat. 7:28, 29; Mako 1:21–27; Ruka 8:22–25; Ruka 9:1; Johani 5:25–27; na Johani 7:38.`

Simba rose nderaJesu seMusiki wevanhu (Johani 1:3) uye Muponesi (VaRoma 3:24). Ndiye Shoko raMwari. Simba raMwari raiva naJesu panguva yehupenyu nebasa Rake repanyika harigoni kutevedzerwa kana kugadzirwa nemunhu upi zvake. Dzidziso yaJesu yakashamisa vateereri nokuti aidzidzisa semunhu ane simba (Mat. 7:29).

Mabhuku ose eEvhangeri anonyora simba raJesu reuporofita rinobva kudenga. Jesu akaregerera zvivi (Mako 2:10, 11), akadzinga madhimoni (Mako 3:15), uye aiziva zvaifungwa nevanhu (Johani 2:24, 25). Akamutsa vakafa uye akapa upenyu husingaperi (Johani 10:28). Paaiva panyika seMudzikinuri wedu, Jesu akashandisa simba raakapiwa naMwari Baba (Johani 17:2), uye aigara achitsamira pana Baba uye achishanda pamwe Navo (Johani 5:19), achiwanzodzokera kuMagwaro, ayo aaiti anopupura pamusoro Pake. Jesu aivawo nesimba rakazara uye aigona kupa simba iroro kuvadzidzi Vake (Mako 6:7). SeShoko raMwari, uchapupu Hwake hwakauyawo kuvaporofita vaakataura kubudikidza navo (Zvakazarurwa 19:10).

`Kunyange paine humbowo hwose hwakapihwa naJesu, vamwe vanhu vakaramba kumugamuchira. Tingangwarira sei kuti tisawira mumusungo mumwe chete wekusatenda, tichifunga nezvezvikonzero zvose zvatinazvo zvekutenda?`
