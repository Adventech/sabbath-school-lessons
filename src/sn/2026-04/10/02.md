---
title:  Kumiririra Simba raMwari
date:  29/11/2026
---

Usati wapa munhu mvumo yekutaura akakumiririra, haungadi here kuva nechokwadi chekuti wasarudza munhu waunovimba naye? Zvaizokosha kuti munhu iyeye azive maonero ako uye aataure nokutendeka.

`Verenga Ekisodho 2:11–15 na Ekisodho 3:1–3, 10–13. Nguva yaMosesi semufudzi yakamugadzirira sei kuva mutauriri waMwari?`

Mosesi akanga apedza makore 40 ekutanga ehupenyu hwake achiwana dzidzo yepamusoro-soro yaigona kupihwa neIjipiti, asi izvi hazvina kumugadzirira kuva mutumwa waMwari. Aifanira kupedza mamwe makore 40 murenje ari mufudzi, asina zvinomuvhiringidza zvenyika. Nenzira dzakawanda aifanira kubvisa zvakawanda zvaakanga adzidza somuchinda. Asati ashanda naMwari mukusunungura Israeri, Mosesi aifanira kuziva Mwari waaizotaura akamumiririra.

`Verenga Ekisodho 4:10–16. Tingadzidzei kubva mukukurukurirana uku pakati paMwari naMosesi pamusoro pekudanwa kunoitwa vaporofita uye mashandiro avo?`

Mosesi akakumbirwa kuita basa raMwari, Aroni achitora basa rekuva muromo waMosesi (Eks. 4:16; onawo Eks. 7:1). Sezvaakaita kuna Mosesi naAroni, Mwari anobatsira vaporofita Vake kuwana nzira yakanakisisa yekusvitsa shoko raanovapa.

Mosesi aiziva kuti akanga ambokundikana kare. Akarangarira kusangana kwake kwekupedzisira nevaIsraeri, apo simba rake rakanga rabvunzwa (Eks. 2:14). Mwari akanzwisisa kutya kwaMosesi. Akamubata nemwoyo murefu mukutya kwake. Zvikonzero zvose zvekuzvipembedza zvakanga zvabviswa, asi kunyange ipapo Mosesi akaramba kudanwa. Kutya kwake kwakanga kwava kusanduka kuva kusatenda. Nokuramba kwake, akanga ava kubvunza kugona kwaMwari kumushandisa. Ipapo Mwari akamupa murayiro wakasimba kuti muporofita aizeza atange kufamba (Eks. 3:14–17).

`Ungasava muporofita, asi Mwari akakudanei kuti umuitire muupenyu hwako? Nzira yakanakisisa yekuzvigadzirira kudanwa uku ndeipi?`
