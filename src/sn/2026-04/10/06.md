---
title:  Vaporofita veCanon neVasiri veCanon
date:  03/12/2026
---

`Verenga 1 Makoronike 29:29 na 2 Makoronike 9:29. Chii chaunoona pamusoro pevamwe vaporofita vanotaurwa mundima mbiri idzi?`

Paiva nevaporofita vazhinji vakadai saNatani, Gadhi, Ahija, naIdho, avo vaizivikanwa sevaporofita, asi mabhuku avo haana kuva chikamu cheBhaibheri.

`Verenga 1 Madzimambo 11:29–39 na Ruka 7:28. Magwaro aya anodzidzisei pamusoro pesimba revaporofita vasiri veCanon?`

Kunyange tisingazivi kuti sei Mwari akabvumira kuti mashoko evamwe vaporofita aiswe muBhaibheri ukuwo evamwe achisiyiwa kunze, tinoziva kuti vaporofita vasiri veCanon, avo vasina kuiswa muMagwaro, vakafemerwawo zvizere naMwari. Bhaibheri rinodzidzisa kuti zvipo zvemweya, kusanganisira chipo chechiporofita, zvicharamba zviripo kusvikira pakuguma kwenguva. Bhaibheri rinofanotaura kuti kuchava nevaporofita vasiri veCanon mushure menguva yeBhaibheri, “kuti vatsvene vagadzirirwe basa reushumiri, kuvakwa kwemuviri waKristu, kusvikira tose tasvika pakubatana kwekutenda” (VaEfeso 4:12, 13, NKJV), uye vachavapo “zuva guru uye rinotyisa raJehovha risati rasvika” (Joere 2:31, NKJV).

Mushure mekunge Dhavhidhi aita upombwe nekuuraya, Mwari akatuma muporofita Natani kuti amutarisane naye (2 Sam. 12:7–14). Kunyange Natani asina kunyora bhuku reBhaibheri, Dhavhidhi haana kubvunza simba raNatani, nokuti simba rezvinyorwa kana mashoko evaporofita rinobva pakufemerwa kwavo, kwete pakuti vakaiswa here kana kuti havana kuiswa muCanon yeBhaibheri. Zvisinei, vaporofita vechokwadi vose vasiri veCanon, kunyanya vaporofita vakazouya mushure menguva yeBhaibheri—uye kunyanya muporofita wemazuva ano akadai saEllen G. White—vaizviona vari pasi pesimba reBhaibheri. Mashoko avo aifanira kuedzwa neBhaibheri uye haatsivi Bhaibheri sesosi yekutenda nedzidziso.

`Ellen G. White akanyora kuti, “Ishe vanoda kuti mudzidze maBhaibheri enyu. Haana kupa chiedza chipi nechipi chekuwedzera kuti chitore nzvimbo yeShoko Rake. Chiedza ichi ndechekuunza pfungwa dzakavhiringidzika kuShoko Rake, iro, kana rikadyiwa nekugayiwa, rinenge ropa roupenyu hwemweya.”—Selected Messages, book 3, p. 29. Isu sekereke tinoteerera zvakadii mashoko aya ake?`
