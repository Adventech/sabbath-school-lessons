---
title:  Kutaura Nesimba
date:  04/12/2026
---

Vaporofita vanotaura nesu nesimba uye nekukosha nokuti Mwari akavasarudza sevaturwa Vake. Kune vaporofita veCanon, kureva avo vane zvinyorwa zvakava chikamu cheBhaibheri; vaporofita vasiri veCanon, kureva avo vasina kunyora bhuku rakaiswa muBhaibheri; uye vaporofita vasiri veCanon vakazouya mushure menguva yeBhaibheri, kureva vaporofita vanoonekwa mushure menguva yeTestamende Itsva. Vose vakafemerwa zvakaenzana, uye mashoko avo aiva nechiratidzo chesimba raMwari.

Ellen G. White ari muboka revaporofita vasiri veCanon vakazouya mushure menguva yeBhaibheri. Asi maSeventh-day Adventist haatarisi zvinyorwa zvake sechinhu chinowedzerwa kuBhaibheri kana chakaenzana naro. Zvisinei, zvinyorwa zvake zvine kukosha uye zvinotaura nesimba, pfungwa yakataurwa naEllen G. White pachake: “Mutsamba idzi dzandinonyora, muzvipupuriro zvandinopa, ndiri kukupai izvo Ishe zvavakandiratidza. Handinyori nyaya imwe mupepa ndichitaura pfungwa dzangu chete. Izvo ndizvo zvandakazarurirwa naMwari muchiratidzo—mwaranzi inokosha yechiedza inopenya ichibva pachigaro choumambo.”—Selected Messages, book 1, p. 29.

Zvichienderana neBhaibheri, Ellen G. White anoratidza chokwadi chechivi uye kudiwa kwedu Jesu, tariro yedu yoga yeupenyu husingaperi, iyo inopiwa nenyasha kune vose vanoigamuchira nokutenda—rimwe dingindira rine simba mumashoko ake.

Kubudikidza nemashoko ake, kana kuti mashoko aMwari kubudikidza naye, tinoona hondo pakati pezvakanaka nezvakaipa, ichitanga nekuputsika kwaLucifer kudenga, ichipfuura kusvika pakuguma kwenguva, apo chivi nehuipi, pamwe naSatani nengirozi dzake dzakawa, zvichaparadzwa nokusingaperi. Zvinyorwa zvaEllen G. White zvinopa kunzwisisa pamusoro pemisimboti yeBhaibheri muchirevo chenguva yekupedzisira. Anotaura, somuenzaniso, pamusoro penjodzi yekubatanidza kereke nehurumende uye kukosha kwerusununguko rwechitendero; anoyambira pamusoro pekuora kwetsika; anosimudzira murayiro waMwari nekukosha kwawo; uye kunyange hazvo kunzwisisa kwedu, semuenzaniso, “chiratidzo chechikara” kusingabvi pazvinyorwa zvake, mashoko ake anotipa kunzwisisa kunoshamisa pamusoro pezviitiko zvekupedzisira.

Anosimbisawo kushandiswa kwechiKristu muhupenyu hwezuva nezuva. Mashoko ake pamusoro pemweya, utano, dzidzo, hukama hwemhuri, kurera vana, hupenyu hwetsika, nezvimwe, akabatsira vazhinji kuchinja mararamiro avo uye nokudaro kuwana upenyu huri nani pamatanho mazhin ji.

Zvinyorwa zvaEllen G. White hazvisi muBhaibheri, asi Mwari akamushandisa kutungamirira vatendi vari kurarama mumazuva okupedzisira enhoroondo yepasi. Zvinyorwa zvake zvichiri kutaura nesimba reuporofita uye nekukosha.
