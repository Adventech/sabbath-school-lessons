title:  Kudanwa kuBasa reUporofita
date:  09/10/2026
---

Mwari anodana vaporofita panguva dzakasarudzika nezvinangwa zvakakosha. Vaivapo nemashoko aidiwa panguva iyoyo. Muchirevo ichi, maSeventh-day Adventist anotenda kuti Ellen G. White aiva mumwe wevanhu vakaratidza kushanda kwechipo chechiporofita munguva yanhasi, achishanda semuporofita asiri weMagwaro matsvene, kureva kuti haana kunyora chikamu cheBhaibheri; zvimwe pamusoro peizvi zvichauya muzvidzidzo zvinotevera. Ellen Harmon [White] akagamuchira kudanwa kwake kwekutanga muna December 1844, mavhiki mashoma mushure mekunge maMillerite Adventist atarisira kuti Jesu aizodzoka maererano na Danieri 8:14: “Kusvikira pamazuva ane zviuru zviviri namazana matatu; ipapo nzvimbo tsvene ichanatswa.”

Kunyange William Miller asina kumbotara zuva chairo, vateveri vake vakazopedzisira vatarisa Gumiguru 22, 1844, maererano nekuverengwa kwenguva yeBhaibheri yeZuva reYananiso, sezuva raizodzoka Kristu panyika. Kutenda uku kwakauyisa tarisiro huru kuvanhu zviuru nezviuru muNorth America nedzimwe nzvimbo dzenyika. Nenguva pfupi vaizobatana neMuponesi wavo kudenga. Miller pachake akazadzwa nemufaro mukuru nekushingaira sezvo nguva yavaitenda kuti Advent yainge yaswedera.

Asi Kristu haana kuoneka, uye vatendi veMillerite vakasangana nechimwe chezviitiko zvinorwadza zvikuru munhoroondo, chinonzi Great Disappointment.

Mukati memamiriro ezvinhu aya, Mwari akapa Ellen Harmon [White] chiratidzo chake chekutanga. Shoko guru rechiratidzo ichi raiva rekukurudzira iye pamwe nevamwe vatendi vakanga vaodzwa mwoyo kuti Mwari akanga achiri pamwe navo, kunyange hazvo vakanga vadudzira zvisiri izvo. Chiratidzo ichi chakaratidza nzira yaibva panyika ino ichienda kuJerusarema rekudenga. Panzira iyi paiva nevatendi veAdvent vaifamba vachienda kuGuta Dzvene. Uye “kana vakaramba vakatarira kuna Jesu, uyo aiva pamberi pavo, achivatungamirira kuguta, vaiva vakachengeteka.”—Ellen G. White, Spiritual Gifts, vol. 2, p. 31.

Chiratidzo ichi chaiva nhau dzakanaka. Mwari akanga ava kutanga kugadzira sangano idzva kubva mukuvhiringidzika kwekuodzwa mwoyo. Jesu akanga achiri navo. Akanga achiri kudzoka. Akanga achiri kuvatungamirira munzira yeruponeso. Vaingofanira kuramba maziso avo “akatarira” kwaAri. Vaiva nezvikonzero zvikuru zvekusasiya tariro, kunyange pakati pekuvhiringidzika nekusuwa kukuru.

Saka Ellen G. Harmon [White], musikana ane makore gumi nemanomwe, akadanwa kubasa reuporofita. Nzira yake yakanga isiri nyore, kunyanya pakutanga.

Kunyange chipo ichi chakazogamuchirwa zvishoma nezvishoma sechokwadi, Ellen G. White aitya kuva mutumwa waMwari. Kuva muporofita waMwari hakuna kumbova nyore kana chinhu chinotsvakwa nevanhu nokuda kwekuremekedzwa. Saka Ellen White aizozviisa sei pasi pekudanwa kwaMwari?

Vhiki rinouya tichatarisa zvakadzama basa rakakosha nematambudziko akasangana nevaporofita veTestamende Yekare, mamwe acho achiratidzwawo muupenyu nebasa raEllen G. White, sezvatichadzidza Chishanu chinouya.
