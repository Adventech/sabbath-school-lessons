---
title:  Isaya, Muporofita weVhangeri
date:  06/10/2026
---

Isaya, muporofita mukuru weTestamende Yekare, akagamuchira kudanwa kwake kubasa reuporofita zvakananga kubva kuna Mwari (ona Isa. 6:1–8). Akakurirwa nehutsvene nehukuru hwaMwari, Isaya akaona kusakodzera kwake. Panguva iyoyo, serafi akabhururuka achiuya kwaIsaya aine zimoto rakabva paaritari yekudenga uye “akachenesa” zvivi zvake. Ipapo Mwari akabvunza kuti, “Ndiani wandichatuma, uye ndiani achatimiririra?” Mhinduro yaIsaya inofanira kuva yeduwo: “Ndiri pano; nditumei” (Isa. 6:8).

Kudanwa kwaIsaya kwakauya panguva yakakosha munhoroondo yaIsraeri. Kunyange zvazvo aiva nezvikanganiso zvake, basa rekuyambira Israeri nevatungamiri vayo pamusoro pezvivi zvavo raiva rakakosha zvikuru. Chiporofita chaIsaya chine ndima dzakakosha dzeMagwaro, dzinopa kunzwisisa kwakadzama pamusoro pehunhu hwaMwari, chimiro chevanhu, uye chirongwa cheruponeso chiri kufambira mberi. Muchokwadi, chiporofita chinonyanya simba chinoratidza kwete chete rufu rwaJesu asiwo zvakaitwa nerufu irworwo panyika yose chinowanikwa muna Isaya, muchitsauko chinozivikanwa zvikuru, Isaya 53.

`Verenga Isaya 53. Chiporofita ichi chinoshamisa, chakanyorwa anopfuura makore mazana mashanu muchinjikwa usati wavapo, chakaratidza sei nesimba rufu rwaKristu nokuda kwedu? Chiporofita ichi chinofanira kutidzidzisei pamusoro pechipo chechiporofita?`

Zviporofita zvaIsaya zvakafanotaura chinangwa nebasa raMesia (Isa. 53:3–5). Zvakatsanangura kuti sei Kristu “akatungamirirwa segwayana rinoenda kunobayiwa” (Isa. 53:7, NIV) uye kuti chibayiro Chake chekuyananisira chakaponesa sei vanhu Vake kubva muzvivi zvavo. Chikamu chekutsiva kwedu pakufa kwaKristu chinoratidzwa pachena ipapo, chichiratidza hwaro hweyananiso yakaitirwa vanhu vose.

“Isu tose takatsauka samakwai; mumwe nomumwe wedu akatsaukira kunzira yake; asi Jehovha akaisa pamusoro pake kuipa kwedu tose” (Isa. 53:6). Hunhu hwechivi hwakabata vanhu vose hunoratidzwa pano; uyewo chimiro chemuchinjikwa, mhinduro yechivi, chinoratidzwawo pano. Chitsauko ichi, pamwe kupfuura chimwe chipi zvacho muTestamende Yekare neItsva, chinoratidza chirongwa cheruponeso nenzira yakajeka uye isingavhiringidzi.

Zvisinei, kudanwa kwaIsaya kweuporofita kwaisava nyore. Vanhu vaiona mashoko ake akaoma ekuporofita kuparadzwa kwavo seasingatendeki, sezvinoitawo vazhinji nhasi kana vachiyambirwa pamusoro pemigumisiro yechivi (VaRoma 6:23). Inzwi reuporofita raIsaya renguva yake nerenguva yedu rakajeka (ona Isa. 2:4; Isa. 5:20). Kuramba kwerudzi kuita kururamisira nekuvandudzwa ndiko kwakatungamirira kuparadzwa kweJerusarema. Chivi chimwe chetecho chicharatidza kuparadzwa kwevakaipa vepanyika kana Kristu adzoka.

`Dzokera muna Isaya 53 wonyora pasi zvinhu zvose zvinoitwa naJesu nokuda kwedu, sezvazvinoratidzwa mundima idzodzo. Tariro huru ipi yaunogona kutora iwe pachako kubva pane zvakaziviswa nemuporofita pamusoro pezvakaitwa nerufu rwaKristu nokuda kwedu?`
