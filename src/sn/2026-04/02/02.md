---
title:  Abrahama, Mudziviriri weSungano
date:  04/10/2026
---

Kubva muna Genesisi kusvika kuna Zvakazarurwa, Mwari agara aine vakasara vakatendeka vakagamuchira kudanwa kwekuteerera vachipindura nyasha Dzake (ona Jer. 31:7, 31–34). Somuenzaniso, makore akati wandei mushure mekupararira kwevanhu kubva paBhabheri, Mwari akasarudza Abhuramu, uyo akazova Abrahama, kuti ave muenzaniso werutendo rwesungano, kwete kumhuri yake chete asi kumazera ose aizouya. Akakomberedzwa nekutsauka pakutenda nekusagadzikana kwetsika, Abhuramu akaramba akatendeka kukudanwa kwaMwari. Ona Gen. 12:1–9.

Mwari akazivisa kuna Abrahama zviga zvesungano uye akamuratidza kuti ruponeso rwevanhu rwaizoitwa sei kuburikidza nechibayiro chaKristu (Gen. 15:6–17). Kuti aedze kutenda kwaAbrahama uye aratidze chibayiro chemunguva yemberi cheMwanakomana waMwari, Abrahama akadanwa kuti ape Isaka, mwanakomana wake, sechibayiro kuna Mwari (Gen. 22:1–17). Uyu waiva muedzo unorwadza zvikuru kuna baba vekutenda. Funga nezvezvikonzero zvose zvaaigona kushandisa kuti arambe murayiro wakajeka waMwari, asi akateerera nokutendeka. Mwari akamisa ruoko rwaAbrahama kuti asauraya Isaka uye akapa gondohwe panzvimbo yake.

Havasi vaporofita vose vakadanwa naMwari kuti vayambire nezvekuparadzwa kwaizouya. Zvisinei, vose vakadanwa kuti vadzorere vanhu pakutendeka, uye kazhinji kuti vayambire pamusoro pekutsauka pakutenda, pamusoro pekudzvinyirirwa kwevarombo nevanoshayiwa, uye pamusoro pekutyora sungano nezvayairaira.

Chinonyanya kukosha pane zvose ndechekuti vaporofita vaifanira kuratidza vimbiso yeruponeso runowanikwa munaMesia. Uchapupu hunoramba huripo hwevaporofita veBhaibheri uye hweMweya Wake munguva yedu yapashure pevaapostora hunotidanira kuti tizive uye tiyambire vamwe nezvedambudziko guru richauya panyika. Asi pakati peyambiro iyoyo pane nhau dzakanaka dzerudzikinuro rwevanhu vakatendeka kuna Jesu, avo vakaramba vari muukama hwesungano Naye (Zvakazarurwa 12:17).

`Verenga Genesisi 15:6, VaGaratia 3:6–9, na VaHebheru 11:8. Chii muupenyu nehunhu hwaAbrahama chakaita kuti akodzere basa raMwari, kunyange hazvo zviri pachena kuti aiva munhu ane zvikanganiso?`

Pane kufanana kwakawanda pakati penguva yaAbrahama nenguva yedu. Sezvo kunamata zvisiri Mwari nekunamata zvifananidzo zvakanga zvakazara munguva yaAbrahama, saizvozvowo nhasi pfungwa dzakawanda dziri munyika dzinokwikwidzana dziri kutsvaga kubvisa kana kudzikisira chiKristu. Kusiyana netsika zhinji dziri munyika, vanhu vaMwari nhasi vanodanwa kuti varatidze mufananidzo Wake nokutendeka mukudyidzana kwavo kwezuva nezuva nevamwe, kusanganisira kuparidza mashoko engirozi nhatu (Zvakazarurwa 14:6–12).

`Rugwaro runoti Abhuramu “akatenda Jehovha; akamutarira izvozvo sokururama” (Gen. 15:6). Unoona sei vimbiso yevhangeri ichiratidzwa nesimba mundima iyi? Tariro ipi yaunogona kutora kubva mairi iwe pachako?`
