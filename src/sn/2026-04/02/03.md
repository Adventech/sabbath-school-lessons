---
title:  Shoko raEria
date:  05/10/2026
---

`Verenga 1 Madzimambo 18:19–39. Chii chatinogona kuona munyaya iyi pamusoro pekuti basa reuporofita rinogona kushanda sei?`

Kudanwa kwaEria nematauriro ake zvakaratidza basa rake semutumwa waMwari. Kuti amise kutsauka pakutenda kwaIsraeri, Mwari akatuma Eria kuna mambo Ahabhi kuti azivise nzara yaizotora makore akawanda. Mwari akashandisa dambudziko iri kuti atendeutse vanhu Vake kubva pakunamata zvifananidzo uye kuvadzorera mukuteerera. Mambo nenyika yose vakapomera muporofita Eria mhosva yedambudziko ravo uye vakatsvaga kumuuraya.

PaGomo reKarimeri, Eria akatarisana nevaporofita vaBhaari naAshera. Mutumwa waMwari akaita chikumbiro chakazadzwa noMweya kuna Israeri yakanga yanyura mukunamata zvifananidzo kuti varambe vamwari vavo venhema uye vadzokere kuna Jehovha. Vanhu vakaziva kuti sarudzo dzavo dzakanga dzavatungamirira mukutsauka kwenyika yose pakutenda. Vakanga zvino vagadzirira kugamuchira Mwari waEria zvakare, uyo akaratidza kuti aiva mukuru kupfuura vamwari vavo venhema, avo muchokwadi vaisatombovapo.

Nguva pfupi Kristu asati auya kechipiri, Mwari anoraira kereke Yake kuti iparidze zvakare shoko raEria, uye kuti vanhu vatendeuke kubva “kuzvifananidzo” zvavo vagadzirire kumira pazuva guru raIshe. Mwari achiri kutaurirana nevanhu Vake kubudikidza nenzira dzaakasarudza. Shoko Rake nechipo chechiporofita zvinopa kuraira, yambiro, zano, uye kurudziro.

“Kumutsiridzwa kwekunamata kwechokwadi pakati pedu ndicho chinhu chikuru uye chinonyanya kukurumidza chatinoda. Kutsvaga izvi kunofanira kuva basa redu rekutanga. Panofanira kuva nekushingaira kukuru kutsvaga ropafadzo raIshe, kwete nokuti Mwari haadi kutipa ropafadzo Rake, asi nokuti isu hatina kugadzirira kurigamuchira.”—Ellen G. White, Selected Messages, book 1, p. 121.

`Verenga Maraki 4:5, 6. Shoko raEria rinokanganisa sei dzimba dzevanhu vanotevera Mwari?`

Kumutsiridzwa kwechokwadi kunotangira mumwoyo nemumba. Mhuri ndiyo hwaro hwekereke uye chinhu chakakosha pakuvakwa kwenharaunda. Kumutsiridzwa mumba kunoparadzira rudo rwekudenga kukereke, kunharaunda, uye kunyika. Ichi ndicho chinangwa chaMwari pamusha wechiKristu. Kumutsiridzwa kunokurudzirawo vanhu vaMwari kuita basa remishoni.
