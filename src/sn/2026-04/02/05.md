---
title:  Danieri, Muoni Akatendeka
date:  07/10/2026
---

VaKristu vazhinji vanoziva nyaya ya Danieri 1, umo akamira akasimba pamitemo yeutano yaakanga adzidziswa achiri mwana. Danieri aiva muenzaniso wekutendeka (Dan. 1:8). Paakapiwa zvokudya zvinonaka zveumambo, akaramba achisimbirira kudya chikafu chinobva muzvirimwa uye kunwa mvura chete, panzvimbo pekudya kubva patafura yamambo (Dan. 1:12–16). Uyu waiva muedzo mudiki kana uchienzaniswa nezvaizouya gare gare, semuna Danieri 6, apo kutendeka kwake kwaigona nyore kumutorera upenyu.

`Verenga Danieri 2:46–48; Danieri 4:18; Danieri 5:13, 14; na Danieri 6:25–27. Ndima idzi dzinotiudzei pamusoro pemhando yeuchapupu hwaDanieri kune vahedheni vakamupoteredza?`

Tarisa uchapupu hwakapiwa nevahedheni ava pamusoro pesimba raMwari waDanieri. Kunyange nyaya idzi dzakasiyana, dzose dzinoratidza musimboti mukuru: kuburikidza neupenyu hwedu, nokutendeka kwedu, uye nesimba raMwari richishanda matiri uye kuburikidza nesu, vamwe vanogona uye vanofanira kudzidza pamusoro paMwari wekudenga, uye kuti Iye ndi“Mwari wavamwari, naIshe wamadzimambo.”

Uyewo, nokuti dingindira rinodzokororwa muzviporofita zvaDanieri kutongwa, richiguma nekuuya kwechipiri kwaKristu, iko kukunda kukuru kwaMwari, zvinoreva zita raDanieri, “Mwari ndiye mutongi wangu,” zvakakodzera zvikuru. Zvinyorwa zvake zvinofanirwa kudzidzwa nokungwarira uye kuteererwa nokuti zvine chekuita nenguva yedu. Zviporofita zvaDanieri zvinoramba zvichitiyeuchidza kuti tigare takangwarira uye tichengete mirayiro yaMwari, kunyange muzvinhu “zvidiki” zveupenyu.

Uyewo, semuporofita, Danieri akafanotaura kumuka nekudonha kwemarudzi uye migumisiro yekusateerera kwavo Mwari. Kubudikidza nezviratidzo zvake zveuporofita, Danieri akaratidza chirongwa chaMwari uye kukunda kwekupedzisira kweumambo hwaMwari (Dan. 7:13, 14).

Funga, somuenzaniso, nezva Danieri 2, yakanyorwa chikamu chemiyenzaniso yaKristu asati auya. Danieri akatsanangura nemazvo kumuka nekudonha kwehumambo hukuru hwepasi rose , kusvika kunyange kunguva yedu, nguva yetsoka nezvigunwe zvemufananidzo, zvichiratidza pachena marudzi eEurope yemazuva ano. Sezvinodzidziswa zvakajeka nechiporofita, “vachasanganiswa nerudzi rwevanhu; asi havazobatani, sezvo dare risingasanganiswi nevhu” (Dan. 2:43). Ichi chiporofita chinoshamisa pamusoro peEurope, kunyange nanhasi, umo, zvisinei nekuroorana pakati pemarudzi uye kuedza kwose kwekubatana, kondinendi iyi inoramba iri marudzi akasiyana ane mitemo, tsika, uye magariro akasiyana. Kunyange makore mashoma Soviet Union isati yaputsika, hapana sangano revasori rakaona kuti zvaizoitika; asi chiporofita chaDanieri chinotipa zvikonzero zvakasimba zvekuvimba nevaporofita vaMwari.

`Humambo hwose huri muna Danieri 2 hwakauya hukapfuura sezvahwakafanotaurwa. Izvi zvinofanira kutidzidzisei pamusoro pekuvimba nechiporofita chinotaura nezvekuuya kweumambo hwaMwari husingaperi hunotevera (Dan. 2:44)?`
