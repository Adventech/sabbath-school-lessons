### Verenga zveChidzidzo cheVhiki Ino
Gen. 15:6; VaGaratia 3:6–9; 1 Madzimambo 18:19–39; Isaya 53; Dan. 2:46–48; Mako 1:2–5; Mat. 11:1–6.

> <p>Ndima Yekuyeuka:</p>
> “Ndakanzwawo inzwi raIshe richiti: ‘Ndiani wandichatuma, uye ndiani achatimiririra?’ Ipapo ndikati, ‘Ndiri pano! Nditumei’ ” (Isaya 6:8, NKJV).

Munguva dzeBhaibheri, vaporofita vaiwanzoita basa guru munhoroondo yevanhu vaMwari. Dzimwe nguva vaive pakati pezvaiitika, vachitora nzvimbo dzine simba, uye dzimwe nguva dzakakosha zvikuru, muupenyu hwevanhu.

Zvisinei, kuva muporofita kwaiva kudanwa kwakaoma. Upenyu hwavo hwaiwanzova husiri nyore, asi huzere nekusuwa, kurambwa, kusanzwisiswa, uye kushungurudzika.

Kazhinji vaporofita vaMwari vaisakurumidza kugamuchira kudanwa kwavo kubasa reuporofita. Uye hazvishamisi. Vaporofita vazhinji vaipikiswa zvikuru nevanhu vaifanira kunge vakagamuchira mashoko avo. Vaporofita vaMwari vaisateererwa, vaisekwa, vairohwa, kana kuiswa mujeri. Vamwe vaitemwa nematombo kana kuvhimwa semhuka dzesango. Vazhinji vakaurayiwa nokuda kwebasa ravo.

Nhasi, muchizvarwa chakazadzwa nemhirizhonga, makaro, kusazvibata, kudzvinyirira vamwe, nezvimwe zvose zvinofurirwa naSatani, inzwi reuporofita rinowanzorambwa kana kungoregeredzwa. Tinodanwa kuti titeerere inzwi revaporofita vaMwari uye tive vatumwa vetariro yatakapiwa kubudikidza nevaporofita.

Vhiki rapfuura takadzidza kuti, mushure meKuwa muEdeni, Mwari akashanda kuti adzorere kutaurirana nevanhu. Vhiki rino tichaongorora dzimwe nguva huru munhoroondo apo Mwari akadana vaporofita kuti vadzorere vanhu Vake kumashoko Ake enyika yakawira muchivi.

_*Dzidza chidzidzo chevhiki rino kugadzirira Sabata, Gumiguru 10._
