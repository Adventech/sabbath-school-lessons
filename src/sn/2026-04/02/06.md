---
title:  Johani Mubhabhatidzi, Kugadzirira Vanhu
date:  08/10/2026
---

`Verenga Mako 1:2–5. Chinangwa chebasa raJohani Mubhabhatidzi chaiva chei?`

Evhangeri yaMako inotizivisa kuti kuuya kwaJohani Mubhabhatidzi kwaiva kuzadziswa kwechiporofita chaIsaya (Isa. 40:3). Kukura kwaJohani kubva paudiki kusvika pakuva murume kwakanga kuchiratidzwa nekutendeka kuna Mwari. Satani akatya kurasikirwa nesimba rake pamusoro pevanhu mushure mekunge anzwa inzwi raJohani murenje (ona Ellen G. White, The Desire of Ages, p. 224). Huipi hwevateereri vaJohani hwakafumurwa nenzira yakaita kuti Satani adedere. Simba raSatani pamusoro paIsraeri rakanga rava kutyisidzirwa. Kakawanda akaedza, asi akakundikana, kutsausa Mubhabhatidzi kubva paupenyu hwake hwekutendeka nekuvimba naMesia.

`Verenga Mateo 11:1–6. Jesu akasimbisa sei kutenda kwaJohani apo muparidzi uyu aiva mujeri?`

Satani akatadza kuedza Jesu kuti aite chivi, uye kukundwa kwake murenje kwakamutsamwisa zvikuru. Akabva ashandura nzira, achiita kuti Johani, hama yaJesu, aiswe mujeri. Kuiswa mujeri kwaJohani kwakarwadza Jesu, kunyange hazvo Satani aisagona kumuedza kuti aite chivi. Iyi yaiva nzira yaSatani yekupwanya Johani, panguva imwe chete achiremedza mwoyo weMuponesi. Asi Johani akagamuchira shoko rekurudziro kubva kuna Jesu. Uye kunyange ari mumamiriro ezvinhu akaoma zvikuru, uye asinganyatsonzwisisi kuti sei akaramba ari mujeri apo Jesu aiva nesimba guru kudaro, Johani akaramba akatendeka, kunyange kusvikira parufu.

Sezvakaita vaporofita vekare, isuwo tinodanwa kuti tiparidze shoko rekutendeuka, rutendo, uye kuzara kwekururama kwaKristu (Zvakazarurwa 14:6–12). Sezvaakaita kuna Johani Mubhabhatidzi, Satani anogona kushandisa kusava nechokwadi kuti atiite tipokane nebasa raMwari muupenyu hwedu. Hatifaniri kuora mwoyo kana tikarangarira kutungamirira kwaMwari muupenyu hwedu, kunyanya kana zvinhu zvisiri kufamba zvakanaka, sezvakaitika kuna Johani, uyo akaiswa mujeri uye akazourayiwa nokuda kwezviito zvemukadzi akaipa nemurume aive asina kusimba pakusarudza zvakarurama. Chero zvingaitwa nevanhu kwatiri, tinogona kuswedera kuna Mwari nerutendo, uye Iye achatipindura. Akativimbisa simba rekuita kuda Kwake (Mabasa 1:8). Munyika yepashure pechiKristu uye munyika isiri yechiKristu, zvakakosha zvikuru kuti tive zvapupu zvakatendeka zvaKristu, chero zvimiro zvatiri mazviri.

`Wakambova here panzvimbo yakaita seyaJohani mujeri, apo waisatombonzwisisa kuti sei zvinhu zvakaipa zvakadaro zvichiitika kwauri? Wakaramba wakachengeta sei rutendo rwako nekuvimba naMwari?`
