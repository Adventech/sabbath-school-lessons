---
title:  Kudzidza Kuenda Mberi
date:  21/08/2026
---

“Hutsvene hwaKristu ndirwo rusimbiro rwemutendi rwevupenyu husingaperi. ‘Uyo unotenda mandiri,’ akadaro Jesu, kunyange akafa, achararamazve: naani nani anorarama anotenda mandiri haazofi. Unotenda here izvi?’ Kristu apa arikutarisa kumberi kunguva yekudzokazve kwake. Apo vatsvene vakarara vachamutswa vasisina kuvora, nevapenyu vakarurama vachatorwa kuinda kudenga vasina kumboraira rufu. Chishamiso icho Kristu akanga ava kuda kuita, mukutsa Razaro kubva kuvakafa, kwaizomiririra kumutswa kwevakarurama vakafa.”

“Nyika inodengenyeka neizwi reMwanakomana waMwari paanodaidza vatsvene vakarara. Vanodaira kukudaidzira vachimuka vakapfekedzwa nembiri yekusafa, vachidaidzira, ‘Kukunda, kukunda, pamusoro perufu neguva! Nhaiwe rufu, rumborera rwako ruripi? Nhaiwe guva kukunda kwako kuripi’” (Tarisa 1 VaKorinde 15: 55.) Zvino vatsvene vapenyu nevamutsva kubva kuvakafa vakasimudzira mazwi avo murwiyo runosimudzira rwekukurira. Miviri yakanga yakarara mumakuva inemavanga ezvirwere nerufu rwakawira pamiviri inorwara nekufa. Vatsvene vapenyu vachashandurwa pakarepo, sekubwaira kweziso nekubatana nevamutswa, vose vosangana naShe mumhepo. Haiwa kusangana kwakadini uku! Shamwari dzakanga dzakaparadzaniswa nerufu dzasanganazve, pasina kuzoparadzanazve.”-Ellen G. White.

**Mibvunzo Yekutaurirana**:

`Funga nezve avo vaiva zvapupu zvekumuka kwaKristu (Mabasa 1:22; Mabasa 2: 32; Mabasa 3: 15; Mabasa 4: 33; Mabasa 5: 30-32). Ko isu kwapera makore angaita 2000 akapfuvura zvabva kuitika, tingava “zvapupu” zvekumuka kwake sei?`

`Rumuko rwaKristu chiitiko chakakosha kwazvo chevhangeri (1 VaKorinde 15: 1-4). Pasina rumuko, kuparidzwa kwevhangeri yerufu rwaKristu kunoshaya basa (1 VaKorinde 15: 14). Rufu rwaKristu chairwo runova nhando. Nemhaka yei? Ko mhinduro yako kumubvunzo uyu inotikudini nesimba rerumuko rwaKristu?`

`Funga zvishoma nezvemubvunzo unoshamisa nekunakidza “Kana vakafa vasingamuki, ‘Ngatidyei tinwe, nekuti mangwana tinofa!’” (1 VaKorinde 15: 32). Zviri kunzi kudini apa?`

`Muboka renyu rekudzidzira, tauriranai nezvechimiro chevakafa. Ko nemhaka yei 1 VaKorinde 15 ichishaya maturo kana parufu, vakaponeswa vachibva vananga kudenga?`
