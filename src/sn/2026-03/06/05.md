---
title:  Chipo Chendimi
date:  05/08/2026
---

Ko chipo chendimi? Takatarisa kushanda kwechipo ichi mune dzimwe ndima muBhaibheri (Marko 16: 17; Mabasa 2: 1-13; Mabasa 10: 44-48; Mabasa 19: 6), chipo chendimi muna 1 VaKorinde kukwaniswa neMweya chaiko kuti vanhu vataure ndimi dzedzimwe nyika.

Pauro anotaura nezvechipo chendimi paanotaura nezvezvimwe zvose muna 1 VaKorinde 12: 8-10 (tarisa wo 1 VaKorinde 12: 28, 30; 1 VaKorinde 13: 1, 8). Asi anodzokerazve kwachiri muna 1 VaKorinde 14. Zvechokwadi izvi rechiGiriki *glossa* (ndimi) rinovonekwa kanopfuura ka 20 muna 1 VaKorinde 12-14 kanokwana ka 15 kacho muchitsauko 14 chete (1 VaKorinde 14). Kunze kweapa, izwi rechiGiriki *heteroglossos* (rumwe rurimi) rinovonekwavo muna 1 VaKorinde 14: 21. Kuvonekwa kwaro kakawanda kudai zvinoreva kuti chipo chendimi chakanga chakakosha kwazvo kuna Pauro. Kushandiswa nekusashandiswa zvakanaka kwechipo ichi pakereke yapaKorinte zvakakonzera nyongano nekusarongeka mukushumira kweruzhinji (1 VaKorinde 14: 23, 27, 33, 40).

`Verenga 1 VaKorinde 14: 5, 13, 26, 27 na 1 VaKorinde 12: 10, 30. Ndeipi rairo yakasimba iyo Pauro akapa nezvechipo chendimi?`

Chikonzero nei chipo chendimi chichifambidzana nechekudzidudzira kana kudziturikira ndechekuti dzinofanira kuva nevuchenjeri (1 VaKorinde 14: 9), zvimwe, zvingashaya chikonzero nei chipo ichi chichishandiswa (1 VaKorinde 14: 6). Ndicho chikonzero nei Pauro akasimbisa nyaya yekududzira nekunzwisisa. Saka haasi kushora chipo chendimi pachacho, asi sezvatichazoona mangwana, mukurumbira wachakapiwa nevaKorinte wakakonzera izvozvo, zvakakonzera kuti vashaye hanya nechipo chekuprofita.

Apa zvino, zvakakosha kuvona kuti kana nepo Pauro aishuvira vaKorinte vose kutaura nendimi dzedzimwe nyika (1 VaKorinde 14: 5), akanga asingazvitarisiri (1 VaKorinde 12: 10). Saka, pfungwa yekuti “vanhu vose ngavataure nendimi vasati vari vakabhapatidzwa neMweya Mutsvene inobva mudzidziso dziri muna 1 VaKorinde 12 na 14.” Raoul Dederen.

`Kune vanhu here pakereke yenyu vanotaura mumwe mutauro? Vangashandisa sei chipo ichi kusvikira vanhu neshoko raKristu? Ko idi iri ringatibatsira sei kunzwisisa chimiro chendimi chechokwadi icho Pauro anotaura nezvacho?`
