---
title:  “Nzira Yakanyanya Kunaka”
date:  04/08/2026
---

“Rudo harusi chipo chimwechete pakati pezvimwe zvakawanda. Ndicho chinokonzera chezvimwe zvipo zvose.” Carl P. Cosaert.

`Verenga 1 VaKorinde 13: 1-7 na 1 Petro 4: 8-11. Ko rudo runoita basa rei muzvipo zvemweya?`

Muna 1 VaKorinde 13 tinodzidzisa kuti kuburikidza nerudo chete umo zvipo zvemweya zvingashanda zvakanaka. Pauro anotanga 1 VaKorinde 13 nekudzokororazve zvipo zvinotaurwa muna 1 VaKorinde 12, achisimbisa kushaya kwawo basa kana asingasundwi nerudo. Saka, ruzivo (1 VaKorinde 12: 8) nekutenda (1 VaKorinde 12: 9), kunyange kutenda “kunobvisa makomo” (1 VaKorinde 13: 2), hakuna basa kana pasina rudo (1 VaKorinde 13: 2. Pasina rudo kugona kutaura nedzimwe ndimi (1 VaKorinde 12: 10, 28, 30) kunoshaya basa zvekungova “bhero rinorira zvaro” (1 VaKorinde 13: 1). Saizvozvovo kunyange chipo chakakosha chekuprofita hachisi chinhu kana pasina rudo (1 VaKorinde 13: 2).

Muna 1 VaKorinde 13: 4-7, Pauro anotarisa kune chinonzi rudo nezvarusiri; zvikuru sei nezvinoitwa nerudo nezvarusingaiti. Mazwi ezviito aanoshandisa kuratidza rudo anoratidza kuti rudo harusi zvatinonzwa asi zvatinoita. Saka, Pauro achiti (1) rune mwoyo murefu; (2) nemwoyo munyoro (3) runofara muzvokwadi; (4) rudo haruna manyawi, (5) haruzvitsvakiri zvarwo; (6) harutsamwi; (7) harurangariri zvakaipa kana kuzvichengeta, (8) harufariri zvisakarurama.

Mazwi ezviito anosvika 15 anopa gwara rakafanira rekurarama zvipo. Zviripachena kuti hurukuro iyi yechimiro chaicho cherudo iri pakati pezvitsauko 1 VaKorinde 12 na 14, umo Pauro anotaura nenzira dzekugadzirisa matambudziko panyaya dzezvipo zvemweya. Hongu, rudo ndirwo chinhu chikurusa mukushandiswa kwezvipo zvemweya. Rudowo runoiswa parutivi nekutenda netariro, “asi chikuru chazvo ndirwo rudo” (1 VaKorinde 13: 13).

`Nemhaka yei rudo rwakakosha zvakadai mukutenda kwedu? Ndeipi nzira irinani yekuratidza rudo rwaMwari ingapfuura kunamata zvakasimba kuti tiratidze rudo rwaMwari kune vamwe?`
