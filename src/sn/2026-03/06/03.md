---
title:  Kubatana Kuburikidza Nekusiyana
date:  03/08/2026
---

Ndimi yehumwe inoziviswa muna 1 VaKorinde 12: 4-6 (“Mweya mumwechete,” “Ishe mumwechete uyu,” “naMwari mumwechete”) inotambanudzwa muchitsauko 12 chaVaKorinte vekutanga (1 VaKorinde 12). Izvi zvinovonekwa nekashandisiro kanoitwa naPauro mazwi ekuti “mumwechete kana Mweya mumwechete” (1 VaKorinde 12: 11), “muviri mumwechete” (1 VaKorinde 12: 12), “muviri mumwe” (1 VaKorinde 12: 12, 13, 20), “Mweya mumwe” (1 VaKorinde 12: 13 [kaviri]), ne “hanya imwechete iyi kuhama” (1 VaKorinde 12: 25).

Pamwechete nepfungwa yehumwe, Pauro anoratidza kusiyana kwenhengo mumuviri waKristu kuburikidza nemazwi akafanana neekuti “mitezo mizhinji” (1 VaKorinde 12: 12, 20), “tose takabhapatidzwa” (1 VaKorinde 12: 13, “usiri mutezo mumwechete asi mizhinji” (1 VaKorinde 12: 14), “muviri wose” (1 VaKorinde 12: 17), “imwe neimwe” (1 VaKorinde 12: 18), “nhengo yemuviri mumwechete” (1 VaKorinde 12: 22, 23), “nenhengo dzose” (1 VaKorinde 12: 26). Kusimbisa pahumwe nekusiyana kunoratidza kuti zvipo zvemweya ndezvekubatanidza kuburikidza nekusiyana.

Kubatana uku mukusiyana kunofanira kuratidza chimiro chaMwari. Baba mumwechete, Mwanakomana ndemumwe, naMweya Mutsvene mumwe. Vatatu vanogara vari mumwe nemumwe akazvimiririra woga asi vachishandira pamwechete kusimbisa kereke nekupa simba rebasa (1 VaKorinde 12: 4-6; VaEfeso 4: 11-13).

`Verenga 1 VaKorinde 12: 12-31. Nemhaka yei mufananidzo wemuviri une mitezo yakawanda wakafanira chaizvo kumiririra kereke nenhengo dzayo?`

Pfungwa imwechete yakakosha muna 1 VaKorinde 12 ndeyekuti kana nepo mitezo yemuviri yakazvimiririra iri yoga yakasiyana (1 VaKorinde 12: 15-20), yose vanotsamira mumwe pane mumwe (1 VaKorinde 12: 21-26). Tsoka dzinotsamira pamaziso kuvona kwekufamba; nemazisowo haagoni kubata chinhu-asi mavoko chete ndiwo anobata. Uye nepfungwa yekuti dzimwe nhengo hadzina simba (1 VaKorinde 12: 22) kana kuti hadzikudzwi sedzimwe (1 VaKorinde 12: 23) kutaura chete, nekuti dzose dzinodikanwa (1 VaKorinde 12: 22).

Nerombo rakaipa, kwakanga kwava netsika pakati pevaKorinte yekukoshesa zvimwe zvipo kupfuvura zvimwe. Kugadzirisa dambudziko iri, Pauro anovakokera kukudanana, iri “nzira yakapfuvurisa nekunaka” (1 VaKorinde 12: 31). Mune kumwe kutaura, hazvinei kuti chipo chipi, kana chikashandiswa nevuchenjeri nerudo, chinofadza pamberi paMwari.

`Tarisa zvinyorwa zvezvipo muna 1 VaKorinde 12:8–10, 28; VaRoma 12: 6-8; na VaEfeso 4: 11. Iwe chipo chako ndechipi? Ungachishandisa sei kusimbisa muviri waKristu?`
