---
title:  Chimiro Chinogona Kuvapo
date:  09/09/2026
---

`Verenga 2 VaKorinde 8: 1-5. Ndechipi chikonzero chinogona kuvapo chakakonzera vanhu veMasedonia kuda kupa zvipo zvavo vakasununguka kudaro nemwoyo wose?`

Chimiro chinogona kuva chaiva muvanhu veMasedonia chinogona kuvonekwa nenzira dzakawanda.

Chekutanga, **vakapa nemufaro mukuru kwazvo (2 VaKorinde 8: 2).** Pauro anoti nezve vanhu veMasedonia “pakati pekuidzwa kukuru kwekutambudzika, mufaro wavo, pamwechete nevurombo hwavo hukuru, zvakawanza zvikuru kusanyima kwavo” (2 VaKorinde 8: 2). Anotaurazve pamberi apo kuti “Mwari anoda munhu anopa nemufaro” (2 VaKorinde 9: 7). Izwi rechiGiriki rinoshandiswa panoti “mufaro” rinovonekwa apa chete muTestamende Itsva. Asi parinobva, pane mamwe anobvavo ipapo: “Uyo anoita mabasa emutsa ngaaite nemufaro” (VaRoma 12: 8). Mazwi anobva ipapo akawanda anowanikwa mune zvinyorwa zvinodudzirwa Bhaibheri, zvinofanana nerufaro. Muna 2 VaKorinde 9: 7, kuva munhu anopa nemufaro zvinoreva kupa usina kushaya hanya.

Chechipiri, **vakapa nemwoyo wose zvikuru (2 VaKorinde 8: 2).** Pauro asati ataura nezvekupa kweveMasedonia, akatanga ataura nezve “kushaiwa kwavo kukuru”. Izwi “kupa zvikuru” (muchiGiriki, haplotetos) rinovonekwa kavirizve muna 2 VaKorinde 8 na9. Pauro anoti, “Muchifumiswa pazvose mugoziva kupa zvakawanda”(2 VaKorinde 9: 11), kureva kuti tinopiwa kuti tigone kupavo. Pamberi zvishoma, anotizve “pamusoro pekupa kwenyu zvakawanda”(2 VaKorinde 9: 13). Mundima iyi, kupa zvakawanda inzira yekupupura vhageri yaKristu.

Chechitatu, **vakapa “nekuda kwavo” (2 VaKorinde 8: 3).** Izvi zvinoreva kuti hakuna akavamanikidza kuti vape. Izvi zvinoyemurika kana munhu achivona kuti havana kupa nekuti vaiva nezvakawandisa, asi vaitova nezvishoma-shoma. Pauro anoshandisa pfungwa yakafanana neyiyi achizivisa nezvekuda kushanyira vaKorinte kwaTito. Akainda kuKorinte nekuda kwake zvake (2 VaKorinde 8: 17).

Chechina, **vakapa nepfungwa yekuti kupa mukana wakanaka (2 VaKorinde 8: 4).** Chimiro ichi chinovonekwa muchikumbiro chevanhu veMasedonia chekuti vapindevo pakuwunganidza. “Vakakumbira nekunyengetera kukuru kwatiri kuti tivatendere kuita basa iri renyasha rekupavo vatsvene”(2 VaKorinde 8: 4).

Pakupedzisira, **vakaitavo basa rekuwunganidza nekuperera kukuru (2 VaKorinde 8: 5).** Pauro anoti, “Vakazvipa kunaShe nekwatiri nekuda kwaShe”(2 VaKorinde 8: 5). Kuzvipa kutanga kunaShe kutungamirira kuzvipa kune vamwe. Vatendi veMasedonia vakawedzera munda wavo webasa nekupa mari. Mukupa nekupa zvakawanda hazvipereri mukupa mari chete.
