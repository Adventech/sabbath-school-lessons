---
title:  Kubatana
date:  10/09/2026
---

Tavona kuti Pauro anokurudzira nhengo dzeKorinte kupinda pachirongwa chekuunganidzira makereke emuJudia aishaiwa. Chimwe chezvikonzero zvake kwaiva kubatidza pfungwa yekubatana. Anoda kuvaratidza kuti makereke evaHedeni inhengo dzemhuri yaMwari sezvakangoita vatendi vechiJudha vari muJerusarema. Kureva kuti, vanhu ava, vaimbova vavengi vavo vanemukurumbira, zvino vave, hongu nhengo pamwechete navo Musungano Itsva yemusaririrwa waMwari. Pauro anoda kuvona kereke yose yeChikristu semhuri, maJudha nevaHedeni, vakabatana munzira yakasimba zvinopupurira kukereke kwemazera nemazera anotevera.

Tito nedzimwe hama mbiri dzairemekedzwa ndivo vaichengeta mari iyi. Mwari vakaisa hanya yemari iyi mumwoyo waTito (2 VaKorinde 8: 16). Kuburikidza nekereke, Mwari vakasarudza dzimwe hama mbiri idzi (2 VaKorinde 8: 18-23). Vanodaidzwa kuti “ndivo vaapostora vekereke, vuye kukudzwa kwaMwari” (2 VaKorinde 8: 23). “Kukudzwa kwaKristu” kana kuchitsanangura hama mbiri idzi dzakatendeka kana makereke pachawo hazvinei. Kupa zvipo chiratidzo chekuteerera Kristu, Musoro wekereke (VaEfeso 4: 15).

Muna 2 VaKorinde 8-9 zvinoratidza kuti zvipo zvinofanira kupiwa kuvanhu vakasarudzwa naMwari kuburikidza nekereke. Mashoko ekuti “makereke ose” (2 VaKorinde 8: 18), vakasarudzwa nekereke” (2 VaKorinde 8: 19), uye “varanda vekereke” (2 VaKorinde 8: 23) zvinoita sezvinongoreva izvozvo. Saka, kutsiura kunotevera kusingashamisi, “Muvaratidze pamberi pekereke” (2 VaKorinde 8: 24).

Kuunza zvipo kukereke-mudziyo waMwari wepanyika-zvinokurudzira kubatana nekuratidza kubatana (2 VaKorinde 8: 13, 14). Mari inogona kubatanidza vanhu zvikuru sa. Kune rumwe rutivi, kana vanhu vasina kubatana mukupa mbiri kuna Mwari chete, mari inogona kuparadzanisa vanhu vo.

`Ko vaRoma 15: 26, 27 inoratidza sei chishuvo chaPauro chekubatana apa?`

Pakupedzisira, Pauro anoratidza kuunganidza serusando kuna Mwari, sebasa renyasha, sechikomborero, senzira yekunamata, uyewo sekuwadzana. Zvose izvi zvichabva pazvipo? Zvifunge.

`Ko kupa kwedu kunhengo dzekereke nekubasa rekuparidza mhiri kwemakungwa, nguva zhinji kunzwimbo dzirikure, kunobatsira kubatana kwekereke yose pasi rose tenderere sei?`
