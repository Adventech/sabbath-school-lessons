---
title:  Kudzidza Kuenda Mberi
date:  11/09/2026
---

“Avo vane mwoyo yakazadzwa nerudo rwaKristu, vachatevedzera muenzaniso wake uyo nekuda kwavo akava murombo, uye kuburikidza nevurombo hwake tiitwe vafumi. Mari, nguva, kukwezvera-zvose zvipo zvavakagashira paruvoko rwaMwari, vachazvikoshesa chete senzira yekufambisa vhangeri. Ndizvo zvazvaiva mukereke yekutanga; uye mukereke yanhasi kana saizvozvo nesimba raMweya Mutsvene nhengo dzabvisa rudo rwadzo pazvinhu zvenyika, uye vagadzirira kuzvipira nekuda kwevanhu kuti vanzwevo vhangeri, zvokwadi zvinoparidzwa zvichava nesimba rekukakata vose vanorinzwa.”

“Tenzi havadi zvipo zvedu. Hatigoni kuvafumisa nezvipo izvi. Muimbi anodaro ‘Zvose zvinobva kwamuri, uye kubva munezvenyu takupai.’ Asi Mwari anotendera kuti tiratidze rudo rwedu rwetsitsi nekuzvipira kuti vamwe vazviwanevo. Ndiyo nzira yoga yatinokwanisa kuratidza vamwe kutenda kwedu rudo rwaMwari. Hakuna imwe nzirazve.”

“Chipo chakakura zvakadini chaMwari kuvanhu, uye saMwari wedu kuchiita! Nekusununguka kusingasviki pane uyo akapa, kudzikinura vanakomana vevanhu vakapanduka nekuvadzosa kuti vavone chinangwa chake nekunzwisisa rudo rwake. Nezvegumi nezvipo zvako, ucharatidza here kuti hapana chaungakoshesa zvekusamupa ‘uyo akapa Mwanakomana wake mumwewoga kuti ani nani anotenda maari arege kufa, asi ave nevupenyu husingaperi’?”-Ellen G. White.

**Mibvunzo Yekutaurirana**:

`Fungisisa nezva 2 VaKorinde 8: 9. Nemhaka yei muenzaniso waJesu wakakosha zvakadaro kana takatarisa hutariri?`

`Johani 3: 16, inozivisa zvishoma nezvendimi yedenga yekupa. Verenga Johani 15: 13; VaEfeso 5: 2, 25; VaGaratia 2: 19, 20; na 1 Johani 3: 16. Ko ndima idzi na Johani 3: 16 dzinechii chakafanana, uye nderipi shoko ratingazviwanira apa?`

`Wakatsamira pakuverenga kwako 2 VaKorinde 8-9, ndezvipi zvaunozviwanira kubva mukupa?`

`Pamusoro pekupa zvipo nenzira yakarongeka nguva dzose, ndezvipi zvimwe zvaungaita kutevedzera kuzvipira kwaJesu?`
