---
title:  Kudzidza Kuenda Mberi
date:  03/07/2026
---

“Mukuparidza vhangeri muKorinte, muapostora akatevera imwe nzira yakasiyana neyaakashandisa muAtenisi. . . . Akazvimisira kudzivirira kupikisana muzvinhu zvakawanda, uye kuti ‘asaziva chimwe chinhu pakati pavo’ vaKorinte ‘kunze kwaKristu nemuchinjikwa wake.”

“Pauro anekubudirira kwakati kuti,” asi akashaya chikonzero chakangwara chekuvakira kereke pamusoro pezvinhu zvaakawana zviripo. Akashaya tariro munezvaiva paKorinte, akasarudza kuzvisiya. . . .

“Paakanga achifunga kusiya guta iri achida kuenda kunedzimwe nzvimbo dzairatidza kuti dzaizobudirira, . . . Ishe vakazviratidza kwaari vusiku, vakati, ‘Usatya, asi taura, . . . . nekuti ndine vanhu vazhinji kwazvo muguta iri.’ Pauro akanzwisisa kuti iyi yaiva rairo yekuti arambe arimuKorinte, nekugutsikana kuti Ishe vaizowedzera kumbeu yakadyarwa. . . . Kereke huru kwazvo yakatangwa pasi pemureza wa Jesu Kristu.”

“Zvakanyorwa kuti Pauro akashanda zvakasimba kwegore nemwedzi mitanhatu muKorinte. Asi mabasa ake akanga asiri eguta iri chete. . . . Asi paKorinte ndipo pakava nemahofisi ake makuru. . . . Makereke akawanda akabva amutswa. . . . Kushaikwa kwaPauro mumakereke aya kunovonekwa pachena nekuvanyorera kwaaiita kwakasimba, uku kwaitorwa seShoko raMwari. . . . Matsamba aya aiverengwa mumakereke.”—Ellen G. White.

**Mibvunzo Yekutaurirana**:

`Pauro akanga akagutsikana kuti aiva muapostora waJesu, uye kuti kudanwa uku kwakabva kuna Mwari. Nemhaka yei zvakakosha kwazvo kuzviziva uye nekuti takadaidzirwei?`

`Kwekanguva, Pauro akanzwa kuda kusiya basa rekuparidza muKorinte achibva muguta. Chii chakashandura pfungwa dzake? Ko izvi zvingatibatsira sei kana tavekunzwa kuda kusiya basa rekuparidza? Asi pangava nenguva here yatingavona kusiya kwafanira?`

`Nhengo dzekereke yepaKorinte dzaikanganiswa zvakanyanya netsika dzevanhu vaiva vakavapoteredza. Iri idi rakakura wo kwazvo nhasi pakati pedu. Ko tingava munyika sei (Johani 17:11, 15) asi isingatikanganisi mukurarama kwedu—muzvinhu zvinoti-kuchiva kwenyama, nekuchiva kwemeso nekuzvikudza kwevupenyu” (1 Johani 2:16)? Munzira dzipizve umo kereke yedu inokanganiswa netsika dzakatipoteredza?`
