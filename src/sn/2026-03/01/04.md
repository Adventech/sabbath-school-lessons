---
title:  Guta ReKorinte
date:  30/06/2026
---

`Verenga Mabasa 18:1–3, 1 VaKorinde 5:9–11, na 1 VaKorinde 8:4. Ndezvipi zvatingadzidza nezvehupfumi hweKorinte, kuzvibata, nevupenyu hwekunamata?`

Guta reKorinte rakanga rakakoshera nyika dzekare, richizivikanwa munyaya dzekutengeserana kukuru. Guta iri rakaparadzwa neRoma muna 146 [b.c.]{.smallcaps} rikavakwazve naJulius Caesar seguta rakabvutwa neRoma muna 44 [b.c.]{.smallcaps} Ndirori Korinte reRoma rinovonekwa muTestamende Itsva. Munguva dzaPauro, Korinte raiita makwikwi neAtenisi rikatorikurira muzvinhu zvakawanda. Korinte raiva nezviteshi zviviri zvezvikepe zvakakosha kwazvo zvaibatsira kutsinhaniswa kwezvaitengeswa nekukura kwezvehufumi hwaro kureruke.

Hongu, Pauro akasarudza Korinte nekuda kwekukosha kwaro nekunaka kwenzvimbo yaraiva. “Saka mukana mukuru wakamuka wekuparidza vhangeri. Rangosimbiswa paKorinte zvakanga zvavanyore kuti ripararire kunyika dzose.”—Ellen G. White.

Nehufumi hwakanga hwakatitekeshe muKorinte hwakabatsira Pauro kuzviraramisa nekugadzira nekutengesa matende achiparidza vhangeri muguta iri (Mabasa 18:2, 3). Ichokwadi kuti kuparidza muguta rakakura zvakadai hakungashayi matambudziko. Korinte raiva nezvinamato zvakawanda zvaiva pachena vo (1 VaKorinde 8:5), zvaivonekwa nemakirawa akawanda kwazvo aivakirwa kukudza zvimwari zvakaita saApollo, Athena, naAphrodite, nezvimwe vo zvakawanda, kusanganira kunamatwa kwezvimwari zveEgipita zvafanana naSerapisi naIsisi.

Pakati penyongano iyi mukunamata, Korinte raizivikanwa vo nezvevupombwe hwaityisa. Strabo, munyori wechiGiriki wenhoroondo nezvenzvimbo anoti kwaiva nemhombwe dzainzi itsvene dzaisvika 1000 dzaiva dzakamisirwa kunamata Aphrodite mutemberi yake yemuKorinte. Kana nepo vadzidzi vakawanda vasinganyatsodaira izvi vachiti mashoko eAtenisi ekunyomba Korinte, vupombwe hwaiva nechekuita nezvekunamata hwaiva hwakajairika kwazvo munyika idzi. Vupombwe hwaiva dambudziko rakakura kwazvo muKorinte, sezviri kose kose vo. Kunamata zvifananidzo nevupombwe zvaiitwa zuva rimwe nerimwe, uye zviitiko izvi zvaisuwisa kwazvo zvinoratidzwa muzvinyorwa zva1 na2 VaKorinde.

`Mumabasa ake ekuparidza muKorinte, Pauro akasangana nedambudziko rekunamatwa kwezvifananidzo nehupombwe hwainyadzisa kwazvo munzvimbo iyi. Ndeapi matambudziko angakonzera kuti kuparidza kuve kwakawoma mazuva ano? Ko tingaakurira sei? Musiano upi, kana uripo pakati peKorinte nemaguta edu nhasi uno?`
