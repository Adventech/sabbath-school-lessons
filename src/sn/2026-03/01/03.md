---
title:  Kubva KuAtenisi Kuinda kuKorinte
date:  29/06/2026
---

`Verenga Mabasa 17:16–34. Ko Pauro aivepi asati ainda kuKorinte, uye akaitei ikoko?`

Mabasa 17:16–34 inotsanangura Pauro achiparidza kuva Atenisi asati ainda kuKorinte. Zvinoratidza kuti akanga asina kuronga kuinda Atenesi panguva iyi, asi akainda achibatsirwa nedzimwe shamwari nekuda kwekupikiswa kwaakasangana nako paBerea (Mabasa 17:13–15).

Avo vakainda naPauro kuAtemisi vakadzoka kuBerea nerairo yaTimotio neSairasi kuti vazobatana naye nekukurumidza (Mabasa 17:15). Mabasa 17:16–34 inotaura nezvakaitwa naPauro akavamirira. Anotaura nezvaJesu musinagoge, pamusika, nemunhandare yeAreyopagasi. Akatadza kurega kutaura nezvaJesu akashandisa mukana wenguva yaivapo kuzviita.

`Verenga Mabasa 18:1–11. Ko Pauro anoitei paanosvika paKorinte uye munguva yose yaaiva muguta iri?`

Pauro akainda kuKorinte murwendo rwake rwechipiri rwekunoparidza. Ruka anotivudza kuti Pauro akagara ko gore rimwechete nehafu.

Senguva dzose, Pauro anotanga mabasa ake evhangeri musinagoge (Mabasa 18:4–6). Mabasa 17:1, 2 inotivudza kuti iyi yaiva tsika yake. Akatevedza hurongwa hwebasa “kutanga-maJudha” (VaRoma 1:16, Mabasa 13:46), sezvakarairwa naJesu kuvaapostora vake (tarisa Mabasa 1:8).

Apo Sairasi naTimotio vakazobatana naye muKorinte, “aiva mubasa reshoko, achipupurira vaJudha kuti Kristu ndiJesu” (Mabasa 18:5). Panguva yake yekugara muKorinte, akanga arimubasa nguva dzose “rekudzidzisa shoko raMwari” (Mabasa 18:11*. Maiva umu, wo, maakataura mashoko anemukurumbira ekuti “handina kutsvaka kuziva chimwe chinhu pakati penyu” imi VaKorinte “kunze kwaJesu Kristu nekuroverwa kwake pamuchinjikwa” (1 VaKorinde 2:2).

`Ko tingadzidzei kubva murwendo rwaPauro rwekunoparidza kuAtenisi nekuKorinte? Chii chinokonzera kuti kuparidza mumaguta edu kuwome zvakadaro nhasi? Ko tingafambisa sei vhangeri nekubudirira munzvimbo dzakawoma idzi? Ndeapi mazano angabudirira chaizvo ebasa atingadzidza kubva murwendo rwaPauro?`
