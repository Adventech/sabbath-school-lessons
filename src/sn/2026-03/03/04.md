---
title:  Vuchenjeri Nekuyaruka
date:  14/07/2026
---

Nekufamba kwenguva zvipoka-zvipoka zvinoisa simba rakawandisa pavatungamiriri vevanhu. Izvi zvinogona kuparadza humwe hwekereke nevutano hwenhengo pamweya, mukutarisa basa raKristu nenzira isina kunaka zvichikonzera kukudza vanhu vanotungamirira, zvichiparadza vamwe. Matuso emaitiro aya anokonzera makwikwi ekuzvienzanisa nevamwe, zvinogona kutsemura kereke. Zvikuru sei kana tikatora vatungamiriri vakakosha kupfuvura kukosha kwezita rekuva Makristu, tinoita dambudziko rekuparadza zvatiri, nekubvisa Kristu panzvimbo yake muvupenyu hwedu.

`Verenga 1 VaKorinde 3:1–4. Ko Pauro anotsanangura sei kukura pamweya kwevaKorinte?`

Pauro anoisa pachena kuti kukura pamweya kunotungamirira kukufara muvuchenjeri hwaMwari zvichibva munhengo (1 VaKorinde 2:6, 7), zvinoziviswa kuburikidza neMweya (1 VaKorinde 2:13), nezvinopesana nevuchenjeri hwenguva ino (1 VaKorinde 2:6), vuchenjeri hwevanhu (1 VaKorinde 2:13). Vuchenjeri hwaMwari hunoratidzwa muMuchinjikwa waKristu (1 VaKorinde 2:1–4). Mumazwi mashoma, vuchenjeri hwaMwari hunoratidzwa mukutambudzika kwaKristu, murufu rwake nerumuko. Saka arikudzokororazve rukoko rwekubatana (1 VaKorinde 3:1–17), Pauro anoda kuti vanoverenga zvaakanyora vavone kukosha kwavuchenjeri hwechokwadi nekukura muna Kristu.

Makristu akachenjera akakura pakutenda, vasingararami munyama, vasingaiti sevacheche (1 VaKorinde 3:1). Vanoenzanisa zvinhu zvemweya nezvinhu zvemweya, nekuti “zvinhu zvemweya . . . zvinoziviswa nemweya” (1 VaKorinde 2:13, 14). Makristu akachenjera akakura pamweya anodya mabhonzo, haanwi mukaka (1 VaKorinde 3:2; enzanisa na VaHebheru 5:12). Mutendi “anorarama nemukaka haana ruzivo rweshoko rekururama, nekuti mwana muduku. Asi mabhonzo ndeevakuru, avo vave nemasimba avo ekufunga akadzidziswa nekurarama kunotsaura zvakanaka kubva kune zvakaipa” (VaHebheru 5:13, 14). Makristu akachenjera akura havati, “Ndiri waPauro” kana kuti “Ndiri waApollo” (1 VaKorinde 3:4), vachizviti ndevevanhu vakasiyana-siyana.

Asi vanhu ava vakangofananawo navo, “Vashandi vaMwari vaibatirana zvakanaka” (1 VaKorinde 3:9). Tiri sekereke, munda waMwari, chivakwa, temberi (1 VaKorinde 3:9, 16, 17). Tose tiri vaMwari kuburikidza naKristu (1 VaKorinde 3:11).

`Wakambokuhuniswa here zvikuru nemunhu wawaiyemura chaizvo? Kana wakambosangana nazvo izvi, ndezvipi zvidzidziso zvawadzidza kubva mazviri?`
