---
title:  Dambudziko Rekupesana Mukereke
date:  12/07/2026
---

Chikumbiro chaPauro “kurege kuva nekupesana pakati penyu, asikuti musanganiswe zvakanaka pamurangariro mumwe wekufamba nekufunga kumwe” (1 VaKorinde 1:10) ndicho chakazara muchitsauko chekutanga cha1 VaKorinde. Vadzidzi vakawanda vanotowirirana kuti humwe ndiyo nyaya huru inobatanidza ndima dzimwe dzose dzetsamba.

`Verenga 1 VaKorinde 1:12–17. Ko ndima iyi inotibatsira sei kunzwisisa kusuwisa kwazvinoita kana vatungamiriri vave zvikwata zvinovatevera? Ko Pauro anopa mhinduro ipi?`

Pauro anoshandisa mamwi akasimba kwazvo kuratidza kudikanwa kwekubatana pakati penhengo dzekereke dzemuKorinte. Anoshandisa izwi rechiGiriki *schisma* (“kupesana,” 1 VaKorinde 1:10) na*eris* (“kunetsana,” 1 VaKorinde 1:11). Nechizita *schisma* (uyezve neizwi rechiito schizō, “kupesanisa”) rinoshandiswazve kune dzimwe nzvimbo muTestamende Itsva kutsanangura pfungwa dzakasiyana-siyana dzinobuda mukupesana. Zvinobva zvaratidza izwi rechizita *eris* (“kupikisana”) rinovonekwa kakawanda mune dzimwe tsika dzakawanda dzisingafaniri kuraramwa neMakristu.

`Verenga VaRoma 1:29, VaRoma 13:13, 1 VaKorinde 3:3, 2 VaKorinde 12:20, na VaGaratia 5:20. Ndezvipi zvimwe zvivi zvinotaurwa apa pamwechete ne eris (“kupesana,” “mhirizhonga”)? Ko izvi zvinotivudzei nekushata kwazvakaita?`

Kusawirirana uku mukereke yepaKorinte kwakabuda pachena—kunyange mukusungisana kumatare edzimhosva (1 VaKorinde 6:1–3). “Ndinodaro kuti munyadziswe. Ko hakuna wakachenjera pakati penyu ungagona kutonga pakati pehama nehama here,” Pauro akavavudza (1 VaKorinde 6:5) munyaya dzekukwirirana kumatare pakati pehama nehama dzemukereke. Chokwadi ndechekuti havana kusiya kupesana kwavo uku kunyange paChirairo ChaTenzi (1 VaKorinde 11:17–22).

Dambudziko rekushaikwa kwekubatana pakati penhengo dzekereke kunotyisa kwazvo, saka Pauro achitambudzika kwazvo nekushamiswa, saka chiri chinhu chekutanga chaanotaura nezvacho mutsamba yekuvaKorinte.

`Verenga zve 1 VaKorinde 1:12–27. Saka ikozvino chifungisisa nezvendima iyi inotibatsira kunzwisisa kupesana kwemapoka nemapoka nenjodzi yekusabatana mukereke? Ko kereke yenyu ingabatsira sei kupedza kupesana uku?`
