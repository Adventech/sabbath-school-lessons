title:  Kudzidza Kuenda Mberi
date:  14/08/2026
---

“Hazvinei kuti munhu anechigaro chakakura zvakadini, uyo ane mwoyo usina kuzadzwa nerudo rwekuda Mwari nevanhu haasi muteveri waKristu weidi. Kana nepo angava nechipo chikuru nekuva nesimba rekuita zvishamiso, asi asina rudo kutenda kwake hakubatsiri chinhu. Anogona kupa zvakawanda kuvanhu; achizviitira zvimwewo zvikonzero zvingapfuura rudo, kana kupa fuma yake yose kubatsira varombo, hazvibatsiri kumuswededza pedyo naMwari. Mukushanda neshungu kwake anogona kufira kutenda kwake, asi kana zvisingabvi murudo, anotorwa naMwari semurevi wenhema aneshungu hake.”-Ellen G. White.

“Mharidzo tinadzo dzakawandisa kwazvo. Chinonyanya kudiwa. . . rudo rwevatadzi varikuparara, rudo urwu runovuya kubva mufuma huru kwazvo yechigaro chaMwari. Chikristu chechokwadi chinoratidza rudo muvupenyu hwemunhu. Runobva mumuviri wose, pfungwa, mwoyo, mavoko anobatsira, tsoka, vanhu vanoshinga kumira apo Mwari anovada vakasimba, kuti nzira dzavo dzose dzisatsauka munzira, kusatsausa vakaremara munzira yavo. Rudo runopfuta rwerudo rwaKristu rwekuda vakarasika ndicho chikonzero chose cheChikristu.” Ellen G. White.

“Rudo rwega runobva mumwoyo waKristu ndirwo runorapa. Sezvinoita ropa mumuviri kana mvura inoraramisa muti ndizvo zvakaita rudo runobva munaKristu kumunhu ndirwo rwega runogona kuporesa akakuvadzwa.”-Ellen G. White.

**Mibvunzo Yekutaurirana**:

`Unofunga kuti Pauro akapedza kunyora zvose zvakanaka nezverudo here? Kana asina, ndezvipi zvimwe zvaungawedzera kune zvaakanyora?`

`Unofunga kuti Pauro airevei paakapa rairo yekuti “Teverai rudo” (1 VaKorinde 14: 1)? Ko izvi zvinei nezvaanoraira muna 1 VaKorinde 13: 4-7?`

`Ndechipi chimiro chikuru cherudo chaunofanira kurarama mazuva ose? Ndezvipi zvinodiwa zvakanyanya pakereke yepenyu? Ko zviya, nemhaka yei Pauro achienzanisa rudo nezvipo zvemweya zvinoti kuprofita, ndimi kana ruzivo (1 VaKorinde 13: 8)?`

`Pauro anopa zano rekuti rudo ndirwo mhinduro huru mudambudziko rekupesana nekusabatana munhengo dzeKorinte. Nemhaka yei? Ko izvi zvikamira sei mumakereke edu nhasi?`
