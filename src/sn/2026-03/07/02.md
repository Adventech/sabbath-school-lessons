---
title:  Zvinotodiwa Murudo
date:  09/08/2026
---

Vhiki yakapera takatarisa panyaya yerudo, sekuvonekwa kwarwo muna 1 VaKorinde 13. Zvino tinoda kutarisa mashoko aPauro apa munzira yakadzama.

`Verenga 1 VaKorinde 13. Taura nemazwi mashoma zvirikutaura nezverudo.`

Pauro haasi kuti ndimi (1 VaKorinde 13: 1), chiprofita, kuziva, vuchenjeri, kutenda (1 VaKorinde 13: 2), nemutsa (1 VaKorinde 13: 3) hazvibatsiri. Arikuti hazvibatsiri kana zvisingasundwi nerudo.

Mhando yerudo iyo yaanotaura nezvayo haibudi mumitsara iyi sekuti “ndinoda mahabrosi” kana kuti “ndinoda shamwari dzangu” kana kuti “ndinoda mudzimai/murume wangu kana vana vangu.” Kana kuti anotaura nezverudo rwatinovona mumabhaisikopo. Harusi rudo runotsvaka zvarwo, kana nepo ndima iyi ichishandiswa nguva zhinji pamichato.

Rudo urwu harugoni kurerutswa kusvika pane zvinongonzwikwa munyama, mukupa, kana chimiro chakanaka kana mutsa. Asi zvose zvinorumiririra neimwe nzira. Rudo urwu inyasha dzakatsanangurika dzakaiswa pamusoro pedu naMweya. Zvechokwadi rudo rwemuna 1 VaKorinde 13 runobva nekusundwa naMweya zvinotikonzera kuita zvinhu nerudo, nekunzwira, nekunaka, nemutsa. Kuzvipira kwakaperera kwemabasa edu, zvatinonzwa munyama, nemupfungwa kuna Kristu nevavakidzani vedu.

`Verenga Mateo 24: 12. Iyambiro ipi yatinopiwa naJesu apa?`

Ndicho chikonzero nei rudo rwe*agape* rwakakosha ruchidikanwa. Musimba raKristu, hatigoni kusiya rudo ruchitonhora mudzimba dzedu, mumakereke, nemuvavakidzani vedu. Tinemuenzaniso waKristu pamuchinjikwa, achitifira. Ndeipi imwe nzira ingaratidza nesimba, mhando iyi yerudo rwakadai? Kana nepo zvedu tisingakwanisi kuratidza rudo rwakadai, nenyasha dzaMwari tinofanira kushingairira kururatidza muvupenyu hwedu kusvika patinokwanisa.

`Inguva dzipi umo, zvechokwadi, kuratidzwa kwerudo rwakadai zvakabata mumwe munhu airuda chaizvo kupfuvura zvimwe zvose?`
