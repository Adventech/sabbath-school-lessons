---
title:  Kutenda, Tariro Nerudo
date:  13/08/2026
---

Kusvika zvino, tadzidza kuti rudo runemwoyo murefu, runonzwira, runofara, runoshingirira, runotenda, runetariro uye runogara nekusingaperi nekuti Jesu zvinhu izvi zvose. Patinongovona zvinhu izvi zvose muna Jesu, chinofanira kutevera kumutevedzera. Ndicho chaiva chishuvo chaPauro kuvaKorinte. Asi kana tikabvisa “haru” muzvimiro 8 zvinopikisana nerudo, “tinogona kuva netsanangudzo yakanakisisa yezvaiitwa neVaKorinte munharaunda yavo: godo, kuzvikudza, kusindimara, hasha, kutsvaka zvavo, kukurumidza kutsamwa, nekutora nguva zhinji munezvakaipa zvevamwe. Pauro arikugadzirisa mazwi ezviito aanoshandisa apa kuchimiro chevaKorinte.”-Verlyn D. Verbrugge.

VaKorinte vaiva nezvakawanda zvavaifanira kudzidza, nesuwo tinazvo. Apedza kutsanangura zvinoitwa nerudo nezvisingaitwi, Pauro anopeta nyaya yacho nekusimbisa chimiro chisingaperi cherudo mukuedza kukurudzira rudo chairwo.

Rimwe zuva, zviprofita zvichapera; tichange tavakautaura ndimi imwechete; neruzivo rwevanhu rusina kuzara ruchashandurwa kuva rutsva, rwakazara, rutsva rwekuziva Mwari (1 VaKorinde 13:12). “Asi rudo runogara narini!” (1 VaKorinde 13: 8).

Ndizvo zvazvichaita pakudzoka kwaKristu, kutenda kuchava kuvona zviripo (2 VaKorinde 5: 7), nazvose zvaishuvirwa kubva kare zvichazadziswa (VaRoma 8: 24). Uye pakupedzisira, rudo ruchagara nekusingaperi semureza wechimiro chaMwari muhutatu. Asi kune nzira iyo kutenda netariro zvichagara nekusingaperi. Kutenda seruponeso (VaRoma 4: 3), uye tariro sechidiso nechinotarisirwa chezvitsva neruzivo munyika itsva, ruchava kurarama kwevakadzikinurwa nekusingaperi. Asi rudo, rudo rwaMwari, ruchakunda nekusingaperi.

Nekukurumidza, tichavona Ishe wedu huso-nehuso (1 VaKorinde 13: 12). Kusvikira musi uyu wasvika, tinofanira kurarama zvimiro izvi zvitatu: kutenda, tariro nerudo. Zvitatu izvi zvinomiririra kuzara kwehupenyu hweChikristu kuburikidza naMweya. Ndicho chikonzero nei zvichitaurwa nezvazvo pakati peMakristu (VaRoma 5: 1-5; VaGaratia 5: 5, 6; VaEfeso 1: 15, 18; 4: 1-5). Rudo sezvinei, ndirwo rukuru; muchirangarira kuti ndirwozve runotsanangura chimiro chaMwari Pachake (1 Johani 4: 8).

`Fungisisa nemashoko anoti “Mwari rudo.” Tinofanira kuanzwisisa zvaanoreva chaizvo sei? Kana nepo tichikwanisa kubata pfungwa iyi zvishoma, nemhaka yei mashoko ari nyasha yakanaka kwatiri?`
