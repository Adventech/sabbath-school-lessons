---
title:  Kudzidza Kuenda Mberi
date:  25/09/2026
---

“Nyasha dzaKristu chete dzinogona kushandura mwoyo wedombo kuva wenyama, nekuupa vupenyu kuna Mwari. . . . Vanhu havana simba rekururamisa mweya, kuchenesa mwoyo. Zvivi zvekusazvibata hazvigoni kurapwa kunze kweMurapi Mukuru. Chipo chikuru kupfuvura zvimwe zvose zvedenga, Ndewakaberekwa Mumwe woga waBaba, akazara nenyasha nezvokwadi, ndiye woga anogona kudzikinura vakarasika”-Ellen G. White.

“Mwari rudo, 1 Johani 4: 16. Chimiro chake, mutemo wake rudo. Ndizvo zvazvakagara zvakangoita, ndizvo zvazvichagara zviri. ‘Wakasimudzirwa Mukuru anogara nekusingaperi,’ uyo anenzira dzinogara nekusingaperi,’ haashanduki. Maari hamuna kushanduka, kana mumvuri wekushanduka.’ Isaya 57: 15; Habhakuki 3: 6; Jakobo 1: 17. Kuratidzwa kose kwesimba rinoshandura chiratidzo cherudo rusingaperi. Kutonga kwaMwari pasirose nekudenga kunosanganisira kuzara kwezvikomborero kuzvisikwa zvose. . . . Nhoroondo yehondo huru pakati pechanaka nechakaipa, kubvira panguva yayakatanga kudenga kusvika pakukurirwa kwekupanduka nekuparadzwa kwechaipa zvachose, kuratidzwa kwerudo rusingaperi rwaMwari.”-Ellen G. White.

Tinofanira kuvona kuti Mweya Mutsvene . . . akaita semunhu sezvo Mwari anechimiro semunhu. . . . Mweya Mutsvene anechimiro semunhu, kudai kusaiva kudaro aisagona kupupurira kumweya nemweya yedu kuti tiri vana vaMwari. Mutsvenezve, aingadai asingakwanisi kunzvera zvakavanzika zvepfungwa dzaMwari.”-Ellen G. White.

Kune vatatu vatsvene vanorarama vekudenga; muzita remasimba matatu makuru aya-Baba, Mwanakomana, naMweya Mutsvene- avo vanogashira Kristu nekutenda kupenyu vanobhapatidzwa, nemasimba aya achashanda nevaranda vanoteerera vedenga mukushingaira kwavo kurarama vupenyu hutsva muna Kristu.”-Ellen G. White.
