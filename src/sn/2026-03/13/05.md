---
title:  Kuwadzana KweMweya Mutsvene
date:  23/09/2026
---

Nyasha dzaJesu hadzingoratidzi rudo rwaMwari kwatiri chete asi dzinowunza kuwandzana neMweya sechiratidzo cherudo urwu. Panguva imwechete iyi, kuwadzana kunobva murudo rwaMwari. Nekuti kana pasina rudo, hapana kuwadzana. Sekunyora kunoita Pauro: “Zvino kana kunyaradzwa kuripo kuna Kristu, kana kunyaradzwa kwerudo, kana kuyanana kweMweya, kana mwoyo munyoro nekunzwirwa tsitsi, zadzisai mufaro wangu, muve nekufunga kumwe, muchiva nerudo rumwe, nemweya mumwe, nendangariro imwe” (VaFiripi 2: 1, 2).

Vamwe vanhu vanoti Mweya Mutsvene isimba chete seremagetsi, asi ichi hachisi chokwadi. Saka Pauro aingataurirei nezvevanhu vaviri-Baba neMwanakomana-achibva aisa “simbavo” zvaro mumuwunganidzwa wehutatu hwehuMwari? Hazvingarevi chinhu. Sekutaurwa kwaBaba neMwanakomana muvukama nevanhu (2 VaKorinde 1: 3; 2 VaKorinde 11: 31), vukama naMweya nevanhu zvinotisvitsa pakudaira kuti munhu (VaRoma 8: 15, 16; tarisavo Johani 14: 16, 17, 26; Johani 15: 26).

Mashoko ekuti “kuwadzana kweMweya” (VaFiripi 2: 1) anogona kunzwisiswa munzira mbiri. Anogona kureva kuwadzana kwevanhu mumwe nemumwe zvichiitwa naMweya kana kuwadzana naMweya Pachake. Vadudziri vazhinji veBhaibheri vanopikisa vachiti dudziro idzi hadzinyatsogutsa. Asi kuwadzana kwevanhu nevanhu kunobva mukuwadzana naMweya.

`Verenga 1 VaKorinde 2: 10, 11; 1 VaKorinde 3: 16; 1 VaKorinde 12: 11; na 2 VaKorinde 3: 6, 17. Ko Pauro anodzidzisei kuvaKorinte nezvaMweya?`

Pauro anezvakawanda zvaanotaura nezve basa raMweya. Muna 1 na2 VaKorinde, mune mavhesi anotaura nezvaMweya Mutsvene anopfuvura 40. Mweya Mutsvene ndiye anokurudzira kusimbiswa kwekereke (1 VaKorinde 14: 12), anopa vanhu simba rebasa muvhangeri (1 VaKorinde 2: 4, 5), anozivisa zvakadzika zvehuMwari kwatiri (1 VaKorinde 2: 10, 11), nekutidzidzisa (1 VaKorinde 2: 13), anogara matiri (1 VaKorinde 3: 16; 1 VaKorinde 6: 19), anoshanda naKristu mukutiruramisa (1 VaKorinde 6: 11), anopa zvipo zvemweya kukereke (1 VaKorinde 12-14), anotisimbisira ruponeso (2 VaKorinde 1: 22), anonyora mutemo mumwoyo (2 VaKorinde 3: 3), nekupa vupenyu hutsva munaKristu (2 VaKorinde 3: 6), nekupa rusununguko kubva muzvivi (2 VaKorinde 3: 17). Zvechokwadi hatigoni kurarama tisina Mweya Mutsvene.

`Nemhaka yei vo, kunzwisisa hutsvene hwaMweya Mutsvene kwakakoshera kunzwisisa zvakazara rudo rwaMwari kwatiri?`
