---
title:  Hutatu HwehuMwari Wedu
date:  24/09/2026
---

Kana tichiverenga 2 VaKorinde 13: 14, mumwe anogona kufunga kuti Kristu ndiye chete anopa nyasha, Mwari ndiye mupi werudo, naMweya Mutsvene ndiye mupi wekuwadzana, asi izvi manyepo makuru kwazvo.

`Verenga 1 VaKorinde 1: 3, 4, 9; 1 VaKorinde 10: 16; 2 VaKorinde 1: 2, 12; VaRoma 8: 35; VaRoma 15: 30; VaGaratia 2: 20; na VaEfeso 3: 19. Ko ndima idzi dzinotikudini nezvenyasha, rudo nekuwadzana muhukama hwazvo nenhengo dzeHumwari Hutatu?`

Baba, neMwanakomana naMweya Mutsvene vanoshandira ruponeso rwedu pamwechete. Nyasha, rudo nekuwadzana hazvibvi kune mumwe chete, asi Kuvatatu veHumwari**.** Asi mumwe nemumwe wavo ane basa rake munhoroondo yeruponeso. Pauro anozviziva izvi saka achizvisimbisa mutsamba dzake. Semuenzaniso, zano reruponeso rinotaurwa nenzira yekuchengetedza mazwi inoshamisa muna VaGaratia 4: 4-6, nemabasa emumwe nemumwe weHutatu hweHumwari. Mwari Baba vakatuma Jesu, zvinoreva kuti Baba ndivo Mavambo ezano iri (VaGaratia 4: 4). Mwanakomana wakaberekwa nemukadzi (VaGaratia 4: 4), zvinoreva kuvaMunhu kwaKristu zvichizadzisa chitsidzo chekare (Genesi 3: 15). Mwanakomana akatidzikinura akatidzorera kuvukama hwakanaka naBaba, uho Satani akangaanyepa nezvaho (Genesi 3: 5). Mweya Mutsvene anoita kuti tive vana vaMwari chaivo (VaGaratia 4:6).

Kune zvimwe zvinyorwa zvakawanda zvinotaura nezveHutatu mumatsamba aPauro. Vatatu vanoshanda pamwechete, vachipa simba kukereke rekuti vashande basa (1 VaKorinde 12: 4-6), vachitipa simba repamweya (VaEfeso 3: 14-19), nekukurudzira kubatana kukura pakati penhengo dzekereke, izvi zvinoratidza humwe hunovonekwa paHutatu hweHumwari (VaEfeso 4:4–6), Hutatu huchishandira ruponeso rwedu (VaEfeso 1: 3, 13, 14). Muna VaEfeso, Pauro anoenda mberi kusvika pakuti tinofanira kuzadzwa nekuzara kwaBaba (VaEfeso 3: 19), Mwanakomana (VaEfeso 4: 13), neMweya Mutsvene (VaEfeso 5: 18).

Mukusvitsa kutaurirana nevaKorinte kwekupedzisira (2 VaKorinde 13: 14), Pauro haana kupedza zvakanaka-chitsidzo chekuti Vakuru Vekudenga vatatu, vachava nesu ikozvino nemumazera anotevera.

`Ko kuwadzana kwenhengo dzekereke kunofanira kuratidza sei vukama hunofadza hweHutatu weHumwari?`
