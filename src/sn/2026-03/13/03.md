---
title:  Rudo RwaMwari
date:  21/09/2026
---

“Nyasha dzaIshe wedu Jesu Kristu, nerudo rwaMwari, nekuwadzana kweMweya Mutsvene ngazvive nemi mose. Amen” (2 VaKorinde 13: 14). Nevhesi iri, Pauro anopedza tsamba yake yechipiri. Tarisa kuti anotaura nezveHutatu hwehuMwari muhurongwa uhu-Mwanakomana, Baba naMweya Mutsvene. Kuburikidza nebasa revatatu ava vose zvinotibatsira kunzwisisa kuti Mwari wakadini nezvaakatiitira.

`Verenga Johani 3: 16, 17; VaRoma 8: 37-39; na 1 Johani 4: 8-11. Ko ndima idzi dzinotiudzei nezve rudo rwaMwari?`

1 Johani 4: 8 inoti “Mwari rudo.” Rudo chimiro chaMwari chakakosha. Johani anosimbisa kuti tinogona kuziva rudo pakuti Mwari wakatipa Mwanakomana wake mumwe woga kutifira (Johani 3: 16). Mwari akatuma Jesu kurwendo rwerudzikinuro (Johani 3: 17), uye iyi yaiva rupandi rwebasa reruponeso (Mabasa 3: 20, 21; 1 Johani 4: 10, 14). Jesu anotaura nezvaBaba kakawanda muzvinyorwa zveMavhangeri achiti ndivo vakamutuma (Mateo 10: 40; Marko 9: 37).

Mumashoko akasimba kwazvo, Pauro anoti, “Mwari anoratidza rudo rwake kwatiri: Pakuti tichiri vatadzi Jesu wakatifira (VaRoma 5: 8). Tinogona kuvona zvishoma zverudo rwaMwari pakudanana kwemurume nemukadzi nepakati pevabereki nevana vavo, muhushamwari hunekurevesa nezvimwevo. Tinogona kuvonazve rudo rwaMwari muzvisikwa. Apa, Ellen G. White anoti, “‘Mwari rudo’ zvakanyorwa paruva rimwe nerimwe rakavhurika, pashizha rimwe nerimwe rehuswa. Shiri dzakanakisisa dzichizadza mhepo nemazwi enziyo dzadzo dzinofadza, nemaruva anoshamisa nemavara akasiyana-siyana mukunhuwirira kwawo mumhepo, miti yakareba-reba yesango nemashizha akawaridzwa pasi payo nezvakasvibira zvehuswa nemiti-zvose zvinopupura kupfava, nerudo rwehuBaba HwaMwari nechidiso chavo chekuti vana vavo vafare.”-Ellen G. White.

Asi hakuna chinobata mwoyo kusvika parudo rwekutipa Jesu sechibairo chezvivi zvedu. Kana tikanzwisisa kuti Mwari wakatida zvekusvika pakutumira Jesu kuisa vupenyu hwake pasi nekuda kwedu, chatinogona kuita apa chete “kubvuma kuisavo vupenyu hwedu pasi nekuda kwehama dzedu” (1 Johani 3: 16).

Pauro aida kuti vaKorinte vagare vakabatana. Asi kana pasina rudo, hapanavo kubatana. Saka ndicho chikonzero nei akavadzidzisa kuti “rudo runowumbiridza” (1 VaKorinde 8: 1 uye kuti kana pasina rudo, zvinhu zvose hazvina maturo (1 VaKorinde 13: 1-3). Saka, zvose zvatingaita ngatizviitei murudo (1 VaKorinde 16: 14), rudo runobva kuna Mwari ruchienderera mberi.

`Chii chataingarasikirwa nacho kudai Jesu Pachake asiri Mwari zvakazara naMwari anogara nekusingaperi?`
