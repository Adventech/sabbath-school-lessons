---
title:  Mwari Werudo
date:  22/09/2026
---

Munyika dzechihedeni dzekare, vanhu vaidaira kuti vamwari havadi vanhu. Asi kutoti vaiitira vanhu hasha nekuvatsamwira saka vaifanira kunyengererwa kuti vafarire vanhu. Pfungwa yaMwari werudo, sezvatinovona muBhaibheri dzaiva ngano zvadzo dzisingadairiki. Sekushamisa kwaiita mashoko aya mumazuva ake, Pauro anoratidza Mwari wedu sa “Mwari werudo nerugare” (2 VaKorinde 13: 11).

`Verenga 2 VaKorinde 13: 11. Ungawana tariro sei kubva munezvinotaurwa apa? Ko unganzwisisa zvirinani sei zviri kudzidziswa nevhesi iri?`

Mashoko ekuti “Mwari werudo nerugare” anogona kududzirwa nenzira dzingaita mbiri dzakasiyana. Apa Mwari anovonekwa sechitubu cherudo nerugare. Kune rumwe rutivi, Mwari anonzi ndiye rudo nerugare. Hazvina basa, hazvo, kusarudza pakati penzira mbiri idzi. Nekuti rudo nerugare hazviparadzani muchiro chaMwari, anotipa rudo nerugare.

Mune dzimwe nzvimbo, Pauro anotaura nezvaMwari sa “Mwari unoshingirira nekunyaradza” (VaRoma 15: 5); “wetariro” (VaRoma 15: 13); “werugare” (VaRoma 15:33; VaRoma 16: 20; 1 VaKorinde 14: 33; VaFiripi 4: 9; 1 VaTesaronika 5: 23), “Baba vengoni” (2 VaKorinde 1: 3), na “Mwari wekunyaradza kose” (2 VaKorinde 1: 3). Mwari ndiye mupi wemaropafadzo ose aya. Anotipa izvozvo zvose kubva murudo rwake rusingakundikani.

Uyezve, kana nepo mashoko ekuti “Mwari werugare” akajairika muBhaibheri, mashoko ekuti “Mwari werudo” anongovonekwa apa chete (2 VaKorinde 13: 11), saka, anoda kufungisisa kwedu.

Sekuvona kwevadudziri vazhinji, kutaura kwaPauro nezvaMwari werudo mumavhesi mashomanana asati aita munamato wekuvhara unobata hutatu muna 2 VaKorinde 13: 14 zvinoita sekuti anofunga Mwari vari Vanhu vatatu. “Kana nepo achishandisa izwi ‘Mwari’ semumwe wevatatu, kunzwisisa kwake Jesu naMweya mune tsamba dzake . . . . zvinotimanikidza kuvona mashoko ose seanotsanangura Mwari mumwechete uyo kereke yekutanga yaitora nenzira nhatu. Zvaizotora makore anopfuvura zana vadzidzi veBhaibheri . . . . kuti vatange kushandisa mazwi akafanana neekuti ‘hutatu’ senzira pfupi yekutsanangura zvaitaura naPauro.”-Tom Wright.

Tinotenda muna Mwari mumwechete, mubatanidzwa weVanhu vatatu vanogara nekusingaperi muvukama hwerudo. Hutatu hwehuMwari hunotida nekutikokera kudanana nenzira inoratidza rudo rwuri pakati peVatatu vanowumba huMwari.
