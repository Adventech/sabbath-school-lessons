---
title:  Wanano Nekuzvigarira Munhu Asinakuwana
date:  23/07/2026
---

Kusimbisa kwaPauro kwekuti “muviri wedu itemberi yeMweya Mutsvene” (1 VaKorinde 6: 19) kunovonekwa mundima iyi seyambiro yekubva kuvupombwe. Kuva temberi yeMweya Mutsvene inzira yoga yekurarama vupenyu hutsvene. Kereke inharaunda yevanhu vakasiyana neruzhinji rwevanhu vavanogara pakati pavo. Huvepo hwaMweya Mutsvene ndiho hunoita kuti zvikwanisike.

`Verenga 1 VaKorinde 6: 19-7: 9. Ko ndima iyi inojekesa sei nzira idzo munhu dzaangararama zviri mumurairo “wekutiza vupombwe” (1 VaKorinde 6: 18)?`

Kune zvidzidzo zvakakosha zvenyaya dzevupombwe muna 1 VaKorinde 7. Nekutaura kusina hanya, chitsauko ichi chinogona kupatsanurwa kuiswa muzvikamu zviviri: (1) Rairo muwanano (1 VaKorinde 7: 1-24) ne(2) Rairo dzekugara munhu asina kuwana/kuwanikwa (1 VaKorinde 7: 25-40). Muchitsauko cha 1 VaKorinde 7 tinobatsirwa kunzwisisa kuti kutaura nezvevupombwe kwakakosha uye kunofanira kuitwa.

Asi kana tichiverenga 1 VaKorinde 7, tinofanira kurangarira kuti Pauro arikupindura mibvunzo yaakatobvunzwa inechekuita nenyaya idzi dzepakereke yepaKorinte. Zvingatoita sekuti Pauro akanga asingakoshesi wanano, zvisiri izvo (1 Timoti 4: 1-3; 5: 14 na VaHebheru 13: 4).

Zviripachena kuti rairo yekuti “tizai vupombwe” muna 1 VaKorinde 6: 18 inovharwa mativi ose nepfungwa yekuti mubatanidzwe naKristu (1 VaKorinde 6: 17) nekuva temberi yeMweya (1 VaKorinde 6: 19). Kungava nenzira irinani here yekutiza vupombwe ingapfuvura iyi? Hakuna zvechokwadi.

Tichizivazve kuti Mwari ndiye akasika bonde, asi richifanira kuitwa muwanano chete. Bonde chipo chevanhu vakawanana, nenzira inodiwa neBhaibheri chete.

Paanoti “tizai vupombwe” Pauro anogona kuva aiva naJosefa mupfungwa (Genesi 39: 6-18). Bhaibheri rinoti Mai Potifera pavaitaura naye zuva nezuva Josefa “akatiza kubva mumba” (Genesi 39:18). Bhaibheri harizvitauri pachena, asi zvinovonekwa kuti Josefa aimirira kuzoita zvepabonde muwanano chete (Genesi 41: 45). Akanga ari murume akazadzwa neMweya Mutsvene (Genesi 41: 38) achida kuita zvakanga zvakanaka pamberi paMwari nguva dzose.

`Ko isu sekereke tingazvikwanisa sei kuzvidzivirira kubva mamaonero akaipisisa akashata akazara mutsika dzedu?`
