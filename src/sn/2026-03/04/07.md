---
title:  Kudzidza Kuenda Mberi
date:  24/07/2026
---

“Zvinofadza nekushamisa kuti mumitsara yakanyorwa tsika dzakaipa muna 1 VaKorinde 5: 10, 11 na 1 VaKorinde 6: 9, 10, tinovonawo kunamata zvifananidzo, kudhakwa, zvirimowo nevupombwe. Sekurangarira kwaPauro muna 1 VaKorinde 10: 7 (enzanisa na Ekisodo 32: 1-6), kuti pamitambo yekunamatwa kwezvifananidzo paiwanikwa kudyisa, kunwa kwakanyanya, izvo zvaivhurira musuwo kuvupombwe (1 VaKorinde 10: 8). Ellen G. White.

Hazvikwanisiki kuti munhu akawana mukana wekucheneswa iye achine chindini achidyisa. . . . Simba rekugona kuramba kudzvinyirirwa kwemuviri ririmo mumunhu asi kusimbira mutsika dzekudyisa nekunwa dzinopedza simba remuviri rekuramba zvakaipa. Mukugutsa nhomba dzakaipa nezvidiso zvenyama kunyange muMakristu chaiwo kunopedza simba remuviri nepfungwa nekukwanisa kuramba zvakaipa.” –Ellen G. White.

“Kana munhu akazvidurura zvakazara, kana zvimwari zvose zvenhema zvikabviswa muvupenyu, pasara panozadzwa naMweya waKristu. Munhu uyu anekutenda kunoshanda nerudo runochenesa mweya kubva mutsvina dzose nekuzvibiswa pamweya. Mwari anotsvaka kutisimudzira, kutiita vakaperera, kusvika pachiyero chedenga. Nekuda kwaizvozvo Mweya wake unoramba uchitinyengerera. . . . Zvidiso zvedu zvekuberekwa nazvo kana zvikasagadziriswa naMweya Mutsvene waMwari, zvinotova nembeu yerufu runobva mukusazvibata.”-Ellen G. White.

**Mibvunzo Yekutaurirana**:

`Vatendi vazhinji vepaKorinte vaitsvaka kurumbidzwa nevanhu. Ko izvi zvinenjodzi huru sei kuMakristu? Ko tingaitei kuti tisapindavo mukukanganisa uku?`

`Mubvunzo waPauro isingadi mhinduro, “Ko hamuzivi here kuti muviri wenyu itemberi yeMweya Mutsvene?” (1 VaKorinde 6 :19, inovhara mibvunzo minomwe iri muna 1 VaKorinde 5-6 yakaziviswa nenzira yekuti: “Ko hamuzivi here kuti?” (1 VaKorinde 5:6; 1 VaKorinde 6:2, 3, 9, 15, 16, 19). Yose mibvunzo iyi inoda mhinduro yekubvuma yakasimba-yakafanana neyekuti “Haa ndizvozvo chaizvo.” Ko mibvunzo iyi inotibatsira sei kunzwisisa hanya yaPauro yekereke? Ko isu nemhaka yei tichifanira kudarowo munyaya idzi?`

`Wanano yakabva kuna Mwari (Genesi 1: 27, 28; Genesi 2: 18-24) uye inofanira kukudzwa (VaHebheru 13: 4). Apo vakawanda vanoti ndezvekare, tingaratidza nyika sei kuti wanano chipo zvechokwadi chinobva kuna Mwari, chakabva muEdeni chaimo?`
