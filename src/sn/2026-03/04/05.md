---
title:  Mushonga Kurapa Vupombwe
date:  22/07/2026
---

`Verenga 1 VaTesaronika 4: 1-8. Ko ndima iyi inoti kudini nezvevukama huri pakati kururamiswa nekurega kuita vupombwe?`

Kana nepo Pauro ainyorera mumwe munhu mumavhesi aripamusoro, mashoko acho anogona kushanda pose-pose, kuMakristu ose zvawo. Kana nepo hazvo zvichitungamirira kumubvunzo wekuti: Chii chaimboitika muKorinte? Nemhaka yei matambudziko ose aya?

Vamwe vagari vemuKorinte vaidaira kuti sezvo vhangeri yakavasunungura vaikwanisa kuita zvose zvavanoda. Vaidaira kuti sezvo dumbu rakagadzirirwa zvekudya, muviri wakagadzirirwa bonde, nebonde rakagadzirirwawo muviri (1 VaKorinde 6: 13). Pauro aipikisa achiti uyu waiva mufananidzo usina kunaka werusununguko runowanikwa muChikristu. Kushaya kuzvidzora munyaya dzepabonde hakufambirani nechimiro cheChikristu uye kutadza kunzwisisa rusununguko runopiwa munhu nevhangeri (VaRoma 8: 2; VaGaratia 5: 13). Tinosunungurwa kubva muzvivi, “hatisunungurwi” kuti tiite zvivi (VaRoma 8:2; VaRoma 6:18, 22). Chokwadi chaicho ndechekuti, “muviri . . . ndewaShe, naShe ndiye muridzi wemuviri” (1 VaKorinde 6: 13). Tiri vaKristu (1 VaKorinde 6: 15), saka uyo watiri wake ndiye anofanira kutaura zvatinoita. Zvakabatana chaizvo zvinhu izvi. Izvi zvinoratidzwa muna 1 VaKorinde 6 munzira nhatu dzakasiyana-siyana.

Yekutanga, tinozivikanwa **setakacheneswa**, **takaruramiswa** “muzita raIshe Jesu Kristu neMweya waMwari wedu” (1 VaKorinde 6: 11). Zvivi zvakanyorwa muna 1 VaKorinde 6: 12-20, hazvina simba muvupenyu weavo vakashambidzwa, vakacheneswa nekururamiswa.

Yechipiri, tiri **nhengo dzaKristu** (1 VaKorinde 6: 15). Izvi zvinoreva kuti tinofanira kubatanidzwa naKristu (1 VaKorinde 6: 17). Vupombwe hunokanganisa humwe uhu (1 VaKorinde 6: 13, 15). Ani nani anozvibatanidza nemumwe muvupombwe “vave muviri mumwechete” naye (1 VaKorinde 6: 16). Humwe naKristu kuburikidza naMweya hunoburitsa kuzvidzora kweChikristu munyaya dzepabonde.

Yetatu, miviri yedu **“itemberi yeMweya Mutsvene”** (1 VaKorinde 6: 19, 20). Nzira yoga yekurarama hupenyu hutsvene nekuzvidzora munyaya dzebonde kuva nevukama hwakasimba naKristu kuburikidza neMweya Mutsvene. Kune imwe nzvimbo Pauro anotaura nezvekuva temberi yeMweya Mutsvene mukuchengetedza muviri “chipiriso chitsvene, chinogashirika pamberi paMwari” (VaRoma 12: 1).

`Funga nematambudziko anounzwa nechitema chevupombwe pamusoro pevanhu. Ko izvi zvinofanira kutiudzei nezvekukosha kwenyaya iyi kuMukristu?`
