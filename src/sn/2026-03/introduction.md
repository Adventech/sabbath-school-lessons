#### Musimboti Wehupenyu HweChikristu Nekupupura

Kunyora tsamba zvinhu zvekare-kare zvavakutopera. Tashandura nzira nemanyorere etsamba. Hongu nzira dzekutaurirana dzawanda kwazvo mapepa haachina basa. Asi musimboti wazvo, matsamba emumhepo nedzimwewo nzira dzekugadzira matsamba nemagetsi zvinongoshanda zvimwechete: zvinobatanidza vanhu nekutaurirana, nezvavanganzwa munyama, nemupfungwa.

Nemhaka yei vanhu vachinyora tsamba? Mhinduro inogona kuva yechokwadi ndeyekuti vane zvavanoda kutaura. Ndizvovo zvakakanga zvakaita Pauro. Kana nepo akanga aine zvakawanda zvekutaura, Pauro akanga asina mukana wekusangana nevanhu vaaida kutaurirana navo.

Saka, ainyora tsamba—dzakafanana nedzekuVaKorinte, idzo dzinezvimwe zvakakosha kwazvo zveMagwaro. Zvimwe zvacho: “Nekuti ndakati handingazivi chinhu pakati penyu, asi Jesu Kristu Iye wakaroverwa pamuchinjikwa” (1 VaKorinde 2: 2), uyezve “Nekuti munoziva nyasha dzaShe wedu, Jesu Kristu, kuti kunyange akanga akafuma, wakava murombo nekuda kwenyu, kuti imi nevurombo hwake muve vafumi (2 VaKorinde 8:9). Ko kuzoti rwiyo runoshamisa rwerudo muna 1 VaKorinde 13?

Kune rumwe rutivi, ani nani anoverenga tsamba dzaPauro anotoshamiswa chete, kusiri kuti nekuda kwemamwe matambudziko anowanikwa mukereke—sevupombwe—asi nekuda kwekunetsana kwaiwanikwa munhengo dzekereke nekuda kwezvikwata-zvikwata. Kana uchifunga kuti kereke yenyu inematambudziko, gadzirira kuvona champupuri nemhepo yematambudziko ayo Pauro aitarisana nawo muKorinte. Zvimwe matambudziko epakereke penyu haana kunyanyisa sezvaungafungidzira. Uchavona kuti paKorinte zvinhu zvakanga zvakatoipisisa kwazvo.

Sekukanganisa kungawanikwa mumatambudziko epaKorinte, tsamba dzekuVaKorinte dzinotibata chaizvo, kusiri kubatwa nematambudziko asi nenzira dzinoshamisa idzo Pauro aidzigadzirisa nadzo. Nekukurudzira nhengo dzekereke kuti vazviidze vomene, zvimiro zvavo, netsika dzeiva munzvimbo iyi kana dzichiinzaniswa nevhangeri yaJesu Kristu, anosimudzira shoko remuchinjikwa. Kushandisa pashoko aPauro pachake paneimwe nzvimbo, chimiro chipi zvacho chingava pasi peevhangeri chinovonekwa se “chakatukwa” (VaGaratia 1: 8, 9).

Munguva dzaPauro Korinte raiva guta raiva nemukurumbira wekufuma nekutengeserana kwakasimba kwazvo, zvose izvi zvichibva pachiteshi chikuru chengarava, zvivakwa, kuvakwa kwezvikepe nezvivezwa zvevhu. Guta raiva muzinda mukuru wezvekutengeserana. Asizve raizivikanwa vo nevupombwe vukuru kwazvo, kusawirirana munezvekunamata, makirawo ekunamatira zvimwewo zvimwari. Zvechokwadi zuva rimwe nerimwe remuKorinte, rairatidzira kunamatwa kwezvifananidzo kunotyisa kwazvo. Zvaiitika kareko muguta iri zvinotibatsira kunzvisisa hanya yaPauro nekuda kweguta iri reChikristu, nekurudziro dzake kwariri.

Kota ino tichatarisa tsamba dzaPauro kuvaKorinte. Mutsamba mbiri idzi dzakakosha kwazvo muTestamende Itsva, muapostora anopa vhangeri semusimboti wevupenyu hweChikristu nekupupura, mawonero anofanira kuitwa zvinhu zvose. Zvisinei nemanetswa emumwe nemumwe wedu kana ekereke yose zvayo murwendo rwedu rwekuenda kudenga—mhinduro munyaya dzose dzinotambudza mubasa redu ratinoitira Kristu yakangofanana neyevaKorinte: “Jesu Kristu nemuchinjikwa wake”! (1 VaKorinde 2:2).

Jesu arikudzoka nekukasika. Iyi inguva yekubatana zvakanyanya munaKristu, kubvumira Mweya Mutsvene kutishandisa kupfuvura dzimwe nguva dzose, kushandiswa kwezvipo zvemweya kusati kwakamboitwa kubva kare, nenguva dzakakosha kwazvo neMuponesi wedu wakamutswa kubva kuvavakafa. Yava nguva yekunyatsoshanda nemwoyo wose basa raMwari, muhutariri nemumabasa ekuparidzira, kurwisana nedzidziso dzenhema zvakasimba, kukura munyasha, rudo, nemukuwadzana nevamwe. Ino ndiyo nguva yekubatirira nesimba nekutendeka kushoko reMuchinjikwa, saka tsamba dzaPauro kuvaKorinte dzichitidzidzisa kuita izvozvo chaizvo.

Adenilton Tavares de Aguiar, PhD, mudzidzisi mukuru wekududzirwa kweBhaibheri paChikoro Chikuru chinodzidzisa Bhaibheri chinonzi Adventist University Center of São Pauroo (UNASP) muBrazil.
