`Verenga 2 VaKorinde 2: 12, 13. Pauro akaindepi abva kunyora “tsamba inochemedza” kwavari? Akanoitei paakasvika po?`

Mwoyo waPauro wakanga usina kugadzikana paakanga akamirira Tito (2 VaKorinde 7: 5, 6). Zvisinei nekusagadzikana uku, haana kumira kutaura nezvaJesu (2 VaKorinde 2: 12). Aida Jesu zvikuru kwazvo. Panguva iyi, akanga asati aziva matuso etsamba yake. Aiva neshungu dzekuvona Tito nekunzwa kuti chii chakabuda mutsamba yake pakati pevaKorinte.

Basa raPauro paTroasi rakabudirira, asi “akanga asingakwanisi kuramba aripo. ‘Hanya yemakereke,’ zvikuru sei kereke yepaKorinte, waiva mutoro unorema kwazvo pamwoyo wake. Akanga akatarisira kusangana naTito paTroasi nekuziva kuti mashoko ake erairo neekutsiura aakanga atumira kuhama dzeKorinte akanga agashirwa here, asi apa akanga akakuhunika. ‘Ndakashaya zororo mumweya wangu,’ akanyora achidaro nezvakanga zvaitika kwaari, ‘nekuti handina kuwana Tito hama yangu.’ Saka akabva paTroasi akayambukira Masedonia, paakasangana naTimotio paFiripai.”-Ellen G. White.

`Verenga 2 VaKorinde 2: 14-17. Ko Pauro akaitei paakasangana naTito muMasedonia nekunzwa nezvemashoko akanaka akabva kuKorinte?`

Mukufara kwake kukuru, Pauro akasimbisa kuti Mwari “nguva dzose anotitungamirira mukukunda muna Kristu” (2 VaKorinde 2: 14). Mashoko anoshamisa zvakadini aya! Mwoyo wakazara mehuvepo hwaKristu huchiparadzira “hwema hwekumuziva munzvimbo dzose” (2 VaKorinde 2: 14).

Pauro anofara muna Kristu nekuti tsamba inochemedza yakabereka michero yakanaka yaaitarisira kukohwa (2 VaKorinde 7: 5-9). Uku kukurira kukuru kwazvo. Panguva imwechete iyi, muna 2 VaKorinde 2: 17, Pauro anosimbisa kurevesa kwake semuapostora waKristu (2 VaKorinde 2: 17; 2 VaKorinde 1: 12). Kana takatarisa pandima iyi, chii chinosianisa muranda waKristu akatendeka nemushumiri wenhema ndechekuti wekupedzisira anofambisa vhangeri nekuda kwezvaanowana iye, asi wekutanga anoparidza Shoko raMwari nemwoyo wose nerudo kuna Kristu.
