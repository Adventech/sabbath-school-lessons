title:  Kudzidza Kuenda Mberi
date:  28/08/2026
---

“Vakashungurudzwa nematambudziko akawandisa ndivo vanogona kunyaradza vamwe, vachiunza mudziya kose kwavanoenda. Ava vakarohwa nekunakiswa nenjodzi dzavo; havana kurasikirwa nechivimbo muna Mwari apo dambudziko rakavarova, asi vakaswededzwa pedyo-pedyo nerudo rwake runodzivirira. Ava zvapupu zvipenyu zvesimba raMwari rekuponesa.”

“Hupenyu hweChikristu hwakaperera hunogara huchipa chiedza, kunyaradza nerugare. Hwakazara nekuchena, vuchenjeri mukuita, kusazvikudza, nekushandisika. Hunosundwa nekushaikwa kwechindi kunokonzera hwema hwakachena. Hwakazara naKristu, nekusiya muzvezve wechiedza pose panoindwa neanaho.” Nyasha DzaMwari Dzinoshamisa, p. 122.

“Muapostora Pauro akavona zvakafanira kutsiura zvakaipa mukereke, asi haana kutadza kuzvidzora mukutsiura kukanganisa. Anozvitsanangura neshungu. Akachenjerera sei kuti asiye chimiro chekuti wakanga ari shamwari yehama yaiva yatadza! Akavabatsira kunzwisisa kuti zvaiva nemutengo kwaari kuti zvivarwadze. Akavasiira pfungwa yekuti zvavaida ndizvo zvaaidavo.”-Ellen G. White.

**Mibvunzo Yekutaurirana**:

`Muna 2 VaKorinde 2: 1-14, Pauro anosimbisa kutendeka kwake mubasa rekuparidza. Nemhaka yei chimiro ichi chakakosha kwazvo mubasa rekuparidza?`

`Ko kushandura kwakaita Pauro zano rake renzendo kunotiudzei nekukosha kwekugona kushanduka mubasa reChikristu? Nemhaka yei zvakakosha kuvona kugona kusiya zano rako mubasa iri?`

`Pauro akasangana nekushungurudzwa nekurwadziwa mubasa rekuparidza. Izvi zvinoratidza pachena kuti vatungamiriri vesangano vanhu vanogona kushungurudzwa sevamwe vanhu wo. Ko nhengo dzesangano dzingaitei kuvarerutsira basa ravo?`

`Pauro anotaura nezvekushaya kwake zororo (2 VaKorinde 2: 13) achiti ataura nezvekukunda kwake muna Kristu (2 VaKorinde 2: 14). Ko aingataura sei nezvekushaya kwake simba panguva imwechete iyi? Ko isu tingazviita sei?`
