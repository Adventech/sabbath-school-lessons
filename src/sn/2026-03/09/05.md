---
title:  Kuregerera Nekusimbisa Rudo
date:  26/08/2026
---

Pane kushanyira vaKorinte kechipiri, Pauro, adzoka kubva kuEfeso, akatumira tsamba yakazivikanwa sekuti “yaivachemedza” (tarisa 2 VaKorinde 2: 3, 4; 2 VaKorinde 7: 8, 12).

`Verenga 2 VaKorinde 7: 5-13. Ko chii chakabuda munezvaakavanyorera, uye Pauro akaitei nazvo nekuda kwezvavakaita?`

Pauro naTito vakasangana paMasedonia, apo Pauro akanzwa nhau dzakanaka kwazvo kubva kuna Tito dzekuti mashoko ake anochemedza akakonzera zvakanaka, izvo zvakazadza mwoyo waPauro nemufaro mukuru. Kana vamwe kare vakanga vagadzirira kupikisana naPauro, zvino kereke yakanga yava kurutivi rwake. Zvakakosha sei kutsigirana nevatungamiriri vedu. Senhengo dzekereke, tinogona kurerutsa basa ravo kupfuvura zvariri.

`Verenga 2 VaKorinde 2: 5-11. Ko nderipi shoko guru apa?`

Ndima iyi inechekuita nekuranga kwekereke. Vadzidzi vanopikisana apa kuti munhu aiva nemhosva ndeuya aiita vupombwe nehama wemuna 1 VaKorinde 5: 1-5 kana mumwevo munhu, munhu aifurira vamwe mukereke mukupa Pauro mhosva yekuti wakanga asingaiti zvinhu zvake nenzira yakafanira nguva dzose munzendo dzake. Nyaya iyi inenge yaibva mupfungwa yekutanga. Chakakosha apa ndechekuziva zvinofanira kuitirwa munhu anenge atadza mukereke.

Ndima iyi inodzidzisa kuti chinangwa chekuranga kwekereke ndechikudzoreredza kuburikidza nekuregerera uye nekusimbisa rudo kumutadzi (2 VaKorinde 2: 6-8, 10). Ndima inoratidza zvishoma kuti kuranga kwekereke kunorwadza, asi kunofanira kuitwa. Kureva kuti chinangwa chekuranga chakanaka, chinoratidza “nyasha”- kana nepo dzimwe kereke dzichitadza kutarisana nekuranga zvachose kunyange chitema chiripachena sei. Kune rumwe rutivi, vamwe vanogona kuwoma, vasingaregereri, vachishandisa hasha. Chitema chinofanira kutaurwa nezvacho nekugadziriswa, asi zvichiitwa murudo. Saka, Pauro aikurudzira kereke kusimbisa rudo kumutadzi (2 VaKorinde 2: 8) nekuti iye aida kereke (2 VaKorinde 2: 4)!

`Kereke yapaKorinte yaigona kuda mutadzi (2 VaKorinde 2: 8) nekuti iyo yaivawo yagashira rudo kubva kuna Pauro. Ko izvi zvinotidzidzisei nezverudo?`
