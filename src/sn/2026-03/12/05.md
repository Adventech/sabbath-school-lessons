---
title:  Kutambudzika Nekuda Kwevhangeri
date:  16/09/2026
---

Apedza kufumura vadzidzisi venhema sevashandi vaSatani (2 VaKorinde 11: 1-15), Pauro zvino “anotamba” mutambo wavo wekuzvikudza zvishoma sebenzi (2 VaKorinde 11: 16-21) kuitira kuti vaKorinte vavone kuti zvaiva zvisina maturo kuteerera kutaura kwevadzidzisi venhema. Kana vaKorinte vaivarumbidza, Pauro aifanira kunyanya kukudzwa. Kutambudzikira evhangeri kwake kwairatidza kuti aiva muranda waMwari akatendeka waKristu (2 VaKorinde 11: 22, 23).

`Verenga 2 VaKorinde 11: 22-28. Ko Pauro arikuti kudini apa?`

Kana nepo zvairatidza huapostora hwaPauro zvakanga zvakafanana nezvevadzidzisi venhema (2 VaKorinde 11: 22), mabasa ake kuna Kristu aipfuvura avo (2 VaKorinde 11: 23). “Ivo vashumiri vaKristu here?” akabvunza. Mhinduro ndeyekuti “zvikuru ini” mabasa ake akanga akawandisa; kusungwa kwake, kakawanda; kurohwa kwake, kwainyanya kurwadza.

Asi izvi hazvisi izvo zvoga. Matambudziko aakanyora ose anosanganisira kurohwa ka39 (2 VaKorinde 11: 24, kurohwa neshamhu, kutakwa nemabwe, kuparara kwezvikepe, njodzi mugungwa rakadzika (2 VaKorinde 11: 25), njodzi munzendo, njodzi munzizi, njodzi kubva kumakororo, njodzi kubva kuhama dzake, njodzi kubva kuvatorwa, njodzi mumaguta aiva nevanhu vazhinji, njodzi mumarenje, njodzi kubva kuvatendi venhema (2 VaKorinde 11: 26), mabasa airwadza, kushanda nguva dzakareba, kushaya nguva yekurara, nzara nenyota, kushaiwa zvekudya, chando, kushaya zvekufuka (2 VaKorinde 11: 27). Kuita sekuti izvi zvakanga zvisina kukwana, aifanira kushandawo nekuwandirwa nezvinhu mupfungwa nekuda kwenguva shoma dzekurarama achifunga makereke (2 VaKorinde 11: 28).

Muranda waKristu woga angada kupinda mumatambudziko akadai nekuda kweevhangeri. Kudai Pauro aida kunyatsozvikudza nekuda kwematambudziko ake, aiva nezvakawandisa zvekutaura. Asi ndima inotevera yetsamba inoratidza kuti kuzvikudza kwake kwakanga kusingabvi pane zvaakaita iye asi zvaakaitirwa naKristu. Pauro aiziva kuti simba raMwari rinonyanya kuvonekwa mukushaya simba kwevanhu (2 VaKorinde 12: 9, 10). Nekumupa munzwa munyama (2 VaKorinde 12: 7), Mwari vakadzivirira Pauro kubva mukuzvikudza nekuda kwezvaakanga aita. Izvi zvikamudzikamisa, achiziva vutera hwake, akatsamira pasimba dzvene, ari muchimiro chekugashira zvimwezve zvenyasha netsitsi dzaMwari.

`Wakambotambudzikira vo vhangeri here? Wakadzidzei kubva mo? Ko zvakaitwa naPauro mukutambudzika kwake zvingakubatsira sei mune ako?`
