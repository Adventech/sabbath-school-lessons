---
title:  Kudzidza Kuenda Mberi
date:  18/09/2026
---

“Tenzi varikuchengetedza vanhu vavo kubva mukudzokorora zvakakanganiswa nevekare. Kwakagara kwazara vadzidzisi venhema vanofambisa dzidziso dzemanyepo nekuita kusiri kutsvene, vachishanda nemirairo yekunyepera, nenzira dzekunyengera kukuru kwazvo, dzakavanzika, vakada kana kunyengedza kana zvichibvira vasangangurwa chaivo.”

“Ishe vakagadzira kuti pfungwa dzedu dziedzwe, kuvona kana pachirikudiwa kunya-tsowongorora zvirevo zvipenyu kana tichiri mukutenda kana kwete. Vakawanda vanoti vanodaira zvokwadi vakasarudza kugara hupenyu husina kuwoma, vachiti, “Ndakafuma, uye ndava nezvakawanda, saka hapana chandinoshaiwa.” Vanhu varikuchengetedza manyepo, zvokwadi iripachena; zvekuti kudai vaiwunza dzidziso dzavo kushoko raMwari, vasingaverengi shoko raMwari kubva mudzidziso dzavo, kuti vavone kuti pfungwa dzakanaka here, vaingadai vasingafambi murima nemuhupofu, kana kufarira manyepo avo. Vakawanda vanovudza Magwaro zvavanoda kuti ataure zvinowirirana nepfungwa dzavo, nekuzvinyengera nevamwevo nekumonyorora shoko raMwari. Patinodzidza shoko raMwari, tinofanira kuzviita nemwoyo yakazvininipisa. Kuzvikudza kose, kuda kubuda nezvitsva kose, zvinofanira kuiswa parutivi. Pfungwa dzagara kwemakore nemakore dzichidikanwa hadzifaniri kutorwa sekusiri kukundikana kana sedzisingakundikani.”-Ellen G. White.

**Mibvunzo Yekutaurirana**:

`Verenga 2 VaKorinde 10: 1-6 zvekare. Ko Pauro anoshandisa nzira dzipi “muhondo” yemweya yekurwira zvokwadi yaMwari, uye tingazviitavo sei muhondo dzedu dzemweya?`

`Bhaibheri rinoti kuguma kusati kwasvika, vadzidzisi vakawanda venhema vachaedza kukwevera vanhu kubva kuzvokwadi. Ko kereke yepenyu ingaitei kudzivirira nhengo dzesangano kukunyengedzwa nevadzidzisi venhema vanogona kutova pakereke yepenyu? Nemhaka yei izvi zvakakoshera kuzadziswa kwebasa rekereke?`

`Nemhaka yei Pauro akavona zvakafanira kuzvikudza nekuda kwenjodzi dzakawanda dzaakatambudzika madziri dzaakanyora (2 VaKorinde 11: 16-33)? Uyewo zvinorevei kuti “kuzvikudza munaShe”?`

`Nemhaka yei zvakakoshera nhengo dzekereke kuzviidza ivo vamene kuti vavone kuti vachiri mukutenda here (2 VaKorinde 13: 5)? Ko zvingapa musiano upi?`
