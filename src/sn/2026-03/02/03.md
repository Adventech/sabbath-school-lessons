---
title:  Vupenzi Kune Vekuparadzwa
date:  06/07/2026
---

Kune rumwe rutivi rwevupenzi hwevanhu kune vuchenjeri hwaMwari, Pauro anosimbisa kuti “shoko remuchinjikwa vupenyu kune vachaparadzwa” (1 VaKorinde 1:18). Ndiko kekutanga mukatanhatu kanotaurwa nezvevupenzi muna 1 VaKorinde 1:18–31.

`Verenga 1 VaKorinde 1:20, 21, 23, 25, na27. Mukutaurwa kwevupenzi mundima idzi kunotibatsira sei kunzwisisa zvinorehwa naPauro paanotaura nezveshoko reMuchinjikwa sevupenzi kune vanoparadzwa?`

Izwi rechiGiriki rekuti vupenzi muna 1 VaKorinde 1:18 ndi *mōria*. Izwi iri rinovonekwa kashanu chete muTestamende Itsva, uye murimuna 1 VaKorinde (1 VaKorinde 1:18, 21, 23; 1 VaKorinde 2:14; 1 VaKorinde 3:19). Kunze kweizwi iri *mōria*, mamwe mazwi anobva pamwechete anovonekwa kakawanda muTestamende Itsva. Maanowanikwa mazwi aya ndemuna1 VaKorinde.

Kutaurwa kwevupenzi muna 1 VaKorinde 1:18, 23 hakusi kushora njere dzeVaKorinte asi kutaura nezvekuramba kwavo kufungisisa nezveidi revhangeri. Matuso achikonzera kuti Pauro achitsiura kushaya kuzvibata nekufunga, zvinoreva kusavona zvirimberi, kusanganisira kupandukira Mwari. Ndicho chikonzero nei akanyanya kutaura nezvenyaya iyi muna 1 VaKorinde yose.

Fungidzira nezvechimiro chaPauro muguta iri. Anosvika muguta rakazara nekuzvikudza kwezvavanoti vuchenjeri nekungwara netsika dzemhando yepamusoro-soro. Saka anotaura nezve muJudha weGarire, Jesu weNazareta, uyo akanga aroverwa pamuchinjikwa nemaRoma, akamutswa kubva kuvakafa—zvose izvi kuri kusabhadhara kwezvivi zvavo chete asi zvivi zvenyika yose. *Muchinda uyu anechokwadi here nezvanotaura? Aitamba nepfungwa dzaani?* Uhu hwakanga husiri huchenjeri hutsva, hwaigona kutsanangurwa zvakazara nevuchenjeri hwenzira dzefirosofi; zvaiita sekutopenga, kurasika pfungwa, izvo zvakanga zvisinga-mbodairwi nemunhu akachenjera kana akadzidza weKorinte.

Nehupenzi hwezvaitaurwa naPauro sekuvona kwevahedheni, nemaJudha akawanda, shoko reMuchinjikwa rakabva ranyanyisavo kusarira. Ndiani muJudha aingagashira Messia aizovurawa nemaRoma? Messia aifanira kupidigura humambo hweRoma, pane kuroverwa kwake navo.

Saka, kubva pakutanga, Pauro akanga anezvakawanda zvaimupikisa muKorinte. Asi kunyange zvakadaro hazvo, mweya—maJudha nevaHedheni—vakagashira vhangeri.

Shoko apa?

Zvisinei nekupikiswa kwatingasangana nako, Mwari anevanhu vake vakagadzirira kugashira zvokwadi. Tinofanira kugadzirira kushandiswa naye kunyange munzvimbo dziriko nhasi dzakaipa kana kupfuura Korinte.
