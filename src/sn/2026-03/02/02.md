---
title:  Vhangeri Yemuchinjikwa
date:  05/07/2026
---

Pauro anoti shoko reMuchinjikwa isimba raMwari kwatiri. Hazvishamisi kuti “Jesu Kristu neMuchinjikwa wake” ndiwo mwoyo wemharizo dzake (1 VaKorinde 2:2).

`Verenga 1 VaKorinde 1:17–31. Nderipi shoko guru iro Pauro anotaura apa?`

Muna 1 VaKorinde 1:18–31, Pauro anoenzanisa pakati pevuchenjeri vupenzi hwevanhu nevuchenjeri hwedenga. Muchinjikwa une simba rekuratidza mutadzi mukuru kwazvo Mwari wakanakisisa. Ndima ye muna 1 VaKorinde inoziviswa nemashoko arimuna 1 VaKorinde 1:17. Nekuti simba reMuchinjikwa waKristu harifaniri kudeurwa (1 VaKorinde 1:17), shoko reMuchinjikwa rinofanira kutora nzvimbo yakakosha mukuparidza kwedu kose (tarisawo 1 VaKorinde 2:2).

Pauro anoti haana kutumwa kunobhapatidza, asi kunoparidza vhangeri yeMuchinjikwa. Mashoko aya anoda kutariswa nenzira mbiri dzakakosha. Yekutanga, izwi rechiGiriki rechiito rinodudzirwa richinzi “kutuma” nderinoti *apostellō*, rinobva paizwi rimwechete rekuti apostora. Saka, kuna Pauro basa remuapostora chairo nderekuparidza. Yechipiri, mashoko aPauro erubhapatidzo akanga asingarevi kuti rubhapatidzo rwakanga rusingakoshi, kana kuti sekuparidza. Asi aidzora avo vaikoshesa zvikuru avo vaibhapatidza achisimudzira Uyo, Jesu wavaibhapatidzwa maari.

Ne “vuchenjeri hwemashoko” (1 VaKorinde 1:17), Pauro akanga asingati kugona kutaura zvakanaka kwakanga kwakaipa pachako. Asi shoko guru nderekuti vuchenjeri hwevanhu haufaniri kudzikatira shoko reMuchinjika. Mashoko aya anotaura nezvinowanikwa mumazwi evuchenjeri emaGiriki-echiRoma. MuAtenisi, Pauro aishandisa pfungwa, sainzi, nevuchenjeri hwepamusoro-soro, asi izvi zvakabudisa michero mishoma-shoma. Saka, “akasarudza kushandisa rimwe zano mubasa rake muKorinte kuti awane vanomuteerera pavanhu vaiva vasina hanya vachirarama zvavaida zvavo. Akadzivirira kupinda munharo dzakareba nehurukuro dzakawanda, nekusada kuziva chimwe chinhu muvaKorinte ‘kunze, kwaKristu neMuchinjikwa wake.’ ”­—Ellen G. White.

`Munzira dzipi umo hurukuro dzakawandisa dzinoratidza vuchenjeri hwevanhu dzichigona kudzikatira Muchinjikwa? Ko nemhaka yei kuparidzwa kwaJesu Kristu neMuchinjikwa kwakapa goho guru muKorinte kupfuura vuchenjeri, sainzi, kana firosofi sezvakaitika muAtenisi? Pane nguva here apo vuchenjeri, nesainzi nefirosofi zvinogona kubatsira mukuparidzwa kwevhangeri?`
