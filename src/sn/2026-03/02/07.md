---
title:  Kudzidza Kuenda Mberi
date:  10/07/2026
---

“Kumazana nemazana evanhu arikurarama izvozvi, Muchinjikwa weKarivhari wakazara nendangariro tsvene. Vukama hutsvene hwezviitiko zvekuroverwa. Asi mumazuva aPauro muchinjikwa waivonekwa sechinhu chanoshoreka nekushamisa. Kusimudzira Muponesi wevanhu vose uyo akasangana nerufu rwemuchinjikwa, zvaingogona kukonzera kushora nekupikisa.

“Pauro ainyatsoziva shoko rake raizotorwa navose vaJudha nemaGiriki eKorinte . . . . Pakati pemaJudha akanga akamuteerera paiva nevamwe vaizotsamwiswa nekuparidza kwake. Mukuvona kwemaGiriki mashoko ake aizongova nhando. Aizovonekwa semunhu asina njere nekuedza kuratidza kuti muchinjikwa waizova nechekuita nezvinosimudzira vanhu kana ruponeso rwevanhu.

“Asi kuna Pauro muchinjikwa waiva chinhu chikuru chinokudzwa. Kubva paakasungwa mubasa rake rekutambudza vateveri veMurume weNazareta haana kumira kufara nekuda kwemuchinjikwa. Kubva panguva iyi akanga azarurirwa rudo rwaMwari rusingaperi, urwo rwakaratidzwa murufu rwaKristu; neshanduko inoshamisa yakanga yaitika muvupenyu hwake, mazano ake ose nezvinangwa zvichiwirirana nezvedenga. . . . Akanga azvivonera kuti kana mutadzi akasangana nerudo rwaBaba, sezvakavonekwa mukuzvipira kweMwanakomana wake, zvinokwevera kuvutsvene, nekushanduka kwemwoyo kuchibva kwaiitika, Kristu ava mavari vose muzvinhu zvose.”—Ellen G. White.

**Mibvunzo Yekutarirana**:

`Mumunda weGetsemane, Jesu akati, “Haiwa Baba vangu kana zvichibvira, mukombe uyu ngaupfuvure kwandiri” (Matt. 26:39). Ko munamato uyu unoti kudini nezvemutengo wakabhadharwa naJesu pamuchinjikwa?`

`Pauro anoti, “Nekuti vupenzi hwaMwari hunopfuvura vanhu nevuchenjeri” (1 VaKorinde 1:25). Munzira dzipi umo vuchenjeri hwaMwari hunopfuvura vuchenjeri hwevanhu?`

`Shoko raKristu akaroverwa raiva chigumbuso kuvaJudha nevupenzi kumaGiriki. Munziri dzipi umo misoro yenyaya iyi inogona kuratidza zvimwechete izvo muvanhu vemazuva ano uye nemhaka yei?`

`Pauro anoti “munhu sezvaakaberekwa haagamuchiri zvinhu zveMweya waMwari” (1 VaKorinde 2:14). Saka, tingataura sei nezvaJesu kuvanhu ava munzira inogona kubata mwoyo yavo? Kana kuti zvatinoita chete zvichavasvikira.`
