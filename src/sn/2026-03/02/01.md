### Verenga Kugadzirira Chidzidzo Chevhiki Ino
1 VaKorinde 1:17–31, VaKorose 1:20, 1 Petro 2:24, Mabasa 13:16–47, 1 VaKorinde 2:1–5.

> <p>Chemusoro:</p>
> “Nekuti shoko remuchinjikwa vupenzi kune vanoparadzwa; asi kwatiri, isu tinoponeswa, isimba raMwari” (1 VaKorinde 1:18).

Sisero munyori wechihedheni weRoma nemutauri mukuru akanga avudza vanhu veRoma kuti vasambofunga nezvemuchinjikwa, nekuti yaiva nzira yakanga isingambotarisirwi kuti munhu aingavurairwapo. Kana nepo Sisero akafa makore 50 Jesu asati azvarwa, mashoko ake anoisa pachena kuzvidzwa kwaiitwa muchinjikwa nemaRoma.

Kune rumwe rutivi Pauro aizonyora achiti: “Shoko remuchinjikwa . . . isimba raMwari (1 VaKorinde 1:18). Nekuti kuna Pauro, muchinjikwa ndiyo nzira yekuyananisa pakati paMwari nevanhu (VaEfeso 2: 16, VaKorose 1:20), chiratidzo chikuru chekuzvininipisa kwaJesu (VaFiripi 2:8), uye panzvimbo iyo chikwereti chedu chikuru chakabhadharwa (VaKorose 2:14).

Muchinjikwa ndiyo mhinduro yaPauro kumatambudziko eKorinte. Haufanira kupinda mukati-kati me1 VaKorinde kuti uvone kuti anehanya yenyaya imwechete: kupesana-pesana mukereke. Pauro anoshamiswa kuti mushure mekungokwaziswa (1 VaKorinde 1:1–3) nendima yekutenda (1 VaKorinde 1:4–9), ndiyo nyaya yekutanga yaanotaura nezvayo (1 VaKorinde 1:10–17). Vhiki ino, tichatarisa kushoko rakasimba reMuchinjikwa semhinduro yedambudziko iri nedzimwe nyaya dzakawanda paKorinte.

_*Verenga chidzidzo ichi kugadzirira Sabata, Chikunguru 11._
