---
title:  Messia Akaroverwa
date:  08/07/2026
---

Pauro akanyora achiti “maJudha anotsvaka zviratidzo maGiriki anotsvaka vuchenjeri” (1 VaKorinde 1:22). Muchinjikwa—pfungwa yaMwari, Messia, achiroverwa—chakanga chisiri chiratidzo chaitarisirwa nemaJudha. Kana kuti hwaiva huchenjeri hwaidiwa nemaGiriki. Zvaipikisana nekutarisira vanhu vose.

Chinotodiwa kuti munhu wose averenge zvakaitwa nevadzidzi kufungwa yekuroverwa kwaJesu (tarisa Marko 8:31, 32; Marko 9:30–32; na Marko 10:32–34) kuti uvone kuti pfungwa iyi yaiva isina kana waiiziva kana kuitarisira, zvikuru sei maJudha. Sezvakataurwa kare, maJudha aitarisira Messia waizokurira vaRoma; asi izvi hazvisi izvo zvakazoitika, zvikuru sei mukuvona kurwisana kwepanyika kuti chii chinonzi “kukurira.”

Kwemazana emakore, muchinjikwa kuMakristu wagara uri chiratidzo chekutenda. Chinhu chakawomera munhu wenguva dzino kunzwisisa pfungwa yaMwari akaroverwa sezvingaita munhu wenguva dzekutanga.

Izvi zvakakonzerwa nekushamisa kukuru kweshoko iri zvingatsvaka kufungisisa. Mufananidzo waMessia akaroverwa unoburitsa pachena kupasi rose kuti Mwari akanga akagadzirira zano reruponeso kusvika kumagumo. Pfungwa yemuchinjikwa pachawo, nekufa kwaShe pamuchinjikwa, inoshamisa kwazvo kwatiri isu vatadzi varipanyika. (Imbofungidzira kuti zvakava sei kuzvisikwa zvisina kumbotadza zvaiziva, nekunamata Ishe Jesu kudenga!)

`Verenga Mabasa 13:16–47 (zvikuru sei mavhesi 26, 38, na47). Ko ndima iyi inotidzidzisei nezvinoreva Muchinjikwa?`

Pauro anoti Kristu akamutuma kunoparidza vhangeri. Saka Pauro achiparidza shoko raMessia akaroverwa (1 VaKorinde 1:23). Anodzokorora pfungwa idzi muna 1 VaKorinde 2:1–5. Muapaostora akatendeka kukutuma kwaKristu. Mukuparidza evangeri, haana kushandisa “kunaka kwemashoko kana vuchenjeri (1 VaKorinde 2:1); asi akasimbira pana “ Jesu Kristu, iye wakaroverwa” (1 VaKorinde 2:2). Kutaura kwake neshoko “zvakakanga zvisiri mumashoko anonakidza kunzwa nevuchenjeri, asi airatidza Mweya neSimba” (1 VaKorinde 2:4) nekuti, zvechokwadi, “vuchenjeri hwevanhu” hunonyatso mira huchipikisana “nesimba raMwari” (2 VaKorinde 2:5).

`Messia wakaroverwa chaiva chinhu chakanga chisingatarisirwi nemaJudha nemaGiriki. Ko izvi zvinotidzidzisei nechokwadi chekuti Mwari havaiti zvatinotarisira nguva dzose? Nemhaka yei iyi iri pfungwa yakakosha kubata, zvikuru sei kana zvinhu zvisiri kufamba sezvataitarisira?`
