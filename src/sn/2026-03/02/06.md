---
title:  Kristu, Simba Nehuchenjeri HwaMwari
date:  09/07/2026
---

Muna 1 VaKorinde 1:19, 20, 30, na31, Pauro anotaura nezvekusiyana kukuru pakati pevuchenjeri hwaMwari nevuchenjeri hwevanhu, saka, pasina kuwirirana. Tarisa, kuti Pauro haasi kuramba vuchenjeri zvachose, asi anoramba mhando yevuchenjeri hunoedza kufanana nehwaMwari. Vuchenjeri hwevanhu haugoni kusunungura munhu kubva muzvivi. Asi Kristu bedzi, vuchenjeri hwaMwari, ndiye anogona kuita basa iri. Tarisa chinyorwa chiripazasi.

|---|---|---|
|Asi kwatiri vekuponeswa|[Shoko reMuchinjikwa] isimba raMwari|*1 VaKorinde 1:18*|
|Asi kune avo vakadanwa|Kristu [i] simba raMwari|*1 VaKorinde 1:24*|

Dzose 1 VaKorinde 1:18 na 1 VaKorinde 1:24 dzinoratidza kuti Kristu isimba raMwari, kureva kuti anesimba rekuponesa vanhu kubva muzvivi zvavo. Hongu, zvakafadza Mwari kuti kuburikidza nevupenzi hweshoko rakaparidzwa kana vose vanotenda” (1 VaKorinde 1:21). Mashoko ekuti “isu tinoponeswa” (1 VaKorinde 1:18), “vanotenda” (1 VaKorinde 1:21), “neavo vakadanwa” (1 VaKorinde 1:24) inotaura nezveboka, rinonzi, vanhu vanorarama muruponeso kuburikidza nekutenda. Vangeri yaKristu . . . isimba raMwari rekuponesa munhu wose anotenda” (VaRoma 1:16).

Kristu haisi simba chete asi vuchenjeri hwaMwari. Izvi zvinoreva kuti kuburikiza naye Mwari akatarisana nekugadzirisa dambudziko rezvivi, dambudziko iro vuchenjeri hwevanhu raiva risina simba rekugadzirisa. Vuchenjeri hwenyika ino haugoni kubatsira vanhu kuziva Mwari (1 VaKorinde 1:21). Saka, kuburikidza naKristu tinova vachenjeri kuruponeso (2 Timoti 3:15).

`Verenga 1 VaKorinde 1:24–29. Tarisa mashoko arimo, akafanana “vupenzi,” “kushaya simba,” “simba,” ne “vuchenjeri.” Ari kuti kudini ko apa?`

Mukuverenga 1 VaKorinde 1:24–29, munhu anofanira kuvonawo mazwi anoshandiswa *vupenzi* nerekuti kushaya simba. Shoko guru apa nderekuti vuchenjeri hwevanhu hunogona kuvona sekuti shoko reMuchinjikwa vupenyu uye risina simba. Asi, “vupenzi hwaMwari hwakachenjera kupfuura hwevanhu, nekushaya simba kwaMwari kunesimba kupfuura vanhu” (1 VaKorinde 1:25). Izvi hazvirevi kuti Mwari haana simba kana kuti haana vuchenjeri, asi mashoko ekuedza kuratidza simba raMwari nevuchenjeri zvinopfuvura zvose zvingafungirwa nevanhu

`Tarisisa mashoko ekuti “havazi vazhinji vakachenjera, vakachenjera panyama vakadanwa” (1 VaKorinde 1:26). Nderipi shoko ratingazviwanira apa?`
