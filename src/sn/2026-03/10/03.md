---
title:  Kutambudzika Nekubwinya
date:  31/08/2026
---

`Verenga 2 VaKorinde 4: 7-18. Nyora njodzi dzose idzo Pauro akasangana nadzo. Akashinga sei mumatambudziko ake?`

John Huss, wekuBohemia mumwe wevakashanda kwavo muRumutsiriro Rukuru, akamboti nezvaJesu, “Ndiye Tenzi wenyika, isu tiri vanhu vanozvidzwa vanofa-asi akatambudzika! Nemhaka yei, saka, kuti isu hatifaniri kutambudzikavo, zvikuru sei kana kutambudzika kwedu kuri kwekutsvaka vutsvene”-Ellen G. White.

Muapostora Pauro akaratidza kare chidiso chekutambudzikira Kristu. Akaziva kuti akanga asiri chinhu kunze kwekungova hari yevhu zvayo (2 VaKorinde 4: 7). Akanzwa achimanikidzwa, achishungurudzwa, achitambudzwa, nekutsikirirwa pasi, asi akanga asina kuparara, asina kupererwa, kana kusiiwa kana kuparadzwa (2 VaKorinde 4: 8, 9). Akanga akagadzirira kutakura “mumuviri wake rufu rwaJesu, kuitira kuti vupenyu hwaJesu “huratidzwe maari” (2 VaKorinde 4: 10, 11).

Nekuti “rufu rwaJesu,” Pauro anogona kuva aitaura nezvekutambudzika kwaakataura nezvako mumavhesi ekumashure. Uyezve, apa, mashoko ekuti “vupenyu hwaJesu” anogona kuva aitaura nezvekudzikinurwa kubva murufu kana kumasimba emweya muvupenyu huno. Asi, pakupedzisira kutaura nezverumuko (2 VaKorinde 4: 12).

Zvinofadza kuvona kuti mashoko ekuti “rufu nevupenyu” anovonekwa katatu muna 2 VaKorinde 4: 10-12. Chirangaridzo chekuti muvupenyu huno, kurarama kunerufu makuri. Asi mumbiri yeremangwana, tichararama pasina rufu (Zvakazarurwa 20: 14; Zvakazarurwa 21: 4).

Zvakanyanya kukosha kuvona kuti 2 VaKorinde 4: 7-18 inoratidza kuti evhangeri inoparidzwa kuburikidza nevanhu vasina simba kuitira kuti mbiri ikwire kuna Mwari chete (2 VaKorinde 4: 15). Kakawanda mamishinari aitambudzika kwazvo mumabasa ekuparidza. Asi kutambudzika kwedu kuduku uye ndekwenguva duku kana kuchienzaniswa nembiri yakatimirira (2 VaKorinde 4: 17). Mutendi unorarama nekutenda, pasina kuvona zvinobatika (2 VaKorinde 4: 18; (2 VaKorinde 5: 7).

Tariro iyi yenguva ichavuya irimupfungwa dzaPauro zvekuti anogara achitaura nezvayo mukufamba kwendima (2 VaKorinde 5: 1-10). Anotaura nezvemuviri wake seimba yevhu. Asi “chiri chivakwa chakabva kuna Mwari” uri mufananidzo wemuviri wakamuka (2 VaKorinde 5: 1), tariro huru yevatendi mumazera ose.

`Nemhaka yei zvakakosha kwazvo, zvisinei nezvingatiwira ikozvino, tinogara tine tariro yerumuko, rumuko rwedu (1 VaKorinde 15: 52) pamberi pedu?`
