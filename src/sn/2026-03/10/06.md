---
title:  Kunyaradzwa Nerufaro
date:  03/09/2026
---

`Verenga 2 VaKorinde 7. Ko Pauro anonzwa sei paakaziva kuti vaKorinte vatendevuka?`

Rudo rwakakura zvakadini runoyerera kubva mumashoko “Muri mumwoyo yedu” (2 VaKorinde 7: 3; tarisavo 2 VaKorinde 6: 11). Muchishuvo chikuru chekuti rudo rwake rudzorerwevo nerudo, Pauro anotizve, “Wanai nzvimbo mumwoyo yenyu yekutida” (2 VaKorinde 7: 2). Nemashoko mashoma ekuti “mumwoyo yenyu” asiri mutsamba yechiGiriki, akawanda echirungu anaro, zviri izvo chaizvo nekuti ndiyo yacho iri mudariro.

Zvechokwadi, vaKorinte vakazarurira Pauro neshamwari dzake mwoyo yavo mubasa. Ndicho chikonzero nei vhesi 4 ichiratidza kuputika nerufaro. Mashoko aPauro anoratidza kufara kwake panguva iyi: “Kusatya kwangu kukuru pakutaura kwamuri, nekuzvirumbidza kwangu kukuru pamusoro penyu; ndizere nekunyaradzwa, ndinofara kwazvo pakutambudzika kwedu kose” (2 VaKorinde 7: 4). Pauro akazadzwa nekunyaradzwa nemufaro. Makereke edu aingadai aine mufaro wakadini kudai vaizvipira kutenda kuna Kristu.

Muna (2 VaKorinde 7: 5-16, Pauro anoenda mberi nekutsanangura chikonzero nei chekunyaradzwa nemufaro wake. Zviitiko zviviri izvi zvakazadza ndima. Izwi rechiito *parakaleo* (“kunyaradza”) kana chizita *paraklesis* (“nyaradzo”) rinovonekwa kanokwana kanomwe muna 2 VaKorinde 7. Ndima iyi inopera sekutanga kwayakaita, nekunyaradzwa muna Mwari (2 VaKorinde 1: 3-7). Kunyaradzwa kwaPauro muna 2 VaKorinde 7 kunobva munezvaakararama nekuda kwetsamba yakavachemedzwa yakaburitsa zvaaitarisira chaizvo nekuda kwayo.

Kana nepo izvi zvakakonzerwa nemashoko aTito akanga akanaka, airatidza kuti ndiMwari chete anowunza kunyaradzwa kwainzwikwa naPauro (2 VaKorinde 7: 6). Mwari zvirokwazvo ndiye “Mwari wekunyaradza, anotinyaradza mumatambudziko edu ose” (2 VaKorinde 1: 3, 4).

Zvinofadza kwazvo kuti, apo Pauro “akazadzwa nekunyaradzwa,” anobva “afara chaizvo” (2 VaKorinde 7: 4, 7, 13). Kana nepo tsamba yake yaichemedza yakakonzera kusuwa kukuru, kwaiva kusuwa kwaiwirirana nekuda kwaMwari nekutendevuka kwaitarisirwa (2 VaKorinde 7: 9-11). VaKorinte “vakachemedzwa sezvinoda Mwari” (2 VaKorinde 7: 11), kuchemedzwa kunokonzera “kutendevuka kunosvitsa kuruponeso” (2 VaKorinde 7: 10) Chii chimwe chaingawunza mufaro waipfuura uyu kumwoyo wemushumiri wezvokwadi waMwari?

`Wakambochemedzwa zvinoda Mwari here muvupenyu hwako? Wakaziva sei kuti kwaiva kuchemedzwa kunowirirana nekuda kwaMwari kwakakutungamirira kukutendevuka?`
