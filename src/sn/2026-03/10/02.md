---
title:  Michero Yehushumiri Hwechokwadi
date:  30/08/2026
---

`Verenga 2 VaKorinde 3: 1-9. Munzira dzipi umo matingava tsamba dzaKristu?`

Tsamba dzekuzivisa nekukurudzira chimiro chemunhu dzakanga dzakajairika munyika YemaGiriki-nemaRoma. Asi Pauro haana kugashira tsamba idzi. Simba rinoshandura muvaKorinte rakava tsamba yehushumiri hwake. Asi Pauro aiziva kuti zvakanga zvisingabvi muhuchenjeri hwake kana kushingaira kwake kwakaita kereke yeKorinte ivepo (2 VaKorinde 3: 4-6). Akanga asimo mukuzvikudza nekuzivisa kugona kwake (2 VaKorinde 3: 5; 1 VaKorinde 2: 2).

Pauro anotaura nezvehushumiri hwake zvishoma-shoma musungano mbiri: yekare yaimiririrwa naMosisi neitsva yaimiririrwa naPauro neshamwari dzake. Muverengi unokasika kuverenga hake anogona kufunga kuti yekare yakanga isingapi tariro yeruponeso, asi ichi hachisi chokwadi. Ruponeso rwaiva muneYekare sezvarwuri muSungano Itsva. Sungano yekare yaiva vhangeri yakatangira kare kumashure. “Rugwaro zvarwakaziva zviri mberi, kuti Mwari ucharuramisa vahedeni nekutenda, rwakaparidza Evhangeri kare kuna Abrahama, ruchiti: Mauri marudzi ose acharopafadzwa” (VaGaratia 3: 8).

Muna 2 VaKorinde 3: 1-4: 6, tinogona kuvona kuti Sungano Yekare inoshandiswa kuratidza dzidziso yekuponeswa nemabasa nekuda kwekuteerera kwavanoita Mwari. Sungano Itsva, sezvineivo, inomiririra avo vanotsamira panaMwari muzvinhu zvavo zvose izvo Mwari anovatarisira kuita.

Pauro arikutaura nezvemhinduro mbiri, nevatendi neyevasingatendi, muvhangeri. Arikutaura nezvemavhangeri maviri, imwe yemuTestamende Yekare neimwe yakasiyana neItsva-nekuti kune vhangeri imwechete, unopiwa naMwari, “uyo wakatiponesa nekutidaidza nerukoko rutsvene, zvisingaitwi nemabasa edu, asi nekuda kwake nenyasha dzake dzatakapiwa muna Kristu Jesu nyika isati yavabwa” 2 Timoti 1: 9).

Uku hakusi kuramba zviri muna 2 VaKorinde 2: 14-4: 6 zvezvinhu zvekare.” Skip MacCarty. Asi Pauro anoshandisa nhoroondo kuburitsa pfungwa yekuti vamwe vavo “varikuponeswa” vamwe “vachiparara” (2 VaKorinde 2: 15). Nekuda kwezvavakaita, kusatenda nekushaya rudaviro mumarushumiro aMosisi, rushumiro rwake runogona kuvonekwa sebasa rekutongera rufu nerufu. Nekuti kereke yepaKotinte yaidaira, basa raPauro kwavari rakaratidza kuti ibasa rekururama, basa raMweya anopa vupenyu.

Kurarama muruponeso kwenhengo dzeKorinte zvaiva chiratidzo chekurevesa kwebasa raPauro.
