---
title:  Kudzidza Kuenda Mberi
date:  04/09/2026
---

“Vhiki yapera, takaverenga mashoko akanyorwa mubhuku rinonzi Mabasa Evaapostor rakanyorwa naEllen G. White. Zvakakosha kuriverenga zvakare. Asi ikozvino, fungisisa nezvendima dzinotaura nezvetsamba yaPauro yaichemedza, zvaakanzwa paakanyora tsamba iyi, nemufaro wake paakagashira shoko rakanaka rezvayakakonzera mukuvatendevutsa kwakaperera. Wobva wafungisisa nezvazvinotivudza nezvehuzvokwadi hwehushumiri hwaPauro semuranda waMwari nezvidzidzo zvatinogona kurarama mubasa ratinoitira Mwari.

“Tinofanira kuratidza varikudenga nepanyika, kunyika yakaputsika neisina kuputsika, kuti kuna Mwari kune kuregererwa, kuti kuburikidza nerudo rwaMwari tinogona kuyananiswa kuna Mwari. Munhu anotendevuka, achirwadziwa pamwoyo, achidaira munaKristu sechibairo chake chinochenesa, nekuziva kuti Mwari ayananiswa naye.”

“Sekereke tagashira chiedza chikuru. Chiedza ichi Tenzi vakavimba nesu kuti chive chikomborero kunyika. Takapiwa basa rekuyananisa. Nesimba rinobva kumusoro tinofanira kutsvakisisa vanhu kuti vayanane naMwari.”

Kana vayananiswa naMwari, vanhu vanofanira kutsvaka hutsvene. Achitsanangura zviri muna 2 VaKorinde 7: 1, Ellen G. White anopa zvishoma nezvaitaurwa naPauro achiti “tikwanise vutsvene pakutya Mwari”(2 VaKorinde 7: 1). Anoti Pauro aitsvaka kubatsira vatendi vatsva “kuti vakwanisa kuzvimirira, vakure seMakristu, vakasimba pakutenda, vachishingairira basa, nemwoyo unorevesa mukuzvipa kuna Mwari nekubasa rekuparidza hushe kwake.” Ellen G. White.

**Mibvunzo Yekutaurirana**:

`Pauro anoti isu “tiri midziyo yevhu” inefuma yevhangeri (2 VaKorinde 4: 7). Ko chokwadi chekuti chimiro chevanhu hachina simba, chinehundonda, chakazara nekukundikana chichishoresa kuparidzwa kwevhageri?`

`Ko zvinorevei kuva “zvisikwa zvitsva” (2 VaKorinde 5: 17)? Ko izvi zvingabata sei vupenyu hwedu hwezuva nezuva? Ko Kristu akakuita chisikwa chitsva sei?`

`Muna 2 VaKorinde 6: 4, 5, Pauro anonyora njodzi dzakawanda nekuda kwevhangeri. Akaitei mukushungurudzwa kwake uku (tarisa 2 VaKorinde 6:6, 7).`

`Pauro anoenzanisa kusuwa kwehumwari nekwenyika (2 VaKorinde 7: 10). Munzira dzipi umo kusuwa kunogona kuva nevukama nekutendevuka? Ungatsanangura sei kuchemedzwa sezvinoda Mwari kana kuchienzaniswa nekwekuda nyika?`
