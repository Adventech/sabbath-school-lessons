---
title:  Basa Rakadzika Midzi MunaKristu Rekuyananisa
date:  01/09/2026
---

`Verenga 2 VaKorinde 5: 11-15. Ko ndima iyi inoratidza sei kuti kushanda kwaPauro kwaiva kwakatsamira pana Kristu chete?`

Pauro aiziva kuti aifanira kuzomira pamberi paKristu nekuda kwebasa rake (2 VaKorinde 5: 10). Aiziva kuti “kutya JEHOVA” nekutsvaka kuwana vazhinji nevhangeri yaKristu (2 VaKorinde 5: 11). Kutya uku kuremekedza nekurumbidza Kristu saka, zvakabatanidzwa nerudo rwaPauro kuna Kristu nekuvimba murudo rwaKristu kwaari. MuTestamende Yekare, kutya Jehova zvinoreva kufamba munzira dzake, kumuda, nekumushandira nemwoyo wose nemweya wose (Deuteronomio 10: 12).

Basa raPauro harisi rekuzviitira asi kuitira Kristu. Haana kuzvirumbidza kana kuzviisa pamberi. Chikonzero chekuzvirumbidza chaiva muna Kristu (2 VaKorinde 12: 9). Akati, “Hazvisi mandiri kuti ndizvikudze asi mumuchinjikwa waIshe wedu Jesu Kristu” (VaGaratia 6:14). Saka, mukana wevaKorinte wekuzvikudza nekuda kwake (2 VaKorinde 5: 12) zvinoreva kuzvikudza nekuda kwebasa raiva rakatsamira panaKristu risina kutsamira paari nevaimupikisa.

`Verenga 2 VaKorinde 5: 16-21, VaKorose 1: 19-23 na VaEfeso 2: 13-16. Ko Pauro airevei achiti “rushumiro rwekuyananisa”?`

Kristu ndiye rushumiro rwekuyananisa rwakaperera. Saka, Iye “akatipa basa rekuyananisa” (2 VaKorinde 5: 18). Pfungwa yekuyananisa inovonekwa nguva nenguva muna 2 VaKorinde 5: 16-21. Iyi ipfungwa yakakosha kuna Pauro, saka ichifanira kukoshavo kwatiri.

Mwari akayananisa vanhu kwaari nerufu rweMwanakomana wake rwekudzikinura. Avo vakayananiswa naMwari vava zvisikwa zvitsva (2 VaKorinde 5: 17). Zvino vava kutarisirwa kufambisa “shoko iri rekuyananisa” mberi nekuparidza vhangeri yaKristu (2 VaKorinde 5: 19). Saka, “tiri nhume dzaKristu, Mwari seanenge achiyananisa nyika kuburikidza nesu” (2 VaKorinde 5: 20).

`Funga zvawakaitirwa naKristu. Funga nemhosva, chitema, nekufanira kutongwa kwako asi nekuda kwezvaakakuitira pamuchinjikwa. Ko idi rinokukonzera kubata vamwe vanhu sei, zvikuru sei avo vasingazivi Ishe?`
