---
title:  Kudaidzirwa Kuhutsvene
date:  02/09/2026
---

Muna 2 VaKorinde 6: 3-10, Pauro anoramba achikurudzira vaKorinte kuti vayanane naMwari. Anotaura matambudziko akawanda nekukurira kunoratidza zvazvinoreva kuva muteveri waKristu nemushumiri waMwari. Nemazwi mapfupi, anonyora zvimiro zvakawoma (2 VaKorinde 6: 4, 5), zvimiro zvakanaka (2 VaKorinde 6: 6), simba rebasa (2 VaKorinde 6: 7), shanduko dzinowanikwa mubasa (2 VaKorinde 6: 8-10). Apedza kuraira nhengo dzepaKorinte kuti dzayanane naMwari, Pauro anovakokera kuvupenyu hutsvene, uye mukuzviita izvi vachizviparadzanisa netsika dzinokuvadza dzevasingatendi nekune zvinhu zvisina kuchena (2 VaKorinde 6: 14-17).

`Verenga 2 VaKorinde 6: 11-7: 1. Kana takatarisa pandima iyi, vupenyu hutsvene hwakaita sei?`

Mundima iyoi Pauro anosimbisa kukosha kwerudo nekuvimbika mukereke (1 VaKorinde 6: 11-13). Chinoratidza kuti vanhu vanhu vakayanana naMwari kuyanana pakati pavo. Zvechokwadi vanobva vava nhume dzekuyananisa pakati pevanhu.

Tevere rukoko rwekuva vatsvene runopiwa mumarukoko matanhatu, anoti, (1) Musasunganidzwa zvisina kufanira nevasingatendi (2 VaKorinde 6: 14); (2) “Ibvai pakati pavo” (2 VaKorinde 6: 17); (3) Ivai vakasiyana” (2 VaKorinde 6: 17); (4) “Musabata chinhu chinetsvina” (2 VaKorinde 6: 17); (5) “Ngatizvichenesei kubva kune zvose zvinosvibisa muviri nemweya” (2 VaKorinde 7: 1) (6) Kwanisai hutsvene hwenyu mukutya Mwari (2 VaKorinde 7: 1). Kurudziro idzi dzinoratidza kuti Mwari mutsvene anotsvaka hupenyu hutsvene nekusiya kunamata zvifananidzo.

Kune rumwe rutivi, ndima iyi inopawo zvitsidzo zvinomwe zvinoratidza basa rekereke yeChikristu setemberi tsvene: (1) “Ndichagara mavari”, (2) “ndichafamba pakati pavo, (3) “Ndichava Mwari wavo”, (4) “Vachava vanhu vangu”, (5) “Ndichakugashirai”, (6) “Ndichava Baba kwamuri”, (7) “Muchava vanakomana nevanasikana vangu”, (2 VaKorinde 6: 16, 17, 18).

Hona kuti zvitsidzo zvina muna 2 VaKorinde 6: 16 ndizvo zvinogadzirira rairo nhatu dzinotofanira kuitwa dziri muna 2 VaKorinde 6: 17 (tarisa izwi rekuti “saka” panotangira 2 VaKorinde 6: 17). Izvi zvinoratidza kuti vutsvene haubvi musimba remunhu asi raMweya Mutsvene mumwoyo. Kana nepo hutsvene huchibva kuna Mwari, vatendi vanofanira kuita rutivi rwavo nekuramba kunamata zvifananidzo nedzimwe tsika dzose dzakaipa.

`Ko zvitsidzo zvaMwari zviri muna 2 VaKorinde 6: 16-18 zvinotivudzei nezvekuti vutsvene chii?`
