`Parapea 1:1-3-rangko poraibo. Johan maiko dakna okamako man∙aha?`

Skangni Kristianrang Jisuni re∙bapilanina ku∙rachakanio janggi tangbaaha. Watata Johan Jisuni aramona doangako nikaha aro Ua apsan bewalon re∙bapilgen ine agananikoba knaaha (Watata. 1:10, 11). Da∙ade skangni mondolini u∙igijaniko chagrongaha. Beben, Jisu re∙bapilgen. Indiba basako?

Patmos chichango watbrangatako man‧engmitingo, Jisuni gnigipa re∙bapilanina ku∙rachakani gimin Johan seaha: “Nibo, Ua aramrango re∙baenga, aro pilak mikron, aro Uko su∙giparang Uko nikgen” (Para.1:7). Bon∙kamani somoini chin-ni giminba Johan apalbate parape∙atako man∙aha. Ge∙sni mondolirangko ka∙dongatani aro manengani baksana (Para.2, 3), bichalni gimin nikani (Para. 14:6-12), a∙gilsakni bon∙ani (Para.20), Kristoni gnigipa re∙baani (Para.14:14-16) aro a∙gilsakko gital daktaiani (Para.21, 22) gimin Parape‧ao man‧chapaha.

Johan an∙tangko “Na∙simangni jongada duk man∙a aro songnoko aro chakchikao na∙simang baksa Jisuo bak man∙pagipa” (Para.1:9) ine talataha. Ua saknaani maidake ong∙achim uko ma∙siaha, sengsoanio duknisan ong∙aija, indiba Isolni Kattako skiprakanina aro Jisu Kristoni Sakki donganina a∙rik sanalanikoba.

Parape‧a jolgimikon Johanara nichina okamako man‧aha aro an∙tangni nikgiminko sechina ge∙etaniko man∙aha. Bon∙kamgipa odhaio “Aro anga Johan iarangko knagipa aro nikgipa” (Para.22:8) ine seaha. Bon∙kamgipa salrangni obostarangko katchinike sakki on‧gipa Johan ong∙achim, jean nikanio una maiarangko mesokahachim uarangko uni somoini mondolirangna sualchina ge∙etaniko man∙aha (Para.22:16). Ua Johan Napbolatgipako gisik ra∙atani ong∙a, jean a∙gilsakni itihaso nangchongmotgipa somoio Jisuni skanggipa re∙baaniko skiprakaha (Johan 1:6-8).

An∙chingba Johan gita sakki on∙china okamako man∙giparang ong∙a. An∙chingba Kristoni Sakkiko rakkigipa ong∙na nanga (Para.19:10)—je sakkikon Ua An∙tangni gimin katchinikgiparangchi on∙aha. Johan gita an∙ching nikanirangko man∙jaoba, indiba an∙ching Jisuni re∙baaniko chakchike sengsomitingo Uni kamko an∙chingni janggi tanganirango mesoke aro an∙chingko jokatani chinrangko nie sakkirangko on∙na man∙a.

`An∙chingna on∙gimin pilak seng∙anirangko chanchiatbo. An∙chingna bang∙e on∙ani gimin maidakgipa daitorang an∙chingni kosako dongenga?`
