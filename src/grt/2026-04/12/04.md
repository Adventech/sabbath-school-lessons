Ia on‧anirang an‧tangtango mitelgrikanirang donggipa ong‧a.

`Ephesus 4:11-14-rangko poraibo. Isol maina ia on‧anirangko Uni mondolina on‧aha? Katchinike agananiko on‧ani maidake mondolina Isolni mangsonganiko chu‧sokatna dakchakaha?`

Pilak gisikni on‧anirangni mangsongani mondoliko dal‧atna aro rikna on‧gimin ong‧a, aro nama kattako skiprakaninaba on‧aha. Paul, gisikni on‧anirangko mingbri serikanio napataha. Gipin on‧anirang changsasan sokbagipa ong‧a. Ia napatgiparangni ong‧ronganiko niate, katchinike aganania gisikni on‧anirangoni mingsa namen nangchongmotgipa ong‧a ine an‧ching aganchotna man‧gen.

Hai, an‧ching katchinikgiparangni gimin on‧gimin cholonrangko niatna:

1. Katchinikgiparangko Isol okamaha (1 Raja.19:16, 19-21; Isaia 6). Uamang pangnan an‧tangtangko gamchate nikjaha aro on‧gimin daitoko ra‧na sikjaoba, indiba uamang katta maniaha.
2. Isol katchinikgiparangna nikanichi, jumanrangchi ba knana man‧gipa ku‧rangchi aganaha. Basakobade Isol mikkangchi ong‧gnini gimin parape‧ataha. Gipin somoio, Ua da‧alo a‧songna ba manderangna ong‧enggipa obostani gimin u‧iataha (Dan.2).
3.  Sastroo be‧enna ong‧gniko talataha: katchinikgiparang be‧enni bilrangni gita bilgria ba rang‧sitaniko dakja, indiba uamang gitalgipa bilchi gapatako man‧aba donga (Dan.10:8-10, 18, 19).
4. Katchinikgiparang uamangni kattako dingtang dingtang bimango u‧itaniko daka—kattachi (Watata.21:10-12), be‧enni bakrangchi dakmesokanichi (Ezek.4), ba chittirangchi (Para.2, 3). Badiaba katchinikgiparang seanio dakchakgipako jakkalaha, jedake Baruk, jean Jeremiako dakchakaha (Jer. 36:1-4).
5. Katchinikgipani katta gitcham katchinikgiparang baksa meligipa ong‧na nanggen, maina Isol An‧tangko bikpilgrikatgipa ong‧ja (Isaia 8:20).
6. Katchinikgipani kattao donggipa aro uni janggi tanganio dakmesokani, ong‧baani gimin sakki ong‧gipa ong‧a (Mati 7:15-20).
7. Satan pangnan Isolko bilpilatna krenga. Ia apsanan katchinikaniko on‧aninaba kakket ong‧aha. Bon‧kaman somoio, uano bang‧a tol‧a katchinikgiparang donggen (Mati 24:24; 2 Pitor 2:1).
