### Antigimiko Poraiani
Joel 2:28-32; Watata. 2:1-21; Eph. 4:11-14; Para. 12:17; Para. 19:10; Watata. 2:36-42.

> <p>Mingani Pod</p>
> “Aro uni ja‧mano indake ong‧gen, pilak be‧enni kosako Anga Angni Gisikko rudapgen; aro na‧simangni depanterang aro na‧simangni demechikrang katchinike agangen, na‧simangni budeparang jumangrangko nikgen, na‧simangni panterang nikanirangko nikgen; aro ua salrango nokkolrangni aro nokkol me‧chikrangni kosakoba Anga Angni Gisikko rudapgen” (Joel 2:28, 29).

Badiaba a‧songni niam tarianio “sal napangona tariani” ingipa dongachim. Ia kattara sal napangani baksade mamung bakde dongtaijachim. Indiba ian badiaba niamo ra‧gatgimin tik somoio an‧tangan gimaanggipa dongani giminsa ong‧skaachim. Bang‧a a‧selrangna indakgipa kattarangko a‧song ba sorkari niamo dona. Toatna gita, uamang indakgipa dingtanggipa niamrangko a‧songna kengni ong‧on a‧songni manderangni bilko komiatna tariaha. A‧song badiaba tariko “sal napangona tariani” niamko ra‧gate uamangni manderangni kan‧dikgipa somoina ra‧anggako man‧gipa bilko on‧taipilanikoba dakaha.

Pagitchamrangni somoio Gisik Rongtalgipani katchinike agananiko on‧anioba sal napangona tarianikoba Isol man‧chapatahama? Jisuni re‧bana skang ia on‧ani bon‧changani somoi banoba dongama? Re‧anggipa antio an‧ching Isol nangchongmotgipa somoirango Uni manderangna katchinikgiparangchi aganaha ine poraiman‧aha. Ia antio, bon‧kamgipa somoio katchinikaniko on‧ani gimin an‧ching porika ka‧e nianggen—uni dingtangmanchagipa kam ka‧ani aro dongkamgipa ong‧aniko—aro Jisuni gnigipa re‧baani sepangbatroroengon Ellen G. White-ni katchinikanio dangdike on‧anikoba niatgen.
