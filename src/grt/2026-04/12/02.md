`Joel 2:28-32-rangko poraibo. Bon‧kamgipa salrango Isol maiko dakna ku‧rachakaha?`

Joel, snigipa ritcha bilsi B.C.-ni katchinikgipa, mikkangchi ong‧gnini gimin nangchongmotgipa ma‧siataniko on‧aha. An‧ching ia seaniko poraion, ua an‧tangni somoioniko aganama ba chel‧begipa mikkangchikon agansoama an‧ching mairongpilman∙a.

Sastroni gimin poraigiparang ba skie ra‧giparang rokom gni katchinike agananirangni gisepo dingtanggrikatna jotton ka‧aha—itihasni gita aro bon‧kaman somoini gita. Itihasni gita katchinike aganania bilakgipa a‧songrangni gimin agana aro chong∙motgipa bimung aro nomborrangko jakkala(mesokna gita, Jer. 25:1-11-ko nibo). Minggipin dake, bon‧kaman somoini gimin katchinike aganania, chinrangchi agangipa ku‧sikko bang∙e jakkalaha, chin-ni nomborrangko somoirangna jakkala aro bang∙bata changon a∙gilsak gimiko ong∙gipa chinrangko janapronga (Dan.7-ko nibo). Basakobade an‧ching ia rokom gni katchinike agananirangni brinako man‧aniko nika (Mati 24).

Joel 2:28-31-ni giminara maiko agangen? Iako gnigipa dolo ong∙a gita nika. Iani ortode indake ong∙a je katchinikani chu∙sokaniko an∙chinga katchinikgipani janggi tangani somoion chu∙sokaniko nikna ka∙dongna nangja ba uni kam ka∙ani ja∙manomangba rang∙san chu∙sokaniko nisengsona nangja. Indakani palo, an∙chinga mikkangchi ia katchinikani chu∙sokanina niatna nanga. Ia katchinikaniko Isolni on∙ani somoiko namedake nibo—“Jihovani dal∙gipa aro kenbegnigipa sal re∙bana skang” (Joel 2:31). Uara badia sal? Jisuni skanggipa re∙baanima? Ma gnigipa re∙baani? Jensalo an‧ching Joelni katchinikanio sal aro jajongo chinrangko janapengon, iarang apsankon gipin Sastroni bakrango seanikoba nikna man‧a ine rang‧san ma‧sianiko man‧gen. Jisu An‧tang sninggiparangna sal, jajong aro askirango chinrangni gimin Uni bon‧kamgipa mingsinggipa skiprakanio aganaha (Mati 24:29). Apsan dake, Johan parape∙gipa ia apsan chinrangko aganaha (Para. 6:12, 13). Jisu aro Johan sakgnian an‧chingni Gitelni gnigipa re‧bana skang a∙gilsako ong‧gnigipa bon‧kamgipa chinrangko miksongaha.

Indake an‧ching Joelni katchinikanio apsan chinrangko mesokanio bon‧kaman somoirango maioba nangegipa ong‧a ine ma‧sisoe ra‧na man‧a.

“Aro salgirango aro a‧ao aiao inmananirangko, an∙chi, aro wa‧al, aro wal‧kuni krongrangko anga mesokgen. Jihovani dal‧gipa aro kenbegnigipa sal re‧bana skang, sal andalpilgen aro jajong an‧chi pil‧gen.” (Joel 2:30, 31).
