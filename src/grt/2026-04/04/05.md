`Luk 2:36-38 aro Wat. 21:9 ko poraibo. Me∙chik katchinikgiparangni gimin uaranga an∙chingna maiko agana?`

Niam Gitchamni somoirango, Isol me∙asa aro me∙chikrangkoba Uni kattako ra∙angtimgiparang ong∙china gita okamaha. Niam Gitalni somoirango, me∙chik katchinikgiparangni gimin rongtalen sakkiko man∙a. Niam Gitalo Anna-kon skanggipa “me∙chik katchinikgipa” ine mingaha. Uan uni janggi tanganio chu∙gimik Isolna dangdike on∙achim, jean “okumu chake aro mol∙mole, wal sal olakkirongachim. Wat. 21:9 o indine agana je Philipo “demechik me∙tra sakbri gnangchim uamang katchinikgipa” ong∙achim. Niam Gitalni itihasko segiparang, jekai Eusebius, uamang bano dongachim aro uamangko bano gopachim uko janapgipa podrangko ja∙mano nikaha. Je ong∙oba, an∙chingni nikgiparanga dambe me∙chikrang ong∙a, jemangan katchinikani gisikko man∙achim. Uamang maiko katchinike aganahachim aro uamangni kamara maidake ong∙achim uarangko an∙ching namedake ma∙sija. Indioba, katchinike agananiko man∙gipa ia dambe me∙chikrangni gimin an∙ching mingsako ma∙sia je uamangni katchinike aganani katta mingsakoba Sastroo sejaha. Chong‧motan, bang‧a sakgipinrang gitan, uamanga Sastroko segijagipa katchinikgiparang ong‧achim, chong‧mot katchinikgiparang jemangni kattarangko, seanirangko ba jekoba Sastroni ki·taprangona dakatako man‧jahachim.

Me‧chik katchinikgiparang baksana, dambe pamongrangba watatarang baksa chapchap ong‧e nangrime kam ka‧aha. Dambe dilgiparangko watatarang kamna skiaha. Mesokna gita, Paul Timothyko “bebera‧ao dechongmotgipa” (1 Tim. 1:2; 1 Tim. 4:12) ine aganaha. Pamongni kamni gimin mongsonggiparangko Paul Timothyna skiaha. Titus 1:4 o, Paul Titusko “apsan bebera‧anini gimin dechongmotgipa” ine aganaha. Ra‧biani ong‧gipa dol, Kreteo donggipa bebera‧giparangna dilgiparangko songani rakgipa kamko Paul Titusna on‧aha. Chadambe Titusna ua ia nangchongmotgipa daitoko maikai dakna nanggenchim uani gimin toromni gita ku‧pattianiko on‧aha, unikode bon‧kame indine aganaha: “Iarangko aganbo aro mol‧molbo aro pilak bilchi manengbo.
