“Aroba, Aganprakgipa gisik gnanggipa ong∙ani gimin, ua u∙ianiko manderangna skikuaha; oe, chanchibewalaha aro am∙aha, aro toe skianirangko namedake donaha. Kusi ong∙atani kattarangko, aro sronge segiminko, chong∙motan bebeni kattarangko Aganprakgipa man∙na ine am∙aha” (Agan. 12:9, 10).

Uni segimin pilak toe skianirangko Solomon an∙tangoniko bikote a∙bachenge sejaha ine mesokna gita ia podrangko poraina man∙gen. Isolni Gisikni dilanichi, ua bang∙a agananirangko “am∙aha” aro “kusi ong∙atani kattarangko” man∙na am∙aha. Bebeko aganode, Toe Skiani 31-o janapgipa gun gnanggipa me∙chikko talatanikoa Lemuel Rajani ma∙gipani agangipa kattarangsa, Solomonni ong∙ja ine agana (Toe Skia. 31:1). Toe Skianirangko seanio Egyptni sea-jotanirangchi salangako man∙anikoba sakkirang mesoka.

Apsandaken, Niam Gitchamni Isolni Gisikchi dilaniko man∙gipa itihasko segiparang an∙chingna indake agana je Isolni manderangni golpoko kakket ong∙e aganna gita uamang segimin ki∙taprangko rakkie dongiminrangko aro gipin itihasrangko jakkalaha.

`1 Raja. 11:41; Luk 1:1-4; aro Juda 14, 15 –rangko poraibo. Basakobade Isolni Gisikchi dilaniko man∙gipa segiparanga Sastroni ong∙gijagipa seanirangko jakkalani gimin ia podranga an∙chingna maiko agana?`

Gisik Rongtalgipachi dilaniko man∙gipa Sastroko segipa jean Kristoni janggi tangani gimin segiminrang aro ku∙sikarichi agangiminrangko gisiko nange bimchipe poraiaha, aro indake chimonge man∙giminrangko dondime golpoko seaha.”—The SDA Bible Commentary, vol. 5, jak 669.

Judakoba 1 Enok 1:9-oniko segata gita nika—ian Sastroni bak ong∙gijagipa (non-canonical), jean Niam Gitcham aro Niam Gitalni gisepo donggipa somoio Jihudini segimin seani ong∙a. Uamangni bimungrangko janapgija, Paulba Greek philosopherrangni segiminrangoniko segataha (Wat. 17:28 aro Titus 1:12).
